# The Black Stallion

> Seventeen hands of winter muscle with a scarred muzzle and old rope-burns around his neck. The horde calls him "the black one." He chooses his rider in the Trial of the Horse.

## Properties
- **Warhorse** (MM p. 340). The player names him.
- He will not carry a rider he does not respect. *"Try to stay respectable."* (Sarnai)
- **The black one's choice** (for the rest of SP-PBS-01):
  - Help arrives on round 1 of the Scene 5 attack.
  - Sarnai grants one council reroll after any failed check.
  - At Favor 4 after the trials, Sarnai invokes his choice and it counts as Favor 5.

## History
*Homebrew.* Someone caught him once and he remembers it. He ate the last priest's mule, according to Chuluun.
