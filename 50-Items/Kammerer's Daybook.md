# Kammerer's Daybook

> Hauptmann Lorenz Kammerer's campaign daybook from 480 CY. It records the release, the halved standard, the gorge, and the oath of silence.

## Properties
- *Document.* Held by [[Feldwebel Jost Kammerer]], who cannot show it while his oath stands.
- **Released by:** Konrad lifting the oath ("whatever my house bound your family to"), or a PC beating Jost in the bout under Kord (*"Kord says I must."*).
- **What it says:** Wolfram released the Hundred to hold the Kalte Klamm for three hundred people, stayed on his wall, and swore the captain to silence "until the head of my house releases you."

## History
*Homebrew.* Passed from Lorenz to his son, then to Jost.

## Continuity
