# The Urn of Saint Merrow

> A plain grey funerary urn holding the ashes of the patron of the quiet dead.

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

## Properties
The urn holds up to 6 handfuls of the saint's ash. When found in [[SP-OP01-What-the-Dead-Fear]] it holds 1d4 + 2. It gains 1 handful each year on the saint's day.

- **Rest.** As an action, you scatter a handful of ash on an undead within 5 feet of you that is willing or is CR 1 or lower. It's destroyed, crumbling to dust at peace.
- **Quiet grave.** As an action, you scatter a handful on a corpse. That corpse can never rise as undead.

## History
Saint Merrow's followers built Merrow's Rest around the urn. The drunaghul couldn't bear to touch it, so it hired the party to carry it out.

## Found In
- [[SP-OP01-What-the-Dead-Fear]]: on the altar of the Saint's Ossuary (Scene 03). Ossian's stated reason for the job.

## Continuity
A reward the party keeps. Useful against any later larder.
