# Torc of the Hound-Mother

> A green-bronze neck-ring, thick as a thumb, cut with a triple spiral, and cold to the touch at any hour.

## Properties
- **Binding.** While it rests on Kethra's breast, her pack sleeps. While it is anywhere else, the pack is awake and hunting for it.
- **The Pull.** On the Dreadverge, carrying the torc adds +1 to the Howl each leg. In combat, the bone beasts prefer its bearer as a target, and the bearer has disadvantage on Stealth checks against them.
- **Cold.** It never warms on a living body. It warms the moment it touches Kethra.
- **Value:** 250 gp (marks) to a Schwartzenbruin collector of Flan antiquities.
- *History or Religion DC 13:* it is a binding-ring, laid on the dead. It was never meant to be worn.

**Curse (if kept or sold after the adventure).** No attunement is required, and *remove curse* ends it only if the torc is then returned. The bearer, or the last PC to profit from it, hears clicking at the edge of every campfire. Each time that PC finishes a long rest outdoors, roll a d20. On a 1, a pack of 1d4 + 1 bone hounds arrives before dawn.

## History
*Homebrew.* It was laid on Kethra's breast at her burial. [[Pell-Marsh]] stole it in Goodmonth 576 CY and sold it to [[Ysolde-the-Tinker]] for 12 kleinmarks (sp).

## Continuity
It is the key to the climax. Returning it lays the pack to rest for good. If it is kept, the curse makes it a recurring liability.
