# Omen-Taper of the Blind Spot

> A thin grey candle twisted with a strand of black thread, smelling of beeswax and juniper.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, common (consumable).* As an action, you light the taper. It burns for 1 hour, then is used up.
- Its flame gutters and goes out if it comes within 30 feet of an area or creature that is hidden from divination: the [[The Gaoler's Link|Gaoler's Link]], *nondetection*, [[Blind Hour]], and similar. It can be relit, but it goes out again until it is moved away.
- While lit, it also flickers toward any invisible magical scrying sensor within 10 feet of it.
- The Archive's no-flame rule applies, and Verenwyr will smell it.

## History
Lecturer Perenna Dolz, Divination & Omenry, makes these for her omen classes. She lends one to students who ask the right question about the fog in her scrying. *Homebrew.*

## Found In
- SP-SQ-01 — The Gaoler's Link: Dolz's lead in Scene 3 (Legwork)
- SP-SQ-02 — Tethers: a box of six on loan for the mole hunt (it flickers at the sleepers' handler's Tether's Eye)

## Continuity
Dolz can make more (25 credits each for students, or 50 gp to outsiders). She keeps a record of who borrows them.
