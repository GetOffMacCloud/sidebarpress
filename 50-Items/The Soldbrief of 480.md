# The Soldbrief of 480

> The original contract between Ritter Wolfram von Eisfeld and the Hundred, binding the company to hold the Eisfeld manor for a season. Without it, there is nothing to strike through and no amends can be sworn.

## Properties
- *Document.* Old vellum in the Hundred's document case, with both seals.
- **The ceremony:** it must be presented before the three witnesses and the Council's clerk and **struck through** before the midnight bell (see [[SP-PBS-03 — Cord, Temper and Trust]]).
- **Stolen:** Little Fenna lifts the case in round 2 of the brawl. At the yard, Dornhalm tries to burn it in his stove on round 3. A PC within reach can snatch it (Dex DC 12; on a failure it is singed but legible).
- **If it burns:** Ilse can swear on the company's copy from [[The Hundred's Chapter-House]], at the cost of a Bell segment.

## History
*Homebrew.* Signed in 480 CY. Struck through at the end of the adventure: *discharged with honour, 480 CY.*
