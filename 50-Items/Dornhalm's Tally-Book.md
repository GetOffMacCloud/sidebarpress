# Dornhalm's Tally-Book

> The timber factor's ledger. Between the board-feet and the river freight, it lists what he paid to ruin a Ritter.

## Properties
- *Evidence.*
- **The entries:** *"Brack — 10 marks — the Ritter's cord. Fenna — 3 marks."*
- **Tucked inside:** a letter from [[The Kettenhaus]] inviting Dornhalm & Söhne to bid on the Eisfeld timber "should the instrument mature." The letter is ordinary, innocent business.
- **Use:** shown to Rudi Brack, it makes him name Dornhalm. Handed to the patrol sergeant, it sees Dornhalm taken for "theft of a sworn instrument."
