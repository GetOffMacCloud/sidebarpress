# The False Wolf Arrows

> Arrows made to pass for Wolf Nomad arrows, found in the Tiger dead. Tsolmon's arrow, which Temur keeps in his sash, matches the ones at the ravine.

## Properties
- *Mundane evidence.*
- **The tell:** grey goose fletching that looks Wolf-style at a glance. But the fletching is **glued**, where Wolf Nomads bind theirs with sinew in a crisscross, and the grey is **paint over brown feather**. Investigation or Survival DC 13. A PC who studied fieldcraft or history recognises the sinew bind without a roll.
- **With them:** a **black-steel arrowhead** in a dead rider's hand ("too well-made for steppe work"). It matches the blades Kezrin sells to Temur's riders. An Ekbiri silver coin lies by the bodies (Investigation DC 15).
- **At the council:** if a PC asks Temur to lay Tsolmon's arrow beside the ravine arrows, Round 2 is won automatically. If Sarnai holds the evidence, she lays them on the felt herself.

## History
*Homebrew.* Made by Kezrin's men for the false-flag ambush on Tsolmon's patrol. The sinew-binding tell is Sidebar Press invention, since canon gives no Wolf fletching details (see [[Wolf-Nomads]]).
