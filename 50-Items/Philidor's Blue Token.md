# Philidor's Blue Token

> A bead of deep blue glass on a leather thong. Look into it long enough and it seems to be looking somewhere else.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement).* As an action, you can cast *nondetection* on yourself from the token, with no material component. The spell lasts 1 hour instead of 8. Once you use this property, you can't use it again until the next dawn.
- While attuned, you know when a scrying sensor is created within 10 feet of you, though not where it is.

## History
Philidor the Blue Wizard, who watches Iuz from the Vesve (WGR5 pp. 6, 21, 88), gives these to those who bring him something worth hiding. He has given very few. *The item is homebrew*; Philidor is canon. *Ruling (Greybeard, 2026-11-05):* Philidor is the keeper the party delivers the Link to in SP-SQ-04.

## Found In
- SP-SQ-04 — The Quiet Cargo: Philidor's gift on delivery, one token per PC

## Continuity
Philidor knows each token and can find its bearer if he chooses. That is a hook for later play.
