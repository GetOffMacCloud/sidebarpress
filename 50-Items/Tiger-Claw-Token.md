# Tiger-Claw Token

> A large tiger claw on a braided leather cord, scrimshawed with tiny tiger-stripe marks. Ilkhan Cligir wears it on his wrist until he gives it away.

## Properties
- *Wondrous item, uncommon (no attunement).*
- **The Tiger's mark.** While worn, the wearer has advantage on Charisma checks made to interact with Baklunish nomads.
- **At the council (SP-PBS-01).** Round 1 Persuasion DC drops from 14 to 12.
- **Safe passage (Plains of Blood and Steel Part 2+).** Chakyik riders let the wearer and companions pass through their camps unharmed.

> **Cligir:** *"The Tiger's mark. Wear it while you speak, and my riders will know you kept faith in winter."*

## History
*Homebrew.* Cligir took it from a tiger he killed in his youth.

## Found In
- [[SP-PBS-01-The-False-Arrow]] — given by [[Ilkhan-Cligir]] at Favor 7+, when he grants the party voice ([[Scene 07 — The War Council]])

## Continuity
Record who holds it. It loses its safe-passage property if the holder breaks faith with the Chakyik.
