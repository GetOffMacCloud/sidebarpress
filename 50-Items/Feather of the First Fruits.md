# Feather of the First Fruits

> Old Gobbler's last gift: a slate-grey feather as long as a sword, warm to the touch.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement).* Once every 7 days, the attuned creature can cast *heroes' feast* without material components. The feast it conjures always includes cranberries.

## History
Homebrew. Left behind when the Gluttonwretch is laid to rest by the First Fruits.
