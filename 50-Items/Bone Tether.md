# Bone Tether

> A pin carved from a finger-bone, as long as a sewing needle, yellow-white and faintly warm.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon. No attunement.* It is created by the [[Tether]] spell.
- **Planting.** Within 7 days of its making, any creature can plant the tether on a humanoid that is charmed by the planter. Planting it means pushing it into the humanoid's collar, hatband, hem or hair, which takes an action, or a DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check if the target is awake and watching. The target makes a Wisdom saving throw against the spell save DC of the tether's maker (Kermin's: DC 18). On a failure, the target becomes a [[Tethered Sleeper]], charmed by the planter for 30 days while the pin stays on its person. If the target succeeds, the tether crumbles to dust.
- **Detection.** *Detect magic* shows faint enchantment. A searcher who knows to look finds the pin with a DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check.
- **Ending it.** Removing the pin ends the effect, and so do *remove curse*, *dispel magic* (5th level), the spell [[Untether]], or the wearer starting its turn within 30 feet of the unboxed [[The Gaoler's Link|Gaoler's Link]].
- An unplanted tether more than 7 days old is an inert bone pin.

## History
Kermin Mind-Bender makes them in Dorakaa and issues them to his tether-mages by the satchel. *Homebrew*, built on Kermin's mind-control specialty (WGR5 p. 86).

## Found In

- SP-SQ-02 — Tethers: on three Ritterstadt sleepers; a satchel of unplanted pins
- SP-SQ-05 — The Ice Road: on the Wolf Nomad trackers

## Continuity
Every tether is evidence. The *tether-register* (SP-SQ-02) lists who carries one. Carrying an unplanted tether marks the bearer for Kermin's diviners.
