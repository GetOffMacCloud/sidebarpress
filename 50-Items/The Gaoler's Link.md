# The Gaoler's Link

> A fist-sized link of black iron, always cold, cut with six sigils: one link of the fetter that held Iuz in Castle Greyhawk from 505 to 570 CY.

> [!art]+ The Gaoler's Link
> ![[99-Assets/SP-SQ/handouts/The Gaoler's Link — Handout.png|360]]

## Properties
*Wondrous item, artifact (minor). No attunement.*
- **Blind Spot.** While the Link is unboxed, divination magic can't perceive anything within 30 feet of it, whoever casts it. Scrying sensors, *clairvoyance*, *locate object*, a familiar's shared senses and similar effects show only grey fog. *Detect magic* registers a strong abjuration that seems to slide off the senses. Because of this, neither Iuz nor his servants can find the Link by magic. They can only find the hole it leaves.
- **Broken Tethers.** Enchantments created by a creature sworn to Iuz are suppressed within 30 feet of the unboxed Link. That includes every [[Bone Tether]], and *charm person* or *suggestion* cast by a [[Tether-Mage]] or a [[Grinning Initiate]]. A creature freed this way is confused for 1 round.
- **The Burn.** A creature sworn to Iuz that touches the Link takes 2d6 radiant damage and must drop it unless it succeeds on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. It takes the damage again at the start of each of its turns while it keeps hold. Anyone else feels only a deep cold.
- **Sealed.** Inside the [[The Ashbox|Ashbox]], or any lead-lined container sealed with bone-ash, the Link has none of these properties.
- *Identify:* "a fetter-link of a binding; its prisoner is gone."
- **Destroying it.** No forge in Ritterstadt can mark it. It can be destroyed only where it was made, in the deepest levels of Castle Greyhawk, which caved in in 570. Leave that to the campaign.

## The Six Sigils
The six sigils are cut around the Link's curve, each about the size of a thumbprint, in a single hard incised line. Each was one imprisoner's ward, and each still does one thing. The designs follow each hero-god's canon holy symbol, simplified the way a ward-smith would cut it into iron in 505 CY.

| # | Maker | Sigil | Canon basis | The ward (what it still does) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **St. Cuthbert** | A short cudgel laid across a starburst of eight rays | Cudgel and starburst | **The Billet.** Strikes any sworn hand that holds it (*The Burn*). |
| 2 | **Zagyg** | Two parallel zigzag lines, like stair-steps gone mad | The rune of insanity, two parallel zig-zags | **The Lock of Folly.** Hid the prisoner from every eye, his own included (*Blind Spot*). |
| 3 | **Heward** | A seven-reed set of pan-pipes, graduated in length | Any musical instrument (pitch pipes) | **The Discord.** Unmakes any enchantment the prisoner's servants weave nearby (*Broken Tethers*). |
| 4 | **Murlynd** | A solid six-pointed star with rounded points | Six-pointed star with rounded points (*Slavers*, p. 128) | **The Maker's Mark.** Keeps the iron whole; no forge marks it. |
| 5 | **Keoghtom** | A round disk cut through by an upward arrow | Round disk bisected by an upward-pointing arrow | **The Shut Road.** Barred every way out to other planes while the prisoner was held. Spent since 570. |
| 6 | **Kelanen** | Nine short swords in a star, points outward, around a small empty ring | Nine swords arrayed in a star, points outward | **The Balance.** Held the fetter closed. Broken in 570; one sword is visibly cracked. |

## History
The six who imprisoned Iuz (Zagyg, St. Cuthbert, Heward, Murlynd, Keoghtom and Kelanen; WGR5 p. 5) each set a ward in the fetter. When Iuz was freed in 570, the backwash caved in the deepest dungeons. City of Greyhawk delvers dug through the rubble in 571–72, and a dealer sold the Link to Greyhawk University in 573. In 574 a sage identified the sigils, and soon after someone attempted a burglary at the parent campus. Chancellor Adenauer sent it north under seal to the Ritterstadt Field Station. There it went into the Archive's sealed stacks, in the keeping of Professor Casimir Vogelsang. *Homebrew*, grounded in WGR5 pp. 3, 5 and 75. Logged as an endorsed deviation in [[Iuz#Sidebar Press Stance]].
