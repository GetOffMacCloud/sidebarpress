# The Long Night Lantern

> A six-sided hooded lantern of pierced tin, twin to Candlecrown's Carol Lantern, painted with the six Common verses of the Long Night Carol.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon.*
- **Undying flame.** It burns without oil and can't be put out by wind, water or magic other than *dispel magic* (DC 13); its bearer can light or hood it as a bonus action. Bright light 30 feet, dim light 30 feet more.
- **Hearth-warmth.** Creatures in its bright light have advantage on saving throws against extreme cold.
- **Sing the first verse.** Once per dawn, its bearer can sing the carol's first verse as an action. Each creature of the bearer's choice within its bright light that has vulnerability to fire, or that is a beast or monstrosity with cold resistance, must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 7 (2d6) fire damage on a failure, or half as much on a success.

## History
*Homebrew.* The Carol Lantern (C4) hangs over the hall's hearth. Pell and Ash made this twin for the party in the last watch of the Short Day; Brannoc and Mother Sedge give it at dawn.
