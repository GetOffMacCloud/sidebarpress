# The Hundred's Cord-Knot

> A red cord tied in the Hundred's knot. Hauptfrau Ilse gives one to each PC at the end of the night.

## Properties
- *Token (no mechanics).*
- In any company tavern in Perrenland, it marks the bearer as someone who kept the peace when it mattered. Mercenaries greet the bearer as one of their own. *This is a courtesy, not a hook.*
