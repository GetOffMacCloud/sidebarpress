# Maus

> The grey mare out of the old Erlenhof line, entered at the Duffkyyn as Ostvelt Grey.

## Properties
- *Riding horse* (MM p. 336); she should never roll initiative. See [[SP-PBS-00 — Bestiary]].
- *Quiet in the Dark:* stands silent in a wagon, stall or ship's box for hours.
- Her Condition (Sound / Sore / Lame) is the adventure's stake: [[SP-PBS-00 — The Mare, the Clock and the Cord]].

## History
*Homebrew.* Bred, broken and trained by [[Adalinde Halbritter]]; two years old; called Mouse because she was small and grey and quiet as a foal.

## Found In
- [[SP-PBS-00-Nobodys-Colors]] — the third wagon ([[Scene 01 — Erlenhof]]); the deck stall ([[Scene 04 — Two Nights on the Quag]]); post time ([[Scene 07 — Post Time]])

## Continuity
Her **first foal**, if she is sound and unsold, is a reward three years out.
