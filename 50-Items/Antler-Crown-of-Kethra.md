# Antler Crown of Kethra

> A circlet of dark bone with branching antler-tines, worn by the Hound-Mother in life and taken from her brow by Brother Ashlock.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement; Ashlock wears it unattuned for the pack-command alone).*
- **Crown-Bearer.** While the pack is awake, the wearer can use a bonus action to choose one bone beast within 60 ft. That beast uses its reaction to move up to half its speed. As an action, the wearer can make a DC 13 Charisma check to make every bone beast within 60 ft stop for 1 round.
- **Hear the Pack.** The wearer hears the pack as a sound like dry sticks dragged over stone. They always know the direction of the nearest bone beast within 1 mile.
- **When attuned (after the adventure):** the wearer can cast *speak with animals* at will, and *animal friendship* once per dawn (DC 13). Both work only on beasts. Once the pack sleeps, the crown's command powers go quiet for good.

## History
*Homebrew.* It was buried with [[Kethra-the-Hound-Mother]]. [[Brother-Ashlock]] took it after the barrow was opened.
