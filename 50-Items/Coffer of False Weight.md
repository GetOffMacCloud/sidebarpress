# Coffer of False Weight

> A black iron coffer, a twin to the one in the Archive, sealed with grey wax pressed with a starburst and a cudgel. It's heavier than it should be.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon. No attunement.* The coffer has 3 charges. As an action, you can expend 1 charge to make it produce a blind spot for 3 days: divination magic can't perceive anything within 30 feet of the coffer, exactly as if the [[The Gaoler's Link|Gaoler's Link]] were inside.
- To anyone hunting the Link by the hole it leaves in scrying, a charged coffer looks exactly like the real thing.
- It has none of the Link's other properties. It doesn't burn the sworn or break tethers, which a clever hunter can test.
- When the last charge is spent, the coffer is ordinary iron.

## History
Lecturer Perenna Dolz and Hogar Irongrip made two of them in Ready'reat 576, for the decoy runs of SP-GQ04. Dolz's notes say she copied the effect "by imitating a silence." *Homebrew.*

## Found In
- SP-GQ04 — The Quiet Cargo: two decoy coffers sent by other roads

## Continuity
A coffer that comes back with charges left is a prize for Dolz's department. One that is captured tells Kermin's diviners exactly how they were fooled.
