# Brindlewick's Cipher Book

> A thick, grease-stained book in Temple cipher: mostly recipes, partly not.

## Properties
Mundane. A DC 17 Intelligence (Investigation) check, or an hour's work, decodes it. Contains the rite that raised the Gluttonwretch ("the bone keeps both the beast and the bird; break it and keep neither") and a *spell scroll* of a 5th-level spell tucked in the back cover.
