# Grave Goods of Kethra

> Amber beads, bronze spear-blades and a boar-tusk helm, laid with the Hound-Mother on her bier. They are worth about 150 gp, and taking them is a test.

## Properties
- *Treasure, about 150 gp in total.*
- **Taking any of it raises the Howl to 6 at once.**
- If the torc has already gone home, the pack does not rise. Instead, the PC who took the goods hears clicking at the edge of every campfire until the goods are returned. The same curse rules apply as for the [[Torc-of-the-Hound-Mother]].

## History
*Homebrew.* Flan burial goods, fifteen centuries old.
