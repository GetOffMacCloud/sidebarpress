# Heart-Rune of Hrimvald

> A fist-sized disk of blue ice that never melts below the summer solstice, carved with a single rune: the first word the immoth ever took.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement).*
- **Listener.** While attuned, you understand the literal meaning of any spoken language you hear (not written).
- **Spend the word.** As an action, you can shatter it to cast *ice knife* at 3rd level (+7 to hit, DC 15) without components. The rune is destroyed.
- A sage pays 1,000 gp for it intact.

## History
*Homebrew.* The heart of [[Hrimvald the Gatherer]]. It survives the immoth's destruction, or lies at the bottom of the Still Pool in [[The Ice Library]].
