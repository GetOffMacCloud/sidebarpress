# The Halved Standard

> Ritter Wolfram von Eisfeld's banner, cut in two on the night of the Kalte Klamm. The von Eisfelds kept one half and called it "torn." The Hundred kept the other and called it "the banner we failed."

## Properties
- *Heirloom; climax key.*
- **The cut:** both halves have a straight, clean edge made by a blade (any PC who looks closely, or Investigation DC 10). Laid side by side, the halves meet.
- **The custom:** a lord who releases a company halves his standard, and the company keeps its half as proof (History DC 13, [[Lecturer Bertram Falk]], or Korporal Mattis). See [[The Hundred]].
- **At the climax:** matching the halves proves the release without Jost breaking his oath. It is the cleanest solution in [[Scene 06 — The Midnight Bell]].

## History
*Homebrew.* Gerhard carried his half north around 510 and hung it in [[The Ritter's Hall]]. Lorenz Kammerer's half lay in the company chest for ninety-six years.

## Found In

## Continuity
Afterwards, Konrad has the halves stitched together and hung above his chair: one banner, with a seam.
