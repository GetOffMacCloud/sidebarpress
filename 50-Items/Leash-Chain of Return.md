# Leash-Chain of Return

> Ten feet of black chain ending in a cruel hook, cold enough to frost the fingers, forged from the throat-chain of a leash fiend.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement).*
- **Hook and Leash.** As an action, you can throw the hook at a creature within 15 feet. Make a ranged attack using Strength or Dexterity, with proficiency. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage and is grappled (escape DC 13). You can pull a grappled target up to 10 feet toward you as a bonus action.
- **Quarry.** Once per long rest, after you have hooked a creature, you can name it your quarry. For the next 24 hours you know the direction to it if it is within 1 mile of you.
- **Curse of the Collar.** While attuned, you dream of a leash around your own throat. Fiends within 1 mile of you know the direction to you. The attunement can be ended only by *remove curse*.

## History
A smith willing to work a leash fiend's hook can make one (Hogar Irongrip will, for a price and an argument). *Homebrew.*

Kermin's people would recognize the chain. A bearer has turned Iuz's tool against him, and Kermin will notice.
