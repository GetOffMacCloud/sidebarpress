# Counterfeiter's Seal-Ring

> A plain brass signet whose face is blank, smooth and faintly soft, like warm wax.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement).* The ring has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.
- As an action, you can study a seal impression or signet for 1 minute and expend 1 charge to make the ring's face copy it exactly. The copy lasts until you study another seal or until the next dawn.
- Impressions you make with the ring are identical to the original. Spotting one takes a DC 18 Intelligence (Investigation) check, made with advantage by anyone who owns the true seal.
- *Detect magic* on a counterfeit impression shows a faint transmutation.

## History
Panshazek's people carry these to move orders under other men's seals. The Horned Society seal on Vesharan's cipher letter (SP-GQ01) was made with one, which is how Kermin's agent got Molag paper. *Homebrew.*

## Found In
- SP-GQ03 — The Molag Contract: on Master Kezrin, or on the rival team's forger

## Continuity
A party that keeps the ring can forge the charter's seal and swap the document past the [[Iron Clerk]]. Using it in Perrenland is a hanging offense if proved.
