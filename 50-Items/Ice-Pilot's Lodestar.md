# Ice-Pilot's Lodestar

> A thumb-sized lodestone bound in wolf-gut on a braided horsehair cord, worn smooth by generations of hands.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, common. No attunement.*
- While you wear it on frozen water, you know whether the ice within 30 feet of you will bear your weight. You also have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to find your way across ice or through fog.
- Once per day, as an action, you can hold it up by its cord and learn the direction of the nearest solid shore.

## History
Wolf Nomad iceskimmer pilots carry these across the winter Quag. Canon has iceskimmers running on the lake in midwinter; the lodestar is house material. Tegin, the *Graue Hecht*'s pilot (SP-SQ-01), wears one.

## Found In
- SP-SQ-05 — The Ice Road: a gift from Tegin if the party freed him from his tether, or taken from the tethered trackers

## Continuity
Wolf Nomads recognize the cord's braid pattern, which shows which clan it came from. Wearing one stolen is an insult.
