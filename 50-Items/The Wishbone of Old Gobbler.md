# The Wishbone of Old Gobbler

> A wishbone as tall as a man, bound in black sinew, that holds both the Gluttonwretch's leash and Old Gobbler's spirit.

## Properties
Can't be cut, burned or smashed; breaks only when two creatures pull it apart (an action each, contested Strength check). **Snapped:** the Gluttonwretch loses Legendary Resistance, Incorporeal Movement and necrotic immunity, its current hit points are halved, and it will not rise in Second Helpings. The winner of the contest keeps the larger half and gains a single *wish*, limited to duplicating a spell of 7th level or lower (as the [[Gluttonwretch]] entry's Spoils). **Cost:** Old Gobbler's spirit is destroyed; the First Fruits can no longer end the fight; the grove loses Obad-Hai's blessing.

## History
Homebrew. Taken from the starved bird's carcass by Deacon Brindlewick and bound in a rite to Zuggtmoy.

## Found In
- [[SP-GS01-The-Gluttonwretch-of-Hommlet]] — The Smokehouse chapel (Scene 05), or in Brindlewick's hands at the feast (Scene 07).

## Continuity
Once its wish is spent, the larger half is a carved trinket with no power.
