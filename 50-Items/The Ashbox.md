# The Ashbox

> A lead casket the size of a loaf, its lid lined with grey bone-ash and lettered inside with an Abyssal word that means *seal*.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon. No attunement.*
- An object sealed inside the Ashbox has its magical properties suppressed until it is taken out. A creature can carry a sealed object safely, even one that would harm it.
- The Ashbox itself can be found by divination as an ordinary object. A sealed [[The Gaoler's Link|Gaoler's Link]] can therefore be scried again.
- Opening and closing the lid takes an action. Reading the Abyssal letters requires a DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) check or *comprehend languages*.
- Hogar Irongrip made the lead box to order. The bone-ash and the lettering were added later by someone who didn't sign them.

## History
Vesharan commissioned the box from the University Forge in Brewfest 576. He lined and lettered it himself with ash from Dorakaa. *Homebrew.*

## Found In
- SP-GQ01 — The Gaoler's Link: open on a crate in the *Graue Hecht*'s hold (Scene 6; H2)

## Continuity
If the party keeps it, it's the safest way to carry the Link, and the easiest way for Iuz to find it. Vogelsang will want it for the collection.
