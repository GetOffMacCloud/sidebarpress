# Gerhard's Longsword

> Ritter Gerhard von Eisfeld's longsword, now always at Konrad's hip. At the Red Cord, it is the blade that matters.

## Properties
- *Longsword (mundane).*
- **The cord:** Mutter Ilka ties Konrad's cord last and tightest through its hilt. When Temper reaches 5, *"his thumb is under the knot."* At 6 he cuts it, and the meeting is void (see [[SP-PBS-03 — Cord, Temper and Trust]]).
