### N. NAME AS THE PLAYERS SEE IT
*(Optional status tag, e.g. "Cult members!")*

> *Read-aloud: at most 5 sentences, second person, present tense. Only what the characters perceive. End on the exits or one image.*

> *(If the occupants are gone / If the party has surprise: alternate box.)*

What this room is and why it looks this way. Hidden features. 2-4 sentences.

**Play:** *(complex rooms only)* one sentence on the order things happen.
**Creatures:** **Name** (count), what they are doing.
**Tactics:** what they do when the party arrives, and on alert.
**Trap:** trigger · effect · tell.
**Treasure:** where and how it is hidden. Clue items name the part they point to.
**Development:** if-then lines for what changes elsewhere.
