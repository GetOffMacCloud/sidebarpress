## House Conventions (apply in every section)
- **Inline NPC notation.** The first time an NPC appears in a scene: **Name** (alignment sex race **statblock**), for example *Sexton Harrow (LN male human* **wight***)*. Statblock names in bold; SRD names as printed.
- **Scene header block.** Every scene opens with Estimated Duration, Location, Prerequisites and Objectives (see the scene template).
- **Area Information.** Dimensions & Terrain, Lighting, and anything else that affects play (sounds, smells, hazards).
- **Creatures/NPCs.** Each with *Objectives/Goals* and *What do they know?*
- **Developments.** What happens when the party does the obvious thing, the clever thing, or nothing.
- **Treasure & Rewards** at the end of each scene that has any.
- **Playing the Pillars** box in each scene: one line each for Combat, Exploration, Social.
- **Adjusting the scene** lives in the encounter tables (4 / 5 / 6 PCs); no separate "weak/strong party" box.
