## 1. The content engine: one game = one content packet

After each session, spend 10 minutes capturing (phone notes are fine):

- ****Moment:**** the single best beat — a crit, a reveal, a player quote, a decision that changed everything
- ****Mechanic:**** one rule interaction worth sharing (a clever spell use, a trial outcome, a faction turn)
- ****Visual:**** one photo — battlemap, dice, handout, table spread (no player faces without permission)
- ****Spoiler check:**** mark anything the players haven't resolved yet as embargoed

One packet per game × your weekly tables = more raw material than this schedule needs. Bank the surplus.

---

## 2. Five pillars (rotate; never post product twice in a row)

1. ****Product Spotlight**** — the adventure itself: hooks, art, maps, FG module features, reviews
2. ****Behind the Screen**** — design notes: why a trial works, how you keyed a dungeon, DC math, faction logic
3. ****Table Tales**** — real moments from your games (the packet above); names changed, spoilers held
4. ****Lore & Continuity**** — Greyhawk canon explainers, continuity deep-dives; this is your moat
5. ****Community**** — polls, questions, AMAs, con appearances, other creators' work you genuinely like

Ratio target: for every 1 Product Spotlight, post 3+ from the other four pillars. Nobody follows an ad feed.

****Session days:**** one lightweight post — a table photo or "tonight we're running X" — no production value required.

## 4. Platform roles (same idea, native adaptation — never identical cross-posts)

- ****X**** — conversation and launch announcements. Threads for design notes and lore. Hashtags: #ttrpg #dnd #greyhawk #fantasygrounds. Reply to other creators daily; the algorithm rewards it.
- ****Instagram**** — visual: art, maps, carousels (carousel = "5 rooms of the dungeon" swipe-through). Reels for the video floor. Link in bio → RPGSidebar.com hub page.
- ****TikTok**** — raw and fast: 30–60s clips, hook in the first 2 seconds, captions on. Post the video floor here first.
- ****Facebook**** — groups over pages. Be a useful member of Greyhawk and FG groups; share product posts to your page. Slightly older demographic = buyers.
- ****Reddit**** — value first, promo never alone. r/DnDBehindTheScreen and r/dndnext for craft/lore posts that funnel; r/rpg for discussion. Respect the 9:1 contribution ratio or you'll get eaten.
- ****Bluesky (optional)**** — big TTRPG community, low effort to mirror X posts.

****Reddit/Facebook rule:**** every promotional post must be surrounded by genuine participation. Drive-by links die.

## 6. The 30-day launch plan

****Week 1 — Brand + lead magnet.**** Announce Sidebar Press properly: who you are, the canon-continuity promise, FG-ready modules. Posts: intro thread (X), logo/brand art (IG), "why continuity matters" video (TikTok), lore post (Reddit). End of week: publish the free lead-magnet adventure/hook PDF on RPGSidebar.com and announce everywhere. Goal: first 100 newsletter signups.

****Week 2 — SP-PBS-01 spotlight.**** The False Arrow, all week, one angle per day: the hook (Mon), the Tiger Nomads and the Favor engine (Tue/Wed), the three trials (Thu), art + map reveals (Fri + video). Every post links the three stores. Goal: first sales; watch which angle gets clicks.

****Week 3 — Behind the screen + the FG edge.**** Design notes from building the adventure; a video walkthrough of the Fantasy Grounds module (this is the differentiator — show it running). Lore post on Yecha/Tiger Nomads canon. Community poll: "what should the next adventure be?" Goal: FG community pickup; gather product feedback.

****Week 4 — Proof + what's next.**** Share any reviews, actual-play quotes, sales milestones (even small ones — "first 25 copies" is a story). Tease the next release without naming SP-001's licensed map. Recap the month: what worked, what's next. Goal: convert attention into the newsletter; set month two's theme.

## 7. Scorecard (review at day 30, then monthly)

Track weekly in a notes file — 5 minutes, no dashboards:

- Link clicks to each store (use per-platform links or UTM tags)
- Newsletter signups (the real metric — you own this list)
- Follower growth per platform
- Which pillar got the most saves/shares (saves > likes for purchase intent)
- DTRPG wishlist adds and FG store views, if visible

****Kill / double-down rule:**** after 30 days, cut the lowest-performing pillar-format combo and double the top one. The schedule serves the data, not the other way around.
