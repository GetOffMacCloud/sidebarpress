# Module Codes — the Sidebar Press Product Code System

*Adopted 2026-11-13 (Greybeard). Modeled on TSR's 1e and 2e module codes. The vault was migrated the same day.*

## 1. What TSR Did

### 1e (1978–1989): one letter (or two) per series, a number per installment
| Code | Series | Examples | What the letters tell you |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B**, **X**, **CM**, **M**, **IM** | D&D Basic, Expert, Companion, Master, Immortals | B2 *Keep on the Borderlands*, X1 *Isle of Dread* | The **rules set and tier** |
| **N** | Novice | N1 *Against the Cult of the Reptile God* | **Tier** (low level, beginners) |
| **T**, **G**, **D**, **Q** | Temple, Giants, Drow, Queen of the Spiders | T1 *Village of Hommlet*, G1 *Steading of the Hill Giant Chief* | **One linked campaign arc per letter** |
| **A** | Slave Lords | A1–A4 | Arc letter; began as tournament modules |
| **C** | Competition | C1 *Hidden Shrine of Tamoachan* | **Origin** (tournament play) |
| **S** | Special | S1 *Tomb of Horrors* | **Type** (one-off showpieces) |
| **U**, **L** | Saltmarsh, Lendore Isle | U1 *Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh* | Arc letter tied to a **region** |
| **I** | Intermediate | I6 *Ravenloft* | A catch-all for standalone adventures |
| **UK** | TSR UK | UK1 *Beyond the Crystal Cave* | **Who made it** (the UK studio) |
| **WG** | World of Greyhawk | WG4 *Forgotten Temple of Tharizdun* | **Setting** |
| **H** | Bloodstone (high level) | H1–H4 | **Tier** (very high level) |

**Compilations** joined the codes: *GDQ1–7 Queen of the Spiders*, *T1–4 Temple of Elemental Evil*, *A1–4 Scourge of the Slave Lords*. The level band went on the cover ("For character levels 5–8"), not in the code.

### 2e (1989–2000): setting letters plus a type letter
| Pattern | Setting | Type letters used | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WG_** | Greyhawk | **A** adventure, **Q** quest, **R** reference, **S** special | WGA4 *Vecna Lives!*, WGQ1 *Patriots of Ulek*, WGR1 *Greyhawk Ruins* |
| **FR_** | Forgotten Realms | sourcebooks numbered FR1–FR16; FRA/FRC/FRE for adventures, campaigns, empires | FR1 *Waterdeep and the North* |
| **R_** | Ravenloft | **A** adventure, **M** module, **Q** quest, **R** reference | RA1 *Feast of Goblyns*, RR1 *Darklords* |
| **DS_**, **SJ_**, **DL_**, **BR_** | Dark Sun, Spelljammer, Dragonlance, Birthright | the same idea | — |
| **MC**, **PHBR**, **DMGR** | rules lines | — | MC1 *Monstrous Compendium*, PHBR1 *Complete Fighter's Handbook* |

### What we take from it
1. **Short and printable.** Two letters and a number fit a cover plaque and a spine.
2. **The letters carry meaning.** In 2e the first letter is the setting and the second is the kind of product, so a reader can tell a Greyhawk adventure from a Greyhawk sourcebook at a glance.
3. **Arcs stay together.** Linked adventures share a code and run in order (T1, T2…; G1, G2, G3), and a compilation names its range (T1–4).
4. **Numbers are never reused**, and gaps are fine.
5. **The level band goes on the cover, not in the code.**
6. **A regional or alternate edition gets its own prefix** (TSR UK's UK series).

## 2. The Sidebar Press System

### Format
- **On the cover and spine:** `GA5` (line letters plus a number, no padding), the way TSR printed WGA4.
- **In the vault, file names, FGU modules and store SKUs:** `SP-GA05`, with the house prefix and two digits so folders sort.
- The level band and party size stay on the cover's right-hand plaque.

### Letter 1: the setting
| Letter | Setting |
|---|---|
| **G** | Greyhawk (the Ritterstadt and Greyhawk University frame) |
| **K** | Kestrel (the serial-numbers-off edition of a Greyhawk product; same number as its twin) |
| **O** | Open (setting-agnostic) |

### Letter 2: the kind of product
| Letter | Kind | TSR model |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Adventure.** A standalone adventure, usually one session. | WGA, I, WG |
| **Q** | **Quest.** A chapter of a linked campaign arc; arcs number in play order. | WGQ; the T, G, D and A arcs |
| **R** | **Reference.** A gazetteer, setting book or player's guide. | WGR, FR, RR |
| **S** | **Special.** Seasonal, convention, tournament or showpiece releases. | S, C, WGS |
| **M** | **Monsters.** A bestiary. | MC |

### Other rules
- **0 is the hub.** A line's 0 is its introduction or arc hub (GQ0 *The Shadow on the Quag*), or a compilation shell (OS0 *Hallow's Eve Quartet*).
- **Compilations** name the range, as TSR did: *GQ1–5*, *OS1–4*.
- **Revisions keep the code.** A corrected or expanded printing is "GA1, revised 2027", never a new number.
- **CV** (conversions of other publishers' modules) keeps its own two-letter line with no setting letter: CV1 *The Blackmire Swamp*.
- **Reserved numbers stay reserved** (GA4 is held for *The Eclipse Circle*).
- **Collisions avoided:** OA (TSR's *Oriental Adventures*) and GAZ (Mystara's *Gazetteers*) aren't used.
- **Out of scope:** Caffeinated Chronicles (CC-L00 and later) is a separate imprint and keeps its own codes.

## 4. Migration (done 2026-11-13)
Applied by script (`~/migrate_codes.py` on the device VM): 2,568 replacements in 529 text files, 8 SVG/SLA files, and 262 renamed files and folders. Text backup: `Images Staging/Sidebar Press vault text backup 2026-11-13 (before code migration).zip`. Installed FGU modules in the Fantasy Grounds folder keep their old names so existing campaigns still find them.

The original plan:
Nothing has been released, so renaming now is cheap. A rename touches:
- folder and file names in `10-Adventures`
- the `adventure:` key in every note's frontmatter
- wikilinks to renamed notes
- FGU module names and the build scripts' `CODE`/`NAME` settings
- `99-Assets/<code>/` folders and image file prefixes
- cover plaques (`99-Assets/_Covers/make_covers.py`)
- project docs, the Release Tracker and the Pipeline Dashboard

Options: **(a)** migrate everything now with a script; **(b)** use the new codes for new products only and keep the legacy codes for the 17 existing ones; **(c)** keep legacy codes in the vault but print the new codes on covers and store pages.
