# Classic Module Review

Score a draft against the Classic Module Standard (Claude Doc "Sidebar Press — The Classic Module Standard"; study notes in the Sidebar Press project). Copy this into the adventure folder as `SP-PBS-NN — Classic Review.md` and tick as you go. Items marked **(must)** block release. Everything else counts toward the score.

## 1. Architecture
- [ ] **(must)** Cover line gives APL band, party size and play time
- [ ] **(must)** Background splits What the Locals Know from What's Really Happening
- [ ] The villain's scheme is written from the villain's side, step by step
- [ ] Adventure Outline is under 100 words, one sentence per part
- [ ] 3-5 hooks, at least one personal, plus a decline path
- [ ] Laws of the place sit before the key, never inside a room
- [ ] Front matter is under 10% of the book
- [ ] The town comes in the order of play
- [ ] The climax has at least 15% of the page weight; no middle section outweighs it
- [ ] Players' maps and DM maps are separate

## 2. Keyed entries
- [ ] **(must)** Every entry uses the field order: name, box, DM paragraph, Creatures, Tactics, Trap, Treasure, Development
- [ ] **(must)** No stat blocks or stat strings in the prose; counts in parentheses
- [ ] Area names are what the players would call them
- [ ] Minor rooms are one line, range entries, type codes or pointers
- [ ] "Empty" rooms are flagged as empty or hide a lure
- [ ] Conditional boxes where the state can differ (surprise, return visit, occupants gone)
- [ ] Big complexes use prefixed area codes

## 3. Read-aloud
- [ ] **(must)** No box exceeds 5 sentences or about 120 words
- [ ] **(must)** No box assigns the characters feelings, conclusions, speech or actions
- [ ] Every box is second person, present tense, perception only
- [ ] Boxes end on the exits, an image or a question
- [ ] Patron briefings are scenes the players can interrupt, not monologues

## 4. Living sites
- [ ] **(must)** Every organized site has at least three reaction tools (alert states, scaled response, reinforcement schedule, fleeing survivors, clocks, recolonization, tension track, schedules)
- [ ] Wandering results are drawn from the key, with errands and home areas
- [ ] Every promised reaction is written down as a Development line
- [ ] Factions inside a site are levers the party can pull, with relations stated
- [ ] Difficulty is set by geography and signalled to the players

## 5. Clues and investigation
- [ ] **(must)** A "facts the players must learn" list exists, and every fact has at least three routes plus a last resort
- [ ] A nudge list for stalled play
- [ ] Essential clues are flagged in the DM text
- [ ] Rumors are tagged True / Partly true / False / True but unrelated, voiced, gated and not padded
- [ ] Floating events state their purpose
- [ ] At least one clue only a particular character or background can spot

## 6. NPCs and factions
- [ ] **(must)** Every named NPC has wants, knows and an escalation or reaction line
- [ ] Dossiers carry a loyalty line and a [Deity] tag
- [ ] Patrons have a public and a private motive; the private one pays off
- [ ] Every ally has a flaw; a named safety valve exists
- [ ] Factions are written as "Wants X, hopes to Y"

## 7. Home base and wilderness
- [ ] The base is keyed (summary table, residents with trade, faith, knows, secret)
- [ ] Two or three hidden hands among ordinary folk, with tells
- [ ] The base changes between forays
- [ ] Wilderness keeps fixed, event, optional and weather content apart, with a Sources of Information list
- [ ] Travel has an arrival guarantee

## 8. Endings and continuity
- [ ] **(must)** Endings branch: full success, partial, retreat or defeat
- [ ] Aftermath gives rewards and consequences for each tier
- [ ] Continuing the Adventure lists 3-5 seeds, including what the villains do next
- [ ] The sequel is planted inside the adventure in objects, not only rumors
- [ ] A Previous Play note covers groups who played earlier adventures differently
- [ ] Continuity section logs threads opened, advanced and closed

## 9. Voice and Greyhawk
- [ ] **(must)** DM text is third person and declarative, with no contempt for players
- [ ] Greyhawk texture: CY dates, month names, local coin, deities on people
- [ ] Humor lives inside entries, not in asides
- [ ] No genre whiplash, no meta-humor, no dated stereotypes
- [ ] One canonical spelling for every name

## 10. Plot protection (each "yes" is a fail; fix before release)
- [ ] **(must)** None of these appear: guaranteed villain escape, invulnerable spectacle NPCs, forced capture, compulsion of characters, a punished refusal, "just tell the players the plot"
