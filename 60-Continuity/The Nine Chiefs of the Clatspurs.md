# The Nine Chiefs of the Clatspurs

> A cell of Nerull's faithful in Schwartzenbruin is opening the bound barrows of nine Flan chieftains in the Clatspurs to raise their guardian dead as a host.

## Status
**Open.** One barrow (Kethra's, at [[The-Three-Sisters]]) has been opened, and eight remain. The cell's agent was [[Brother-Ashlock]]. His superior is "Sister Morrow."

## History
- **Patchwall 576 CY** — introduced in [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]]: Ashlock steers Kethra's pack with her crown and plans a rite at Celene-set. *(Update with the outcome after play.)*

## Involved
- NPCs: [[Brother-Ashlock]], Sister Morrow (not yet written), [[Kethra-the-Hound-Mother]]
- Locations: [[The-Three-Sisters]], [[The-Dreadverge]], the Clatspur Range, Schwartzenbruin
- Items: [[Antler-Crown-of-Kethra]], [[Torc-of-the-Hound-Mother]]

## Possible Resolutions
- The party tracks the cell to Schwartzenbruin through Ashlock's letter and hands it to a cantonal Rechter.
- A race to the next barrow (each chief has a different binding object and a different pack).
- A Flan-descended highland clan of Clatspurgen turns out to have guarded the barrows for centuries.
- Iuz's agents across the ridges learn of the barrows and want the host for themselves. This works best after 591 CY, when the treaty makes open action costly for both sides.
- Do not lock any of these in until an adventure does.

## Notes
This works well as a series spine for a 1–5 level band. Each barrow can be one one-shot, each in a different Perrender valley.
