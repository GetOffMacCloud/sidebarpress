| Patchwall 576 CY, dark of Luna | The Dreadverge haunting, Waycombe, Canton of Clatspurgen: Brother Ashlock's rite at Celene-set. *(Homebrew; outcome per table)* | [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]] |

> **Why 576 CY for SP-PBS-02:** It is the *World of Greyhawk* baseline, before Perrenland's 583 CY pact with Iuz, when Iuz is an open threat over the Clatspurs. It is also close enough to Iggwilv's reign (481–491 CY) for living grandparents to remember it. Any sequel in *The Nine Chiefs of the Clatspurs* that moves to 598 CY should treat Waycombe's events as 22 years past.
