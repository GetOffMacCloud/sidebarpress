# The Rotting Table

> A Zuggtmoy cell that survived the Temple's fall, feeding the Lady of Rot one gorged soul at a time.

## Stakes
Deacon Brindlewick's cell raised the Gluttonwretch to gorge Hommlet at Brewfest and crack one of Zuggtmoy's seals beneath the Temple. His cipher book names "the second table" in the Temple's lower halls.

## If Ignored
Rannos and Gremag, if they escape, carry the scheme to Nulb. The second table is laid by Brewfest 577.
