# Glyrthiel

> Brubgrok's chief assistant: an elf fighter-mage who negotiates the stone sales and interrogates suspicious visitors.

## Appearance
An elf woman of refined taste: her room is genteel, bright with a continual flame chandelier and hung with woodland tapestries.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Businesslike and probing. She asks pointed questions about how the stone will be used, quotes double the going price (1,000 gp per ton), and pushes for 20% down. In a fight she uses her bow, shield against counterattacks, and sleep on as many foes as she can while catching few allies, even waiting until some allies fall.

## Goals & Secrets
Knows that "Bazili Erak" is an alias and the details of the slaving operation. Present in her room on a 1–2 on 1d6; otherwise supervising or leading patrols (it can take minutes to two hours for her to answer a summons).
