# Brubgrok

> "Bazili Erak": half-orc fighter, quarry master, and slave broker for the Highport network.

## Appearance
Passes for Bazili Erak, a flame-haired dwarf with a beard to match, hunched (so taller than he looks), with peculiarly long arms and red eyes. In his own room he drops the act: a dais of greasy furs, gnawed bones and dirty bowls.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Humorless and unkind in public; a smooth talker with genuine skill at mining and stonework. Posing as the industrious dwarf is a drain on him, and he relaxes by acting like an orc. In a fight he kills the most dangerous foe first, opening with arrows at spellcasters when he has allies.

## Goals & Secrets
Buys slaves from bands of slavers, hides them in the quarry and ships them to Highport by the river caverns. He staged the "midnight raid" on the slavers to get rid of suspected traitors, and invented the gold in the quarry to explain his income. He shelters the clerics of the Eye. He fears the network more than the party: once committed, he makes a stand.
