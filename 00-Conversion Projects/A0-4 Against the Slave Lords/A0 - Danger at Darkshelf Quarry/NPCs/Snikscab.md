# Snikscab

> Bugbear taskmaster who trains and disciplines the guardhouse slaves (with Nubgrod).

## Appearance
A big, brutal "goblin" to the slaves, who don't know what bugbears are.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Used to making slaves cringe with a look. In a fight he goes for weak foes he can beat quickly, to sow despair.

## Goals & Secrets
Shares the old chapel (20) with Nubgrod; one of them always carries the keys to every manacle in the guardhouse.
