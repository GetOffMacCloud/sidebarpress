# Neistor

> Overlord of Darkshelf, who hires the party to look into the quarry.

## Appearance
A stocky, middle-aged man with graying hair and a beard to match; he paces and counts his doubts on his fingers.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Wary but not bold. He suspects the quarry's results are too good to be true and the raid on the slavers too quick and clean.

## Goals & Secrets
He granted Bazili Erak the title to the quarry for a share of the revenue, which has quintupled. He sent Kwon to investigate the attacks and has heard nothing since.

## Stat Block
No statblock (noncombatant).

## Continuity
Pays 500 gp (not scaled) for the truth, an end to the trouble, and Kwon. Tells the party they can keep any "gold" they find in the quarry. Takes prisoners off the party's hands and gets the Highport link out of them.
