# Zodznog

> Half-orc overseer of the guardhouse kitchen.

## Appearance
A female half-orc with a leathery face, surrounded by knives, cleavers and heavy pots.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Tolerates no interlopers in her kitchen, not even Brubgrok or Glyrthiel; gives an uninvited visitor one chance to leave gracefully, then throws a cleaver.

## Goals & Secrets
Keeps her four slave cooks on the brink of starvation and exhaustion. Knows the ladder in the buttery (13) leads to the spring chamber (26).
