# Uzgrod

> Half-orc chief groom of the guardhouse stable.

## Appearance
A half-orc groom with a scimitar and a belt of throwing daggers.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Cagey: he pretends to accept a story he thinks is bunk so he can ambush the teller. Prefers to throw daggers from behind the dividing wall or a draft animal.

## Goals & Secrets
Has a key to the spring room (26). Six slaves do the real work in the stable.
