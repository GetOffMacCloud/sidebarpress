# Nubgrod

> Bugbear taskmaster who trains and disciplines the guardhouse slaves (with Snikscab).

## Appearance
A big, brutal "goblin" to the slaves, who don't know what bugbears are.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Stealthy and cunning, but grown lazy with fear doing his work for him.

## Goals & Secrets
Shares the old chapel (20) with Snikscab; the pile of furs hides two pouches of coin.
