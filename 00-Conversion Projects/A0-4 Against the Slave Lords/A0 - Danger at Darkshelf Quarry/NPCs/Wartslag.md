# Wartslag

> Bugbear blacksmith of the guardhouse lower level.

## Appearance
A tall, powerful "goblin" (the slaves' words) at a forge of glowing coals, with an anvil, a bellows and a bench of hammers and tongs.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Tinkers, snoozes, or plays knucklebones with his two goblin assistants. If he notices trouble in the stable he hides behind the forge to ambush intruders, or throws hammers at lightly armored foes.

## Goals & Secrets
Shoes the quarry's mules and horses and makes or mends any iron or steel. Has a key to the spring room (26).
