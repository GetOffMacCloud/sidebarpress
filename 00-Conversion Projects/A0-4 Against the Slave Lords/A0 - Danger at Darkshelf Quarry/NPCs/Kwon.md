# Kwon

> Neistor's agent: a human monk posing as a slave in the quarry's holding area.

## Appearance
A lean, half-starved man among the slaves in the northern alcoves of the eastern cellblock, manacled like the rest.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Patient and careful. He has learned to pick his manacles and makes secret forays through the trash chute (area 50), always back in time for the head count.

## Goals & Secrets
Sent by Neistor to find what has been killing Darkshelf's people, he found the hidden jetty and the ventilation shaft (both in his reports) and slipped into a slave shipment to scout the quarry. He is not in immediate need of rescue.
