# Clerics of the Eye

> The three evil clerics of the Elder Elemental Eye whom Brubgrok shelters in the quarry's depths.

## Appearance
Men in robes over studded leather with bronze studs, a shield close at hand, each with an ordinary mace and a stone-headed one.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Pretend to welcome intruders long enough to use cause fear, then fight with sling and mace, keeping clear of their mudtigers' bursts.

## Goals & Secrets
They tend the Elemental Temple (42), breed mudtigers and keep the killer frogs in the caverns, and serve as Brubgrok's accountants and scribes, so they know the Highport link. Their chests hold three potions of healing and the herbal cures for puffball rot.
