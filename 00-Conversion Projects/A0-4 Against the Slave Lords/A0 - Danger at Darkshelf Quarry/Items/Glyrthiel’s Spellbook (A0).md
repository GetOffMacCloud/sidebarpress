# Glyrthiel's Spellbook

> A slim spellbook bound in green leather, hidden under a false drawer bottom.

## Properties
*Spellbook.* Contains *comprehend languages*, *shield*, *sleep*, and *unseen servant* (all 1st level). A wizard can copy them in the usual way (2 hours and 50 gp per spell level).

## History
1e: *friends, read magic, shield, sleep, unseen servant*. *Friends* is a cantrip in 5e, so it isn't in the book; *read magic* becomes *comprehend languages*.

## Found In
- [[A0-01 The Guardhouse#18. Glyrthiel’s Chamber]]: in a false drawer bottom (DC 15 Investigation)

## Continuity
Ordinary treasure once found.
