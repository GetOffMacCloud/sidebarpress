# Herbal Cure

> A stoppered vial of bitter green paste smelling of moss and vinegar.

## Properties
*Potion, common (Sidebar Press).* Swallowing one dose as an action ends **puffball rot**, the disease carried by the spores of the cavern puffballs (area 61). It has no other effect.

## History
The clerics of the Eye brew it against the puffball spores of their own caverns (1e: "an herbal preparation that cures the malady caused by the spores").

## Found In
- [[A0-02 The Quarry#44. Clerics’ Quarters]]: three vials
- [[A0-03 The Caverns#65. Cleric’s Chamber]]: two vials

## Continuity
Useless outside the Darkshelf caverns, unless the DM puts puffballs elsewhere.
