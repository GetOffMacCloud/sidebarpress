# Stone-Headed Mace

> A mace with a head of dense grey stone, bound to an oak haft.

## Properties
*Weapon (mace), mundane.* Works as a mace (1d6 bludgeoning), but you have a **−1 penalty to attack rolls** against a creature wearing medium or heavy armor. It holds no ferrous metal, so a mudtiger's Magnetize doesn't affect it.

## History
The clerics of the Eye fight beside mudtigers with these (1e: −1 to hit against AC 5 or better).

## Found In
- [[A0-02 The Quarry#42. Elemental Temple]], [[A0-02 The Quarry#44. Clerics’ Quarters]], [[A0-03 The Caverns#65. Cleric’s Chamber]]: one per cleric

## Continuity
Handy for anyone fighting mudtigers.
