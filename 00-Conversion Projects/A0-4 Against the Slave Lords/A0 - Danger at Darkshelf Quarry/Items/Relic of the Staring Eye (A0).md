# Relic of the Staring Eye

> A steep pyramid of blackened, greased iron set with discs of black onyx and tarnished copper that look like staring eyes.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon (Sidebar Press).* The relic radiates an unholy aura in a 30-foot radius. Undead in the aura have **advantage on saving throws against effects that turn undead**. A creature that casts *animate dead* within the aura can control **one additional undead** with that casting. As an art object it is worth **100 gp**.

## History
A relic of the Elder Elemental Eye (1e: −2 to turn undead, +2 to control undead; 500 gp art object, ÷ 5).

## Found In
- [[A0-03 The Caverns#60. North Rubbish Heap]]: hidden in the junk

## Continuity
A thread to the Elder Elemental Eye. Destroying or consecrating it is a story award at the DM's discretion.
