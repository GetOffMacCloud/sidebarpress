# A0 — Bestiary (5e)

Every creature and NPC in *Danger at Darkshelf Quarry*, converted per [[A0 - 5e Conversion Ledger]] §3–§4. Rules are the 2014 *Monster Manual* (MM) and *Dungeon Master's Guide* (DMG).

> [!info] How to read this bestiary
> - **MM creatures** are listed by name, CR and the module's riders only. The FGU module carries their SRD 5.1 statblocks.
> - **Custom blocks** are given in full in Fantasy Grounds import format (the FGU build reads these blocks). Each opens with its 1e source line.
> - **CR** follows the DMG table; **XP** is the MM value for the CR.
> - **Party size:** counts in the site keys are for 6 PCs, with the 4-PC count in brackets.

## MM creatures

### Worg
**Worg** (MM, CR 1/2). Goblin worg-riders saddle a worg in 2 rounds; a rider can split its movement around an attack.

### Riding Horse
**Riding Horse** (MM, CR 1/4). The patrols' light warhorses.

### Commoner
**Commoner** (MM, CR 0). Slaves who fight from fear (areas 12, 24) use an improvised **club (1d4)** or a pitchfork or stave (**quarterstaff, 1d6**). The free quarry workers of area 39 carry **picks (1d6)** and fight only in self-defense. Slaves in 21 and 53 don't fight.

### Bugbear
**Bugbear** (MM, CR 1). **Snikscab** and **Nubgrod** (areas 20–21) carry the keys to every manacle in the guardhouse; hostage rule in area 21 at 15 hp or fewer. The checkpoint bugbears (51–53) use the javelin line for their spears and thrust them through the bars.

### Zombie
**Zombie** (MM, CR 1/4). Ordered to attack any living thing in area 60; they tolerate the ghouls.

### Ghoul
**Ghoul** (MM, CR 1). They attack any living thing except the clerics of the Eye.

### Boar
**Boar** (MM, CR 1/4). Its Relentless trait is the 1e rule that a boar fights on below 0 hp.

### Black Bear
**Black Bear** (MM, CR 1/2).

### Wolf
**Wolf** (MM, CR 1/4). 2–5 (2–3 for 4 PCs).

### Panther
**Panther** (MM, CR 1/4).

**Mudtiger fingerlings** (area 63, north pool) are a hazard, not a creature: a creature that enters the pool or starts its turn there takes **2d4 piercing damage** (1d4 if it wears medium or heavy armor). The school produces one **2d6 lightning burst** (DC 11 Dexterity for half) filling the pool and 10 feet around it, Recharge 5–6.

**Lore.** Mudtigers dwell on the elemental planes where water and earth meet to form mud and ooze, and come to the Material Plane through elemental vortices or when summoned. Evil elemental clerics have lately bred colonies of them. A mudtiger looks like a cross between a shark and an aquatic lizard: a sinuous, scaled body, a pair of triangular ventral fins, a blunt, toothy snout, and two stubby forelimbs tipped with broad, rayed fins, iridescent green with blue-green stripes. On land it slithers and hunches, sometimes rearing on its forelimbs; in water it wriggles and steers with them, and it burrows through sand, ooze, and mud.
