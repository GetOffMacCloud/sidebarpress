- **Fighting Style: Defense.** +1 AC while wearing armor.
- **Second Wind.** Bonus action: regain 1d10 + 2 hit points. Once per short rest.
- **Action Surge.** One extra action on your turn. Once per short rest.
- **Arcane Recovery.** Once a day after a short rest, recover spell slots totalling up to 2 levels.
