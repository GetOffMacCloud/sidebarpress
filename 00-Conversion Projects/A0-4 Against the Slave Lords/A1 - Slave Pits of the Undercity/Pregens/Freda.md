## Features

- **Favored Enemy.** Humanoids (orcs and humans): advantage on Survival checks to track them and Intelligence checks to recall information about them.
- **Natural Explorer.** Underdark.
- **Fighting Style: Archery.** +2 to attack rolls with ranged weapons.
- **Primeval Awareness.** Spend a spell slot to sense aberrations, celestials, dragons, elementals, fey, fiends and undead within 1 mile for 1 minute per slot level.
- **Hunter: Colossus Slayer.** Once per turn, +1d8 damage to a creature below its hit point maximum.
- **Extra Attack.** Attack twice when you take the Attack action.
- **Ability Score Improvement.** Dexterity 18 to 20.
