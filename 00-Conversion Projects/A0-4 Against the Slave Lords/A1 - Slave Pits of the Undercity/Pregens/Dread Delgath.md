## Features

- **Arcane Recovery.** Once a day after a short rest, recover spell slots totalling up to 3 levels.
- **Evocation Savant.** Copying evocation spells costs half.
- **Sculpt Spells.** Protect 1 + spell level creatures from your evocation spells: they auto-succeed and take no damage.
- **Wand of fireballs.** Up to 3 charges per use: fireball (DC 15), +1d6 per charge after the first.
- **Ability Score Improvement.** Intelligence 19 to 20.
