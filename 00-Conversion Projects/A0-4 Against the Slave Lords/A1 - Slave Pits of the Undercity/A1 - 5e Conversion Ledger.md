# A1 — 5e Conversion Ledger

Every number the 5e table or the FGU build has to touch: the 1e source, the 5e conversion, and the reason. **Review it before vault notes, statblocks or FG records are generated.** Page numbers are PDF pages in `_source/A0-A4 source.pdf` (printed page = PDF page − 1). Maps: tournament maps pp. 52–53, full campaign maps pp. 54–55.

> [!info] Scope of this pass
> - **The 1980 tournament module, run as chapter two of the A-series campaign chain** (A0 1–3 · **A1 4–5** · A2 5–6 · A3 6–7 · A4 7–8). Highport in the Pomarj, as printed.
> - **Rules:** 5e 2014 (PHB, DMG, MM) with the C12 / T1 / A0 ledger rules ([[A0 - 5e Conversion Ledger]] §1). MM statblocks where one exists; custom blocks marked **(custom)**. Non-SRD MM creatures get full blocks written in our own words for the FGU build, as A0's troglodyte and water weird did.
> - **Text:** stays close to Cook. This ledger changes numbers, not prose.
> - **Tournament material** is kept as extras (Greybeard, 2026-11-25): the nine pregens as real FG pregen sheets and a *Convention Mode* page (§9).
> - **Decided already (2026-11-25):** pregens built once at **5th level** and reused through A4 (**D1**); real **FG pregen sheets** (**D2**); **6 PCs = as printed, 4 PCs = ⅔** of every group of two or more (**D3**); magic items **capped at rare, consumables kept** (**D4**). Open questions are **⚑ D5–D10** in §11.

## 0 · Recommended 5e level

**Start at 4th level, reach 5th on leaving the temple, finish A1 at 5th.** A2 opens at 5th.

| Section | Areas | 5e level | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The temple compound** (surface) | T1–T19, the ruins, the walls, the cemetery | 4 | The temple chamber (T18) and the false slavers (T12) are the two big fights; the rest is traps, hazards and smaller guard posts |
| **The sewers and the aspis nest** | S1–S21 | 5 | Three set-piece fights: the aspis breeding chamber (S6), the orc and ogre outpost (S12), and the Slave Lord's Den (S21) |

**Milestones:** 5th level when the party goes below (by the altar shaft at T18, the cemetery crypt at T8, or the false slavers' trap door at T12). The adventure ends at 5th; the party rises to 6th during A2.

**Entrances.** The party can come in by the back secret door (T1, the tournament start), the stable (T6), the front gate posing as slave buyers (T16), or straight down into the sewers by the tournament's boulder shaft (S1). Posing as buyers routes them to T12 first (the false slavers' test), as printed.

**Party size (D3).** Numbers as printed are the **6-PC** version. For **4 PCs**, every group of two or more identical creatures is cut to ⅔ (rounded); single creatures and named NPCs never change. **A1 was written for 6–8 1e characters, and several rooms land well past Deadly in 5e even at the printed size** (§5). **They stay as printed and are staged in waves (D5)**: T12's assassins strike from the shadows, S6's ants arrive over five rounds, S12's reserves wait out of sight, S15's orcs call for help; each area page carries its wave plan.

## 1 · Conversion rules (additions to A0 §1)

| 1e | 5e rule applied | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Trap damage | DMG severity: **temple (4th level): Setback 1d10 / Dangerous 2d10 / Deadly 4d10; sewers (5th level): 2d10 / 4d10 / 10d10**. Save DCs 10–11 / 12–15 / 16–20 | DMG p. 121 |
| "FOR TOURNAMENT USE: N points of damage" | **Kept on the Convention Mode page only** (fixed damage, no rolls). Campaign play uses the dice | The tournament fixed damage for fairness between tables |
| Wandering Monster Roster (caps on numbers) | **Kept** as printed; each wandering creature killed is struck off | It's the module's population model |
| 1e half-orc "HD 1" rank and file | **Half-Orc Slaver** (custom, CR 1/4): the MM Bandit with Relentless Endurance | Tougher than a bandit, below a thug |
| Named NPC classes | Fighter 3 → Temple Guard (custom, CR 1). Fighter 4 → Gatekeeper (CR 2). Fighter 7 → **Veteran** (MM). Assassin 4–5 → Cutthroat (CR 2). Cleric 6 → High Priestess (CR 4). Fighter/cleric 5/4 → Slaver Priest (CR 3). Thief 7 → Den Master (CR 4). Magic-user 6 → Slaver Mage (CR 4). Cleric/magic-user 3/3 → Witch-Doctor (CR 1) | |
| Overbear / grapple, *bend bars/lift gates* | Grapple rules; **Bend Bars → DC 20 Strength**; "18 strength to lift" → DC 18 Strength (Athletics) | |
| *Open doors −1* | DC 17 Strength (Athletics) | as A0 |
| "Strikes as an N-HD monster" | Attack bonus = 2 + ½ N (rounded down), damage as printed in 5e dice | Keeps the relative threat |
| Thief detect traps / open locks | DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) to find; DC 15 thieves' tools to disarm or pick. Traps the text says *only a thief* can find need **proficiency with thieves' tools** to find | Keeps the class niche the tournament built around Blodgett |
| Spells | *cure light wounds* → *cure wounds*; *protection from good* → *protection from evil and good*; *resist fire* → *protection from energy* (fire) at 3rd, or *absorb elements* is not used (not PHB); *prayer* → *bless* (allies) and *bane* (foes); *spiritual hammer* → *spiritual weapon*; *scare* → [[Cause Fear]] (our homebrew); *affect normal fires* → *prestidigitation* (snuff or dim a flame); *paralyzation* (illusionist) → *hypnotic pattern*; *monster summoning I* → dropped (*conjure* spells are too strong; the mage gets *misty step* instead) | |

## 12 · Errata and source notes

- **Credits** in A1 list "Able Assistance" and other 1980 staff; they go on the module's credits page.
- **T12** spell list prints *darkness* as a 1st-level clerical spell (1e) — 5e *darkness* is 2nd level; the Slaver Priest's slots allow it.
- **T16a** "reduce the damage by" is missing its fraction (½), as is **T17**'s ("reduce damage by").
- **T18** the cleric's armor reads "AC 1 due to dexterity" with plate; 5e AC 19 (plate + Dex doesn't add) plus a shield.
- **S18b** "a **pearl of wisdom** set into a brooch" (D7).
- **S19a** "one ot his fingers" → "one of his fingers".
- **Same nine pregens** appear in A2 (p. 93), A3 (pp. 121, 123) and A4 (p. 158): checked by name; their gear changes per module (A4 starts with none).

## Random numbers of creatures (Greybeard, 2026-11-25)

Where the module rolls how many creatures appear, the FGU module keeps the dice instead of fixing a number:
- **Wandering monsters:** the Wall (d8), Ruin (d6) and Sewer (d10) encounter tables are FG tables with output set to Encounter. A count is written `[2d4x]` beside the NPC link; percentage riders (50% ghasts, the campfire extras, the slaver band's caster) are d100 sub-tables linked from the row.
- **Keyed rooms with rolled counts** (T8 ghouls 2–5 and worker ants 2–8; S8 chambers 2–12 workers and 1–3 soldiers) are FG random encounters: each creature has a dice expression, and the GM presses Generate.
- **4 PCs:** every table and random encounter has a 4-PC version at about ⅔ of the printed range (2–8 → 1d4+1, 2–12 → 1d6+1, 3–18 → 2d6, 2–20 → 2d6, 1–4 → 1d3).
