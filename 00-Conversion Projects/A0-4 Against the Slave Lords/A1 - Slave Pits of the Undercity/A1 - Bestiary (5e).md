# A1 — Bestiary (5e)

Every creature and NPC in *Slave Pits of the Undercity*, converted per [[A1 - 5e Conversion Ledger]] §3. Rules are the 2014 *Monster Manual* (MM) and *Dungeon Master's Guide* (DMG).

> [!info] How to read this bestiary
> - **MM creatures** are listed with the module's riders. The FGU module carries their SRD 5.1 statblocks.
> - **Custom blocks** are given in full in Fantasy Grounds import format (the FGU build reads them). Each opens with its 1e source line.
> - Counts in the site keys are for 6 PCs, with the 4-PC count in brackets.

## MM creatures

### Orc
**Orc** (MM, CR 1/2). Crossbowmen (T12b, T16a, S12, S16b, S19a, S21) swap the javelin for a **light crossbow** (*Ranged:* +3, 80/320 ft, 1d8 + 1 piercing). The halberd orcs of T7 and the patrols swap the greataxe for a **halberd** (*Melee:* +5, reach 10 ft, 1d10 + 3 slashing). In the T7 shed, cornered orcs fight with advantage out of panic. In S15a, orcs within sight of the tribal standard have +1 to attack rolls.

### Ogre
**Ogre** (MM, CR 2). The S12 captains carry six rocks each: *Ranged:* +6, 30/120 ft, 2d6 + 4 bludgeoning.

### Ghoul
**Ghoul** (MM, CR 1). The T3 ghouls and ghasts never attack the orcs or the High Priestess.

### Ghast
**Ghast** (MM, CR 2).

### Zombie
**Zombie** (MM, CR 1/4). Wall patrols only.

### Skeleton
**Skeleton** (MM, CR 1/4). Wall patrols only.

### Wight
**Wight** (MM, CR 3). The T9 wight has a shaky alliance with the High Priestess and is fed victims by the half-orcs.

### Basilisk
**Basilisk** (MM, CR 3). The T10 pen holds two; any round spent in the pen, roll a d6 for each: on a 1 it finds the party.

### Harpy
**Harpy** (MM, CR 1). Tallow from the T13 candles in the ears gives advantage on saves against Luring Song.

### Stirge
**Stirge** (MM, CR 1/8). They avoid creatures carrying open flames.

### Doppelganger
**Doppelganger** (MM, CR 3).

### Troll
**Troll** (MM, CR 5). Shrunk in the T18 poorbox by the [[Stone of Diminution (A1)]]; it bursts out at full size in round 2.

### Gray Ooze
**Gray Ooze** (MM, CR 1/2). In the north grain trench of S4.

### Giant Rat
**Giant Rat** (MM, CR 1/8).

### Crocodile
**Crocodile** (MM, CR 1/2).

### Green Slime
**Green slime** (DMG hazard, p. 105).

### Veteran
**Veteran** (MM, CR 3). The S19a caravan fighter carries a **battleaxe** (1d8 + 3, versatile 1d10 + 3) in place of the longsword and a [[Stone of Good Luck (A1)]]. The 4th-level fighter among the S19a slaves is a Veteran too, and joins the party if they can equip him.

### Commoner
**Commoner** (MM, CR 0). The slaves who fight (T16b) grapple and shove.

## Lore

**Aspis.** Considered unfathomable by humans, aspis seldom come to settled lands except to raid for food. A nest lives underground in sloping tunnels bored through soft rock or earth: one to three egg chambers, two to four granaries, one to six grub hatcheries and a central chamber, the tunnels about 4 feet high and well guarded by traps. *Drones* are the adult males: 6-foot giant weevils with long proboscises and faceted eyes, without individual names, fanatically loyal to their one cow. They use any weapon but bows, travel on six legs and fight standing on two, wielding two weapons and two shields. They speak in clicks and whistles and "write" with scents; about one in twenty speaks Common. *Larvae* are blind white grubs, 1½ to 3 feet long, that swim and burrow in rotting hatchery pits. The drones raise one larva in each nest to become the *cow*: a bloated egg-layer 10 to 15 feet long whose skin exudes an acidic slime. A tenth of nests share their tunnels with giant ants.

**Giant sundew.** A near-sentient carnivorous plant: a 3–4-foot mound of grey-green tarry ropes or rags that smells of sweet syrup and draws flies. It anchors itself with hairlike roots and can pull itself along with its sticky tendrils.
