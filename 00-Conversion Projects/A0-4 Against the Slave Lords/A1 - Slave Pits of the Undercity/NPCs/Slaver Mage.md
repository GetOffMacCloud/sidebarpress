# Slaver Mage

> The caravan's magic-user, inspecting the slave stock before the march out of Highport.

## Appearance
Robes, a staff and a dagger, silver bracers, a wand hidden up one sleeve.

## Tactics
Opens with *web* across the cage corridor, then *slow* on the front rank and magic missiles from the wand. *Misty step* or *invisibility* when cornered.

## Goals & Secrets
Works with the caravan merchant and the fighter; the five orcs are their guides and interpreters. Each organizer carries three 100 gp gems.
