# Caravan Organizers

> A slave merchant and a hard-bitten woman fighter, choosing the hardiest slaves for the caravan.

## Appearance
The merchant hides a dagger under his robes; the fighter wears chain mail and carries a battleaxe.

## Tactics
The fighter wades in with her battleaxe while the mage casts; the merchant tries to slip away with his map.

## Goals & Secrets
The merchant carries the map of the caravan route (it leads to A2). The fighter carries a stone of good luck. The merchant is a [[A1 - Bestiary (5e)#Commoner|Commoner]] with a dagger; the fighter a [[A1 - Bestiary (5e)#Veteran|Veteran]] with a battleaxe.
