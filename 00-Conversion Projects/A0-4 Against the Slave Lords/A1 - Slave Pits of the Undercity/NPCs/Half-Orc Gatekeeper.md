# Half-Orc Gatekeeper

> The half-orc fighter who works the outer portcullis winch.

## Appearance
Eating gruel at a table, dagger and longsword beside the bowl, three collared slaves glaring at him from the wall.

## Tactics
Tries to escape the room if attacked. The chained slaves grapple and shove him if he comes within reach unarmed.

## Goals & Secrets
Carries the key to the slaves' collars and a 250 gp gem. The same statblock leads some orc patrols.
