# Orc Witch-Doctor

> The tribe's cleric-mage, holding the ledges across the sewer at S16.

## Appearance
An orc with a sling and a mace behind a low rock wall on the southern ledge.

## Tactics
Reads his scroll of *bless* over himself and two orcs, casts *shield*, then *darkness* on the leading figure and *cause fear* on the second; snuffs lights with *prestidigitation*. Half cover behind his wall.

## Goals & Secrets
Two sacks of 50 gp and two gems (25 gp, 250 gp) in the chamber behind him.
