# Orc Chieftain

> Chief of the orc tribe that guards the slavers' sewers, flanked by two ogre advisors and eight bodyguards.

## Appearance
An extremely large orc on a platform heaped with tasteless furs, under tapestries of torture scenes.

## Tactics
Fights to the death with his whole court. They rush the party, push past, and trap them in the den; losing anyway, they burn the room or fill it with smoke.

## Goals & Secrets
His chest is locked and warded with a *glyph of warding* only he knows; the key is buried in the northwest corner. Inside: copper, four gems and three poisoned daggers.
