# Den Master

> The thief who runs the slave pits from a moated den beneath the temple cemetery.

## Appearance
A man at a table heaped with crates and boxes, five giant weasels lounging round him, ten orc crossbowmen on the ledge above the sewage moat.

## Tactics
Sends the weasels over the moat first, then ducks behind the crates, sips his potion of invisibility and waits to Sneak Attack, sipping again after each strike. If the fight is lost he climbs the ladder to the cemetery (T8).

## Goals & Secrets
On his table: the records of the slavers' work in the area and a map of the caravan route and its stops. In the crates: supplies for the caravan, 1,000 gp in coin and three pieces of jewelry (250 gp each).
