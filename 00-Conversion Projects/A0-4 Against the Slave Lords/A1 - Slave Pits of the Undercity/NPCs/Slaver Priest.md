# Slaver Priest

> A half-orc fighter-cleric who runs the false slavers' trap for would-be infiltrators.

## Appearance
Dressed as a slave among twenty "slaves" by the door, his shackles false.

## Tactics
Stands back by the door and casts *bless* as the false slaves rise, then *hold person* and *silence* on the party's casters; fights with his +1 short sword only when pressed. Leads patrols outside.

## Goals & Secrets
Every new buyer is shown this room first and watched; only after several visits is a buyer taken to the real pens. He wears a 100 gp ring.
