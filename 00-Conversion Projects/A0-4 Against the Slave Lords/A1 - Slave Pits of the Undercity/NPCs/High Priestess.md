# High Priestess

> A 6th-level priestess of the one-eyed orc god, mistress of the burnt temple above the slave pits.

## Appearance
A woman in plate mail with a mace, standing behind three half-orc guards before the statue of the one-eyed god, in a room of braziers, candles and incense.

## Tactics
Stays behind her guards and casts. Warned, she opens with *silence* at the door, *bless* on herself and the guards, then *protection from energy* (fire) and *protection from evil and good*, keeping *bless* up. Drinks her *potion of speed* when the fight turns.

## Goals & Secrets
The undead of the temple (ghouls, ghasts, the walls' zombies and skeletons) never attack her, and the wight of T9 keeps a shaky alliance with her. Under the statue is the trapped trap door to the aspis lair; in the niche at its top lie 180 gp, a 500 gp gem and the papers for the next caravan.
