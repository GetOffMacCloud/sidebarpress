# Pearl of Wisdom

> Lustrous white pearl in a brooch

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by a cleric; Sidebar Press).* A lustrous white pearl, here set in a brooch.
- **Steeping.** Until you have been attuned to the pearl for 30 days, you can add 1d4 to one Wisdom check per long rest.
- **Wisdom.** Once you have been attuned to the pearl for 30 days, your Wisdom score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20, and Steeping ends. You keep the increase only while you remain attuned; if your attunement ends, you lose it after your next long rest.
- **Curse.** A cursed pearl looks and identifies as an ordinary one. Its curse shows only after 30 days of attunement, when your Wisdom *decreases* by 1 instead. While cursed, you are unwilling to part with the pearl and won't willingly end your attunement. *Remove curse* ends the attunement; the lost point returns after your next long rest.
- **Which pearls are cursed (Greybeard, 2026-11-25).** If an adventure holds only one pearl of wisdom, it is cursed. If it holds more than one, roll a d20 for each; a 1 means cursed.

**This pearl (A1, S18b) is cursed.**
