# Oil of Slipperiness

> Black oil in a potion bottle

## Properties
*Potion, uncommon.* This sticky black unguent can cover one Medium or smaller creature (10 minutes to apply): the creature gains the effect of *freedom of movement* for 8 hours. Poured on the ground as an action, it covers a 10-foot square, duplicating *grease* for 8 hours.

## Found In
- S8b, under the queen
