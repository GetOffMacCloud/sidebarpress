# Stone of Diminution

> Small grey stone

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon (Sidebar Press).* A small grey stone with **1d6 charges**. As an action, a creature holding it can expend 1 charge to shrink: it is reduced as by the *reduce* effect of *enlarge/reduce*, but to a few inches tall, for 1d4 + 1 × 10 minutes (no concentration) or until it lets go of the stone, when it returns to normal size at once. (1e: as a *potion of diminution* while held, 2–5 turns, 1–6 charges.)

## Found In
- T18, the troll in the poorbox
