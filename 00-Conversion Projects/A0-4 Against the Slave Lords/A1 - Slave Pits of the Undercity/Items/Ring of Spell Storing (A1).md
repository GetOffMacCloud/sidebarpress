# Ring of Spell Storing

> Plain silver ring

## Properties
*Ring, rare (requires attunement).* The ring stores spells cast into it, holding them until the attuned wearer uses them. It can store up to 5 levels of spells at a time. When found, it holds *charm person* (1st), *jump* (1st) and *shatter* (2nd), cast at DC 13, +5 to hit. While wearing it, you can cast any spell stored in it, using the slot level, save DC, attack bonus and spellcasting ability of the original caster. Any creature can cast a spell of 1st to 5th level into the ring by touching it as the spell is cast; the spell has no effect except to be stored.

## Found In
- T5b, under the sundew (1e: friends, shatter, jump; friends becomes charm person)
