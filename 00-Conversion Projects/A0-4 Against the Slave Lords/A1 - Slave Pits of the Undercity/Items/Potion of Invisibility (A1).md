# Potion of Invisibility

> Clear potion

## Properties
*Potion, very rare (a consumable, kept under D4).* When you drink this potion, you become invisible for 1 hour; anything you wear or carry is invisible with you. The effect ends early if you attack or cast a spell. **The Den Master sips it:** each sip (a bonus action) gives the effect for up to 10 minutes; the flask holds 8 sips, so whatever he hasn't drunk is left for the party.

## Found In
- S21, the Den Master
