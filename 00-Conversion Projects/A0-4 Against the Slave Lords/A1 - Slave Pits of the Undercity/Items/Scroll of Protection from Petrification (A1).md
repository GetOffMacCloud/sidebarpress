# Scroll of Protection from Petrification

> Scroll

## Properties
*Scroll, rare (Sidebar Press, as the DMG scrolls of protection).* Using an action to read the scroll encloses you in an invisible barrier extending from you to form a 5-foot-radius, 10-foot-high cylinder. For 5 minutes, no creature in the cylinder can be petrified, and creatures in it have advantage on saving throws against effects that would turn them to stone. The cylinder moves with you. (1e: protection from petrification, 10-foot radius.) Handy against the T10 basilisks.

## Found In
- S6, in an egg case
