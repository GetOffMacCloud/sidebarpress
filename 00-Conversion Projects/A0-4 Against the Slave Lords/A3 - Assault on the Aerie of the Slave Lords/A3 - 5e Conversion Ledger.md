# A3 — 5e Conversion Ledger

Every number the 5e table or the FGU build has to touch: the 1e source, the 5e conversion, and the reason. **Review it before vault notes, statblocks or FG records are generated.** Page numbers are PDF pages in `_source/A0-A4 source.pdf` (printed page = PDF page − 1). Maps: the cave map (Part A) and the "sewer map" (the catacombs, Part C) p. 125; tactical situations A3-A p. 126; the DM's map of Suderham p. 127; the players' map of Suderham p. 128; the Isle of the Slave Lords with its cut-away p. 129. All five are raster scans.

> [!info] Scope of this pass
> - **The 1981 module, run as chapter four of the A-series chain** (A0 1–3 · A1 4–5 · A2 5–6 · **A3 6–7** · A4 7–8). Three parts, as printed: **Part A** the caves of Drachensgrab (A1–A9), **Part B** the hidden city of Suderham (B1–B68), **Part C** the catacombs and the Council Chamber of the Slave Lords (C1–C9).
> - **Rules:** 5e 2014 (PHB, DMG, MM) with the A0, A1 and A2 ledger rules. MM statblocks where one exists; custom blocks marked **(custom)**. Non-SRD MM creatures (the killer mimic's base, the storoper's roper base is SRD) get full blocks in our own words.
> - **Carried over:** 6 PCs = as printed, 4 PCs = ⅔ of every group of two or more; magic items capped at rare, consumables kept; treasure ÷ 5 with DMG tier snapping; rolled counts kept as FG tables (dice in the result column); area codes unique per part (A, B, C prefixes are already printed, so no renumbering this time).
> - **Text:** stays close to Hammack. This ledger changes numbers, not prose.
> - **Open questions are ⚑ D1–D14 in §11.** Nothing below them is built until they're answered.

> [!warning] Name clash in the source
> Part A's areas are printed **A1–A9**, the same codes as the module names *A1* and *A2*. The site notes and FG pages keep the printed codes but always write "area A2" (never bare "A2") when an area is meant, and the module names in italics (*A2*). (⚑ D2)

## 0 · Recommended 5e level (⚑ D1)

**Proposal: Part A at 6th; 7th from the start of Part B; finish A3 at 7th.** A4 opens at 7th.

| Section | Areas | 5e level | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Part A: the caves** | A, B, A1–A9 | 6 | Gnoll guards, the piercer cavern, the hyaenodons, the blue-fire curtain, the storoper, Wimpell Frump's illusion throne room (A9 is the set piece) |
| **Part B: Suderham** | B1–B68 | 7 | A stealth and investigation section: four clue-givers (B1, B14, B29, B44) point to the Sign of the Rose (B13). Fights are optional (B19 cutpurse, B64 rakes); the garrison is an alarm clock, not an encounter |
| **Part C: the catacombs** | C1–C9 | 7 | Golem, killer mimic, hell hounds, the minotaur maze, two gelatinous cubes, the shambling mound and snakes, then the Council Chamber |

**Milestone:** 7th level when the party emerges at Suderham (the module's own time skip: the lords who hired them heal and re-equip them before the semifinal). **Running Part B/C alone** (the tournament semifinal): start at 7th.

**Party size.** Numbers as printed are the 6-PC version; for 4 PCs, groups of two or more are cut to ⅔ (rounded). Single creatures and named NPCs never change. Hammack wrote for six to nine characters; the tournament ran nine.

## 2 · Module-wide mechanics

| Mechanic | 5e |
|---|---|
| **The Sword of Lyons** (A5) | See §7. Sheathed, it makes its bearer invisible until they attack |
| **The salt slide** (A1) | Fall 15 ft: **1d6 bludgeoning**, then the slide: **slowed** (as the spell) for 50 minutes; the bark toboggans from room B, flying or levitating avoid it; sliding on a shield: DC 15 Dexterity save to avoid the salt. Items in hand: a creature that falls drops one (1 on a d6 per hand); dropped items are lost in the salt on 1–3 on a d4. Fragile items: DC 11 save as an object (or just break on a 1–2 on a d6) |
| **The illusion throne** (A9) | Doubles Wimpell's spell ranges. The illusions are **"real" while unstruck**: each illusory creature has AC 10 and vanishes when any attack hits it; until then it attacks for real damage (as the creature it copies). Disbelief (Intelligence (Investigation) against Wimpell's DC) doesn't end them, as printed; the gem of seeing does |
| **The glass wall** (A9) | Blocks all ranged weapon attacks from the front (total cover from the front); *lightning bolt*, *thunderwave*, *shatter* break it (AC 13, 20 hp, immune to piercing) |
| **Suderham passes and disguises** | The aristocrats' passes get the party in (B1). Patrols demand them from groups of more than four after dark: a **DC 12 Charisma (Deception)** check by the speaker if the party looks suspicious (armor under robes, weapons out). A patrol leader can be bribed once for **200 gp** (bribes are prices, not treasure: not divided by 5) |
| **The alarm** | A whistle in the city: nearby patrols arrive in 1d4 rounds; **60 soldiers with a 5th-level mage and priest in 20 minutes**. The citadel (B59) can call **200 more** in 10 minutes and the whole garrison in 20. This is a **capture clock**, not an encounter: a party that fights the garrison is taken to the Slave Cells (B43) and the DM can run A4 from there (§9) |
| **Suderham money** | 15% to change money (B6); unofficial coin after one warning: everything confiscated (as printed) |
| **Clues to the catacombs** | B1 beggar ("Seek out the ivory paladin") → B14 (10 gp / 50 gp) → B13 and B29; B29 (100 gp) → B44 and B13; B44 (50 gp) → the "rows of rouge". Prices kept as printed (they're bribes, not treasure) |
| **The tournament capture** (C9) | As printed: wall of stone behind, *wall of force* ahead, sleeping gas: no save. The party wakes in A4 |

## 11 · Decisions for Cloud

- **⚑ D1 Levels.** Part A at 6th, 7th from Suderham on, A3 ends at 7th (A4 opens at 7th). *Recommend: yes.*
- **⚑ D2 Area codes.** Keep the printed A1–A9 / B1–B68 / C1–C9 codes (they're already unique within A3) and always write "area A2" in prose when the room is meant. *Recommend: yes* (renumbering to A-1 or CA1 would break the printed map labels).
- **⚑ D3 Pregens.** Keep the nine at 5th level with two gear sets (Part A, Semifinal) as separate pregen records. *Recommend: yes.* Alternative: level them to 7th for A3–A4.
- **⚑ D4 The piercers.** Thirty statblocks are no use; run A3 as a **hazard**: 2 MM piercers (upgraded to 4d6 drop damage, as the 1e "largest size") fall each round on anyone in the marked area; spells drop more as printed. *Recommend: yes.*
- **⚑ D5 The storoper's "stone puppet".** 1e gives no save. *Recommend: DC 15 Wisdom save*, the two first creatures hit only; it looks like petrification for one round, then they fight the party while it lives.
- **⚑ D6 Wimpell Frump.** CR 5 illusionist (8th level) with the printed spell order mapped to 5e (*fear, hypnotic pattern, blur, bane, blindness/deafness ×2, color spray ×2, darkness*). *Recommend: yes.*
- **⚑ D7 The flesh golem's hit points.** 1e: "poorly made", 35 hp, nonmagical weapons half damage. MM flesh golem: 93 hp. *Recommend: 60 hp, AC 15* (iron plates), MM otherwise — still an Easy fight at 7th.
- **⚑ D8 The Council Chamber, campaign version.** Run as printed (Deadly ×2, losing leads into A4) with full 5e blocks for all five; Mordrammo always escapes. Their very rare items: Ajakstu's **staff of power** has 3 charges and he won't use the last one — *recommend: it becomes a powerless staff if he's killed* (its magic is bound to him, a Sidebar Press rule), and the **crystal ball** is fixed in the Slave Lords' citadel above (B59), not carried. That keeps the rare cap. *Recommend: yes.*
- **⚑ D9 City NPCs.** The rakes (8th-level fighters, 40 hp) as **Veterans** with 40 hp; the B24 magic-users as **Acolytes** with wizard cantrips (they never fight); Ayares as **Spy**. *Recommend: yes.*
- **⚑ D10 Wandering tables.** Caves: leprechaun → **"a sprite thief"** (custom trickster, CR 1/4: steals one item and vanishes); leucrotta: custom block (CR 3). City: rakshasa (CR 13) → *recommend:* keep it as a **disguised noble who is not hostile** (a roleplay encounter, no fight), assassin → **Spy** (the MM Assassin is CR 8). Others as §3.1.
- **⚑ D11 The Sword of Lyons.** Rare, attunement: *shortsword +1*, invisible blade, its sheathed bearer invisible until they attack (once per short rest after re-sheathing). *Recommend: yes.* (A permanent at-will invisibility would be legendary.)
- **⚑ D12 Item caps.** Longsword +3 → **+2**; the ioun stone: **protection** (+1 AC only); gauntlets of dexterity → **Dexterity 19** (custom, uncommon). *Recommend: yes.*
- **⚑ D13 Suderham as a capture clock.** Garrison fights are not built as encounters; a party that brawls with the city is captured and can go straight to A4 (as the module allows for C9). *Recommend: yes.*
- **⚑ D14 Maps.** Six maps as §10, using the printed players' map of Suderham as the Player version of the city. *Recommend: yes.*

## 12 · Errata and source notes

- The cave map labels the hyaenodons "HYENADON" (p. 126); the text says hyaenodon. We use **hyaenodon**.
- A3's catacombs map is titled **"Sewer Map"** on p. 125; the text calls the area the Catacombs (map C). We use **The Catacombs**.
- C9's tournament box says the men are laughing with no sound; the non-tournament box drops the silence (no *wall of force* yet). Kept as printed.
- The Slave Lords table (p. 118) lists "dispel magic, fireball, shocking grasp, slow, wall of force at L9" in Ajakstu's notes column next to his ring of spell storing. A 5e ring of spell storing holds only 5 spell levels, so his block treats these as **prepared spells**; the ring holds *shield* and *misty step* (his escape), and is treasure only if he dies (rare).
- Mordrammo "appears again in A4"; the round plan has him leave in round 2. Kept.
