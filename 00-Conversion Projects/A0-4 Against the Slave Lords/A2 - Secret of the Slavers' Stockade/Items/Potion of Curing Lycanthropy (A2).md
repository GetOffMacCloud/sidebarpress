# Potion of Curing Lycanthropy

> Dark green potion smelling of herbs

## Properties
*Potion, uncommon (Sidebar Press).* Drunk within an hour of being bitten by a lycanthrope, it ends the curse of lycanthropy on a 1–9 on a d20 (45%); afterwards it has no effect. It contains belladonna: on a 1 on a d20, the drinker is poisoned for 1 hour and takes 3d6 poison damage instead.

## Found In
- D25a, in the engineer's coffer
