# Potion of White Dragon Control

> Icy white potion

## Properties
*Potion, very rare (a consumable, kept under D4; Sidebar Press).* When you drink this potion, choose one white dragon you can see within 60 feet: it makes a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or is charmed by you for 1 hour, as the *dominate monster* spell (no concentration). A wyrmling or young dragon makes the save with disadvantage.
