# Flask of Smoke

> Flask of blue liquid

## Properties
*Consumable, common (Sidebar Press).* As an action, you throw this flask at a point within 30 feet. It bursts into a 10-foot-radius cloud of blue smoke that is heavily obscured for 6 rounds; a creature that leaves the smoke is blinded until the end of its next turn.

## Found In
- D22, Markessa's shelf (if she hasn't thrown it)
