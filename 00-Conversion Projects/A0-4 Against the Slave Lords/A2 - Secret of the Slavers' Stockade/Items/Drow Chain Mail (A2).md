# Drow Chain Mail

> Black mesh chain mail

## Properties
*Armor (chain shirt), rare to very rare (Sidebar Press).* Drow-made mesh of special manufacture that doesn't radiate magic. It comes as **+1** (the guards), **+2** (the captain) or **+3** (the merchant): add that bonus to AC. It can be worn under clothes and doesn't hinder spellcasting. **Sunlight:** exposed to daylight, it loses its bonus at once and crumbles to nothing within two weeks; kept in darkness it lasts. (Greybeard, 2026-11-25: kept as printed; nothing above rare survives to A3.) Drow weapons follow the same rule.

## Found In
- D38, the drow caravan (merchant +3, captain +2, six guards +1)
