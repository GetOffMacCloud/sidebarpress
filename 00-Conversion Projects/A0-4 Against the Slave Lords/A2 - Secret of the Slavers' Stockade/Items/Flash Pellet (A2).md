# Flash Pellet

> Black, earthy pellet

## Properties
*Consumable, common (Sidebar Press).* As an action, you throw this pellet at a point within 20 feet. It bursts in a blinding flash: each creature within 20 feet of that point that can see it makes a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or is blinded for 2 rounds. Five are found together.

## Found In
- D13, in a small wooden box (five pellets)
