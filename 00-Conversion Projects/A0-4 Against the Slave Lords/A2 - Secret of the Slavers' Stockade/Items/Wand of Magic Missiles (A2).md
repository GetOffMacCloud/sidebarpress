# Wand of Magic Missiles

> Black wand

## Properties
*Wand, uncommon.* This wand has 7 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 or more charges to cast *magic missile*: 1 charge for the 1st-level version, +1 spell level per extra charge. It regains 1d6 + 1 charges daily at dawn; if you expend the last charge, roll a d20, and on a 1 it crumbles to ashes. (1e: 12 charges.)

## Found In
- D35, on Markessa's dresser
