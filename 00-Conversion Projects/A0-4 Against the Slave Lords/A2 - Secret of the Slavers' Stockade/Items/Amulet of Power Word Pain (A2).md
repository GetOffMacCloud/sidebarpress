# Amulet of Power Word Pain

> Coinlike bronze amulet

## Properties
*Wondrous item, rare (Sidebar Press).* Once a day, holding the amulet, you can use an action to speak its word: each creature of your choice within 20 feet makes a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or is wracked with pain for 1 minute: its speed is 10 feet, and it has disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws other than Constitution saving throws. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. The amulet regains its use at dawn. It is found floating in a jar of altered ochre jelly (see D25g).

## Found In
- D25g, the mahogany case
