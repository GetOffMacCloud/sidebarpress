# Dust of Disappearance

> A pinch of fine powder

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon.* One use: as an action, throw it into the air; you and each creature and object within 10 feet of you become invisible for 2d4 minutes (attacking or casting does not end it). Markessa has a pinch on her powder puff.

## Found In
- D35, Markessa's powder puff
