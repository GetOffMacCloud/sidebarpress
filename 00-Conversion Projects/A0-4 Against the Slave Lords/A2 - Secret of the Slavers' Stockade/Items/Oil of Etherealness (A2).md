# Oil of Etherealness

> Cloudy gray oil

## Properties
*Potion, rare.* Beads of this cloudy gray oil form on the outside of its container. Applied to a Medium or smaller creature (10 minutes to apply), it lets the creature enter the Ethereal Plane for 1 hour, as the *etherealness* spell.

## Found In
- D25g, Chest #6 (Icar's hoard)
