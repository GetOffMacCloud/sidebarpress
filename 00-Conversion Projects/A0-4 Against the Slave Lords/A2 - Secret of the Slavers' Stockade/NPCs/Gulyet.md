# Gulyet

> Goblin witch-doctor, Markessa's assistant and apprentice.

## Appearance
A goblin woman in cut-down dwarven chain, hung with charms, a double whistle on a thong around her neck; her winter wolf is never far away.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Scheming and superstitious; meditates in incense smoke and reads the omens.

## Goals & Secrets
The only apprentice to meet Markessa's standards, and so the only one to survive. A secret compartment in her chair holds her savings.
