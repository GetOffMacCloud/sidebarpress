# Icar

> Blind fighter, commander of the hill fort garrison.

## Appearance
A 7-foot-tall black man in black plate armor whose helm has no eyeholes; carries the greatsword Death's Master.

## Personality & Mannerisms
A calm, battle-trained veteran who hears the party coming before they open the door, and sets an ambush. Plays with his foes the way he turns the roast.

## Goals & Secrets
Blind from birth; monastery training made his other senses keen enough to "see" better than a sighted man. He keeps a chained medusa in his closet (F39), and he is immune to her gaze. Lieutenant: Executioner.
