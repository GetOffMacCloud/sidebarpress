# The Madman

> Escaped slave, a fighter tortured to madness.

## Appearance
Soot-covered, in a bizarre mixture of rags, hair and beard tangled; clambers through the rafters.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Believes the slavers are still after him; has forgotten that he meant to escape. Loves "the pretty things" (a pouch of topazes).

## Goals & Secrets
Has turned the burnt wing into a "haunted" maze of tricks to keep the hobgoblins away.

## Continuity
Calmed and rescued, he is a living witness to the slavers' cruelty.
