# Markessa's Double

> Elf fighter/wizard altered to look exactly like Markessa.

## Appearance
Identical to Markessa: ivory skin, golden hair, amber eyes, studded leather, shortsword and darts.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Brainwashed to obey, but a spark of rebellion still flickers (on a 9–10 on a d10 she begs for help).

## Goals & Secrets
Assists Markessa with the slave merchants. Beloved by the elf bodyguard (D31), who alone can tell the two apart. Markessa watches her through the painting.
