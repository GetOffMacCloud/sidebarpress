# Estelrath Tancred

> Slave merchant waiting for his selection of slaves.

## Appearance
A merchant in a traveling cloak with chain mail and a longsword beneath; two cavelings, Cari and Filch, guard him.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Bored by the delay; happy to talk with "mercenaries" until they prove by ignorance to be otherwise, then stalls until his guards return.

## Goals & Secrets
His chest holds papers detailing the slave route: the lead to A3. His orcs (F29) despise the hobgoblins.
