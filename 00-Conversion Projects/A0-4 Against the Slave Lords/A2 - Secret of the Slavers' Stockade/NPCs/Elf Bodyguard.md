# Elf Bodyguard

> Elf warrior remade as Markessa's bodyguard and lover.

## Appearance
A tall, slender, muscular elf in padded armor and shield, drilling at the quintain; old operation scars on the back of his neck.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Lightning-fast and silent; devoted to the double rather than to Markessa.

## Goals & Secrets
Once ugly and weak, he was rebuilt to love Markessa, but the brainwashing fixed on her double instead.
