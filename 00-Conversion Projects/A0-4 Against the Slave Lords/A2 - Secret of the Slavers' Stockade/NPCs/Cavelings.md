# Cavelings

> Markessa's failed experiments, living in the caves north of the slave pens.

## Appearance
Each twisted differently. **Mouth**: legless, with 4-foot arms and double thumbs; **the Inner Markessa**: three-fingered, fanged, in a breastplate of stone plates; **Jabber**: frog-mouthed, one arm a bony spear; **Whisper**: huge-headed, tentacled, telepathic.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Mad by any outside measure, but organized: Leaders, Warriors, Workers, Hunters and Thinkers. Mouth laments and spits; the Inner Markessa hates intruders; Whisper speaks for the Leaders.

## Goals & Secrets
They worship a fanged idol of Markessa and await the "Messengers of Light" who will destroy the Outer Markessa and lead them to the "Bright Place". A party that proves itself the Messengers can lead a caveling revolt.
