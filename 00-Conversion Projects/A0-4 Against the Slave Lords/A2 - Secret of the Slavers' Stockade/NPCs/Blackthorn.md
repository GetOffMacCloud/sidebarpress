# Blackthorn

> Oni, the Slave Lords' representative, captain of the dungeon guard and treasurer.

## Appearance
Always polymorphed: a 7-foot, hunched, skeletal grey man with sunken eyes and a hollow voice, his clothes and armor hanging loose.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Cordial and ghastly; rubs his long hands together counting coins. Enjoys the effect of his appearance on Markessa's troops.

## Goals & Secrets
Sent by the Slave Lords to watch Markessa. Appalled by her experiments and plotting to remove her; he may offer to lead the party to her, then fetch help to capture them after the deed. Only Markessa knows he is an oni. Her listening plant hangs in his room.
