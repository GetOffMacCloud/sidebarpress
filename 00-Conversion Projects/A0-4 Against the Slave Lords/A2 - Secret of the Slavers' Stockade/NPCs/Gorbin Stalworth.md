# Gorbin Stalworth

> Human veteran, captain of the gatehouse guard.

## Appearance
A man in banded mail drinking at a table with his half-orc lieutenant, a crossbow resting on the stool beside him.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Talks to buy time until reinforcements arrive, then shoots the least armored intruder from under the table.

## Goals & Secrets
Interrogates captured intruders. Keeps the keys to the gatehouse and curtain wall on a peg.
