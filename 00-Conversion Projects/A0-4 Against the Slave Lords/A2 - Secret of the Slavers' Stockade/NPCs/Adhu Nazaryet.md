# Adhu Nazaryet

> Hobgoblin shaman of Maglubiyet.

## Appearance
A hobgoblin priest in chain mail, carrying holy icons, escorted by 2d4 warriors.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Pious and fierce; he will fight to the death to keep the icons from being defiled.

## Goals & Secrets
Bound for a secret shrine of Maglubiyet to perform a sacrifice.
