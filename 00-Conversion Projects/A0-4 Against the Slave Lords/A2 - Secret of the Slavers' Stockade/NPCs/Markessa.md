# Markessa

> Elf fighter/wizard, director of the stockade and maker of monsters.

## Appearance
Ivory-skinned, golden-haired and amber-eyed, beautiful and vain; studded leather, a slender shortsword and a bandolier of darts. Her double (D30a) looks exactly like her.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Cold, curious and utterly without pity: slaves are raw material for her experiments in surgery and magic. She is proud of her "improvements" and enjoys showing them off.

## Goals & Secrets
Runs this part of the Slave Lords' operation and refines her craft on the slaves: the cavelings, the double, the bodyguard, the listening plant are all her work. She doesn't trust Blackthorn and spies on him. She always has a way out: the D22 ambush, the fireplace tunnel in D35, her double to take the blame.
