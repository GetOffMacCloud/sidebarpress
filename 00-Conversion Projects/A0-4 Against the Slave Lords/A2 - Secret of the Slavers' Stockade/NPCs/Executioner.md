# Executioner

> Ogre, Icar's battle-won friend and second in command.

## Appearance
An ogre in fine black chain mail and a black executioner's hood, lovingly polishing a hooked bastard sword.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Proud to be smarter than other ogres; welcomes "new mercenaries", offers wine and cheese, and invites them to sit.

## Goals & Secrets
His sword was a gift from a respectful slave merchant. The great bow over his bed takes Strength 18 to draw.
