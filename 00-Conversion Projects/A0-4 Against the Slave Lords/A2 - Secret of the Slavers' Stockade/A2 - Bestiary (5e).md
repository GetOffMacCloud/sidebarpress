> [!info] How to read this bestiary
> - **MM creatures** are listed with the module's riders. The FGU module carries their SRD 5.1 statblocks.
> - **Custom blocks** are given in full in Fantasy Grounds import format. Each opens with its 1e source line.
> - Counts in the site keys are for 6 PCs, with the 4-PC count after them.

## MM creatures

### Hobgoblin
**Hobgoblin** (MM, CR 1/2). Fort guards carry longswords and longbows (the 1e composite bows); the F8a pair carry halberds (*Melee:* +3, reach 10 ft, 1d10 + 1 slashing). Asleep or off duty they are **AC 12** (no armor) until they arm (10 minutes, or the round they grab a shield: AC 14).

### Goblin
**Goblin** (MM, CR 1/4). The D1 guards use light crossbows and clubs; the D12 torturers slings and shortswords; the Snarlers (D26a) are asleep and unarmored (AC 12).

### Kobold
**Kobold** (MM, CR 1/8). The fort's servants (F33).

### Orc
**Orc** (MM, CR 1/2). Estelrath's escort (F29), lax and drunk; AC 13 until they grab their shields.

### Thug
**Thug** (MM, CR 1/2). The half-orc smith (F26) and orc officers (F30); Kairn uses his own block. The dungeon cook (D24) and the engineer Carlstar Wiorfether (D25a, with a light crossbow and a military pick) are Thugs too.

### Commoner
**Commoner** (MM, CR 0). Slaves, servants, Fyndax the alchemist, Kyvin Trist the scribe, the halfling in F33, "Lady Morwin Elissar". The monk in D28t has Unarmored Defense (AC 13) and an unarmed strike (+3, 1d4 + 2).

### Veteran
**Veteran** (MM, CR 3). Estelrath Tancred, the slave merchant (F31), in chain mail with a longsword.

### Ankheg
**Ankheg** (MM, CR 2). F5: it shows only its front half and its belly (AC 12); it doesn't hold its victims (no acid damage from the bite) but flings them into the mud. Its Acid Spray comes at half hit points.

### Wereboar
**Wereboar** (MM, CR 4). The three "barbaric men" of F35, who throw hand axes (+5, 20/60 ft, 1d6 + 3) before they change.

### Boar
**Boar** (MM, CR 1/4). The wereboars' pet (F37).

### Medusa
**Medusa** (MM, CR 6). Chained in Icar's closet (F39): she can't leave the closet. She fears Icar, who is immune to her gaze.

### Madman
**Berserker** (MM, CR 2), named **Madman**. He fights with a dagger (+5, 1d4 + 3) or a light crossbow from his rafter fort (three-quarters cover), and drops a noose on chimney-climbers (see F16).

### Winter Wolf
**Winter Wolf** (MM, CR 3). Gulyet's bodyguard; it comes 2 rounds after she blows the silent end of her whistle.

### Werewolf
**Werewolf** (MM, CR 3). The three in D25e; Blackthorn's agents.

### Owlbear
**Owlbear** (MM, CR 3). Caged either side of the D22 doors.

### Minotaur
**Minotaur** (MM, CR 3). Markessa's guard at D33; he carries a morningstar (+6, 2d8 + 4) as well as his horns.

### Worg
**Worg** (MM, CR 1/2). The torture-chamber dens (D12c), the goblin lancers' mounts (D18c), the watch-worgs at D21 (chained; they accept anyone carrying the D18c scent pouches).

### Wolf
**Wolf** (MM, CR 1/4). The female worgs of D19 (the cubs don't fight), the dungeon wanderers, the rabid wolf of D12b (its bite carries rabies: see D12b), and the wolf cub of F9c (5 hp, harmless).

### Giant Spider
**Giant Spider** (MM, CR 1). The D4 pair, hidden in the cobwebs over the arches.

### Bugbear
**Bugbear** (MM, CR 1). Squad leaders (D12a, D25c), with halberds or longswords.

## Hazards

### Phantom
*1e: a soulless image left by a traumatic death; save vs. spells at −2 or flee in fear; can't be harmed or turned; dispelled by exorcism.* A **hazard**, not a creature: anyone who sees the D6 phantom makes a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** or is **frightened** of it for 1 minute and must flee (repeating the save at the end of each turn). It can't attack, be attacked or be turned; *dispel evil and good* ends it. It replays the elf slave's escape every hour.

## Lore

**Boggles** are 3-foot, vaguely humanoid creatures, blackish-blue to dark gray, with large bulbous heads and mismatched limbs (big noses, arms of different lengths, spindly legs). They live in loose tribes, whine and gibber at other creatures, are cowardly and thieving away from home and voracious in their lairs. They secrete a slippery black oil, stretch to twice their length, climb walls and ceilings, smell invisible things, and step through any hole or frame to come out of another nearby; their lairs are honeycombs of holes and grillwork. Other races keep them as watchbeasts for their nose and their wail.

**Cloakers** are shadow-dwelling creatures of the deep caverns: a large semicircular cloak with two claws at its tips, a mace-like tail, button-like eyespots across its back, and a gaping mouth and two glowing red eyes on its underside. They are highly intelligent but alien; usually only wizards can talk with them. How the slavers got the services of this one is unknown.

**Haunts** are the restless spirits of people who died with a vital task unfinished. A haunt appears as a hovering ball of light (like a will-o'-wisp) or a translucent image of its former body, and seeks a living body to finish its task. Jon, the haunt of F2f, was the sergeant of the guard who died defending the inner walkway; to rest, he must drive off every intruder.

**Phantoms** are images left by a traumatic death: a three-dimensional replay of the death trial or of the dying creature's last thoughts. They are neither alive nor undead and can't be turned.

**Cavelings** are Markessa's failed experiments: slaves surgically "improved" into twisted shapes and driven mad by the pain. She lets them live in the caves north of D28c, where they worship her as a fanged idol and have built a society of tribes (Leaders, Warriors, Workers, Hunters and Thinkers) in which madness is normal.
