# A2 — 5e Conversion Ledger

Every number the 5e table or the FGU build has to touch: the 1e source, the 5e conversion, and the reason. **Review it before vault notes, statblocks or FG records are generated.** Page numbers are PDF pages in `_source/A0-A4 source.pdf` (printed page = PDF page − 1). Maps: the hill fort and its upper floors p. 95, the dungeon level p. 96 (both line art; no tournament maps in A2).

> [!info] Scope of this pass
> - **The 1981 module, run as chapter three of the A-series chain** (A0 1–3 · A1 4–5 · **A2 5–6** · A3 6–7 · A4 7–8). The hill fort in the Drachensgrab Hills, 120 miles inland from Highport, as printed.
> - **Rules:** 5e 2014 (PHB, DMG, MM) with the A0 and A1 ledger rules ([[A0 - 5e Conversion Ledger]] §1, [[A1 - 5e Conversion Ledger]] §1). MM statblocks where one exists; custom blocks marked **(custom)**. Non-SRD MM creatures (cloaker, medusa is SRD) get full blocks in our own words.
> - **Carried over from A1:** 6 PCs = as printed, 4 PCs = ⅔ of every group of two or more; magic items capped at rare, consumables kept; the nine pregens at 5th level for Convention Mode (rebuilt by point buy, A1 §8); rolled creature counts kept as FG random encounters and tables (A1, "Random numbers of creatures").
> - **Text:** stays close to Johnson and Moldvay. This ledger changes numbers, not prose.
> - **Open questions are ⚑ D1–D12 in §11.** Nothing below them is built until they're answered.

## 0 · Recommended 5e level (⚑ D1)

**Proposal: start at 5th, reach 6th on going below, finish A2 at 6th.** A3 opens at 6th.

| Section | Areas | 5e level | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The hill fort** (surface) | F1–F39, the walls, the parade ground, the keep | 5 | Hobgoblin garrison under Icar and Executioner. The kitchen ambush (F35: Icar with three wereboars) is the set piece |
| **The dungeon** | D1–D39, the caveling caves, the exit tunnel | 6 | Markessa's laboratory (D22) is the set piece; Blackthorn the oni, the werewolves, the minotaur and the drow caravan are side fights |

**Milestone:** 6th level when the party goes below: by the stairs behind F38b, the trapdoor under Icar's bed (F39), or the hobgoblin guardroom stair. **Running the dungeon alone** (the alternate tournament start, the fort already destroyed): start at 6th.

**Party size.** Numbers as printed are the 6-PC version; for 4 PCs, groups of two or more are cut to ⅔ (rounded). Single creatures and named NPCs never change.

### 3.3 The big fights (6 PCs, adjusted XP)

| Fight | Creatures | Adjusted XP | 5th level | 6th level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F8b gatehouse barracks (unarmored) | 22 hobgoblins, 2 sergeants | 6,500 | Hard | Hard |
| F13 courtyard ambush | 12 hobgoblins, 4 carnivorous apes | 7,500 | Deadly | Hard |
| F34 tower garrison | 20 hobgoblins, 2 sergeants | 6,000 | Hard | Hard |
| **F35 kitchen ambush** | **Icar, 3 wereboars, 8 hobgoblins** | **11,800** | **Deadly ×1.8** | Deadly |
| D18c elite goblin cavalry | 9 lancers, captain, 9 worgs | 5,000 | Hard | Medium |
| **D22 Markessa's laboratory** | **Markessa, 2 owlbears, 13 elite goblins** | **12,500** | Deadly ×1.9 | **Deadly** |
| D38 drow caravan | merchant, captain, 6 guards | 3,525 | Medium | Easy |
| Most guard posts (F1d, F9, F11, F19, D1, D26…) | 3–13 hobgoblins or goblins | 400–2,900 | Easy | Easy |

**Read the table with M1 in mind.** Room by room the hobgoblins are Easy at 5th level, but A2 is a fort under alarm: once the gong sounds, the posts converge, and two or three Easy rooms arriving together are a Deadly fight. That is how the module plays, and it should stay that way.

**The two Deadly rooms (⚑ D4):** proposal: keep them as printed and **stage them in waves**, as A1 did. F35: the wereboars charge in round 1, Icar rolls the cask in round 2, the hobgoblins throw oil from behind cover. D22: the owlbears come out in round 2 as printed, the balcony archers swing down in round 4.

## 9 · Convention Mode (tournament extras)

- **Two sessions, 3½–4 hours each:** the hill fort (start: the slave's rope up the curtain wall, F1) and the dungeon (alternate start: the trapdoor in the rubble, D1). Same pregens; the tournament areas are marked on the maps.
- **No wandering monsters**; monsters fight to the death; they don't pursue out of their area (but fake pursuit).
- **Scoring:** 5 points per surviving character + 45 per area completed, plus the DM's ±3 per area; the fixed-damage numbers ("for tournament use") on this page only. As A1 (⚑ D11).
