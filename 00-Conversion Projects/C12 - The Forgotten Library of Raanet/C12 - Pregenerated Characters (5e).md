# C12 Pregenerated Characters (5e, level 6)

The module's eight convention pregens, converted to **5e level 6**, the level the conversion ledger recommends for a six-player table. Six play at a time. The 1e sheets have more than six, so the GM picks.

## Conversion rules for the pregens

- **Ability scores carry straight across**; 1e and 5e use the same 3–18 scale. 1e exceptional Strength (18/27) becomes 18. Each character then takes the **5e ability score improvements** a level-6 character would have (noted on each sheet).
- **Class and level:** a 1e level-5 single-class character becomes **5e level 6** in the matching class. The **1e 4/4 multiclasses** become 5e level 6 too: **Elinthri as a Fighter 6 (Eldritch Knight)**, the 5e class built for 1e's fighter/magic-user, and **Merah as a Fighter 2 / Rogue 4**, a real multiclass because 5e has no single class that covers both.
- **Hit points:** 5e rules (maximum at level 1, average after that). Most are higher than the 1e sheets; that is 5e, not padding.
- **Gear:** 1e items become the 5e item, the same way as the ledger. **1e "+1 protection" items stay +1** (the 5e cap), and items with no 5e version are custom (see *Custom items*, below).
- **Spells:** the 1e memorised spells become the 5e versions, and each list is filled out to 5e's larger prepared count.
- **Languages** carry over from the 1e sheets as written.
- **Backgrounds** are picked from each sheet's *Special Notes*.

> [!tip] What the dungeon rewards
> - **Elinthri is half Xuel** (his father traces back to the Great Migration). That passes the **Area 24 door runes and the Area 25 stone guardian**, the only heritage check any pregen meets.
> - **Adria, Kiralee and Aliza read Ancient Xuloise** (Adria and Aliza fluently; Creek through his helm). That opens every history book, the diary and the scripture, and the scripture **adds +2 to calming Avraasha**.
> - **Kiralee's Charisma 18** with a persuasion bonus is the party's best voice for the Avraasha scene.
> - **Only Kiralee's and Elinthri's +1 swords** get past nothing that needs a +2. The **swordwraiths (Area 32)** halve them. The **Area 8 sword of sharpness** and **Area 13 flail +2** matter.
