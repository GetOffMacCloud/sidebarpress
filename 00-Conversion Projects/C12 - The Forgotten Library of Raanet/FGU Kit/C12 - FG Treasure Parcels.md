# C12 - FG Treasure Parcels

**24 parcels**, one per place the party finds treasure. Build each in FG under **Parcels**, then drag in the item records. Every name below is the exact record name from `C12 - FG Item Import.txt` (**C**) or FG's own item library (**FG**).

> [!warning] Xuel coins are items, never coins
> Enter **Xuel Gold Coin** as an item with a quantity. Don't put it in the parcel's coin field; at 50 gp each, a coin field would value them at 1 gp. **No parcel has a coin-field entry.** C12 has no ordinary coinage.

## Notes for the build

- **P24 is conditional.** Set the ember quantity to the party size; 6 assumes the convention table.
- **Charged and quiescent Sigil rods are different records.** P08, P10 and P16 hold *charged* rods; P23 holds the *quiescent* one. When the party charges it, swap the record.
- **Set uses on FG core items:** Dust of Disappearance **5**, Nolzur's Marvelous Pigments **3 pots**.
- **Weight check:** P01's barrier (200 lb) and P12's casks (250 lb each) are deliberately heavy; the module expects the party to need a plan to haul them.
