# Summoning Bestiary (5e)

Every creature on the [[C12 - Monster Summoning I-VII (5e)]] tables that has **no 5e statblock**, converted from its **1e *Monster Manual*** entry. Every other table entry uses an existing 5e statblock (MM 2014, or *Volo's Guide* / *Monsters of the Multiverse* where marked).

> [!info] How these were converted
> - **Source line first.** Each block opens with its 1e stats (MM page = printed page) so the conversion can be checked.
> - **AC** is 19 − 1e AC, then checked against a comparable MM creature. **Movement** 12" = 30 ft. **Hit points** are built to the 5e CR table, never carried from 1e Hit Dice.
> - **"Save or die" poison** becomes heavy poison damage on a failed Constitution save. The MM made the same change for the giant poisonous snake.
> - **CR** is worked out from the DMG table (defence and offence averaged). **Hydras follow 1e: no head regrowth.** That regrowth is the 1e *Lernaean* hydra's, not the ordinary one's.
> - **No custom damage types or FG-only effects.** Everything is plain 5e.
