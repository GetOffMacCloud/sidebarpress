# T1 — Bestiary (5e)

Every creature and NPC in *The Village of Hommlet*, converted from the conversion ledger ([[T1 - 5e Conversion Ledger]] §3–§5). Rules are the 2014 *Monster Manual* (MM) and *Dungeon Master's Guide* (DMG).

> [!info] How to read this bestiary
> - **MM creatures** are listed by name and page reference only, plus the module's riders. Fantasy Grounds already ships those statblocks.
> - **Variants** say "Use the MM X, with these changes." Everything not changed is the MM block.
> - **Custom blocks** are given in full. Each one opens with its 1e source line, so the conversion can be checked.
> - **CR** follows the DMG table. **XP** is the MM value for the CR.
> - Where a stat is in a key note (for example a guard's weapons), the key note governs.

## Hommlet

### Villagers and Militia
Most Hommlet folk are level 0. Gygax says to treat them as 1−1 HD.
- **Unarmored or lightly armored villagers** are **Commoners** (MM p. 345; CR 0; hp 4; +2 to hit, weapon die +0).
- **Militiamen in scale, ring or chain** are **Guards** (MM p. 347; CR 1/8; hp 11; melee +3, weapon die +1; light crossbow +3, 1d8 + 1).
- **AC** comes from the armor named in the key: plate and shield 20, chain and shield 18, splint and shield 19, scale and shield 16, ring and shield 16, studded and shield 14, leather and shield 13, padded and shield 13, leather 11, padded 11, unarmored 10. Where the 1e figure gave a different number, the key note follows the ledger (§3.1).
- **Weapons** are the ones named in the key, with their PHB damage. Polearms convert as follows: bill-guisarme, guisarme, voulge, fauchard and fauchard-fork become a **glaive** (1d10, heavy, reach); a military fork or spetum becomes a **spear**.
