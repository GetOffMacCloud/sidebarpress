# Wilderness Encounters: Hommlet, the Moathouse and Nulb

> The outdoor encounter tables for the country between Hommlet and Nulb. The 1985 text prints them at the start of Part 2 (Nulb), for parties of 5th level and up, and Gygax points DMs to "the random encounter charts for the Hommlet and Nulb areas" from T1. In this edition they serve T1 as well, because Map 1 puts the Moathouse some 15 miles from Hommlet (see [[T1-00 Introduction#Development Notes]]). **Map 1.**

## When to Check

The 1985 rule, unchanged:
- **On the road:** check once on each trip, about halfway. **At night, check twice.**
- **Off the road:** check six times a day: morning, noon, afternoon, nightfall, midnight and before dawn.
- **On the water:** check only once a trip by vessel. If the characters go under the water for some reason, check each hour. If they watch the river from cover, check each hour, but only if they're exposed to attack. Crayfish and gar aren't seen in most cases; simply leave the encounter unmentioned.

Roll a d12 on the table for the terrain the characters are crossing. You may modify these tables as desired.

**Which column to use.** The **As printed** column is Gygax's numbers, converted. They were written for parties of 5th level and up, so use them for the Interlude and for T2-4. For a T1 party of **1st to 4th level**, use the **T1 party** column. It applies the module's own rule for the Moathouse: take creatures away, never add them.

| Route | Terrain |
|---|---|
| Hommlet to the Moathouse | Forest or scrub for the first dozen miles; swamp for the last few miles and around the ruins |
| Moathouse to Nulb, along the track | Swamp, then forest or scrub |
| Hommlet to Nulb (High Road and Low Road) | Forest or scrub |
| The Velverdyva and the Imeryds Run | River |

## Statistics

All of these are in [[T1 - Bestiary (5e)#Wilderness]] or the MM.

| Creature | 5e statblock | CR |

| Wolf | **Wolf** (MM); its howl may make herbivores panic (50%) | 1/4 |

**Disguised Evil pilgrims** are Temple servants travelling as pilgrims. Use **cultists** (MM p. 345) under an **acolyte**, or **bandits**. They report what they see to Nulb.

**Ships and caravans.** The 1985 text gives no crew sizes. A merchant caravan is 2–4 wagons with 1d4 + 1 merchants and twice as many guards. A pirate ship carries a **bandit captain** and 3d6 **bandits**. A warship carries a **veteran** and 2d6 **guards**.
