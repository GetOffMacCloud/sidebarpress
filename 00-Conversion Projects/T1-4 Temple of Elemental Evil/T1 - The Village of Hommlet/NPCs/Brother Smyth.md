# Brother Smyth

> Hommlet's smith and the druid's assistant, religious advisor to the Old Faith.

## Appearance
Short but brawny, vigorously pounding red-hot iron at the forge.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Hard-working, forthright and likeable. He readily admits he can't do fine armoring.

## Goals & Secrets
Serve the faithful. He gives most of his wealth to the needy, and in no event will he leave his fireside to go adventuring.
