# Zert

> A fighter at the Inn, "awaiting a caravan from the south": in fact a Temple spy who will join a party and betray it.

## Appearance
A fighter in scale mail with a shield. His warhorse and lance are in the stable.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Friendly and eager to join adventurers for an equal share. He can drink great quantities without becoming drunk.

## Goals & Secrets
Join a party, then betray it, help to slay it and take its goods.
