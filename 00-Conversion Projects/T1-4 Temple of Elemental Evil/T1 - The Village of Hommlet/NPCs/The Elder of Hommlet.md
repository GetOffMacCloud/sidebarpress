# The Elder of Hommlet

> Hommlet's wise and greatly respected village elder, head of the council and Justice of the Peace.

## Appearance
A retired farmer; his four grown sons work the fields and are proud of their horses.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Quite wise. Holds a village meeting each new moon to hear ideas and complaints.

## Goals & Secrets
Keep Hommlet safe and prosperous. His walled manor is the village's refuge in an attack.

## Stat Block
Commoner (MM). His sons and hired hand are listed in the area 27 key.
