# Jaroo Ashstaff

> The druid of the Grove and spiritual caretaker of the Old Faith, sent by the Gnarley druids to make sure the Temple stays dead.

## Appearance
A druid in padded armor and a *cloak of protection*, carrying a *staff of the python*. A huge black bear is always nearby and out of sight.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Expects an offering from all comers, and several gold pieces from those not of the Old Faith. Listens to those who contribute.

## Goals & Secrets
Repress any rise of Temple-style evil. He gives aid by spells but won't accompany a party; he follows suspicious visitors at a distance.
