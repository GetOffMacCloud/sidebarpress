# Kobort

> A hulking, dim fighter who fell in with the monk Turuko, and does what Turuko says.

## Appearance
Huge; splint mail and shield, longsword and dagger. His lance, flail, battleaxe and warhorse are in the stable.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Strong and not clever (Int 6).

## Goals & Secrets
Turuko's plan: join a small party, hang back, then kill the weakened survivors and rob them.
