# Mytch the Miller

> Hommlet's miller, a pillar of the Old Faith community and council member, cool toward strangers.

## Appearance
Runs the mill with his family; his wife bakes bread for the village. A large dog guards the millhouse.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Suspicious of strangers, with good cause.

## Goals & Secrets
Prosperity and safety for the village.
