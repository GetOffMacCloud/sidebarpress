# Ostler Gundigoot

> Innkeeper of the Welcome Wench and sergeant of the militia, with a sharp eye for character.

## Appearance
Always bustling about the inn, apron pockets heavy with about 100 gp in coins.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Talks freely but says little. Serves all comers, and rents a room to anyone not causing trouble. A very kind master to his potboys and scullions.

## Goals & Secrets
Run the best inn for many miles. If anyone of highly suspicious nature appears, he gets the druid of the Grove to spy on the room through sliding ceiling panels; his first potboy carries messages to Jaroo.
