# Spugnoir

> A poor evoker lodging at the Inn, hungry for spells and planning a quiet search of the moathouse.

## Appearance
Nondescript garb, so as not to be recognized as a wizard. Three zircons are sewn in the hem of his cloak.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Claims to work for a sage.

## Goals & Secrets
Gather spells. He has heard a warlock lived in the moathouse. He will join a party if offered all the scrolls found; otherwise he shadows it.
