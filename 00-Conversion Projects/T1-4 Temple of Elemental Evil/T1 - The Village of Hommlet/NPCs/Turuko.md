# Turuko

> A small, thin Baklunish monk who believes himself highly clever, and plans to rob adventurers returning from the moathouse.

## Appearance
Small and thin, from unknown parts; two daggers and a quarterstaff always at hand.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Convinced of his own cleverness. "Something must be done soon!"

## Goals & Secrets
Accompany a small party, hang back during fights, then slay the weakened characters; or spy on a large group and attack only once it is reduced by deaths and wounds.
