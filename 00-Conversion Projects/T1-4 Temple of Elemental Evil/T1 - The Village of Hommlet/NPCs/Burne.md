# Burne

> "His Most Worshipful Mage of Hommlet": a clever, slightly greedy 8th-level wizard who holds the village for the Viscount with his partner Rufus.

## Appearance
On the young side for a wizard, average in appearance and dress. Often at the Inn of the Welcome Wench.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Clever, cautious and a trifle greedy. Every service is paid for handsomely; any adventure he joins earns him a third of the treasure. Not likely to risk his life or be duped.

## Goals & Secrets
Protect the village and watch for evil, as a follower of St. Cuthbert and the Viscount's man.
