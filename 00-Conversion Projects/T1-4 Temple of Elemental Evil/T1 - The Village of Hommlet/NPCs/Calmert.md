# Calmert

> The honest, zealous lesser priest of St. Cuthbert, who handles everyone who comes to the church.

## Appearance
Robed for services, with thurible and billet; in plate and shield when trouble comes.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Known for his zeal in obtaining contributions. He leaves petitioners on hard benches with the poorbox while he steps out.

## Goals & Secrets
Most of the money he collects "for the church" goes to building Burne and Rufus's castle.
