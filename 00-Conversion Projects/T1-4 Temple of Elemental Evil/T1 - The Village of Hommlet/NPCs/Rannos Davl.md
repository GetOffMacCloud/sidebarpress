# Rannos Davl

> Senior partner of Hommlet's trading post: slow, fat, clumsy and placid on the surface, a 10th-level thief and Temple agent underneath.

## Appearance
Fat and slow-moving. He wears *studded leather +1* and a *ring of protection*, with an *amulet of proof against detection and location* under his jerkin.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Placid in the shop; chaotic, evil and highly suspicious in fact. Claims disinterest in all alignments, and will gladly buy anything the characters find.

## Goals & Secrets
Spy on Hommlet for the Temple, through a brigand courier; supply Lareth with wagons; aid any evil creature in Temple service. He wants his agent with every party, and will follow a successful party himself, bringing out the stock of magic hidden in the shop.
