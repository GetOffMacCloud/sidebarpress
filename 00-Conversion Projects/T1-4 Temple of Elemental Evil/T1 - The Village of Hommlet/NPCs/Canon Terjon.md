# Canon Terjon

> The stern new chief cleric of St. Cuthbert's church in Hommlet.

## Appearance
Stern of demeanor; the villagers find him not particularly friendly.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Distant. He sees petitioners only after a donation to the needy, and is unavailable half the time.

## Goals & Secrets
Holds the church since the Canoness Y'dey left unexpectedly. He doesn't know of her cache in his own mantle.
