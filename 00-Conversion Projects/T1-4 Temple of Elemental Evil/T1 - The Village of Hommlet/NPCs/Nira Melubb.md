# Nira Melubb

> Hommlet's moneychanger and jeweler, obliging and polite, who asks no questions.

## Appearance
Proprietor of the sturdy new building with three yellow balls on its sign, guarded by a man in chain mail and two war dogs.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Always obliging and polite. Asks no questions.

## Goals & Secrets
Business. The rise of evil would hurt it, but he has no other interest in events.
