# Lubash

> An ogre lured into Lareth's service by baubles and the promise of fresh human meat.

## Appearance
An ogre with a bardiche, bedded on old clothing and skins.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Hungry. He devours the slain at the first opportunity and tosses the wounded into his pantry (25).

## Goals & Secrets
Meat and trinkets. He attacks anyone who doesn't wear the yellow eye of fire *and* make the crossed-arms sign.
