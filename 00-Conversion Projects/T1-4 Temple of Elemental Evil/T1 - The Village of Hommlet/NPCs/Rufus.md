# Rufus

> Burne's partner, a careful 6th-level fighter, commander of Burne's Badgers and of all the village troops.

## Appearance
In *chain mail +1* and *shield +1*, with a *battleaxe +1*.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Will not risk his life or join foolish adventures. Any service to a party costs at least 20% of the treasure gained.

## Goals & Secrets
Hold the area for the Viscount; one day return to Verbobonc for special service.
