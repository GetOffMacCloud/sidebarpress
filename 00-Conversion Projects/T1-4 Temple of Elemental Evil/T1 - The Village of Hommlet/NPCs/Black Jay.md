# Black Jay

> A grizzled herdsman and retired warrior who wants no company.

## Appearance
Grizzled; three dogs guard his cottage. He keeps *boots* and a *cloak of elvenkind* and ten *arrows +1*.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Doesn't like company or strangers, and says so.

## Goals & Secrets
Left alone with his flock. His wife and children were slain in a raid while he was away at the wars. Friendly with the elves to the northwest; will not adventure.

Thug variant; see [[T1-01 Village of Hommlet#19. MODEST COTTAGE]].
