# Furnok of Ferd

> A "treasure finder" and card sharp at the Inn, who will join a party for an equal share and steal what he can.

## Appearance
Leather armor; a *dagger +1* hidden in his boot; a pair of loaded knucklebones.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Loves to gamble, and cheats carefully enough not to be caught, mostly skinning passing merchants.

## Goals & Secrets
An equal share, plus everything he can surreptitiously lift, particularly magic items. He puts up his *scroll of protection from magic* as a show of good faith.
