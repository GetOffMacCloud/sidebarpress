# Captain of the Militia (Hommlet)

> Elmo's elderly father: a retired fighter who is secretly captain of Hommlet's militia.

## Appearance
An elderly farmer on a clean but slightly run-down farm. His scale mail, shield, longsword and light crossbow are kept in a chest in the front room.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Says nothing of his rank. Shakes his head sadly over his son Otis, who "took service with some gentlemen."

## Goals & Secrets
Keep Hommlet safe. He sits on the council, third in seniority after Jaroo and Terjon.
