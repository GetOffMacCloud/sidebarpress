# Elmo

> Hommlet's town drunk, who is really a 4th-level ranger and the Viscount's agent, waiting to join the next party of newcomers.

## Appearance
Big, strong and slow-spoken, usually with a jack of ale in his fist at the Inn of the Welcome Wench. In town he wears no armor, only the *dagger +2* his "brudder Otis" gave him.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Slow and halting speech, tipsy and jovial; he talks about money "for ale" and asks any employer for "chain armor and a BIG axe." All of it is an act.

## Goals & Secrets
Watch newcomers and insinuate himself into their expeditions, for the Viscount of Verbobonc. Once hired, he wears his own *chain mail +1* and *shield +1* (or wears the mail under a lighter type) and uses his own *battleaxe +1*. His gear is buried in a lead-lined chest in his parents' barn.

## Canon Notes
Elmo is Gygax's own: T1 *The Village of Hommlet* (1979), revised in T1-4 (1985), area 2.

## Continuity
If anything untoward befalls Elmo, his brother **Otis** (Nulb area 2, T2-4) hears of it and seeks redress. He reports everything he sees to the Viscount.
