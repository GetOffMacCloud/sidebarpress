# Gremag

> Rannos Davl's fussy, dithering partner at the trading post, and a 7th-level assassin of the Temple.

## Appearance
Tall and thin, with sharp features and protruding eyes. A *chain shirt +1* under his clothes.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Dithers and fusses at customers, and at Rannos as well. Even more chaotic than his partner.

## Goals & Secrets
As Rannos: spy, recruit, supply, and remove anyone who threatens the Temple's work.
