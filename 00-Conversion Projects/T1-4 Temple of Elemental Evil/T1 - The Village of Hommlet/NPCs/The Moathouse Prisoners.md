# The Moathouse Prisoners

> Two merchants of Dyvers and a badly beaten gnome spy, held in Lubash's pantry.

## Appearance
The merchants are mildly tortured; the gnome is badly beaten.

## Personality & Mannerisms
All three beg to be freed. The merchants promise large rewards; the gnome offers a plain iron ring to whoever first agrees.

## Goals & Secrets
Go home. The merchants send 100 sp each to Hommlet four weeks after their release. The gnome was caught spying by the gnolls.

## Stat Block
Commoners (MM); the gnome has *Gnome Cunning*.

**Items:** [[Gnome's Iron Ring (T1)]].
