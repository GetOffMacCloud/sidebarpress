# Lareth the Beautiful

> The "New Master" of the moathouse: a young, handsome, brilliant cleric sent to rebuild the Temple's forces, and the dark hope of chaotic evil.

## Appearance
Young and handsome, in *plate armor +1*, a mace in his left hand and a *staff of striking* in his right; a *phylactery of action* bound on his left arm.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Thoroughly wicked, depraved and capricious, and cunning. When in doubt he uses bribery and honeyed words. He gladly takes new recruits, adventurers included, but tests them continually; the suspicious and the too-promising are murdered in their sleep.

## Goals & Secrets
Rebuild a force of men and humanoids, gather loot and restore the Temple of Elemental Evil to its former glory. He serves both the Temple and Lolth.

## Continuity
Raids never nearer than three or four leagues, carried in the traders' wagons; no victim is left alive. If he is slain and his possessions turn up, an assassin comes for the slayers in 3d6 + 2 days. If he is ransomed, he lives to return. "Whoever harms Lareth had best not brag of it in the presence of one who will inform the Demoness Lolth!"
