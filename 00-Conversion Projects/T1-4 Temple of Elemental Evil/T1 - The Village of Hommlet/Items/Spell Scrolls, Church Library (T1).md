# Spell Scrolls, Church Library

> Seven cleric scrolls hidden in a dull law book.

## Properties
*Spell scrolls.* Seven cleric scrolls, one each of 1st through 7th level. The 1985 text leaves the spells to the DM; this edition's defaults are *bless* (1st, common), *lesser restoration* (2nd, uncommon), *remove curse* (3rd, uncommon), *death ward* (4th, rare), *raise dead* (5th, rare), *heal* (6th, very rare) and *regenerate* (7th, very rare). Change them freely.

## History
Hidden in *Legal Affairs in Veluna, 213 to 312 CY*, with the amulet of life protection.

## Found In
- [[T1-04 Church of St Cuthbert#C 11. Church Library]]

## Continuity
They belong to the church. Taking them is theft from St. Cuthbert.
