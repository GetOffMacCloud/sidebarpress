# Ring of Animal Influence

> Terjon's second ring.

## Properties
*Ring, rare.* 3 charges, regaining 1d3 daily at dawn. While wearing it, you can expend 1 charge to cast *animal friendship* (DC 13), *fear* (DC 13, beasts with Int 3 or lower only) or *speak with animals*.
