# Decanter of Endless Water

> A stoppered flask that never runs dry.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon.* Speak a command word to make it pour 1 gallon ("stream"), 5 gallons ("fountain") or a 30-foot geyser of 30 gallons ("geyser"), which can knock a creature prone (DC 13 Strength save).

## History
Jaroo's.

## Found In
- [[T1-01 Village of Hommlet#24. THE GROVE]]: [[Jaroo Ashstaff]]

## Continuity
NPC-only.
