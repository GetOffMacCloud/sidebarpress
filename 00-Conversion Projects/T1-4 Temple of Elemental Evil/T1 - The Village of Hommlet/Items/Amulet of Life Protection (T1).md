# Amulet of Life Protection

> A plain amulet hidden in a law book.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, rare (custom; requires attunement).* While wearing it, you have advantage on saving throws against being possessed and against *magic jar*, and your soul can't be trapped, stolen or held by any effect (such as *magic jar*, *soul cage* or a devourer's ability). When you die, your soul goes to its rest unhindered.

## History
1e amulet of life protection. *Custom 5e item, modelled on the DMG periapt of proof against poison.*
