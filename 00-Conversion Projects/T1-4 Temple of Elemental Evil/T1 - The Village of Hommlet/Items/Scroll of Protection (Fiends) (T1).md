# Scroll of Protection (Fiends)

> A scroll hidden in the chief priest's mantle.

## Properties
*Scroll, rare.* As an action, read it to raise an invisible barrier in a 5-foot radius around you for 5 minutes. Fiends can't enter it or affect anything inside it, but can cast spells or make ranged attacks into it. If you move, the barrier moves with you.

## History
1e scroll of protection from demons. Y'dey's.

## Found In
- [[T1-04 Church of St Cuthbert#C 14. Chief Priest's Chamber]]: Y'dey's cache

## Continuity
Valuable in the Temple, where demons are served.
