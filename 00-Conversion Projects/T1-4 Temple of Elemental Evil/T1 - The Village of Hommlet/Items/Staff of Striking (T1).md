# Staff of Striking

> Lareth's staff, held in his right hand.

## Properties
*Staff, very rare (requires attunement).* A *+3 quarterstaff* with 10 charges. When you hit with a melee attack using it, you can expend up to 3 charges: for each, the target takes an extra 1d6 force damage. It regains 1d6 + 4 charges daily at dawn; if you expend the last charge, roll a d20: on a 1, it becomes a nonmagical *+3 quarterstaff*.

## History
1e staff of striking, 20 charges. The 5e staff holds 10 (ledger D2).

## Found In

## Continuity
The prize of T1. Selling it reveals Lareth's slayers.
