# Shield +1

> A plain shield with a faint sheen; blows slide off it.

## Properties
*Armor (shield), uncommon.* +3 to AC (2 + 1).

## History
1e shields +1 and +2. The +2 shields of Elmo and the Badger captain are capped at +1 in 5e (ledger §7.2).

- [[T1-05 Guard Tower]]: Rufus, the Badgers' captain (1e +2) and lieutenant

## Continuity
The one in the giant lizard is the only one the characters are meant to find.
