# Scroll of Protection (Magic)

> Furnok's show of good faith.

## Properties
*Scroll, rare (custom).* As an action, read it to raise an invisible barrier in a 10-foot radius around you for 5 minutes. Spells and magical effects that originate outside the barrier can't affect creatures or objects inside it. Spells cast inside work normally. The barrier moves with you.

## History
1e scroll of protection from magic. *Custom 5e item, modelled on the DMG scroll of protection.*

## Found In
- [[T1-02 Inn of the Welcome Wench#I 13. Private Room]]: [[Furnok of Ferd]]

## Continuity
Furnok offers it to a party he joins, hoping to parlay it into far more.
