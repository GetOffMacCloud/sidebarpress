# Gnome's Iron Ring

> A plain iron ring, given by a grateful gnome.

## Properties
*Plot item, not magical.* Gnomes within 100 miles of Hommlet recognize it as the sign of an ally of the gnomes. The bearer has **advantage on Charisma checks** to make them friendly and helpful.

## History
Given by the gnome rescued from Lubash's pantry to whoever first agrees to free him. 1e: recognized 70% of the time, +25% friendly.

## Found In
- [[T1-07 Moathouse Dungeon#25. OAK DOOR]]

## Continuity
The Kron Hills hold nearly 20,000 gnomes.
