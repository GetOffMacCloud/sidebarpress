# Dagger of Venom

> A thin dagger with a groove down the blade, never far from Gremag's hand.

## Properties
*Weapon (dagger), rare.* +1 to attack and damage rolls. As an action, you can cause thick black poison to coat the blade for 1 minute or until an attack with it hits. A creature hit must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution save or take 2d10 poison damage and be poisoned for 1 minute. In the DMG this works once a day; **Gremag's has 3 doses left** (1e), after which it is an ordinary *dagger +1*.

## History
1e dagger of venom, 3 doses left.
