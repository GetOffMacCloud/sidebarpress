# Staff of the Python

> Jaroo's staff, carved with scales.

## Properties
*Staff, uncommon (requires attunement by a cleric, druid or warlock).* As an action, speak its command word and throw it on the ground within 10 feet: it becomes a giant constrictor snake under your control, acting on its own initiative. A bonus action and the command word turn it back. If the snake is reduced to 0 hit points, it dies and the staff is destroyed; otherwise it reverts with full hit points.

## History
