# Ring of Invisibility

> A ring that only the most trusted servants of the Old Faith and St. Cuthbert carry.

## Properties
*Ring, legendary (requires attunement).* While wearing it, you can turn invisible as an action. Anything you wear or carry is invisible with you. You remain invisible until the ring is removed, until you attack or cast a spell, or until you use a bonus action to become visible again.

## History
Two in T1, both NPC-only: Terjon's and Jaroo's. Furnok's 1e ring is replaced by two potions of invisibility in this edition (ledger §7.2).
