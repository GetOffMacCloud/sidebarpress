# Potion of Undead Control

> A dark, grave-cold potion found in the ghouls' den.

## Properties
*Potion, rare (custom).* For 1 hour after you drink it, you can use a bonus action on each of your turns to cast *command* (save DC 13) on one undead creature you can see within 60 feet. Undead that are immune to being charmed are unaffected.

## History
1e potion of undead control. *Custom 5e item, built from the DMG's potions of animal friendship and giant strength as models.*

## Found In
- [[T1-07 Moathouse Dungeon#31. BURIAL CRYPTS]]: the ghouls' den (31a)

## Continuity
Useful against the zombies of area 21 and much that lies under the Temple.
