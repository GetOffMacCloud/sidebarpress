# Wand of Magic Missiles

> Burne's wand.

## Properties
*Wand, uncommon.* **7 charges** (1e 49). As an action, expend 1 or more charges to cast *magic missile* (1 charge for 1st level, +1 slot level per extra charge). Regains 1d6 + 1 charges daily at dawn; if the last charge is expended, a d20 roll of 1 destroys it.
