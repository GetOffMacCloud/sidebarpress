# Spell Scrolls, Burne's Coffer

> Seven wizard scrolls Burne can't decide whether to copy or keep.

## Properties
*Spell scrolls.* *Comprehend languages* (1st, common), *mirror image*, *pyrotechnics*, *suggestion* and *web* (2nd, uncommon), *ice storm* (4th, rare), *transmute rock* (5th, rare). *Pyrotechnics* and *transmute rock* are from *Xanathar's Guide to Everything*; at a PHB-only table, use *flaming sphere* and *passwall*.

## History
In the locked coffer in Burne's rafter chest.

## Found In
- [[T1-05 Guard Tower#GT 6. Burne's Chamber]]

## Continuity
Burne's property.
