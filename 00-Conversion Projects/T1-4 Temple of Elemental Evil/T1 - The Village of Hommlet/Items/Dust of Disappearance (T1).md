# Dust of Disappearance

> Small packets of fine grey powder.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, uncommon.* As an action, throw one pinch into the air: you and each creature and object within 10 feet become invisible for 2d4 minutes (the duration is the same for all). The invisibility ends early for a creature that attacks or casts a spell. **Each pinch is one use.**

## History
Gremag carries 3 pinches; Rufus carries 12 (1e "12 doses").
