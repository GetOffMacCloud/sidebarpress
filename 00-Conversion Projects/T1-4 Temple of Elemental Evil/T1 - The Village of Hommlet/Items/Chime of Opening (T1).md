# Chime of Opening

> A hollow metal tube that rings open locks.

## Properties
*Wondrous item, rare.* 10 uses left. As an action, strike it while pointing at an object within 120 feet: one lock or latch on it opens (bars, bolts, chains, shackles, locks) unless the sound can't reach it. If nothing is locked, the object itself opens if it can. The chime cracks after its last use.

## History
Burne's. 1e "51 charges"; 5e chimes have 10 uses, and Burne's has all of them left.
