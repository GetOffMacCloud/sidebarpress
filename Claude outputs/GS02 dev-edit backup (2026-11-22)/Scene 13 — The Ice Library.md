# Scene 13 — The Ice Library (optional Session 4)

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS02 — The Long Night at Candlecrown/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 12 — Dawn at Candlecrown]] · Location: [[The Ice Library]]

## Purpose
If Hrimvald escaped with voices, the party follows it to its cave-library in the high Tors and gets them back before the thaw shatters them. A short dungeon (six areas) and a rematch on its ground.
**Budget:** one session of 3–4 hours.

## The Clock
Stolen voices last in their runes until the thaw. In the cave they last until **Fireseek 7** (ten days after Needfest 4); if the party comes in spring, after a *Silenced* ending, they last until Coldeven 1. After that, each rune shatters and its voice is lost. Hrimvald regains 10 hit points a day after it flees; if it fled at 50 or fewer, it is at 92 by the ninth day.

## Getting There
Corin's drawing or Dob's tracking shows the route: east along the cliffs and up into the high Tors, two days on foot in winter (DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) each day; a failure adds a day). Hrimvald's storm sits over the cave, so it's easy to see and hard to reach. Compress the journey to one encounter (a tyrg or two, if any survive) unless the table wants more.

## Arrival
> [!art]- Map — the Ice Library (M4)
> ![[SP-GS02 - M4 The Ice Library - Print (300dpi, 7.5in).png|500]]

> *High on a bare tor, a cave mouth opens in a wall of blue ice. Wind pours out of it, and with the wind come voices, faint, overlapping, in a dozen languages: a man reciting a list, a woman laughing, a child counting, someone singing a tune you know. Frost-runes cover the ice around the entrance, thick as moss.*

**To the table:** *"You can hear Wenna's voice in there. And Dob's. What's your approach?"*

## Laws of the Place
- **Cold.** Extreme cold (DC 15 save hourly) everywhere inside; the cave's ice doesn't melt under normal fire, but magical fire melts a 5-foot square per 10 damage.
- **Voices.** Every area echoes with stolen voices; speech is hard to hear (Perception relying on hearing has disadvantage) except in I4.
- **Alert:** Hrimvald knows the party is here when anyone speaks in I3 (as M9 in the mine).

## Key
**I1. THE MOUTH.** The entrance tunnel, 20 feet wide, slick. Difficult terrain unless the party uses crampons or salt. Pawprints of the last tyrgs, if any; their den is here (tyrgs (0–2), sleeping).

**I2. THE DOOR OF WORDS.**
> *The tunnel ends at a smooth wall of ice carved into the shape of a door. In its center, a single rune glows. Beside it stands a pillar of ice as thick as an ox.*
**Creatures:** an **ice-rune guardian** (1; 2 for five or six PCs). Its word is "shut." The door opens when its rune melts (10 fire damage) or when someone speaks a word to it that it hasn't heard before (a real word in a language no one at the table has used tonight).

**I3. THE GALLERY OF RUNES.** The long, narrow passage from the Door of Words to the Still Pool, its walls cut into shelves on both sides, each holding hundreds of runes, each rune a word or a voice. Most are centuries old: dead languages, strangers. **The Candlecrown runes** are together on one shelf near the far end, still warm (DC 12 Investigation to find among thousands; automatic if someone calls a villager's name and listens for the answer). Melting a rune (10 fire damage, or *dispel magic* DC 15) frees its voice, which flies home. Hrimvald hears anything said here.

**I4. THE STILL POOL.** A round chamber with a pool of black water that doesn't freeze. Here, and only here, every voice is quiet. Anyone who speaks over the pool hears their own words come back in the voice of someone they've lost (a dead parent, a friend). It is harmless and unbearable. The **Heart-Rune** sleeps at the bottom (see [[Heart-Rune of Hrimvald]]; 15 feet down, DC 15 Constitution save to dive in the cold).

**I5. HRIMVALD'S HEARTH.** A dome of ice 60 feet across, with a throne-shaped hollow in the far wall and a hole in the roof to the sky. Hrimvald sits here among the lines of the seventh verse it gathered, each written in frost on the floor around it.
**Creatures:** **Hrimvald** (1), at the hit points the clock gives it.
**Tactics:** it parleys first, as always. It would trade the Candlecrown runes for the lines it lacks, line for voice. If fought, it uses the hole in the roof (fly) and the walls (Icewalking); it has no siege actions here, but the dome counts as a lair: on initiative 20 it can make the floor slick (a 20-foot square becomes difficult terrain; DC 13 Dexterity save or fall prone). It doesn't flee from its own home. It bargains when bloodied.
**Development:** destroying it here frees every rune in the cave, Candlecrown's included.

**I6. THE OLD HOARD.** Behind the throne, at the head of the dome where the moonlight falls, a narrow cleft holding what the immoth's victims carried: a Keoish envoy's satchel (sealed letters to the Yeomanry dated 571 CY, a *ring of warmth*, 120 pp), a dwarf's carved bone wand (a *wand of web*, 7 charges), coin in a dozen mintings (340 gp in total).

## Development
- **Every Candlecrown voice recovered** goes home, faster than the party can walk back. They return to a village that sings.
- **Hrimvald destroyed or bargained with:** as the Saved or Unbound endings in [[Scene 12 — Dawn at Candlecrown]].
- **Too late:** voices whose runes shattered are gone. *Greater restoration* or *wish* can still restore them.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** blue ice, shelves of glowing marks, a still black pool, a throne of frost.
- **Sound:** a thousand voices whispering, and then, at the pool, none.
- **Smell:** the clean nothing of deep cold.
- **Touch:** frost forming on your clothes while you stand still.
- **Taste:** snowmelt, from a canteen that has to be kept inside your coat.

## Transition
- → The end: back at Candlecrown, the party arrives to the sound of the carol, all seven verses, sung by every voice they brought home.
