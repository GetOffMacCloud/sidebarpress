# Ebbo Kühn

> The Hafenmeister's clerk at Osterschlauss, who writes down everything that goes aboard.

## Appearance
Ink to the second knuckle, a roll of paper on a board, a pen he wets between entries.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Exact, unhurried, and incurious about anything that is already on the roll.

## Goals & Secrets
- **Wants:** every entry in its column.
- **Knows:** that the Frachtrolle is read aloud in Aerden market every Godsday, and so a roll Otwin hears.
- **Reacts:** asks about anything undeclared. Takes a 10-mark bribe and asks why, and remembers. Can be distracted (Deception or Performance DC 13).
