# Wenna Cobb

> Waycombe's herbwife, in her seventies, and the second captive on the Rite Track. Her grandmother "saw the Witch's dead march."

## Appearance
Small and very still at her stone, her lips moving.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Calm and folk-wise, and she prays under her breath. She makes the warding sign against Iggwilv, not against Nerull.

## Goals & Secrets
- **Wants:** to see the miller's wife again. That is the reunion in the denouement.
- **Knows:** the old song and the warding signs, in folk-memory form (see [[Iggwilvs-Conquest-of-Perrenland]]).
- **Reacts:** when freed, she walks downhill rather than runs, still praying.

## Stat Block
**Commoner** (MM p. 345), with proficiency in Medicine.
