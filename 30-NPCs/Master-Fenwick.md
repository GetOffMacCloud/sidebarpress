# Master Fenwick

> Master of Field Studies at [[Greyhawk-University]]. He posts field jobs on the board, reads them aloud to anyone who'll listen, and grades students on their patrons' reports.

## Appearance
Broad and bearded, missing two fingers to a winter wolf twenty years ago.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Cheerful about danger in a way that worries students. Reads every posting aloud with relish.

> *"It's cold, it's dangerous, and it's the most interesting thing on the board. Who's going?"*

## Goals & Secrets
- **Wants:** students who come home with stories and all their fingers. Mostly.
- **Runs:** field credit. A patron's report decides the award, which counts toward the 600-credit limit.

## Stat Block
**Veteran** (MM p. 350). Not met in the field.
