# Bett Marsh

> Tam's small sister. She gives a folk-hero PC a carved wooden stag "so it knows you're friends."

## Appearance
Very small, muddy-kneed, and always about two steps behind the party.

## Personality & Mannerisms
She follows the PC she has chosen everywhere and says almost nothing.

## Goals & Secrets
- **Wants:** Tam home.
- **The stag:** if the carving is shown to the Barrow Stag, the stag hesitates for one round. *Never explain why.*
