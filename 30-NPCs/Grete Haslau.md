# Grete Haslau

> Widow and keeper of the Mountainview Rest in Aerden, who has kept the party alive for three weeks on arrangements.

## Appearance
Fifties. Her husband's Furyondy coin at her throat. A slate board and a stick of chalk.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Refuses to be thanked. Has already decided the party are decent and is waiting for them to catch up.

## Goals & Secrets
- **Wants:** her board squared and her house quiet after vespers.
- **Knows:** everyone's debts to the pfennig; that Sergeant Amtmann has never bought his own ale in fifteen years.
- **Reacts:** makes arrangements, never gives credit. "Don't. I'll only put it on the board."
