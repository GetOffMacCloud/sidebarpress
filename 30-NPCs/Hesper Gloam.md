# Hesper Gloam

> "The Crow-Wife." The party's only real ally in Thornbury: a scarred, sour woman on the ridge whose grandmother opposed the pact and was burned for it by her own cousins.

## Appearance
Fifty, lean, a burn scar from jaw to collarbone that she doesn't hide. Dressed in sacking and crow feathers. A heavy crossbow, always loaded. Crows on every thorn around her hut, and one on her shoulder more often than not.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Terrifying to look at, kind underneath, and never says anything kind. Assumes the worst, is relieved to be wrong, and shows relief by lowering the crossbow an inch. Speaks in short, flat sentences. Looks at the sky when she's listening to the crows.

## Goals & Secrets
- **Wants:** the pact ended, and nobody else burned for saying it should be.
- **Knows:** the Harvest Lord is a tree that ate too much; the heart is under the barn floor; cutting it kills the valley's plenty; the pact was written in a hurry and has holes in it; the rear door has a key and Tamsin has it.
- **Secret:** *Gloam* is one of the six founding names on the pact. Her grandmother was burned by the Sheaf, and the Sheaf were her family. Hesper has never left the valley because she is, by blood, as bound to it as anyone.
- **What she won't do:** go into the pit. *"Somebody has to be here when it all goes wrong."*

## Stat Block
**Druid** (MM 346), with *spike growth* prepared in place of *barkskin*. She joins the party at the barn if asked, and fights from the rear door or the lofts.

## Continuity
Gives the party a *bag of beans* ("Honest seed. Mostly."). If the pact is broken, she comes down off the ridge for the first time in thirty years and teaches Thornbury to farm. If the pact is paid, she stays on the ridge and the crows keep counting. If the barn burns, she takes the blame for the party and lets the Sheaf drive her out; she has been expecting it her whole life.
