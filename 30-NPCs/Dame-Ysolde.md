# Dame Ysolde

> The fieldcraft mistress at [[Greyhawk-University]]. She teaches trail-sign, weather-reading, and "how to be a guest in someone else's country without getting killed."

## Appearance
Lean and windburned, grey at the temples, with a knight's bearing and a shepherd's boots.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Teaches by rules of three. Expects students to remember them under pressure.

> *"In another people's country you are a guest from the moment they see you. Put your steel down before you're asked. Let their taster drink first. Eat what they put in front of you."*

## Goals & Secrets
- **Wants:** students who come home because they behaved like guests.

## Stat Block
**Knight** (MM p. 347). Not met in the field.

- **Scene 2:** her guest-craft locks in the restraint bonus (Favor +1).
- **Scene 4:** recognize Wolf sinew-fletching without a roll.
