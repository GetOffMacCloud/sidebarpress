# Hessel Amtmann

> Sergeant of the Erlenhof house guard, Corwin's accomplice, and the man who sold the route.

## Appearance
A plain grey house-guard's jerkin with no band on the sleeve. Fifteen years in the job.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Answers a different question than the one asked. Says "I couldn't spare a man" three different ways. Changes the subject when asked who else knows.

## Goals & Secrets
- **Wants:** his debt gone and his hands clean.
- **Knows:** everything. Ten days ago he sold the route, the boat and the date to have a 60-mark Schuldbrief struck out. He doesn't know the paper is the Kettenhaus's.
- **Reacts:** lies badly about who knows (Insight DC 12 catches the fear). Buys his own ale the night he posts the notice, for the first time in fifteen years. Never leaves Aerden.
