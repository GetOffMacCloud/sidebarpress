# Mother Sarnai

> Shaman of Istus and the horde's fate-reader. She sets the three trials, translates for the Ilkhan, and cannot be lied to comfortably.

## Appearance
Ancient, blind in one eye (milky), the other dark and sharp. Bone and feather fetishes in grey hair. She smells of juniper smoke.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Quiet, certain, faintly kind. She has no spell for lies; she watches faces the way hawks watch grass. She asks hard questions the way you'd ask about the weather. She speaks of threads, cloth, and the Lady's loom.

> *"I only look at the cloth. The weaving is the Lady's. Sit down. You are making the threads nervous."*

## Goals & Secrets
- **Wants:** the horde to choose its war with open eyes, not be led into one.
- **Ally if told the truth:** given the false-flag evidence, she posts riders on Kezrin and can carry the evidence into the council herself.
- **Knows:** that the party carries a second sealed letter (Batzorig told her), the custom of every trial, and what she leaves out when she translates.
- **Escalation:** she tests first (the trials), then judges (the Fire), then acts (riders on Kezrin, the soft fail). Caught lying to, she turns to stone and stays that way.

## Stat Block
**Priest** (MM p. 348), Istus's domain of fate. Noncombatant in this adventure.

**Endorsement:** if a PC gentled the black stallion, she named the party "the ones the black one chose." **Caught lying:** her face was stone at the council. Either way she met them at the edge of the tents with a juniper sprig if they were refused or cast out.
