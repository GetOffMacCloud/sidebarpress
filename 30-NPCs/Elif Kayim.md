# Elif Kayim

> [!art]+ Portrait
> ![[Ritterstadt elif-kayim.jpg|300]]


> A garrulous, road-worn Wolf Nomad trader who has come a very long way south, half to sell amber and furs and half because the north has stopped feeling like home. The first of his people to trade at Ritterstadt, and an unwitting witness to something he can't name.

## Source
The campaign vault's Wolf Nomad trader, renamed from **Temür Bayan** to avoid a clash with Khan Temur in [[SP-GA01-The-False-Arrow]]. Everything below that isn't in that note is Gazetteer elaboration.

## Appearance
Weather-cured and wind-burned, in layered felt and white-fur travelling clothes hung with amber beads and horn trinkets. A string of shaggy ponies follows him. One hand is always drifting to the wolf-tooth charm at his neck.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Talks the way a man talks who has been alone on a cold road too long. Superstitious, generous with tea, stingy with bad news until the third cup.

> *"The raiding has stopped up north. That is the bad part. Now they ride in rows."*

## Goals & Secrets
- **Wants:** worked metal, salt and Verbobonc steel for his amber, furs, horn and felt; a place to winter that isn't the north.
- **Knows:** the Wolf Nomads' routes and the old standing stones at G5, and how his people expect to be approached there: respectfully, and overnight.
- **Carries:** the **Winter Vanguard** rumours (below). He thinks it's a bad winter with a war in it. He has never heard the name "Velvet Shadow."
- **Secret:** his cousin rode north to look closer and has not come back. He won't say so until he trusts someone.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> **The rumours he carries** (true; deliver one a day while he's in town):
> - *"A winter that has captains."* The clans no longer squabble over horses. They ride under one cold order, in grey iron that doesn't rust.
> - *"The burned knight."* At the head of the iron riders rides a knight in black plate with a face like cooled ash. Where he points, the clans go without a word, and the fires near him burn the wrong colour.
> - *"The diggers under the ice."* Deep in the Burneal, grey-skinned dwarves who hate the sun cut a shaft into the old ice day and night. The herds won't graze within a day's ride of it.
>
> **Bringing him in.** He arrives with the first hard frosts, or whenever the north needs to come closer. His arrival is the Wolf Nomads' first trade at Ritterstadt; how the town treats him sets their relationship for years ([[RG-06 — Factions]], [[RG-07 — Perrenland & the Neighbours]]).

## Stat Block
**Scout** (MM p. 349), as a hardy long-road trader. Languages: Common (broken), the Wolf Nomad tongue.
