# Ulf Hadric

> A half-orc Junior with a Logistics minor who keeps Lecturer Holmgard's kit store. Every expedition's bog poles, bedrolls, and lamp-oil pass through his hands.

## Appearance
Twenty-two, big, careful, a kit-ledger in a waxed pouch and a pencil behind each ear.

## Personality & Mannerisms
Patient, literal, and proud of the store. Counts everything out and counts it back in.

> *"Two bog poles a party. She says so, I hand them out, you bring them back."*

## Goals & Secrets
- **Wants:** to make Senior and stay on as Quartermaster when Holmgard retires.
- **Knows:** exactly which parties came back short of kit, and from where.
- **Secret:** a Curio Club member has twice tried to buy blank Archive countersign slips from him.

## Stat Block
**Thug** (MM p. 350) without the malice.
