# The Archive

> The only building in Ritterstadt with a slate roof, because paper burns. Maps, field reports, Publications — and the sealed stacks.

## Description
*Stone walls, a slate roof, narrow windows of good glass. Inside it is cold, dry, and quiet: map-presses along one wall, reading desks under the windows, and at the back an iron grille with a padlock the Archivist did not install.*

## Rooms
- **The reading room:** six desks; students may read anything not sealed.
- **The map presses:** the Scouts & Surveyors' archive. Nothing leaves without their countersign.
- **The Publications shelf:** every Field Report the station has filed since 569.
- **The sealed stacks:** behind the grille; the Registrar holds the key.

## Who's Here
[[Saelithan Verenwyr]] (Archivist) · senior student Oswin Kettler (map archive) · the Illuminators' Guild at the copy-table

## What It Offers
- **Training — Research:** a day's study grants advantage on one History, Arcana, or Religion check about a named subject, if the Archive holds a source.
- **Services:** fair copies (1 gp a page, Illuminators' Guild); map copies (for credits or 10 gp to outsiders).
- **Missions:** lost documents; Hassenrath's missing notebook.

## Hooks
- The Archivist wants to know why the Registrar seals what she seals.
- A map is missing from the presses, and its countersign is forged.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> Hesta Varnholm reads the Archive for the Illuminators' Guild and copies more than she's asked. The Grey Sickle's barrow list came out of the Historical Society's archive kept here.
