# The Burrows

> Gnome-cut rooms under Market Hill, dry and warm, home to the town's gnomes: the Seam-Finders' chart room, Pim Wobblewright's workshop, and four households.

## Description
*Round doors in the hill's south face, brass lamps, a smell of oil and lathe-shavings. The passages are gnome-height; everyone else stoops.*

## Who's Here
[[Nessa Quillfeather]] (the Seam-Finders) · [[Pim Wobblewright]] (instrument-maker) · [[Tobrin Fennelwick]] lives here

## What It Offers
- **Trade:** compass 25 gp; bog-sounder 15 gp; lamp-oil; chart copies.
- **Training — Instruments:** Pim teaches the use of compass, astrolabe, and bog-sounder.
- **Information:** mineral geography; the Seam-Finders' charts of the eastern valleys.
- **Missions:** prospecting claims; Pim's sounder readings near the Hush.

## Hooks
- Pim's sounders read wrong near the Hush. He'll pay for someone to walk one along the tree line.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> Nessa's second set of charts marks three places where the ground is warm in winter, all on the binding network. Pim's "wrong" readings pulse every 77 heartbeats.
