# Dornhalm's Yard

> A timber yard below the lake mouth, where the Velverdyva starts to run.

## Description
Stacks of squared pine taller than a house, a sawpit, a crane boom with one log hanging from its chain, a tally-office with a lamp and a stove, and the river running fast along the quay edge.

## Notable Features
- **Log stacks:** full cover; can be toppled
- **The sawpit:** 8 ft deep
- **The boom crane:** an action at the lever drops the log
- **The tally-office:** the stove, the tally-book, and the Kettenhaus letter
- **The river edge:** a creature that goes in is swept downstream

## Inhabitants
- [[Ulrich Dornhalm]] and his hired yard crew
