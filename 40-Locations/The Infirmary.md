# The Infirmary

> The north wing of St. Erika's Chapel, given to the University in 574. Six cots, a still, and an herb garden under glass. The Vater and the Doktor disagree about what does the healing and agree that both are charged to the Bursar.

## Description
*Clean linen, lye, and something bitter on the still. Prayer-candles on one wall, drying herbs on the other, and a door between the chapel and the wing that neither party will close.*

## Who's Here
Doktor Agathe Sauerbrunn (Infirmarian; head of Physic & Alchemy) · [[Lecturer Jorund Vesk]] (Necrology; lays out the dead) · [[Vater Sigmund Wolfherz]] next door

## What It Offers
- **Healing:** potions of healing at cost (25 gp) to the enrolled, full price (50 gp) to others; disease and poison treated.
- **Training — Medicine & Herbalism:** practicals here.
- **Information:** who came back hurt and how.
- **Missions:** plague-rumour, poison, the Bloom-sick.

## Hooks
- A patient from the last expedition won't stop shivering, and the cot frosts over at night.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> Old Hebe Strasser laid out the two watchmen killed on 18 Planting and saw something in their wounds. She told the Vater, who told Vesk, who has kept their notes in the still-room.
