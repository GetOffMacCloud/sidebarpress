# The University Forge

> Larger and louder than the village smithy. It makes the watch's spears and the expeditions' steel, and it is the one place a Clan Irongrip dwarf drinks with a Clan Brock dwarf.

## Description
*Two hearths, a trip-hammer driven off the millrace, racks of bog-iron blooms, and a bench where the journeymen eat at noon. The noise is constant; conversation is shouted or not had.*

## Who's Here
[[Hogar Irongrip]] (Master of the Forge) · Forge Row journeymen

## What It Offers
- **Trade:** expedition arms and armour; repairs at cost for the enrolled; PHB prices for others (weapons in 2–4 days).
- **Training — Smithing:** for Forge Row only, unless Hogar likes you.
- **Information:** dwarf-hold politics. Never ask about Bitter Pass.

## Hooks
- A Clan Irongrip emissary came to court day and won't say why.
- A Traft factor wants a season's iron delivered to a barge at night.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> Hogar's exile from Bitter Pass and his post here are a standing irregularity neither the clan nor the University has formally noticed. If either does, someone has to choose.
