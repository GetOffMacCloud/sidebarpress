# Hollen Ford Relay

> A Greyhawk University courier relay on the drove road: a stone tower with a beacon cage, a stable, a well and a ford.

> [!art]- Map
> ![[SP-GA05 map-hollen-ford-relay.jpg|500]]

- **What it is:** two riders and a signaller carry field reports south to Ritterstadt. A lit beacon brings the garrison.
- **In SP-GA05:** everyone here is dead and walking; the last order in the log recalls the second patrol and promises no relief before Readying ([[SP-GA05 — E2 — The Hollen Ford Relay]]).
- **Afterward:** unmanned until someone holds it ([[Hollen Ford Must Be Manned]]).
