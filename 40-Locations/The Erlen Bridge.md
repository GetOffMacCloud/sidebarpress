# The Erlen Bridge

> The Halbritters' own toll bridge on the Aerden–Osterschlauss road, and the summer camp of a Meerijder drover crew.

## Description
A stone toll bridge three wagons wide over the slow, four-foot-deep Erlen, with waist-high walls and a slate-roofed toll house at the near end. Every summer week a Meerijder crew camps here with stock for the Osterschlauss boats.

## Notable Features
- **The toll house** — half cover; [[Old Hartwin]]'s stool
- **The parapet** — a shove over it is 1d6 falling and a wet drover, not a drowning
- **The cattle on the road** — terrain; loud noise sets them milling

## Inhabitants
- [[Old Hartwin]]; [[Boedic of the Meerijder]] and his crew (in summer)

## History
Homebrew. The tolls are Aerden's, and Aerden's council seat is Otwin's. Corwin mortgaged them on a forged seal.
