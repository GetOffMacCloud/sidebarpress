# The Hundred's Chapter-House

> The Hundred's hall in Schwartzenbruin, which is both its barracks and its shrine to Kord. The company chest and its sealed records are kept here.

## Description
A tall, narrow stone house a few streets from the Red Cord. A red cord-knot is carved over the door. Inside are a drill-floor with a Kord ring painted on the boards, a shrine with an eight-pointed star of spears and maces, and racks of pikes.

## Notable Features
- **The company chest:** iron-bound and sealed. It holds Hauptmann Lorenz Kammerer's release-copy, under a seal no captain has had cause to break.
- **The company's copy of the 480 Soldbrief**, which is used if the original burns at Dornhalm's Yard.
- **The shrine of Kord** (see [[Kord]]).
