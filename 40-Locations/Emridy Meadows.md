# Emridy Meadows

> The grassy clearings where the Righteous Host broke the Horde of Elemental Evil in 569 CY.

## Description
Pale grass over long, low grave mounds; rusted spearheads in the turf. In SP-GS01, a stripped oak where the Gluttonwretch roosts.

## Notable Features
- The grave mounds (Hollow Guests rise here in SP-GS01)
- The stripped oak (homebrew roost)

## Inhabitants
- The dead of 569 CY

## History
Canon: the Battle of Emridy Meadows, 569 CY (T1-4; *World of Greyhawk*, 1983). Sources place the meadows south of the Velverdyva, northwest of Nulb, along the Emridy Run, which flows into Imeryds Run from the west.
