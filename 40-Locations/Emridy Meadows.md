# Emridy Meadows

> The grassy clearings where the Righteous Host broke the Horde of Elemental Evil in 569 CY.

## Description
Pale grass over long, low grave mounds; rusted spearheads in the turf. In SP-GS01, a stripped oak where the Gluttonwretch roosts.

## Notable Features
- The grave mounds (Hollow Guests rise here in SP-GS01)
- The stripped oak (homebrew roost)

## Inhabitants
- The dead of 569 CY

## History
Canon: see [[The-Battle-of-Emridy-Meadows|The Battle of Emridy Meadows]]. T1-4 describes the great battle but never names the field. Sources place the meadows south of the Velverdyva, northwest of Nulb, along the Emridy Run, which flows into Imeryds Run from the west.
