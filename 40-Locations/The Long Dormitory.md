# The Long Dormitory

> Forty bunks in two bays, built with the Ritter's Hanselmann loan in 573. Home to the station's students, and to the Proctor at the end of the hall.

## Description
*A long low timber hall with a peat-fire at each end and a common hearth in the middle where the student organizations hold their meetings. Boots by the door, maps pinned over half the bunks, and somebody always awake.*

## Who's Here
[[Mathias Grell]] (Proctor) · the 36 students of the station · club meetings in the evening

## What It Offers
- **Rest:** a bunk, for the enrolled only.
- **Information:** what students saw in the field; which clubs are feuding; the gate rota.
- **Missions:** student trouble; the Ramblers' unsanctioned trips; the gate-rota gaps.

## Hooks
- The Fencing Club owes the Scouts a wager it can't pay.
- A bunk has been empty a week, and its owner's kit is still in the store.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> The Curio Club meets in the eastern bay, and its members have tried twice to buy blank Archive countersign slips from Ulf Hadric.
