# Hospice of St. Cuthbert

> A stone hospice in a high Yatil pass on the Perrenland–Ekbir caravan road. The caravans can't afford to lose the pass, and the Tiger Nomads have started taxing it with arrows.

## Description
*The hospice squats against the pass like a fist that forgot how to open. Frost furs the lintels; the chapel bell is muffled with sacking so it won't crack in the cold. Above the gate, someone has chalked the Cuthbertine cudgel, and under it, in a smaller hand: come back alive.*

## Notable Features
- **The chapel bell:** muffled in deep winter, rung once for every party that leaves.
- **The yard:** mules, a flat-eyed yard-dog, and the stable doorway where Halya gives her letter.
- **The mounting block:** where Brennin sits down when the news is good.
