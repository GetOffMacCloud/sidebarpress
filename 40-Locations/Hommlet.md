# Hommlet

> A farming village on the High Road, rebuilt after the Temple of Elemental Evil fell at Emridy Meadows in 569 CY.

## Description
A village of some 950 souls around a green, with the Inn of the Welcome Wench, the Church of St. Cuthbert, a traders' establishment, a mill, and an Old Faith grove at its edge.

## Notable Features
- The green, where Brewfest is held
- [[Inn of the Welcome Wench]]
- [[Jaroo's Grove]]
- The Elder's house, the traders' establishment, Mytch's mill (see SP-GS01 Scene 02)
