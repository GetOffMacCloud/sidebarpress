# The Three Sisters

> Three standing stones on the turf mound of Kethra the Hound-Mother's barrow, under the Clatspur ridges.

## Description
*The mound is a long turf back, twice the height of a man, crowned by three standing stones taller than a horse. On the south face, the turf has been cut away, and a black doorway stands open in the earth.*

## Notable Features
- **The ring:** a 30-ft triangle of 15-ft stones carved with the triple spiral.
- **The barrow mouth:** in the south peat-cut face. A 5-ft passage leads 20 ft to a round chamber.
- **The bier:** Kethra lies on it with amber, bronze spear-blades, and a boar-tusk helm (about 150 gp, and taboo).
- **Turf stacks** on the south slope; a 10-ft cut face.

## Inhabitants
- [[Kethra-the-Hound-Mother]]
- Her pack, including the Barrow Stag

## History
*Homebrew.* A Flan chieftain-barrow about fifteen centuries old, opened in Goodmonth 576 CY by Traft peat-cutters working on Reeve [[Odila-Marsh]]'s lease.
