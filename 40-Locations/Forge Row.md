# Forge Row

> The dwarves' quarter, hugging the inside of the eastern palisade: seven households, the Mining Consortium's bunkhouse, the Assay House, and the University Forge. Twenty-four souls of Clan Irongrip and Clan Brock.

## Description
*Stone-footed houses with turf roofs, a gutter of black forge-water, and the constant ring of the trip-hammer. The eastern gate's breach is at the end of the row; the dwarves were first to it on 18 Planting.*

## Who's Here
[[Hogar Irongrip]] · [[Drunik Runevault]] · [[Brunna Deepdelve]] · [[Orrim Stoneweir]] · five unmarried journeymen in the bunkhouse

## Places
- [[The University Forge]] · [[The Assay House]] · the Consortium's bunkhouse

## What It Offers
- **Trade:** bog iron (2 gp per 100 lb), dwarven ironwork.
- **Information:** the bogs, the cuttings, and what the Consortium is planning.
- **Missions:** cutting crews lost in the bogs; the Traft factor's night-barge offer.

## Hooks
- Orrim saw black ice in the cuttings a month before anyone else.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> Clan Irongrip and Clan Brock share Forge Row because neither hold will acknowledge the other's presence here. A dispute on the Row is a dispute between holds.
