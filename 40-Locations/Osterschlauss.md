# Osterschlauss

> Aerden's landing on Lake Quag: a hundred houses and a stone quay.

## Description
A hundred houses and a stone quay on the western shore. The Hafenmeister's clerk stands at the head of every gangway with the Frachtrolle, and the roll is read aloud in Aerden market every Godsday.

## Notable Features
- **The quay** — where the Kortheim Maid ties up
- **The Frachtrolle** — the cargo roll; anything declared on it reaches Aerden by Godsday

## Inhabitants
- The Hafenmeister's clerk; [[Captain Marda Vosk]] when the Maid is in
