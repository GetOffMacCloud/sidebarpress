# The Assay House

> A squat stone house on Forge Row where ore is tested, bog iron graded, and — by charter — every Bloom crystal and relic is logged before the University sees it.

## Description
*Acid and hot metal. A long bench of crucibles and scales, a locked crystal cabinet, and the logbook chained to the wall.*

## Who's Here
[[Brunna Deepdelve]] (assayer, Clan Brock)

## What It Offers
- **Services:** assay 1 gp; appraisal 5% of value; Bloom crystals logged for credits.
- **Training — Appraisal & Provenance:** lectures held here for Antiquities students.
- **Information:** what came out of the ground, and where.
- **Missions:** ore and relic disputes; the Consortium's claims.

## Hooks
- Three logged Bloom crystals are missing from the cabinet.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> The Pale Ledger has the three crystals. Brunna suspects the Curio Club.
