# Merrow's Rest

> A roadside mortuary chapel with catacombs below, where the dead of the road have been buried for forty years.

## Description
A squat stone chapel behind a lychgate hung with funeral bells. Fresh earth on every grave in the yard. Candles in the nave at noon.

## Notable Features
- **Nave and vestry.** The burial register is in the vestry. The bone chute opens behind the altar.
- **C1. The Bone Gallery.** Skulls turned to face the wall; slime on the ceiling.
- **C2. The Embalming Room.** Hooks, salt barrels, cured and dated limbs, the larder ledger.
- **C3. The Columbarium.** Urn niches; the mouth of the burial crawls.
- **C4. The Iron Doors.** Barred from the outside; gouged high on the outside.
- **C5. The Saint's Ossuary.** 40 × 60 ft. vault under black water: eight sarcophagi, bone shelves, a drain channel, the altar and the Urn of Saint Merrow.
- **The burial crawls.** 2-ft. tunnels between C3 and C5; too narrow for anything Large.

## Inhabitants
- [[Brother Ossian]]
- [[Sexton Harrow]] and the larder ghouls (the Widow Brisk, the Collier twins, Old Tam)

## History
Founded by the followers of Saint Merrow, patron of the quiet dead (house saint, setting-agnostic). Kept for forty years by the real Brother Ossian, until something came up from below.
