# Kalbach

> A sheep hamlet of forty to sixty souls around a stone well, a day's walk (about 15 miles) southeast of Ritterstadt.

> [!art]- Map
> ![[SP-GA05 map-kalbach.png|500]]

- **What it is:** thirteen turf-roofed houses around a stone well, a walled churchyard with a lychgate and a small slate-roofed chapel, a sheepfold. No priest: the chapel has been locked since the last one died, and the sexton, [[Udo Pfaff]], keeps the key and the graves.
- **Maps:** `map-kalbach.png` (colour, top-down, 3,000 × 1,700 px, 50 px = 5 ft) for Fantasy Grounds · `map-kalbach-dm.png` (grid and K1–K7 labels) · `map-kalbach-print.png` (black-and-white ink redraw for print).
- **People:** [[Udo Pfaff]]; Anka Roth, a widow; Lise, a child; Dieter Kalb, the miller's son. Yati drovers such as [[Brenna]] pass through each autumn.
- **Faith:** Pelor on holy days, the Old Faith in the fields. Nobody names the Reaper.
- **In SP-GA05:** the Grey Sickle cursed the square well. Four died and rise on the fifth night ([[SP-GA05 — E1 — The Kalbach Churchyard]]).
- **Afterward:** if saved, Kalbach remembers the wardens and sends word to the Yati. If lost (Ending C), it is a village of the dead and a standing hazard on the drove road.
