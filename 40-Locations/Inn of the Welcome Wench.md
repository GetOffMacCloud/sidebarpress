# Inn of the Welcome Wench

> Hommlet's inn, opened by Ostler Gundigoot after the Battle of Emridy Meadows.

## Description
A two-story inn on the green with a big common room and a busy kitchen. At Brewfest its door stays propped open all night.

## Notable Features
- **The Open Door** (homebrew, SP-GS01): every Brewfest the door doesn't shut, and anyone who walks in gets fed, because a stranger fed Gundigoot once.
- The kitchen, where Pip Haddle gorges in Scene 01
- Full site key: [[T1-02 Inn of the Welcome Wench]] (T1 conversion)

## Inhabitants
- [[Ostler Gundigoot]] and Goodwife Gundigoot
- [[Pip Haddle]], scullery boy (SP-GS01)

## History
Canon: T1 (1979). The Open Door custom is homebrew.
