# The Larch Camp

> Three family-groups of sylvan elves in bark-and-felt shelters at the tree line outside Ritterstadt's east gate, keeping a watch on the Hush.

## Description
*Shelters you only see when you're among them. A bowyer's rack of curing staves, a fire that makes no smoke, and someone always looking toward the Hush.*

## Who's Here
[[Caelith of the Larch]] (kin-leader) · [[Ysmay Larchwind]] (ranger) · 14 sylvan elves · [[Lecturer Ilvadriel]] most evenings

## What It Offers
- **Trade:** bow-staves (2 gp; never arrows).
- **Training — Archery and Woodcraft:** with Ysmay, paid in grain.
- **Information:** what moves at the tree line; the Hush.
- **Missions:** the forest edge; the Vesve road; the pale figure at the Hush.

## Hooks
- Ysmay has seen the same pale figure at the Hush's edge three nights running.
- Caelith will carry a letter to the Vesve for grain, and won't promise an answer.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> The kin-band trades staves for grain off the Moot's book (Hanz's grain discrepancy). They know where the Hush is thickest and haven't told the University. What they fled in the Vesve was not Grugach.
