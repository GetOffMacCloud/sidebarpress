# Yecha

> The capital of the [[Chakyik]]: a walled hill town in the Yecha Hills under the Yatils. In winter, the horde's tent-city spreads around its walls.

## Description
*Between seven low hills, the steppe is covered in tents — hundreds of them, felt and hide, smoke going straight up in the still air. On the highest hill, earth-and-timber walls ring a squat old town, and the tents spill down from it like grain from a split sack.*

## Notable Features
- **The great pavilion:** forty paces of striped hides beside the walls. Tiger skins on the floor, tiger skulls on the poles, the Ilkhan's low throne in a saber-tooth robe.
- **The horse lines:** along the frozen creek. The black stallion stands alone at the far picket.
- **The guest tents:** felt over willow, dung-braziers, within sight of the war camp.
- **Temur's war camp:** torchlit drill ground; new black-steel blades.
- **Sarnai's tent:** set apart, with a juniper fire before it.

## Inhabitants
- [[Ilkhan-Cligir]], [[Khan-Temur]], [[Mother-Sarnai]], [[Batzorig]]
- Naran (tent-guard, 16), Chuluun (rider)
- [[Master-Kezrin]] (trading, in winter)
