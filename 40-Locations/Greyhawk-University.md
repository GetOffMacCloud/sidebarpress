# Greyhawk University

> The Sidebar Press campaign frame: a jumble of halls, towers, chapels, and dormitories on the north shore of Lake Quag, below the town of Ritterstadt. Students earn field credit by taking jobs off the board.

## Description
*Below the town, grey as hammered tin, lies Lake Quag. Along its northern shore sprawls a jumble of halls, towers, chapels, and dormitories, smoke coming out of all of them at once. Over the gatehouse arch, the university arms: an open book, a cudgel and a quill crossed beneath it. Under that, in student hand: abandon naps, all ye who enter here.*

## Notable Features
- **The Registrar's Hall:** lamp-oil and old paper; Mistress Pell; the Scholar's Oath in gilt.
- **The Practical Colleges' hall:** the field board.
- **The Porter's Lodge:** where letters and frostbitten caravan masters arrive.
- **The Illuminated Margin:** Ritterstadt's tavern. Honest beer, better gossip.

## Credits
Students are paid in **credits**, not money. The total credit limit is **600**. Patrons' reports award **field credit** toward that limit; some letters (such as Sister Halya's letter of field expertise) award **extra credit** outside it.
