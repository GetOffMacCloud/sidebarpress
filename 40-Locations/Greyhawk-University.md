# Greyhawk University — Ritterstadt Field Station

> The campaign frame shared with A Tourist In Greyhawk. Greyhawk University operates a **field station** in **Ritterstadt**, a fragile frontier hamlet on the edge of the Sepia Uplands, founded in 565 CY by the Four Founders as guardians of the Fiend's Embrace. Students earn credits by taking contracts from the Job Board.

## Quick Facts (from the canon source)
- **Ritterstadt:** a hamlet behind a palisade under repair; census 12 of 50 souls (Patchwall 576 CY).
- **Contracts:** posted on the **Job Board** at the Fiend's Rest Public House.
- **Students:** Freshmen (level 3) are undeclared; Sophomores (level 4) declare a Major; Juniors (level 5) may add a Minor. Two semesters a year; a semester turns over after four sanctioned quests.
- **Departments and heads:** Arcane Studies (Magistra Odilia Sternwald) · Natural Sciences (Prof. Berthold Grasmeyer) · Physic & Alchemy (Doktor Agathe Sauerbrunn) · Antiquities (Prof. Casimir Vogelsang) · Exploration & Cartography ([[Magister-Roswitha-Kartner]]) · War Studies & Engineering (Prof. Dietrich Mauerbrecher) · Letters & Cultures ([[Professor-Ilsabet-Federkiel]]) · Statecraft & Commerce (Prof. Aldemar Goldwaage).
- **Visiting faculty:** Professor Wilhelmina Brandt (Antiquarian Wardcraft, City of Greyhawk).

## Credits (Sidebar Press usage)
Students are paid in **credits**, not money. The total credit limit is **600**. Patron reports award **field credit** toward that limit; some sources award **extra credit** outside it (e.g., Sister Halya's letter of field expertise; Historical Society discovery bonuses).
