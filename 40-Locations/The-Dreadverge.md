# The Dreadverge

> A high peat heath between Waycombe and the Clatspur ridges, cut by the Weeping Beck and crowned by the Three Sisters.

## Description
*Black mud between walls of heather, and wind with nothing on it. Far ahead, on a low swell of ground, three upright shapes stand darker than the sky.*

## Notable Features
- **The Turf Track:** a mud road from Waycombe east to the old cuttings.
- **The Weeping Beck:** a fast brown stream, waist-deep and 30 ft wide. The **ford-stones** are the only crossing the undead pack uses.
- **The Cut-Trenches:** a field of 10-ft-deep peat cuttings with turf-stack causeways. This is the tactical set piece.
- **The Cutters' Camp:** three turf huts north of the trenches. The Traft crew died here.
- **The bog path:** north of the mound; Ashlock's escape route.

## Inhabitants
- Kethra's bone pack (while awake)
- [[Brother-Ashlock]] (camp)

## History
*Homebrew.* The name is a Stamtaal corruption of an older Flan word for "the Mother's verge," the edge of her land. Waycombe folk cut turf on its western edge; nobody grazed the Sisters' slope until this autumn.
