# The Duffkyyn Flats

> The Derby ground outside Schwartzenbruin's walls, a city of its own in Richfest week.

## Description
Striped pavilions in clan colors, cook-fires, a sale ring, and beyond it all the long green curve of the course. During Richfest week the Flats are under a Landfrieden, and every blade is peace-bonded at the gate.

## Notable Features
- **The Flats gate** — where the stewards' men tie the red peace-cord and seal it with wax
- **The stewards' tent** — beside the Roodberg pavilion; the colors register and the odds-book; [[Steward Alric Roodberg]]
- **The clan enclosures** — Roodberg blue-over-white the biggest, Meerijder and Vuurzward beside it
- **The stable rows** — forty stalls under one long roof, rows a wagon wide, a lantern every twenty feet
- **The course** — a green mile with a crowd four deep at the rail; the public stand
- **The sale ring** — yearlings at a hundred marks a head; the morning after the Derby

## Inhabitants
- [[Steward Alric Roodberg]] and the stewards' men; the clans in Richfest week

## History
Homebrew. The Duffkyyn runs on the middle day of Richfest every year. The Derby's red cord is older than the company taverns' custom at [[The Red Cord]].
