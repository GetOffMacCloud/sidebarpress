# Aerden

> A slate-and-plaster market town where the last Yatil foothills flatten into meadow, ten miles inland from its landing at Osterschlauss.

## Description
Grey slate and cream plaster; a clan band painted on every doorpost but two. Cheese caves in the hill behind the market, the copper smelters at the foot of the pass, and a green with arrow-chewed straw targets where the militia shoots on Godsday mornings. No wall, no keep, no lord's tower: Aerden has never needed one, and it shows in how people stand.

## Notable Features
- **The Mountainview Rest** — [[Grete Haslau]]'s inn on the north edge of the green: a long table for those who pay by the week, small tables that cost more, and Grete's chalk board above the bar.
- **Erlenhof** — the Halbritter stud at the top of the hill: a grey house with a bare doorpost, paddocks, a stable yard and three wagons.
- **The green and the butts** — militia archery on Godsday mornings; longbows on foot and short bows from the saddle.
- **The well** — where a Roodberg farrier shoes in the open and takes Derby bets.
