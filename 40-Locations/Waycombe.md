# Waycombe

> A highland hamlet of eleven houses, a mill, and a stone sheep-pound in a high valley of the Clatspurs, on the western edge of the Dreadverge.

## Description
*Waycombe is eleven houses, a mill, and a stone sheep-pound built against the wind. Turf-smoke rises from every chimney. To the east, the heather goes brown and then black, and above it the Clatspur ridges stand against the sky.*

## Notable Features
- **The pound:** a 40-ft square of waist-high drystone wall that gives half cover.
- **The mill:** on a millrace off the Weeping Beck. Mother Gest sings the old song here.
- **The reeve's house:** the warmest kitchen in the hamlet, with the tally-stick by the hearth.
- **The bow-butts:** a turf bank behind the pound where every household's longbow is practiced on feast days.
- **The wayside shrine:** a cairn and bowl a mile east on the Dreadverge track.

## History
*Homebrew.* A highland herding hamlet that owes a 40-mark (gp) annual levy to the cantonal council of Clatspurgen. Folk speak Common and Stamtaal. They fear Iuz over the ridges and remember Iggwilv's dead. Its folk song is the last remnant of a warning about the Hound-Mother's barrow.
