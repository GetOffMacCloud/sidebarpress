# The Landing (the Quay)

> Ritterstadt is a lake port, and this is its front door: a timber quay below the palisade on Lake Quag's north shore, the town's road south by water.

## Outside
*Below the palisade, where the town meets the lake: a timber quay with painted Rhennee barges tied along it in summer and hauled up on rollers for winter, nets drying on poles, the tally-house with its stove-pipe smoking, and at the end the smokehouse, where the wind takes the smoke out over the grey water.*

## Inside
- **The quay:** room for four barges and the halfling ferry; the Ritter's fishing tithe is taken here, one fish in twelve.
- **The tally-house:** a stove, a bench, and no visible ledger.
- **The smokehouse:** fish by the barrel, and McCockin cider casks waiting for the next barge south.
- **The hard:** the winter rollers where the barges sleep.

## Proprietors
- [[Baba Yeska Marovna]], Warden of the Landing by the Moot Roll — small, elderly, a knotted cord tally at her waist and a clay pipe she never lights indoors. *"The lake does not take tolls. I do. One of us is reasonable."*
- [[Dorrin Hollin]], the halfling ferryman, Tam Hollin's nephew, who takes the late crossings nobody else will.

## Hours
Dawn to dusk in the open season; the tally-house stove is lit all year.

## What It Offers
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Fresh fish (in season) | 2 cp |
| Smoked fish | 1 gp a barrel |
| "Quag salt" | 5 sp a pound |
| Passage to Traft and the lake towns | by arrangement |
| Toll | 1 sp a hull, 1 gp a cargo |

- **Information:** lake news, Traft prices, who crossed and when.
- **Missions:** crossings; seized barges; what the night barges carry.

## Hooks
- Quagfludt's harbour-reeve seizes a barge for unpaid toll.
- Passage to Traft at half toll — for a night's silence.
- A barge comes in at night and leaves lighter.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> Half the salt is Traft salt sold back to Traft. The other half comes from a pan at the Mire margin on **Node 3**. Twice a season a sealed crate goes north to Iuz's recruiter with a letter from Yeska to her daughter.
