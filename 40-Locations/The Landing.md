# The Landing

> A timber quay below the palisade, four Rhennee barges wintering on the hard, the tally-house, and the smokehouse. The lake is the fastest road south, and the Rhennee own it.

## Description
*Woodsmoke and fish. Barges hauled up on rollers, nets drying on poles, a halfling ferry tied at the end of the quay. Baba Yeska's tally-house has a stove and no visible ledger.*

## Who's Here
[[Baba Yeska Marovna]] (Warden of the Landing) · [[Dorrin Hollin]] (ferryman) · four barge-families

## What It Offers
- **Trade:** smoked fish (1 gp a barrel), "Quag salt" (5 sp a pound), passage to Traft by water.
- **Tolls:** 1 sp a hull, 1 gp a cargo.
- **Information:** lake news; Traft prices; who crossed and when.
- **Missions:** crossings; seized barges; what the night barges carry.

## Hooks
- Quagfludt's harbour-reeve seizes a barge for unpaid toll.
- Passage to Traft at half toll — for a night's silence.
- A barge comes in at night and leaves lighter.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> Half the salt is Traft salt sold back to Traft. The other half comes from a pan at the Mire margin on **Node 3**. Twice a season a sealed crate goes north to Iuz's recruiter with a letter from Yeska to her daughter.
