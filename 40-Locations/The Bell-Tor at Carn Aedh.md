# The Bell-Tor at Carn Aedh

> A hill above Hollen Ford crowned with a ruined Flan sun-shrine, an old oak and a bronze bell twice a man's height.

> [!art]- Map
> ![[SP-GA05 map-bell-tor.jpg|500]]

- **What it is:** a shrine of the Flan priesthood of Pelor, built over the barrows of Cathal Red-Torc and his war-dead. [[The Sun-Bell of Carn Aedh]] was rung once, to lay them to rest.
- **What the Yati say:** the bell "rang the dead to sleep", and nobody climbs the Tor after dark.
- **In SP-GA05:** the Grey Sickle tries to hollow the bell on the dark of Luna ([[SP-GA05 — E4 — Thirteen Tolls]]).
- **Afterward:** a consecrated bell keeps every grave within a mile at rest. A hollowed one is gone, and nothing grows on the barrow field for a generation.

## Canon Notes
Homebrew, consistent with canon Flan barrows in the region.
