# The Red Cord

> A company tavern in Schwartzenbruin's hiring-hall quarter, where every blade is tied into its sheath at the door.

## Description
A low, long common room with a fire at each end, company colours on every beam, and a cord-board bristling with sheathed steel inside the red door. The sign burned into the lintel-beam reads: WOOD IS KORD'S. STEEL IS MURDER.

## Notable Features
- **The cord-board and Ilka's chair** by the door
- **The Kord ring:** benches pulled into a square for formal bouts
- **The clerk's table**, with its bell-glass, where sworn instruments are witnessed until the midnight bell
- **The kitchen door** to the river alley

## Inhabitants
- [[Mutter Ilka Sennhauser]], landlady, and her two bouncers (retired Ernes)

## History
Homebrew. In Schwartzenbruin, companies from opposite sides of a war drink under one roof in winter. The red cord is how this house keeps that peace.
