# The Station House

> The seat of Greyhawk University's Ritterstadt Field Station: a two-storey timber hall beside the Ritter's palisade where credits are paid, majors declared, and commissions sealed.

## Outside
*A two-storey timber hall beside the Ritter's palisade, the University's arms painted over the door: an open book, a cudgel and a quill crossed beneath it. Ink and sealing-wax on the air. On the yard side, a kit store with bog poles stacked like a hedge.*

## Inside
- **The hall:** benches, a notice-rail, the Scholar's Oath in a frame, and the portrait of [[Chancellor Adalwin Adenauer]] at the far end, facing the door, so everyone who enters is looked at first.
- **The Registrar's counting-room:** the credit ledgers; the locked cabinet of sealed files.
- **The Bursar's room:** an iron-bound strong-box with two keys.
- **The Warden's office (upstairs):** desk, correspondence, the brass commission seal.
- **The yard:** the Quartermaster's kit store.

## Proprietor and Staff
- [[Rektor Albrecht Weissmantel]], Station Warden — 58, stooped, ink on his right cuff, a brass seal on a chain. Tired, exact, fair. *"I seal commissions. I do not sign confessions. Which is this?"*
- [[Registrar Hedwig Ammerlin]] — the credit ledgers and the sealed files; keys never leave her belt.
- [[Tobrin Fennelwick]], Bursar — a gnome of 71 with eleven pocketed ledgers.
- [[Lecturer Dagny Holmgard]], Quartermaster — *"Two bog poles a party. Not one."*; her kit store is kept by the student [[Ulf Hadric]].

## Hours
Second bell to sunset. The Warden's light burns later.

## What It Offers
- **Services:** credits paid out; majors and minors declared; grants assessed; expedition kit issued (bog poles, bedrolls, lamp-oil).
- **Missions:** commissions too sensitive for the Job Board; Regents' errands.
- **Information:** the Warden's correspondence (with leave); the credit ledgers.

## Hooks
- The Bursar is 40 gp short at the quarter, and Karet's chits are the difference.
- A Probationer swears the Chancellor's portrait moved.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> The Regents' Commission holds a second brass die of the commission seal. A commission sealed with it is an order the Warden cannot refuse. The sealed files hold AR-001, Hassenrath's second notebook, and the Founders' Compact correspondence.
