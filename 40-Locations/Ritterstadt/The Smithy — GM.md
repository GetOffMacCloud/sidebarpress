# GM Secret

> [!NOTE|ghost] GM Secret
> Bruni is hiding something in the smithy's underground coal store — a large iron coffer, sealed with a lock she made herself and has never opened. It belonged to Dietrich. He told her once that it contained something he'd found in the northern pass long before she knew him, something he'd recovered from a cave above the snowline. He told her to never open it, that it was safer locked, and that if anything ever happened to him she should throw it in the deepest part of whatever river was nearest. She has not opened it. She has not thrown it away either. She thinks about it constantly.

# GM Notes

- **Bruni as information hub:** Because everyone needs ironwork, Bruni sees nearly every adult in Ritterstadt regularly. She knows who is behind on their tool repairs (financial trouble), who orders unusual hardware (potential smugglers or builders of something secret), and who has been in fights recently (weapons with fresh edge damage). DC 12 Persuasion to get her talking; she gossips freely once she trusts someone.
- **The coffer:** DC 18 to pick the lock (Bruni made it). Inside: a wrapped bundle of oilcloth containing a single iron statuette, vaguely humanoid, with no face and jointed fingers. It is warm to the touch regardless of ambient temperature. It is not magical in any detectable way. It is deeply, instinctively wrong. Bruni cannot explain why she can't throw it away. She has tried twice and walked back each time.
- **Stock limitations:** Bruni does not keep a large weapons inventory. She can make them, but she prioritizes agricultural and structural ironwork. Adventurers who need weapons will get them — after the village's orders are filled, which takes 2–4 days.
- **Bruni's strength:** She is statistically a Commoner with +5 to Strength checks and proficiency in improvised weapons (smith's hammer: 1d6 bludgeoning). She is not a fighter. She is, however, very large and very annoyed when threatened.
