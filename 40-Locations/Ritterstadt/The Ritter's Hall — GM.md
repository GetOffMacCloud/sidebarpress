> [!warning|no-title] DM-only companion to [[The Ritter's Hall]]. Kept out of the public wiki.

# GM Secret

> [!NOTE|ghost] GM Secret
> The iron chest beneath the Ritter's dais holds more than tax ledgers and the village charter. Buried at the bottom, wrapped in oilcloth, is a set of old correspondence between Ritter Konrad's father and a merchant consortium from the south — evidence of a land-grant dispute that, if it came to light, could invalidate Konrad's claim to the village entirely. Konrad doesn't know the letters exist. The Vogt does.

# GM Notes

- **Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld** holds court here once per tenday. Disputes, tax assessments, and requests for aid are heard in the main hall. He is fair but not merciful.
- **The Armory:** Holds 8 spears, 6 short swords, 4 crossbows with 40 bolts, 3 suits of chainmail (2 functional, 1 in need of repair), and a battered iron shield bearing an unfamiliar coat of arms — no one in the village knows whose it was.
- **The Grain Store:** Contains approximately 6 months of emergency grain rations for the whole village. The Vogt maintains the inventory and is the only one with a key besides the Ritter.
- **Palisade Defense:** In a siege situation, the palisade can shelter the entire village population. The fighting platform allows ranged attacks. AC 15 for cover purposes.
- **Atmosphere:** The hall smells of woodsmoke, tallow candles, and old dog. Two enormous grey wolfhounds sleep near the hearth at almost all hours.
