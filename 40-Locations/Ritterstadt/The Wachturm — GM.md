> [!warning|no-title] DM-only companion to [[The Wachturm]]. Kept out of the public wiki.

# GM Secret

> [!NOTE|ghost] GM Secret
> The geometric carvings at the tower's base are not decorative. They are a ward-script in an old dialect of Flan, intended to prevent something from entering the tower from below. The script is incomplete — two panels are missing stones, and the ward is partially broken. Something in the dark beneath the tower occasionally makes noise. The garrison soldiers who pull night watch here all report the same thing: a slow, rhythmic sound from the flagstones underfoot, like breathing. None of them have reported it officially. Hildegard knows. She has blocked the trapdoor to the sub-floor with an iron bar and not yet told the Ritter.

# GM Notes

- **Visibility:** On a clear day, a character at the top of the Wachturm can see approximately 8 miles in any direction. They can spot moving figures at 4 miles with a DC 12 Perception check, and identify them at 2 miles with DC 15.
- **The Bell:** If rung, every adult in Ritterstadt will be armed and at their post within 10 minutes. It is only rung for genuine emergencies; a false alarm is a flogging offense.
- **The Sub-Floor:** There is a trapdoor in the ground-floor flagstones, currently iron-barred from above. The stair descends into a low stone chamber that appears to be a pre-existing structure the tower was built over. The chamber contains old iron torch-sconces, a dry stone cistern, and the source of the breathing sound — a large, seamless iron door set into the north wall, slightly warm to the touch. The ward-script above the door is fully intact; above the lintel, in old Flan: *"Let it remember that it sleeps."*
- **The Night Watch:** Soldiers pull 2-hour rotations here. DC 14 Wisdom save after midnight or the soldier on watch begins to feel drowsy — the faint rhythmic sound from below has a soporific effect on anyone who listens to it long enough.
