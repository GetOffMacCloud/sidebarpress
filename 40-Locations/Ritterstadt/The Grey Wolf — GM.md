# GM Secret

> [!NOTE|ghost] GM Secret
> Lotte keeps a book. Not a ledger — a small journal, locked in a box beneath the bar. She has been writing down everything she overhears for twelve years. Names, dates, what was said, what wasn't said, and what she suspects. She has never shared it with anyone. It contains: evidence of the Vogt's skimming, the Ritter's debt conversations with the southern merchant's representative, the identity of the soldier spy (Ewald, who talks too clearly in his sleep when drunk), and several passages about the northern cave that hunters have brought back in fragments over the years. If the party ever got access to this book, it would function as a complete intelligence brief on Ritterstadt. Lotte is not malicious — she keeps it because she believes information is the only real protection a woman without soldiers has.

# GM Notes

- **Lotte as quest hub:** Virtually every rumour in Ritterstadt eventually surfaces at the Grey Wolf. Lotte is the best source of local intelligence available. DC 10 Persuasion (she's friendly); DC 16 to get her to share something from her private observations. She will not share the book under any circumstances unless the party has proven themselves genuinely trustworthy over multiple sessions.
- **The upstairs rooms:** Two private rooms, each with a single bed and a lock (DC 12 to pick). Lotte has a spare key. She does not snoop on guests — but she does notice if they come and go at odd hours.
- **The common room as stage:** The common room fills up on market days (every 9 days in summer, once a month in winter). During full-room events, roll on the rumour table with advantage — two results apply simultaneously.
- **The hearth wolf:** A large grey dog lives under the bar. His name is Grauer. He does not react to most people. He growls at exactly two categories: people who are afraid, and people who are lying. Lotte pretends not to notice this pattern. She absolutely notices.
