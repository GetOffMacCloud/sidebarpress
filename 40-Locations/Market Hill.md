# Market Hill

> The open slope between the gate and the chapel. Fair days fall on the first of each month; the Needfest Fair runs a week. Yusuf al-Rashad's is the only stone stall; everyone else sets boards on trestles.

## Description
*Trestles and awnings on a muddy slope, a brewer's cart, a baker's oven-cart, and the stone stall with an indigo awning that never comes down. The Burrows' doors open into the hill's south face.*

## Who's Here
[[Mathilde Tauber]] (market-mistress) · [[Yusuf al-Rashad]] · the Market Hill tradesfolk: chandler, tanner, baker, weaver, brewer · [[Dominie Anselm Roth]], preaching on fair days

## What It Offers
- **Trade:** cloth and dye, salt, candles, leather, bread, ale (4 cp a mug).
- **Fees:** stall 2 cp a day, 1 sp on fair days (to the Moot).
- **Information:** everything, on market day.
- **Events:** the Needfest Fair; the Needfest bonfire.

## Hooks
- The brewer's cask-split: the Fiend's Rest gets the better ale, and the Grey Wolf wants to know why.
- Yusuf wants a letter carried to Ket and will pay in salt.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> The Pale Ledger's calfskin ledger goes south sewn into Yusuf's cloth bolts. Mathilde has noticed the Suel tailor ships more than he sews.
