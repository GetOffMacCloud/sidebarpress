# Market Hill

> The open slope between the gate and the chapel, where the market sets up on the first of each month and for the week of the Needfest Fair. Around it, the town's tradesfolk keep shops year-round. The Burrows open into its south face.

## Outside
*Trestles and awnings on a muddy slope, a baker's queue before the second bell, the clack of looms from a long low house, and at the top a single stone stall under an indigo awning that never comes down. Downhill, steam from a brewhouse copper.*

## Market-Mistress
[[Mathilde Tauber]] — small, sharp-eyed, a satchel of tallies; knows every stallholder's prices and every household's debts. Stall fees (2 cp a day, 1 sp on fair days) go to the Moot.

## Also Here

## Events
The **Needfest Fair** (one week); the **Needfest bonfire**; fair days on the first of each month.

## What It Offers
Trade of every everyday kind; information — everything, on market day.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> The Pale Ledger's calfskin ledger goes south sewn into Yusuf's cloth bolts. Mathilde has noticed the Suel tailor ships more than he sews. Talg's lamp-oil buyer is the Kettenhaus's man, stockpiling for a winter when the town can't pay.
