# The Smokehouse (Hommlet)

> A burned Temple-era rendering house 2 miles up the High Road, now Deacon Brindlewick's chapel to Zuggtmoy.

## Description
A burned timber barn leaning over a muddy yard, a smoking hall with a brick firepit, and a cellar chapel where six pigs sit in the pews.

## Notable Features
Keyed in SP-GS01 Scene 05 (S1–S6), with alert states.

## Inhabitants
- [[Deacon Mungo Brindlewick]]
- The Brothers of the Rotting Table
- Wat Thistlewood's pigs

## History
Homebrew. Before 569 CY the Temple collected forced tithes of meat and grain here; the Righteous Host burned it on the way to Emridy Meadows.
