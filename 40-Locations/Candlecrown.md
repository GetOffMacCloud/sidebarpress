# Candlecrown

> A lantern-and-toy hamlet of about 60 souls on the flat crown of a tor on the Yeomanry's southern edge, reached by one switchback path.

## Description
> *Inside the gate, the crown of the tor is flat as a tabletop and about four hundred yards across. A ring of iron posts runs along the rim, half of them hung with lit lanterns. Low stone houses with turf roofs huddle against the wind.*

## Notable Features
- **The gate and the warden's hut** (C1): the only break in the drystone wall; hallowed each Needfest as the threshold of "one house."
- **The Stair-head winch** (C2).
- **The workshop** (C3): benches, forge, the elder's office (the miners' map), the wool loft, the coal-store hatch into [[The Old Workings (Candlecrown)]].
- **The hall** (C4): the Carol Lantern over the hearth; benches for forty singers.
- **Mother Sedge's shrine** to Telchur (C5): a windbreak cave; tyrgs won't enter.
- **The green** (C6), **the homes** (C7, twelve households), **Corin's fold** (C8), **the capped mine head** (C9).
- **The lantern line**: 24 iron posts around the rim.

## Inhabitants
- [[Brannoc Tull]], [[Old Mother Sedge]], [[Wenna Tull]], [[Corin the Shepherd]], [[Pell and Ash]], [[Dob Kettle]], [[Tansy Kettle]], [[Hob Fallow]], [[Marra Fallow]]

## History
*Homebrew.* Founded as the mine head of Suel refugees who came through the Passage of Slerotin after the Rain of Colorless Fire. The silver ran out generations ago; the village turned to lanterns and toys. Its Long Night Carol to Telchur hides a verse of a Suel curse.
