# Candlecrown

> A lantern-and-toy hamlet of about 60 souls on the flat crown of a tor on the Yeomanry's southern edge: the party's home base for Needfest, and the village they come back to save.

## At a Glance
- **Population:** about 60, all human (Suel and Oeridian blood mixed for twenty generations), in 12 households plus the workshop's eight journeymen and apprentices.
- **Rule:** no resident official; [[Brannoc Tull]] speaks for the hamlet as elder and master of the workshop. The Yeomanry's spokesmen are a day's ride away in Loftwick.
- **Faith:** [[Telchur]] has the carol and [[Mother Sedge's Shrine]]; [[Fortubo]] has the workshop and the mine. No priest but Mother Sedge.
- **Coin:** standard coin; Keoish and Yeomanry silver both pass.
- **Known for:** carved lanterns, tin toys, painted Needfest figures; the Long Night Carol.
- **This winter:** threat 3 of 5 (the storm, the pack, the voices); economy strained (the gift wagons can't go down).
- **Defense:** the gate-watch (6 guards); a drystone wall; the lantern line of 24 posts; one way up.

## Arrival
**The gate.** [[Dob Kettle]] waves arrivals up the [[The Candle Stair|Candle Stair]] with a lantern on a pole, hauls them in at [[The Candlecrown Gate]] and asks one question: *"You're not from Loftwick, are you? Never mind. Cider's on the elder."* Anyone who looks like they can fight is taken straight to [[Brannoc Tull]] at [[The Lantern Workshop]]; anyone who looks cold is taken to [[Mother Sedge's Shrine]] first. Every stranger at Needfest is a guest of the whole village.

> *Inside the gate, the crown of the tor is flat as a tabletop and about four hundred yards across. A ring of iron posts runs along the rim, half of them hung with lit lanterns. Low stone houses with turf roofs huddle against the wind. In the middle stands a long timber hall joined to a workshop, every window glowing, and from it comes the sound of hammers, a fiddle, and somebody laughing too loudly.*

### 1. THE ELDER'S HOUSE (THE TULLS)
- **Who:** [[Brannoc Tull]] [Fortubo], elder and master lantern-maker · makes speeches · [[Wenna Tull]] [Telchur], lead singer.
- **Knows:** the village, the trade, the charter; from Needfest 2, that Corin is mute.
- **Secret:** hides Corin in the wool loft to keep the feast going. Wenna has written the seventh verse down (her sewing box).
- **Hidden cache:** 40 gp of Needfest takings in a lantern-maker's tin under the hearthstone.
- **Will adventure?** No; Brannoc will hold the hall.
- **Rooms:** the house is semi-private; the party lodges in the hall loft (communal). *Overlooks:* the green.

### 2. THE WARDEN'S HUT (THE KETTLES)
- **Who:** [[Dob Kettle]] [Fortubo], gate-warden, widower · talks to the gate · [[Tansy Kettle]], 7.
- **Knows:** the Stair, the pack (16 and an alpha), "a man standing on the cliff."
- **Secret:** Tansy leaves milk for the cold man (Aurel).

- **Will adventure?** Dob holds the gate; nothing else.
- **Rooms:** see [[The Candlecrown Gate]].

## Hidden Hands
Nobody in Candlecrown is a spy. The village's hidden hands are outside it, and one small one inside:
- **Hrimvald's ear.** The frost-runes on the rim stones and in the mine (M9) listen: anything said aloud near them reaches the immoth. *Tell:* blue scratches in ice that melt under a flame.
- **Aurel Vasch** watches from the cliffs every night and knows who keeps which watch. *Tell:* a figure standing on bare rock, no rope; footprints that stop at the salt line.
- **Tansy Kettle**, unwittingly. She tells the cold man things through the gate when she brings his milk. *Tell:* a clean cup on the outside sill every morning.

## Between Forays
- **Visit 2 (Needfest 7, after any ending but *Silenced*):** the wagons go down the Stair with the party's tin figure on top; every household gives the party one small gift (a goose, a glass bead, a tin badge, a scarf).
- **Visit 3 (spring, 577 CY):** the winch is mended; Ivo Pike has painted a seventh pane (blank but for a single lantern); Aldo Holm has died, or hasn't, and wants to tell the party the rest. If Aurel left under a bargain, a letter from Dark Gate waits at the storehouse.
- **If *Silenced*:** the village keeps Needfest with slates; Osric's ledger is the only voice left. In spring the Yeomanry sends a spokesman to see why the lanterns stopped coming.

## History
*Homebrew.* Founded as the mine head of Suel refugees who came through the [[Passage-of-Slerotin|Passage of Slerotin]] after the Rain of Colorless Fire, with the apprentice who hid a verse of [[The Curse of Binding]] in the village's hymn to Telchur. The silver ran out generations ago; the village turned to lanterns and toys. The miners stopped going down after a Keoish monster-hunter died in the mine seventy years ago, and Aldo Holm's grandfather sealed the shaft.
