# Jaroo's Grove

> The Old Faith's grove at Hommlet's edge, with standing stones and an offering shrine.

## Description
A ring of standing stones around brown grass; a flat stone shrine carved with birds, sheaves and open hands; a moss-covered trough; an empty roost in the largest oak.

## Notable Features
- The offering shrine and its carvings (the First Fruits rite)
- Old Gobbler's empty roost (homebrew)
- The Offering-Heap behind the oak (homebrew, SP-GS01)
- Canon key: Jaroo's grove in [[T1-01 Village of Hommlet]] (T1 conversion)

## Inhabitants
- [[Jaroo Ashstaff]]
- The Offering-Heap (see [[SP-GS01 — Bestiary]])

## History
Canon: T1 places an Old Faith grove and its offering shrine at Hommlet, tended by Jaroo. Old Gobbler, the first-fruits rite and the Heap are homebrew.
