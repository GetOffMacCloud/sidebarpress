# The Station House

> A two-storey timber hall beside the Ritter's Hall, with the University's arms over the door: an open book, a cudgel and a quill crossed beneath it. The seat of the Field Station's administration.

## Description
*Ink, wax, and woodsmoke. A long hall with a counting-room off one side and the Warden's office up a narrow stair. The Chancellor's portrait hangs at the far end, facing the door, so that everyone who enters is looked at first.*

## Rooms
- **The hall:** benches, a notice-rail, the Scholar's Oath in a frame, the Chancellor's portrait.
- **The Registrar's counting-room:** the credit ledgers and the locked cabinet of sealed files.
- **The Bursar's strong-box room:** iron-bound, two keys, one of them the Bursar's.
- **The Warden's office (upstairs):** desk, correspondence, and the brass commission seal.
- **The yard:** Lecturer Holmgard's kit store, kept by Ulf Hadric.

## Who's Here
[[Rektor Albrecht Weissmantel]] · [[Registrar Hedwig Ammerlin]] · [[Tobrin Fennelwick]] · [[Lecturer Dagny Holmgard]] · [[Ulf Hadric]]

## What It Offers
- **Missions:** commissions too sensitive for the Job Board; Regents' errands.
- **Services:** credits paid out; grants assessed; majors declared; expedition kit issued.
- **Information:** the Warden's correspondence (with leave), the credit ledgers.

## Hooks
- The Bursar is 40 gp short at the quarter.
- A Probationer swears the Chancellor's portrait moved.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> The Regents' Commission holds a second brass die of the commission seal. A commission sealed with it is an order the Warden cannot refuse. The sealed files hold AR-001, Hassenrath's second notebook, and the Founders' Compact correspondence.
