# The Covenant
*9th-level oblamancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, B (hit points equal to the slot level × your Intelligence modifier, minimum 2 per slot level; payable by the Tithe)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

This is the whole of the art in one working. Their blood is the army's blood now, and the army is you. Some of them won't be standing when it ends. The ones who are will have won.

Choose up to eight creatures with blood that you can see within range (you can choose yourself). The targets don't need to be willing. For the duration, the targets are covenanted, and the following rules apply:

- **One mind.** Covenanted creatures can communicate telepathically with one another, and each knows the location of any creature that another covenanted creature can see.
- **One body.** Once on each of your turns (no action required), each covenanted creature can move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **One debt.** Once per round, when a covenanted creature fails a saving throw, you can choose a **different** covenanted creature (no action required). That creature loses 15 hit points, and the save succeeds instead. This hit point loss can't be reduced or prevented, and it isn't damage. A creature reduced to 0 hit points this way is dying.
- **One reaction.** When a covenanted creature could take a reaction but has already used its own, another covenanted creature within 60 feet of it can spend its reaction to let the first creature take that reaction.

The covenant ends early for a creature that drops to 0 hit points.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Each casting adds 1 Stain to you, and 1 more if the Tithe for it drops a creature to 0 hit points. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
