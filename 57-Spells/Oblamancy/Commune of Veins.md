# Commune of Veins
*5th-level oblamancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, B (hit points equal to the slot level × your Intelligence modifier, minimum 2 per slot level; payable by the Tithe)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

For as long as you hold it, their blood is one river. They hear each other in their pulse. And you can pour any one of them into the place where another is standing.

Choose up to six creatures with blood that you can see within range (you can choose yourself). The targets don't need to be willing. For the duration, the targets are linked.

- **Shared pulse.** Linked creatures can communicate telepathically with one another while they are within 120 feet of each other.
- **Exchange.** As a bonus action, a linked creature can magically swap places with another willing linked creature that it can see within 60 feet.
- **Your hand on the river.** As a bonus action, you can make any two linked creatures that you can see, and that are within 60 feet of each other, swap places. They don't need to be willing.

A creature can be swapped only once per turn. The link ends early for a creature that drops to 0 hit points.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, you can link one additional creature for each slot level above 5th.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Each casting adds 1 Stain to you, and 1 more if the Tithe for it drops a creature to 0 hit points. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
