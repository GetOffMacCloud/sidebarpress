# Red Thread
*Oblamancy cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, B (a single drop of blood; no hit points)
**Duration:** 1 round
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

You flick a drop of blood at the ground, and a thread as fine as spider silk runs from it to your target's heart. It weighs nothing. It doesn't break.

Choose one creature with blood that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be tethered to the point where it stands. Until the start of your next turn, the target can't willingly move more than 10 feet from that point. The tether ends early if the target is moved farther than 10 feet from the point by another effect, such as a shove or teleportation.

This spell deals no damage.

You can target one additional creature with this spell when you reach 5th level (two creatures), 11th level (three creatures), and 17th level (four creatures). Each target must be within 30 feet of another target.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Cantrips add no Stain. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
