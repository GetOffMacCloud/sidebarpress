# Ligature
*1st-level oblamancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, B (hit points equal to the slot level × your Intelligence modifier, minimum 2 per slot level; payable by the Tithe)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

A surgeon ties off a vessel to stop the bleeding. You tie off the whole man. Red cords burst from the target's skin at wrist, knee, and throat, then draw tight.

Choose one creature with blood that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be restrained for the duration. A creature of Huge size or larger has advantage on the save.

At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st. The creatures must be within 30 feet of each other when you target them.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Each casting adds 1 Stain to you, and 1 more if the Tithe for it drops a creature to 0 hit points. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
