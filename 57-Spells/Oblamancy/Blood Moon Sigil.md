# Blood Moon Sigil
*6th-level oblamancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S, B (hit points equal to the slot level × your Intelligence modifier, minimum 2 per slot level; payable by the Tithe)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

You let the blood fall and it draws itself: a circle sixty feet across, a sigil like a red moon on the ground. Inside it, your people can't be turned. Everyone else is wading.

A sigil of blood appears on the ground in a 30-foot radius centered on a point you can see within range. When you cast the spell, choose any number of creatures with blood that you can see. Those creatures are **warded**.

- **Warded creatures** in the sigil can't be charmed or frightened. A charm or fear effect already on a warded creature is suppressed while it is in the sigil.
- **Every other creature with blood** in the sigil has its speed halved. When such a creature enters the sigil for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be unable to take reactions until the start of its next turn.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 8th level or higher, the sigil's radius increases to 60 feet.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Each casting adds 1 Stain to you, and 1 more if the Tithe for it drops a creature to 0 hit points. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
