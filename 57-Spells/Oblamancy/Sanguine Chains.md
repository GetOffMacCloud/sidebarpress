# Sanguine Chains
*4th-level oblamancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, B (hit points equal to the slot level × your Intelligence modifier, minimum 2 per slot level; payable by the Tithe)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

You draw a line in the air between two bodies, and the line becomes a chain: red, wet, and as strong as the hearts it's anchored in. Where one goes, the other goes. You decide who that's worse for.

Choose two creatures with blood that you can see within range. Each must be Large or smaller, and they must be within 30 feet of each other. Each target makes a Strength saving throw. If both succeed, the spell fails. Otherwise, a chain of blood binds the two together for the duration.

While chained:

- Neither creature can willingly move more than 15 feet from the other.
- If one creature would be moved more than 15 feet from the other by any other means (a shove, a fall, teleportation), the other makes a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it is dragged along and ends within 15 feet of the first creature. On a success, the first creature's movement stops at 15 feet, and teleportation fails.

At the end of each of its turns, a chained creature that failed its initial save can repeat the saving throw. On a success, the chain breaks and the spell ends.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the targets can be Huge or smaller.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Each casting adds 1 Stain to you, and 1 more if the Tithe for it drops a creature to 0 hit points. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
