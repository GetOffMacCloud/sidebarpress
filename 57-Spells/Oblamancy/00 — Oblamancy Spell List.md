# Oblamancy Spell List

The fourteen spells of the **School of Oblamancy**, the second of [[The Schools of Blood Magic]]. Oblamancy binds: battlefield control and coordination, with **no damage, no healing, and no temporary hit points**.

**Rules every spell shares**
- **Wizard only**, and only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. They appear as he gains levels and can't be copied or found.
- **Creatures with blood only.** Constructs, elementals, oozes, plants, and most undead can't be targeted or affected.
- **Blood (B) component:** hit points equal to the **slot level × the caster's Intelligence modifier** (minimum 2 per slot level). The oblamancer can pay it with **the Tithe**. Cantrips cost a drop of blood and no hit points.
- **No consent.** Spells that help a creature never require it to be willing.
- **Stain:** 1 per leveled casting, to the caster alone, plus 1 if the Tithe drops a creature to 0 hit points.
