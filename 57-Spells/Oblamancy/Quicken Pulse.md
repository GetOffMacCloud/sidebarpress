# Quicken Pulse
*Oblamancy cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, B (a single drop of blood; no hit points)
**Duration:** 1 round
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

You snap your fingers in time with someone else's heartbeat, then snap faster. Their heart follows yours. They'll feel it in their teeth for an hour.

Choose one creature with blood, other than yourself, that you can see within range and that isn't hostile to you. The target doesn't need to be willing. Until the start of your next turn, the target's speed increases by 10 feet, and the first opportunity attack it makes doesn't use its reaction.

The speed increase becomes 15 feet when you reach 5th level, 20 feet at 11th level, and 25 feet at 17th level.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Cantrips add no Stain. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
