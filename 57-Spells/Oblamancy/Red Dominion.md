# Red Dominion
*8th-level oblamancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Self (60-foot radius)
**Components:** V, S, B (hit points equal to the slot level × your Intelligence modifier, minimum 2 per slot level; payable by the Tithe)
**Duration:** Until the end of the encounter
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

A battle is a hundred hearts beating out of time. You take the baton. From this moment each of them moves when you say, and not before.

Choose any number of creatures with blood that you can see within 60 feet of you. A creature that is hostile to you makes a Constitution saving throw and is unaffected on a success. Other creatures you choose get no save.

At the start of the next round, **you rearrange the initiative order**: you place each affected creature anywhere in the order you like. Unaffected creatures keep their places. The new order lasts until the encounter ends.

In addition, each hostile creature that failed its save can't take reactions until the end of its first turn in the new order.

This spell doesn't grant or remove turns: every creature still takes exactly one turn each round. It has no effect on legendary actions or lair actions.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a 9th-level spell slot, hostile creatures have disadvantage on the saving throw.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Each casting adds 1 Stain to you, and 1 more if the Tithe for it drops a creature to 0 hit points. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
