# Arterial Tide
*3rd-level oblamancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Self (60-foot line)
**Components:** V, S, B (hit points equal to the slot level × your Intelligence modifier, minimum 2 per slot level; payable by the Tithe)
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

You open your arms and a red wave rolls out along the ground, knee-deep and fast. It doesn't hurt anyone. It just puts them where you need them and leaves the ground slick behind it.

A wave of blood surges from you in a line 60 feet long and 10 feet wide. Each creature with blood in the line must make a Strength saving throw. A creature can choose to fail. On a failed save, you move the creature up to 20 feet in a straight line, either directly away from you or directly toward you (your choice for each creature). This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.

The wave doesn't distinguish friend from foe, and it deals no damage. The ground in the line becomes difficult terrain until the end of your next turn.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the distance you can move each creature increases by 10 feet for each slot level above 3rd.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Each casting adds 1 Stain to you, and 1 more if the Tithe for it drops a creature to 0 hit points. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
