# Stilled Heart
*7th-level oblamancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 90 feet
**Components:** V, S, B (hit points equal to the slot level × your Intelligence modifier, minimum 2 per slot level; payable by the Tithe)
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

You hold your breath, and for one beat every heart you've chosen holds with you. Yours is the only one in the circle you can't stop. Whether your friends are standing in it is a question you settled before you cast.

Choose a point you can see within range. Each creature with blood in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is stunned until the end of your next turn. On a successful save, a creature can't take reactions until the start of its next turn.

The spell doesn't distinguish friend from foe, and it deals no damage.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 8th level or higher, the radius increases by 10 feet for each slot level above 7th.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Each casting adds 1 Stain to you, and 1 more if the Tithe for it drops a creature to 0 hit points. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
