# Blood Bond
*3rd-level oblamancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, B (hit points equal to the slot level × your Intelligence modifier, minimum 2 per slot level; payable by the Tithe)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

You cut a line across your palm and name them, one by one. A red line appears on each of their palms to match. From now until you let go, what one of them can't endure, another will.

Choose up to four creatures with blood that you can see within range (you can choose yourself). The targets don't need to be willing. For the duration, the targets are bonded.

When a bonded creature fails a saving throw, another bonded creature within 60 feet of it can use its reaction to let the first creature reroll the save. The first creature must use the new roll.

The bond ends early for a creature that drops to 0 hit points.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can bond one additional creature for each slot level above 3rd.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Each casting adds 1 Stain to you, and 1 more if the Tithe for it drops a creature to 0 hit points. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
