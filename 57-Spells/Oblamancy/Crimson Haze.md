# Crimson Haze
*2nd-level oblamancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S, B (hit points equal to the slot level × your Intelligence modifier, minimum 2 per slot level; payable by the Tithe)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

The mist is blood, fine as breath, and it hangs where you put it. Everyone inside is blind in the red, except the one whose pulse you tuned it to.

You create a 20-foot-radius sphere of red mist centered on a point within range. The sphere spreads around corners, and its area is heavily obscured. It lasts for the duration or until a wind of moderate or greater speed (at least 10 miles per hour) disperses it.

When you cast the spell, choose **one creature with blood** that you can see within range (you can choose yourself). That creature sees through the haze as if it weren't there.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you can choose one additional creature to see through the haze for each slot level above 2nd.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Each casting adds 1 Stain to you, and 1 more if the Tithe for it drops a creature to 0 hit points. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
