# Veinlock
*2nd-level oblamancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, B (hit points equal to the slot level × your Intelligence modifier, minimum 2 per slot level; payable by the Tithe)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

You close your hand, and every vein in the target's arms and legs closes with it. They stand like a statue with a living face. They can still talk. Most beg.

Choose a humanoid with blood that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed for the duration. Unlike a normally paralyzed creature, the target can still speak, though it can't cast spells.

At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd or 4th level, you can target one additional humanoid for each slot level above 2nd. The humanoids must be within 30 feet of each other when you target them.

When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, you instead target **one creature with blood of any type**, plus one additional creature for each slot level above 5th.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Each casting adds 1 Stain to you, and 1 more if the Tithe for it drops a creature to 0 hit points. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
