# Heart's Spur
*1st-level oblamancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, B (hit points equal to the slot level × your Intelligence modifier, minimum 2 per slot level; payable by the Tithe)
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Wizard *(School of Oblamancy only)*

You press two fingers to your own pulse and push. Thirty feet away, someone's body lunges before they've thought about it. Most of them thank you afterward. You never asked.

Choose one creature with blood, other than yourself, that you can see within range and that isn't hostile to you. The target doesn't need to be willing. If it has its reaction available, the target immediately uses that reaction to do one of the following (its choice):

- Move up to its speed.
- Make one weapon attack.

If the target has no reaction available, the spell has no effect, and the spell slot and Blood cost are still spent.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st.

**Oblamancy.** Affects only creatures with blood. Exists only in an oblamancer's Hemotheurgical Codex. Each casting adds 1 Stain to you, and 1 more if the Tithe for it drops a creature to 0 hit points. See [[The Schools of Blood Magic]].
