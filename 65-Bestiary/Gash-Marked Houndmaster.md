## Lore
Every war-band of the Gash-Marked (see [[Gash-Marked Raider]]) runs on its noses. The houndmaster is the band's tracker and breeder. It raises [[Tunnel Scarhound|tunnel scarhounds]] in the dark from the day they are whelped, and it cuts each pup with the same tally-marks it wears itself, so that hound and master carry one count between them. A houndmaster speaks to its pack in clicks and low barks, and the pack answers to nobody else.

In a raid, the houndmaster's job is captives. The Gash-Marked count the living as well as the dead, and the [[Bone-Tally Chanter|bone-tally chanters]] want names to add to the band's tally. The houndmaster's hounds drag a target down, its net pins it, and its barbed spear makes sure it stays pinned. When the band's warlord, [[Grakh Unscarred]], wants a village emptied quietly, it is the houndmasters he sends first.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** tunnel networks and root-caverns below old forests, and the hill country and villages near a tunnel mouth.
- **Encountered as:** with 2–4 tunnel scarhounds, usually at the head of a raiding party of Gash-marked raiders.
- **Signs:** hound tracks that run up a wall and stop; scraps of weighted net snagged on brambles; a bone whistle left hanging on a fence post as a claim.

## Tactics
The houndmaster stays one rank behind its hounds. It uses Sic Them every round, sending a scarhound to knock a target prone, then follows up with Barbed Spear attacks, which deal extra damage to a prone or restrained target. It nets anyone trying to flee or reach the band's chanter. If its hounds die, it falls back to a tunnel and uses its Stealth to escape. It doesn't fight to the death without its pack.

**Running it:** the houndmaster and its hounds act as one unit. Run them on the same initiative count so the prone-then-spear combination resolves cleanly.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A shepherd's dogs have been vanishing, and some come back months later, scarred and savage. A houndmaster is breeding them into scarhounds, and the shepherd's own best dog now leads the pack. It still answers to his whistle.
2. Travelers are being netted and dragged off a forest road alive. They aren't being eaten; they are being counted. The band's chanter needs a set number of living names before the next new moon, which gives the party days, not hours.
3. A houndmaster offers to sell the party its pack in exchange for safe passage away from its war-band. Grakh sent it: the hounds are trained to turn on their new masters at a single word of Gash-cant.

## Spoils
A houndmaster carries a barbed spear (a spear), a net, studded leather armor, 2d6 sp, and a bone whistle carved with its pack's tally. A creature that blows the whistle has advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks made to calm or command that houndmaster's surviving scarhounds.

The statblock is otherwise unchanged apart from house formatting. The barbed spear uses Dexterity, as a light hunting spear; that is left as submitted.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive:* 45 hp is the CR 1/4 band (36–49). AC 15 is two above the expected 13, which moves it one step, so **defensive CR is 1/2**.
- *Offensive:* two Barbed Spear attacks deal 12 damage a round. Its hounds knock targets prone, so assume one spear in each round gets the 2 extra damage, and Blood Tally adds 3 on about every other round: about 16 a round, the CR 2 band (15–20). +5 to hit is two above the expected +3, which moves it one step, so **offensive CR is 3**.
- *Average:* (0.5 + 3) ÷ 2 = 1.75, which gives **CR 2**. That matches the stated CR, so it is kept. Sic Them adds a scarhound bite each round that isn't counted here, so with its pack the houndmaster plays at the top of its CR.

**IP substitutions:** Ravnica (in the Languages line) removed.
