# Enforcer Minion

*It plants its shield between you and the one you came for, and dies there gladly.*

An **enforcer minion** is hard to hit and protects others. It's the living wall in front of the boss, and every enforcer is one more blow the party has to land before it reaches its real target.

> [!example]+ Enforcer Minion Template
> Apply it with the steps in [[04 — Minions#How Minions Work|How Minions Work]], using the **Minion Table** row for your party's APL. Then:
> - **Armor Class:** the table's AC +2.
> - **Damage:** the table's **Enforcer** column (×0.75).
> - **Reach:** 10 ft. if its weapon allows (a polearm, a hook, long arms).
>
> **Signature (pick one):**
> - ***Interpose (Reaction).*** When another creature the minion can see within 5 feet of it is hit by an attack, the minion makes the attack hit it instead.
> - ***Hold the Line.*** Creatures provoke opportunity attacks from the minion when they move 5 feet or more within its reach, even if they took the Disengage action. When it hits with an opportunity attack, the target's speed becomes 0 for the rest of the turn.
> - ***Shield Wall.*** Allies within 5 feet of the minion have half cover (+2 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws).

## Running Enforcer Minions
- Ring the boss with them. With Interpose, each one is effectively a hit point the party must strip away.
- Put them in doorways and on bridges with Hold the Line; the party has to go through, not around.

## Worked Example: Kobold Bulwark-Bearer at APL 3
Built from the [[Kobold Bulwark-Bearer]] with the Enforcer Minion template, at APL 3. Rebuild it at your own APL with the Minion Table: change the AC, attack, DC, damage, Challenge, and XP, and keep everything else.

> [!example]+ Kobold Bulwark-Bearer, Enforcer Minion (CR 3 minion, 175 XP)
> *Small humanoid (kobold), lawful evil*
> **Armor Class** 15 (scrap plate, tower shield) · **Hit Points** 1 (minion) · **Speed** 20 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 8 (−1) | 9 (−1) | 8 (−1) |
>
> **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9 · **Languages** Common, Draconic · **Challenge** 3 (175 XP)
>
> ***Minion.*** If the minion takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.
>
> ***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the bulwark-bearer has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Slash-Hook.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 slashing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, the bulwark-bearer can pull it 5 feet toward itself.
> 
> **Reactions**
> ***Interpose (Enforcer).*** When another creature the bulwark-bearer can see within 5 feet of it is hit by an attack, the bulwark-bearer shoves its shield into the blow. The attack hits the bulwark-bearer instead.

**How it was built:** APL 3 row; AC 13 + 2 = 15; Enforcer damage 7; the hook's reach and pull kept; Sunlight Sensitivity kept as its one non-damaging trait; Interpose as its signature.
