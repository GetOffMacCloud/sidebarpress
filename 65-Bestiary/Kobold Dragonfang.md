# Kobold Dragonfang

*A broad-chested kobold in scale mail plants a long pike. Its scales shine like a dragon's, and the smaller kobolds around it stand straighter.*

## Lore
A kobold war-crew is a warren's answer to a world of bigger folk: five trades and one plan. A dragonfang leads it, [[Kobold Bulwark-Bearer|bulwark-bearers]] hold the line, [[Kobold Pot-Slinger|pot-slingers]] and [[Kobold Pyrotechnic|pyrotechnics]] throw fire from behind the shields, and a [[Kobold Blistercoil|blistercoil]] carries the heavy weapon. Crews sell their work to a tinker-guild that pays in scrap, powder, and coin. They clear old mines, breach vaults, and fight the fights the guild doesn't want its name on. Where no guild hires them, a fire cult usually will.

Now and then a warren hatches a kobold with the dragon showing: true-colored scales, a deep chest, and eyes that can bear the sun. The warren calls it a dragonfang, raises it apart, and gives it a crew. Its color matches the dragon the warren once served, or still worships, and the crew paints that color on its shields. Kobolds fight harder in sight of a dragonfang, and their blows carry a sting of its element. Every dragonfang knows that a real dragon would eat it without a thought, and that knowledge makes it proud, careful, and cruel.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** warrens, old mines, ruins, and the undercity of any town with a tinker-guild.
- **Encountered as:** one at the heart of every war-crew; a warren rarely has more than two or three.
- **Signs:** shields painted a single color, crude dragon-icons scratched at tunnel junctions, and a patch of stone scarred by breath (melted, frosted, acid-eaten, or stained green).

## Tactics
The dragonfang stands in the second rank, behind a [[Kobold Bulwark-Bearer|bulwark-bearer]], where its 10-foot pike reaches over the shield. It opens with Breath of the Bloodline into the enemies pressed against the wall, then spends its turns piking whoever the bearers have hooked. Heir of the Wyrm is the reason the crew stays within 30 feet of it: every kobold hit carries 2 extra damage. A blue (lightning) dragonfang breathes across its own [[Kobold Blistercoil|blistercoil]] to recharge the rig. If the dragonfang falls, the crew usually breaks and runs; if the crew falls, the dragonfang retreats to report to its patron rather than die.

**Suggested crew encounters** (four characters; adjusted XP per DMG p. 82):

| APL | Crew | Adjusted XP | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 1 bulwark-bearer, 1 pot-slinger | 300 × 1.5 = 450 | Medium |
| 3 | 1 bulwark-bearer, 1 pyrotechnic, 2 pot-slingers | 600 × 2 = 1,200 | Hard |
| 4 | 1 blistercoil, 1 bulwark-bearer | 900 × 1.5 = 1,350 | Medium |
| 5 | 1 dragonfang, 1 bulwark-bearer, 1 pyrotechnic, 2 pot-slingers | 1,700 × 2 = 3,400 | Hard |
| 7 | 1 dragonfang, 1 blistercoil, 1 bulwark-bearer, 1 pyrotechnic, 2 pot-slingers | 2,400 × 2 = 4,800 | Hard |

**Running it:** pick the dragonfang's color to match the crew. Red pairs with fire-slingers and the pyrotechnic; blue pairs with the blistercoil. Heir of the Wyrm applies on each ally's own turn, so remind yourself with a token in the dragonfang's color on every kobold within 30 feet. When the dragonfang drops, take the tokens away where the players can see it.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A warren that has been quiet for a generation has hatched a dragonfang, and its crews are suddenly bold. *Twist:* the dragonfang is white, and the warren's old white dragon, long thought dead, is very much alive and wants its hatchling's warren back.
2. A dragonfang offers the party a truce and a share of a vault if they help it kill a rival crew. *Twist:* the rival crew is led by its own sibling, and whichever survives inherits the warren.
3. A fire cult crowns a red dragonfang as the herald of the Dragon Queen. *Twist:* the dragonfang doesn't believe a word of it and wants the party to help it escape the cult's worship before the cult tests its "divinity" in a bonfire.

## Spoils
The Wyrmfang Pike has a head cut from a dragon's tooth and is worth 50 gp as a curiosity (it works as an ordinary pike). A vial of the dragonfang's blood is a key ingredient for a *potion of resistance* of its Wyrm-Blooded type; an alchemist pays 50 gp for it. The crew's pay chest, usually in the dragonfang's keeping, holds 3d6 × 10 gp in coin and scrap silver.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review.

The dragonfang was written as one of a five-kobold war-crew for Greybeard's Ravnica campaign (the source sat in the Ravnica homebrew monster folder). The statblock itself named no guild or Ravnican place. The source-vault link and art embed were dropped, and the Lore gives the crew a generic tinker-guild (or a fire cult) as its patron in place of a Ravnican guild. The third hook's "Dragon Queen" is the house epithet for the queen of chromatic dragons.

| Change | As submitted | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alignment | unaligned | lawful evil | "Unaligned" is for creatures without moral reasoning; the dragonfang is an Int 10 crew leader, and its crew is lawful evil |
| Armor Class | 16 (scale mail, shield) | 16 (scale mail) | Scale mail plus Dex (max +2) is 16; a shield would make it 18, and the pike needs two hands |
| Scaled Rebuke average | 4 (1d6) | 3 (1d6) | 1d6 averages 3.5 |
| Heir of the Wyrm | "Once on each of its turns, when an allied kobold … hits" (read as the dragonfang's turn, when allies rarely attack) | once on each of the ally's turns | The only reading that does anything outside opportunity attacks |
| Breath name | "Breath of the Line" in the trait, "Breath of the Bloodline" in the action | Breath of the Bloodline | Consistency |
| Wyrm-Blooded | colors and types on separate lines; "Choose … when you add the Dragonfang to your game" | one sentence; "when you place" | House format |
| Damage types | "(per Wyrm-Blooded)" | "of its Wyrm-Blooded type" | Copy |
| Copy | "15ft cone", "it's", "the attacker take", "Dragonfang"/"dragonfang", "(-1)", "Small humanoid" | corrected; "Small humanoid (kobold)" | No rules change |

The Wyrmfang Pike uses Dexterity (+5, 1d10 + 3), although a pike is a Strength weapon. We kept it as a signature exception: it is a custom weapon, and switching to Strength would drop its damage to 6 a hit. *Contributor to confirm.*

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive:* 65 hp is the CR 1/2 band (50–70). AC 16 is three above the expected 13, about one and a half steps, so **defensive CR is about 1.5**.
- *Offensive:* round 1, Breath of the Bloodline on two targets, 42. Rounds 2 and 3, two pike attacks, 16. Scaled Rebuke adds 3 a round. That averages about 28, the CR 4 band (27–32). +5 to hit matches the CR 4 expectation, and DC 13 is one below, so **offensive CR is 4**.
- *Average:* about 2.75, which gives CR 3. That is within 1 of the stated CR 4, so **CR 4 stands**. Heir of the Wyrm, which the formula doesn't count, adds about 2 damage per kobold per round (8 or more with a full crew), which supports the higher figure.
