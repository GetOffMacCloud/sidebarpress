## Lore
The caravan masters say Tharazul is the weather, and they are nearly right. The blue dragon rides at the heart of the great sandstorms that cross the deep desert, and it steers them: across the trade roads, over the oases, and onto the walls of any city that has grown too proud to fear it. When the storm moves on, nothing is left but new dunes and the glass threads that lightning leaves in sand.

Tharazul doesn't loot the cities it buries. It waits. A buried city draws treasure-seekers for centuries, and Tharazul, which burrows and sleeps light, lets each expedition dig deep before it comes for them. Its hoard is four hundred years of other people's excavations: picks, maps, journals, and whatever they found before it found them. Its lair is the first city it ever buried, a palace of fused glass beneath a mile of sand, where the lightning of that first storm still crawls across the ceilings.

Tharazul took the Dragon Queen's favor because it was flattered and broke faith with her within a decade because it was bored. It still carries her gift, a charge of lightning it releases when badly hurt, and it suspects she will want it back. It has begun burying her shrines too.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** deep desert, salt flats, and the ruined cities under the dunes.
- **Encountered as:** alone, usually inside a sandstorm it is steering. Gnoll war-bands terrified of the storm sometimes run ahead of it, driven like game.
- **Signs:** sandstorms that move against the wind; fulgurites, tubes of lightning-fused glass, standing up out of the dunes; an excavation camp with its tents still pitched and no one in them.

## Tactics
Tharazul fights from inside its storm whenever it can, and it uses its burrow speed to rise beneath the party without warning. Its breath is a line, so it spends its movement, and the flight from Wing Attack, lining up as many targets as it can before it breathes. Between breaths, it uses Tail attacks from 15 feet away to stay out of reach and Wing Attack to scatter anyone who closes. When it drops below half its hit points, it releases the Queen's lightning and burrows away. It is vain enough to come back the same night.

**Running it:** show the players the 90-foot line on the map before Tharazul breathes. Seeing the shot lined up is half the terror, and a party that learns to stand out of line has earned it.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A dig master hires the party to guard an expedition to a city buried a century ago. She knows exactly what sleeps beneath it. She owes Tharazul a debt, and fresh treasure-seekers are how she pays it.
2. An oasis city's augurs warn that the storm will reach its walls within a month, and the council wants the dragon killed. The augurs know why it's coming: the council hid a shrine of the Dragon Queen under the city, and Tharazul is coming for the shrine, not the people.
3. A gnoll war-band hunts the party across the open desert, desperate and starving, with a storm on the horizon behind them. If the party fights them, Tharazul eats both sides. If the party helps them, the gnolls know the buried city's back door.

## Spoils
Tharazul's hide yields enough blue scales for one suit of *dragon scale mail* (lightning). Its lair holds hundreds of fulgurites; each serves as the glass rod that *lightning bolt* calls for, and a collector pays 25 gp for a fine one. The hoard is a Challenge 17+ treasure hoard (DMG p. 139), heavy on maps, journals, and the tools of lost expeditions, several of which point to cities Tharazul hasn't yet emptied.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source file sat in a "CR 12 Monsters" homebrew folder and names no campaign or adventure of origin.

**Why CR 17, not 12.** As submitted, Tharazul was rated CR 12, but a 60-damage line breath on two targets and three legendary Tail attacks a round give it CR 20.5 offense, and its defense rates CR 14. That averages 17.25, CR 17. Raising its proficiency to +6 for CR 17 lifts its attack bonus a full step, which would push the average to 17.75 and the CR to 18. Trimming one die from the breath (11d10 to 10d10) brings it back to a clean CR 17. Everything else is unchanged.

| Challenge | 12 (8,400 XP) | 17 (18,000 XP) | The block computes to CR 17 |
| Proficiency | +4 | +6 | CR 17 |
| Attack bonus | +11 | +13 | Str +7 and proficiency +6 |
| Saving throws | Dex +4, Con +10, Wis +6, Cha +8 | Dex +6, Con +12, Wis +8, Cha +10 | Proficiency +6 |
| Skills / passive | Intimidation +8, Perception +10 / 20 | Intimidation +10, Perception +14 / 24 | Proficiency +6; Perception keeps its submitted expertise |
| Lightning Breath | 60 (11d10), DC 18 | 55 (10d10), DC 20 | DC is Con-based (8 + 6 + 6); one die trimmed for a clean CR 17 average |
| Pulse DC | 18 | 20 | Con-based: 8 + 6 + 6 |
| Wing Attack DC | 19 | 21 | Str-based: 8 + 6 + 7 |

| Tiamat's Favor | Trait restating its resistances, plus a pulse "using its reaction" | Reaction: Favor of the Dragon Queen (1/Day); the resistances stay on the defense line only | A reaction belongs under Reactions; the resistances were listed twice; IP |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 212 hp plus 60 for Legendary Resistance (2 uses × 30) gives 272 effective hp, the CR 14 band (266–280). Two resistances and one immunity aren't broad enough for a multiplier. AC 18 matches the expected 18, so **defensive CR is 14**. Over three rounds (Lightning Breath on two targets for 120 plus three legendary Tail attacks for 48, then Multiattack for 53 plus three Tails for 48, twice), it averages 123 damage per round, the CR 20 band (123–140). +11 to hit is one above the expected +10, so **offensive CR is 20.5**. *Average:* 17.25, **CR 17**.
- *Retuned:* defensive CR is unchanged at **14**. With the breath at 55, the first round deals 158 and the average falls to 120 per round, the CR 19 band (117–122). +13 to hit is three above the expected +10, so **offensive CR is 20.5**. *Average:* 17.25, which gives **CR 17**. The once-a-day bloodied pulse (about 28 damage on two targets) isn't counted.

**IP substitutions:** "Tiamat" → "the Dragon Queen" (the trait "Tiamat's Favor" is now "Favor of the Dragon Queen").
