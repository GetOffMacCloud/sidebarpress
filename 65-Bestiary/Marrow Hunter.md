## Lore
When a hunter, a poacher, or a forester dies in the Marrowmother's territory, she doesn't waste the skill. She stitches the corpse back together, fuses its bow into its arm with grey silk and splinted bone, and sets it kneeling in the ferns at the edge of her hunting grounds. It shoots splinters plucked from its own frame, and it shoots at anything that runs.

Marrow hunters are the brood's sentries. They are slow on their feet, but they have the longest reach of any brood creature, and the first sign of one is often a sliver of bone quivering in a tree beside your head. A marrow hunter raised mid-fight by Reknit has only 1 hit point. Like the rest of the brood, it is a patchwork held together by her silk (compare the relic-bound [[Bone Beast Template|bone beasts]]).

## Ecology
- **Environment:** ridgelines, ravine edges, and forest clearings that overlook the approaches to [[The Marrowmother]]'s larder.
- **Encountered as:** one or two, posted where they can see a long way, covering a pack of [[Coursehound|coursehounds]] or an [[Antlerwrack]].
- **Signs:** bone splinters the length of a finger buried in tree trunks; a flattened patch of ferns where something knelt for days without moving; a hunter's cap or quiver left on a stump.

## Tactics
Marrow hunters stay at long range and shoot whoever is fighting the rest of the brood. They don't move unless they lose line of sight. When melee reaches them, they keep shooting, at disadvantage, until they are destroyed.

**Running it:** place marrow hunters before the fight starts, high and far away, and let the first shots come from somewhere the players can't yet see.

## Adventure Hooks
1. The village's best archer went into the woods to hunt "the bone-dogs" and never came back. *Twist:* her family recognizes her bow, now grafted to a marrow hunter, and begs the party to bring it home unbroken.
2. A forester's lodge has been under siege for two days by something shooting from the ridge. *Twist:* the "siege" is a single marrow hunter, and the foresters inside are hiding a much worse secret: the body that drew the brood was a murder victim they buried in the woods.
3. The reeve posts a bounty on "the ridge archer" and wants its head as proof. *Twist:* the archer was the reeve's own brother, a poacher the reeve hanged in those woods, and he wants the corpse destroyed before anyone recognizes it.

## Spoils
The grafted bow can be cut free and still works as a shortbow, though it looks ghastly. The corpse may still carry the hunter's gear: 2d6 gp, a hunting horn, and a skinning knife.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: proficiency +2 with Dex +1 gives +3 to hit; DC 12 is 8 + 2 + Con 2; passive Perception 9 is correct.
- *Defensive:* 32 hp is the CR 1/8 band (7–35), and AC 13 matches the expected 13, so **defensive CR is 1/8**.
- *Offensive:* one shot a round deals 4, the CR 1/4 band (4–5). +3 to hit matches the expected +3, so **offensive CR is 1/4**.
- *Average:* halfway between CR 1/8 and CR 1/4. Rounded up for its 320-foot reach, Collapse, and undead immunities, that gives CR 1/4. The stated **CR 1/2** is within one step and stands. It is the softest of the brood; for a gentler encounter, budget it as CR 1/4 (50 XP).
