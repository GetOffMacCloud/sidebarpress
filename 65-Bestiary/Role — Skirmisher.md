# Skirmisher

*It's never where you swung. It's already behind you.*

A **skirmisher** moves, strikes, and moves again. It goes for the back line, the wounded, and the isolated, and it's gone before the fighter can turn around. Skirmishers make positioning matter.

> [!example]+ Applying the Skirmisher Role
> Adjust any statblock:
> - **Speed** +10 feet (or add a climb or fly speed that fits).
> - **AC** +1.
> - **Hit Points** −10%.
> - **Damage** unchanged, but see Charge.
>
> **Signature traits (pick one or two):**
> - ***Hit and Run.*** The skirmisher doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when it moves out of the reach of a creature it attacked this turn.
> - ***Charge.*** If the skirmisher moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a melee attack on the same turn, the attack deals one extra damage die.
> - ***Nimble Escape.*** The skirmisher can take the Disengage or Dash action as a bonus action on each of its turns.
> - ***Pack Tactics.*** The skirmisher has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of its allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
>
> **CR:** usually **unchanged**. Mobility isn't counted by the DMG formula, but Pack Tactics or Charge can push offense up by one step on a creature that attacks often.

## Running a Skirmisher
- Go around the enforcers to the wizard, the cleric, and the archer.
- Use terrain: walls (climbers), gaps, ledges, and anything that slows pursuit.
- Skirmishers flee at half hit points and come back from a different side.

## Skirmisher Minions
A skirmisher minion keeps **Hit and Run**. A pack of them darts in, bites, and pulls out, so the party has to chase or hold position rather than surround them.

> [!example]+ Iron-Marked Wolf Minion (CR 1 minion, 50 XP)
> *Medium beast, unaligned*
> **Armor Class** 14 (natural armor) · **Hit Points** 1 (minion) · **Speed** 50 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 16 (+3) | 15 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 3 (−4) | 13 (+1) | 6 (−2) |
>
> **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +4
> **Senses** passive Perception 13 · **Languages** understands Common but can't speak · **Challenge** 1 (50 XP)
>
> ***Minion.*** If the wolf takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.
>
> ***Hit and Run (Skirmisher).*** The wolf doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when it moves out of the reach of a creature it attacked this turn.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

Built from the [[Iron-Marked Wolf]]: 1 hit point, Minion and Hit and Run added, Bound Vitality, Iron Mark, and Pack Tactics dropped (the sire-linked traits belong to the full wolf and [[Sorrowbay, the Chained Sire|Sorrowbay]]), and the bite set to the CR 1 fixed damage.

## Design Notes
Skirmishers pay a little HP for mobility, which the DMG formula doesn't price. Charge works like the boar's and stag's charges elsewhere in this book: count it in the first round of the three-round damage model. Pack Tactics adds about a quarter to the hit rate, which on a creature with several attacks is worth one offensive step.
