## Lore
Boars were sacred to many old hunting peoples, and their bones went into the barrows beside chieftains as often as hounds did. Raised as bone beasts, they keep the boar's famous refusal to die. A bone boar with its ribs smashed in keeps charging until its spine is broken.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** oak woods, hill barrows, the edges of old hunting grounds.
- **Encountered as:** one or two among a pack of hounds, or a sounder of three to five on its own.
- **Signs:** rooted-up earth with nothing eaten, saplings snapped at knee height, and tusk-bands of green bronze.

## Tactics
Boars charge the nearest target in a straight line, every time, and they keep coming. Relentless means the first killing blow usually doesn't finish one. Warn the table with the narration when it happens.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A lord's annual boar hunt flushes a quarry that doesn't bleed.
2. Woodcutters find a chieftain's barrow torn open from the *inside*.
3. A druid asks the party to put down a bone boar that is guarding a grove she needs, without harming the grove.

## Spoils
Tusk-bands are worth 5 gp each in bronze. A pair of intact tusks is worth 10 gp to a carver.
