# Enforcer

*It plants its feet between you and the one you came for, and it does not move.*

An **enforcer** holds ground and protects its allies. It's hard to hit, hard to get past, and punishes anyone who ignores it. Enforcers make the party fight the battle the monsters want: through the wall, not around it.

> [!example]+ Applying the Enforcer Role
> Adjust any statblock:
> - **AC** +2 (heavier armor or a shield).
> - **Hit Points** +10%.
> - **Damage** −25% on its main attack.
> - **Reach** 10 ft. is a strong option (a polearm, a hook, long arms).
>
> **Signature traits (pick one or two):**
> - ***Hold the Line.*** Creatures provoke opportunity attacks from the enforcer when they move 5 feet or more while within its reach, even if they took the Disengage action. When the enforcer hits with an opportunity attack, the target's speed becomes 0 for the rest of the turn.
> - ***Interpose (Reaction).*** When another creature the enforcer can see within 5 feet of it is hit by an attack, the enforcer makes the attack hit it instead.
> - ***Challenge.*** When the enforcer hits a creature, the target has disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than the enforcer until the end of its next turn.
> - ***Shield Wall.*** Allies within 5 feet of the enforcer have half cover (+2 AC and Dexterity saves).
>
> **CR:** usually **unchanged**. The higher AC raises defense by one step, and the lower damage lowers offense by about one. Recheck with the DMG (p. 274).

## Running an Enforcer
- Put it in the doorway, on the bridge, or in front of the leader or the artillery.
- Use Challenge on whoever is hitting hardest; use Interpose to save the leader.
- Enforcers don't chase. If the party goes around, they shift to cover the next most valuable ally.

## Enforcer Minions
An enforcer minion keeps **Interpose**. It throws itself in front of a blow meant for an ally and dies doing it, which turns every minion into a hit point the party must chew through to reach the boss.

> *Small humanoid (kobold), lawful evil*
> **Armor Class** 16 (scrap plate, tower shield) · **Hit Points** 1 (minion) · **Speed** 20 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 8 (−1) | 9 (−1) | 8 (−1) |
>
> **Skills** Athletics +3
> **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9 · **Languages** Common, Draconic · **Challenge** 1 (50 XP)
>
> ***Minion.*** If the bulwark-bearer takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.
>
> ***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the bulwark-bearer has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Slash-Hook.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 slashing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, the bulwark-bearer can pull it 5 feet toward itself.
>
> **Reactions**
> ***Interpose (Enforcer).*** When another creature the bulwark-bearer can see within 5 feet of it is hit by an attack, the bulwark-bearer shoves its shield into the blow. The attack hits the bulwark-bearer instead.

Built from the [[Kobold Bulwark-Bearer]]: 1 hit point, Minion added, Multiattack, Shield Bash, Bulwark, Pack Tactics, Sentinel Hook, and Wyrm-Plated Bulwark dropped, Interpose kept as its signature, and the hook set to the CR 1 fixed damage.

## Design Notes
Enforcers shift damage from offense to defense. +2 AC is one defensive step; −25% damage is about one offensive step down. Interpose doesn't change CR on a full creature, but on minions it effectively gives the boss extra hit points, so count each enforcer minion near a boss as worth its full XP even if it never attacks.
