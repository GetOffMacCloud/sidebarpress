## Lore
In the deepest pits of Hell, where the damned are punished, something has to clear away the screaming. The hellborne are the beasts that do it. They are vast, hound-shaped fiends that feed on fear itself, and they grow fat on the terror of souls that can't die. A hellborne doesn't need to eat flesh. It eats the dread in a creature's heart, and it bites only to make more.

Hellborne are rarely seen on the Material Plane, because nothing there is frightened enough to feed them for long. When one does come through (loosed by a careless summoner, sent as a punishment, or escaped through a rift), it hunts in a frenzy. It terrifies whole towns at a time, and it lingers wherever the fear is thickest. Folk who survive a hellborne describe the howl more than the beast. It carries the voices of everyone it has fed on, and those voices are still screaming.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** volcanic mountains, deep caverns, and ruins near planar rifts; anywhere a hole has opened into Hell, or someone has dug one.
- **Encountered as:** alone. Hellborne won't tolerate one another, and the larger one eats the smaller one's fear until it dies.
- **Signs:** a howl heard for miles on a still night; townsfolk too frightened to leave their houses or to explain why; deep claw-marks high on cliff faces; and cattle found dead without a mark on them, their eyes wide open.

## Tactics
The hellborne opens with Howl of the Pit to frighten the whole party. From then on, each of its turns starts with Devour Dread on the most frightened creature, which heals it, shakes off any control effect, and lets it make an extra Claw attack. It uses Taste the Air to keep a fresh frightened target ready, and Pounce to reach whoever is fleeing. It goes Reckless whenever its enemies are frightened, since frightened creatures can't easily take advantage of it. It flees only when no one around it is afraid anymore.

**Running it:** the fight is about fear. Track frightened characters openly, and let the players feel the relief when Devour Dread leaves them unafraid, knowing the beast is stronger for it.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A town has gone silent, and its people won't leave their homes. A hellborne has made its den in the hills above, feeding on the town night after night. The town's priest summoned it, trying to terrify his flock back into faith.
2. A devil offers the party a bargain: bring back a hellborne that escaped from its pit, alive. The hellborne wasn't escaping punishment; it fled from something in Hell that frightened even it.
3. A warlord rides at the head of an army no one will fight, because a hellborne runs at his side. The warlord isn't its master. It's feeding on his fear, and it's using him to find more.

## Spoils
A hellborne's throat, cured and fitted with brass, can be made into a *horn of blasting* by a skilled crafter. Its fangs are worth 500 gp each to an alchemist or a warlock (1d4 are intact). Its den holds the gear of everyone who fled into it: 2d6 × 100 gp in coin and gear, and one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table F.

Statblock unchanged apart from house formatting and the copy fixes above. The DC 18 Wisdom saves are Strength-based (8 + 5 + 5), which is unusual for fear effects but matches an ability, so it stays.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted (and now, since nothing changed):* 212 hp × 1.25 (nonmagical weapon resistance at expected CR 11–16) gives 265 effective hp, the CR 13 band (251–265). AC 17 is one below the expected 18, so **defensive CR is about 12.5**. Over three rounds (Howl of the Pit on two targets for 54 and three legendary Claws for 42 in round 1; then Devour Dread for 16, a Gorged Multiattack for 65, and three legendary Claws for 42 in rounds 2 and 3), it averages about 114 damage per round, the CR 18 band (111–116). +10 to hit matches the expected +10, so **offensive CR is 18**. *Average:* about 15.25, which gives **CR 15**, matching the stated CR.
- The house method doesn't count Devour Dread's 15 temporary hit points a round. Counting them as 45 extra hit points over three rounds would raise its defensive CR to about 15.5 and its final CR to about 17.

**Still worth watching:** the hellborne is lopsided toward offense, mostly because of its three 1-action legendary Claws (42 extra damage a round). If it is overrunning your table, make Claw cost 2 actions, as we did for the Gluttonwretch's legendary attack.

**IP substitutions:** none. "Hell" is used generically.
