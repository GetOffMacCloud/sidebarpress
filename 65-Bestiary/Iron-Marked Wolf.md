## Lore
Long ago, an order of wardens chained a great wolf, the sire of a whole valley's packs, and set him to guard their border. To bind his get to him, they hammered rings of cold iron through each pup's ears and branded its shoulder with the same iron. The marks did their work. An iron-marked wolf fights harder near its sire, shrugs off fear in his presence, and, when he is hurt, bleeds for him: the chained sire can pass half of any wound into one of his marked children.

The wardens carried iron whistles that could quiet the marks and call the pack to heel. The order is long gone, but its marks are not. The pack still runs the old border, still answers to the sire, and still obeys a voice that speaks Common in the wardens' tone of command. Each generation is marked by the last, with iron pulled from the dead.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** forests, hills, and mountain passes along old borders, near ruined watchtowers and forts.
- **Encountered as:** a pack of 3–6, usually with their chained sire nearby, or a hunting party of 2–3 ranging within 120 feet of him.
- **Signs:** howls that come in a strict, repeating order; wolf tracks with a faint drag mark beside them where a chain once ran; rusted iron rings in the bones of old kills.

## Tactics
Iron-marked wolves fight as a pack, pairing up on one target for Pack Tactics and knocking it prone. They stay within 120 feet of their sire so that Iron Mark stays active, and they put themselves between him and the party. A party that ignores the wolves and hits the sire finds the damage spreading into the pack through Share the Wound. Blowing a warden's whistle breaks both links at once.

**Running it:** keep a range ring for the sire's 120 feet. A wolf that leaves it loses its extra damage, and clever players will try to lure the pack out of it.

## Named Example
**The pack of [[Sorrowbay, the Chained Sire]]** (created by GrimmGrixis). The source statblock was written as Sorrowbay's pack, and the chained sire in this entry's traits is Sorrowbay. His Share the Wound reaction and Howl of the Broken Chain both call on iron-marked wolves. The *warden's iron whistle* comes from the same source.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A frontier fort's last warden dies, and his iron whistle is buried with him. Every night, the pack comes to his grave, not to dig it up but to guard it.
2. Ranchers hire the party to kill the chained sire. If he dies, every iron mark fails at once, and the pack becomes an ordinary starving wolf pack with no master and no reason to stay on the old border.
3. An iron-marked wolf walks into a village square and lies down at a party member's feet. The marks answer warden blood as well as whistles, and that party member's grandmother wore the order's iron.

## Spoils
Each wolf carries 2d4 rings of cold-forged iron; a smith pays 5 gp for a full set. A pack's lair sometimes holds a *warden's iron whistle* (wondrous item, uncommon). As an action, a creature can blow the whistle. For 1 minute, every creature within 300 feet that can hear it loses the benefits of Iron Mark and Bound Vitality, and its chained sire can't use Share the Wound. Once used, the whistle can't be used this way again until the next dawn.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive:* 37 hp is the CR 1/4 band (36–49). AC 14 is one above the expected 13, not enough to move it, so **defensive CR is 1/4**. Bound Vitality can cost it hit points when its sire is in the fight, but that doesn't change its own rating.
- *Offensive:* the Bite deals 10 damage, or 13 with Iron Mark active: the CR 1 band (9–14). +5 to hit is two above the expected +3, which moves it one step, so **offensive CR is 2**.
- *Average:* (0.25 + 2) ÷ 2 ≈ 1.1, which gives **CR 1**. That matches the stated CR, so it is kept.

**IP substitutions:** none. Sorrowbay and the warden's iron whistle are the contributor's own.
