## Lore
Gnolls say the Ravening Lord made them from the hunger of hyenas, and that hunger is the only prayer he hears. A warshaman is a gnoll that has heard him answer. The Lord's ichor runs in its veins, black and slow, and drips from its claws as a lash that reaches farther than any spear. Even its *mage hand* is a dripping hand of ichor. Gnolls call such a shaman *Blessed of the Ravening Lord*, and they follow it with a devotion they show nothing else.

The blessing has a price, and the warshaman pays it in flesh. When the Lord turns aside a curse or a killing blow for it, he takes his due from its body. A warshaman that has been blessed too often grows gaunt and hollow-eyed, and it must gorge to survive: every death near it feeds it, whether the dead are enemies, captives, or its own pack. Warshamans hurl their followers into the path of blades without hesitation. The pack doesn't resent it. To be eaten by the Blessed is to be offered to the Lord.

Warshamans speak of the Gnawing Star, a red light the gnolls say rises in the sky before every great famine. The Maw a warshaman tears open is its grin.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** grasslands, scrub deserts, hills, and forest edges, wherever gnoll packs roam; ruined towns they have stripped.
- **Encountered as:** leading a war-pack of 2d6 gnolls, a gnoll pack lord, and 1d4 hyenas or giant hyenas.
- **Signs:** bone charms tied to thornbushes in a long line; carrion birds following a pack at a distance; laughter heard after dark, closer every night; corpses half eaten and arranged in a grin.

## Tactics
The warshaman keeps at least one gnoll or hyena adjacent to it at all times, so it can use Offer the Meat. It opens with Maw of the Gnawing Star on the densest cluster of enemies, then lashes from 15 feet behind its pack, choosing Hunger of the Marrow to knock fighters prone and Hunger of the Gut against anyone being healed. It uses Howling Pack Spirits when the party's melee fighters close in, and Yip and Drive every other round. Gorge keeps it fed: if its pack is dying, it lets them, because every death heals it. It casts *fear* or *blindness/deafness* on a cleric or paladin early, and saves Blessed Toll for saves that would end the fight.

**Running it:** track Gorge carefully. It triggers once on each creature's turn, including the pack's own deaths, and it is easy to forget in a crowded fight.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A gnoll pack has stopped raiding and started herding refugees across the plains. The warshaman is fattening them for a feast that will draw the Ravening Lord's eye, and the refugees' own leader has struck a deal to be the last one eaten.
2. A gaunt, dying warshaman comes to the party's camp under a peace sign: the Lord's toll is eating it alive. If it dies unblessed, its pack loses its leash, and three farming villages lie within a night's run.
3. A bone charm sold at a market as a lucky trinket is a warshaman's ward. The pack can smell it, and the party member who bought it is now being hunted.

## Spoils
A warshaman's bone wards are worth little to anyone but a gnoll. It carries 2d4 ichor-stained bone charms (25 gp each to a collector of curios) and a sealed gourd of the Ravening Lord's ichor, worth 100 gp to a necromancer or as a component for *bestow curse*. Its pack's hoard holds 3d6 × 10 gp in looted coin and gear and one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table B.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive:* 120 hp. Blessed Toll adds 20 hp for each of its three daily uses (expected CR 5–10), less the 13 hp each use costs, for +21. Gorge, counted conservatively as triggering once in a fight, adds 30 (20 hp plus 10 temporary). That gives about 171 effective hp, the CR 7 band (161–175). AC 16 is one above the expected 15, not enough to move it, so **defensive CR is 7**. Necrotic resistance alone isn't broad enough to count. Offer the Meat isn't counted either, but it turns aside one hit a round whenever a gnoll stands beside it.
- *Offensive:* Round 1 is Maw of the Gnawing Star on two targets (72) plus Howling Pack Spirits on two creatures (14), or 86. Rounds 2 and 3 are Multiattack (24) plus the spirits (14), 38 each. The average is 54, the CR 8 band (51–56). +9 to hit is two above the expected +7, a full step up; DC 17 is one above the expected 16, half a step. Weighting the two, **offensive CR is about 8.5**.
- *Average:* (7 + 8.5) ÷ 2 ≈ 7.75, which gives CR 8. That is within 1 of the stated CR 9, so the stated CR is kept. Offer the Meat, the Hunger riders, Gorge triggering more than once, and its control spells all push it toward 9.

**IP substitutions:** Yeenoghu → "the Ravening Lord" (every reference, including Blessed Toll). Gnoll, hyena, and the spell names are SRD and are kept. "The Gnawing Star" is the contributor's own.
