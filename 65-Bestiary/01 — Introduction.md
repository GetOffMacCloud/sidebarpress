# Introduction

This bestiary collects the creatures of Sidebar Press: the things that walked out of our adventures, and new ones made for tables everywhere. Every creature is **setting-agnostic**. None of them needs a particular world, pantheon, or map. Where a creature first appeared in one of our adventures, its entry names that adventure under **Named Example**, so you can see it in its original context.

The creatures share a mood. They are country horrors and threshold things: bargains that came due, households with standards, bridges that charge for crossing, and bells that ring for the wrong reasons. Most of them can be outwitted as well as outfought, and many of them *should* be.

## What's Inside
- **Twenty creatures and one template**, from CR 0 to CR 9, in alphabetical order.
- **Harvested** creatures, rebuilt from Sidebar Press adventures and verified against the *Dungeon Master's Guide*.
- **New** creatures and **remixes** of classic monsters.
- **Folklore** creatures from Slavic, Orcadian, Bavarian, and English tradition, with their sources noted.
- Two appendices: creatures by challenge rating, and creatures by environment.

## Rules
All statblocks use the **2014 fifth edition** rules. Where a creature is built on a *Monster Manual* statblock, the entry cites the page and lists every change. Every challenge rating has been checked against the *Dungeon Master's Guide* (p. 274), and the working is shown in each entry's Design Notes. Where the published rating differs from the formula, the entry says so and explains why.
