## Lore
Gnoll war-bands that follow the Ravening Lord waste nothing, not even their dead. When a giant hyena that ran with the band falls, its priests hollow it out, dry its hide in the sun, and pack its empty ribcage with the bodies of the band's own fallen. Then they call it back up. What rises is a witherling: a husk of a hyena, all leather and bone, with a cage of gnoll corpses rattling in its chest.

A giant hyena witherling remembers the pack and nothing else. It runs at the front of the war-band, and it takes the death of any packmate personally, lunging at whoever made the kill. Killing it is only the first half of the job. Unless it is burned out with radiant light or driven off by holy power, its ribs crack open and the dead gnolls inside crawl out to finish the fight.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** savanna, badlands, desert edges, and the ruins that gnoll war-bands use as camps.
- **Encountered as:** one leading a gnoll war-band, or two or three guarding the band's shrine to the Ravening Lord. It is often accompanied by gnolls, gnoll husks (zombies), and living hyenas.
- **Signs:** a dry, rattling laughter carried on the wind at night; tracks of a huge hyena that leave no scent for dogs to follow; the hollowed carcasses of other beasts left drying on racks around a gnoll camp.

## Tactics
The witherling charges the nearest enemy, bites to knock it prone, and rakes it while it is down. It uses Hollow Cackle as soon as it is recharged, to scatter the party's front line. With Rampage, it bounds from a kill straight to the next target. Its gnoll and undead allies are its trigger: every one that falls near it gives it a free bite, so it fights hardest in the middle of a pack.

**Running it:** tell the party something is moving inside its ribs before it dies. When the Carrion Cage opens, players who saved a radiant attack or a Turn Undead for the killing blow will feel clever.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A caravan master hires guards against "a gnoll pack with a dead hyena." *Twist:* the witherling isn't leading the gnolls. It is hunting them. Their own priests lost control of it, and the band will bargain for help putting it down.
2. A desert shrine to the Ravening Lord is guarded by a witherling whose ribcage holds the bodies of the shrine's last defenders. *Twist:* one of those bodies is a missing scout from the party's own patron, still carrying the map they were sent to recover.
3. Villagers find a giant hyena carcass hollowed out and left drying on a rack outside their walls. *Twist:* the gnolls aren't finished. They need more dead to fill its cage, and they've come to collect.

## Spoils
The war-band hangs trophies from the witherling's hide: teeth, ears, and stolen jewelry worth 3d6 × 10 gp in all. Its dried skull, inscribed with gnollish prayers, is worth 50 gp to a scholar of fiendish cults.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: 14d10 + 56 averages 133; proficiency +3 with Str +4 gives +7 to hit; the prone DC is Str-based (8 + 3 + 4 = 15), and the Cackle's DC 15 matches Con (8 + 3 + 4); 2d8 + 4 averages 13, 2d6 averages 7, and 2d6 + 4 averages 11; Perception +4 and passive Perception 14 are correct.
- *Defensive:* 133 hp is the CR 5 band (131–145). Necrotic and poison immunity is too narrow to adjust for. AC 14 is one below the expected 15, which doesn't move it, so **defensive CR is 5**.
- *Offensive:* Multiattack deals 31 damage a round (Bite 20, Bone Rake 11), the CR 4 band (27–32). +7 to hit is two above the expected +5, which moves it one step, so **offensive CR is 5**.
- *Average:* **CR 5**, which matches the stated CR. Vengeful Lunge, Rampage, and the two zombies from Carrion Cage are uncounted and make it play hard in a pack.

**IP substitutions:** gnoll witherling (a non-SRD WotC creature) → gnoll husk (zombie statblock). The lore credits the gnolls' god only as "the Ravening Lord," the house epithet; the source names no deity. "Witherling" is kept from the contributor's creature name.
