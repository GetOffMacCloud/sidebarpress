*Something long and red uncoils from the treeline at dusk. When it opens its mouth, it speaks with a dead man's voice.*

> ***Blood Scent.*** The bloodfiend has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Wisdom (Survival) checks to find or track a creature that is missing any of its hit points.
>
> ***Bloodcurse.*** A creature that fails a saving throw against the bloodfiend's Bite or Marrow Scream becomes Bloodcursed. While Bloodcursed, the creature regains only half the normal number of hit points from magical healing (rounded down), and every bloodfiend within 1 mile of it knows its direction and distance. If the creature dies while Bloodcursed, it rises 24 hours later as a bloodfiend. The curse has no duration, and destroying the bloodfiend that inflicted it doesn't end it. It ends only if the creature is the target of a *greater restoration* spell, a *remove curse* spell cast using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, or a spell of 5th level or higher that restores it to life, such as *raise dead*. A *revivify* spell returns the creature to life still cursed. While a Bloodcursed creature is in sunlight or within the bright light of a *daylight* spell, the curse is suppressed: its healing works normally, bloodfiends can't sense it, and it doesn't count as Bloodcursed for Rising Blood or Sanguine Feast.
>
> ***Blur of Red.*** The bloodfiend can take the Dash or Disengage action as a bonus action. It has advantage on initiative rolls, and it can't be surprised while it can smell blood.
>
> ***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the bloodfiend fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
>
> ***Radiant Aversion.*** Whenever the bloodfiend takes radiant damage, it loses 1 Blood Rite and can't gain another until the end of its next turn.
>
> ***Rising Blood.*** The first time on each of its turns that the bloodfiend deals damage to a Bloodcursed creature, it gains a Blood Rite (maximum 3). It loses all its Blood Rites if it ends its turn with no Bloodcursed creature within 60 feet of it. Blood Rites grant the following cumulative benefits:
> - *1 Rite (Quickened):* Its speed increases by 15 feet, and opportunity attacks against it have disadvantage.
> - *2 Rites (Ravening):* Its attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 against a Bloodcursed creature.
> - *3 Rites (Apotheosis):* Its Multiattack includes a third Claw attack, and creatures have disadvantage on saving throws against its Bloodcurse.
>
> ***Sanguine Feast.*** When a Bloodcursed creature within 60 feet of the bloodfiend drops to 0 hit points, the bloodfiend regains 20 hit points and gains 1 Blood Rite (maximum 3). This is in addition to the Blood Rite it gains from Rising Blood.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Multiattack.*** The bloodfiend makes one Bite attack and two Claw attacks. If it has 3 Blood Rites, it makes three Claw attacks instead of two.
>
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 23 (4d8 + 5) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or become Bloodcursed.
>
> ***Claw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (3d6 + 5) slashing damage.
>
> ***Marrow Scream (Recharge 5–6).*** The bloodfiend screams with a hundred stolen throats. Each creature in a 30-foot cone must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 21 (6d6) necrotic damage and becomes Bloodcursed. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and isn't cursed.
>
> **Reactions**
> ***Arterial Answer.*** When a creature the bloodfiend can see within 30 feet of it takes damage from any source, the bloodfiend can move up to half its speed toward that creature. This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.
>
> **Legendary Actions**
> The bloodfiend can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The bloodfiend regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.
>
> ***Prowl.*** The bloodfiend moves up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
>
> ***Rend (Costs 2 Actions).*** The bloodfiend makes one Claw attack.
>
> ***Boil the Blood (Costs 3 Actions).*** Each Bloodcursed creature within 60 feet of the bloodfiend must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 17 (5d6) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Lore
A bloodfiend begins as a person who died with a curse in the blood. It rises a day later in the place it fell, long-limbed and skinned in raw red, with a mouth full of other people's voices. It remembers nothing of who it was except the voices of the people it loved, and it uses those voices to call the living out of their houses after dark.

Sages argue about where the first bloodfiend came from. Some say a demon of slaughter seeded the curse into a battlefield, and others say a plague-priest bred it in a pit. What every source agrees on is that the curse spreads. Each bloodfiend hunts until its victims rise to hunt beside it, and a single bloodfiend left alone for a season can empty a valley. Killing the bloodfiend that bit you does nothing to save you. The only cures are powerful magic or sunlight, and sunlight only holds the curse at bay.

That is why the bloodfiend hunts at dusk. It bites in the last light, and then it follows its wounded prey home. It waits outside in the dark until the healing fails, the fever comes, and the door opens.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** forests and the roads that run through them, outlying villages, and graveyards; anywhere people travel after dark and live far from a temple.
- **Encountered as:** alone, or as a "family" of 1d3 bloodfiends that rose from the same victims. A bloodfiend never shares a hunting ground with one it didn't make.
- **Signs:** voices calling by name from the treeline at dusk; wounded travelers who won't heal properly; long claw-gouges high on the trunks of trees; and fresh graves that have been opened from the inside.

## Tactics
The bloodfiend wins initiative and opens with Marrow Scream to curse as many enemies as it can, then spends its first legendary actions on Boil the Blood. From there, it builds Blood Rites by biting and clawing cursed creatures, and it uses Arterial Answer to close on anyone who is bleeding. It focuses on whoever looks weakest, since a cursed creature dropping to 0 hit points heals it and feeds its Rites. It flees from sustained radiant damage or full daylight, using Blur of Red to Disengage and climb out of reach, and it tracks its cursed victims home later to finish them.

**Running it:** keep a visible Blood Rite counter (three tokens) in front of you. The players should watch it build and know that radiant damage strips it.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A village's dead are calling their families from the woods at dusk. The villagers want the party to kill the bloodfiend. Its first victim is the reeve's son, and the reeve, who is cursed too, intends to make sure no one learns he was bitten.
2. A traveling physician claims she can cure the Bloodcurse with a tincture. She can't, but the tincture suppresses the curse as sunlight does, and she has been selling it to the same town for a year while the bloodfiend she keeps in her cellar curses new customers.
3. A knight survived a bloodfiend's bite and was raised with *revivify*. He is still cursed, and he knows it. He asks the party to escort him to a temple with a high priest who can cure him before he dies again.

## Spoils
A bloodfiend's heart, cut out and burned in sunlight, leaves 1d4 ounces of red ash. Any creature that drinks a pinch of it in water has the Bloodcurse suppressed for 24 hours, as if it stood in sunlight; each ounce is worth 500 gp to a temple. Its fangs are worth 250 gp to an alchemist. Its den holds the belongings of everyone it has hunted: 2d6 × 100 gp in coin and gear, and one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table F.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 190 hp plus 90 for Legendary Resistance (30 per use at expected CR 11+) gives 280 effective hp, the CR 14 band (266–280). Its two resistances aren't broad enough to adjust. AC 16 is two below the expected 18, so **defensive CR is 13**. Over three rounds (Marrow Scream on two targets for 42; then Multiattack for 53; then Multiattack with three Rites for 68, with Boil the Blood on two cursed targets for 34 each round), it averages about 88 damage per round, the CR 14 band (87–92). +10 to hit is two above the expected +8, so **offensive CR is 15**. *Average:* **CR 14**.
- *Now:* the DC fix doesn't change the result, since the attack bonus dominates. Defensive CR is **13**, offensive CR is **15**, and the *average* is **CR 14**, which matches the stated CR. Sanguine Feast's healing (20 per kill) and Ravening's expanded critical range aren't counted.

**IP substitutions:** none. The bloodfiend is an original creature, not tied to any setting.
