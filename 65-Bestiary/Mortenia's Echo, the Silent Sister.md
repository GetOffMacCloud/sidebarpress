## Lore
Three sisters once kept the catacomb of a mortuary order, and each held one of its three offices. The eldest, Mortenia, held the Bell: she tolled for every one of the order's dead and marked each body for its place in the walls. The Bell came with a vow of silence, and she had not spoken in thirty years. Her sister Saiyora held the Blade, and her youngest sister Velka held the Veil.

On the night a warlord's company came to strip the catacomb of its grave goods, Mortenia rang the great bell to call her sisters home. She rang until her hands bled, and no one came. When the raiders broke into the upper crypts, she did the last thing her order allowed. She sealed the sanctum with the rite of quiet, lay down on the dais, and bound her spirit into the stone saints so the dead would never be left unguarded. Her Echo believes her sisters heard the bell and chose not to answer. It never learned the truth. That morning, a sexton in the warlord's pay had stolen the great bell's iron tongue, and every toll Mortenia rang that night was silent.

Her Echo still keeps the silence. She marks intruders the way she once marked the dead, and her Final Toll is the bell she rang that night: soundless and cold. Her sisters remember the night differently. [[Saiyora's Echo, the Iron Sister]] died on the stair outside the doors Mortenia sealed, and [[Velka's Echo, the Veiled Sister]] came home too late, carrying something she has never unwrapped.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** the sealed sanctum at the heart of a catacomb, a ruined mortuary chapel, or the crypts beneath an old town.
- **Encountered as:** alone in her sanctum among her statues. Her sisters' Echoes haunt the same catacomb: Saiyora on the stair outside the sealed doors and Velka in the dark passages beyond them.
- **Signs:** bodies with a black fingermark on the brow; stone saints whose heads face a different way each time someone looks; a great bell with no tongue.

## Tactics
**Statue form.** Mortenia stays on her dais and lets the party come to her. She Marks a new creature at the end of every turn and opens with Final Toll once two or more enemies are within 30 feet. She saves her Slams for anyone who reaches the dais. She works toward the Bell Echoes, so she Marks creatures that haven't been Marked yet and ignores those who already have been.

**Changing vessels and forms.** When her vessel drops to 0 hit points, her spirit flies to the intact statue farthest from the party's heavy hitters, and she rises there at the start of her next turn. In a Lesser Statue she makes only one Slam, but she still Marks and tolls. When she drops to 0 hit points and no intact statue is within 60 feet of her, she rises at the start of her next turn in her **Spectral Form**, with 59 hit points. Marks, and Final Toll's recharge, carry over between forms. In Spectral Form she flies through the walls and statues and uses Reaper's Touch on Marked creatures to drain their hit point maximums. She never leaves the sanctum. When her Spectral Form drops to 0 hit points, she is destroyed.

**Running it:** the Mark is the bait. It hurts, but it also gives advantage against her and her statues. Say so the first time you place one, and let the players decide whether to shake it off or use it. Have the Lesser Statues turn their heads toward the party in the first round, so the players can work out that smashing the empty statues ends the fight sooner. When a Mark is carried into her Spectral Form, the creature uses the DC of the form she is now in to shake it off.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A mason hired to restore a ruined mortuary chapel wants the party to clear "a few old statues" out of the sanctum. *Twist:* the mason is descended from the sexton who stole the bell's tongue, and Mortenia Marks him first.
2. Gravediggers in the town above are dying of cold in midsummer, each with a black fingermark on the brow. *Twist:* Mortenia hasn't left her sanctum. Someone carried one of her Lesser Statues up into the town church, and she is reaching out through it.
3. A scholar will pay the party to hear the sanctum's great bell rung once. *Twist:* the bell has no tongue, and only Velka knows where it is. Ringing the bell with its tongue lets all three sisters finally hear the call and rest. The scholar's patron, the warlord's heir, would much rather they stayed awake.

## Spoils
When she is destroyed, the Sacred Statue's head survives the crumbling: a stone saint with a finger to her lips, worth 250 gp to any mortuary order. The great bell (tongueless, far too heavy to carry off) is worth 1,500 gp as bronze. Behind the dais lie the grave goods the warlord never reached: 3d6 × 100 gp in silver funerary masks and coins, and one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table F.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. Mortenia is one of three Echo Sisters, with [[Saiyora's Echo, the Iron Sister]] and [[Velka's Echo, the Veiled Sister]]. Their Lore sections tell the same night from three sides. The source doesn't say where she first appeared.

**Two forms, two CRs.** Her statue form is **CR 12 (8,400 XP)**, and her Spectral Form is **CR 6 (2,300 XP)**. The frontmatter uses the statue form's CR.

**Encounter XP.** The two forms are fought one after the other, never together, so don't apply the multiple-monster multiplier. Her statue form's CR already counts one move into a Lesser Statue. A fight with the Sacred Statue, one Lesser Statue, and the Spectral Form is **10,700 XP**: a hard fight for four 11th-level characters. Each Lesser Statue beyond the first is another body with 80 hit points and AC 16 that makes one Slam, worth about a CR 6 creature (defensive CR 5, offensive CR 7). Add **about 2,300 XP for each one**. The original sanctum, with eight Lesser Statues, comes to about 26,800 XP, which is deadly even for four 15th-level characters. For four 11th- or 12th-level characters, use one to three Lesser Statues.

**Statue form.** Its CR stays at 12, and only its arithmetic and wording changed.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Following the house convention for all three sisters, a feature that brings her back from 0 hit points is counted once, at the hit points she returns with.
- *Statue form, as submitted and now:* 178 hp (180 as submitted), plus one move into an 80-hp Lesser Statue, is about 258. Applying ×1.25 for necrotic and nonmagical weapon resistance (expected CR 11–16) gives about 322 effective hp, the CR 17 band. AC 17 is two below the expected 19, so **defensive CR is 16**. Over three rounds (Final Toll on two targets for 56; Multiattack for 50 plus one Mark for 7; Multiattack for 50 plus two Marks for 14), she averages 59 damage per round, the CR 9 band. +9 to hit is two above the expected +7, so **offensive CR is 10**. *Average:* **13**, which is within 1 of the stated 12, so she stays **CR 12**.
- *Spectral Form, as submitted:* 60 hp × 1.5 (resistances, expected CR 5–10) is 90, the CR 2 band. AC 18 is five above the expected 13, so **defensive CR is 4**. Over three rounds (Final Toll on two targets for 56; two Touches for 42 plus a Mark for 7; two Touches for 42 plus two Marks for 14), it averages about 54 damage per round, the CR 8 band. +8 to hit is one above the expected +7, so **offensive CR is 8**. *Average:* **6**.
- *Spectral Form, now:* 59 × 1.5 is about 89, the CR 2 band, plus two steps for AC, so **defensive CR is 4**. Its damage is 56, then 51, then 58, for an average of 55, the CR 8 band. +7 to hit is exactly as expected, so **offensive CR is 8**. *Average:* **CR 6**.

**Why CR 6, not 12.** As a final phase, the Spectral Form is meant to be short and sharp. With 59 hit points it can't last long, but it hits like a CR 8 creature. Rated CR 12, it would double-count a fight whose real weight is in the statues.

**IP:** none. No setting-specific names appeared in the source.
