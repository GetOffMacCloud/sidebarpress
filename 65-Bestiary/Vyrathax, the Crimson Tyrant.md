## Lore
Most red dragons are content to own a mountain. Vyrathax wanted a kingdom, and it has one: three valleys, eleven towns, and a dwarf-built fortress it burned out and took for its throne hall. It wears the crown of the valleys' last human king hooked over one horn. It holds court every new moon, and once a year it burns one of its own towns to the ground and makes the other ten kneel in the ash.

Vyrathax's government is real. It levies taxes in gold, cattle, and songs of praise. It appoints a seneschal to govern in its name, it keeps the roads free of bandits by hanging them at every crossroads, and its heralds read its decrees in Draconic and Common in every market square. Nothing frightens a peasant more usefully than order. Its subjects are safe from everything except their ruler.

The tyrant is vain past the point of reason, and it isn't perceptive. A good flatterer can lead it anywhere, and each of its last three seneschals tried. It accepted the Dragon Queen's favor as tribute paid by one monarch to another, and it has no idea that she considers it a vassal.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** a mountain fortress and the hill valleys and towns it rules.
- **Encountered as:** on its throne, attended by its seneschal (use the **noble** or **mage** statblock) and a court of kobolds; or in the air over a town that is late with its taxes.
- **Signs:** gallows at every crossroads; prayers to "the Crimson King" in village shrines; one town of ash that no one is allowed to rebuild; heralds in red tabards reading proclamations.

## Tactics
Vyrathax wants witnesses. It fights where its subjects can see, opens with Fire Breath, and then closes to melee with whoever looks most like a leader. It spends its legendary actions on Tail attacks and uses Wing Attack to clear anyone who surrounds it, landing somewhere higher afterward. It won't retreat in front of its court. Away from its subjects, it flees when below half its hit points and plans a public revenge.

**Running it:** its weakness is its pride. A character who kneels and succeeds on a DC 18 Charisma (Persuasion) or Charisma (Deception) check can buy the party a parley. A character who humiliates it in front of its court draws all of its attacks for the rest of the fight.

## Adventure Hooks
1. The tyrant's seneschal secretly hires the party to kill Vyrathax. She plans to blame the valley towns and rule as regent with the dragon's kobolds behind her, and she will be a worse tyrant than the dragon ever was.
2. A town has been chosen for this year's burning, and its people beg the party to lead them out. The neighboring towns, which will be chosen next if this one is spared, have barred the roads.
3. An emissary arrives at the throne hall demanding that its "vassal" kneel before the Dragon Queen's champion. Vyrathax refuses, and it wants a champion of its own to answer the challenge. The party is the best one on offer.

## Spoils
The crown of the last king is worth 5,000 gp, and anyone who wears it has a plausible claim to the valleys' throne. Vyrathax's hide yields enough red scales for one suit of *dragon scale mail* (fire). Its tax hoard is a Challenge 17+ treasure hoard (DMG p. 139), with ledgers recording which families paid and which didn't.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source file sat in a "CR 12 Monsters" homebrew folder and names no campaign or adventure of origin.

**Why CR 17, not 12.** As submitted, Vyrathax was rated CR 12, but a 56-damage breath on two targets and three legendary Tail attacks a round give it CR 19.5 offense against CR 14 defense, an average of 16.75. At CR 17 its proficiency rises to +6, and the average (17.25) still rounds to 17. Its attacks and damage are unchanged.

| Copy | "fly 80 ft., (hover)"; "all creatures within a 10 ft area must make…"; "Vyrathax, the Crimson Tyrant can take…"; "the dragon" and "Vyrathax" mixed | Corrected to house style and 2014 wording | No rules change |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 212 hp plus 60 for Legendary Resistance (2 uses × 30) gives 272 effective hp, the CR 14 band (266–280). Two resistances and one immunity aren't broad enough for a multiplier. AC 18 matches the expected 18, so **defensive CR is 14**. Over three rounds (Fire Breath on two targets for 112 plus three legendary Tail attacks for 48, then Multiattack for 53 plus three Tails for 48, twice), it averages 121 damage per round, the CR 19 band (117–122). +11 to hit is one above the expected +10, so **offensive CR is 19.5**. *Average:* 16.75, **CR 17**.
- *Retuned:* defensive CR is unchanged at **14**. Damage is unchanged at 121 per round (CR 19 band). +13 to hit is three above the expected +10, so **offensive CR is 20.5**. *Average:* 17.25, which gives **CR 17**. The once-a-day bloodied pulse (about 28 damage on two targets) isn't counted.

**IP substitutions:** "Tiamat" → "the Dragon Queen" (the trait "Tiamat's Favor" is now "Favor of the Dragon Queen").
