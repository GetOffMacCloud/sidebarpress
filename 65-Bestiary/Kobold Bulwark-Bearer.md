## Lore
A kobold war-crew is a warren's answer to a world of bigger folk: five trades and one plan. A [[Kobold Dragonfang]] leads it, bulwark-bearers hold the line, [[Kobold Pot-Slinger|pot-slingers]] and [[Kobold Pyrotechnic|pyrotechnics]] throw fire from behind the shields, and a [[Kobold Blistercoil|blistercoil]] carries the heavy weapon. Crews sell their work to a tinker-guild that pays in scrap, powder, and coin. They clear old mines, breach vaults, and fight the fights the guild doesn't want its name on. Where no guild hires them, a fire cult usually will.

The bulwark-bearer is the crew's wall. Its shield is a braced slab of riveted scrap, faced with dragon scale scavenged from a shed skin or an old carcass, and the scale's color is the bearer's pride and its name in the warren: Redplate, Bluewall, Greenback. Bearers are chosen for stubbornness rather than strength. Their job is to plant the shield, hook whatever comes close, and take the blows meant for the slingers behind them. A bearer who drops its shield in a fight is never trusted with another.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** warrens, old mines, ruins, and the undercity of any town with a tinker-guild.
- **Encountered as:** one or two at the front of a war-crew; in larger numbers guarding a warren gate or a guild workshop.
- **Signs:** drag marks from heavy shields, scratched-out arrow slits at kobold height, and flakes of colored scale caught in the gravel.

## Tactics
Bearers plant themselves in a doorway or tunnel mouth with the rest of the crew tucked behind, inside Bulwark's 5 feet. The Slash-Hook reaches 10 feet to drag an enemy into the wall, and the Shield Bash pins it there, unable to back away, while the slingers behind take turns. Anyone who tries to slip past eats an opportunity attack and Sentinel Hook. When an enemy finally lands a blow on a slinger or the blistercoil, the bearer uses Interpose and takes it instead. A bearer faced with the same element as the crew's slingers or breath can stand inside the splash with little harm.

**Running it:** Bulwark is directional. Picture a line through the bearer's square: allies behind it get half cover against anything coming from the other side, and nothing against an enemy who flanks around. Knock the bearer prone (it has no protection against that) and both Bulwark and Interpose switch off, which tells the players how to break the wall.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A dwarven mine wants a tunnel cleared of "a few kobolds." *Twist:* the bearers have sealed the tunnel because something worse is below, and the crew will hold the wall against the party only until the party sees what it's holding back.
2. A dragon's shed skin is stolen from a sage's collection. *Twist:* it was taken by a warren whose bearers need new facing before a war, and the sage sold the location to the warren in the first place.
3. A town guard captain wants to hire kobold bearers as a shield line for a siege. *Twist:* the crew agrees, and its pay is the right to scavenge the attacking army's dead dragon.

## Spoils
The shield's scale facing can be pried off whole: 1d4 + 1 scales of its element, each worth 25 gp to an armorer or alchemist. The shield itself is too heavy and awkward for anyone Medium to use. Each bearer carries 2d6 sp of crew wages.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review.

The bulwark-bearer was written as one of a five-kobold war-crew for Greybeard's Ravnica campaign (the source sat in the Ravnica homebrew monster folder). The statblock itself named no guild or Ravnican place. The source-vault link and art embed were dropped, and the Lore gives the crew a generic tinker-guild (or a fire cult) as its patron in place of a Ravnican guild.

| Change | As submitted | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hit Points | 29 (5d6 + 10) | 27 (5d6 + 10) | 5d6 averages 17.5, + 10 = 27.5 |
| Slash-Hook average | 6 (1d8 + 1) | 5 (1d8 + 1) | 1d8 averages 4.5, + 1 = 5.5 |
| Shield Bash average | 5 (1d6 + 1) | 4 (1d6 + 1) | 1d6 averages 3.5, + 1 = 4.5 |
| Wyrm-Plated Bulwark | "When you add this creature to your game"; extra damage repeated in the trait and in Shield Bash | "when you place"; the trait names the type, and Shield Bash carries the damage | One place for each rule |
| Damage Resistances | "see Wyrm-Plated Bulwark" | "the damage type of its Wyrm-Plated Bulwark" | Copy |
| Copy | "slash-hook", "shield bash" lowercase; "(-1)"; "Small humanoid" | corrected; "Small humanoid (kobold)" | No rules change |

Shield Bash's DC 12 is Constitution-based (8 + 2 + 2), not Strength-based. It matches an ability, so we kept it.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive:* 27 hp is the CR 1/8 band (7–35). AC 16 is three above the expected 13, which moves it up about one and a half steps, so **defensive CR is about 1/4–1/2**. Interpose and Bulwark protect the crew rather than the bearer, and the formula doesn't count them.
- *Offensive:* Slash-Hook (5) plus Shield Bash (4 + 2) is 11 a round, the CR 1 band (9–14). +3 to hit and DC 12 match the CR 1 expectation, so **offensive CR is 1**.
- *Average:* about 0.7, between CR 1/2 and CR 1. That is within 1 of the stated CR 1, so **CR 1 stands**, and the uncounted Interpose and Bulwark support the higher figure.
