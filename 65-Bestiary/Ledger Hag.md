# Ledger Hag

*The old woman behind the counting-desk dips her pen without looking up. "Sign here, dearie. And here. And just a little initial here."*

> [!example]+ Ledger Hag (CR 3, 700 XP)
> *Medium fey, lawful evil*
> **Armor Class** 16 (natural armor) · **Hit Points** 82 (11d8 + 33) · **Speed** 30 ft.
>

> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) |
>
> **Skills** Deception +5, Insight +4, Investigation +6, Persuasion +5
> **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12 · **Languages** Common, Infernal, Sylvan, and any language in which someone has signed her ledger · **Challenge** 3 (700 XP)
>
> ***The Ledger.*** The hag carries a ledger (an object with AC 12 and 15 hit points, vulnerable to fire, and immune to poison and psychic damage). A creature whose signature, or true name in its own hand, is written in the ledger is **Signed**. The hag has advantage on attack rolls against Signed creatures, and Signed creatures have disadvantage on saving throws against her spells and her Collect action. If the ledger is destroyed, every signature in it is void, and the hag is stunned until the end of her next turn.
>
> ***Fine Print.*** Any document the hag drafts contains a clause that makes its signer Signed. A creature that reads the document before signing can find the clause with a DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check.
>
> ***Innate Spellcasting.*** The hag's innate spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 14). She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
> At will: *comprehend languages, disguise self, minor illusion*
> 1/day each: *command, hold person, suggestion*
>
> **Actions**
> ***Multiattack.*** The hag makes two Quill attacks.
>
> ***Quill.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) psychic damage.
>
> ***Collect (Recharge 5–6).*** The hag calls in a debt from one Signed creature she can see within 60 feet. The target must make a DC 14 Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 22 (4d10) psychic damage, and the hag gains temporary hit points equal to the damage dealt. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage. Collect can't target a creature that isn't Signed.

## Lore
Where green hags lurk in swamps and sea hags haunt the shore, ledger hags keep offices. You'll find one behind a pawnbroker's grille, at a moneylender's desk, or as the kindly old clerk at the guildhall who helps you fill in the forms. They trade in signatures. A name freely signed gives a ledger hag a hold on its owner that no court can break. She collects years of life, firstborn children, and, more and more often, the *debts* of the desperate, bought cheaply and called in cruelly.

Every ledger hag's power is in her book. She keeps it closer than her own skin, and she fears fire more than any blade.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** towns and cities, especially the parts where money changes hands.
- **Encountered as:** alone or in a hag coven. A ledger hag often handles a coven's paperwork.
- **Signs:** a moneylender whose rates are oddly generous, debtors who all seem to fall ill on the same day, and contracts written in ink that never quite dries.

## Tactics
A ledger hag avoids fighting anyone who hasn't signed. She uses *suggestion* and *command* to get a pen into their hand first. In a fight, she opens with Collect on the most-Signed target and quills whoever reaches for her ledger. She keeps a desk, a counter, or a hired thug between herself and the party. If her ledger is threatened, she bargains: she'll void one signature to save the book.

**Coven:** a ledger hag can join a coven (MM p. 176) in place of a green hag.

## Adventure Hooks
1. Half the dockworkers in the city have borrowed from the same kindly old woman. This week, they all started coughing ink.
2. A PC's rich uncle dies and leaves them everything, including a debt to a counting-house nobody has heard of.
3. A guild asks the party to steal a ledger from a rival's office. The ledger is very old and very heavy, and the clerk who guards it doesn't blink.

## Spoils
The ledger itself is the treasure, and also the trap. Reading it reveals every name and debt in it, some of them important. Signing it, even by accident, makes the reader Signed to whoever holds it next. Her desk holds 3d6 × 10 gp in coin and promissory notes.

## Design Notes
**Remix of the Green Hag (MM p. 177).** Changes: lawful evil; AC 16; a mental ability array (Int 18, Cha 16); The Ledger, Fine Print, and Collect replace Amphibious, Mimicry, Illusory Appearance, and Invisible Passage; Intelligence-based innate spellcasting; and Quill attacks replace Claws.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274):** *Defensive:* 82 hp is the CR 1 band (71–85). AC 16 is three above the expected 13, so defensive CR is about **2.5**. *Offensive:* two Quill attacks deal 28 damage per round, which is the CR 4 band (27–32). +4 to hit is one below the expected +5, so offensive CR is about **3.5**. Collect adds burst damage, but only against Signed creatures. *Average:* **CR 3**. Against a fully Signed party, with advantage on every attack and Collect always available, she plays like CR 4. Price the encounter up if the party walked in having already signed.
