## Lore
Scarhounds are bred in the dark by the Gash-Marked (see [[Gash-Marked Raider]]), and they have forgotten the sun. They are hairless, grey-skinned, and long in the claw, and their hooked nails find purchase in any crack, so a pack can pour along a tunnel's walls and ceiling as easily as its floor. They hunt by smell and by a close, quivering sense of the air around them that lets them strike what they can't see.

Each pup is cut with its master's tally-marks the day it opens its eyes. The scars are a brand and a bond: a scarhound runs for one [[Gash-Marked Houndmaster|houndmaster]] its whole life, and it fights harder within earshot of that voice. The war-band's [[Bone-Tally Chanter|chanters]] say a hound's scars are part of the band's count, and they sometimes cut more than a tally into them. Under [[Grakh Unscarred]], the chanters have begun cutting tunnel routes into the hounds' flanks, so that a pack carries the band's map on its skin.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** tunnels, mines, root-caverns, and the forests and hills above them.
- **Encountered as:** a pack of 2–4 with a houndmaster, or a feral pack of 3–6 that has lost its master.
- **Signs:** claw-scrapes across a ceiling; livestock pulled down and dragged into a hole too small for a man; a silence where there should be dogs barking.

## Tactics
Scarhounds hunt as a pack. Two or three converge on one target to gain Pack Tactics, and each bite is a chance to knock it prone for the others. In tunnels, they come from above, dropping from the ceiling onto the back rank. A feral pack breaks off once half its number is down; a pack with its houndmaster fights until its master falls.

**Running it:** use the ceiling. A scarhound that attacks from overhead in a 10-foot tunnel tends to be ignored by the front rank until it is too late.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A child finds a scarhound pup in a dry well and keeps it in the barn. Its pack is coming for it, and the houndmaster wants it back alive.
2. Scarhounds are killing livestock miles from any known tunnel. They are a feral pack that broke from its war-band, and the war-band is hunting them too. The enemy of the party's enemy has teeth.
3. A noble wants a live scarhound for her menagerie and pays well for one. The hound she buys has a tunnel map cut into its flank, and the war-band wants it back before anyone reads it.

## Spoils
A scarhound pup can be trained as a guard animal by a creature that spends a month with it and succeeds on a DC 15 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check. Some adult hounds carry tunnel routes cut into their scars; a creature that studies one and succeeds on a DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check can read a route to the war-band's nearest camp.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive:* 22 hp is the CR 1/8 band (7–35). AC 13 matches the expected 13, so **defensive CR is 1/8**.
- *Offensive:* the Bite deals 6 damage a round, the CR 1/2 band (6–8). +4 to hit is one above the expected +3, and DC 12 is one below the expected 13; neither moves it, so **offensive CR is 1/2**.
- *Average:* (0.125 + 0.5) ÷ 2 ≈ 0.31, which gives CR 1/4. That is within 1 of the stated CR 1/2, so the stated CR is kept. Pack Tactics (near-constant advantage) and the prone rider justify the upper end, and with a houndmaster's Hound-Bond its bite averages 8.5.
