## Lore
In a fever year, the people of a hill parish buried their dead too fast, and some of them weren't dead. After that, every grave in the churchyard was dug with a bell: a cord ran from the coffin up through the soil to a little iron bell on a post, so that anyone who woke underground could ring for help. Sister Amarantha Wick of the parish's small burial order was the yard's keeper. She swore before the Great Bell that she would answer every bell in the yard until the ground was quiet.

She kept the oath for forty years. She dug up the ones who rang, and she buried the ones who didn't, and in time she stopped being able to tell which was which. When she died, the vow didn't let her go. The yard still rings at night. Its dead stir and tug their cords, and some of them climb out after their bells (see [[Bell-Strung Corpse]]). Amarantha still walks out to answer every one. Anyone who lies still in her yard, she buries.

Her vow can end in one of two ways. In **the Answering**, every bell in the yard is answered in a single night: each grave is opened or each cord is cut, and then the Great Bell is rung once, so that the ground is finally quiet. In **the Release**, someone with the standing of her order (its last living member, or a priest who has taken its vows) stands at the Great Bell and formally releases her from her oath. Either way, she lays down her spade and goes into her own grave.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** an old churchyard or burial ground whose graves have bells, with a Great Bell hung in a lychgate, belfry, or frame at its center.
- **Encountered as:** alone, or among the bell-strung dead she tends ([[Bell-Strung Corpse]]).
- **Signs:** freshly dug open graves; bell cords cut or neatly knotted; living vagrants found tucked into graves at dawn, stiff with cold but alive.

## Tactics
Amarantha isn't malicious. She is answering a call. She goes first for whoever rang, struck, or broke a bell, because her Bell-Sense tells her where they are. She uses Open the Ground early, so that someone is already in a grave, and Answering Peal when she is surrounded. Tolling Touch shuts off healing, so she spends both touches on whoever the party is trying to keep standing. She ignores anyone who lies still, and she drags the fallen to the nearest open grave. If she drops the first time, she re-forms at the Great Bell, so a party that silences the bell or cuts its rope first wins the fight early.

**Running it:** let the players discover that standing still or playing dead makes her stop attacking them, then show them the catch: she tries to bury them. The Answering and the Release should both be findable through the parish records, a gravestone, or a surviving member of the order. A party that ends her vow should get the full XP for her.

## Adventure Hooks
1. The village's new chapel bell hasn't rung for a week, and its ringer was found tucked into an open grave, alive and blue with cold. *Twist:* Amarantha thought he was a body. She is answering the chapel bell too now, and the next funeral procession will draw her out among every mourner who stands still.
2. A family pays the party to recover an heirloom ring from a grave in Amarantha's yard. *Twist:* that grave's bell has never stopped ringing. Its occupant was buried alive, and the family knew.
3. A young priest wants Amarantha released from her vow, but he can't speak for her order, which is long gone. *Twist:* the order's last living member is his great-grandmother, who refuses to release her. Amarantha's watch is all that keeps the yard's dead in the ground.

## Spoils
Her habit carries 113 small iron bells. Strung across a doorway, they ring for anyone who crosses it, even invisible creatures, and the set is worth 50 gp to a temple. If she is released rather than destroyed, she gives up her ring of keys to the parish ossuary, which holds 3d6 × 10 gp in grave offerings and a *periapt of wound closure*.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source doesn't say where she first appeared.

**The Echo Sisters.** Amarantha has no connection to the Echo Sisters ([[Mortenia's Echo, the Silent Sister]] and the others) in the contributed material. The two share bell imagery and the title "Sister," but nothing more, so this entry doesn't tie them together. Her clearest companion in the contributed set is the [[Bell-Strung Corpse]], which rises from bell-tethered graves and answers the loudest bell.

**The Answering and the Release.** The source names these two ways of ending her vow but doesn't define them. The definitions in Lore are Sidebar Press's, and the contributor should confirm them.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** As for the Echo Sisters, the house counts a feature that brings her back from 0 hit points once, at the hit points she returns with.
- *Defensive:* 91 hp, plus 46 from The Vow, is 137. Applying ×1.5 for nonmagical weapon resistance (expected CR 5–10) gives about 205 effective hp, the CR 9 band. AC 14 is two below the expected 16, so **defensive CR is 8**. Without The Vow (if the Great Bell is silenced first), she has about 136 effective hp, the CR 5 band, and AC 14 is one below the expected 15, so her defensive CR is 5.
- *Offensive:* over three rounds (Answering Peal on two targets for 32, then two rounds of two Tolling Touches for 20), she averages about 24 damage per round, the CR 3 band. +6 to hit is two above the expected +4, so **offensive CR is 4**. Open the Ground, the healing lock, and the deafness aren't counted.
- *Average:* **6** (4.5 with the Great Bell silenced). Both are within 1 of the stated 5, so she stays **CR 5**.
