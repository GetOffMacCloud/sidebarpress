## Lore
A straw double is a scarecrow dressed in someone's clothes and stuffed with a handful of that person's memories, then glamoured to pass for them. Hags make them, and so do hedge-witches who have gone sour and any fey with a grudge against a village. The original is taken first. They sleep, unharmed and unaging, in a loft, a barrow, or the hollow of a mill, while the double walks their life in their place.

Every double is tied to a **binding** that keeps its glamour lit. The binding is a turning mill wheel, a candle that never gutters, a bell rung at dusk, or a hag's spinning wheel. Stop the binding and every double it feeds stands revealed at once as sackcloth and straw.

**Tells** (roll d6 or choose): **1** a stalk of straw at the cuff or collar · **2** it doesn't blink when the wind blows · **3** it repeats the same greeting word for word · **4** it never eats, and its plate goes to the dog · **5** birds won't land near it · **6** it doesn't know about anything that happened more than three weeks ago.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** farmland, villages, any place with fields and a harvest.
- **Encountered as:** one to six in a village, rarely seen together. A maker seldom keeps more than a dozen.
- **Signs:** missing scarecrows, villagers who have become oddly polite, a dog that won't stop barking at its master, and fresh straw on a floor that was swept the night before.

## Tactics
A double doesn't fight to kill. It fights to take. Doubles grab the nearest stranger and drag them toward their maker. When they're cornered, they drop the act and fall limp. A double that has been found out tries to reach fire, because burning destroys the evidence.

## Variants
- **Officer's Double (CR 1/4, 50 XP).** Copies a guard captain, reeve, or someone similar. Str 12 (+1), Con 12 (+1), **AC** 16 (chain mail), **HP** 22 (4d8 + 4). It replaces Club with **Longsword**: *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) slashing damage.
- **A PC's Double.** If a PC fell under the maker's spell, the double uses this block with that PC's Charisma, their voice, and one of their cantrips or weapon attacks (+3 to hit, 1d6 damage). It knows the PC's name, their companions' names, and one secret the PC has told at the table.

## Named Example
**Auntie Chaff's doubles at Hollow Ford** ([[SP-HAL-01-Straw-Men]]). [[Auntie Chaff]], a green hag, fed her doubles from the mill wheel. Captain Voss's double is the Officer's Double above.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A farmer pays the party to find out why his wife has stopped singing at the churn, and why she hums the same three bars at supper every night.
2. Three scarecrows vanish from the same field in one night. By the next morning, three day-laborers are back at work, smiling.
3. A PC gets a letter from home, in their mother's handwriting, that mentions nothing from the last three weeks.

## Spoils
A double leaves nothing behind but straw and clothes. Its "memory stuffing" is a fistful of grain tied with the original's hair, and burning it frees a lingering memory as a vision (DM's choice).

## Design Notes
**Changes from the Commoner block:** construct type; fire vulnerability; poison and exhaustion immunity; False Appearance, Glamour, Shallow Memory, and Unravel; Club replaces the commoner's attack; Grab and Drag added.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274):** *Defensive:* 9 hp is in the CR 1/8 band (7–35). AC 10 is three below the expected 13, which lowers it to about CR 0. *Offensive:* about 2 damage per round is the CR 1/8 band (2–3), and +2 to hit is one below the expected +3. *Average:* between CR 0 and 1/8. **It is published at CR 1/8** because Grab and Drag takes PCs out of the fight, and that is the real threat. *Officer's Double:* 22 hp with AC 16 gives a defensive CR of about 1/2, and 5.5 damage per round at +3 gives an offensive CR of 1/4, so it averages **CR 1/4**.
