## Lore
A beast-warden at a frontier keep once bred war-wolves for its lord. To make the pack obey, the warden used an old chain-rite. Every pup was branded with an iron mark bound to its sire, and the sire was kept on an iron chain in a pit beneath the keep. Whoever held the sire held the pack. For twenty years, the sire's howling carried across the bay below the keep every night, and the folk who heard it named him Sorrowbay.

One winter, the chain broke. Sorrowbay killed the warden in the pit and walked out through the gate with his children behind him. The chain is gone, but the bond isn't. Every [[Iron-Marked Wolf]] still answers his howl, still fights harder when he is near, and still bleeds when he is wounded. He knows it. A wolf that strays too far from him loses the mark's strength, so his pack never strays. The warden's iron whistle, which once quieted the marks, was never found.

Sorrowbay isn't evil. He is a great beast who spent twenty years in a pit and won't go back. He hunts the keep's lands because they are the only lands he knows, and he kills anyone who smells of iron and kennels.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** forested hills and high pasture within a few days' run of the keep where he was chained.
- **Encountered as:** with his pack, usually four to eight iron-marked wolves. He is never far from them, because they are his armor.
- **Signs:** wolf tracks the size of dinner plates among ordinary ones; livestock killed with a single bite and left uneaten; a length of snapped iron chain hung in a tree like a warning.

## Tactics
Sorrowbay lets his pack engage first, then comes in with Pack Tactics. He knocks the strongest fighter prone with his Bite and shoves the next out of formation with Bodycheck. He spends legendary actions on Snaps, or on Call the Pack when a wolf is badly placed. He uses Harry and Prowl to stay out of a surrounding ring. He opens or reopens with Howl of the Broken Chain whenever it recharges, because it is a free attack from every wolf in range. Share the Wound sends half of every hit he takes into a wolf, so killing the wolves first is the party's best plan. When the last wolf falls, Chainbond and Share the Wound go with it. If he drops below a quarter of his hit points with no wolves left, he runs.

**Running it:** say aloud which wolf yelps and staggers when Share the Wound triggers. The players should work out quickly that the pack is his armor.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A shepherd's guild has posted a bounty on the great grey wolf. *Twist:* the bounty's real backer is the warden's heir, who wants Sorrowbay alive and back on the chain, and with him the pack.
2. A keep's new warden finds his grandfather's iron whistle in a sealed kennel-house. *Twist:* blowing it quiets the iron marks, but Sorrowbay hears it from miles away, and he remembers.
3. A party member bitten by an iron-marked wolf starts waking at night to a howl no one else hears. *Twist:* the chain-rite can be reforged to bind Sorrowbay to the party, but someone has to wear the other end.

## Spoils
Sorrowbay's broken collar is an iron band etched with binding runes, the focus of the chain-rite. A spellcaster who studies it for a week learns to suppress the iron marks, as the warden's whistle did. His pelt is worth 400 gp. His den holds the bones and gear of the hunters who came for him: 2d6 × 50 gp in coin and gear.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. His pack uses the contributed [[Iron-Marked Wolf]] statblock, whose Iron Mark and Bound Vitality traits feed Sorrowbay's. The warden's iron whistle comes from that statblock. The source doesn't say where he first appeared.

**Why CR 9, not 6.** As submitted, Sorrowbay hit about as hard as a CR 6–7 creature, but his defense rated CR 12. Chainbond gives him resistance to nonmagical weapons whenever his pack is near, which is nearly always. On top of 149 hit points, Relentless, and Legendary Resistance, that makes him much harder to bring down than CR 6 allows, even before Share the Wound pushes half of every hit into his wolves. We kept every ability and moved him to CR 9, which changes his proficiency bonus to +4.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 149 hp × 1.5 for Chainbond's nonmagical weapon resistance (expected CR 5–10) is about 224. Adding 14 for Relentless (the DMG value at expected CR 5–10) and 20 for one daily Legendary Resistance gives about 258 effective hp, the CR 13 band. AC 15 is three below the expected 18, one step down, so **defensive CR is 12**. Share the Wound isn't counted. His best round is Multiattack (26) plus two legendary Snaps (18), or 44 damage per round, the CR 6 band (39–44). +8 to hit is two above the expected +6, so **offensive CR is 7**. *Average:* **9.5**.
- *Retuned:* defensive CR is unchanged at **12**. He still deals 44 damage per round. +9 to hit is three above the expected +6, one step up, so **offensive CR is 7**. *Average:* **9.5**. This rounds down to **CR 9**, because Chainbond is counted at full strength even though it ends when the last wolf falls.

**Encounter XP.** Sorrowbay and two iron-marked wolves (CR 1 each) come to 5,400 XP. ×2 for three monsters gives 10,800 adjusted XP, a deadly fight for four 9th-level characters. Sorrowbay with one wolf (5,200 XP, ×1.5 = 7,800 adjusted XP) is a hard fight for four 9th-level characters. Don't run him without wolves: Chainbond and Share the Wound are the heart of the fight.

**IP:** none. No setting-specific names appeared in the source.
