## Lore
The maulbone is the carrier of the Marrowmother's brood. She builds it on the biggest frame her larder offers, a bear, an ox, or an ogre, and fuses the leftover femurs into a knotted club where one forearm used to be. It is slow and stupid, and it has one job: to take hold of whatever the hounds have run down and drag it back to her.

It is the only brood creature that deals bludgeoning damage, and it is just as brittle as the rest. A maulbone raised mid-fight by Reknit has only 1 hit point. Like the rest of the brood, it is a patchwork held together by her silk, not a whole beast raised and bound to a relic (see [[Bone Beast Template]]).

## Ecology
- **Environment:** the trails between [[The Marrowmother]]'s larder and her hunting grounds.
- **Encountered as:** alone, following a pack of [[Coursehound|coursehounds]], or set as a door-ward at the larder's entrance.
- **Signs:** a broad drag-trail through leaf litter, with heel-gouges where something was pulled along; and trees with their lower branches snapped off by a club swung at head height.

## Tactics
A maulbone grabs the nearest creature and drags it away from its allies, at half speed while it hauls its load. It doesn't let go to defend itself. It holds on until it is destroyed or its target breaks free.

**Running it:** a grappled character being hauled toward the dark is a strong scene. Give the rest of the party a reason to chase.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A drover lost an ox from his team overnight, and found it three days later, walking the road with a club for a foreleg. *Twist:* he wants it back. It was his best ox, and he believes a priest can "lift the curse" from it.
2. A maulbone has dragged a child into the woods. *Twist:* the child broke free and is hiding in the Marrowmother's larder, the one place the brood never searches.
3. Ogre bones are missing from a famous battle-cairn. *Twist:* the ogres' living kin have come to take them back, and they assume the party did it.

## Spoils
A maulbone's club of fused femurs can be pried loose and used as a greatclub. It is worth 5 gp to a curio dealer as a grisly trophy.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: proficiency +2 with Str +2 gives +4 to hit; the escape DC and the Collapse DC are both 8 + 2 + 2 = 12; 2d4 + 2 averages 7; passive Perception 9 is correct.
- *Defensive:* 37 hp is the CR 1/4 band (36–49), and AC 13 matches the expected 13, so **defensive CR is 1/4**.
- *Offensive:* one Grafted Attack a round deals 7, the CR 1/2 band (6–8). +4 to hit is one above the expected +3, which doesn't move it, so **offensive CR is 1/2**.
- *Average:* halfway between CR 1/4 and CR 1/2. Rounded up for the grapple (a control effect the DPR doesn't count), that gives **CR 1/2**, which matches the stated CR.
