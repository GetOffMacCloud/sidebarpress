# Lurker Minion

*The ceiling moves. Then the scout is gone, and so is the thing that took him.*

A **lurker minion** waits in the dark and strikes first. A pack of them springing from the ceiling in round one is the whole encounter: survive that moment and they're easy.

> [!example]+ Lurker Minion Template
> Apply it with the steps in [[04 — Minions#How Minions Work|How Minions Work]], using the **Minion Table** row for your party's APL. Then:
> - **Stealth:** expertise (Stealth bonus = Dexterity modifier + 2 × the table's proficiency bonus).
> - **Damage:** the table's standard column.
>
> **Signature (pick one):**
> - ***Ambush.*** In the first round of combat, the minion has advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn, and its first hit deals extra damage equal to its normal damage.
> - ***Drop from Above.*** If the minion drops onto a creature from at least 10 feet above it, the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw (the table's DC) or be knocked prone, and the minion's first attack against it this turn has advantage.
> - ***Fade.*** While in dim light or darkness, the minion can take the Hide action as a bonus action.

## Running Lurker Minions
- Place them before the fight: in the rafters, under the water, among the dead.
- After round one, they fight badly. Let them try to slip away and strike again later.

## Worked Example: Tunnel Scarhound at APL 3
Built from the [[Tunnel Scarhound]] with the Lurker Minion template, at APL 3. Rebuild it at your own APL with the Minion Table: change the AC, attack, DC, damage, Challenge, and XP, and keep everything else.

> **Armor Class** 13 (natural armor) · **Hit Points** 1 (minion) · **Speed** 40 ft., climb 30 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 15 (+2) | 15 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 3 (−4) | 12 (+1) | 6 (−2) |
>
> **Senses** blindsight 30 ft., darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11 · **Languages** — · **Challenge** 3 (175 XP)
>
> ***Minion.*** If the minion takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.
>
> ***Drop from Above (Lurker).*** If the scarhound drops onto a creature from at least 10 feet above it, the target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone, and the scarhound's first attack against it this turn has advantage.
>
> ***Wall-Runner.*** Climbing costs the scarhound no extra movement, and it can move along walls and ceilings without making an ability check.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 piercing damage.

**How it was built:** APL 3 row; standard damage 9; the bite's prone rider dropped (Drop from Above already knocks prone); Wall-Runner kept as its one non-damaging trait; passive Perception reset to 10 + Wisdom (no proficiency).
