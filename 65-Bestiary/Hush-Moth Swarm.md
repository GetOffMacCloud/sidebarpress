# Hush-Moth Swarm

*A grey cloud lifts from the belfry. As it drifts toward you, the birdsong stops, then the wind, then the sound of your own breath.*

> ***Moth to Flame.*** At the start of the swarm's turn, if no creature within 120 feet has made a sound louder than a whisper since its last turn and a flame the size of a torch or larger is within 60 feet of it, the swarm must spend its turn flying to the flame and circling it.

The target must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be blinded by wing-dust until the end of its next turn.

## Lore
Hush-moths are grey, finger-length moths that feed on sound. A single moth eats the rustle of a mouse. A swarm can eat a church choir. They nest in belfries, organ lofts, and the rafters of great halls, wherever sound is richest. Over years, a nest drains a place: bells crack and won't ring true, singers lose their voices, and a town grows quiet without knowing why.

Wizards hate them. A swarm that has learned what a spell sounds like will cross a valley to eat the next one.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** belfries, chapels, theaters, old halls, and the quiet woods around them.
- **Encountered as:** one swarm per nest. A large belfry nest can hold three swarms.
- **Signs:** cracked bells, grey dust on the pews, dead birds that look unharmed, and a town where nobody sings in church anymore.

## Tactics
The swarm drifts toward the loudest creature and settles on it. Once it has tasted a spell, it targets the spellcaster first. It is no tactician. A silent party with a lit torch can lead it anywhere, including into a bonfire.

**Running it:** describe the silence spreading before the swarm arrives. Let the players work out on their own that talking draws it and a flame lures it. The fight is the reward for that discovery.

## Lair / Regional Effects
A belfry with a hush-moth nest has these effects within 1 mile:
- Bells ring flat and short, and none can be heard more than 300 feet away.
- Thunder damage dealt in the nest itself is halved.
- Echoes don't return.

## Adventure Hooks
1. The village bell didn't ring when the raiders came, and the bell-ringer swears he pulled the rope until his hands bled.
2. A famous singer has lost her voice before the festival. A bard friend says he saw grey dust on her pillow.
3. A town is plagued by a [[Bell-Wight]] that rises whenever its bell tolls. An old sexton suggests "the grey moths" might be the answer, if anyone can carry a nest up the tower without speaking.

## Spoils
A sack of hush-moth dust (gathered from a destroyed swarm with a DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) check) works as a single-use *silence* effect (a 10-foot radius for 1 minute) when it is thrown as an action. It sells for 25 gp to thieves and to bell-founders, who use it to test for cracks.

## Design Notes
**Original.** A sound-eating swarm built to give casters a problem and the whole party a puzzle: stay silent and use fire.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274):** *Defensive:* 45 hp doubled for broad resistances at expected CR 1–4 gives 90 effective hp, which is the CR 2 band (86–100). AC 12 is one below the expected 13, so defensive CR is about **1.5**. *Offensive:* 10 damage per round is the CR 1 band (9–14). +4 to hit is one above the expected +3, so offensive CR is about **1.5**. *Average:* 1.5, rounded to **CR 2**. The area silence, which shuts down verbal spellcasting entirely, isn't counted by the formula and justifies rounding up.
