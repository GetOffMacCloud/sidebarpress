# Brute Minion

*It doesn't dodge. It doesn't need to. By the time it falls, it has already swung.*

A **brute minion** hits harder than any other minion and dies just as easily. A pack of them is a wall of axes: the party has to kill them fast, or eat the damage.

> [!example]+ Brute Minion Template
> Apply it with the steps in [[04 — Minions#How Minions Work|How Minions Work]], using the **Minion Table** row for your party's APL. Then:
> - **Armor Class:** the table's AC −1.
> - **Damage:** the table's **Brute** column (×1.5).
>
> **Signature (pick one):**
> - ***Death-Blow.*** When the minion drops to 0 hit points from a melee attack, it can make one attack against the attacker before it dies.
> - ***Overwhelm.*** When the minion hits a creature with a melee attack, the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw (the table's DC) or be pushed 10 feet away or knocked prone (the minion's choice).
> - ***Reckless.*** The minion has advantage on its melee attack rolls, but attack rolls against it have advantage.

**In Fantasy Grounds:** generic brute minions for every APL 1–20 are in [[Brute Minion — FGU Templates]].

## Running Brute Minions
- Send them at the front line in a wave; every fighter who kills one may still take the Death-Blow.
- Pair them with an enforcer or leader that stays alive, so the party can't just mow through to the boss.

## Worked Example: Gash-Marked Raider at APL 3
Built from the [[Gash-Marked Raider]] with the Brute Minion template, at APL 3. Rebuild it at your own APL with the Minion Table: change the AC, attack, DC, damage, Challenge, and XP, and keep everything else.

> [!example]+ Gash-Marked Raider, Brute Minion (CR 3 minion, 175 XP)
> *Medium humanoid (orc), chaotic evil*
> **Armor Class** 12 (hide armor) · **Hit Points** 1 (minion) · **Speed** 30 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 7 (−2) | 11 (+0) | 10 (+0) |
>
> **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10 · **Languages** Orc, Gash-cant · **Challenge** 3 (175 XP)
>
> ***Minion.*** If the minion takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.
>
> ***Death-Blow (Brute).*** When the raider drops to 0 hit points from a melee attack, it can make one Gash-Axe attack against the attacker before it dies.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Gash-Axe.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 slashing damage.

**How it was built:** APL 3 row (AC 13, +4, DC 13, Brute damage 14); AC 13 − 1 = 12; the raider's axe, darkvision, and languages; Aggressive and Blood Tally dropped; Death-Blow as its signature.
