## Lore
Velka was the youngest of three sisters who kept a mortuary order's catacomb, and she held its Veil. She was the order's hunter, veiled so that the grave-robbers she tracked never learned her face. On the morning a warlord's company marched on the catacomb, she was already gone. The sexton had fled in the night with something wrapped in sacking, and the Veil's duty was to bring back whatever was stolen from the dead. She ran him down two nights out, took his buyer's name from him and the bundle from his hands, and came home.

She came home to sealed doors and Saiyora's body on the stair. Her Echo believes both her sisters died because she was too slow. She clawed at the doors of the sanctum for nights on end, until her arms grew long and her nails became hooks, and she never heard anything from the other side. She doesn't know that [[Mortenia's Echo, the Silent Sister]] rang the great bell for her until her hands bled, or that the bell made no sound. She has never unwrapped the bundle at her waist, and it holds the great bell's iron tongue.

Her Echo hunts the weakest creature in reach, because the weak were the ones she failed. Her Veiled Whispers are the names of the order's dead, with the sexton's name among them. She haunts the dark approaches to the stair where [[Saiyora's Echo, the Iron Sister]] still stands. Of the three Echoes, she is the only one carrying what could end all their vigils, and she doesn't know it.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** the dark passages, ossuaries, and ceilings of a catacomb, especially the approaches to a sealed sanctum.
- **Encountered as:** alone. She is usually the first of the Echo Sisters a party meets, before Saiyora on the stair and Mortenia in the sanctum.
- **Signs:** deep parallel gouges in stone doors, up where no one could reach; grey funeral veils snagged on ceiling hooks; the weakest member of a crypt-robbing gang found dead and the rest untouched.

## Tactics
Velka stays in the dark overhead, using Shadow Stealth to hide as a bonus action, and attacks from 20 feet away. At the start of every turn, she knows exactly who is weakest, and she goes for them. She uses Shadow Jump to land beside her Quarry for the critical-hit window, and Veiled Whispers when two or more enemies are within 20 feet. Skewering Claw punishes anyone who turns to fight something else, which matters most if she is met alongside her sister. When she rises again from her veils, she goes straight back to the Quarry.

**Running it:** telegraph the Quarry. Her veiled head turns toward the most wounded character every round, so the party can protect them. Lighting matters too: bright light stops her ordinary Shadow Jump, though not the jump to her Quarry. Make holy water findable before this fight (in the order's vestry, or with a priest in the town above), so the players have the tools for Rejuvenation once they see it.

## Adventure Hooks
1. Each night, someone in the almshouse above the catacomb is found dead, and it is always the frailest resident. *Twist:* the deaths stop when a party member is wounded, because Velka has changed her Quarry and followed them up into the town.
2. A relic-hunter offers good coin for "the bundle of sacking the veiled thing carries." *Twist:* it is the great bell's iron tongue. Rung with it, the bell lays all three Echo Sisters to rest. The relic-hunter's buyer wants them kept awake to guard what lies behind the doors.
3. The party's cleric goes to the town temple for holy water and finds that the whole supply has been spoiled. *Twist:* the sexton's descendants have been quietly spoiling it for generations, because Velka's hunting keeps everyone else out of the catacomb.

## Spoils
The bundle at her waist holds the great bell's iron tongue, the key to laying all three sisters to rest (see [[Mortenia's Echo, the Silent Sister]]). Her grey veils can be cut and sewn into a *cloak of elvenkind*. In the passages where she roosts lie the tools and purses of crypt-robbers she has killed: 3d6 × 50 gp in coin and gear.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** For all three sisters, the house counts a feature that brings her back from 0 hit points once, at the hit points she returns with. Rejuvenation can repeat, but holy water stops it with a single action, so it is counted once.
- *Defensive:* 150 hp, plus 150 from one Rejuvenation, is 300. Applying ×1.25 for cold, necrotic, and nonmagical weapon resistance (expected CR 11–16) gives 375 effective hp, the CR 20 band. AC 15 is four below the expected 19, two steps down, so **defensive CR is 18**.
- *Offensive:* over three rounds (Veiled Whispers on two targets for 42, then two rounds of two Claw hits on her Quarry at 23 each plus Lacerating Claw for 18, for 64), she averages about 57 damage per round, the bottom of the CR 9 band. +8 to hit is one above the expected +7, too little to move it, so **offensive CR is 9**. Skewering Claw and the critical-hit window aren't counted. As submitted, with 15-damage claws and DC 16, it averaged 58, also the CR 9 band.
- *Average:* **13.5**. It rounds down to 13, because a single flask of holy water switches off the hit points that drive her defense. That is within 1 of the stated 12, so she stays **CR 12**.

**IP:** none. No setting-specific names appeared in the source.
