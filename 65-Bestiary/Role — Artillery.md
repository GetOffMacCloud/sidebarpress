# Artillery

*The first anyone knows of them is the arrow. The second is the fire.*

**Artillery** stays back and deals damage at range: arrows, spells, thrown pots, spat acid. It's fragile up close, so it needs enforcers in front or terrain between it and the party. Artillery forces the party to choose between pushing through the line and taking fire.

> [!example]+ Applying the Artillery Role
> Adjust any statblock:
> - **AC** −1.
> - **Hit Points** −25%.
> - **Ranged attack:** give it one (range 60/240 for weapons, or 120 feet for spells) dealing the same damage as its best melee attack +25%.
> - **Melee:** keep a weak backup attack (a dagger or a bite).
>
> **Signature traits (pick one or two):**
> - ***Steady Aim.*** If the artillery hasn't moved this turn, it has advantage on its first ranged attack roll this turn.
> - ***Volley (Recharge 5–6).*** The artillery targets a point within range. Each creature within 10 feet of that point must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + proficiency bonus + the attack's ability modifier), taking twice its ranged attack's average damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
> - ***Keep Distance (Reaction).*** When a creature moves within 5 feet of the artillery, the artillery moves up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
> - ***Spotter's Mark.*** When the artillery hits a creature with a ranged attack, the next attack roll against that creature before the start of the artillery's next turn has advantage.
>
> **CR:** usually **unchanged**. The ranged damage bonus raises offense by about one step; the lower HP and AC lower defense by about one.

## Running Artillery
- Start it high, behind cover, or across something the party has to cross: a ravine, a pit, deep water.
- Focus fire on whoever is casting concentration spells.
- When the line breaks, artillery runs for the next good position. It never stands and fights in melee by choice.

## Artillery Minions
An artillery minion keeps **one death or splash trait** if its base creature has one (the pot-slinger's Volatile Satchel is ideal). A minion that hurts the party *as it dies* turns the fighter who wades in to cut them down into a calculated risk.

> [!example]+ Kobold Pot-Slinger Minion (CR 1/2 minion, 25 XP)
> *Small humanoid (kobold), lawful evil*
> **Armor Class** 14 (leather armor) · **Hit Points** 1 (minion) · **Speed** 30 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 8 (−1) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 9 (−1) | 8 (−1) |
>
> **Skills** Stealth +5
> **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9 · **Languages** Common, Draconic · **Challenge** 1/2 (25 XP)
>
> ***Minion.*** If the pot-slinger takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.
>
> ***Pot Payload.*** Choose acid, fire, or poison when you place the pack. A creature *doused* by its pot suffers the payload's effect until the end of that creature's next turn. *Acid:* its speed is reduced by 10 feet. *Fire:* it sheds bright light in a 10-foot radius and can't benefit from being hidden. *Poison:* it is poisoned.
>
> ***Volatile Satchel (Artillery).*** When the pot-slinger drops to 0 hit points, each creature within 5 feet of it must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 3 damage of its payload type.
>
> ***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the pot-slinger has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Shatterpot.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 damage of its payload type, and the target is doused.

Built from the [[Kobold Pot-Slinger]]: 1 hit point, Minion added, Shatterpot's splash, Dagger, Pack Tactics, the damage resistance, and Scurry dropped, Volatile Satchel kept, and the pot set to the CR 1/2 fixed damage.

## Design Notes
Artillery moves value from defense to offense, at range. The DMG counts ranged attacks the same as melee, but a creature that can't be reached easily plays harder than its CR. At CR 10 or lower, the DMG suggests treating a flier with ranged attacks as +2 AC; apply the same logic to artillery on a wall or across a gap.
