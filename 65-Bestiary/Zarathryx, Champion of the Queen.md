## Lore
Zarathryx was hatched for war. Its egg was laid in the Dragon Queen's own hoard, from a red and a blue she chose for the purpose, and its scales show both: crimson along the left flank, storm-blue along the right, meeting in a jagged seam down its spine. It breathes fire and lightning in a single roaring blast. It has never had a lair of its own, only a war-camp, and it has never had a name the Queen didn't give it.

It takes its title literally. A champion answers challenges, so any creature that calls Zarathryx out by name, in the old formal words, gets a duel, and Zarathryx has never lost one. When it isn't dueling, it leads the Queen's war-hosts against the metallic dragons and the kingdoms that shelter them. It also sits in judgment on the lesser chromatic dragons who carry the Queen's favor, and several have been found wanting.

Zarathryx is proud, courteous, and entirely without mercy. It addresses its enemies with formal respect and orders their cities burned in the same tone of voice. Its one fear is that the Queen will someday choose another champion, and it hunts down anything that might be raised to replace it, including its own hatchlings.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** wherever the Dragon Queen's war is being fought; mountain war-camps, burned borderlands, and the ruins of kingdoms it has broken.
- **Encountered as:** at the head of a war-host of kobolds, cultists, half-dragons, and young chromatic dragons; or alone, answering a formal challenge.
- **Signs:** banners in five colors; burned fields scored with long scars of fused glass; heralds who ride ahead of the host to offer surrender terms exactly once.

## Tactics
Zarathryx opens with Frightful Presence and Dual Breath, then spends its legendary actions casting *fireball* and *lightning bolt* at anyone who stayed out of the cone. It closes to melee with the strongest enemy, which it considers the honorable part of a battle. When it drops below half its hit points, the Queen's dual pulse breaks loose. In a formal duel, it fights alone and casts no spells until its opponent does. Outside a duel, it retreats only on the Queen's orders.

**Running it:** Zarathryx is a set piece. Before the fight, have its herald read terms; during the fight, have it name each character it faces. If the party issues a formal challenge, it honors it, and a duel is the likeliest way for a party to survive this dragon at all.

## Adventure Hooks
1. Zarathryx's herald brings an offer: the city will be spared if its champion defeats Zarathryx in single combat. The city's champion has already fled. The dragon will accept a substitute, but only one, and only someone the city names.
2. Zarathryx is coming to judge a lesser dragon who carries the Queen's favor, such as [[Vyrathax, the Crimson Tyrant]]. The lesser dragon offers the party its hoard to help it survive the judgment. Zarathryx offers the party their lives to help carry out the sentence.
3. The last egg of an ancient metallic dragon's line is hidden somewhere in the party's city. Zarathryx's host is three days away, and the egg has started to hatch.

## Spoils
Zarathryx's two-colored hide yields one suit of *dragon scale mail*; the smith chooses fire or lightning resistance when it is made. Its war-chest is a Challenge 17+ treasure hoard (DMG p. 139), carried in iron-bound wagons. Among the chest's papers is the Queen's commission, written in Draconic and sealed in five colors of wax. Any chromatic dragon will recognize it, and several would kill to possess it.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild) as *Zarathryx, Champion of the Queen*. The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source file sat in a "CR 14 Monsters" homebrew folder and names no campaign or adventure of origin.

**Why CR 21, not 14.** As submitted, Zarathryx was rated CR 14, but it fights like an ancient dragon. Its 70-damage breath fills a 90-foot cone, its legendary actions drop a *fireball* or *lightning bolt* every round on top of a Tail attack, and its Multiattack deals 63. That works out to about 162 damage per round, CR 23 offense, against CR 19 defense, an average of exactly 21. At CR 21 its proficiency rises to +7, which would push the average to 21.5. Rather than lean on the uncounted Frightful Presence to round that up to 22, we trimmed the breath by two dice per element to keep a clean CR 21.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 256 hp plus 90 for Legendary Resistance (3 uses × 30) gives 346 effective hp, the CR 19 band (341–355). Its three resistances and two immunities are broad, but at the CR it lands (17+), resistance adds nothing, and two immunities alone aren't broad enough for the ×1.25. AC 19 matches the expected 19, so **defensive CR is 19**. Over three rounds (Dual Breath on two targets for 140, then Multiattack for 63, twice), with a legendary *fireball* or *lightning bolt* on two targets (56) and a Tail attack (17) every round, it averages 162 damage per round, the CR 22 band (159–176). +13 to hit is two above the expected +11, so **offensive CR is 23**. *Average:* **21**.
- *Retuned:* defensive CR is unchanged at **19**. With the breath at 62, the first round deals 197, and the average falls to 156 per round, the CR 21 band (141–158). +15 to hit is four above the expected +11, so **offensive CR is 23**. *Average:* 21.0, which gives **CR 21**. Frightful Presence (control), the once-a-day pulse (about 56 damage on two targets), and *storm sphere* aren't counted. Without the breath trim, the retuned block would average 21.5, and Frightful Presence would justify rounding that up to 22.

**IP substitutions:** "the Queen" and "Tiamat" → "the Dragon Queen" (the creature's name is now "Zarathryx, Champion of the Dragon Queen"; the trait "Tiamat's Dual Favor" is now "Dual Favor of the Dragon Queen"). The file keeps its submitted name. **Flag for the publisher:** *storm sphere* is from *Xanathar's Guide to Everything*, not the SRD. It is kept as submitted; an SRD swap would be *call lightning* or *chain lightning*.
