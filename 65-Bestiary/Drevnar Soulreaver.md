## Lore
Drevnar was once a paladin of great renown, a champion of a sun-god's order who rode at the head of a crusade. The crusade failed. Drevnar's company was besieged in a ruined abbey for a winter, and when the god did not answer his prayers, Drevnar stopped praying and began bargaining. Something in the abbey's crypt answered him. He walked out in the spring alone, and the abbey has been a graveyard ever since.

His oath did not break so much as turn over. He keeps it still, with the same discipline and the same rites, but every blessing he speaks now curses, and every wound he heals is borrowed from someone else's life. His sword drinks from the people it cuts. The healing in his hands can be turned inside out to stop a heart. The grass dies in a circle around him, and the people near him find their courage and their wills rotting away like the grass.

Drevnar believes he is still righteous. He thinks the god failed him, not the other way around, and he hunts the order that once knighted him with the patience of a man who has decided they are all heretics.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** ruined abbeys, battlefields, and the graveyards that grow up around them; the old pilgrim roads between shrines of his former order.
- **Encountered as:** alone, or at the head of a company of fallen knights, cultists, and the undead raised from his old comrades.
- **Signs:** circles of dead grass 60 feet across along a road; desecrated shrines with the holy symbol carefully turned upside down rather than smashed; and wounded travelers who heal and then sicken a day later.

## Tactics
Drevnar walks straight into the middle of the party so that his aura covers as many enemies as possible. He casts *bane* on the casters first, since his Aura of Malignance gives them disadvantage on the Charisma save. He fights with his sword and heals himself from every hit. He saves *banishing smite* for the strongest melee enemy and *destructive wave* for when three or more enemies surround him. He holds his Lay on Hands pool in reserve. When he drops below half his hit points, he spends it on himself. If he has the full pool when a caster falls to low hit points, he spends it to kill that caster outright.

**Running it:** Corrupted Lay on Hands can deal 80 damage in one action with no roll, which is enough to kill most casters below 12th level. Telegraph it. Let the party see Drevnar's hand blacken and reach first, and use it only on a target that has already been hurt.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A paladin order asks the party to escort its last relic to a new temple along the old pilgrim road. Drevnar is on the road, and the relic is the holy symbol he threw down in the abbey; he wants it back.
2. Pilgrims healed at a roadside shrine are dying a week later. A kindly knight has been tending the shrine, and he is Drevnar, practicing.
3. The sun-god's high priest believes Drevnar can be redeemed, and he will pay the party to capture him alive. Drevnar is willing to be captured, because the temple's sanctum is where he means to finish the work he started.

## Spoils
Drevnar's armor is **+2 plate armor**, blackened, with the order's sigil scored out of the breastplate. The **Sword of the Soulreaver** is a +1 greatsword that deals an extra 1d6 necrotic damage on a hit; it requires attunement by a creature of evil alignment, and while attuned, the wielder regains hit points equal to the necrotic damage it deals. A creature of good alignment that touches the sword takes 3d6 necrotic damage. His sanctum holds the treasury of his old crusade: 2d6 × 1,000 gp in coin and plate, and one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table G.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 162 hp is the CR 7 band (161–175). Radiant resistance and two immunities aren't broad enough to adjust. AC 20 is five above the expected 15, and Magic Resistance adds 2 more, so **defensive CR is about 10.5**. Over three rounds (three sword hits for 31 each, or 93; *banishing smite* for 27 in round 1 and *searing smite* for 7 in rounds 2 and 3; and the aura failing on two targets for 28), it averages about 135 damage per round, the CR 20 band (123–140). +13 to hit is three above the expected +10, so **offensive CR is about 21.5**. *Average:* **CR 16**.
- *Now:* defense is unchanged at about **10.5**. Over three rounds (three sword hits for 30 each, or 90; the same smites; the aura on two targets for 26), it averages about 130 damage per round, still the CR 20 band. +11 to hit is one above the expected +10, so **offensive CR is about 20.5**. *Average:* 15.5. We round up to **CR 16** because the formula doesn't count his healing: up to 30 hit points a round from his sword, and an 80-point Lay on Hands pool he can spend on himself, which would add about 80 effective hit points. The stated CR stands.

**Still worth watching:** Drevnar is lopsided, with offense four CRs above defense. His healing makes up much of the gap, but a party that focuses him down can kill him in two rounds, and a party that doesn't can lose a member every round. Fight him with his company around him, or give him a lieutenant who can keep the party off him.

**IP substitutions:** none. The sun-god's order is a generic placeholder; set it to any lawful good deity in your campaign. The epithet "Soulreaver" is the contributor's and is kept; editors may want to note that it echoes a video-game title.
