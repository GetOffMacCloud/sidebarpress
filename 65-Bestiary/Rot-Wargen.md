## Lore
A rot-wargen is a big, lean wolf that lives on carrion and nothing else. Generations of eating the long-dead have given it a living rot: its gums are black, its lips have sloughed back from its teeth, and its bite carries a sickness that turns a strong stomach in seconds. It is no undead thing. It breeds, raises cubs, and grows old, and it is fiercely, cunningly alive.

Rot-wargen go where the dead are. They follow armies, plague carts, and famine roads, and they gather wherever something else does the killing for them. Where [[The Marrowmother]] keeps a larder, a pack is never far away: the wargen eat what she leaves, and she leaves them alone. Woodcutters treat a rot-wargen howl as a warning that something worse is near.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** forest edges, hill country, old battlefields, and the outskirts of ruined or plague-struck villages.
- **Encountered as:** packs of three to six, led by the oldest and most rotted animal; often scavenging at the edges of [[The Marrowmother]]'s territory or trailing her brood.
- **Signs:** a sweet, sick smell on the wind; carcasses gnawed down to the bone and the bones cracked for marrow; greenish slaver on leaves at knee height.

## Tactics
A pack stalks downwind, then strikes from cover to make the most of Ambush Predator. After the first round, the wargen pair up on one target, so each has Pack Tactics, and knock it prone. They break off as soon as two of the pack are badly hurt. Rot-wargen want an easy meal, not a fair fight.

**Running it:** the stink is their tell. Let any character with a passive Perception of 12 or higher catch the smell a round before the ambush, and reward players who stop to sniff the wind.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A plague village's survivors are trapped in the church by a pack of rot-wargen. *Twist:* the wargen aren't after the living. They want the plague dead in the crypt, and the priest won't let anyone burn them.
2. A war-band's camp followers keep vanishing at night. *Twist:* the camp's own quartermaster has been leaving bodies out to keep the wargen fed and away from his stolen supplies.
3. A rot-wargen pack has moved into the hills for the first time in living memory. *Twist:* they are following the brood of [[The Marrowmother]], who has just moved into the old mine above the village.

## Spoils
Rot-wargen carry no treasure, and their pelts are too diseased to sell. A den holds gnawed bones and whatever its victims carried: 2d10 gp in coin and scattered gear.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source statblock doesn't mention the Marrowmother; the link between them is Sidebar Press lore. The source file doesn't name the adventure it first appeared in.

**Why CR 1, not 3.** As submitted, the rot-wargen was rated CR 3, but it is close kin to the MM's dire wolf (CR 1): one bite a round for 7 damage with a prone rider, Pack Tactics, and 45 hit points. Its poison and Ambush Predator are a little better than the dire wolf's kit, but nothing about it supports CR 3, which expects about 101–115 hit points and 21–26 damage a round. We kept its bite and every trait, added 7 hit points to give a clean CR 1 average, and changed nothing else. All its bonuses already use proficiency +2, which is the same at CR 1 and CR 3.

| Change | As submitted | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Challenge | 3 (700 XP) | 1 (200 XP) | Its offense and defense rate CR 1/2–1 |
| Hit Points | 45 (7d8 + 14) | 52 (8d8 + 16) | Lifts defense one band, for a clean CR 1 average |
| Rotten Mouth | separate trait ("All attacks… require a DC 12 Constitution saving throw") | folded into Bite | It repeated what Bite already said, and Bite is its only attack |
| Poison duration | "poisoned for one round" | "poisoned until the end of its next turn" | 2014 MM duration wording |
| Skills order | Stealth +5, Perception +4 | Perception +4, Stealth +5 | Alphabetical, house format; no rules change |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: proficiency +2 with Str +3 gives +5 to hit; the prone DC is Str-based (8 + 2 + 3 = 13) and the poison DC is Con-based (8 + 2 + 2 = 12); Perception +4, Stealth +5, and passive Perception 14 are correct; 1d8 + 3 averages 7 and 2d8 averages 9.
- *As submitted:* 45 hp is the CR 1/4 band (36–49). AC 14 is one above the expected 13, which doesn't move it, so **defensive CR is 1/4**. Over three rounds (an ambush bite for 16, then two bites for 7), it averages 10 damage a round, the CR 1 band (9–14). Pack Tactics counts as +1 to hit, so its effective +6 is three above the expected +3, which moves it one step, and **offensive CR is 2**. *Average:* halfway between CR 1/2 and CR 1, at least two steps below the stated CR 3. Re-rated.
- *Retuned:* 52 hp is the CR 1/2 band (50–70), and AC 14 still doesn't move it, so **defensive CR is 1/2**. Offense is unchanged at **CR 2**. *Average:* **CR 1**.

**IP substitutions:** none.
