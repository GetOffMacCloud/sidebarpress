## Lore
Sylvarith rules a drowned forest from underneath it. Its lair is a sunken manor at the bottom of a black lake, and the manor's school is still in session. For three hundred years the green dragon has taken one child a year in tribute from the fen villages, never more and never one who would be missed. It raises them in the manor's dry upper halls and teaches them letters, courtesy, patience, and poison. Then it sends them out into the courts of the world as clerks, physicians, and food-tasters. They write to it, every one of them, for the rest of their lives.

Sylvarith is lawful the way a contract is lawful. It keeps its word to the syllable and expects the same. It has never told a lie, though it rarely says anything true by accident, and it sells the antidote to every venom its students carry, at a fair price, to anyone who asks. It considers a poisoning that can be traced back to its school a disgrace, and a student who is caught doesn't write again.

The Dragon Queen's favor came to Sylvarith as a commission, not a gift. It serves as her envoy among the lesser chromatic dragons and in the infernal courts, and its wage is the tide of venom it can release when wounded. Of all the dragons who carry her favor, Sylvarith is the only one she has never had to threaten.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** drowned forests, black fen lakes, and coastal marshes. Its students reach every city.
- **Encountered as:** alone in its lair, or with 1d4 grown students (use the **assassin** or **spy** statblock) when it travels.
- **Signs:** fen villages where every family has lost exactly one child, and no one speaks of it; letters sealed in green wax in a flowing Draconic hand; dead fish floating in a ring around the middle of a lake.

## Tactics
Sylvarith talks first. It surfaces well out of reach, names its terms, and fights only when a deal fails or someone breaks one. Then it opens with Poison Breath from the water, dives, and resurfaces behind the party to use its Multiattack. It spends its legendary actions on Tail attacks against spellcasters. When it drops below half its hit points, it releases its toxic pulse and retreats underwater. It will offer terms at any point in a fight, and it honors them.

**Running it:** let the party strike a deal with Sylvarith. A dragon that has been bargained with is more dangerous than one that has been fought, because the deal will come due.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A fen village's tribute child is due to go to the lake this autumn, and her parents want her rescued. She wants to go: she has read the letters the dragon's old students send home, and none of them ever died poor.
2. A wave of poisonings is sweeping the capital, and every victim's taster survived. The tasters are Sylvarith's students, but they aren't the poisoners. Someone is framing the school, and Sylvarith will pay handsomely to learn who.
3. Sylvarith offers the party a fair contract: kill a rival dragon and keep its hoard. The rival also carries the Dragon Queen's favor, and the contract was written in the Queen's own hand.

## Spoils
Sylvarith's venom glands hold 2d4 doses of poison, each equivalent to a vial of purple worm poison (DMG p. 258) and worth 2,000 gp to the wrong buyer. Its hide yields enough green scales for one suit of *dragon scale mail* (poison). The manor holds a Challenge 11–16 treasure hoard (DMG p. 138) and three centuries of letters from its students, enough leverage to topple a dozen courts.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source file sat in a "CR 12 Monsters" homebrew folder and names no campaign or adventure of origin.

**Why CR 16, not 12.** As submitted, Sylvarith was rated CR 12, but its offense, counting a 56-damage breath on two targets and three legendary Tail attacks a round, rates CR 18, and its 207 hit points plus two uses of Legendary Resistance put its defense at CR 14. That averages exactly 16. At CR 16 its proficiency rises to +5, which lifts the offense half a step, and the average (16.25) still rounds to 16. Its attacks and damage are unchanged.

| Change | As submitted | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Challenge | 12 (8,400 XP) | 16 (15,000 XP) | The block computes to CR 16 |
| Proficiency | +4 | +5 | CR 16 |
| Attack bonus | +10 | +11 | Str +6 and proficiency +5 |
| Saving throws | Dex +5, Con +9, Wis +6, Cha +8 | Dex +6, Con +10, Wis +7, Cha +9 | Proficiency +5 |
| Skills / passive | Deception +8, Perception +10, Persuasion +8 / 20 | Deception +9, Perception +12, Persuasion +9 / 22 | Proficiency +5; Perception keeps its submitted expertise |
| Poison Breath and pulse DC | 17 | 18 | Con-based: 8 + 5 + 5 |
| Wing Attack DC | 18 | 19 | Str-based: 8 + 5 + 6 |
| Poison Breath timing | "Action Inhale, Action Exhale" | Recharge 5–6 | A two-action breath has no 2014 rule; Recharge 5–6 is the MM dragons' standard |
| Tiamat's Favor | Trait restating its resistances, plus a pulse "using its reaction" | Reaction: Favor of the Dragon Queen (1/Day); the resistances stay on the defense line only | A reaction belongs under Reactions; the resistances were listed twice; IP |
| Copy | "fly 80 ft., (hover)"; "must make a DC 17 Constitution Saving throw dealing or take…"; "Sylvarith, the Venomous Wyrm can take…" | Corrected to house style and 2014 wording | No rules change |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 207 hp plus 60 for Legendary Resistance (2 uses × 30) gives 267 effective hp, the CR 14 band (266–280). Two resistances and one immunity aren't broad enough for a multiplier. AC 18 matches the expected 18, so **defensive CR is 14**. Over three rounds (Poison Breath on two targets for 112 plus three legendary Tail attacks for 45, then Multiattack for 50 plus three Tails for 45, twice), it averages 116 damage per round, the CR 18 band (111–116). +10 to hit matches the expected +10, so **offensive CR is 18**. *Average:* **16**.
- *Retuned:* defensive CR is unchanged at **14**. Damage is unchanged at 116 per round (CR 18 band). +11 to hit is one above the expected +10, so **offensive CR is 18.5**. *Average:* 16.25, which gives **CR 16**. The once-a-day bloodied pulse (about 28 damage on two targets) isn't counted.

**IP substitutions:** "Tiamat" → "the Dragon Queen" (the trait "Tiamat's Favor" is now "Favor of the Dragon Queen"). The assassin and spy statblocks are SRD.
