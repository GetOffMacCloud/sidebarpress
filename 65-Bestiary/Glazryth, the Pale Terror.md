## Lore
Glazryth is old, vast, and not clever, and it has never needed to be. For six centuries it has hunted the ice fields at the top of the world, and it remembers every creature that ever hurt it. It doesn't eat the ones it kills. It drags them into the glacier it calls home, breathes on them until they are sealed in clear ice, and stands them along its tunnels like a hall of statues. Some of the frozen have stood there so long that the steppe tribes use their silhouettes as landmarks.

The tribes call it the Pale Terror, and they pay it no tribute, because it wants none. It wants to be feared, and it wants its gallery to grow. A hunter who wounds it and escapes has made an enemy for life: Glazryth will tunnel under the snow for weeks, following one scent through the drifts, to add one more face to the ice.

A generation ago, something answered Glazryth's hatred of warmth. Its scales stopped blistering under fire and lightning, and a cold that isn't its own now coils in its chest, waiting for the moment it is badly hurt. Glazryth doesn't know the gift came from the Dragon Queen. It believes its own hatred made it stronger, and the Queen's envoys who come north to collect on the debt rarely come back.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** glaciers, ice shelves, frozen mountain passes, and the tundra below them.
- **Encountered as:** alone. Glazryth tolerates no other dragon in its range. Winter wolves sometimes trail it to scavenge what it leaves.
- **Signs:** bodies standing upright in the snowdrifts, all facing the same way; ice clear as glass for yards at a stretch; reindeer that won't cross a certain valley; holes in the snow crust as wide as a wagon.

## Tactics
Glazryth hunts from under the snow. It lies buried with only its nostrils above the crust, feeling for footsteps with its blindsight, then erupts beneath the party and breathes before anyone can spread out. It enjoys melee and stays in it, spending its legendary actions on Tail attacks against whoever hurt it last. It uses Wing Attack only to break free when surrounded. When it drops below half its hit points, the Dragon Queen's cold bursts out of it, and it burrows away into the ice to heal. It always comes back for whoever wounded it.

**Running it:** Glazryth is a grudge with wings. Note which character deals it the most damage, and let it hunt that character by name across sessions, even to their bedroll.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A trapper's widow hires the party to bring her husband's body back from the Pale Terror's glacier. The ice has kept him alive, barely, and he saw where Glazryth sleeps. Thawing him without killing him is a race against the dragon, which wants its statue back.
2. A cult of the Dragon Queen hires the party to escort an envoy north with a gift for Glazryth: a jeweled collar meant to bind the dragon to the Queen's service. The envoy doesn't expect to survive the delivery, and he doesn't intend for the party to survive it either.
3. A frozen knight in the gallery still holds a famous sword. Cutting it free is the easy part. The knight's descendants, who have spent a century preparing to kill the dragon themselves, want the sword left where it is, as bait.

## Spoils
Glazryth's hide yields enough white scales for one suit of *dragon scale mail* (cold). Its hoard is its gallery: the frozen dead still wear and carry what they brought, amounting to a Challenge 17+ treasure hoard (DMG p. 139). Most of it is sealed in ice, and freeing it takes days of chipping and fire.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source file sat in a "CR 12 Monsters" homebrew folder and names no campaign or adventure of origin.

**Why CR 17, not 12.** As submitted, Glazryth was rated CR 12, but it carries an adult dragon's full kit: a 54-damage breath, three legendary Tail attacks a round, and two uses of Legendary Resistance. Counting all of it, its offense rates CR 19.5 and its defense 13.5, an average of 16.5. Moving it up raises its proficiency bonus, and with it the attack bonus that drives its offense: at CR 16 (+5) the average would be 16.75, which doesn't sit cleanly, and at CR 17 (+6) it averages exactly 17. Every attack and damage figure is unchanged.

| Hit Points | 213 (16d12 + 108) | 212 (17d12 + 102) | The dice and bonus disagree (16d12 + 96 is 200; +108 implies 18d12, or 225). 17d12 + 102 averages 212, the closest to the stated 213 |
| Proficiency | +4 | +6 | CR 17 |
| Attack bonus | +11 | +13 | Str +7 and proficiency +6 |
| Saving throws | Dex +4, Con +10, Wis +5, Cha +6 | Dex +6, Con +12, Wis +7, Cha +8 | Proficiency +6 |
| Skills / passive | Perception +9, Stealth +4 / 19 | Perception +13, Stealth +6 / 23 | Proficiency +6; Perception keeps its submitted expertise |
| Cold Breath and pulse DC | 18 | 20 | Con-based: 8 + 6 + 6 |

| Copy | "burrow 40 ft., (ice/snow only), fly 80 ft., (hover)"; "all creatures within 10 ft must make…"; "Glazryth, the Pale Terror can take…" | Corrected to house style and 2014 wording | No rules change |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 213 hp plus 60 for Legendary Resistance (2 uses × 30 at expected CR 11+) gives 273 effective hp, the CR 14 band (266–280). Two resistances and one immunity aren't broad enough for a multiplier. AC 17 is one below the expected 18, so **defensive CR is 13.5**. Over three rounds (Cold Breath on two targets for 108 plus three legendary Tail attacks for 48, then Multiattack for 53 plus three Tails for 48, twice), it averages 119 damage per round, the CR 19 band (117–122). +11 to hit is one above the expected +10, so **offensive CR is 19.5**. *Average:* **16.5**.
- *Retuned:* 212 + 60 = 272 effective hp, still the CR 14 band, and AC 17 keeps **defensive CR at 13.5**. Damage is unchanged at 119 per round (CR 19 band). +13 to hit is three above the expected +10, so **offensive CR is 20.5**. *Average:* 17.0, which gives **CR 17**. The once-a-day bloodied pulse (about 28 damage on two targets) isn't counted.

**Still worth watching:** the profile is lopsided. Glazryth hits like a CR 20 creature but takes damage like a CR 13–14 one, and a focused party can drop it in three rounds. Its burrow speed is its escape: let it use it.

**IP substitutions:** "Tiamat" → "the Dragon Queen" (the trait "Tiamat's Favor" is now "Favor of the Dragon Queen").
