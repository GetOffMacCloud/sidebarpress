# Leader

*Kill that one first. Everyone at the table already knows it. That's the point.*

A **leader** makes everything around it better: braver, faster, harder to kill, and better at killing. It's rarely the most dangerous creature alone, but every other creature in the fight is more dangerous while it stands. Leaders give the party a clear priority target and a reason to push through the line.

> [!example]+ Applying the Leader Role
> Adjust any statblock:
> - **Damage** −25% on its own attacks.
> - **Defense** unchanged. A leader with an enforcer beside it doesn't need more.
> - **Add one or two leader traits** (below).
>
> **Signature traits (pick one or two):**
> - ***Command (Bonus Action).*** One ally the leader can see within 30 feet of it can use its reaction to make one weapon attack.
> - ***Rally (Recharge 5–6).*** As a bonus action, each ally within 30 feet of the leader that can hear it gains temporary hit points equal to twice the leader's CR (minimum 5) and is no longer frightened.
> - ***War-Cry Aura.*** While the leader isn't incapacitated, its allies within 30 feet of it add 1d4 to their attack rolls and saving throws.
> - ***Last Order.*** When the leader drops to 0 hit points, each ally that can see it can immediately move up to half its speed and make one weapon attack.
>
> **CR:** usually **unchanged** for the leader itself. **Price the encounter one step harder** when a leader is present, because its traits raise its allies' damage and survival, which the formula doesn't count on the leader's line.

## Running a Leader
- Keep it at the second rank, behind an enforcer, where it can see the whole fight.
- Use Command on the brute every round. That's the best damage the leader can deal.
- When the leader falls, the others should break: give minions a morale check (DC 10 Wisdom save) or have them flee.

## Leader Minions
A leader minion keeps **one aura or buff**, active only while it lives. A drummer or standard-bearer minion in the back of a pack is a priority target that the party has to reach.

> **Armor Class** 13 (leather armor) · **Hit Points** 1 (minion) · **Speed** 30 ft.

> **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13 · **Languages** Common, Goblin · **Challenge** 1/4 (12 XP)
>
> ***Minion.*** If the drummer takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.
>
> ***War-Drum (Leader).*** While the drummer isn't incapacitated, its allies within 30 feet of it that can hear it gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls. This bonus doesn't stack with other war-drums.
>
> ***Nimble Escape.*** The drummer can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of its turns.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Dagger.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2 piercing damage.

Built from the [[Goblin Battle-Drummer]]: 1 hit point, Minion added, Beat the Drums replaced with an always-on War-Drum (+1, smaller than the full drummer's +2 so a pack of drummers can't stack), Nimble Escape kept so it can hide behind the line, and the dagger set to the CR 1/4 fixed damage.

## Design Notes
Leaders are force multipliers. The DMG prices each creature alone, so a leader's CR undersells it. A practical rule: when a leader with Command or War-Cry Aura is present, treat the encounter as one difficulty step harder (Medium plays as Hard). Last Order is a dramatic death; use it on a villain's lieutenant so the party feels the line surge when it falls.
