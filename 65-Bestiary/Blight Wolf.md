## Lore
Where a blood-fed tree spreads its curse, the brush around it wakes. Most of what wakes is twig blights: scuttling, man-shaped bundles of dead wood. But a tree that has fed on wolves learns the shape of a wolf, and in time its seedlings come up on four legs. A blight wolf is a thornbush that hunts. Its body is a knot of roots and dead stems, its teeth are splinters, and it carries the cunning of the pack its parent tree once ate.

Blight wolves run in packs and serve their tree the way wolves serve a pack leader. They drag prey back to be drained at its roots, and when they howl, the blights in the soil come up to answer. Farmers who find thornbushes growing in a ring around the edge of a field, where no thornbush was planted, burn them. The ones who wait usually lose a dog first, then a child.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** blighted woods, overgrown farmland, briar-choked ruins, and the edges of swamps.
- **Encountered as:** packs of three to six, usually with a handful of twig blights, and always within a few miles of the cursed tree that seeded them.
- **Signs:** dead shrubs that weren't there yesterday; furrows of freshly turned soil where something burrowed; animal carcasses drained dry and dragged toward the deep wood.

## Tactics
Blight wolves wait motionless along a trail until prey walks into the middle of the pack, then attack together to use Ambush and Pack Tactics. They knock targets prone and swarm them. A wolf that is badly hurt burrows away. They use Blight Howl early, to surround the party with twig blights before the wolves close in.

**Running it:** Blight Howl can flood the field in a pack fight. In a pack of three or more, let only one wolf howl each round, and cap the twig blights at twice the number of wolves.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A farmer's dogs have all gone missing, and a ring of thornbushes has appeared around his farmhouse overnight. *Twist:* he planted the seed himself, a cutting he was sold as a hedge "that guards the house," and the seller is selling more.
2. The party is hired to clear a briar-choked ruin for a new chapel. *Twist:* the pack doesn't serve a tree. It serves a druid who has been nursing the blight as a weapon against the chapel's builders.
3. A woodcutter's family begs for help after a blight wolf pack took their eldest. *Twist:* he is alive, bound in roots at the foot of the cursed tree, and the tree is slowly turning him into something new.

## Spoils
A blight wolf leaves only dead wood behind. Its heartwood knot, the size of a fist, is worth 5 gp to a druid or herbalist and burns for a full night with a sickly green flame.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: proficiency +2; Perception +3, Stealth +5, and passive Perception 13 are correct; 2d4 + 1 averages 6 and 2d4 averages 5.
- *As submitted:* 14 hp is the CR 1/8 band (7–35), and AC 13 matches the expected 13, so **defensive CR is 1/8**. Over three rounds (an ambush bite for 12, then two bites for 7), it averages 8.7 damage a round, at the top of the CR 1/2 band (6–8). +4 to hit, plus 1 for Pack Tactics, is two above the expected +3, so **offensive CR is about 1**. *Average:* CR 1/4–1/2.
- *As fixed:* defense is unchanged at **CR 1/8** (16 hp). Over three rounds (an ambush bite for 11, then two bites for 6), it averages 7.7 damage a round, the CR 1/2 band. +3 to hit, plus 1 for Pack Tactics, is one above the expected +3, which doesn't move it, so **offensive CR is 1/2**. *Average:* **CR 1/4**, which matches the stated CR. Blight Howl's twig blights (CR 1/8 each) are uncounted; budget them as part of the encounter.

**IP substitutions:** none. Twig blights are SRD creatures.
