# Kobold Blistercoil

*A kobold staggers under a humming backpack of copper coils and glass jars. Blue sparks crawl along its whiskers, and the air smells of storms.*

*Created by **GrimmGrixis**, Writers Guild.*

> [!example]+ Kobold Blistercoil (CR 3, 700 XP)
> *Small humanoid (kobold), chaotic neutral*
> **Armor Class** 15 (scrap-plate harness) · **Hit Points** 60 (11d6 + 22) · **Speed** 30 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 7 (−2) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 8 (−1) |
>
> **Skills** Arcana +3, Sleight of Hand +5, Stealth +5
> **Damage Resistances** lightning
> **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10 · **Languages** Common, Draconic · **Challenge** 3 (700 XP)
>
> ***Blistercoil Rig.*** The blistercoil wears a backpack of capacitors that holds up to 3 charges. It starts each combat with 3 charges and regains 1 at the start of each of its turns. Whenever the blistercoil takes lightning damage, even from an ally, the rig gains 1 charge (no action required). Overcharge, Coil Nova, and Static Discharge spend charges. If the rig is destroyed, the blistercoil can't spend or gain charges.
>
> ***Grounded.*** The blistercoil has advantage on saving throws against effects that would knock it prone.
>
> ***Pack Tactics.*** The blistercoil has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the blistercoil's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
>
> ***Rig Target.*** A creature can attack the rig instead of the blistercoil, using a ranged attack or a melee attack made with disadvantage. The rig is an object with AC 13, 10 hit points, and immunity to lightning, poison, and psychic damage. Lightning damage dealt to the rig gives it 1 charge instead. When the rig drops to 0 hit points, it is destroyed, and Unstable Coil triggers, centered on the blistercoil.
>
> ***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the blistercoil has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
>
> ***Unstable Coil.*** When the blistercoil drops to 0 hit points or its rig is destroyed, the capacitor stack ruptures. Each creature within 15 feet of the blistercoil must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 3 (1d6) lightning damage for each charge left in the rig on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature wearing metal armor or wielding a metal weapon has disadvantage on this saving throw. The rig ruptures only once.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Multiattack.*** The blistercoil makes two attacks with its Arc Bolt or Spark Prod, in any combination.
>
> ***Arc Bolt.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 60/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) lightning damage. Before rolling the attack, the blistercoil can spend 1 charge to overcharge the bolt. On a hit, an overcharged bolt deals an extra 5 (1d10) lightning damage, and lightning leaps to a second creature of the blistercoil's choice within 10 feet of the target, dealing 5 (1d10) lightning damage to it.
>
> ***Spark Prod.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) lightning damage.
>
> ***Coil Nova (Recharge 5–6; Costs 2 Charges).*** The blistercoil dumps the stack. Each creature within 15 feet of it, allies included, must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 18 (4d8) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature wearing metal armor or wielding a metal weapon has disadvantage on this saving throw. The blistercoil takes no damage from its own Nova.
>
> **Bonus Actions**
> ***Skitter.*** The blistercoil takes the Disengage or Hide action.
>
> **Reactions**
> ***Static Discharge (Costs 1 Charge).*** When a creature the blistercoil can see hits it with a melee attack, the blistercoil discharges a prong into the attacker. The attacker takes 5 (1d10) lightning damage, and if it is wearing metal armor, its speed is reduced to 0 until the end of its next turn as its armor seizes.

## Lore
A kobold war-crew is a warren's answer to a world of bigger folk: five trades and one plan. A [[Kobold Dragonfang]] leads it, [[Kobold Bulwark-Bearer|bulwark-bearers]] hold the line, [[Kobold Pot-Slinger|pot-slingers]] and [[Kobold Pyrotechnic|pyrotechnics]] throw fire from behind the shields, and a blistercoil carries the heavy weapon. Crews sell their work to a tinker-guild that pays in scrap, powder, and coin. They clear old mines, breach vaults, and fight the fights the guild doesn't want its name on. Where no guild hires them, a fire cult usually will.

The blistercoil isn't of the warren. It belongs to the guild: a clever kobold apprentice strapped into an experimental capacitor rig and sent out with a crew to find out what the rig does in a real fight. Most rigs work, more or less. A blistercoil that survives five fights wears its burn scars like rank, talks back to the dragonfang, and keeps its own counsel. The lawful warren tolerates that only because the guild insists, and the crew gives it room, since everyone has seen a rig go up.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** guild workshops, warrens, old mines, and ruins where the guild wants something dug out.
- **Encountered as:** one per war-crew, never more; the guild has only so many rigs.
- **Signs:** fused copper drips, glass shards from blown capacitor jars, scorched fern-patterns on stone, and the hair on everyone's arms standing up.

## Tactics
The blistercoil hangs back with the slingers and fires two Arc Bolts a turn, overcharging whenever it has a charge to spare, because the rig refills one charge every turn anyway. When enemies close on it, it uses Coil Nova, which doesn't care whose side anyone is on; a good crew steps back first, or leaves only a lightning-faced bulwark-bearer in range. It saves one charge for Static Discharge if a fighter in metal armor is coming. Its signature move with a blue (lightning) [[Kobold Dragonfang|dragonfang]] is to stand inside the dragonfang's breath on purpose: it takes half damage and the rig gains a charge.

**Suggested crew encounters** (four characters; adjusted XP per DMG p. 82):

| APL | Crew | Adjusted XP | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 1 bulwark-bearer, 1 pot-slinger | 300 × 1.5 = 450 | Medium |
| 3 | 1 bulwark-bearer, 1 pyrotechnic, 2 pot-slingers | 600 × 2 = 1,200 | Hard |

| 5 | 1 dragonfang, 1 bulwark-bearer, 1 pyrotechnic, 2 pot-slingers | 1,700 × 2 = 3,400 | Hard |
| 7 | 1 dragonfang, 1 blistercoil, 1 bulwark-bearer, 1 pyrotechnic, 2 pot-slingers | 2,400 × 2 = 4,800 | Hard |

**Running it:** track charges with a d4 or three coins beside the initiative entry, and add the rig's 10 hit points as a second line. Describe the rig as obviously fragile, with humming jars and loose wires, so the players think to shoot it: breaking it ends the charges and sets off Unstable Coil at once, which is often the safest moment to do it. The rupture happens only once, so a blistercoil that loses its rig doesn't explode again when it drops. Lightning spells feed the rig rather than break it, which a wizard will remember.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A tinker-guild offers a bounty for a missing prototype rig "and the apprentice wearing it." *Twist:* the blistercoil has deserted on purpose, and it is selling the rig's plans to the guild's rivals.
2. Lightning keeps striking the same hilltop on clear days. *Twist:* a crew is charging rigs from a buried storm-relic, and every strike draws it closer to the surface.
3. A blistercoil offers to switch sides in the middle of a fight. *Twist:* it's sincere, but its rig is wired to rupture if it's taken more than a mile from the guild hall.

## Spoils
The rig ruptures when the blistercoil falls or the rig breaks, but its wreck still holds 25 gp of copper coil and glass that a tinker or artificer will buy. A guild work order is usually tucked in the harness, naming the crew's job, its pay, and the guild clerk who signed it. A blistercoil taken alive with its rig intact is worth 150 gp to any rival guild, and the guild that owns it will pay twice that to keep the matter quiet.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive:* 60 hp is the CR 1/2 band (50–70). AC 15 is two above the expected 13, so **defensive CR is 1**. The rig gives the party a soft target, which the formula doesn't count.
- *Offensive:* round 1, Coil Nova on two targets (2 charges), 36. Round 2, with 2 charges, two overcharged Arc Bolts (8 + 5 + 5 each), 36. Round 3, with 1 charge, one overcharged and one plain bolt, 26. That averages 33, the bottom of the CR 5 band (33–38). +5 to hit is one below the expected +6 and DC 13 two below the expected 15, so **offensive CR is about 4**. (A death rupture usually finds 0–1 charges left and adds almost nothing.)
- *Average:* 2.5. Pack Tactics, which the formula doesn't count, justifies rounding up, so **CR 3 stands**, matching the stated CR.
