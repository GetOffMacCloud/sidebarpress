# Rogues' Gallery

The bestiary so far describes kinds of creature: things a party might meet more than once. This chapter is different. Every entry here is **one individual**: a dragon with a name and a grudge, a warlord with a war-band, a dead sister who won't stay dead. Each one is a ready-made villain, with a full statblock, a history, the people and creatures that serve them, and three ways to bring them into a campaign.

Gallery villains are deliberately unattached to any world. Where their original story named a god, a demon lord, or a city, we've used a generic epithet instead (the Dragon Queen, the Ravening Lord), so you can name it for your own setting. Many villains link to the creatures that serve them in the main bestiary. The [[Iron-Marked Wolf|Iron-Marked Wolves]] run with [[Sorrowbay, the Chained Sire|Sorrowbay]], the [[Gash-Marked Raider|Gash-Marked]] follow [[Grakh Unscarred]], and the Marrow brood answers to [[The Marrowmother]].

Most of these villains are high-CR and legendary, and several are deadly by design. Read the **Design Notes** in each entry before you run one. They show the CR working and say where a villain plays harder than its number.
