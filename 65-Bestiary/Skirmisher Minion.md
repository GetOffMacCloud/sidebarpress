# Skirmisher Minion

*It's never where you swung. It's already at the wizard.*

A **skirmisher minion** is fast and slippery. It runs past the line to the healer and the archer, bites, and pulls away.

> [!example]+ Skirmisher Minion Template
> Apply it with the steps in [[04 — Minions#How Minions Work|How Minions Work]], using the **Minion Table** row for your party's APL. Then:
> - **Speed:** +10 feet (or add a climb or fly speed that fits).
> - **Damage:** the table's standard column.
>
> **Signature (pick one):**
> - ***Hit and Run.*** The minion doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when it moves out of the reach of a creature it attacked this turn.
> - ***Charge.*** If the minion moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a melee attack on the same turn, the attack deals extra damage equal to half its normal damage.
> - ***Pack Tactics.*** The minion has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of its allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

## Running Skirmisher Minions
- Ignore the fighter. Go for whoever is concentrating on a spell, or whoever is already hurt.
- Use terrain the party can't cross easily: skirmishers with a climb speed own the walls.

## Worked Example: Iron-Marked Wolf at APL 3
Built from the [[Iron-Marked Wolf]] with the Skirmisher Minion template, at APL 3. Rebuild it at your own APL with the Minion Table: change the AC, attack, DC, damage, Challenge, and XP, and keep everything else.

>
> **Senses** passive Perception 11 · **Languages** understands Common but can't speak · **Challenge** 3 (175 XP)
>
> ***Minion.*** If the minion takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.
>
> ***Hit and Run (Skirmisher).*** The wolf doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when it moves out of the reach of a creature it attacked this turn.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**How it was built:** APL 3 row; speed 50 + 10 = 60 ft.; standard damage 9; the bite's prone rider kept at the table DC; the sire-linked traits (Bound Vitality, Iron Mark) dropped; passive Perception reset to 10 + Wisdom; Hit and Run as its signature.
