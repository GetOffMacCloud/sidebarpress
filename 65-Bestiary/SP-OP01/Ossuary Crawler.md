# Ossuary Crawler

*Original Sidebar Press creature. 2014 rules.*

- **Description:** A pale, segmented scavenger the length of a coffin, with a fringe of wet feeding tendrils and a pair of grinding mandibles. It lives on the ceilings of charnel houses and eats what the dead leave behind.
- **In this adventure:** [[Scene 02 — The Catacombs]], the Bone Gallery fight (C1) and Wandering Husks result 5. Ossian tolerates them because they clean up, and the party may blame them for his slime trail.
- **CR note:** 51 hp and 11 damage a round rate CR 1 by the numbers. The paralysis (DC 13 Con, repeated each turn) is what makes it CR 2.
