# Cured Hand

*Original Sidebar Press creature. 2014 rules.*

- **Description:** A severed hand from an old burial, packed in salt and hung on a hook until Ossian's leftover necromancy sets it twitching. It scuttles like a crab and grips like a vise.
- **In this adventure:** [[Scene 02 — The Catacombs]], the kitchen fight (C2). Mindless: these hands are not the larder ghouls.
