# Kobold Pyrotechnic

*A soot-blackened kobold with singed whiskers whoops and lights a paper tube. Green and gold fire shrieks across the room.*

*Created by **GrimmGrixis**, Writers Guild.*

> *Small humanoid (kobold), lawful evil*
> **Armor Class** 12 · **Hit Points** 27 (5d6 + 10) · **Speed** 30 ft.
>

> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 7 (−2) | 15 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 8 (−1) | 7 (−2) | 8 (−1) |
>
> **Saving Throws** Dex +4 · **Skills** Acrobatics +4, Performance +1
> **Damage Resistances** fire
> **Senses** passive Perception 8 · **Languages** Common, Draconic · **Challenge** 1 (200 XP)
>
> ***Firesparkers.*** The pyrotechnic fights with Firesparkers, paper-and-reed tubes packed with colored fire. It starts each combat with one Firesparker lit and a pack holding 2d10 more (or 11). A lit Firesparker holds 1d6 + 1 charges (roll when it is lit), and each Firesparker attack spends 1 charge. When its lit Firesparker runs out of charges, the pyrotechnic can use its Light Firesparker bonus action to light another from its pack, even partway through its Multiattack.
>
> ***Pack Tactics.*** The pyrotechnic has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the pyrotechnic's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
>
> ***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the pyrotechnic has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
>
> ***Volatile Cargo.*** When the pyrotechnic drops to 0 hit points, the Firesparkers left in its pack go off in a grand finale. Each creature within 10 feet of it must make a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 7 (2d6) fire damage plus 3 (1d6) fire damage for every two Firesparkers left in the pack, to a maximum of 21 (6d6) fire damage, on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Multiattack.*** The pyrotechnic makes three Firesparker attacks, or one Dagger attack and two Firesparker attacks.
>
> ***Firesparker.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) fire damage.
>
> ***Dagger.*** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
>
> **Bonus Actions**
> ***Light Firesparker.*** The pyrotechnic lights a new Firesparker from its pack (see Firesparkers).

## Lore
A kobold war-crew is a warren's answer to a world of bigger folk: five trades and one plan. A [[Kobold Dragonfang]] leads it, [[Kobold Bulwark-Bearer|bulwark-bearers]] hold the line, [[Kobold Pot-Slinger|pot-slingers]] and pyrotechnics throw fire from behind the shields, and a [[Kobold Blistercoil|blistercoil]] carries the heavy weapon. Crews sell their work to a tinker-guild that pays in scrap, powder, and coin. They clear old mines, breach vaults, and fight the fights the guild doesn't want its name on. Where no guild hires them, a fire cult usually will.

The pyrotechnic is the crew's artillery and its showman. Pyrotechnics make the Firesparkers that warrens sell for festival nights, and in a fight they fire them flat across the floor, shrieking and whooping. Years of flash powder have burned the dark-sight out of their eyes. A pyrotechnic sees no better in the dark than a human does, yet it still squints in sunlight, so it relies on its own fireworks and on the crew's fire-slingers to light its targets. Every pyrotechnic dreams of its grand finale, and most get one.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** warrens, old mines, ruins, and the undercity of any town with a tinker-guild or a fire cult.
- **Encountered as:** one or two within a war-crew; alone as a hired entertainer, or as a lookout who signals with colored flares.
- **Signs:** spent paper tubes, scorch stars on the walls, burned-out patches of moss, and kobold songs with a lot of loud bits.

## Tactics
The pyrotechnic stays at long range, behind the shield wall, and fires three Firesparkers a turn at whichever target a pot-slinger has lit. It moves in close only to finish off a fallen enemy with its dagger. The crew keeps a wary distance from it, because Volatile Cargo burns everyone within 10 feet when it falls. A crew losing a fight will sometimes shove a badly wounded pyrotechnic forward into the enemy line to make that finale count.

**Suggested crew encounters** (four characters; adjusted XP per DMG p. 82):

**Running it:** roll the pack (2d10) when combat starts, or use 11, and track it on a spare die along with the charges in the lit Firesparker. Because every Firesparker holds at least 2 charges, the pyrotechnic never needs more than one Light Firesparker in a turn. When the pack is down to 7 or fewer, the finale shrinks below its 6d6 maximum, so a long fight makes it safer to kill. A lit Firesparker throws light like a torch, which is how the pyrotechnic fights in the dark at all.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A town council hires a kobold fireworks troupe for its founding-day festival, and the show is spectacular. *Twist:* the troupe is a war-crew, and the festival is cover for setting charges under the counting-house.
2. Colored flares rise from the hills every third night. *Twist:* a lone pyrotechnic, its crew dead, is signaling for a rescue that isn't coming, and it will guide the party anywhere in exchange for a ride home.
3. A fire cult wants a pyrotechnic's recipe for green fire and pays well for a live one. *Twist:* the cult means to feed the kobold to its idol, and the kobold knows it.

## Spoils
Volatile Cargo consumes the pack when a pyrotechnic falls. One taken alive still carries its remaining Firesparkers. Each is a one-use ranged attack for anyone (range 30/120 ft., 1d6 fire damage on a hit, using the improvised weapon rules) and sells for 2 gp. Its recipe tin is worth 25 gp to a fireworks maker or alchemist, and it carries 2d6 sp of crew wages.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review.

The pyrotechnic was written as one of a five-kobold war-crew for Greybeard's Ravnica campaign (the source sat in the Ravnica homebrew monster folder). The statblock itself named no guild or Ravnican place. The source-vault link and art embed were dropped, and the Lore gives the crew a generic tinker-guild (or a fire cult) as its patron in place of a Ravnican guild.

The submitted block has no darkvision, unlike every other kobold in the crew, though it keeps Sunlight Sensitivity. We kept it as submitted and explained it in the Lore (flash-burned eyes). *Contributor to confirm.*

| Change | As submitted | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Armor Class | 12 (natural armor) | 12 | Dex 15 gives AC 12 unarmored; "natural armor" added nothing |
| Firesparker charges and pack | spread across the attack text and a Notes section | one Firesparkers trait; one Firesparker starts lit; the pack is 2d10 (or 11) | Every rule now sits in the statblock where it's used |
| Lighting a Firesparker | "can draw and light another as a bonus action" inside the attack | **Light Firesparker** under **Bonus Actions**; usable partway through Multiattack | House format; makes a three-attack turn work when a tube runs out |
| Volatile Cargo damage | "(see table in notes)": 2d6 plus 1d6 per two Firesparkers in the pack, no maximum (up to 12d6) | 7 (2d6) plus 3 (1d6) per two Firesparkers, to a maximum of 21 (6d6) | A full pack of 20 dealt 42 fire damage to everyone within 10 feet, enough to kill most 3rd-level characters outright and to push the block two CRs high (see below). The average pack of 11 (7d6) loses only one die |
| Copy | "it's", "(-2)", "Small humanoid" | corrected; "Small humanoid (kobold)" | No rules change |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive:* 27 hp is the CR 1/8 band (7–35). AC 12 is one below the expected 13, so **defensive CR is 1/8**.
- *Offensive:* three Firesparkers deal 15 a round. Volatile Cargo at its 6d6 maximum deals 21 to two creatures once, adding 14 a round across three rounds, for 29, the CR 4 band (27–32). +4 to hit is one below the expected +5, so **offensive CR is about 3.5**.
- *Average:* about 1.8, which gives CR 2. That is within 1 of the stated CR 1, so **CR 1 stands**.
- *As submitted, with no maximum:* a full pack (12d6, 42 damage to two creatures) adds 28 a round, for 43, the CR 6 band; at +4 to hit that's offensive CR 5, and the average rises to about 2.6 (CR 3), two above the stated CR. The 6d6 maximum is what keeps the stated CR honest.
