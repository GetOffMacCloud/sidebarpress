## Lore
A clawcloud is a flock of ravens sworn to Iuz's priests and spies. They're called down by the [[Clawcloud (spell)|*clawcloud*]] spell or reared in Dorakaa's cathedral towers (WGR5 p. 11). The flock is a watcher before it is a weapon. It follows a party for days, at a distance, and peels off a bird at a time to report.

The ravens of the Vesve and the Sepia Uplands have learned to fear the clawclouds and scatter when one passes. Any old forester says that when the crows go quiet and one big flock stays, you are being watched.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** above roads, ridges and forest margins, wherever Iuz's people want eyes.
- **Encountered as:** one swarm shadowing a party; two at APL 4 if the party has been noticed.
- **Signs:** one flock of ravens that keeps pace with you; a single raven flying off with purpose; ordinary crows silent.

## Tactics
The swarm keeps its distance and watches. It uses Tail and Tell every few minutes, so each raven that flies off carries the party's last minute to Iuz's people. It fights only when ordered or cornered, and then goes for eyes.
**Running it:** the real danger is information. Every raven that reaches its keeper should change what the hunters do next. Reward the party for shooting down messengers, using cover, or traveling inside the blind spot of the [[The Gaoler's Link|Gaoler's Link]].

## Adventure Hooks
1. Ravens follow the party out of Ritterstadt. *Twist:* they're following the cargo, not the people, and the cargo is a decoy.
2. A raven lands on a PC's pack and says a word in a dead friend's voice. *Twist:* the friend isn't dead; he's a prisoner, and the flock heard him in Dorakaa.
3. A Vesve elf asks the party to kill a clawcloud's keeper. *Twist:* the keeper is a child.

## Spoils
None. A cut-down messenger raven sometimes carries a bone band on its leg with a priest's mark.

## Design Notes
**Remix** (Sidebar Press, 2026-11-05) of the MM swarm of ravens. It is the creature summoned by the WGR5 cult spell *clawcloud* (p. 11), whose ravens tail a party and report back.

| Change | Swarm of ravens | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hit Points | 24 (7d8 − 7) | 31 (7d8) | Con 10, so a sturdier flock |
| Perception | +5 | +5, with Mimicry added | Ravens mimic voices |
| Tail and Tell | — | added (bonus action) | WGR5's tail-and-report spell |
| Challenge | 1/4 | 1/2 | Reflects the added hit points (see below) |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: 7d8 averages 31; Dex +2 with proficiency +2 gives +4; Perception is Wis +1 with double proficiency (+5), so passive Perception is 15.
- *Defensive:* 31 hp with resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing (×2 at CR 1–4) is 62 effective, the CR 1/2 band (50–70). AC 12 is 1 below the expected 13, which doesn't move it. **Defensive CR 1/2.**
- *Offensive:* 7 a round, the CR 1/2 band (6–8). +4 is 1 above the expected +3, which doesn't move it. **Offensive CR 1/2.**
- *Average:* **CR 1/2.**

**IP:** "clawcloud" is a TSR name (WGR5). The Kestrel edition must rename it (suggestion: "tell-flock").
