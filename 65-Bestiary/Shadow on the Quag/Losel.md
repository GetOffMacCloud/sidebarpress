## Lore
Losels are an old crossbreeding of orc and baboon. They're too stupid to make good soldiers, too fierce to tame properly, and perfect as scouts. Iuz's forest armies use them on the Vesve margin to range ahead of the Jebli orcs and climb where orcs can't (WGR5 p. 29). They are pack creatures. They screech, swing and bicker, and they take orders only from orcs with whips.

Wood elves of the Vesve hate them more than they hate orcs, because a losel climbs and an orc doesn't. Elven tree-forts were built on the assumption that orcs couldn't reach them.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** forest margins and wooded hills on Iuz's frontiers.
- **Encountered as:** 3–6 in a scouting troop, often with an orc handler. APL 4: four losels and an orc is a medium fight.
- **Signs:** bark torn off in long strips high up the trunks; baboon-like droppings with bits of armor buckle in them; a shriek that's answered from two directions.

## Tactics
Losels drop from above, gang up on one target, and shriek the moment they see anything they can't kill quickly. They scatter up the trees when hurt, then come back.
**Running it:** the Signal Shriek is the real threat. Every shriek that gets out brings more of Iuz's scouts to the area, so give the party a reason to silence losels fast.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A Highfolk ranger offers a bounty on losel ears. *Twist:* some of the ears she has bought are elven.
2. A tame losel in a Ritterstadt cage was bought for the University's bestiary. *Twist:* it still shrieks every night, and something in the Vesve has started to answer.
3. A losel troop is stalking a caravan. *Twist:* they're tracking something in the caravan's cargo, not the caravan.

## Spoils
Nothing of value. Orc handlers carry 2d6 sp and a whip of knotted hide.

## Design Notes
**Harvested creature** (Sidebar Press, 2026-11-05). The losel is a TSR creature, and WGR5 names losels as Iuz's Vesve scouts (p. 29). It is converted here to 2014 5e. "Losel" is a TSR name, not SRD, so the Kestrel edition must rename it (suggestion: "baboon-orc").

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: 4d8 averages 18, plus 4 from Con is 22; Str +2 with proficiency +2 gives +4; skills check out; passive Perception 13.
- *Defensive:* 22 hp is the CR 1/8 band (7–35). AC 13 is expected. **Defensive CR 1/8.**
- *Offensive:* bite and claw for 9 a round, the CR 1 band (9–14). Pack Tactics counts as +1 to hit, so its effective +5 is 2 above the expected +3, which moves it one step. **Offensive CR 2.**
- *Average:* **CR 1/2.**
