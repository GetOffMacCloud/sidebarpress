## Lore
Kermin Mind-Bender doesn't like waiting for a charm to take. Whisper-leeches are his answer: small, bred things from the cellars under the Boneheart Citadel that crawl into a sleeper's bedding and talk all night in a voice the sleeper takes for their own. A bone tether holds a mind; a whisper-leech softens one first.

They are kept in waxed jars and fed on honey and blood. Tether-mages carry two or three, and use them on the targets too strong-willed for a simple *charm person*. Students would call that a bad sign.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** bedding, hoods, hatbands, the warm folds of a sleeping person's clothes.
- **Encountered as:** 1–3 in a sleeper's room; 2–6 loosed as a last-ditch defense from a mage's satchel.
- **Signs:** a bead of dried blood behind the ear; a person who wakes certain about something new; a waxed jar with a honey smear inside.

## Tactics
In a fight, a leech goes for whoever is nearest and smallest, attaches, and holds on. Charmed hosts don't swat their leech, and defend it.
**Running it:** leeches are a nuisance, not a killer. Use two or three alongside a tether-mage or sleepers to pull PCs out of the fight. Any PC can spend an action helping a friend pull one off.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A Probationer wakes up determined to transfer to "a better school in the north." *Twist:* a leech in her pillow put the idea there, but the school is real and has already written back.
2. Grain-sacks from the Landing carry a jar of leeches into town. *Twist:* the jar was meant for a different target, and the leeches got out.
3. A faculty member asks students to collect leeches alive for study. *Twist:* she wants to learn how to talk to people while they sleep.

## Spoils
A live leech in a jar (Physic & Alchemy or Arcane Studies pays 10 credits for one; a fence pays 15 gp). A waxed jar with Abyssal letters scratched in the seal.
