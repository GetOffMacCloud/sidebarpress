## Lore
A tethered sleeper is an ordinary person wearing someone else's will. A bone pin hidden in a collar, a hatband or a hem keeps them charmed for weeks or months. They work, eat and gossip as they always have. When the handler needs hands, they get up from the table without finishing their sentence.

Sleepers don't know what they are. They remember their handler's suggestions as their own ideas, and they defend those ideas with the stubbornness of the honest. That makes them excellent couriers, terrible witnesses and heartbreaking enemies.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** wherever a tether-mage works. Ports and market towns are ideal, because people come and go and nobody looks twice.
- **Encountered as:** 1–4 with a handler, or 2–6 sent alone to grab or block. APL 3: four sleepers is an easy fight on paper and a hard one morally.
- **Signs:** a regular customer who orders something different; a person who answers a question nobody asked; a bone pin glimpsed in a collar (DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)).

## Tactics
Sleepers fight like the frightened amateurs they are, but without the fear. They grab, block doorways and pile on. They don't flee.
**Running it:** treat every sleeper as an innocent. Remind the table that the ferryman has a family. Reward PCs who pull pins, fight with nonlethal blows (*knock out* at 0 hit points), or bring the [[The Gaoler's Link|Link]] close to wake them. A party that kills sleepers should hear about it from the town afterward.

## Variants
- **Long-Tethered.** A sleeper tethered for more than three months has 1 level of exhaustion when freed and needs a long rest before it remembers its own name with certainty.

## Adventure Hooks
1. Three of Ritterstadt's people have been "not themselves since spring." *Twist:* one of them is faking it, to get close to the handler and kill him.
2. The party frees a sleeper who begs them to put the pin back. *Twist:* while tethered he was happier than he has been in years.
3. A sleeper has been tethered so long that the handler is dead. *Twist:* the sleeper is still carrying out the last order.

## Spoils
The [[Bone Tether]] itself (evidence). A sleeper's ordinary belongings: 1d6 sp, a work knife, a letter or two they don't remember writing.

## Design Notes
**New creature** (Sidebar Press, 2026-11-05). Built from the commoner. It gains hit points, leather armor, two attacks, and the tether rules, and sits at CR 1/4 so a group of them can block a handler's escape at APL 3.

| Change | Commoner | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hit Points | 4 (1d8) | 32 (5d8 + 10) | A sleeper must survive long enough to be freed instead of killed |
| Armor Class | 10 | 12 (leather armor) | Working folk in work leathers |
| Attacks | one club, +2 | two cudgel attacks, +3 | Gives a group real pressure at APL 3 |
| Traits | — | Sleeper's Calm, Tether-Bound, Handler's Window | The tether is the creature |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: 5d8 averages 22, plus 10 from Con is 32; Str +1 with proficiency +2 gives +3; passive Perception 9.
- *Defensive:* 32 hp is the CR 1/8 band (7–35). AC 12 is 1 below the expected 13, which doesn't move it. **Defensive CR 1/8.**
- *Offensive:* two cudgels for 8 a round, the CR 1/2 band (6–8). +3 is expected. **Offensive CR 1/2.**
- *Average:* **CR 1/4.**

**Minion Pack:** use the Enforcer minion at the scene's APL if the crowd rushes in, and remind the table that they are innocents.
