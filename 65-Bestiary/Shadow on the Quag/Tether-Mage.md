## Lore
Tether-mages are the low rung of a long ladder. Kermin Mind-Bender, the Bakluni diviner of Iuz's Greater Boneheart, takes gifted children from across the empire and its borders and trains them in the mind's arts (WGR5 p. 86). Most never rise. The ones who survive are sent out with a satchel of bone pins, a familiar, and an order: find something, or someone, and bring it home.

They work through other people. A tether-mage charms a merchant, a ferryman or a porter, plants one of Kermin's prepared pins in the victim's collar, and from then on wears that person like a coat. The victims don't know. They remember the mage's suggestions as their own ideas.

The University's mirror image is the uncomfortable part. A tether-mage is what a gifted student becomes when the school is Dorakaa.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** towns, ports, trade roads. Rarely seen in the open; always found behind someone else.
- **Encountered as:** alone with a familiar (usually a quasit or a raven) and 1–4 [[Tethered Sleeper|tethered sleepers]]. APL 2: one mage and one sleeper. APL 3: one mage, its familiar, and two sleepers. APL 4: add a hired sword.
- **Signs:** a respectable person suddenly "not himself"; letters carried by a boy because the sender won't pass a gate; a familiar that giggles where no one stands.

## Tactics
The mage fights from behind its puppets. It opens with *hold person* on the most dangerous melee character, uses Puppet's Shield every round it can, and spends its action on Splinter of Will from 30 to 60 feet away. It never closes to melee by choice.
**Running it:** the mage values its own skin over the mission until the mission is nearly won. At half hit points it runs, and it always has a planned exit (a boat, a back door, a potion). If cornered, it bargains, and the bargain is usually an offer to recruit the PCs.

## Named Example
**Dawud Vesharan**, alias "Herr Fesch" (SP-GQ01, SP-GQ02, SP-GQ05). A Bakluni man of about 24, taken from a Ket schoolroom at nine and trained under Kermin. He carries a *potion of water breathing* as his exit, and his familiar is **Snick**, a quasit (MM p. 63). He has a burn scar across his left palm from the one time he tried to hold a relic. He wants a place among Kermin's mid-level mages and dreads his master's attention more than death. Unlike most tether-mages, he can be turned (see SP-GQ05).

## Adventure Hooks
1. A Perrender merchant hires students to recover a "family heirloom" from the University. His clerk does the talking. *Twist:* the merchant has never heard of the heirloom when he isn't wearing his coat.
2. Three townsfolk start finishing each other's sentences. *Twist:* they share one handler, and the handler is the new tutor everyone likes.
3. A tether-mage asks the party for help escaping her master. *Twist:* she's telling the truth, and her master already knows where she is.

## Spoils
A satchel of unplanted bone tethers (1d4; each a [[Bone Tether]] awaiting a target), a spellbook (*charm person, command, disguise self, hold person, mage armor, suggestion*), 3d10 gp in mixed coin, and a cipher letter to "my master in the Heart of Bone."

## Design Notes
**New creature** (Sidebar Press, 2026-11-05). Built for the Shadow on the Quag arc to replace the reflavored cult fanatic in the SP-GQ01 outline. It keeps the same CR and XP (2, 450), so Part 1's encounter math is unchanged.

| Change | As outlined | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Statblock | cult fanatic, spells reflavored | Tether-Mage (new) | An enchanter needs ranged psychic damage and a puppet mechanic, not a melee dagger fanatic |
| Spellcasting | slot-based (MM) | 1/day each (MotM style) | Matches the FGU import mode and is easier to run |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: 13d8 averages 58; Int +3 with proficiency +2 gives +5 to hit and DC 13; Dex +2 gives the dagger +4; saves and skills check out; passive Perception 11.
- *Defensive:* 58 hp is the CR 1/2 band (50–70). AC 15 (with *mage armor*, cast before the scene) is 2 above the expected 13, which moves it one step. **Defensive CR 1.**
- *Offensive:* two Splinters for 16 a round, the CR 2 band (15–20). +5 is 2 above the expected +3, which moves it one step. **Offensive CR 3.**
- *Average:* **CR 2.**

**Canon basis:** Kermin Mind-Bender, his mind-control specialty, and his recruitment of gifted children (WGR5 p. 86). Tether-mages as a rank, the bone tethers, and Vesharan are house material. *Ruling (Greybeard, 2026-11-05):* Kermin sits in the Greater Boneheart by 576 CY.
