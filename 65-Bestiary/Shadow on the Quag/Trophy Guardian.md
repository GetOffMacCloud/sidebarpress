## Lore
Collectors who also have something to guard sometimes hire a wizard with a sense of humor. A trophy guardian is a mounted beast (a pike, a boar's head, a bear on its hind legs) bound to wake and defend one room. Thieves look for traps in the locks and never at the wall.

The Archive Pike in Ritterstadt hangs in the sealed stacks. A long-gone antiquarian mounted it, and nobody now living remembers who. Its watchword, "*by leave of the Registrar*," is passed from Registrar to Registrar, and the occasional Probationer finds it out.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** collections, archives, lodges, rich houses.
- **Encountered as:** alone, or a matched pair (APL 4: a pike and a gar).
- **Signs:** a mounted beast with a fresh, unfaded glass eye; scratches on the floor under the board; a mounting plate with a small brass mouth-hole for the maker's word.

## Tactics
It blocks the only exit and bites whoever is carrying the most. It doesn't chase beyond its room. Once it has grappled someone, it drags them back toward its mount.
**Running it:** each round of combat is noisy. In a heist, use it as a Heat clock rather than a killer. The watchword ends the fight instantly, so give the players ways to learn it.

## Variants
- **Boar's Head.** Medium; its Bite becomes Gore (piercing) and it has no swim speed. Tail Sweep becomes Thrash (5 ft.).
- **Hunting-Lodge Bear.** Use the same block with a climb speed of 30 ft. and Tail Sweep replaced by Maul (bludgeoning, 5 ft.).

## Adventure Hooks
1. A burglar's corpse lies in a locked trophy room with nothing missing. *Twist:* the stuffed boar killed him, and its owner forgot to mention it to the insurer.
2. A guildhall's guardian has lost its watchword because the only man who knew it died. *Twist:* his ghost will trade it for a favor.
3. Students are sent to dust the Archive's high shelves. *Twist:* someone has changed the watchword.

## Spoils
The guardian's glass eye (a 25 gp piece of rock crystal) and, for anyone willing to haul it, the mount itself: a curiosity worth 50 gp to a collector, or 5 credits to the Antiquities department.

## Design Notes
**New creature** (Sidebar Press, 2026-11-05). Modeled on animated armor (Antimagic Susceptibility and False Appearance kept) and reshaped as a beast-form construct for SP-GQ01's sealed stacks.

| Change | Animated armor | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Form | Medium armor | Large mounted beast | The Archive Pike |
| Attacks | two slams | Bite (grapple) and Tail Sweep (recharge) | A guardian that holds thieves rather than killing them |
| Watchword | — | added | Gives the heist a non-combat solution |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: 7d10 averages 38, plus 14 from Con is 52; Str +3 with proficiency +2 gives +5 and DC 13; 2d6 + 3 averages 10 and 1d8 + 3 averages 7.
- *Defensive:* 52 hp is the CR 1/2 band (50–70). AC 14 is 1 above the expected 13, which doesn't move it. **Defensive CR 1/2.**
- *Offensive:* Tail Sweep on two creatures in round 1 (14), then two bites (10 each), averages 11 a round, the CR 1 band (9–14). +5 is 2 above the expected +3, which moves it one step. **Offensive CR 2.**
- *Average:* **CR 1.**
