## Lore
The great counting-houses of Schwartzenbruin don't trust men to guard their money, because men can be bribed. They trust iron clerks: seated, ledger-holding constructs made by Clatspur dwarf smiths a century ago and handed down with the houses. Each clerk is bound to one counting-room. It notices everything that moves there and forgives nothing.

Perrenders joke that an iron clerk is the only honest banker in the canton. Thieves don't laugh. A clerk can't be bribed, charmed or frightened, and its alarm bell carries across a city square.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** counting-houses, guild treasuries, a rich merchant's strongroom.
- **Encountered as:** alone, seated at a desk behind a grille. A very rich house has two.
- **Signs:** a seated iron figure in the corner of a counting-room, never moving; ink-blackened iron fingertips; a ledger left open at the same page.

## Tactics
The clerk stays seated until something in its room moves. Then it rises, clamps the nearest thief, and fists anyone who tries to free them. It won't leave the room.
**Running it:** in a heist, the clerk is the lock. The best way past is not to move anything while it's watching. That means switching documents faster than it can register, or getting it out of its room (it has to be lured, not ordered). Give clever players room to beat the Counting Eye with a swap rather than a fight.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A counting-house wants its iron clerk tested by "thieves of good character." *Twist:* the house is insured, and the owner wants the test to fail.
2. An iron clerk stops working in the middle of an audit. *Twist:* someone moved its ledger, and the clerk now guards the wrong room.
3. The party needs one document from a clerk's room. *Twist:* the clerk's maker carved the way to switch it off on the clerk's own ledger.

## Spoils

- *Defensive:* 60 hp is the CR 1/2 band (50–70). AC 16 is 3 above the expected 13, which moves it one step. **Defensive CR 1.**
- *Offensive:* two fists for 14 a round, the CR 1 band (9–14). +5 is 2 above the expected +3, which moves it one step. **Offensive CR 2.**
- *Average:* CR 1.5, rounded up to **CR 2** for Shackle Clamp, a restraining effect the damage math doesn't count.
