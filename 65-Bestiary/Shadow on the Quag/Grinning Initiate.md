## Lore
Iuz's priesthood rules by fear: every rank fears the one above it, and the lowest ranks fear everyone (WGR5 p. 7). The initiates sent abroad are the old-guard faithful from the homeland, not converts. They carry the cult's own spells, wear any face they please, and grin. Nobody outside the cult knows whether the grin is devotion or terror.

In 576 CY, many of them serve **Panshazek**, Iuz's priest-spy inside the Horned Society (WGR5 p. 88). Panshazek's people work the trade between Molag and the west. They despise Kermin Mind-Bender's mages as clever children who don't believe in anything, and the feeling is mutual. When the two arms of Iuz want the same thing, the initiates would rather burn it than let Kermin have it.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** pilgrim roads, counting-houses, inns on the trade routes from Molag.
- **Encountered as:** one initiate with 2–4 hired thugs (who don't know whom they serve) or 2–4 [[Tethered Sleeper|tethered]] dupes on loan. APL 4: two initiates is a hard fight.
- **Signs:** a holy symbol worn under the shirt, never shown; skulls scratched small on inn doorposts; a pilgrim who can't name the shrine she's walking to.

## Tactics
The initiate opens with *hold person* or *command* ("Grovel!"), then closes and flails, saving Grin of the Old One for whoever is about to be healed. It uses *bonechain* to prepare an ambush ahead of time, and *blackhand* to strip a paladin's or cleric's wards.
**Running it:** initiates don't surrender to anyone they think they can frighten. They break and run only when another initiate is watching them fail, because their superiors will hear of it either way.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A Schwartzenbruin counting-house has a new pious clerk. *Twist:* she's there to steal the same charter the party is after, for a rival master.
2. Pilgrims on the lake road are walking to a shrine that doesn't exist. *Twist:* the shrine is a bonechain ambush site, and the pilgrims are its bait.
3. An initiate surrenders to the party to escape her own superior. *Twist:* her superior's spy is in the party's hireling.

## Spoils
A holy symbol of Iuz (a grinning skull on a cord; carrying it openly is a crime in Furyondy and Highfolk, and a risk anywhere in Perrenland), 2d10 gp, a pilgrim's badge from a shrine that doesn't exist, and orders signed with a Horned Society seal.

## Design Notes
**New creature** (Sidebar Press, 2026-11-05). A field priest of Iuz for the arc's rival arm, built from the priest's frame with a melee kit and the WGR5 cult spells.

| Change | Priest | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alignment | any | chaotic evil | The old-guard faithful of Iuz |
| Attacks | one mace | two flails plus Grin of the Old One | A front-line cultist |
| Spells | cleric slots | at will plus 1/day (MotM style), with the cult spells *blackhand* and *bonechain* | WGR5 pp. 9–13 |
| *disguise self* at will | — | added | WGR5 p. 83: Iuz's specialty priests gain a change-self power |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: 11d8 averages 49, plus 22 from Con is 71; Str +2 with proficiency +2 gives +4; Wis +2 gives DC 12 and +4 with spells; skills and saves check out; passive Perception 12.
- *Defensive:* 71 hp is the CR 1 band (71–85). AC 14 is 1 above the expected 13, which doesn't move it. **Defensive CR 1.**
- *Offensive:* two flails (12) plus Grin (7) is 19 a round, the CR 2 band (15–20). +4 is 1 above the expected +3, which doesn't move it. **Offensive CR 2.**
- *Average:* CR 1.5, rounded up to **CR 2** for its control spells (*hold person*, *command*, *bonechain*), which the damage math doesn't count.

**Canon basis:** the priesthood's culture of fear, its homeland old guard, and the change-self power (WGR5 pp. 7, 83); Panshazek as Iuz's spy in the Horned Society before the Wars (WGR5 p. 88). *Ruling (Greybeard, 2026-11-05):* Master Kezrin (SP-GA01) belongs to Panshazek's arm, the rival of Kermin's mages.
