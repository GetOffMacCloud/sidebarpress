## Lore
Lake Quag keeps its dead. Canon says undead walk in the grey fogs at the lake's center, where ships vanish, and ghost ships haunt the places their crews died. The fog-drowned are the crews. They are fishermen, bargemen, Rhennee and smugglers, risen in the fog and walking it still, looking for someone to take down with them.

They don't leave the fog willingly. In winter, when the lake freezes and the fog lies on the ice, they walk the ice instead of the water. Wolf Nomad iceskimmer crews carry fire for that reason.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** the fog banks over Lake Quag, its islands, and its winter ice.
- **Encountered as:** 2–4 together. APL 4: four fog-drowned is a hard fight in fog and a medium one in clear air.
- **Signs:** wet footprints on dry ice; a smell of weed and cold iron; voices in the fog calling names.

## Tactics
The fog-drowned close in from several sides, grab, and hold on. They don't let go of a grappled victim until it stops moving. Each one works on a single victim.
**Running it:** fire and clear air are the counters. Torches, a *gust of wind*, or the [[Thaw Line]] spell change the fight. Let the players discover that the fog heals them.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A Ritterstadt fisherman says he saw his drowned brother on the ice. *Twist:* he did, and the brother is trying to warn him of something.
2. A Rhennee barge wants an escort through the fog. *Twist:* her crew drowned there last year, and she's taking them home.
3. The fog-drowned are gathering around one island. *Twist:* someone living is on that island, and the dead are standing guard.

## Spoils
Sodden purses with 2d6 cp and lake-tarnished silver. Now and then a ring or a ship's token that a Ritterstadt family would pay to have returned.

## Design Notes
**New creature** (Sidebar Press, 2026-11-05). It gives canon's Lake Quag undead (WoG; LGG) a body for SP-GQ05's fog-bound island.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: 6d8 averages 27, plus 18 from Con is 45; Str +3 with proficiency +2 gives +5 and DC 13; 1d10 + 3 averages 8 and 2d4 averages 5.
- *Defensive:* 45 hp, plus 9 for Fog-Fed (3 × 3), is 54 effective, the CR 1/2 band (50–70). AC 12 is 1 below the expected 13, which doesn't move it. **Defensive CR 1/2.** A single resistance doesn't count toward the multiplier.
- *Offensive:* 8 from the grip plus 5 drowning is 13 a round, the CR 1 band (9–14). +5 is 2 above the expected +3, which moves it one step. **Offensive CR 2.**
- *Average:* **CR 1.**

**Canon basis:** undead in the lake's central fogs and ghost ships near where crews died (WoG 1980/1983; LGG). The fog-drowned as a kind is house material.
