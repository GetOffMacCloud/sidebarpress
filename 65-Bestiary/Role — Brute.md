# Brute

*The one the others get out of the way of. It doesn't dodge; it doesn't need to.*

A **brute** hits harder than anything else in the fight and shrugs off the first few blows. It's easy to hit and doesn't care. Brutes give the party an obvious, frightening target and punish anyone who stands toe to toe with them.

> [!example]+ Applying the Brute Role
> Adjust any statblock:
> - **AC** −2 (minimum 10). Swap armor for hide, scraps, or bare skin.
> - **Hit Points** +25% (add hit dice).
> - **Damage** +50% on its main attack: add a damage die, or raise the die size.
> - **Attack bonus** unchanged.
>
> **Signature traits (pick one or two):**
> - ***Overwhelm.*** When the brute hits a creature with a melee attack, the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC 8 + proficiency bonus + Strength modifier) or be pushed 10 feet away or knocked prone (brute's choice).
> - ***Reckless.*** At the start of its turn, the brute can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during that turn, but attack rolls against it have advantage until the start of its next turn.
> - ***Cleave.*** When the brute reduces a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack, it can move up to half its speed and make one melee weapon attack as part of the same action.
> - ***Brutal Critical.*** The brute scores a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20, and a critical hit deals one extra damage die.
>
> **CR:** usually **+1**. The damage increase raises offense by one step; the lower AC and higher HP roughly cancel. Recheck with the DMG (p. 274).

## Running a Brute
- Point it at the party's front line and let it trade blows.
- Use Overwhelm to break formations: knock the fighter away from the wizard, then the skirmishers pour in.
- A brute retreats only when it's alone. Brutes with a leader fight to the death.

## Brute Minions
A brute minion keeps **one** signature: **Death-Blow** (below) is the best fit, because a minion dies so easily that its threat has to land even as it falls. Its attack uses the Minion Table's fixed damage (see [[04 — Minions & Roles]]).

Built from the [[Gash-Marked Raider]]: 1 hit point, Minion and Death-Blow added, Aggressive and Blood Tally dropped, and the axe set to the CR 1 fixed damage.

## Design Notes
Brutes trade defense for offense. In the DMG's terms, −2 AC lowers defensive CR by one step, +25% HP raises it by about one, and +50% damage raises offensive CR by one or two. The net is usually +1 CR. A brute with Reckless plays harder than its number, because advantage raises its hit rate by roughly a quarter.
