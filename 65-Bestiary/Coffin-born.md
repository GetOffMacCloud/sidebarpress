## Lore
A coffin-born is someone who was buried alive and died screaming where no one could hear. The scream never left. It stayed in the lungs, packed in with the soil that came through the split lid, and the hate stayed with it: hate for the mourners who walked away, the sexton who didn't listen, and every living thing that breathes easily above ground.

It makes no sound at all. It doesn't breathe, its feet don't fall, and it doesn't cry out when it is cut. It waits in its grave, or in any grave, for as long as it takes, and it feels footsteps through the earth. When it finally opens its mouth, everything it couldn't say comes out at once, as a choking blast of soil and sound that hurts the mind more than the ears.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** churchyards, potter's fields, crypts, and the rubble of collapsed tombs; anywhere the ground is loose enough to dig through.
- **Encountered as:** alone, or in twos and threes in a yard where several burials went wrong at once, such as after a plague, a flood, or a hasty mass grave.
- **Signs:** a coffin lid scratched from the inside; a grave that is open but has no spoil heap beside it; a churchyard where the birds have stopped singing.

## Tactics
A coffin-born waits in a grave or under rubble until its tremorsense picks up someone standing close, then rises behind them. It opens with Dirt-Choked Scream if two or more creatures are in the cone, then rends whoever is nearest. It holds prone creatures down with Pull Down, and its Rend attacks have advantage against them. It fights to destruction and never flees.

**Running it:** silence is its signature. Describe the fight in sight and touch only, with no sound effects, until the Scream. Then make that one sound loud.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A plague village is digging up its hasty graves for reburial in consecrated ground. *Twist:* the digging party keeps finding coffins with scratched lids, and one of the diggers is the man who did the burying while his patients were still breathing.
2. A crypt-robber offers to split a noble's tomb treasure with the party. *Twist:* the noble's heir was walled in alive with the treasure, and the robber knows it. He needs someone else to open the door first.
3. A widow begs the party to stand watch over her husband's grave for three nights. *Twist:* she isn't afraid of grave-robbers. She buried him alive, and she wants to be sure he stays down.

## Spoils
A coffin-born carries whatever it was buried with: grave goods worth 2d10 gp, often a ring or locket that identifies who it was. The scratches on its coffin lid sometimes spell out a name.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: 9d8 + 18 averages 58; proficiency +2 with Str +3 gives +5 to hit; both DC 13 saves are Str-based (8 + 2 + 3); 1d8 + 3 averages 7, 1d6 averages 3, and 4d6 averages 14; Stealth +4 and passive Perception 10 are correct.
- *Defensive:* 58 hp is the CR 1/2 band (50–70). Necrotic and poison immunity is too narrow to adjust for. AC 13 matches the expected 13, so **defensive CR is 1/2**.
- *Offensive:* round 1 is the Scream on two targets (28); rounds 2 and 3 are two Rends (20 each). That averages 23 damage a round, the CR 3 band (21–26). +5 to hit is one above the expected +4, which doesn't move it, so **offensive CR is 3**.
- *Average:* halfway between CR 1 and CR 2. Rounded up for Pull Down (which keeps targets prone and gives its Rend attacks advantage) and the Scream's deafness, that gives CR 2. The stated **CR 3** is within one step and stands. It plays as a hard CR 2 in the open and a full CR 3 when it gets its ambush.
