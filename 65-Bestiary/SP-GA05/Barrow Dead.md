- **Description:** Flan dead from the barrows under Carn Aedh, woken by the Hollowing Toll: brown bone, rotted leather, a copper torc at every neck, bronze leaf-blades green with age.
- **In this adventure:** One rises per toll in [[SP-GA05 — E4 — Thirteen Tolls|E4]] and reaches the ring 2 rounds later. Their copper torcs match the dead walking out of the Vesve ([[The Copper-Torc Dead]]).

## Lore
The Flan of the Uplands buried their war-dead around their kings, each with a copper torc and a bronze blade, and rang a holy bell over them once so they would sleep. The barrow dead are those sleepers, woken out of turn.

They were laid down by a priesthood of the sun and they remember it. Fire and lamplight are the last things they saw before the barrow was closed, and they go to a light first. Whether that is hatred or longing, no scholar has got close enough to ask.

They are not the Reaper's by right. [[Hollowing Toll]] gives a Nerullite priest their obedience for as long as the bell is his. Their king, [[Cathal Red-Torc]], owes that priest nothing at all.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** barrow fields, howes, stone rings and old Flan shrines in hill country.
- **Encountered as:** one per toll of a hollowing bell, arriving singly; or 4–12 standing guard in a barrow that has been opened and robbed.
- **Signs:** grass browning in a widening ring; a barrow door pushed out from inside; green stains on the turf where bronze has lain; a single low bell-note with no bell in sight.

## Tactics
The barrow dead attack whoever carries fire or a light source, then whoever holds the thing that woke them. Half use shortbows from the barrow slope while the rest climb. They hold formation out of habit: two abreast, shields side (they carry none now).

If the bell that laid them down is consecrated, every barrow dead within a mile falls at once.

**Running it:** tell the table what the dead are looking at. A party that douses its torches changes the fight; a party that throws one changes it more.

## Variants
- **Copper-torc dead of the Vesve.** The same block without Bell-Bound. No bell laid them down, so nothing simple lays them down again.
- **Barrow captain.** Maximum hit points (20), a bronze spear with reach 10 ft., and the other barrow dead within 30 feet have advantage on saving throws against being turned.

## Adventure Hooks
1. **The robbed howe.** Someone with a spade and a map is selling grave-silver. *Twist:* the barrow dead aren't hunting the robber. They are following the coins, one buyer at a time.
2. **The second bell.** Dead in copper torcs are walking out of the northern forest. *Twist:* the torcs match Carn Aedh's, and the old kingdom had more than one bell.
3. **A light in the window.** A shepherd's hut on a barrow slope is besieged every night and untouched every morning. *Twist:* the dead only want the lamp put out. They stop the night it is.

## Spoils
A copper torc (2 gp as metal, 15 gp to Antiquities with a note of where it was found) and a bronze leaf-blade too corroded to use (5 gp to a collector). The Yati consider both grave goods; returning them to the barrow earns Old Faith goodwill, and selling them in a hill market earns the opposite.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Defensive: 13 hp is the CR 1/8 band; AC 13 is on target; vulnerability to bludgeoning is not adjusted for.
