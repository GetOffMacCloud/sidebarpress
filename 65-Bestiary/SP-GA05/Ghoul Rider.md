# Ghoul Rider

*Something in riding leathers crouches in the hay loft, a pewter token swinging at its throat. Its tongue is too long. It is waiting for one of you to be alone.*

*Homebrew variant of the ghoul (MM p. 148); Loft Ambusher and Hunger Before Courage added. 2014 rules.*

- **Description:** The Hollen Ford couriers drank the most after a long ride. The Tithe made them hungry rather than slow. They still wear riding leathers and pewter warden tokens.
- **In this adventure:** [[SP-GA05 — E2 — The Hollen Ford Relay|E2]]: they hide in the stable loft and wait for the wardens to split up.

## Lore
When [[Reaper's Tithe]] is cast at its full strength, those who drank deep do not rise as the slow dead. A courier in off a long road, a horse, a harvester at noon: the curse takes the thirst they had and keeps it. They rise on the third night as ghouls.

A ghoul rider remembers its work. It knows the relay, the loft ladder, which stair creaks. It remembers that travellers come to the door tired and split up to see to horses and fires. It does not remember why any of that used to matter.

The Grey Sickle counts ghoul riders as a failure of the rite, since they serve hunger and not the count. Brother-Reaper Ansgar leaves them where they rise, as a lock on the door behind him.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** waystations, stables, ferry houses and inns on any road where a full-strength Tithe was cast.
- **Encountered as:** one or two, with the [[Penny-Risen]] of the same well. They do not cooperate with the slow dead but use them as bait.
- **Signs:** a saddled horse that won't leave a yard; a cold beacon; a log with no entries for days; gnawed tack; riding boots at the foot of a ladder.

## Tactics
The riders hide above (Stealth +4) and wait until the party is divided. Each drops on a different target to spread paralysis, then both pile onto whoever is paralyzed. At 5 hit points or fewer a rider breaks off for one round, into water or shadow, and comes back.

**Running it:** say aloud when a claw fails to take hold of an elf. Paralysis is the threat, and the table should learn the rule from the fiction. Let the ghoul's retreat read as hunger and not fear: it circles, it doesn't run.

## Variants
- **Ghoul mount.** A riding horse that drank deep: use the ghoul's traits on a riding horse block (speed 60 ft., Hooves in place of Bite). It still comes to a whistle.

## Adventure Hooks
1. **The dark beacon.** A relay hasn't signalled for four nights. *Twist:* the log's last entry thanks the pilgrims who blessed the well.
2. **The late courier.** A sealed dispatch arrives a tenday late, carried by a rider who won't dismount or lower her hood. *Twist:* she still wants to deliver it. She can't remember what happens after.
3. **Tokens.** The Field Office wants two pewter warden tokens brought home from a silent post. *Twist:* the things wearing them still answer to their names.

## Spoils
Whatever the rider carried in life: a warden token (worth nothing, and everything to the Field Office), dispatch pouch, good boots. A ghoul rider's claws hold their taint for a day; a DC 13 Wisdom (Medicine) check harvests enough for one dose of a paralytic (DC 10 Constitution save or paralyzed for 1 minute, repeating the save each turn), 50 gp to the wrong buyer.

## Design Notes
**Sidebar Press homebrew** for SP-GA05, built on the Monster Manual ghoul.

| Change | Ghoul (MM) | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skill added | — | Stealth +4 | The ambush is the encounter |
| Trait added | — | Loft Ambusher | Drop attack with advantage on the first attack |
| Trait added | — | Hunger Before Courage | A written retreat-and-return, so the fight has a rhythm |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Defensive: 22 hp is the CR 1/8 band; AC 12 is one under expected, no step. Offensive: Bite at 9 damage is CR 1; +2 and +4 to hit bracket the expected +3. Average: about CR 1/2. Paralysis is uncounted control and Loft Ambusher adds a round of advantage, which justifies rounding up to the printed ghoul's CR 1. **CR 1 is kept.** Internals check: 5d8 = 22; Claws +4 = +2 proficiency +2 Dexterity; Bite +2 as printed in the MM (no proficiency); DC 10 as printed; Stealth +4.
