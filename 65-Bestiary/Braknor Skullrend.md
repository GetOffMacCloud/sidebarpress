## Lore
Braknor Skullrend was born a mortal beast of the mazes, a horned brute who ruled a labyrinth of tunnels and ate whatever wandered into it. He was clever enough to know he would die there. When a devil came down the corridors offering him a longer life, he took the bargain without asking the price. The price was his soul, his maze, and an eternity of service. Braknor considers it the best trade he ever made.

He came back from Hell larger, redder, and fireproof, with a greataxe forged in the infernal pits that leaves wounds no bandage can hold. Now he is a warden for hire. Cults, warlords, and other fiends pay him to guard their prisons, vaults, and mazes, and he guards them well, because he never forgets a path and never stops enjoying the hunt. He keeps the skulls of everyone who has tried to escape him and splits each one with the same blow, which is how he earned his name.

Braknor is cruel but not careless. He honors a contract to the letter, and he will let a prisoner walk free if the paperwork says so. He will then follow them out of the gate and kill them on the road, which the contract never mentioned.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** labyrinths, prison-vaults, underground temples, and the ruined strongholds of fallen cults; any place with corridors and a door someone wants kept shut.
- **Encountered as:** alone, or as the warden of a vault with a handful of cultists, imps, or hell hounds under his command.
- **Signs:** split skulls set in niches along a corridor; deep gouges in stone floors where something charged and missed; wounds on the dead that bled long after the fighting stopped.

## Tactics
Braknor opens with a charge down the longest straight corridor he can find, aiming to pin a target against a wall for the stun. Once enemies bunch up around him, he uses Blasting Slam to knock them flat, then goes Reckless and hacks at whoever is prone. He spreads infernal wounds across as many enemies as he can, then stays on the one who can't heal. He knows every turn of his maze and retreats through it to break line of sight, then comes back around for another charge.

**Running it:** track infernal wounds on index cards in front of each player. Watching the number climb every time Braknor hits makes the healers' choices matter.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A temple pays the party to recover a relic from a sealed vault. Braknor guards it, and his contract says he must surrender it to anyone carrying the vault key. The temple gave the party the key, then sold Braknor the right to kill whoever carries it out.
2. Prisoners have begun escaping from an infernal labyrinth, and every one is found dead on the road a day later. Braknor isn't failing his duty; he has found a loophole that lets him hunt.
3. A devil hires the party to kill Braknor. His soul is the devil's property, and Braknor has been quietly selling it to a rival on the side.

## Spoils
Braknor's **infernal greataxe** is a +1 greataxe that deals an extra 1d6 fire damage on a hit. It requires attunement by a creature of evil alignment, and any other wielder takes 1d6 fire damage each time it hits. His horns, ground down, are worth 250 gp to an alchemist. His vault holds the belongings of everyone he has caught: 3d6 × 100 gp in coin and gear, and one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table C.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 125 hp × 1.5 (nonmagical weapon resistance at expected CR 5–10) gives about 187 effective hp, the CR 8 band (176–190). AC 16 matches the expected 16, and Magic Resistance counts as +2 AC, so **defensive CR is 9**. Over three rounds (Blasting Slam on two targets for 54, then two greataxe hits for 52 each, plus a growing infernal wound of 6 and then 12), it averages about 59 damage per round, the CR 9 band (57–62). +8 to hit is one above the expected +7, so **offensive CR is about 9.5**. *Average:* about 9.25, which gives **CR 9**.
- *Now:* 126 × 1.5 = 189, still the CR 8 band, so **defensive CR is 9**. Over three rounds (Blasting Slam for 54, then two greataxe hits for 54 plus the wound for 6, then 54 plus 12), it averages 60 damage per round, the CR 9 band. +8 to hit and DC 16 sit at or just above CR 9's expectations, so **offensive CR is about 9.5**. *Average:* about 9.25, which gives **CR 9**.
- CR 9 is within 1 of the submitted CR 8, so per house policy the **stated CR 8 stands**. He is a strong CR 8; for a tight encounter budget, treat him as a hard CR 8 or count him as CR 9.

**IP substitutions:** "the Nine Hells" became "Hell" / "hellfire" to keep the entry setting-agnostic. Labyrinthine Recall, Charge, and Reckless follow the SRD minotaur and are fine to keep.
