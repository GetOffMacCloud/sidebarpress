# Minions & Roles

A monster's statblock says what it *is*. Its **role** says what it *does* in a fight: hit hard, hold a line, harry the flanks, rain arrows from the back, pin the party down, strike from hiding, or drive the others on. This chapter gives you seven **role templates** that turn any statblock in this book (or any other) into a creature with a clear job. Each role also comes with a **minion** version: a 1-hit-point fodder creature that fights in packs, so a single encounter can feel like a horde without running a hundred hit-point totals.

## The Seven Roles

| Role | Job in the fight | Defense | Offense | Signature move |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Role — Brute\|Brute]] | Hits hardest, soaks blows | Low AC, high HP | High damage, modest accuracy | Overwhelm, Reckless, Cleave |
| [[Role — Enforcer\|Enforcer]] | Holds the line, protects allies | High AC | Modest damage | Hold the Line, Interpose, Challenge |
| [[Role — Skirmisher\|Skirmisher]] | Hits and runs, flanks | Medium AC, mobility | Burst on the move | Hit and Run, Charge, Nimble Escape |
| [[Role — Artillery\|Artillery]] | Damages from range | Low AC, low HP | Ranged and area attacks | Steady Aim, Volley, Keep Distance |
| [[Role — Controller\|Controller]] | Hinders, slows, reshapes the field | Medium | Low damage, strong effects | Hamper, Zone, Hex |
| [[Role — Lurker\|Lurker]] | Strikes from hiding, then vanishes | Low HP, stealth | One big hit | Ambush, Sneak Strike, Fade |
| [[Role — Leader\|Leader]] | Makes the others better | Medium | Low damage | Command, Rally, War-Cry |

Each role note has the same parts:
1. **Applying the role**: adjustments to an existing statblock, and a short list of **signature traits** (pick one or two).
2. **Running it**: how the role plays at the table.
3. **Minions of this role**: the one trait a minion of this role keeps.
4. **A worked minion**, built from a creature in this book, with FGU import text so you can drop a pack straight into a fight.

## Minions

A **minion** is a creature built to die. It hits like a real threat but falls to a single blow. Minions fill a room fast, make the party feel heroic, and give area spells something to do.

> [!example]+ The Minion Template
> Apply to any statblock. The minion keeps its base creature's CR for **AC, attack bonus, and save DCs**, so it still hits and still has to be hit.
> - **Hit Points** 1.
> - ***Minion.*** If the minion takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.
> - **One attack.** The minion loses Multiattack, legendary actions, lair actions, and any recharge ability. It keeps **one** attack and, at most, **one** trait or reaction: its role's signature (see each role).
> - **Fixed damage.** Its attack deals the fixed damage in the Minion Table for its CR (no damage roll), plus any rider (prone, doused, marked). Fixed damage keeps a pack of twelve fast to run.
> - **XP:** one quarter of the base CR's XP.

When you convert an existing creature, keep its own AC, attack bonus, and DCs. They're usually close to the table. Use the table's **fixed damage**, not the creature's dice.

### Minions in Encounters
- **XP:** add each minion's XP (one quarter of its base CR) to the encounter total.
- **Multiplier:** when counting monsters for the DMG's encounter multiplier, count **every four minions as one creature** (round down).
- **Group size:** use 4–12 at a time. Fewer than four isn't worth the bookkeeping; more than twelve turns into a slog of rolling.
- **The 4:1 rule:** four minions of a CR are worth one standard creature of that CR, in both XP and the multiplier count.
- **Tier tip:** at higher levels, give minions a reason to matter beyond damage: a death effect (the [[Role — Artillery|pot-slinger's satchel]]), a sacrifice (the [[Role — Enforcer|bulwark-bearer's Interpose]]), or a buff to the boss when they fall.

### Running Minions at the Table
- Roll **one attack roll per group** of up to four minions targeting the same creature, if you want speed: the attack hits a number of times equal to the number of minions if it beats AC, or none if not. (Optional; individual rolls are fine for small packs.)
- Track minions with simple tokens. In Fantasy Grounds, add them through the import text in each role note: 1 hit point means any hit drops them.
- Describe their deaths in a word or two. The players should feel each swing land.

## Combining Role and Minion
A **role** is an overlay for a full creature. A **minion** is an overlay that cuts a creature down to one hit point. You can use both at once: an enforcer **minion** keeps the enforcer's signature reaction and dies using it. You can also mix them: a typical war-band is one leader (full creature), two brutes (full), and eight minions split between enforcers and artillery.

### Suggested mixes (4 PCs, checked against DMG pp. 82–83)

| APL | Mix | Base XP | Count (minions ÷ 4) | Adjusted XP | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 [[Goblin Battle-Drummer]] (leader) + 4 [[Role — Brute\|Gash-Marked Raider minions]] | 50 + 200 = 250 | 1 + 1 = 2 → ×1.5 | 375 | Hard (300–399) |
| 2 | 1 [[Bone-Tally Chanter]] (controller) + 6 Gash-Marked Raider minions | 100 + 300 = 400 | 1 + 1 = 2 → ×1.5 | 600 | Hard (600–799) |
| 3 | 2 [[Iron-Marked Wolf\|Iron-Marked Wolves]] (skirmishers) + 6 [[Role — Skirmisher\|Iron-Marked Wolf minions]] | 400 + 300 = 700 | 2 + 1 = 3 → ×2 | 1,400 | Hard (900–1,599) |
| 5 | 1 CR 5 leader + 4 CR 4 minions | 1,800 + 1,100 = 2,900 | 1 + 1 = 2 → ×1.5 | 4,350 | Hard (3,000–4,399) |
| 9 | 1 CR 7 brute or leader + 4 CR 6 minions | 2,900 + 2,300 = 5,200 | 1 + 1 = 2 → ×1.5 | 7,800 | Hard (6,400–9,599) |

Minion XP adds up fast: every extra four minions also bumps the multiplier. Add minions four at a time and recheck the total.
