# Wicker Colossus

*A thirty-foot man of woven willow and wheat steps over the hedge. In the cage of its chest, a lantern burns and someone is screaming.*

> [!example]+ Wicker Colossus (CR 9, 5,000 XP)
> *Huge construct, unaligned*
> **Armor Class** 14 (natural armor) · **Hit Points** 133 (14d10 + 56) · **Speed** 20 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 20 (+5) | 9 (−1) | 18 (+4) | 3 (−4) | 8 (−1) | 1 (−5) |
>
> **Damage Immunities** acid, poison, psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks that aren't adamantine
> **Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned
> **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9 · **Languages** understands its makers' hymn but can't speak · **Challenge** 9 (5,000 XP)
>
> ***Burning Wicker.*** Fire damage doesn't harm the Colossus. Instead, it ignites for 1 minute. While burning, its Slam attacks deal an extra 7 (2d6) fire damage, and a creature in its Chest-Cage takes 3 (1d6) fire damage at the start of each of the Colossus's turns. Any cold damage, or a large amount of water, puts the fire out. A burning Colossus that reaches a wooden structure sets it alight.
>
> ***Chest-Cage.*** The Colossus carries one Medium or smaller creature in a willow cage in its chest. A creature inside is restrained and has total cover. It can escape with a DC 15 Strength check (an action), or it can be cut free from outside: the cage has AC 15 and 30 hit points and is immune to poison and psychic damage.
>
> ***Berserk.*** Whenever the Colossus starts its turn with 60 hit points or fewer, roll a d6. On a 6, it goes berserk. On each of its turns while berserk, it attacks the nearest creature it can see. If no creature is within reach, it attacks an object, preferring its own cage. It stays berserk until it is destroyed or regains all its hit points.
>
> ***Immutable Form.*** The Colossus is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.
>
> ***Magic Resistance.*** The Colossus has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
>
> ***Magic Weapons.*** The Colossus's weapon attacks are magical.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Multiattack.*** The Colossus makes two Slam attacks.
>
> ***Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage, plus 7 (2d6) fire damage if it is burning. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or its hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken. The target dies if this reduces its hit point maximum to 0. The reduction lasts until it is removed by the *greater restoration* spell or other magic.
>
> ***Haste (Recharge 5–6).*** Until the end of its next turn, the Colossus magically gains a +2 bonus to its AC, has advantage on Dexterity saving throws, and can use its Slam attack as a bonus action.

## Lore
Harvest cults build a wicker colossus once a generation, for the year when the old pact comes due. Every family weaves a piece of it: a willow rib, a sheaf for the thigh, a braided wheat-plait for the hand. The finished giant is consecrated with a hymn, and on the appointed night it walks. It carries the year's offering in its chest-cage to wherever the pact is paid.

A colossus has no will of its own. It goes where the hymn sends it and takes what the hymn names. Those who try to stop it by burning it learn the oldest joke of the harvest cults: the colossus was *made* to burn.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** farmland, open fields, and the roads between a pact-village and its place of offering.
- **Encountered as:** alone, sometimes with cultists riding its shoulders. The cult leads it in procession.
- **Signs:** willow stripped from every riverbank for miles, a field cut early and left in sheaves, a village that is weaving and won't say what.

## Tactics
The colossus walks its offering to the destination and swats aside anything in the way. It doesn't pursue fleeing creatures. When it goes berserk, it turns on its own cage, so a party that hurts it badly risks killing the person they came to save. Fire makes it hit harder and also cooks its passenger.

## Named Example
**The Sheaf's Colossus at Thornbury** ([[SP-HAL-04-The-Tithe-of-Thornbury]]). It is built new every hundred years and walks the guest to the Tithe Barn, but it doesn't go inside. **Scaling:** at APL 8 it has 100 hit points. At APL 10, two **cult fanatics** (MM p. 345) ride its shoulders 30 feet up, casting *hold person*, and they fall with it.

## Adventure Hooks
1. Every family in the valley is weaving. The party's hireling has been told she's "the guest of honor" at the festival.
2. A colossus was built and never walked, because the village chose not to finish the hymn. A rival has found the last verse.
3. A druid wants a colossus destroyed *without* fire. She says the ash is what the pact really feeds on.

## Spoils
The colossus's lantern-heart, an iron cage holding a coal that never goes out, works as an *everburning torch* that can light any fire. The hymn-sheaf woven into its head can be read as a ritual text, and it names the pact.

## Design Notes
**Changes from the Clay Golem block:** Burning Wicker replaces Acid Absorption; Chest-Cage added; Berserk now targets the cage. Huge size and reach 10 ft. fit the thirty-foot frame. Every statistic that affects CR is unchanged.

**CR verification:** The clay golem is CR 9 in the MM, and the same chassis is kept. The DMG formula overrates golems because of their broad immunities, so the MM rating is used. **Burning raises its damage.** Two Slams deal 32 damage per round, or 46 while burning, which puts its offense around CR 7–8 when it is set alight. That matters at APL 8. If the party is likely to use fire, start the encounter with the colossus at 100 hit points (the SP-HAL-04 APL 8 scaling).
