## Lore
Most chimeras die young. The three heads quarrel, the dragon's fire scars the lion's lungs, and the beast burns itself out within a few decades. A few don't. A chimera that survives its first century grows huge, its hide bleaches to the grey-white of cold ash, and its heads stop quarreling and start *planning*. The dragon head sleeps then. It lies folded against the chest, half-lidded, and wakes only when the beast is badly hurt. Old hunters call these survivors **pale chimeras**, and they don't hunt them.

A pale chimera understands Draconic and often serves a dragon, a fire giant, or a cult that mistakes it for a dragon's herald. It can also melt its extra heads and wings into its body and walk as a great white lion or goat. That is how herders on the high pastures lose a flock without ever seeing what took it.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** high mountains, volcanic hills, ruined fortresses, and the open grassland below them.
- **Encountered as:** alone, or with a dragon or fire giant that it serves. A pale chimera never tolerates another chimera in its territory.
- **Signs:** scorched, ash-white hair on thornbushes; goats found dead with a single serpent-bite; lion tracks that stop in the middle of a field where something took wing.

## Tactics
The chimera opens from the air with Fire Breath, then lands among the most heavily armored enemies. It bites with the lion and serpent and claws to knock them prone. Melee attackers set off its smoke plume. When it drops below half its hit points and the dragon head wakes, it fights without restraint. It flees only from overwhelming radiant or cold magic, and it always comes back.

**Running it:** announce Draconic Fury aloud. The dragon head's eyes open, and the table should feel the fight shift.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A shepherd swears a great white lion has been taking her flock. A ranger who followed its tracks found them ending at a cliff edge, with no body at the bottom.
2. A fire-giant forge-lord sends a pale chimera to collect tribute from the valley each autumn. This year, the valley refuses to pay.
3. An alchemist will pay a fortune for the venom of a pale chimera's serpent head, taken while the dragon head sleeps.

## Spoils
The serpent head's venom sacs hold 1d4 doses of a potent poison (a DC 18 Constitution save, or 21 (6d6) poison damage and poisoned for 1 hour), worth 300 gp each. Its ash-white hide can be made into *armor of fire resistance*. A lair holds the bones and gear of everyone who hunted it: 4d6 × 100 gp in coin and gear, and one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table F.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild) as *Haltorix the Pale Chimera*. Per the bestiary's villain rule, the entry is the generic **Pale Chimera**, and Haltorix is its named example. The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review.

**Why CR 15, not 12.** As submitted, Haltorix was rated CR 12 but played like CR 14–15. Its +12 attack bonus was four above the CR 12 expectation (and itself didn't match CR 12's proficiency of +4), its Multiattack dealt about 81 damage a round, and its resistances put its defense at about CR 14. Following the Gluttonwretch precedent, we kept its attacks and moved it to the CR they actually support.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 231 hp × 1.25 (nonmagical weapon resistance at expected CR 11–16) gives about 289 effective hp, the CR 15 band. AC 16 is two below the expected 18, so **defensive CR is 14**. Over three rounds (Fire Breath on two targets for 90, then Multiattack for 81 and 81), it averaged about 84 damage per round, the CR 13 band. +12 to hit is four above the expected +8, so **offensive CR is about 15**. *Average:* about **14.5**.
- *Retuned:* defensive CR is unchanged at **14**. Over three rounds (Fire Breath on two targets for 108, then Multiattack for 81 and 81), it averages 90 damage per round, the CR 14 band (87–92). +11 to hit is three above the expected +8, so **offensive CR is about 15.5**. Draconic Fury raises later rounds to 90. *Average:* about 14.75, which gives **CR 15**.
