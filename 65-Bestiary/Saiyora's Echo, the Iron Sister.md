## Lore
Saiyora was the middle of three sisters who kept a mortuary order's catacomb, and she held its Blade. The order fought by an old code: one champion against one, face to face, and never a blade raised against a back. When a warlord's company forced its way into the upper crypts, she met them on the stair to the sanctum and called for champions. They sent them, one at a time, and she won six duels before dawn.

She never heard a bell. What she heard, as the sixth champion fell, was the sanctum doors grinding shut behind her, and then her sister's rite of quiet sealing them. Her Echo believes that [[Mortenia's Echo, the Silent Sister]] locked her out to die so the sanctum could be sealed, and that [[Velka's Echo, the Veiled Sister]], who should have been at her side, was off chasing a thief. After the sixth duel, the company stopped sending champions and came at her all at once. She died on the stair still calling for the seventh.

Of the three Echoes, Saiyora is the only one who isn't cruel. She still keeps the code, and she still guards the stair. Her Echo isn't sure any more whether she is keeping the living out of the sanctum or keeping her sister in. Neither of her sisters knows what the warlord's sexton stole that morning, and Saiyora would hear it from no one but a champion who has beaten her.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** the stair or gate before a sealed sanctum, deep in a catacomb or beneath a ruined mortuary chapel.
- **Encountered as:** alone. Her sisters haunt the same catacomb: Mortenia in the sanctum behind her and Velka in the dark passages beyond.
- **Signs:** six sets of old arms and armor laid out neatly on the steps, each with a single killing wound; a rusted greatsword's scrape worn into the stone; a voice on the stair asking who will stand for the seventh.

## Tactics
Saiyora names the strongest-looking melee combatant as her Rival and fights that creature with everything she has. If her Rival hangs back behind others, she uses her bonus action to name someone in reach instead. She saves Sundering Cleave for when enemies crowd her on the stair, and then she presses her Rival while its armor is rent open. She spends Honor's Reserve only against her Rival's best attacks. When she drops to one knee the first time, she expects to rise. A cleric who has seen her kneel will know what to do with radiant damage.

**Running it:** point at the Rival out loud ("She levels her sword at you"). If one champion steps forward and the rest of the party stands back, play Saiyora to the code: she fights only that champion and doesn't use Sundering Cleave. If the champion beats her, she yields the stair. This is the encounter's honorable solution, and it should be possible to find from her words alone.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A fencing school sends its best student to "the stair that never yields" as a rite of passage. He hasn't come back. *Twist:* he is alive. Saiyora beat him, and by the code he owes her the seventh duel, so she won't let him leave. He is starving on the landing, waiting for a champion.
2. A respectable baron hires the party to recover his family's "lost property" from the catacomb. *Twist:* he is the warlord's descendant. Saiyora recognizes his crest on the party's writ of passage and names whoever carries it as her Rival.
3. A paladin wants to lay Saiyora to rest honorably by winning the seventh duel. *Twist:* if he wins, the stair is open, and nothing stands between the town and the sanctum of [[Mortenia's Echo, the Silent Sister]].

## Spoils
If she is beaten in a fair duel and yields, Saiyora gives her greatsword to the victor: a notched *+1 greatsword*. If she is destroyed, the sword is only rusted iron. On the steps lie the arms and armor of the six champions she killed: 2d6 × 100 gp in arms, armor, and coin, and one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table C.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** For all three sisters, the house counts a feature that brings her back from 0 hit points once, at the hit points she returns with.
- *Defensive:* 195 hp, plus 60 from Vengeful Echo, is 255. Applying ×1.25 for necrotic and nonmagical weapon resistance (expected CR 11–16) gives about 319 effective hp, the CR 17 band. AC 18 is one below the expected 19, too little to move it, so **defensive CR is 17**.
- *Offensive:* over three rounds (Sundering Cleave on two targets for 54, then two rounds of three Greatsword hits on her Rival at 21 each, for 63), she averages 60 damage per round, the CR 9 band (57–62). +9 to hit is two above the expected +7, so **offensive CR is 10**. Honor's Reserve's riposte isn't counted.
- *Average:* **13.5**. It rounds down to 13, because her disadvantage against everyone but her Rival is a real weakness the math doesn't count. That is within 1 of the stated 12, so she stays **CR 12**.

**Running the sisters together.** The three Echoes are built to be met one at a time: Velka in the approaches, Saiyora on the stair, and Mortenia in the sanctum. Two of them at once (16,800 XP, ×1.5 for two monsters, so 25,200 adjusted XP) is deadly even for four 14th-level characters.

**IP:** none. No setting-specific names appeared in the source.
