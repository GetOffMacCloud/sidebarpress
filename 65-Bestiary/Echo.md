## Lore
Echoes are the townsfolk of a place caught in a time loop, a curse, or a bargain gone wrong. Their souls have been taken and stored, and their bodies keep doing whatever they did on the last ordinary day. Visitors at first notice only that the town is very quiet and very friendly. Then they notice that everyone says the same things in the same order. Then they notice that the baker sets out six loaves every night and has never sold one.

When something breaks the routine late in the loop, Echoes *snap*. The hollow body fills with the panic of a soul that knows it is missing, and it turns on whatever broke the pattern.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** a village or town district under a loop or curse.
- **Encountered as:** the whole population. In a fight, four Echoes snap together.
- **Signs:** identical conversations, food that is set out and never eaten, dogs that won't enter the town, and a clock that always reads the same hour at dusk.

## Tactics
At rest, Echoes don't fight; a struck Echo just staggers and resumes. A snapped Echo attacks the person who broke its routine, then anyone nearby, and it uses Clutching Hands first. Snapped Echoes don't pursue past the town's edge.

**Running it:** Echoes are innocents. Reward parties who drop snapped Echoes with nonlethal damage. Killing one only resets it at dusk anyway, but the players don't need to know that at first.

## Named Example
**The villagers of Vesper Hollow** ([[SP-HAL-03-The-Night-That-Doesnt-End]]). Their souls hang in bone lanterns in the Hollow Choir. They snap after 11 PM.

## Adventure Hooks
1. The party stops for the night in a town where everyone is kind. In the morning, the innkeeper greets them as new arrivals.
2. A widow says her husband came home from the next village last month and hasn't changed his shirt, his supper, or his story since.
3. A PC finds their own name in a town's register, dated forty years ago, in their own handwriting.

## Spoils
None from an Echo. Breaking the soul-vessels restores the town, and the grateful townsfolk remember everything.

## Design Notes
**Changes from the Commoner block:** Wis 6 and Cha 4; immunity to charmed and frightened; Hollow, Reset, and Snap added. **Snapped Echo:** the Ghoul block with its type changed from undead to humanoid, Still Living added, and its attacks renamed.

**CR verification:** The Echo matches the commoner at **CR 0**. The Snapped Echo keeps every ghoul statistic that matters to CR (AC 12, 22 hp, the same attacks, and paralysis), so it stays **CR 1**. Losing the undead type removes the turning weakness, which is a mild buff that doesn't change the CR band.
