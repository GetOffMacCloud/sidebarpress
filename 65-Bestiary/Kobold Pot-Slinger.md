## Lore
A kobold war-crew is a warren's answer to a world of bigger folk: five trades and one plan. A [[Kobold Dragonfang]] leads it, [[Kobold Bulwark-Bearer|bulwark-bearers]] hold the line, pot-slingers and [[Kobold Pyrotechnic|pyrotechnics]] throw fire from behind the shields, and a [[Kobold Blistercoil|blistercoil]] carries the heavy weapon. Crews sell their work to a tinker-guild that pays in scrap, powder, and coin. They clear old mines, breach vaults, and fight the fights the guild doesn't want its name on. Where no guild hires them, a fire cult usually will.

The pot-slinger is the crew's grenadier, and usually its youngest member. Each slinger mixes its own pots and guards the recipe, and the warren names it by its payload: acid, fire, or poison. Crews prize a fire-slinger most, because its pots set targets glowing for the pyrotechnic, whose eyes are burned out. A crew that knows its business faces its bulwark shields with scale that matches its slingers' pots, so a bad throw only stings the wall.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** warrens, old mines, ruins, and the undercity of any town with a tinker-guild.
- **Encountered as:** two or three within a war-crew; alone only as a scout or courier.
- **Signs:** shards of glazed clay, acid-pitted stone, puddles that smoke, and a whiff of lamp oil and sulfur on the draft.

## Tactics
The pot-slinger stays behind a bulwark-bearer's shield, where Bulwark gives it half cover, and lobs Shatterpots at enemies bunched against the shield wall. It aims at a target standing next to another enemy to catch both in the splash. When a melee fighter reaches it, it uses Scurry to Disengage and slip back behind the crew, or to Hide if there is cover. A fire-slinger dousing a hidden rogue is the crew's answer to stealth.

**Running it:** choose each slinger's payload before the session and note it beside its initiative entry. Doused lasts until the end of the affected creature's next turn, so a fire pot thrown on the slinger's turn keeps a target lit through the rest of the crew's turns. Volatile Satchel goes off when the slinger falls, so a slinger killed inside the shield wall splashes its own crew.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A potter in the lower town has sold glazed jars by the hundred to a "tinker" who pays in copper wire, and the last order is due tomorrow. *Twist:* the potter's quiet apprentice is a former pot-slinger in a hooded coat, and it will trade the crew's plans for safe passage out of town.
2. Sheep on the hill farms are turning up acid-burned but uneaten. *Twist:* the slingers aren't raiding. They are testing a new batch of pots on the guild's orders, and the real target is the millpond dam.
3. A pot-slinger is captured alive and begs to be handed to the city watch. *Twist:* its satchel is still full, its crew has sworn to silence it, and the watch-house cells are packed with prisoners.

## Spoils
A fallen slinger's satchel is a mess of broken clay, but 1d4 − 1 pots survive. Each is a one-use thrown weapon (range 20/60 ft.) that deals 2d6 damage of its payload type on a hit and splashes as Shatterpot (DC 13). An alchemist pays 10 gp a pot, or 25 gp for the slinger's greasy recipe card. Each slinger also carries 2d6 sp of crew wages.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive:* 13 hp is the CR 1/8 band (7–35). AC 14 is one above the expected 13, not enough to move it, so **defensive CR is 1/8**.
- *Offensive:* Shatterpot deals 7 to its target and 3 to one other creature in the splash (the area effect covering two targets), 10 a round. Volatile Satchel adds about 2 a round across three rounds (3 to two creatures, once). That's 12, the CR 1 band (9–14). +5 to hit is two above the expected +3, so **offensive CR is 2**.
- *Average:* about 1.06, which gives CR 1. That is within 1 of the stated CR 1/2, so **CR 1/2 stands**. The slinger hits like a CR 1 creature and dies like a CR 1/8 one, and its low hit points are the point.
