## Lore
The Gash-Marked (see [[Gash-Marked Raider]]) believe that every war-band owes the deep a count. A band that climbs out of the Gash must fill its tally, a fixed number of the dead and the taken, before it can claim land of its own. The chanter keeps that count. It strings a knucklebone onto its cords for every death, friend or foe, and it sings the running total aloud in battle so that every raider knows how far the band has left to go.

When a chanter points and names a creature, it is adding that creature to the count before the fact. The mark glows a cold grey, and every raider, [[Gash-Marked Houndmaster|houndmaster]], and [[Tunnel Scarhound|scarhound]] in the band turns toward it. When one of the band's own falls, the chanter sings the fallen warrior's bones into the tally, and the band surges forward on the strength of the new number.

Chanters answer to the warlord, but only just. [[Grakh Unscarred]] wears eleven tally-lines because his chanters agreed eleven times that his count was full. A warlord who loses his chanters' support soon finds his count disputed.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** tunnel networks and root-caverns below old forests, and the hill country and villages near a tunnel mouth.
- **Encountered as:** one or two with a war-band of Gash-marked raiders, always behind the front rank.
- **Signs:** knucklebones strung on cords and hung from branches over a trail; doorposts with fresh tally-marks and a single bone nailed above them; a low, counting chant heard from underground.

## Tactics
The chanter stays 10 feet behind the raiders and lashes past them with its Bone Scourge. It opens with Marking Chant on the most dangerous-looking foe, especially an invisible or hidden one, and uses it again whenever it recharges. It wants its allies close together, so that when one falls, Tally of Wounds reaches the rest. If the raiders around it die, it flees and tries to reach another band.

**Running it:** the chanter is a force multiplier. Alone, it is a CR 1/2 nuisance. Behind four raiders, it decides who dies first.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A chanter's bone-strings turn up in an abandoned camp, with the village's names cut into the bones. The count is one short: one villager has already been marked and doesn't know it, and they glow faintly in the dark.
2. A captured chanter offers to "uncount" the village and lift the band's claim. Uncounting requires a replacement name, and the chanter chooses one of the party.
3. Two chanters in the same war-band dispute the count, splitting it in half. The party can tip the quarrel either way, but the winning chanter's count includes the party's patron.

## Spoils
A chanter carries a bone scourge (a whip of bone-studded cord), hide armor, 3d6 sp, and 1d4 tally-cords strung with dozens of knucklebones. A creature that studies a tally-cord for 10 minutes and succeeds on a DC 13 Intelligence (Religion) check can read the count it records: the band's raids, in order, and how many fell at each. Returning those bones to the families of the dead earns the party goodwill and, often, a reward.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review.

**Origin.** Written for Greybeard's campaign *The Chronicle*, Session 1: *The Deeproot Cradle*. Its setting references (Ravnica) have been removed.

**Why CR 1/2, not 2.** The chanter has the hit points of a CR 1/4 creature and deals 8 damage a round, a CR 1/2 output. Its real strength is support: Marking Chant and Tally of Wounds make the raiders around it hit more often, and that value shows up in their kills, not on its own line. At CR 2, it would cost an encounter 450 XP for about the threat of one raider. At CR 1/2, it is priced as what it is, a force multiplier. CR 1/2 still has proficiency +2, so none of its bonuses change.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive:* 39 hp is the CR 1/4 band (36–49). AC 13 matches the expected 13, so **defensive CR is 1/4**.
- *Offensive:* the Bone Scourge deals 8 damage a round (5 slashing + 3 necrotic), and Marking Chant deals none: the CR 1/2 band (6–8). +4 to hit is one above the expected +3, and DC 12 is one below the expected 13; neither is enough to move it, so **offensive CR is 1/2**.
- *Average:* (0.25 + 0.5) ÷ 2 = 0.375, exactly halfway between CR 1/4 and CR 1/2. We round up, because Marking Chant (advantage for allies) and Tally of Wounds (temporary hit points and advantage) are uncounted support effects. That gives **CR 1/2**. The stated CR 2 is two steps higher (1/2 → 1 → 2), so it is re-rated. The numbers are identical as submitted and as re-rated.

**IP substitutions:** Ravnica (in the Languages line) removed.
