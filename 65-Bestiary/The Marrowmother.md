## Lore
Nobody remembers what the Marrowmother was before she died. The oldest story in the hill villages says she was a giant spider that nested in a plague pit and grew fat on the marrow of the dead, until her hunger outlived her. What hangs in the dark now is Huge and hollow. Her own legs are splinted with the long bones of horses and people. Her body is a cage of ribs with nothing inside, and the grey silk that binds it all together is spun from marrow.

She isn't cruel. She has no more thought in her than a spider has. But she keeps a larder, and she knows every corpse in it. When the larder is threatened, she *mothers* the dead: she throws silk over them and pulls them up again, grafted and stitched into her brood. Antlerwracks charge, coursehounds run prey down, maulbones drag it home, and marrow hunters shoot it from the ferns. Every brood creature is Marrow-Bound. While she is alive and near, holy symbols that would send a skeleton running hardly slow it down.

Hunters who have faced a [[Bone Beast Template|bone beast]] pack often mistake her brood for one. They aren't the same. A bone beast is a single animal raised whole and bound to a relic. A brood creature is a patchwork of many dead, and the only thing holding it together is her silk. Kill the Marrowmother and her brood keeps fighting, but it fights like any other mindless undead.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** deep ravines, old mine workings, collapsed barrows, plague pits, and the caves under old battlefields.
- **Encountered as:** alone in her web-hung larder, with two to four brood creatures on guard. A pack of [[Rot-Wargen]] often scavenges at the edges of her territory.
- **Signs:** graves opened from below; carcasses picked clean of their long bones but not their meat; grey threads strung between trees at head height that smell of marrow-fat; and tracks that don't match from one foot to the next.

## Tactics
The Marrowmother fights from the ceiling. She opens with Grave Silk on the strongest melee fighter, then bites the restrained target with advantage. She uses Lash to strike from 15 feet away, out of reach of maces and hammers, and she saves Bone Spray for when the party bunches up. Whenever someone falls, and whenever a brood creature finishes off a target, she spends her legendary actions on Reknit. If she takes a heavy bludgeoning blow, she climbs out of reach and lets her brood screen her until her frame stops ringing.

**Running it:** mark every corpse on the map, because each one is a brood creature waiting to happen. When a hammer lands for 20 or more, describe her ringing like a cracked bell and her legs locking up. That is the table's tell that bludgeoning works.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A gravedigger reports that the newest graves in the churchyard are empty, opened from beneath. *Twist:* the village has been feeding her for a generation, quietly carrying its dead to the old mine, because the brood keeps the wolves away. The elders will lie to protect the bargain.
2. A hunting lord's lost hounds come home from the ravine, stitched with antler and bone. *Twist:* one coursehound wears his missing heir's signet ring grafted into its jaw. The heir is in the larder, wrapped in silk and still alive.
3. A necromancer hires the party to bring back a spool of grave silk. *Twist:* the silk is the last component of a binding relic. With it, the necromancer means to make the Marrowmother obey him, as a pack of bone beasts obeys its crown.

## Spoils
The webs of her larder hold 1d4 spools of grave silk. Each is 50 feet of rope that is as strong as silk rope but burns like paper, and a necromancer or alchemist pays 25 gp for each one. Her hollow legs can be cut into eight bone shafts, each worth 10 gp as a material for staffs and wands. The larder's dead carry 4d6 × 10 gp in coin and gear, plus one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table B.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source file doesn't name the adventure the Marrowmother first appeared in.

Reknit raised "Stitched Horrors" in the source, without a statblock. The four brood creatures contributed alongside her (Antlerwrack, Coursehound, Maulbone, and The Hunter) share a frame, a Marrow-Bound trait, and a "Grafted Attack," so they are clearly those forms. Reknit now rolls among them, and "The Hunter" is filed as the [[Marrow Hunter]].

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** The internals all check out: 16d12 + 48 averages 152; proficiency +3 with Str +4 gives +7 to hit; Grave Silk's +5 is Dex +2 plus proficiency; both DC 14 saves are Con-based (8 + 3 + 3); saves, skills, and passive Perception 14 match. Every damage average is correct.
- *Defensive:* 152 hp plus 20 for one use of Legendary Resistance (expected CR 5–10) gives 172 effective hp, the CR 7 band (161–175). Necrotic and poison immunity is too narrow to adjust for, and her bludgeoning vulnerability isn't counted. AC 15 matches the CR 7 expectation, so **defensive CR is 7**.
- *Offensive:* round 1 is Bone Spray on two targets (34) plus two Lashes (14), for 48. Rounds 2 and 3 are Multiattack (27) plus two Lashes (14), for 41 each. That averages 43 damage per round, the CR 6 band (39–44). +7 to hit is one above the expected +6, which doesn't move it, so **offensive CR is 6**.
- *Average:* 6.5. Grave Silk and Reknit are uncounted, but so are Brittle Frame and her vulnerability to the party's commonest weapons. The stated **CR 6** is within one step and stands.

**IP substitutions:** none.
