## Lore
Shrikes are small birds with a butcher's habits. They catch more than they can eat and hang the rest on thorns to keep. Somewhere on an old road, a shrike nested in the thorn tree that served as the crossroads gallows, and it fed on what the hangman left. It grew. It learned the voices of the condemned, then the voices of the people who came to cut them down, and it learned that a voice calling for help brings more food than any lure.

Folk call it the Ninefold Voice because it never keeps more than nine voices at a time. Each new victim on the thorns gives it a new voice and takes away the oldest. It calls travelers off the road in the voices of children, lost friends, and the recently dead. It is a beast, not a schemer, but it has learned exactly what people will walk toward. Its great thorn tree, the Gallows, is its larder. While the thorns hold meat, it can't bring itself to fly far from them, and it heals as it guards them.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** open country with scattered thorn trees, crossroads, old gallows hills, and moorland roads.
- **Encountered as:** alone, at its Gallows. It doesn't share its larder.
- **Signs:** a great black thorn tree hung with impaled carcasses (sheep, deer, and worse); grey-and-black feathers as long as a sword; a voice on the moor calling a traveler's name.

## Tactics
The Voice opens with Shrike's Stoop down the length of the party, then fights from the air at reach with its Beak. It uses Stolen Voice every round on whoever is about to attack it. It grapples the smallest creature with its Talons and uses Carry Off to hang them on the Gallows, which keeps Larder Bond running. It flies back out of reach with Flyby and Wingbeat. Its hollow bones are its weakness: a big hit or a grapple can bring it down, and on the ground it loses most of its advantages. When it drops below a third of its hit points and the Gallows is empty, it flees.

**Running it:** the Gallows is the key to the fight, and the players should see that early. Let them notice the Voice's wounds closing while bodies hang on the thorns. Pulling down the impaled (or cutting the tree) ends Larder Bond and frees the Voice to fly high, which turns the fight into a running battle.

## Adventure Hooks
1. Travelers on the moor road hear a child calling for help from somewhere off the track. *Twist:* the child is real and still alive, impaled on the Gallows. The Voice is calling in her voice to lure her rescuers, and she has watched it long enough to know its weakness.
2. A town council wants its old crossroads gallows-tree cut down at last. *Twist:* the council has been quietly feeding the bird its condemned for years to keep it off the road, and it is getting hungry.
3. A bard wants the voice of a famous singer who died on the moor road, and he believes the Voice still carries it. *Twist:* the ninth voice it uses belongs to someone the party knows, who is supposed to be alive and well in town.

## Spoils
The Voice's hollow wing bones make fine flutes, and 2d4 are worth 50 gp each. Its voice box, removed whole with a DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check, is worth 250 gp to a bard's college. The thorns of the Gallows hold what is left of its victims: 3d6 × 10 gp in coin and gear, and anything a missing traveler was carrying.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive:* 133 hp plus 20 for one daily Legendary Resistance (expected CR 5–10) is 153. At its Gallows, Larder Bond adds 30 (3 × 10 per round), for 183 effective hp, the CR 8 band. AC 15 is one below the expected 16, too little to move it, so **defensive CR is 8**. Away from its larder, it has 153 effective hp, the CR 6 band, so its defensive CR is 6.
- *Offensive:* over three rounds (Shrike's Stoop on two targets for 34; Multiattack for 24 plus an impaling Carry Off for 10; Multiattack for 24), with two legendary Rakes each round (18), it averages about 49 damage per round, the CR 7 band. +7 to hit is one above the expected +6, too little to move it, so **offensive CR is 7**.
- *Average:* **7.5** at the Gallows, rounding down to 7 (Wingclipped is a real weakness the math doesn't count), or **6.5** away from it, rounding down to 6. Both are within 1 of the stated 6, so it stays **CR 6**.

**IP:** none. No setting-specific names appeared in the source.
