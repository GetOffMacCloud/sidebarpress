## Lore
Coursehounds are the hunting pack of the Marrowmother's brood. She builds them from whatever dogs, wolves, and foxes reach her larder, and from pieces of other things: a badger's jaw, a man's shoulder blade fixed over the ribs like a shield, a hare's long legs splinted onto a mastiff. They run prey down and herd it toward the larder, and they never tire.

A [[Bone Hound]] is silent. A coursehound isn't. Its mismatched bones knock together as it runs, and a pack sounds like hail on a roof long before it reaches the treeline. A coursehound raised mid-fight by Reknit has only 1 hit point, but it joins the pack at once. See [[Bone Beast Template]] for how the brood differs from relic-bound bone beasts.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** moorland, forest, and open hill country within a day's run of [[The Marrowmother]]'s larder.
- **Encountered as:** packs of three to six, often with an [[Antlerwrack]] to open the hunt and a [[Maulbone]] following to drag the kill home.
- **Signs:** a rattling like hail carried on the wind; paw-prints of three different sizes in one stride; sheep run to exhaustion and left alive in a ditch.

## Tactics
Coursehounds surround one target, so every hound has Pack Tactics, and they stay on that target until it falls. They don't break and run, but they do herd: a pack drives a fleeing creature toward the larder rather than simply chasing it.

**Running it:** a pack of coursehounds is fast (50 feet) and fragile. Let them close quickly, and let a single mace blow or Collapse burst scatter bone across the map so the players see how brittle they are.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A coaching road loses its night mail-rider; the horse comes in lathered, with bone splinters in its flanks. *Twist:* the rider is alive in a ditch, herded there and left for the Maulbone, and he has seen where they took the rest.
2. A kennel-master's prize hounds have vanished one by one. *Twist:* he has been selling the corpses of dogs he put down to a "bone-carver" who is in fact a cultist feeding the Marrowmother.
3. Coursehounds are seen running a deer to ground miles from any known lair. *Twist:* they're herding it toward a second larder. The Marrowmother has moved, and her old ravine is now empty and full of silk.

## Spoils
Coursehounds carry nothing. A pack's collars, if any of the dogs had them, are worth 1d6 sp each and can identify the owners.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: proficiency +2 with Str +2 gives +4 to hit; DC 12 is 8 + 2 + Con 2; 2d4 + 2 averages 7; passive Perception 9 is correct.
- *Defensive:* 19 hp is the CR 1/8 band (7–35), and AC 13 matches the expected 13, so **defensive CR is 1/8**.
- *Offensive:* one Grafted Attack a round deals 7, the CR 1/2 band (6–8). Pack Tactics counts as +1 to hit, so its effective +5 is two above the expected +3, and **offensive CR is 1**.
- *Average:* halfway between CR 1/4 and CR 1/2. Rounded up for Collapse and its undead immunities, that gives **CR 1/2**, which matches the stated CR.

**IP substitutions:** none.
