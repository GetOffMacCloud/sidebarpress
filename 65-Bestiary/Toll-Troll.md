## Lore
Every land has a bridge where you don't cross without paying. Most are guarded by greedy men. A few are guarded by something older. A toll-troll is a troll that struck a bargain with a bridge, or with the river under it, long ago: it would guard the crossing forever, and in return, it would never die while a toll went unpaid. Fire still hurts it. Payment ends it, at least for a day.

Toll-trolls aren't stupid, and they aren't honest, but they are *exact*. A toll-troll never asks for more than it named, never refuses a toll fairly paid, and remembers every debt for centuries.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** old stone and timber bridges over rivers, gorges, and marsh channels, especially on roads that are less traveled than they used to be.
- **Encountered as:** alone. Two toll-trolls never share a bridge.
- **Signs:** a bridge in perfect repair on a road that has fallen to ruin, gnawed bones in the water below, and a little cairn of odd payments on the near bank: a doll, a lute string, a lock of hair.

## Tactics
The troll talks first and always. It fights only those who refuse the toll or try to cross without paying. Even then, it heaves the strongest fighter into the river before anything else. If the fight turns against it, it names a new, cheaper toll, and it means it.

**Running it:** the fight is the fail state. A party that pays, trades, or tricks its way across has beaten it.

## Adventure Hooks
1. The only bridge over the gorge has a new toll: the youngest member of any party, for a single night's service. The troll swears they'll come back unharmed. It's telling the truth, mostly.
2. A miser in the village has been paying the troll for forty years with other people's secrets. The troll has started to hint about what it knows.
3. The troll wants out of its bargain with the river. It will let the party cross free forever if they can find a way to end it.

## Spoils
Under every toll-troll bridge is a hoard of tolls: odd keepsakes, letters, coins, and a few genuinely valuable trinkets (roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table A twice). Names, songs, and shadows held by the troll return to their owners when it dies.

## Design Notes
**Remix of the Troll (MM p. 291).** Changes: lawful evil, a swim speed, Bridge-Bound, Name the Toll, and Heave (as an option inside Multiattack). Regeneration is now stopped by fire alone (acid no longer works) and is switched off for a day by payment. Int 9 and Cha 9 suit a creature that negotiates.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274):** *Defensive:* 84 hp plus 30 from three rounds of regeneration gives 114 effective hp, which is the CR 3 band (101–115). AC 15 is two above the expected 13, so defensive CR is **4**. *Offensive:* Bite plus two Claws deals 29 damage per round, which is the CR 4 band (27–32). +7 to hit is two above the expected +5, so offensive CR is **5**. *Average:* 4.5, which gives **CR 5**, matching the MM troll. With its toll paid, it has no regeneration, and its defensive CR drops to about 2.
