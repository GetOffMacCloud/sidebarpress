# Leader Minion

*Kill the drummer first. Everyone at the table knows it. That's the point.*

A **leader minion** makes the rest of the pack better while it lives. It's the priority target the party has to reach: a drummer, a standard-bearer, a chanting acolyte at the back.

> [!example]+ Leader Minion Template
> Apply it with the steps in [[04 — Minions#How Minions Work|How Minions Work]], using the **Minion Table** row for your party's APL. Then:
> - **Damage:** the table's **×0.5** column.
> - **One leader minion per pack.** Leader abilities don't stack.
>
> **Signature (pick one):**
> - ***War-Drum.*** While the minion isn't incapacitated, its allies within 30 feet of it that can hear it gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls. This doesn't stack with other leaders.
> - ***Command (Bonus Action).*** One ally the minion can see within 30 feet of it can use its reaction to make one attack.
> - ***Last Order.*** When the minion drops to 0 hit points, each ally within 30 feet of it that can see it can immediately move up to half its speed.

**In Fantasy Grounds:** generic leader minions for every APL 1–20 are in [[Leader Minion — FGU Templates]].

## Running Leader Minions
- Keep it in the second rank, behind enforcers.
- When it falls, the pack should waver: give the remaining minions a DC 10 Wisdom saving throw or have them flee.

**How it was built:** APL 3 row; ×0.5 damage (5); Beat the Drums replaced with an always-on War-Drum; Nimble Escape kept as its one non-damaging trait; passive Perception reset to 10 + Wisdom.
