# Controller

*Your feet won't move. Your sword arm won't rise. Somewhere behind the brutes, something is chanting.*

A **controller** changes the shape of the fight. It slows, pins, blinds, marks, and splits the party, so the brutes and skirmishers get the fight they want. Controllers deal little damage themselves. They're the reason the party's plan falls apart.

> [!example]+ Applying the Controller Role
> Adjust any statblock:
> - **Damage** −25% on its attacks.
> - **Add one control action** (below). Its save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + the creature's best mental ability modifier.
> - **Defense** unchanged.
>
> **Signature traits and actions (pick one or two):**
> - ***Hamper (Recharge 5–6).*** One creature the controller can see within 60 feet must make a saving throw (Strength, Dexterity, or Wisdom; choose when you build the creature). On a failure, it is restrained (or knocked prone, or its speed becomes 0) until the end of its next turn.
> - ***Zone.*** As an action, the controller turns a 15-foot cube within 60 feet into difficult terrain (grasping roots, bone-shards, ice, ankle-deep blood) for 1 minute. It can maintain one zone at a time.
> - ***Hex.*** As a bonus action, the controller targets one creature it can see within 60 feet. Until the start of the controller's next turn, the target subtracts 1d4 from its attack rolls and saving throws.
> - ***Mark.*** One creature the controller can see within 60 feet must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be marked until the end of its next turn: it can't benefit from being invisible or hidden, and the first attack roll against it each round has advantage.
>
> **CR:** usually **unchanged**. Control effects aren't counted by the DMG formula; the damage cut is roughly offset by what the effect does to the party. Rate it as playing one step hard.

## Running a Controller
- Keep it behind the line, with an enforcer close by.
- Control the party's best attacker first, then whoever is coming for the controller.
- Controllers retreat once the line breaks. Without it, they're the softest target in the fight.

## Controller Minions
A controller minion keeps **one control effect, used at will, lasting only until the end of the target's next turn**. A pack of them can lock down the fighter for a round each, which the party has to break by killing them fast.

Built from the [[Bone-Tally Chanter]]: 1 hit point, Minion added, Aggressive and Tally of Wounds dropped, Marking Chant kept at will with a one-round duration, and the scourge set to the CR 1/2 fixed damage.

## Design Notes
Control is the strongest thing a monster can do and the hardest to price. These effects are tuned to last one round or allow a save each turn, so no single failed save takes a player out of the game for long. Avoid stacking three different controllers on one target.
