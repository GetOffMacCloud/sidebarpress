## Lore
The wolpertinger is the hunter's joke of the Bavarian Alps: a hare (or a squirrel, depending on who's telling it) with antlers, wings, and fangs, seen only by moonlight, and supposedly only by those with the right company and the right frame of mind. Bavarian taxidermists have long stitched wolpertingers together from spare parts and sold them to tourists, and the beast is a fixture of hunting-lodge walls. Sending a city cousin into the woods at night with a sack to catch one is a time-honored prank.

In a world with owlbears, the joke is that the wolpertinger is real, and it finds *you* very funny.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** alpine forests, high meadows, and the edges of hunting grounds.
- **Encountered as:** alone, or a "warren" of two to four.
- **Signs:** hare tracks that end abruptly, shed antler-nubs no bigger than a thumb, and laughter in the trees.

## Tactics
A wolpertinger fights only when it's cornered or mocked. Otherwise, it leads hunters in circles with Fool's Trail for its own amusement. In a fight, it charges, flies off, cackles, and charges again. It never fights to the death. At half its hit points, it laughs one last time and is gone.

**Running it:** play it as comic relief with a sting. A warren of four at APL 1 is a real fight.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A pompous noble's son insists on bagging a wolpertinger for his trophy wall. The locals hire the party to make sure he doesn't find one, and the wolpertingers hire nobody.
2. A taxidermist's shop has a dozen stuffed wolpertingers in the window. One of them blinks.
3. The party is sent out with a sack and a candle as a prank. They actually catch one, and now it's following them.

## Spoils
A wolpertinger's antlers (worth 5 gp) are prized as good-luck charms. A live wolpertinger that has been befriended (DC 15 Wisdom (Animal Handling), with a gift of schnapps-soaked bread) can serve as a *find familiar* form, at the DM's discretion.

## Design Notes
**Folklore source:** the wolpertinger of Bavarian hunting folklore and taxidermy tradition. Game statistics are original.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274):** *Defensive:* 27 hp is the CR 1/8 band (7–35). AC 12 is one below the expected 13, though flight and Flyby make it hard to hit back, so defensive CR is about **1/8**. *Offensive:* modeled over three rounds (8.5 on the charge round, then 5.5 and 5.5), it deals about 6.5 damage per round, which is the CR 1/2 band (6–8). +4 to hit is one above the expected +3, so offensive CR is about **3/4**. Mocking Cackle is a defensive bonus. *Average:* about 0.45, which gives **CR 1/2**.
