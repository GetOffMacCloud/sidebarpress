# Vespera Pixiecoil, the Prism-Coiled

*A fox-sized dragon somersaults through the air, scales flashing every color of a prism. She laughs, and you find yourself laughing too.*

*Created by **GrimmGrixis**, Writers Guild.*

> [!example]+ Vespera Pixiecoil, the Prism-Coiled (CR 23, 50,000 XP)
> *Small dragon, chaotic good*
> **Armor Class** 20 (glamourweave scales) · **Hit Points** 332 (35d6 + 210) · **Speed** 40 ft., fly 80 ft. (hover)
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 10 (+0) | 22 (+6) | 22 (+6) | 20 (+5) | 20 (+5) | 26 (+8) |
>
> **Saving Throws** Dex +13, Con +13, Wis +12, Cha +15 · **Skills** Arcana +12, Deception +15, Perception +12, Performance +15, Stealth +20
> **Senses** passive Perception 22 · **Languages** Common, Draconic, Elvish, Sylvan, telepathy 120 ft. · **Challenge** 23 (50,000 XP)
>
> ***Fey Step.*** As a bonus action, Vespera can teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space she can see.
>
> ***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If Vespera fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
>
> ***Magic Resistance.*** Vespera has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
>
> ***Superior Invisibility (3/Day).*** As a bonus action, Vespera magically turns invisible for 1 minute. The invisibility doesn't require concentration, and anything she wears or carries is invisible with her. While invisible, she has advantage on attack rolls, and attack rolls against her have disadvantage.
>
> ***Telepathic Shroud.*** Vespera is immune to scrying and to any effect that would sense her emotions, read her thoughts, or detect her location.
>
> ***Innate Spellcasting.*** Vespera's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 23, +15 to hit with spell attacks). She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
> At will: *dancing lights, druidcraft, mage hand, minor illusion, prestidigitation, vicious mockery* (4d4)
> 5/day each: *faerie fire, hypnotic pattern, major image, suggestion*
> 3/day each: *confusion, dispel magic, greater invisibility, polymorph*
> 2/day each: *dominate monster, mass suggestion, mirage arcane, mislead*
> 1/day each: *feeblemind, prismatic spray, project image*
>
> **Actions**
> ***Multiattack.*** Vespera can use her Whimsical Presence. She then makes three attacks: one with her Bite and two with her Claws, or three with her Cantrip Bolt. She can replace one attack with an innate spell of 3rd level or lower that has a casting time of 1 action.
>
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +13 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (2d10 + 6) piercing damage plus 18 (4d8) psychic damage.
>
> ***Claw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +13 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) slashing damage.
>
> ***Cantrip Bolt.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +15 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 22 (4d10) force damage. The bolt shimmers a different color each time she casts it.
>
> ***Whimsical Presence.*** Each creature of Vespera's choice that is within 120 feet of her and aware of her must succeed on a DC 23 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by her for 1 minute. A creature charmed in this way regards Vespera as a wondrous and beloved friend and has disadvantage on attack rolls against her allies. At the start of each of its turns, it rolls a d4 to determine its behavior that turn:
> - **1.** It is dazzled by her beauty and uses its action to do nothing but stare, smile, and laugh.
> - **2.** It uses its action to shout her praises in whatever language it speaks. Its movement and bonus action are unaffected.
> - **3.** It uses all its movement to caper, twirl, or skip in place or in a circle, then acts normally with its action and bonus action.
> - **4.** It shakes off the worst of the haze and acts normally this turn, though it remains charmed.
>
> A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a charmed creature takes damage from Vespera or her allies, it can immediately repeat the saving throw with advantage. A creature that succeeds on its saving throw is immune to Vespera's Whimsical Presence for the next 24 hours.
>
> ***Faerie Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** Vespera exhales sparkling, rainbow-tinted breath in a 60-foot cone and chooses one of the following effects:
> - ***Iridescent Cascade.*** Each creature in that area must make a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw, taking 88 (16d10) damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Roll a d8 for the damage type: 1, acid; 2, cold; 3, fire; 4, lightning; 5, poison; 6, psychic; 7, radiant; 8, thunder.
> - ***Euphoric Mist.*** Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by Vespera for 1 minute. While charmed in this way, the creature is incapacitated and giggles helplessly. At the start of each of its turns, it rolls a d6. On a 1–4, it uses all its movement to wander in a random direction; on a 5 or 6, it doesn't move. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
> - ***Slumbering Pollen.*** Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 21 Constitution saving throw or fall unconscious for 1 minute. The effect ends for a creature if it takes damage or if another creature uses its action to shake it awake. Creatures immune to being charmed aren't affected.
>
> **Legendary Actions**
> Vespera can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Vespera regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.
>
> ***Cantrip.*** Vespera casts one of her at-will cantrips.
>
> ***Tail Lash.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +13 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 21 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away from her.
>
> ***Pixie Step (Costs 2 Actions).*** Vespera teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space she can see. She can then expend one use of Superior Invisibility to turn invisible.
>
> ***Cast a Spell (Costs 3 Actions).*** Vespera casts a spell from her Innate Spellcasting list, expending a daily use as normal.

> [!example]+ Vespera Pixiecoil, the Prism-Coiled — Lair Actions
> Vespera's lair is a hollow hill beneath a meadow where it is always the first warm day of spring. On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Vespera takes a lair action to cause one of the following effects. She can't use the same effect two rounds in a row.
> - ***Living Vines.*** Flowering vines erupt from the ground in a 20-foot-radius circle centered on a point Vespera can see within 120 feet of her. Each creature on the ground in that area must succeed on a DC 21 Strength saving throw or be restrained until initiative count 20 on the next round.
> - ***Wonderland Shift.*** Vespera chooses a 30-foot cube she can see within 120 feet of her. Until initiative count 20 on the next round, the area is whimsically transformed: it is thick with petals and is difficult terrain, and gravity and distance wobble within it. A creature that ends its turn in the area must succeed on a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw or be pushed 15 feet in a random direction.
> - ***Pixie Tricks.*** A swarm of unseen pixies harasses up to three creatures Vespera can see within 120 feet of her. Each target must succeed on a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws until initiative count 20 on the next round.

> [!example]+ Vespera Pixiecoil, the Prism-Coiled — Regional Effects
> The region within 6 miles of Vespera's lair is warped by her presence, which creates one or more of the following effects:
> - Flowers bloom in impossible colors and wild patterns. Plants sometimes whisper riddles in Sylvan when no one is looking directly at them.
> - Beasts in the region behave as if fey-touched. They speak in their own tongues to those who listen carefully, and they lead travelers helpfully or mischievously.
> - Time slips. A visitor who spends a day in the region often finds, on leaving, that 1d4 days have been lost or gained.
>
> If Vespera dies, the riddles and the time slips fade over 1d10 days, but the flowers stay unnaturally vivid for years.

## Lore
Vespera Pixiecoil is the size of a fox and older than most kingdoms. She was born one of the tiny butterfly-winged dragons that haunt the borders of the faerie lands, the kind that steal pies and swap boots between sleeping travelers. Somewhere in her first thousand years, she stopped growing in size and never stopped growing in power. Her scales shift through every color of a prism as she moves, and her wings throw rainbows on whatever she flies past.

Her lair is a hollow hill beneath a meadow where it is always the first warm day of spring, and she rules it as a hostess. She throws revels and holds riddle contests, and she welcomes any traveler who arrives with a good story. She is good the way the weather is good: generous, unpredictable, and impossible to argue with. She loathes cruelty, and she loathes boredom almost as much. A tyrant who crosses her meadow may wake as a toad. A dull guest may sleep through a week.

Vespera is no one's servant and no one's ally for long, but she has a soft spot for underdogs and an old, unforgiving grudge against the chromatic dragons, who once burned the meadow she loved best. She rarely fights. When she does, the fight looks like a party, right up until the *prismatic spray*.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** enchanted forests and meadows, hollow hills, faerie rings, and the borderlands where the faerie realms touch the world.
- **Encountered as:** the hostess of a revel, with a court of sprites, satyrs, and dryads; or alone and invisible, watching to see what kind of people the party are.
- **Signs:** flowers in colors that have no names; animals that answer back; rainbows at night; travelers who come out of the woods certain it's Tuesday when it's Friday.

## Tactics
Vespera starts every fight invisible and charming. She uses Whimsical Presence, then Faerie Breath: Euphoric Mist or Slumbering Pollen against foes she likes, and Iridescent Cascade against foes she doesn't. She never stays in melee. Fey Step and Pixie Step keep her 60 feet or more from anyone who closes, while her Cantrip Bolts fly. She saves Cast a Spell for *dominate monster* on the strongest fighter or *polymorph* on whoever annoyed her most. Below a quarter of her hit points, she retreats into her hill's illusions, and she holds the grudge for a century.

**Running it:** most meetings with Vespera shouldn't be fights. Play her as a hostess with the power of a god who wants, above all, to be entertained. A fight with her is a punishment, and it should feel like one: whimsical, humiliating, and very hard to win.

## Lair / Regional Effects
Vespera's lair actions and regional effects are in the callouts above. In her lair, the lair actions add restraint and disadvantage on top of her charm effects, so treat a fight there as a hard CR 23.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A baron's heir crossed Vespera's meadow on a dare and came out a goat. The baron wants him restored. Vespera will restore him only when the party proves he has learned his lesson, and he hasn't.
2. Vespera invites the party to her revel to settle a wager with a hag. The party is the wager, and the hag bet against them.
3. A chromatic dragon's war-host is marching through the faerie borders, and Vespera offers to help the party stop it. Her help is chaotic: the time slip that hides the party from the host for a day also makes them a week late for everything else.

## Spoils
Vespera is worth far more as a friend than as a corpse. A creature she counts as a friend may receive a single favor (one casting of any spell on her list, on her terms) or a gift from her hoard, which she never asks to have back. If she is slain, each of her prismatic scales is worth 1,000 gp to an enchanter and can serve as a focus for *prismatic spray* or *prismatic wall*. Her hoard is a magpie's: 2d6 × 1,000 gp of baubles, music boxes, stolen boots, and wedding rings, plus two rolls on the DMG's Magic Item Table H.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source file sat in a "CR 21 Monsters" homebrew folder, tagged fey, and names no campaign or adventure of origin. Its art embed (*Vespera Pixiecoil.webp*) was dropped.

**Why CR 23, not 21.** As submitted, Vespera was rated CR 21, and her defense nearly is: 332 hit points plus three uses of Legendary Resistance, with AC 20 and Magic Resistance, rate CR 22.5. Her offense is higher. An 88-damage breath across a 60-foot cone and +15 Cantrip Bolts rate CR 23, and that is before her charm, invisibility, and teleportation, none of which the formula counts. The average is 22.75. Her proficiency bonus (+7) is the same at CR 21 and CR 23, so re-rating changes no numbers on the block.

| Challenge | 21 (33,000 XP) | 23 (50,000 XP) | The block computes to CR 22.75 |
| Hit Points | 333 (37d6 + 222) | 332 (35d6 + 210) | 37d6 + 222 averages 351; 35d6 + 210 averages 332, the closest to the stated 333 |
| Bite average | 15 (2d10 + 6) | 17 (2d10 + 6) | 2d10 averages 11, + 6 = 17 |
| Tail Lash average | 11 (2d6 + 6) | 13 (2d6 + 6) | 2d6 averages 7, + 6 = 13 |
| Whimsical Presence | Trait; disadvantage on attacks "against her and her allies" | Action (used through Multiattack); disadvantage on attacks against her allies | Multiattack already calls it as an action, as the MM dragons do Frightful Presence; a charmed creature can't attack its charmer anyway |
| Superior Invisibility | No duration | 1 minute | The trait needs a duration; matches *greater invisibility* |
| Pixie Step | "can turn invisible as if she used her Superior Invisibility" | Expends one use of Superior Invisibility | Otherwise the 3/Day limit means nothing |
| Euphoric Mist | Incapacitated, "can't take reactions," and "takes no action or bonus action" | Incapacitated, with the d6 movement roll | Incapacitated already covers actions, bonus actions, and reactions |
| Wonderland Shift | Petals, sideways gravity, "or" stretched distances | Always difficult terrain, with the same push | The three options had no rules of their own; the push applied to all of them |
| Living Vines | "a 20-foot-radius point" | A 20-foot-radius circle centered on a point | Copy; no rules change |
| Lair actions | No initiative or repeat rule | Initiative count 20 (losing ties); no repeats on consecutive rounds | The standard MM lair-action rule |
| Regional effects | Under "Notes" | Separate callout | House format |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 333 hp plus 90 for Legendary Resistance (3 uses × 30) gives 423 effective hp, the CR 21 band (401–445). AC 20 counts as 22 with Magic Resistance, three above the expected 19, so **defensive CR is 22.5**. Over three rounds (Iridescent Cascade on two targets for 176 plus a legendary *prismatic spray* on two targets for 70, then three Cantrip Bolts for 66 plus three Tail Lashes for 33, twice), she averages 148 damage per round, the CR 21 band (141–158). +15 to hit is four above the expected +11, so **offensive CR is 23**. *Average:* **22.75**.
- *Retuned:* 332 + 90 = 422 effective hp, still the CR 21 band, so **defensive CR is 22.5**. With Tail Lash at 13, the later rounds deal 105, and the average is 152 per round, still the CR 21 band, so **offensive CR is 23**. *Average:* 22.75, which gives **CR 23**. Whimsical Presence, Euphoric Mist, Slumbering Pollen, Superior Invisibility, and her lair actions aren't counted. Against a party without protection from charm, she plays harder than CR 23.

**IP substitutions:** none. The entry avoids the *Monster Manual*'s "faerie dragon" (not in the SRD) and the plane name "Feywild"; she is described as a fey dragon of the faerie lands. All of her spells are SRD.
