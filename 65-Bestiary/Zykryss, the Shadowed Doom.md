## Lore
Under the old harbor city, the canals run down into a second city: drowned streets, flooded cellars, and a cistern as big as a cathedral. Zykryss lives there. The black dragon came up the river two hundred years ago, found the city's foundations sunk in tidal mud, and decided to stay until the whole city fell into the dark. It has been helping ever since, one dissolved piling at a time.

Almost no one above knows it exists. Zykryss is patient, and very quiet for its size. It hunts the smugglers who use the undercity and the poor who go down to scavenge, and it leaves their bones on the canal ledges so that the city watch blames wererats and sends hunters into the tunnels. It eats the hunters too. For a century, the city's oracles have warned of a "shadowed doom" beneath the streets. Zykryss heard the phrase from a dying priest and has used it as its name ever since.

Zykryss sought out the Dragon Queen's favor deliberately: it offered her the city. She accepted and gave it a corrosive burst to release when wounded. On the day the city falls, Zykryss means to raise a temple to her in the ruins and rule the survivors from its steps.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** the flooded undercity beneath a harbor town; sewers, cisterns, and sunken ruins; the salt marshes at the river mouth.
- **Encountered as:** alone. Wererats and smugglers sometimes pay it tribute in exchange for passage through its tunnels.
- **Signs:** buildings that lean a little further every year; the sour stink of acid in cellars; mooring chains eaten through overnight; bones picked clean and laid out on ledges; a canal surface that ripples when there is no wind.

## Tactics
Zykryss is an ambusher. It lies submerged in black water and waits for a party to string out along a narrow ledge; its breath is a line, and the undercity is all lines. After it breathes, it sinks, swims under the party, and rises behind them. It uses Tail attacks from the water at 15 feet and Wing Attack only to escape a dead end. When it drops below half its hit points, it releases its corrosive burst and vanishes into the flooded tunnels. It won't die before the city falls. It intends to watch.

**Running it:** run Zykryss as horror. Keep it unseen as long as you can: a ripple, the hiss of stone dissolving, a ledge that gives way. Let the party find its handiwork long before they find it.

## Adventure Hooks
1. The harbor guild hires the party to learn why its warehouses are sinking. The guild master already knows. He has been feeding Zykryss his debtors for years in exchange for his own warehouse being spared.
2. An engineer has proved the cathedral will collapse during the city's high festival, and someone keeps destroying her surveys. The saboteur isn't the dragon. It's a cult of the Dragon Queen among the cathedral's own clergy, who want the collapse to happen on the holy day.
3. Wererats from the undercity ask the party for help: something is eating their warrens. They mean it, and once the dragon is dead, the undercity is theirs. The city above is next.

## Spoils
Zykryss's hide yields enough black scales for one suit of *dragon scale mail* (acid), and its acid glands are worth 500 gp to an alchemist. The cistern holds a Challenge 11–16 treasure hoard (DMG p. 138), mostly smugglers' cargo and coins corroded together in lumps. Its most valuable treasure is a map scratched into the cistern wall, marking which pilings it has weakened and which are next. The city council would pay a fortune for it, or kill to keep it quiet.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source file sat in a "CR 12 Monsters" homebrew folder and names no campaign or adventure of origin.

**Why CR 16, not 12.** As submitted, Zykryss was rated CR 12, but a 54-damage line breath on two targets and three legendary Tail attacks a round give it CR 18 offense, against CR 13 defense, an average of 15.5. Raising its proficiency to +5, as either CR 15 or CR 16 requires, lifts the offense half a step, and the average becomes 15.75, which rounds to 16. Its attacks and damage are unchanged.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 195 hp plus 60 for Legendary Resistance (2 uses × 30) gives 255 effective hp, the CR 13 band (251–265). Two resistances and one immunity aren't broad enough for a multiplier. AC 18 matches the expected 18, so **defensive CR is 13**. Over three rounds (Acid Breath on two targets for 108 plus three legendary Tail attacks for 45, then Multiattack for 50 plus three Tails for 45, twice), it averages 114 damage per round, the CR 18 band (111–116). +10 to hit matches the expected +10, so **offensive CR is 18**. *Average:* **15.5**.
- *Retuned:* defensive CR is unchanged at **13**. Damage is unchanged at 114 per round (CR 18 band). +11 to hit is one above the expected +10, so **offensive CR is 18.5**. *Average:* 15.75, which gives **CR 16**. The once-a-day bloodied burst (about 28 damage on two targets) and its ambush play (Stealth +12 while submerged) aren't counted, and both support rounding up.

**IP substitutions:** "Tiamat" → "the Dragon Queen" (the trait "Tiamat's Favor" is now "Favor of the Dragon Queen").
