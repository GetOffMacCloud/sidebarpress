## Lore
Every harvest has its feast, and every feast has someone left outside the door. The Gluttonwretch is what rises when a great house gorges while its village starves. It is the spirit of the feast-bird itself, swollen with every meal that was hoarded and every crust that was refused. It comes on the night of the harvest banquet. It eats the food, then the table, then the guests, and it is never full.

Folk in pact-villages and market towns know the signs. Gravy that won't set, cranberries that crack like glass, and a gobble heard far off on a still night all mean the Wretch has woken somewhere. The old advice is to share the feast, open the doors, and feed the poor first. Few great houses take it.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** manor halls, guild banquets, and market towns at harvest time; any place where a feast is held in sight of hunger.
- **Encountered as:** alone. It is a unique creature, and there is only one Gluttonwretch.
- **Signs:** larders emptied overnight with the doors still barred, guests who can't stop eating, gravy dripping up the walls, and shards of red crystal in the hearth-ash.

## Tactics
The Wretch opens with Gobble of Gluttony to drag the party into its hunger aura, then spends its legendary actions on Feast on Fear against whoever is stunned. It strafes from the air with Razorberry Barrage and lands only to Peck a target that is already weakened. It uses Wing Buffet to clear melee attackers, who also take the shards of its ichor. It is drawn to food. A heaped table or a thrown haunch of meat can pull it off a fallen ally for a round.

**Running it:** the hunger aura takes away player actions, which can feel bad at the table. Describe it vividly, remind players that one success makes them immune for the day, and let creatures who carry rations choose to eat those instead of their own flesh.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A duke's harvest banquet is three days away, and his cooks keep finding the larder stripped bare. He wants the party at the high table, armed, and he won't hear of inviting the town.
2. A village that once refused its poor at the feast-day door has been eaten down to the foundations, all but the almshouse.
3. A gourmand guild has learned to bind the Wretch's hunger into a single dish. Someone has served it to a king.

## Spoils
The Wretch's wishbone, a forked bone as long as a spear, can be broken by two creatures to grant one of them a single *wish* limited to duplicating a spell of 7th level or lower. Its crop holds the half-digested treasure of every feast it has ruined: 2d6 × 1,000 gp in plate, jewelry, and coin, plus one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table G.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press around the contributed statblock, and they need the contributor's review.

**Why CR 20, not 14.** As submitted, the block was rated CR 14, but a CR 14 creature should deal 87–92 damage per round. With three legendary Pecks (37 damage each) on top of a 70-damage Barrage, this one dealt close to 200, enough to drop a 20th-level wizard from full hit points in a single round. Its defense, meanwhile, rated only about CR 16. The block already averaged out near CR 20, but lopsidedly: a glass cannon. Rather than cut the attacks that make it memorable, we kept every signature ability, moved it to CR 20, and evened out the two sides.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 230 hp plus 90 for Legendary Resistance (30 per use at expected CR 11+) gives 320 effective hp, which is the CR 17 band. Its resistances add nothing at that CR. AC 17 is two below the expected 19, so **defensive CR is about 16**. Over three rounds (Gobble, then two rounds of Barrage), with three legendary Pecks each round plus the hunger aura and ichor shards, it dealt about 193 damage per round, the CR 23 band. With +12 to hit, **offensive CR is about 23.5**. The average is about 20, but the profile was lopsided.
- *Retuned:* 276 + 90 = 366 effective hp is the CR 20 band (356–400). AC 17 is two below the expected 19, so **defensive CR is 19**. Modeled over three rounds (Gobble, then Barrage twice), with one legendary Peck each round, the aura (two failures, 21 damage), and the shards (14 damage), it deals about 119 damage per round, the CR 19 band (117–122). +13 to hit is three above the expected +10, so **offensive CR is 20.5**. *Average:* **CR 20**.

**Still worth watching:** Gobble of Gluttony can stun a whole party at once, and Feast on Fear feeds on exactly that. If your table finds the combination too swingy, give Gobble a Recharge of 6 only.
