# Penny-Risen

> [!art]+ Plate
> ![[SP-PBS-05 token-zombie-villager.jpg|360]]

*Homebrew variant of the zombie (MM); Penny Under the Tongue and Ash-Drawn added; animal variant. 2014 rules.*

- **Description:** The weak who drank from a well cursed by [[Reaper's Tithe]]: the old, the sick, infants, livestock. They die within three days and rise on the fifth night, still in their shrouds.
- **Animal variant:** Lambs, dogs and foxes use the same block as **Small** undead with speed 30 ft. and a Bite (+3 to hit, 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing) in place of Slam. Same CR and XP.
- **In this adventure:** [[SP-PBS-05 — E1 — The Kalbach Churchyard|E1]] (the Kalbach dead) and [[SP-PBS-05 — E2 — The Hollen Ford Relay|E2]] (the signaller, travellers and fox).
