# Barrow Dead

> [!art]+ Plate
> ![[SP-PBS-05 token-barrow-skeleton.jpg|360]]

*Homebrew variant of the skeleton (MM); Bell-Bound and Drawn to Flame added. 2014 rules.*

- **Description:** Flan dead from the barrows under Carn Aedh, woken by the Hollowing Toll: brown bone, rotted leather, a copper torc at every neck, bronze leaf-blades green with age.
- **In this adventure:** One rises per toll in [[SP-PBS-05 — E4 — Thirteen Tolls|E4]] and reaches the ring 2 rounds later. Their copper torcs match the dead walking out of the Vesve ([[The Copper-Torc Dead]]).
