## Lore
Erinyes are the hunters of Hell, sent after oathbreakers, deserters, and souls that slipped their contracts. A **lesser erinyes** is one that failed. Perhaps she let a quarry escape, or showed it mercy, or questioned an order. Her masters didn't destroy her, because Hell wastes nothing. Instead, they sheared off her wings and sent her back to the hunt on foot, with a smaller share of power and a longer list of names.

A lesser erinyes is bitter, patient, and relentless. She can't fly down a fleeing oathbreaker anymore, so she walks the roads instead. She waits at the crossroads, the city gates, and the ferry landings her quarry must pass, and she watches with eyes that see through any disguise. Every quarry she brings back earns her a feather. Enough feathers, the legend goes, will buy her wings again.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** roads, crossroads, city gates, and the ruins where fugitives hide; anywhere a hunted soul might pass.
- **Encountered as:** alone, or in a pair hunting the same quarry; sometimes leading a pack of 1d4 hell hounds or a handful of bound mortal trackers.
- **Signs:** a single black feather left at a crossroads; a traveler who has stopped sleeping and keeps looking over their shoulder; and arrow wounds that blacken and won't heal without magic.

## Tactics
A lesser erinyes opens from long range with her longbow, poisoning her quarry and anyone protecting it. She closes only when the target is weakened or cornered, then switches to her longsword and parries anyone who gets in her way. She cares only about her quarry and will ignore or bypass everyone else if she can. She won't bargain, but she will honor any legally binding claim to her quarry's soul, and she leaves if someone convinces her that the contract has been satisfied.

**Running it:** play her as a bailiff, not a berserker. She announces whom she has come for, and she tells everyone else they may leave.

## Variants
**Lieutenant of the Dark Prodigy.** The [[Lieutenant of the Dark Prodigy]] uses this same chassis, trading Hellish Weapons' poison for fire and a stacking curse. Use it for an erinyes who serves a single fiendish warlord rather than Hell's courts.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A lesser erinyes is hunting a merchant who sold his soul for his fortune. The merchant hires the party to protect him. His contract has a loophole the erinyes hasn't noticed, and the party can find it only by reading the contract in Hell's own archive.
2. A lesser erinyes approaches the party for help. Her quarry is a fugitive cultist who escaped her once, and she needs only one more feather to win back her wings. The cultist is the party's patron.
3. A village is terrified of the armored woman waiting at the crossroads. She is waiting for the next person to break a sworn oath in that village, and the next oath is the one a party member made this morning.

## Spoils
A lesser erinyes carries 1d4 black feathers, the tally of her captures, each worth 100 gp to a collector of infernal curios or usable as a component for *planar binding* against devils. Her longsword and longbow lose their magic when she dies. She carries a written warrant in Infernal naming her quarry and the contract it broke; reading it reveals the terms of that soul's bargain.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source file names no campaign or session.

**Why CR 8, not 11.** The lesser erinyes is the SRD erinyes with less: 120 hit points instead of 153, two attacks instead of three, and no fly speed. Its defense rates about CR 10, but its two attacks deal only about 40 damage a round, the damage of a CR 6 creature. Its submitted proficiency (+4) and saves were correct for CR 11, but the creature itself averages out near CR 8. We kept every attack and feature and moved it to CR 8, adjusting its proficiency-derived numbers.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 120 hp × 1.25 (nonmagical, nonsilvered weapon resistance at expected CR 11–16) gives 150 effective hp, the CR 6 band (146–160). AC 18 is three above the expected 15, and Magic Resistance adds 2 more, so **defensive CR is about 8.5**. Two two-handed longsword hits deal 20 each (9 + 11), or 40 damage per round, the CR 6 band (39–44). +8 to hit is two above the expected +6, so **offensive CR is 7**. *Average:* about 7.75, which gives **CR 8**. That is three below the stated CR 11, so it is re-rated.
- *Retuned:* at expected CR 8, the resistance multiplier is ×1.5, so 120 × 1.5 = 180 effective hp, the CR 8 band (176–190). AC 18 plus 2 for Magic Resistance is four above the expected 16, so **defensive CR is 10**. Two longsword hits deal 19 each (9 + 10), or 38 damage per round, the CR 5 band (33–38). +7 to hit is one above the expected +6, so **offensive CR is about 5.5**. *Average:* about 7.75, which gives **CR 8**.

**IP substitutions:** none. *Erinyes* is an SRD creature name. Hell is used generically.
