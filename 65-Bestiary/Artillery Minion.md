# Artillery Minion

*The first anyone knows of them is the arrow. The second is the fire.*

An **artillery minion** shoots from the back. Alone it's nothing; six of them behind a line of enforcers turn every round into a storm the party must push through.

> [!example]+ Artillery Minion Template
> Apply it with the steps in [[04 — Minions#How Minions Work|How Minions Work]], using the **Minion Table** row for your party's APL. Then:
> - **Armor Class:** the table's AC −1.
> - **Attack:** a ranged attack with a range of at least 60 feet (or 120 feet for a spell), at the table's attack bonus.
> - **Damage:** the table's standard column.
>
> **Signature (pick one):**
> - ***Steady Aim.*** If the minion hasn't moved this turn, it has advantage on its ranged attack roll.
> - ***Death Splash.*** When the minion drops to 0 hit points, each creature within 5 feet of it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (the table's DC) or take damage equal to the table's ×0.5 column, of its attack's damage type.
> - ***Keep Distance (Reaction).*** When a creature moves within 5 feet of the minion, the minion moves up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

## Running Artillery Minions
- Place them high, behind cover, or across something the party has to cross.
- Death Splash makes the fighter who wades in to cut them down pay for it.

## Worked Example: Kobold Pot-Slinger at APL 3
Built from the [[Kobold Pot-Slinger]] with the Artillery Minion template, at APL 3. Rebuild it at your own APL with the Minion Table: change the AC, attack, DC, damage, Challenge, and XP, and keep everything else.

>
> ***Minion.*** If the minion takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.
>
> ***Death Splash (Artillery).*** When the pot-slinger drops to 0 hit points, the pots in its satchel shatter. Each creature within 5 feet of it must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 5 damage of its payload type.
>
> ***Pot Payload.*** Choose acid, fire, or poison when you place the pack. A creature *doused* by its pot suffers the payload's effect until the end of that creature's next turn. *Acid:* its speed is reduced by 10 feet. *Fire:* it sheds bright light in a 10-foot radius and can't benefit from being hidden. *Poison:* it is poisoned.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Shatterpot.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 60/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 damage of its payload type, and the target is doused.

**How it was built:** APL 3 row; AC 13 − 1 = 12; standard damage 9; Shatterpot's range raised to 60/120 to meet the artillery rule; Pot Payload kept as the attack's rider; Volatile Satchel becomes Death Splash at the ×0.5 column (5).
