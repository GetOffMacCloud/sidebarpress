# Minions

Every villain needs hands. **Minions** are the lesser creatures that fill a fight: the war-band's spear-carriers, the cult's acolytes, the pack running ahead of the alpha. They hit hard enough to matter and die to a single blow, so a room full of them feels like a horde without dozens of hit point totals to track.

A minion isn't a creature in this book. It's a **template you apply to any statblock**. Take a monster (a gnoll, a kobold, a skeleton, a bone hound), pick one of the seven **minion types** below, and you have a pack of lesser versions built to fight *your* party.

## How Minions Work
- **Built to the party.** A minion's numbers come from the **party's level (APL)**, not from the monster it's based on. Any statblock can supply minions for any table: a level-12 party can face kobold minions that are a real threat.
- **The source monster supplies the creature:** its name, size, type, alignment, speeds, senses, languages, ability scores, and the look, name, and damage type of its main attack.
- **The minion type supplies the job:** small changes to its numbers and one signature ability.
- **One hit.** Every minion has 1 hit point.

> [!example]+ Applying a Minion Template
> 1. **Pick the APL** and read that row of the **Minion Table** below.
> 2. **Pick a minion type** (Brute, Enforcer, Skirmisher, Artillery, Controller, Lurker, Leader) and apply its adjustments from its note.
> 3. **Take from the source monster:** its name (add "Minion"), size, type, alignment, speeds, senses, languages, and ability scores (used for saving throws and checks). Keep **one** non-damaging trait if it fits, such as a sense, a weakness, or Wall-Runner.
> 4. **Write the minion:**
>    - **Armor Class:** from the table, adjusted by the type.
>    - **Hit Points:** 1 (minion).
>    - **Saving throws:** the source's ability modifiers, with no proficiency.
>    - **One attack**, named after the source's main attack and using its damage type. It uses the table's attack bonus (or save DC) and the type's **fixed damage** (no roll). Keep a rider (prone, push, poison) if the source's attack had one, using the table's DC.
>    - **The Minion trait** and the type's **signature ability**.
>    - No Multiattack, legendary actions, lair actions, or recharge abilities.
> 5. **Challenge and XP:** write its Challenge as the APL, with the table's XP (one quarter of a CR = APL creature).

> [!example]+ The Minion Trait (every minion)
> ***Minion.*** If the minion takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.

## The Seven Minion Types

| Minion type | Job | Adjustments | Signature (pick one) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Brute Minion]] | Hits hardest | AC −1, damage ×1.5 | Death-Blow, Overwhelm, Reckless |
| [[Enforcer Minion]] | Holds the line, shields allies | AC +2, damage ×0.75 | Interpose, Hold the Line, Shield Wall |
| [[Skirmisher Minion]] | Hits and runs | Speed +10 ft. | Hit and Run, Charge, Pack Tactics |
| [[Artillery Minion]] | Shoots from the back | AC −1, ranged attack | Steady Aim, Death Splash, Keep Distance |
| [[Controller Minion]] | Pins and hinders | Damage ×0.5, control action | Hamper, Mark, Hex |
| [[Lurker Minion]] | Strikes from hiding | Stealth expertise | Ambush, Drop from Above, Fade |
| [[Leader Minion]] | Makes the pack better | Damage ×0.5 | War-Drum, Command, Last Order |

Each type's note has its signature abilities written out, tactics, and a worked example: a minion built from a creature in this book at APL 3, with FGU import text.

## Minions in Encounters
- **XP:** add each minion's XP from the table.
- **Multiplier:** count **every four minions as one creature** (round down) for the DMG's encounter multiplier.
- **Group size:** use 4–12. Fewer than four isn't worth the bookkeeping; more than twelve slows the table.
- **Mixing:** one full-size monster plus a pack of minions is the classic shape: the war-chief and his spears, the necromancer and her dead.

### Suggested mixes (4 PCs, checked against DMG pp. 82–83)

| APL | Mix | Base XP | Count (minions ÷ 4) | Adjusted XP | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 [[Goblin Battle-Drummer]] + 4 APL-1 minions | 50 + 200 = 250 | 1 + 1 = 2 → ×1.5 | 375 | Hard (300–399) |
| 3 | 1 [[Bone-Tally Chanter]] + 4 APL-3 minions | 100 + 700 = 800 | 1 + 1 = 2 → ×1.5 | 1,200 | Hard (900–1,599) |
| 3 | 6 APL-3 minions, no boss | 1,050 | 1 → ×1 | 1,050 | Hard (900–1,599) |
| 5 | 1 CR 3 creature + 4 APL-5 minions | 700 + 1,800 = 2,500 | 1 + 1 = 2 → ×1.5 | 3,750 | Hard (3,000–4,399) |
| 9 | 1 CR 4 creature + 4 APL-9 minions | 1,100 + 5,000 = 6,100 | 1 + 1 = 2 → ×1.5 | 9,150 | Hard (6,400–9,599) |
| 13 | 6 APL-13 minions | 15,000 | 1 → ×1 | 15,000 | Hard (13,600–20,399) |

Minion XP adds up fast, and every fourth minion also bumps the multiplier. Add them four at a time and recheck.

### Running Minions at the Table
- **Fantasy Grounds:** 140 ready-made generic minions (every type at every APL 1–20) are in [[FGU Minion Templates — Index]]. Import the one you need, rename it, and reskin it.
- Optionally, roll **one attack roll for each group** of up to four minions attacking the same target. If it beats AC, every minion in the group hits.
- In Fantasy Grounds, paste a minion's import text once and drag in as many copies as you need. With 1 hit point, any hit drops them.
- Describe each death in a word or two, so the players feel every swing land.

> [!info]- Optional: full-size roles
> The same seven jobs can be given to a **full** monster instead of a minion. Adjust its statblock, then recheck CR with the DMG (p. 274):
>
> | Role | Adjustments to a full monster | Typical CR change |
> |---|---|---|
> | Brute | AC −2, HP +25%, damage +50% | +1 |
> | Enforcer | AC +2, HP +10%, damage −25%, reach 10 ft. | none |
> | Skirmisher | Speed +10 ft., AC +1, HP −10% | none |
> | Artillery | AC −1, HP −25%, a ranged attack with damage +25% | none |
> | Controller | Damage −25%, plus one control action | none (plays harder) |
> | Lurker | HP −10%, Stealth expertise, plus Sneak Strike | +1 |
> | Leader | Damage −25%, plus Command or a war-cry aura | none (price the fight one step harder) |
>
> A full-size role monster takes **two** signature abilities from its type's minion note, at full strength: the damage it adds is based on its own attacks, not the Minion Table.
