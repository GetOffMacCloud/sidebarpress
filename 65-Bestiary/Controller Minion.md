# Controller Minion

*Your feet won't move. Somewhere behind the spears, something is chanting.*

A **controller minion** deals little damage. Instead it pins, marks, and hinders, so the brutes and skirmishers get the fight they want.

> [!example]+ Controller Minion Template
> Apply it with the steps in [[04 — Minions#How Minions Work|How Minions Work]], using the **Minion Table** row for your party's APL. Then:
> - **Damage:** the table's **×0.5** column.
> - **Control action:** it gains one of the signature actions below, which it can use **in place of its attack**, at the table's DC. Every effect lasts only until the end of the target's next turn.
>
> **Signature (pick one):**
> - ***Hamper.*** One creature the minion can see within 60 feet must succeed on a saving throw (choose Strength, Dexterity, or Wisdom when you build it) against the table's DC or, until the end of its next turn, its speed becomes 0 (or it is knocked prone, or it can't take reactions).
> - ***Mark.*** One creature the minion can see within 60 feet must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against the table's DC or be marked until the end of its next turn. A marked creature gains no benefit from being invisible or hidden, and the first attack roll against it each round has advantage.
> - ***Hex.*** One creature the minion can see within 60 feet must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against the table's DC or subtract 1d4 from its attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn.

## Running Controller Minions
- Spread the effects around the party. Don't stack three Hampers on one target.
- Kill them early, the party should learn; that's the lesson the controller teaches.

## Worked Example: Bone-Tally Chanter at APL 3
Built from the [[Bone-Tally Chanter]] with the Controller Minion template, at APL 3. Rebuild it at your own APL with the Minion Table: change the AC, attack, DC, damage, Challenge, and XP, and keep everything else.

>

>
> **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12 · **Languages** Orc, Gash-cant · **Challenge** 3 (175 XP)
>
> ***Minion.*** If the minion takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Bone Scourge.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 slashing damage.
>
> ***Marking Chant (Controller).*** In place of its Bone Scourge, the chanter names one creature it can see within 60 feet of it. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be marked until the end of its next turn. A marked creature gains no benefit from being invisible or heavily obscured against the chanter's allies, and the first attack roll made against it each round by an ally of the chanter has advantage.

**How it was built:** APL 3 row; ×0.5 damage (5); the scourge's 10-foot reach kept; Marking Chant becomes its Mark signature at the table DC; Aggressive and Tally of Wounds dropped.
