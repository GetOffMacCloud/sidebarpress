*Something the color of old bone is rooted in the pit beneath the barn. Its crown is lost in the rafters. Every stalk of wheat in the valley whispers in its voice.*

> [!example]+ Harvest Lord — Lair Actions
> A harvest lord's lair is the field, barn, or grove where it is rooted. On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the Harvest Lord takes one lair action. It can't take the same lair action two rounds in a row.
> - ***Grasping Wheat.*** Each creature of its choice on the ground within its lair must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be restrained by roots. A restrained creature can use its action to make a DC 14 Strength (Athletics) check, freeing itself on a success.
> - ***Harvest Bounty.*** The Harvest Lord regains 20 hit points by drawing life from the fields, and one field within sight withers to black stubble. After the third use in a single fight, any village bound to the lord's pact sees its harvest being eaten. A DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check turns the villagers against the lord, and it loses its lair actions for the rest of the fight.

> [!example]+ Harvest Lord — Regional Effects
> The land within 1 mile of a harvest lord's lair is altered by its pact:
> - Crops bound by the pact never fail, even in drought or blight. Unbound fields nearby always do.
> - Scarecrows in the region turn their heads to follow strangers.
> - Anyone who sleeps in the open within the region dreams of being *counted*.
>
> If the lord dies, these effects end. The next harvest is ordinary for the first time in living memory.

## Lore
A harvest lord was once a treant, an ancient oak or ash that a farming people revered. Somewhere in its long life, it struck a bargain: the fields will never fail, and in return, something is owed. The tithe might be a guest every hundred years, a tenth of every child's life, or the village's own dead returned to the soil. The lord has kept every word of the bargain. It cannot understand why the villagers have come to hate it for doing exactly what they asked.

Its bark bleaches as the pact ages, until it is the color of old bone. Its roots grow down under the threshing floor, the churchyard, and the wells, and it speaks through the wheat.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** farmland, especially under tithe barns, old groves, and sacred fields.
- **Encountered as:** alone. A lord is served by a pact-village, cultists, scarecrows, shambling mounds, and sometimes a [[Wicker Colossus]] or a [[Tithe-Reaper]].
- **Signs:** improbably rich harvests, a village that won't sell its seed grain, and a barn with no hay in it that the village guards day and night.

## Tactics
A harvest lord doesn't want to fight. It wants what it is owed. It grabs the creature nearest to the offering and drags it away from safety. It uses Harvest Bounty when it drops below half its hit points and doesn't understand why that turns the village against it.

## Named Example
**The Harvest Lord of Thornbury** ([[SP-HAL-04-The-Tithe-of-Thornbury]]). It is rooted in the pit beneath the Tithe Barn and made its pact a hundred years ago. In that adventure, it has 110 hit points if [[Josiah Thorne]] is present or at APL 8. Its Animate Trees animates two of the barn's great timbers. The adventure's encounter math assumes awakened trees, and the full treant-statted timbers are an option for harder tables. When Harvest Bounty turns the Sheaf, [[Prudence Thorne]] calls the field's name.

## Adventure Hooks
1. The richest valley in the region has never known a bad harvest. It has also never had a visitor stay past the festival.
2. A dryad begs the party to destroy her sister tree, which is now a harvest lord. She knows its pact and its price.
3. A village wants out of its pact. The lord will agree, but only if someone takes the pact's last debt onto themselves.

## Spoils
Heartwood from a harvest lord is a rare material (worth 500 gp to a druid or a woodworker) and can serve as the focus for a *staff of the woodlands*. The pit beneath its roots holds a century of tithes: the belongings of everyone who was offered.

## Design Notes
**Changes from the Treant block:** neutral evil; bone-white; speed 0 with Rooted; Long Reach gives Slam a 20-ft. reach; it speaks through grain; Animate Trees creates awakened trees by default; lair actions and regional effects added.

**CR verification:** The treant is CR 9, and the defensive and offensive statistics are unchanged. Rooted lowers its threat against ranged parties, and Long Reach raises it against melee parties, so they roughly cancel out. **In its lair**, Harvest Bounty adds up to 60 effective hit points over three rounds, which raises its defensive CR by about one step. Treat a lair fight as a hard CR 9, or add 900 XP to the encounter budget.
