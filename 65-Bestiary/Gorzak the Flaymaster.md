# Gorzak the Flaymaster

> ***Appetite for Conquest.*** Gorzak has advantage on attack rolls against any creature that is frightened of him.
>
> ***Aura of the Commander.*** While Gorzak isn't incapacitated, each gnoll within 30 feet of him gains a +2 bonus to attack rolls and has advantage on Wisdom saving throws against being frightened.
>
> ***Hunger of the Ravening Lord.*** A creature cursed by Gorzak's Warleader's Spear is gripped by the Ravening Lord's hunger. Whenever the cursed creature starts its turn within 30 feet of a corpse it can see, it must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or spend that turn moving to the nearest such corpse and devouring as much of it as it can. After it has fed on five corpses this way, its features turn gnollish and it becomes quick to anger. After it has fed on ten, the curse is fulfilled: the creature transforms into a hulking gnoll, loyal to the Ravening Lord and under the DM's control. Until the curse is fulfilled, a *greater restoration* spell or similar magic ends it. Once it is fulfilled, only a *wish* spell can reverse it.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Multiattack.*** Gorzak makes three Warleader's Spear attacks.
>
> ***Warleader's Spear.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. If the target is a creature that isn't already cursed, it must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be cursed with the Hunger of the Ravening Lord.
>
> **Bonus Actions**
> ***Tactical Leader.*** Gorzak commands up to three gnolls he can see within 60 feet of him. Each of them can use its reaction to make one weapon attack against a target in its range.
>
> **Reactions**
> ***Conqueror's Roar.*** When a creature within 10 feet of Gorzak makes a death saving throw, Gorzak roars. Each enemy within 60 feet of him that can hear him must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of him for 1 minute. A frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the start of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

## Lore
Gorzak was a gnoll pack lord who ate his way to the top of his warband and kept eating. Somewhere on the long road of raids, the Ravening Lord, the demon prince whom every gnoll pack howls to, noticed him and took him in. Gorzak died at the head of a charge, and he came back from the Abyss three days later in plate that was still smoking. He is no longer a gnoll in any way that matters. He is a fiend wearing a gnoll's shape, and the Ravening Lord's hunger rides on the point of his spear.

He earned the name Flaymaster at his first conquest. He skinned the town's council, stretched their hides on spears, and planted them at every road into the valley as a message to the next town. The messages work. Many settlements surrender to him at the sight of the banners, and he spares none of them anyway.

What makes Gorzak more dangerous than an ordinary warlord is that he wins recruits from his enemies. Every soldier his spear wounds and fails to kill carries the hunger home with it. Within a season, the hunger turns the soldiers into gnolls, and Gorzak's warband is bigger than it was before the battle.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** open grassland, badlands, and hills; anywhere a gnoll warband can range for weeks between raids. His war camps sit in ruined forts and burned villages.
- **Encountered as:** at the head of a warband of 2d6 gnolls and 1d4 gnoll pack lords, or with a bodyguard of hyenas. He never fights alone if he can help it.
- **Signs:** flayed hides on spears at crossroads; villages emptied of the living and the dead; and once-human scouts with yellowing eyes and a taste for raw meat.

## Tactics
Gorzak fights from the second rank. His 10-foot spear lets him strike over the gnolls in front, and every round he spends his bonus action sending three of them at the same target. He picks the strongest-looking enemy, spears it three times, and lets the gnolls finish it. When an enemy drops and begins making death saves, he roars, frightens the party, and then attacks the frightened creatures with advantage. He doesn't retreat while his pack stands. When the pack is broken, he does retreat, and he goes to raise another one.

**Running it:** the curse is a slow-burn threat. Don't resolve it at the table in the middle of the fight; tell a cursed player privately, the first time they pass a corpse after the battle, that they are hungry.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A border fort's garrison held off Gorzak's warband and won. A month later, the survivors are eating the fort's dead. The garrison commander begs the party to find a cure before the town finds out, and he is already cursed himself.
2. A rival warlord offers the party gold to kill Gorzak. She is a devotee of the Ravening Lord too, and she wants Gorzak's spear so she can take his place.
3. Gorzak has sent a flayed hide to a city as a warning. The hide belongs to a noble the city believes is still alive, and the person wearing his face in the council chamber is something else.

## Spoils
Gorzak's **Warleader's Spear** is a +1 spear with a 10-foot reach that deals an extra 1d6 necrotic damage on a hit. It requires attunement, and any creature that attunes to it is cursed with the Hunger of the Ravening Lord without a saving throw. His plate armor is ordinary plate, scorched black. His war chest holds the tribute of a dozen towns: 4d6 × 100 gp in coin, 2d4 art objects worth 250 gp each, and one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table D.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* the block gave no hit points; its 18d8 dice and Con 20 give 171, the CR 7 band (161–175). Fire resistance alone isn't broad enough to adjust. AC 18 is three above the expected 15, so **defensive CR is about 8.5**. Three spear hits deal 23 each, or 69 damage per round, the CR 11 band (69–74). +10 to hit is two above the expected +8, so **offensive CR is 12**. *Average:* about 10.25, which gives **CR 10**.
- *Now:* **defensive CR is unchanged at 8.5**. Offense is still 69 per round, the CR 11 band, and +9 to hit is one above the expected +8, so **offensive CR is about 11.5**. *Average:* **CR 10**, which matches the stated CR. The gnolls he commands, the +2 aura, and advantage against frightened targets aren't counted; build those into the encounter budget by counting the gnolls as normal.

**IP substitutions:** Yeenoghu (the demon lord of gnolls) → "the Ravening Lord," as directed by the house naming rule. "Flind" (a non-SRD gnoll variant) → "a hulking gnoll." Gnolls and the Gnoll language are SRD and stay.
