## Lore
Dorukai Thunderclub is an oni, an ogre-mage, who has made himself lord of the storms over a range of high passes. Years ago he looted a hilltop shrine of the storm giants and carried off its relic, the great hammer he now calls the Hammer of the Thunder Lord. With it, he claims to command the weather itself. The mountain villages beneath him pay a "thunder tithe" of coin, cattle, and, when he demands it, their children, and the storms stay away from their fields as long as the tithe is paid.

Like all oni, he is clever, vain, and cruel, and he would rather be feared than fought. He flies on the storm wind, goes unseen when it suits him, and speaks Infernal, which he learned from a devil he once did business with. That bargain still hangs over him, and he keeps a careful distance from anyone who smells of brimstone. His wounds close almost as fast as they are made, and he has come to believe he can't be killed. So far, no one has proved him wrong.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** high passes and storm-wracked hills, sea cliffs, and the remote villages he taxes.
- **Encountered as:** alone, or at the head of a band of ogres, hill giants, or bandits who collect the thunder tithe for him.
- **Signs:** rings of lightning-split trees; hammer-shaped craters in the road; villages that ring a bell each dusk to show they have paid; sudden storms over any village that hasn't.

## Tactics
Dorukai opens invisible, flying above the fight, and drops into the middle of the party. His first round is a Thunder Slam and two Great Hammer blows, with *chain lightning* as a legendary action. After that, he hammers whoever is still standing and spends his legendary actions on *lightning bolt* until both uses are gone, then on extra hammer blows. He uses his claws on a creature he wants taken alive: a paralyzed captive is worth a ransom. When he drops below 50 hit points, he turns invisible and flies off on the storm, letting Regeneration close his wounds, and he returns at night to collect a double tithe.

**Running it:** his Regeneration has no off-switch. Tell the table, through a sage, a survivor, or the shrine's carvings, that only killing him outright in a single push will do it.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A mountain town has paid the thunder tithe for a generation. Dorukai doesn't control the weather at all: the storms come from the looted shrine, and returning the hammer to it would end them, and his hold on the town.
2. A storm giant's herald comes seeking the stolen hammer. She will reward the party richly, but she wants Dorukai brought to her alive for a giant's justice, which is worse than death.
3. Dorukai offers the party a contract to kill a rival who has been raiding his tithe-villages. The "rival" is the devil who holds Dorukai's old debt, and it has come to collect.

## Spoils
The **Hammer of the Thunder Lord** is a giant's maul, too large for a Medium wielder. A storm giant's shrine-keeper can reforge it into a *+1 maul* that deals an extra 1d6 thunder damage on a hit (rare, requires attunement). Dorukai's Large chain mail is worth 150 gp as scrap. His lair holds a generation of thunder tithe: 4d6 × 100 gp in coin, livestock tallies, and heirlooms, plus one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table F.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. Dorukai is a named individual, so he keeps his name and appears in the rogues' gallery. The campaign he came from isn't recorded in the source.

**Why CR 15, not 13.** Dorukai's defense rates about CR 11, but his offense rates about CR 19. Three hammer blows a round already deal 66–69 damage, and his legendary Cast a Spell lets him throw *chain lightning* and *lightning bolt* at the end of other creatures' turns on top of that. Over three rounds, he averages about 140 damage a round. Those two figures average to CR 15, two above the submitted rating. We kept every attack and spell, fixed the save DCs (which matched none of his ability scores), and moved him to CR 15. CR 15 has the same proficiency (+5) as CR 13, so his attack, save, and skill bonuses are unchanged.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive (as submitted and retuned):* 152 hp × 1.25 (nonmagical weapon resistance, expected CR 11–16) = 190, plus 45 for Regeneration (3 × 15), gives 235 effective hp, the CR 11 band (221–235). AC 16 is one below the expected 17, not enough to move it, so **defensive CR is 11**.
- *Offensive, as submitted:* Round 1 is Multiattack with two hammer blows (44) and Thunder Slam on two targets (42), plus a legendary *chain lightning* on two targets (90), or 176. Rounds 2 and 3 are three hammer blows (66) plus a legendary *lightning bolt* on two targets (56), 122 each. The average is about 140, the CR 20 band (123–140). +10 to hit matches the expected +10, but most of that damage comes from saves, and DC 15 is four below the expected 19, two steps down. Weighting the two, **offensive CR is about 19**. *Average:* (11 + 19) ÷ 2 = 15.
- *Offensive, retuned:* with the hammer's corrected 23 damage, rounds are 178, 125, and 125, an average of about 143, the CR 21 band (141–158). +10 to hit is one below the expected +11; DC 16 (spells) is four below the expected 20, and DC 18 (Thunder Slam) is two below. Weighting them, **offensive CR is about 19**. *Average:* (11 + 19) ÷ 2 = **CR 15**.

**Still worth watching:** Dorukai is lopsided, with a CR 11 body and a CR 19 punch. A focused party can bring him down in two rounds, and he can drop a character in one. If your table finds him too swingy, limit his legendary Cast a Spell to his at-will spells. That drops his offense by about three steps, and he plays as a hard CR 13.

**IP substitutions:** none. *Oni* is an SRD creature, and Dorukai, the Thunder Lord, and the thunder tithe are the contributor's own.
