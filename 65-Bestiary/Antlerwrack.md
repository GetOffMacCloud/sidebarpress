## Lore
Antlerwracks are the chargers of the Marrowmother's brood. Each is built on the frame of an elk, a stag, or a plough-horse, splinted out with whatever long bones the larder holds, and crowned with every antler she has to hand. No two crowns match. Some carry a dozen tines; some carry the curled horns of a ram stitched beside the antlers of a deer.

An antlerwrack has no herd instinct and no fear. It patrols the mouth of the Marrowmother's ravine in a slow, rattling circle, and it charges anything that moves. One raised in the middle of a fight by her Reknit has only 1 hit point, but it still charges. From a distance it is easy to mistake for a [[Bone Elk]]; up close, the grey silk and the mismatched bones give it away (see [[Bone Beast Template]] for the difference).

## Ecology
- **Environment:** forest edges, hill ravines, barrow-fields, and churchyards within a few miles of the Marrowmother's larder.
- **Encountered as:** one or two, usually alongside a pack of [[Coursehound|coursehounds]], guarding the approach to [[The Marrowmother]].
- **Signs:** bark gouged at chest height by many points at once; deer tracks with one hoof that drags; tufts of grey silk caught on thorns.

## Tactics
An antlerwrack opens every fight with a 20-foot charge to knock the front-liner prone, so the coursehounds can pile on. If it can't get a straight run, it backs off to make room for one. It never retreats.

**Running it:** telegraph the charge. Let the antlerwrack lower its crown and paw the ground at the end of its turn, and the players will learn to stand where it can't run straight at them.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A deer-hunt comes back short a hunter, with a story about a stag "wearing a dozen crowns." *Twist:* the missing hunter's own bow is stitched into a [[Marrow Hunter]] that now walks with the antlerwrack.
2. A trophy-collector's hall is raided at night, and every antler on its walls is gone. *Twist:* the collector sold the Marrowmother's location to a rival years ago and wants the trail followed before anyone connects the two.
3. A church's ancient relic, the antler of a saint's white stag, has been grafted into an antlerwrack's crown. *Twist:* while the relic is part of it, the antlerwrack can't be turned at all, and the priests want the relic back intact.

## Spoils
Its crown holds 2d4 antlers worth 1 gp each to a carver. There's a 1-in-6 chance that one of them is something rarer (an antler from a giant elk or a sacred beast) worth 25 gp. Its silk yields about 10 feet of grave silk thread.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** Internals: proficiency +2 with Str +2 gives +4 to hit; both DC 12 saves are 8 + 2 + 2; 2d4 + 2 averages 7; passive Perception 9 is correct.
- *Defensive:* 30 hp is the CR 1/8 band (7–35). Poison immunity is too narrow to adjust for, and the vulnerability isn't counted. AC 13 matches the expected 13, so **defensive CR is 1/8**.
- *Offensive:* one Grafted Attack a round deals 7, the CR 1/2 band (6–8). +4 to hit is one above the expected +3, which doesn't move it, so **offensive CR is 1/2**.
- *Average:* CR 1/4. The prone charge, Collapse, and undead immunities are uncounted. The stated **CR 1/2** is within one step and stands.
