## Lore
The stone guardian is the innermost ring of the **Fivefold Watch**, the guardians made by the warden-priests of a temple culture that is now mostly ruins and root-split stairs. The wardens didn't build their guardians from clay or bronze. They raised a hawk, a jaguar, and a viper on offerings and rites until each grew huge and tireless; they coaxed a strangler fig to stand up out of a grave garden; and they called an earth spirit into a carved idol at the tomb door. Into each they set a **heart-sigil**, a thumb-sized carving of green stone, and gave it one post and one rite. A full watch stood in rings: the [[Hawk Guardian]] over the approaches, the [[Jaguar Guardian]] in the precinct after dark, the [[Root Guardian]] in the grave gardens, the [[Viper Guardian]] in the cisterns and treasury, and the stone guardian at the tomb itself. The wardens are long gone. The guardians aren't.

The stone guardian is the only one of the five that was never alive. It is an earth spirit bound into a figure of fitted blocks, with its heart-sigil set in its brow, and it stands in the tomb wall beside the sealed door, part of the masonry until it is needed. It has one duty: no one breaks the seal. It doesn't care who walks the halls, what is taken from the treasury, or how many of its siblings have fallen. It cares about the door. When the door is threatened, it glides out of the wall, the floor, or the ceiling, and it doesn't stop until the threat is rubble or gone.

Any guardian of the watch lets a party pass untouched if one of them openly carries a **warden's seal** (a palm-sized disc of green stone carved with all five beasts) and the party keeps that guardian's rite. The stone guardian's rite is the hardest: before the door, a seal-bearer must speak the **burial name** of the one entombed, the true name the wardens gave the dead, which was never spoken in life. The name is carved somewhere in the tomb in the wardens' cipher; a character who studies the tomb's inscriptions for 10 minutes can find and read it with a DC 17 Intelligence (Religion) check. Spoken correctly, the guardian steps back into the wall and opens the door for them. Without a seal, a heart-sigil taken from another guardian of the same temple will serve, but only with the name spoken exactly. Seals turn up in warden graves and in the hands of the wardens' descendants, who sometimes don't know what they hold.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** sealed tombs, burial vaults beneath temple pyramids, mountain sepulchers, and deep underground shrines.
- **Encountered as:** alone, in the wall beside the tomb door; with a [[Hawk Guardian]] or two at an open-air tomb. Its Tremor knocks down its ground-bound siblings, so the wardens never set it beside them.
- **Signs:** a section of wall with no mortar lines where every other wall has them; fresh cracks radiating from a tomb door that no one has touched; dust that sifts from the ceiling in time with the party's footsteps.

## Tactics
The guardian waits in the wall, feeling the room with its tremorsense, until someone touches the seal or attacks the door. Then it glides out behind the party, between them and the exit. It opens with Tremor once two or more intruders stand within 30 feet, then slams the nearest prone target with its 10-foot reach. It is slow, and it has no ranged attack, so a party that scatters can stay out of reach. When it can't reach anyone, it sinks into the floor and rises again beside the door. It never pursues out of the tomb, and it never retreats from it.

**Mixed watch by APL** (four characters; DMG p. 82 multipliers). In a mixed fight, the stone guardian is the anchor that holds the door while the [[Hawk Guardian]] harries anyone who backs away from it.

## Variants
**Lesser Stone Guardian (CR 6, 2,300 XP).** A younger idol at a lesser tomb. It has 105 hit points (10d10 + 50) and is otherwise identical. Its effective hit points (about 158) fall in the CR 6 band, and AC 17 is two above the expected 15, so its defensive CR is 7. Its offensive CR is 6, and the average (6.5) rounds to **CR 6**. Use it where an adventure wants the guardian at its submitted CR.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A valley has started to suffer small earthquakes, always at night and always moving. The twist: the quakes are a stone guardian gliding through the bedrock in search of its tomb's occupant, whom grave robbers carried off a month ago.
2. A mining company breaks into a sealed tomb, and its foreman begs the party to clear the "stone demon" from the door. The twist: the guardian let the miners walk right in. It was made to keep the tomb's occupant inside.
3. A warden's descendant has the burial name and a seal, and hires the party to escort her to her ancestor's tomb. The twist: the seal is cracked in half, and the guardian honors exactly half of it. It opens the outer door and fights them at the inner one.

## Spoils
Its heart-sigil, carved as a squared stone face, sits in its brow. It is worth 250 gp to a collector and can stand in for a warden's seal (see Lore). Its rubble includes 1d4 fitted blocks carved with the wardens' cipher, which a scholar would pay 100 gp each to study. Beyond the door is the tomb itself: 4d6 × 100 gp in grave goods and one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table C.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. It is one of a five-creature set ([[Hawk Guardian]], [[Jaguar Guardian]], [[Root Guardian]], [[Viper Guardian]], and stone), and the shared Fivefold Watch lore, the heart-sigils, and the warden's seal were written for Sidebar Press to tie them together.

**Why CR 8, not 6.** The stone guardian's offense is an honest CR 6, but its defense isn't. More than 150 hit points behind resistance to nonmagical weapons and AC 17 add up to CR 11 defense, and the average of the two comes to 8.5, two and a half steps above the stated CR. Its proficiency bonus is +3 at both CR 6 and CR 8, so every number on the block already fits CR 8; only the Challenge line changes. For an adventure that needs the guardian at CR 6, use the lesser stone guardian under Variants, which trims its hit points instead.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 150 hp × 1.5 (nonmagical weapon resistance at expected CR 5–10) is 225 effective hp, the CR 11 band (221–235). AC 17 is exactly the expected 17, so **defensive CR is 11**. Over three rounds (Tremor on two targets for 42, then Multiattack for 28 and 28), it averages about 33 damage per round, the CR 5 band (33–38). +8 to hit is two above the expected +6, so **offensive CR is 6**; DC 16 is one above the expected 15, and the attack bonus dominates. *Average:* **8.5**, two and a half steps above the stated CR 6, so it is re-rated.
- *Retuned:* 157 hp × 1.5 is about 235 effective hp, the top of the CR 11 band. AC 17 is the expected 17, so **defensive CR is 11**. Offense is unchanged at **6**. *Average:* **8.5**, which rounds down to **CR 8**. We don't round up: Tremor's prone is offset by its slow speed, its Dex 9, and its lack of any ranged attack, so a party that scatters can keep out of its reach. By CR 8 most parties carry magic weapons, which also makes its resistance matter less than the multiplier assumes.

**Set check.** The five guardians were submitted as a CR 3–6 ladder. By the numbers:

| Guardian | Stated | Defensive / Offensive / Average | Final |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Root Guardian]] | 3 | 1/2 / 3 / 1.75 | 3 |
| [[Hawk Guardian]] | 3 | 1/2 / 4 / 2.25 | 3 |
| [[Jaguar Guardian]] | 4 | 1 / 6 / 3.5 | 4 |
| [[Viper Guardian]] | 5 | 4 / 8 / 6 | 5 |
| [[Stone Guardian]] | 6 | 11 / 6 / 8.5 | **8 (re-rated)** |

**The stone guardian is the set's outlier.** The other four step up cleanly from CR 3 to CR 5, and all of them are glass cannons: low defense, high damage. The stone guardian reverses that profile, a slow wall of hit points with modest damage, and its defense lifts it three steps clear of the viper. At CR 8 it reads as what the lore makes it, the capstone at the tomb door. The lesser variant restores the original CR 3–6 ladder for adventures that want it.
