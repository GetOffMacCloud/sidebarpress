## Lore
Threshold lords are fey of the in-between places: crossroads, toll-gates, lych-gates, and doorways at midnight. They are the folk that country people mean when they leave a coin on the gatepost or refuse to invite a stranger across the step. A threshold lord is never cruel and never kind. It is *exact*. It keeps every bargain to the letter and expects the same, and the price of a bargain is always something the bargainer didn't think they'd miss: a name, a voice, a harvest, a child's first word.

Threshold lords seldom fight. They prefer to stand where they can't be harmed and talk until someone agrees to something. Most mortals who meet one never learn that it is a creature at all. They remember only a well-dressed traveler who knew their name.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** roads, crossroads, toll-gates, and village doorways; anywhere one place becomes another.
- **Encountered as:** alone. Sometimes a lord travels with a servant creature bound by an old bargain, such as a hag, a straw double, or a tithe-reaper.
- **Signs:** milestones that read the wrong distance, coins left on gateposts that are gone by morning, travelers who arrive a day earlier than they should have.

## Tactics
A threshold lord fights only when a bargain is broken, or when someone tries to take what it is owed. It stays on its threshold and uses Take the Toll. It *dimension doors* from gate to gate rather than walking. If it is drawn off its ground and wounded with cold iron, it offers terms at once.

**Running it:** Threshold Ward is a plot shield. Before you run a threshold lord as a fight, make sure the party can learn about cold iron or find a way to lure it off its ground. Otherwise, run it as a negotiation.

## Named Example
**The Gentleman in Grey** ([[The Gentleman in Grey]]). He appears on the landing in [[SP-HAL-01-Straw-Men]] and at the barn doors in [[SP-HAL-04-The-Tithe-of-Thornbury]]. In both adventures he can't be harmed, and he leaves when asked. This statblock is for a later adventure that needs his numbers. As written, his *detect magic* aura is an overwhelming fey presence, and he can serve as an archfey warlock patron.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A village hasn't been able to leave by the north road for a generation. Everyone who tries ends up back at the crossroads, where a polite gentleman asks what they'd give to pass.
2. A PC's grandparent made a bargain at a toll-gate sixty years ago. Today the lord has come to collect, and the debt passes down the family line.
3. A merchant wants the party to *break* a bargain cleanly. The only way is to find the lord a better one.

## Spoils
A lord who is bested in fair terms pays what it owes. That means one boon of the party's choosing, worth no more than a *wish* used to duplicate a 7th-level spell, and always granted exactly as worded. A lord can also release a bargain it holds.

## Design Notes
**Built from:** the SP-HAL-01 sketch, which called for a noble base, the green hag's Illusory Appearance and Invisible Passage, Legendary Resistance 3/day, and immunity on roads and thresholds. That sketch had a CR 1/8 chassis, which was far too fragile for a fey lord. This block rebuilds him at CR 6. The hag's traits become at-will *disguise self* and *invisibility*, and Legendary Resistance drops to 2/day to keep the defensive math honest.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274):** *Defensive:* 117 hp plus 40 for two Legendary Resistance uses (20 each at expected CR 5–10) gives 157 effective hp, which is the CR 6 band (146–160). AC 15 is as expected for CR 6, so defensive CR is **6**. Threshold Ward isn't counted, because it is a plot condition rather than a statistic. *Offensive:* Take the Toll on two targets (2 × 27 = 54), then two rounds of Multiattack (2 × 17.5 = 35 each), gives about 41 damage per round over three rounds, which is the CR 6 band (39–44). +6 to hit and DC 16 are on target or slightly above, so offensive CR is **6**. *Average:* **CR 6**.
