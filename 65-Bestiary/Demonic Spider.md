## Lore
Demonic spiders are what happens when a demon is bound into a giant spider and the binding goes wrong. The summoner wants a guardian. The demon wants out. The spider wants to eat. Neither wins outright, and what is left is a huge, clever predator with the demon's resistance to fire and its taste for suffering, and the spider's patience. The webs it spins burn like fuse-cord, and it can keep a victim alive in them for days.

They are less malicious than most demons. A demonic spider doesn't plot, scheme, or bargain. It hunts, and it is curious about what its prey will do next, and it isn't loyal to anyone. Many a cultist has been surprised to learn that the guardian they bound to their temple considers them food too.

Where one appears, a summoner usually isn't far away, or wasn't. The ruins of a failed cult are a demonic spider's favorite home: they're dark, full of corners to web, and full of the bones of the people who brought it there.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** caverns, deep forests, ruined temples, and abandoned summoning sites; anywhere dark and high-ceilinged enough for a Huge web.
- **Encountered as:** alone, or with 1d4 giant spiders or a swarm of spiders that it has drawn into its web.
- **Signs:** scorched strands of web strung across a passage; cocooned bodies with burn-scars beneath the silk; a smell of hot iron and singed hair; and a cold, dead summoning circle with a cultist's bones in the middle of it.

## Tactics
The spider waits in the dark above the main path through its web, hidden. It opens with Searing Web on whoever looks strongest, then drops onto a lightly armored target and bites and claws. It saves its webs for creatures trying to flee or to reach it with ranged attacks. When a victim is paralyzed by its poison, it drags them up into the web and leaves them for later, then turns on the next target. It retreats upward across the ceiling if it is badly hurt, where melee attackers can't easily follow.

**Running it:** describe the web burning in the dark. The light reveals the spider and the cocoons hanging around it, and that light is the first thing the party sees.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A cult has bound a demonic spider to guard its temple. The party must get past it to stop the cult's ritual, but the spider has already eaten the cult. The ritual is now running on its own, with no one left who knows how to stop it.
2. Miners report webs in a new tunnel that burn when touched. A demonic spider has made its lair there, and it is guarding the miners it paralyzed. Several are still alive, cocooned in the web.
3. A wizard asks the party to bring her a demonic spider's venom, alive and fresh. She is trying to break the binding on a spider she summoned herself, which escaped her, and she needs the venom to finish what she started.

## Spoils
A demonic spider's venom glands hold 1d4 doses of its poison (a DC 15 Constitution save, or 9 (2d8) poison damage and paralysis on a fail as per the Bite), worth 200 gp each. Its unburned silk can be woven into 50 feet of rope that is immune to fire, worth 100 gp. Its web holds what remains of its prey: 3d6 × 10 gp in coin and gear, and one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table B.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 114 hp is the CR 3 band (101–115). Its resistances (necrotic and radiant) and immunities (fire and poison) aren't broad enough to adjust. AC 15 is two above the expected 13, so **defensive CR is 4**. Bite (19 + 5 + 9 poison) and Talon (14 + 4) deal 51 damage per round, the CR 8 band (51–56). +7 to hit matches the expected +7, so **offensive CR is 8**. *Average:* **CR 6**.
- *Now:* 115 hp is still the CR 3 band, so **defensive CR is 4**. Bite (17 + 4 + 9) and Talon (13 + 3) deal 46 damage per round, the CR 7 band (45–50). +7 to hit is one above the expected +6, so **offensive CR is 7.5**. *Average:* 5.75, which gives **CR 6**.
- CR 6 is within 1 of the stated CR 5, so per house policy the **stated CR 5 stands**. It hits hard for a CR 5, and its paralyzing poison can take a character out of a fight entirely. Use it as a hard CR 5.

**Alignment:** demons are normally chaotic evil. The contributor's chaotic neutral is kept, and the Lore explains it: the spider's hunger outweighs the demon's malice.

**IP substitutions:** none.
