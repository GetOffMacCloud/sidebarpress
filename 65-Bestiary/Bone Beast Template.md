# Bone Beast Template

*The hounds come out of the mist without a sound. There is nothing left of them but bone, and a green-bronze ring around each neck.*

A bone beast is an animal skeleton raised and bound into a pack by a necromancer, a barrow-king, or a cult with a binding relic. Unlike a common skeleton, a bone beast keeps the instincts of the animal it was. Hounds still hunt in packs, boars still charge, and elk still lower their heads.

> [!example]+ Applying the Template
> Any beast in the *Monster Manual* can become a bone beast. Keep its statistics, apply the changes below, and leave its CR unchanged.
> - **Type** becomes undead. **Alignment** becomes unaligned.
> - **Damage Vulnerabilities** bludgeoning · **Damage Immunities** poison · **Condition Immunities** exhaustion, poisoned
> - **Senses** It gains darkvision 60 ft. It loses *Keen Smell* (it has no nose) but keeps *Keen Hearing* and *Keen Sight*.
> - ***Pack-Bound.*** The beast obeys whoever wears or carries its pack's binding relic: a crown, a torc, a bone horn, or a ring. With no one holding the relic, it hunts the relic, guards its maker's tomb, and drags carrion home.
> - ***Binding Law.*** Every pack is made under one law that its beasts can't break. Choose one, or invent one: *never attack a creature standing in running water* · *never cross a hallowed threshold* · *never attack a creature that holds out an offering of meat* · *never attack a creature bound as a sacrifice*. A party that learns the law can use it.

**Every pack beast wears a ring of its maker's metal** (a collar, tusk-band, or antler-band) cut with its maker's sigil. A DC 12 Intelligence (History) check identifies the culture and age.

**Tell the table early that bludgeoning works.** The vulnerability balances the immunities for low-level parties armed with maces, staves, and clubs, but only if they know to use them. An NPC who says "the smith's hammer broke one like a pot" is enough.

**Why CR is unchanged:** Vulnerability to the most common damage type cancels out the poison immunity and the darkvision. This has been playtested at CR 1/4 in [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]].
