## Lore
Zhulkar was a necromancer long before he was a monster. He spent a mortal lifetime in the deep libraries and darker courts, learning the tongues of demons, dragons, and the things below, and he spent all of it afraid of one thing: that his own bones would fail him. When they began to, he replaced them. First a finger, then a hand, then a spine knotted together from a dozen others. By the time his heart stopped, there was nothing left of the man but the will that held the frame together. The frame kept growing.

He is called the Boneweaver because that is what he does. Every skeleton that falls near him is drawn up into his body, and his plating thickens with each battle. He rules a dead valley from a necropolis at its head. There he sits on a throne built from the skulls of the first army that came to stop him, while the green pall over the valley turns every death into another servant. He doesn't want to conquer the living. He wants more bones, and the living are where bones come from.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** a ruined necropolis or barrow-city at the head of a dead valley, with hills of bone around it.
- **Encountered as:** never alone. He is surrounded by skeletons, zombies, and stranger undead raised by *animate dead* and *danse macabre*, and his lair raises more every round.
- **Signs:** the regional effects; bones stacked neatly at field edges overnight; burned corpses, because the valley's survivors have learned to burn their dead before dusk.

## Tactics
Zhulkar opens with Grave Storm on the largest cluster of enemies, then keeps three Withering Bolts a round on the party's healers. He spends his legendary actions on more bolts, because their healing lock is the point. His undead stand between him and the party and do double duty as armor: Bone Bulwark sends damage into them, and each one that falls feeds Bone Harvest. He uses Command the Dead every round. If he is cornered in melee, he uses *misty step* or *dimension door* to escape, *hold monster* on the strongest warrior, and *finger of death* on anyone who drops low. Before any fight he can see coming, he casts *false life* and *animate dead*.

**Running it:** the minions are his armor, so show it. When Bone Bulwark triggers, describe a skeleton beside him shattering as the blow lands. A party that clears the undead first is playing the fight correctly, and they should feel it.

## Lair / Regional Effects
Zhulkar's lair actions and regional effects are in the callouts above. Inside his necropolis, Marrow Rise gives him a new body for Bone Bulwark and Bone Harvest every other round.

## Variants
**Marrowbound.** For a returning nemesis rather than a one-time boss, give Zhulkar this trait:

***Marrowbound.*** Destroying Zhulkar's body doesn't end him. Within 1d10 days, he weaves a new form from the bones of his valley and appears at his seat of power. He is destroyed for good only if he is reduced to 0 hit points while standing on sanctified ground (for example, within a *hallow* spell's area), or if his Ossuary Heart is found and destroyed. The Ossuary Heart is a fist-sized knot of fused vertebrae hidden somewhere in his necropolis (AC 17, 40 hit points, immunity to poison and psychic damage).

Marrowbound doesn't change his CR, because it works over days, like a lich's phylactery, not during the fight.

## Adventure Hooks
1. The villages below a green-shrouded valley have stopped burning their dead, because "the Boneweaver pays": coins appear on the doorstep of any house with a fresh corpse. *Twist:* Zhulkar isn't buying soldiers. He is buying one body big enough for his next frame, because his Ossuary Heart is failing.
2. A sage needs Zhulkar's spellbook, which is said to hold the cure for a wasting sickness spreading through the lowlands. *Twist:* the sickness is his. It is how he grows his valley, and the cure is written in the book because he meant to sell it.
3. A paladin order has consecrated a ring of ground for the final battle and needs the party to lure Zhulkar onto it. *Twist:* the order's founder hid the Ossuary Heart in the order's own reliquary generations ago, as part of a bargain, and the current grandmaster knows.

## Spoils
Zhulkar's spellbook holds every spell he has prepared, plus 1d6 other necromancy spells of 7th level or lower. His bone scepter functions as a *wand of fear*. His necropolis holds a Challenge 17+ treasure hoard (DMG p. 139), much of it the arms and regalia of the army whose skulls make up his throne.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source doesn't say where he first appeared.

**Why CR 19, not 17.** As submitted, Zhulkar's defense rates about CR 16, but his offense rates about CR 22. That gap comes from his three legendary Withering Bolts, which add 81 damage a round on top of an 81-damage Multiattack. The two average to exactly 19. CR 17 through 20 share a proficiency bonus of +6, so every number in his statblock was already right for CR 19. Re-rating him changes only his XP.

| Spellcaster level | 17th-level | 13th-level | His slots (4/3/3/3/2/1/1) are exactly a 13th-level caster's; a 17th-level caster would also have 8th- and 9th-level slots |

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive:* 218 hp, plus 90 for Legendary Resistance (30 per daily use at expected CR 11+), is 308 effective hp, the CR 16 band (296–310). His two damage immunities aren't broad enough for a multiplier. AC 18 is exactly the expected 18, so **defensive CR is 16**. Bone Bulwark and Bone Harvest aren't counted, because both depend on undead the party can destroy.
- *Offensive:* over three rounds (Grave Storm on two targets for 110, then two rounds of Multiattack for 81), with three legendary Withering Bolts each round (81), he averages about 172 damage per round, the CR 22 band (159–176). +11 to hit is exactly the expected +11, so **offensive CR is 22**.
- *Average:* **19**. That is two above the stated 17, so he is re-rated to **CR 19**.

**Still worth watching.** Three legendary bolts can drop a character between Zhulkar's turns, and every one of them blocks healing. If you want him closer to the contributor's CR 17, make the legendary Withering Bolt cost 2 actions. His legendary damage then falls to 27 a round, his offense to about CR 19 (118 damage per round), and his average to about **17.5, or CR 17 (18,000 XP)**.

**IP:** none. No setting-specific names appeared in the source. *Toll the dead* and *danse macabre* are non-SRD spells. Swap them for *chill touch* and *animate dead* if the book needs to stay SRD-only.
