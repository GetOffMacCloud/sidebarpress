## Lore
The old hunters say the first wolf that ever followed a devil's hunt out of the Hells never found its way back. It came out on a burning hillside on the night of a great fire, and it began to howl, and it has never stopped. The Endless Howl is that wolf, or the latest wolf to carry its howl. Its fur smolders in the rain, its bite is cold as a grave, and its eyes catch the light like coals. It is far cleverer than any wolf should be. It avoids traps it has never seen, and it circles an armed camp for hours to find the one sentry who is falling asleep.

Its howl can be heard for miles, and it carries something of the place the wolf came from. People who hear it close at hand don't decide to run; they find they are already running. Ordinary wolves gather to the Howl, and whatever pack it runs with turns bolder and hungrier than wolves should be. Where it settles, shepherds abandon the high pastures, and villages keep their fires lit all night.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** burned forests, scrubby hills, and the high passes, especially land that has seen a great fire.
- **Encountered as:** alone at first. When it calls its pack, ordinary wolves and dire wolves come running.
- **Signs:** a howl at night that doesn't end; scorched paw prints in fresh snow; sheep found with frost on their throats and scorch marks on their wool.

## Tactics
The Howl opens with Howl of the Hells to scatter the party, then picks off whoever is left standing. It knocks targets prone with its Bite and claws them while they are down. It calls its pack once the fight turns, using them to set up Pack Tactics and to chase the creatures that fled. It is clever enough to retreat out of reach of anyone wielding radiant magic, and to come back for them when they are asleep.

**Running it:** the Howl of the Hells can take a player out of the fight for several rounds. Keep it fun: describe what the fleeing character sees and hears, remind them that they can repeat the save as soon as they lose sight of the Howl, and remember that one success makes them immune for a day.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A hill town hears a howl every night that never ends, and its shepherds have stopped going up to the pastures. *Twist:* the Howl isn't hunting the town at random. It is collecting a debt that one of the townsfolk owes to a devil.
2. A ranger asks the party to help him hunt the Howl's pack before it grows. *Twist:* the ranger's own hound went missing a week ago, and it is now running at the Howl's side with embers in its jaws.
3. A priest says the Howl can be ended only at dawn, on the hilltop where it first came out of the fire. *Twist:* the hill now belongs to a lord who pays the Howl's devil to keep poachers off his land.

## Spoils
The Howl's black pelt never cools. Made into a cloak, it keeps its wearer warm in the worst cold (as a *ring of warmth*), or it sells for 500 gp. Its fangs smoke faintly in daylight, and a full set is worth 100 gp to an alchemist.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. The source doesn't say where it first appeared.

**Why CR 8, not 5.** The Howl's damage really is about CR 5, but its defense is much stronger. It has 157 hit points with resistance to nonmagical weapons and immunity to fire and necrotic damage, which rates about CR 11. The two average to CR 8. CR 5 through 8 share a proficiency bonus of +3, so the re-rate changes none of its bonuses. The other changes below are arithmetic fixes and rules for its two traits that had none.

| Change | As submitted | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Challenge | 5 (1,800 XP) | 8 (3,900 XP) | Its defense rates CR 11 and its offense CR 5; proficiency stays +3 |
| Hit Points | 155 (15d12 + 60) | 157 (15d12 + 60) | 15d12 averages 97, + 60 = 157 |
| Bite damage | 15 (2d8 + 7) | 13 (2d8 + 4) | Str +4; +7 was its attack bonus, not its damage modifier |
| Claw damage | 13 (2d6 + 7) plus 4 (2d4) fire | 11 (2d6 + 4) plus 5 (2d4) fire | Str +4; 2d4 averages 5 |
| Bite prone DC | 16 | 15 | Str-based: 8 + 3 + 4 |
| Howl of the Hells | a trait with no trigger or frequency; DC 17; "remake the save once line of sight has been broken" | an action (Recharge 5–6); DC 14; a repeat save at the end of each turn out of sight; immunity for 24 hours on a success | As a trait, it had no rules for when it happens. DC 17 matched no ability (Wis-based is 8 + 3 + 3 = 14) |
| Call the Pack | a trait: "summoning 1d6 wolves and 1d2 dire wolves" | an action (1/Day), with arrival, initiative, and duration rules | Gives the summon usable rules |
| Trait wording | "The wolf has advantage…" | "The Endless Howl has advantage…" | Names the creature |
| Copy | "Saving throw", "frightned", "Languages None", Vulnerabilities listed last | corrected; MM order (Vulnerabilities, Resistances, Immunities) | No rules change |

**For the contributor.** The Howl is filed as a beast, as submitted, but it has Intelligence 16, immunity to fire and necrotic damage, vulnerability to radiant damage, and a howl "of the Hells." Those all suggest a fiend (like the hell hound). Changing its type would matter for spells such as *protection from evil and good* and *hallow*, so it is your call.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *As submitted:* 155 hp × 1.5 for nonmagical weapon resistance (expected CR 5–10) is about 232, the CR 11 band. AC 16 is one below the expected 17, too little to move it, so **defensive CR is 11**. Its Multiattack dealt 17 + 17 = 34 damage per round, the CR 5 band. +7 to hit is one above the expected +6, too little to move it, so **offensive CR is 5**. Howl of the Hells did no damage and isn't counted. *Average:* **8**.
- *Retuned:* 157 × 1.5 is about 235, the top of the CR 11 band, so **defensive CR is 11**. The corrected Multiattack deals 15 + 16 = 31 damage per round, the CR 4 band. +7 to hit is two above the expected +5, one step up, so **offensive CR is 5**. *Average:* **CR 8**. Howl of the Hells and Call the Pack aren't counted. Count the summoned wolves (50 XP each) and dire wolves (200 XP each) as extra monsters if they arrive early.

**IP:** none. "The Hells" is used generically.
