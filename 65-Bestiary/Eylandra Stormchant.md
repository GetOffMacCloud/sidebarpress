## Lore
Eylandra Stormchant is a merfolk storm-singer, the last keeper of a reef-temple whose priesthood drowned generations ago. She sings the weather. When she chants, squalls gather out of a clear sky, lightning walks across the waves, and the sea rises on a column to carry her into the air. Coastal folk know her by the green-white flash on the horizon that comes before a storm with no wind behind it.

She is neither cruel nor kind. Eylandra serves the sea and the reef, and she treats the land-folk the way a shepherd treats weather: as something to be endured, and occasionally corrected. Fleets that fish within their limits sail unharmed. Ships that dredge the reef, poison the shallows, or pull up the bones of her drowned priesthood don't come home. She can be reasoned with, and she keeps her bargains, but she never forgets a debt.

Away from water, she is diminished. Her wounds close only while the sea touches her, and her spout collapses without rain or surf to feed it. She knows this weakness better than anyone, and she never willingly leaves the coast.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** storm-lashed coasts, reefs, sea caves, and drowned ruins in shallow water.
- **Encountered as:** alone, or with merfolk guards, water elementals, or a reef's sharks and giant crabs.
- **Signs:** storms that rise without wind; ship's timbers fused with coral; lightning-scorched rocks in a ring around a tidal pool; fishermen who leave a share of every catch on the rocks at low tide.

## Tactics
Eylandra fights from the water, riding her spout 20–40 feet above the waves. She opens with Wrath of the Tempest, then Maelstrom on the densest group, and uses *chain lightning* and Thunderhead Volley on the next two turns. She spends her legendary actions on Crackling Bolt, and on Stormstep when anyone reaches her. Backlash of the Deep throws melee attackers back into the surf. She uses Surge of the Tide whenever she drops below 150 hit points. If she is drawn away from water, she retreats to it at once.

**Running it:** the fight's real question is where it happens. Let clever players find ways to pull her inland or onto dry rock; it is the only fair fight they will get.

## Lair / Regional Effects
Eylandra's lair actions are in the separate callout above. She has no regional effects beyond the storms that follow her. In her lair, Charged Skies adds about 11 damage a round and Wave Surge keeps the party off its feet; treat a lair fight as a hard CR 17.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A fishing guild hires the party to kill the sea-witch after storms sink three of its ships. The ships were dredging the reef where her priesthood's dead lie, and the guildmaster knew it.
2. A drought-struck coastal town begs Eylandra to bring rain, and she agrees. The storm she calls will flood the low town, where the poor live, and the town council has already accepted the price.
3. Eylandra asks the party to escort her inland to a dry salt lake, where a storm-relic of her temple lies buried. Away from the sea she is vulnerable, and her rivals know exactly when she will be weakest.

## Spoils
Eylandra's **coral armor** functions as half plate that doesn't give disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks and doesn't hinder swimming (worth 1,500 gp). Her reef-temple holds 2d6 × 100 gp in pearls, salvaged coin, and ship's silver, and one roll on the DMG's Magic Item Table G. A conch from her temple, if the party earns her favor, can call her once.

## Design Notes
**Contributed by GrimmGrixis** (Realms / Writers Guild). The lore, ecology, tactics, hooks, and spoils were drafted by Sidebar Press and need the contributor's review. Eylandra is a named individual, so she keeps her name and appears in the rogues' gallery. The campaign she came from isn't recorded in the source.

**Why CR 17, not 13.** Eylandra was rated CR 13 but plays well above it. With three Crackling Bolts a round as legendary actions (42 damage), a Recharge 4–6 Volley, a Maelstrom, and *chain lightning*, she deals about 119 damage a round over three rounds, the CR 19 band. Her defense is also strong while she is in water, because Surge of the Tide restores 90 hit points over a fight. Those average to CR 17. We kept every ability and moved her up, raising her proficiency from +5 to +6 and every bonus and DC that follows from it.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).**
- *Defensive (as submitted and retuned):* 195 hp, plus 90 from three uses of Surge of the Tide (one bonus action a round in a three-round fight, in water), gives 285 effective hp, the CR 15 band (281–295). AC 17 is one below the expected 18, not enough to move it, so **defensive CR is 15**. Her resistances (cold, lightning, thunder) aren't counted: none is a weapon type, and most parties deal weapon, fire, radiant, or force damage. Out of water, with no Surge, her defense falls to about CR 9.
- *Offensive, as submitted:* Round 1 is Maelstrom on two targets (72); round 2 is *chain lightning* on two targets (90); round 3 is Thunderhead Volley on three targets (54). Each round adds three legendary Crackling Bolts (42) and Charged Waters (5). That totals 119, 137, and 101, an average of about 119, the CR 19 band (117–122). DC 18 is one below the expected 19, not enough to move it, so **offensive CR is 19**. *Average:* (15 + 19) ÷ 2 = 17, four above the stated CR 13.
- *Retuned:* defensive CR is unchanged at **15**. Offense averages the same 119 a round, and DC 19 now matches the expected 19, so **offensive CR is 19**. *Average:* (15 + 19) ÷ 2 = **CR 17**.
- Lair actions aren't counted in the rating; see Lair / Regional Effects.

**IP substitutions:** none. Merfolk is an SRD creature, and Eylandra is the contributor's own.
