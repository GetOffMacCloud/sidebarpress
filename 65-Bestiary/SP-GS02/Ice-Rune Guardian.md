## Lore
An immoth that wants a place watched cuts one of its lesser words (a "hush," a "stay," a "no") into a rune and drives it into a column of ice. The rune wakes the ice and gives it one instruction. Ice-rune guardians are the immoth's sentries, doorkeepers and messengers. They don't think; they do what their word says until the word melts.

## Ecology
- **Environment:** wherever an immoth has passed: mine shafts, ice caves, frozen passes, the doorways of the places it means to come back to.
- **Encountered as:** alone or in pairs, guarding a door or a path.
- **Signs:** a column of ice where no water drips; a faint blue glow in the dark; a cold so hard that breath freezes in the air.

## Tactics
- It waits motionless until something passes, then strikes with its reach and opens with Hush to silence spellcasters.
- It never leaves the area it was set to guard and never retreats. It doesn't flee from fire, and it doesn't understand that it should.
- **Running it:** this is a lesson fight. A party that throws lantern oil or casts a fire spell ends it in a round, and has just learned the immoth's own weakness.

## Adventure Hooks
1. A dwarf hold's lower gate has been "frozen shut" for a winter. Something inside it moves.
2. A guardian carries a rune that holds a word the party needs: a password, a name, a place.
3. A sage wants an intact ice rune. Bringing one back means not killing the guardian with fire.

## Spoils
- **The rune:** if the guardian is destroyed by anything but fire, its rune survives for 1 hour (a fist-sized disk of blue ice). Read with *comprehend languages*, it names its word (in Candlecrown: "stay"). A sage pays 50 gp for one kept frozen.

## Design Notes
**New creature** (Sidebar Press, 2026-11-21, for [[SP-GS02-The-Long-Night-at-Candlecrown]]): the outline's ice-rune guardian, the Session 2 mine sentry. Its defenses follow the [[Immoth]] (resistance to piercing and slashing, cold immunity, fire vulnerability), so meeting one teaches the party how to fight the immoth. Rune Heart is the outline's "last resort" for Fact 2: lantern oil kills the first guardian in one splash.

**CR verification (DMG p. 274).** *Defensive:* 68 hit points; resistance to two physical types against fire vulnerability, treated as ×1.25 = 85 effective, the top of the CR 3 band; AC 14 is one above the expected 13: **defensive 3**. Rune Heart makes it far weaker against fire; the CR is rated for a party without fire. *Offensive:* two Rime Fists at 11.5 each = 23, the CR 3 band (21–26); +6 is two above the expected +4: **offensive 4**. *Average:* 3.5, rated **CR 3**.
