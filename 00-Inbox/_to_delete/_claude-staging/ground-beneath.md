## The Ground Beneath: the Wachturm and the Binding Network

Everything in this book circles one secret. Reveal it in layers, never all at once, and only as fast as the party earns it.

### What the Wachturm is
The town calls it the Watch-Tower. The University's survey calls it "a pre-Oeridian fortification of unclear purpose." Both are wrong in the same direction. The Wachturm is a **binding structure**: the anchor of a containment apparatus older than every human people in the Flanaess. It was not built to watch the horizon. It was built to hold something down.

**What players see.** A tower of ochre limestone about sixty feet tall, no windows in its lower third, one iron-banded door facing south, and an iron bell on the roof. The mortar has become indistinguishable from the stone. It is sounder than anything that old has a right to be.

**What lies below.** Past Stahlwulf's barred trapdoor and the edge of Old Ritter Gerhard's backfilled shaft is a circular chamber thirty feet across, cut from the bedrock and covered in interlocking Ur-Flan script. In the centre of the floor is a socket eight inches across. **It is empty.** Sound behaves oddly in the chamber. No animal will enter it.

### What it holds
The apparatus contains a thin place in the seals laid on **Tharizdun** in the Age Before Ages. Not the god; not a demon; not an artifact. An **influence**, pressing against the seal the way deep water presses against a hull. The network does not destroy it. It channels the pressure through six anchor stones and bleeds it harmlessly into the bedrock.

Nobody in 576 CY has the whole truth, and each who has a piece has a name for it:
- The druids of Ehlonna's Mount call it **the Cold Behind the Cold**.
- Hassenrath called it **the Sleeping Weight**.
- Varek Doss wrote *catastrophic* and underlined it.
- Lecturer Tock read one line of the clay tablet: *"the one who knew first is not asleep; it is counting."*

### The Blackfrost Bloom
When the network works, the pressure bleeds away unnoticed. As it fails, the influence seeps through the worn seals uncontrolled, and that seep is the **Bloom**: black ice out of season, undead rising closer to the wall, predators driven from their ranges, and spirit-sites in distress. This year, for the first time, crystals grow on stone near affected ground. The Bloom is not Tharizdun's doing. It is old plumbing that nobody left alive knows how to maintain.

### The six nodes, as of 2 Patchwall 576
The network runs at about **15%** of its design capacity.

| Node | Where | State |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The Wachturm** | Ritterstadt, on the Rustrock Escarpment | **Leaking.** The anchor stone, a fist-sized piece of black basalt carved with Ur-Flan notation, is missing from its socket. Without it the pressure bleeds as a deep, sub-audible resonance that draws predators and agitates spirits. |
| **2. The Flan Waymark** | Hex E3, an incised monolith in the escarpment face; under the Eisfeld timber rights | **Degraded.** Anchor cracked; binding script worn. Repairable with the tablet's schematic and a skilled hand. |
| **3. The Quag margin** | About 11 miles NE, under Baba Yeska's salt pan | **Unknown.** Submerged about fifty years ago. Two University students, Osa Veld and Renn Farr, went that way and have not come back. |
| **4. The road mound** | On the line of the Velverdyva trade road | **Destroyed.** Levelled in 571 by a Furyondian road crew clearing a mound. Cannot be repaired; its load must be carried another way. |
| **5 and 6.** | Unknown; probably the southeastern Sepia toward the Vesve, and beyond the Rust Seams to the north | **Unlocated.** Their positions are on the clay tablet. |

**The east forest shrine** near Ritterstadt is not one of the six, but a minor node with its own small earth-spirit, an *Erdgeist*. It is the last working anchor near the town, and it is agitated, sending dreams outward. The Beckenhaus family's offerings there have kept it calmer than it would otherwise be. They do not know why that matters.

### Where the missing pieces are
| Piece | Where it is | How the party gets it |
|---|---|---|
| **The Node 1 anchor stone** | In Bruni Hammerschlag's iron coffer at the Smithy, unopened. Her husband Dietrich carried it out of a cave in the northern pass three winters ago. It is warm and faintly "listening," and its resonance drew the winter wolves that killed him. | Bruni asks the party once to look at what Dietrich left. Honesty earns her lifelong loyalty. |
| **The clay tablet** | With Drunik Runevault, the Mining Consortium's factor, who is "making inquiries." | Ask Drunik; Giseth of the druid enclave can translate it; Lecturer Tock has read one line. |
| **Hassenrath's notes** | With Vater Sigmund Wolfherz. | Trust. Ask him about the east shrine's distress, or show him Doss's notes. |
| **Hassenrath's second notebook** | The University's sealed stacks. | The Registrar, the Chancellor's leave, or a burglary. |
| **Gerhard's journal of the dig** | The Ritter's locked study. | The Ritter, or the Vogt. |
| **Doss's research notes** | Vethkar Graun's luggage at the Grey Wolf. | Corner Graun with evidence; he negotiates. |
| **Karet's Hush ledger** | The Fiend's Rest. It maps the six stress points without knowing what they are. | Karet sells it to the right buyer. |

**What Hassenrath's notes hold:** a sketch of the chamber and its socket labelled in Old Flan; directions to four "warm-ground points" (Nodes 1–4, not 5 or 6); the observation that the ward-panels dim and brighten with the moon; and two lines worth reading aloud: *"Something beneath the primary socket is not geological. I will not approach it again,"* and *"I entered it once. I left quickly. My notes from inside are illegible. I do not know what I wrote."* He knew the tower was a binding. He did not know what it bound.

### If nobody acts
| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Now | Node 1 leaking; predators displaced; the Erdgeist agitated |
| The next Bloom year, c. 587 CY | Node 1 fails. The resonance becomes audible to human ears |
| After Node 1 fails | The Quag visibly spreads; undead pressure doubles; livestock die |
| Then | The east shrine fails; Ritterstadt loses its last buffer and becomes barely tenable |
| One to three Blooms later | The network collapses. Permanent Bloom. The Uplands are abandoned within a decade, and the pressure moves south toward Traft |

Eleven years is long enough that the threat is not panic, and short enough that every season counts. Compress or stretch it as your campaign needs.

### What a repair takes
1. The Node 1 anchor stone back in the Wachturm's socket.
2. Node 2's script retraced, using the tablet.
3. Node 3 found, assessed, and mended if it can be.
4. Nodes 5 and 6 located and checked.
5. Node 4's load redistributed or replaced. This is the hardest problem and the best one.
6. A practitioner who can do the work: the druids, a Wolf Nomad band leader, a Flan-blooded character, or the party once it knows enough. The Beckenhauses are an unwitting shrine-tender line; Kurt, with guidance, could learn.

### The Wachturm and the Embrace
Iuz's scholar believes the Wachturm's binding apparatus and the Fiend's Embrace are connected. He is not entirely wrong. This book leaves the exact connection to you. One reading: the Founders brought the Embrace here because a working binding was the safest place in the north to keep it, and the binding has been holding both ever since. If so, the network will notice the day the Embrace leaves the palisade.
