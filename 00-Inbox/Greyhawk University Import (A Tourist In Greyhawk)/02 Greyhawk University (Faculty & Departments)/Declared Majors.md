# Majors & Minors

Every student at Greyhawk University belongs to one of the [[00 — Department Index|Eight Departments]]. Your **Major** is your department's full course of study — it shapes what you discover, how you're trained, and what you bring back from the field. Your **Minor**, if you take one, is a narrower field borrowed from a *different* department.

---

## Declaring a Major

**You may not declare a Major until Level 4 (Sophomore Year).** At Level 3, you are an undeclared Freshman, still building the foundational knowledge that will let you choose a direction. Once you level up to 4, Declaration is required before your next sanctioned quest.

Speak with your Faculty Advisor or the Department Registry. Your Major is recorded in your Conduct Record, and you become a member of that department.

**The eight Majors:** Arcane Research · Natural Philosophy · Medicine & Herbalism · Archaeology · Cartography · Military Theory · Anthropology & Cultural Studies · Political Philosophy & Diplomacy. Full boon listings live in each [[00 — Department Index|department's note]].

---

## Declaring a Minor

At Level 5 (Junior Year), you may optionally declare a **Minor** — one of the two narrower fields offered by each department. Rules:

- Your Minor must come from a **department other than your own**. (Your Major already covers your home department's ground.)
- A Minor grants its **single listed boon** — no Field Certification, no Advanced Study, no credit bonus.
- You may hold only **one** Minor.

## How Major Boons Are Earned

Your Major grants three boons, earned progressively through academic and field achievement — not simply through leveling.

| Boon | Name | Requirement |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Boon 1** | Declaration Boon | Declare your Major at Level 4. Earned immediately. |
| **Boon 2** | Field Certification | Reach Level 5 **and** submit 3 satisfactory Field Reports for quests that earned your Major's credit bonus. |
| **Boon 3** | Advanced Study | Reach Level 6 **and** complete one Research Grant in your Major's domain. |

> [!info] **The Credit Bonus**
> Every Major includes a **10% credit bonus** on quests within your field's expertise, applied automatically when the DM designates a quest as relevant to your department. It counts toward your Field Certification requirement — doing the work in your field earns both the bonus credits *and* progress toward Boon 2.

---

## Changing Your Major

Changing your Major after Declaration is possible but costly — the University treats it as a significant academic decision. A change requires DM approval, a narrative justification, and the forfeiture of all Boons earned from the previous Major; the new Major's progression begins from the beginning. Your Minor is unaffected unless your new Major's department now offers it — in that case, choose a replacement Minor from another department.

%%
> [!tip] **DM Note**
> Major changes are most interesting as story events — the Natural Philosophy student who pivots toward the Mortuary Archives after one bad expedition has a character arc built in. Don't discourage it; just make sure it means something.
