# AR-001 — *Yrith Kael*, the Registry Sealed

> [!info] Accession Summary
> **Accession Number:** AR-001 *(first holding of the Ritterstadt Satellite Station registry)*
> **Provenance:** Central socket, Octagonal Sanctum, the Flan Waymark at [[E3 - Ochre Escarpment|Hex E3]]
> **Recovered:** Flocktime 576 CY (Ritterstadt Questers, expedition GHU1)
> **Custodian:** [[Sigmund Holt]], Field Research Officer
> **Held at:** Academic Registry lockbox, [[The Ritter's Hall]] annex
> **Status:** Restricted holding; supervised examination only

## 1 — Provenance and Chain of Custody

The vessel was recovered from the central anchor-socket of the Octagonal Sanctum (Room 5) of the Flan Waymark complex at Hex E3, during the conclusion of Operation GHU1. It is the first physical artifact recovered from a Flan binding-network node by a Ritterstadt party.

**Prior custody (established by physical evidence):**

|                  Period                   | Custodian                                                          | Notes                                                                                                                            |
| :---------------------------------------: | :----------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|         ~4,000 BCE — ~800 CY-Flan         | Pair-Walker Order (the *Geminel*)                                  | Vessel placed in socket as secondary deposit; sealed with original Pair-Walker wax (inner seal layer)                            |
| ~800 CY-Flan — ~14 days prior to recovery | *Vessel in situ, undisturbed*                                      | The order's collapse left the vessel in its socket; no documented contact for ~800 years                                         |
|        ~14 days prior to recovery         | [[Osa Veld]] / [[Renn Farr]] (University, unauthorised expedition) | Vessel opened, surface contents read, vessel re-sealed with University-grade wax (outer seal layer); position in socket restored |
|          Recovery date — present          | Greyhawk University, Ritterstadt Satellite Station                 | Held in Registry lockbox                                                                                                         |

The Osa Veld / Renn Farr breach is established by the two distinct wax layers on the vessel's neck seam. The outer layer is University-issue sealing wax of the type stocked in standard field survey kits for the period 573 CY onward. No record of the Veld/Farr expedition having returned the vessel to the Station exists; the inference is that they re-sealed it and returned it to the socket as a deliberate act of preservation in place. Their reasoning is not, at present, recoverable.

## 2 — Physical Description

**Form.** A closed clay vessel of approximately spherical body with a narrow vertical neck, in the general profile of a stoppered flask. The body diameter measures 11.4 cm at its widest; the neck rises 4.8 cm above the body's apex, with an external diameter of 2.2 cm at the seal. Overall height with neck: 25.1 cm. Weight: 1.94 kg (substantially heavier than appearance suggests; the clay is dense, fired at high temperature).

**Material.** Dark brown fired ceramic, monolithic — no joint, no seam, no separable lid. The vessel was formed and fired as a single closed shape with the contents placed inside *during* the firing process. The narrow neck was opened post-firing by deliberate cut, then sealed with wax (see §3 below). This is a one-shot vessel: it cannot be re-sealed clay-on-clay once opened.

**Surface treatment.** The entire exterior carries continuous Ur-Flan script in incised low relief, spiralling from the base of the body to the rim of the neck without break or punctuation. The script density is approximately 240 glyph-units per linear metre of script-line. A finer second register, traced so shallowly it is visible only at oblique angles under raking light, runs beneath the primary text in a faint repeating six-pointed diagram corresponding to the binding network's site map.

**Thermal property.** The vessel is warm to the touch in any ambient temperature within human comfort range — measurably warmer than the surrounding stone or surface it rests upon. This warmth is constant and is not depleted by handling, by removal from the original socket, or by extended storage. As of catalog date the warmth has been continuous for the full 800+ years of estimated dormancy.

**Vibration.** When held against the palm, the vessel produces a faint vibration on a 4-second rhythm matching the amber pulse of the original socket. The vibration is below auditory threshold; it is detectable by touch only.

**Seal.** The neck is closed with two distinct layers of wax:
- *Inner layer* (original): dark Pair-Walker resin compound, hardened to near-stone consistency. Bears a small impressed seal in the form of two open hands with cords wrapped around the wrists — the Pair-Walker greeting glyph (see [[E3 - The Flan Waymark]], §Living Echoes).
- *Outer layer* (Veld/Farr, ~14 days pre-recovery): standard University-grade sealing wax, applied with a 568 CY pattern press. The press impression is partial — Osa appears to have re-sealed in haste.

## 3 — Inscriptional Analysis

The exterior script is a **registry title-page inscription** — the vessel's nameplate, in the Pair-Walker documentary convention. The full translation, completed by [[Sigmund Holt]] and verified against the second-edition Pre-Migration Flan lexicon held at the City of Greyhawk:

> *"Node Registry, Second Iteration. Held by the Waymark of Hex-E, Third Generation Keepers. Do not open without the Second Voice present. What was bound by many cannot be released by one."*

**The name *Yrith Kael* itself** does not appear on the exterior surface. It is recorded as the vessel's designation in the Pair-Walker maintenance ledger (the *east-wall mural caption convention*) of the originating Waymark — translated approximately as "**The Door Held Open**" or, in some readings, "**The Open Threshold.**" The name refers not to the vessel's physical form but to its function: the registry held open across generations, available to the next pair, never sealed against the order's own successors.

**Interior content (vellum, surface layer).** Within the vessel: a single piece of treated vellum, rolled, written in Ur-Flan on both sides. Surface content recovered after Osa Veld's solo opening:
- Approximate coordinates of Nodes 5 and 6 (the two previously-undocumented binding sites)
- The first three steps of the three-stage Repair sequence for a degraded node
- A handwritten note in Common, added by Osa: *"Return here when we know what the Wachturm holds. — R."*

**Interior content (clay-fired wall, deeper layer).** Additional text is fired directly into the inner wall of the vessel, beneath the vellum. This text is *partially legible* under examination but cannot be fully decoded without the *Second Voice* ritual key (see §5).

## 4 — Magical Analysis

**Ward signature.** *Detect Magic* reveals active conjuration and abjuration auras across the entire surface, dense and layered. Twelve discrete ward-signatures have been provisionally identified; at least four are believed to be redundant reinforcements of the principal preservation matrix.

**Broadcast.** The vessel's principal active enchantment is a low-frequency resonance broadcast pegged to the binding network's operating frequency. Any binding node within an estimated 40-mile radius (the precise figure has not been measured against active nodes) registers the vessel's presence. This broadcast was the mechanism by which the Yeenoghu-touched gnoll pack-boss known as Grel sensed the vessel's significance without being able to articulate what he was sensing.

> [!warning] Custody Implication
> The vessel's broadcast does not cease in the Registry lockbox. Any agent attuned to the binding network's frequency — including, by [[0001 - DM Design Notes — Ore, Iuz, and the Gnolls|Sigmund's working theory]], certain factions operating southward — can in principle locate the vessel by triangulating against other binding nodes. **The lockbox does not magically silence the vessel.** Considerations for permanent housing, shielding, or planned relocation are pending faculty discussion.

**Preservation matrix.** The interior contents — both the vellum and the clay-fired text — are held in a magically stabilised state. The vellum has not degraded in 4,000 years. Ink colour is consistent with the day it was applied. The clay-fired text on the inner wall is similarly preserved.

**Removal from socket.** Removing the vessel from the original Sanctum socket has caused, by measurable instruments, a slight slowing of the socket's 4-second pulse rhythm and a measurable cooling of the socket interior. The vessel's own warmth and vibration are unchanged. This indicates that the vessel was *not* the original anchor of the binding node — the socket's anchor function was lost separately, and the vessel was placed in the socket later as a secondary deposit using the socket's remaining structural integrity for protection.

## 5 — The "Second Voice" Requirement

The vessel's title-page inscription is not, as initial reading might suggest, a literary flourish. It is a **functional ritual constraint** embedded in the vessel's enchantment.

A solo opener — even a fully competent ritualist — has access only to the surface layer (the vellum scroll). The deeper clay-fired text on the inner wall remains *visible but not fully legible* without the Second Voice ritual.

**Second Voice Ritual — Requirements:**

| Component | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| Participants | Two — and only two — ritually-paired individuals |
| Pairing form | A pair-binding ceremony of the Pair-Walker tradition, OR equivalent (see [[E3 - The Flan Waymark — DM Addendum]]) |
| Implement | Both halves of a Pair-Walker ring, paired and held in contact during the rite |
| Gesture | The two-figure socket-holding gesture depicted in the east-wall mural of the Waymark Processional Hall (DC 15 Arcana to identify; DC 12 to replicate) |
| Duration | The clay-fired text becomes fully legible for one (1) hour following the gesture's completion |

**Successful Second Voice opening reveals:**
- Precise coordinates of all six binding network nodes
- The complete three-stage Repair ritual for a degraded node
- The original architects' compact: *"The Erdgeist is not sealed against its will. It was shown what its movement costs. It agreed to stillness. It may be persuaded again. It may not. But it remembers the first agreement. Use that."*

The Second Voice rite has not been performed on this vessel since the Pair-Walker order's collapse approximately 800 years ago. **No competent pair currently exists** within the Sepia Uplands to perform it. Reconstructing a pair is the subject of independent investigation; see [[E3 - The Flan Waymark — DM Addendum|the Waymark DM addendum]] for the relevant heirloom-rings, the Pair-Day rite, and the [[Vater Sigmund Wolfherz|Vater Sigmund]] connection.

## 7 — Access Notes

**Examination requests** should be made in writing to the Field Research Officer ([[Sigmund Holt]]) with at least 7 days' notice. Sigmund will respond personally to all requests originating from credentialed members of the Ritterstadt Questers — the recovering party — under the standing 90-day priority examination right.

**Magical examination** (Detect Magic, Identify, or higher-order divination) is permitted under direct supervision only. Any divination applied to the vessel is to be logged in the examination ledger with caster, spell, target attribute, and result. As of catalog date, this ledger has three entries (Sigmund Holt, Detect Magic; Holt, Identify; Holt, Detect Magic with extended duration).

**Physical contact** for the purpose of cleaning, measurement, or photographic documentation requires the use of the Registry's set of bone-handled tools. Bare-hand contact is discouraged not because the vessel is dangerous but because surface oils degrade the surviving pigment in the script.

**Removal from the lockbox** has occurred once in the catalog period, for purposes of the Holt translation pass. Removal was logged, supervised, and the vessel was returned to the lockbox within 6 hours.

> [!warning] Standing Restriction
> The vessel is not to be moved beyond the Ritter's Hall annex without joint signature of both custodians AND a 30-day notice period. This restriction was imposed by Sigmund Holt on grounds of "preservation hazard" and is, in Sigmund's private view, only partly about preservation.

## 8 — Curator's Notes

> *"This is the most significant Ur-Flan artifact recovered in the Sepia Uplands in living memory. I want to be careful about how I record that, because I do not want subsequent generations to assume my certainty was confirmation. It may not have been. But the vessel reads as a registry, the inscription confirms the existence of a six-node binding architecture that until now has been speculative, and the interior contents — even at the surface layer — give us coordinates that the standing literature has not had.*
>
> *The Questers brought it back intact. They brought it back clean. They brought it back without opening it on the road, which is a discipline I admire. The University owes them more than the standard 600 UC and we will, in time, find ways to demonstrate that.*
>
> *On the matter of the deeper text: I will not authorise an unsupervised Second Voice attempt. I will not authorise a supervised one either, not at present, and not until we have resolved the question of who would be the second voice. The vessel's instruction is precise. It does not say two ritualists. It says a Pair. There is a difference. I have been thinking about that difference for a month.*
>
> *— S. Holt, FRO, 12 Flocktime 576 CY"*

## 10 — DM Section *(GM-Only)*

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only — Beneath the Catalog Entry
>
> The player-facing entry above is honest within its limits. What it does *not* say:
>
> - The vessel's broadcast at the binding network's frequency makes it a **passive beacon to Iuz's intelligence apparatus.** Jumper of the Boneheart has been tracking Node 2's degradation for years via the Grel copper-amulet tracker (see [[0001 - DM Design Notes — Ore, Iuz, and the Gnolls|0001 DM Design Notes]]). The vessel's recovery doesn't end that surveillance — it *relocates* the beacon to Ritterstadt. Sigmund knows this. He has not put it in writing.
> - The vessel's recovery is, by Sigmund's private assessment, the most significant Boneheart counter-intelligence loss in the last decade. Their best window into Node 2 just closed. They will respond. The timeline is months, not years.
> - The **Second Voice ritual** is the gate to the Negotiation Capstone (Option C of the binding network arc). Reconstructing a viable pair requires the [[E3 - The Flan Waymark|Horchen heirloom rings]], the [[E3 - The Flan Waymark|Pair-Day installation rite]], and the [[E3 - The Flan Waymark|Aerd & Velka grave couplet]]. None of these is impossible. All of them require time.
> - Sigmund's curator note line *"there is a difference"* between two ritualists and a Pair is doing significant work. He has come to suspect — without evidence he can defend — that he himself may be eligible for the rite. The ring in his reliquary box is one half. The other half is on Hilde Horchen's cord. He has not said this aloud. He is not yet ready to.
> - **If the vessel is ever opened with a valid Second Voice present,** the campaign clock advances dramatically. The full coordinates make all six nodes addressable. The architects' compact line — *"It may be persuaded again"* — opens the Negotiation path. This is the moment the players have *standing* to negotiate with the Erdgeist directly.
> - **If the vessel is ever physically destroyed or stolen,** the Repair Capstone path becomes unachievable. Negotiation becomes the only remaining option. This is not necessarily a bad campaign outcome — the negotiation arc is the most narratively interesting of the three capstones — but it removes a player choice.
>
> Treat the vessel as a campaign-defining object. It is the single physical artifact that, more than any other, the whole long arc turns on. Handle accordingly.
