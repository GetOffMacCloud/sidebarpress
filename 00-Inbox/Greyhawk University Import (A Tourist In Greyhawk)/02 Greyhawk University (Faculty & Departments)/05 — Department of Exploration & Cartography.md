# 🗺️ Department of Exploration & Cartography

*Know the ground before you walk it. Know the exits before you enter.*

The Field Station exists to map the frontier, and this is the department that holds the pens. Exploration & Cartography covers surveying, route-finding, wilderness craft, and the deep places under the Uplands — everything between "here" and "unknown."

**Department Head:** Magister Roswitha Kartner — never lost, never hurried. It is said she once corrected a river.

---

## Major: Cartography

*The science and art of mapping — terrain, geography, dungeon layouts, and unexplored regions.*

- **Boon 1 — Declaration:** Proficiency with navigator's tools. +10% credits on quests involving exploration, hex mapping, or route discovery.
- **Boon 2 — Field Certification:** The party has advantage on checks to avoid getting lost. Maps you formally submit to the University earn an additional 25 credits per submission.
- **Boon 3 — Advanced Study:** Once per expedition, study your current region for 1 hour. The DM identifies one feature in the surrounding area that standard travel would miss — a hidden path, a concealed structure, an unmarked crossing, or an unusual landmark.

---

## Minors Offered

*Open to students whose Major belongs to another department.*

### Subterranean Studies
Advantage on Survival checks made underground. By spending 1 minute reading rock and air, you determine the general direction to the surface and your approximate depth.

### Wilderness Fieldcraft
Advantage on checks to forage, track, and locate shelter. You can always retrace any route you yourself traveled within the past week.

## Student Organizations

### The Scouts & Surveyors
*Open to all Majors.* The University's reconnaissance society — terrain reading, tracking, and pre-expedition intelligence. Maintains both the running **terrain log** and the student **map archive** for the region around Ritterstadt.

**Member Benefit:** Access to the terrain log and map archive: advantage on Survival checks in areas previously scouted or mapped by members, and you may request a copy of any archived member map. Additionally, advantage on Stealth checks in natural outdoor environments.

### The Ramblers' Fellowship
*Membership by contribution.* A walking-and-picnicking club. They ramble. They picnic. They are unusually fit for a picnicking club, and their boots are always freshly resoled.

**Entry Criteria:** Bring the Fellowship a sketch of one place — a viewpoint, a spring, a ruin, a shortcut — that appears on no University map, and lead a member walk to it.

**Member Benefit:** The Fellowship's shared trail-notes grant advantage on checks to find water, campsites, and walkable routes within one day's travel of Ritterstadt.

%%
> [!warning] **DM Note — The Ramblers' real business**
> The Fellowship keeps the **Undermap**: a master chart of every site the University has sealed, censored, or officially denies exists — collapsed digs, quarantined hexes, the door in the cliff that the Registry says is a natural formation. Rambles are reconnaissance.
> - **True entry criteria:** the entry sketch must be of somewhere *forbidden*, not merely unmapped — and the candidate must go back with two members and come home quiet. Candidates who report the trip to faculty are cheerfully demoted to actual picnickers and never told anything again.
> - The Undermap is drawn in sections held by different members; no one carries the whole thing. Assembling it is a campaign goal in itself.
> - The Curio Club would pay handsomely for even one section — the Undermap marks where unregistered relics still sit in the ground. The Ramblers know this, and it is why the sections stay separate.
