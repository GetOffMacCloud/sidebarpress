# 🌿 Department of Natural Sciences

*The Flanaess is full of things no one has properly looked at yet.*

The department of muddy boots and full specimen jars. Natural Sciences studies the living world empirically — creatures, plants, weather, and the systems that bind them — and considers a week indoors a week wasted.

**Department Head:** Professor Berthold Grasmeyer — boundless enthusiasm, permanently sunburnt, has been "nearly finished" with his grand taxonomy of the Sepia Uplands for eleven years.

---

## Major: Natural Philosophy

*The empirical study of the natural world — creatures, plants, geological formations, and environmental systems.*

- **Boon 1 — Declaration:** Proficiency with the herbalism kit. +10% credits on quests involving creature cataloguing, environmental phenomena, or field ecology.
- **Boon 2 — Field Certification:** Advantage on Nature checks to identify flora, fauna, environmental phenomena, and weather patterns.
- **Boon 3 — Advanced Study:** Once per short rest, identify one of the following about a creature you can observe: a damage resistance, a damage immunity, or one notable special ability. No action required — this is passive recognition from field expertise.

## Minors Offered

*Open to students whose Major belongs to another department.*

### Zoology & Beastmastery
Proficiency in Animal Handling. Advantage on Nature checks to predict a creature's behavior or assess its likely reaction to the party.

### Astronomy & Weatherlore
With 10 minutes of observation, you accurately predict the next 24 hours of weather. Advantage on navigation checks made at night under open sky.

---

## Student Organization

### The Naturalist's Club
*Open to all Majors.* Maintains the Field Station's specimen collection — preserved flora, fauna, and biological oddities contributed from member expeditions.

**Member Benefit:** Access to the specimen collection, granting advantage on Nature checks to identify creatures or plants whose type is represented there. When you contribute a new specimen, the DM notes it — future consultations of the collection cover that type.
