# The Publication System

Fieldwork has no value until it is shared. Greyhawk University expects its students to contribute to the broader scholarly record — not just to complete quests, but to document what they found, what it means, and what others should know about it. The Publication System is how that expectation is formalized and rewarded.

---

## What Is a Publication?

A **Publication** is a formal academic submission — a written document (in-character) based on a completed quest or independent research that has been accepted by the University's journal, *The Flanaess Field Record*. Publications are distinct from Field Reports:

- A **Field Report** is a brief post-quest summary submitted to your Department Head. It earns a small credit bonus and is part of your Field Certification requirement for Major boons.
- A **Publication** is a longer, polished document making an argument, presenting evidence, or documenting a discovery. It goes through a peer review process and is permanently added to the University's archive.

Publications do not have to be the work of a single student — co-authored publications with other party members are accepted and count toward each co-author's total.

---

## Submitting a Publication

To submit a publication:
1. **Write the document** (in-character, any length — but it should have substance beyond a field report)
2. **Submit it to the Department Head** for your Major (or the relevant department if it crosses fields)
3. **Await peer review** — the DM may introduce a brief in-world delay (1–2 weeks in-game) and may request revisions if the submission is underdeveloped
4. Upon acceptance, the publication is **logged in your Conduct Record** and enters the world — other parties, NPCs, and institutions can reference and build upon it

> [!tip] **DM Note**
> Don't make the peer review process punishing. If a player took the time to write something in good faith, accept it. The revision process exists for submissions that are clearly just recycled field reports with a title pasted on. Reward the engagement.

---

## Publication Tiers & Boons

Publications accumulate on your record and unlock boons at three thresholds. These boons are permanent — you do not lose them if your publication count doesn't increase further.

---

### 📄 Tier 1 — Contributing Scholar *(2 Publications)*

You have demonstrated a commitment to the scholarly record beyond the minimum required of field students.

**Boons:**
- Each accepted publication earns a flat **+50 credit bonus** (applied retroactively to your first two publications upon reaching this tier)
- Your publications **exist in the world** — they can be found in the University archive, referenced by other parties, and cited by NPCs with relevant expertise. The DM may have NPCs reference your work or ask follow-up questions.
- You are recognized by your Department Head as a student worth investing in.

---

### 📚 Tier 2 — Cited Scholar *(5 Publications)*

Your work has accumulated enough presence that your name is beginning to circulate in scholarly circles.

**Boons:**
- The +50 credit bonus per publication **increases to +75 credits** (applied to all subsequent publications from this point forward)
- **Social recognition:** In interactions with academics, sages, clergy with relevant expertise, and educated NPCs, you may identify yourself as a published researcher in your field. This grants advantage on Persuasion checks in those interactions when the subject relates to your published work.
- Other student parties who read your publications gain advantage on one relevant check during expeditions that take them to locations or subjects you documented.

---

### 🏆 Tier 3 — Established Researcher *(8 Publications)*

You have produced a body of work that stands on its own — a genuine contribution to the Flanaess's scholarly record.

**Boons:**
- The credit bonus per publication **increases to +100 credits**
- **Field expertise invocation:** Once per expedition, you may declare that you have relevant prior research on the subject at hand. Roll with advantage on one check in your Major's domain — no other trigger required. This represents the practical depth of accumulated scholarly knowledge.
- You are eligible to be approached by the University to **co-author faculty research** — a narrative opportunity that grants access to resources, archives, and connections unavailable through the standard quest system.
- Your name appears in the University's public register of recognized researchers, which is visible to external institutions, patrons, and the broader scholarly community.

---

## Cross-Major Publications

You may publish in fields outside your declared Major — a Cartography student publishing findings on the architectural style of a ruin they mapped, for example. Cross-major publications count toward your total publication count and earn all standard credit bonuses, but they do not count toward the **3 Field Reports in your Major's domain** requirement for Boon 2 of your Major.

---

## Publications in the World

Every accepted publication is a real document in the campaign world. Consider:

- Other parties who access the University archive may read and benefit from your findings
- NPCs with relevant expertise may have opinions about your work — positive or critical
- Later expeditions to locations you documented may find that your description has become the standard reference
- Rival scholars (inside or outside the University) may attempt to challenge, replicate, or build upon your conclusions

> [!info] **The Archive**
> The Flanaess Field Record maintains a physical archive at the University. Students with Library Tier 2 or higher access can browse publications by other students and by faculty. A well-stocked archive is one of the reasons Greyhawk University is worth attending — and a reason students who publish are contributing to something larger than themselves.
