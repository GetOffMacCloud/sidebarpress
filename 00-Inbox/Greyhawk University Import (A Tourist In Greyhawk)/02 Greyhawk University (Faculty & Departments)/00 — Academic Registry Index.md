# The Academic Registry — Index

*The official catalog of physical artifacts held in trust by the Ritterstadt Satellite Station of Greyhawk University. Items in this registry are physically housed in the Academic Registry lockbox in the annex of [[The Ritter's Hall|the Ritter's Hall]], under the joint custody of [[Sigmund Holt|Field Research Officer Sigmund Holt]] and [[Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld|Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld]].*

---

## What Belongs In This Registry

Items that have been **physically recovered** by a sanctioned University expedition and **returned intact** to the Satellite Station. This is the *custody* registry — the record of what is in the lockbox, what has been removed (and by whom and when), and what is still pending recovery in the field.

For the *intelligence* record — translated content, scholarly analysis, derivative copies, and unverified intelligence on documents not yet in custody — see [[Player Records — The Archive]]. For *locations* explored, see [[Player Records — Explored Locations]].

> [!info] Distinction
> The Archive records what we *know.* The Registry records what we *hold.* An item can be in the Archive (we've copied its inscription) without being in the Registry (the original is still in the field). An item in the Registry is by definition also in the Archive.

## Custody & Access Policy

**Lockbox Location:** The Academic Registry lockbox is housed in the locked annex room of [[The Ritter's Hall|the Ritter's Hall]], the only stone structure in Ritterstadt secure enough for the purpose. The key is held by Ritter Konrad. The duplicate key is held by Sigmund Holt. Access to the lockbox requires the presence of both keyholders OR explicit written authorisation from the absent keyholder.

**Examination Privileges.** Sanctioned University researchers (faculty and credentialed students) may examine items in the Registry under the following conditions:
- Examination must be supervised by at least one keyholder.
- No item may be removed from the Ritter's Hall annex without joint keyholder signature.
- Notes taken during examination must be filed with the [[Player Records — The Archive|Archive]] within 30 days.
- Magical examination requires Sigmund's prior written authorisation.

**External Loans.** No item in the Registry has, to date, been loaned to an external institution. The standing policy is that loans require Ritter Konrad's signature, Sigmund's signature, and a 30-day notice period during which any party with standing in Ritterstadt may file objection.

**Recovery Party Rights.** The expedition that recovered an item retains:
- Priority examination rights for 90 days after item return.
- Right of first refusal on any decision to transfer the item from the Registry.
- Citation as discovering party on all subsequent academic work derived from the item.

This last is not a small thing. It is one of the principal mechanisms by which University Credit accrues to student parties over the long arc of the campaign.

## Reading Notes

- Each catalog entry follows the standard University Antiquities Classification format: Header → Provenance → Physical Description → Material Analysis → Inscriptional / Magical Analysis → Translation Status → Access Notes → Curator's Notes → Cross-References.
- DM-only material (campaign-arc implications, hidden mechanics, NPC reactions) is segregated into a clearly-marked DM section at the bottom of each entry. The body of the entry is player-facing and may be shared.
- Items removed from the lockbox (loaned, examined offsite, lost, destroyed) are *not* removed from the index — their entry is updated with a status change and a custody-log entry. The Registry is an append-only record.
