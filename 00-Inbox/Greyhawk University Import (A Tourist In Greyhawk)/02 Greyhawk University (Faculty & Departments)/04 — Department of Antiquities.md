# 🏺 Department of Antiquities

*What's buried should be found. What's found should be understood. What's understood should be shared.*

The department of shovels and provenance ledgers. Antiquities runs the Field Station's excavation permits, catalogues everything the Sepia Uplands gives up, and fights an eternal two-front war: against looters outside the walls, and against the Registry's paperwork inside them.

**Department Head:** Professor Casimir Vogelsang — romantic about ruins, ruthless about provenance. Quotes dead poets while brushing dirt off dead kings.

---

## Major: Archaeology

*The study of ancient structures, lost civilizations, and recovered material culture.*

- **Boon 1 — Declaration:** Proficiency with cartographer's tools. +10% credits on quests involving ruins, inscriptions, or recovered artifacts.
- **Boon 2 — Field Certification:** Advantage on History checks when examining ruins, inscriptions, or recovered objects in the field.
- **Boon 3 — Advanced Study:** Once per expedition, spend 10 minutes examining a ruin or significant structure and ask the DM one lore question about it. The DM provides a truthful answer based on what can be deduced from the physical evidence.

---

## Minors Offered

*Open to students whose Major belongs to another department.*

### Epigraphy & Iconography
Advantage on checks to decipher inscriptions, date carvings and stonework, and determine the cultural origin of symbols, scripts, and iconography.

### Appraisal & Provenance
Advantage on checks to determine the value, origin, or authenticity of objects — and to spot forgeries and counterfeits.

## Student Organizations

### The Archaeological Society
*Open to all Majors.* Manages member access to excavation and recovery operations around the Sepia Uplands.

**Member Benefit:** First notice on dig permits (24-hour lead before general posting). Borrow excavation equipment — picks, rope, block-and-tackle, lanterns — at no credit cost for one expedition per semester, provided it comes back.

### The Curio Club
*Open to all Majors. Invitation only.* A genteel collectors' salon: sherry, show-and-tell, and spirited argument over whose oddity is oldest. Meets monthly in a room above the Ritter's Hall.

**Entry Criteria:** Present a curiosity of genuine interest at a monthly salon — as a guest of a current member — and survive the ensuing questioning about where you got it. Membership is extended by unanimous vote of those present.

**Member Benefit:** Once per semester, consult the Club's network of collectors and dealers to appraise, source, or find a buyer for an unusual object — the DM introduces a relevant contact.
