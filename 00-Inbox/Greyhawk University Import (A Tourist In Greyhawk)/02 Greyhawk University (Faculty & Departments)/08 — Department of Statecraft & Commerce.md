# 🏛️ Department of Statecraft & Commerce

*If you can't argue it, you don't understand it yet. If you can't fund it, you don't get to argue.*

Power and the purse. Statecraft & Commerce studies governance, negotiation, law, and the flow of wealth across the Flanaess — and, not coincidentally, produces most of the University's administrators and all of its auditors.

**Department Head:** Professor Aldemar Goldwaage — courteous, unhurried, unreadable. Wins most negotiations by letting the other side explain themselves at length.

---

## Major: Political Philosophy & Diplomacy

*The study of governance, statecraft, negotiation, and the structures of power across the Flanaess.*

- **Boon 1 — Declaration:** Proficiency with one gaming set (for navigating social situations). One additional language. +10% credits on quests involving diplomatic missions, political negotiations, or governance investigations.
- **Boon 2 — Field Certification:** Advantage on Insight checks during formal negotiations. Advantage on Persuasion when making a structured, reasoned argument rather than an emotional appeal.
- **Boon 3 — Advanced Study:** Once per session, when interacting with an NPC in a significant negotiation or social encounter, ask the DM whether the NPC is concealing their true position or agenda. The DM answers truthfully.

## Minors Offered

*Open to students whose Major belongs to another department.*

### Economics & Trade
Learn one major trade language. Advantage on checks to appraise market value, read contracts, and sense bad-faith dealing in a negotiation or transaction.

### Law & Heraldry
Advantage on History checks concerning noble houses, legal codes, and jurisdiction. Advantage on Persuasion checks in formal proceedings when you correctly observe protocol.

## Student Organizations

### The Debater's Society
*Open to all Majors.* Formal debates, structured argumentation, and the University's best lecturers as judges.

**Member Benefit:** Advantage on Persuasion checks when making a structured, reasoned argument — explicit premises, evidence, conclusion — rather than an emotional appeal. Does not stack with the Political Philosophy Major's Boon 2 (use one, not both).

**Initiation:** Successfully argue an assigned position in a formal debate before a panel of three member judges.

### The Mercantile Society
*Open to all Majors.* A student-run sponsorship fund: members pool a portion of their credits and vote each semester on which expeditions the Society will back. Part investment club, part mutual-aid society, part ferocious budget meeting.

**Member Benefit:** Once per semester, a member expedition may win **Society sponsorship**: a modest stipend of standard supplies and equipment for one sanctioned quest, awarded by member vote. Sponsored expeditions fly the Society's little green pennant.

### The Keys & Tumblers Society
*Open to all Majors.* Locksport and puzzle hobbyists — speed-picking contests, trick boxes, and the proud maintenance of the "Challenge Wall," a board of donated locks nobody has opened yet.

**Member Benefit:** Regular practice grants advantage on checks made with thieves' tools to open non-magical locks or disarm simple mechanical traps.

**Entry Criteria:** Open any lock from the Challenge Wall's bottom row, unassisted, while a member watches. No tools are provided; bringing your own is considered the first half of the test.
