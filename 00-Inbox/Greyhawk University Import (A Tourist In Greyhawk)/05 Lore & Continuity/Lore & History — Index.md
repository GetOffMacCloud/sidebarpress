# Lore & History — Index

> [!tip] How This Section Works
> This section has two distinct layers. **DM Reference** contains material that should never be directly read by players — it is the full, unredacted history of the Sepia Uplands, the Wachturm, and the forces at play in the campaign. **Player Records** contains what the players themselves have accumulated: places they have explored, documents they have found, and lore they have confirmed in play.
>
> Move information from DM Reference to Player Records only when it has been *earned in the fiction* — found, translated, or told by a reliable NPC.
