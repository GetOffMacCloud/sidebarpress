# Explored Locations — Field Log

*Maintained by the Greyhawk University Field Survey Office, Ritterstadt Satellite Station.*
*Updated after each sanctioned expedition. Student parties are required to file a location report within 48 hours of return.*

> [!info] How to Use This Log
> **Explored** means a party has physically been there and filed a report.
> **Surveyed** means a party reached the area but did not fully investigate.
> **Reported** means reliable second-hand intelligence — not firsthand University verification.
> **Unknown** means it's on the map but no one who came back has been there.

## Active Operations

### ⚔️ The Ochre Escarpment — Hex E3
**Status:** Active — University Operation GHU1 (The Rust-Red Extraction)
**Terrain:** Sepia Uplands, escarpment edge
**Distance:** ~13 miles NE of Ritterstadt
**Danger Rating:** ⚔️⚔️⚔️ Significant

> *What's known:* A dramatic cliff face of ochre limestone dropping forty feet into the marsh fringe. A mineral deposit opening at the cliff base, partially surveyed by Osa Veld and Renn Farr before their disappearance. A gnoll presence using the base camp site. A Flan waymark stone incorporated into the escarpment face. The full interior of the deposit has not been documented.
>
> *Field warning:* Gnoll patrol activity is organized and territorial. Wind gusts at the cliff rim are significant. The interior of the deposit opening is incompletely mapped — proceed with rope, light, and chalk.

## Surveyed — Partial Reports on File

### 🔍 The Rust Seams — Hex D4
**Status:** Partially Explored — perimeter survey only
**Terrain:** Quag Mire fringe
**Distance:** ~6 miles N of Ritterstadt
**Danger Rating:** ⚔️⚔️⚔️ Significant (marsh hazards)

> *What's known:* The marsh fringe north of Ritterstadt where the sepia soil grades into waterlogged fen. Named for the iron-stained drainage channels that run south from this area. The streambed rerouting observed by travelers (~mile 3 northeast of the palisade) originates here. A water table shift has been ongoing for approximately fifty years. Unusual warmth in certain ground areas — easily mistaken for geothermal activity.
>
> *Field notes:* Passable in winter when the channels freeze. Treacherous in spring and summer. Do not travel this hex without a guide or reliable tracking.

## Reported — Intelligence Only, Not University-Verified

### 🗺️ Drowned Kessmark — Hex D3
**Status:** Unknown — no University expedition
**Terrain:** Quag Mire
**Distance:** ~12 miles N of Ritterstadt
**Danger Rating:** ⚔️⚔️⚔️⚔️ Extreme (estimated)
**Source:** Trappers at the Fiend's Rest; Wolf Nomad outrider account (second-hand)

> *What's reported:* A submerged structure — possibly a pre-Migration settlement or fortification — in the cold marsh channel approximately twelve miles north. Called "Kessmark" in old Perrenish maps, translated roughly as "the cold boundary." Trappers avoid it. Wolf Nomad oral tradition names it as a place "where the marsh remembers what it covered." No University party has approached.
>
> *Intelligence note:* The area northeast of D3, in the Quag Mire margin, is believed to be near the location where University researchers Osa Veld and Renn Farr were last reliably placed. This hex should be treated as a priority for future expedition planning.

### 🗺️ The Standing Stones — Hex G5
**Status:** Unknown — visual survey only from distance
**Terrain:** Sepia Uplands, open moor
**Distance:** ~18 miles E of Ritterstadt
**Danger Rating:** ⚔️⚔️ Moderate (estimated)
**Source:** Ritterstadt shepherd accounts; University distance survey

> *What's reported:* Seven upright monoliths in an irregular oval, tallest approximately twelve feet. Pre-Migration Flan ceremonial stonework, extensively carved. A flat altar stone at center with a bowl depression. An eighth position in the oval appears vacant — possibly a fallen stone. Seasonal reports from shepherds describe the site as "different" at solstice and equinox.
>
> *Intelligence note:* Wolf Nomad outriders have been observed near this site at intervals consistent with their migration schedule. The site appears to hold significance to them. Approach with care and cultural awareness. Do not touch the stones without consultation.

### 🗺️ The Frozen Library — Hex A2
**Status:** Unknown — no University expedition
**Terrain:** Quag Mire / Lake Quag northern shore
**Distance:** ~25 miles NW of Ritterstadt
**Danger Rating:** ⚔️⚔️⚔️⚔️ Extreme (access alone)
**Source:** Perrenish monastery archive records; private letter from Ritterstadt estate archive

> *What's reported:* A pre-Migration structure on the northern lakeshore, referenced in Old Flan as *Brekessen-thal* — "the place where words are kept cold." A monastic record describes "a great hall of stone at the marsh-edge of the northern water." Local Flan-descended workers reportedly considered it common knowledge, referring to it as "the library under the ice."
>
> *Intelligence note:* No one who has reported entering this site has also reported exiting it with an account of the interior. Access difficulty is extreme — 25 miles through Quag Mire terrain, passing through Hex D3. This is a Tier 3 Priority site. Do not approach without full expedition preparation and at minimum two experienced guides.

### 🗺️ Ehlonna's Mount — Hex J8
**Status:** Known Location — not yet University-visited
**Terrain:** Sepia Uplands highland
**Distance:** ~38 miles SE of Ritterstadt
**Danger Rating:** ⚔️ Low (approach); Unknown (interior)
**Source:** Established knowledge — druid enclave location is publicly known

> *What's known:* The standing stone circle maintained by the Old Faith druid enclave, located on a highland prominence in the southeastern Sepia Uplands. The enclave maintains extensive ecological records of the region. They are neither hostile nor welcoming — they are evaluating. A formal relationship with the enclave is considered one of the highest-priority diplomatic objectives available to the current student body.
>
> *Approach guidance:* Respectful. Patient. Do not collect specimens without permission. Do not discuss the Quag Mires as a resource or exploration opportunity. Come with questions, not demands.
