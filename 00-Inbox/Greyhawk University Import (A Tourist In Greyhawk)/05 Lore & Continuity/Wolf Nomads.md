# Wolf Nomads

**The Wegwuir · nomadic horse-folk of the northern steppes, along the north shore of [[Regional Guide - The Sepia Uplands & Lake Quag|Lake Quag]].**

Territorial herders following ancestral migration routes across the plains north and east of the Sepia Uplands. They share Lake Quag's fish and waterfowl with the Perrenlanders of the southern shore, and their own legends speak of the lake's deadly center — the **Jaws of Quag**.

[[Perrenland — Concatenated Cantons|Perrenland]] courted a tenuous alliance with the Wolf and Tiger nomads during the Greyhawk Wars, and they remain an important buffer and wildcard on the northern frontier — pressed, like everyone here, by the shadow of **[[Iuz]]** to the north.
