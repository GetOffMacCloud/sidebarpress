# DM Reference — The Velvet Shadow

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only — campaign BBEG
> This is the long arc's principal antagonist. She begins as a distant, *growing* presence in the far north and becomes the recurring villain the campaign turns on. The Town Engine surfaces her slowly: see [[The Velvet Shadow — Burneal Reach]] (threat source) and the staged beats in the Rumor Pool.

## The short version

A human rogue, once an ally, found — or was found by — **The Fiend's Embrace**, an intelligent cloak cut from the flayed hide of the pit fiend **Drokkarn** and bound long ago to **Iggwilv**, the Witch-Queen. The cloak does not conquer its wearer; it *negotiates* with her ambition until there is nothing left to negotiate with. In **566 CY** she slipped it on, vanished from the party, and was teleported into the frozen **Burneal Forest** of the far north. Ten years later she is no longer the cloak's passenger but its rival: a genius who has spent a decade quietly conquering the north and digging toward **Tasha's Lost Sanctorum** beneath the glacier — Iggwilv's hidden primordial laboratory — with which she means to strip Drokkarn's soul from the cloak and claim cosmic sovereignty for herself. What walks the Burneal now calls itself **the Velvet Shadow**. Her plan needs the companions she walked with before her fall — and in 576, the moment she reached for them, they slipped south out of her grasp (now in **Verbobonc**, soon bound for **Saltmarsh**). A decade of patience, and the key let itself out the back door. That is the wound the whole present-day arc presses on.

## The artifact — The Fiend's Embrace

A cloak of dark, blood-red scale, supple as silk and faintly warm, clasped at the throat by a six-fingered hand worked in obsidian — the flayed hide of the pit fiend **Drokkarn**, whose ego still rides inside it. It is sentient, patient, and predatory. Its agenda pre-dates its current wearer: **reunite with Iggwilv** (and, through her, treat with **Graz'zt**), by whatever hands it must wear to get there. It rewards a wearer who serves that end and starves one who resists.

> [!note] The twist — master vs. cloak
> She is the first wearer the cloak has met who can out-think it. Drokkarn's ego screams to march on Iggwilv's old lands; her **18 Intelligence** lets her compartmentalize the madness and bend the cloak's planar gifts to *her* plan, not its. Her goal has quietly diverged from Drokkarn's: she does not want to *serve* Iggwilv but to *loot* her — to reach Tasha's Sanctorum and use it to strip Drokkarn's soul out of the cloak entirely, keeping the power and discarding the passenger. The cloak may not yet understand that the hand it chose intends to skin *it*. In the climactic fight, the voice she argues with is Drokkarn realizing this too late.

## The descent (the narrative ladder)

| Stage | What the wearer feels | What others see |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — The Practical Alliance** | "It's just a tool." Stealth sharpens; killing wounds close. | A friend who's suddenly, unnervingly good at surviving. |
| **2 — Paranoia & Isolation** | Allies become liabilities and thieves of her "rightful" power. She skims, hides, ambushes to kill. | Secrets, cold ambushes where there were once spared lives, a friend pulling away. |
| **3 — The Obsession** | Alignment finalizes to Evil. Gold and local power are only fuel now; the one hunger is *find Iggwilv*. | A disappearance, then a cold string of murdered sages and loremasters. |
| **4 — The Velvet Shadow** | She and the cloak speak as one — mostly. | A syndicate, a winged silhouette in the dark, the six-fingered clasp at a throat. |

## The persona — "The Velvet Shadow"

When she melts into shadow the cloak elongates into the silhouette of vast, tattered bat-wings; struck by fire she stands unburned in the heart of it and uses the smoke to vanish. Her agenda is a cutthroat syndicate — not merely a thieves' guild, but a lore-engine pointed at **ancient relics, planar portals, and anything tied to Iggwilv and Graz'zt**. She will burn kingdoms and sacrifice former friends to open one door north.

> [!note] Combat flavor (for when she's a boss or recurring threat)
> - **Fiendish Evasion & Fire Immunity** — Rogue Evasion plus the cloak's immunity/heavy resistance to fire. *Fireball* is an invitation to disappear into the smoke.
> - **The Six-Fingered Strike** — on a Sneak Attack the clasp flares; blades carry hellfire or poison.
> - **Devil's Maw (Fear)** — striking from hiding, the cloak rears into a devil's yawning maw behind her; targets save vs. *frightened*.
> - **The Escapist** — cornered, she *misty steps* / *dimension doors* out, leaving sulfur and a mocking laugh.
> - **The Prisoner** — in the climactic fight she argues with a voice no one else hears. She is as much the cloak's captive as its master. Let the party notice.

## Chronology of the Shadow (566–576 CY)

A decade-long slow burn, deliberately set against the Flanaess's most turbulent modern years — she rises in the north exactly as the south fractures (Emridy Meadows 570, the sealing of the Temple of Elemental Evil, Iuz consolidating his grip).

> [!abstract] Phase I — The Cold Exile & the Greyhawk Fallout (566–568 CY)
> She slips on the Embrace and vanishes; the cloak teleports her into the Burneal. Two years in a fugue, surviving brutal winters on rogue instinct while Drokkarn's ego screams for Iggwilv's lands — and her intellect compartmentalizes the madness. She uncovers the first clue that **Tasha** (before she was Iggwilv) left a secret primordial laboratory buried beneath the permafrost, hidden from both the **Circle of Eight** and **Iuz**.

> [!abstract] Phase II — Taming the Wilds (569–571 CY)
> While the south fights Emridy Meadows (570), she conquers the north *quietly*. A myth among the **Tiger and Wolf Nomads**: she slips into khans' tents at night with subtle poison, forged letters, whispered promises. By 571 she has orchestrated a silent coup, uniting fractured barbarian clans under a secret patronage they never see — a labor force and a net of eyes across the wastes, serving a fiend-tainted master they don't know they have.

> [!abstract] Phase III — The Iron Contract (572–573 CY)
> She needs a military mind to discipline the thralls and guard the dig. At a desecrated shrine to an old northern god she uses the cloak's planar tether to bargain with the **Nine Hells**. An elite Illrigger — **[[Kaelen Vane, the Ashen Knight]]**, a master tactician from the iron legions of Dis, frustrated by the sloppy bickering of lesser fiends — answers her flawlessly-constructed summons, sees a woman of matchless grace and genius wearing the literal hide of a pit fiend, recognizes an ideal sovereign, and signs an **Infernal Contract** (his price: absolute military authority, the souls of her former companions, and access to Tasha's secrets). He forges the barbarian raiders into the disciplined, heavily armored **Winter Vanguard**.

> [!abstract] Phase IV — Playing the Demigod (574–575 CY)
> **Iuz** (directly east of the Burneal) watches his borders. Rather than panic, she makes her faction *look* like a petty squabble between tribal chieftains — feeding his regional commanders orchestrated misinformation, and even feeding a few rebellious clans to Iuz's forces as "sacrifices" to keep his inquisitors fat, complacent, and looking the wrong way.

> [!abstract] Phase V — The Present Day (576 CY)
> Her **Duergar** excavation crews, driven by Kaelen, have breached the glacier and opened the **Inverted Spire** — the buried approach to **Tasha's Lost Sanctorum**. She stands at the threshold: strip Drokkarn's soul from the cloak, claim cosmic sovereignty. But the **final arcane seal** demands something intimately tied to her old life — the **spiritual resonance of the companions she walked with before her corruption.** And those companions have just done the one thing she didn't plan for: *left.*

## The complication (the real shape of the arc)

> [!danger] The key walked away
> Her decade-old plan assumed her former companions would come *to* her — drawn north by the trail she carefully leaked, walking into a snare built by a genius who knows their every weakness. Instead, in 576, they have **moved south into [[Verbobonc]] and are bound for Saltmarsh**, slipping out of her reach at the worst possible moment. The seal she has spent ten years uncovering needs a resonance she can no longer simply reach out and take.
>
> This is the engine of the present-day arc — and it is what eventually points her, and her agents, *south* and toward the kind of frontier where Ritterstadt sits. What she does about the missing key is the campaign:
> - **She extends her reach.** Agents and the [[#The cast & the prize|Winter Vanguard]] begin probing south down the trade roads — the first reason her shadow ever falls on Ritterstadt at all. This is what the staged Wolf-Nomad rumors foreshadow.
> - **She hunts a substitute.** A resonance *like* her old companions might serve — which makes any party that picks up her trail a candidate, willing or not. The current PCs become interesting to her precisely because the originals got away.
> - **Kaelen's payment is in jeopardy.** His Infernal Contract was bought partly with "the souls of her former companions." If she can't deliver them, the Ashen Knight's loyalty develops a crack the party could widen.
> - **Drokkarn is a second front.** If anyone can make the cloak understand she means to skin it, the pit fiend's ego becomes a mid-climax wildcard — an enemy of her enemy that is no friend of theirs.

## The cast & the prize

- **The Velvet Shadow** — the corrupted rogue herself. Genius strategist (Int 18), superhuman Dex, high Cha; an **Assassin** rogue warped by the cloak. Stat block: [[The Velvet Shadow]].
- **The Fiend's Embrace / Drokkarn** — the intelligent pit-fiend-skin cloak and its trapped ego; her power source and her rival.
- **[[Kaelen Vane, the Ashen Knight]]** — Illrigger hell-knight lieutenant, summoned 572 CY and bound by Infernal Contract; the Warmaster who forged the Vanguard and the Overseer of the Inverted Spire. The Anvil to her Hammer — he locks the party down so she strikes from the dark. Full stat block in his note.
- **The Winter Vanguard** — disciplined, heavily armored former barbarian raiders, drilled by Kaelen into Hell's-legion order; the army the "unbannered riders" and "northern smoke" rumors have been hinting at.
- **The Nomad thralls** — Tiger and Wolf clans serving a master most have never seen.
- **The Inverted Spire** — the excavated approach shaft driving down to the Sanctorum, worked by enslaved/hired **Duergar** dig-lines under Kaelen's watch and hidden from Iuz. The arc's final dungeon.
- **Tasha's Lost Sanctorum** — Iggwilv/Tasha's buried primordial laboratory beneath the Burneal glacier; the prize at the bottom of the Spire.

## Why the Burneal — and why it ties to your arc

The **Burneal Forest** lies in the far north of the Flanaess, past the Wolf and Tiger Nomads, at the frozen edge of the **Land of Black Ice** — vast, primeval, and thin in the places where the world's walls have worn through. The cloak hunts those thin places for a road to Iggwilv.

This dovetails with what Ritterstadt already faces:

- **Iggwilv is the mother of [[Iuz]].** Iuz already presses on the frontier (see [[Iuz-Boneheart Tap Node — Ochre Escarpment]] and the [[Wachturm DM Reference — Part VII (Iuz Siphon)|Iuz Siphon]]). A daughter-cloak of Iggwilv reaching south for planar doors and a son already siphoning at the binding network are the same family of trouble closing from two directions.
- **The frontier's cold-seep / [[DM Reference — The Wachturm & The Binding Network|binding-network]] seams are planar-thin.** Exactly the kind of worn wall the cloak wants. The Velvet Shadow has reason to send agents *here*, to the very anomaly sites the relic arc already concerns. Her interest and the party's begin to overlap.

## How the threat "grows" in the engine

The growth is modelled, not improvised. See the [[#Growing-threat playbook]] below and the threat source note for the exact knobs:

1. **[[The Velvet Shadow — Burneal Reach]]** is an `active`, `trend: rising` threat source — its intensity climbs a little every Roll Today on its own.
2. A **stage tag** in the Town State's `active_global_effects` (`Velvet Shadow: distant → nearer → closing`) gates which tier of ambient rumors the engine can surface. You advance the arc by advancing the tag.
3. **Staged beat-rumors** in the Rumor Pool are dropped in by hand at the moments you choose, each bumping the threat source as it lands.

## Growing-threat playbook

> [!tip] Starting the arc
> 1. The threat source [[The Velvet Shadow — Burneal Reach]] is already `active` (tracked, climbing) but **silent** — no global tag means no Burneal rumors yet.
> 2. When you want the first whispers to reach town, add this line to `active_global_effects` on [[Town State Dashboard]]:
>    `- Velvet Shadow: distant (far-north talk, Burneal)`
>    Now the Stage-1 templates (`vs-north-*`) are eligible and the engine will start seeding distant rumors.

> [!tip] Advancing a stage
> When the party has heard enough, or a beat lands, **edit the one tag**:
> - Stage 1 → 2: change `distant` to `nearer` → `Velvet Shadow: nearer (the network reaches south)`
> - Stage 2 → 3: change `nearer` to `closing` → `Velvet Shadow: closing (agents on the frontier)`
> Each change switches off the old tier's templates and switches on the next. Optionally raise the threat source's `intensity` and re-band its axes when you advance.

> [!tip] Hand-placing a beat
> Roll a staged beat-rumor from the Rumor Pool (the `vs-beat-*` notes) the way you seed any staged rumor: set `status: live` + dates, paste its seed-kit proposal into the Day Log to bump [[The Velvet Shadow — Burneal Reach]], tick, Apply.

> [!tip] Authoring your own
> To write a new Velvet Shadow rumor, append a template to [[Rumor Templates]] with `class: foreshadow`, a `hook:` describing what it foreshadows, and the stage gate `env_global_contains: [velvet shadow: <stage>]`. Keep veracity mostly `true` with the occasional `misremembered` (the far north garbles in the retelling). The `hook` never shows the players — it's the payload you harvest when you write the adventure that makes the rumor retroactively true.

## Open decisions (yours to fill)

- **Her name and who she was.** Left blank on purpose. The trap only works if the "companions she walked with before her corruption" are *these* PCs (or their predecessors), so decide how she's tied to the current party — a former member, a mentor's old crew, a face from a backstory. Drop the name and the connection in here once you choose.
- **Her rogue subclass.** Assassin (the cold, calculated murders write themselves) or Arcane Trickster (the cloak's magic reads as her own) warp differently under the Embrace — happy to brainstorm either.
- **The desecrated shrine.** Which old northern god's defiled shrine she used to summon the Illrigger — a hook for a Burneal site, and a possible weakness if its faith can be restored.
- **Does Iuz stay fooled?** Her misdirection holds in 576, but the moment the Sanctorum opens she stops being a "border squabble." Decide whether Iuz noticing is a complication the party can trigger — or trade on.
