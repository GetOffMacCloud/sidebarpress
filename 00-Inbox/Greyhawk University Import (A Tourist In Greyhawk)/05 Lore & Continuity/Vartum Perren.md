# Vartum Perren

**Ranger-knight of [[Beory]] · founder and first Voorman of [[Perrenland — Concatenated Cantons|Perrenland]] (fl. 389–400 CY).**

A hero of legendary stature from one of the upland cantons. In **389 CY** he launched a guerrilla campaign — the **Yatil Uprising** — against Kettite interests and the minions of the decadent Ferrond-appointed governor **Ivixius the Proud**. The uplands were liberated by **394 CY**.

Perren then carried the war into the lowlands, aided by mercenaries sickened by Ivixius' atrocities, winning battle after battle. He slew Ivixius in the final engagement in **Harvester 399 CY** — a duel the bards still sing of — and by **400 CY** had united the cantons into the nation that bears his name.

## Hooks
- His memory is a national touchstone; oaths, festivals, and canton pride trace back to him.
- Lost relics, tombs, or war-sites of the Uprising make ready dungeon/mystery seeds.
