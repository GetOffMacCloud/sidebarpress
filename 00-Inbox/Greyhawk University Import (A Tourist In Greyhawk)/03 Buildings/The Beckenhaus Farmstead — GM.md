# GM Secret

> [!NOTE|ghost] GM Secret
> Kurt is the grain thief — the drawn-down surplus in Hanz's mill log ends up as offerings at the pre-Pholtine tree-shrine in the east forest, left on moonless nights in private desperation over little Marta's illness. The Erdgeist at the shrine has noticed. Ilse knows he goes; she has not asked why, because she already understands.

# GM Notes

- **The household mood:** Careful. The family talks around the difficult thing — Marta's illness — with the practiced efficiency of people who have been doing it for months. Visitors will feel the weight of something unsaid in the first five minutes.
- **Ilse:** The most important Beckenhaus. She will not volunteer information about the family's past or about what Kurt is doing. If a player character earns her trust slowly — genuine interest, genuine patience — she can become an extraordinary source of lore. She knows, in the way that some people just know, that the old forest shrine is real. She doesn't have a theology for it. She has a feeling she inherited from her mother who inherited it from her mother.
- **Reading the room:** A DC 12 Insight check within five minutes of meeting the family confirms that something is wrong beyond the stated problem of the sick child. A DC 15 Insight check, or extended conversation, identifies that at least one adult in the household is carrying a secret they believe is shameful.
- **The wundstein:** A DC 13 Religion or History check identifies the stone figure behind Pelor's disc as Flan. A DC 16 check identifies it specifically as a *wundstein* — a pre-Migration ward-object, used to maintain a physical connection to a genius loci site. Ilse will notice if someone picks it up. She will not explain it. She will ask, very quietly, that they put it back.
- **The bark-parchment:** Hidden under the hearthstone. DC 14 Perception to notice the stone is slightly loose. The words on it are a mix of Oeridian prayer structure and intuited placation — "I ask the one who watches here to see my daughter. I bring grain as is right. I ask only that she be allowed to continue." Not the correct Flan ritual. Not without effect.
