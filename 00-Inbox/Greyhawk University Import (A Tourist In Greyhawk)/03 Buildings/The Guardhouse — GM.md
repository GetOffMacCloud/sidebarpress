# GM Secret

> [!NOTE|ghost] GM Secret
> One of the garrison soldiers — a quiet, young man named **Ewald** — is not who he claims to be. He arrived six months ago claiming to be from a village to the west that was destroyed in a gnoll raid. Hildegard checked his story as well as she could and found nothing to contradict it. But Ewald knows things about the eastern passes that a farmboy shouldn't. He is in fact a scout planted by a rival power (or a criminal guild, or a rival noble — DM's choice) to map the village's defenses and report on the Ritter's movements. He is not cruel and has not yet been asked to do anything harmful. He is beginning to feel genuinely loyal to Ritterstadt.

# GM Notes

- **Garrison Strength:** 7 soldiers + Captain Hildegard. One bunk is currently empty — the eighth position has been vacant for two months since a soldier was killed by a winter wolf in the northern pass. Hildegard has not filled the slot yet; she is being selective.
- **Watch Schedule:** Two-on, four-off rotation. The night watch is 2 hours per soldier. Hildegard does not pull watch duty herself — she inspects it.
- **The Duty Roster:** A DC 13 Investigation check on the roster reveals that watch coverage on the eastern gate is thinner between the 2nd and 4th hour past midnight — a gap Hildegard is aware of and irritated about (she's short-staffed).
- **The Weapons Rack:** Beyond standard-issue spears and short swords, Hildegard keeps a personal crossbow of unusually fine construction — southern make, not local. She doesn't comment on where it came from.
- **Morale:** Generally high. Hildegard runs a fair but demanding posting. The soldiers grumble, but they do not complain to her face.
