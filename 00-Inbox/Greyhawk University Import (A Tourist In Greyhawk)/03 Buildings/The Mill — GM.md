# GM Secret

> [!NOTE|ghost] GM Secret
> Old Hanz knows the path to the cave above the northern snowline where Dietrich Hammerschlag (Bruni's late husband) died. He guided a hunting party there once, years ago, when the cave was first discovered. What they found inside — he hasn't told anyone. What he does know: the cave has a second entrance, much further into the mountain, which opens onto something that should not be there. He calls it "the Kammer" (the Chamber). He has not been back. He has been dreaming about it every winter since.

# GM Notes

- **The Grain Log:** Hanz's chalk ledger on the mill wall accounts for every bushel in the village. A DC 11 Investigation check reveals a discrepancy over the past three months — someone has been drawing grain from the surplus store slightly faster than the harvest accounts for. Not dramatically, but consistently. Either the village has more mouths than the census says, or someone is selling surplus grain to outside parties.
- **Hanz as lore source:** Hanz knows the old Flan names for every hill, stream, and pass within 30 miles of Ritterstadt. He also knows which places are avoided, and why. He is the only person who knows the full extent of the old path network through the Sepia Uplands, many of which are not on any map. DC 13 Persuasion + sharing a drink with him will unlock this knowledge over the course of an evening.
- **The brook in winter:** The brook freezes from Needfest through roughly mid-Readying. During freeze, Hanz uses hand-querns and is generally in a foul mood. He is also, ironically, most willing to talk during this period — he has nothing else to do.
- **The mill cat:** An enormous, battle-scarred orange tom named **Kaiserschnurrbart** ("Emperor Whiskers") lives in the mill and controls the rodent population with terrifying efficiency. He regards strangers with professional contempt. He regards Hanz as his property.
