# GM Secret

> [!NOTE|ghost] GM Secret
> The stained-glass window was not made locally. It arrived by cart from the south approximately forty years ago, paid for by an anonymous benefactor. Vater Sigmund knows who paid for it — a man named Hassenrath, a scholar from the City of Greyhawk who was passing through Ritterstadt and became obsessed with the old carvings on the Wachturm. Hassenrath donated the window, asked Sigmund several pointed questions about the tower, and disappeared north a week later. He never returned. Sigmund has his notes. He has read them many times. He is afraid of what they say.

# GM Notes

- **Vater Sigmund's Records:** The chapel sacristy holds three large vellum ledger-books of village records going back 87 years. A DC 14 Investigation check on these records can reveal: specific family lineages, dates of unusual deaths, a gap of 11 years (CY 489–500) where no records were kept and no explanation is given, and a note in a different hand on the last page that reads simply: *"Do not open the floor of the tower. — H."*
- **Healing:** Sigmund can provide basic herbalist care (healer's kit equivalent) and is proficient in Medicine. He cannot cast spells, but his tinctures grant advantage on death saving throws if administered within 1 hour of injury (1 use per patient per day).
- **Confession:** Sigmund takes confessional privacy extremely seriously — he will not break it under any circumstance short of direct divine compulsion. However, he can sometimes be persuaded to confirm or deny whether *someone has* confessed to something, without revealing what.
- **The Pholtine faith here:** Frontier Pholtus worship is less dogmatic than urban practice. Here it is about endurance, light against darkness, and community over doctrine. Sigmund is gentle, not fanatical. He disapproves of the Greyhawk orthodoxy's harsher pronouncements.
