# GM Secret

> [!NOTE|ghost] GM Secret
> The oldest trees — the **Heart Ring**, eleven vast wild apples at the orchard's center — were never planted by any McCockin. They were already growing in a near-perfect circle when Chuck first walked the slope, and they are the reason he chose this ground over better-looking land nearer the road: the wild ring was fruiting when nothing else on the hillside was. The Ring stands over a buried Flan blessing-stone, a cousin of the *wundstein* tradition. Chuck's custom of pouring the year's first pressing at the roots of the Heart Ring ("for the orchard's luck," Grant says, as Chuck said before him) is, unknowingly, a maintained offering — and it is the real reason the soil does not fight here. The family knows none of this. If the offerings ever stopped, the orchard would have three ordinary harvests, and then it would have the same soil as everyone else.

# GM Notes

- **Grant as patron:** The University's biggest donor is a standing narrative lever. He can sponsor expeditions, endow grants, lean on the Field Station for favors, and open doors in the City of Greyhawk. He expects nothing crass in return — but he *notices* who treats his daughter well, and Ritterstadt's faculty notice whom he notices. If a patron NPC is ever needed for a Capstone stipend at Sophomore tier or above, Grant is the obvious first candidate.
- **New money vs. old title:** The McCockins are wealthier than [[Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld]], and Konrad has quiet money troubles. Whether Grant has ever discreetly covered a shortfall for the Ritter — and what that would mean for both men if it were known — is left to the DM as a live thread. Grant would never speak of it. Neither would Konrad. That silence is a plot hook.
- **Bethany at home:** The estate is [[Bethany McCockin-Simmons|Bethany]]'s childhood home. Her Folk Hero background is not a contradiction but a choice — she works like a hired hand, drinks at [[The Grey Wolf]] with the farm families, and is mildly embarrassed by the slate roof. Scenes here give her player free characterization: the porch, the press, the father who funds the institution that grades her.
- **Harvest season:** From late Patchwall through the first frosts, the orchard employs a dozen or more villagers and any able-bodied traveler willing to work — the best legitimate cover in the campaign for putting strangers, spies, or new PCs inside Ritterstadt's social web for a tenday.
- **The cider as plot currency:** A cask of McCockin Reserve opens doors that coin cannot — it is the standard gift when Ritterstadt needs goodwill from a caravan master, a Perrenland factor, or a City scholar. If the party needs a diplomatic token, Grant can be persuaded to part with one.
