# The Favor Tracker

Back to [[SP-PBS-01-The-False-Arrow]]

The adventure runs on one track. Put it on the table where the players can see it: a row of nine boxes on an index card, with a coin as the marker. **Say what it is at the start of Scene 2, and move it in front of them every time it changes.** The number is the adventure.

## Favor (0–9)

The party starts at **0**: strangers, tolerated because they came under a parley-flag with gifts.

| When | Earned | Lost |
|---|---|---|
| Scene 2 — the Cold Welcome | **+1** handle Batzorig without drawing steel (Kartner's guest-craft locks it in) | **−1** draw a weapon in the pavilion |
| Scene 3 — Trial of the Horse | **+2** success · **+1** partial | — |
| Scene 4 — Trial of the Bow | **+2** success (horn-bow killing blow) · **+1** partial (tiger dies another way) | — |
| Scene 4 — the ravine dead | **+1** bring the bodies and evidence back openly | — |
| Scene 5 — Night in Yecha | **+1** don't spy ("the threads stayed clean") · **0** spy unseen | **−2** caught spying |
| Scene 6 — Trial of the Fire | **+1** per costly honest answer (max +2) | **−1** caught in a lie |

**Maximum: 9.** Favor never goes below 0.

## What Favor Buys (check at the start of Scene 7)

| Favor | Result |
|---|---|
| **7–9** | **Voice and the tiger-claw token.** Cligir gives them the floor and takes the token off his own wrist. Round 1 Persuasion DC drops from 14 to 12. |
| **5–6** | **Voice.** Run the council. |
| **4** | **Voice only with Sarnai's endorsement** (gentled the stallion). Otherwise, below. |
| **0–4** | **Expelled.** If Sarnai holds the ravine evidence → **Soft fail** (she presents it). If not → **Cast out**. |

## Other Standing Effects

- **Weapons.** Batzorig holds the party's blades from Scene 2. Horse success: returned at once. Partial: after the Bow. Fail: when they leave Yecha.
- **Sarnai's endorsement** (gentling success only): help arrives on round 1 of the night attack; one council reroll; carries Favor 4 over the threshold.
- **Spied unseen:** advantage on council Round 2.
- **Told Sarnai about Kezrin before the council:** Kezrin is captured at the flap, no roll; the soft fail is open.
- **Caught lying at the Fire:** Sarnai's face is stone at the council; she won't take their evidence, and the soft fail is closed.

## The Council Matrix

| Rounds won | The parley | The war | Ending |
|---|---|---|---|
| Round 2 **and** Round 3 | Passes | Temur stands down | **The parley holds** |
| Any two, not both 2 and 3 | Passes | Temur rides east: half his riders if Round 2 won, all if not | **A year bought** |
| Round 2 only (or soft fail) | Refused | Temur rides with half | **Truth without trust** |
| None, or Round 1 or 3 alone | Refused | Temur rides with all | **Heard and refused** |
