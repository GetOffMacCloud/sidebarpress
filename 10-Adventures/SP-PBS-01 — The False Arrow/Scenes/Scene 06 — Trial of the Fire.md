# Scene 06 — Trial of the Fire

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-01 — The False Arrow/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 05 — Night in Yecha]] · Next → [[Scene 07 — The War Council]]

## Purpose
Three honest answers. If the party tells Sarnai about the false flag, she becomes their ally — the key to Kezrin's capture and to the soft fail.
**Budget:** 15 minutes. Running long? Ask two questions instead of three.

## Sarnai's Fire
> *At dusk they bring you to a small juniper fire before Sarnai's tent.*

Then deliver these one at a time:
- The sky is going violet-black and thick with early stars.
- No pavilion, no crowd — just the old woman, the fire, and the wind moving through the guy-ropes like breath through teeth.
- The smoke smells of resin and something older.
- Sarnai doesn't look up. She feeds the fire one twig at a time. There is room on the felt across from her.

**Then wait.** Say nothing until a player speaks. The silence is part of the trial; don't tell them so. If it runs past half a minute, give them one nudge: *"Sarnai adds a twig. The fire pops. She is waiting."*

## The Three Questions
[[Mother-Sarnai]] asks them the way you'd ask about the weather. Pick three, tailored to what happened. If a PC tried to bluff Cligir in Scene 2, her **first question goes to that PC**.

1. *"You carry two letters. I have heard one read. Why did your priests send you — truly?"* (Batzorig told her about the second letter the day they arrived. She doesn't know what's in it; she knows it exists. A lie about it is a lie she can see.)
2. *"What did you find in the ravine?"*
3. *"If the horde rides to war tomorrow, will you ride with us or against us?"*
4. *(To a cleric or paladin.)* *"Your god and mine both claim the thread. What does yours say about a man who is told his fate and fights it?"*

Any answer can pass, as long as it's honest. She reads faces; she uses no magic. **Deception against her is at disadvantage.** **Each honest answer that costs the speaker something: Favor +1 (max +2).** Admitting what Halya asked — whether or not they did it — always counts as costly. So does admitting they took Kezrin's money.

For honest answers about hard things, she nods slowly and feeds the fire:
> **Sarnai:** *"Good. The cloth holds."*

## If They Tell Her About Kezrin and the False Flag
Her face goes very still. The twig stops halfway to the fire.
> **Sarnai:** *"Show me, tomorrow, before the council. Bring what you found. Do not tell Temur's riders tonight — grief makes men deaf."*

She looks up; her one good eye is wet and furious at once.
> **Sarnai:** *"Someone made arrows to look like Wolf arrows and put them in our dead. Someone wants my people to bleed for a lie. Istus weaves. Tomorrow, we pull the thread and see what unravels."*

**This is a key.** If the party gives Sarnai the evidence before the council:
- she posts riders on Kezrin (Scene 7, *Kezrin's exit*, option 1), and
- if Favor falls short, she can carry the evidence into the council herself (Scene 7, *Soft fail*; the rule is in [[SP-PBS-01 — The Favor Tracker]]).

## Caught in a Lie
The trial ends. Sarnai sets down her twig and folds her hands.
> **Sarnai:** *"You tried to cut the thread. We will see what holds."*

**Favor −1.** She won't speak to the party again before the council; there, her face is stone. She will not accept their evidence, and the soft fail is closed.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** one milky eye and one dark one; a twig stopped in midair
- **Sound:** guy-ropes in the wind; the fire popping; a silence nobody wants to break
- **Smell:** juniper resin and something older
- **Touch:** cold at the back, heat at the face
- **Taste:** smoke

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **A PC refuses to answer.** Sarnai accepts silence as an answer: no Favor, no penalty. *"That is also true."*
- **The party arrives without the evidence (stolen in Scene 5).** They can still describe what they saw. Sarnai believes a costly honest description: Favor counts, and the soft fail is still open, because Sarnai will fetch Tsolmon's arrow from Temur herself (see the soft-fail rule in [[SP-PBS-01 — The Favor Tracker]]).
