# Scene 03 — Trial of the Horse

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-01 — The False Arrow/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — The Cold Welcome]] · Next → [[Scene 04 — Trial of the Bow]]

## Purpose
The table's first taste of nomad values: the horde honors the rider who masters a horse and despises the one who breaks it. Success returns their weapons; gentling earns Sarnai's endorsement.
**Budget:** 30 minutes. Running long? One attempt, by the party's best rider. Take the break after this scene.

## The Horse Lines at Dawn
> *Dawn comes grey and sideways, the sun a rumor behind the Yatils.*

Then deliver these one at a time:
- The horse lines stretch along the frozen creek: a hundred shaggy steppe ponies, stamping and steaming.
- Apart from them, alone at the far picket, a black stallion with a scarred muzzle tracks you the whole way down the line.
- A crowd has gathered — riders, children, old women with milky tea. Someone is already taking bets.
- A child points at the black one and holds up four fingers. Four riders. Four falls.

**To the table:** *"Ride him or gentle him — and who goes first?"*

[[Mother-Sarnai]] explains the trial in the cold:
> **Sarnai:** *"Our young riders earn their name with the Horse. Ride the black one — or gentle him, if you have the patience for the harder road. The horde respects mastery. Not domination. Remember the difference. It is the whole trial."*

Temur's riders narrate like a sports crowd. [[Batzorig]] is among them, the hide bundle of the party's weapons on his saddle. **Chuluun**, a scar-faced young woman, calls out in broken Common:
> **Chuluun:** *"South-man! The black one ate the last priest's mule! Maybe he likes holy meat!"*

**The stallion.** Seventeen hands of winter muscle, breath in hard white plumes, ears pinned the moment anyone steps inside ten feet. Old rope-burns ring his neck. Someone caught him once, and he remembers.

## The Challenge (best of the party's efforts)
| Approach | Check | Success |
|---|---|---|
| **Ride him** | Athletics or Acrobatics DC 14, three rounds (one check each) | Two successes. Failure by 5+: thrown, 1d6 bludgeoning. |
| **Gentle him** | Animal Handling DC 16, three checks over an hour | Two successes. Slower and rarer — earns **Sarnai's endorsement**. |

Anyone may attempt; the party's best result counts. Disadvantage on the first check if a weapon was drawn in the pavilion.

Play it moment by moment. **Riding:** the stallion explodes sideways the instant weight hits the saddle; snow in sheets; Chuluun shouting odds. **Gentling:** the slow circling, the testing, the stallion finally standing close enough to touch; the crowd goes quiet, and Sarnai steps forward without being asked.

## Results
| Result | Favor | Weapons |
|---|---|---|
| **Success** (two successes) | **+2** | Batzorig drops the bundle at the rider's feet: *"Rider's blades. Take them."* Returned now. |
| **Partial** (one success) | **+1** | Returned after the Trial of the Bow. |
| **Fail** | 0 | Held until the party leaves Yecha. |

If someone is thrown spectacularly and gets up laughing, Sarnai smiles: award the partial anyway. The horde loves spirit.

## Sarnai's Endorsement (gentling success only)
Sarnai lays her hand on the horse's neck and speaks to the crowd as much as the party:
> **Sarnai:** *"Istus weaves strange threads. The black one has chosen."*

Then, quieter, to the PC:
> **Sarnai:** *"He is yours now, if you want him. He will not carry a rider he does not respect, so — try to stay respectable."*

From now on the party are, in Yecha's eyes, *the ones the black one chose*:
- **The stallion** belongs to the PC who gentled him (warhorse stats; the player names him).
- **The night:** help arrives on **round 1** of the Scene 5 attack.
- **The council:** once, after any failed council check, Sarnai speaks for them and they reroll (they must take the new result).
- **The threshold:** at Favor 4 after the trials, she invokes the black one's choice; treat it as Favor 5.

## Temur
If Temur is present, he watches stone-faced. If the party succeeds, he gives one short nod and walks away. It is the most respect he shows all adventure.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a black horse alone at the far picket; rope-burn scars; snow kicked up in sheets
- **Sound:** a hundred ponies stamping; Chuluun's odds in Baklunish; then, for gentling, silence
- **Smell:** horse, frozen dung, milky tea
- **Touch:** a hot neck under a cold hand
- **Taste:** blood from a split lip, for whoever gets thrown

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **Nobody attempts it.** Sarnai waits a long moment, then: *"Then the Horse has judged you."* Favor 0; weapons held. Run the Bow anyway.
- **A PC uses magic (*animal friendship*, *speak with animals*).** Allow it as a gentling attempt with advantage, but Chuluun calls it out: *"Witch-riding!"* Sarnai rules it fair if the PC still does the slow work.
