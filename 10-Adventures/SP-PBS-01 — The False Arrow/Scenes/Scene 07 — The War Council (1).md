# Scene 07 — The War Council

[[00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 06 — Trial of the Fire]]

## Purpose
The climax. Three rounds decide two questions — does the parley pass, and does the war stop — and Kezrin tries to slip away.
**Budget:** 50 minutes, plus 5 for the ending. Running long? Argue Round 3 instead of dueling, and run the chase as one DC 13 check (success = papers recovered).

## Check Favor First
| Favor | What happens |
|---|---|
| **7–9** | Voice **and** the tiger-claw token. Run the council. |
| **5–6** | Voice. Run the council. |
| **4 with Sarnai's endorsement** | Treat as 5. |
| **Below 5** | Temur moves to expel them; Cligir, bound by custom, allows it. **Sarnai holds the evidence → Soft fail. Otherwise → Cast out.** |

## The Packed Pavilion
> *The great pavilion is full to the walls.*

Then deliver these one at a time:
- Khans in tiger skins, warriors shoulder to shoulder; the air thick with brazier-smoke, mutton-grease, and anticipation.
- Cligir sits on the low throne in the saber-tooth robe, Sarnai at his right hand like a carved omen.
- Temur stands before them all.
- The moment you duck through the flap, fifty faces turn — and the talking dies, the way talking dies before weather.

**Translation.** The council runs in Baklunish. Sarnai murmurs Common to the party under the roar and softens Temur's worst lines. A PC who attended [[Professor-Ilsabet-Federkiel]]'s Baklunish lectures follows it unaided and catches what she leaves out.

## Temur Speaks First
Let him have it; he's good at this. He draws Tsolmon's arrow from his sash and holds it up.
> **Temur:** *"One of our riders came home with this in his back! My brother came home on a sledge, with more of them in him!"*

*(If the party didn't bring the bodies back: "My brother is still out there in the snow, with more of them in him!")*

> **Temur:** *"The Wegwiur have raided our herds all winter. They grow bold because we grow soft. I say we ride east before the thaw!"*

Spear-butts bang on the ground until the hides shake. Cligir watches, expressionless.

## The Party Is Given the Floor
> **Sarnai:** *"The south-men have earned the trials. By custom, they have voice at this fire. Speak, then — and speak as if the threads are listening, because they are."*

**The token (Favor 7+).** Before they speak, Cligir works a tiger-claw pendant off his own wrist — slowly; his fingers are old — and holds it out to their speaker.
> **Cligir:** *"The Tiger's mark. Wear it while you speak, and my riders will know you kept faith in winter."*

See [[Tiger-Claw-Token]].

## The Three Rounds
Each round is **won or lost**. Any two rounds pass the parley. Rounds 2 **and** 3 stop the war.

### Round 1 — The Parley
Present Brennin's offer. **Persuasion DC 14** (DC 12 with the tiger-claw token).
- **Won:** the older khans lean in; Temur's jaw works.
- **Lost:** Temur jeers — *"Shall we weave the salt into victory garlands?"* — and the young warriors laugh.

### Round 2 — The Evidence
Present the ravine findings. **Intelligence (Investigation) DC 13**, or reward good roleplay over dice. **Advantage** if they spied unseen and can tie the black steel in Temur's camp to the arrowhead.
- **Automatic win:** a PC asks Temur to lay Tsolmon's arrow beside the ravine arrows. He hesitates, then does it. Same glue, same paint over brown feather, side by side on the felt before the throne. No roll.
- **No evidence:** Round 2 is lost.
- **Won:** the pavilion goes silent, the kind of silence with weight. The braziers hiss. Temur's face does something complicated — grief, fury, and the vertigo of a man realizing his war-cry was written by someone else's hand. **Kezrin moves** (below).
- **Lost:** the khans look to Temur, and Temur doesn't look at the arrows.

If Kezrin was already exposed, Sarnai speaks into the silence after Round 2 is won:
> **Sarnai:** *"Someone put false arrows in our dead. Ask yourselves, riders of the Tiger — who profits if we bleed ourselves against the Wolves?"*

### Round 3 — The Choice
> **Temur:** *"You say my brother was murdered by a merchant's lie. Then the lie dies with the liar. But the Wolves still raided our herds. That part was true."*

The party must give his grief somewhere to go: a border patrol instead of a war, the hunt for Kezrin, his brother's honor.
- **Argue it:** Persuasion or Insight **DC 14**, advantage if Round 2 was won.
- **Champion's duel** by nomad law: the party's champion vs. Temur's champion (**berserker**), first to three hits, non-lethal; Sarnai stops it. The pavilion forms a roaring ring; she raises her hand and the noise drops like a stone. Map: `SP-PBS-01 - Duel-Ring`. **Win the duel = Round 3 won.**

**Sarnai's endorsement:** once during the council, after any failed check, she speaks for them and they reroll.

## Kezrin's Exit
Kezrin is among Temur's guests — unless he was already exposed, in which case he left before dawn with his papers.

The moment Round 2 is won, or the false flag is spoken aloud, he starts for the flap. The party's options, cleanest first:
1. **They prepared.** They gave Sarnai the evidence or named Kezrin to her: Naran and two riders hold the flap. **Captured, papers recovered.** No roll.
2. **They see him go.** Perception **DC 14** (passive counts). No one spots him → gone, with his papers.
3. **They stop him on the spot.** Any single action that stops a running man — a grapple, *hold person*, *command*, a thrown net, an arrow to the leg, a body in the doorway. **Captured, papers recovered.**
4. **They chase.** Three rounds through the tent-city to his horse; the lead chaser makes a **DC 13** check each round (Athletics, Acrobatics, Perception, or Animal Handling if mounted); others can Help.
   - **3 successes:** captured at the picket line, papers recovered.
   - **2 successes:** he makes his horse, but the oilskin packet tears from his coat — escaped, papers recovered.
   - **0–1:** gone with the papers.

Recovered papers: [[SP-PBS-01 — Handout — Kezrin's Papers]].

## Council Results
| Rounds won | The parley | The war | Ending |
|---|---|---|---|
| Round 2 **and** Round 3 | Passes | Temur stands down | **The parley holds** |
| Any two, not both 2 and 3 | Passes | Temur rides east: half his riders if Round 2 won, all if not | **A year bought** |
| Round 2 only | Refused | Temur rides with half | **Truth without trust** |
| None, or Round 1 or 3 alone | Refused | Temur rides with all | **Heard and refused** |

**Cligir's ruling.** He rises, slowly, with Sarnai's hand under his elbow. For once she barely needs to translate.
- *Parley passes:* *"The south offers grain and salt for the road. I accept — for one year, and we will see if southern words hold."*
- *Parley refused:* *"You have shown us your courage. You have not yet shown us your priest's. Tell him to come in spring and speak with his own mouth."*
- *False flag exposed:* *"And the merchant Kezrin is declared wolf-head: any rider may take his head, and keep his horses."* (If he is a prisoner: *"Bring the merchant to me. He will tell us about the Wolves he paid."*)

## Soft Fail — Through Sarnai's Mouth
Favor is below 5, but Sarnai holds the evidence. Temur has the party walked out under guard. They stand outside the flap in the cold with Naran translating in snatches through the hides.
- Temur's speech happens; they hear the roar.
- **Round 2 is Sarnai's.** She lays the arrows on the felt herself. No roll — Round 2 is won. Naran whispers: *"She says — not Wolf arrows. Someone's idea of Wolf arrows."*
- Rounds 1 and 3 are not run.
- **Kezrin comes out the flap — toward them.** Option 1 still works if they warned Sarnai; otherwise options 2–4, from outside.

Result: **Truth without trust.**

## Endings
**The parley holds.** The party rides back to the hospice with Cligir's sealed acceptance. Brennin reads the seal twice, sits down suddenly on the mounting block, and weeps, laughing through it: *"You did it. You wonderful, frostbitten lunatics, you did it."*

**A year bought.** The parley is agreed, but Temur rides east with his young warriors "to patrol the border." *"You bought us a year, south-men. Spend it well."*

**Truth without trust.** The lie is exposed and half of Temur's riders won't ride for it, but the parley is refused. The party rides out on borrowed ponies, under escort. Brennin reads Cligir's message about spring and goes quiet. *"Then I'll go myself. Will you come with me?"*

**Heard and refused.** Temur rides east with every rider who'll follow. The party is escorted to the edge of the tents and left there, with their horses and their dignity.

**Cast out.** Favor below 5 and no evidence with Sarnai. *"The trials are done. The trust is not earned. Leave Yecha on foot, in winter."* Survival montage in falling snow; no shame in it.

**Sarnai at the edge of the tents** (Heard and refused, or Cast out — unless she caught them lying): she presses a juniper sprig into the leader's hand. *"The Lady's loom is patient. Come back in spring, if you are still alive."*

**Kezrin (any ending).** Captured, escaped without his papers, or gone. Recovered papers bear Iuz's skull seal and name **Yulbar** of the Wolf. → [[The War Iuz Wants]]

**Halya (any ending).** At the hospice gate, she reads their faces and writes to the Field Station that night. Spied: 25 credits each. Didn't: 10 each, "for the report I didn't get." *"The report will say 'strength: sufficient.'"*

**Back at Ritterstadt.** Brennin's report reaches [[Magister-Roswitha-Kartner]] before the party does. If they bring Kezrin's papers to the Historical Society, Professor Federkiel's people log it as a documented discovery: 50 bonus credits each.
