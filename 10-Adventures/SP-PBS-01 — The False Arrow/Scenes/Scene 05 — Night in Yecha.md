# Scene 05 — Night in Yecha

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-01 — The False Arrow/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 04 — Trial of the Bow]] · Next → [[Scene 06 — Trial of the Fire]]

## Purpose
The party learns two things: Kezrin is dirty, and the horde is closer to war than Brennin believes. Halya's letter forces a choice.
**Budget:** 40 minutes. Running long (this scene starts after 2:20 on the clock; it should start at 2:10)? Run Kezrin's visit straight into the attack, and resolve Halya's letter with one Stealth roll made during the fire.

## The Guest Tent
> *Your tent is felt over willow, warm from a dung-brazier.*

Then deliver these one at a time:
- Someone has left you the good things: a haunch of mutton still on the bone, flatbread, salt, and a skin of kumiss — fermented mare's milk, sour enough to make your eyes water.
- Outside, Yecha settles: dogs barking, a far-off song in Baklunish, the endless small thunder of the herds.
- For the first time since the pass, your fingers are warm.

**To the table:** *"A boy is whittling by the tent flap and pretending not to listen. What do you do with your first quiet evening in Yecha?"*

The boy is **Naran**, sixteen, Sarnai's tent-guard, dying to practice his three words of Common ("hello," "horse," "good").

Two things happen tonight, in either order. If the players bite on neither, Kezrin comes to them.

## A. Kezrin's Visit
[[Master-Kezrin]] comes after dark with a wineskin of something southern and expensive. He ducks in like he owns the tent, clasps the nearest PC's elbow, and settles by the brazier uninvited.

> **Kezrin:** *"My friends! The south-men who rode the black one — or gentled him, the stories differ. I am Kezrin. I buy horses, I sell steel, and I know everyone worth knowing between Ekbir and the Burneal."*

He pours, generous, and leans in:
> **Kezrin:** *"The old khan will die. The young khan will rule. Priests' letters do not survive successions. Go home. Tell Brennin the horde can't be trusted. I'll make it worth your while."*

He offers **200 gp now, 300 after**, in Ekbiri silver, coin by coin on the felt like he's dealing cards.

**Whatever they answer, Kezrin's men come tonight.** Anyone who has heard his offer is a loose end, and Kezrin trusts no one he has bought only once.

| Their answer | Kezrin says | That night |
|---|---|---|
| **Take the money** | *"Practical people! How rare. Sleep well."* | A quiet theft past midnight: his men slip in for the ravine evidence — and the silver. No fire. |
| **Refuse politely** | *"Pity. Practical people are so rare. Sleep well — Yecha is safe tonight. Probably."* | His men burn the tent and grab the evidence. |
| **Threaten him** | The smile stays; the eyes go flat. *"Threats. From guests. In winter. I will remember that you were rude. It's the only thing about tonight I will remember."* | His men burn the tent and want the party bloody before they run. |

## Encounter 5 — The Night Visit
> *You wake to the smell — the sharp reek of lamp-oil under the familiar dung-smoke. The tent wall is glowing orange at the seams. Outside, Naran is shouting, and someone is shouting back in Ekbiri, and then the felt gives way with a soft* whoomph *and the night comes in burning.*

**To the table:** *"You have one action before the roof comes down. What do you grab?"*

*(Quiet theft instead: "You wake to a cold draft across your face — and a hand in your pack.")*

| Party | Creatures | Difficulty (level 3) |
|---|---|---|
| 4 PCs | 2 thugs + 1 scout | Medium (600 adj.) |
| 5–6 PCs | 3 thugs + 1 scout | Easy–Medium (800 adj.) |

Statblocks: [[SP-PBS-01 — Bestiary]]. Map: `SP-PBS-01 - Night-Camp` ([[SP-PBS-01 — Map Assets]]).
- **Non-lethal intent.** They knock PCs out rather than kill. The party may be unarmored and, if the Horse failed, unarmed.
- **What the attackers can take.** The ravine evidence is taken **only if no PC is carrying it, or its carrier is knocked out.** Anything a PC grabs with the one action before the roof comes down is safe. Evidence left wrapped in a pack in the tent is gone.
- If the party has no ravine evidence, the job is to scare them out: burn the tent, break a bow, run.
- **Naran** arrives with two riders and a bucket line on **round 3** (**round 1** with Sarnai's endorsement).
- **The fight ends** when Naran arrives or when two attackers drop, whichever comes first. The rest flee into the tents.
- **The attackers' leader** has a grinning-skull brand inside his left forearm. It is the same skull as on Kezrin's wax seal. Religion DC 12, or any PC who knows the Old One's sign, recognises the mark of **Iuz**.
- **A captured attacker** made to talk (Intimidation DC 13) names Kezrin. If the party takes that to Sarnai, Kezrin is watched from dawn (see Scene 7, *Kezrin's exit*).

## B. Halya's Letter
Sometime tonight the sealed letter comes out — or doesn't. If a player breaks the seal, hand them [[SP-PBS-01 — Handout — Halya's Sealed Instruction|Handout 2]]. Now they have to decide.

The war camp is visible from the guest tents: Temur's riders drilling by torchlight, the glint of new blades.

**If they spy:** whoever goes makes a **group Stealth check, DC 13** (if at least half succeed, nobody is seen). The spying can also happen *during* the fire, while the camp is busy with the bucket line. Use the same check.
- **Unseen:** about **four hundred riders** drill under Temur's banner against perhaps half that under the Ilkhan's standard, and a third of Temur's riders carry **new black-steel blades** — the same dark, oily metal as the ravine arrowhead. No Favor change, but **advantage on council Round 2**.
- **Seen:** **Favor −2**, and Temur throws them out of the camp:
> **Temur:** *"South-men count our spears by night and speak of trust by day! Get out of my sight before I count yours."*

**If they don't spy** — letter sealed, or read and put away — Sarnai notices. Next morning, as they pass her tent, she says without looking up from her fire:
> **Sarnai:** *"The threads stayed clean last night. The Lady noticed. So did I."*

**Favor +1.** The adventure rewards the players for being better than their orders.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** coins dealt onto felt; torchlight on black steel across the camp; orange seams in a tent wall
- **Sound:** Naran's three words; a far-off Baklunish song; a soft *whoomph*
- **Smell:** sour kumiss; southern wine; lamp-oil under dung-smoke
- **Touch:** Kezrin's hand on an elbow, steering
- **Taste:** kumiss, which nobody finishes the first time

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **The party wants to go straight to Sarnai about Kezrin tonight.** She hears them out, says *"Tomorrow, at the fire,"* and posts Naran's cousin outside their tent. The attack still comes.
- **The party ambushes the attackers.** Let them. Prisoners talk (Intimidation DC 13).
