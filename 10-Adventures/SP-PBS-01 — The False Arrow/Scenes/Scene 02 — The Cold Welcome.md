# Scene 02 — The Cold Welcome

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-01 — The False Arrow/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 01 — The High Pass]] · Next → [[Scene 03 — Trial of the Horse]]

## Purpose
Disarm the party, make them face Cligir's question, set the three trials, and **explain the Favor track out loud**. This scene is pure roleplay.
**Budget:** 25 minutes. Running long? Cut the interception to "blades on the snow" and go straight to the pavilion.

## The Interception
> *They come out of the low sun: six riders at a canter, spread in a crescent, bows strung and half-drawn. Steppe ponies, shaggy and quick, breathing steam. Their leader rides a hand ahead of the rest — a broad young warrior with a tiger-tail standard tied at his saddle, his mouth set hard.*

**To the table:** *"Six bows, ten yards, and nobody's smiling. What are your hands doing?"*

[[Batzorig]], Temur's rider-captain (**scout** stats), reins up ten yards off and doesn't dismount. His six riders are men and women both. He speaks broken Common, slowly, like each word costs him.

> **Batzorig:** *"South-men. You walk into the Tiger's mouth with gifts? Open it."*

If they refuse, six bows come to full draw, unhurried: *"Open it, or we open you."* The taster drinks, waits, grins, and nods.

> **Batzorig:** *"Perrender brandy. The old khan will drink. You — blades. On the snow. Now."*

*(Batzorig's Common is rough: "Perrender" is his word, not a typo.)*

### Kartner's guest-craft
A PC who attended [[Magister-Roswitha-Kartner]]'s Wilderness Fieldcraft lectures remembers her first one: *"In another people's country you are a guest from the moment they see you. Put your steel down before you're asked. Let their taster drink first. Eat what they put in front of you."* If that PC lays their blades on the snow before Batzorig finishes the word *Now*, or hands them over hilt-first, the **restraint bonus (Favor +1) is earned on the spot**. Batzorig's lip uncurls, a little. Nothing else in this scene can cost it, short of steel drawn in the pavilion.

### Disarmed
A rider collects swords, axes, bows, and daggers with surprising care and ties them in a hide bundle that Batzorig hangs from his own saddle. **Holy symbols, component pouches, and arcane foci he leaves alone:** *"Your gods are yours."* The weapons stay with Batzorig until the Trial of the Horse.

### The second letter
The rider patting down packs finds Halya's plain-sealed letter. Batzorig turns it over once, weighs it, and hands it back unopened. He watches the face of whoever takes it.
> **Batzorig:** *"Letters are yours. Blades are ours. Custom."*

*(Sarnai will hear about this by nightfall. See Scene 6.)*

They are blindfolded for the last half-mile. If anyone objects: *"Custom. The shaman's word, not mine. Walk, or be carried."*

## The Great Pavilion
> *The blindfold comes off all at once, and the light hits like a slap.*

Then deliver these one at a time:
- A cavern of striped hides, forty paces across, warm with braziers and close with the smell of mutton-fat, leather, and smoke.
- Tiger skins cover the floor; tiger skulls ring the central poles.
- On a low throne draped in a robe the color of old blood — saber-tooth fur — sits a small old man with eyes like chipped flint.
- Beside him stands a blind-eyed woman in juniper smoke. Behind them both, arms crossed, a young khan built like a gate.
- Fifty warriors line the walls. Every one of them is watching you.

**To the table:** *"Nobody has told you where to stand. Where do you stand?"*

[[Mother-Sarnai]] speaks first, quiet Baklunish, then careful Common:
> **Sarnai:** *"I am Sarnai, who looks at the cloth for the Ilkhan. You come under parley-flag, with gifts, in winter. This is either courage or arithmetic. The Ilkhan will decide which."*

She reads Brennin's letter aloud, translating as she goes. Halfway through, [[Khan-Temur]] barks a laugh and pushes off the dais, pacing, one hand on the broken arrow in his sash.
> **Temur:** *"Grain and salt! Three weeks ago my brother rode out with four riders. One came home — with a Wolf arrow in his back. My brother has not come home at all. And the south sends groceries."*

[[Ilkhan-Cligir]] raises one finger. Temur stops mid-pace. Let the silence hang. Then Cligir speaks, and Sarnai translates, flat as a drum:
> **Cligir:** *"You came in winter. Either you are very brave or your priest pays very well. Which is it?"*

**Let them answer.** Cligir watches whoever speaks, never Sarnai. Any honest answer works — "both," "neither," "we're students and he's paying in credits." A flattering or evasive answer gets silence. If a PC answers him directly, with a bow or a formal address, Sarnai nods once. **He remembers it:** that PC gets +1 on their Persuasion check in council Round 1 (Scene 7).

> **Cligir:** *"Your priest wants my word. Words are cheap in the south; here they cost. Three trials, as our own young riders earn it. The Horse. The Bow. The Fire. Live through them, and you may speak at my council fire."*

A beat. The ghost of a smile.
> **Cligir:** *"Fail, and you leave Yecha on foot, in winter, and we keep the brandy."*

The pavilion laughs, and there's warmth in it. Temur doesn't laugh.

## Explain the Favor Track
Now, out of character, put the card on the table: [[SP-PBS-01 — The Favor Tracker]]. Tell them it starts at 0, that 5 earns a voice at the council, and that everything they do in Yecha moves it. Then move it for the restraint bonus if they earned it.

## Favor This Scene
- **+1** handled Batzorig without drawing steel (or locked in by Kartner's guest-craft)
- **−1** a weapon drawn in the pavilion (Favor never drops below 0), and disadvantage on the first check of the Horse trial

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a crescent of drawn bows; tiger skulls; a robe the color of old blood
- **Sound:** fifty people breathing; a laugh that cuts off; Sarnai's quiet translation under everything
- **Smell:** mutton-fat, leather, smoke, juniper
- **Touch:** the blindfold's rough wool; the warmth after three days on the open steppe
- **Taste:** the taster's brandy, if anyone is offered a sip

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **A PC refuses to surrender a weapon.** Six bows to full draw. If they still refuse, Batzorig takes that PC to Yecha under guard, and the party starts the trials having lost the restraint bonus. Sarnai sets the trials anyway: *"Even the rude may try."*
- **The party fights.** The riders fall back and shoot to wound; they want prisoners, not bodies. The party enters Yecha as prisoners at Favor 0, and Sarnai sets the trials anyway.
- **The party tries to bluff Cligir.** He lets them finish, says nothing, and gestures for the trials. No Favor change. **Sarnai remembers the face:** at the Fire (Scene 6), her first question goes to that PC.

## Transition
Sarnai assigns them a guest tent and a guard. *"Sleep. The Horse is at dawn."* → [[Scene 03 — Trial of the Horse]]
