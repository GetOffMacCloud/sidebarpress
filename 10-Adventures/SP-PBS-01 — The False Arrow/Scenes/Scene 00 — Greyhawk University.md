# Scene 00 — Greyhawk University

[[00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 01 — The High Pass]]

## Purpose
Frame the party as [[Greyhawk-University]] students, learn what each studies (every course pays off later), and put the posting in front of them.
**Budget:** 5 minutes. Running long? Start at the posting.

*Sidebar Press campaign frame (homebrew). For tables not continuing from GHR-KEO21-01, skip the Derby line in the table prompt.*

## Arrival
> *The north gate of Ritterstadt, at last — the place where the road gives up arguing with the hills and becomes a street.*

Then deliver these one at a time, as their attention finds them:
- Below the town, grey as hammered tin, lies Lake Quag.
- Along its northern shore sprawls a jumble of halls, towers, chapels, and dormitories, smoke coming out of all of them at once.
- Over the gatehouse arch, someone has painted the university arms: an open book, a cudgel and a quill crossed beneath it.
- Under that, in student hand: *abandon naps, all ye who enter here.*

**To the table:** *"The coin from the Duffkyyn Derby bought your passage this far. Who's first through the gate?"*

## The Posting
Midwinter. A frostbitten caravan master staggers into the Porter's Lodge with a letter from the high pass. By breakfast, [[Master-Fenwick]] — Master of Field Studies, broad, bearded, two fingers short from a winter wolf — is reading it aloud in the Practical Colleges' hall:

> **Fenwick:** *"The hospice of St. Cuthbert sits in a pass the caravans can't afford to lose, and the Tiger Nomads have started taxing it with arrows. Father Brennin wants a parley carried by people the nomads won't shoot on sight. That rules out priests, soldiers, and tax collectors."*

His gaze lands on the party.

> **Fenwick:** *"That leaves students. Field credit for winter term — and the good father's report on you comes straight to my desk. Who's going?"*

If anyone hesitates: *"The last field party to the steppe came home in spring with stories and all their fingers. Mostly."*

> [!info]- Optional color — Ritterstadt life (only if you're ahead of the clock)
> **Enrollment.** The Registrar's Hall smells of lamp-oil and old paper. Mistress Pell, sixty, spectacles, takes names without looking up, then looks up and looks each of them over. *"Course of study? Practical Colleges, then. Sign here. The Scholar's Oath is on the wall. Read it before you sign."* Nobody does.
>
> **The oath, in gilt:** *I shall not steal manuscripts, duel on the quad, set fire to the boathouse (again), or bring livestock into the library. I shall attend my lectures, honor my masters, and remember that tuition is non-refundable.*
>
> **Day to day.** The bell runs their lives: dawn lectures, afternoon practicals, mutton in the dining hall, the lake wind rattling dormitory shutters. Work-study pays for extras. The tavern **The Illuminated Margin** has honest beer and better gossip.

## The Gates
> *You leave by the north gate at dawn, Brennin's posting folded in oilskin inside the steadiest coat, your packs roped to a mule that already hates you. Behind you, the bell rings for lectures you'll miss. Ahead, the road climbs toward the Yatils, white and waiting. Three weeks of winter road lie between you and a stone hospice in a high pass.*

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** grey lake, a hundred chimneys smoking at once, painted arms over a gate
- **Sound:** the lecture bell; the lake wind in the shutters
- **Smell:** lamp-oil, old paper, mutton
- **Touch:** a mule's rope burning through cold gloves
- **Taste:** honest beer at the Illuminated Margin
