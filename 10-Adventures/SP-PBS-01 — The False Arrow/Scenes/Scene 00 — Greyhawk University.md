# Scene 00 — Greyhawk University

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-01 — The False Arrow/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 01 — The High Pass]]

## Purpose
Frame the party as Greyhawk University students at the **Ritterstadt Field Station**, learn which lectures each attends (every choice pays off later), and put the contract in front of them.
**Budget:** 5 minutes. Running long? Start at the Job Board.

*Continuity: Ritterstadt and the Field Station follow the A Tourist In Greyhawk canon (see `00-Inbox/Greyhawk University Import`). At level 3 the PCs are **Freshmen — undeclared**, attending lectures across departments.*

## Arrival
> *Ritterstadt is a palisade, a watchtower, and a scatter of roofs on the edge of the Sepia Uplands, with the Quag Mires grey and flat beyond it.*

Then deliver these one at a time, as their attention finds them:
- Fresh-cut stakes stand pale among the old ones where the palisade is under repair.
- The Wachturm rises over the wall; a lamp burns in it all day in winter.
- Smoke from the Fiend's Rest Public House hangs low over the lane. Through its door: voices, and a board crowded with pinned paper.
- Twelve souls live here, and every one of them knows when a stranger walks in.

**To the table:** *"Midwinter at the Field Station. Who's first through the door of the Fiend's Rest?"*

## One Question Per Player
**Whose lectures has your character been sitting in on this term?** Freshmen are undeclared and shop around. Write the answers down.

| Lectures | Department head | Pays off in |
|---|---|---|
| Wilderness Fieldcraft, route-finding, "be a guest before you're asked to be" | [[Magister-Roswitha-Kartner]], Exploration & Cartography | Scene 2 (restraint bonus), Scene 4 (fletching) |
| Linguistics & Philology — Baklunish | [[Professor-Ilsabet-Federkiel]], Letters & Cultures | Scene 7 (council translation), Handout 3 (the ledger) |
| History or Antiquities — dull, occasionally, infuriatingly useful | Professor Casimir Vogelsang, Antiquities | Scene 4 (fletching), the beguilement tradition |

## The Contract
A frostbitten caravan master staggers into the Fiend's Rest with a letter from the high pass. By morning it is pinned to **the Job Board**, and Magister Kartner — never lost, never hurried — is standing in front of it with a mug of something hot, reading it aloud to anyone who'll listen:

> **Kartner:** *"The hospice of St. Cuthbert sits in a pass the caravans can't afford to lose, and the Tiger Nomads have started taxing it with arrows. Father Brennin wants a parley carried by people the nomads won't shoot on sight. That rules out priests, soldiers, and tax collectors."*

Her gaze lands on the party.

> **Kartner:** *"That leaves students. Field credit, and the good father's report comes to me. Three weeks of road each way. Know the exits before you enter."*

If anyone hesitates: *"I'd go myself, but somebody has to correct the rivers while I'm gone."*

A Scouts & Surveyors student offers the party a copy of the terrain log for the Yatil road; a Ramblers' Fellowship member asks them, too casually, to sketch anything along the way that isn't on a University map.

## The Gate
> *You leave by the palisade gate at dawn, Brennin's posting folded in oilskin inside the steadiest coat, your packs roped to a mule that already hates you. Behind you, the Wachturm lamp. Ahead, the road south and west toward the Yatils, white and waiting. Three weeks of winter road lie between you and a stone hospice in a high pass.*

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** pale new stakes in an old palisade; a lamp burning in daylight
- **Sound:** the Job Board's pinned papers rattling in the draft from the door
- **Smell:** peat-smoke and wet wool in the Fiend's Rest
- **Touch:** a mule's rope burning through cold gloves
- **Taste:** whatever Kartner is drinking, offered once and not again
