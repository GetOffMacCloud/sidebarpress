# Scene 04 — Trial of the Bow

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-01 — The False Arrow/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 03 — Trial of the Horse]] · Next → [[Scene 05 — Night in Yecha]]

## Purpose
A real fight, and the adventure's payload: the ambushed patrol's bodies and the false Wolf arrows.
**Budget:** 40 minutes. Running long? Skip the tracking roll; the tiger waits at the den mouth.

## The Briefing
> *The trial is named for the bow, but it starts with grief.*

Then deliver these one at a time:
- A small tent at the edge of the herds. Inside, a boy of maybe ten lies with his leg splinted in birch and rawhide, pale and furious at being kept from his chores.
- His mother hovers, wringing a cloth.
- The tent smells of willow-bark tea and fear-sweat.

> **Sarnai:** *"A saber-tooth. Three ponies this month. Yesterday, the boy. He lived. The tiger is winter-mad and starving. Slay it — with the horn bow, as our riders would."*

> **The boy:** *"Shoot it in the eye. For me."*

**To the table:** *"The boy is looking at you. Does anyone answer him?"*

The horde provides horses and **horn bows** (1d8 piercing, range 150/600; no proficiency needed for the trial). Two nomad beaters (**scouts**) ride with them to the ravine and loose arrows from the rim, but won't descend.

| Result | Favor |
|---|---|
| **Success:** the tiger dies with a horn-bow arrow as the killing blow. A critical hit went in the eye; bring the arrow to the boy. | **+2** |
| **Partial:** the tiger dies any other way, or is driven off and its den fired or blocked so it can't return to the herds. | **+1** |
| **Fail:** the tiger escapes, or the party retreats. | 0 |

## The Ravine
> *The ravine opens below you like a wound.*

Then deliver these one at a time:
- Steep shale sides, sharp-crested drifts, a frozen stream at the bottom.
- At the far end, a dark cleft in the rock — the den.
- The air smells of old blood and cat-musk, thick enough to taste.
- Somewhere in the dark of the cleft, something breathes: slow, wet, enormous.
- At the rim, the beaters stop. One makes a warding sign. Neither of them is coming down.

**To the table:** *"How do you go down?"*

**Tracking:** Survival DC 12 to follow the blood-trail to the den (on a failure, the tiger gets surprise).

## Encounter 4 — The Saber-Tooth
| Party | Creatures | Difficulty (level 3) |
|---|---|---|
| 4 PCs | 1 saber-toothed tiger | Easy (450) |
| 5–6 PCs | 2 saber-toothed tigers (its mate joins from the den on round 2) | Hard (1,350 adj.) |

Statblocks: [[SP-PBS-01 — Bestiary]]. Map: `SP-PBS-01 - Ravine` ([[SP-PBS-01 — Map Assets]]). It is an ambush predator: out of the cleft fast and silent, going for whoever looks smallest. It flees at 10 hp, or when its mate falls (at 5–6 PCs).

## The Discovery (the scene's real payload)
Half-buried in snow near the tiger's kills lie the missing four of the ambushed patrol — **Temur's brother among them**, recognizable by the tiger-tail standard knotted at his belt. The cold has kept them. **Investigation or Survival DC 13:**
- **They were brought here.** Drag-marks run under the newer snow from the ravine mouth; the bodies were dumped by the den so the tiger would finish the work. The snow has been churned and refrozen many times over; this happened weeks ago.
- **Purses gone, bows still strapped on.** Raiders on the steppe take bows.
- **The arrows.** Grey goose fletching, Wolf-style at a glance. But Wolf Nomads bind fletching with sinew in a crisscross; these are glued, and the grey is paint over brown feather. *(A PC who studied fieldcraft or history recognizes the sinew bind without a roll.)*
- **A black-steel arrowhead** in one dead hand — dark, oily metal, too well-made for steppe work.
- **A silver coin** (Investigation DC 15): Ekbiri silver, stamped with a horse-head.

**Don't explain it.** Let the players work it out. A beater, asked about the arrows, goes pale under his windburn:
> **Beater:** *"Those are not Wolf arrows. I have pulled Wolf arrows out of my cousin. These are… someone's idea of Wolf arrows."*

If someone asks about **Tsolmon's arrow**, the beater nods slowly: *"The khan keeps it in his sash. He has not let anyone look at it."* That arrow matches these. Hold the thought for the council.

## Favor This Scene
- **+2 / +1** for the Bow trial (above)
- **+1** for bringing the bodies and evidence back openly. Temur, told his brother has been found, goes somewhere alone for an hour and comes back red-eyed and silent.
- Pocketing the evidence silently: no Favor either way, but they still have it for the council.
- **Weapons:** a partial on the Horse means Batzorig returns the bundle as they ride back.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** blood-trail on shale; a tiger-tail standard stiff with frost; grey paint flaking off brown feather
- **Sound:** wet breathing in the cleft; a bowstring; then the beaters very quiet on the rim
- **Smell:** cat-musk and old blood. *Absence:* the bodies have no smell at all; the cold has kept them.
- **Touch:** glue where sinew should be
- **Taste:** iron in the air of the den

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **The party misses the evidence (fails DC 13).** The beater finds the arrowhead while they load the bodies and holds it up without a word. Give the fletching on a second look.
- **The party leaves the bodies.** Temur's riders recover them the next day, and Temur hears the south-men left his brother in the snow. No Favor; Round 3 at the council is at disadvantage.
- **The party retreats before reaching the den.** Temur's riders go in after the tiger the next day and bring the bodies out. The beater brings Sarnai one of the arrows, and she shows it to the party at the Fire (Scene 6), where the fletching clue can be found. No Favor; Round 3 at the council is at disadvantage, as above.
