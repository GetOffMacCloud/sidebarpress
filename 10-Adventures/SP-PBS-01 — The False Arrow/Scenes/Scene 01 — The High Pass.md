# Scene 01 — The High Pass

[[00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 00 — Greyhawk University]] · Next → [[Scene 02 — The Cold Welcome]]

## Purpose
Hand over the parley letter and the gift, plant Halya's sealed letter without explaining it, and show that the steppe itself is hostile.
**Budget:** 20 minutes. Running long? Skip the journey checks and the hazard.

## The Hospice at Dawn
> *The hospice of St. Cuthbert squats against the pass like a fist that forgot how to open.*

Then deliver these one at a time, as their attention finds them:
- Frost furs the lintels; the chapel bell is muffled with sacking so it won't crack in the cold.
- In the yard, mules stamp and blow steam. The yard-dog watches you pack with the flat stare of an animal that has seen caravans leave before.
- Above the gate, someone has chalked the Cuthbertine cudgel — and under it, in a smaller hand: *come back alive.*

**To the table:** *"Father Brennin is waiting at the gate with a sealed letter. Who steps up to take it?"*

## Father Brennin
[[Father-Brennin]] gives them the **parley letter** (sealed with the cudgel, ribboned in hospice grey — [[SP-PBS-01 — Handout — Brennin's Parley Letter]]), a squat cask of **Perrenlander brandy**, and a blessing he means. He presses the letter into the steadiest PC's hands with both of his and holds on a beat too long.

> **Brennin:** *"They are not monsters, whatever the caravans say. They are people with hungry winters. Remember that when they test you — and they will test you."*

> **Brennin:** *"The brandy is for the Ilkhan's taster first, mind. It's custom; no insult meant. And if it goes badly, the pass will still be here in spring. Walk out. I will not think less of you."*

## Halya's Sealed Letter
Before the gate, [[Sister-Halya]] draws the most honest-looking PC into a stable doorway, out of the wind, and presses a second letter into their hand: sealed plain, no ribbon, no mark.

> **Halya:** *"Brennin's letter is the mission. This one is for later. Open it once you're inside Yecha — not on the road, not before."*

- If asked what's in it: *"If I wanted to say it out loud, I wouldn't have sealed it."*
- If asked whether Brennin knows: a pause. *"Brennin has enough to carry."*

**Do not hand over [[SP-PBS-01 — Handout — Halya's Sealed Instruction|Handout 2]] yet.** Give it to the player only when they break the seal inside Yecha (Scene 5). Let the letter sit in the player's mind for three weeks of road.

## The Descent
> *The pass lets you go the way a miser gives coin — grudgingly, a switchback at a time. The wind finds every seam in your clothes. Below, the Yatils fall away into white: foothills, then the steppe, a flat grey sea under a flatter sky. The hospice bell rings once behind you for the road — thin, bright, already lost in the wind.*

**To the table:** *"Three weeks of winter road. Tell me how each of you is keeping the party alive on it."*

**The journey** is a single skill sequence: each PC makes one check of their choice — Survival to find the trail, Athletics to break a drift, Perception to spot the ice, Animal Handling to calm the mules (DC 12). **Two or more failures:** Encounter 1.

## Encounter 1 — Steppe Predators (earned)
> *The mules smell it first — heads up, ears flat. Then the snowfield moves: low shapes, grey on grey, circling wide.*

| Party | Creatures | Difficulty (level 3) |
|---|---|---|
| 4 PCs | 1 winter wolf | Medium (700 adj.) |
| 5–6 PCs | 1 winter wolf + 2 wolves | Hard (1,600 adj.) |

Statblocks: [[SP-PBS-01 — Bestiary]]. The winter wolf **speaks first**: it offers to let them pass for a mule. It is lying about letting them pass. The fight is short and cold. Say aloud afterward that the Chakyik live through this every winter.

## First Sight of Yecha
> *The wind drops all at once, the way it does when a hand closes.*

Then deliver these one at a time:
- Below you, between seven low hills, the steppe is covered in tents — hundreds of them, felt and hide, smoke going straight up in the still air.
- On the highest hill, earth-and-timber walls ring a squat old town, and the tents spill down from it like grain from a split sack.
- Banners snap everywhere: tigers, painted and sewn. Beside the walls, a pavilion of striped hides flies a banner twice the size of the rest, with tiger skulls grinning from poles beside it.
- Somewhere below, a horn blows — not a welcome. An announcement.

**To the table:** *"They've seen you. Do you ride down openly, or wait for them to come to you?"*

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a chalked cudgel; white switchbacks; a sea of tents and smoke below
- **Sound:** a muffled bell; wind in every seam; a horn from the valley
- **Smell:** mule, wet wool, snow. *Absence:* on the steppe, no woodsmoke — the Chakyik burn dung.
- **Touch:** Brennin's two hands holding the letter a beat too long
- **Taste:** snow-melt in a frozen waterskin
