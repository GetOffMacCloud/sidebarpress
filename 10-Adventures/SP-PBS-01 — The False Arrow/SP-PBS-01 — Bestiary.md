## Winter Wolf (CR 3, 700 XP)
*Large monstrosity, neutral evil* · MM p. 340 · **Scene 1, Encounter 1**

**Armor Class** 13 (natural armor) · **Hit Points** 75 (10d10 + 20) · **Speed** 50 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 (+4) | 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 7 (−2) | 12 (+1) | 8 (−1) |

**Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +3 · **Damage Immunities** cold · **Senses** passive Perception 15 · **Languages** Common, Giant, Winter Wolf · **Proficiency** +2

***Keen Hearing and Smell.*** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.
***Pack Tactics.*** Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one ally is within 5 ft. of it and isn't incapacitated.
***Snow Camouflage.*** Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in snowy terrain.

**Actions**
***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing. If the target is a creature, DC 14 Strength save or be knocked prone.
***Cold Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** 15-ft. cone. DC 12 Dexterity save; 18 (4d8) cold on a failure, half on a success.

> **Sidebar Press dressing.** It speaks Common first, reasonably: *"One mule, and you pass."* It is lying. It opens with Cold Breath on the mules if the party bargains.

**Senses** passive Perception 10 · **Languages** Baklunish · **Proficiency** +2

***Reckless.*** At the start of its turn, it can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls that turn, but attack rolls against it have advantage until its next turn.
