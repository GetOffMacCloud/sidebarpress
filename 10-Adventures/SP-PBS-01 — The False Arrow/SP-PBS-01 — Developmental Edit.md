# SP-PBS-01 — Developmental Edit (structure, pacing, railroad check)

Back to [[SP-PBS-01-The-False-Arrow]] · Board: [[SP-PBS-01 — Board]] · Run sheet: [[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-01 — The False Arrow/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]]

> [!info] How to use this note
> Tick the box next to each finding you want applied, then ask Claude to "apply the ticked fixes." Untick, or strike through, anything you reject. The board's *Developmental edit* card stays open until the Major findings are resolved.

## Verdict
The structure is sound and close to ready. The chain Horse → Bow → Night → Fire → Council builds properly. The climax key (Tsolmon's arrow in Temur's sash) is planted in Scene 02 and paid off as a no-roll win, and the Favor track gives every choice weight. The serious problems are all in the plumbing around **Kezrin and the evidence**:
- Four files give different capture and escape rules for Kezrin, and one of them is a guaranteed escape.
- Nothing says whether the evidence survives the night attack.
- The "Iuz" fact hangs on a single object.
- Scene 05 is overloaded, so the likely run is **4:30–5:00**, not 4:00.

There are no punished refusals. The Halya choice is a model of how to handle one.

**Classic-module musts: 6 of 12 pass.**
- **Fail:** cover line (1), Locals Know split (2), boxes assigning actions (6), Iuz fact routes (8), NPC Knows/escalation lines (9), Kezrin's plot armor (12).
- **Pass:** 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 11.

## Findings

### Major
- [x] **1 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-17.* Railroad/Consistency — Kezrin's guaranteed escape.** S07 *Kezrin's Exit*: "unless he was already exposed, in which case he left before dawn with his papers." This contradicts S05 ("Kezrin is watched from dawn"), S06 (Sarnai "posts riders on Kezrin") and the Favor Tracker (captured at the flap, no roll). Exposing him early, which is the smart play, means he escapes with the Iuz reveal. **Fix:** "If exposed before the council *but not reported to Sarnai*, he flees before dawn; run the chase at the picket line." Put a single capture rule in the Tracker and point the other files to it.
- [x] **2 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-17.* Structure — the evidence and the night attack.** S07: "No evidence: Round 2 is lost." S05: his men "grab the evidence." No rule says when the grab succeeds, so the pivotal round can be lost by DM fiat. **Fix (S05):** "The evidence is taken only if no PC carries it, or its carrier is knocked out." **Fix (S07):** "Without ravine arrows, Tsolmon's arrow alone: Investigation DC 15."
- [x] **3 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-17.* Structure — "Iuz wants the war" has one source.** The Clue Ledger lists two routes, both from Kezrin's papers in S07, and no last resort. The Board ticks "3+ routes" anyway. **Fix:** add a captured S05 attacker with a skull brand or "Dorakaa" on his lips, and Cligir's interrogation of a captured Kezrin. Last resort: Sarnai names the thread herself. *Or* mark this fact as an optional sequel hook ([[The War Iuz Wants]]) rather than a must-learn.
- [x] **4 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-17.* Pacing — Scene 05 is the middle bloat.** The budget is 30 minutes, but the scene holds Kezrin's visit, a mandatory fight, an interrogation, Halya's choice and a spying run. Realistically it takes 45–60 minutes. **Fix:**
  - End the fight on a schedule: "The attackers break when Naran arrives (round 3) or two drop."
  - Let the spying run happen while the fire is being fought.
  - Add a clock checkpoint: "If S05 starts after 2:40, use the running-long line."

## Guardrails reviewed and kept
These read like railroading at first glance but are defensible:
- The disarming on the snow. It is the restraint test, and the Horse trial is how the party wins the blades back.
- The blindfold.
- The fixed order of the trials. That is custom, and the approach inside each trial stays open.
- Kezrin's visit and the night attack. The villain is acting, not protected.
- "The party must give his grief somewhere to go." This states the win condition; "wins Round 3 by giving…" would read better.
- The forced reroll after Sarnai's endorsement.

## What works (don't cut)
- **Tsolmon's arrow:** seeded in S02, pointed to by the S04 beater, and a no-roll win in Round 2.
- **Halya's letter:** each choice both pays and costs, and S06 rewards honesty either way.
- **The Favor track:** visible on the table, explained out loud, and feeding a banded council outcome that includes Soft fail and Cast out.
- **Kezrin's answer table** (take / refuse / threaten), with non-lethal attackers who flee.
- **Run-sheet discipline:** a clock column, per-scene cuts and a good break.
