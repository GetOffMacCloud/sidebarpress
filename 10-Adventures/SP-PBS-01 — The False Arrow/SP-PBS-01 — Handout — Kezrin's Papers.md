# Handout 3 — Kezrin's Papers

Back to [[SP-PBS-01-The-False-Arrow]] · Recovered in [[Scene 07 — The War Council]] · Opens [[The War Iuz Wants]]

> **DM:** an oilskin packet. One letter under black wax pressed with a grinning skull, in Common. One folded ledger page in Ekbiri merchant's shorthand: a PC who has attended [[Professor-Ilsabet-Federkiel]]'s Baklunish lectures reads it on Intelligence DC 12, or Sarnai reads it for them.
>
> **Extra credit:** if the papers reach the Field Station's Historical Society, it counts as a documented, historically significant discovery — **50 bonus credits** each, outside the 600-credit limit.

---

## The Letter

*K. —*

*The old Tiger will not bend. Very well: he need not. Let the young one carry the Horde east for us. Sell him steel at whatever price makes him feel clever. When the Tiger and the Wolf have bled each other white, there will be no one on the steppe to stand in the way of what comes after.*

*Our friend among the Wolves has been paid to keep the Tiger's herds thin this winter. See that the Tiger's dead are found with the right arrows in them, and that no one looks too closely at the feathers.*

*Burn this. You will not, of course. You never do.*

*— from Dorakaa*
