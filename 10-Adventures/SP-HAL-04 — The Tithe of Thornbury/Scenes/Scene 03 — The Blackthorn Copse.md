# Scene 03 — The Blackthorn Copse

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-04 — The Tithe of Thornbury/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — The Long Night]] · Next → [[Scene 04 — The Procession]]

## Purpose
Give the party their one ally and the cost of every ending. Show them, in the fields, what the pact's plenty is made of.
**Budget:** 40 minutes. Running long? One shambling mound, and Hesper's first three answers.

## Arrival
> *The gold stops at the ridge like a tide line. Past it the ground is black thorn and bare stone, and nothing grows that a farmer would want. A hut squats in the middle of the thicket, roofed in crow feathers and old sacking. The door opens before you knock, and a crossbow comes out first. Behind it is a woman with a burn scar from jaw to collar, who looks at you the way you'd look at a storm. "You ate," she says. "Didn't you."*

**To the table:** *"Who lowers their hands first?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Hesper Gloam]]**, "the Crow-Wife." *Wants:* the pact ended and nobody else burned for saying so. *Knows:* where the heart is and what cutting it costs. *Reacts:* assumes the worst, is relieved to be wrong, never says so.
- **Crows**, dozens, on the thorns. They watch the valley for her.

## Beats

### The fields wake *(on the way up, or on the way back)*
If the party cuts through the Gold Fields rather than taking the ridge path, or tries to leave the valley, or approaches the barn before 10 PM, the wheat closes behind them. From the rows rise **shambling mounds** built of wheat and the bones of a century of guests: an old pewter button here, a jawbone there.

- The wheat is difficult terrain. A creature that ends its turn in the rows makes a **DC 14 Strength saving throw** or is restrained by roots until it uses an action to pull free (**Strength (Athletics) DC 14**).
- The mounds don't pursue past the field's edge. The ridge path is safe; the Sheaf keeps to the roads for a reason.

### Hesper
She doesn't trust anyone from the village side of the ridge, and the party came from there. A sheaf-mark on a palm earns a hard, long look and the crossbow going down. Once she believes they're not with the Sheaf, she answers questions. Don't deliver this as a speech: let the players ask.

| If asked about... | Hesper says |
| --- | --- |
| The Harvest Lord | *"It's not a god. It's a tree that ate too much. The heart's under the barn floor, rooted in a hundred years of guests."* |
| Breaking the pact | *"Cut the heart and the pact dies. So does every field in this valley, and maybe Thornbury with it. My grandmother said that. They burned her."* |
| Another way | *"Read the words on the lintel. Josiah wrote them in a hurry. A hungry thing doesn't check the fine print."* |
| The Sheaf | *"Good people. That's the worst of it. Every one of them would die for their children, and that's exactly what they're asking you to do."* |
| Why she stays | *"Somebody has to be here when it all goes wrong."* |
| The barn | *"Front doors face the green; that's where they'll bring you. There's a root cellar under the back, and the Reap girl has a key. Lofts above, full of a hundred years of what people brought with them."* |
| Fire | *"Burn it and you kill the tree. You also kill the harvest, and they'll hunt you for it until the last of them starves. Think before you strike a light."* |

### What she offers
- She joins the party at the barn if asked. Use the **druid** stat block with *spike growth* prepared in place of *barkskin*. She will not go into the pit.
- **Hesper's gift:** a *bag of beans*. *"Honest seed. Mostly."*
- The ridge path to the barn's rear, which no lantern watches.

### The crows
If the party asks what the crows see, Hesper looks at the sky for a moment. *"The procession's forming on the green. They've got the big man out of the barn."* This is the DM's cue for the clock: it's past ten.

## Encounter (if any)
| APL | Creatures | Difficulty (4 PCs) |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | 1 shambling mound | easy (1,800 XP) |
| 9 | 2 shambling mounds | medium (2 × 1,800 × 1.5 = 5,400 XP) |
| 10 | 3 shambling mounds | hard (3 × 1,800 × 2 = 10,800 XP; the mounds are slow and the field's edge is 60 ft away, so it plays as hard, not deadly) |

**Terrain:** waist-high wheat, difficult terrain, restraining roots (DC 14). The field's edge, 60 feet off, is safety. · **Tactics:** a mound engulfs the restrained PC and drags them deeper into the rows; the others shamble between the party and the edge. Lightning heals them, so a storm sorcerer learns something. · **DM direction:** this is the pact's plenty with the varnish off. Describe the buttons and bones in the wheat. The goal is to reach the edge, not to win.

## Clues Available Here
- Bones and buttons in the mounds → the fields are fed on guests
- *"Under the barn floor"* → where the heart is
- *"Josiah wrote them in a hurry"* → the pact has loopholes
- *"Burn it and they'll hunt you"* → the cost of the fire ending
- *"The Reap girl has a key"* → Tamsin's rear door

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** gold stopping dead at the ridge; crow feathers on a roof; a pewter button in a wheat-mound's chest
- **Sound:** crows; wheat moving against the wind; Hesper's crossbow string creaking
- **Smell:** thorn-smoke; wet root; under the wheat, old bone
- **Touch:** roots closing on an ankle; the scar on her jaw when she turns to the fire
- **Taste:** the dry beans she offers, which taste of nothing at all

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party attacks Hesper:** she runs into the thorns (*pass without trace*) and the crows blind the party for a round. She reappears at the barn anyway, because somebody has to be there when it goes wrong.
- **If the party never goes to the ridge:** Hesper comes down to the procession route at 11 PM with her crossbow and gives the three key answers in the dark, in a hurry.
- **If the party is already sure of the heart's location:** make this the short rest, and let Hesper tell her grandmother's burning in full. It's the emotional case for breaking the pact.
