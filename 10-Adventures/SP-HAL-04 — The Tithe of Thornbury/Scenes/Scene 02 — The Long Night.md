# Scene 02 — The Long Night

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-04 — The Tithe of Thornbury/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 01 — The Hundredth Feast]] · Next → [[Scene 03 — The Blackthorn Copse]]

## Purpose
Give the party the pact's words and its four loopholes, and a door into the barn. Let them feel watched the whole time.
**Budget:** 45 minutes. Running long? Skip the flameskull unless they attack a lantern; give the pact through Tamsin instead of the Elder's house.

## Arrival
> *The bonfire throws the green into orange and black. Every doorstep holds a carved turnip lantern, and as you pass, the faces turn. Not the flicker of candlelight: the whole grinning head swivels on its step to follow you down the street. Behind you, the inn's lantern has stopped grinning and is simply watching.*

**To the table:** *"Where do you go, knowing every step of it is being reported?"*

## Who Is Here
- **The turnip lanterns**, on every step, the Sheaf's eyes.
- **The watchman**, a **flameskull** inside the carved turnip on the inn's step: the skull of Josiah's brother. A second one sits on [[Prudence Thorne]]'s porch at APL 9–10.
- **[[Tamsin Reap]]**, slipping out of the inn's back door with a jug she doesn't need to carry.
- **[[Prudence Thorne]]**, at home, with the lamp lit and the door unlocked.

## Beats

### Turnip Lanterns *(Eerie Encounter)*
Anything the party says within 10 feet of a lantern reaches Prudence within minutes. If the party threatens anyone, the nearest lantern shrieks, and the village comes running: not to fight, but to stand in the street and watch.

- **Snuffing a lantern** (an action; they're just turnips with candles) blinds the Sheaf on that street for 10 minutes. Snuff every lantern on a street and the party can move unseen to the Elder's house or the barn's rear door.
- **The watchman.** If a PC puts out the inn's lantern, or attacks any lantern on the green, the turnip on the inn's step bursts into green fire and the **flameskull** inside rises. It fights until destroyed, screaming the party's names so the whole village hears. At APL 9–10 the second flameskull on the Elder's porch joins on round 2.

### The Elder's House
Prudence is in, serene, with tea on. She does not stop the party from looking at anything. The pact is on a sheaf of vellum in a glass case on the mantel, in Josiah's cramped hand. (Hand out [[SP-HAL-04 — Handout — The Pact]].)

**Intelligence (Investigation) DC 14** to read it carefully, or automatic if the party studied the bone calendar in [[SP-HAL-03-The-Night-That-Doesnt-End]]. On a success, point out the four loopholes:

- [ ] *"One who came uninvited"* — the guest doesn't have to be alive, or even a person, as long as it arrived uninvited.
- [ ] *"Welcomed at the table"* — a seat laid, hospitality offered, something eaten.
- [ ] *"Given to the field"* — the field accepts anything genuinely given, and anything genuinely its own kind.
- [ ] *"Else the field takes back all it gave"* — break the pact and the century of plenty rots overnight.

| If asked… | Prudence says |
|---|---|
| Whether it's true | *"Every word. Ninety-nine years and not one bad one. Look at the children."* |
| Why them | *"Because you came. Because you sat. I am so sorry, and I would do it again."* |
| What if they refuse | *"Then the tinker. And if not him, then we draw lots among ourselves, and the field takes a child, because that's what it likes best. Is that better?"* |
| What happens at midnight | *"We walk to the barn. We sing. It's quick, they tell me. Nobody's ever come back to say."* |

She does not threaten. She does not need to.

### The Green and the Corn Maze
At 8 PM villagers steer the party toward the Corn Maze of the Last Sheaf, "for the honoured guests to open." Inside, the wheat walls close behind them (difficult terrain to go back), and the lanterns at every junction turn to watch. It's a 10-minute set piece, not a fight: **Wisdom (Survival) DC 13** to find the center, where a scarecrow in a wedding-garland of wheat holds a card: *"Reserved."* If the party cuts through the walls rather than following the paths, run **The fields wake** from Scene 03 here.

### Tamsin
She finds the party when they're alone, or sends a note under a door: *"Rear door of the barn. I'll have it open at eleven. Don't tell my father."* If the party offers her hope (**Charisma (Persuasion) DC 15**, or showing her one of the loopholes), she tells them the procession route (the long way round the north field, so the whole village can walk behind the Colossus) and that the heart is "under the threshing floor, where the wheat comes up through the boards." She will not leave Thornbury. *"Someone has to be here after."*

### 10 PM: the Choosing
Wherever the party is, at ten the bonfire flares white and a **wheat-sheaf mark** appears on the palm of whoever ate the loaf: a PC, or Ned. It doesn't hurt. It can't be scrubbed off; *remove curse* fades it for 1 hour. Prudence names the guest aloud from the green, warmly, like a prize.

**Terrain:** the green, the bonfire (10-ft radius, 2d6 fire to anyone shoved in), villagers in the street as soft cover that the party won't want to shoot through. · **Tactics:** *fireball* once, then *magic missile* at whoever snuffed the lantern; it flies above the crowd and screams names. · **DM direction:** the flameskull is loud, not deadly. Its job is to announce where the party is. Remember Rejuvenation: unless the skull is doused in holy water or *dispel magic*'d, it reforms in an hour, in a new turnip.

## Clues Available Here
- The lanterns report → nothing in Thornbury is private; snuffing them is the counter
- The pact's four phrases → the loopholes (Clue Ledger: the pact has loopholes)
- Prudence's *"we draw lots and the field takes a child"* → why the Sheaf does this
- Tamsin's rear door and *"under the threshing floor"* → where the heart is
- The sheaf-mark → who the guest is

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** turnip faces turning in unison; green fire in a carved mouth; the pact's cramped brown handwriting under glass
- **Sound:** a lantern's shriek like a kettle; the village coming out onto the street and saying nothing
- **Smell:** scorched turnip; tea and lamp oil at the Elder's
- **Touch:** the sheaf-mark warm on the palm; wheat walls closing at the shoulder
- **Taste:** cider gone sour in the mouth

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party kills Prudence:** Barnaby takes her place and the Sheaf hardens; Tamsin still helps, and hates them for it. The pact's vellum is still on the mantel.
- **If the party tries to leave the valley:** run Scene 03's **fields wake** on the road out. The wheat closes behind them and the shambling mounds rise; the valley does not let the guest go.
- **If they never visit the Elder's house:** the pact is also on the barn's lintel (Scene 04/05), and Hesper quotes the key phrases in Scene 03.
- **If the party skips this scene:** the lanterns still turn, the Choosing still happens at 10, and Tamsin finds them on the road to the copse.
