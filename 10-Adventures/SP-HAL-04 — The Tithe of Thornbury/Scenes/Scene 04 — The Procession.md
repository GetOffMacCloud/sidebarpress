# Scene 04 — The Procession

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-04 — The Tithe of Thornbury/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 03 — The Blackthorn Copse]] · Next → [[Scene 05 — The Tithe Barn]]

## Purpose
Put the guest in the cage, and deal with the Wicker Colossus here, on the road, so it is never in the same fight as the Harvest Lord. The scene ends with the party at the barn doors, with or without the guest.
**Budget:** 35 minutes. Running long? End the Colossus fight when the cage is open or the barn doors are reached.

## Arrival
> *The bonfire goes out, and the dark is full of singing. Thirty feet tall, a man of woven willow and wheat walks out of the green with a lantern burning behind the bars of its chest. Inside the cage, someone is sitting very still. The whole village walks behind it, four abreast, carrying candles and singing a harvest hymn, and every one of them is crying.*

**To the table:** *"The cage is passing you right now. What do you do about it?"*

## Who Is Here
- **The Wicker Colossus**, carrying the guest in its chest-cage. See [[SP-HAL-04 — Bestiary]].
- **The guest:** the marked PC, or [[Old Ned Tallow]], garlanded in wheat, with the sheaf of wheat in his hatband.
- **[[Prudence Thorne]]**, walking ahead with a sickle of plain iron.
- **[[Barnaby Reap]]**, leading the hymn, badly.
- **Two cult fanatics** riding the Colossus's shoulders at APL 10 (the Reap cousins, with *hold person*).
- **The Sheaf**, two hundred singing villagers. They are not combatants.

## Beats

### The Choosing *(if it hasn't happened)*
If the party has avoided the village since the feast, the Choosing happens here: Prudence lifts the guest's marked hand to the crowd and names them. If the guest is a PC walking free, the Colossus simply reaches for them. The cage opens like a ribcage.

### The route
The long way, round the north field, so the whole village can walk behind. It's a quarter mile, about 10 minutes at a hymn's pace. Tamsin told the party the route (Scene 02); Hesper's crows can confirm it. The party can ambush it anywhere along the road, meet it at the barn doors, or walk in the procession unchallenged. Nobody stops a guest's friends from walking with them.

### The Colossus
It does not attack unless the party attacks it, blocks its path, or touches the cage. Then it fights to reach the barn, not to kill: it swings at whoever is between it and the doors, and keeps walking.

- **The cage.** A creature inside is restrained and has total cover. It can escape with a **DC 15 Strength check**, or be cut free from outside (**AC 15, 30 hit points**). Ned can't make the check; a PC might.
- **Burning Wicker.** Fire doesn't harm the Colossus; it ignites it. For 1 minute its slams deal an extra 2d6 fire, and the cage burns too: the guest takes 1d6 fire at the start of each of the Colossus's turns. Cold damage or a bucket of water puts it out. A burning Colossus that reaches the barn sets the doors alight, which is the seed for the fire ending.
- **The village.** The Sheaf keeps singing through the fight. They are in the road, in the way, and not hostile. Area spells have to choose.

### If the party walks with it
Let them. The procession reaches the barn doors at 11:45, the Colossus kneels, and the cage opens into the hands of six men who carry the guest in. The Colossus then stands at the doors as a guard. The party enters as guests, and the Colossus is dealt with on the way *out*, or not at all.

### Bundle: Thorne on the road
If Thorne escaped in [[SP-HAL-03-The-Night-That-Doesnt-End]], this is where he arrives: a **wraith** riding a dead horse out of the north field at 11 PM, to claim what he was owed. The Sheaf doesn't know him. Prudence does; he's in the family portraits. He walks beside the Colossus like a man come home, and joins the climax at the pit.

**Terrain:** a cart road between two gold fields (the wheat is Scene 03's hazard, if anyone is pushed in); two hundred villagers filling the road behind. · **Tactics:** Slow at the first PC who tries for the cage; then it walks. It stops only if its path is physically blocked. · **DM direction:** the clock is the enemy here, not the hit points. The party doesn't need to kill it; they need the cage open before the doors. If they do kill it, it collapses into a bonfire of straw at the barn's threshold, and the fire ending is suddenly on the table.

## Clues Available Here
- The Sheaf cries while it sings → the cult believes it has no choice
- Prudence's iron sickle → she means to do the giving herself
- The Colossus burns → fire is an ending, with a cost
- Thorne in the portraits (bundle) → the reaper was a Thorne

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a thirty-foot man of willow with a lantern in its chest; two hundred candles; tears on every face
- **Sound:** a harvest hymn in four parts; willow creaking; the cage's lantern guttering
- **Smell:** candle wax; dry wheat; if it burns, a bonfire smell that's almost cheerful
- **Touch:** woven willow, rough and dry; the cage bars warm from the lantern
- **Taste:** wheat chaff on the wind

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the guest is freed on the road:** the Sheaf stops singing and stands in the road. Prudence says, *"Then who?"* and looks at the party, and then at the children in the crowd. The procession goes on to the barn anyway, with the Colossus's cage empty, and the Harvest Lord will take what it's given at midnight: the pit does not care who.
- **If the party goes to the barn early:** the Colossus is at the doors instead of on the road (20 min), and the procession arrives mid-fight in Scene 05 with Ned in a cart.
- **If the Colossus is destroyed with fire at the doors:** the barn is already burning when Scene 05 starts. Run the climax with the lofts alight and 10 minutes before the roof comes down.
- **If the party skips the scene** (hiding, or already in the root cellar): the procession arrives at the barn as the opening of Scene 05, and the Colossus stays at the doors as a guard. It is never in the pit.
