# Scene 00 — Harvest Home

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-04 — The Tithe of Thornbury/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 01 — The Hundredth Feast]]

## Purpose
Make the players like Thornbury, and make them a little uneasy about how much Thornbury likes them. Plant Ned, the banner, and the Crow-Wife.
**Budget:** 20 minutes. Running long? Narrate the welcome; keep the banner and one child with a roll.

## Arrival
> *Thornbury looks like a painting of itself. Gold fields run right up to the whitewashed cottages, every window box overflows, and every face you pass is rosy and smiling. Children run up to press warm rolls into your hands. Above the green, a banner reads WELCOME, HONOURED GUESTS. Someone has painted your names on it.*

**To the table:** *"Who takes the roll, and who notices that nobody asked your names?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Prudence Thorne]]**, Elder, waiting on the green with both hands out. *Wants:* the party at her table by six. *Knows:* everything. *Reacts:* presses food, asks after their journey, never asks their business.
- **[[Barnaby Reap]]**, innkeeper, carrying the party's bags before they're offered. *Wants:* them comfortable and fed. *Reacts:* laughs loud; the laugh stops a half-second early.
- **[[Old Ned Tallow]]**, on a bench outside the Bountiful Board with a tankard, three days fatter and delighted. *Reacts:* greets the PC he knows like a lord greeting a cousin.
- **Tamsin Reap** ([[Tamsin Reap]]), hauling cider, looking at the party too long.

## Beats

### The welcome
Play it as completely sincere. Nobody here lies about being glad the party has come. Prudence offers the best rooms at the Bountiful Board, free, and asks only that they sit at the head of the table tonight. **Wisdom (Insight) DC 16** shows one thing and one thing only: everyone is a little *too* eager for the party to eat.

| If asked… | Prudence says |
|---|---|
| Why the honor | *"A hundredth harvest wants a hundred guests, and we've only ever had one at a time. You'll be the first in a century."* True. |
| How they knew the names | *"The tinker told us. He talks about you."* If Ned isn't tied to a PC: *"Word travels. We've been expecting company."* |
| About the Crow-Wife on the ridge | *"Poor Hesper. Her grandmother was the same. Leave her be tonight; she doesn't like the festival."* |
| About the barn | *"The Tithe Barn. Oldest building in the valley. We'll show you after supper, if you like."* She means it. |

### Ned
If a PC knows him, Ned is at his happiest. *"Three days, and not a copper spent. They keep asking if I've eaten. I've eaten!"* He shows a sheaf of wheat tucked in his hatband, a gift from the Elder. He has no idea what he's been fed.

### The fields
A PC who walks to the field's edge notices the wheat stands taller than a man and the rows don't move with the wind. **Wisdom (Perception) DC 14:** the roots at the edge of the road shift, very slightly, toward footsteps. **Intelligence (Nature) DC 15:** nothing grows like this without something feeding it.

### The ridge
The Blackthorn Copse is visible from the green, a black knot on the gold hillside. Crows circle it. A thin line of smoke rises from a hut among the thorns. Any villager asked about it changes the subject kindly.

## Encounter (if any)
None. If the party is hostile from the first minute, the village does not fight; it weeps, apologizes, and keeps feeding them.

## Clues Available Here
- The banner with their names → the village expected them
- Insight DC 16: too eager for them to eat → the food matters
- Ned, fatter and fed → the backup guest; what eating here means
- Roots that turn toward footsteps → the fields are alive

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** whitewash so fresh it glares; gold wheat to the doorsteps; the black knot of the copse on the ridge
- **Sound:** children laughing; a fiddle tuning; no birdsong in the fields
- **Smell:** bread, cut wheat, woodsmoke
- **Touch:** a warm roll pressed into the hand; garlands dropped over the shoulders
- **Taste:** sweet new cider; the rolls are very good

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party refuses hospitality entirely:** Prudence is hurt but gracious, and asks them to at least sit at the feast "so the village can thank you." The Sheaf's plan only needs one of them at the table.
- **If the party goes straight to the barn:** let them. The lintel's pact is in plain sight (Scene 02 clue), and the barn is empty until 11 PM except for the wheat on the floor. Run the fields waking (Scene 03) if they go in after dark.
- **If the party skips this scene:** the banner, Ned, and the copse all resurface at the feast.
