# The Tithe of Thornbury

**APL 9 (levels 8–10) · 4–5 characters · one 4-hour session**

> *A village that has never gone hungry invites you to dinner, and means it.*
>
> A folk-horror one-shot for **average party level 8–10** (written for APL 9, with scaling for 8 and 10). Thornbury has had ninety-nine perfect harvests, and tonight it celebrates the hundredth Hallow's Eve with a feast in honor of its guests. The people are kind, the bread is warm, and every door is open to the party. The pact that made Thornbury rich demands a guest on the hundredth Hallow's Eve, and its reaper isn't coming this year to fetch one. So the village has invited its own. It is the **finale** of the Hallow's Eve Quartet and runs standalone with no changes.

## Adventure Code
`SP-HAL-04` — the Hallow's Eve series uses `SP-HAL-NN`, kept apart from the Greyhawk `SP-PBS` line. Never reuse codes, even for scrapped adventures.

## Date
**All Hallows' Eve, the hundredth harvest since the pact.** Setting-agnostic: drop it into any calendar that has a harvest festival and a night for the dead.

## Bundle
Fourth and last adventure of the [[SP-HAL-00-Hallows-Eve-Quartet]]. Previous: [[SP-HAL-03-The-Night-That-Doesnt-End]]. When run as a chain, the party arrives at 9th level carrying the Vesper Bell, the Gentleman in Grey's calling card, and the pact's exact wording from the bone calendar. Each pays off below under **Bundle Payoffs**. When run standalone, the hooks and the Elder's house supply everything the chain would.

## Design Frame
- **Thesis:** Kindness is not innocence. Everyone in Thornbury would die for their children, and that is exactly what they are asking the party to do.
- **Expectation broken:** *The friendly village is hiding a cult.* It isn't hiding anything. The cult is the village, it is sincere, and its scarred, sour "witch" on the ridge is the only ally. The players should like the people who mean to kill them.
- **Second break:** the monster can be out-lawyered. The pact's wording is the puzzle, and a hungry thing doesn't check the fine print.
- **What this adventure refuses to do:** It never lets the party win by being stronger. The Harvest Lord can be killed, but killing it starves the valley. Every ending has a price, and the players choose which one.
- **Session spine:** the **Midnight Clock** (arrival → feast → Choosing → procession → the pit) and the **Harvest Bounty** tracker: every time the Harvest Lord heals, a field dies in front of the people who made the pact.
- **Tone:** a painting of a harvest village with the varnish too thick. Warm rolls, rosy children, a banner with your name on it, and the wheat moving when no one is looking.

## What the Locals Know
- **On the road in:** Thornbury has never had a bad year. Not one. Nobody from Thornbury ever marries out, and nobody who goes there for the festival talks much about it afterward.
- **In the Bountiful Board:** the Guest's Chair has been empty a hundred years, "for luck." The Crow-Wife on the ridge is mad, and her grandmother was worse. The tinker Ned Tallow has been here three days and has put on weight.
- **In the fields:** don't walk the rows after dark. The wheat is taller than you.

## What's Really Happening
A century ago, famine was killing Thornbury. Elder Josiah Thorne led the founding families into the fields at midnight and bargained with the **Harvest Lord**, an ancient spirit of soil and root. It is now a corrupted **treant** rooted beneath the Tithe Barn, and its roots run under every field in the valley. The pact's words are carved into the barn's lintel, kept on vellum in the Elder's house, and copied onto the bone calendar in Vesper Hollow:

> *"Ninety-nine harvests of plenty, and none shall fail. Each Hallow's Eve, the reaper's tithe of souls from the roads. And on the hundredth Hallow's Eve, a guest: one who came uninvited and was welcomed at the table, given to the field. Else the field takes back all it gave."*

Thorne became the reaper. Every hundredth year he was supposed to lure a stranger to Thornbury's table. This year he is sealed in a bell, or destroyed, or he never came (see [[SP-HAL-03-The-Night-That-Doesnt-End]]). If he escaped, he is riding west. Either way, the village elders have had to find a guest of their own. They have chosen the party.

The cult, which calls itself **the Sheaf**, is not evil in its own eyes. Its members are farmers, parents, and grandparents who know their children will starve if the pact fails. They will smile, feed the party, and give one of them to the field at midnight with tears in their eyes.

**The loopholes.** Josiah wrote the pact in a hurry, in a famine year, by lantern light. Four phrases decide the ending:

| Phrase | What it actually requires |
|---|---|
| *"One who came uninvited"* | The guest doesn't have to be alive, or even a person, as long as it arrived uninvited. |
| *"Welcomed at the table"* | A seat laid, hospitality offered, and something eaten. The Hundredth Loaf is the binding. |
| *"Given to the field"* | The field accepts anything genuinely given, and anything genuinely its own kind. |
| *"Else the field takes back all it gave"* | Break the pact, and a century of stolen plenty rots overnight. |

## Hook
- **The Calendar (bundle chain).** The party comes from Vesper Hollow knowing the pact's terms, and maybe carrying the Vesper Bell with Thorne inside. Thornbury's elders greet them by name before they've introduced themselves.
- **The Invitation.** A beautiful card arrives, bound with a ribbon of wheat: *"The good people of Thornbury request the honour of your company as Guests of Honour at our Hundredth Harvest Home."* Hospitality and a generous fee are promised.
- **The Missing Tinker.** Old Ned Tallow, a friend of a PC or a patron, went to Thornbury for the festival. His last letter says: *"They're so kind here. They keep asking if I've eaten."*

## PC Objectives (the plot, as goals rather than events)
1. **Work out why Thornbury is so glad to see you.** Routes: Insight on the welcome (DC 16); the Empty Chair's whisper; Tamsin almost saying something; the pact on the barn lintel; Hesper on the ridge.
2. **Don't eat the loaf, or learn what eating it means.** Routes: *detect magic* on the board; Arcana/Nature DC 15; the chair's warning *"don't eat the bread"*; refuse it and watch the hall go silent; let Ned take a second slice and learn from what happens to him.
3. **Get the pact's exact words.** Routes: the lintel of the Tithe Barn (plain sight, if they go early); the vellum in the Elder's house (Investigation DC 14, or Tamsin's key); the bone calendar from Adventure 3; Hesper's *"read the words on the lintel."*
4. **Find the heart.** Routes: Hesper tells them; the wheat grows thickest over it; Tamsin's rear door into the root cellar; follow the procession.
5. **Keep the guest out of the pit.** Routes: break the Colossus's cage on the road; cut the guest free in the barn; take the seat yourself; put something else in it (see the endings).
6. **Decide what Thornbury's future is.** Routes: five endings, below. Optional door: return the past guests' belongings to their families (a sequel).

## Subplots (keyed to PCs; assign at the table)
- **The welcomed guest.** [[Old Ned Tallow]] is someone from a PC's past: an old friend, a patron, the tinker who sold them their first blade. He has already eaten the loaf. If the party refuses the chair, it's his.
- **The reflection comes due (bundle).** If a PC ate the apple in [[SP-HAL-01-Straw-Men]], the Gentleman in Grey owns their reflection. When he arrives at midnight he produces it, and the "doll for the field" ending costs that PC a shadow they never get back.
- **A founding name.** A PC with a farming or noble background finds their family name among the founders who signed the pact. [[Prudence Thorne]] greets them as kin, and the Sheaf expects them to understand.

## Act Structure
### Act 1 — Harvest Home (≈60 min)
Arrival at 4 PM and the hero's welcome (Scene 00). The Hundredth Feast at 6 PM: the Empty Chair and the Hundredth Loaf (Scene 01). By the end of the act the party has been welcomed at the table, and someone has eaten.

### Act 2 — The Long Night (≈85 min)
Turnip lanterns that watch, the flameskull on the inn step, the pact in the Elder's house, and Tamsin's doubt (Scene 02). The Blackthorn Copse: Hesper's answers, and the fields waking if the party tries to leave or cut across (Scene 03). By the end of the act the party knows the loopholes and where the heart is.

### Act 3 — The Tithe (≈95 min)
The Choosing at 10 PM and the procession behind the Wicker Colossus at 11 (Scene 04). The Tithe Barn at midnight: the Harvest Lord, the guest, and the choice (Scene 05).

## Climax at a Glance
Every ending is seeded before the party reaches the pit. If a solution wasn't seeded, don't offer it; if the players invent a sixth, price it honestly.

| Ending | Seeded in | What it needs at the pit |
|---|---|---|
| **Break the pact** (kill the Harvest Lord) | Hesper, Scene 03: *"cut the heart and the pact dies."* | Win the fight. Harvest Bounty withers fields as it heals; after three, the Sheaf turns and the lair actions stop. |
| **The Reaper as guest** (bundle) | The pact's *"one who came uninvited,"* Scene 02; the Vesper Bell from SP-HAL-03 | Set a place for the bell at the Empty Chair in Scene 01, then give it to the field. |
| **A doll for the field** (bundle) | The Gentleman's card from SP-HAL-01; *"its own kind,"* Scene 02 | Call in the favor at midnight. The Gentleman arrives and makes a corn-dolly double. |
| **Pay the pact** | The Choosing, Scene 04; the Empty Chair, Scene 01 | A guest is given. A PC may volunteer. |
| **Burn it all** | The Colossus's Burning Wicker, Scene 04; the barn's black timber | Fire in the barn. Same as breaking the pact, and the Sheaf blames the party forever. |

## Key NPCs
- [[Prudence Thorne]] — Village elder, Josiah's great-granddaughter, leader of the Sheaf. Grandmotherly, never raises her voice, will give the party to the field to save her people.
- [[Barnaby Reap]] — Innkeeper of the Bountiful Board, second in the Sheaf. Bakes the Hundredth Loaf; can't meet the PCs' eyes after 10 PM.
- [[Tamsin Reap]] — Barnaby's daughter and a doubter. Unlocks the barn's rear door if given hope.
- [[Hesper Gloam]] — "The Crow-Wife." The party's only real ally. Her grandmother opposed the pact and was burned for it.
- [[Old Ned Tallow]] — Traveling tinker, the backup guest, tied to a PC. He's eaten at the table and is delighted to be treated like a lord.
- [[The Harvest Lord]] — A corrupted treant rooted beneath the Tithe Barn. Speaks through rustling wheat.

## Key Locations
- **The Bountiful Board** — the inn, site of the Hundredth Feast, with the Empty Chair at the head of the table.
- **The Elder's House** — Prudence's tidy home; the founders' records and the pact on vellum.
- **The Green** — bonfire, the Corn Maze of the Last Sheaf, turnip lanterns on every step.
- **The Gold Fields** — wheat taller than a person, with roots that move when no one's looking.
- **The Blackthorn Copse** — Hesper's hut among the thorns, the only ground in the valley the pact doesn't touch.
- **The Tithe Barn** — a cathedral of black timber at the valley's heart. Wheat grows up through the floorboards. Areas T1–T5 keyed in [[Scene 05 — The Tithe Barn]].

## Encounters
DMG thresholds for four PCs: **APL 8** easy 1,800 / medium 3,600 / hard 5,400 / deadly 8,400 · **APL 9** 2,200 / 4,400 / 6,600 / 9,600 · **APL 10** 2,400 / 4,800 / 7,200 / 10,800.

| # | Name | Type | Scene | APL 8 | APL 9 | APL 10 | Adjusted XP (APL 9 build) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Empty Chair | social / horror | 01 | — | — | — | Wis save DC 15; no combat |
| 2 | Turnip Lanterns | exploration / combat | 02 | 1 flameskull (medium) | 1 flameskull (easy) | 2 flameskulls (medium) | 1,100 |
| 3 | The Fields Wake | combat | 03 | 1 shambling mound (easy) | 2 shambling mounds (medium) | 3 shambling mounds (hard) | 2 × 1,800 × 1.5 = 5,400 |
| 4 | The Procession | combat | 04 | Colossus, 100 hp (hard) | Wicker Colossus (hard) | Colossus + 2 cult fanatics (deadly) | 5,000 (solo) |
| 5 | The Pit | combat / social | 05 | Harvest Lord, 110 hp, 1 awakened tree | Harvest Lord + 2 awakened trees (hard) | as APL 9, + lair action tempo | (5,000 + 2 × 450) × 2 = 11,800 |

**A warning on the math.** Encounter 5 is already above the deadly line on paper; the lair actions and the restrained condition make it play harder than the number. Three things keep it fair: the Colossus is **never** in the same fight as the Harvest Lord (it is met on the road in Scene 04 or at the barn doors, and dealt with before the pit); [[Prudence Thorne]] stands at the pit to speak, not to cast, and fights only if attacked; and Harvest Bounty's cost is visible, so a party that fights smart turns the village in three rounds. The real contest is over the guest, not the hit points.

**Adventuring-day check (APL 9):** encounters 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 ≈ 23,300 adjusted XP against a daily budget of 4 × 7,500 = 30,000. Two short rests are available (after the feast, and in the copse). Fine.

## Treasure & Rewards
- **Heartwood staff** — a *staff of the woodlands*, cut from the Harvest Lord's heart, or given freely by it if the pact ended peacefully.
- **A century of offerings (T3)** — 2,500 gp in tithe-gold and heirlooms. Returning the past guests' belongings to their families could start a whole new adventure.
- **Hesper's gift** — a *bag of beans*. "Honest seed," she says. "Mostly."
- **Supplies** — 3 *potions of superior healing* from Barnaby's cellar.
- **Information as treasure** — the names of ninety-nine guests, carved on the root-cellar bones. Each is a family somewhere that never learned what happened.
- **Advancement** — the party reaches 10th level, which is the end of the bundle.

## Denouement
### Endings at a Glance
| Ending | How | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Break the pact** | Destroy the Harvest Lord. | The fields rot overnight, and the village faces its first hungry winter in a century. Hesper and Tamsin start teaching the village to farm ordinary seasons. Bittersweet, honest, hard. |
| **The Reaper as guest** (bundle) | Bring the Vesper Bell to the feast, set a place for it at the Empty Chair, and give it to the field at midnight. | Thorne came uninvited and was welcomed at the table, so the Harvest Lord accepts its own reaper. The pact is fulfilled and ends. Thornbury keeps its plenty, but future harvests will be ordinary. |
| **A doll for the field** (bundle) | Call in the Gentleman in Grey's favor. His carriage arrives at midnight, and he makes a corn-dolly double of the guest. | The field accepts its own harvest, given in the guest's place, and the pact closes. The Gentleman keeps the guest's reflection for his collection. |
| **Pay the pact** | Let a guest be taken, perhaps a PC who volunteers. | Thornbury keeps a hundred more years of plenty. The darkest ending, and the right one for some tables. |
| **Burn it all** | Set fire to the barn with the Harvest Lord inside. | Works like breaking the pact, but the Sheaf blames the party, and Thornbury becomes an enemy. |

### Bundle Payoffs
- **The Vesper Bell.** If the party brought the bell and rings it during the fight (an action), every awakened tree and shambling mound within 60 feet makes a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure it is frightened of Thorne's presence until the end of its next turn.
- **Thorne free (if he escaped in SP-HAL-03).** He arrives at 11 PM to claim his guest. Add a **wraith** with the SP-HAL-03 changes to the climax, and reduce the Harvest Lord to 110 hit points.
- **The Gentleman's card and the reflection.** The card calls his carriage at midnight. If a PC lost their reflection in SP-HAL-01, he already owns it, which is why the double is made so quickly, and why that PC casts no shadow afterward.
- **Allies from earlier villages.** Mother Nettle, Tobias Marrow, Ambrose Hale, or Annick Rook's crows can show up at the barn if the party sent word ahead. Each one is worth one useful action: freeing the guest, breaking the procession, or holding a door.

### Epilogue: After the Last Harvest
If you ran the whole bundle, close by sending a message from each village the party saved. Hollow Ford sends a corn dolly that doesn't watch anyone. Marrowmere sends a letter from Tobias Marrow, signed with his true name. Vesper Hollow sends a bell-shaped cake and news that the sun came up. Thornbury sends whatever its ending earned. Then, if the party called on the Gentleman in Grey, a final grey card arrives:

> *"Splendid harvest. Do call again. — G."*
