# Bestiary — The Tithe of Thornbury

Back to [[SP-HAL-04-The-Tithe-of-Thornbury]] · Index: [[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-04 — The Tithe of Thornbury/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]]

All blocks are 2014 *Monster Manual* (MM). Two creatures are reskins with changes spelled out in full; the rest are referenced by page.

## Wicker Colossus
*Huge construct, unaligned* — **clay golem** base (MM 168) with the changes below. CR 9 (5,000 XP).

> A thirty-foot man of woven willow and wheat, built new every hundred years by the Sheaf. A lantern burns in its chest behind the bars of a cage. It walks the guest to the barn; it does not come inside.

**Armor Class** 14 (natural armor) · **Hit Points** 133 (14d10 + 56); 100 at APL 8 · **Speed** 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 (+5) | 9 (−1) | 18 (+4) | 3 (−4) | 8 (−1) | 1 (−5) |

**Damage Immunities** acid, poison, psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks not made with adamantine weapons
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9 · **Languages** understands the Sheaf's hymn but can't speak
**Challenge** 9 (5,000 XP)

***Burning Wicker.*** *(Replaces Acid Absorption.)* Fire damage doesn't harm the Colossus. Instead, it ignites: for 1 minute, its slam attacks deal an extra 2d6 fire damage, and a creature in its Chest-Cage takes 1d6 fire damage at the start of each of the Colossus's turns. Any cold damage, or a large amount of water, puts the fire out. A burning Colossus that reaches a wooden structure sets it alight.

***Chest-Cage.*** The Colossus carries one Medium or smaller creature in a willow cage in its chest. A creature inside is restrained and has total cover. It can escape with a DC 15 Strength check (an action), or be cut free from outside: the cage has AC 15 and 30 hit points, and is immune to poison and psychic damage.

***Berserk.*** Whenever the Colossus starts its turn with 60 hit points or fewer, roll a d6. On a 6, it goes berserk: on each of its turns while berserk, it attacks the nearest creature it can see, and if nothing is in reach, it attacks an object (preferring the cage). It stays berserk until destroyed or until it regains all its hit points.

***Immutable Form.*** The Colossus is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.

***Magic Resistance.*** The Colossus has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Magic Weapons.*** The Colossus's weapon attacks are magical.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The Colossus makes two slam attacks.

***Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage (plus 7 (2d6) fire if burning). If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have its hit point maximum reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken. The target dies if this reduces its hit point maximum to 0. The reduction lasts until removed by the *greater restoration* spell or other magic.

***Haste (Recharge 5–6).*** Until the end of its next turn, the Colossus magically gains a +2 bonus to its AC, has advantage on Dexterity saving throws, and can use its slam attack as a bonus action.

**Scaling:** APL 8, 100 hit points. APL 10, two **cult fanatics** (MM 345) ride its shoulders, 30 feet up, casting *hold person*; they fall with it.

## The Harvest Lord
*Huge plant, neutral evil* — **treant** base (MM 289) with the changes below. CR 9 (5,000 XP).

> A treant the color of old bone, rooted in the pit beneath the Tithe Barn, its crown lost in the rafters. It made the pact a hundred years ago and has kept every word. It speaks through the wheat.

**Armor Class** 16 (natural armor) · **Hit Points** 138 (12d12 + 60); 110 if Thorne is also present, or at APL 8 · **Speed** 0 ft. (rooted)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 23 (+6) | 8 (−1) | 21 (+5) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) |

**Damage Resistances** bludgeoning, piercing
**Damage Vulnerabilities** fire
**Senses** passive Perception 13 · **Languages** Common, Druidic, Elvish, Sylvan; it speaks through any wheat within 1 mile
**Challenge** 9 (5,000 XP)

***Rooted.*** The Harvest Lord's speed is 0 and it can't be moved. It can't be knocked prone.

***Long Reach.*** The Harvest Lord's slam attacks have a reach of 20 feet; its roots run under the whole threshing floor.

***False Appearance.*** While the Harvest Lord remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a dead tree.

***Siege Monster.*** The Harvest Lord deals double damage to objects and structures.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The Harvest Lord makes two slam attacks.

***Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. *Hit:* 16 (3d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage.

***Rock.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, range 60/180 ft., one target. *Hit:* 28 (4d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage. (It hurls the pit's stones and, in the barn, loft-timbers.)

***Animate Trees (1/Day).*** The Harvest Lord magically animates two of the barn's great timbers it can see within 60 feet of it. These timbers have the same statistics as a treant, except they have Intelligence and Charisma scores of 1, they can't speak, and they have only the Slam action option. An animated timber acts as an ally of the Harvest Lord. It remains animate for 1 day or until it dies, until the Harvest Lord dies or is more than 120 feet from it, or until the Harvest Lord takes a bonus action to turn it back into an inanimate timber. The timber then takes root if possible. *(For a lighter fight, use two **awakened trees**, MM 317, CR 2, in place of treant-statted timbers; the encounter math in the adventure note assumes awakened trees.)*

### Lair Actions
On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the Harvest Lord takes one lair action; it can't take the same lair action two rounds in a row.

- ***Grasping Wheat.*** Creatures of the Harvest Lord's choice on the threshing floor must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be restrained by roots until they use an action to pull free (DC 14 Strength (Athletics)).
- ***Harvest Bounty.*** The Harvest Lord regains 20 hit points by drawing life from the fields. Each time, a field outside withers to black stubble in plain sight of the doors. After the third use, the Sheaf sees the pact eating its own promise: the singing stops, and a DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check turns the village against it. Prudence Thorne then calls the field's name, and the Harvest Lord loses its lair actions for the rest of the fight.

**Tactics.** It does not want to fight; it wants what it is owed. It grabs the creature nearest the guest and drags them from the lip. It uses Harvest Bounty below half hit points, and does not understand why that makes the singing stop.
