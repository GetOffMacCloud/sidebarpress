# Handout — The Pact of Thornbury

*Give to the players when they read the lintel of the Tithe Barn, the vellum in the Elder's house, or (bundle) the bone calendar in Vesper Hollow. It is written in a cramped, hurried hand, by lantern light, in a famine year.*

---

> *Ninety-nine harvests of plenty, and none shall fail.*
>
> *Each Hallow's Eve, the reaper's tithe of souls from the roads.*
>
> *And on the hundredth Hallow's Eve, a guest: one who came uninvited and was welcomed at the table, given to the field.*
>
> *Else the field takes back all it gave.*
>
> *Signed in the field, in the famine year, by the families of Thornbury.*
> *J. Thorne, Elder.*
> *Reap. Harrow. Furrow. Glean. Tallow. Gloam.*

---

**DM note.** The six founding names are the lever for the *founding name* subplot: swap one for a PC's family name before the session. *Tallow* is there so Old Ned's claim to the table is older than he knows; *Gloam* is there so Hesper's grandmother was burned by her own cousins. The four phrases and what each actually requires are in [[SP-HAL-04-The-Tithe-of-Thornbury]] under **What's Really Happening**.
