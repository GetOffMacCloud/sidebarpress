# Handout — Aunt Sedge's Letter

*For hook 4. Give it to the player whose character has kin in Candlecrown. Change the greeting to fit.*

> Sunsebb, the 20th day
>
> Dear one,
>
> Come for Needfest. Come early if you can, and bring your friends, the ones with swords.
>
> Something is wrong with the snow. It came a month early and it falls up the cliff when the wind is from the east. The tyrgs are on the Stair. Last night I heard our carol in the wind, all of it, every verse, and nobody here was singing.
>
> You will remember the rule about the seventh verse. Do not tell anyone why you are coming. Tell them it is for the goose.
>
> Your great-aunt, who taught you the tune,
> **Sedge**, at the shrine, Candlecrown
>
> *P.S. Wear wool. Real wool. Not that southern stuff.*
