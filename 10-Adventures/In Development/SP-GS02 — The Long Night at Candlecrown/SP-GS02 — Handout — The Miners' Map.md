# Handout — The Miners' Map

*Hanging behind Brannoc's desk in the workshop office (C3). A founders'-era survey of the Old Workings on a sheet of hide, inked in old Common letters with Suel numerals. Give it in [[Scene 02 — Candlecrown at Needfest]] or [[Scene 03 — The Second Day]]. It is the player version of map M3 with these labels and nothing else (Map Assets: [[SP-GS02 — Map Assets]]).*

- A square at the top marked **HEAD** (M1–M2), with a line down to **THE DEEP** (M9).
- A short branch marked **GOOD STRAW** (M3), scratched over later with a cross.
- Five short lines marked **PICKS** (M4).
- A small square with a hammer marked **CHAPEL — FORTUBO KEEP US** (M5).
- A circle marked **WATER** (M6).
- A crossing of four lines (M7), unlabeled.
- Three lines going down marked **LOWER** (M8).
- A long line marked **THE SEAM** running to the edge of the hide, where it is marked **CHUTE — CLIFF** (M10).
- In a different, later hand, along the bottom: *Sealed by order of the founders. Let the deep keep what it keeps.*
