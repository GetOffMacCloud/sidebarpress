# Handout — The Long Night Carol

*Give it in [[Scene 02 — Candlecrown at Needfest]] when the village sings, or in [[Scene 07 — The Rehearsal]]. The six verses are painted on the panes of the Carol Lantern. The seventh is never written, and isn't written here.* (Original lyrics, Sidebar Press, 2026.)

> **The Long Night Carol**
> *A hymn to Telchur for Midwinter Night, as sung at Candlecrown*
>
> **I.** Father Frost, the year is old, / The fields are bare, the folds are cold; / We set our lanterns on the hill / To show the North Wind we are still.
>
> **II.** You strip the tree and still the stream, / You stop the plough and stop the dream; / We do not ask you to be kind, / But leave the youngest lamb behind.
>
> **III.** We carved the stone, we dug the deep, / We took the silver, could not keep; / Now tin and glass and candle-light / Are all we offer you tonight.
>
> **IV.** Count us, Father, one by one, / The old, the young, the work undone; / And if you take one when you go, / Then let them sleep beneath the snow.
>
> **V.** The longest dark, the smallest moon, / The wind that hums the oldest tune; / We'll sing it back the way it's sung, / No lantern dark, no verse unsung.
>
> **VI.** And when the dawn comes thin and pale, / And frost is set on every rail, / We'll hang the lamps and send them down / To every hearth from Candlecrown.
>
> **VII.** *(sung in the old tongue)*

**For the DM:** verse III remembers the mine; verse IV is the line the village sings for its dead each year (Mother Sedge's sister; Dob's wife); verse V's "the wind that hums the oldest tune" is truer than anyone knows.
