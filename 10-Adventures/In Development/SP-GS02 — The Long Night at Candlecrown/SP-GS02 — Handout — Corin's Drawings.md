# Handout — Corin's Drawings

*Give it in [[Scene 03 — The Second Day]] when Corin draws. Four charcoal sketches on a board of packing pine, made fast and hard by a man who can't read. Art brief: [[SP-GS02 — Art Brief]] (H1).*

1. **The giant.** A figure three times the height of a man (Corin draws himself beside it for scale, with his crook), covered all over in small marks like scratches. A long tail. Two round eyes, filled in hard.
2. **The fold.** Corin at his sheepfold at night, mouth open, little notes coming out of it (he draws them as tiny birds).
3. **The taking.** A line from Corin's mouth to the giant's chest. Where it ends, he has drawn one of the marks, larger, and circled it three times.
4. **The lantern.** The Carol Lantern from the hall, with its six panes. Beside it, a seventh pane, scribbled over so hard the charcoal has torn the wood.
