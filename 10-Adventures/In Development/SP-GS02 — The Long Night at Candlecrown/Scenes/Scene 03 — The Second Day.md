# Scene 03 — The Second Day

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS02 — The Long Night at Candlecrown/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — Candlecrown at Needfest]] · Next → [[Scene 04 — The Wind That Sings]]

## Purpose
A day of free investigation. The party should find the mute shepherd, learn from Mother Sedge that the seventh verse is a curse that must never be written, and find the goat tracks that lead into the coal store.
**Budget:** 60 minutes. Running long? Let Tansy lead them to Corin, and have Mother Sedge come to them.

## Arrival
> *Morning comes late and grey. The snow has drifted to the windowsills and is still falling, fine as flour. Out on the east rim, a flock of sheep is crying at a gate nobody has opened. A boy with a bucket stands at the fold, looking back toward the hall. The door of the shepherd's hut is open, and snow has blown in across the floor.*

**To the table:** *"The boy shouts that Corin's gone. What do you do?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Brannoc Tull]].** *Wants:* the feast to go on without panic. *Knows:* found Corin mute at his fold at first light, frightened him into silence, and hid him in the wool loft to keep the village calm. *Reacts:* tells everyone, "Corin went down the Stair at dawn to fetch help from the valley." He holds to that until the party finds Corin or catches the lie (below).
- **[[Corin the Shepherd]]**, in the wool loft (C3). *Wants:* to be understood. *Knows:* what took his voice (he can draw it). *Reacts:* shrinks from strangers until someone speaks gently or brings his dog; then he draws.
- **[[Old Mother Sedge]]**, at the shrine (C5). *Wants:* the seventh verse kept safe. *Knows:* her grandmother's warning, the gate's hallowing, the founders' story. *Reacts:* tells the truth to anyone who asks her in private, and to her kin at once.
- **[[Tansy Kettle]].** *Knows:* she heard someone crying in the wool loft and "it didn't have a voice, it just cried." *Reacts:* tells the first PC who's kind to her.
- **Pell and Ash**, in the workshop, painting.

## Beats
Run these in any order. Each check is one try; a failed check can be retried by another approach.

**1. The empty fold.** Corin's hut: bed slept in, boots gone, his crook leaning by the door. A DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) check on the snow: a man's footprints go out to the rim and come back again; on top of them, *one* set of boot prints, bigger, leads from the fold to the workshop's back door. (Brannoc carried him.) On the rim stones, a thin glaze of blue ice is scratched with the same rune as the Stair. It melts under a flame.

**2. The elder's lie.** Catching it:
| Route | Check | Result |
|---|---|---|
| "In this storm?" | Wisdom (Insight) DC 12 against Brannoc | He's lying, and he's frightened, not wicked. |
| The Stair | Wisdom (Survival) DC 10 at the gate | No tracks have gone down the Stair since the party came up. |
| Dob | ask him | "Nobody's been through my gate since you." |
| Tansy | kindness | "Someone's crying in the wool loft." |

Confronted, Brannoc gives in: *"He can't speak. Not a word. If the village sees that, there's no Needfest."* He leads the party to the loft.

**3. Corin's drawings.** Corin is unhurt and mute; he can hear, nod and write a few letters (he can't read). Given charcoal and a board, he draws (see [[SP-GS02 — Handout — Corin's Drawings]]): a figure three times a man's height, covered in tiny marks, with a long tail; himself at his fold, singing to his sheep; a line from his mouth to the giant's chest, where he draws one of the marks; and the Carol Lantern with its six panes, and a seventh pane, which he scribbles over hard. **ESSENTIAL CLUE (Fact 1):** whatever took his voice wanted the carol, and specifically the verse that isn't on the lantern. A DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) or (History) check on the drawing: a cold giant covered in runes matches tales of the immoth, a winter spirit "that collects words" (Fact 1, partial).

**4. Mother Sedge.** At the shrine, privately, she tells what her grandmother told her:
- *"The seventh verse is not a prayer. It's a curse, a piece of one, older than Telchur's worship here. The founders hid it in the hymn so it would never be written down and never be lost. Something that hunts that curse would hear it written the way you hear your name across a room."* (**Fact 1**.)
- *"Never write the seventh verse. That was the only rule my grandmother made me swear."*
- **Ward-lore (Fact 4, route a):** the founders hallowed the whole crown as one house. "A thing that walks dead can't come over that gate unless someone who lives here asks it in. That's old law, older than the curse."
- She doesn't know about the counter-verse, but she knows "the miners kept a chapel below. Fortubo's. The founders sealed it." (Fact 5 seed.)

**5. The goat tracks.** The Ambersons are a nanny goat short this morning. A DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) check: something dragged it from their shed (no blood, no tracks but the goat's) around the back of the workshop, to the coal store, where the trail stops at the nailed hatch. The nails have been pulled from below and pushed back. **ESSENTIAL CLUE (Fact 5):** something comes and goes through the coal store. (Aurel took the goat; he climbed up through the mine.)

**6. Optional: the oil-wick crates.** If the party goes down the Stair to dig out the crates under the broken winch (C2): two hours, a DC 12 Strength (Athletics) group check to haul them up. Success means the lantern line has 18 posts lit from tonight (not 12). Tyrgs watch them from switchback 5 and don't attack in daylight.

## Development
- **If Corin is found,** the village learns by evening that his voice is gone. Brannoc calls a meeting at the hall, which Mother Sedge turns into the case for going on: "We sing. We always sing."
- **If Corin isn't found by dusk,** Tansy leads the party to him at supper.
- **If the party opens the coal-store hatch now,** run [[SP-GS02 — The Old Workings]] early: Aurel is asleep in M8 (it's day), and Lax.

## Clues Available Here

- Mother Sedge's warning → **Fact 1** (route b); ward-lore → **Fact 4** (route a).
- The rim rune melting → **Fact 2** (confirms route a).
- The goat tracks → **Fact 5** (route b).

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** white on white; a shepherd's crook by a door; charcoal on a board; a leafless tree carved in rock.
- **Sound:** sheep crying; a mute man breathing hard; Mother Sedge's dry, unhurried voice; the brazier.
- **Smell:** lanolin and wet fleece in the loft; incense of juniper in the shrine.
- **Touch:** fleece; the shrine is warm, the only warm stone on the crown.
- **Taste:** Marra's leek broth, brought round at noon.

## Alternates & Re-hooks

- **If Hob Fallow lost his voice instead** (Scene 02, beat 5): swap him for Corin. Hob can read and write, so he writes what Corin would draw, and adds, "It knew my name."
- **If the party wants to leave:** the Stair is passable today, just. The tyrgs come for anyone who goes down after dusk.
