# Scene 10 — The Long Night

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS02 — The Long Night at Candlecrown/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 09 — The Short Day]] · Next → [[Scene 11 — The Seventh Verse]]

## Purpose
The siege. Three waves of tyrgs on a verse-by-verse track while the village sings. The party holds the lantern line, the mine and the gate, and reaches the seventh verse with as many singers, lanterns and hit points as it can.
**Budget:** 75 minutes. Running long? Run wave 2 as a skill challenge (three checks), and fight waves 1 and 3.

## Arrival
> *The posts along the rim are lit, a ring of small lights in a white dark. Behind you, through the hall's shutters, forty voices begin the first verse of the Long Night Carol. Out beyond the lanterns, the snow is falling so thick it seems to be the dark itself. Then, from the Stair, from the cliffs, from everywhere at once, the pack begins to howl.*

**To the table:** *"First verse. Where is everyone?"*

## Who Is Here
- **The choir** in the hall or the singing room (count from the choir track, 37 by default).
- **Mother Sedge** leading; **Brannoc** at the hall door with a lantern-hook; **the gate-watch** or militia at the gate; **Pell and Ash** running oil.
- **Hrimvald's pack:** 3 waves, below. **Hrimvald** on the north cliff, unseen, until [[Scene 11 — The Seventh Verse]].
- **Aurel Vasch**, inside or outside (his Development lines from [[Scene 08 — The Knock at the Gate]]).

## The Verse Track
The night is seven verses long. Each verse is about 20 minutes of singing. Waves come on the even verses; the odd verses are **lulls**. At the end of every verse, apply the choir's benefit ([[SP-GS02 — The Lantern Line#The Carol and the Choir]]).

| Verse | Event |
|---|---|
| **I** | Lull. Positions. The howling. Hrimvald's Snuff begins (siege actions run only in rounds of combat; between waves, roll 1d4: that many posts go out). |
| **II** | **Wave 1: the Stair.** |
| **III** | Lull. Relight. |
| **IV** | **Wave 2: the mine (or the north cliff).** Aurel's move, if he's outside. |
| **V** | Lull. Relight. |
| **VI** | **Wave 3: the gate,** led by the alpha. Aurel strikes, if he's inside. |
| **VII** | → [[Scene 11 — The Seventh Verse]]. |

**In a lull,** each PC can do one thing: relight 2 posts; spend Hit Dice (a lull is too short for a short rest, but Mother Sedge's blessing at the shrine lets one PC per lull spend up to two Hit Dice as if resting); reposition; ready a fire pot; tend a fallen villager; talk.

**The choir and the waves.** At the end of any round in which a tyrg is inside the hall (not the singing room), 1d6 singers flee or are hurt: take them off the count. If the choir is ever Broken, Hrimvald comes at once (go to Scene 11).

## Wave 1: The Stair (Verse II)
The pack comes up the Candle Stair and over the gate wall, as in [[Scene 06 — Dusk on the Stair]].
- **Fire on the Stair:** one tyrg dies in the fire, the rest take 10 (3d6) fire damage and arrive a round late.
- **Tactics:** two at the gate, the rest along the rim knocking out posts on the Stair side and the east rim.

## Wave 2: The Mine (Verse IV)
- **If the coal store and the cliff mouth are open:** the wave comes up *inside* the workshop, through the hatch: 3 tyrgs and the **ice-rune guardian** from the cliff mouth. The guardian forces the hatch (Brannoc's planks last 1 round) and comes up last. Fight among the benches, the racks of toys and the forge (terrain as in [[Scene 04 — The Wind That Sings]]); the hall is one door away.
- **If either is barred or sealed:** the tyrgs climb the north cliff instead and come over the north rim 3 rounds into the wave, with no guardian. They go for the north posts first.
- **Aurel, if outside and refused:** during this wave he tries the wool loft window (C3) and asks whoever is nearest to let him in. A PC within 30 feet of the loft hears him (DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) over the fighting). If Corin is there, Corin can't answer; if Tansy is, she can.

## Wave 3: The Gate (Verse VI)
**The alpha**, the Grey Bitch of the Tors, leads the last of the pack straight at the gate. She is Fire-Hardened, and tyrgs near her ignore the lanterns' difficult terrain.
- **Tactics:** she opens with Great Howl from 60 feet, then charges with the pack. Two tyrgs break off to run the rim. She fights to the death tonight (Hrimvald is driving her).
- **Aurel, if inside:** he strikes now. Pell (if his thrall) puts out the lanterns on the workshop side (2 per round), and Aurel attacks the choir from inside the hall: a singer a round until a PC engages him. He fights to escape, not to win, and goes over the cliff if dropped below 40 hit points.
- **If the alpha survives the wave,** she withdraws to the north rim and fights at Hrimvald's side in Scene 11 (Deadly; see the Bestiary).

Subtract any tyrgs the pack roster says are already dead: the pack is 16 and the alpha in all. If the pack can't fill a wave, the wave is what's left.

**Terrain:** the lantern line (track the posts; the Flame-Shy rule); the gate and wall; the bonfire (if built); the workshop (wave 2); the hall door; the rim (the edge rule: the north and east rims are a 400-foot drop). The Long Night storm: heavily obscured beyond 60 feet except within 10 feet of a lit lantern or fire.
**Tactics:** see each wave. The pack's aim is lanterns first, then the hall.
**DM direction:** keep it moving. Run each wave as a single fight, and resolve the lulls in a sentence per player. Read the verse number aloud every time it changes. The players should feel the night getting longer and the village holding.

## Development
- **Count at the end of verse VI:** lantern posts lit · choir · the alpha (dead or with Hrimvald) · Aurel (where, and what he's done). These set up Scene 11.
- **If 12 or more posts are lit** when verse VII begins, the party sees Hrimvald coming over the north rim (no surprise).
- **If the choir broke** before verse VII, go to Scene 11 now, with Hrimvald arriving while the defenders are still fighting.

## Clues Available Here
- None new. This is where the clues get used.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a ring of lanterns going out and coming back; white shapes at the edge of the light; the bonfire; a toy goose crushed under a paw.
- **Sound:** the carol through the shutters, verse after verse; howls; the gate bell; Mother Sedge's voice holding the tune when the others falter.
- **Smell:** burning oil, wet fur, singed hair, pine smoke.
- **Touch:** the cold biting through gloves; the heat of the bonfire on one side of the face.
- **Taste:** smoke; blood from a split lip.

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the village voted for silence** (Scene 07): there is no choir and no verse track; run the waves at dusk, at midnight and before dawn, and Hrimvald comes at the third.
- **If Hrimvald already has the verse** (the Paid ending in Scene 06): there is no siege. The village keeps Midwinter with five fewer voices.

## Transition
- → [[Scene 11 — The Seventh Verse]]: the sixth verse ends; in the hall, Mother Sedge draws breath for the seventh, and on the north rim the lanterns' light catches something enormous climbing over the edge.
