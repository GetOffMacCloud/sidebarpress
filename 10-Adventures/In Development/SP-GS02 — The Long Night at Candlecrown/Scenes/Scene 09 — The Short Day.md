# Scene 09 — The Short Day

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS02 — The Long Night at Candlecrown/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 08 — The Knock at the Gate]] · Next → [[Scene 10 — The Long Night]]

## Purpose
The preparation phase. The party has the few grey hours of Midwinter day to get ready, and every choice changes the siege. This is where a Deadly night becomes a Hard one.
**Budget:** 40 minutes. Running long? Give each player two picks, not four, and resolve them in one round of the table.

## Arrival
> *Midwinter Day hardly dawns at all. The sky lightens to the color of pewter, and the snow comes sideways now, hissing against the shutters. Around the hall, the village waits: men and women with tools in their hands, children with lanterns, the gate-watch with their spears. The elder stands in the middle of them with his hat in his hands. "Tell us what to do," he says.*

**To the table:** *"You have until dark. What's the plan?"*

## Who Is Here
Everyone in Candlecrown. Mother Sedge at the shrine; Brannoc and the workshop; Dob's gate-watch; Pell and Ash; the households. The village does what the party asks, within reason, and argues about everything else.

## How the Day Works
The short day has **four watches** (about two hours each) before dusk. In each watch, each PC picks **one task** from the menu and says how they do it. Most tasks need one PC; some need two, or a crew (a crew is a dozen villagers, and the village has three: the **workshop**, the **gate-watch** and the **households**; each crew can do one task per watch if a PC is with them or Brannoc runs it). Tick the tasks on the Preparation Track; each one says what it changes in [[Scene 10 — The Long Night]] and [[Scene 11 — The Seventh Verse]].

**Starting state:** lantern posts lit at dusk = 12, or 18 if the crates came up (Scene 03), minus 6 if wave 1 left fewer than 6 lit (Scene 06). The **oil-wick cap** is 12 (18 with the crates; 24 if the crates come up today).

## Beats
**1. Orders.** The elder's question. Let the players plan out loud; the villagers ask practical questions ("How many lanterns on the east side? The wind's worst there.").
**2. Four watches.** Resolve picks one watch at a time. Interrupt once, in the second or third watch, with one of these (choose one):
- Hrimvald's voice in the wind, from the newest rune: Wenna's voice, singing the seventh verse, getting it slightly wrong. (It's practicing.)
- A tyrg on the cliff below the north rim, watching the work.
- Aurel (if outside) calling up from the cliff in daylight shadow: one more offer.
**3. Dusk.** The village eats together in the hall at the last of the light: Midwinter's meal, cold goose and hot bread. Brannoc raises his cup: *"No lantern dark, no verse unsung."*

## Development
- **Everything ticked** feeds [[Scene 10 — The Long Night]] and [[Scene 11 — The Seventh Verse]]. Read the track aloud before the first verse so the players see what they built.
- **A party that rests twice and builds nothing** fights the full siege: see the adventuring-day note in [[SP-GS02 — Bestiary]].

## Clues Available Here
- Corin's climbing route → where Hrimvald will come (the north rim, above M10).
- Dob's count → the pack's size.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** pewter sky; snow sideways; lanterns being hung by children on tiptoe; a bonfire stacked taller than a man.
- **Sound:** hammers again, but no fiddle; arguments; the hiss of snow on the shutters.
- **Smell:** lantern oil on everyone's hands; cold goose.
- **Touch:** oil-stiff gloves; the heat of the forge as fire pots are sealed.
- **Taste:** hot bread, eaten standing up.

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party wants to evacuate down the Stair:** sixty people, half of them children and old folk, in the Howling storm, with the pack on the switchbacks. The village refuses, by vote, unless the party carries it (DC 18 Charisma (Persuasion)); then run the siege on the Stair, which is worse.
- **If the party wants to hunt Hrimvald by day:** it's in the cliffs; it won't be found before dusk. It offers to parley instead (as Scene 06).
