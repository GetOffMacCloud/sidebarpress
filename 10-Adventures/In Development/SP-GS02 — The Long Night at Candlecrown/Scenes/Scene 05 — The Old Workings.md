# Scene 05 — The Old Workings

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS02 — The Long Night at Candlecrown/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 04 — The Wind That Sings]] · Next → [[Scene 06 — Dusk on the Stair]] · Site key: [[SP-GS02 — The Old Workings]]

## Purpose
The party goes down. They should come back up with the truth (the chapel's warning, and maybe the counter-verse), proof that fire works (the rune-pillar), and a first meeting with Aurel on his own ground.
**Budget:** 90 minutes. Running long? Go M1 → M5 → M7 → M8, and give the rest one line each.

## Arrival
> *Under the hatch, the coal store breathes cold at you. Behind the heaped coal, a doorway of dressed stone has been unbricked, and a passage slopes down into the dark. The walls are smooth with old pick marks. Far down, there's a faint sound like wind across the mouth of a bottle. Pell holds his lantern over the hole and says he hasn't been down here since he was nine.*

**To the table:** *"Who's carrying the light?"*

## Who Is Here
- **Pell and Ash** will guide the party as far as M4 ("the ghost tunnels"), and no farther. *Knows:* the way to M2, M3 and M4; "there's a door with a hammer on it we never opened." *Reacts:* Ash goes back up at the first sign of trouble; Pell stays if a PC asks him to.
- **Aurel Vasch** (M8), **the rune-pillar** (M7), tyrg scouts at night (M10). Full entries in [[SP-GS02 — The Old Workings]].

## Beats
**1. Down.** M1 and M2. The Suel letters around the shaft (Fortubo).
**2. The hunter (M3).** The trophy and the shield: Facts 2 and 3.
**3. The chapel (M5).** The heart of the scene. Give the players the handout ([[SP-GS02 — Handout — The Apprentice's Carving]]) and let them read it aloud. Offer the rubbing: a PC with paper and charcoal (Pell has both) can take one in 10 minutes. **Remind the table of Mother Sedge's rule if they start to copy the seventh verse from memory**: the counter-verse isn't the seventh verse. It is safe to write. (It is the one thing in this book that is.)
**4. The rune-pillar (M7).** The lesson fight. See Encounter.
**5. Aurel (M8).** By day he's asleep, and the party can find his trunk and decide what to do with a sleeping vampire spawn (see Development). By night or if Wary, he speaks from the ceiling. Play him as the book says: courteous, cold, asking nothing. Let the players notice the missing shadow.
**6. The seam and the mouth (M9–M10).** The frost-writing listens. The cliff mouth is the second way in, and it shows the party the storm from the outside: from here they can see a shape on the cliff face to the east, very still, watching the village.

**DM direction:** this fight exists to teach Fact 2. If the party has no fire and no oil, have Pell point at his lantern.

## Development
- **Aurel asleep (by day).** The party can stake him (Stake to the Heart: paralyzed until the stake is removed), drag the trunk into daylight (it's 400 pounds), or leave him. Killing a sleeping, polite man in his bed should feel like what it is; the village will be glad, and Tansy will cry. If he is destroyed, cut [[Scene 08 — The Knock at the Gate]] and Fact 4's routes are moot.
- **Aurel awake.** If the party promises to speak for him at the gate, he tells everything in his dossier except the threshold. If they refuse, he says, "Then I'll ask someone else," and goes to the cliffs at dusk.
- **The counter-verse** is in the party's hands only if they took a rubbing or read it with magic. Note it: it unlocks a bargain in Scene 11.
- **Voices heard in M9** set the 4-hour clock in the site key.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** pick-scarred stone; a hammer carved over an altar; blue writing in frost; the white square of the cliff mouth.
- **Sound:** wind in the shaft like a bottle; the guardian's ice creaking as it turns; a goat bleating; a polite voice from the ceiling.
- **Smell:** coal dust, old wet stone; in M8, nothing at all.
- **Touch:** the cold deepening with every step; a chair that is colder than the air.
- **Taste:** coal dust; metal.
