# Scene 02 — Candlecrown at Needfest

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS02 — The Long Night at Candlecrown/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 01 — The Candle Stair]] · Next → [[Scene 03 — The Second Day]]

## Purpose
The home base, in the order the party meets it. The players should come out of this scene fond of Candlecrown, knowing its people by name, and with three seeds planted: the draft under the workshop floor, the carol nobody may write down, and the wind that sings.
**Budget:** 50 minutes. Running long? Run the gate, the workshop and the feast; give the shrine and the homes one line each.

## Arrival

Low stone houses with turf roofs huddle against the wind. In the middle stands a long timber hall joined to a workshop, every window glowing, and from it comes the sound of hammers, a fiddle, and somebody laughing too loudly.*

Everyone in Candlecrown is human: Suel and Oeridian blood mixed for twenty generations, fair or red-haired, short and broad from the mine years. About 60 live here: 12 households, the gate-watch of six, the workshop's journeymen and apprentices.

## Candlecrown in Play Order

**C1. THE GATE AND THE WARDEN'S HUT.** A timber gate in a drystone wall across the top of the Stair, the only break in the wall around the crown. Mother Sedge renews its hallowing every Needfest with salt and a verse: the founders hallowed the whole crown as "one house," so the gate is a threshold to the undead (see [[Aurel Vasch]]). Dob Kettle and Tansy live in the hut beside it. A line of salt runs under the gate, crusted with snow.

**C2. THE STAIR-HEAD WINCH.** A timber crane and windlass for hauling crates up the cliff face. Its rope froze and snapped on Sunsebb 26; the crates of oil-wicks for the full lantern line are at the bottom of the cliff, buried in snow. (That is why only 12 posts are lit; see [[SP-GS02 — The Lantern Line]].)

**C3. THE WORKSHOP.** Long benches of carvers, tinsmiths and painters; a forge at the north end; Needfest figures drying on racks (Telchur with his icicle beard, Fortubo with his hammer, a dozen fat geese). [[Brannoc Tull]] runs it from a glassed-in **office** where the founders' charter and the **miners' map** hang behind his desk (see [[SP-GS02 — Handout — The Miners' Map]]; Fact 5). Above, the **wool loft** stores packing fleece. At the back, a nailed-down **coal-store hatch** leads down into the [[SP-GS02 — The Old Workings|Old Workings]]. **ESSENTIAL CLUE (Fact 5):** a DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check notices that the sawdust by the hatch lifts and settles in a cold draft, as if the floor were breathing.

**C4. THE HALL.** The workshop hall, joined to the workshop by a wide door: rafters hung with lanterns of every kind the village has ever made, benches for forty singers, a great hearth. Over the hearth hangs the **Carol Lantern**, a six-sided lantern of pierced tin with six panes painted with the six Common verses (see [[The Long Night Lantern]]). There is no seventh pane.

**C5. MOTHER SEDGE'S SHRINE.** A windbreak cave cut into a knuckle of rock beside the hall, a shrine to Telchur: a leafless tree carved on the back wall over a bare stone altar, an ever-burning brazier. It is the safety valve ([[SP-GS02 — The Lantern Line#The Safety Valve]]). Old Mother Sedge sleeps here in winter.

**C6. THE GREEN.** The open crown between the hall and the gate, where the feast tables stand under an awning of sailcloth. Twelve lantern posts are lit along the Stair side and the workshop side.

**C7. THE HOMES.** Twelve households in low stone houses. One line each if the party visits: the Fallows (kiln, gossip), the Pikes (three generations of painters), the Ambersons (tinsmiths; a new baby), the widow Rusk (weaves the packing straw; deaf in one ear), and eight more as needed.

**C8. CORIN'S FOLD.** A drystone sheepfold on the east rim, the farthest building from the hall, with a one-room hut. Corin's forty sheep huddle in the lee of the wall. Two lantern posts here are dark.

**C9. THE MINE HEAD.** A round stone cap the size of a cart, on the north rim, with a rusted iron ring. The old Suel shaft is under it, sealed with rubble two generations ago. (It is solid; the only open way down is the coal store, C3.)

## Beats
**1. The welcome.** Dob takes the party to the workshop, where [[Brannoc Tull]] greets them with both hands and a speech about Fortubo's hospitality. Every stranger at Needfest is a guest of the whole village. He'll pay the party's hook (the wagons, the shelter) with lodging in the hall and a seat at every feast.

**2. Needfest work.** The workshop is behind on the gifts. Invite each player to pick one task and describe how their character does it: wrapping lanterns in fleece, painting a Telchur figure, tuning the hall's fiddle, helping Marra with the feast. Each one is a chance to meet a villager and hear a rumor (d10 table in [[SP-GS02 — Front Matter#Running the Adventure]]).

**3. The carol.** At the feast, someone (Wenna, by default) starts the first verse of the Long Night Carol, and the whole table joins in. Six verses only: "The seventh is for Midwinter Night." If any PC asks to write it down, the table goes quiet and [[Old Mother Sedge]] says, flatly, *"No."* (Fact 1 seed.) She'll explain only in private: see [[Scene 03 — The Second Day]].

**4. The wind.** At full dark, step outside with whoever is near a door. Mother Sedge says, *"Listen to the wind tonight. It's singing our tune."* A DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check makes out the tune of the carol in the gusts, a long way off, with no words.

**5. Night of Needfest 1.** The party sleeps in the hall. In the small hours, Hrimvald takes Corin's voice at his fold (C8). Nobody sees it.
- **If a PC is keeping watch at the fold** (or anywhere on the east rim): run the encounter from [[Scene 04 — The Wind That Sings]] beat 2 early, with Corin as the victim, but Hrimvald won't fight. It withdraws over the rim when a lantern is brought within 30 feet, and takes **Hob Fallow**'s voice at his kiln instead. Note who lost a voice; the next scenes use that name.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** lanterns everywhere, in every color the glassblower can make; painted geese drying on racks; snow on turf roofs.
- **Sound:** hammers, a fiddle, the hiss of the forge, forty people singing in rough harmony.
- **Smell:** pine shavings, hot tin, roasting goose, wet wool, lantern oil.
- **Touch:** a warm mug; fleece; the cold breath through the floorboards by the hatch.
- **Taste:** goose fat, black bread, honey cake, cider with cloves.

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party is all business:** Brannoc asks them to help bring the oil-wick crates up from the foot of the cliff (C2) tomorrow. That sends them to the Stair, the runes and the east rim.
- **If a PC is a cleric of Telchur or knows Suel hymns:** Mother Sedge seeks them out tonight, not tomorrow.
- **Hook 4:** Mother Sedge greets her kin at the gate, tells them nothing in public, and says, "Come to the shrine at dawn."
