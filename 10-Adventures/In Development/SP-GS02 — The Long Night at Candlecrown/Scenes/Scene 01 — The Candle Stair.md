# Scene 01 — The Candle Stair

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS02 — The Long Night at Candlecrown/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 02 — Candlecrown at Needfest]]

## Purpose
Get the party up the cliff and into the village, teach the edge and footing rules, and plant the first clue: ice runes that melt under a lantern.
**Budget:** 25 minutes. Running long? Cut the climb to one check and start the fight at switchback 3.

## Getting There
Whichever hook brought them, the party reaches the foot of the [[The Candle Stair|Candle Stair]] on the afternoon of Needfest 1, in thickening snow. Two loaded gift wagons stand abandoned at the bottom under tarpaulins (hook 1); the carters went back to the valley on foot. Compress the valley road into one sentence.

## Arrival
> *The tor rises out of the snow like a table set on end, four hundred feet of grey rock with a crown of lights. A path is cut into its face in tight switchbacks, barely wide enough for a cart. Halfway up, two dead mules lie frozen across the path. Above them, something on the rock glints blue, like writing scratched in ice. Far overhead, a lantern on a pole swings back and forth.*

**To the table:** *"Someone up there is waving you on. Who goes first?"*

## Who Is Here
- **Dob Kettle** (gate-warden, see [[Dob Kettle]]) is at the top of the Stair with a lantern on a pole. *Wants:* the strangers up before dark. *Knows:* the Stair has been "too dangerous for mules" for three days; tyrgs were seen on the eastern switchbacks yesterday. *Reacts:* waves faster when the howling starts; drops rope ladders over the top switchback if the party is in trouble.
- **The pack.** **Tyrgs** (4) lie in the snow on switchbacks 4 and 5, silent, waiting for whatever comes up.

## Beats
**1. The climb.** The Stair is six switchbacks. In the Gathering storm it is difficult terrain all the way. A group DC 10 Strength (Athletics) check gets everyone up without trouble; on a failure, the party reaches switchback 4 tired (the first character to fall prone in the fight can't stand up this round unless they spend their whole movement).

**2. The mules (switchback 3).** Two carters' mules, frozen solid. A DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine) or Intelligence (Investigation) check: they weren't frozen to death; huge bites opened their throats first (*the tyrgs*). Their harness bells have been torn off.

**3. The runes (switchback 3).** On the rock face above the mules, a hand's width of blue ice is scratched with angular marks, like a word in a script nobody here reads (Suloise, cut by Hrimvald as a marker: "here"). **ESSENTIAL CLUE (Fact 2):** if a lantern or torch is held within a foot of it, it hisses and runs in seconds, and doesn't refreeze. Natural cold doesn't do that. A DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) check: the marks are a rune in old Suloise script, and the ice is magical.

**4. The pack (switchbacks 4–5).** As the party rounds switchback 4, the tyrgs rise out of the snow ahead and behind (two each way) and one howls. See Encounter.

**5. The top.** Dob hauls the party in at the gate and gives the first welcome of the feast: a mug of hot cider each, "on the elder." See [[Scene 02 — Candlecrown at Needfest]].

## Encounter
| PCs (APL 6) | Creatures | Adjusted XP | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | **Tyrgs** (3) | 2,700 (×2) | Medium |
| 5 | **Tyrgs** (4) | 3,600 (×2) | Medium |
| 6 | **Tyrgs** (6) | 4,050 (×1.5) | Medium |

**Terrain:** the switchback is 10 feet wide, with the cliff wall on one side and a drop on the other (the edge rule in [[SP-GS02 — The Lantern Line#Laws of the Mesa]]: a fall goes to the switchback below, 1d4 × 10 feet). Snow makes it difficult terrain, but not for the tyrgs (Snow Runner). The turns are 20-foot landings, the only places to stand three abreast.
**Tactics:** two tyrgs cut off the retreat downhill and two block the way up. The first to act uses Stunning Howl. They bite to knock prey prone near the edge, then rake. They go for the lantern-bearer last: a lit lantern held in hand makes its 10-foot radius difficult terrain to them.
**DM direction:** this fight teaches the edge. Let the players shove tyrgs off (contested Strength (Athletics) versus the tyrg's Athletics or Acrobatics, +3/+2) and let the tyrgs try it back. Tyrgs flee down the Stair when two have fallen.

## Development
- **Every tyrg killed here** comes off Hrimvald's pack (see the roster in [[SP-GS02 — Bestiary]]).
- **If the party fled up the Stair,** the surviving tyrgs lie in wait on the switchbacks and return in [[Scene 06 — Dusk on the Stair]] as part of wave 1.
- **If a tyrg went over the edge,** its body lies on switchback 1 in the morning, frozen with its jaws open.

## Clues Available Here
- The runes melt under a lantern → **Fact 2, fire is its weakness** (Clue Ledger).
- The bitten mules and the torn bells → the tyrgs are hunting on the Stair.
- The rune script → something writes in Suloise (Fact 1, later).

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** grey rock, white snow, a lantern swinging far above, blue scratches of light in the ice.
- **Sound:** wind in the switchbacks; no birds; a howl that makes knees go weak.
- **Smell:** snow, cold iron, the mules.
- **Touch:** ice under the snow; a rope ladder stiff with frost.
- **Taste:** hot cider at the top, too sweet, perfect.

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party flies or climbs the cliff** past the Stair: the tyrgs come over the top switchback after them at the gate. Run the fight at the gate with Dob and two guards helping.
- **If the party arrives at night:** Dob has 12 lanterns lit along the rim, and the tyrgs won't come within 10 feet of the last switchback. They attack lower down.
- **If they hauled the gift wagons up:** a wagon takes all six PCs and an hour; the tyrgs attack the wagon at switchback 4. A wagon pushed off the edge is a 2-ton weapon (one tyrg, no save).

## Transition
- → [[Scene 02 — Candlecrown at Needfest]]: the gate closes behind them, the snow thickens, and the lanterns along the rim come on one by one.
