# Scene 06 — Dusk on the Stair

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS02 — The Long Night at Candlecrown/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 05 — The Old Workings]] · Next → [[Scene 07 — The Rehearsal]]

## Purpose
The first wave, and the first time Hrimvald speaks for itself. The party fights tyrgs at the gate and hears the immoth's offer, which is the last resort for Facts 1 and 3.
**Budget:** 35 minutes. Running long? Cut the wave to three tyrgs and go straight to the offer.

## Arrival
> *The gate bell is ringing. Below the wall, the Candle Stair is full of moving white: tyrgs, five or more, coming up the switchbacks in silence. The lanterns along the rim make a ragged line of light. On the cliff face to the east, two hundred feet away and level with the gate, something enormous clings to the bare rock, still as a carving, its blue eyes on the village.*

**To the table:** *"The first of them is at the top switchback. Where are you standing?"*

## Who Is Here
- **Tyrgs** (5), wave 1.
- **Hrimvald the Gatherer**, on the east cliff, 200 feet from the gate. *Wants:* to be heard. *Reacts:* watches the fight without moving, then speaks (beat 3). It won't come closer tonight.
- **Dob Kettle** and the **gate-watch** (6 guards), at the gate with spears and lanterns.

## Beats
**1. The wave.** See Encounter. The guards hold the gate; the tyrgs try to come over the wall and along the rim, to put out lanterns.

**2. The watcher.** A PC who looks at the cliff and makes a DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) or Intelligence (Nature) check knows what they're looking at (**Fact 2, route c**): an immoth, a cold spirit of the high places, hoarder of words. *Fire burns it. Edged weapons slide off it.* (At DC 18: and it is a sorcerer.)

**3. The offer.** When the last tyrg falls or flees, the wind drops, and Hrimvald speaks. Every creature on the crown hears it, as if it stood at their shoulder. Its Common is old-fashioned and very polite:

> *"Lanterns. I am Hrimvald, and I gather what was scattered. Your village keeps a verse that is mine. Sing it to me once, into the wind, from the gate, and I will take that and the singer's voice, and go. Refuse, and on the Long Night I will take every voice on this hill."*

**ESSENTIAL CLUE (Facts 1 and 3, last resort):** it wants the seventh verse, and it bargains.
- **It answers questions,** briefly, truthfully, and always about words: what it wants (the verse), what it is (a gatherer), why (it was made to; it has come to like it). It won't say what it would take instead. A DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check: it is curious about the party; it would listen to an offer.
- **If someone sings the verse into the wind now:** the bargain is struck. See Development.
- **If a PC makes a counter-offer:** Hrimvald says, *"Bring it on the Long Night, and we will see what it weighs,"* and goes.
- **If attacked:** it's out of reach (200 feet, on a cliff). A spell that reaches it gets one *counterspell* and its silence. It leaves.

**Terrain:** the gate (a 10-foot timber gate in a 6-foot drystone wall), the lantern posts along the rim (12 lit, or 18 if the crates came up), the top switchback below the wall. The Howling storm: lightly obscured beyond 60 feet; ranged attacks beyond normal range miss.
**Tactics:** two tyrgs press the gate (and the guards); the rest climb the wall to either side and run the rim to knock out lanterns, then come at the defenders from the dark side. They howl only when they're out of a lantern's 10 feet.
**DM direction:** this is the first test of the lantern line. Track the posts. Every lantern still lit at the end of the fight is one the village doesn't have to relight in the storm.

## Development
- **Pack roster:** tyrgs killed here come off the siege.
- **If the verse is sung to Hrimvald now** (by Wenna, by Mother Sedge, or by a PC who learned it): Hrimvald keeps its word. The singer's voice is taken; Hrimvald takes the verse and leaves the Tors; the storm breaks by morning. Skip to [[Scene 12 — Dawn at Candlecrown]] (the *Paid* ending) on Needfest 4, and run the Needfest feast as a real feast. The voices it already took leave with it. It is a legitimate, sad choice.
- **Lanterns out:** relight costs the gate-watch the night; if fewer than 6 posts are lit at the end, the village is short on oil-wicks for the Long Night (6 fewer posts at the start of Scene 09).

## Clues Available Here
- The Arcana/Nature check → **Fact 2** (route c).
- The offer → **Fact 1** and **Fact 3** (both last resorts, and route a for Fact 3).

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** white shapes on the switchbacks; lanterns going out one by one; a huge still figure on the cliff.
- **Sound:** the gate bell; claws on drystone; a howl; then silence and a courteous voice in your ear.
- **Smell:** wet fur, lantern oil, blood on snow.
- **Touch:** a spear-shaft frozen to a glove.
- **Taste:** snow blown into an open mouth.

- **If the party is away from the gate:** Dob and the guards hold for 3 rounds, then the tyrgs are over the wall.
