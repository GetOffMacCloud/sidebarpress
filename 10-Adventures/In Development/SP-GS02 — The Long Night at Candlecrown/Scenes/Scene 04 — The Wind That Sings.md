# Scene 04 — The Wind That Sings

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS02 — The Long Night at Candlecrown/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 03 — The Second Day]] · Next → [[Scene 05 — The Old Workings]]

## Purpose
Session 1's ending. The party sees a voice taken (or nearly), glimpses what takes it, and learns that something can come up through the floor of the workshop.
**Budget:** 30 minutes. Running long? Skip the tyrg in the coal store and end on Dob.

## Arrival
> *The wind has dropped. Snow falls straight down now, so thick the lanterns on the rim are only smudges. Out on the east rim, Dob Kettle walks his watch with a lantern, humming the Long Night Carol. He reaches the end of the sixth verse and keeps going, into words that aren't Common. Beyond him, at the rim, the falling snow stops falling.*

**To the table:** *"Who's close enough to see what happens next?"*

Use this box if a PC is on the east rim or within sight of it. Otherwise, run beat 3 first and the scream from beat 2 is what brings them.

## Who Is Here
- **[[Dob Kettle]]** on watch, humming the seventh verse as he always does on the watch before Midwinter ("my wife's verse; she sang it lead"). *Reacts:* freezes, then tries to shout and can't.
- **Hrimvald**, unseen at first: a column of snow rising from the cliff below the rim, 20 feet high, with two points of blue light. *Wants:* Dob's voice, and the verse in it. *Reacts:* won't fight tonight. See beat 2.
- **A tyrg** (1), a scout, coming up through the mine to the coal store.

## Beats
**1. The verse.** Dob gets four lines into the seventh verse. Anyone listening and making a DC 15 Intelligence (History) check recognizes the sound of it as Suloise, the old Suel tongue: "the tongue of grave-markers in the Lortmils" (no one here reads it).
- **Character clue:** a cleric or paladin of Telchur, Fortubo or any Suel god, or a PC of Suel blood, needs no check. The metre is wrong for a hymn: it is the shape of a binding, the kind of chant that holds something rather than praises it. (**Fact 1, character route.**)

**2. The taking.** The snow at the rim rises into a shape twice the height of the gate, glazed and glittering, and leans toward Dob. A thread of frost runs from his mouth to its chest. His humming stops mid-word. Then the shape falls apart into snow and is gone over the rim.
- A PC who sees it makes a DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Religion) check: a cold elemental of some kind, not a ghost; it *took* something (Fact 1, partial).
- **If a PC steps between, or brings a flame within 30 feet before it acts:** Hrimvald doesn't take Dob's voice. It speaks instead, in a courtly, unhurried Common, from everywhere in the snow at once: *"Not tonight, little lantern. I will have it on the Long Night either way."* Then it goes, and takes no voice tonight. (This is a gift to a watchful party; it costs Hrimvald one rune it will want back.)
- **If a PC attacks it:** the snow-shape is only snow; the attack falls through. Hrimvald is on the cliff below, 60 feet down, and doesn't stay to be found.

**3. The coal store.** Within a few minutes, a crash and a shout come from the workshop. Pell, working late, has found the coal-store hatch smashed open from below and a **tyrg** in the workshop, among the drying toys. See Encounter. **ESSENTIAL CLUE (Fact 5, last resort):** something big came up through the floor; there is a way in under the village.

**4. Dob.** If Dob lost his voice, he comes to the hall with his lantern, white-faced, and writes on a slate: *IT HAD MY WIFES VOICE IN IT.* Hrimvald spoke to him in the voice of his dead wife, whose seventh verse he was humming. Tansy won't leave his side.

**5. End of session.** The village gathers in the hall. Mother Sedge leads the six Common verses, as loud as forty people can sing. Outside, the wind picks up again, and it is singing too.

## Encounter
| PCs (APL 6) | Creatures | Adjusted XP | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4–6 | **Tyrg** (1) | 450 | Trivial; it bolts |

**Terrain:** the workshop's benches (half cover), racks of drying toys (a creature pushed into one knocks it over; the area becomes difficult terrain), the forge (a creature shoved into it takes 1d6 fire damage), the smashed hatch (a 10-foot drop to the coal store).
**Tactics:** the tyrg is a scout. It snaps at whoever is nearest, then flees back down the hatch when hurt or when a lantern is waved at it.
**DM direction:** this isn't a fight; it's a door opening. The point is the hole in the floor.

## Development
- **Record whose voice was taken tonight** (Dob by default, or nobody if the party stopped it). It is a rune on Hrimvald.
- **The hatch is open.** From now on, unless the party bars it, the Old Workings are open to the village, and the village to them. Brannoc has it nailed shut with planks by morning (it holds against a tyrg but not a determined vampire).
- **If the party chases the tyrg into the mine tonight,** run [[SP-GS02 — The Old Workings]] at night: Aurel is awake (Wary), and the tyrg flees to M10 and out.

## Clues Available Here
- The verse is in Suloise → **Fact 1** (detail).
- The snow-shape takes a voice → **Fact 1**.
- The tyrg from the coal store → **Fact 5** (last resort).

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** snow falling straight down; a lantern on the rim; snow rising instead of falling; a frost-thread.
- **Sound:** humming, then nothing; a dead woman's voice from the snow; splintering wood and painted tin toys clattering.
- **Smell:** cold so hard it smells of nothing.
- **Touch:** the hair on your arms rising; ice forming on your eyelashes.
- **Taste:** frost on the lips.

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If no one is outdoors:** the party hears Tansy screaming from the gate. By the time they reach the rim, Dob is standing alone and mute.
- **If the party set a watch on every rim:** Hrimvald takes no voice tonight and the tyrg still comes up through the floor.
