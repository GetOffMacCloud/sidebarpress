# Scene 12 — Dawn at Candlecrown

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS02 — The Long Night at Candlecrown/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 11 — The Seventh Verse]] · Optional → [[Scene 13 — The Ice Library]]

## Purpose
Concluding the adventure: dawn, what the village gives back, and what follows. The players get the last word.
**Budget:** 15 minutes.

## Arrival
> *The snow stops at first light, all at once, as if someone had closed a door. The sky over the Tors is pale green and gold. Lanterns are still burning along the rim, small and yellow against the dawn. In the hall, the choir is still singing, hoarse and slow, because nobody has told them they can stop.*

**To the table:** *"Who tells them?"*

Use this box for every ending but *Silenced* and *Driven Off*. For those, the snow keeps falling, and the hall is quieter.

## The Endings
| Ending | What happened | Dawn |
|---|---|---|
| **Unbound** | The counter-verse freed Hrimvald. | Every voice comes home at dawn, including three strangers' (one is a Keoish envoy's; the sequel can find the others). The storm breaks. The lanterns go down to the valley on Needfest 7. |
| **Saved** | Hrimvald destroyed, or bargained off with a word worth the verse. | As Unbound if Hrimvald was destroyed (every rune breaks; the strangers' voices go home too). If it was bargained off, it keeps the strangers' voices. The PC who paid with a word or confession can never say it again. |
| **Fooled** | The false verse. | As Saved. In a year, the false verse reaches the Mountain of Ultimate Winter and doesn't fit. Hrimvald comes back, and it's not polite this time (a sequel). |
| **Paid** | The verse given (sung, or Wenna's paper). | One voice is gone with Hrimvald (the singer's), unless the paper paid. The storm breaks. The village sings six verses next year, and the seventh is gone. Mother Sedge says nothing about it, ever. |
| **Driven Off** | Hrimvald fled with some lines and runes. | The storm thins but doesn't break. It returns every dusk; each night it comes back for the lines it lacks. The voices it holds last until the spring thaw. Run [[Scene 13 — The Ice Library]] to get them back. |
| **Silenced** | It gathered all seven lines. | Hrimvald leaves with every voice it holds. The storm breaks. Candlecrown keeps Needfest in silence, with slates. In spring, the Yeomanry hears that the lantern village has stopped speaking, and the immoth's library in the high Tors is a known place. |

**Aurel Vasch:**
- **Destroyed:** Mother Sedge buries his ashes outside the gate, which is the kindest place she could think of.
- **Refused, with a bargain kept:** he walks down the Stair on Needfest 5 at dusk, bows to the gate, and is gone. He owes the party one favor, and he'll pay it (Continuing).
- **Inside and fled:** he went over the cliff in the fighting. Pell, if he was a thrall, recovers in three days and remembers nothing; the bite marks stay.
- **Inside and still there at dawn:** the sunrise finds him in the hall. Let the players decide what happens.

## Rewards
- **The village's thanks.** For every ending but *Silenced*: on Needfest 7, the workshop makes a new Needfest figure, and it's the party (all of them, in painted tin, with lanterns). Every family in the valley will have one next year.
- **[[The Long Night Lantern]]**, given by Brannoc and Mother Sedge to the party for Unbound, Saved, Fooled or Driven Off endings (they keep the Carol Lantern; this is its twin, made for the party in Scene 09's last watch by Pell and Ash).
- **Coin.** Brannoc pays 50 freegolds (50 gp) each from the Needfest takings, counted out on the hall table in a clatter of commons (the wagons go down late, but they go). Ardo Pennick pays hook 1 (150 gp, +50 for the story). Sage Imra Vell pays nothing, and is told nothing, unless the party gives her the curse-verse (don't).
- **Spoils** from Hrimvald (if destroyed): [[Heart-Rune of Hrimvald]]; the ice runes (see [[Immoth]]). From the mine: the hunter's purse and signet, the Fortubo pendant, the immoth tail-tip.
- **Experience:** the encounters as fought (see [[SP-GS02 — Bestiary]]). If you use milestone advancement, the party reaches level 7 at dawn for any ending but *Silenced*.

## Continuing the Adventure
- **The hidden verses.** The apprentice's carving says one verse was hidden here. Others were hidden elsewhere in the Sheldomar Valley by other Suel refugees: in a Keoish lullaby, a Gran March marching song, a dwarven work-chant in the Lortmils. Each one has a hunter. (If *Unbound*: Hrimvald asks the party to sing the counter-verse to the others.)
- **The Mountain of Ultimate Winter.** Where the immoths take what they gather. Nobody has been there and come back. (Not yet.)
- **Melkot.** The Sons of Marchanter, the arcane order of Melkot in the western Tors, hear that a curse-verse was in Candlecrown. They send someone polite to buy it.
- **The Passage of Slerotin.** Aurel's master, Ilvan Dross, died at a sealed door in the Passage, near Dark Gate. Aurel knows where his master's grave and papers are, and he owes the party a favor (or the party owes him one). See [[C12 - 5e Conversion Ledger|C12, The Forgotten Library of Raanet]], where Bleak Gate is the Passage's eastern mouth.
- **The house of Halvard.** The hunter's signet (M3) belongs to a Keoish noble family that still offers a reward for "the bones of Sir Edric Halvard, lost in the Tors in 506 CY."
- **Previous play.** If the party played [[SP-GS01-The-Gluttonwretch-of-Hommlet]], Hommlet's lanterns arrive late this year, and Gundigoot hangs the party's tin figure over the bar.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a green-gold dawn; lanterns still burning in daylight; forty hoarse people looking at the door.
- **Sound:** the choir stopping; then everyone talking at once; or, in the bad endings, no one talking at all.
- **Smell:** woodsmoke; the bonfire's ashes; porridge.
- **Touch:** the first warmth on the face.
- **Taste:** Marra's Needfest porridge with honey, the best thing anyone has ever eaten.

## Transition
- **Unbound, Saved, Fooled, Paid:** the end. Give each player one sentence for their character on Needfest 7, watching the lantern wagons go down the Stair.
- **Driven Off:** → [[Scene 13 — The Ice Library]].
- **Silenced:** the end, or a spring sequel: [[Scene 13 — The Ice Library]] run as a rescue months later.
