**Adventuring day.** Session 3 is one day: three waves and the climax come to 17,600 adjusted XP (Hrimvald alone) or 21,750 (the alpha survives to the climax), against a 2014 day budget of 20,000 for five 6th-level characters (DMG p. 84). There is no time for a short rest between waves unless the preparation phase bought one. **The preparation phase, the lantern line, the bonfire, the choir's Inspiration and the bargain are the equalizers**: every preparation in [[Scene 09 — The Short Day]] removes foes, softens a wave or grants a resource. A party that prepares well fights a Hard night; one that doesn't fights a Deadly one.

**Session 2** (the mine, wave 1 and maybe Aurel) comes to about 5,200–7,000 adjusted XP, well under budget: the danger there is information, not attrition.

**Live dials:** add or remove tyrgs one at a time (450 XP each), or add tyrg minions four at a time (see the scene Minion Packs). For six characters, Hrimvald's Word-Theft recharges on a 4–6; for four, use 152 hit points (see [[Immoth]]).

## Hrimvald the Gatherer
*Huge elemental, neutral evil.* CR 9 (5,000 XP). **Full block: [[Immoth]].** Dossier: [[Hrimvald the Gatherer]].

**In SP-GS02 only** (adventure riders):
- **Starting runes.** Hrimvald arrives with 3 runes (a Keoish envoy's voice, a Tors goatherd's, a dead dwarf's) plus one for each Candlecrown voice taken: Corin (S03), Dob Kettle (S04), Wenna Tull (S07), and any taken since. Spend a Word uses the oldest first, so the village's voices come back only after the three strangers' runes are gone.
- **Siege actions** on Midwinter Night: the lair block in [[Immoth]] (Snuff, Whiteout, Icefall, The Pack Answers).
- **The bargain.** Hrimvald won't attack during a parley it opened, and it keeps a bargain to the letter. See [[Scene 11 — The Seventh Verse]].
- **It hears the verse.** Any creature that sings or speaks the seventh verse aloud within 1 mile of Hrimvald during the storm draws it: it arrives in 1d4 rounds (outside the siege, 1d4 × 10 minutes).

## Aurel Vasch
*Medium undead (human), neutral evil.* **Vampire spawn** (MM p. 298) with no changes to the block: AC 15 (natural armor), 82 hit points (11d8 + 33), Regeneration 10, Spider Climb, Vampire Weaknesses (Forbiddance, Harmed by Running Water, Stake to the Heart, Sunlight Hypersensitivity), claws and bite. CR 5 (1,800 XP). Dossier: [[Aurel Vasch]].

**Riders (SP-GS02):**
- **Languages:** Common, Keolandish, and enough Suloise to read a grave-marker.
- **The threshold.** Forbiddance is the whole of his problem. The gate of Candlecrown counts as a residence's threshold (the village was hallowed as one household by its founders, and Mother Sedge renews it each Needfest). He can't pass the gate line, cross into any home, or enter the workshop without an invitation from someone who lives there. **An invitation from a PC doesn't count**; only a villager's does. He can climb the cliffs, but the mesa edge is inside the line.
- **The thrall.** Each night he is inside, he drinks from one sleeping villager. A villager he feeds on unnoticed becomes his thrall by the next night (DM's choice; Pell is his first choice) and will open doors, put out lanterns and hide him.
- **No charm.** Aurel has no power to compel anyone. Everything he gets, he gets by asking.
- **What he knows:** see the dossier. He'll trade it for an invitation, or for safe passage down the Stair after the storm.
