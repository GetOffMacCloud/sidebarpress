# The Old Workings

> The worked-out Suel silver mine under Candlecrown: home to a vampire spawn and an immoth's sentry, and the place the answers are.

## Recent Events
The founders' miners worked these tunnels for silver until the seam gave out, and they stopped going down entirely after a monster-hunter crawled in from the cliffs and died here in their grandparents' day (M3). The founders sealed the miners' chapel and nailed the coal-store hatch, and the mine was forgotten except by children. Since Sunsebb, **Aurel Vasch** has slept in the lower shafts (M8), climbing out through the cliff mouth (M10) to hunt. Three nights ago Hrimvald's tyrgs found the cliff mouth, and Hrimvald set an **ice-rune guardian** at the crossing (M7) and another outside the mouth.

## Laws of the Place
- **Dark.** No light but what the party brings. Ceilings 7 feet in the galleries, 12 feet in the chambers.
- **Cold.** Below the frost line (M6 and deeper) it is as cold as the storm outside: the cold save applies hourly (DC per the storm stage; see [[SP-GS02 — The Lantern Line]]).
- **Old timber.** The props are 1,000 years old and brittle. Any area effect that deals 20 or more damage, or a creature shoved into a prop, brings down a 10-foot section of ceiling (DC 13 Dexterity saving throw; 3d6 bludgeoning and restrained under rubble on a failure, half and not restrained on a success).
- **Ice speaks.** Anything said aloud in M9 or at the cliff mouth (M10) is heard by Hrimvald (see How the Site Reacts).
- **Map symbols:** P = rotten prop; R = rune-ice; S = shaft.

## How the Site Reacts
### Alert States
| State | Trigger | Who stands where |
|---|---|---|
| **Lax** | Default by day | Aurel asleep in his trunk (M8). Guardian motionless (M7). |
| **Wary** | Fighting anywhere; any word spoken in M9; night | Aurel awake, clinging to the ceiling of M8 or following the party unseen through the galleries (Stealth +6). |
| **Alert** | Aurel's trunk disturbed, or Aurel hurt | Aurel leaves by M10 and climbs to the cliffs; he won't come back until the party leaves. He'll still knock at the gate tonight ([[Scene 08 — The Knock at the Gate]]), and he'll be colder about it. |

### Responses
- **Party looks weak:** Aurel offers to guide them out for "a little blood, freely given; I'm not a beast."
- **Party looks capable:** Aurel stays courteous, says nothing about the threshold, and offers what he knows about immoths for a promise to speak for him at the gate.
- **Party looks overwhelming** (or carries a holy symbol of Pelor or St. Cuthbert openly): Aurel watches from the ceiling and doesn't show himself; he speaks only from the dark in M8, then leaves by M10.
- **Survivors flee to:** the cliffs outside M10 (Aurel); the Stair (tyrgs).
- **Reinforcements:** none in daylight. At night, a tyrg (1) arrives from M10 every 10 minutes the party spends in M9–M10, up to 3.
- **Recolonization:** if the party leaves the cliff mouth unbarred, tyrgs use the mine every night; wave 2 of the siege comes up through it ([[Scene 10 — The Long Night]]). If the guardian at M7 is destroyed, Hrimvald doesn't replace it.

### Clocks
- **Dusk:** Aurel wakes and climbs out through M10 to the cliffs. He knocks at the gate after dark.
- **4 hours after the party first speaks in M9:** Hrimvald knows that "lanterns have come to the mouth," and it makes its first offer at dusk by name if it heard any names.

## Key

### M1. THE COAL STORE
> *Below the hatch, a ladder drops ten feet into a cellar heaped with coal and broken crates. The air is colder than the night outside. In the north wall, behind the coal, an old doorway of dressed stone leads into a passage that slopes down into the dark. The coal is scuffed, and a goat's hoof-prints run through the dust.*

The workshop's coal store was dug into the old mine head. The founders bricked the doorway; the bricks lie broken behind the coal (children, then Aurel).
**Treasure:** none. **Development:** barring this doorway (6 hours of work, or 1 watch with the whole workshop in [[Scene 09 — The Short Day]]) is how the party closes wave 2's route.

### M2. THE SUEL MINE HEAD
> *A round chamber, the stone smoothed by centuries of hands. A shaft drops through the middle of the floor into blackness, crossed by a rotted timber frame with a great stone winding-drum. Suel letters are cut around the rim of the shaft. Galleries lead off east and west.*

The old main shaft (S) drops 120 feet to M9; the rubble-capped top is C9 above. The letters say, in Suloise, *"Fortubo guards the deep."*
**Development:** a rope down the shaft is the fast way to M9 (DC 10 Strength (Athletics)). The winding-drum is too rotten to use.

### M3. THE HUNTER'S END
> *A side passage ends at a bed of old straw and a collapsed lean-to of boards. A skeleton in rusted mail sits against the wall with a broken spear across its knees. Beside it, half-buried in the straw, lies a long, curved shard of something like blue glass, as thick as a man's arm and gnawed all over by teeth.*

Seventy years ago, a monster-hunter from Keoland tracked an immoth in the high Tors, cut the tip from its tail, and fled with the trophy down the cliffs and into the mine through M10, where the tail's frost-poison finished him. Tyrgs gnawed the trophy but couldn't break it. **ESSENTIAL CLUE (Fact 2, route b):** the hunter scratched on the inside of his shield: *FIRE ONLY FIRE — EDGES SLIDE OFF IT — IT TALKS FIRST.* (Facts 2 and 3.)
**Treasure:** the tail-tip (an immoth relic: as cold as ice that never melts; a sage pays 100 gp). The hunter's purse: 23 gp and a Keoish signet ring (the house of Halvard; 25 gp, and a sequel hook). His spear is rusted through.

### M4. THE GALLERY OF PICKS (range: M4a–M4f)
Six short dead-end galleries where the seam was followed and lost. Pick marks, old tallies scratched in Suel numerals, rotten props (P). Pell and Ash's childhood marks in charcoal ("ASH WAS HERE," "PELL IS A GOAT") are in M4b. Nothing of value.

### M5. THE APPRENTICE'S CHAPEL
> *A low door of dressed stone opens into a small chapel. A hammer is carved large on the far wall, above a plain stone altar. Every other wall is covered in close-cut writing, two scripts side by side. Half of it is in plain old Common letters; the other half is in Suel characters. A lamp niche beside the altar still holds a clay lamp, dry as dust.*

The miners' chapel to Fortubo, sealed by the founders. The **apprentice's carving** fills the walls: a warning in archaic Common and, beside it, the **counter-verse** in Suloise (see [[SP-GS02 — Handout — The Apprentice's Carving]]).
- **The warning (Common, readable by anyone with DC 10 Intelligence for the old letters):** **ESSENTIAL CLUE (Facts 1 and 3):** the seventh verse is a piece of a binding curse; "the cold ones" gather it; "sing it, never write it"; "at Kendeen they bargained, and it took the words and went."
- **The counter-verse (Suloise):** the apprentice's unbinding word for the immoths. Reading the meaning needs *comprehend languages* or a reader of Suloise (Aurel can; see M8). **Without** the meaning, a DC 15 Intelligence (History) check (or a rubbing taken to the village and a day's work) gives its sounds, enough to sing it. The carving says only: *"This is the word that unbinds them. Give it only if you would free what hunts you."*
**Treasure:** the altar has a hollow under its top (DC 13 Investigation): a silver hammer pendant of Fortubo (50 gp; a holy symbol).
**Development:** a party that takes a rubbing of the counter-verse has the key to the best bargain in [[Scene 11 — The Seventh Verse]].

### M6. THE FROZEN SUMP
> *The passage opens onto a black pool, frozen smooth from wall to wall, forty feet across. On the far side, the passage climbs again. Something under the ice is pale and round.*

The ice is 2 inches thick: a creature weighing more than 100 pounds (with gear) breaks through at the middle on a 1–3 on a d6 per crossing, into 6 feet of freezing water (the cold save at once, and a DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check to climb out). The pale thing is a miner's helmet. Fire or a ladder across makes it safe. There is a ledge along the east wall (DC 12 Dexterity (Acrobatics) to edge along it).

### M7. THE RUNE-PILLAR
> *Four galleries meet in a chamber with a high, timbered roof. In its center stands a pillar of ice as thick as an ox, glittering in your light. Deep inside it, a single mark glows faint and blue. The ice is clear enough to see the far wall through, distorted, as though seen through water.*

Hrimvald's sentry. **Creatures:** an **ice-rune guardian** (1; 2 for six PCs), motionless (False Appearance). Its word is "stay."
**Tactics:** it waits until a creature is within its 10-foot reach, then attacks with surprise if no one noticed it (a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check spots the rune turning to follow the light). It opens with Hush on a spellcaster or anyone speaking. It never leaves M7 and never retreats.
**ESSENTIAL CLUE (Fact 2, last resort):** a flask of burning oil, or 20 fire damage from one source, melts its Rune Heart: it collapses into slush in one splash.
**Treasure:** its rune, if destroyed by anything but fire (see [[Ice-Rune Guardian]]).
**Development:** if the party fights here, the mine goes to Wary. The noise carries to M8.

### M8. THE LOWER SHAFTS (AUREL'S SHELTER)
> *The gallery ends in a cross-cut where three shafts drop into darkness. Someone has made a room of it: a wooden trunk, a folded cloak, a single chair set facing the passage. A goat is tethered in the corner, bleating. The goat's breath steams in your light. There is no other breath here.*

Aurel Vasch's shelter. The trunk holds a layer of earth from his master's grave at Bleak Gate, the Passage's eastern mouth (his resting place).
**Creatures:** **Aurel Vasch** (1): by day, asleep in the trunk (Lax); by night or Wary, on the ceiling above the chair.
**Meeting Aurel.** He is polite, cold, and asks for nothing yet. He opens: *"You'll forgive me for not rising. Is it still snowing above?"* He calls himself a pilgrim who took shelter from the storm, "like you." He'll say:
- He is going to Loftwick and wants nothing more than a warm room. (True.)
- *"What's in the storm collects words. I saw one in the Passage of Slerotin once, at a sealed door. It bargains before it kills."* (**Fact 3, route c**.) He'll tell more for a promise to speak for him at the gate.
- He can read the Suel on the chapel wall, "a little." He'll read the counter-verse for an invitation, and not before.
- **Tells (Fact 4, route c):** in the party's lantern light, he casts no shadow on the wall behind his chair (DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) to notice, automatic if anyone looks for it).
**Tactics:** he won't fight unless cornered or staked at. He flees up the shaft (Spider Climb) toward M10.
**Treasure:** in the cloak, a letter of introduction to "the Spokesman at Dark Gate" for "Master Ilvan Dross and his man Aurel Vasch, explorers," dated Goodmonth 575 (his master; see [[Aurel Vasch]]).
**Development:** if the trunk is destroyed or *hallowed*, Aurel has nowhere to rest by day; he goes Alert and becomes desperate. He'll knock at the gate tonight and beg.

### M9. THE SILVER SEAM
> *A long, low stope follows a vein of dull grey rock that has been dug hollow. The walls are furred with blue frost, and across the frost runs fine angular writing, line after line, as if someone had scratched words into the ice with a needle. The writing goes on into the dark, toward a faint grey light and a sound of wind.*

The worked-out seam, and Hrimvald's ear: the frost-writing is its runes, and they "listen." Anything said aloud here is heard by Hrimvald (see Clocks). A DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) check: the writing isn't a spell, it's a *record*, words in a dozen languages.
**Development:** if a PC reads any of the writing aloud, it repeats back in their own voice from the far end of the stope.

### M10. THE CLIFF MOUTH
> *The stope ends at a ragged opening onto the night, or the day, a hundred feet below the rim of the tor. Snow blows straight in. Outside, a narrow ledge runs along the cliff face to the west. Big pawprints come and go through the drift at the mouth. Beyond the ledge there is only white.*

The miners' old spoil chute, onto the north cliff, 200 feet above the scree. The ledge runs 300 yards west and down to the Stair's fifth switchback (a DC 12 Dexterity (Acrobatics) or Strength (Athletics) check in the Howling stage; a failure is the edge rule).
**Creatures:** a second **ice-rune guardian** stands outside the mouth on the ledge, 30 feet away, motionless (it comes up with wave 2 in [[Scene 10 — The Long Night]]); it attacks only if a creature comes within reach.
**Development:** barring the mouth with stones (1 watch) or bringing down the stope roof (a deliberate cave-in, 20 damage to a prop) closes the mine to the tyrgs. Wave 2 then has to climb the north cliff (it arrives 3 rounds late).
