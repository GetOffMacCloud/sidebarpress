# The Long Night at Candlecrown

**APL 6 (levels 5–7) · 5 characters (scales to 4–6) · three sessions of 3–4 hours (optional fourth)**

> *Every Midwinter Night, Candlecrown sings seven verses to Telchur. This year, something has come down out of the cold to collect the seventh.*

## Date
**Needfest 1–5, 576 CY** (the wagons go down on Needfest 7). Midwinter Night is Needfest 4. Logged in [[Timeline]].

## Pitch
A mesa-top lantern-and-toy village in the Tors is snowed in for Needfest. Over three nights, voices vanish, tyrgs climb the cliffs, and a polite stranger keeps asking to be let in from the storm. The village's carol to Telchur hides a Suel curse-verse, and an immoth, an ice-giant hoarder of words, has come to take it. On Midwinter Night the party holds the workshop hall while the village sings: fight it with fire, trick it, or bargain with words worth more than the verse.

## Design Frame
- **Thesis:** what a community keeps alive by singing together can't be taken one voice at a time, unless it stops singing. The choir is the party's armor; every voice lost is a hole in it.
- **Expectation broken:** *Kill the monster at the siege.* The best ending isn't a kill: it's giving the hunter the one word that frees it. The monster is a prisoner of the same curse the village is guarding.
- **Second break:** the vampire at the gate looks like the villain and is a side problem the players can solve by doing nothing: he can't come in unless someone says yes. Saying no costs nothing but kindness, and he is the best source of help in the book.
- **What this adventure refuses to do:** it never writes down the seventh verse. Not in the book, not in a handout, not in the FGU module. And it never lets a PC's invitation count: only the village can let the dark in.
- **Session spine:** the storm's three stages (Gathering, Howling, the Long Night), the lantern line, and on Midwinter Night the **verse track** (seven verses, three waves, the seventh verse in seven lines). See [[SP-GS02 — The Lantern Line]].
- **Tone:** cozy with teeth. A real Needfest (toys to paint, goose to eat, a carol everyone knows by the second session) and a real threat. Comedy comes from the village; horror comes from silence.

## PC Objectives
| Objective | Routes |
|---|---|
| Find out what is taking the voices, and why | Corin's drawings; Mother Sedge; the chapel; Hrimvald's own words |
| Get the stolen voices back | Fire (melt the runes); bargain; destroy Hrimvald; the counter-verse; the Ice Library |
| Keep the seventh verse out of Hrimvald's hands | Hold the choir and the lantern line; fire on it each round; the singing room; the trick; the bargain |
| Deal with the stranger at the gate | Refuse him; bargain from outside; hunt him by day; let him in and watch him |
| Get the gift wagons down (hook 1) | Any ending but *Silenced*; or haul them up and down the Stair yourselves |
