# GS2 · The Long Night at Candlecrown — Outline

**Cover line:** APL 6 (levels 5–7) · 5 characters (scales to 4–6) · three sessions of 3–4 hours (an optional fourth)
**Code:** GS2 / SP-GS02 (Greyhawk Special; seasonal). **Year:** 576 CY, Needfest. **Rules:** 5e 2014.
**Frame:** standalone Greyhawk (no University frame). **Tone:** cozy with teeth: a real Needfest, a real threat.
**Cover rule:** the cover never shows the immoth. Show the lit village on its mesa in the snow, or the tyrgs on the switchback.

> Canon vs. homebrew: the Tors, the Yeomanry, Melkot, Needfest (also kept in places as the Feast of Telchur), Midwinter Night, Telchur and Fortubo are canon. Candlecrown, its people, the carol, the curse-verse and the vampire are homebrew. The immoth is the 3e *Monster Manual II* creature (pp. 127–128), converted. Tyrgs are the 2e MC5 / *Monstrous Manual* creature, converted.

---

## 1 · What the Locals Know

- **Candlecrown** is a hamlet of about 60 souls on the flat crown of a tor on the Yeomanry's southern edge, reached by one switchback path cut into the cliff (the Candle Stair).
- It began as a Suel mine head. The silver ran out generations ago. The village now lives by its **lantern-and-toy workshop**: carved lanterns, tin toys and painted Needfest figures, sent down to the valley farms every year on the first day of Needfest.
- Every **Midwinter Night** (Needfest 4, the Dim Night) the whole village sings the **Long Night Carol** in the workshop hall, a Yeomanry hymn to Telchur, the winter god, older than anyone's grandmother. Six verses are in Common; the seventh is in a tongue no one speaks. The lanterns stay lit until dawn. "No lantern dark, no verse unsung."
- This winter is wrong. The snow came early and won't stop; two carters' mules froze on the Stair; tyrg packs have come up out of the eastern Tors further than anyone remembers.

## 2 · What's Really Happening

- The carol's **seventh verse**, sung in old Suel, is a fragment of a curse. Long ago a mad sorcerer of the Suel Imperium bound a host of cold elementals with a curse whose words they must gather back to the Mountain of Ultimate Winter. Those elementals became the **immoths** (MM2 legend). The sorcerer's apprentice fled the Rain of Colorless Fire with the miners and hid one verse of the curse inside the local hymn to Telchur, in old Suel, where it would be sung, and so kept alive, but never written down.
- An immoth, **Hrimvald the Gatherer**, has finally heard the verse carried on a midwinter wind. It has come to the Tors. Its storm sits over the mesa; the tyrgs it drives before it are the cold's hunting dogs.
- Immoths hoard words. Hrimvald will **take the verse, and the voices that carry it**: a singer who meets it loses their voice, the words frozen into an ice rune on its body. If it gets the whole verse, it leaves, and Candlecrown never sings again (and perhaps never speaks).
- Hrimvald **bargains** before it fights. It would trade for words it values more, and it takes what it wants by force when bargaining fails (MM2).
- **The stranger at the gate.** **Aurel Vasch**, a vampire spawn whose master died in the Passage of Slerotin, has been sheltering in the old mine shafts. The storm and the immoth have driven him up. He will ask to be let in. He cannot cross the gate, a threshold or the workshop door uninvited. Invited in, he feeds through the night and makes a thrall of a singer.

## 4 · Facts the Players Must Learn

Each has three routes plus a last resort.

1. **The immoth wants the seventh verse, not the village.**
   (a) The mute shepherd writes or draws what he saw. (b) Old Mother Sedge remembers her grandmother's warning: "never write the seventh verse." (c) The apprentice's warning carved in the old mine chapel. **Last resort:** Hrimvald says so itself when it first speaks.
2. **Fire is its weakness; edged weapons barely bite.**
   (a) Ice runes on the Stair melt under a lantern. (b) A tyrg-chewed immoth trophy from an earlier hunter in the mine. (c) Arcana/Nature checks on its first appearance. **Last resort:** the workshop's lantern oil kills the first rune-guardian in one splash.
3. **It can be bargained with.**
   (a) It opens with an offer. (b) The mine-chapel carving records "it took the words and went". (c) The vampire, if questioned, knows immoths from the Passage. **Last resort:** it offers again when bloodied.
4. **The stranger must be invited.**
   (a) Mother Sedge's ward-lore. (b) He never steps over the gate line, even when urged by gesture. (c) He casts no shadow on the lantern-lit snow. **Last resort:** a villager's child says, "He's waiting for someone to say yes."
5. **The old mine runs under the village** and opens onto the cliffs. It is a second way in, and where the answers are.
   (a) The miners' map in the workshop office. (b) The goat tracks that go into the coal store and never come out. (c) The draft under the workshop floor. **Last resort:** a tyrg wave comes up out of it.

## 5 · Adventure Outline (under 100 words)

The party reaches Candlecrown as Needfest begins and a storm that won't break closes the Candle Stair. Over three nights, voices vanish, tyrgs climb the cliffs and a stranger asks to come in from the cold. The party learns the carol's seventh verse is the prize of an immoth, an ice-giant hoarder of words, and that the old Suel mine beneath the village holds the truth. On Midwinter Night they hold the workshop hall while the village sings: fight the immoth with fire, trick it, or bargain with words worth more than the verse.

## 6 · Session Plan

### Session 1 — The Candle Stair (Needfest 1–2)
- **Start:** hooks (below); the climb up the Candle Stair in rising snow. **Fight:** tyrg pack (4) on the switchbacks, edge and footing rules.
- **Home base:** Candlecrown in play order: the gate, the workshop, the hall, the shrine, homes. Needfest texture: gift wrapping, painting toys, the first feast.
- **Mystery seeds:** the ice runes on the Stair; the mute shepherd (Needfest 2); the goat tracks into the coal store.
- **Ends on:** a voice is taken at the eastern edge in the night; the wind seems to be singing.

### Session 2 — The Old Workings (Needfest 3)
- **The mine under the mesa:** a short site (8–12 keyed areas). The Suel mine head, the apprentice's chapel and carving, the lower shafts where Aurel shelters, an ice-rune guardian, and the cliff exit.
- **First meeting with Aurel**, in the dark, on his ground. He is polite and cold, and asks for nothing yet.
- **Dusk, wave 1:** tyrgs come up the Stair; Hrimvald watches from the cliff and speaks its first offer.
- **Night:** rehearsal; a singer loses her voice mid-verse. **The knock at the gate** (the avoidable problem).
- **Ends on:** the village decides to sing on Midwinter Night anyway. The party has one day to prepare.

### Session 3 — Midwinter Night (Needfest 4)
- **Preparation phase:** a short, structured day. The party fortifies: lantern lines, fire traps, the bonfire, barring the mine, placing singers, arming villagers. Each choice changes the siege (written as "if X, then Y").
- **The siege:** waves on a round/time track. Wave 1: tyrgs on the Stair. Wave 2: tyrgs over the mine exit. Wave 3: the tyrg alpha and her pack at the gate. Climax: Hrimvald comes over the cliffs at the seventh verse.
- **The climax** (at least 15% of the page weight): fight it, trick it (a false verse, a written verse it can't resist), or bargain (words it values more: a true name, a secret, a player character's confession, the apprentice's own counter-verse).
- **Dawn:** endings (below).

### Optional Session 4 — The Ice Library
If Hrimvald escapes with voices, it flees up the cliffs to a cave-library in the high Tors. Recover the stolen voices (ice runes) before the thaw shatters them. A short dungeon and a rematch on its ground.

## 7 · Hooks (3–5, one personal, plus a decline path)

1. **The gift wagons.** A Loftwick toy-seller paid in advance for Candlecrown's lanterns. The wagons never came down. Fetch them, or find out why.
2. **Shelter.** The party is on the valley road when the storm hits. Candlecrown's lights are the only ones in sight.
3. **The carol.** A sage, or a cleric of Telchur, wants a copy of "the Telchur carol with the verse in a dead tongue." The village won't let it be written.
4. **Personal.** A character's kin sang in the carol as a child, and has just written: "Come for Needfest. Something's wrong with the snow."
5. **Decline path:** the wagons never come; in spring, the Yeomanry hears that Candlecrown has gone silent. The village is still there, mute.

## 8 · Running the Adventure

- **Laws of the mesa:** the storm (visibility, cold exposure), the cliff edges (falls), the Candle Stair (one path; difficult terrain; shove-off-the-edge rules), fire (lanterns as weapons and wards).
- **The lantern line** (the cozy mechanic): lit lanterns around the mesa's edge slow the tyrgs and show the immoth's approach. Every wave tries to put them out.
- **Alert states:** Hrimvald's storm rises over three nights (Gathering, Howling, the Long Night), each with written effects.
- **Nudge list:** three steps for the five facts.
- **Safety valve:** Mother Sedge's shrine is a sanctuary the tyrgs won't enter.

## 11 · Endings

- **Full:** the verse is safe (bargained away for something better, or Hrimvald destroyed), no voices lost, the lanterns go down to the valley on Needfest 7. The village names a Needfest figure after the party.
- **Partial:** Hrimvald is driven off with some voices. The carol is sung without them; Session 4 is the way to get them back.
- **Retreat or defeat:** the verse is taken; Candlecrown goes silent. In spring the immoth's library is a known place in the high Tors.
- **The vampire:** destroyed, driven off, or (if a bargain is struck) let go into the storm with a debt owed.

**Continuing the Adventure:** the counter-verse in the apprentice's chapel points to other hidden verses across the Sheldomar Valley; the immoths' Mountain of Ultimate Winter; Melkot and the Sons of Marchanter want the verse too; Aurel's master's grave in the Passage of Slerotin (a link to C12 and Bleak Gate).

## Decisions (2026-11-21)

- Title and village: **The Long Night at Candlecrown** (kept).
- Aurel keeps the link to the Passage of Slerotin and C12's Bleak Gate.
- The carol honors **Telchur**, the Oeridian winter god (Needfest is the Feast of Telchur in places); the curse-verse is the Suel apprentice's addition. Fortubo stays with the miners.
- Balanced for **5 characters**, scaling to 4–6.
- Immoth at **full strength** (about CR 9); **at least three sessions**.
