# Scene 02 — Terms

> [!art]+ Scene art (screen)
> ![[SP-GQ01 — S02 Terms.png|600]]

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GQ01 — The Gaoler's Link/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 01 — Brewfest on the Landing]] · Next → [[Scene 03 — Legwork]]

## Purpose
Strike the deal or refuse it. Hand over Handout 1. Make sure someone at the table remembers the Scholar's Oath.
**Budget:** 10 minutes. Running long? They take the first number.

## Arrival
> *Brandmeier sets a leather purse on the table and doesn't open it. Fesch turns his ledger to a fresh page and uncaps his ink. Across the room, the Job Board's paper rustles every time the door opens.*

**To the table:** *"He's waiting for an answer. Does anyone ask a question first?"*

## Who Is Here
As Scene 01. [[Karet]], the Fiend's Rest's keeper, is wiping the same mug for the fourth time and listening.

## Beats

### The offer
- **Pay:** **25 marks each**, half now (12 marks and 5 kleinmarks in the hand). The mark is the gold piece, and a laborer earns 3 kleinmarks a day, so this is the better part of a year's wages for one night's work: generous enough to tempt, and far too generous for a careful student not to ask why. **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)** raises it to 30 marks, after a nod from Fesch.
- **The hint:** Brandmeier "knows a Schwartzenbruin house that hires University graduates." (True. It is his own. He doesn't remember that he said it.)
- **The job as he describes it:** the coffer, the sealed stacks behind the grille in the Archive, the handover on the quay at the foot of the *Hecht*'s gangplank, before dawn on Brewfest 4.

### Proof
If asked for proof, Brandmeier produces a **deed of pledge** ([[SP-GQ01 — Handout — The Deed of Pledge]]). It is a good forgery. **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation):** the ink is under a year old, and the University's arms (book, cudgel and quill) are drawn, not pressed.

### The Oath
If no player mentions it, ask a student character what the Scholar's Oath says about this. *"I shall not steal manuscripts…"* Taking University property is a standing offense ("selling research property"). Brandmeier waves it away: *"It isn't the University's. That's the whole point."* Fesch adds, quietly: *"Nothing is being sold. Something is being returned."*

### Refusal
Brandmeier takes it graciously: *"Then drink the ale, and no hard feelings."* Fesch does not, and watches them leave. Go to the decline path in [[SP-GQ01-The-Gaolers-Link#Hook]]: [[Konstanz Weil]] and [[Rudi Weil]] take the job.

Told with **any one of F1–F4**, the Field Station acts: go to the **Confession** approach in [[Scene 07 — Turning the Job]], and run Scene 05 as a **sting** (Vogelsang lets the "theft" happen while Garrok waits at the Landing).
- **The party asks to see the clerk's hands** → Fesch keeps his left hand gloved. The burn scar is under it (Scene 06).

## Alternates

- **A PC goes straight to the Proctor** → Grell takes a statement and asks for proof: *"Bring me one fact I can put in a ledger by the dinner, and I'll act on it."* He isn't ignoring them; he needs evidence. Run Scene 03 as evidence-gathering, and switch to the sting as soon as they bring him a fact.
