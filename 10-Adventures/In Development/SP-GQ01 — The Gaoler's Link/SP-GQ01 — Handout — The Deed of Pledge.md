# Handout — The Deed of Pledge

Back to [[SP-GQ01-The-Gaolers-Link]] · Given in [[Scene 02 — Terms]]

## Player Text
![[SP-GQ01 Handout - The Deed of Pledge.png|420]]

*(Good rag paper, folded in four. Brown ink. At the foot, a seal drawn in ink: an open book above a crossed cudgel and quill.)*

> **Be it known** that I, **Ulrich Brandmeier** of Schwartzenbruin, merchant, do pledge to the holder of this deed one **reliquary of iron, black, of the old work**, sealed in a coffer of iron with a seal of grey wax,
> as surety for a loan of **forty marks** at Ritterstadt in the year **565** of the Common Reckoning,
> the said reliquary to be returned to me or my heirs **upon the repayment of the said sum** with its lawful increase.
>
> Witnessed at Ritterstadt in the presence of the **Greyhawk University**,
> the third day of Richfest, 565 CY.
>
> *U. Brandmeier*
> *[the University's arms, drawn]*

## DM Notes
- **The forgery** (Vesharan's work, made last spring):
  - **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation):** the ink is less than a year old and still smells of gall.
  - The University's arms are **drawn**, not pressed. A real Field Station seal leaves an impression in the paper.
- **The date** gives it away too. The Field Station's charter is from **568**. In 565 there was no University in Ritterstadt to witness anything (DC 10 History, or automatic for a student who has read the charter in the Station House hall). **(F1 route.)**
- **Ulrich Brandmeier** was Ottokar's grandfather. He was real, and he never came within a hundred miles of Ritterstadt. Margarethe Brandmeier would say so.
- Vesharan built the story on the Link's real coffer and seal, from the manifest. The details are right, and the provenance is invented.
