# The Gluttonwretch of Hommlet — Front Matter

**APL 12 (levels 11–13) · 5–7 characters (written for 7) · one 3½-hour session** · Setting: Greyhawk, Brewfest 576 CY · Region: Hommlet, Viscounty of Verbobonc

## Introduction
This is a one-session Brewfest adventure for high-level heroes, set in the village of Hommlet seven years after the Battle of Emridy Meadows. Read the Background first; everything else follows from it. Run Act 1 in Hommlet, let the party choose its paths in Act 2, and keep the feast bell at dusk on Day 2 no matter what.

*Read-aloud text appears in italic blocks. Read it as written or paraphrase it, but keep every clue.*

**Conventions.** **Bold** names mark a creature or NPC's first appearance in a scene. Counts in parentheses, such as Gorgers (2), are the scaling dial. Statblocks live in [[SP-GS01 — Bestiary]]; prose names the block. Every damage figure carries its dice. DCs appear inline.

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- Brewfest is tomorrow night, and the Welcome Wench is putting on the biggest feast since the Temple fell.
- "Brewfest fever" has been going round. Three people this week started eating things that aren't food. Gundigoot says it's seasonal.
- Something gobbles in the Gnarley at dusk. The dogs won't go out.
- Jaroo Ashstaff has stopped coming into the village. He and the Elder aren't speaking.
- Nobody has seen Old Gobbler, the grove's great turkey, since last winter. People change the subject.

### What's Really Happening
For three generations a giant wild turkey called **Old Gobbler** has lived in Jaroo Ashstaff's Old Faith grove, holy to Obad-Hai. Every Brewfest, each household carries the **first fruits** of its harvest to the grove — the first sheaf cut, the first berries picked, the first pour from the new cask — and Old Gobbler eats first. The harvest is blessed.

The fields are still sour seven years after the Temple's blight. In 575 CY the village council, led by the Elder, voted to keep the offering in their own cellars "just this once." Jaroo was outvoted. Old Gobbler wandered the Gnarley edge looking for a feast that never came, and starved over the winter. The hoarded 575 offering rotted, uneaten, in the Elder's cellar.

**Deacon Mungo Brindlewick**, a surviving priest of the Temple who serves the imprisoned demon queen Zuggtmoy, found the carcass in the spring. In the cellar of a burned Temple-era rendering house, **the Smokehouse**, he raised it with a rite to the Lady of Rot and bound it with its own wishbone. Every soul it gorges feeds Zuggtmoy's rot. The traders **Rannos Davl** and **Gremag** supply him.

The **Gluttonwretch** still wants what Old Gobbler always wanted: to be fed first, by Hommlet, freely. Its wet Sylvan gobbling is one sentence over and over: *"You did not feed me."* Six offerings of first fruits, freely given by the village, will lay it to rest.

### The Villain's Scheme
1. **Spring 576 CY.** Brindlewick raises the Wretch at the Smokehouse and binds it with the Wishbone.
2. **Harvester.** He lets it roost on Emridy Meadows and feed on stragglers, testing the binding.
3. **Brewfest week, Day 1.** The Wretch's hunger reaches into Hommlet. Villagers start gorging (Pip Haddle is the fourth).
4. **Day 2, noon.** Rannos and Gremag deliver forty barrels of "cranberry preserves" to the feast green. The barrels are full of Smokehouse rot.
5. **Day 2, dusk.** At the Naming of Thanks, Brindlewick calls the Wretch through the feast table. With nobody to stop it, it gorges the village, and two hundred hungry souls crack one of Zuggtmoy's seals beneath the Temple.

## Hooks
- **Patron:** [[Canon Terjon]] invites the heroes to bless the Brewfest table. *(Public motive: famous guests draw donations for the church's rebuilding. Private motive: he knows about the gorgings and wants someone who can fight in the room.)* Terms: board at the Wench, a seat at the head of the table, and the church's thanks.
- **Personal:** a PC was among the Righteous Host at Emridy Meadows in 569 CY, or fought beside someone who is buried there. Jaroo's letter reaches them by name ([[SP-GS01 — Handout — Jaroo's Letter]]).
- **Rumor:** at any inn on the Verbobonc road: "Hommlet's throwing the biggest Brewfest since the Temple fell. Free ale for heroes. Mind the fever."
- **The Old Faith:** a druid or ranger PC feels the grove's grief as a wrongness in the season, and Jaroo's letter finds them.
- **If the party refuses:** Terjon fights alone at the feast. The Wretch gorges thirty villagers before Jaroo lays it to rest with the few offerings he can scrape together, and he dies doing it. The village survives, smaller and ashamed; the Rotting Table goes on in the dark.

## Running the Adventure

### Laws of the Place
- **The hunger.** From Day 1, anyone in Hommlet who ate food from the 575 harvest risks gorging (see [[Scene 01 — Brewfest Eve]]). *Lesser restoration* or a DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check ends a single gorging; the curse ends only when the Wretch does.
- **First fruits.** Only the first portion of a household's own 576 harvest counts, freely given by that household. Bought food, the heroes' food and last year's food do not count.
- **The Wishbone.** While the Wishbone is unbroken, the Wretch cannot be turned, banished or held by any effect that would keep it from the feast. It always comes.
- **Lightning in the grove.** Jaroo's druids never called the storm in the grove. The Offering-Heap feeds on lightning.

### Factions
- **Hommlet** — wants the feast to go ahead and the fever to be a fever; hopes the heroes fix it without anyone having to say what they did.
- **The Old Faith (Jaroo)** — wants Old Gobbler at rest and the village to own its sin; spies on nobody, but the grove's birds see everything.
- **The Rotting Table (Brindlewick, Rannos, Gremag)** — wants the village gorged at the feast; hopes for promotion in Zuggtmoy's eyes. Rannos and Gremag spy on the Wench.

### Nudge List (if play stalls)
1. **A rumor surfaces:** Widow Bree, crying into her honey cakes, says Old Gobbler "would have had the first of these."
2. **An NPC suggests:** Wat Thistlewood bursts into the Wench and won't stop talking about the singing in the Smokehouse.
3. **A flash of insight:** the next villager to gorge whispers *"I'm sorry, I'm sorry, we didn't feed him"* in Sylvan they never learned.

### Safety Valve
**Canon Terjon** casts *spirit guardians* once at the feast, and **Jaroo** can spend his own life to complete the rite with fewer than six Offerings (four, if the party is losing badly). If he does, he dies, and the ending is B, not A.

## Concluding the Adventure

### Endings
- **A — The First Fruits (full success).** Six Offerings are made. The Wretch eats, lies down and is laid to rest. The 577 harvest is blessed, Jaroo is a lifelong ally, and the village never skips Brewfest again.
- **B — The Carving or the Wishbone (partial success).** The Wretch is destroyed by force or by snapping the bone. The village survives. If the Wishbone was snapped, Obad-Hai's blessing leaves the grove: the 577 harvest fails and Jaroo never forgives it.
- **C — The Feast Lost (retreat or defeat).** The Wretch gorges the village. Hommlet loses half its households, and a seal cracks under the Temple. The survivors are not grateful.

### Aftermath and Rewards
- **Ending A:** the [[Feather of the First Fruits]]; Jaroo's friendship; free board at the Wench for life.
- **Ending B:** [[Brindlewick's Cipher Book]] and the Deacon's hoard (a *spell scroll* of a 5th-level spell); with the Wishbone snapped, its larger half gives one automatic natural 20 before dawn.
- **All endings:** the village knows who stood at its table. Canon Terjon writes to Verbobonc naming the party.

### Continuing the Adventure
- **The second table.** Brindlewick's cipher book names "the second table" in the Temple's lower halls, laid for a guest who has not yet arrived.
- **What Rannos and Gremag do next:** if not caught, they run for Nulb and the Temple, and the next Temple raid is expected.
- **What happens to Hommlet:** in Ending A it prospers and keeps the Open Door every Brewfest. In Ending C it becomes a village that does not trust strangers.

### Previous Play
- **If the group has played T1 or a Hommlet campaign:** use the village as they left it. If Rannos and Gremag are already exposed, their place in the scheme goes to a new pair of Temple agents running a pie stall.
- **If the Moathouse is active or cleared in your campaign:** nothing here touches it.
