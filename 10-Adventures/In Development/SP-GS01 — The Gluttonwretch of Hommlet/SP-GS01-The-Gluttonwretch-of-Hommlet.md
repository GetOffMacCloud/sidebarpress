# The Gluttonwretch of Hommlet

**APL 12 (levels 11–13) · 5–7 characters (written for 7) · one 3½-hour session**

> *Hommlet forgot to feed someone last year. He has come back for dinner.*
>
> A Brewfest one-shot for **average party level 12**, written for seven characters and built for a long evening at the table or a 3½-hour stream. The heroes come to [[Hommlet]] as guests of honor at its harvest feast and find the village quietly eating its spoons. Last year the village kept back the first fruits it owed to the Old Faith, and the sacred turkey of [[Jaroo Ashstaff]]'s grove starved. A surviving priest of the Temple of Elemental Evil raised the carcass as the **Gluttonwretch**, and tomorrow night it comes to the feast. The party can lay it to rest with a feast freely given, break the cult's binding at a terrible price, or carve it twice. It is funny until somebody eats their own fingers, and it ends with the whole village around one ruined table, giving thanks.

## Adventure Code
`SP-GS01` — Greyhawk **Special** (seasonal), first of the GS line. Printed as **GS1**. Never reuse codes, even for scrapped adventures.

## Date
**Brewfest 3–4, 576 CY.** Brewfest is the 7-day festival between Harvester and Patchwall (see [[The-Common-Year-Calendar]]). The party arrives on Brewfest 3; the feast is the night of **Brewfest 4**, when the small moon Celene is full.

## Design Frame
- **Thesis:** Gratitude is something you *do*. The village's sin was taking without giving; the cure is giving when you have little.
- **Expectation broken:** *Kill the monster at the feast.* Killing it is the hardest and least happy ending. The monster is the guest the village forgot.
- **Second break:** the funniest thing in the adventure (a giant undead turkey that critiques the cranberry relish) is also the saddest (it only ever wanted to be fed first).
- **What this adventure refuses to do:** It never lets the heroes pay Hommlet's debt for it. Player characters can stand in for at most half the offering. The village has to give.
- **Session spine:** the **First Fruits** tracker (six Offerings lay the Wretch to rest) and the **feast bell** (a hard clock: the feast starts at dusk on Day 2, whatever the party has done).
- **Tone:** a harvest supper painted by a cartoonist with a grudge. About a third comedy. Horror and heart land at the moments listed in the [[SP-GS01 — Comedy Kit]], and three scenes are comedy-free.
