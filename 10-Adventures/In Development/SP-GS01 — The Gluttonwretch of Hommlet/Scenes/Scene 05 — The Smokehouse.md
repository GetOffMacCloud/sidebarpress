# Scene 05 — The Smokehouse

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS01 — The Gluttonwretch of Hommlet/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 04 — The Grove and the Offering-Heap]] · Next → [[Scene 06 — The Hunt on Emridy Meadows]]

## Purpose
The Wishbone path. The party raids Brindlewick's chapel to Zuggtmoy in the old rendering house, takes or smashes the Wishbone, and deals with the Deacon before he can bring it to the feast.
**Budget:** about 30 minutes of Act 2's shared 65. Running long? Skip the upper floor and open in the cellar chapel (S5).

## Recent Events
Before the battle of 569 CY, the Temple collected forced tithes of meat and grain at a rendering house on the Gnarley edge, 2 miles up the High Road from Hommlet. The Righteous Host burned it on their way to the Meadows. In the spring of 576, **[[Deacon Mungo Brindlewick]]** moved into its cellar, raised Old Gobbler there, and has been holding services ever since. Three days ago Wat Thistlewood's pigs walked in. They have converted.

## Laws of the Place
- **The rot.** The cellar (S4–S6) is thick with Zuggtmoy's spores. A creature that ends its turn there without holding its breath makes a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or is poisoned until the end of its next turn.
- **The Wishbone** can't be cut, burned or smashed. Like any wishbone, it breaks only when two creatures, each holding one end, pull against each other (an action each and a contested Strength check). Breaking it here has the same cost as breaking it at the feast (see [[Scene 07 — The Brewfest Feast]]).

## Alert States
| State | Trigger | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Lax** (default) | — | Brindlewick is saying grace in S5. The Brothers are cooking in S2. The elementals sleep in the walls. |
| **Wary** | The traders' clerk arrived (Scene 02); or noise in S1–S2 | The Brothers wait at the top of the stairs (S3). Brindlewick takes the Wishbone down off its hooks and holds it. |
| **Alert** | Combat anywhere; or the party is seen | The earth elementals step out of the cellar walls at the start of round 2. Brindlewick casts *greater invisibility* and tries to leave with the Wishbone by the coal chute (S6). |

**Survivors flee to the feast.** If Brindlewick escapes with the Wishbone, he goes straight to the feast green and hides among the crowd until the Naming of Thanks. He will not run anywhere else; the Wretch is his whole career.

## Keyed Areas

**S1. THE YARD**
> *A burned timber barn leans over a muddy yard. The doors hang open on one hinge. A pigpen by the wall stands empty, its gate unlatched. Deep cart ruts run in from the north road and up to the doors. Somewhere inside, voices are singing slightly different words to the same tune.*

The ruts come from Emridy Meadows (a DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) check follows them north). The singing is the Brothers in S2 practicing grace to the tune of Hommlet's Brewfest hymn.
**Development:** walking in through the doors puts the site at **Wary**.

**S2. THE SMOKING HALL**
> *Iron hooks hang from blackened rafters over a long brick firepit. Smoke drifts up through holes in the roof. Two big men in stained bibs stand at the fire, turning a spit. A slate on the wall lists forty barrels, crossed off one by one.*

**Creatures:** **Brothers of the Rotting Table** (2), gourmands in bibs with cleavers; use the **Gladiator** statblock. They are cooking lunch and arguing over the second verse.
**Tactics:** they fight at the stairhead (S3) and shout warnings down. Whenever one fails a saving throw, he bursts into the wrong verse of the hymn and the other corrects him.
**Treasure:** the slate is a delivery list: forty barrels "to the green, Brewfest noon."
**Development:** combat here puts the site at **Alert**.

**S3. STAIRHEAD AND RENDERING VATS.** Three iron vats of gravy-dark ichor and a stone stair down. Nothing of value; the ichor is Wretch-leakings Brindlewick bottles for the "preserves."

**S4. THE CELLAR STAIRS.** The spores begin here (see Laws).

**S5. CHAPEL OF THE ROTTING TABLE**
> *A long cellar opens out below, lit by candles stuck into rotten gourds. Six pigs sit upright in two rows of pews, facing a table black with mold. Above the table, a wishbone as tall as a man hangs from chains, bound in sinew. A man in brewer's leathers stands before it with his arms raised. The pigs turn to look at you.*

**Creatures:** **[[Deacon Mungo Brindlewick]]** (1); use the **Mage** statblock with the reskin in [[SP-GS01 — Bestiary]]. **Earth elementals** (2), the old foundation stones, asleep in the cellar walls until **Alert**. **Wat's pigs** (6): Mabel, Little Ham, Sir Trotsworth, Petunia, the Reverend and Big Unpleasant Gerald, well fed on mushrooms and devout.
**Brindlewick, at first sight:**
> "Guests! Before the feast, even. You'll want to say grace. Everyone says grace here." *(He smiles.)* "She hears it, you know. The Lady. Every mouthful."
**Tactics:** at **Wary** or **Alert**, he holds the Wishbone and casts from behind the pews. When reduced below half his hit points, or when the elementals fall, he goes for the coal chute in S6 with the Wishbone.
**The pigs:** a loud noise, or Wat whistling if he came along, starts a **pig stampede** through the pews. Run it like a Gravy Flood that only hits the cultists: each Brother or Brindlewick in the aisle makes a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or is knocked prone and takes 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage.
**Treasure:** the Wishbone (see Laws). *Detect magic* shows necromancy and enchantment; a DC 17 Intelligence (Religion) check tells a PC that the bone holds the bird's spirit as surely as it holds the leash.
**Development:** if Brindlewick is captured, he bargains with nothing; he wants to see the feast. If he dies, the Wretch still comes to the feast (the binding outlives him) but no longer has a handler.

**S6. BRINDLEWICK'S CELL AND THE COAL CHUTE**
> *A narrow room off the chapel holds a cot, a lectern and a shelf of jars. A thick book lies open on the lectern, weighted with a ladle. A sloping coal chute in the corner leads up to daylight.*

**Treasure:** [[Brindlewick's Cipher Book]] (the full cipher; mostly recipes, partly not). Its last pages hold the rite that raised the Wretch, including the line *"the bone keeps both the beast and the bird; break it and keep neither."* A *spell scroll* of a 5th-level spell (DM's choice; *cloudkill* suits the Deacon) is tucked in the back cover. The jars are pickled mushrooms, very good.
**Development:** the chute is Brindlewick's escape route. A PC standing on it stops him.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** pigs in pews, candles in gourds, a wishbone as tall as a man.
- **Sound:** two slightly different hymns at once; Big Unpleasant Gerald grunting along.
- **Smell:** smoke, gravy, a mushroom cellar.
- **Touch:** sticky floors; spores that settle on the tongue.
- **Taste:** mushrooms. Everything tastes of mushrooms.

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party comes at Alert:** Brindlewick is already gone up the chute with the Wishbone; they find the Brothers, the elementals and the cipher book.
- **If the party snaps the Wishbone here:** apply the Snap and the cost from [[Scene 07 — The Brewfest Feast]] now. The Wretch comes to the feast weakened and unbound.
- **If Wat is with them:** he will not leave without all six pigs.
