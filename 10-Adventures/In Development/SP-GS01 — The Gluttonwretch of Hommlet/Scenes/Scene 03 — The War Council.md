# Scene 03 — The War Council

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS01 — The Gluttonwretch of Hommlet/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — What Hommlet Isn't Saying]] · Next → [[Scene 04 — The Grove and the Offering-Heap]]

## Purpose
Jaroo, Wat and Terjon each argue for one path, and the party sets its plan for the one day left. The players leave knowing the clock, the three options in story terms, and the price of the Wishbone.
**Budget:** 10 minutes. Running long? Give each NPC one line and let the players pick.

## Arrival
> *The Welcome Wench's common room has been cleared for the feast, and the benches are stacked against the walls. Three people stand around the one table left: a druid in a bark-brown cloak, a priest with a cudgel on his belt, and a swineherd with straw in his hair. A map of the village has been drawn on the tabletop in spilled ale. The front door stands wide open behind them.*

**To the table:** *"Three people, three plans, one day. Who are you listening to?"*

## Beats

**1. Jaroo speaks first** (comedy-free). **[[Jaroo Ashstaff]]** has come down from the grove for the first time in a year. He doesn't look at the Elder, if the Elder is present.
> "It's Old Gobbler. I fed him from my hand for forty years. Your council fed him a vote." *(He breathes.)* "He can still be laid to rest. Six offerings of first fruits, this year's, given by the households that owe them. Given freely. Not bought, not taken, not given by you. Win me six households and I'll finish it at the feast."

**2. Wat speaks over everyone.** **[[Wat Thistlewood]]** has been waiting to tell this since dawn.
> "Mabel, Little Ham, Sir Trotsworth, Petunia, the Reverend and Big Unpleasant Gerald. All six. Walked off into the Gnarley in a line, singing. Well, squealing in tune. I followed them to the old Smokehouse up the High Road, and there's a man in there in brewer's leathers saying grace to a bone. A wishbone, big as me, hung over a table gone black with rot. And my pigs are sitting in the pews."

**3. Terjon speaks last.** **[[Canon Terjon]]** taps the ale-map where the Gnarley meets the north road.
> "Drovers have seen it roosting on Emridy Meadows, where we fought in sixty-nine. Wound it now and it comes to the feast weaker. Saint Cuthbert approves of that sort of arithmetic."

**4. The Wishbone warning.** If anyone suggests breaking the bone, Jaroo goes still.
> "Break the bone and the priest loses his leash. So does the bird. You'd be killing him twice. There'd be nothing left of him to lay down, and nothing left of the grove's blessing either."

**5. The plan.** The players decide. Say the clock out loud: the feast bell rings at dusk, everyone is pulled back to the green, and whatever isn't done by then stays undone.

## DM Note: Presenting the Three Paths
Don't announce "three paths." The NPCs above do the work; each promises something in story terms (rest for the bird, control of the bird, a weaker bird). Never state the mechanics.
- **Splitting the party.** Seven players split well into 3+2+2. Cut between groups on cliffhangers, never mid-turn (see [[Scene 04 — The Grove and the Offering-Heap|S04]]–[[Scene 06 — The Hunt on Emridy Meadows|S06]]).
- **On stream.** Post a three-line summary in Fantasy Grounds chat so viewers can follow:
  - *Grove — win six households' first fruits (Jaroo).*
  - *Smokehouse — take the wishbone from the cult (Wat).*
  - *Meadows — hurt the Wretch before the feast (Terjon).*
- **Skipped paths still move.** Skip the Grove and no household is won in advance, though Offerings can still be won during the feast. Skip the Smokehouse and Brindlewick brings the Wishbone to the feast. Skip the Hunt and the Wretch arrives at full health.

## Clues Available Here
- Jaroo → **six offerings, freely given, will lay it to rest** (Clue Ledger).
- Wat → **a Temple priest binds it with a wishbone at the Smokehouse** (Clue Ledger).
- Jaroo's warning → **snapping the Wishbone destroys Old Gobbler's spirit** (Clue Ledger).

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** an ale-drawn map, straw on Wat's collar, Jaroo's knuckles white on his staff.
- **Sound:** bunting snapping outside, Wat naming pigs, a fiddler practicing the Brewfest hymn badly.
- **Smell:** stale ale, pig, woodsmoke through the open door.

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party already knows everything:** run this as a two-minute planning huddle and move on.
- **If Jaroo is never met:** he comes to the council anyway. He has been watching the party since the crow.
- **If Wat is ignored:** he follows the party around until someone listens.
