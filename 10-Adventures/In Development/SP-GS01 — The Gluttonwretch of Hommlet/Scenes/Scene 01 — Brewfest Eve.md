# Scene 01 — Brewfest Eve

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS01 — The Gluttonwretch of Hommlet/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[SP-GS01-The-Gluttonwretch-of-Hommlet]] · Next → [[Scene 02 — What Hommlet Isn't Saying]]

## Purpose
Show the hunger in action, plant the Open Door, and get Jaroo's letter into a PC's hand. The party should leave this scene knowing that something is wrong in Hommlet and that the village would rather not say what.
**Budget:** 15 minutes. Running long? Skip the tour of the green and go straight to Pip in the kitchen.

## Arrival
> *Woodsmoke and cider hang over Hommlet's green. Paper lanterns sway between the eaves of the Welcome Wench, and a goat wearing a wreath of dried corn is eating the wreath. Long trestle tables stand on the grass beneath a banner that reads WELCOM, HEROES. Beyond the palisade the fields are thin and patchy. The inn's front door is propped wide open with a butter churn.*

**To the table:** *"You're the guests of honor at the biggest feast this village has thrown in seven years. What does your character do when a whole village is staring at them?"*

## Beats

**1. The welcome.** **[[Ostler Gundigoot]]** comes down the steps with a barrel on his shoulder and a grin that doesn't reach his eyes.
> "Heroes! In Hommlet! At Brewfest! First pour's yours, second's for anyone who helps me carry the third. Mind the goat. She's called Pudding, and she has no friends."

**[[Pudding]]** follows the party everywhere from now on. She eats whatever they set down.

**2. The Open Door.** **Goodwife Gundigoot** is wedging the door wider with the churn. If anyone asks, she explains without stopping work:
> "Every Brewfest since we opened, this door doesn't shut. Ostler walked into Hommlet with nothing after the battle, and a family he'd never met fed him. So anyone who walks in, gets fed. Nobody's ever taken us up on it but drunks and tinkers. That's not the point."

Plant it now so it lands later: in [[Scene 07 — The Brewfest Feast]], the Wretch comes in through this door.

**3. The patron.** **[[Canon Terjon]]** finds the party within the hour. He is warm, tired and a little too relieved to see them.
> "You'll bless the table tomorrow at the Naming of Thanks. Short and plain, mind. Saint Cuthbert prefers plain speech." *(Quieter.)* "And keep your weapons close at supper. I'd rather not say why in front of the children."

A DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check shows he is frightened. Pressed, he admits only that "the fever" has taken four people this week and that he does not think it is a fever.

**4. Jaroo's letter.** A crow lands on the shoulder of the PC with the Old Faith or Emridy Meadows hook (or the party's druid or ranger) and drops a strip of birch bark: [[SP-GS01 — Handout — Jaroo's Letter]]. The crow waits for an answer. It will lead anyone to [[Jaroo's Grove]] tonight or tomorrow.

**5. The first gorging.** At dusk a gobble rolls in from the treeline, wet and deep, felt more in the teeth than heard. Every dog in Hommlet starts howling. A crash comes from the inn's kitchen.

> *The kitchen is hot and close and smells of onions. Copper pots hang from the beams, and one lies on the floor in a spreading puddle of gravy. A boy of about twelve kneels beside it. He is chewing a wooden spoon, and the handle is half gone.*

The boy is **[[Pip Haddle]]**, the scullery boy. He is in the second stage of the hunger (see below).
> "I'm not hungry," Pip whispers through splinters, crying. "I'm not. I'm not. I'm *so* hungry."

- *Lesser restoration*, any greater healing magic, or a DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check to hold him and get water into him ends the gorging. Pip sleeps for an hour and wakes ashamed and starving for ordinary food.
- **Gundigoot** goes grey: "That's the fourth this week. It's a fever. Brewfest fever. Very… seasonal."
- **Pip**, if asked what he ate today, says honestly: "Just the jam. The Elder sent over a crock of last year's jam. Said it was a shame to waste it." *(That jam is from the hoarded 575 offering; see the Clue below.)*

## The Hunger (laws of the place)
From Day 1, anyone in Hommlet who has eaten food from the 575 harvest can gorge. The Wretch's hunger reaches them across miles. It escalates in three stages:

| Stage | What they eat | Tell | Cure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Peckish** | Bread crusts, raw dough, unshucked corn, apple cores and stems, candle tallow | Cheerful, apologetic, can't stop | Talking them down: DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine) or Charisma (Persuasion) |
| **Gorging** | Wooden spoons and bowls, harness leather, seed corn meant for spring, chalk, hymnal pages, bark | Crying while they chew | *Lesser restoration* or DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) |
| **Hollow** | Table legs, their own sleeves and hair, fingernails, then their fingers | Silent and smiling | None. They rise as Hollow Guests at the feast. |

A cure ends one gorging. The hunger itself ends only when the Wretch does.

## Clues Available Here
- Pip ate jam from the Elder's cellar → **the 575 offering was kept back** (Clue Ledger; leads to [[Scene 02 — What Hommlet Isn't Saying]]).
- Jaroo's letter → **the Wretch was the grove's bird** (leads to [[Scene 04 — The Grove and the Offering-Heap]]).
- The gobble from the treeline → something large lives in the Gnarley or beyond it.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** paper lanterns, a misspelled banner, thin fields beyond the palisade, a goat with a mouthful of wreath.
- **Sound:** a fiddler tuning up, dogs howling at dusk, splintering wood in a boy's teeth.
- **Smell:** woodsmoke, cider, onions, gravy on a hot floor.
- **Touch:** the warm barrel Gundigoot hands over; the boy's cold, shaking hands.
- **Taste:** the first pour of the new Brewfest ale, nutty and a little sour.

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party cures Pip with magic before anyone explains:** Gundigoot still says "fever," and Pip still mentions the jam.
- **If the party goes straight to the grove tonight:** let them. Jaroo is waiting; run the first half of [[Scene 04 — The Grove and the Offering-Heap]] by lantern light and return to Scene 02 in the morning.
- **If nobody goes into the kitchen:** Pip staggers out into the common room with the spoon. The crash is not optional.
