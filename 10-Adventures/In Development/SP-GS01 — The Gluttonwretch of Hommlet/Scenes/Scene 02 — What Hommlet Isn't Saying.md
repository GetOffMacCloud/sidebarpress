# Scene 02 — What Hommlet Isn't Saying

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS01 — The Gluttonwretch of Hommlet/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 01 — Brewfest Eve]] · Next → [[Scene 03 — The War Council]]

## Purpose
Hommlet as the home base, keyed in the order the party will most likely play it. By the end the party should know that the village kept back its offering, that Old Gobbler starved, and that the Temple has a hand in what came back.
**Budget:** 40 minutes. Running long? Run only the Elder and one other lead. Wat Thistlewood arrives at 0:55 whatever has been found.

## Arrival
The party wakes on Day 2 (or works through the night of Day 1). Hommlet is setting up for the feast and pretending nothing is wrong. Let them choose where to go; the keyed entries below are in the likeliest order.

**To the table:** *"You've got one day before the feast bell. Who in this village do you lean on first?"*

**H5. CHURCH OF ST. CUTHBERT.** Terjon has two gorged villagers abed in the vestry, tied gently to their cots. The third, **Hob Tanner**, slipped his cords in the night and is gone. He is Hollow now and will rise at the feast.

**H6. WELCOME WENCH COMMON ROOM.** Roll or choose from the rumor table. Gundigoot denies everything cheerfully.

**H1. THE ELDER'S HOUSE**
> *A tidy stone house stands at the top of the green, its shutters freshly painted. A wreath of wheat hangs on the door. Inside, a long table is covered with feast plans: seating charts, a sketch of the green, a list headed BARRELS. A trapdoor in the kitchen floor has a new iron bolt.*

The Elder is spending Brewfest planning a perfect feast so that nobody will ask about the last one. He led the 575 vote and knows exactly what it cost.

**Creatures:** **[[The Elder of Hommlet]]** (1), and his granddaughter **[[Lissa (Hommlet)|Lissa]]** (1), age eight, who answers questions nobody asked her.

**Escalation script.** Play him through these in order. A DC 16 Wisdom (Insight) or Charisma (Persuasion) check, the jam from Scene 01, or opening the cellar moves him one step.
1. **Deny.** "Fever. Brewfest fever. We've had it before." *(We haven't.)*
2. **Blame the economy.** "Do you know what grain fetched last year? The *economy*, friends. The economy did this."
3. **Redirect.** "Jaroo's sulking. He cursed us for skipping one festival. One! The gods can count, can't they?"
4. **Confess** (comedy-free). He stops saying "the economy." "We kept it. The first fruits. All of them, in my cellar. I said it was just the once. Jaroo said the bird would starve, and I said birds can forage. He did starve. I've been eating the jam so it wouldn't be wasted."

**Lissa**, at any point: "Grandpa says Old Gobbler went away to be with his friends." *(A beat.)* "I don't think turkeys have friends."

**The cellar.** Behind the bolted trapdoor:
> *Steps lead down into a cold cellar lined with shelves. Crocks, sacks and barrels fill every shelf, each labeled in a careful hand: FIRST FRUITS, 575. A black crust covers everything, wet and faintly fizzing. The air smells of a mushroom house.*

The hoarded 575 offering has rotted, and Zuggtmoy's rot has taken root in it. A DC 15 Intelligence (Religion) check recognizes the Lady of Fungi's touch. Nothing here can be offered; it isn't this year's, and it isn't given.

**Treasure:** none. The Elder's feast plans show the Open Door, the seating, and the forty barrels of "cranberry preserves" ordered from the traders.
**Development:** If the Elder confesses, he becomes the first household willing to give in [[Scene 04 — The Grove and the Offering-Heap]] (his household is won automatically). If he is publicly shamed instead, his household cannot be won until he is privately forgiven by a PC or by Jaroo.

**H2. WIDOW BREE'S COTTAGE**
> *Beehives in straw skeps line the path to a low cottage. Honey cakes cool on the windowsill, a full tray of them. Inside, a feather as long as a sword hangs above the hearth. The kitchen table is set for two.*

**Creatures:** **[[Widow Marta Bree]]** (1), seventy, still in her apron.
**Knows:** everything about Old Gobbler, and nothing about the cult.
> "Forty Brewfests I fed that ridiculous bird. He stole Canon Terjon's hat during the blessing, the year the Canon came. Ate the feather off it. The Canon laughed so hard he had to sit down." *(She looks at the honey cakes.)* "He'd have had the first of those. Somebody should have had the first of those."

She cries, then insists the party take honey cakes. She will give her first honeycomb to the Wretch only if someone promises it will reach "him, not the thing wearing him."
**Treasure:** the feather above the hearth is one of Old Gobbler's. Anyone holding it has advantage on the Wisdom saving throw against the Aura of Endless Hunger once.
**Development:** Bree's household is won if a PC makes her that promise and means it.

---

**H3. THE TRADERS' ESTABLISHMENT**
> *The shop's shelves are nearly bare: rope, lamp oil, a single pair of boots. Crates are stacked to the ceiling along the back wall, each stenciled PRESERVES — CRANBERRY. Two men behind the counter stop talking as the door opens. One smiles too widely; the other doesn't smile at all.*

**Creatures:** **[[Rannos Davl]]** (1), who does the talking, and **[[Gremag]]** (1), who watches the door. Use their statblocks from the T1 conversion (see each NPC note). A **cultist clerk** (1), blank-faced, sweeps the same patch of floor for the entire scene.

**Escalation script.**
1. **Deny.** "Preserves? Yes. Cranberry. For a… wedding."
2. **Worse.** Each further question: "Two weddings." "A very large wedding." "The bride is a bog."
3. **Infiltrate.** Rannos offers to "help with the feast" and asks where the heroes will be sitting.
4. **Betray.** If pressed hard or caught, they send the clerk running up the High Road to warn Brindlewick and bolt for Nulb that night.

**Tactics:** They do not fight twelfth-level heroes. Caught, Gremag bargains: the Smokehouse's location and "the bird's bone" in exchange for a head start.
**Treasure:** the crates hold black, fizzing rot packed in cranberry jelly. A ledger under the counter is written in Temple cipher (DC 17 Intelligence (Investigation) to find it unnoticed; see [[SP-GS01 — Handout — The Cipher Ledger]]).
**Development:** If the clerk reaches the Smokehouse, it goes to **Wary** (see [[Scene 05 — The Smokehouse]]). If the traders are exposed before noon on Day 2, the "preserves" never reach the green, and no Flinger climbs out of the barrels at the feast.

---

**H4. MYTCH'S MILL**
> *The millwheel turns slowly over brown water. Flour dust hangs in the doorway in a pale haze. Sacks of new flour stand stacked against one wall, and a single sack sits apart from the rest on a stool, tied with a red ribbon.*

**Creatures:** **[[Mytch the Miller]]** (1), flour to the elbows.
> "That's my first sack. I tie a ribbon on it every year. Didn't take it up the hill last year, did I? Nobody did." *(Lower.)* "Those traders bought every cranberry in the Viscounty. Who needs that many cranberries? Nobody needs that many cranberries."

**Development:** Mytch's household is the hardest to win: he wants a reason that pays (see the good stories in Scene 04). The ribboned sack is his offering if he is won.

## Clues Available Here
- The Elder's confession and the cellar → **the 575 offering was kept back** (Clue Ledger).
- Widow Bree → **the Wretch was Old Gobbler**.
- The traders' crates and ledger → **the Temple's hand; the Smokehouse** (Clue Ledger).
- Mytch → the traders are buying up cranberries (supports the traders' lead).

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** bunting going up, a misspelled banner being re-hung, forty crates of cranberry, a feather as long as a sword.
- **Sound:** hammering on the green, bees, a millwheel, a clerk sweeping the same patch of floor.
- **Smell:** honey cakes, flour dust, a cellar that smells of mushrooms.
- **Touch:** cold cellar steps, sticky jam crocks, flour on everything.
- **Taste:** Widow Bree's honey cakes, which are excellent.

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party goes straight to the traders:** fine. The ledger leads to the Smokehouse, and the Elder's lead resurfaces through Pip's jam or Widow Bree.
- **If the party uses divination:** *commune* or *contact other plane* answers plainly ("a bird the village starved; a bone a priest keeps"). *Speak with dead* on Old Gobbler is impossible; his body is walking.
- **If the party skips Hommlet entirely:** the war council in [[Scene 03 — The War Council]] delivers the essential facts through Jaroo and Wat.
