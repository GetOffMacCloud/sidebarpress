# Scene 06 — The Hunt on Emridy Meadows

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS01 — The Gluttonwretch of Hommlet/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 05 — The Smokehouse]] · Next → [[Scene 07 — The Brewfest Feast]]

## Purpose
The Carving path. The party catches the Wretch at its roost and hurts it before the feast. Every hit point it loses here stays lost, because it cannot heal until it feeds.
**Budget:** about 25 minutes of Act 2's shared 65. Running long? End after the Wretch's third round, whatever has happened.

## Getting There
[[Emridy Meadows]] lies 12 miles due north of Hommlet, along the Emridy Run (see the area map in the main note). On foot that is half a day; mounted, a few hours. Twelfth-level magic (*wind walk*, *teleport*, *phantom steed*) makes it a short hop. Compress the journey into one sentence unless the party wants to talk on the way.

## Arrival
> *Grass grows high and pale over long, low mounds where the dead of 569 were buried. Rusted spearheads and a broken shield poke through the turf. The ground between the mounds is crusted with red shards that crunch underfoot. At the center of the meadow, an old oak stands stripped of its bark. Something huge and dark is perched in its crown.*

**To the table:** *"It hasn't seen you yet. What's the plan?"*

## Beats

**1. The roost.** The **Gluttonwretch** is resting in the oak, digesting. It wakes if anyone comes within 300 feet without hiding (a DC 18 group Dexterity (Stealth) check against its passive Perception). A party that reaches the oak unseen gets a surprise round.

**2. The Hollow Guests.** Under the mounds lie villagers and travelers the Wretch has gorged since Harvester. When it wakes, they claw out of the turf.
- **Gorgers** (3) rise beside the party (melee).
- **Flingers** (3) rise on the mounds 60 feet away and throw bones and razorberry shards (ranged).
Use the barks table in [[SP-GS01 — Comedy Kit]] freely.

**3. Three rounds.** The Wretch fights for **three rounds**, then its hunger pulls it home: at the end of its third turn, it takes off and flies toward Hommlet at 60 feet a round. It will not be held. Anything that would stop it (a *forcecage*, a grapple) holds it only until the end of its next turn, then the binding drags it free. *(While the Wishbone is unbroken, it cannot be kept from the feast.)*

**4. What carries over.** Note the damage dealt. **The Wretch starts the feast with those hit points gone.** Any hit point maximum reduction on PCs from Gluttony's Peck carries over too.

**5. The cart ruts.** Wheel tracks run from the foot of the oak south toward the High Road (a DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) check to follow). They end at the Smokehouse. This is a route to the Wishbone clue for a party that skipped Scene 02.

**Terrain:** grave mounds (Flingers on top have half cover), the stripped oak, razorberry crusts (difficult terrain in 5-foot patches; clearing one takes 10 minutes).
**Tactics:** the Wretch opens with Gobble of Gluttony to pull the party into its aura, then Pecks whoever is frightened or stunned. The Gorgers mob anyone the Gobble stunned. The Flingers stay on the mounds.
**DM direction:** this is a raid, not a kill. If the party is about to finish it here, let them (a dead Wretch at noon is a fine ending), but the binding drags it free at the end of round 3 unless it is at 0 hit points.

> [!warning] If the party kills it here
> Unless the Wishbone was snapped, the Wretch rises at the feast in **Second Helpings** form at full Second Helpings hit points. A dead Gluttonwretch is never quite dead until it has been fed or the bone is broken.

**Timing:** run it at dusk if you can. In daylight the Flingers fight at disadvantage (Sunlight Sensitivity), which you can treat as a gift to a party that set out early.

## Clues Available Here
- The cart ruts → **the Smokehouse** (Clue Ledger).
- The Wretch's Sylvan, *"You did not feed me"* → **it is Old Gobbler** (last resort for Fact 1).
- The battlefield mounds → the Hollow Guests are Hommlet's own and Verbobonc's dead.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** pale grass, grave mounds, red shards like broken glass, a turkey the size of a barn in a dead oak.
- **Sound:** wind through the grass; the crunch of razorberries; a gobble that rattles the rusted shields.
- **Smell:** wet earth, old iron, gravy.
- **Touch:** shards that cut through boot leather.
- **Taste:** iron on the wind.

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party scouts instead of fighting:** they see the Wretch, hear its Sylvan, and find the ruts. Nobody rises.
- **If the party arrives after dark:** the Wretch is already on its way to Hommlet. Run the Hollow Guests alone and send the party racing back for the feast bell.
