# Scene 06 — The Hunt on Emridy Meadows

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS01 — The Gluttonwretch of Hommlet/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 05 — The Smokehouse]] · Next → [[Scene 07 — The Brewfest Feast]]

## Purpose
The Carving path. The party catches the Wretch at its roost and hurts it before the feast. Every hit point it loses here stays lost, because it cannot heal until it feeds.
**Budget:** about 25 minutes of Act 2's shared 65. Running long? Skip the chase and let the Wretch fly off after the first round.

## Getting There
[[Emridy Meadows]] lies 12 miles due north of Hommlet, along the Emridy Run (see [[Emridy Meadows]] and [[Viscounty-of-Verbobonc|Viscounty of Verbobonc]]). On foot that is half a day; mounted, a few hours. Twelfth-level magic (*wind walk*, *teleport*, *phantom steed*) makes it a short hop. Compress the journey into one sentence unless the party wants to talk on the way.

## Arrival
> *Grass grows high and pale over long, low mounds where the dead of 569 were buried. Rusted spearheads and a broken shield poke through the turf. The ground between the mounds is crusted with red shards that crunch underfoot. At the center of the meadow, an old oak stands stripped of its bark. Something huge and dark is perched in its crown.*

**To the table:** *"It hasn't seen you yet. What's the plan?"*

## Beats

**1. The roost.** The **Gluttonwretch** is resting in the oak, digesting. It wakes if anyone comes within 300 feet without hiding (a DC 18 group Dexterity (Stealth) check against its passive Perception). A party that reaches the oak unseen gets a surprise round.

**2. The Hollow Guests.** Under the mounds lie villagers and travelers the Wretch has gorged since Harvester. When it wakes, they claw out of the turf.
- **Gorger** (1) rises beside the party (melee).
- **Flinger** (1) rises on a mound 60 feet away: **Benno**, Mytch's apprentice, still in his floury apron (gorging 5) and throw bones and razorberry shards (ranged).
Use the barks table in [[SP-GS01 — Comedy Kit]] freely.

**3. One round.** The Wretch fights for **one round**. At the start of its second turn its hunger pulls it home: it takes the Dash action and flies south toward Hommlet at 120 feet. It will not be held. Anything that would stop it (a *forcecage*, a grapple) holds it only until the end of its next turn, then the binding drags it free. *(While the Wishbone is unbroken, it cannot be kept from the feast.)*

**4. The chase.** The party can pursue. Run **three chase rounds**, each about a minute of hard running, riding or flying over fields and hedges. The Hollow Guests stay behind on the meadow.
- **Each chase round, each pursuer chooses one:**
  - **Keep pace:** a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check on foot, or a DC 13 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check mounted. A creature with a flying speed of 60 feet or more, or under *wind walk*, keeps pace automatically.
  - **Strike:** a pursuer who kept pace last round can make one ranged attack or cast one spell at the Wretch. It is at long range, so attacks have disadvantage.
  - **Track:** a DC 15 Wisdom (Survival) or Wisdom (Perception) check keeps it in sight for everyone this round.
- **The Wretch fights back** once per chase round: a Razorberry Shard (+13 to hit, 35 (10d6) piercing damage) at the nearest pursuer.
- **If nobody keeps pace or tracks** in a chase round, the party loses it then.
- **The party always loses it.** At the end of the third chase round, it drops into the Gnarley's canopy two miles south of the Meadows and is gone. **Where it vanished** is over the High Road, near the burned barn: anyone who looks down sees a column of smoke from the Smokehouse chimney (a lead to [[Scene 05 — The Smokehouse]]).

**5. What carries over.** Note the damage dealt in the fight and the chase. **The Wretch starts the feast with those hit points gone.** Any hit point maximum reduction on PCs from Gluttony's Peck carries over too.

**6. The cart ruts.** Wheel tracks run from the foot of the oak south toward the High Road (a DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) check to follow). They end at the Smokehouse. This is a route to the Wishbone clue for a party that skipped Scene 02.

**Terrain:** grave mounds (Flingers on top have half cover), the stripped oak, razorberry crusts (difficult terrain in 5-foot patches; clearing one takes 10 minutes).
**Tactics:** in its one round, the Wretch opens with Gobble of Gluttony to pull the party into its aura and spends its legendary actions on whoever is stunned. The Gorger mobs anyone the Gobble stunned. The Flinger stays on its mound.
**DM direction:** this is a raid, not a kill. The point is the damage that carries into the feast and the lead to the Smokehouse. If the party somehow drops it to 0 hit points here, let them (see below).

> [!warning] If the party kills it here
> Unless the Wishbone was snapped, the Wretch rises at the feast in **Second Helpings** form at full Second Helpings hit points. This is the one case where Second Helpings is always used, because it is the only Wretch left for the climax. A dead Gluttonwretch is never quite dead until it has been fed or the bone is broken.

**Timing:** run it at dusk if you can. In daylight the Flingers fight at disadvantage (Sunlight Sensitivity), which you can treat as a gift to a party that set out early.

## Clues Available Here
- The cart ruts → **the Smokehouse** (Clue Ledger).
- The Wretch's Sylvan, *"You did not feed me"* → **it is Old Gobbler** (last resort for Fact 1).
- The battlefield mounds → the Hollow Guests are Hommlet's own and Verbobonc's dead.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** pale grass, grave mounds, red shards like broken glass, a turkey the size of a barn in a dead oak.
- **Sound:** wind through the grass; the crunch of razorberries; a gobble that rattles the rusted shields.
- **Smell:** wet earth, old iron, gravy.
- **Touch:** shards that cut through boot leather.
- **Taste:** iron on the wind.

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party scouts instead of fighting:** they see the Wretch, hear its Sylvan, and find the ruts. Nobody rises.
- **If the party arrives after dark:** the Wretch is already on its way to Hommlet. Run the Hollow Guests alone and send the party racing back for the feast bell.
