# Scene 08 — The Last Toast

[[10-Adventures/In Development/SP-GS01 — The Gluttonwretch of Hommlet/Scenes/00 — Scene Index|00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 07 — The Brewfest Feast]] · Next → [[SP-GS01 — Bestiary]]

## Purpose
Close the session at the ruined table. Whatever ending the party reached, the village stays, gives thanks, and remembers who isn't there. The players get the last word. **This scene is comedy-free,** apart from the goat.
**Budget:** 10 minutes. Don't cut it; it is the reason for the adventure.

## Epilogue
> *Nobody goes home. The benches are splintered and the goose is on the grass, so Gundigoot brings out bread and the last of the new ale. The village eats standing up, shoulder to shoulder in the cold. Pip Haddle falls asleep against his mother with cranberry on his chin. Pudding the goat is asleep in the Welcome Wench's open doorway.*

Then, in the DM's own voice:
- **Ending A:** **[[Jaroo Ashstaff]]** sits down beside **[[The Elder of Hommlet]]**. Neither speaks for a long time. Then the Elder passes him the bread first.
- **Ending B (the Wishbone snapped):** Jaroo's place is empty. The Elder sets a plate there anyway, and leaves it.
- **Ending B (the Carving):** Jaroo comes down late, sits at the end of the table, and eats nothing.
- **Ending C:** there is no toast. The survivors sit in the dark around the one table still standing. Skip to the out-of-character closing.

**[[Canon Terjon]]** raises what is left in his cup:
> "To the ones who aren't at this table. The ones from the meadows. The ones we lost this week. And an old bird who only ever wanted to be fed first." *(The whole village drinks. Then, quieter.)* "And to the ones who are. We are still here, and we are still together. That's the harvest."

## The Last Word Goes to the Players
Bookend the Naming of Thanks from [[Scene 07 — The Brewfest Feast]]: ask each player for one line about what their character is thankful for *now*, after tonight. Don't comment on the answers. Let them land, then move on to the next player.

## Rewards
Award per the endings in [[SP-GS01 — Front Matter]]:
- **Ending A:** the [[Feather of the First Fruits]]; Jaroo's friendship; free board at the Welcome Wench for life.
- **Ending B:** [[Brindlewick's Cipher Book]] and the Deacon's *spell scroll*; the larger half of the Wishbone if it was snapped.
- **Every ending:** Canon Terjon writes to Verbobonc naming the party, and the village remembers who stood at its table.

> [!tip] Out-of-character closing (DM to players, and to chat on stream)
> A draft to put in your own words:
>
> "That's where we leave Hommlet. Before we sign off, I want to do what they did at that table. This week a lot of us will sit down with family, the ones we were born to and the ones we found along the way. Some of us will be looking at an empty chair. Some of us will be the traveler, eating somewhere far from home. Thanksgiving was never really about the food. It's about looking around the table and saying out loud: I'm glad you're here. So, I'm glad you're here. Thank you to my players for bringing these heroes to life, and thank you to everyone watching for spending your night with us. Go hug your people. Keep a door open if you can. Happy Thanksgiving."

*(Strip the tip box from the printed book if you prefer; it ships as a DM sidebar.)*

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** one candle relit at a time down the green; bread passed hand to hand.
- **Sound:** the fiddler, finally, playing the Brewfest hymn properly; a goat snoring.
- **Smell:** fresh bread, spilled ale, cold night air.
- **Touch:** a shared cup; the cold.
- **Taste:** plain bread, which is the best thing anyone has eaten all night.

## Transition
- Out of the adventure: [[Brindlewick's Cipher Book]] names "the second table," laid in the Temple's lower halls for a guest who has not yet arrived (see [[SP-GS01 — Front Matter]], Continuing the Adventure).
