# Comedy Kit

Back to [[SP-GS01-The-Gluttonwretch-of-Hommlet]]

The rule for this table is **laugh first, then land the punch**. The jokes give the horror and the heart somewhere to land. About a third of the session should be comedy.

**Comedy-free zones:** Jaroo's grief (Scenes 03, 04), the Elder's confession (Scene 02, step 4), and the Last Toast (Scene 08, apart from the goat).

## Running Gags
| Gag | Setup | Escalation | Payoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[[Pudding]] the goat** | Scene 01: wearing a corn wreath, eating the wreath | Turns up in every scene, whichever path the party takes: eating the grove's moss, a cultist's robe, Wat's map. The Aura of Endless Hunger has no effect on her; she was already like this. | Scene 07: she wanders through the battle eating razorberry shards like candy. Scene 08: asleep in the Open Door. |
| **Gundigoot will not cancel Brewfest** | "Nobody's cancelling Brewfest. Not even a ghost turkey." | Scene 01: "It's a fever. Brewfest fever. Very seasonal." Act 2: he hires a fiddler to drown out the gobbling. Scene 07, mid-fight: "Second barrel's tapped! Nobody leaves!" | Scene 08: he is the one who brings out the bread. |
| **Wat's pigs** | Wat names every missing pig: Mabel, Little Ham, Sir Trotsworth, Petunia, the Reverend, and Big Unpleasant Gerald | In the Smokehouse they sit in the pews like a congregation, and Wat identifies each one out loud, mid-fight | The pig stampede (Scene 05) |
| **"The economy"** | The Elder blames the skipped offering on the economy | He blames everything else on it too: the blight, the Wretch, the goat | He stops saying it at his confession. That is the payoff. |
| **The traders' cover story** | "Preserves? Yes. Cranberry. For a… wedding." | "Two weddings." "A very large wedding." "The bride is a bog." | The cipher ledger decodes mostly to a recipe. |
| **The banner** | WELCOM, HEROES | Someone re-hangs it as WELCOME, HERDES | By Scene 07 it reads WELCOME, HEROES, and the Wretch tears it in half. |

## Set Pieces
- **The Brothers of the Rotting Table** sing grace to Zuggtmoy to the tune of Hommlet's Brewfest hymn and can never agree on the words. Whenever one fails a saving throw, he bursts into the wrong verse and the other corrects him.
- **The cipher.** It is a real cipher and a real DC 17 check. The decoded text reads: *"Cranberries, 40 barrels. Gravy, ritual-grade. DO NOT let Gremag make the stuffing again."*
- **The Offering-Heap's manners.** Calmed, it apologizes at length, in mime, for the whole lurching business, and keeps handing people rotten pumpkins as gifts.

## Comedy in the Fights
- **Lair-action flavor.** *Gravy Flood:* a brown tidal wave with a single crouton riding the crest. *The Table Turns:* the carving knife has clearly been waiting years for this. *Second Hunger:* every stomach in range growls at once, in harmony.
- **The Wretch's Sylvan.** Most of its gobbling is "You did not feed me," but anyone who speaks Sylvan also hears it reviewing the menu mid-fight: "The relish is underseasoned." "Who brought goose? To a turkey's funeral?"
- **Burst Seams** sprays stuffing like confetti. Describe it lovingly.
