# Handout — Who's Who in Hommlet

*Canon Terjon's list for the guests of honor, written in a neat priest's hand on the back of a Brewfest seating chart. He hands it over in [[Scene 01 — Brewfest Eve]], beat 3. It is player-facing: it says what Terjon knows and suspects, nothing more.*

---

> **Friends — the people of Hommlet you'll want to know before the feast.**
> *Plain speech, as Saint Cuthbert likes it. — T.*

| Who | Where to find them | Who they are | Why you might want a word |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ostler Gundigoot** and **Goodwife Gundigoot** | The Inn of the Welcome Wench, on the green | Innkeepers; hosts of the feast | Everyone passes through the Wench. They know who's been taken by the fever, and what's been served in their kitchen. |
| **Pip Haddle** and the **Haddles** | The Wench's kitchen; the Haddle cottage | The scullery boy and his family | Pip was taken by the fever. Ask him what he ate. |
| **The Elder** | The stone house at the top of the green | Head of the village council; planner of the feast | He knows everything that happens in Hommlet, and he decided what the village did last Brewfest. He's been short with me since. |
| **Jaroo Ashstaff** | The grove of the Old Faith, at the village edge | The druid; keeper of the grove | He hasn't set foot in the village for a year. He and the Elder aren't speaking. If anyone knows what gobbles in the Gnarley at dusk, it's Jaroo. |
| **Widow Marta Bree** | The cottage with the beehives | Beekeeper; has lived here seventy years | She remembers every Brewfest there's ever been, and she's been crying since Harvester. |
| **Mytch the Miller** | The mill on the stream | The miller | He sees every sack of grain that comes and goes, and he's been complaining about the traders. |
| **Rannos Davl** and **Gremag** | The traders' establishment | The village's traders | Their shelves are empty but their storeroom is full. They're supplying the feast. |
| **Wat Thistlewood** | Wherever there's shouting about pigs | The swineherd | His pigs went missing this week. He wants someone to listen. His brother Tam is one of the sick in my vestry. |
| **The Rheddens** and **Dunwold the farrier** | Their farms on the south road | Young farm family; the village farrier | Ordinary folk with first harvests in. The farrier's mother is in my vestry with the fever. |
| **Canon Terjon** (that's me) | The Church of St. Cuthbert | Priest of St. Cuthbert | I'm keeping the sick in my vestry. Come and see them for yourselves. |

> One more thing. **Old Gobbler**, the great bird of Jaroo's grove, hasn't been seen since last winter. Nobody will tell me why. Ask.

---

## DM Notes
- Each name points to a lead in [[Scene 02 — What Hommlet Isn't Saying]]: Gundigoots and Pip (Scene 01, the jam), the Elder (H1), Widow Bree (H2), the traders (H3), Mytch (H4), the vestry (H5), Wat (Scene 03), Jaroo (Scene 04), the Rheddens and Dunwolds (Scene 04 households).
- Terjon doesn't know about the 575 vote, the cult or the Smokehouse. The list only points the party at the people who do.
