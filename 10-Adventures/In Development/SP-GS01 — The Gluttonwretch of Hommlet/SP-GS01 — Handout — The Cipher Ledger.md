# Handout — The Cipher Ledger

*A grease-stained ledger from under the traders' counter ([[Scene 02 — What Hommlet Isn't Saying]], area H3). It is written in a Temple cipher of shifted letters and fungus symbols. Give the players the decoded page after a DC 17 Intelligence (Investigation) check, or after they puzzle it out at the table.*

---

> **For the Table of Our Lady — Brewfest accounts**
>
> Cranberries, 40 barrels — bought, all of them, every last one in the Viscounty
> Gravy, ritual-grade — from the vats at the Smokehouse, the Deacon's own
> Jelly to cover — Gremag
> Delivery — the green, Brewfest noon
>
> The bone stays on its hooks until the Deacon carries it down himself. NOBODY touches the bone.
>
> Grace at the feast when the old fool asks for thanks. She'll be listening.
>
> DO NOT let Gremag make the stuffing again.

---

## DM Notes
- **Clues:** the Smokehouse (where the gravy comes from), the forty barrels (the "preserves" on the green), the bone (the Wishbone), and the timing (grace at the Naming of Thanks).
- If the traders are exposed before noon on Day 2, the barrels never reach the green and no Flinger rises from them at the feast.
