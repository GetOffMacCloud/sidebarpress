# The Gorgings

Back to [[SP-GS01-The-Gluttonwretch-of-Hommlet]] · Stages and cures: [[Scene 01 — Brewfest Eve]] (The Hunger) · Feast clock: [[Scene 07 — The Brewfest Feast]]

Who gorges, when, and where the party meets them. Every gorging is written into its scene; this note is the DM's one-page schedule.

## Why these people
Ashamed of the hoard and unable to throw away the gods' own food, the Elder spent the year slipping crocks of the 575 offering around the village: "Last year's jam. Shame to waste it." Everyone below ate some. That is why the hunger reaches them and not their neighbors.

## Cures (from The Hunger, Scene 01)
- **Peckish:** talk them down with a DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine) or Charisma (Persuasion) check.
- **Gorging:** *lesser restoration*, or a DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check to hold them and get water into them.
- **Hollow:** no cure. They rise as Hollow Guests.
- A cure ends one gorging. The hunger itself ends only when the Wretch does.

## Pay-offs
- **Curing Ma Dunwold** gives advantage on winning the Dunwold household. The farrier stops calling it superstition.
- **Curing Tam Thistlewood** gives advantage on winning the Thistlewoods, and Wat will follow the party anywhere.
- **Curing Arlo Rhedden** before the apples are gone wins the Rhedden household outright.
- **Uncured,** Ma Dunwold and Tam Thistlewood rise beside Hob Tanner at the feast as two more Gorgers (700 XP each). Cure them and the feast stays as the Bestiary rates it.

## At the Feast: Order of Gorging
After round 3 of the feast, if no Offering has yet been made, one more household gorges at the end of each round (see [[Scene 07 — The Brewfest Feast]]). Take them in this order, skipping any household that has already delivered its Offering:

1. The Thistlewoods
2. The Dunwolds
3. The Rheddens
4. Mytch the Miller's household
5. The Haddles
6. Widow Bree
7. The Gundigoots
8. The Elder's household

Each gorged household loses its Offering and adds one Gorger to the fight.
