## New maps to make (CharGen prompts)
Paste each prompt into CharGen. Ask for a gridded and a gridless version of each, then export at 100 px per square.

### M1 — The Feast Green (Scene 07) · 30 × 24 squares (150 × 120 ft)
```
Top-down battle map of a medieval village green at night during a harvest festival, true overhead view, 30 by 24 squares of 5 feet. Along the north edge, the front of a two-story timber inn with its wide front door propped open by a butter churn, warm light spilling onto the grass. Three long trestle tables with benches run east to west across the green, each 60 feet long, covered in candles, roast geese, bread, pies and baskets of apples, squash and corn; a shorter head table across the west end. A stack of forty barrels near the east edge. Strings of paper lanterns overhead, a small stone well, a hand-painted banner between two poles, a goat pen in one corner, scattered hay bales, a dirt road along the south edge, a few autumn trees at the corners. Grass, packed earth, candlelight. No text, no labels.
```

### M2 — The Smokehouse (Scene 05) · two levels
**Ground floor, 20 × 16 squares:**
```
Top-down battle map of a burned timber barn on the edge of a forest, true overhead view, 20 by 16 squares of 5 feet. A muddy yard to the south with an empty pigpen and deep cart ruts leading to sagging double doors. Inside, a long smoking hall with a brick firepit down the middle, iron meat hooks hanging from blackened rafters, a spit over the fire, holes in the scorched roof. At the north end, three large iron vats of dark brown liquid and a stone staircase leading down. Charred beams, ash, scattered straw. No text, no labels.
```
**Cellar, 18 × 14 squares:**
```
Top-down battle map of a damp stone cellar turned into a fungus chapel, true overhead view, 18 by 14 squares of 5 feet. Stairs down in the south-east corner. Two rows of rough wooden pews face a long table at the north end, the table black with mold. Above the table, a giant wishbone taller than a man hangs from chains. Candles stuck in rotten gourds light the room; pale mushrooms and patches of black mold grow on the walls and floor. A narrow side room to the west holds a cot, a wooden lectern with an open book, and shelves of jars; in its corner, a sloping coal chute leads up to daylight. Stone walls with cracked foundation blocks. No text, no labels.
```

### M3 — Jaroo's Grove (Scene 04) · 24 × 24 squares (120 × 120 ft)
```
Top-down battle map of a druid grove in autumn, true overhead view, 24 by 24 squares of 5 feet. A ring of nine standing stones about 80 feet across encloses brown grass. At the center, a flat carved stone altar and a long moss-covered stone trough. On the north side of the ring, a huge ancient oak with a large woven-branch nest in its crown. Behind the oak, a giant mound of rotting pumpkins, gourds, corn husks and leaves, about 15 feet across. A footpath enters from the south-west. Along the south edge, the edge of a cranberry bog with red berries in shallow water. Fallen leaves everywhere, a few crows. No text, no labels.
```

### M4 — The Roost on Emridy Meadows (Scene 06) · 30 × 30 squares (150 × 150 ft)
```
Top-down battle map of an old battlefield meadow at dusk, true overhead view, 30 by 30 squares of 5 feet. Tall pale grass over six long, low grave mounds, each about 10 by 40 feet. At the center, a huge dead oak stripped of its bark with a wide bare crown. Patches of glittering red crystal shards scattered across the ground, each about 5 to 10 feet across. Rusted spearheads, a broken shield and old helmets half buried in the turf. A small stream crosses the north-west corner. Wheel ruts run from the foot of the oak to the south edge. Long dusk shadows. No text, no labels.
```
