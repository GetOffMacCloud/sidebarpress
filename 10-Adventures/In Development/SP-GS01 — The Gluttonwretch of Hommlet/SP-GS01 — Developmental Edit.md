## Verdict
The structure holds. It is a situation, not a plot:
- The party can take any two of three Act 2 paths, or all three if it splits, and each skipped path has a written consequence.
- The climax has three routes (First Fruits, Wishbone, Carving), each seeded at least one scene earlier (Climax at a Glance, Scene 07).
- There are three endings (A, B, C), including defeat, and a refusal path in the hooks.
- Act 2 now sets the climax's difficulty: tying off both Brindlewick and the traders makes the feast Hard instead of Deadly.

**Classic-module musts: 12 of 12 pass.** Must #12 (no plot protection) passes by ruling: Greybeard kept "Bound by the Wishbone" as written (2026-11-18). It has counterplay, because the Wishbone can be found and snapped.

**Pacing is the weak point** at seven players (see Needs you).

## Findings

### Major
- [x] **1 · ✅ Rules — the villagers couldn't survive carrying their offerings.** A commoner carrying a basket into the aura fails a DC 18 Wisdom save and takes 10 (3d6) necrotic damage, enough to kill them. That killed the escort mechanic. **Fix:** a creature carrying an Offering is ignored by the Aura of Endless Hunger; the Wretch wants what it carries. *(Scene 07; [[SP-GS01 — The First Fruits]] rule 3.)*
- [x] **2 · ✅ Continuity — the gorging count didn't add up.** Terjon said four people had gorged before Pip, Gundigoot called Pip "the fourth," and the church held three plus Hob. **Fix:** three before Pip (two abed in the church plus Hob), Pip the fourth. *(Scenes 01, 02; Front Matter cast table.)*

### Minor
- [x] **3 · ✅ Canon — Rannos and Gremag used the Spy block.** The vault's T1 conversion already gives them statblocks (Rannos has 58 hit points). **Fix:** point to the T1 conversion's blocks. *(Scene 02, H3; Bestiary.)*
- [x] **4 · ✅ Prose — two similes in one box.** The Wretch's entrance in Scene 07 had "feathers like wet slate" and "eyes like coals." **Fix:** "slate-grey feathers"; the coals stay.
- [x] **5 · ✅ Pacing note added to Scene 07:** realistic round times at seven players, and Second Helpings as optional.

## Needs you (I can't do these, or they change the design)
- [ ] **A · The First Fruits can end the climax in round 1.** Seven PCs can deliver six Offerings in a single round if six households were won in Act 2. Now that carriers ignore the aura, that is even easier. **Recommendation:** the Wretch accepts **one Offering per round** (it eats slowly, savoring each). Six Offerings then take six rounds of protecting the carriers, which is the scene the adventure wants. Say the word and I'll apply it.
- [ ] **B · The Hunt can't fit its budget.** Three rounds of a CR 20 fight at seven players is roughly an hour, against a 25-minute budget. **Options:** (1) the Wretch fights **one** round, then flies home; (2) run the Hunt as a chase with one exchange of attacks; (3) give the Hunt 50 minutes and cut a path elsewhere.
- [ ] **C · The feast is long.** About three rounds fit in 70 minutes. Second Helpings is now marked optional in Scene 07. Decide whether it is on or off by default for the stream.
- [ ] **D · Read every box aloud once** and cut any stumble (Board item).
