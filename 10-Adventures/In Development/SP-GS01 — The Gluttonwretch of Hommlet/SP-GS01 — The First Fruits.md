# The First Fruits

Back to [[SP-GS01-The-Gluttonwretch-of-Hommlet]] · Used in [[Scene 04 — The Grove and the Offering-Heap]] and [[Scene 07 — The Brewfest Feast]]

The signature mechanic. Six **Offerings** of first fruits, freely given by Hommlet's households and delivered to the Gluttonwretch's beak, lay Old Gobbler to rest.

## Rules
1. **What counts.** Only the first portion of a household's own 576 harvest, freely given by that household. Bought food, the heroes' food and the rotted 575 hoard never count.
2. **Winning a household** (Scene 04, or mid-feast). One success: DC 15 Persuasion, Insight or Performance in Act 2; DC 18 Persuasion and an action at the feast. A good story wins a household outright. Two failures in a row with the same household loses it.
3. **Delivering an Offering** (Scene 07). An action within 15 feet of the Gluttonwretch, inside its aura. A villager can carry their own basket if a PC escorts them. A creature carrying an Offering is ignored by the Aura of Endless Hunger.
4. **Each Offering delivered:** the Wretch has 1 fewer legendary action per round.
5. **At 3 Offerings:** it loses all damage resistances and will not rise in Second Helpings.
6. **At 6 Offerings:** it stops fighting and eats. Jaroo's rite (1 round, uninterrupted, within 30 feet) lays it to rest. Ending A.
7. **Old Gobbler's feather** (from the calmed Offering-Heap), placed by Jaroo, counts as one Offering.
8. **Standing in.** A PC can give one Offering by freely giving something that costs them: a magic item, a permanent reduction of 1d10 to their hit point maximum (restored only by *wish*), or a true memory spoken aloud and forgotten. **At most 3 Offerings may come from PCs.**
9. **The Offering-Heap.** If it was calmed and is given one Offering at the feast (that Offering does not count toward the six), it fights beside the party for 3 rounds.
10. **The clock.** After round 3 of the feast, if no Offering has been made, one more household gorges at the end of each round. Cross it off; it joins the fight as a Gorger.
11. **The Wishbone closes the rite.** If the Wishbone is snapped, there is no bird left to feed. The First Fruits can no longer end the fight.
12. **Jaroo's last gift.** If the party is losing badly, Jaroo can complete the rite with only 4 Offerings by giving his life. Ending B.

## Why it works this way
The adventure's thesis is that gratitude is something you *do*. The cap on PC Offerings keeps the village paying its own debt; the heroes can carry it to the table but cannot settle it.
