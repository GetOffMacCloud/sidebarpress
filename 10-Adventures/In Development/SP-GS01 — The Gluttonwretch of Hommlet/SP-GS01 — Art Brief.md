# Art Brief — The Gluttonwretch of Hommlet

Back to [[SP-GS01-The-Gluttonwretch-of-Hommlet]] · House rules: [[Art-Style-Guide]]

Every image is made **twice**:

- **Color** — an epic, full-color fantasy illustration for the Fantasy Grounds module and the PDF (screen only, never printed).
- **Print** — a separate black-and-white cross-hatched prompt for the printed book, using the house spines from the [[Art-Style-Guide]] verbatim.

**No `--sref` yet.** Generate **#01 (the cover) in color first**, pick the look, and lock its image as the color set's `--sref`. Do the same with the first approved **print** image for the ink set. Never mix the two `--sref`s. All prompts use `--v 8.1` and the product seed `--seed 112601`.

**Print production:** upscale 2×, threshold to 1-bit at 600 dpi, clean specks (see the [[Art-Style-Guide]]). Files go in `99-Assets/SP-GS01/` as `SP-GS01 - NN Name (color).png` and `SP-GS01 - NN Name (ink).png`.

## Spines

**COLOR (screen):** `epic high-fantasy tabletop roleplaying sourcebook illustration, richly painted digital art, dramatic cinematic lighting, warm autumn palette of amber, cranberry red and slate grey, deep shadows, highly detailed textures, painterly brushwork`

Color tail: `--v 8.1 --stylize 250 --seed 112601 --no text, letters, signature, watermark, frame, border` plus `--ar`.

**PRINT:** the house SCENE, PLATE and PORTRAIT spines from the [[Art-Style-Guide]], with the house print tail `--v 8.1 --style raw --stylize 75 --seed 112601 --no color, grey wash, gradient shading, painting, watercolor, 3d render, photograph, text, letters, signature, watermark` plus `--ar`.
