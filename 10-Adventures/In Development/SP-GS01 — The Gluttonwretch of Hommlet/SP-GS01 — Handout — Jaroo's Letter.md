# Handout — Jaroo's Letter

*Burned into a strip of birch bark with a hot nail, in a careful, old-fashioned hand. A crow delivers it in [[Scene 01 — Brewfest Eve]].*

---

> To the one who stood at the Meadows (or who walks the old ways),
>
> Something walks the Gnarley edge that I once fed by hand.
> It has forgotten its name. The village has forgotten what it owes.
> I can't make them remember on my own.
>
> Come before the feast.
> The crow knows the way. Don't feed it; it'll never leave.
>
> — J. A., of the grove

---

## DM Notes
- The crow waits for an answer and leads anyone to [[Jaroo's Grove]].
- "What it owes" is the first fruits. Jaroo won't say more in writing; he is ashamed for the village and won't shame it in a letter.
