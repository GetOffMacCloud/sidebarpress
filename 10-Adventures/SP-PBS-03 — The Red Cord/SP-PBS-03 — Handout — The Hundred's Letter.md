# Handout — The Hundred's Letter

Back to [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]] · Given in [[Scene 01 — The Ritter's Hall]]

## Player Text
*(Heavy paper, folded in thirds, and closed with a red cord tied in a knot rather than a seal.)*

---

To Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld, of Ritterstadt, head of his house —

In the year of the Witch, the Hundertschaft contracted to hold your house's seat for one season and did not hold it. Your grandfather named this a breach before the Council of Hetmen. The company has never answered him. It will answer you.

On the night of the fourth of Ready'reat, at the Red Cord in Schwartzenbruin, before the three voices and the Council's clerk, the Hundred will swear the breach, strike the Soldbrief, and make your house an offer at your choosing: one hundred swords for one season, or the fee of 480 returned in full.

Come wearing your colours. Leave your steel at the door, as we will.

— Ilse Brandauer, Hauptfrau of the Hundred, by the company's vote

---

## DM Prop Notes
- **Paper:** good, heavy and cream. Tie it with real red string in a reef knot.
- **Hand:** clear and upright, a soldier's, with no flourishes.
- **The date** is a placeholder (4 Ready'reat). Set it once the house calendar is fixed for SP-PBS-03.
- **What the letter does not say:** that a rival company has petitioned to strike the Hundred, or why it is answering *now*. Ilse explains both in Scene 3 if asked.
