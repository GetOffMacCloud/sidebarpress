# Bestiary — The Red Cord

Back to [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]]

All statblocks use **2014 5e** rules. MM page numbers refer to the 2014 *Monster Manual*. Every creature here is a stock NPC block. The only custom rules are the **brawl swap** and Jost's age.

## The Brawl Swap (Scene 4, Scene 6)
Under the cord, replace every weapon attack with **Unarmed or Improvised: melee, +to hit as the creature's best Str weapon, 1d4 + Str bludgeoning.** Multiattack keeps its number of attacks. Damage is nonlethal by house custom, so a creature reduced to 0 HP is knocked out. Spells and ranged attacks are not used.
