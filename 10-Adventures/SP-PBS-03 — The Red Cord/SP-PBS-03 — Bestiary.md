# Bestiary — The Red Cord

Back to [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]]

All statblocks use **2014 5e** rules. MM page numbers refer to the 2014 *Monster Manual*. Every creature here is a stock NPC block. The only custom rules are the **brawl swap**, Little Fenna's thief's kit, the yard foreman's crane, and Jost's age, so the night runs from this file alone.

## The Brawl Swap (Scene 4, Scene 6)
Under the cord, every weapon attack is replaced:
- ***Unarmed or Improvised.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* the creature's Str-based to-hit (listed per block below), reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* **1d4 + Str** bludgeoning.
- **Multiattack** keeps its number of attacks.
- **Nonlethal by house custom.** A creature reduced to 0 HP is knocked out, not dying. The same applies to PCs.
- **No spells, no ranged attacks,** no poison, no Pack Tactics against a creature that is not hostile to it (the rest of the room is not on anyone's side).
- **PCs** use the same rule: unarmed strikes and improvised weapons deal 1d4 + Str under the cord. Staves and cudgels are wood and deal normal damage.
