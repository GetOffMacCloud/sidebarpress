# The Red Cord

> *In the mercenary taverns of Schwartzenbruin, every blade is tied into its sheath with a red cord at the door. Cut your cord and draw, and every company in the room becomes your enemy. Tonight, Perrenland's oldest free company will apologise to Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld for a contract it broke ninety-six years ago, and somebody in the room needs that apology to fail.*
>
> A Social / Intrigue / Urban one-shot for **average party level 1–4** (written for APL 2–3, with scaling tables for 1 and 4). Greyhawk University students cross Lake Quag with Ritterstadt's lord to serve as the scholars' witness at a sworn amends. They have to keep a proud man's hand off his sword, win back a stolen contract before the midnight bell, and learn what really happened the night the Witch came. The story is complete in one sitting: it opens no threads and leaves none dangling.

## Date
**Ready'reat, 576 CY** — the house default year (see [[Timeline]]). The free companies are coming in off the roads to winter in Schwartzenbruin, the lake is still open, and the Council of Hetmen holds its winter review of the company charter-roll. It follows [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]] (Patchwall 576 CY) by a month. In Ritterstadt continuity, the eastern gate is still under repair and [[Vethkar Graun]]'s report on the Eisfeld Schuldbrief is still unfiled.

## Design Frame
- **Thesis:** Honouring a debt late is still honouring it. The adventure sets two broken obligations side by side: the Hundred's contract of 480 CY and Konrad's Schuldbrief to [[The Kettenhaus]].
- **Expectation broken:** *Violence solves it.* At the Red Cord, the first person on Konrad's side who draws steel loses the night. Fists, chairs and words are legal. Steel and harmful magic are not.
- **Second break:** the "wronged noble" turns out to be the house that did the wronging. The Hundred never broke its contract, and the von Eisfelds have slandered it for three generations.
- **What this adventure refuses to do:** It has no dungeon and no villain lair, and it leaves no sequel hook. Every question it raises is answered before the party sails home.
- **Session spine:** three trackers on one card: Konrad's **Temper**, Konrad's **Trust**, and the **Midnight Bell**. See [[SP-PBS-03 — Cord, Temper and Trust]].
- **Tone:** warm-lit tavern drama with a hard edge. Old soldiers, old grudges, good beer, and a law everybody in the room will enforce.

## Perrenland Color
*Canon framing; see Canon Check.*

- **The companies' neutral ground.** Per [[Perrenland]], every Perrender serves in the army and many go on to the free companies. Rivals who met across a field in spring drink at the same table in winter, and anyone who draws steel at that table has made enemies of every company in the room. The red cord is how Schwartzenbruin's company taverns keep that peace. *(The cord is homebrew; the custom it enforces is the region report's.)*
- **Contracts are sacred.** Perrenland remembers the company that broke its word. An unanswered breach on the charter-roll is a stain no company survives for long.
- **The three voices.** The confederation's three unofficial voices are the merchant men, the swords and the scholars. A sworn amends under Perrender custom is witnessed by one of each. The students are the scholars.
- **The Witch.** Do not name her at the table. Perrenders say *the Witch* and *the Tears*. Iggwilv's conquest (480–481 CY) is family memory here, not history. See [[Iggwilvs-Conquest-of-Perrenland]].
- **Kord.** The companies keep Kord, loudly. A fist-fight "under Kord" (a formal bout, champion against champion) is how an argument between soldiers is settled without breaking the cord.
- **Money.** Perrenders count in **marks** (gp) and **kleinmarks** (sp). NPCs say "marks."
- **The patrol.** Schwartzenbruin is open to strangers and watches them closely. The border patrol writes down every answer and compares it with the next one.

## Background (the hidden truth)
*Homebrew; see Canon Check.*

**480 CY.** The Witch's legions come out of the Yatils. The von Eisfelds hold a fortified manor at the head of a southern valley under the ice-fields that gave the family its name. **Ritter Wolfram von Eisfeld** has contracted [[The Hundred|the Hundertschaft]] ("the Hundred"), the oldest chartered free company in Perrenland, to hold the manor for a season. Their captain is **Hauptmann Lorenz Kammerer**.

On the last night, Wolfram sees that the manor cannot be held, but the **Kalte Klamm**, the gorge road out of the valley, can, for a few hours. Three hundred people are on that road. Wolfram releases the Hundred from its contract in writing. By Perrender custom, he cuts his standard in half and gives Kammerer one half as proof of honourable release. Then he swears Kammerer to silence "until the head of my house releases you," because he does not want his eight-year-old son **Gerhard** to grow up knowing his father chose the valley over his own house. The Hundred holds the gorge. Wolfram dies on his own wall. Gerhard and his mother get out on the Klamm road behind the Hundred's pikes.

**After.** Gerhard grows up believing that the Hundred abandoned his father and only happened to be on the road. Around 505 CY, he files a complaint of breach against the company with the Council of Hetmen. Kammerer, bound by his oath, does not answer it. The company records the night as its shame and keeps the half-standard in its chest as "the banner we failed." Gerhard takes a frontier land-grant nobody wants around 510 CY, goes north, and builds the Hall at Ritterstadt. He hangs the Eisfeld half-standard among his banners, where the family remembers it as "torn." See [[RG-01 — History of Ritterstadt]].

**576 CY.** A rival company has petitioned the Council of Hetmen to strike the Hundred from the charter-roll under Gerhard's ninety-year-old unanswered complaint. The Hundred's newly elected captain, **[[Hauptfrau Ilse Brandauer]]**, decides to answer it at last with a sworn amends to Gerhard's grandson: a hundred swords for one season, or the return of the 480 contract fee in coin, at Konrad's choice. Her letter reaches Ritterstadt by the Traft carrier.

The company's oldest serving man, **[[Feldwebel Jost Kammerer]]**, is Lorenz's grandson. He has his grandfather's daybook and knows the truth. Accepting false guilt is a lie, and saying why would break the oath. He opposes the amends and can't explain himself, which makes him look like the saboteur.

**The real saboteur** is **[[Ulrich Dornhalm]]**, factor of Dornhalm & Söhne, a Schwartzenbruin timber concern. The Kettenhaus has approached his firm as the buyer for the Eisfeld timber rights if the Schuldbrief forecloses. Hiring-hall gossip says the Hundred is about to hand the Eisfeld debt a hundred swords or a purse of coin. Either would spoil the foreclosure. Dornhalm wants the meeting voided, and the cleanest way is to make Konrad cut his own cord. *(The Kettenhaus knows nothing of this. Per its own note it never threatens and never orders violence. Dornhalm is acting alone.)*

**If the PCs never come:** Konrad goes alone, is baited into drawing steel before the second round of drinks, and comes home in disgrace. The Hundred loses its charter that winter.

## Hook
Pick one, or let each PC take a different one.

1. **The Job Board (default).** A commission under [[Rektor Albrecht Weissmantel]]'s seal, posted by Professor Aldemar Goldwaage of Statecraft & Commerce: *"Wanted, a scholars' witness to a sworn amends under Perrender custom. Schwartzenbruin, three days, passage paid. Field credit."* Goldwaage briefs them in one line: *"The Cantons will not send a garrison. They will send a form. Tonight you are the form."*
2. **The garrison's request.** [[Hauptmann Hildegard Stahlwulf]] finds the party privately: *"He'll go. He'll go wearing his grandfather's sword. Keep his hand off it."*
3. **Personal stake.** A PC with a soldier, mercenary or Perrender background has kin who came out on the Kalte Klamm road in 480, or has served beside a Hundred man. Either way, the PC has a reason to want the story told right.

## PC Objectives (the plot, as goals rather than events)
Each objective has more than one route. Objectives 5 and 6 are optional.

1. **Get Konrad to Schwartzenbruin willing to sit down.** *(Persuade him in the Hall, enlist Hildegard or Vater Sigmund, or appeal to the Perrender law of contract.)*
2. **Keep the cord on Konrad's sword.** *(Calm him, absorb the provocations, answer them with wit, or take the insult on a PC's own honour.)*
3. **Find out who is baiting him.** *(Trace the "timber" remark, shadow the provocateur, read the room, or follow the cutpurse.)*
4. **Recover the Soldbrief before the midnight bell.** *(Win the chase, ambush the cutpurse at the yard, or bargain with Dornhalm's men.)*
5. *(Optional)* **Learn why Jost opposes the amends.** *(Match the two half-standards, win the bout under Kord, persuade Ilse to open the company chest, or get Konrad to lift an oath he does not understand.)*
6. *(Optional)* **See Dornhalm answer for it.** *(Hand the patrol the evidence from the yard, or make him confess in front of the Ernes witness.)*

## Subplots (keyed to PCs; assign at the table)
- **Soldier, mercenary or fighter:** Jost takes this PC for one of his own and drinks with them. He comes as close as his oath allows: *"Some debts are paid by saying nothing. Mine is."* If this PC fights as Konrad's champion, Jost respects the result whatever it is.
- **Noble, sage or anyone proficient in History:** this PC can name the halved-standard custom (History DC 13, or automatically after consulting Lecturer Bertram Falk in Scene 1). It is the cleanest key to the climax.
- **Cleric, acolyte or paladin:** Konrad confesses something to this PC on the boat, not the debt yet, but how much he hates owing anyone. Their counsel is worth +1 Trust once.
- **Criminal, urchin or charlatan:** this PC spots the cutpurse's partner before the brawl (passive Perception 13 or Insight DC 12) and can follow the Soldbrief without losing a step in the chase.
- **Perrender or folk hero:** the landlady of the Red Cord, [[Mutter Ilka Sennhauser]], is Kalte Klamm stock. She tells her grandmother's story to this PC first.

## Act Structure
Likely path: Scenes 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6. Each scene note lists alternates and re-hooks.

### Act 1 — Ritterstadt and the Lake (≈30 min)
- [[Scene 01 — The Ritter's Hall]]: The commission, the Hundred's letter, Konrad's cold refusal, and the "torn" banner among Gerhard's standards, which Konrad brings as his house colours.
- [[Scene 02 — Across the Quag]]: A night on the lake boat. Konrad tells the story his grandfather told him, and the party earns Trust. At the Schwartzenbruin dock, the patrol takes everyone's answers down.

### Act 2 — Under the Red Cord (≈45 min)
- [[Scene 03 — Under the Red Cord]]: The tavern, the cord at the door, the three witnesses, Ilse's dignity, Jost's coldness, and a stranger who knows about the timber. Konrad's Temper climbs.
- [[Scene 04 — The Brawl]]: The provocation boils over. Fists, chairs and tankards are fine; steel is not. In the confusion, a cutpurse lifts the Hundred's 480 Soldbrief, without which the amends cannot be sworn.

### Act 3 — Before the Bell (≈45 min)
- [[Scene 05 — Dornhalm's Yard]]: A chase through the lakeside streets to a timber yard on the Velverdyva quays, and a fight among the log stacks (steel is legal here). The evidence names Dornhalm, and the patrol takes him.
- [[Scene 06 — The Midnight Bell]]: Back at the Red Cord before the bell. Jost refuses the amends. The truth comes out, the apology runs both ways, and Konrad chooses swords or coin.

## Climax at a Glance
See [[Scene 06 — The Midnight Bell]] for the full run. Jost will not let the company swear to a guilt he knows is false, and he cannot say why. Every route below is seeded earlier:

| Solution | What it takes | Seeded in |
|---|---|---|
| **Match the standards** | Lay Konrad's "torn" half beside the half Ilse brings as "the banner we failed." The cut is clean and the halves meet. Name the halved-standard custom (History DC 13, Falk, or Jost's silence when asked). The proof speaks, so Jost never has to. | S1 (the banner in the Hall, Konrad brings it), S3 (Ilse's relic), S1 Falk consult |
| **Lift the oath** | Konrad, as head of his house, releases Jost from "whatever my house bound you to," without knowing what that is. The DC is set by the Trust track. | S2 (Gerhard's memory: "my father on the wall, cutting the banner down"), S3 (Jost's "paid by saying nothing") |
| **Win the bout under Kord** | A PC champion beats Jost in a formal fist-fight. Under Kord, Jost may then hand over the daybook: *"Kord says I must."* | S3/S4 (the Kord ring, Jost's challenge) |
| **Open the company chest** | Persuade Ilse (DC 15, or DC 12 with Mutter Ilka's story) to open the Hundred's sealed records, where Kammerer's release-copy lies. | S3 (Mutter Ilka's grandmother: "the Ritter sent them to us") |
| **Swear it anyway** | Bully the amends through over Jost's objection. It is legal, and the charter is saved, but the truth stays buried and Konrad goes home believing the lie. | — |

**After any of the first four**, the apology turns around. Konrad withdraws Gerhard's complaint before the witnesses, which saves the Hundred's charter. He then makes the choice the Hundred offers as gratitude, not penance: **a hundred swords for one season, or the 480 contract fee (600 marks) in coin.**

## Key NPCs
- [[Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld]] — Lord of Ritterstadt, third of his line. He is proud, measured, and drowning in a debt he has told no one about. He is the noble wronged, and he turns out to be the heir of the wrong.
- [[Hauptfrau Ilse Brandauer]] — Newly elected captain of the Hundred. She is dignified, precise, and determined to clean the company's name on the charter-roll this winter.
- [[Feldwebel Jost Kammerer]] — 76, the Hundred's oldest serving man and grandson of the 480 captain. He is bound by an oath and looks guilty because of it. He is the red herring.
- [[Ulrich Dornhalm]] — Timber factor of Dornhalm & Söhne. He is polite, well-booted, and the saboteur.
- [[Mutter Ilka Sennhauser]] — Landlady of the Red Cord, keeper of the cords, and Kalte Klamm stock.
- **Witnesses** (minor, inline): **Benedikt Roos**, a factor of Zilchus (the merchants' voice), and **Korporal Mattis of the Ernes**, Jürgen of Traft's company (the swords' voice).
- **Dornhalm's people** (minor, inline): **Rudi Brack**, a cashiered sergeant and the hired provocateur, and **Little Fenna**, the cutpurse.
- **At Ritterstadt** (Scene 1 only): [[Rektor Albrecht Weissmantel]], Professor Aldemar Goldwaage, [[Lecturer Bertram Falk]], [[Hauptmann Hildegard Stahlwulf]] and [[Vater Sigmund Wolfherz]].

## Treasure & Rewards
- **Field credits (Konrad's report to Goldwaage, per PC; counts toward the 600-credit limit):** Truth told and the amends sworn before the bell: 40. Amends sworn without the truth: 30. Sworn after the bell: 20. Steel drawn: 10.
- **Extra credit (outside the limit):** a written account of the halved-standard custom, lodged with [[Lecturer Bertram Falk]] (Law & Heraldry), is worth 15 bonus credits each.
- **The cord-knot:** Ilse gives each PC a red cord tied in the Hundred's knot. In any company tavern in Perrenland, it marks the bearer as someone who kept the peace when it mattered. *(This is a courtesy, not a hook.)*
- **Dornhalm's purse** (taken by the patrol as evidence unless the party pockets it first): APL 1–2: 30 gp. APL 3–4: 60 gp and a silver measuring-chain fob (25 gp).
- **Konrad's choice:** the Hundred's 600 marks or its hundred swords go to Konrad, not the party. See the Denouement.
- **Story XP (milestone note):** Award objective XP equal to one Medium encounter at the party's APL for each of these: the cord never cut, the Soldbrief back before the bell, the truth told, and Dornhalm in the patrol's hands.

## Denouement
The amends is sworn and Gerhard's complaint is withdrawn. The Hundred's name comes off the petition, and the rival company's case dies with it. Dornhalm is in a patrol cell, or on a barge south with no firm behind him.

- **Konrad chooses.** *Swords:* the Hundred garrisons Ritterstadt for one season at its own charge, which covers the broken eastern gate until spring. *Coin:* 600 marks, most of the way toward the Kettenhaus Schuldbrief (about 900 gp). Taking the coin means saying, in front of the Zilchus witness, that he needs it. Whichever he picks, he asks the party what they think first. That is the trust he has learned.
- **The banner.** At home, Konrad has the two halves stitched together and hung back above his chair. Gerhard's grudge comes down from the beams with the old thread.
- **Jost** gives the daybook to Konrad, which ends his grandfather's duty. He drinks one more cup with the party and goes back to the company.
- **Nothing is left open.** If the players ask what happens next, the honest answer is *"winter."*
