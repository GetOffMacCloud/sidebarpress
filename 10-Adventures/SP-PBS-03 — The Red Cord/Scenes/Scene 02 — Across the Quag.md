# Scene 02 — Across the Quag

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-03 — The Red Cord/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 01 — The Ritter's Hall]] · Next → [[Scene 03 — Under the Red Cord]]

## Purpose
The Trust scene. Konrad tells his grandfather's story, which contains the second clue without knowing it. At the dock, teach the table that Schwartzenbruin writes down every answer.
**Budget:** 15 minutes. Running long? Cut the patrol to one question.

## Arrival
> *(Draft) The boat keeps the shore in sight, as Quag boatmen always do. Out past the lantern-light, the open water is black and very flat. Konrad sits at the stern with his cloak around his grandfather's sword.*

**To the table:** *"Somewhere past midnight he asks, without looking round, whether any of you know what it is to owe someone. Who answers?"*

## Beats
- **Gerhard's story** (told only if a PC engages; otherwise it surfaces in Scene 3). Konrad says: *"He was eight. The Witch's people were in the valley. He remembered the Hundred's pikes going out the gate, and his mother carrying him down the gorge road, and the last thing he saw, looking back, was his father on the wall, alone, cutting the banner down. He thought it was despair. So did I."*
- **Listening** without judging it: +1 Trust. Pushing him to forgive: Temper +1.
- **Subplot hook.** A cleric or paladin PC hears the confession about owing (not the debt itself). See the main note's Subplots.
- **The dock patrol.** Schwartzenbruin's lake-gate. A sergeant, a priest, and a clerk with a ledger: *"Business? Lodging? Company?"* Each PC answers. **Konrad's answer is "a private matter of my house."** A PC who matches it ("we are witnesses to a private matter") keeps the patrol satisfied. +1 Trust if a PC vouches for him in words that match his. *Mismatched answers:* the patrol notes it and turns up again in Scene 5 (Bell +1 if they detain the party there).

## Clues Available Here
- "Cutting the banner down" → The Hundred did not break its contract
- The patrol's consistency → foreshadows the patrol taking Dornhalm in Scene 5

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** the shore-line of lamps, the flat black middle of the lake
- **Sound:** oars, the Rhennee steersman humming, water against the strakes
- **Smell:** pitch, lake-weed, wet rope
- **Touch:** the cold coming up through the planks
- **Taste:** smoked fish and the steersman's plum spirit

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **No one talks to Konrad.** He tells the story at the Red Cord instead, to Mutter Ilka, in the party's hearing (Scene 3).
- **The party lies to the patrol.** That is legal and unwise. The clerk underlines the lie.
