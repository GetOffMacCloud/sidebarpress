# Scene 02 — Across the Quag

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-03 — The Red Cord/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 01 — The Ritter's Hall]] · Next → [[Scene 03 — Under the Red Cord]]

## Purpose
The Trust scene. Konrad tells his grandfather's story, which carries the second clue without his knowing it. At the dock, teach the table that Schwartzenbruin writes down every answer and compares it with the next one.
**Budget:** 15 minutes. Running long? Cut the patrol to one question.

## The Crossing
> *The lake-boat keeps the shore lamps in sight, as Quag boatmen always do. Past the lantern-light, the open water is black and very flat. The Rhennee steersman hums to himself at the tiller. Konrad sits at the stern with his cloak drawn around his grandfather's sword, facing the way you have come.*

**To the table:** *"Somewhere past midnight, without looking round, he asks whether any of you know what it is to owe someone. Who answers?"*

The crossing is one night along the shore. Nothing on the lake threatens the boat; this is the quiet before the noise. The steersman shares smoked fish and a plum spirit with anyone who sits by him, and he will not row out of sight of land for any money.

## Beats

### Gerhard's story
Konrad tells it only if a PC engages with his question. Read it or paraphrase it, but keep the last sentence whole:

*"He was eight. The Witch's people were in the valley. He remembered the Hundred's pikes going out through the gate, and his mother carrying him down the gorge road in the dark. And the last thing he saw, looking back, was his father on the wall, alone, cutting the banner down. He thought it was despair. So did I."*

| Response | Effect |
|---|---|
| A PC listens and does not judge it (asks a question, or says nothing and stays) | **+1 Trust** |
| A PC tells him to forgive the Hundred, or says the story sounds wrong | **Temper +1.** He stops talking. |
| A PC notes aloud that a man cutting down his own banner is doing something deliberate | Konrad is silent for a long time, then: *"Go to sleep, scholar."* No Trust change. Tick the clue anyway. |

**Subplot (cleric, acolyte or paladin).** Later, Konrad says something to that PC alone, not the debt but its shape: *"I have never been able to bear owing anyone. My grandfather couldn't either."* Good counsel here is worth **+1 Trust** once (it counts as this scene's Trust if the story did not earn one).

### The dock patrol
At dawn, the boat comes in under Schwartzenbruin's lake-gate. The patrol meets every boat: **a sergeant** of the border patrol with a lantern, **a priest** who watches faces, and **a clerk** with a ledger who writes every answer down.

> *A sergeant with a lantern steps onto the planks before the boat is tied. Behind him, a clerk opens a ledger on a stand and wets his pen. "Business. Lodging. Company," the sergeant says, and waits for each of you in turn.*

Each PC answers the three questions. **Konrad answers first:** *"A private matter of my house. The Red Cord. No company."*

| A PC's answer… | Patrol reaction | Record on the card |
|---|---|---|
| Matches Konrad's ("witnesses to a private matter of his house") | The sergeant nods and moves on. | **Matched** |
| Vouches for him in words that fit his ("we are the scholars' witness for the Ritter") | As above, and Konrad notices. **+1 Trust** (once). | **Matched** |
| Contradicts him (names the amends, a debt, or a different business) | The clerk underlines something. The sergeant says, *"We'll talk again, I expect."* | **Mismatched** |
| Is a plain lie | Legal, and unwise. The clerk underlines it twice. | **Mismatched** |

**Development.** If any PC's answer is **Mismatched**, the same sergeant detains the party for questioning when he arrives at the yard in Scene 5 (**Bell +1**). If every answer matched, he trusts them at the yard and takes Dornhalm on their word.

The day in the city passes in one sentence: lodging near the hiring halls, a meal, and the sound of companies coming in off the roads. Konrad keeps to his room.

## Clues Available Here
- "Cutting the banner down" → the Hundred did not break its contract
- The patrol's ledger → foreshadows the patrol taking Dornhalm in Scene 5

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** the shore-line of lamps, the flat black middle of the lake, the lake-gate's towers at dawn
- **Sound:** oars, the steersman humming, water against the strakes, the clerk's pen
- **Smell:** pitch, lake-weed, wet rope
- **Touch:** cold coming up through the planks
- **Taste:** smoked fish and the steersman's plum spirit

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **No one talks to Konrad.** He tells the story at the Red Cord instead, to Mutter Ilka, in the party's hearing (Scene 3). No Trust is earned for it.
- **The party sleeps through the crossing.** The steersman wakes the nearest PC at the second watch: *"Your lord's been sitting there since the lamps. Somebody should."*
- **A PC wants to scout the city.** The day in Schwartzenbruin is free. A PC who asks at the hiring halls hears that a rival company has petitioned to strike the Hundred from the charter-roll, and that "some timber man" has been asking about the Eisfeld business (Investigation or Persuasion DC 13). That is Dornhalm, and the name means nothing yet.
