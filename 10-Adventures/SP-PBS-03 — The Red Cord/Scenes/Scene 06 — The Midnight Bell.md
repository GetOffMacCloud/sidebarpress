# Scene 06 — The Midnight Bell

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-03 — The Red Cord/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 05 — Dornhalm's Yard]]

## Purpose
The climax and denouement. Jost blocks the amends, the truth comes out by whichever route the party has earned, the apology turns around, and Konrad chooses swords or coin. Close every thread.
**Budget:** 25 minutes (15 climax, 10 denouement). Running long? Skip the bout and go to the standards.

## Arrival
> *(Draft) The Red Cord has gone quiet. The fires are low, the squeezebox is in its case, and every company in the room has stayed to watch. The clerk's bell-glass is nearly empty. Ilse stands with the Soldbrief flat under her hand. Across the table, Jost Kammerer has not moved.*

**To the table:** *"Ilse asks the scholars' witness whether the house of Eisfeld is ready. Is it?"*

## Beats
- **Bell check.** See the return-state table in [[SP-PBS-03 — Cord, Temper and Trust]].
- **The block.** Ilse begins the oath of guilt. Jost stands: *"No. Not that oath. Not while I'm in the room."* He will not say why. Temper +1 if it has not already fired.
- **The routes to the truth** (see the main note's *Climax at a Glance*):
  - **Match the standards.** Konrad's half and Ilse's "banner we failed" lie side by side. The cut is straight and the halves meet. Anyone who knows the custom (History DC 13, Falk's line from Scene 1, or Korporal Mattis if asked) can name it: *a lord who releases a company halves his standard.* The room goes silent, and Jost sits down and puts his face in his hands. **This is the cleanest route: the proof speaks, and Jost never breaks his oath.**
  - **Lift the oath.** A PC asks Konrad to release Jost from "whatever my house bound your family to." The DC depends on the Trust track. Konrad says the words, and Jost reads from his grandfather's daybook: Wolfram's release, the gorge, and the oath of silence.
  - **The bout under Kord.** Jost challenges: *"Then Kord decides."* A PC champion fights him unarmed, to yield or drop (Encounter 6; [[SP-PBS-03 — Bestiary]]). If the champion wins, Jost lays the daybook on the table: *"Kord says I must."* If Jost wins, he still sits down, slowly, and the standards route is still open.
  - **Open the chest.** Persuade Ilse (DC 15, or DC 12 with Mutter Ilka's story) to break the company seal on Kammerer's papers. The release-copy is inside.
- **What the truth says** (read from the daybook, or summarise from the standards): Wolfram released the Hundred to hold the Kalte Klamm for three hundred people. He stayed on the wall. He swore the captain to silence so that his son would not grow up knowing his father had chosen the valley over his house.
- **Konrad.** Give him the beat in silence first, and then let a PC speak to him. He realises that his grandfather built the Hall on a grudge and that his great-grandfather died keeping an obligation. He has spent three years failing one.
- **The amends, reversed.** Konrad withdraws Gerhard's complaint before the three witnesses, and the clerk strikes it from the roll. Then Konrad apologises to the Hundred for his house. Ilse strikes the Soldbrief through: *discharged with honour, 480 CY.* Jost gives Konrad the daybook.
- **The gratitude.** Ilse makes the offer anyway, *"for the road, not for the wall"*: **a hundred swords for one season, or the 480 fee, 600 marks.** Trust decides how Konrad chooses (see the mechanic note). At Trust 3, he says aloud, in front of the Zilchus witness, that he owes the Kettenhaus, and asks the party which to take.
- **If they swore the false oath anyway** (no route taken): the charter is saved, but Jost walks out, and Konrad goes home believing the lie. The offer is still made, and the half-banners stay in two chests.

## Denouement
- **The cord-knots.** Ilse gives one to each PC (see Treasure & Rewards).
- **Dornhalm** is in a patrol cell, waiting for the Assembly of Judges. If the party didn't hand him over, he is on the first barge south, and his firm has disowned him by morning.
- **Home.** On the boat, Konrad asks what the University will make of it. Back in the Hall, he has the two halves stitched together and hung above his chair. It is one banner now, with a seam.
- **Credits.** Konrad writes to Goldwaage himself, so the field credits are paid by ending.
- **Nothing is left open.** If a player asks what comes next: *"Winter."*
