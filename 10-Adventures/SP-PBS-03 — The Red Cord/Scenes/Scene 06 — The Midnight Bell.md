# Scene 06 — The Midnight Bell

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-03 — The Red Cord/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 05 — Dornhalm's Yard]]

## Purpose
The climax and denouement. Jost blocks the amends, the truth comes out by whichever route the party has earned, the apology turns around, and Konrad chooses swords or coin. Close every thread.
**Budget:** 25 minutes (15 climax, 10 denouement). Running long? Skip the bout and go to the standards.

## Bell Check
Look at the card as the party comes through the red door, and use the matching box. Return states are in [[SP-PBS-03 — Cord, Temper and Trust]].

> *(Bell 0–5: in time.) The Red Cord has gone quiet. The fires are low, the squeezebox is in its case, and every company in the room has stayed to watch. The clerk's bell-glass is nearly empty. Ilse Brandauer stands at the table with her hands folded. Across from her, Jost Kammerer has not moved from his bench.*

> *(Bell 6: the bell is ringing.) Above the roofs, the cathedral bell has begun to strike. At the clerk's table, the thin man in black is buttoning his coat, and his ledger is already closed.*

One **Persuasion DC 13** holds him, or the Soldbrief slapped down on his table. Otherwise he leaves; go to *After the bell*.

> *(Bell 7+: after the bell.) The clerk's chair is empty, and his bell-glass lies on its side. Ilse has not sat down. "Dawn," she says. "The Hetmen sit at dawn. It can still be done there."*

Run the climax below at the Hetmen's dawn sitting, as a filing. The truth works the same way. The offer becomes **coin only, 300 marks** (Ending C).

**To the table:** *"Ilse asks the scholars' witness whether the house of Eisfeld is ready. Is it?"*

## Beats

### The Soldbrief
Ilse lays the Soldbrief flat before the clerk. If it is singed, the clerk turns it over twice and accepts it. If it burned and the company copy was fetched, the clerk accepts that, with a sniff. Konrad's colors hang above his chair; Ilse's half-banner lies folded by her hand.

### The block
Ilse begins the oath of guilt: *"The Hundred, before the three voices, owns that in the year of the Witch it…"* Jost stands up. *"No. Not that oath. Not while I'm in the room."* He will not say why. If Ilse orders him out, he stays. If anyone threatens him, he says: *"Then Kord decides,"* and steps into the ring of benches.
**Temper +1** if Jost's refusal has not already fired in Scene 3.
Ilse can legally swear over his objection. She looks at the party before she does. **This is the moment the players must act.** Every route below was seeded earlier; check the Clue Ledger to see which ones this table holds.

### Route 1: Match the standards
A PC lays Konrad's half beside Ilse's "banner we failed," or asks to.

> *Laid edge to edge on the table, the two halves meet. The cut runs straight down both, and the faded white and the faded blue go on across the join as if they had never been apart. Nobody in the room is drinking.*

Anyone who knows the custom can name it: **History DC 13**, automatic with Falk's line from Scene 1, or **Korporal Mattis** if a soldier asks him (or, failing all else, if the halves are laid out and nobody can name them, Mattis says it himself). *"A lord who releases a company halves his standard. You don't tear a banner like that. You cut it, so it fits again."*
**Jost sits down and puts his face in his hands.** The proof has spoken, and he has broken no oath. Go to *What the truth says.*
**This is the cleanest route,** and the best ending for Jost.

### Route 2: Lift the oath
A PC asks Konrad to release Jost from "whatever my house bound your family to." Konrad does not know what that is. The check depends on **Trust**:

| Trust | Konrad |
|---|---|
| 0 | Persuasion DC 18 |
| 1 | DC 15 |
| 2 | DC 12 |
| 3 | He does it the moment a PC asks |

Konrad stands, and says it in the old form: *"As head of the house of Eisfeld, I release you from whatever my house bound you to."* Jost takes a small, black, much-mended book from inside his coat ([[Kammerer's Daybook]]) and opens it to a marked page. Go to *What the truth says*, and read the daybook box.

### Route 3: The bout under Kord
Jost's challenge, or a PC's: *"Then Kord decides."* A PC champion fights Jost unarmed in the ring of benches. **Wager it aloud first:** if the champion wins, Jost lays the daybook on the table. If Jost wins, the company swears its oath.
Run **Encounter 6** (below). If the champion wins: *"Kord says I must,"* and he opens the daybook. If Jost wins, he sits down slowly, breathing hard, and **the standards route and the chest are still open.** Losing the bout loses only the bout.

### Route 4: Open the chest
A PC persuades Ilse to break the company's seal on Lorenz Kammerer's papers, which came in the company chest with the banner. **Persuasion DC 15**, or **DC 12** if the PC tells her Mutter Ilka's story (or brings Ilka to tell it). Inside, under the seal, is Kammerer's copy of Wolfram's written release.
Ilse reads it twice, and then reads it aloud to the room. Go to *What the truth says.*

### Route 5: Swear it anyway
If no route is taken, or the party tells Ilse to proceed, she swears the oath over Jost's objection. It is legal, and the charter is saved. **Jost** takes off his company knot, lays it on the table, and walks out into the dark. Go to *The amends*, Ending B.

### Last resort
If the bell-glass is nearly empty and the party has the pieces but has not put them together, **Mutter Ilka** gets up from her chair for the first time all night and tells the whole room her grandmother's story. Then she says to Konrad: *"Lay your colors beside hers, Ritter. Go on."* The cut does the rest (Route 1).

## What the Truth Says
Read this from the daybook or the release-copy, or summarize it from the standards. Keep the last line.

> *"Kalte Klamm, the last night. The Ritter has released us from his walls, in writing, before me and two of my sergeants, and cut his standard and given me the half. He asks us to hold the gorge road while his people go down it, and we will. He has sworn me to say nothing of this until the head of his house releases me. He does not want the boy to know his father chose the valley over the house. God of strength keep him. He is staying on the wall."*

Wolfram released the Hundred to hold the Kalte Klamm for three hundred people. He stayed on his wall and died there. He swore the captain to silence for his son's sake. The Hundred kept its word twice: once on the road, and once for ninety-six years afterward.

**Konrad.** Give him the beat in silence first. He sits down, which he has not done all night. Then let a PC speak to him, and let him answer. His grandfather built the Hall on a grudge, and his great-grandfather died keeping an obligation; Konrad has spent three years failing to admit one. What he says next is the players' to draw out. If nobody speaks to him, he says only: *"Then we owe them. We have always owed them."*

## The Amends, Reversed
1. **Konrad withdraws Gerhard's complaint** before the three witnesses. The clerk strikes it from the roll. The rival company's petition dies with it.
2. **Konrad apologizes to the Hundred** for his house: three generations of slander, in front of every company in Schwartzenbruin. Let a PC help him with the words if he asks.
3. **Ilse strikes the Soldbrief through** and writes beneath it: *discharged with honor, 480 CY.* The clerk signs it. Benedikt Roos writes it down.
4. **Jost gives Konrad the daybook.** His grandfather's duty is done.

### The gratitude
Ilse makes the offer anyway, *"for the road, not for the wall"*: **a hundred swords for one season, at the company's charge, or the 480 fee returned, 600 marks.** How Konrad chooses depends on Trust:

| Trust | Konrad's choice |
|---|---|
| 0 | He decides alone, and takes the swords out of pride. |
| 1 | He decides alone, after a long pause. |
| 2 | He asks the party's opinion, and listens. |
| 3 | He says aloud, in front of the Zilchus witness, that he owes the Kettenhaus of Traft, and asks the party which to take. Roos writes it down and, afterward, shakes his hand. |

Do not steer the choice. *Swords* garrison Ritterstadt through the winter and cover the broken eastern gate. *Coin* pays most of the Schuldbrief. Either is a good ending.

## Encounter 6 — The Bout under Kord (optional)
| APL | Jost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thug chassis, **20 HP** | Fists: two attacks, +4, 1d4 + 2 |
| 2 | Thug chassis, **32 HP** | Fists: two attacks, +4, 1d4 + 2 |
| 3 | Veteran chassis, **40 HP** | Fists: two attacks, +5, 1d4 + 3 |
| 4 | Veteran chassis, **58 HP** | Fists: two attacks, +5, 1d4 + 3 |

Full block, with *Old Knees*, in [[SP-PBS-03 — Bestiary]].

**The ring.** Benches pulled into a 20-ft square, soldiers standing on them.
- One champion, unarmed, no magic, no armor heavier than a coat. A champion's fists deal **1d4 + Str** (the house wraps their knuckles).
- **A second may step in once.** The first champion tags out, and the second takes their place for the rest of the bout.
- **Win** by knockout, by yield, or by **three ring-outs** (a shove that sends the opponent over the benches; the soldiers push them back in).
- **Jost yields** at a quarter of his HP if he is clearly beaten. He never fights dirty.
- **DM direction:** this is the old man's last fight and he knows it. Describe it as a good one whoever wins.

## Denouement
> *(Ending A, B or C.) Ilka pours one more round on the house. Nobody makes a speech. Somebody gets the squeezebox down from the rafters, and it plays something slow.*

- **The cord-knots.** Ilse gives each PC a red cord tied in the Hundred's knot ([[The Hundred's Cord-Knot]]): *"Any company house in Perrenland. Show them that."* (Not in Ending D: the company cannot be seen giving gifts to a cord-cutter's party. Ilse gives them privately at the chapter-house door instead.)
- **Jost** drinks one cup with the party, says *"Thank you, scholars,"* and goes back to his company. In Ending B he is gone.
- **Dornhalm** is in a patrol cell, waiting for the Assembly of Judges. If the party didn't hand him over, he is on the first barge south, and his firm has disowned him by morning.
- **Home.** On the boat, Konrad asks what the University will make of it. Back in the Hall, he has the two halves stitched together and hung above his chair: one banner now, with a seam. *(Ending B: the half-banner goes back up on the beam, "torn.")*
- **Credits.** Konrad writes to Goldwaage himself; the field credits are paid by ending. A PC who writes up the halved-standard custom for Lecturer Falk earns 15 extra credit.
- **Nothing is left open.** If a player asks what comes next: *"Winter."*

- **Sight:** two faded half-banners on one table, a bell-glass running out, and forty soldiers not drinking
- **Sound:** the quarter-bells, then a room so quiet you hear the fire

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **They are back after the bell.** Run it at the Hetmen's dawn sitting (Ending C). The clerk is replaced by a Hetman's secretary who is in a hurry; Roos and Mattis attend, yawning.
- **The cord was cut.** Run it at [[The Hundred's Chapter-House]] at dawn (Ending D). Ilse, Jost and the two half-banners are there; the witnesses are not. The truth is still available, but there is no offer.
- **Konrad refuses to withdraw the complaint** even after the truth (a Temper 5+ Konrad might). A PC can talk him round (Persuasion DC 12, or automatic at Trust 2+). If no one does, Ilse swears no oath at all, the clerk records the truth as read, and the petition fails anyway. The Hundred is cleared; Konrad goes home without an offer, and without the daybook.
