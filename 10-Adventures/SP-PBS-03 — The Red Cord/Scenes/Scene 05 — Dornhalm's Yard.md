# Scene 05 — Dornhalm's Yard

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-03 — The Red Cord/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 04 — The Brawl]] · Next → [[Scene 06 — The Midnight Bell]]

## Purpose
Clear Jost, expose Dornhalm, and win back the Soldbrief with steel, which is legal out here. Hand the saboteur to the patrol so the story closes.
**Budget:** 20 minutes (5 chase, 15 fight). Running long? Replace the chase with one Perception or Survival check (DC 12; failure is Bell +1).

**Bell:** +1 for the trip out (it is now about 4 on the likely path). +1 more if the chase fails.

## Encounter 4 — The Chase
> *The river alley drops between blind walls toward the sound of moving water. Fish-crates are stacked against the walls, and a line of washing hangs across the way at head height. Ahead, a small figure in a dark shawl goes over a crate-pile without slowing down.*

Skill challenge: **3 successes before 2 failures.** Each PC makes one check per round and says how. Two PCs may not use the same approach in the same round.

| Approach | Check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Straight after her: over a wall, through a fish-market stall | Athletics or Acrobatics DC 12 | |
| The right turning; a dropped cord-knot in the gutter | Perception or Survival DC 12 | |
| Guess where she'll cut, from how she runs | Insight DC 12 | |
| Cut her off by magic or a familiar | DC 12 with the spellcasting ability | Harmless magic is fine out here. Steel is legal too, past the Red Cord's door. |
| A criminal, urchin or charlatan PC who spotted her in Scene 3 | none | One automatic success |

- **Success:** the party reaches the yard on Fenna's heels. The case is still in her hands at the gate and goes to Dornhalm in front of them. Fenna drops it and runs if a PC gets adjacent; she gives up the name for a head start if caught.
- **Failure:** they reach the yard after the case is on Dornhalm's desk and the stove is lit. **Bell +1.** Use the second Arrival box.

**Point it out if no one does:** the trail runs to the river quays, not uphill to the Hundred's chapter-house. Whoever took the Soldbrief, it was not for the company.

## Arrival
> *Dornhalm's Yard lies below the lake mouth, where the Velverdyva starts to run. Squared pine is stacked taller than a house in rows, a sawpit gapes in the middle, and a crane boom leans over the water with one log still hanging from its chain. A lamp burns in a tally-office on the river side. A man in very good boots stands in its door.*

> *(Chase failed.) As above, but the man in the good boots is sitting at his desk, and the stove behind him is lit. Its door is open.*

**To the table:** *"He wishes you good evening. Out here, nobody has tied your swords. What do you do?"*

**[[Ulrich Dornhalm]]**, factor of Dornhalm & Söhne, is polite and unafraid. He does not deny the case is here. He says it was *"found in the street, and will be returned to its owners in the proper way, through the Council, in a week or so."* He offers 50 marks for the party to go away. He means it.

## The Yard (key)
**Y1. GATE.** Double timber gates, open. A lamp on a post. **Creatures:** Fenna (1), if the chase succeeded, catching her breath. **Development:** the patrol comes in this way (see below).

**Y2. THE STACKS.** Rows of squared pine 12 ft high with 10-ft lanes between them. Full cover behind a stack. Climbing one is Athletics DC 12. **Toppling** one end of a stack is an action and Strength DC 15: 2d6 bludgeoning in a 10-ft line beyond it (Dex DC 12 halves), and the lane becomes difficult terrain. Each stack can be toppled once.

**Y3. THE SAWPIT.** A pit 8 ft deep and 10 ft by 20 ft, with a two-man saw across it on trestles. A creature shoved in takes 1d6 bludgeoning and lands prone in sawdust. Climbing out is Athletics DC 10.

**Y4. THE BOOM CRANE.** A timber crane on the quay with one log hanging from its chain over the lane by the office. **An action at the lever** drops the log: 3d6 bludgeoning in a 5-ft square (Dex DC 13 halves). It can be dropped once. *Telegraph it:* the log turns slowly on its chain all fight.

**Y5. TALLY-OFFICE.** One room, a desk, a ledger shelf, and an iron stove with its door ajar. The Hundred's document case is on the desk. **Treasure:** see below. **Development:** Dornhalm is here from round 1.

**Y6. THE RIVER EDGE.** The quay drops 4 ft into fast black water. A creature shoved in is swept 20 ft downstream and can climb out on its next turn (Athletics DC 12; failure, swept another 20 ft). Nobody drowns tonight unless they are knocked out in the water; a creature at 0 HP there washes up on the shallows below the yard.

## Encounter 5 — The Yard
| APL | Creatures | Difficulty (4 PCs) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 bandits | Medium (200 adj.) |
| 2 | 1 thug + 3 bandits | Easy+ (350 adj.) |
| 3 | 2 thugs + 1 scout + 2 bandits | Medium (700 adj.) |
| 4 | **yard foreman** (bandit captain) + 2 thugs + 2 bandits | Medium-Hard (1,400 adj.) |

For 5 PCs, add one bandit. For 3 PCs, remove one creature (never the foreman). If **Rudi** escaped the brawl, he is one of the thugs (APL 2+). Statblocks: [[SP-PBS-03 — Bestiary]]. **Out here, everyone uses real weapons.**

**Creatures:** Dornhalm's yard hands (the bandits), sitting on a log by the office with a jug; the thugs, Rudi's kind, waiting to be paid; at APL 3, a **scout** on top of a stack (Y2) with a longbow; at APL 4, the **yard foreman**, a big man in a leather apron, by the crane (Y4).

**Tactics:**
- **Round 1:** the hirelings put themselves between the party and the office. They want time, not blood. The scout shoots from full cover at the first PC who heads for the office.
- **Round 2:** the foreman goes to the lever and drops the log on the lane by the office (Y4). Say it at the top of the round: **"The stove door is open."**
- **Round 3:** Dornhalm puts the Soldbrief into the stove (see below).
- **Morale:** the hirelings surrender when half of them are down, or when Dornhalm is taken. Nobody here will die for Dornhalm's wages. The foreman fights until the log has dropped and he is below half HP, then he wades into the river and lets it take him downstream.

**DM direction:** The stove is the clock inside the clock. Every PC who reaches the office door has a choice: the case, or Dornhalm.

## Beats

### The stove
On round 3 (or round 2 if the chase failed), Dornhalm takes the Soldbrief from its case and feeds it into the stove.
- **A PC within 5 ft** can snatch it out as a reaction or on their turn: **Dex DC 12**. On a success it is whole. On a failure it is singed at one corner but legible, and still serves.
- **If no PC is within reach,** it burns. See *The Soldbrief burns*, below.
- Dornhalm does not fight. If grabbed, he lets go of everything and says: *"Gently. These boots cost more than your tuition."*

### The tally-book and the letter
On the desk, in [[Dornhalm's Tally-Book]], in a neat clerk's hand: *"Brack — 10 marks — the Ritter's cord. Fenna — 3 marks."* Beside it, a letter from [[The Kettenhaus]] inviting Dornhalm & Söhne to bid on the Eisfeld timber rights "should the instrument mature." *(The letter is ordinary business. The Kettenhaus knows nothing of the scheme, and a PC who reads it carefully, Investigation DC 12, sees that it is a form letter with the name written in.)*

### Dornhalm talks
Cornered, with the case gone, he is perfectly frank: *"I bought nothing that wasn't for sale. Your Ritter's temper was cheap. Ten marks."* He explains the timber if asked: the Schuldbrief matures, the rights are sold, and his firm has the only mill on the quays. He offers 50 marks again, for the tally-book. He will not run and will not fight; he would rather be arrested than chased.

### The patrol
Drawn by the noise, the **sergeant from the dock** arrives with four riders (veterans; not fought) at the end of the fight, or three rounds after it starts if it is still going. He listens to everyone, then opens his own small ledger.
- **If the party's dock answers matched:** he takes the tally-book, the letter and Dornhalm on the party's word. *"Theft of a sworn instrument. The Assembly of Judges will want him."* He sends a rider to the Red Cord to tell the clerk the Soldbrief is coming.
- **If any answer was mismatched:** he detains everyone for questioning first, in the tally-office, by lamplight. **Bell +1.** Then he takes Dornhalm anyway.
- **If the party hands him nothing,** he takes Dornhalm on suspicion and lets him go by morning. The firm disowns him regardless.

## Treasure
- **The Soldbrief of 480**, in the document case. It is the only thing the party must carry back.
- **Dornhalm's purse:** APL 1–2, 30 gp in new marks; APL 3–4, 60 gp and a silver measuring-chain fob (25 gp). The patrol takes it as evidence unless the party has pocketed it first. Taking it is theft, and the sergeant's ledger will say so if he sees.
- **Dornhalm's Tally-Book** and the Kettenhaus letter: evidence. They go to the patrol.

## Clues Available Here
- The tally-book; the trail to the river; Dornhalm's own words → it was Dornhalm, not Jost
- **Last resort** (if the party has not connected Dornhalm by the end of the fight): the sergeant arrives with **Fenna** by the collar, caught running with an empty case-strap. She names Dornhalm on the spot.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** pale pine stacked like a city, one lamp, the hanging log turning slowly on its chain
- **Sound:** the river, the chain creaking, a stove door
- **Smell:** fresh sawdust and resin, then burning paper
- **Touch:** splinters, and cold iron on the crane lever
- **Taste:** sawdust in the mouth

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **The party negotiates.** The hirelings will sell the case back for 20 marks and a head start, before Dornhalm reaches the stove. Dornhalm will not sell, and he will not stop them selling.
- **The party is beaten (Ending E).** The hirelings don't kill. They dump the PCs in the river shallows below the yard and leave. The Soldbrief burns. Dornhalm is named only if the party comes back with the patrol (a rider can be fetched from the lake-gate: **Bell +1**).
- **The Soldbrief burns.** Ilse can still swear on the company's own copy in the chapter-house chest, but fetching it costs **Bell +1**. The ceremony runs as written.
- **The party skips the yard** and goes straight back. Without the Soldbrief there is nothing to strike; Ilse sends for the company copy (**Bell +1**), and Dornhalm goes free unless someone names him to the patrol later.
