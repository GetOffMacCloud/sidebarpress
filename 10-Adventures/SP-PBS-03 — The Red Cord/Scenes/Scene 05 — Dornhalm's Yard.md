# Scene 05 — Dornhalm's Yard

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-03 — The Red Cord/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 04 — The Brawl]] · Next → [[Scene 06 — The Midnight Bell]]

## Purpose
Clear Jost, expose Dornhalm, and win back the Soldbrief with steel, which is legal out here. Hand the saboteur to the patrol so the story closes.
**Budget:** 20 minutes (5 chase, 15 fight). Running long? Replace the chase with one Perception check.

## The Chase (Encounter 4)
Skill challenge: **3 successes before 2 failures**. Each PC makes one check per round and describes how.
- Athletics or Acrobatics DC 12: over a wall, through a fish-market stall
- Perception or Survival DC 12: the right alley, a dropped cord-knot
- Insight DC 12, or knowledge of Fenna's habits: guess where she'll cut
- A Criminal PC who spotted her earlier counts as one automatic success
- **Failure:** they reach the yard after the case is locked in Dornhalm's tally-office. **Bell +1.**

Notice that the trail leads to the river quays, **not** to the Hundred's chapter-house. That clears Jost.

## Arrival
> *(Draft) Dornhalm's Yard lies below the lake mouth where the Velverdyva starts to run: stacks of squared pine taller than a house, a sawpit, and a crane boom with one log still hanging from its chain. A lamp burns in the tally-office. A man in very good boots stands in its door.*

**To the table:** *"He wishes you good evening. Out here, nobody has tied your swords. What do you do?"*

Statblocks: [[SP-PBS-03 — Bestiary]]. **[[Ulrich Dornhalm]]** (noble, noncombatant) does not fight. He retreats into the tally-office and tries to burn the Soldbrief in the stove on round 3.
**Terrain:** log stacks (full cover; climbing DC 12; a stack can be toppled with DC 15 Strength, 2d6 bludgeoning in a 10-ft line), the sawpit (8 ft deep), the **boom crane** (an action at the lever drops the hanging log, 3d6 in a 5-ft square, Dex DC 13 halves), and the river edge (a creature shoved in is swept 20 ft downstream).
**Tactics:** the hirelings buy time for the stove. They surrender when half are down. Nobody here will die for Dornhalm's wages.
**DM direction:** The stove is the clock inside the clock. Say "the stove door is open" at the top of round 2.

## Beats
- **The Soldbrief.** It is in the case on the tally-office desk. If Dornhalm reaches the stove, one PC within reach can snatch it (Dex DC 12; on a failure it is singed but legible).
- **The tally-book.** It reads *"Brack — 10 marks — the Ritter's cord. Fenna — 3 marks."* There is also a letter from the Kettenhaus inviting Dornhalm & Söhne to bid on the Eisfeld timber "should the instrument mature." *(The Kettenhaus letter is innocent, ordinary business.)*
- **Dornhalm talks** (if cornered): *"I bought nothing that wasn't for sale. Your Ritter's temper was cheap."*
- **The patrol.** The same sergeant from the dock arrives with four riders, drawn by the noise. He compares answers. If the party's dock answers matched, he takes the tally-book and Dornhalm: *"Theft of a sworn instrument. The Assembly of Judges will want him."* If they didn't match, he detains everyone for a Bell segment first.

## Clues Available Here
- The tally-book; the trail to the river; Dornhalm's own words → it was Dornhalm, not Jost

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** pale pine stacked like a city, one lamp, the hanging log turning slowly on its chain
- **Sound:** the river, the chain creaking, a stove door
- **Smell:** fresh sawdust and resin
- **Touch:** splinters, and cold iron on the crane lever
- **Taste:** sawdust in the mouth

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **The party negotiates.** The hirelings will sell the case back for 20 marks and a head start. Dornhalm will not.
- **The Soldbrief burns.** Ilse can still swear on the company's copy, but it takes a Bell segment to fetch it from the chapter-house.
