# Scene 03 — Under the Red Cord

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-03 — The Red Cord/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — Across the Quag]] · Next → [[Scene 04 — The Brawl]]

## Purpose
Teach the law, seat the witnesses, show Ilse's relic, make Jost look guilty, and let the provocateur work on Konrad until something gives. The Temper track runs.
**Budget:** 30 minutes. Running long? Two provocations instead of four.

## Arrival
> *(Draft) The Red Cord is low, loud and warm, with a long room full of company colours and a fire at each end. Inside the door, an old woman with a lap full of red cord ties a knot through every hilt that passes her. Nobody argues. Above her head, a sign is burned into the beam: WOOD IS KORD'S. STEEL IS MURDER.*

**To the table:** *"She holds out her hand for your swords. What do you give her?"*

## Beats
- **The cord.** [[Mutter Ilka Sennhauser]] ties every blade, Konrad's last and tightest. **Say the law aloud** (see [[SP-PBS-03 — Cord, Temper and Trust]]). Bell starts at **2**.
- **The witnesses.** **Benedikt Roos**, a factor of Zilchus (the merchants' voice), is fat, courteous, and memorizing everything. **Korporal Mattis of the Ernes** (the swords' voice) is lean and bored, and knows the halved-standard custom if anyone asks a soldier. The Council's **clerk** sits with a bell-glass and his ledger.
- **[[Hauptfrau Ilse Brandauer]]** explains the ceremony: the Soldbrief of 480 is presented, the company's guilt is sworn, the document is struck through, and the offer is made. She lays a folded half-banner on the table, "the banner we failed," kept in the company chest for ninety-six years. **Do not unfold it yet** unless a PC asks to. *(If they do, the climax's standards solution is open early. Let them have it.)*
- **[[Feldwebel Jost Kammerer]]** refuses to stand for the oath. *"I'll not swear to it."* Asked why: *"Some debts are paid by saying nothing. Mine is."* Temper +1 the first time. **Let the table suspect him.**
- **Konrad hangs his colours.** If anyone looks at both halves side by side, see Scene 6.
- **Mutter Ilka's story** (to a Perrender/folk-hero PC first, or to anyone who asks about the Hundred): *"My grandmother came out on the Klamm road. She said the Ritter sent them to us. Everyone else says they ran. She was there. Everyone else wasn't."*
- **The provocateur.** **Rudi Brack**, a cashiered sergeant, drinks alone and talks loudly. He works through barbs, one every few minutes:
  1. *"Eisfeld. Isn't that the lot that ran north?"* (+1)
  2. *"Funny thing, a Ritter needing a free company's charity."* (+1)
  3. *"How's the timber, Ritter? Holding up?"* (**+2**. The clue is that only someone who knows about the Schuldbrief would say it.)
  4. He names the Witch, deliberately (+1).
- **Reading Rudi.** Insight DC 12: he is working, not drunk. Ilka (if asked): *"Never seen him with a company. Paid in new coin."* A criminal-background PC spots **Little Fenna**, the cutpurse, watching the Hundred's document case (passive Perception 13).
- **The breaking point.** When Temper reaches **5**, or after the fourth barb, Rudi throws the first punch, at a PC if one has been answering him, or at Konrad if not. → Scene 4.

## Clues Available Here
- The timber remark; Rudi's new coin; Ilka's "never seen him with a company" → someone is baiting Konrad on purpose
- Ilka's grandmother; Jost's line; Ilse's relic; Mattis's custom → the Hundred did not break its contract
- The clerk's bell-glass; Ilka's "Clerk goes home at the bell" → the deadline

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** company colours, two fires, and a cord-board bristling with sheathed steel
- **Sound:** a dozen dialects, dice, and a squeezebox that stops when Konrad's name is spoken
- **Smell:** dark beer, roast pork, wet wool drying
- **Touch:** the knot on the hilt, too tight to slip with one hand
- **Taste:** Ilka's black beer, which is better than anything in Ritterstadt

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **A PC answers every barb.** That is excellent. Rudi turns on that PC, and the brawl starts on them instead.
- **A PC confronts Rudi early.** He shouts that the Ritter's people are threatening him, which raises Temper +1. Proof of the coin or the timber remark shuts him up and sends him to the door. Fenna moves anyway, in the confusion.
- **Konrad cuts his cord.** See *Designed partial failure* in the mechanic note.
