# Scene 03 — Under the Red Cord

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-03 — The Red Cord/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — Across the Quag]] · Next → [[Scene 04 — The Brawl]]

## Purpose
Teach the law, seat the witnesses, show Ilse's relic, make Jost look guilty, and let the provocateur work on Konrad until something gives. The Temper track runs all scene.
**Budget:** 30 minutes. Running long? Use barbs 1 and 3 only.

## The Door
> *The Red Cord is low, loud and warm: one long room under black beams hung with company colours, and a fire at each end. Inside the red door, an old woman sits with a lap full of red cord. Every hilt that passes her gets a knot through it, and nobody argues. Burned into the beam above her chair are the words WOOD IS KORD'S. STEEL IS MURDER.*

**To the table:** *"She holds out her hand for your swords. What do you give her?"*

**[[Mutter Ilka Sennhauser]]** ties a red cord through every hilt and scabbard-mouth, unstrings every bow and hangs it on the cord-board, and pats down anyone who looks like a knife-carrier (she is usually right). Konrad's cord goes on last, and tightest.

**Say the law aloud, word for word** (from [[SP-PBS-03 — Cord, Temper and Trust]]):
1. Fists, chairs, tankards and wood are Kord's. Steel is murder.
2. Cut your cord and draw, and every company in this room is your enemy.
3. Magic that harms or binds is steel.

**Start the Bell at 2.** Put the card where the players can see it.

## Who Is Here
Forty soldiers from half a dozen companies, drinking. The ones who matter sit near the clerk's table.

| Who | Wants | Knows | Reacts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[[Hauptfrau Ilse Brandauer]]**, captain of the Hundred | The breach answered before the Hetmen's winter review | The ceremony; the company's own record of its shame | Courteous and exact. Rebukes a provocateur once if a PC appeals to her (Temper −1). |
| **[[Feldwebel Jost Kammerer]]**, 76 | That the company not swear a false guilt | The truth, and his oath | Silent, then one line, then a challenge under Kord (Scene 6) |
| **[[Benedikt Roos]]**, factor of Zilchus (merchants' voice) | A clean record for his house | The Eisfeld-debt gossip came from his clerks | Writes down every promise. Leaves rather than witness a cord cut. |
| **[[Korporal Mattis]]** of the Ernes (swords' voice) | His beer | The halved-standard custom | Names the custom only if a soldier asks him |
| **The Council's clerk**, a thin man in black | To go home at midnight | The rules of a sworn instrument | Taps his bell-glass; reminds anyone who asks: *"Midnight, sirs."* |
| **[[Rudi Brack]]**, alone at a side table | His 10 marks | Dornhalm hired him; the timber remark | Baits Konrad on a schedule (below) |
| **[[Little Fenna]]**, in a dark shawl by the kitchen door | Out of the city | The way to the yard | Watches the Hundred's document case |

## Beats

### The ceremony
Ilse rises when Konrad's party is seated and explains it once, fully, and will not repeat herself:
- The **[[The Soldbrief of 480|Soldbrief of 480]]**, the company's copy of the contract, is presented to the clerk.
- The company swears its guilt before the three voices.
- The clerk strikes the Soldbrief through, and the breach is closed on the charter-roll.
- The offer is made: a hundred swords for one season, or the 480 fee returned. The house chooses.
- *"Before the bell. The clerk does not stay past it, and the Council does not reopen what the clerk has closed."* (Ilka, from her chair: *"Clerk goes home at the bell. Always has."*)

She lays a folded half-banner beside the Soldbrief's case: **"the banner we failed,"** kept in the company chest since 480, which the company now returns to the house it failed. She does not unfold it.
**If a PC asks to unfold it,** she allows it. It is white over ice-blue, and one edge is cut straight. *If Konrad's half is also on view,* the standards route of Scene 6 is open now. Let the players have it. Run Scene 6's standards beat here if they press it, and move Jost's block and the oath to after the brawl.

### Konrad hangs his colours
Custom puts each party's colours over its end of the table. Konrad hangs the Eisfeld half-banner on a nail above his chair.

> *(If both halves are visible at once.) Two half-banners now hang a table's length apart, one above Konrad and one folded under Ilse's hand. Both are white over ice-blue. Both are faded the same way.*

Do not prompt beyond this box. A PC who compares them closely sees that both have one straight edge.

### Jost refuses
When Ilse calls the company to stand for the oath, every Hundred man stands except one. **Jost** stays seated. *"I'll not swear to it."* Ilse orders him to stand. He doesn't. Asked why, by anyone, he says only: *"Some debts are paid by saying nothing. Mine is."* He will say nothing else.
**Temper +1** (once): Konrad hears it as the company refusing even its apology.
**Let the table suspect him.** He is old, stubborn, hostile, and in the right. The yard clears him in Scene 5.
**Subplot (soldier, mercenary or fighter).** If that PC drinks with Jost later in the scene, he comes as close as his oath allows: *"My grandfather was a better man than anyone in this room knows. That's all I'm allowed."*

### Mutter Ilka's story
Ilka tells it to a Perrender or folk-hero PC first, unasked, when that PC sits near her chair. Anyone else gets it by asking her about the Hundred or about 480:

*"My grandmother came out on the Klamm road. She was six. She said the Ritter sent them to us. Everyone else says they ran. She was there, and everyone else wasn't."*

She has never told Konrad. Nobody from his family has ever asked her.

### The provocateur
**Rudi Brack** drinks alone and talks loudly. He works through his barbs, one every few minutes of play (roughly every two or three exchanges at the table). Each is aimed at Konrad. Give the table a beat to answer before Konrad does.

| # | Barb | Temper |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *"Eisfeld. Isn't that the lot that ran north?"* | +1 |
| 2 | *"Funny thing, a Ritter needing a free company's charity."* | +1 |
| 3 | *"How's the timber, Ritter? Holding up?"* | **+2** |
| 4 | He names the Witch, by name, deliberately. The squeezebox stops. | +1 |

**Barb 3 is the clue.** Konrad goes white. Only someone who knows about the Kettenhaus Schuldbrief and the timber it is secured on could say it, and Konrad has told no one. A PC with Trust 2+ can see that it landed (Insight DC 10). Konrad, asked later, says only: *"He should not know that."*

**Answering the barbs.** Each barb can be defused before Konrad answers (Persuasion, Performance or Intimidation DC 12: **Temper −1**). A PC who takes the insult on their own honour makes Rudi turn on *them*. That is excellent play, and the brawl will start on that PC.
**Speaking to Konrad quietly** (Insight or Persuasion DC 12, or DC 10 at Trust 2+): **Temper −1**, once per barb.
**At Temper 5**, describe his hand on the cord: *"His thumb is under the knot."* Give the table one last chance.

### Reading Rudi
- **Insight DC 12:** he is working, not drunk. His eyes keep going to the kitchen door.
- **Ilka, if asked:** *"Never seen him with a company. Paid in new coin. Bright as fish-scales."*
- **His purse:** new Schwartzenbruin marks, unworn (Perception DC 12 when he pays, or automatic if a PC sits with him).
- **Fenna:** a criminal, urchin or charlatan PC spots her watching the Hundred's document case (passive Perception 13, or Insight DC 12 if they are watching Rudi). Any other PC who says they are watching the room makes passive Perception 15.

### Room talk (d8, for any PC who mingles)
| d8 | Overheard | Truth |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "The Grey Lances petitioned to strike the Hundred. They want the Hundred's winter contracts." | True |
| 2 | "The Hundred's buying off the Eisfeld debt, swords or coin. Heard it at a factor's house." | Partly true (the offer is real; the "debt" in the gossip is the Schuldbrief) |
| 3 | "Old Jost won't swear. Never would. Thinks he's better than the company." | False (the company's view) |
| 4 | "Kammerer, the 480 captain? Kept a book of every night of his life. Took it to his grave, I heard." | False (Jost has it) |
| 5 | "That red-faced one got cashiered from the Blue Pikes for selling their muster lists." | True (Rudi) |
| 6 | "Fight under Kord, and the loser does what the winner says. That's law, not custom." | Partly true (it binds only what was wagered) |
| 7 | "The Ernes sent Mattis because Jürgen's in his bed with a cold. He's furious about it." | True but unrelated |
| 8 | "Some timber firm's been sniffing round the Eisfeld business. Dornhalm's lot." | True. The name means nothing until the yard. |

### The breaking point
When Temper reaches **5**, or after barb 4, Rudi throws the first punch: at the PC who has been answering him, if one has, or at Konrad if not. → [[Scene 04 — The Brawl]].

**Development.**
- If a PC confronts Rudi early, he shouts that the Ritter's people are threatening him (**Temper +1**). Proof of the new coin or the timber remark shuts him up, and he heads for the door. On the way, he shoulders a Yatil Mountaineer into the fire, and the brawl starts anyway. Fenna moves in the confusion.
- If the party watches Fenna openly, she waits. The theft happens in round 2 of the brawl regardless; the watching PC simply sees it.
- If Konrad reaches **Temper 6**, he cuts his cord. Run *Designed partial failure* from the mechanic note. Do not run the brawl; move Fenna's theft into the silence that follows.

## Clues Available Here
- The timber remark; Rudi's new coin; Ilka's "never seen him with a company"; room talk 5 and 8 → someone is baiting Konrad on purpose
- Ilka's grandmother; Jost's line; Ilse's relic; Mattis's custom → the Hundred did not break its contract
- The clerk's bell-glass; Ilse's "before the bell"; Ilka's "Clerk goes home at the bell" → the deadline

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** company colours, two fires, and a cord-board bristling with sheathed steel
- **Sound:** a dozen dialects, dice, and a squeezebox that stops when Konrad's name is spoken
- **Smell:** dark beer, roast pork, wet wool drying
- **Touch:** the knot on the hilt, too tight to slip with one hand
- **Taste:** Ilka's black beer, which is better than anything in Ritterstadt

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **A PC answers every barb.** Rudi turns on that PC, and the brawl starts on them instead.
- **No one engages Ilka.** She tells her story to the room at the last quarter-bell in Scene 6 (the Clue Ledger's last resort).
- **The party never asks Mattis anything.** He mentions the custom himself in Scene 6 if a PC is a soldier, or if the standards are laid side by side and nobody can name what they are looking at.
- **Konrad cuts his cord.** See *Designed partial failure* in [[SP-PBS-03 — Cord, Temper and Trust]].
