# Scene 01 — The Ritter's Hall

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-03 — The Red Cord/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 02 — Across the Quag]]

## Purpose
Get Konrad to agree to go, and get the half-banner onto the boat. Plant the climax key, the clean cut, as set dressing.
**Budget:** 15 minutes. Running long? Skip the Falk consult, and have Hildegard take the banner down herself.

## Arrival
> *(Draft) The Ritter's Hall smells of pine smoke and wet dog. Banners hang from the black beams, a dozen of them, faded to the colour of tea. Below them, Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld stands at the hearth with a letter in his hand and does not offer anyone a seat.*

**To the table:** *"He reads the letter to the end. Then he says: 'They want to apologise. Ninety-six years late.' Which of you answers him?"*

## Beats
- **The commission.** Goldwaage's Job Board notice, under [[Rektor Albrecht Weissmantel]]'s seal (Hook 1), or Hildegard's private word (Hook 2). Students serve as the **scholars' witness**. Passage, lodging and field credit are covered.
- **The letter.** Give the players [[SP-PBS-03 — Handout — The Hundred's Letter]]. Konrad's first answer: *"A von Eisfeld does not take alms from the men who left my great-grandfather on his wall."*
- **Persuading him** (Objective 1). The routes:
  - Appeal to contract law: refusing a lawful amends leaves the matter open forever (Persuasion DC 12; +1 Trust).
  - [[Hauptmann Hildegard Stahlwulf]] or [[Vater Sigmund Wolfherz]], if the party enlists them (automatic success).
  - Pragmatism: the town needs swords or coin (DC 15, since this route touches his finances; no Trust).
- **The banner.** Among Gerhard's standards hangs a half-banner in the Eisfeld colours. The family calls it "torn." *Any PC who looks closely:* the edge is a straight, clean cut, made by a blade, not by tearing. Konrad: *"My grandfather carried it north. It was all he carried."* Custom requires both parties to show their colours at an amends, so this is the one he takes. *(If no one asks, Hildegard takes it down and folds it into his saddlebag.)* +1 Trust if a PC asks about it and listens.
- **Gerhard's sword.** Konrad buckles on his grandfather's longsword. Hildegard catches a PC's eye.
- **Optional: [[Lecturer Bertram Falk]].** A PC who asks Law & Heraldry about Perrender amends gets the halved-standard custom outright: *"A lord who releases a company halves his standard. The company keeps the half as proof. Heraldry is just law that learned to dress."* Falk does not connect it to the Hall's banner, because he has not been asked.

## Clues Available Here
- The clean cut → The Hundred did not break its contract
- Falk's custom → The Hundred did not break its contract (and the climax's cleanest solution)

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** tea-coloured banners, a hearth that never goes out, two wolfhounds, and a letter sealed with a red cord-knot
- **Sound:** the hounds' tails on the boards, rain on the shingles
- **Smell:** pine smoke, wet dog, old wool
- **Touch:** the banner's cut edge, which is smooth enough to run a thumb along
- **Taste:** Konrad's thin ale, offered only once he has agreed to go

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **Konrad refuses outright.** Hildegard goes over his head to Vater Sigmund, who simply walks him to the Landing. *"You'll go, Konrad. Your grandfather couldn't."*
- **The party skips the banner.** It travels anyway (Hildegard packs it). The clean cut can be noticed again at the Red Cord when Konrad hangs it.

- → [[Scene 02 — Across the Quag]]: a Rhennee lake-boat at the Landing, the evening wind, and Konrad saying nothing until the shore is gone.
