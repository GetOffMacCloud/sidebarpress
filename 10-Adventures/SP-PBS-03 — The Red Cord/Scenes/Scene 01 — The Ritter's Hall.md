# Scene 01 — The Ritter's Hall

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-03 — The Red Cord/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 02 — Across the Quag]]

## Purpose
Get Konrad to agree to go, and get the half-banner onto the boat. Plant the climax key, the clean cut, as set dressing that nobody is told to look at.
**Budget:** 15 minutes. Running long? Skip the Falk consult, and have Hildegard take the banner down herself.

## Before the Hall (Hook 1 only)
The commission hangs on the Job Board at [[The Fiend's Rest Public House]] under [[Rektor Albrecht Weissmantel]]'s seal. [[Professor Aldemar Goldwaage]] (Statecraft & Commerce) signs the students out in one line: *"The Cantons will not send a garrison. They will send a form. Tonight you are the form."* He adds, if asked, that a sworn amends under Perrender custom needs three witnesses (merchants, swords and scholars), and that the University has agreed to be the third. Passage on the Rhennee lake-boat, two nights' lodging and field credit are covered.

**Common-room talk (d6, while the party reads the notice).** Roll or choose; each speaker says it in their own voice.

| d6 | Rumor | Truth |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "The Ritter got a letter tied with a red string. Hildegard's been in a mood since." | True |
| 2 | "The von Eisfelds came north because a witch burned them out. Don't say her name in the Hall." | True |
| 3 | "That company left old Wolfram to die on his wall. Everyone knows it." | False (what the town believes) |
| 4 | "Konrad's borrowed money from somebody in Traft. That's why the quay got built so fast." | True (the Kettenhaus Schuldbrief; Konrad has told no one) |
| 5 | "Schwartzenbruin writes your name down if you sneeze at the gate." | True |
| 6 | "Perrender mercenaries fight with their fists in taverns. It's a religion with them." | Partly true (the bout under Kord) |

## Arrival
> *The Ritter's Hall smells of pine smoke and wet dog. A dozen banners hang from the black beams, faded to the color of weak tea, and two wolfhounds lie across the hearthstones. Rain ticks on the shingles. Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld stands at the fire with a letter in his hand, a length of red cord looped over his wrist. He has not offered anyone a seat.*

**To the table:** *"He reads the letter to the end. Then he says, 'They want to apologize. Ninety-six years late.' Which of you answers him?"*

The Hall is Konrad's seat and Ritterstadt's court of first resort ([[The Ritter's Hall]]). The banners are Gerhard's: the standards of the southern manor's vassals, carried north in 510 and never taken down. One of them is a half-banner in the Eisfeld colors, white over ice-blue.

## Who Is Here
- **[[Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld]]** (Trust 0, Temper 1 from the moment he reads the letter). *Wants* to be left alone with a grudge he inherited. *Knows* his grandfather's version of 480, and the size of his debt to [[The Kettenhaus]], which he will not mention. *Reacts:* refuses first, argues second, and agrees only to an argument he can respect.
- **[[Hauptmann Hildegard Stahlwulf]]**, captain of the garrison, by the door. She has already decided he is going. *Reacts:* she backs any PC who argues from law or duty, and she says nothing in front of Konrad about the sword.
- **[[Vater Sigmund Wolfherz]]**, if the party sends for him. He has known Konrad since the boy's naming and is the one man who can tell him what to do.
- **The hounds** (2), old and friendly. They thump their tails for anyone who sits on the floor.

## Beats

### The letter
Hand the players [[SP-PBS-03 — Handout — The Hundred's Letter]]. Konrad's first answer is flat: *"A von Eisfeld does not take alms from the men who left my great-grandfather on his wall."* He is not shouting. He is waiting to hear whether these students are worth arguing with.

### Persuading him (Objective 1)
Any one of these routes works. Let the players find the argument; give the check only when they have made it.

| Route | Check | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **The law of contract.** Refusing a lawful amends leaves the breach open forever, and the von Eisfelds' complaint dies unanswered with him. | Persuasion DC 12 | He agrees. **+1 Trust.** *"Then I will hear them say it."* |
| **Hildegard or Vater Sigmund**, if a PC enlists them first. | none | He agrees, irritated. No Trust. |
| **Pragmatism.** The town needs swords for the broken eastern gate, or coin. | Persuasion DC 15 (it touches his finances) | He agrees, coldly, and Temper stays at 1. No Trust. |
| **Honor.** A von Eisfeld who will not face the men who wronged him looks afraid of them. | Intimidation or Persuasion DC 13 | He agrees, and Temper rises to 2. *That* is the man who will need watching. |

**Escalation if every argument fails:** he refuses → Hildegard asks for a word with him alone → Vater Sigmund arrives within the hour and walks him to the Landing (*"You'll go, Konrad. Your grandfather couldn't."*). He never fails to go. The party's arguments decide only how he goes.

### The banner
Once Konrad has agreed, he says custom requires both parties to show their colors at an amends, and he looks up at the beams. A PC who follows his look sees the half-banner.

> *(If a PC asks to see it, or stands under it.) The half-banner hangs lower than the rest, white over ice-blue, the Eisfeld colors, and it is exactly half a banner. The near edge is not ragged. It runs straight from top to bottom, and the threads lie flat and even along it.*

The edge is a blade-cut, made in one stroke along the weave. **Investigation DC 10** or a PC who runs a thumb along it: *"This wasn't torn. Somebody cut it, carefully."* Konrad: *"My grandfather carried it north. It was all he carried. We call it the torn one."* He has never looked at the edge.
**+1 Trust** if a PC asks about the banner and listens to his answer without arguing with it.
**If no one asks,** Hildegard takes it down and folds it into his saddlebag. The cut can be noticed again in Scene 3, when he hangs his colors.

### Gerhard's sword
Konrad takes down a plain, old longsword in a black scabbard and buckles it on. It was his grandfather's ([[Gerhard's Longsword]]). Hildegard catches one PC's eye across the Hall and holds it for a moment. If that PC goes to her afterward, she says it plainly: *"He'll go wearing it. Keep his hand off it."* (This is Hook 2, if it was not the party's hook already.)

### Optional: Lecturer Bertram Falk
A PC who asks the University about Perrender amends before sailing finds **[[Lecturer Bertram Falk]]** (Law & Heraldry) in his rooms, delighted to be asked anything. He gives the halved-standard custom outright: *"A lord who releases a company halves his standard. The company keeps one half as proof that it left with leave. Heraldry is just law that learned to dress."*
Falk does not connect it to the banner in the Hall, because nobody has asked him about the banner. If a PC describes the cut edge to him, he sits up: *"Then somebody in that family was released, or released someone. Go and find out which."*
A PC who consults Falk needs no History check to name the custom in Scene 6.

**Development.** If the party has the custom and the cut edge both, they hold the climax's cleanest key before leaving Ritterstadt. Let them. Scene 6 is still a scene: Jost, the bell, and Konrad's face are all still to come.

## Clues Available Here
- The clean cut → the Hundred did not break its contract
- Falk's custom → the Hundred did not break its contract (and the climax's cleanest solution)
- Rumor 4 → Konrad owes money in Traft (why the timber remark lands in Scene 3)

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** tea-colored banners, a hearth that never goes out, two grey wolfhounds, and a letter tied with red cord
- **Sound:** the hounds' tails on the boards, rain on the shingles
- **Smell:** pine smoke, wet dog, old wool
- **Touch:** the banner's cut edge, smooth enough to run a thumb along
- **Taste:** Konrad's thin ale, offered only once he has agreed to go

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **Konrad refuses outright.** Vater Sigmund walks him to the Landing (see Escalation). No Trust is earned, and Konrad sails in silence.
- **The party skips the banner.** It travels anyway (Hildegard packs it), and the cut can be noticed in Scene 3.
- **The party wants to read the old complaint.** The Ritterstadt copy of Gerhard's 505 filing is in the Hall chest: four angry lines about "abandonment," and nothing about a release. It proves only that Gerhard believed what he wrote.
