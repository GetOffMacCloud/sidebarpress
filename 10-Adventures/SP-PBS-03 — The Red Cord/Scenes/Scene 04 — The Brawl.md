# Scene 04 — The Brawl

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-03 — The Red Cord/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 03 — Under the Red Cord]] · Next → [[Scene 05 — Dornhalm's Yard]]

## Purpose
A loud, legal, joyful fight that is secretly a theft. The Soldbrief leaves the room. The test is whether anyone on Konrad's side draws steel.
**Budget:** 15 minutes. Running long? Ilka ends it at the end of round 2.

## Arrival
> *(Draft) Rudi's tankard misses. It hits a Yatil Mountaineer, and the Mountaineer stands up. Then the whole room stands up.*

**To the table:** *"Somebody swings a bench at you. What's in your hands?"*

## Encounter 3 — The Brawl
| APL | Creatures | Difficulty (4 PCs) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 bandits | Medium (200 adj.) |
| 2 | 2 thugs + 2 bandits | Medium (500 adj.) |
| 3 | 3 thugs + 2 bandits | Medium (700 adj.) |
| 4 | 4 thugs + 2 bandits | Easy+ (900 adj.) |

Statblocks: [[SP-PBS-03 — Bestiary]]. Rudi is one of the thugs (or a bandit at APL 1). Every creature fights **unarmed or improvised: 1d4 + Str bludgeoning**. Damage is nonlethal by house custom, so a creature reduced to 0 is knocked out.

**Terrain:** benches (difficult terrain once overturned), two long tables (climb onto them for +1 to hit against someone on the floor), the hearths (a creature shoved into one takes 1d6 fire, *legal, but Ilka will remember*), and the cord-board by the door.
**Tactics:** Rudi's hirelings go for whoever has been answering him. The rest of the room brawls for fun and ignores the PCs unless they're hit. **Konrad stays out** at Trust 2+, or wades in at Trust 0–1 (Temper +1 per round he is hit).
**DM direction:** Play it as a comedy with one serious thread. Every round, show a cord under strain somewhere in the room.

## Beats
- **Round 2: the theft.** **Little Fenna** lifts the Hundred's document case from Ilse's table and slips out the kitchen door. *Passive Perception 13*, or automatic for the PC who spotted her in Scene 3. Anyone watching the case sees it go.
- **The temptation.** One of Rudi's men pulls a hidden knife (APL 3–4), *illegally*. If a PC answers steel with steel, that PC has cut the cord. If they disarm him bare-handed, the room cheers and the Ernes witness takes note (+1 Trust).
- **End of round 3:** Ilka's two bouncers, both retired Ernes, end it. Hirelings still standing make for the door.
- **The discovery.** Ilse finds the case gone: *"The Soldbrief. Without it there is nothing to strike."* The clerk looks at his bell-glass. **Bell +1.**

## Clues Available Here
- Fenna moved on Rudi's signal → the brawl was cover
- Rudi's men head for the river, not the hiring halls

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** flying benches, a squeezebox in mid-air, and one old woman perfectly still by the door
- **Sound:** cheering, wood breaking, the hollow clunk of a tankard on a skull
- **Smell:** spilled beer and scorched wool
- **Touch:** a knuckle split on someone's helmet
- **Taste:** blood and beer

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **Nobody notices Fenna.** Ilse's cry tells them. Ilka saw the kitchen door swing: *"River way."*
- **The party catches Fenna before she leaves.** Skip the chase. She has already passed the case to a runner through the kitchen window, so it is still the yard, but the party has a prisoner who will talk (Intimidation DC 12: *"Dornhalm's yard. He pays in new coin."*).
