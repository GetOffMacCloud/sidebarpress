# Scene 04 — The Brawl

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-03 — The Red Cord/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 03 — Under the Red Cord]] · Next → [[Scene 05 — Dornhalm's Yard]]

## Purpose
A loud, legal, joyful fight that is secretly a theft. The Soldbrief leaves the room. The test is whether anyone on Konrad's side draws steel.
**Budget:** 15 minutes. Running long? Ilka ends it at the end of round 2.

## Arrival
> *Rudi's tankard misses. It hits a Yatil Mountaineer on the back of the head, and the Mountaineer stands up, and then the whole room stands up. A bench goes over. Someone is laughing. By the door, Mutter Ilka folds her hands in her lap and does not move.*

**To the table:** *"Somebody swings a bench at you. What's in your hands?"*

Roll initiative. Remind the table of the law in one line: *fists, chairs and wood are legal; steel and harmful magic are not.*

## Encounter 3 — The Brawl
| APL | Creatures | Difficulty (4 PCs) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rudi** (bandit) + 3 bandits | Medium (200 adj.) |
| 2 | **Rudi** (thug) + 1 thug + 2 bandits | Medium (500 adj.) |
| 3 | **Rudi** (thug) + 2 thugs + 2 bandits | Medium (700 adj.) |
| 4 | **Rudi** (thug) + 3 thugs + 2 bandits | Easy+ (900 adj.) |

For 5 PCs, add one bandit. For 3 PCs, remove one bandit. Statblocks and the **brawl swap** are in [[SP-PBS-03 — Bestiary]]: every hireling fights unarmed or with improvised weapons (1d4 + Str bludgeoning), and a creature reduced to 0 HP is knocked out, not killed.

**PCs in the brawl.** A PC's unarmed strike or improvised weapon (a chair, a tankard, the poker) deals 1d4 + Str bludgeoning. Staves and cudgels are wood and legal at their normal damage. A PC who knocks a creature out with a legal blow need not declare it nonlethal; the house custom does that.
**Spells.** Cantrips that harm no one are rude but legal (see the mechanic note). Anything that deals damage, charms or frightens is steel.

### The room
- **Benches** become difficult terrain once overturned. A PC can upend one as a bonus action.
- **Two long tables.** A creature standing on a table gains +1 to hit against creatures on the floor, and can be shoved off it (prone).
- **The hearths**, one at each end. A creature shoved into one takes 1d6 fire. *Legal, but Ilka will remember.*
- **The cord-board** by the door, hung with forty corded swords. Any creature that reaches it can take one down. Using it is cutting a cord.
- **The kitchen door** at the far end, and the river alley beyond it.
- **The rest of the room** (about thirty soldiers) brawls for fun. They ignore the PCs unless a PC hits one of them, in which case that PC spends the next round with a stranger (one Athletics or Acrobatics DC 12 to shake them off, no damage either way).

### Tactics
- **Rudi's hirelings** go for whoever has been answering Rudi, or for Konrad. They want Konrad hit, humiliated, and near the cord-board.
- **Rudi** throws the first punch, fights for two rounds, and slips out the front door at the end of round 2. A PC adjacent to him can stop him with a grapple (escape DC 12 at APL 1, DC 13 at APL 2+).
- **Konrad** stays out at Trust 2+ (he stands by Ilse's table, arms folded, and lets the party fight for him). At Trust 0–1 he wades in. **Temper +1 for each round in which he is hit.** A PC who stays adjacent to him and stands between him and the fight ("I keep in front of him") keeps him from being targeted that round.

## Round by Round
| Round | What happens |
|---|---|
| **1** | Rudi's punch, and the room explodes. Every round, show one cord under strain somewhere: a Mountaineer's thumb on a knot, a hilt half out of a scabbard, Ilka watching it. |
| **2** | **The theft.** Little Fenna takes the Hundred's document case from Ilse's table and slips out the kitchen door. A PC who spotted her in Scene 3 sees it automatically; anyone else notices on passive Perception 13. At APL 3–4, **the knife** (below) comes out this round too. Rudi then heads for the front door. |
| **3** | **Ilka's bouncers**, two retired Ernes, wade in at the end of the round and end it. Any hireling still standing makes for the doors. |

### The knife (APL 3–4)
Before he runs, **Rudi** pulls a **hidden dagger** from his boot and goes for the nearest PC who has been answering him (or for Konrad). It is his last try at making someone on Konrad's side draw. This is illegal, and the room sees it.
- **A PC who answers steel with steel** has cut the cord. That PC's ruin is that PC's, but if the PC came in with Konrad, the meeting is void. Run *Designed partial failure*.
- **A PC who disarms him bare-handed** (a grapple and an Athletics contest, or an attack against the dagger with disadvantage) gets the room's roar. Korporal Mattis stands on his bench and cheers. **+1 Trust.**
- **If nobody stops him,** Ilka's bouncers take him on round 3 and throw him into the lake. The cut is Rudi's ruin, not Konrad's, and he does not reach the yard (fished out by the patrol, he names Dornhalm by morning).

## The Discovery
> *The room is righting its benches. Somebody's squeezebox is wedged in the rafters. At the clerk's table, Ilse Brandauer is standing very still, with one hand on the place where the document case was.*

Ilse: *"The Soldbrief. Without it there is nothing to strike."* The clerk looks at his bell-glass and says nothing. **Bell +1** (the brawl ends). She cannot leave: the amends must be sworn before the clerk, and the clerk does not leave his table. She asks the party to bring the Soldbrief back.
Jost, asked about it, says: *"I didn't take it. I'd have burned it in front of you."* Let that sound as bad as it sounds.

**Development.**
- **Prisoners.** Any hireling held after the brawl talks under Intimidation DC 12: *"Rudi paid us. A mark each and beer. Didn't say who paid him."* Rudi himself, if caught, holds out until shown proof or beaten (Intimidation DC 13), then names **Dornhalm** and the yard on the Velverdyva quays.
- **Rudi escapes.** He goes to the yard to be paid. He is there in Scene 5 at APL 2+ as one of the thugs.
- **Fenna caught before the door.** She has already passed the case to a runner through the kitchen window. The party has a prisoner who trades a name for a head start (Intimidation or Persuasion DC 12): *"Dornhalm's yard. Below the lake mouth. He pays in new coin."* Skip the chase and go straight to the yard.
- **Ilka saw it.** Asked, she points: *"Kitchen door. River way. The small one in the shawl."*

## Clues Available Here
- Fenna moved on Rudi's signal → the brawl was cover
- Rudi's men head for the river, not the hiring halls or the Hundred's chapter-house
- Rudi or a hireling, questioned → Dornhalm's name

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** flying benches, a squeezebox in mid-air, and one old woman perfectly still by the door
- **Sound:** cheering, wood breaking, the hollow clunk of a tankard on a skull
- **Smell:** spilled beer and scorched wool
- **Touch:** a knuckle split on someone's helmet
- **Taste:** blood and beer

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **Nobody notices Fenna.** Ilse's cry tells them, and Ilka points the way.
- **The party refuses to fight.** Legal and dignified. They take the Dodge action, protect Konrad, and the brawl washes round them. Fenna still goes in round 2, and Konrad (if untouched) earns them **+1 Trust** for keeping him out of it.
- **A PC uses harmful magic.** That is steel. If the PC came in with Konrad, the meeting is void; see the mechanic note.
