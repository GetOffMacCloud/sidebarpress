# Cord, Temper and Trust

Back to [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]]

The night runs on one law and three tracks. Put the tracks on an index card where the players can see them, with coins or d6s as markers. **Temper** is how close Konrad is to his sword. **Trust** is how much he will let the party lead him. The **Bell** is time.

## The Red Cord (the law)

At the Red Cord's door, [[Mutter Ilka Sennhauser]] ties a red cord through every hilt and scabbard-mouth, and every bow is unstrung and hung on the cord-board. *"Wood is Kord's. Steel is murder."*

| Allowed | Breaks the cord |
|---|---|
| Fists, kicks, grapples, shoves | Drawing a corded blade (cutting or slipping the cord) |
| Chairs, tankards, benches, the poker (improvised, 1d4 bludgeoning) | Any weapon with an edge or point, including a hidden dagger |
| Staves and cudgels (Ilka allows wood) | A spell or effect that deals damage, or charms or frightens someone |
| Cantrips that harm no one (*prestidigitation*, *light*, *mage hand*) | Poison, alchemist's fire, or anything "a coward would carry" |

**Rude but legal:** harmless magic in the common room. Every company in the room notices, and it counts as +1 Temper if Konrad is the one embarrassed by it.

**Cutting the cord** (Konrad, or any PC who came in with him): the meeting is void under the cord. Every company in the room turns its back on Konrad's party at once. See *Designed partial failure* below. A creature on the other side cutting its cord is only that creature's ruin. Ilka's bouncers throw them into the lake, and the fight continues without them.

> **DM direction.** Say the law aloud at the door, word for word, and let the players see Ilka tie Konrad's cord. Everything in Act 2 depends on the table believing it.

## Temper (0–6)

Konrad starts at **1**. He is already wearing his grandfather's sword. When Temper reaches **6**, he cuts his cord.

**Rises when:**
- The provocateur lands a barb (+1; +2 for the timber remark, which only someone who knows about the Schuldbrief could make)
- Someone names the Witch or mocks the von Eisfelds' "flight north" (+1)
- Jost refuses the amends in public (+1, once)
- A PC humiliates Konrad, contradicts him in public, or uses rude magic on his behalf (+1)

**Falls when:**
- A PC defuses a barb before Konrad answers it, with wit, a counter-insult, or by taking the insult on their own honour (Persuasion, Performance or Intimidation DC 12; −1)
- A PC speaks to Konrad quietly and well (Insight or Persuasion DC 12 at Trust 0–1, DC 10 at Trust 2+; −1, once per provocation)
- Ilse publicly rebukes the provocateur (−1, once; Ilse does this if the party appeals to her sense of the room)

**At 5**, describe his hand on the cord, not on the hilt: *"His thumb is under the knot."* Give the table one last chance.

## Trust (0–3)

This matches the trust gates in [[Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld]]'s NPC note. Konrad starts at **0** (stranger).

**+1 each, to a maximum of 3:**
- **S1:** Someone asks about the "torn" banner and listens to the answer, or argues him into going with an appeal to contract law rather than charity.
- **S2:** Someone hears his grandfather's story on the boat without judging it, or vouches for him to the patrol with an answer that matches his.
- **S3/S4:** Someone defends his honour without steel, or keeps him out of the brawl.
- **S5:** Someone hands the Soldbrief back to him personally.

**Trust at the climax sets what he will do:**

| Trust | Lifting the oath ("whatever my house bound you to") | Swords or coin |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Persuasion DC 18 | He decides alone, and takes the swords out of pride |
| 1 | DC 15 | He decides alone, after a long pause |
| 2 | DC 12 | He asks the party's opinion |
| 3 | He does it himself the moment a PC asks | He asks the party, then admits the debt aloud |

## The Midnight Bell (0–6)

The Council's clerk sits at the Red Cord until the cathedral bell strikes midnight. An amends must be sworn before him, under the cord, with all three witnesses present, and **the 480 Soldbrief must be presented and struck through**. Each segment is about 20 minutes of fiction.

**The Bell starts at 2** when the party walks into the Red Cord (supper, the cords, the introductions).

**Advances by 1 when:**
- The brawl ends (Scene 4)
- The chase fails (+1 extra)
- The party travels to Dornhalm's Yard, or back from it
- The party takes a short rest, or searches the yard thoroughly
- The patrol detains the party for questioning (if their answers did not match at the dock)

**Return state:**

| Bell on return to the Red Cord | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | **In time.** The full ceremony runs as written in Scene 6. |
| 6 | **The bell is ringing.** The clerk is putting on his coat. One Persuasion DC 13 (or the Soldbrief slapped on the table) holds him. |
| 7+ | **After the bell.** The clerk is gone. The amends moves to the Hetmen's dawn sitting as a legal filing. The truth can still come out, and the charter is still saved, but the Hundred's gratitude shrinks to **coin only, 300 marks**. Field credits drop to 20. |

## Designed partial failure: the cord is cut

Do not end the adventure. Instead:

1. The room goes silent and every company turns its chairs away. Ilse, white-faced, says, *"Not here. Not now. Not under the cord."*
2. Dornhalm leaves smiling. The Soldbrief may still be stolen (move Scene 4's cutpurse into the silence).
3. At dawn, Ilse meets Konrad privately at the Hundred's chapter-house of Kord. The truth can still be found there: the banner, the oath and the daybook all still work. But the Hundred cannot be seen giving gifts to a cord-cutter, so **there is no swords-or-coin offer**. Konrad goes home with the truth and nothing else. Field credits are 10.

*Partial failure costs Konrad the gratitude, never the truth. Do not add punishment beyond this.*
