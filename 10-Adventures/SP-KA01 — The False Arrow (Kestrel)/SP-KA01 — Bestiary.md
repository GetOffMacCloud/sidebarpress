## Winter Wolf (CR 3, 700 XP)
*Large monstrosity, neutral evil* · MM p. 340 · **Scene 1, Encounter 1**

**Armor Class** 13 (natural armor) · **Hit Points** 75 (10d10 + 20) · **Speed** 50 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 (+4) | 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 7 (−2) | 12 (+1) | 8 (−1) |

**Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +3 · **Damage Immunities** cold · **Senses** passive Perception 15 · **Languages** Common, Giant, Winter Wolf · **Proficiency** +2

***Keen Hearing and Smell.*** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.
***Pack Tactics.*** Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one ally is within 5 ft. of it and isn't incapacitated.
***Snow Camouflage.*** Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in snowy terrain.

**Actions**
***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing. If the target is a creature, DC 14 Strength save or be knocked prone.
***Cold Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** 15-ft. cone. DC 12 Dexterity save; 18 (4d8) cold on a failure, half on a success.

> **Sidebar Press dressing.** It speaks Common first, reasonably: *"One mule, and you pass."* It is lying. It opens with Cold Breath on the mules if the party bargains.

## Ochir the Cut-Rein — Leader of Kezrin's Men (CR 2, 450 XP)
*Medium humanoid (human), neutral evil* · Sidebar Press homebrew (built on the scout) · **Scene 5, Encounter 5**

**Armor Class** 15 (studded leather) · **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16) · **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +5 · **Skills** Animal Handling +3, Intimidation +4, Perception +3, Stealth +5 · **Senses** passive Perception 13 · **Languages** Karashi, Common, Amshari · **Proficiency** +2

***Tent-City Born.*** Ochir has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made in the Karashi camp. Riders who don't know his face take him for one of their own.

**Actions**
***Multiattack.*** Two scimitar attacks or two horn bow attacks.
***Scimitar.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing.
***Horn Bow (Longbow).*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing.

**Bonus Actions**
***Cut-Rein's Order (Recharge 5–6).*** One ally within 30 ft. that can hear Ochir uses its reaction to move up to half its speed and make one weapon attack.

> **Sidebar Press dressing.** A Karashi rider cast out of the horde years ago (a "cut-rein": his rein was cut and his horse given away), now Kezrin's man in the tent-city. The grinning-skull brand of Malkor is inside his left forearm (Religion DC 12). He shoots from the tent-line and spends his Order on whoever holds the ravine evidence. He knocks PCs out rather than kill them, and he is the first to run when Naran's riders arrive. Captured, he says nothing: a captured *thug* is the one who names Kezrin.
>
> **CR math (2014 DMG):** defense CR 1 (52 hp, AC 15); offense CR 3 (about 17 damage a round with the Order, +5 to hit). Average: CR 2.
