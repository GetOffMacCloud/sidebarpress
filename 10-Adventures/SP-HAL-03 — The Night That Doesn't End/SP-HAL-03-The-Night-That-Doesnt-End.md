# The Night That Doesn't End

**APL 6 (levels 5–8) · 4–5 characters · one 4-hour session**

> *In Vesper Hollow it is always Hallow's Eve. The bell tolls twelve, the night starts over, and one more neighbor wakes up hollow.*
>
> A Time-Loop / Dread one-shot for **average party level 5–8** (written for APL 6, with scaling for 5, 7 and 8). The characters walk into Vesper Hollow at sunset on Hallow's Eve. At midnight the chapel bell tolls twelve times, the night folds back to dusk, and something under the church takes a soul. The PCs keep their memories. Nobody else does, except a drunken old sexton who has been ringing that bell for forty years. Three loops to find out why, and one way out that leaves nothing standing.

**All Hallows' Eve, repeating.** Setting-agnostic: any autumn festival of the dead works. The adventure assumes a night from 6 PM to midnight; the village has lived that night for forty years.

## Origin

Developmental edit applied the same day: Loop-Weariness replaced standard exhaustion, belfry cold reduced to 1d6, thirteenth-toll timing loosened, lore delivered as Q&A.

## Design Frame
- **Thesis:** A cage that leaks is still a cage, and the jailer who built it is the only one who knows the door was never meant to open. The loop *protects* the village from something worse, and the only way out is through that something.
- **Expectation broken:** *The time loop is the villain.* It isn't. The loop is a prison built by a good man, and breaking it frees the real monster. The sexton the players will assume is the problem is the only ally who remembers them.
- **Second break:** *Dying resets the night.* It does, but each death and each reset costs Loop-Weariness that never resets. The clock is the party's own fraying.
- **What this adventure refuses to do:** It never lets the party leave without a choice. There is no ending where the loop stays and the PCs go free, unless one of them takes the rope forever.
- **Session spine:** **Loop-Weariness** (one level per reset, cumulative −1d4 to saves, Echo at 4) and the **Crows Count** (the night's death toll, cawed from the chapel roof at 9 PM; it drops as the party protects souls). See [[SP-HAL-03 — Loop Rules]].
- **Tone:** Lantern Night in an orchard valley. Apples, candle smoke, a bell that is too loud for its tower, and neighbors who say the same sentence every evening.

## What the Locals Know
- **At the gate:** old Hale sits on the wall every dusk with a bottle. He says "new faces" to strangers. He has said it a great many times.
- **At the Last Toll:** Marta's stew is the best in the valley. Half the room toasts in the same words every night, and nobody finds that odd.
- **In the chapel yard:** Little Wren skips rope and sings a rhyme nobody taught her. The Prior thinks the druid on the hill taught it to her.
- **On the hill:** Annick Rook's crows gather on the chapel roof at nine and caw all at once. The Prior calls it witchcraft. Annick says they're counting.

## What's Really Happening
Ninety-nine years ago, **Elder Josiah Thorne** of Thornbury made a pact with a power in the fields, the Harvest Lord, and became its reaper. Each Hallow's Eve he walked the roads as a **wraith**, gathering souls to feed the Harvest Lord's fields, and he carved a tally of every harvest into bone.

Forty years ago, a young cleric named **Ambrose Hale** trapped him under Vesper Hollow's chapel. Hale's rite needed a bell that had rung over the unburied dead, so he bought the Marrowmere bell ([[SP-HAL-02-The-Hollow-Lantern]]) and hung it above the crypt. Every midnight, the bell's twelve tolls fold Hallow's Eve back on itself. Thorne's night of reaping never ends, so it can never finish, and he can never leave.

The cage leaks. At each midnight, Thorne's shadow reaches up through the floor and steals one soul. The loop resets bodies, buildings, and memories, but not souls. A taken villager wakes at the next dusk as an **Echo**: a hollow-eyed shell who repeats the same words and motions forever. Over forty years of nights, more than half of Vesper Hollow has gone hollow. Hale, now old, half-mad, and the only one who remembers, keeps ringing the bell because he thinks the alternative is worse. He's right.

The first soul Thorne ever took in Vesper Hollow was Hale's wife, **Elsbeth**. Hale has never told anyone. Her lantern hangs apart from the others in the ossuary.

## Hook
- **The Letters (bundle chain).** Tobias Marrow has written to Vesper Hollow every year about buying his family's bell back, and every reply is dated All Hallows' Eve, in the same hand. He asks the party to go and make the purchase in person.
- **The Lost Patrol.** A lord hires the party to find tax collectors who rode into Vesper Hollow last autumn and never came out.
- **The Rook's Message.** A crow lands on a PC's shoulder and drops a slip of paper: *"Come before dusk. Leave before midnight. You won't."*

## PC Objectives (the plot, as goals rather than events)
- **Work out that the night is repeating** — witness the reset; notice Hale's "not again"; the crows caw the same count; an Echo repeats a line the party heard yesterday.
- **Find out what the bell does** — Hale's notes; Prior Benedikt's parish record of the bell's purchase; Wren's rhyme; *detect magic* on the belfry.
- **Protect a soul through midnight** — a *magic circle* or *protection from evil and good* on a villager; carry Marta out of the valley before the twelfth toll; hold the belfry so the shadows don't drive Hale off the rope.
- **Get into the crypt** — Benedikt's key by persuasion; pick the lock (DC 15); steal the key at the feast; wait for Midnight Mass when the door opens anyway.
- **Learn how to end it** — Hale's confession; Thorne's own offer (a lie with the truth inside it); the chalk wards and the bone circle read with Religion (DC 15).
- **Choose an ending** — bind Thorne with the thirteenth toll; destroy him at full strength; take Hale's place on the rope.
- **Optional door:** return the bell (and whatever is in it) to Marrowmere or carry it west to Thornbury ([[SP-HAL-04-The-Tithe-of-Thornbury]]).

## Subplots (keyed to PCs; assign at the table)
- **A conversation that hasn't happened.** One Echo repeats a conversation with a PC, word for word, from a loop the party doesn't remember. It implies the PCs have been here before and failed. The DM decides whether that's true.
- **One of the lost patrol.** If the Lost Patrol hook is used, one of the tax collectors is a PC's sibling or old comrade, now an Echo in the lanes.
- **The reaper knows you.** Thorne greets a PC by a name only their dead parent used. He reaped someone from that family's road, years ago, and he remembers every face.

## Act Structure
Each loop is an act. The party cannot survive a fourth reset.

### Act 1 — Loop 1: Confusion (≈60 min)
Arrival at the gate, Lantern Night, the feast, the crows, the first midnight. The party witnesses the Bell Ringer fight or Marta's taking, then the twelfth toll puts them back at the gate. Scenes [[Scene 00 — The Gate at Dusk]] and [[Scene 01 — Midnight, the First Time]].

### Act 2 — Loop 2: Investigation (≈75 min)
Hale drops the act. The crypt key, the Echo Lanes, Wren's rhyme, the ossuary's upper levels, and Elsbeth's lantern. Scenes [[Scene 02 — Not Again]] and [[Scene 03 — The Ossuary]].

### Act 3 — Loop 3: Confrontation (≈85 min)
Hale sober at the gate. The chain crypt, Thorne's offer, and the split climax: the bell above, the bone circle below, and a thirteenth toll. Scenes [[Scene 04 — The Chain Crypt]] and [[Scene 05 — The Thirteenth Toll]].

## Climax at a Glance
| Solution | What it takes | Seeded in |
|---|---|---|
| **Bind him** (thirteenth toll) | The bone in the circle, Thorne inside it at the twelfth toll, a thirteenth toll rung before the end of the following round | Wren's rhyme (Scene 00); Hale's Q&A (Scene 02); Thorne's finger bone atop the first column (Scene 03); the shaft from the bone circle to the belfry (Scene 04) |
| **Destroy him** | Silence the bell or let the loop break, then kill a CR 7 wraith at full strength before dawn | Hale's warning at the gate (Scene 00); the chalk wards that hold Thorne but not his shadows (Scene 03) |
| **Take the rope** | A PC volunteers to ring the bell forever; the loop holds, the rest go free | Hale's forty years (Scene 02); the empty bottle on every step of the belfry (Scene 01) |
| **Free the Echoes** (any ending) | Shatter the bone lanterns in the Hollow Choir | Wren's chant; Hale's "break a lantern" (Scene 02); the lanterns themselves (Scene 03) |

## Treasure & Rewards
- **Hale's mace** — a *mace of disruption*, carried by Hale in his days as a cleric. He gives it to the party at the end: "I'm done with it."
- **The tithe coins** — 900 gp in old coins in Thorne's crypt, each stamped with a wheat sheaf and the word *THORNBURY*.
- **Supplies** — 2 *potions of superior healing* and a *scroll of remove curse* in Hale's cottage.
- **The Vesper Bell (if Thorne was bound)** — the Marrowmere bell, now a prison. 300 pounds, cold to the touch, humming faintly when the wind blows from the west. Pays off in [[SP-HAL-04-The-Tithe-of-Thornbury]].
- **Information** — the pact's wording on the Bone Calendar ([[SP-HAL-03 — Handout — The Bone Calendar]]).
- **Advancement** — in the chain, the party reaches 7th; award a second level of downtime advancement before Thornbury (chain runs 2 → 4 → 6 → 9).

**XP for story objectives:** 500 per PC for ending the loop by any route; 250 per PC for every soul protected through a midnight; 250 per PC for freeing the Echoes.

## Denouement
Dawn comes to Vesper Hollow for the first time in forty years, or it doesn't. Hale walks the party to the Bone Calendar and reads the last column aloud.

### Endings at a Glance
| Ending | How | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Bound** | Thirteenth toll with Thorne in the circle | Thorne sealed in the bell; the loop ends; freed souls return at dawn; the bell comes with the party |
| **Destroyed** | Bell silenced, Thorne killed before dawn | The loop ends; souls go free; the bell is an ordinary bell again |
| **Escaped** | Bell silenced, Thorne not killed | The loop ends; Thorne rides west and appears in [[SP-HAL-04-The-Tithe-of-Thornbury]] as an extra threat |
| **The rope** | A PC takes Hale's place | The loop holds; the rest of the party walks out at the next dusk; the volunteer rings forever |
