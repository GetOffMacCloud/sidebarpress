## Josiah Thorne, the Tithe-Reaper
*Medium undead, neutral evil* · Wraith (MM 302) with the changes below. Roughly **CR 7 (2,900 XP)**.

- **Armor Class** 14
- **Hit Points** 99 (18d8 + 18)
- **Speed** 0 ft., fly 60 ft. (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 (−2) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 17 (+3) |

- **Damage Resistances** acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks that aren't silvered
- **Damage Immunities** necrotic, poison
- **Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained
- **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
- **Languages** Common, and the language of whoever he's reaping
- **Challenge** 7 (2,900 XP)

**Incorporeal Movement.** Thorne can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. He takes 5 (1d10) force damage if he ends his turn inside an object.

**Legendary Resistance (1/Day).** If Thorne fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

**Sunlight Sensitivity.** While in sunlight, Thorne has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

**Bound to the Bone.** While his severed finger bone is within 30 feet, Thorne must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw at the start of each of his turns or move toward it by the most direct route. If the bone lies at the center of the Bone Circle and Thorne is inside the circle when the twelfth toll sounds, he is restrained until the end of the following round.

**Chained (until the first toll).** While chained in the Chain Crypt, Thorne can't move or attack. Each grave-iron link has AC 19 and 50 hit points; two links hold him.

### Actions
**Life Drain.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 22 (4d8 + 4) necrotic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or its hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken. This reduction lasts until the target finishes a long rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0.

**Create Specter.** Thorne targets a humanoid within 10 feet of him that died in the ossuary during this loop and hasn't been undead for more than a minute. The target's spirit rises as a **specter** in the space of its corpse or in the nearest unoccupied space. The specter is under Thorne's control. Thorne can have no more than seven specters under his control at one time.

### Running Him
He never raises his voice. He greets one PC by a name only their dead parent used. He wants the bone, then the rope, then the road west. See [[Scene 04 — The Chain Crypt]] and [[Scene 05 — The Thirteenth Toll]].

## Echo
*Medium humanoid (any race), unaligned* · Commoner (MM 345) variant. **CR 0 (10 XP)** at rest; **CR 1 (200 XP)** when snapped.

- **Armor Class** 10
- **Hit Points** 4 (1d8)
- **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 6 (−2) | 6 (−2) | 4 (−3) |

- **Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened
- **Senses** passive Perception 8
- **Languages** repeats what it knew; can't converse
- **Challenge** 0 (10 XP)

**Hollow.** An Echo's soul hangs in a bone lantern in the Hollow Choir. It repeats the words and motions of its last ordinary night. It doesn't register the passage of time and can't learn anything new. Magic that reads or alters thoughts finds only the loop.

**Reset.** An Echo whose body is destroyed is whole again at the next dusk. Its soul returns to it only if the loop ends and its lantern is broken.

**Snap.** If an Echo's routine is disrupted after 11 PM, it and up to three Echoes within 30 feet become hostile until the next reset. Use the **ghoul** stat block (MM 148) for a snapped Echo, without the ghoul's undead type: it is still a living neighbor. Nonlethal damage that drops a snapped Echo to 0 hit points returns it to its routine.

### Actions
**Unarmed Strike.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1 bludgeoning damage.
