# Scene 03 — The Ossuary

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-03 — The Night That Doesn't End/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — Not Again]] · Next → [[Scene 04 — The Chain Crypt]]

## Purpose
Loop 2, under the chapel. The party reads the Bone Calendar, finds Thorne's finger bone, meets the Hollow Choir and its ghasts, and finds Elsbeth's lantern. The second midnight should catch them down here, so they learn what the tolls sound like from below.
**Budget:** 35 minutes. Running long? One ghast; narrate the Bone Calendar's reading as a single Religion check.

## Arrival
> *Below the chapel the air is dry and smells of chalk. The walls are bones: skulls set in neat columns, finger bones laid in rows like tally marks, thousands of them, running off into the dark. Chalk sigils cover the stair at knee height, smudged by forty years of one man's boots. Far below, a chain clinks, and a kind old voice says, "Come in, come in. I've counted you already."*

**To the table:** *"The wards on the stairs are chalk. Who's stepping over them first?"*

## Who Is Here
- **Two ghasts** (one at APL 5), Thorne's first victims from the roads, guarding the Hollow Choir.
- **[[Josiah Thorne]]**, below, unseen. His voice carries up the shaft. He's courteous and never raises it.
- **[[Elsbeth Hale]]**, a soul in a single lantern, set apart.

## Beats

### O1 — The Crypt Stair
Hale's chalk wards line the walls at knee height. **Intelligence (Religion) DC 12:** they're a binding against one specific undead, drawn and redrawn hundreds of times. Thorne can't pass them. His shadows can.

### O2 — The Bone Calendar
Ninety-nine columns of skulls, one for each of Thorne's harvests, each with a tally of finger bones for the souls he reaped. The last column is unfinished. Carved above it: *"Thornbury — the 99th. The guest must come."* ([[SP-HAL-03 — Handout — The Bone Calendar]])

- **Intelligence (Religion or History) DC 15:** the calendar records a pact. Every hundredth year the reaper fetches a *guest* for Thornbury, and the hundredth is this year.
- Thorne's own finger bone, grey and ringed with iron, caps the first column. **That is the bone.** Taking it makes the chain below rattle hard, once. Thorne, pleasantly: *"Ah. You've found my hand. Do bring it down."*

### O3 — The Hollow Choir
Hundreds of bone lanterns hang from the ceiling, each holding a faint light: the stolen souls of the Echoes, about seventy. **Two ghasts** step out from behind the hanging lanterns. They fight to keep the lanterns whole. Thorne's voice during the fight: *"Careful. Those are mine."*

Shattering a lantern (AC 11, 5 hp, or any fire) frees that soul; it returns to its body when the loop ends. Shattering all of them is twenty minutes of work the party doesn't have tonight; let them break a few, and come back.

### O4 — Elsbeth's Niche
A single lantern in its own niche, polished, the only clean thing down here. Thorne keeps it where he can see it. If the party brings it to Hale, he will ring the thirteenth toll no matter what it costs him, and he'll go below with them if they ask. *"Forty years. Give it here."*

### The second midnight, from below
If the party is still down here at the tolls, they hear them as a physical blow down the shaft: dust from the ceiling, the lanterns swinging, and Thorne's chains lifting a few inches off the floor at each stroke and settling again. At the twelfth, white, silent, and the gate at dusk. Every PC takes a second level of **Loop-Weariness**. The crows caw three (less any souls protected).

**Terrain:** a long vaulted hall hung with lanterns at head height (half cover for Medium creatures weaving through), floor of packed bone dust. **Tactics:** ghasts use their stench, then paralyze and drag a PC toward the lanterns. **DM direction:** area attacks here break lanterns, which is good for the Echoes and bad for stealth. Let the party choose.

## Clues Available Here
- Thorne's finger bone on the first column → the thirteenth toll binds him
- "Thornbury — the 99th. The guest must come." → the bundle's next hook
- Elsbeth's lantern set apart → Hale is the jailer; his price
- The lanterns as souls → how to free the Echoes
- Thorne's voice up the shaft → he is under the chapel, and he listens

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** skulls in columns like library shelves, one polished lantern among hundreds grey with dust · **Sound:** a chain lifting and settling, a voice that never rises · **Smell:** chalk, grave dust, and under it, apples · **Touch:** bone dust fine as flour · **Taste:** dry

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party goes straight down to O5–O6 now:** run [[Scene 04 — The Chain Crypt]] in Loop 2. Thorne makes his offer early. The climax then runs with the party better informed but one loop shorter on souls protected; the crow count in Loop 3 is 5 plus whatever they lost.
- **If they try the thirteenth toll tonight, without the bone in the circle:** the bell cracks further; Thorne laughs; nothing binds. Hale, in the belfry: *"That's why it's the bone."*
- **If they skip the calendar:** Thorne quotes it to them in Scene 04. The bone is still on the first column; Hale names it in Loop 3 if they haven't found it.
