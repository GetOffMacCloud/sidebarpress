# Scene 02 — Not Again

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-03 — The Night That Doesn't End/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 01 — Midnight, the First Time]] · Next → [[Scene 03 — The Ossuary]]

## Purpose
Loop 2, dusk to about 10 PM. Hale drops the act and hands the party the shape of the solution through questions. The party gets the crypt key by one of three routes and learns from the Echo Lanes who Elsbeth was. They should enter the ossuary before Midnight Mass.
**Budget:** 40 minutes. Running long? Hale's Q&A only; Benedikt hands over the key on his own judgment.

## Arrival
> *Hale hasn't opened the bottle. He sits on the wall with it between his knees and watches you come up the road the way a man watches weather. "Right," he says. "How many of you remember?" Behind him the lanterns are just starting in the yews, and somewhere a skipping rope is already going.*

**To the table:** *"You have six hours and you know the rules now. What's the first thing you want to know, and who do you ask?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Ambrose Hale]]**, at the gate and then at his cottage. *Wants:* to be asked. *Knows:* the rite, the bell, the bone, the circle; not whether it works. *Reacts:* sour, precise, answers only what's asked. Goes quiet about Elsbeth.
- **[[Prior Benedikt]]**, in the chapel. *Wants:* proof the druid is the threat. *Knows:* the bell's purchase record. *Reacts:* hands over the key if convinced; otherwise opens the crypt at 11:45 for Mass regardless.
- **[[Annick Rook]]**, on the hill. Befriended (**Animal Handling DC 14**, or a crow's feather carried through a reset), her crows name tonight's taken.
- **[[Little Wren]]**, skipping. *Speak with dead* (she's not dead, it fails) or *calm emotions* gets one clear sentence.

## Beats

### Hale's cottage
Packed with empty bottles and forty years of notes in tiny, cramped writing. If the party shows him they remember (any detail from Loop 1), he drops the act. His notes describe the binding rite and the bell. From then on he answers questions. Don't deliver this as a speech: let the players ask.

| If asked about… | Hale says |
|---|---|
| The loop | *"It's a cage. Twelve tolls fold the night over on itself, and he's stuck inside the fold. So are you, now. Sorry."* |
| Thorne | *"Josiah Thorne of Thornbury. Reaper for something in the fields out west. Charming man. Don't let him talk."* |
| Ending it | *"Thirteen tolls while he stands in his own bone circle, and the bell takes him instead of the night. Someone below with the bone, someone above with the rope."* |
| Why he never tried | *"I never had the nerve to go below. Forty years. You'd think I'd have found it."* |
| The Echoes | *"Hollow. Their lanterns are down there with him. Break a lantern, the soul comes home when the night ends. If the night ends."* |
| Elsbeth | He goes quiet, then: *"The first lantern he ever lit down there. Don't ask me again."* |
| The bone | *"His own finger. I took it off him the night I chained him. It's on the first column. He can't abide being near it, and he can't stay away."* |

Hale's cottage also holds 2 *potions of superior healing* and a *scroll of remove curse* in a drawer; he waves at them. *"Take what you need. I've stopped needing."*

### The crypt key
Three routes, any works:
- **Benedikt by persuasion** (**Charisma (Persuasion) DC 15**): convince him the druid isn't the threat. A crow's feather from a previous loop, Wren's rhyme explained, or the parish record of the bell's purchase (he'll fetch it; it says *"one bell, bought of T. Marrow of Marrowmere, that had rung over the drowned"*) gives advantage.
- **Pick the lock** (**Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) DC 15**). The crypt door is at the back of the nave.
- **Steal the key** at the 8 PM feast (**Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) DC 13**); Benedikt eats at Marta's table.
- **Or wait:** Benedikt opens the crypt for Midnight Mass at 11:45, which leaves fifteen minutes before the tolls. Tight.

### The Echo Lanes
Back streets where the hollow repeat their last ordinary night. Each Echo holds one fragment. Spend ten minutes here and the party can assemble:
- The takings go back forty years, one a night.
- The first taken was *"the sexton's wife, Elsbeth, who went up to bring him his supper."*
- Nobody in the lanes can say what year it is.
- One Echo repeats a conversation with a PC that hasn't happened (see Subplots). Let the player hear their own words.

**Disrupting an Echo** before 11 PM does nothing; they reset their line. After 11 PM, four nearby Echoes snap and act as **ghouls** until the reset.

### Wren
*Calm emotions* or a kind, patient hour: *"The grandfather in the chains asks me to sing it."*

**Terrain:** narrow lanes, lantern-lit, doorways every 10 feet. **Tactics:** they grab and hold; they don't pursue past the lane's end. **DM direction:** only if the party interferes after 11 PM. These are neighbors. Nonlethal damage drops them back into their routine.

## Clues Available Here
- Hale's Q&A → the thirteenth toll binds him; the bone; Elsbeth
- Parish record of the bell → the bell makes the loop
- Echo Lanes: "the sexton's wife" → Hale is the jailer with a loss
- Wren: "the grandfather in the chains" → Thorne is under the chapel
- The crows naming tonight's taken (via Annick) → who to protect

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** bottles in rows on every sill, ink gone brown, a key on a priest's belt · **Sound:** the same three sentences from three doorways · **Smell:** candle fat, apple rot, dust · **Touch:** paper soft as cloth · **Taste:** Hale's tea, which is mostly not tea

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party refuses to trust Hale:** the parish record and Wren's sentence carry the same facts. Thorne will tell them too, backwards, in [[Scene 04 — The Chain Crypt]].
- **If the party spends the whole loop protecting souls instead:** good. The crow count drops. The ossuary waits for Loop 3, which compresses the climax; run Scene 03 and Scene 04 as one 40-minute pass.
- **If they attack Benedikt:** the village turns on them for the loop; the key is still on his body; the crypt is still there. Hale: *"That one was innocent, you know."*

## Transition
- → [[Scene 03 — The Ossuary]]: the crypt door opens on a dry stair and a smell of chalk. From far below, a chain clinks once.
