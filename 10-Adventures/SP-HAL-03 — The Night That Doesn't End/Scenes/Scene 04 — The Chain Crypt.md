# Scene 04 — The Chain Crypt

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-03 — The Night That Doesn't End/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 03 — The Ossuary]] · Next → [[Scene 05 — The Thirteenth Toll]]

## Purpose
Loop 3, the last the party can survive. Hale is sober at the gate. The party reaches the Bone Circle and the Chain Crypt, hears Thorne's offer, and decides who goes above and who stays below before midnight.
**Budget:** 25 minutes. Running long? Thorne's offer in two lines; straight to the fight.

## Arrival
> *The circle is skulls set flush in the floor, twenty feet across, directly under a shaft that climbs to a square of lamplight far above: the belfry. Beyond it, in the last crypt, a thin old man in a Thornbury elder's coat sits in grave-iron chains with his hands folded, one of them missing a finger. He stands as you come in, as a host does. "There you are," he says. "I was beginning to think you'd lost your nerve. Sit. We've a great deal to discuss and very little night."*

**To the table:** *"He's offering you a chair. Who sits, who stays in the doorway, and who's looking up the shaft?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Josiah Thorne]]**, the Tithe-Reaper, in chains. *Wants:* the chains off, the bell silent, the roads. *Knows:* the rite, exactly; he's lived under it for forty years. *Reacts:* grandfatherly, patient, greets one PC by a name only their dead parent used. Never lies outright; lies by arrangement.
- **[[Ambrose Hale]]**, if the party brought him. Stands at the circle's edge with the mace and won't look at Thorne.

## Beats

### Loop 3's dusk (play first, briefly)
Hale at the gate, sober for the first time in years: *"Tonight, then. One way or another."* The crows caw five (less what's been protected). The bell's crack is visible from the ground. Everything the party fixed in Loop 2 is undone except the souls.

### O5 — The Bone Circle
A ring of skulls in the floor under the shaft. The bell's voice booms down it at midnight. **Intelligence (Religion) DC 15:** a binding circle, and it needs the bound thing's own bone laid at the center to close. **Investigation DC 12:** scuff marks show Thorne's chains reach *exactly* to the circle's edge. He's been pacing it.

### O6 — The Chain Crypt
Thorne hangs his chains in grave-iron from the ceiling. The tithe coins, 900 gp stamped *THORNBURY*, are stacked in neat towers along the wall. He'll offer them.

**The offer.** *"Unchain me, and I'll spare every one of you and leave Vesper Hollow forever. The sexton can keep his bell. You can keep your sleep."* He's lying about the second part; he means to take Hale first and then walk west to finish the hundredth harvest. **Wisdom (Insight) DC 17** catches that the first part is true and the second isn't.

Let the players talk to him. He answers questions, and the truth is in them backwards:

| If asked… | Thorne says |
|---|---|
| What he is | *"A man who kept his word to something that keeps its word. There aren't many of us left."* |
| The thirteenth toll | *"The old rite? Yes, I know it. It needs my bone in that circle and me standing in it like a fool, and someone on the rope who can ring thirteen with the shadows at his throat. Nobody's managed it. There's been nobody to try."* |
| Thornbury | *"They'll have the tables laid by now. The hundredth year. If I'm not there to bring the guest, they'll find one themselves. Good people. Frightened."* |
| Elsbeth | *"The sexton's wife brought him supper. I hadn't meant to take her. One does, though."* |
| The PC he named | *"Your mother called you that. On the north road, eleven years ago. She asked me to look after you. I always keep my word."* |

### The split
Before midnight the party must divide: someone above on the rope (and holding the belfry), someone below with the bone, and Thorne has to be *in the circle* at the twelfth toll. He's drawn to his own bone and will step toward it; he's also clever, and will stop at the edge unless pushed, grappled, or baited. If the party brought Elsbeth's lantern to Hale, Hale takes the rope himself and the shadows above are at −2 to hit (he knows their rhythm).

**Hale's advice if asked:** *"He'll come to the bone. He can't help it. Don't be standing between them."*

## Encounter (if any)
None yet. Thorne's chains hold until the first toll. If the party attacks him in chains, he takes it with good humor; he's immune to nonmagical damage and his chains are grave-iron (AC 19, 50 hp per link; two links hold him). Breaking a link early is "Unchain me." See [[Scene 05 — The Thirteenth Toll]] for the fight.

## Clues Available Here
- The circle under the shaft; "it needs my bone" → the thirteenth toll binds him
- "Nobody's managed it. There's been nobody to try." → Hale alone; now the party
- "They'll have the tables laid" → the Thornbury hook
- Chains reach exactly to the circle → he can enter it if he chooses, or is made to

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** grave-iron black and dull, coin towers, a missing finger · **Sound:** chain links, a courteous voice, the bell's crack creaking above · **Smell:** iron, cold earth, wheat · **Touch:** a chair offered, its seat warm · **Taste:** dust on the teeth

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party unchains him:** he keeps the first promise and breaks the second: he goes for Hale, then the belfry. Run Scene 05 as "Destroy him," full strength, with Thorne starting on the stair.
- **If the party decides to take the rope forever:** a PC volunteers. Thorne, almost gently: *"Forty more years, then. I'll wait."* Go to Scene 05's "The rope" ending.
- **If the party tries to bargain for Thornbury:** he agrees to anything; chains stay on; it changes nothing. He's already told them what the field wants.
- **If the party skipped Scene 03:** the bone is still on the first column. Thorne tells them where it is himself, because he wants it brought near.

## Transition
- → [[Scene 05 — The Thirteenth Toll]]: far above, the first toll. Thorne's chains fall open on the floor like dropped rope, and he smiles. *"Well. Shall we?"*
