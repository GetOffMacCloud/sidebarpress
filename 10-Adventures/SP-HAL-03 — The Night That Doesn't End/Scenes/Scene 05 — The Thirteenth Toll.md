# Scene 05 — The Thirteenth Toll

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-03 — The Night That Doesn't End/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 04 — The Chain Crypt]]

## Purpose
The climax, split between the belfry and the Bone Circle, ending in one of four ways. Then the denouement: dawn, the calendar read aloud, the bell. **Never cut this scene.** End Scene 04 one exchange earlier instead.
**Budget:** 50 minutes (35 for the fight, 15 for the dawn).

## Arrival
> *The first toll comes down the shaft like a thrown stone. Thorne's chains open on the floor and he steps out of them, brushing his coat, and the shadows in the belfry above peel off the walls toward the rope. Below, the skulls of the circle begin to glow, faint and white, one for each stroke, and the old reaper looks at the bone in your hand the way a starving man looks at bread.*

**To the table:** *"Twelve rounds. Who's on the rope, who's holding the bone, and who's between Thorne and the circle?"*

## Who Is Here
- **Above:** whoever holds the rope; **[[Ambrose Hale]]** if he's there; the shadows (and a specter at APL 7–8).
- **Below:** **[[Josiah Thorne]]** and whoever stayed; ghasts and specters at higher APL.

## Beats

### Run two fights on one initiative
Roll once. Alternate: belfry round, crypt round. Each toll is one round; the belfry ringer's action rings it. Cross-cut like film: *"Above, the shadows close on the rope. Below, Thorne takes one step toward the bone."*

### Above — the Bell Ringer
Per [[SP-HAL-03 — Loop Rules]]: each toll, DC 13 Constitution or 1d6 cold; the shadows swarm the ringer. Two silent rounds and the loop breaks (Thorne at full strength, no binding possible this loop). If Hale has Elsbeth's lantern, he rings and the shadows are at −2 to hit him.

### Below — Thorne
Full stat block in [[SP-HAL-03 — Bestiary]]. He's drawn to the bone: if it's within 30 feet he must make a **DC 15 Wisdom save** at the start of his turn or move toward it (he has Legendary Resistance once). He uses Life Drain on whoever holds it. He'll try to seize the bone and throw it up the shaft; a PC can catch it with a **DC 13 Dexterity check** or lose a round fetching it.

Getting Thorne into the circle: he steps in on his own if the bone is placed at the center and nobody is standing in his way (he can't help it). Otherwise: grapple and drag (he's Medium, Strength 6, but incorporeal; grappling works only with a magic weapon or *mage hand*-style force), shove, *thunderwave*, or bait him with the bone held by someone standing in the circle. **Hale's advice** holds: don't stand between them.

### The twelfth toll
If Thorne is inside the circle with the bone at its center at the twelfth toll, the skulls flare white and he's held fast. He screams for the first time. Now the thirteenth: the belfry has **until the end of the next round** to ring again. The shadows go for the rope with everything.

### The thirteenth toll
Thorne is drawn up the shaft like smoke up a chimney, into the bell, which seals around him with a ring like a struck anvil. The shadows fall apart. The night goes on past midnight, and the lanterns in the yews gutter and go out as the first grey shows. Dawn comes to Vesper Hollow for the first time in forty years.

He'll Create Specter from any PC who dies here and send it up the shaft. **DM direction:** the fight is won by *positioning*, not damage. Tell the players the circle glows a little brighter each toll so they can count.

## Resolutions
| Ending | How | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **Bind him** | Bone in the circle, Thorne inside at the twelfth, a thirteenth before the end of the next round | Thorne sealed in the bell. Loop ends. Every soul whose lantern was broken returns at dawn; the rest stay Echoes until someone goes down and finishes the job (an hour's work, safe now). |
| **Destroy him** | Silence the bell or let the loop break, then kill him before dawn at full strength | Loop ends. Souls go free. The bell is a bell. Deadly; Hale will die in it if he's there. |
| **Escaped** | The loop breaks and Thorne reaches the road | Loop ends anyway. He rides west. [[SP-HAL-04-The-Tithe-of-Thornbury]] gains a wraith. |
| **The rope** | A PC takes Hale's place as the eternal ringer | The loop holds. The rest of the party walks out at dusk. Hale, free, walks with them and doesn't look back. Tragic and legitimate. |

## Denouement — Dawn
Hale walks the party to the Bone Calendar and runs his finger along the unfinished last column.

> *"Ninety-nine harvests. He went out every Hallow's Eve to fetch souls for it, the thing in the fields. And every hundredth year he fetched a guest for Thornbury." Hale taps the carving. "Except he's not going anywhere this year. Which means whatever's waiting in Thornbury is going to be hungry. And the good people of Thornbury," he says, "are going to have to find a guest on their own."*

If Thorne is sealed in the bell, he speaks once from inside it, very softly: *"They'll have the tables laid by now. Do give them my regards."* Overhead, Annick Rook's crows take off together and fly west.

**Rewards:** Hale's *mace of disruption* (*"I'm done with it"*); 900 gp in tithe coins; the Vesper Bell (300 lb, cold, humming when the wind is from the west) if Thorne is in it. See the main note for XP.

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the ringer drops at the eleventh toll:** anyone who can reach the rope and spend an action keeps the count; Hale will crawl to it. If nobody does, the loop breaks at the twelfth-that-isn't, Thorne is loose, and the "Destroy him" ending is on.
- **If the bone is lost down the shaft or thrown:** it lands in the circle (Thorne can't aim it away; the bone wants the circle too). Treat it as placed.
- **If a PC became an Echo this loop:** their lantern is in the Choir. Break it before dawn and they wake with the others.
- **If the party wins with souls still hollow:** say so plainly. Someone has to go down and break the rest of the lanterns, and the village will ask who.
