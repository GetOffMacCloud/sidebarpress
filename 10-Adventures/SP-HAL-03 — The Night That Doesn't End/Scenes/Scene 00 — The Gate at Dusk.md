# Scene 00 — The Gate at Dusk

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-03 — The Night That Doesn't End/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 01 — Midnight, the First Time]]

## Purpose
Make Vesper Hollow feel like a cozy festival with something wrong at the edges, and plant three clues the players will only understand after the first reset: Hale's warning, Wren's rhyme, and the Echoes' repeated toasts. Nobody should say "time loop" yet.
**Budget:** 30 minutes. Running long? Skip the orchard; keep Hale, Wren's rhyme, and the feast toasts.

## Arrival
> *The road crests a hill and Vesper Hollow lies below you: orchards red with apples, a squat grey chapel, and lanterns blooming one by one in the dusk. An old man sits on the gate wall nursing a bottle. He looks up at you with eyes like an old dog's. "Ah," he says. "New faces. Well. Enjoy the stew. Don't go into the church after eleven."*

**To the table:** *"It's Lantern Night and you've been walking all day. Who heads for the inn, and who wants to know why the old man said that?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Ambrose Hale]]**, sexton, on the wall. *Wants:* the party to be somewhere safe at midnight, and not to have to explain. *Knows:* everything. *Reacts:* sardonic, tired, drinks between sentences; answers a question with a smaller question. If pressed on the church: *"Bell's loud. You'll want to be indoors."*
- **[[Prior Benedikt]]**, in the chapel yard at 7 PM, lighting the yew lanterns. *Wants:* his flock protected from the druid. *Knows:* nothing of the loop; the crypt key is on his belt. *Reacts:* stiff and courteous, warms to any show of piety.
- **[[Little Wren]]**, skipping rope by the yews. *Reacts:* never stops skipping, never answers a question. Chants: *"Toll the twelve and round we go, the reaper sleeps where bones lie low."*
- **[[Marta Greaves]]**, at the Last Toll from 8 PM. *Wants:* everyone fed. *Reacts:* warm, loud, a second bowl before the first is empty.
- **[[Annick Rook]]**, on the hill, if the party climbs it. *Reacts:* curious about strangers, quick to trust an animal companion or a druid.

## Beats

### 7 PM — Lantern lighting
Villagers hang lanterns on the chapel-yard yews "to guide the dead home." Benedikt blesses each one. Half the villagers move oddly: **Wisdom (Insight) DC 13** on any one of them shows their eyes don't track anything, and their lips move a half-second ahead of their words. These are **Echoes**. Don't name them yet.

Wren's rhyme is audible from anywhere in the yard. Benedikt, if asked, says the druid taught it to her. (She didn't.)

### 8 PM — The feast at the Last Toll
Marta's stew is excellent. The room is full. Three toasts are raised, in order, and they'll be raised in the same words every loop:
- *"To the harvest, and the hands that pulled it!"*
- *"To old Hale, and his bell, and may it ring us all home!"*
- *"To Elsbeth, wherever she's gone!"* — at this one, half the room goes quiet, and nobody can say who Elsbeth was.

A PC who talks to a patron for more than a minute (**Insight DC 12**) notices that about half of them answer only with sentences they've already said tonight.

### 9 PM — Crows Count
Hundreds of crows land on the chapel roof. The whole village goes still. Then one caw, all together. Marta laughs it off: *"They do that."* See [[SP-HAL-03 — Loop Rules]].

### 10 PM — Hale climbs
Hale walks past the inn window with a fresh bottle and goes into the chapel. The belfry door locks behind him. A PC who follows and listens hears him talking, low, to someone who doesn't answer.

### Optional: the orchard
Annick Rook sits with her crows above the village. She doesn't know about the loop, but she knows the crows "count something" at nine and that "they've counted one for a long time now." If a PC is a druid or ranger, she offers cider and a warning: *"The Prior says I'm the witch. I'm not the one who locks the church at night."*

## Encounter (if any)
None in this scene unless the party attacks an Echo or the crows. The crows are a **swarm of ravens** (MM 339); Annick will not forgive it.

## Clues Available Here
- Hale's warning → the bell makes the loop
- Wren's rhyme → the bell makes the loop; Thorne is under the chapel
- The three toasts (identical each loop) → the night repeats
- "To Elsbeth" with no one able to say who she was → Hale's loss
- The Echoes' half-second lag → something has been taken from them

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** red apples in the dusk, lantern flames in the yews, breath fogging · **Sound:** a skipping rope, a fiddle at the inn, the same toast twice · **Smell:** woodsmoke, hot stew, cider lees · **Touch:** cold stone on the gate wall · **Taste:** Marta's stew, too much pepper, exactly right

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party tries to leave before midnight:** the road curls back to the gate after a few minutes. Hale is still there. *"Told you."*
- **If the party goes straight to the chapel after eleven:** the door is barred from inside. Benedikt opens it for Midnight Mass at 11:45. Run [[Scene 01 — Midnight, the First Time]] from inside the nave.
- **If the party skips the feast:** the toasts resurface in Loop 2 as the thing that proves the repetition; Marta is still the soul taken.
- **If they interrogate an Echo hard:** nothing before 11 PM. After 11 PM, the Echoes nearby snap (see [[Scene 02 — Not Again]] encounter).

## Transition
- → [[Scene 01 — Midnight, the First Time]]: at 11 PM, every Echo in sight stops, turns toward the chapel, and goes still. The fiddle stops mid-bar.
