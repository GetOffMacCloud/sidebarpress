# Scene 01 — Midnight, the First Time

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-03 — The Night That Doesn't End/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 00 — The Gate at Dusk]] · Next → [[Scene 02 — Not Again]]

## Purpose
Show the midnight procedure once, with the party under-informed: the shadows in the belfry, Hale alone on the rope, a soul taken, and the reset. The players should leave this scene knowing the night repeats and that they remember.
**Budget:** 30 minutes. Running long? Run the Bell Ringer for 4 rounds, then narrate the rest of the tolls to the reset.

## Arrival
> *The first toll hits like a dropped anvil. Dust falls from the rafters, lantern flames bend flat, and every hollow-eyed villager in the street turns toward the chapel at once. In the belfry, the shadows come off the walls. They come off the floor. They come off the old man on the rope, and he keeps pulling. Somewhere below, a woman laughs in the middle of a sentence and stops.*

**To the table:** *"Where are you standing when the bell starts: the inn, the street, or the stairs to the belfry?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Ambrose Hale]]**, on the rope. *Wants:* to get to twelve. *Reacts:* shouts at anyone who comes up: *"Don't touch the rope unless I drop it!"* If a PC takes the rope, he fights beside them with a mace he's too old to swing well.
- **[[Marta Greaves]]**, at the inn. The soul taken this loop unless the party acts. She goes grey and still mid-laugh at the sixth toll.
- **The shadows**, out of the walls and floor of the belfry.

## Beats

### Where the party is
- **In the belfry:** they're in the fight. Run the Bell Ringer procedure from [[SP-HAL-03 — Loop Rules]]. Hale has 22 hit points and AC 12 (old man, chain shirt under a cassock). If he drops, the rope needs a PC's action.
- **At the inn:** at the sixth toll, a cold draft comes up through the floorboards. Marta goes grey. A PC who makes a **Wisdom (Perception) DC 14** check sees a long shadow with a stooped man's outline pass *down* through the floor with something cupped in its hands. *Protection from evil and good* cast on her before the sixth toll saves her this loop.
- **In the street:** the Echoes stand still and stare. A PC within 10 feet of one hears it whisper, in Thorne's voice, *"Not you. Not yet."*

### The Bell Ringer (if they're up there)
Twelve rounds. Each toll: DC 13 Constitution save or 1d6 cold. Shadows swarm the ringer. If nobody rings for 2 consecutive rounds, the loop breaks (go to [[Scene 05 — The Thirteenth Toll]] now, with Thorne at full strength; that's a hard ending, not a failure).

### The reset
At the twelfth toll, everything goes white and silent. Then:

> *The road crests a hill. Vesper Hollow lies below you, orchards red with apples, lanterns blooming in the dusk. An old man sits on the gate wall. He looks up. His face falls. "Oh," he says. "Not again."*

Every PC takes one level of **Loop-Weariness**. Hit points and slots are restored. Marta, if taken, is at the inn at 8 PM, grey-eyed, ladling stew and saying *"They do that"* to nobody.

**Terrain:** a 20 × 20 belfry, the bell in the center, a 3-foot shaft in the floor beside it running down into the dark. Dim light from Hale's lantern. **Tactics:** shadows target whoever holds the rope; they use the shaft to retreat and re-emerge. Strength drain on the ringer is the real threat. **DM direction:** the party can't win the loop here. Let them learn the procedure and take the hits.

## Clues Available Here
- Shadows rise through the belfry floor → Thorne is under the chapel
- *Detect magic* on the bell: abjuration, old and cracked → the bell makes the loop
- Hale on the rope, alone, by choice → Hale is the jailer
- Thorne's "Not you. Not yet." → he counts, he chooses
- An empty bottle on every step of the belfry stair → forty years

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** flames bent flat, shadows with too many elbows, Hale's knuckles white on the rope · **Sound:** the toll, then ringing silence, then the toll · **Smell:** cold iron, old wine · **Touch:** the cold coming up through the floor · **Taste:** copper

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If a PC protects Marta:** the crows caw *zero* in Loop 2 for the base count, then add two for the loop's escalation (net 2). Tell the players the number went down; that's the scoreboard working.
- **If the party silences the bell on purpose in Loop 1:** the loop breaks. Thorne rises. Run [[Scene 05 — The Thirteenth Toll]] as "Destroy him," full strength, no bone, no circle. Hale, dying, tells them what they've done. A brutal ending for a table that wanted it.
- **If a PC dies:** they wake at the gate with two levels of Loop-Weariness. Hale notices: *"You're going quicker than most."*
- **If the party skips the belfry entirely:** they still see Marta taken and still reset. The Bell Ringer procedure is shown in Loop 2 instead.
