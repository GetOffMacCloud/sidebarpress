# SP-HAL-03 — Handout — The Bone Calendar

Back to [[SP-HAL-03-The-Night-That-Doesnt-End]] · Found in [[Scene 03 — The Ossuary]] (area O2)

## The Prop
A wall of the ossuary, twenty feet long. Ninety-nine columns of skulls set flush in mortar, each column headed by a larger skull and footed by a row of finger bones laid like tally marks. Some columns have a dozen tallies; some have forty. The carving above each column is a place name and a number, cut into the mortar with a knife.

The first column is capped by a single grey finger bone ringed with iron: Thorne's own.

The last column is empty of tallies. Above it, cut deeper and newer than the rest:

> **THORNBURY — THE 99TH.**
> **THE GUEST MUST COME.**

## At the Table
Hand the players the carving on a card. If they ask what the other columns say, read a few: *"Marrowmere road — 14." "The north road — 7." "Vesper Hollow — 1."* The last of those is Elsbeth.

**Intelligence (Religion or History) DC 15:** this is the ledger of a pact. Every Hallow's Eve, a tithe of souls from the roads; every hundredth year, a *guest* for Thornbury. The hundredth is this year.

## Continuity
The carving is the hook into [[SP-HAL-04-The-Tithe-of-Thornbury]]. Thorne reads it aloud to the party in [[Scene 04 — The Chain Crypt]] if they haven't seen it.
