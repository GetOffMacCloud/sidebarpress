# SP-HAL-03 — Loop Rules

Back to [[SP-HAL-03-The-Night-That-Doesnt-End]] · Scenes: [[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-03 — The Night That Doesn't End/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]]

The session spine. Read the Loop Rules to the players after the first reset, or let them work the rules out from what they see. Copy the trackers onto a card.

## The Loop
Anyone who enters Vesper Hollow between dusk and midnight is caught.

- **Reset.** At the twelfth toll, the night starts over at 6 PM at the village gate. The PCs keep their memories, knowledge, and XP.
- **Restored.** Hit points, spell slots, expended consumables, and the state of the village all return to how they were at the first dusk. Treat each reset as a long rest.
- **Not restored.** Souls. A soul Thorne takes stays taken. A soul the party protects through a midnight stays protected. Loop-Weariness stays.
- **Death.** A PC who dies comes back at the next reset with their body whole, but gains a second level of Loop-Weariness that loop.
- **Leaving.** The road out of the valley curls back to the gate after a few minutes of walking. A villager carried out before midnight is safe for that loop (their soul is beyond Thorne's reach), but they wake at the gate at the next dusk like everyone else.
- **The Hollowing.** A PC who would take a fourth level of Loop-Weariness becomes an Echo instead. Run the adventure as three loops: Confusion, Investigation, Confrontation.
- **Breaking the loop early.** If no one rings the bell for 2 consecutive rounds at midnight, the loop breaks, Thorne's chains fall, and he rises at full strength. Skip to [[Scene 05 — The Thirteenth Toll]].

## Loop-Weariness
A custom condition. It is *not* exhaustion; standard exhaustion would cripple the party before the climax.

| Level | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | −1d4 to all saving throws. The PC's reflection lags a half-second behind them. |
| 2 | −2d4 to all saving throws. The PC sometimes answers a question before it's asked. |
| 3 | −3d4 to all saving throws. The PC's shadow stands still when they move. Final warning. |
| 4 | The PC becomes an **Echo** (see [[SP-HAL-03 — Bestiary]]). They are not dead; their soul hangs in the Hollow Choir and returns if the loop ends and the lantern is broken. |

Each reset gives every PC one level. Dying in a loop gives one more. Nothing short of ending the loop removes a level. *Greater restoration* reduces it by one, once per PC.

**Reset checklist (per PC):** ☐ Reset 1 ☐ Reset 2 ☐ Reset 3 (final warning)

## The Night, Hour by Hour
Every loop follows this schedule until the party changes it. Changes last until the reset, except for souls saved or lost.

| Time | Event | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 6 PM | The party arrives at the gate. Hale is waiting on the wall. | Loop 1: "Ah. New faces." Later: "Not again." |
| 7 PM | Lantern lighting in the chapel yard. Wren skips rope and chants. | Prior Benedikt preaches against "the witch on the hill." |
| 8 PM | The feast at the Last Toll. | Marta serves stew. Echoes repeat the same toasts word for word. |
| 9 PM | **Crows Count.** | See below. |
| 10 PM | Hale climbs the belfry with a bottle. | The crypt is locked until Benedikt opens it for Midnight Mass. |
| 11 PM | Every Echo stops, turns, and stares at the chapel. | Silent and motionless until midnight. |
| Midnight | The bell tolls twelve times. Thorne's shadow rises and takes a soul. | **The Bell Ringer.** At the twelfth toll, the night resets. |

## Crows Count (9 PM)
Hundreds of crows settle on the chapel roof and the village goes quiet. Then they caw, all together, once for each soul that will be lost before the night ends. It's the party's scoreboard.

| Loop | Base count | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Marta Greaves |
| 2 | 3 | Marta, Prior Benedikt, Annick Rook |
| 3 | 5 | The above, plus two villagers the party has spoken to |

- Each soul the party protects through a midnight drops the next loop's count by one.
- **Protection that works:** *protection from evil and good* or *magic circle* on the villager through the twelfth toll; a *sanctuary* that holds; carrying them out of the valley before midnight; a PC physically holding them inside a consecrated space (the chapel nave counts, the belfry doesn't).
- **Protection that doesn't:** hiding, locking doors, stuffing their ears. Thorne's shadow comes up through the floor.
- The PCs can't be protected this way. They're tied to the loop.
- **Annick Rook** can talk to her crows. If befriended (Wisdom (Animal Handling) DC 14, or showing her a crow's feather from a previous loop), the crows tell her *who* will be taken tonight.

## The Bell Ringer (Midnight)
Thorne wants the bell silenced. As the first toll sounds, the belfry's shadows peel off the walls and swarm the ringer. Hale has survived this for forty years by luck and stubbornness.

**Procedure, each round for 12 rounds:**
1. **Roll initiative** at the first toll. The shadows act on their own count.
2. **Ring.** Someone must spend their action on the rope or the toll doesn't sound. Two consecutive silent rounds break the loop (see above).
3. **The toll drains warmth.** Every creature in the belfry makes a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking 1d6 cold damage on a failure. Over twelve tolls that averages about 25 damage per character after saves.
4. **Shadows.** 4 at APL 5, 6 at APL 6, 8 plus a specter at APL 7–8. They target the ringer first. Strength drain on a ringer is the real danger.
5. **At the twelfth toll:** the night resets, unless Loop 3's thirteenth toll is in play ([[Scene 05 — The Thirteenth Toll]]).

**The thirteenth toll (Loop 3 only):** it must sound before the end of the round *after* the twelfth. The shadows swarm the rope the moment the twelfth sounds, so whoever rings has to survive one more round at the center of the fight.
