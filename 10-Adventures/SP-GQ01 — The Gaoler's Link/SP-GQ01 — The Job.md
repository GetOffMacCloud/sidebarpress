# The Job

Back to [[SP-GQ01-The-Gaolers-Link]]

The adventure runs on one law, one economy and two sets of alert states. Put them on an index card where the players can see the Edges and the Heat. **The Link's laws** are what make the double-cross work. **Edges** are what the players earn by casing the job. **Heat** is what they spend by being careless. The **alert states** are what the Field Station and the *Graue Hecht* do about it.

## The Laws of the Link
Full rules on [[The Gaoler's Link]]. The table version:
- **The blind spot (30 ft).** Divination can't perceive anything near the unboxed Link, whoever casts it. Scrying shows grey fog.
- **The broken tether (30 ft).** Enchantments laid by Iuz's servants are suppressed. A tethered creature is incapacitated until the end of its next turn, then wakes, confused, and stops fighting.
- **The ache (30 ft).** A creature sworn to Iuz feels the unboxed Link nearby as a cold ache behind the eyes, and hangs back. Within 30 feet it **can't target the Link's bearer** with an attack or a spell (areas, others, and range beyond 30 ft still work).
- **The burn.** A creature sworn to Iuz that touches it takes 2d6 radiant damage and must drop it (DC 13 Constitution save to hold on, taking the damage again each turn).
- **The Ashbox** seals all of it, and lets Iuz's divinations find the box again.

**Say it out loud the first time it matters.** The first time a tethered creature comes within 30 feet of the Link, read Brandmeier's Waking (Scene 06) or describe the bargeman's blink. Players who see the tether break once will start carrying the Link like a lantern. That is the point.

## Edges (preparation)
- Each lead completed in [[Scene 03 — Legwork]] earns **1 Edge**, to a maximum of **4**. Record what the Edge *is* ("the gate password," "a wax copy of the stacks key").
- During the job or the turn, a player may spend an Edge to:
  - **Flashback:** declare something the party prepared earlier ("We already bribed Jens," "I pressed the key in wax at the counting-room"). It must follow from legwork the party actually did. The DM may veto with a one-line reason.
  - **Make one failed check a success.**
- Unspent Edges carry into Scene 06 and Scene 07 (a forged link, a friend on the quay, Ruder's goodwill).
- **Running short?** Award 2 Edges for a montage of legwork instead of playing each lead.

## Heat (0–6)
Track it openly, with a die on the table.

| Heat | Field Station | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | **Lax** | Clean. Nobody knows until Brewfest 5. |
| 3–4 | **Wary** | Seen but not caught. Grell's inquiry opens at noon on Brewfest 4. |
| 5–6 | **Alert** | Grell's inquiry opens before dawn. The students start Scene 06 with a proctor looking for them. |

**+1 Heat:** seen where students shouldn't be; a check failed by 5 or more; a loud noise; each round of combat in the Archive; taking anything besides the Link; opening a sealed file.
**+2 Heat:** Oswin Kettler or a gate-rota student raises a shout; a lamp seen in the Archive windows.
**+3 Heat:** the grille's *alarm* rings in the Station House.
**−1 Heat:** an Edge spent on a cover story; a successful DC 13 Deception or Persuasion to explain themselves to whoever saw them.

## Field Station Alert States
- **Lax (Heat 0–2).** The Warden's dinner is in full swing in the Station House hall. The Registrar and Vogelsang sit at high table until midnight. Two gate-rota Probationers walk the palisade path with a lantern once an hour.
- **Wary (Heat 3–4).** **[[Mathias Grell]]** leaves the dinner and walks the Field Station every 20 minutes. **[[Oswin Kettler]]** goes back to the Archive to "check the presses."
- **Alert (Heat 5–6).** Grell and two gate-rota students (**guards**, nonlethal) go to the Archive with a covered lantern. **[[Garrok Stonebrow]]** leaves the dinner and shuts the palisade gate to the Landing; nobody passes without a story.

**Scaled response.**
- **Careful party:** the Field Station barely notices. Any inquiry blames "a Perrender sailor."
- **Clumsy party:** Grell raises the alarm, and the students must get out unseen or own up.
- **Violent party:** hurting a student or drawing steel inside the palisade (a standing offense) gets them reported. Conclusion D's discipline applies whatever else happens.

## The *Graue Hecht*'s Alert States (Vesharan)
- **Lax.** He plays the clerk, stays aboard, and keeps [[Snick]] on the students.
- **Wary** (pointed questions to Brandmeier, Snick spotted, or a visit to Hogar about the box). The handover moves up to midnight, and the *Hecht* readies to sail at the change of the watch.
- **Alert** (the students go to Vogelsang, Dolz, Grell or Garrok, or capture Snick). He tries to have the Link taken *from* the students by his tethered bargemen, then sails at once.

## Snick's Watch
Each day of Scene 03, Snick shadows one PC (DM's choice: whoever is doing the most interesting legwork). Spotting it takes a **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception)** check: a draft, a flicker, a whispered giggle. If it isn't spotted, Vesharan learns what that PC did. **Once the party holds the unboxed Link, Snick won't come within 30 feet of them** (the ache), and Vesharan can't see through its eyes near them anyway. It hisses about it.
