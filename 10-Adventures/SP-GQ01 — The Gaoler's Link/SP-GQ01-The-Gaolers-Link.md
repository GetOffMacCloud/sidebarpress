# The Gaoler's Link

**APL 3 (levels 3–4) · 4–6 characters · one 4-hour session**

> *A merchant pays students to steal back his grandfather's reliquary. He has no grandfather in Ritterstadt, and the reliquary held a god.*
>
> A Heist / Double-Cross one-shot for **average party level 3–4** (written for APL 3, with scaling for APL 4). During Brewfest week, a Perrender merchant hires Greyhawk University students to lift an iron "heirloom" from the Archive's sealed stacks. At the dawn handover the merchant wakes from an enchantment he didn't know he was under, and his quiet clerk turns out to be an agent of Iuz. The students are holding the one thing in Ritterstadt the Old One cannot see, and they have until the *Graue Hecht* sails to turn the job. Part 1 of [[SP-GQ00 — The Shadow on the Quag]].

## Adventure Code
`SP-GQ01`: never reuse codes, even for scrapped adventures. **SP-SQ** is the Shadow on the Quag series (Greybeard, 2026-11-05); SP-GQ00 is the arc hub.

## Date
**Brewfest 1–5, 576 CY.** Brewfest is the festival week between Harvester and Patchwall. The job falls on the **night of the Warden's Brewfest Dinner** (the third night), now a fixed point on the Field Station calendar ([[RG-08 — Greyhawk University, Ritterstadt Field Station]]). The handover is before dawn on Brewfest 4. 576 is a Regents' audit year, so Commissioner Dray arrives in Patchwall (SP-GQ02).

## Origin
New for Sidebar Press (2026-11-04/05). Built from the project doc `claude/SP-SQ-arc-outline.md` and *Iuz the Evil* (WGR5, Sargent 1993). It is cast entirely from the real Field Station roster ([[RG-09 — Faculty & Staff Roster]]).

## Design Frame
- **Thesis:** an honest-looking job is the easiest way to make good people do evil work. The students are hired precisely *because* they are clean: the relic burns any hand sworn to Iuz, and Iuz's divinations can't find it.
- **Expectation broken:** *the heist is the adventure.* The heist is the first act. The real job is the second: undoing the one you just did, before dawn, without being expelled.
- **What it refuses to do:** no one in it needs killing. The merchant and his bargemen are victims; the night porter is a student; the villain is a lonely young man who can be beaten, bought, captured, or turned. A party that fights its way through the Field Station has failed the job even if it succeeds.
- **Session spine:** **Edges and Heat**, the heist economy in [[SP-GQ01 — The Job]]. Legwork earns Edges; mistakes raise Heat; Heat drives the Field Station and the *Hecht* through Lax, Wary and Alert.
- **Tone:** a frontier campus at festival time. Malt on the air, fiddle music from the Station House, a grey cog in the fog, and a box lined with bone-ash.

## Opening Narration
*Read or paraphrase this at the start of the session, before Scene 01. It is longer than a keyed box on purpose: it sets the town, the week and the common knowledge. It reveals nothing from What's Really Happening.*

> *The barge comes out of the morning mist with the Quag flat and grey around it, and Ritterstadt rises on the north shore the way the bargemen promised: a timber palisade on a low hill, a squat stone tower, smoke from two hundred chimneys and one smokehouse. Down at the water, the Landing is already loud. Rhennee barges crowd the quay, trestles stand in rows under strings of hop-garlands, and someone is rolling barrels toward a ring of shouting farmhands.*
>
> *It is the first of Brewfest, 576. For one week the brewers of the Uplands sell their year's work on the Landing, and Ritterstadt drinks it.*
>
> *Inside the palisade waits the Greyhawk University Field Station: the Station House with its hall and its counting-room, the forge, the long dormitory, and the Archive, a squat stone building with narrow windows and a slate roof. You are its students. The University pays you in credits, not coin, and on the Landing this week a student's purse is as light as a student's purse ever is.*
>
> *Everyone knows the shape of the week. Stalls every day. Barrel-wrestling every afternoon. Malzer's ale at the Fiend's Rest, Lotte Kesselring's at the Grey Wolf, and each house swears the other is selling lake water. On the third night the Warden gives his Brewfest dinner in the Station House hall, and every master and senior sits at one table under the Chancellor's portrait until the midnight bell.*
>
> *Everyone knows the rules, too. You signed the Scholar's Oath in the Station House, the one that begins "I shall not steal manuscripts." Selling University property is a standing offense. And no flame is ever carried into the Archive. The Archivist will smell it.*
>
> *And everyone has heard the talk. Two years ago a crate came up from the City under seal, guarded by two City Watchmen, and went into the Archive's sealed stacks behind the iron grille. Nobody saw what was in it; only the Registrar holds that key. A week ago a Perrender cog out of Schwartzenbruin, the* Graue Hecht, *tied up at the end of the quay with furs and salt. Her owner, a big, generous merchant named Brandmeier, has been buying copper and asking people about "the University's old things." His thin clerk does most of the talking.*
>
> *Farther off, past the lake and the hills, the old fear sits where it always does. The Old One has been free for six years. Up here, people say it quietly.*
>
> *The barge bumps the quay. A bargeman throws a line. The smell of smoked pike and new ale comes over the water to meet you.*

**To the table:** *"What brings each of you down to the Landing on the first day of Brewfest?"* Then go to [[Scene 01 — Brewfest on the Landing]].

**What the players now know** (the common knowledge; everything else they must learn in play):

- They are Field Station students, paid in credits, bound by the Scholar's Oath. Selling University property is a standing offense.
- The Archive allows no flame. Its sealed stacks sit behind an iron grille, and the Registrar holds the key.
- A sealed crate came up from the City two years ago and went into those stacks.
- The *Graue Hecht* came in a week ago. Brandmeier is asking about "the University's old things," and his clerk does most of his talking.

## Ritterstadt Color
> [!art]+ Ritterstadt
> ![[scene-ritterstadt.png|600]]

- **Brewfest.** Brewers' stalls along the Landing, barrel-wrestling, pike smoked at the smokehouse. Malzer's ale at the Fiend's Rest; Lotte Kesselring's at the Grey Wolf; both houses say the other's is water.
- **The Warden's Dinner.** The one night the whole faculty sits at one table, in the Station House hall under the Chancellor's portrait, from the first toast until the midnight bell.
- **The Scholar's Oath.** *"I shall not steal manuscripts…"* It hangs in the Station House. Nobody reads it before signing. "Selling research property" is a standing offense (RG-08).
- **Money.** Perrenders count in **marks** (gp), **kleinmarks** (sp) and **pfennigs** (cp). A laborer earns 3 kleinmarks a day. Students are paid in credits, never coin, which is why 25 marks in the hand looks like a fortune.
- **No flame in the Archive.** Ever. Verenwyr will smell it.

## What the Locals Know
- Brewfest week: stalls on the Landing every day; the Warden's dinner in the Station House hall on the third night.
- A Schwartzenbruin cog, the ***Graue Hecht***, came in a week ago with furs and salt. Her owner, **Herr Ottokar Brandmeier**, has been buying copper and asking about "the University's old things." His clerk, **Herr Fesch**, does most of his talking.
- The Archive's **sealed stacks** sit behind an iron grille. The Registrar holds the key.
- Two years ago a crate came up from the City under seal, with two City Watchmen. Nobody saw what was in it.

## What's Really Happening
- **What's in the stacks.** The crate held **[[The Gaoler's Link]]**, one link of the fetter that held Iuz in Castle Greyhawk from 505 to 570, cut with the sigils of the six who imprisoned him. It lies in a warded coffer in the sealed stacks. Only [[Professor Casimir Vogelsang]] (its keeper of record), [[Registrar Hedwig Ammerlin]], [[Rektor Albrecht Weissmantel]] and the Chancellor's office know what it is.
- **What it does.** Divination can't perceive anything within 30 feet of it; enchantments laid by Iuz's servants fail near it; and it burns any hand sworn to Iuz. Full rules: [[The Gaoler's Link]].
- **Who the clerk is.** "Herr Fesch" is **[[Dawud Vesharan]]**, a [[Tether-Mage]] in the service of **Kermin Mind-Bender** of Iuz's Greater Boneheart. He traced the Link to a manifest that said "Ritterstadt." His divinations show only a hole where the Archive stands, and he can't touch the Link. So he needs untainted hands to find it, take it, and lay it in his **[[The Ashbox|Ashbox]]**.
- **Why students.** Vesharan can't find the Link by magic, can't touch it, and can't send his thralls, because a tethered man wakes the moment the coffer opens. Outsiders can't walk into the Field Station after dark either. Students can do all of it, and the Link won't burn them.
- **Brandmeier is tethered.** A **[[Bone Tether]]** in his collar has made Vesharan's suggestions into his own memories since Flocktime. The "family reliquary" is one of them. **Within 30 feet of the unboxed Link, the tether fails** and he wakes.
- **Where the *Hecht* is really going.** Her papers say Schwartzenbruin. Her stores are laid in for a winter run up the lake's northern shore into hostile Wolf Nomad water, with a bought, tethered pilot ([[Tegin]]), and on overland to the Vesve margin and Dorakaa.

## Vesharan's Scheme
1. **Brewfest 1–2.** Brandmeier makes his offer to students who look capable, broke or bored.
2. **Brewfest 2–3.** The students case the Field Station. [[Snick]], invisible, watches and reports.
3. **Brewfest 3, night (the Warden's dinner).** The theft. If the PCs refused, the Weil cousins do it (Decline path).
4. **Brewfest 4, before dawn.** The handover at the foot of the *Hecht*'s gangplank: Fesch alone on the quay with the open Ashbox, and Brandmeier kept aboard, out of range.
5. **Brewfest 4, dawn.** The *Hecht* sails. If the students look like a risk, Snick is left behind to poison their ale at the Fiend's Rest.
6. **Brewfest 5.** Vogelsang finds the coffer empty; [[Mathias Grell]] opens an inquiry. Anyone seen near the Archive is suspended from the Board. The Link reaches Dorakaa by Sunsebb.

## Hook
1. **The Offer (default).** Brandmeier approaches the students at the Brewfest stalls, having seen them do something competent. **25 marks each**, half now, for "an hour's honest burglary of a thing that's mine."
2. **Tuition.** A PC is short of credits for their next step on the Tuition & Leveling Table, or still owes part of the 80-mark first-term bond. Brandmeier offers to cover it. Snick told him.
3. **The Perrender PC.** A letter from home: the Brandmeiers are an honest house, so why hasn't Ottokar written to his wife since Flocktime?
4. **Thread (if SP-GA04 ended in Conclusion E).** Hendrik Saal's cipher letter carried the same skull-and-grin countermark as Fesch's. [[Lecturer Perenna Dolz]] asks the students to watch Perrender traders.

**Decline path.** Fesch hires two indebted Freshmen, [[Konstanz Weil]] and [[Rudi Weil]]. On Brewfest 4 the coffer is empty, the cousins are missing, and Grell asks the students to find out why. Run Scenes 06–08 as a pursuit: a contrary wind holds the *Hecht* at the Landing until mid-morning.

## Subplots (keyed to PCs; assign at the table)
- **The broke student** (Hook 2): Brandmeier's money solves a real problem. Vesharan offers more.
- **The Perrender PC** (Hook 3): Brandmeier is someone's neighbor. Freeing him matters at home.
- **The Bakluni or Ket-born PC:** Vesharan sees himself in them and makes his recruiting pitch to them first.
- **The divination caster:** their spells keep failing near the Archive, and Dolz wants to know why.
- **The dwarf, or Hogar's friend:** [[Hogar Irongrip]] made the lead box and doesn't like Perrender secrets.
