# Scene 07 — Turning the Job

> [!art]+ Scene art (screen)
> ![[SP-GQ01 — S07 Turning the Job.png|600]]

[[10-Adventures/SP-GQ01 — The Gaoler's Link/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 06 — The Handover]] · Next → [[Scene 08 — Dawn on the Landing]]

## Purpose
The double-cross, made the students' own. Use this scene when the party knows enough (any two of F1–F4) **before** the handover, or after a failed or partial Scene 06. It also keys the *Graue Hecht* for anyone who boards her.
**Budget:** 20 minutes. Running long? If they already know everything, fold this into Scene 06 and play the approach they chose.

## Arrival
> *Brewfest 4, the dead hour before dawn. The dinner is over and the Station House is dark. Down at the Landing, the* Hecht's *lantern still burns, and someone aboard is coiling rope as if there's somewhere to be by sunrise.*

**To the table:** *"You know what you're holding, and you know who wants it. You have until the lines come off the bollards. What's the play?"*

## Who Is Here
- Everyone from Scene 06, aboard and waiting.
- Possible allies, if the party went to them:
  - **[[Hogar Irongrip]]**, at the Forge with the coals still banked.
  - **[[Lecturer Fennimore Tock]]**, who can cut passable sigils.
  - **[[Lecturer Perenna Dolz]]**, who will help with a confession.
  - **[[Professor Casimir Vogelsang]]**, who wants his Link back quietly.
  - **[[Garrok Stonebrow]]**, at the gate with two gate-rota students.
  - **[[Baba Yeska Marovna]]**, who can hold any boat at the Landing for a toll dispute.

## Beats

### Approaches the text supports (not the only ones)
- **The false Link.**
  - Hogar forges a convincing fake overnight. That takes the Hogar Edge, or DC 14 Persuasion and 2 marks.
  - Tock cuts sigils good enough to pass a glance (DC 12 Persuasion to wake him).
  - The students box the fake in the Ashbox, get paid, and the *Hecht* sails north with a lump of iron. Vesharan finds out at the first camp, and it's **Conclusion B**.
  - A suspicious Vesharan may try to touch it; a fake doesn't burn. DC 15 Charisma (Deception) to explain why the cold has gone out of it ("it lost its chill in the hold").
- **The trap.** Bring the real Link aboard **unboxed**, with Garrok and two gate-rota students (**guards**) waiting on the quay. Brandmeier's Waking happens in front of witnesses. Vesharan goes over the rail on round 1 unless someone is already at the rail. **Conclusion A.**
- **Confession.**
  - Go to Vogelsang or Dolz before dawn, confess, and plan the trap with them. Fewer credits lost, more questions asked, and **Conclusion A** with the Field Station's backing.
  - Vogelsang wants the Link back without the Warden hearing about his private study. Agreeing puts him in the party's debt (arc seed, SP-GQ04).
- **Hold the boat.** Yeska declares a toll dispute and keeps the *Hecht* tied up until the Ritter's man comes. Vesharan has to act on the quay, in public. Play Scene 06 on the planks.
- **Board her quietly.** Slip aboard by night, steal the Ashbox and the cipher letter, and pull Brandmeier's tether pin (key below).

### The Graue Hecht — key
**H1. THE DECK**
> *Wet planks, coiled line, a mast with its grey sail furled, and a brazier under an awning where a man sits whittling with his back to the shore.*

The working deck. A ladder leads down to the hold (H2), and the clerk's cabin door is aft (H3).
**Creatures:** **[[Jobst Ruder]]** (1), the *Hecht*'s master, not tethered. **[[Tegin]]** (1), at the tiller.
**Tactics:** Ruder distrusts the clerk and will help the students if shown **F2** ("He's not been right since spring. I *knew* it."). Tegin wants only to be paid and keeps out of it, unless he is within 30 feet of the unboxed Link, in which case he wakes as well (he's tethered too, and doesn't know it).
**Development:** a fight on deck brings Piet and Arnold up from the hold in 1 round.

**H2. THE HOLD**
> *A low space stacked with copper ingots and bundled furs. Two hammocks sway near the ladder, and on a crate in the middle a lead box sits open, its lid lined in grey.*

**Creatures:** **[[Piet Lanz]]** and **[[Arnold Fenn]]** (2), tethered, asleep in the hammocks (DC 12 group Stealth to pass).
**Treasure:**
- **[[The Ashbox]]** (plot key).
- A sealed crate of winter furs for a northern route (**F3**: the *Hecht* isn't going to Schwartzenbruin).
- Brandmeier's strongbox: **25 marks for each student**, which he pays as a reward if freed.
**Development:** taking the Ashbox leaves Vesharan with no safe way to carry the Link. He will try to make the students carry it all the way north, and that offer is his last card.

**H3. THE CLERK'S CABIN**
> *A cupboard of a room: a cot, a writing slope, an empty birdcage, and a smell of brimstone and cloves.*

Vesharan's berth.
**Trap:** the writing slope is warded with *alarm* (mental; only Vesharan hears it). Trigger: opening the slope. Tell: DC 13 Arcana or *detect magic*.
**Treasure:**
- **[[SP-GQ01 — Handout — The Cipher Letter]]**: to "my master in the Heart of Bone," on paper sealed with a Horned Society seal and countermarked with a grinning skull. It names "the Link" and "the girl on the levy in the Archive" (**F3**; arc seed).
- A *potion of water breathing*, if not yet used.
- Vesharan's spellbook: *charm person, command, disguise self, hold person, mage armor, suggestion*. No Iuz taint; a wizard PC may copy from it.
- A satchel of three unplanted [[Bone Tether|bone tethers]], each wrapped in black silk.
**Development:** if the *alarm* sounds and Vesharan is aboard, he comes aft at once. Otherwise he learns of the theft when he next opens the slope.

### Pulling the pin
Brandmeier's tether is in his collar lining. Finding it takes a DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check, made with advantage if anyone knows what to look for. Pulling it wakes him exactly as the Link would. Piet's, Arnold's and Tegin's are in their caps.

### Minion Pack (just in case)
Use the **Hecht Bargeman, Enforcer Minion** pack in [[Scene 06 — The Handover#Minion Pack (just in case)]]. The same crew and the same caution apply: they are tethered innocents, and each drops out within 30 feet of the unboxed Link.

## Development
- **The false Link sails** → **Conclusion B**. Vesharan is gone, Brandmeier is still tethered and still aboard, and the party has a conscience problem that opens SP-GQ02.
- **The trap springs** → **Conclusion A**, with the watch as witnesses.
- **Confession** → **Conclusion A or C**, depending on whether the party was already caught.
- **The cipher letter recovered** → +25 extra credit (Scene 08), and the arc's thread to Liesl and Molag.

## Clues Available Here
- The winter furs → **F3**
- The cipher letter (the Molag seal and skull countermark) → **F3**
- The Ashbox's Abyssal lid, DC 14 Arcana → **F4**

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a whittled shape that's turning into a pike; a cage with no bird
- **Sound:** hammocks creaking, a ward chiming in someone else's head
- **Smell:** brimstone and cloves; copper; wet fur
- **Touch:** the lead box, heavier than it looks
- **Taste:** the copper in the air of the hold

## Alternates
- **The party wants to sail with the *Hecht*** → let them. Vesharan turns north at the first island; this becomes SP-GQ05 early. Better: Ruder refuses to sail with the clerk aboard once he knows about F2.
