# Scene 03 — Legwork

> [!art]+ Scene art (screen)
> ![[SP-GQ01 — S03 Legwork.png|600]]

[[10-Adventures/SP-GQ01 — The Gaoler's Link/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — Terms]] · Next → [[Scene 04 — The Archive and the Sealed Stacks]]

## Purpose
The open phase. The players choose leads, earn **Edges**, and gather the first routes to F1–F4. Every lead is half a day of game time. Allow about four in a 4-hour slot.
**Budget:** 60 minutes. Running long? Play three leads, then montage the rest for 2 Edges total.

## Arrival
> *Brewfest 2. The Field Station is half asleep after the first night's ale. The Archive's slate roof is wet with mist, smoke rises from the University Forge, and down at the Landing the grey cog hasn't moved. You have a day and a half before the Warden's dinner.*

**To the table:** *"Who goes where? Split up if you like. Each lead takes half a day."*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Saelithan Verenwyr]]**, Archivist. *Wants:* to know why the Chancellor sends her crates without reasons. *Knows:* the stacks; the book-chute; the no-flame rule. *Reacts:* cold to bluff, warm to real scholarship.
- **[[Registrar Hedwig Ammerlin]]**. *Wants:* everything in the ledger. *Knows:* the key; the docket. *Reacts:* by the book; will talk about procedure forever.
- **[[Professor Casimir Vogelsang]]**. *Wants:* the Link safe and his study secret. *Knows:* F1 and F4 (but not who "the sworn" are). *Reacts:* superior, then uneasy.
- **[[Lecturer Perenna Dolz]]**. *Wants:* to know why her scrying shows fog. *Knows:* Ket schoolroom hands; Kermin's reputation. *Reacts:* curious, then alarmed, then a quiet ally.
- **[[Hogar Irongrip]]**, at [[The University Forge]]. *Wants:* honest work and no Perrender secrets. *Knows:* the Ashbox commission.
- **[[Jobst Ruder]]** and the *Hecht*'s bargemen; **[[Baba Yeska Marovna]]**; **[[Garrok Stonebrow]]** (Drill-Sergeant; runs the gate rota and the palisade gate); **[[Jens Ober]]** (gate rota).
- **[[Liesl Amsel]]**, on her labor-levy shift in the Archive reading room. **[[Snick]]** is watching her too.

**One Edge per lead,** maximum 4. Write down what each Edge *is*.

### Snick's watch
Each day, Snick shadows the PC doing the most interesting legwork. **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception)** to notice a draft, a flicker, a whispered giggle. If unnoticed, Vesharan learns what that PC did (raise the *Hecht* toward Wary if it was pointed). If caught:
- Snick bargains for sugar, lies about everything, and lets one true thing slip. Pick whichever the party lacks: *"Ooh, the Old One's jewelry"* (**F1**) or *"The master's master lives in the Heart of Bone"* (**F3**).
- Snick escapes by turning invisible the moment anyone relaxes. If it is caught and *kept*, the *Hecht* goes to **Alert**.

### Liesl
If any lead brings the party into the Archive, [[Liesl Amsel]] is shelving there. She complains, to no one in particular, that *"something keeps reading over my shoulder."* There's nothing to find. It's Snick, and it's the arc's first seed. Play it as a passing line.

## Development
- **A lead is botched** (a check failed by 5 or more) → +1 Heat, and someone remembers the face.
- **Vogelsang is asked about the coffer directly** → he denies it exists, then asks who wants to know. If told about Brandmeier: *"A Perrender wants an iron box? Let him want."* He's wrong, and he'll remember he said it.
- **Dolz is told everything** → she asks for one night to read the omens and comes back with a single symbol: a closed eye. She offers to help with a confession (Scene 07).

## Clues Available Here
- Piet: the master hasn't been right since spring → **F2**
- Garrok: the clerk won't pass the gate; Dolz: a Ket schoolroom hand → **F3**
- Dolz: the fog; Hogar: the ash-lined box → **F4**
- Snick, caught → **F1** or **F3**

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** wet slate, forge-smoke, a grey sail furled tight
- **Sound:** the trip-hammer at the Forge, quills in the reading room, Rhennee dice
- **Smell:** ink and cold stone; hot iron; tar and fish at the Landing
- **Touch:** a key on a ribbon, still warm from a pocket
- **Taste:** the Fiend's Rest's small beer

## Alternates
- **The party tries to case the Archive at night now** → no dinner cover; Lax still, but any light in the windows is +2 Heat. Let them, and let it cost.
- **The party ignores legwork and wants to go now** → fine. Run Scene 05 with no Edges and higher Heat.
- **The party confesses during legwork** with a fact in hand → the job becomes a sting. Scene 05 runs with Vogelsang's blessing and no Heat; the real play moves to the quay (Scene 06) with the Field Station watching.

## Transition
