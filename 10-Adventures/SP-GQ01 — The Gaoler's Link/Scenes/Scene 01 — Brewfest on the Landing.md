# Scene 01 — Brewfest on the Landing

> [!art]+ Scene art (screen)
> ![[SP-GQ01 — S01 Brewfest on the Landing.png|600]]

[[10-Adventures/SP-GQ01 — The Gaoler's Link/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 02 — Terms]]

## Purpose
Meet Brandmeier and Fesch, plant F2 (Brandmeier repeats someone else's words) and F3 (Fesch keeps clear of the University), and get an offer on the table.
**Budget:** 20 minutes. Running long? Skip the barrel-wrestling; Brandmeier picks the party anyway.

## Arrival
> *Trestles line the Landing under strings of hop-garlands, and the smokehouse wind carries pike-smoke out over the water. A Perrender cog rides at the end of the quay, her furled sail the grey of the lake. Someone is losing a wrestling match on a barrel. A broad man in a fur-trimmed coat watches you over the rim of his tankard; beside him a thin young clerk writes in a ledger without looking up.*

**To the table:** *"It's the first day of Brewfest, and none of you has a kleinmark to spare. What are you doing on the Landing?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Ottokar Brandmeier]]**, tethered. *Wants:* "his reliquary." *Knows:* only Fesch's story. *Reacts:* genial, generous, repetitive; glances at Fesch before every number.
- **[[Dawud Vesharan]]** as "Herr Fesch." *Wants:* capable, clean, needy students. *Knows:* everything. *Reacts:* speaks only to correct figures or fix the time; keeps a table between himself and anyone with a holy symbol.
- **[[Snick]]**, invisible on the trestle, eating someone's honey cake.
- **[[Baba Yeska Marovna]]**, at the tally-house door, watching the *Hecht* the way she watches weather.
- Brewfest crowd: Rhennee, drovers, students, three brewers' stalls, and **[[Konstanz Weil]]**, who owes everyone money.

## Beats

### Something competent
Brandmeier notices the party when one of them does something capable. Offer one of these, or let the players volunteer:
- **The barrel-wrestle:** DC 13 Strength (Athletics), three wins needed; a Rhennee bargeman the size of a door is the champion.
- **The fight:** two drovers squaring off over a spilled cask; DC 12 Persuasion or Intimidation to stop it before Garrok does.
- **The haggle:** a smoked-pike seller who cheats students; DC 12 Insight spots the thumb on the scale.

Whatever they do, Brandmeier raises his tankard: *"Now, that's a crew. Sit. The ale's mine."*

### The story (to a back table at the Fiend's Rest)
Brandmeier moves them out of the wind to a back table at [[The Fiend's Rest Public House]]. He tells the story Fesch gave him:
> *"My grandfather pledged our reliquary to a moneylender up here, in sixty-five. An iron piece, black, very old: family, you understand. The University took it with everything else when it came. Now the papers are lost and the Station House won't hear me. It sits in a box in their cellar, and holds, and holds, and holds."*

He can describe the coffer exactly: black iron, a grey wax seal with a starburst and a cudgel. That description is Vesharan's, from a manifest.

**A Cuthbertine at the table** knows that seal (DC 10 Religion). Asked why a family heirloom wears St. Cuthbert's seal, Brandmeier says *"Grandfather was devout,"* and glances at Fesch. It's a good catch, and a step toward **F1**.

### What a careful listener notices
- **The dates don't work.** The Field Station charter is from **568** (it's framed in the Station House hall). "The University took it in 565" is three years before there was a University here. DC 10 Intelligence (History), or automatic for any student who has read the charter. **(F1 route.)**
- **The repetition.** Asked for details (which saint, which chapel, what loan), Brandmeier repeats himself word for word and glances at Fesch. DC 14 Wisdom (Insight). **(F2 route.)**
- **"The tide of the gate."** Fesch, fixing the time: *"Before dawn on the fourth, at the tide of the gate, as we say."* Lake Quag has no tide. Automatic for a Perrender PC; DC 12 Intelligence for anyone else. A wrong idiom from a man pretending to be a Perrender clerk.
- **Magic.** *Detect magic* on Fesch shows an illusion aura (his *disguise self*), and on Brandmeier a faint enchantment at the collar. Either is a strong early route to **F3** or **F2**. Let it work; Fesch will notice the spell and go to Wary.
- **Snick.** Anyone who sits near the honey cakes and succeeds on a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check sees a cake lose a bite by itself.

## Development
- **The party asks Fesch a direct question** → he answers in perfect, slightly old-fashioned Common, and his Perrender accent slips on one word. Note it; it pays off in Scene 03 with Dolz.
- **A PC with a holy symbol sits beside Fesch** → he moves. If asked, *"Drafts."*
- **A PC refuses to drink with them** → Brandmeier isn't offended. Fesch is, and watches that PC for the rest of the adventure.

## Clues Available Here
- "The University took it in 565" → **F1**
- Word-for-word repetition; glances at Fesch → **F2**
- Fesch avoids holy symbols; a wrong idiom → toward **F3**

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** hop-garlands, a grey cog, a clerk's ink-stained cuffs
- **Sound:** fiddles, a Rhennee song, a barrel hitting the planks
- **Smell:** pike-smoke, malt, wet rope
- **Touch:** a sticky trestle, the warmth of the Fiend's Rest after the wind
- **Taste:** Malzer's new ale, too young

## Alternates
- **The party is already suspicious of strangers** → Brandmeier approaches through Konstanz Weil, who introduces them for a cut.
- **No one is broke** → lead with Hook 3 (the Perrender PC's letter) or Hook 4 (Dolz's request).
