# Scene 06 — The Handover

> [!art]+ Scene art (screen)
> ![[SP-GQ01 — S06 The Handover.png|600]]

[[10-Adventures/SP-GQ01 — The Gaoler's Link/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 05 — The Night of the Warden's Dinner]] · Next → [[Scene 07 — Turning the Job]]

## Purpose
The reveal. Everything learned so far pays off here. **The scene turns on one question: is the Link boxed?**
**Budget:** 45 minutes. **Never cut this scene.** Running long? Open on the gangplank.

## Arrival
> *Brewfest 4, before dawn. Fog lies on the lake like wool, and the Rhennee barges along the Landing are dark. The* Graue Hecht's *lantern is a yellow smear at the end of the quay, and two bargemen stand at her rail with their arms folded. At the foot of the gangplank, alone, Herr Fesch waits with a lead box open in his arms, its lid lined with something grey.*

**To the table:** *"Fesch holds out the box. 'Lay the piece in here, please, and close the lid. The master is asleep aboard. No need to wake him.' Who's carrying it?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Dawud Vesharan]]** as Fesch. *Wants:* the Link boxed. *Knows:* the laws of the Link. *Reacts:* see the escalation script below.
- **[[Ottokar Brandmeier]]**, tethered, aboard at the far rail, about 40 feet from the quay. Vesharan keeps him out of range on purpose.
- **[[Piet Lanz]]** and **[[Arnold Fenn]]**, tethered bargemen at the top of the gangplank, about 35 feet away, ready to come down.
- **[[Snick]]**, invisible on a bollard near Fesch, keeping its distance from whoever smells of cold iron (the ache).
- **[[Jobst Ruder]]**, under the awning, whittling, not tethered, not happy.
- **[[Tegin]]**, the Wolf Nomad pilot, at the tiller (APL 4: he fights for pay).

## Beats

### If the Link is boxed on the quay
The deal closes. Fesch pays the rest of the fee and shakes no one's hand. Brandmeier never wakes. The *Hecht* sails at dawn. If the party knows two or more of F1–F4, they can still act before the lines are cast off: go to [[Scene 07 — Turning the Job]].

**Safety valve (Dolz).** The moment the Link is boxed, Lecturer Dolz's scrying clears for the first time in two years. She works out what that means and reaches the Landing, out of breath, as the lines come off: *"The fog's gone. Whatever was hiding in the Archive just left it. Was it you?"* The party gets one last chance: [[Scene 07 — Turning the Job]] (**Hold the boat** with Baba Yeska, or board her). If they shrug, it is **Conclusion E** ([[Scene 08 — Dawn on the Landing]]).

### If the Link comes within 30 feet of Brandmeier
This happens if the party carries it up the gangplank, insists on handing it to "the master" himself, or argues on the quay long enough (3 rounds) that Brandmeier comes down to see what's keeping them. **Brandmeier's Waking:**
> *Brandmeier stops in the middle of a word and looks at his own hand as if he has never seen it. "Who — where is my ship? Where is my* wife*?" He stares at you, then at the thin shape at his elbow. "Who in all the hells is Fesch?"*

At the same moment, Piet and Arnold blink, lower their arms, and look around as if waking on the wrong boat. **(F2 last resort; F1 last resort if Brandmeier is asked about his grandfather.)** Fesch, still disguised, says calmly: *"Box it, students."*

### Vesharan's escalation script
1. **Coax:** *"Five marks more each. You've a good thing; don't spoil it."*
2. **Recruit:** he drops the disguise to show a young Bakluni face, tired around the eyes. *"The University will teach you to catalogue things like that. My master would teach you to use them."* He makes this pitch to a Bakluni or Ket-born PC first, if there is one. (**F3 last resort.**)
3. **Threaten:** Snick appears on the crate, grinning. *Hold person* on whoever holds the Link.
4. **Grab:** he orders Piet and Arnold to wrestle the Link into the box. This works only while they are tethered, that is, more than 30 feet from the unboxed Link.

### Snick and the iron
If Snick tries to take the Link, it shrieks as its claws smoke and drops it (the burn). **(F4 last resort.)** Then it bolts for the fog, and it will bargain later for sugar and its life.

Statblocks: [[Tether-Mage]] (Vesharan), quasit (MM p. 63; Snick), [[Tethered Sleeper]] (bargemen), scout (MM p. 349; Tegin). See [[SP-GQ01 — Bestiary]].
**Terrain:** the deck (H1) and the open hold (H2), 10 feet below by ladder; the gangplank; fog beyond 30 feet (lightly obscured). The rail is the way out for anyone who can swim.
**Tactics:**
- **Vesharan** never touches the Link. He fights from range with *command* and *hold person*, uses Puppet's Shield every round he can, and keeps a crate between himself and any holy symbol. **The Ache's Shield:** within 30 feet of the unboxed Link he can't target its bearer, so he backs off to 35–60 ft before casting at the bearer, or works on everyone else. A bearer who closes on him shuts down his best spells against them; tell the players the first time he flinches away.
- **The bargemen** wake the moment they come within 30 feet of the unboxed Link, and stop fighting. Carrying the Link toward them is a tactic; tell the players once.
- **Snick** can't attack the Link-bearer (the Ache's Shield), so it tries to snatch the Link instead: a contested Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check against the bearer's Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics). It pays for it either way (the Burn).
- **At half hit points** Vesharan drinks his *potion of water breathing* and dives over the rail into the fog, making for the reed-shore. He is built to escape, but he can be stopped: grapple him, guard the rail, or put a PC in the water.
**DM direction:** the fight ends when Vesharan is gone, captured or talking. Don't play it to the last hit point.

## Development
- **Vesharan captured** → he says nothing until he is within 30 feet of the Link. Then he talks, frightened: *"He'll know I failed the moment that thing is boxed. Don't box it. Please."* (Arc seed: the defection path, SP-GQ05.)
- **Vesharan escapes** → he's in the reeds by sunrise and gone by Patchwall. Record it for SP-GQ02.
- **Brandmeier awake** → he is horrified, then grateful, then furious. He pays his reward (25 marks each) and gives the party the run of the *Hecht*. Ruder searches the clerk's cabin with them (H3, Scene 07).
- **The Link boxed in the fight** → Iuz's divinations can find it again. If it leaves the Landing boxed, it's Conclusion E unless the party gets it back before dawn.
- **Heat 5–6 from Scene 05** → Grell arrives on the quay mid-fight with two proctors. He stops the fight first and asks questions second, which helps the party more than it hurts.
