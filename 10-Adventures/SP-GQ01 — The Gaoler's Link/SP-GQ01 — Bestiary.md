# Bestiary — The Gaoler's Link

Back to [[SP-GQ01-The-Gaolers-Link]]

All statblocks use **2014 5e** rules; MM page numbers refer to the 2014 *Monster Manual*. Three creatures are new Sidebar Press creatures (full entries, lore and FGU import in `65-Bestiary/Shadow on the Quag/`). The rest are stock NPC blocks, used as printed unless a change is listed.

**APL 3, four PCs:** easy 300 · medium 600 · hard 900 · deadly 1,600 (adjusted XP).

## New Creatures

> [!example]- Tether-Mage (CR 2, 450 XP)
> *Medium humanoid (any race), neutral evil*
> **Armor Class** 12 (15 with *mage armor*) · **Hit Points** 58 (13d8) · **Speed** 30 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 9 (−1) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 17 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) |
>
> **Saving Throws** Int +5, Wis +3 · **Skills** Arcana +5, Deception +4, Insight +3
> **Senses** passive Perception 11 · **Languages** Common plus any two languages · **Challenge** 2 (450 XP)
>
> ***Spellcasting.*** The mage casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit with spell attacks):
> At will: *friends*, *mage hand*, *minor illusion*
> 1/day each: *charm person*, *command*, *disguise self*, *hold person*, *mage armor*, *suggestion*
>
> ***Tether's Eye.*** As a bonus action, the mage perceives through the senses of its familiar, or of any creature wearing one of its [[Bone Tether|bone tethers]], if that creature is within 1 mile of it. This lasts until the start of its next turn, and during it the mage is blinded and deafened with regard to its own surroundings.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Multiattack.*** The mage makes two Splinter of Will attacks.
>
> ***Splinter of Will.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +5 to hit, range 60 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) psychic damage.
>
> ***Dagger.*** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
>
> **Reactions**
> ***Puppet's Shield.*** When a creature the mage can see targets it with an attack, the mage chooses a creature charmed by it within 5 feet of it. That creature becomes the attack's target instead.

>
> **In this adventure:** **[[Dawud Vesharan]]** (Named Example). *Mage armor* is cast before Scene 06 (AC 15). He carries a *potion of water breathing* and dives for the reeds at half hit points. **Scenes 06 (fight), 01–02, 07 (social).** Full entry: [[Tether-Mage]].

> [!example]- Tethered Sleeper (CR 1/4, 50 XP)
> *Medium humanoid (any race), any alignment*
> **Armor Class** 12 (leather armor) · **Hit Points** 32 (5d8 + 10) · **Speed** 30 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 13 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 8 (−1) | 10 (+0) |
>
> **Condition Immunities** frightened
> **Senses** passive Perception 9 · **Languages** any one language (usually Common) · **Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)
>
> ***Sleeper's Calm.*** The sleeper speaks in flat, borrowed phrases and can't be frightened.
>
> ***Tether-Bound.*** The sleeper is charmed by its handler, a creature that planted a [[Bone Tether]] on it. While charmed this way it makes no saving throws to end the effect. If its tether is removed, or if it starts its turn within 30 feet of the unboxed [[The Gaoler's Link|Gaoler's Link]], it is incapacitated until the end of its next turn. After that, it is no longer charmed and stops fighting, confused about where it is and why.
>
> ***Handler's Window.*** The sleeper's handler can perceive through its senses (see the [[Tether-Mage]]'s Tether's Eye).
>
> **Actions**
> ***Multiattack.*** The sleeper makes two Cudgel attacks.
>
> ***Cudgel.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage.

>
> **In this adventure:** **[[Piet Lanz]]** and **[[Arnold Fenn]]**, the *Hecht*'s bargemen, with cargo hooks used as cudgels. Tegin is tethered too, but uses the scout block. **Scenes 06, 07.** Innocents: they wake within 30 ft of the unboxed Link. Full entry: [[Tethered Sleeper]].

> [!example]- Trophy Guardian (CR 1, 200 XP)
> *Large construct, unaligned*
> **Armor Class** 14 (natural armor) · **Hit Points** 52 (7d10 + 14) · **Speed** 15 ft., swim 40 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 16 (+3) | 8 (−1) | 14 (+2) | 1 (−5) | 5 (−3) | 1 (−5) |
>
> **Damage Immunities** poison, psychic · **Condition Immunities** blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned
> **Senses** blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 7 · **Languages** — · **Challenge** 1 (200 XP)
>
> ***Antimagic Susceptibility.*** The guardian is incapacitated while in the area of an *antimagic field*. If targeted by *dispel magic*, the guardian must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against the caster's spell save DC or fall unconscious for 1 minute.
>
> ***False Appearance.*** While the guardian remains motionless on its mount, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary mounted trophy.
>
> ***Watchword.*** The guardian stops, goes still, and returns to its mount when a creature within 30 feet of it speaks its watchword aloud. Its maker sets the watchword.
>
> **Actions**
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it is grappled (escape DC 13). Until this grapple ends, the guardian can't bite another target.
>
> ***Tail Sweep (Recharge 5–6).*** Each creature within 10 feet of the guardian must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage and is knocked prone. On a success, it takes half as much damage and isn't knocked prone.

>
> **In this adventure:** **the Archive Pike** (A5). Watchword: *"by leave of the Registrar."* At APL 4 a matched lake gar joins it. **Scene 05.** Full entry: [[Trophy Guardian]].
