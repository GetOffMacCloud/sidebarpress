# Handout — The Provenance Slip

Back to [[SP-GQ01-The-Gaolers-Link]] · Found in [[Scene 04 — The Archive and the Sealed Stacks]] (A6), under the felt

## Player Text
![[SP-GQ01 Handout - The Provenance Slip.png|420]]

*(Two slips of good paper in a neat, sloped hand. The first is ruled like a catalogue card. The second is crowded with notes, some crossed out.)*

> **ANTIQUITIES — SEALED STACKS — ITEM 574/1**
> *Acquired:* 573 CY, City of Greyhawk, from Varno the dealer (salvage, Castle Greyhawk, lower works).
> *Description:* Fetter-link, black iron, warded. Six sigils. "Zagig period."
> *Identified:* 574 — *the Six.*
> *Transferred:* Ritterstadt, by the Chancellor's order, 574. Keeper of record: C. Vogelsang.

> *Notes —*
> *Divination fails in its presence. Br. Dolz's students: "fog." Mine also.*
> *The Chancellor's letter: "the sworn cannot hold it." WHICH sworn?*
> *Cold. Always cold. Does not warm in the hand.*
> *Starburst and cudgel — Cuthbert. Zigzags — ? Pipes — ? The rest — ask Tock? NO. Not yet.*
> *If this is what I think it is, it should never have left the City. Or never have stayed.*

## DM Notes
- **F1:** "the Six" and "Castle Greyhawk, lower works," together with the sigils, mean the six who imprisoned Iuz (DC 15 History or Arcana to make the leap).
- **F4:** "divination fails" and "the sworn cannot hold it" are the Link's two powers. Vogelsang doesn't know who "the sworn" are. The party learns that in Scene 06.
- **"Ask Tock? NO."** Vogelsang has been keeping his study private, even from his own department. That's leverage, and it's an arc seed (SP-GQ04).
