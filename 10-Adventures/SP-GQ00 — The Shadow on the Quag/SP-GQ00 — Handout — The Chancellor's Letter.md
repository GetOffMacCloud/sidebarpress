# Handout 1 — The Chancellor's Letter

> [!art]+ Handout 1
> ![[SP-GQ00 Handout - The Chancellor's Letter.png|500]]

*Give this only to a reader who opens the letter-case: Vesharan (by report, in GQ1), or the students if they break the seal (Conclusion C). Vogelsang reads it in Scene 7.*

> *Greyhawk, the 2nd of Goodmonth, 576*
>
> *Casimir,*
>
> *The Commission will ask you this autumn to send south the consignment of 574. You will say it cannot travel before spring. You will say it in writing, and you will copy me.*
>
> *I have now had the second opinion I asked for. It is as we feared: the sworn cannot hold it. I will not write more than that on paper, and I would ask you to stop writing anything at all. Keep it dark, keep it below, and keep your hours with it short.*
>
> *I will come north when the lake freezes, if my knees allow. Until then you are its only keeper. Do not make me regret that.*
>
> *— A. Adenauer, Chancellor*

## For the DM
- **What it gives away:** that Vogelsang keeps a "consignment of 574" that "the sworn cannot hold," and that the Regents (Dray, GQ2) want it sent south. It does **not** name the Link or say what it does.
- **The "second opinion"** came from [[Ouderling Machteld Vreeswijk]], the Old Kerk's Keeper of Oaths in Schwartzenbruin. In 574 the crate lay three nights in her undercroft on its way north, and it burned the palm of a recanted Iuzite lay-brother. Varek Doss, still assessing the Fiend's Embrace (RG-08), is not involved. Who gave it stays an open thread for the players until GQ4.
- **Continuity:** this is the "Chancellor's letter" Vogelsang quotes in his study notes in GQ1 (Handout: [[SP-GQ01 — Handout — The Provenance Slip]]). The Chancellor's promise to "come north when the lake freezes" is an open thread for GQ5.
