# The Crossing

**APL 2 (levels 1–3) · 4–6 characters · one 3-hour session**
*The prologue to [[SP-GQ00 — The Shadow on the Quag|The Shadow on the Quag]]*

> *"Three days on the lake, and the grey in the middle. Say your prayers before the grey, not in it."*
> — Rhennee saying

**Pitch.** The students are going home. A Job Board commission under the Warden's seal and Karet's stamp sent them to Schwartzenbruin with the term's field reports (a courier run counts as levy duty, so Probationers may go without an enrolled party), and they come back with the Field Station's post, a sealed letter for Professor Vogelsang that must go into no hand but his, and a new Probationer who hears things nobody else does. The only cog sailing north is the *Graue Hecht*, whose genial owner gives University folk free passage and whose quiet clerk asks a great many questions. Something aboard wants the letter, and in the middle of the lake the fog wants everyone.

## How to Use This Adventure
This is the prologue to the GQ series. It introduces the arc's cast before *The Gaoler's Link* (GQ1), which begins a week after the *Hecht* reaches Ritterstadt. Nothing here reveals [[The Gaoler's Link]]; the students learn only that someone wants Vogelsang's post. Record the outcomes on the **Arc Ledger** (see [[SP-GQ00 — The Shadow on the Quag#Running the Arc|Running the Arc]]); GQ1 reads them.

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- The *Graue Hecht* is a Schwartzenbruin cog, owned by **Herr Ottokar Brandmeier**, a merchant in furs, salt and copper, and mastered by **Jobst Ruder**. She sails for Ritterstadt with the last salt before winter.
- Brandmeier is known along the waterfront as a big-voiced, generous man. His new clerk, **Herr Fesch**, has been with him since spring.
- The **grey** at the center of Lake Quag is a fog-bank that never fully lifts. Ships go into it and some don't come out. The Rhennee won't sail through it; Perrender cogs skirt its edge.
- The University keeps an agent in Schwartzenbruin, **Factor Agnes Feldhaus**, who receives the City's post for the Field Station (it comes up the Velverdyva with the river post) and sends it north by whatever ship is going.

### What's Really Happening
- **Fesch is [[Dawud Vesharan]]**, Kermin Mind-Bender's field agent, in *disguise self*. Since Flocktime he has kept Brandmeier **tethered** with a [[Bone Tether]] in his collar. He is going to Ritterstadt to find the thing the Field Station's scryings won't show him.
- **The Chancellor's letter.** Among the post is a letter under Chancellor Adenauer's seal "to Professor Vogelsang, by hand." Feldhaus mentions it on the waterfront; Snick hears. Vesharan wants to know what it says, because any letter from the Chancellor to the head of Antiquities may name what was sent north in 574. (It does, obliquely: see [[SP-GQ00 — Handout — The Chancellor's Letter|Handout 1]].)
- **[[Snick]]**, Vesharan's quasit, is aboard and invisible. It will try three times to open or take the letter-case. It also can't keep away from **[[Liesl Amsel]]**, whose untrained wild talent lets her hear the invisible. She thinks the ship is haunted.
- **Liesl** is fifteen-going-on-sixteen, from the fishing village of Unterquag on the south shore. Her village priest of Delleb wrote to the University about "a girl who answers voices in empty rooms." She has a place as a Probationer; she can't pay the 80-mark first-term bond, so she will work it off on the Archive's labor levy (RG-08). Kermin's diviners marked her in summer; Vesharan has been told to look at her, and he does.
- **The grey.** Ruder means to skirt the fog. On the second night the wind dies and the current carries the *Hecht* into it. The fog holds the **fog-drowned**, the lake's lost sailors, who climb aboard any ship that strays in. This is the Blackfrost Bloom year, and the Rhennee say there are more of the drowned than anyone remembers.

### Vesharan's Scheme (if nobody interferes)
1. **Harvester 19.** Brandmeier offers the students free passage ("University folk sail free on the *Hecht*") and takes Liesl too. Snick learns the letter-case is in a student's pack.
2. **Harvester 19, night.** Snick tries the letter-case latch.
3. **Harvester 20, day.** Fesch makes friends: arithmetic help, questions about the Field Station, Vogelsang, and "the University's old things."
4. **Harvester 20, night.** The *Hecht* drifts into the grey. In the chaos of the fog-drowned boarding, Snick takes the letter-case below and reads the letter by the clerk's candle.
5. **Harvester 21.** The *Hecht* reaches Ritterstadt. Vesharan now knows the Chancellor's warning and the name of the keeper. He has his target, and in a week he has his thieves.

## Adventure Outline
In Schwartzenbruin the students collect the Field Station's post and a new Probationer, and take free passage north on the *Graue Hecht* (Part 1). Aboard, something invisible worries at the letter-case while the owner's clerk asks polite questions (Part 2). On the second night the ship drifts into the grey at the lake's heart, and the drowned come aboard (Part 3). At Ritterstadt the students deliver the letter, or explain why they can't (Part 4).

## Part 1: The Post-House

### Scene 1: Factor Feldhaus (15 min)
*Purpose:* the job, the letter, Liesl, the passage.

> *The University's post-house is two rooms over a chandler's on the Schwartzenbruin waterfront, smelling of tar and sealing-wax. A grey-haired woman with spectacles on a ribbon stacks oilcloth bundles on the counter. Beside the stove a thin girl in a borrowed cloak sits on a sea-chest, reading a pamphlet she has clearly read before.*

**Factor [[Agnes Feldhaus]]** hands over the Field Station's post: two oilcloth bundles of letters and a **letter-case** of black leather, locked, with the Chancellor's seal on the strap. *"That one goes into Professor Vogelsang's hand and no other. Not the Registrar's. Not the Warden's. His."* She gives the key to nobody; Vogelsang has the other.

She also hands over **[[Liesl Amsel]]**: *"New Probationer. She's to be on the Archive's labor levy. See she gets there."*

The only ship going north this week is the *Graue Hecht*. Feldhaus has already arranged it: *"Herr Brandmeier won't take a pfennig from University folk. Lucky you."*

**To the table:** *"Who's carrying the letter-case?"* Note the answer; Snick will go to that pack.

**Development:**
- Feldhaus says, loudly enough to be overheard on the waterfront, *"from the Chancellor himself."* That's how Snick learns of it.
- If asked, she has had the letter a week; it came up the Velverdyva with the river post. She doesn't know what's in it and doesn't want to.
- **Reward on delivery:** 30 credits each, entered in the Registrar's ledger when Vogelsang signs Feldhaus's chit.

**The lake-gate.** On the way to the quay, **[[Sergeant Ruwen Hask]]** asks each student his three questions (*"Business. Lodging. Company."*) and writes the answers down. His ledger has logged the *Hecht*'s crew since spring: *"Fesch, clerk, joined Flocktime; sponsor a counting-house of Molag."* A student who reads over his clerk's shoulder (Investigation DC 12) or asks politely (Persuasion DC 12) sees it (P2).

## Part 2: The *Graue Hecht*

### Scene 2: Casting Off (Harvester 19, noon) (15 min)
*Purpose:* meet the ship, Brandmeier, Fesch and Ruder.

> *The* Graue Hecht *is a broad-bellied cog with a grey-painted pike's head on her stem post and salt sacks stacked to the gunwales. A big red-faced man in a fur-trimmed coat booms a welcome from the quarterdeck and waves away your purse before you can open it. At his elbow a thin clerk in black writes in a ledger and does not look up.*

**[[Ottokar Brandmeier]]** (tethered): generous, booming, repetitive. *"University folk sail free on the* Hecht. *Learning is the only cargo that never spoils."* (He says this twice in an hour, word for word. That line is Fesch's.)
**"Herr Fesch"** ([[Dawud Vesharan]], disguised): polite, precise, a glove on his left hand. He corrects the cook's arithmetic and the PCs' if they do any aloud.
**[[Jobst Ruder]]**: weathered, slow-spoken, whittling at the tiller. He watches the clerk.
**Six bargemen**, two of them tethered (they speak in flat, borrowed phrases).

**Berths:** the students and Liesl sleep in the hold forward, among the salt (see the ship key). Brandmeier offers the letter-case "a dry berth in my cabin, with my own strongbox." If the students accept, Snick has an easier time.

### Scene 3: The First Night (Harvester 19–20) (30 min)
*Purpose:* Snick's first and second tries; Liesl's talent; legwork aboard.

> *Night on the lake is black water and the creak of the yard. The salt in the hold smells of the sea it came from. Somewhere aft the clerk's candle burns late behind his curtain. Liesl lies awake with her eyes open, listening to something.*

**Snick's first try (the latch).** In the small hours, Snick tries the letter-case latch in its pack (or in Brandmeier's cabin). The PC on watch, or the pack's owner, notices with a **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception)** check: a tug at the pack, a breath of brimstone. Snick flees invisibly if noticed. In the morning the latch has fresh scratches (P1).
**Liesl at night.** She whispers to a PC who is awake: *"There's something in here with us. It's small. It talks to itself. It wants to read your letter, and it thinks I'm* interesting." (P1, P3). She is frightened but sharp; if a PC takes her seriously, she trusts that PC from now on (Arc Ledger: **Liesl's trust**).

**Day on the lake (Harvester 20).** Let the players spend the day; each of these takes about an hour of ship time:
| Approach | Check | Learns |
|---|---|---|
| Talk with Ruder at the tiller | Persuasion DC 12, or help with the sheets (Athletics DC 10) | P2 ("not a note since spring"); the grey: "We skirt it. If the wind dies, the current takes you in." |
| Drink with the bargemen | Persuasion DC 12 | The two who "talk like the clerk" (the tethered); the clerk "doesn't come on deck when a priest prays." |
| Help [[Mother Gunde]] in the galley | Survival or cooking DC 10 | "Sugar's going missing. Something with a sweet tooth and no feet." The Rhennee fog-charm: a lit lamp in each hand keeps the drowned off (fire and radiant stop them regenerating). |
| Talk with Fesch | Insight DC 14 | He asks about the Field Station's departments, who keeps "the University's old things," and whether the Antiquities professor "has a great many visitors." He never asks about the letter. |
| Talk with Brandmeier | Insight DC 14 | P2: he repeats Fesch's lines; he can't say when he hired his clerk. |
| Watch Liesl | — | She flinches at nothing three times; once she answers it. (P3) |
| Search the clerk's cabin (H3) | Stealth DC 13 while Fesch is on deck; Investigation DC 12 | The sugar pot, the empty birdcage, the ledger's Ket hand. Fesch knows within the hour if anything is moved (+1 **Suspicion**). |

**Suspicion (0–3).** Rises when the students search H3 or H4, accuse the clerk, or catch Snick and let it go. At 2, Fesch keeps the tethered bargemen near the students. At 3, he asks Brandmeier to put them ashore "at the first village," which can't happen until Ritterstadt; the bargemen become surly. Suspicion carries into GQ1 (Vesharan starts **Wary** if it ended at 2 or more).

**Snick's second try (the day).** While the students are busy, Snick slips into the hold. A PC who stays with the letter-case notices it on a **DC 13 Perception** check. If caught, see **Snick Cornered** below.

#### Snick Cornered (optional encounter)
If the students corner Snick (for example by covering the hatch and swinging lanterns, or Liesl pointing at "there!"), it drops invisibility and bargains: *"Snick wants sugar. Snick knows* things." It lies about everything except that it serves "a clever man" and "the master's master is very far north." It will not name Fesch unless frightened (Intimidation DC 15).
**Creatures:** [[Snick]] (**quasit**) (1). **Encounter check:** 200 XP, Easy for any party size.
**Tactics:** Scare, then claws, then invisibility and the bilge. It flees at half hit points.
**Development:** if Snick is killed, Vesharan loses his familiar. He doesn't show it, but in GQ1 he works without Snick (cut Snick's lines; Vesharan's legwork uses a hired urchin instead). If Snick is bribed (sugar), it becomes the unreliable informant early. Record it on the Arc Ledger.

## Part 3: The Grey

### Scene 4: Into the Fog (Harvester 20, night) (30 min)
*Purpose:* the set piece. Navigation, Liesl's talent, the boarding.

> *The wind dies at dusk and does not come back. The* Hecht *drifts on a slow black current, and ahead a wall of fog stands on the water, pale under no moon. Ruder stops whittling. Mother Gunde lights two lamps and holds one in each hand.*

The current carries the ship into the grey. **Navigating out** is a group effort: **3 successes before 3 failures**. Each character may try one approach per round; describe a round as about ten minutes of drifting.
| Approach | Check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Read the current and the wind | Survival DC 13 | Ruder grants advantage if he's on good terms with the party. |
| Listen for the shore bells of Unterquag or the Wachturm bell | Perception DC 13 | — |
| Sound the depth with a weighted line | Athletics or Nature DC 12 | Shallows mean an isle; steer away. |
| Pray to Osprem, Procan or the lake | Religion DC 13 | Ruder prays to Osprem; a PC who joins him adds advantage. |
| **Liesl listens** | — | Automatic success **once**, if a PC asks her: *"They're singing on that side. The shore's the other way."* |
| Row (the ship's sweeps) | Athletics DC 12 | Two PCs together. |

**Each failure:** the drowned come closer. The first failure triggers the boarding (Scene 5) if it hasn't started; later failures add one **fog-drowned** to the deck.
**Three failures:** the *Hecht* grounds softly on a fog-bound isle. Through the grey the students see a timber lodge with a smoking chimney and an iceskimmer drawn up under a tarpaulin (P4). The ship floats free at dawn. Vesharan, at the rail, sees it too, and remembers it (this is the lodge of GQ5).
**Three successes:** the *Hecht* drifts out of the grey before midnight, with the drowned left behind on the water.

### Scene 5: The Drowned Come Aboard (Encounter, Medium)
*Purpose:* the fight, and Snick's real try for the letter.

> *Something knocks on the hull. Then a hand comes over the rail, swollen and grey, with a rope still knotted round the wrist. A drowned bargeman pulls himself up out of the fog, water running from his mouth, and behind him another face rises out of the lake.*

**Creatures:**
| Party | Creatures | Adjusted XP |
|---|---|---|
| 4 PCs | [[Fog-Drowned]] (1), **zombies** (1): a drowned cabin boy | 375 (just under Medium; each failed navigation check adds a fog-drowned) |
| 5 PCs | [[Fog-Drowned]] (1), **zombies** (2) | 600 (Medium) |
| 6 PCs | [[Fog-Drowned]] (2), **zombies** (1) | 675 (Medium) |

APL 2 thresholds: 4 PCs Easy 200, Medium 400, Hard 600; 5 PCs 250/500/750; 6 PCs 300/600/900 (multiplier one step lower for 6+).

**Tactics:**
- The drowned climb the rail where the deck is darkest, grab, and drag toward the water. A creature grappled by a fog-drowned at the rail is pulled overboard on the drowned's next turn unless it escapes (DC 13) or someone holds on (contested Athletics).
- **Fog-Fed:** the fog-drowned regain 3 hit points each turn in the fog unless they took fire or radiant damage. Mother Gunde's lamps, a thrown lantern (1d4 fire and the oil burns), *sacred flame* and Turn Undead all work. She tells anyone who'll listen.
- The **crew** fight their own battle off-stage with boathooks; say so, and show a bargeman go over the side if the students don't help. Two tethered bargemen fight in flat silence.
- **Fesch** stays below "with the master." If a PC goes to H3–H4, he's at his writing slope with a candle, and the letter-case is or isn't beside him (see below).

**Snick's grab.** On the second round, Snick takes the letter-case (or the pack holding it) and makes for the clerk's cabin (H3) invisibly. A PC who said they'd guard it, or who keeps a hand on it, stops the grab automatically. Otherwise a **DC 13 Perception** check notices the pack moving by itself toward the stern hatch; it moves 40 ft a round (fly 40). If no one stops it, Snick reaches H3 in two rounds and Vesharan reads the letter by candle, then has Snick put the case back where it was, re-latched (Investigation DC 15 later shows the strap's been opened; the Chancellor's seal is intact because the case opens at the hinge pins).
**Overboard.** If the case goes into the lake (a fumble, a drowned grabbing the bearer), it's gone.

**Development:**
- **Nobody lost overboard** is worth extra credit; Ruder remembers it.
- If a PC confronts Fesch with the case in his cabin, he says smoothly that he "found it on the stair and brought it somewhere dry." Insight DC 16 sees the lie. He will not fight. If the students attack him, use **Unmasking the Clerk** below.

#### Unmasking the Clerk (if the students force it)
If the students attack Fesch, pull his glove, or drag him into the light, his *disguise self* drops when he's hit: a young Bakluni man with a burn-scarred palm. He doesn't fight to win. He casts *command* ("Flee!") on the nearest PC, swallows his *potion of water breathing* and goes over the side into the fog. The two tethered bargemen block the rail for one round.
**Consequences:** Brandmeier is still tethered (the pin isn't removed by this). GQ1 changes: Vesharan returns to Ritterstadt under a new face as a different merchant's clerk, and the students are already on his list (he starts **Alert**). This is a legitimate outcome; don't prevent it.

**Finding the tether.** If a PC uses *detect magic* near Brandmeier (faint enchantment on his collar) or searches his coat (Investigation DC 13, only if they think to look), they can pull the [[Bone Tether]]. Brandmeier wakes mid-sentence: *"Where is my wife? Why am I on my own ship with — who is* that*?"* Fesch leaves the same way, over the side. **GQ1 changes:** Brandmeier is the students' friend from the start; Vesharan hires through a new tethered dupe (use the Weil cousins' creditor, a Ritterstadt factor), and the "family reliquary" story comes from him instead.

## Part 4: Landfall

### Scene 6: The Landing (Harvester 21) (20 min)
*Purpose:* deliver the letter; set up GQ1.

> *Ritterstadt comes out of the morning haze: a palisade on a rise above the lake with a raw new stretch of timber at the eastern gate, peat-smoke, the Wachturm on the highest ground. The* Hecht *noses in among the Rhennee barges at the Landing. An old woman in layered skirts stands on the quay with a knotted cord at her belt, counting the salt sacks before the gangplank is down.*

- **[[Baba Yeska Marovna]]** charges the *Hecht*'s toll and looks the ship over. If told the *Hecht* went into the grey: *"And came out. Mind you're grateful. The Ket woman up at the University read me calm water all week. Calm is what puts you in the grey."* If asked about the isle (P4): *"There's an isle in the grey. Nobody fishes it. Somebody keeps a fire there in winter."*
- **[[Garrok Stonebrow]]**, the Drill-Sergeant, has the gate this morning and notes that the clerk stays on the quay: *"Won't come inside. Poor sailor on land, he says."* (A GQ1 clue planted early.)
- **Liesl** is delivered to the Registrar's counting-room and the Archive. She asks the PC she trusts to visit her there.
- **Brandmeier** shakes every hand: *"Brewfest is in a week! You'll drink with me on the Landing."* He means it, and in GQ1 he finds them.

### Scene 7: Professor Vogelsang (10 min)
**[[Professor Casimir Vogelsang]]** receives the letter-case in his study at the Station House, opens it with his own key, reads the letter, and goes pale. He thanks the students curtly, signs Feldhaus's chit, and asks one question: *"Was it ever out of your sight?"*
- **Honest answer, case intact:** he relaxes and offers each student a reading pass for the Archive's reading room for the term (useful in GQ1).
- **Honest answer, case tampered with:** he goes paler. He thanks them for telling him, asks them to say nothing, and that evening walks to the Archive with no lamp. (In GQ1, Vogelsang has already moved his study hours, and the Vogelsang lead is DC 15 instead of 13.)
- **Lies:** Insight DC 12 catches it. He signs the chit, and later remembers who lied.
- **The seal broken by the students:** see Conclusion C.
