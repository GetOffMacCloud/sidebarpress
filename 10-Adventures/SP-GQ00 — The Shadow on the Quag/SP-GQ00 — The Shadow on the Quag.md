# GQ0 — The Shadow on the Quag

**An arc guide and prologue for the GQ series · six adventures · levels 1–4 · Harvester 576 to Needfest 577 CY · 4–6 characters**

> *"His agents began to scour the Flanaess, seeking arcane evils and relics."*
> — on Iuz after his release, *Iuz the Evil* p. 3

This book holds the whole arc. Part One is the **arc guide**: what the DM needs to run all six adventures as one campaign. Part Two is the **prologue**, [[SP-GQ00 — Prologue — The Crossing|The Crossing]], a one-session adventure that brings the students home across Lake Quag in the company of the people who will hire, betray and haunt them for the next four months.

**Boxed text:** read it as written or paraphrase it, but keep every clue. **Conventions:** creature counts in parentheses are the scaling dial; statblocks are 2014 5e.

## Part One: The Arc Guide

### What the Arc Is
Six years after his release, Iuz is rebuilding. The Greyhawk Wars are still six years away. What reaches Ritterstadt in 576 isn't an army but people: agents and dupes, a merchant who isn't himself, and a student someone else marked for recruitment before the University ever saw her. The students meet the Old One's war through its quiet beginnings, and every adventure is a **job**: a crossing, a theft, a mole hunt, a contract, a smuggling run and a rescue on the ice.

**The theme is the mirror.** Greyhawk University takes gifted young people and teaches them to catalogue the dangerous things of the world. Kermin Mind-Bender takes gifted children and teaches them to use those things. Dawud Vesharan, the arc's villain, is what a student becomes in the other school, and Liesl Amsel is the student both schools want.

### What the Locals Know
- Iuz the Old One is free and rules from Dorakaa, far to the north and east. Ritterstadt folk know the name and little more. "The war in the north" is something that happens to other people.
- Perrender merchants trade with everyone, including counting-houses in Molag that work for the Horned Society. That's disreputable, not illegal.
- Two years ago a crate came up from the City of Greyhawk under seal and went into the Archive's sealed stacks. Commissioner Dray was on the quay for his audit when it landed. Nobody saw what was in it.
- 576 is the **Blackfrost Bloom** year (RG-01): black ice in Planting, undead closer to the wall than anyone remembers, and the eastern gate still under repair.
- Lake Quag has a grey fog at its center where ships go missing. The Rhennee won't sail through it, and they won't say why.

### What's Really Happening
- **The Gaoler's Link.** The crate held [[The Gaoler's Link]], one link of the fetter that held Iuz in Zagig's dungeons from 505 to 570, cut with the sigils of the Six who imprisoned him. After sixty-five years holding a demigod, it is a **blind spot**: no divination perceives anything within 30 feet of it. It **burns** any hand sworn to Iuz, and sworn creatures feel it nearby as **the Ache**. Chancellor Adenauer sent it north in 574 "where no one would think to look." Professor Vogelsang is its keeper of record and has studied it in secret ever since. The crate sits in the Archive's sealed stacks. **Dray saw it arrive** in Patchwall 574, during his first audit, and logged it as *"consignment of 574, Chancellor's seal, not inventoried"*; he has wanted it inventoried ever since. Vogelsang has told him only "a fetter fragment of the Zagyg period, for study." The Chancellor's **second opinion** on it came from [[Ouderling Machteld Vreeswijk]], the Old Kerk's Keeper of Oaths, whose lay-brother it burned in 574. The Regents' Commission knows a consignment came north in 574 and wants it back; the Chancellor agrees with the Commission that the Fiend's Embrace should come south, but not the Link (RG-06).
- **Kermin Mind-Bender** of the Greater Boneheart, a Bakluni diviner who specializes in mind control and recruits gifted children (WGR5 p. 86), wants the Link. It would let Iuz's enemies hide a war council or a fortress from the Old One, and whatever Zagyg's magic did inside that cell may be the secret of Iuz's godhood. Kermin wants it to study, and perhaps as leverage over his master. The arc never says which.
- **Dawud Vesharan**, Kermin's field agent, has hunted the Link for two years. Every scrying of the Field Station shows a hole, and the hole is his only clue. He can't carry the Link himself, so he needs untainted hands. In Flocktime 576 he planted a [[Bone Tether]] in the collar of the Schwartzenbruin merchant **Ottokar Brandmeier**, and he travels as "Herr Fesch," Brandmeier's clerk.
- **Liesl Amsel**, a girl from a Quag fishing village, has an untrained wild talent: she hears what the invisible say. Kermin's diviners marked her this summer. She is the second reason Vesharan is on the lake.
- **The recruiter.** The recruiter who took Baba Yeska's daughter north in 575 is Kermin's (ruled 2026-11-13; RG-06): **Edrik Saumann**. In 575 he was the yeoman clerk of Quagflood's harbour-reeve, cataloguing every hull in and out of the Landing from Yeska's own tally-house; that is where he found her daughter. By the 576 audit he is Commissioner Dray's junior secretary, appointed on a Regent's letter nobody has seen. In GQ2, Yeska can recognize him. Yeska writes to her daughter by the northbound crate and burns the replies. When the recruiter comes for Liesl in GQ2, Yeska has her own reason to act, and her crate is a ready road north for the Link or the girl.
- **Iuz's other operation.** Ewald Wennerberg and his handler at Kottsmort (RG-06) are after the Fiend's Embrace, not the Link. Neither network knows the other is in Ritterstadt; Vesharan would burn Ewald as a rival. In GQ2, Ewald is the mole hunt's red herring.
- **The rival arm.** Panshazek, Iuz's priest-spy inside the Horned Society (WGR5 p. 88), runs a network that competes with Kermin's. Master Kezrin of [[SP-GA01-The-False-Arrow|The False Arrow]] is Panshazek's man.

**Kermin Mind-Bender never appears.** His tools are domination (the tethers), divination (which the Link defeats) and young agents he trained himself.

### Factions in the Arc
- **Kermin's network** (Vesharan, Snick, tethered dupes, the recruiter): wants the Link and Liesl. Spies on the Field Station; is spied on by Panshazek.
- **Panshazek's arm** (Kezrin, the Molag priest-spies): wants Kermin to fail without Iuz noticing who arranged it. A rival the party can use (WGR5 p. 74, *sow dissension*).
- **The Field Station** (Vogelsang, Dolz, Grell, the Warden, the Registrar): wants the Link kept quiet. Split on where it belongs.
- **The Regents' Commission** (Dray): wants the Link in the City of Greyhawk, and 10% of every relic.
- **The lake.** Brandmeier and Ruder want honest trade and no fog-ships. The Quag Bargemen under Baba Yeska want the Landing, untolled night barges and their secrets kept; Yeska's northbound crate already runs to Kermin's recruiter (RG-06).
- **Iuz's Embrace network** (Ewald, the Kottsmort handler): wants the Fiend's Embrace. Unaware of Kermin's people, and they of it.

### Running the Arc
**Between jobs.** Each adventure takes a few days; the weeks between are downtime at the Field Station. Use the Job Board for side work, give each student a week of study (one Lore Ladder-style discovery about Iuz per downtime), and run one **between-job beat** from the table below before the next adventure.

| After | Between-job beat |
|---|---|
| GQ0 | Liesl starts her labor levy in the Archive and asks a PC to "come and listen with her" in the reading room. Nothing is there to see. |
| GQ1 | Brandmeier (if freed) writes from Schwartzenbruin with a crate of pike-smoke and a warning about a counting-house. |
| GQ2 | Dray's audit report names the Link as "a consignment better kept in the City." Vogelsang agrees in public. |
| GQ3 | [[Elif Kayim]] arrives with the first hard frosts, the Wolf Nomads' first trader at Ritterstadt (RG-07), and leaves a raven's skull on the Landing: *the Old One's eyes are on the Uplands roads*. |
| GQ4 | The lake freezes. Liesl's bunk in the Long Dormitory is empty and her boots are by the door. |

**Carrying outcomes forward.** Keep a one-page **Arc Ledger** for the table: the letter's fate (GQ0), the Link's location, Brandmeier's state, Snick's state, Vesharan's state, Liesl's trust, and Kermin's attention (none, noticed, marked). Each adventure's "Previous Play" section reads from it.

**Kermin's Attention.** A three-step track. It rises when the party foils Vesharan in a way he can't hide from his master (GQ1 full success, GQ3 success, GQ4 with the Link delivered). At *Noticed*, GQ4's hunters are doubled. At *Marked*, the hired fiend in GQ5 comes for the party first.

**Safety valve.** Lecturer Dolz is the arc's named help: if the party is stuck, she has a dream, a scrying that shows fog where it shouldn't, or a former student in Ket who answers letters.

### Arc Ending States
- **Kermin's ledger** (GQ5 decides):
  - *Marked:* the party foiled him, and his diviners watch them from now on.
  - *Forgotten:* Vesharan took the blame, and the party is beneath notice.
  - *Owed:* Vesharan defected. The party has an informant inside Kermin's network and targets on their backs.
- **The Link** ends hidden in the Vesve with Philidor, held by the University, or lost north. Each opens a different thread into the 580s.
- **Liesl** ends at the Field Station, in Dorakaa, or somewhere of her own choosing.

## Rulings (Greybeard, 2026-11-05 unless dated)
- Series code **GQ** (2026-11-13; was SP-SQ, and before that the provisional SP-PBS-05 to 09).
- **GQ0 is both the arc guide and a prologue** (2026-11-13): *The Crossing*, APL 2, one 3-hour session.
- **Kermin Mind-Bender** sits in the Greater Boneheart by 576 CY (house extrapolation from WGR5 p. 86; logged in [[Iuz]]).
- **Philidor the Blue Wizard** is the Link's keeper in GQ4 (WGR5 pp. 6, 21, 88).
- **Professor Wilhelmina Brandt** stays out of this arc. The Wachturm/Binding Network threads and the Registrar's sealed files are untouched.
- **Master Kezrin** (GA1) belongs to **Panshazek's** arm, the priest-spies who are Kermin's rivals. He is the second thief in GQ3 and a wildcard in GQ5.
- The **Warden's Brewfest Dinner** is in the Field Station's Academic Calendar (RG-08, both vaults).
- **Edrik Saumann** (2026-11-13): Kermin's recruiter; Quagflood harbour clerk at the Landing in 575 (where he recruited Yeska's daughter), Dray's junior secretary by 576. *Quagflood* is the English form of canon Quagfludt, used in all Sidebar Press text.
- **Dray saw the crate arrive** (Patchwall 574). The Chancellor's second opinion is [[Ouderling Machteld Vreeswijk]] of the Old Kerk (2026-11-13).
- **Mesh with the Ritterstadt Gazetteer (2026-11-13):** the City's post comes up the Velverdyva to the post-house in Schwartzenbruin; the alarm is the Wachturm bell; Hask is Schwartzenbruin's lake-gate sergeant and Garrok has Ritterstadt's gate; the Link's crate is in the sealed stacks; Yeska's daughter's recruiter is Kermin's; the Quag freezes right across by late Sunsebb because of the Bloom-year cold (RG-05).
- **576 CY is a Regents' audit year.** Commissioner Lucan Dray is in Ritterstadt for Patchwall, during GQ2.
- **Fee (2026-11-07):** Brandmeier pays **25 marks each** (was 5), half up front; 30 if haggled; his reward if freed is 25 marks each.
- **Coin:** the mark is the gold piece (kleinmark sp, pfennig cp, grossmark pp; [[Perrenland]], LGG p. 85).
- **The Ache's Shield** (2026-11-06): within 30 ft of the unboxed Link, a creature sworn to Iuz can't target its bearer with an attack or a spell (areas, other targets and range beyond 30 ft still work). Full text: [[The Gaoler's Link]]. **Carry it forward:** in GQ4, Kermin's hunters must strike the bearer from range or hit the others. In GQ5, the hired fiend and the [[Leash Fiend]] are sworn, so design their tactics around it: grapples on companions, area attacks, or making someone else carry the Link.

## Canon Check (arc-wide)
- [x] Iuz references verified against [[Iuz]] (lore note, 2026-11-05)

- [x] No 5.5e-only lore used

**Deviations (house, endorsed; reasoning in [[Iuz#Sidebar Press Stance]]):**
- **[[The Gaoler's Link]]:** its provenance, its blind spot and its burn are house material. Grounded in WGR5 p. 5 (the six imprisoners, the 570 cave-in) and p. 75 (the third ebon skull, an object Iuz can't detect).
- **Kermin in the Greater Boneheart by 576:** house extrapolation from WGR5 p. 86.
- **Tether-mages, bone tethers, whisper-leeches:** house material, built on WGR5 p. 86 and pp. 89–90.
- **Kezrin as Panshazek's man:** house ruling linking GA1 to WGR5 p. 88.
- **The six sigils:** based on each hero-god's later holy symbol; matching each sigil to one of the Link's powers is house material.
- **Conversions:** *blackhand*, *bonechain* and *clawcloud* (WGR5 pp. 9–13) and the losel (WGR5 p. 29) are converted to 2014 5e. They are TSR names; rename them for Kestrel.
- **Fog-drowned:** a house body for canon's Lake Quag fog-undead (WoG; LGG).
- **Liesl's wild talent and her village:** house material (GQ0).
