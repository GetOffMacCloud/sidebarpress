# Bonechain
*3rd-level necromancy*

**Casting Time:** 10 minutes
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a chain of finger-bones threaded on black wire, which the spell consumes)
**Duration:** Until triggered or 24 hours
**Classes:** Cleric (Iuz only), Wizard

You touch a patch of ground no larger than a 20-foot square. Beneath it, buried or hidden, are the bones of at least four Medium creatures. You set a trigger: a creature entering the area, a word spoken nearby, a door opening, or something similar. When the trigger occurs, four **skeletons** (MM p. 272) burst up in the area. They act immediately after the creature that triggered them, then on that creature's initiative count. They attack every creature in or near the area that you didn't name when you cast the spell. After 1 minute, or when reduced to 0 hit points, they collapse into inert bone.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, one additional skeleton rises for each slot level above 3rd, provided there are enough bones in the area.

## Notes
- **Conversion** of WGR5's 4th-level priest spell *bonechain* (pp. 9–13): hidden bones that spring up as skeletons, made for ambushes and corridor traps.
- **Encounter math:** four skeletons are 200 XP (×2 for the group, 400), an easy-to-medium ambush at APL 3–4. Give the party a sign to spot first (turned earth, a bone fragment, a DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check).
- **IP:** "bonechain" is a TSR name (WGR5). The Kestrel edition must rename it (suggestion: *grave-snare*).
- Used by the [[Grinning Initiate]] (1/day), and by Jebli orc shamans on the Vesve margin (SP-GQ04).
