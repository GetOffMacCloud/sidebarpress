# Blind Hour
*2nd-level abjuration (ritual)*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of ash from a burned letter)
**Duration:** 1 hour
**Classes:** Bard, Cleric, Wizard

Choose a point you can see within range. For the duration, creatures and objects within a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point can't be targeted by divination magic or perceived through magical scrying sensors. A creature that tries to scry into the area sees only grey fog. The fog tells the scryer that something is being hidden there, but nothing about what it is.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the radius becomes 40 feet and the duration becomes 8 hours.

## Notes
- Lecturer Perenna Dolz worked this out at Ritterstadt in 576 by studying the fog the [[The Gaoler's Link|Gaoler's Link]] made in her students' scrying. She calls the visible fog "the honest flaw," since it hides the secret but not the fact that there is one.
- It is a teachable University spell. A student of Divination & Omenry can learn it from Dolz after SP-GQ01 (cost: a term's tuition step, or 30 credits).
- In SP-GQ04 it turns a camp into a hole in Kermin's sight. Combined with the decoy [[Coffer of False Weight|coffers]], it makes the shell game work.
- *Homebrew.*
