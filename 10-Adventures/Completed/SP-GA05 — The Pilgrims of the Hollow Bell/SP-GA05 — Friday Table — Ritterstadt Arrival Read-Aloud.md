# Ritterstadt Arrival — Friday Night Field Trips Opening

The party's first session in Ritterstadt. Read before the hook; it ends on the Field Office notice at the Fiend's Rest job board. About 2½ minutes aloud.

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> *The road from Greyhawk ends where the maps start guessing.*
>
> Three days out from the city the cobbles gave way to cart ruts, the cart ruts to a sheep track, and somewhere along the way the University's banners stopped meaning anything to the people you passed. Now the Sepia Uplands roll out on every side, brown and grey and endless under a low, bruised sky. The wind comes off them smelling of wet stone and woodsmoke.
>
> And there, on a rise above the river, is **Ritterstadt**.
>
> It isn't much. A palisade of sharpened pine stakes, banded with iron. A wooden watchtower, the **Wachturm**, leaning a little into the wind like an old man listening for something. A mill wheel turning slow in the ford, thatched roofs huddled close together, and at the heart of it all the long, black-timbered bulk of the **Ritter's Hall**, where they say the hearth hasn't gone cold in living memory.
>
> This is the edge of the civilized world. It's also, as of this morning, your classroom.
>
> Your enrollment letters all said the same thing in the same careful clerk's hand: *Report to the Ritterstadt Field Station for practical instruction. Bring your own kit. The University assumes no liability for injury, loss, or death incurred during fieldwork.* At the time, that last line seemed like a formality.
>
> A guard in a dented kettle helm squints down at you from the gate, notes the University seal on your papers, and waves you through with the bored pity of someone who's watched students arrive before and isn't sure how many he's watched leave.
>
> Past the square, past the smithy's clang and the chapel bell of St. Erika's, the road leads you to a sagging, lamplit building with a carved sign swinging over the door: a horned devil, sound asleep in an armchair. **The Fiend's Rest.**
>
> Inside, it's warm and loud: trappers, pack-merchants, a pair of tired-looking soldiers, and a scattering of other students pretending they aren't nervous. The woman behind the bar, sharp-eyed and unbothered, slides a mug toward you before you've asked for one. By the door hangs a **corkboard** crowded with notices: lost goats, mining surveys, a bounty on wolves.
>
> And one sheet, pinned dead center over all the others, is stamped across the top in fresh red ink.
>
> Out here, the professors are a week's ride away. The Knights have their own walls to hold. If something goes wrong in the Uplands, there's no one to send for and no one coming to look for you.
>
> *Welcome to Ritterstadt. Class is in session.*
