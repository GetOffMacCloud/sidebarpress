# Friday Table 2 — Opening Read-Aloud

For the second party picking up *The Pilgrims of the Hollow Bell*. Runs from the Field Office at dawn on Day 2 to the road just past Kalbach. About 6–7 minutes read aloud, plus the character introductions.

---

## Out of character (stream recap, 1 minute)

Last Friday, a team of Greyhawk University students was sent into the Sepia Uplands to shadow a procession of grey-robed pilgrims. The orders were to watch and report, not to fight. They followed the pilgrims to a hamlet called Kalbach, where the dead didn't stay buried, and they fought them in the churchyard. Then they rode home with a handful of strange coins. Tonight, a new team picks up where they left off.

---

## 1. The Field Office (dawn, Patchwall 24)

> *It is the twenty-fourth of Patchwall, and the first frost has come to Ritterstadt.*
>
> *You were each pulled out of bed before dawn by a porter with a lantern and a single sentence: "Field Office. Now." The streets are still dark. Frost silvers the palisade, and along the top of it the crows sit in a long black row, watching the road east.*
>
> *The Field Office smells of ink, lamp oil, and wet wool. Maps of the Uplands cover one wall, pinned with coloured thread. Behind the desk sits Wendel Krafft, the duty clerk, and from the look of him he hasn't slept. His collar is undone. His tea has gone cold with a skin on it.*
>
> *On the desk in front of him is a field report, written fast in a hand that didn't care about spelling, its edges stained with mud. Next to it is a twist of grey cloth.*
>
> *Krafft unties the cloth without a word. Three coins roll out across the blotter. They are lead, dull and heavy, and each one is stamped with a skull and a scythe.*
>
> *"A team came in an hour before you," he says. "Rode all night. Eight days ago we sent them to shadow a procession: ten pilgrims in grey, walking the drove roads east of here behind a handcart. They call themselves mendicants of Wee Jas. They go from hamlet to hamlet offering to pray safe passage for the dead and to bless the wells. Nobody turns that down."*
>
> *He taps the report.*
>
> *"Everywhere they bless a well, people die. The old ones, the sick, the babies, the lambs. Three days later. And then, on the fifth night…" He stops and starts again. "At Kalbach, on the fifth night, the dead got up out of the churchyard and walked. Our students fought them in the lane, by the well, in the rain. They pulled these out of the mud afterwards."*
>
> *He pushes one coin toward you with the end of his pen. He doesn't touch it with his fingers.*
>
> *"Folk out there don't say the name that goes with that mark. They call him the Reaper. Him Below. You'll hear that."*

**DM:** let anyone who wants to examine the coins do so. A DC 10 Religion check identifies the skull-and-scythe as Nerull's symbol (Lore Ladder rung 2); *detect magic* shows necromancy. Hand out the **Reaper's Penny** image.

> *"Here is the part that keeps me awake," Krafft says.*
>
> *"Their orders were to report to our relay at Hollen Ford. Two riders and a signaller, a stone tower above the ford with a beacon on the roof. That relay is how news gets out of those hills. When the beacon burns, someone answers."*
>
> *"Our team never got there. They came straight home. And Hollen Ford…" He turns to the map and puts his finger on a pin past Kalbach, where the coloured thread stops. "Hollen Ford hasn't lit its beacon in three nights."*
>
> *"Half the garrison went north to the Vesve line a week ago. The Reeve has nobody. I have no riders left to send." He looks at each of you in turn. "So I'm sending you."*

> *He hands each of you a fresh copy of a notice. At the bottom, in red ink, the same stamp as before: DO NOT ENGAGE.*
>
> *"Horses are saddled in the yard. Ride to Hollen Ford. Find out why the relay is dark, and put this report in a rider's hand if there's still a rider to take it. Then go up and watch the hill."*
>
> *"Which hill?" someone asks.*
>
> *"Carn Aedh. There's an old shrine on top, with a bronze bell. The last thing our team saw before they turned back was the procession walking toward it." He folds the report and holds it out. "Just watch them. Someone else will deal with them."*
>
> *Nobody in the room believes that, Krafft included.*

**DM:** hand out **Handout 4 (Field Office Notice)** and **Handout 1 (Sketch Map)**. Deliver any personal hooks here.

### ⏸ Pause: introduce your characters

Go round the table: name, what you look like, and why the Field Office called *you* at four in the morning.

## 2. The Ride East

> *You ride out through the east gate as the sky goes from black to iron grey. The guards don't stop you; they barely look up. Behind you, the crows lift off the palisade all at once and fly east ahead of you, over the road, as if they know where you're going.*
>
> *The road climbs into the Sepia Uplands: long brown hills, stone walls, sheep pasture going on forever. The wind is out of the north and it cuts straight through wool. By mid-morning the low cloud starts to tear apart, and short showers of sleet come hissing across the grass and pass on.*
>
> *There's nobody on the road. No carts, no drovers, no one walking to market. A farmhouse you pass has its shutters barred in daylight.*
>
> *Then you pass a stone trough beside the road, fed by a little spring. All around it, in a perfect circle about ten paces across, the grass is dead. Not brown with winter, but grey and flattened, as if something has been lying on it. Inside the ring, the water in the trough is perfectly still. Your horses won't go near it.*

**DM:** a DC 11 Nature or Survival check reads the ring: the water here has been poisoned, and recently (F2 Route 3). If anyone looks in the trough, there's a lead penny in the silt.

---

## 3. Through Kalbach (late morning)

> *Kalbach comes into view just before noon: a dozen turf-roofed houses around a stone well, a little church, and a churchyard with a lychgate.*
>
> *You can tell where the fighting was from the road. The ground in the churchyard is torn open. A grave mound, far too wide for one body, has been broken from underneath, and the earth around it is churned and trampled. The lychgate hangs off one hinge. Somebody has scratched something into its post and then tried to scrape it off again.*
>
> *Around the well, the grass is dead in the same perfect ring.*
>
> *On two of the doors facing the well, someone has drawn a circle in ash. Somebody else has tried to scrub it off. You can still see it.*
>
> *Whatever the first team did here, Kalbach is still standing. But nobody comes out to greet you. You see faces at the gaps in the shutters, and they watch you all the way through the village. In one doorway, a small girl stands with a wooden spoon held in both fists like a weapon. She doesn't wave.*
>
> *As you pass the last house, an old man calls after you from behind his door, without opening it:*
>
> *"They went that way. The grey people. Up toward the Ford. Don't drink the water."*

**DM:** if the players want to stop in Kalbach, let them, but keep it short; the clock is running (the ritual starts at dusk). Sexton Pfaff can confirm F2 Route 2 ("only the ones who drank from the square well").

---

## 4. Past Kalbach (the adventure begins)

> *Beyond Kalbach the road narrows into a drove track and climbs north-east into higher, emptier country. Stone walls give way to open hill. The wind gets colder.*
>
> *And there, on the skyline ahead of you, still miles off, is a single hill standing apart from the rest: steep-sided, crowned with a ring of tumbled stone, and an old dead oak leaning out of it like a gallows. Carn Aedh.*
>
> *Between you and that hill, somewhere down in the next fold of land, is the ford, and the relay tower, and the beacon that hasn't burned in three nights.*
>
> *The crows are already up there, circling.*
>
> *Nobody has heard from Hollen Ford. Nobody is coming behind you. It's past noon, the light is going grey again, and the frost hasn't lifted all day.*
>
> *What do you do?*

**DM:** if you're using Brenna, she's at the next stream crossing with her flock, heading the other way, with a fire going. Otherwise, go straight to **Scene 4: Hollen Ford** (use **Map — Hollen Ford Relay Day**).
