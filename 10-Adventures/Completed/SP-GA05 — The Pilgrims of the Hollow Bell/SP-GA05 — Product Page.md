## Full description

*"Toll for the reaped, toll for the sown; ring the bell hollow and the dead walk home."*

It is Ready'reat in the Sepia Uplands, and the frost is hard. A procession of grey-robed pilgrims is crossing the hills toward a ruined Flan sun-shrine, and every hamlet they bless buries its weakest a few days later.

You are new Field Wardens of Greyhawk University. Your orders are stamped in red: **DO NOT ENGAGE.** Shadow the pilgrims. Report to the courier relay at Hollen Ford. Somebody else will deal with them.

Then you reach the relay, and everyone there is dead.

**The Pilgrims of the Hollow Bell** is a one-session adventure of pursuit, dread, and hard choices, built around a single idea: *nobody's coming to save you.*

### What you get
- **A complete 4-hour adventure** for four to six 3rd-level characters, with scaling for every party size and cut lines if you run long.
- **Four encounters on a rising curve** (Easy, Medium, Hard, Deadly), none of which must be won by fighting.
- **A thirteen-toll climax** with seven ways to stop the bell, and five written endings, including the one where you lose.
- **A villain with a plan and a calendar.** The Grey Sickle's scheme runs whether or not the characters interfere.
- **Seven stat blocks** (the Penny-Risen, Ghoul Rider and Barrow Dead with full bestiary entries), **three new spells** and **five new magic items**.
- **Six handouts**, including the relay log, a cultist's notes, and a set of **God Cards** your players earn by learning who Nerull, Pelor, Obad-Hai, and Beory are.
- **The Field Warden's Primer**, a two-page player handout that sets the scene and spoils nothing.
- **Six side quests** that grow out of what your table did.
- **Six maps** (the route, Kalbach, the Hollen Ford relay and its tower, the orc camp, and the Bell-Tor) and more than thirty illustrations: black-and-white ink in the PDF, full colour in the Fantasy Grounds module.

### Built for the table
Every essential fact has three routes and a last resort. Every solution to the climax is planted in an earlier scene. Read-aloud text is short. Monster counts are your scaling dial.

### Set in Greyhawk
Old gods, Flan barrows, the Old Faith's winter myth, and the shadow of Iuz's war in the Vesve. Drops into any Greyhawk campaign near Perrenland or the Vesve, or serves as an episode in Sidebar Press's Greyhawk University series.

### Content notes
Undead, the deaths of villagers including children (off-screen), poisoned wells, a hanged animal, religious horror.

### Product details
- PDF, 45 pages, black-and-white interior for easy printing, plus the two-page Field Warden's Primer for players.
- Fantasy Grounds Unity module: the full adventure as a reference manual, all art in colour, battle maps shown at dusk and night as they are played (daylight copies included), every encounter scaled for 4, 5, or 6 players with its tokens already on the map, round transparent tokens, linked NPCs, spells, items, quests, and handouts.
