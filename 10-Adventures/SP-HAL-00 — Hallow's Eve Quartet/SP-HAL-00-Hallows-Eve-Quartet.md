# Hallow's Eve Quartet

**Four Halloween one-shots for 2014 5e · levels 1–10 · about 4 hours each**

> Four setting-agnostic Halloween one-shots, each a small village with a friendly inn, rival faiths, enemies hidden among the townsfolk, and a ruin down the road, and each a different flavor of horror. Every one runs on its own. A recovered clue at the end of each points to the next, so a DM can chain them into a campaign from 1st to 10th level.

## Series Code
`SP-HAL-NN` — the Halloween line, kept apart from the Greyhawk `SP-PBS` sequence. `00` is this overview; `01`–`04` are the adventures. Codes are never reused.

## Running Standalone
Each adventure opens with its own hooks. The "hook out" at the end is optional: ignore it, or turn it into a rumor for your campaign.

## Running the Chain
The chain runs **2 → 4 → 6 → 9**. Each adventure's rewards advance the party one level; award a second level of downtime advancement between Adventures 2 and 3, and between 3 and 4. Use at least one "Hooks for Your Party" subplot per adventure so the stakes belong to a player, not an NPC.

## Bundle Threads
Each adventure ends with an object that names the next village. Carried forward, they pay off in the finale.

| Thread | Found in | Points to | Finale payoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Gentleman's calling card | 01 | (bonus) | Call in the favor at midnight: the Gentleman makes a corn-dolly double of the guest, and the field accepts it. If a PC ate the apple in 01, he already owns their reflection. |
| Chaff's ledger and Ysolde's ring | 01 | Marrowmere | The ring is the key to the bride's reckoning in 02. |
| The Marrowmere chapel bell | 02 | Vesper Hollow | Ringing it in the Tithe Barn frightens the Harvest Lord's trees. |
| The ossuary calendar | 03 | Thornbury | Gives the pact's exact wording, so the party arrives knowing its loopholes. |
| The bound wraith | 03 | Thornbury | Sealed in the Vesper Bell, Thorne can be offered as a substitute guest. Escaped, he joins the climax as an enemy. |

## Design Notes
- **One broken expectation per adventure:** the hag loses on contract law; the bride is the killer; the jailer is the drunk and the loop is protective; the "witch" is the ally and the cult is sympathetic.
- **Four different clocks:** an hourly schedule, a tide, a loop, a procession.
- **Every climax can be won by someone who never draws a sword.**
- **Monsters** are 2014 *Monster Manual* unless a reskin is noted; each bestiary names the base block and the changes.
