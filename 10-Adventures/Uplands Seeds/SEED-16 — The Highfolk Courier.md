# SEED-16 — The Highfolk Courier

**APL 8 (levels 7–9) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> *"What the Old One cannot hold, he follows."* — Highfolk border saying

**Pitch.** A dying elf courier staggers through Ritterstadt's gate with a satchel that must reach Highfolk, and the students carry it south. The satchel is a beacon: Iuz's hunters can follow it wherever it goes, and every hour of rest lets them close the gap.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- Six years after the Old One's return, Iuz's raiders probe the Vesve, and Highfolk's elves fight a quiet war in the trees.
- Elven couriers sometimes cut through the Sepia Uplands to avoid patrols on the forest roads.
- Grey-cloaked riders were seen two days ago near Kottsmort. Nobody knows whose they were.

### What's Really Happening
- **Lethwen** of Highfolk took a battle plan from an Iuz war-camp in the northern Vesve. It covers a spring push on Highfolk's northern thorps.
- The camp's priest wove a *tracking hex* into the satchel's clasp. While the satchel stays closed, the hunters always know its direction.
- The hunters are a **cult fanatic** pair under **Grask**, an **orc war chief**, with **worgs** and a bound **vrock**. They are a day behind and closing.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. Lethwen dies in Ritterstadt that night. The satchel stays in the Reeve's strongroom.
2. The next night the hunters reach Ritterstadt. The vrock tears the strongroom roof open, and the satchel goes north.
3. In spring, Highfolk's northern thorps burn without warning.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The courier collapses at the gate, and the Reeve asks the students to carry her satchel to Highfolk (Part 1). On the road they learn the satchel is hexed and that the hunters can follow it anywhere (Part 2). A three-leg race south runs on a Pursuit Clock: rest, ford, bridge (Part 3). At the Highfolk border they choose to run, break the hex, or turn the hunters' trick against them (Part 4).

## Facts the Players Must Learn
| # | Fact | Route 1 | Route 2 | Route 3 | Last resort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | The satchel is tracked. | *Detect magic*: a divination aura on the clasp. | Lethwen, lucid at dawn: "They knew every turn I took." | Arcana DC 15 on the clasp's scratched sigils (Iuz's grinning skull). | The worgs howl from the exact direction the satchel was carried, every night. |
| F2 | Opening the clasp breaks the hex, but ruins part of the plan. | Arcana DC 17 or *identify*. | A Highfolk warden's coded reply via messenger bird (leg 2). | Lethwen warns them: "Don't break the clasp. Half of it is in the binding." | When the satchel is forced, the outer pages blacken. |
| F3 | The hex can be turned into a lure. | A warlock, cleric, or wizard PC reads the weave (Arcana DC 18). | The Highfolk warden proposes it at the border. | Grask's captured cultist explains how the hex points. | The warden's archers are already waiting. |

## Hooks
1. **Job Board / the Reeve's request (default).** *"Courier service, urgent, Highfolk. University fellows of proven field record. 50 credits each, paid on delivery by Highfolk's own hand."*
2. **Personal.** An elf or half-elf PC recognizes Lethwen's house badge. She is kin, or owes the PC's family a debt.
3. **Thread.** If hook 17 or 10 was run, the deserters or Grumbold name Grask's band as Iuz's "fetchers."
- **Decline path:** Lethwen dies, and the strongroom is torn open (above). Ritterstadt is blamed in Highfolk, the Reeve loses face, and in spring a refugee train of elves arrives at the gate.

## Running Notes
- **Pursuit Clock (0–8):**
  - Each long rest adds 3, each short rest adds 1, and a hard march subtracts 1 (Constitution DC 13 or a level of exhaustion).
  - Leaving false trails subtracts 1, at most once per leg.
  - At 4, the hunters catch them on the current leg.
  - At 8, they ambush with surprise.
- **Reaction tools:**
  - Grask splits the pack when the party splits.
  - Wounded worgs flee to Grask and bring him faster (+1 clock).
  - If two cultists fall, the vrock is unleashed early.
- **Nudges:**
  1. Lethwen mutters about the clasp.
  2. A Highfolk messenger bird finds them.
  3. The worgs howl from the precise bearing of the satchel.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** a ring of elven border stones above a ravine. Highfolk archers are an hour away, or already in place if F3 was learned.
- **Choices:**
  - Deliver it intact, tracker and all. Highfolk gets the full plan but has to fight the hunters at its border.
  - Break the hex. The hunters lose them, but half the plan is gone.
  - Turn the hex into a lure. An elven ambush destroys Grask's band, but the party has to hold the stones until the archers loose.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — Plan delivered, hunters broken:** Highfolk's thanks, and a standing welcome for the students in the Vesve.
- **B — Plan delivered damaged, or hunters escaped:** the spring raid is blunted but not stopped.
- **C — Satchel lost:** Grask carries it north, and the spring raid lands.
- **Credits:** 50 base. Extra credit: +5 for Lethwen's survival, +5 for a written field report on Iuz's tracking magic.

## Key NPCs
- **Lethwen**, Highfolk courier. *Deity:* [Sehanine Moonbow].
  - *Wants:* the plan delivered, and to die at home.
  - *Knows:* the camp, the plan's outline, and that the hunters "never lost her."
  - *Reacts:* proud and refuses help → accepts if the PCs carry the satchel, not her → gives her ring as a token to the warden.
  - *Stats:* scout (wounded).
- **Grask One-Ear**, orc war chief of Iuz's fetchers. *Deity:* [Iuz].
  - *Wants:* the satchel, and a promotion.
  - *Knows:* how the hex points.
  - *Reacts:* hound → ambush → bargain (he offers the PCs safe passage for the satchel) → a berserk stand if cornered.
  - *Stats:* orc war chief.
- **Warden Caelith Ashbough**, Highfolk border warden. *Deity:* [Ehlonna].
  - *Wants:* the plan, and Grask's head.
  - *Knows:* how to turn the hex.
  - *Reacts:* suspicious of humans → trusts them on seeing Lethwen's ring.
  - *Stats:* veteran with longbow, or scout.
- **The Reeve** of Ritterstadt (existing). Sergeant Ruwen Hask can supply horses.
