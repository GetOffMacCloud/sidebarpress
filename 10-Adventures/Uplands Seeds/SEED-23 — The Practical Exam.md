# SEED-23 — The Practical Exam

**APL 3 (levels 2–4) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> *"Candidates will be assessed on initiative, caution, and the recovery of the brass idol. Candidates will not be assessed on screaming."*
> — Field Methods examination rubric

**Pitch.** The final exam for Field Methods is a staged dungeon in an abandoned mine, complete with padded traps and upper-years in kobold suits. Halfway through, the students find a real corpse: something has moved into the lower galleries and taken the "monsters" hostage.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- The Wendelschacht, a played-out copper and silver drift, has been the University's exam dungeon for three terms.
- Upper-years earn credit playing "kobolds" in felt masks with blunted spears.
- The miners who died in the 540s cave-in were never recovered. Ritterstadt folk call the lower galleries "the Quiet."

### What's Really Happening
- A **nothic** crept up from a natural fissure into the Quiet about ten days ago. It woke the cave-in dead as **ghouls** (2–4).
- Last night the nothic took the four costumed upper-years who went down early to set traps. It keeps them in the old pump chamber and feeds on their secrets.
- The examiner has noticed the missing staff and assumes it's a prank meant to test the candidates.
- The nothic knows each examinee's secrets the moment it sees them. It whispers them through the galleries to sow distrust.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. **Exam hour 1:** the candidates work through the staged upper level.
2. **Hour 2:** a ghoul drags a hostage's lantern into the upper level. The examiner calls it "a nice touch."
3. **Hour 3:** the nothic feeds on one hostage, leaving Marta catatonic, and moves its lair deeper.
4. **Next day:** the ghouls start coming up the shaft at night, and the first Ritterstadt goat goes missing.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The students start the exam and laugh through the fake traps (1). They find real blood, a real body, and a felt mask torn in half (2). They must decide whether to keep playing for marks or go down into the Quiet (3). In the pump chamber they face a nothic that bargains in secrets (4). Their choices decide whether anyone passes, and whether anyone comes out (5).

## Facts the Players Must Learn
| # | Fact | Route 1 | Route 2 | Route 3 | Last resort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Something real is in the mine. | The miner's body (fresh claw wounds, Medicine DC 10) | The torn kobold mask with real blood | Ghoul tracks on the lower ladder (Survival DC 12) | A scream echoes up the shaft |
| F2 | The upper-years are hostages, not pranksters. | Their signed exam roster shows four checked in at dawn | A dropped spear is chalked "MARTA — HELP" | A nothic whisper repeats Marta's secret | The examiner admits he hasn't seen them |
| F3 | The nothic can be bargained with. | It speaks first, offering a trade | Marta's notes ("it only wants to *know*") | Arcana DC 13: nothics crave secrets | It stops attacking when someone confesses something |

## Hooks
1. **Job Board / curriculum (default).** *"Field Methods Practical, 3rd bell, the Wendelschacht. Bring rope, light, and a pencil."* A pass is worth 20 credits each.
2. **Personal.** A PC's friend or rival is one of the costumed upper-years.
3. **Thread (hook 21).** Examiner Grenwald is grading too, and his report rides on how the exam goes.
- **Decline path:** another team sits the exam. Two of them come back. The Dean asks the students to go down after the rest.

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** each 30 minutes in the Quiet, roll a d4. On a 1 the nothic feeds on a hostage, who is catatonic until a *lesser restoration* or a long rest.
- **Reaction tools:**
  - The nothic withdraws deeper if it's reduced below half or out-numbered.
  - Ghouls answer the sound of combat within 2 rounds.
  - The nothic's whispers can turn candidates against each other: each secret it reveals forces an Insight DC 12 check, or the target distrusts the speaker.
- **Examiner escalation:** dismisses ("all part of it") → scolds ("you're losing marks") → comes down himself if shown F2 → stays to help if he sees a ghoul.
- **Nudges:** (1) the idol is missing and its pedestal is bloodied; (2) a kobold suit staggers into view, empty; (3) the examiner's lamp goes out above.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** the flooded pump chamber has rotted beam walkways over black water and hostages tied to the pump wheel. Collapsing the gallery is possible (Athletics DC 14 on the prop beam).
- **Choices:**
  - **Fight** the nothic.
  - **Trade:** the nothic wants one true secret per hostage. Each secret should matter to the player whose character gives it.
  - **Collapse the gallery:** everyone gets out and the Quiet is sealed. The exam fails unless the examiner is persuaded (Persuasion DC 13 with evidence).

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — full success:** all hostages out, the nothic dead or bargained off, the examiner convinced. Pass plus 10 extra credit each for "exceptional field judgment."
- **B — partial:** the hostages are out but the exam is failed, or the nothic is loose in the deep galleries (seeds a later delve).
- **C — retreat:** the students flee and the University sends a faculty team. The hostages are recovered, and one is changed.
- **Credits:** 20 base for a pass, +10 extra credit for A. None for a failed exam unless the Dean overrules (Scene 6 Persuasion).

## Key NPCs
**The examining professor** *(GU roster)*. *Wants:* a rigorous exam and no embarrassment. *Knows:* the staged layout, and that the staff are "late." *Reacts:* the escalation above.

**"Kobold" Marta Velz**, upper-year hostage. [Fharlanghn]. *Wants:* out, and to never wear felt again. *Knows:* where the nothic sleeps, and that it stops hunting while it "thinks." *Reacts:* panics if untied in the dark, steady once she has a light.

**Arnulf, Greta and Dov**, the other costumed upper-years. One is catatonic if the clock has struck.
