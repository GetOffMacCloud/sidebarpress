# SEED-19 — Lights on the Quag

**APL 7 (levels 6–8) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

**Pitch.** Lights rise from Lake Quag at night, and Ritterstadt's fishers won't go out. The dead of a drowned Flan village hold a procession every year, and this year they're coming ashore, because a Ritterstadt fisherman sold their bell.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- Every year at the dark of Luna, lights walk on the water off the Murrow Shoal. Sensible folk stay in.
- This year the lights have come closer every night, and a net-mender saw figures standing waist-deep off the quay.
- Tobbe Raske suddenly paid off his debts, then started drinking.

### What's Really Happening
- **Murrowick**, a Flan fishing village, drowned centuries ago when the shore slumped. Its dead keep a procession around their bronze **drowned bell**, which hangs in the sunken shrine.
- Raske dragged the bell up in his net and sold it to **Ottwin Kranzler**, a dealer who supplies Flan Antiquities.
- Without the bell, the procession has no end. Each night it walks a little further, and on the third night it reaches the town.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. Night 1: the lights are a hundred yards off the quay. A boat is overturned.
2. Night 2: drowned dead walk the shingle, and Raske's shed is found waterlogged.
3. Night 3: the procession comes up the quay at midnight looking for the bell. Anyone in its path is drowned on dry land.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The fishers' guild hires the students to find out what's on the water (Part 1). A night on the lake shows them the procession and the empty shrine (Part 2). Tracing the bell leads to Raske, then to the dealer who won't give it up (Part 3). At midnight the procession reaches the quay, and the students must return the bell, fight, or destroy it (Part 4).

## Hooks
1. **Job Board (default).** *"The Fishers' Guild of Ritterstadt seeks capable hands to clear the lake of its present trouble. 35 credits each."*
2. **Personal.** A PC's boarding-house keeper is a fisher, and has stopped going out.
3. **Thread.** If hook 4 or 5 was run, Antiquities' habit of buying Flan grave-goods is already known.
- **Decline path:** the procession walks the quay on night 3. Two people drown on the stones, and the guild never forgives the University.

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** three nights (above). The students start on night 1.
- **Reaction tools:**
  - The procession advances further each night.
  - Destroyed drowned dead re-form the next night unless the bell is returned.
  - The wisps lure lone rowers toward the central fogs (canon grey fogs).
- **Kranzler's escalation:** polite refusal → higher price → hires thugs → hides the bell in a cart for Schwartzenbruin.
- **Nudges:**
  1. Raske avoids the students' eyes.
  2. A child finds a green bronze flake on the shingle.
  3. The procession tolls a phantom bell.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** Ritterstadt's quay in fog, lanterns guttering, the procession wading out of the lake with **Nairn** at its head.
- **Choices:**
  - Return the bell and row it back to the shoal to ring it. Peace, and Nairn's blessing.
  - Fight the procession on the quay. Hard, and it returns next year.
  - Destroy the bell. The dead are scattered, and a curse falls on Raske's line: no net of his will hold.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — Bell returned and rung:** the fishers return to the water, and Nairn grants each PC one *water breathing* blessing (1/day for a year).
- **B — Procession beaten, bell kept:** the town is safe this year, and the lights return next Luna-dark.
- **C — Bell destroyed or town flooded:** casualties, a curse, and lasting hostility from the guild.
- **Credits:** 35 base. Extra credit: +5 for Old Flan transcriptions from the shrine, +5 for a study of the procession.

## Key NPCs
- **Tobbe Raske**, fisherman. *Deity:* [Xerbo].
  - *Wants:* to undo it without losing the money.
  - *Knows:* where he dragged the bell, and who bought it.
  - *Reacts:* denies → drinks → confesses under fear or kindness.
  - *Stats:* commoner.
- **Nairn**, bell-keeper of Murrowick (ghost). *Deity:* [Beory].
  - *Wants:* the bell home.
  - *Knows:* the drowning, and the procession's rite.
  - *Reacts:* asks → warns → possesses a PC to walk them to the bell.
  - *Stats:* ghost.
- **Ottwin Kranzler**, Antiquities dealer. *Deity:* [Zilchus].
  - *Wants:* his profit.
  - *Knows:* the bell's buyer in the City.
  - *Reacts:* see Running Notes.
  - *Stats:* noble, plus thugs (2–4).
- **Sergeant Ruwen Hask** (existing): quay watch.

## Canon Notes
- Canon: on Lake Quag, undead are seen in the central region, ghost ships stay near where their crews died, grey fogs hang where ships vanish, and nixies live in the western depths (LGG; FtA). Also canon: Luna, Xerbo, and Beory.
- House material: Murrowick, the Murrow Shoal, the bell, and the NPCs. Optional: nixies as witnesses, who saw Raske's net.
