# SEED-06 — Blank Hex Bounty

**APL 2 (levels 1–3) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> *"A map is a promise that the land will keep still."* — motto over the Registrar's map-table

**Pitch.** The University pays credit for every verified hex of the blank Uplands, and a rival team is filing maps far too fast to be honest. The students go to prove forgery and find that one of the invented valleys is real, and someone is waiting in it.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- The Registrar pays 5 credits per hex verified by a second team.
- **Ansel Quill's** team, the Quillers, filed eleven hexes in nine days. Nobody walks that fast in the Uplands.
- The Quillers have been drinking with a Yati peddler-woman who sells "hill knowledge" for silver.
- Shepherds say not to follow a woman who asks your name twice.

### What's Really Happening
- The peddler is **Mother Fennick**, a **green hag** who wears a Yati grandmother's face.
- She sold the Quillers convincing fakes, plus one true description: **Hollowmere**, a valley hidden under her illusion, where she lives.
- The map is bait. Any student who walks to Hollowmere arrives tired, alone, and owing her a favor.
- Two Quillers, including Ansel's cousin **Tobiah Quill**, went to check the valley three days ago. Tobiah is in her cottage as a "guest."

### If Nobody Interferes
1. Day 1: the Registrar pays the Quillers. Their maps go into the University survey.
2. Day 3: another student team follows the Hollowmere map. One student disappears.
3. Moonrise of day 5: Fennick trades Tobiah back for a promise from Ansel, a true map of Ritterstadt's wells and wards. She uses it to stalk the town's children.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The students are hired to verify the Quillers' maps. They shadow the rivals or walk the hexes, and find three false valleys and one real one. Hollowmere is green, misty, and silent, and a Quiller is missing in it. The students reach Fennick's cottage before the moonrise bargain. There they fight, outwit, or bargain with her, and decide what to do about the forged map.

## Facts the Players Must Learn
| # | Fact | Route 1 | Route 2 | Route 3 | Last resort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | The maps are forged. | Walk any filed hex; the stream on the map isn't there (Survival DC 10). | Ansel's ledger shows nine days of walking for eleven hexes. | A Quiller confesses over drink (Persuasion DC 12). | The Registrar's clerk notices identical ink on all eleven sheets. |
| F2 | One hex, Hollowmere, is real but hidden. | The map's landmarks match a valley that appears only at dusk. | Tobiah's dropped survey stake, freshly cut. | A shepherd's child: "the grandmother's valley." | A Quiller arrives in panic: Tobiah hasn't come back. |
| F3 | The peddler is a hag who wants a map of Ritterstadt. | *Detect magic* or Arcana DC 13 on her "charms." | Ansel admits she asked for "wells and walls." | Tobiah, once found, repeats her price. | She names her price herself. |

## Hooks
1. **Job Board (default).** *"Second-survey teams wanted for hexes 14–24, Quill filing. Verify or refute."* 5 credits per hex verified, 20 credits each for proving forgery.
2. **Personal.** A Yati-descended PC's grandmother warns them about "the woman who asks your name twice."
3. **Thread.** If SEED-13 has been run, the archmage's cat knew Fennick, and the students may carry a token she respects.
- **Decline path:** the Registrar pays the forgers. Two weeks later a first-year student goes missing in a valley that is on the official survey.

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** moonrise on day 5. After that, Tobiah is sealed into the bargain.
- **Reaction tools:** Fennick's blights scout the valley's edge and report to her. If they are destroyed, she prepares an illusion of an open path to a pit. If she is wounded and outnumbered, she flees into the mist and comes back during the bargain at night. The Quillers lie, then panic, then help.
- **Nudges:** (1) A survey stake turns up pointing *into* the fog. (2) The shepherd's child sings the "name twice" rhyme. (3) Ansel comes to the students for help.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** a cottage of woven willow on an islet in a marsh pool. The fog is lightly obscuring and the water is difficult terrain. Tobiah sleeps in a hanging cage.
- **Choices:**
  - Fight the hag (dangerous at APL 2; she uses illusions and flees at half hit points).
  - Out-bargain her with a gift she values: a true secret, a song never sung, or a map of somewhere she can't hurt.
  - Free Tobiah and burn the forged map. The valley "un-maps" and the cottage fades for a year.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — full success:** forgery proved, Tobiah rescued, and the map burned or the bargain beaten. The Registrar bars the Quillers for a term.
- **B — partial:** Tobiah is rescued, but Fennick escapes and keeps a grudge and a student's name.
- **C — retreat or defeat:** the bargain is struck. Fennick gains a foothold, and a recurring threat is planted.
- **Credits:** 20 base each, +5 for proving forgery, +5 for Tobiah's safe return, +5 for an accurate map of Hollowmere filed without its secret.

## Key NPCs
**Ansel Quill**, rival team leader. *Deity:* [Zilchus]. *Wants:* top marks and a fellowship. *Knows:* the source of the maps and the hag's price. *Reacts:* bluster → bribe → confess when Tobiah's danger is clear.

**Tobiah Quill**, Ansel's cousin, captive. *Wants:* home. *Knows:* the cottage layout and Fennick's question. *Reacts:* grateful, then talks too much.

**Mother Fennick**, green hag. *Wants:* a map of Ritterstadt's wells and wards. *Knows:* every footpath in the valley and the true names of two shepherds. *Reacts:* charm → trick → bargain → flee.
