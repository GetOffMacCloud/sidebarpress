# SEED-22 — The Runaway Grimoire

**APL 2 (levels 1–3) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> *"Return all volumes to their shelves. All volumes."* — sign in the field library, recently underlined

**Pitch.** A spellbook in the field library keeps escaping its shelf. The librarian wants it caught. It is trying to get home to a hill ruin where its companion volume lies, and whoever follows it may earn new spells.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- The *Lesser Codex of Ammon Vell* arrived in a crate of donated books last month.
- It escapes every night: through windows, across rooftops, and once into the lake.
- The librarian *(GU roster)* has had enough and offers credits for its capture.

### What's Really Happening
- **Ammon Vell** was a hedge-wizard who bound his two spellbooks to each other before he died in his tower, 80 years ago.
- The *Lesser Codex* wants to go back to the *Greater Codex*, which lies in the ruin's study.
- The ruin is guarded by **Edda**, Vell's apprentice. She died there and is now a **specter**, bound to protect the books. She also wants release.
- The *Greater Codex* is served by a **homunculus** that wants a new master.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. Night 3: the book escapes for good and flutters south.
2. Day 5: a curious first-year follows it to the ruin and is hurt or killed by Edda.
3. The two books reunite and wait for the next unwary reader.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The students try to catch the escaping book across Ritterstadt's rooftops, market, and lake shore. It always heads south. They follow it to the hill ruin of Vell's Tower, through animated guardians, to a study where the *Greater Codex* waits, guarded by Edda's specter and a hopeful homunculus. They reunite the books, take one, or burn both, and lay Edda to rest or not.

## Hooks
1. **Job Board (default).** *"Library volume at large. Return it intact."* 10 credits each.
2. **Personal.** A wizard PC wakes up to find the book reading *them*: it is open on their bunk.
3. **Thread.** If SEED-13 has been run, the archmage named Vell as an old rival.
- **Decline path:** a first-year follows the book. The students hear about it at their funeral, or their convalescence.

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** the book's binding pulls harder each night. On night 3 it can't be held.
- **Reaction tools:** the ruin's guardians wake one floor at a time. Edda's escalation runs whisper → chill → attack → retreat into the walls at half hp. The homunculus bargains and betrays depending on who seems strongest.
- **Nudges:** (1) The book taps the window. (2) The bookplate. (3) The homunculus sends a dream: "Bring it home."

## Climax
- **Set piece:** a collapsed tower study: a broken roof open to the sky, bookshelves as cover, and Edda in the walls.
- **Choices:**
  - Reunite the books: Edda is laid to rest, each wizard PC may copy two spells, and the library gets the pair.
  - Take only the *Greater Codex*: Edda fights on and the *Lesser* keeps escaping.
  - Burn both: Edda is released bitterly, and the spells are lost.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — full success:** books reunited, Edda at rest, and the homunculus a library familiar.
- **B — partial:** books recovered, but Edda is unresolved and the tower stays haunted.
- **C — retreat or defeat:** the students flee. The books stay in the ruin, waiting.
- **Credits:** 10 base each, +5 for both books delivered, +5 for Edda laid to rest, +5 for a catalog note on Vell's binding.

## Key NPCs
**Edda**, specter (Vell's apprentice). *Wants:* the books kept together, and rest. *Knows:* Vell's wards, and which spells the books hold. *Reacts:* whisper → chill → attack → at peace if the books touch.

**Wick**, homunculus of the *Greater Codex*. *Wants:* a new master. *Knows:* the tower's safe paths. *Reacts:* flatter → bargain → betray if losing.

**The field librarian** *(GU roster)*.
