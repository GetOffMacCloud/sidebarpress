# SEED-20 — The Brass Pilgrim

**APL 14 (levels 13–15) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

*Tier 3 framing: the party are graduate fellows, or the original students at the end of their careers. This is the Dawn Hold thread's finale.*

**Pitch.** A dwarven automaton walks into Ritterstadt asking the way to Bitter Pass. It has walked for 900 years to warn Clan Irongrip that the seal on the Dawn Hold is failing, and Iuz wants the memory-core that holds the warning.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- A brass figure seven feet tall walked out of Lake Quag's shallows at dawn and asked, in archaic Dwarvish, for "the Iron Grip."
- It calls itself **Tolle**. It repeats parts of its message and forgets others.
- Hogar Irongrip went white when he saw its maker's mark.

### What's Really Happening
- Nine centuries ago, Irongrip smiths and Flan wardens jointly kept the Dawn Hold's seal. Tolle was built to carry word if the seal ever weakened. It has walked since the seal first cracked.
- **What is sealed:** the Horned King's line ended in the hold. Behind the black door lies what the last heir bound, a demon the Tally names only as the **Bound One**, and the last heir's undead remains. *(House placeholders, consistent with SP-PBS-04's Tally.)*
- Iuz's hunters, a **nalfeshnee** and **vrocks** led by Vathra's successor or Vathra herself, want Tolle's memory-core: it holds the seal's binding-words.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. Tolle walks west alone and is ambushed in the Clatspur foothills.
2. The hunters take the core to Iuz, who learns the binding-words.
3. Within a year, the Dawn Hold's black door opens, under Iuz's control.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
Tolle arrives in Ritterstadt with fragments of its warning, and the Dean asks the fellows to escort it (Part 1). On the road west, the hunters strike and Tolle begins to fail (Part 2). At Bitter Pass, the clan must hear the warning while Iuz's force breaks in (Part 3). The fellows decide whether Tolle's message is delivered, traded, or carried by them (Part 4).

## Running Notes
- **Tolle's Condition (6 to 0):** each hit Tolle takes, or each forced march, costs 1. At 2 it loses its words. At 0 it falls, and the core can still be transferred.
- **Reaction tools:**
  - The hunters strike when Tolle is separated from the party.
  - The nalfeshnee teleports in reinforcements.
  - In the hold, Hrodric's faction blocks or aids based on hook 2's ending.
- **Nudges:**
  1. Tolle stops and points east, toward the Mounds.
  2. Hogar translates a fragment.
  3. Vrock feathers on the trail ahead.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** the Irongrip audience hall, its gate burning, while Tolle speaks the warning to the assembled clan and the nalfeshnee breaks through.
- **Choices:**
  - Deliver the warning. Clan Irongrip and the University ally, setting up a campaign finale at the Dawn Hold.
  - Trade Tolle's core to save the hold. Iuz gets the binding-words. Very dark.
  - Carry the warning themselves after Tolle falls (core transfer). A PC becomes the seal's living key.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — Warning delivered:** a grand alliance, and the next adventure is the Dawn Hold.
- **B — A PC carries the core:** that PC holds the binding-words, a lasting boon and burden.
- **C — Core lost to Iuz:** the black door will open. The finale is now a race.
- **Credits:** 80 base. Extra credit: +10 for Tolle's history recorded (within the 600 cap).
