# SEED-07 — The Sepia Stain

**APL 11 (levels 11–12) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

*Tier 3 framing: the party are graduate fellows on a faculty emergency commission, or the original students years later.*

**Pitch.** The brown rock-stain the Sepia Uplands are named for spreads across a whole valley overnight, and the naturalists want samples. The stain is alive, the waking body of a spore-colony, and it is moving toward Ritterstadt at a mile a day.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- Yati shepherds say the brown stain on the rocks is "the star's shadow," from a sky-stone that fell in their great-grandmothers' grandmothers' time.
- Overnight, the Lindgraben valley went brown from rim to rim.
- The hamlet of Lindhof hasn't sent anyone to market in three days.

### What's Really Happening
- A star-fallen spore-colony lies under the Lindgraben in a glassy crater. Most of the Uplands' stain is its dormant runners. A late summer earthquake cracked the crater, and it woke.
- Its **heart-node** grows in the crater. The colony makes **spore hosts** of anything it touches, and a **behir** has come to bask in the heart's heat.
- Yati star-lore holds a rite, the "star-sleep," which put the colony to sleep once before. It needs a cold-iron spike and the old song.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. Day 1–3: Lindhof is taken, and its people become spore hosts.
2. Day 4–10: the stain crosses the downs. Kottsmort's flocks fall.
3. Day 12: the stain reaches Ritterstadt's fields, and the field campus is abandoned.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The naturalists send fellows for samples (Part 1). Lindhof is silent, and its people are spore hosts (Part 2). Mapping the spread leads to the crater and to Yati lore of the star-sleep (Part 3). In the crater, the fellows burn the heart, sing it back to sleep, or let a faculty member bottle it (Part 4).

## Running Notes
- **Spread clock:** a mile a day. Each long rest the fellows take near the stain adds a spore-exposure check (Constitution DC 15, or 1 level of exhaustion).
- **Reaction tools:**
  - The heart sends hosts toward fire.
  - The behir defends the heart's heat.
  - Hosts of anything killed rise again in 1d4 rounds unless burned.
- **Faculty interest:** the head naturalist *(GU roster)* wants a living sample, and escalates from asking to ordering to sabotaging the burning.
- **Nudges:**
  1. A sample jar cracks overnight.
  2. A spore-host fox follows the camp.
  3. Moira finds them.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** a glassy crater whose walls ripple with growth. The heart is a pulsing mass hung from ropy tendrils, and the behir coils on the warm glass.
- **Choices:**
  - Burn the heart. The colony dies, and the star-relic is lost.
  - Perform the star-sleep with Moira. It sleeps for generations, and the University must keep the site.
  - Let the naturalist bottle a living culture. A weapon or a cure, and a seed for trouble later.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — Star-sleep:** the valley recovers, and the Yati trust the fellows.
- **B — Heart burned:** the valley is scarred but safe, and the relic is lost.
- **C — Bottled culture, or a failed attempt:** the threat continues in another form.
- **Credits:** 50 base. Extra credit: +5 for a map of the spread, +5 for transcribed star-lore.
