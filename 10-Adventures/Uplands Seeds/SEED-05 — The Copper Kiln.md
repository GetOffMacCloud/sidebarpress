# SEED-05 — The Copper Kiln

**APL 6 (levels 5–7) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

**Pitch.** Every morning, finished copper tools turn up in the cold ash of a smelting site 4,000 years old. Antiquities wants to know why. A dealer wants it never to stop. And the spirit doing the work only wants to hear that the job is done.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- Gnome miners from Gildenhand found the site last autumn: a ring of slag heaps and a beehive kiln, with new copper axes in the ash each dawn.
- An Antiquities dealer named Grisel bought the finds and is selling them in the City of Greyhawk as "Flan antiquities."
- Two gnomes who slept at the kiln woke with burned hands and won't go back.

### What's Really Happening
- Flan smiths bound an **earth elemental**, called "the Smith Below," to work the kiln "until the count of tools is met." The count was met long ago. The last smith died before speaking the release.
- The Smith works nightly with **magma mephits** (3–4) as its bellows, drawing copper from the hill.
- The count is cut on **tally-stones** half buried around the slag ring, in Flan notches.
- Grisel knows the tools are new, not ancient. He wants the Smith kept working and anyone who might free it gone.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. **Week 1:** Grisel sells another crate in the City.
2. **Week 2:** the Smith, drawing deeper, collapses a Gildenhand gallery. Miners die.
3. **Week 3:** Grisel hires toughs to keep the gnomes away from "his" kiln.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The students camp at the kiln to watch the night's work (1). The Smith rises and works, and lashes out when interrupted (2). The gnomes and the half-buried tally-stones reveal the count and the missing release (3). Grisel tries to buy, then stop, the students (4). At the kiln they speak the release, rebind the Smith, or destroy it (5).

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** each night the Smith draws deeper. On the third night, the Gildenhand gallery collapses.
- **Reaction tools:**
  - **The Smith's temper:** Working (ignores watchers) → Disturbed (mephits harry) → Wrathful (attacks) if the kiln is damaged.
  - Grisel escalates: buy → lie → hire toughs (**thugs** (4)) → sabotage the tallies.
  - Mephits flee to the kiln if the Smith is calmed.
- **Nudges:** (1) a tool stamped with a tally-mark; (2) the Smith pauses at the count of a gnome's song; (3) a gnome remembers "the stones with scratches."

## Climax
- **Set piece:** the beehive kiln at midnight: red heat, slag heaps, tally-stones in a ring, and Grisel's men on the rim.
- **Choices:**
  - **Release:** speak the count and "the work is done" in Flan or with Old Faith rites. The kiln goes cold, and the Smith leaves one perfect +1 weapon or tool.
  - **Rebind** the Smith to University work. There's an ethical cost, and a future hook.
  - **Destroy** it (a full fight).

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — released:** 35 credits plus 10 extra credit each; the parting gift; Gildenhand's gratitude (discounted mining gear).
- **B — bound or destroyed:** 35 credits. Bound: Antiquities is pleased and the gnomes are uneasy. Destroyed: the hill is safe and the site is dead.
- **C — retreat:** Grisel keeps selling, and the collapse happens.

## Key NPCs
**Grisel Hanfstengl**, Antiquities dealer. [Zilchus]. *Wants:* the tools to keep coming. *Knows:* they are new. *Reacts:* buy → lie → hire → sabotage. *Stats:* spy.

**The Smith Below**, bound earth spirit. *Wants:* release. *Knows:* the count and the smiths' names. *Reacts:* works → harries → rages. *Stats:* earth elemental.

**Foreman Nib Coppercoil**, Gildenhand gnome. [Garl Glittergold]. *Wants:* the tunneling stopped. *Knows:* the tally-stones' locations.
