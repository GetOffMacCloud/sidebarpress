# SEED-18 — The Red Journal

**APL 8 (levels 7–9) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

**Pitch.** A dead traveler's journal records experiments in Ur-Flan blood necromancy on hill wildlife, and the Provost wants the materials recovered "discreetly." The handwriting belongs to a member of the University's own faculty, and the University would rather bury the truth than report it.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- Shepherds above the Grauwasser have found sheep drained white, and an owlbear that kept walking after an arrow went through its heart.
- A University field assistant, **Dietlind Varr**, went missing three weeks ago "on leave."
- A body was found in a gully below the Hohlwand cliffs with a journal bound in red calfskin.

### What's Really Happening
- A faculty member *(GU roster: choose a plausible suspect)* found Ur-Flan blood-rites in a Flan cairn and began testing them on animals in a hidden lab: a shepherd's bothy dug into the Hohlwand.
- Dietlind was their assistant. She stole the journal to expose them, and fled. A blood-sick **owlbear** ran her off the cliff.
- The Provost *(GU roster)* has read the journal and wants it gone. Their agent, **Mattis Grell**, is already in the field to destroy the lab.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. Grell burns the lab. The faculty member moves the surviving specimens to a second cave.
2. The specimens escape at the next new moon. Two shepherds die, and a flesh golem of grafted beasts stalks the Uplands.
3. The University blames "wild beasts," and the faculty member goes on teaching.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The Provost hands over the journal and asks for quiet recovery (Part 1). The death site and the shepherds lead to the lab and to the realization that the author is faculty (Part 2). Tracking the specimens brings the students into a race with the Provost's agent (Part 3). The lab confrontation forces the choice: public scandal, quiet bargain, or burning it all (Part 4).

## Hooks
1. **The Provost's commission (default).** *"Confidential recovery of University field materials. Discretion essential. 40 credits each."*
2. **Personal.** A PC was taught by Dietlind, or shared rooms with her.
3. **Thread.** If hook 14 was run, the students already know how the University handles uncomfortable truths.
- **Decline path:** another team takes the job. A month later the golem kills a shepherd family, and the Yati blame the University.

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** Grell reaches the lab at dusk on day 2. If the students arrive after him, the lab is burning and the golem has been loosed.
- **Reaction tools:**
  - **Lab alert:** Lax (the professor is absent) / Wary (the doors are warded) / Alert (the golem is woken).
  - **Grell's escalation:** shadows the students → lies → sabotages → ambushes.
  - **The professor's escalation:** deny → blame Dietlind → offer co-authorship → flee by *misty step*.
- **Nudges:**
  1. Another drained sheep.
  2. Gerlach comes to them.
  3. Grell is seen buying lamp oil in quantity.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** the bothy dug into the cliff, with cages, a blood-trough, and a ledge 60 feet above the gully, while Grell's fire spreads.
- **Choices:**
  - Bring the professor and the journal to the town Reeve. The scandal threatens the field campus's charter.
  - Hand everything to the Provost. Credits, and the Provost owes the students a favor.
  - Burn it all. No evidence, no scandal, and the professor walks free.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — Truth told, specimens destroyed:** the University is shaken, and the Yati trust the students.
- **B — Quiet deal:** a favor owed, and a guilty conscience.
- **C — The professor flees with the notes:** a recurring villain.
- **Credits:** 40 base. Extra credit: +5 for a field report on Ur-Flan blood-rites, +5 for recovering Dietlind's body.

## Key NPCs

  - *Knows:* the Flan cairn and the rites.
  - *Reacts:* see the escalation in Running Notes.

- **Mattis Grell**, the Provost's agent. *Deity:* [Zilchus].
  - *Wants:* clean ashes and his fee.
  - *Knows:* the Provost's orders.
  - *Reacts:* cooperative mask → sabotage → a knife in the dark → sells the Provost out if captured.

  - *Wants:* his flock safe.
