# SEED-08 — The Fog-Taken Flock

**APL 2 (levels 1–3) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> *"The fog takes; the fog gives back. Leave milk."* — Yati shepherds' saying

**Pitch.** A Yati boy and forty sheep walked into a fog bank on Corrow Fell three days ago, and the fog hasn't lifted. The students find the boy safe and well fed, keeping company with a lonely young dragon, and they have to decide who goes home.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- The fog sits on Corrow Fell like a lid and never drifts.
- **Tam**, 10, son of herder **Bran Ruadh**, went after strays and didn't come back.
- Fog off the Quag Mires has taken folk before. Elders leave milk at the fog's edge.
- Bran has hired the hunter **Brogan Hale**, who is paid by the head for "whatever's in there."

### What's Really Happening
- The fog is the breath of a **mist dragon wyrmling**, a young dragon who calls itself *Wisp*. It hatched in an old Flan cairn on the fell.
- Wisp is lonely. Tam found it, fed it, and named it. The sheep are its herd now. A few have been eaten.
- Tam doesn't want to leave: Wisp is the first thing that ever needed him.
- **Wolves** (a pack) are following the flock's scent into the fog.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. Day 4: Brogan goes in with poisoned bait.
2. Day 5: Wisp is poisoned, panics, and blinds the fell with fog. Tam is lost in it, and the wolves close in.
3. Day 6: the fog lifts. Tam is dead or wild, and Wisp is dead or gone south, angry at people.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The students go into the fog to find Tam. They cross the fell by landmarks and sound while wolves follow. They find Tam's camp in a ruined cairn, where a small, curious dragon is dozing in a curl of mist. Tam refuses to leave. Brogan arrives to collect his bounty, or the wolves strike. The students choose between the dragon's life, the boy's wish, and his father's grief.

## Facts the Players Must Learn
| # | Fact | Route 1 | Route 2 | Route 3 | Last resort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Tam is alive. | Fresh small bootprints and a cookfire (Survival DC 10). | A tin whistle answering theirs in the fog. | Sheep with neat rope halters. | Tam walks out of the fog to meet them. |
| F2 | The fog is a creature's breath. | It flows *uphill* toward the cairn (Nature DC 12). | It smells of cold rain, not marsh (Perception DC 12). | Bran's elders: "Fog that never moves is breathing." | They see Wisp exhale it. |
| F3 | Brogan means to poison the dragon. | His pack holds wolfsbane-salted meat (Investigation DC 11). | He boasts in Kottsmort-road taverns. | Bran admits he hired "something sharper than me." | Brogan lays the bait in front of them. |

## Hooks
1. **Job Board (default).** *"Boy and flock lost in the fog on Corrow Fell. Reward from the Ruadh family, with University thanks."* 15 credits each.
2. **Personal.** A druid or ranger PC is sent by the Ehlonna's Mount enclave: "Something new is breathing on Corrow."
3. **Thread.** If SEED-15 has been run, the Corrish families vouch for the students to Bran.
- **Decline path:** Brogan goes in alone. The fog lifts on day 6, and Tam comes home silent. Rumors spread of an angry dragon in the Mires.

**Short slot (3 hours):** cut navigation to one check; the wolves and Brogan arrive together.

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** Brogan enters the fog at dawn on day 4, half a day behind the students.
- **Reaction tools:** the wolves scale with noise and light. Wisp's mood track runs curious → wary → frightened (it uses its fog breath and flees). Brogan's escalation runs bargain → sneak → poison → bow.
- **Nudges:** (1) A haltered sheep wanders out of the fog. (2) The whistle. (3) Tam's voice calling "Wisp!"

## Climax
- **Set piece:** a ruined Flan cairn in a hollow of rolling mist (heavily obscured beyond 20 feet). Standing stones give cover.
- **Choices:**
  - Kill or drive off Wisp. The fog lifts, Tam is grieving, and Bran pays.
  - Persuade Tam to go home with a promise that he can visit. This requires Bran's consent and Brogan stood down.
  - Find Wisp a home: the druids of Ehlonna's Mount, the Brannagh hills, or the fell itself, with the clans' blessing.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — full success:** Tam is home, Wisp lives, and the wolves are driven off. Wisp becomes a recurring fog-friend.
- **B — partial:** Tam is home, but Wisp is dead or driven away. Tam doesn't forgive easily.
- **C — retreat or defeat:** Tam stays in the fog, or the wolves take the flock. Bran never trusts the University again.
- **Credits:** 15 base each, +5 for Tam returned unharmed, +5 for Wisp alive and settled, +5 for a field report on the mist dragon.

## Key NPCs
**Tam Ruadh**, 10, Yati shepherd boy. *Deity:* [Beory]. *Wants:* to keep Wisp. *Knows:* Wisp's habits and the cairn paths. *Reacts:* hides → argues → agrees if Wisp is safe.

**Bran Ruadh**, herder, his father. *Wants:* his son. *Knows:* the fell, the milk custom. *Reacts:* grief → anger at the University → accepts a compromise if his son asks for it.

**Brogan Hale**, bounty hunter. *Deity:* [Kord]. *Wants:* the bounty, plus a dragon's head to sell. *Knows:* poisons and tracking. *Reacts:* bargain → sneak → poison → shoot. Backs down if outnumbered and paid off.

**Wisp**, mist dragon wyrmling. *Wants:* company and food. *Knows:* the cairn's Flan carvings (it sleeps on them). *Reacts:* curious → wary → fogs and flees.
