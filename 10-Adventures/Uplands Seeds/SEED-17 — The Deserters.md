# SEED-17 — The Deserters

**APL 3 (levels 2–4) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> *"We did not run from you. We ran from him."* — Gutch, at Kettle Cave

**Pitch.** Orcs have taken a cave above town, and the militia is mustering to burn them out. The students are sent ahead to scout. They find deserters from Iuz's horde with their young, asking for asylum, and Iuz's hunters a day behind them.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- Shepherds saw a dozen orcs on the Kettle path at dawn.
- Militia **Captain Arno Haupt** has called the muster. Torches and oil are being bought.
- An alderman wants "scouts ahead" so the militia doesn't walk into an ambush.

### What's Really Happening
- **Gutch** led thirteen orcs (seven fighters and six young or elders) away from Iuz's forces in the Vesve after a massacre they wouldn't join.
- They have no fight with Ritterstadt and want passage or a place to stay.
- Iuz's hunters are tracking them by a cursed token sewn into Gutch's cloak: an **orc Eye of Gruumsh**, **orcs** (4), and **worgs** (2).
- The hunters will kill the deserters, and anyone sheltering them.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. Evening, day 1: the militia storms Kettle Cave. Orcs and townsfolk die.
2. Night, day 2: the hunters arrive, kill the survivors, and burn the nearest farmstead as a message.
3. The town learns to fear Iuz and hate orcs at once.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The students scout Kettle Cave and parley with Gutch, who tells the truth. They race back to stall Haupt's militia with evidence, argument, or delay. That night the hunters strike, at the cave or the town. The students choose to stand with the orcs, guide them on toward Perrenland, or let the militia have them.

## Hooks
1. **Job Board (default).** *"Scouts wanted ahead of the militia. Alderman's commission."* 15 credits each.
2. **Personal.** A half-orc PC is recognized by Gutch's people, and they look to the PC for a promise.
3. **Thread.** If SEED-10 has been run, Grumbold the giant vouches for Gutch.
- **Decline path:** the militia attacks and the hunters finish the job. Ritterstadt buries four of its own.

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** the militia marches at the 5th hour after noon on day 1. The hunters arrive at midnight.
- **Reaction tools:** the militia mood track runs 0–4 (at 4 they attack). Scaled hunter response: a probe against a strong defense, a full assault against a weak one. Survivors flee north and report to Iuz, which opens a thread. The token lets the hunters know where Gutch is.
- **Nudges:** (1) A young orc offers the students bread. (2) Worg howls. (3) Haupt's torches on the road.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** the cave mouth and its rocky slope, or the town's palisade if the orcs have been brought inside. Darkness; the worgs flank.
- **Choices:**
  - Stand with the orcs against the hunters, winning the town over if the militia sees it.
  - Guide the orcs onward toward Perrenland after cutting out the token.
  - Hand them to the militia (dark; the hunters may still come).

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — full success:** hunters defeated, the deserters spared, and the militia won over. Gutch's band could become a homebrew ally.
- **B — partial:** the deserters escape onward, but there are losses on one side or the other.
- **C — retreat or defeat:** the militia or the hunters wipe out the orcs. The token goes north to Iuz.
- **Credits:** 15 base each, +5 for no townsfolk deaths, +5 for the hunters defeated, +5 for the token recovered for University study.

## Key NPCs
**Gutch**, deserter chief. *Deity:* none now (renounced Gruumsh and Iuz). *Wants:* his band alive and a place to stay. *Knows:* Iuz's Vesve raid routes, and the hunters' leader. *Reacts:* wary → honest → desperate → fights to the death only if cornered.

**Captain Arno Haupt**, militia captain. *Deity:* [Heironeous]. *Wants:* the town safe and his reputation. *Knows:* the town's defenses. *Reacts:* scorn → listens to evidence → leads the defense if convinced.

**Sergeant Ruwen Hask** (existing vault NPC). *Reacts:* can be won to either side; his word sways the militia.

**Hunt-leader Vorg Splitlip**, orc Eye of Gruumsh in Iuz's service. *Wants:* the deserters' heads. *Reacts:* taunt → probe → assault → flee north at half force.
