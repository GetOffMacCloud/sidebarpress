# SEED-21 — The Visiting Examiner

**APL 2 (levels 1–3) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> *"The field campus is either a frontier of learning or a very expensive tent."* — Examiner Grenwald's draft report, page 1

**Pitch.** An auditor from the City of Greyhawk joins the next field run, and his report decides the field campus's funding. He is hopeless in the wild. The students must keep him alive while they win him over, frighten him off, or find his price.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- **Examiner Ludo Grenwald** arrived with six trunks, a folding chair, and an opinion.
- The parent campus is weighing whether to close Ritterstadt's field program.
- The field-campus head *(GU roster)* has told the students, "Bring him back pleased, or bring him back at all."

### What's Really Happening
- Grenwald is pompous but not cruel. He was sent to cut costs and quietly hopes to find something worth defending.
- His real price is a **publishable discovery** with his name on it, which would make his career.
- The Brennock Fells are an ordinary field route: a ford, wolves, goats, and weather. Nothing is staged. The danger is that Grenwald does everything wrong.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. Grenwald goes out with whoever is free and falls in the ford on day 1.
2. He writes a damning report from his sickbed.
3. The field program's budget is halved next term (fewer jobs and lower credit rates).

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The students escort Grenwald and his trunks into the Brennock Fells. Three set pieces test them: a swollen ford, a stalking wolf pack, and a goat stampede, then a night storm. A Grenwald Mood track records every rescue, kindness, and humiliation. Back at camp he writes his report, and the students try to shape it with a shared discovery, a fright, or a bribe.

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** a two-day route; the report is written on the second night.
- **Grenwald Mood track (0–6, start at 2):**
  - **+1** for a rescue, a kindness, a shared find, or a good meal.
  - **−1** for a humiliation, an injury, a lost trunk, or mockery.
  - At 0 he leaves; at 6 he champions the campus.
- **Reaction tools:** the wolves scale with noise and light (Grenwald's lantern draws them). Grenwald's escalation runs complain → insist → wander off → demand to go home.
- **Nudges:** (1) He picks up a "pretty rock," a fossil. (2) He asks what the carvings mean. (3) He sighs about his career.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** the second night, by firelight, with the draft report on his knee.
- **Choices:**
  - Win him over: share a real find, credited to him (Mood 4+).
  - Frighten him off: a staged or real scare. The budget is cut, but there's no oversight next year.
  - Buy him: a bribe or a flattering arrangement. It works now, and he mentions it in his next report.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — full success:** a glowing report, the program expanded, and +2 credits on all Job Board rates for a term.
- **B — partial:** a lukewarm report; the program is unchanged.
- **C — retreat or defeat:** Grenwald hurt or fled, the budget cut, and Job Board rates −2 credits for a term.
- **Credits:** 20 base each, +5 for Grenwald unharmed, +5 for a shared discovery filed, +5 for Mood 6.
