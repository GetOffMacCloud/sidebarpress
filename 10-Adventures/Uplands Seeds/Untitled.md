# SEED-01 — The Dawn Hold Survey

**APL 6 (levels 5–7) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> _"Few return from the Mounds. Those who do speak of the dead, and of wounds that closed overnight."_ — Notice of the Prefect of Exag

**Pitch.** Flan Antiquities hires the students to finish a survey route through the Mounds of Dawn to an Ur-Flanne hold beneath them. Someone keeps moving the stakes, the survey captain is ready to use force, and whatever lies under the hills stirs whenever the line points true.
## Background

### What the Locals Know

- The Mounds of Dawn are hills "almost too regular to be natural," with haunted ruins along the Vestflow and lost tombs toward Lake Quag (canon).
- The prefect of Exag pays a standing reward for credible reports on the Mounds, and on the tales of wounds healed and youth restored (canon).
- Hugelrote clanfolk graze the lower slopes and avoid the high mounds after dark. Small dwarf enclaves keep entirely to themselves (canon).
- Last spring a University survey party came back two men short, with its stakes "pointing at nothing."

### What's Really Happening

- Under the central mounds lies **the Dawn Hold**, the sealed hall where the Flan bound the **Horned King** c. −1200 CY (house material).
- The **dwarf enclave** of Stonekeep Hollow are its wardens by inheritance. They pull up and replant the survey stakes so that every line bends away from the hold.
- **Dorran Vex**, the Antiquities survey captain, has worked this out. He has hired Perrender toughs to "clear the locals" and a scout to find the enclave.
- When stakes are set on the true line, they act as a sighting rule. At night, **gargoyles** wake on the old waystones, and an **earth elemental** rises along the line to break it.

### If Nobody Interferes

1. **Day 1:** Vex follows the corrected line and loses a man to the gargoyles.
2. **Day 2:** Vex's scout finds Stonekeep Hollow. Vex burns its sheep-pens to force a parley.
3. **Day 3:** the enclave fights. Two dwarves and three crew die, and Vex takes the elder's warden-key.
4. **Day 4:** Vex files a complete map in Exag. Within a month a copy reaches Iuz's agents.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)

The students cross Lake Quag and pick up the old survey at the Vestflow (1). The stakes lie and something walks the line at night (2). Tracking the stake-movers leads to a hidden dwarf enclave with an old duty (3). Vex forces the true line and the guardians of the approach answer (4). The students decide what map leaves the Mounds, if any (5).

|F3|Vex will use force, and his map will go to Iuz.|His crew boasting at camp|His letter promising "a buyer in the north" (Investigation DC 15)|The surviving survey man from last spring|Vex says it himself if confronted|

## Hooks

1. **Job Board (default).** _"Flan Antiquities seeks a field team to complete the Mounds of Dawn approach survey. Barge passage provided. 40 credits each; the Prefect of Exag's reward to be shared."_
2. **Personal.** A PC with dwarf kin in the Clatspurs is told by Hogar Irongrip: "If you meet the Hollow folk, show them this," a stamped iron ring.
3. **Thread (SP-PBS-04).** If the Watchers' door was opened, the students already know the hold's hall. The stakes point to it.

- **Decline path:** Vex goes alone. A month later the students hear that Stonekeep Hollow burned, and a stranger in Ritterstadt is asking about "the Watchers."

## Running Notes

- **Clock:** Vex moves one step per day (If Nobody Interferes). A delay of more than a day means Scene 5 starts with the Hollow already burning.
- **Reaction tools:**
    - **Enclave alert:** Lax → Wary (strangers seen) → Alert (pens burned). At Alert, enclave **dwarf veterans** (4) ambush any surveyors.
    - Vex's crew rout if Vex falls, and his scout flees to Exag.
    - The guardians reset each night; destroyed gargoyles are not replaced.
- **Nudges:** (1) a stake planted backward with a fresh knot; (2) small boot prints, iron-shod; (3) the guide refuses to go on.

## Climax

- **Set piece:** the central mound at dusk, with the line of stakes, toppled waystones, and Vex's crew digging. The earth heaves along the line.
- **Choices:**
    - **Finish the map** for Antiquities. The Iuz thread advances.
    - **Falsify it** and side with the wardens. The enclave becomes an ally.
    - **Map it and seal it** with the enclave. Antiquities gets a "classified" copy, and the students gain the elder's trust.

## Concluding the Adventure

- **A — sealed with the wardens:** 40 credits each; the enclave's friendship; the elder's warden-token (opens doors in hook 2).
- **B — map delivered:** 40 credits plus a 10 extra credit bonus. The prefect pays 50 gp each. The Dawn Hold is now known, and hook 3 follows within a season.
- **C — retreat:** 15 credits. Vex finishes alone, and outcome B happens without the students.

## Key NPCs

**Dorran Vex**, Antiquities survey captain. [Zilchus]. _Wants:_ the map, and the buyer's gold. _Knows:_ the line wakes guardians, and a buyer "in the north." _Reacts:_ charm → threaten → burn → fight. _Stats:_ veteran.

**Elder Grimna Hollowell**, warden of Stonekeep Hollow. [Moradin]. _Wants:_ the line broken forever. _Knows:_ what the Horned King was (in part), and that the seal weakens with each true line. _Reacts:_ hide → warn → bargain → fight.

**Aart Rodenbrug**, Hugelrote clan guide. [Kord]. _Wants:_ to be paid and home by dark. _Knows:_ the paths and the old tales. _Reacts:_ leaves if the line is walked at night.

**The Antiquities professor** _(GU roster)_, reachable by letter only.

# SEED-02 — Silvervein's Key

**APL 12 (levels 11–13) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

_Tier 3 framing: the party are graduate fellows on a faculty commission, or the original students years later._

**Pitch.** A dwarf peddler sells the University a rune-key "from the deep hold," and the fellows are sent to test it. It opens the inner gate of Bitter Pass, a hold closed to outsiders for four centuries. Clan Irongrip wants to know where the key came from, because it belonged to their envoy, who vanished ten years ago.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know

- Clan Irongrip holds the fort at Bitter Pass and keeps "grudging coexistence" with Ritterstadt. Never ask a Clatspur dwarf about Bitter Pass politics.
- Hogar Irongrip, Ritterstadt's master smith, is a clan exile. He will not say why.
- A dwarf peddler calling himself **Silvervein** has been selling "deep-hold curios" in town.

### What's Really Happening

- Behind the fort lies the **deep hold**, a sealed underhall where the clan's elders and archives live. Ten years ago its envoy, **Dain Irongrip**, went east to find the Watchers' secret and never returned.
- Silvervein is a **doppelganger** in Iuz's service. It killed Dain, took his key, and is using the University to get someone through the gate it cannot open alone. The gate's glyphs test for living dwarven blood and University seals.
- Inside, the clan is split. **Matriarch Orla Irongrip** wants the surface back. **Thane Hrodric Irongrip** wants the gate sealed forever, and blood for Dain.

### If Nobody Interferes

1. The University tests the key itself. A careless junior faculty member opens the gate, with Silvervein in tow.
2. Silvervein slips inside wearing Dain's face, and steals the archive tablet that records the Dawn Hold's seal.
3. Hrodric blames Ritterstadt for the theft, closes the pass road, and raids the town's ore carts. Iuz learns where the Dawn Hold lies.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)

The fellows test the key and enlist Hogar to get them to Bitter Pass (Part 1). The deep hold opens. They are brought before the clan and accused (Part 2). Clues in the hold point to an impostor among them (Part 3). The hunt for the doppelganger runs against Hrodric's march to war, ending in the archive vault (Part 4).

## Facts the Players Must Learn

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|F1|The key was Dain's.|Hogar recognizes the maker's mark.|Orla names it at audience.|The key's bow is cast with Dain's rune (History DC 15).|Hrodric accuses them of murdering Dain.|
|F2|Silvervein is not a dwarf.|It never eats, and reacts wrongly to clan oath-salt (Insight DC 17).|_Detect thoughts_ or _true seeing_.|The real Dain's bones, found in Silvervein's town lodging.|It is found wearing a guard's face in the archive.|
|F3|The archive tablet holds the Dawn Hold's seal.|The archivist, **Kelda**, tells them.|Tolle's warning, if hook 20 has been run.|Reading the tablet (History DC 18, Dwarvish).|Silvervein runs for the vault.|

## Hooks

1. **The faculty commission (default).** _"Fellows wanted to authenticate a dwarven key of uncertain provenance. Field travel to the Clatspurs. 60 credits each."_
2. **Personal.** A dwarf PC has Irongrip kin, or carries a clan grudge.
3. **Thread.** If hook 25 was run, the key's bow is eye-shaped, matching the Watchers' Eye.

- **Decline path:** the "If Nobody Interferes" steps happen. Ritterstadt loses its ore trade, and in hook 3 Iuz's agents know where to dig.

**Short slot (3 hours):** golems only animate if the glyphs are failed. Merge Scenes 3 and 4.

## Running Notes

- **Clock:** Hrodric's war-moot convenes in 12 hours. At the moot he moves to seal the gate and march.
- **Reaction tools:**
    - **Hold alert:** Lax (guests under oath) / Wary (accusation) / Alert (blood spilled).
    - Silvervein changes faces whenever it is suspected.
    - Survivors of Hrodric's faction carry tales to the moot.
- **Nudges:**
    1. A guard swears he spoke to "Silvervein" in two places at once.
    2. Kelda reports the vault's ward disturbed.
    3. Hogar's letter from his sister inside the hold names Dain's last journey.

## Climax

- **Set piece:** the archive vault, with tablet-shelves, a ward-pool, and a single stair, while Hrodric's veterans pour in.
- **Choices:**
    - Expose Silvervein before the clan. Alliance, and the gate opens to the University.
    - Seize the tablet and flee. The clan is hostile, but the knowledge is kept.
    - Side with Hrodric. The hold is sealed, and Hogar's exile becomes permanent.

## Concluding the Adventure

- **A — Alliance:** Clan Irongrip is a standing ally. Hogar may be restored, and the tablet is copied for the University.
- **B — Escape with the truth:** the clan is cold, and the pass road is tolled.
- **C — Silvervein escapes with the tablet:** Iuz knows the seal. Run hook 3 with the agents a step ahead.
- **Credits:** 60 base. Extra credit: +5 for the authentication report, +5 for a copy of the tablet.

# SEED-03 — The Old One's Road

**APL 13 (levels 12–14) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

_Tier 3 framing: the party are graduate fellows sent by Antiquities and the Provost, or the original students years later._

> _"He who came before me came for me."_ — attributed to Iuz by his priests, 576 CY

**Pitch.** A landslide in the Mounds of Dawn uncovers a paved causeway to the Dawn Hold: the Horned King's road of conquest, c. −1200 CY. Iuz's agents are already racing along it. Their master wants proof that an ageless "Old One" ruled the north before him, and the milestones say no such thing.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know

- The Mounds of Dawn are full of hills too regular to be natural, haunted ruins on the Vestflow's west bank, and lost tombs. The prefect of Exag pays for news of them.
- Ogres and hill giants come down from the Yatils and cut Exag off.
- Survivors of the Mounds tell of vengeful undead, and of springs that heal wounds and restore youth.

### What's Really Happening

- The causeway runs 30 miles through the Mounds. Three **waystations** each hold a milestone-stele recording the Horned King's march. He was a mortal Ur-Flanne demon-binder, and his "agelessness" was a mask worn by successive heirs.
- **Vathra**, Iuz's agent, wants the precedent. Each stele she reaches, she either destroys or carves over with a forgery naming "the Old One who was, and is."
- The road's last Warden-General, **Karaith** (a **mummy lord**), still keeps the final waystation at the Dawn Hold's gate.

### If Nobody Interferes

1. Vathra's team reaches waystation 1 on day 1 and waystation 2 on day 2. The forgeries are carved.
2. On day 3 she bargains with or destroys Karaith and carves the final forgery at the gate.
3. Iuz's priests proclaim the record. His cult swells in the north, and the agents begin digging at the Dawn Hold's gate.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)

The fellows reach the slide and the first waystation, already defaced (Part 1). Racing Vathra to waystation 2, they find a stele intact and learn the truth (Part 2). Demons, golems, and the road itself slow both teams (Part 3). At the Dawn Hold's gate, the fellows, Vathra, and Karaith meet, and the fellows decide what the record will say (Part 4).

|F2|The Horned King was mortal, a line of masked heirs.|The intact stele at waystation 2 (History DC 16).|Karaith's testimony.|The Tally of the Horned King (if hook 25 was run).|The heirs' masks are found in the final waystation.|
|F3|Karaith can be bargained with.|He speaks before he strikes.|The steles' warden oath, read aloud.|A Yati or Flan PC's courtesy, by Old Faith rite.|He halts when Vathra attacks him first.|

## Hooks

1. **The commission (default).** _"Fellows to secure and map a newly exposed causeway, Mounds of Dawn. Antiquities and the Provost jointly. 75 credits each, plus the prefect of Exag's reward."_
2. **Personal.** A Flan-descended PC's family keeps a song about the masked king.
3. **Thread.** If hook 25 ended with Saal escaping (Conclusion E), the agents have his rubbing and are a day ahead.

- **Decline path:** Vathra succeeds. Within a year Iuz's priests in the north preach the forged record, and hook 20's Tolle arrives to find the Dawn Hold's gate already being dug.

## Running Notes

- **Race clock (0–6 per leg):** the fellows gain on shortcuts (with hazards) and lose ground on rests. Whoever reaches a waystation first controls its stele.
- **Reaction tools:**
    - Vathra summons the **glabrezu** to bargain with or delay the fellows.
    - The **vrocks** scout and harry.
    - Survivors fall back to Vathra.
    - Karaith grows hostile toward anyone who damages a stele.
- **Nudges:**
    1. Fresh orc spoor on the road.
    2. A dying Exag scout's warning.
    3. The vrocks' screech ahead.

## Climax

- **Set piece:** the gate of the Dawn Hold, a black stone portal banded in copper that matches the Hall of Lintels' door, with the final stele before it and Karaith on his throne.
- **Choices:**
    - Stop Vathra and keep the truth. Iuz's claim is denied, and Karaith seals the road.
    - Let the forgery stand. The fellows survive, but Iuz's cult grows.
    - Ally with Karaith to bury the road. The truth is lost to everyone, Iuz included.

## Concluding the Adventure

- **A — Truth kept:** the University publishes the record, and the prefect of Exag pays out.
- **B — Road buried:** no one gets the record, and the Dawn Hold stays lost.
- **C — Forgery stands:** Iuz's propaganda spreads, and Vathra recurs.
- **Credits:** 75 base. Extra credit: +5 for rubbings of all three steles, +5 for a report to the prefect of Exag.

**APL 2 (levels 1–3) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> _"The fog takes; the fog gives back. Leave milk."_ — Yati shepherds' saying

**Pitch.** A Yati boy and forty sheep walked into a fog bank on Corrow Fell three days ago, and the fog hasn't lifted. The students find the boy safe and well fed, keeping company with a lonely young dragon, and they have to decide who goes home.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know

- The fog sits on Corrow Fell like a lid and never drifts.
- **Tam**, 10, son of herder **Bran Ruadh**, went after strays and didn't come back.
- Fog off the Quag Mires has taken folk before. Elders leave milk at the fog's edge.
- Bran has hired the hunter **Brogan Hale**, who is paid by the head for "whatever's in there."

### What's Really Happening

- The fog is the breath of a **mist dragon wyrmling**, a young dragon who calls itself _Wisp_. It hatched in an old Flan cairn on the fell.
- Wisp is lonely. Tam found it, fed it, and named it. The sheep are its herd now. A few have been eaten.
- Tam doesn't want to leave: Wisp is the first thing that ever needed him.
- **Wolves** (a pack) are following the flock's scent into the fog.

### If Nobody Interferes

1. Day 4: Brogan goes in with poisoned bait.
2. Day 5: Wisp is poisoned, panics, and blinds the fell with fog. Tam is lost in it, and the wolves close in.
3. Day 6: the fog lifts. Tam is dead or wild, and Wisp is dead or gone south, angry at people.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)

The students go into the fog to find Tam. They cross the fell by landmarks and sound while wolves follow. They find Tam's camp in a ruined cairn, where a small, curious dragon is dozing in a curl of mist. Tam refuses to leave. Brogan arrives to collect his bounty, or the wolves strike. The students choose between the dragon's life, the boy's wish, and his father's grief.

# SEED-11 — The Hill Moot

**APL 3 (levels 2–4) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> _"Stones don't lie. Men lift them."_ — Yati proverb

**Pitch.** Two Yati clans are heading toward blood feud over Greystair Valley, and both ask the University to judge between them. Both claims are true. Someone moved the boundary stones to empty the valley, because there's silver in it.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know

- The Lennagh and Corrish clans have shared Greystair under a stone-marked boundary for six generations.
- This spring the stones say the Lennaghs' summer grazing belongs to the Corrishes, and the Corrishes say the Lennaghs' spring is theirs.
- Two young men have already fought at the ford. Next time someone dies.
- The **Reeve of Fields** asked the University for neutral "assessors" for the moot at the next new moon.

### What's Really Happening

- Ritterstadt prospector **Udo Brenner** found silver float in Greystair's side-gully last autumn.
- With hired men, he dragged six boundary stones by night, so that each clan's map now cuts the other's grazing.
- If the clans feud, the valley empties, and Brenner files a claim with the Reeve as "unoccupied."
- His dig is hidden under brush in the gully, with a tailings pile and an assay receipt from Kottsmort.

### If Nobody Interferes

1. Moot day: the assessors (or nobody) rule for one clan. The other refuses.
2. Three nights later: a Corrish herdsman is killed at the ford. Feud is declared.
3. Within a month both clans move their flocks out. Brenner files his claim and begins mining.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)

The students hear both clans' claims in their own halls. They walk the boundary and find stones with fresh drag marks and old lichen on the wrong side. A gully dig, a tailings pile, and an assay receipt point to Udo Brenner. At the moot, his hired men try to provoke a killing before the truth comes out. The students expose him, broker a split of the silver, or let the University take the find.

## Facts the Players Must Learn

|#|Fact|Route 1|Route 2|Route 3|Last resort|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|F1|The stones were moved.|Drag marks and torn turf (Survival DC 12).|Lichen grows on the "down" side (Nature DC 12).|Each clan's elder recites the old line, and both match the _original_ sockets.|A child shows them the empty socket she plays in.|
|F2|There's silver in the valley.|The gully dig and tailings (Investigation DC 12).|Shiny grit in a Corrish lamb's fleece.|A Kottsmort assayer's gossip.|Brenner's men are seen hauling sacks.|
|F3|Brenner did it.|The assay receipt in his name.|Clay from the gully on his boots and cart.|His hired men drinking in Ritterstadt, flush with coin (Insight DC 13).|He offers the students a share to rule "unoccupied."|

## Hooks

1. **Job Board (default).** _"Assessors wanted for the Greystair moot, at the request of the Reeve of Fields. Tact required."_ 15 credits each.
2. **Personal.** A Yati PC is kin to one clan and is asked to "help your own."
3. **Thread.** If SEED-14 has been run, the clans already distrust the University's word.

- **Decline path:** the moot fails and the feud begins. Brenner's claim crosses the Reeve's desk, and a Yati spear crosses the University gate.

|2|Two Halls|Hear Lennagh and Corrish; hospitality customs|45 min|

|4|The Gully|Find the dig; Brenner's men on guard|35 min|
|5|Climax: The Moot|Social set piece under threat of violence|45 min|
|6|Conclusion|Ruling and aftermath|15 min|

**Short slot (3 hours):** combine the two halls into a single meeting at the ford.

## Running Notes

- **Clock:** the moot at the new moon, two days out. The feud tension track runs 0–6; at 6, blood is spilled.
    - **+1** for each insult, a ruling seen as unfair, or Brenner's provocation.
    - **−1** for each act of respect or piece of evidence shown publicly.
- **Reaction tools:** Brenner's escalation runs deny → bribe → frame (plants a Lennagh knife at the dig) → provoke (an arrow at the moot) → flee to Kottsmort. His men guard the gully by day and are drunk in town by night.
- **Nudges:** (1) An empty socket. (2) Silver-bright grit. (3) A hireling's careless boast.

## Climax

- **Set piece:** the moot circle at the ford: both clans armed, elders seated, and Brenner present as the "neutral" witness. His archer is in the rocks above.
- **Choices:**
    - Expose Brenner with evidence; the clans turn on him (stop a lynching or let it happen).
    - Split the silver between the clans under a written University compact.
    - Let the University claim it for the field campus: credits and a grudge for a generation.

## Concluding the Adventure

- **A — full success:** feud averted, Brenner exposed, and the stones reset with both clans present.
- **B — partial:** feud averted, but the silver goes to the University. Trust is damaged.
- **C — retreat or defeat:** blood is spilled. The feud is on and the valley is lost to both clans.
- **Credits:** 15 base each, +5 for the evidence presented, +5 for no deaths, +5 for a written compact both elders sign.

**Ailsa Corrish**, clan head. _Deity:_ [Obad-Hai]. _Wants:_ her son, who fought at the ford, spared a feud. _Knows:_ where the stones stood when she was a girl. _Reacts:_ cold → pragmatic → ally.

# SEED-12 — The Perrenlander Recruiter

**APL 6 (levels 5–7) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

**Pitch.** A Perrender captain is signing up Ritterstadt's young men for "border work," and none of them have written home. The contract is with the Horned Society: legal on paper, and a death sentence in practice.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know

- Captain Ruprecht Stahl came through in spring with a chartered company's papers and a good purse. Fourteen young men signed on.
- None have written. Stahl says the post is slow from "the northern stations."
- Mothers are pooling coin, and the Reeve has added credits from Ritterstadt's fund.

### What's Really Happening

- Stahl's contract is with the **Horned Society** of Molag, a separate state in 576 and technically not Iuz. Perrender charters forbid nothing in its wording.
- The recruits are drilling in a camp two days northeast, in the reeds at the edge of the Quag Mires, on a drove road toward the Vesve's northern fringe.
- The Hierarchs mean to pass the recruits on to Iuz's border lords as fodder. Their liaison **Mirek** reports to Iuz on the side.
- Mirek burns the recruits' letters home. Stahl doesn't know about the handover; he thinks he's selling garrison troops.

### If Nobody Interferes

1. **Week 1:** a second muster in Ritterstadt takes six more.
2. **Week 2:** the handover party (Mirek with **orcs**) arrives at the camp.
3. **Week 3:** the recruits march east. Few are heard from again.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)

The students attend Stahl's muster and choose to sign on undercover or tail the column (1). At the camp they find burned letters and a contract worded too carefully (2). Mirek's arrival reveals the handover (3). The students free the recruits, expose the contract, or buy them out, before the march east (4). Ritterstadt learns what its sons were sold into (5).

|F3|The recruits will be passed to Iuz.|Mirek's letter (Investigation DC 15)|Orc escort arriving|A captured orc talks|Mirek says it when cornered|

## Hooks

1. **Job Board (default).** _"Families of Ritterstadt seek word of their sons."_ The Reeve adds 25 credits each.
2. **Personal.** A PC's sibling or friend signed on.
3. **Thread (SP-PBS-00).** Perrender companies and their charters, with Sergeant Ruwen Hask as a contact.

- **Decline path:** the recruits march east. A year later one comes home with an Iuz brand and a story.

**Short slot (3 hours):** start the students at the camp.

## Running Notes

- **Clock:** the handover is one week after the muster. Each day of delay is a day closer.

    - **Camp alert:** Lax → Wary (questions asked) → Alert (letters found missing).
    - Stahl turns if shown F3: he values his charter and his neck.
    - Mirek flees toward the orcs if exposed.
- **Nudges:** (1) a recruit sings a homesick song; (2) the clerk's guilty face; (3) orc drums in the reeds.

## Climax

- **Set piece:** the drill camp at dawn: reed huts, a palisade, a boat landing, and Mirek's orc escort on the drove road.
- **Choices:**
    - **Break the camp** by force.
    - **Expose the contract:** turn Stahl, and take the papers to Schwartzenbruin (a political ending; Jürgen of Traft as a possible ally).
    - **Buy out the contracts:** 20 gp per recruit, or credits pledged by the students.

## Concluding the Adventure

- **A — all home:** 25 credits plus 10 extra credit each; Stahl's company disgraced or reformed; the families' gratitude.
- **B — partial:** some recruits lost, or the contract stands but the handover fails.
- **C — retreat:** the recruits march east, and Ritterstadt blames the Reeve.

# SEED-13 — The Hermit's Will

**APL 12 (levels 11–13) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

_Tier 3 framing: the race is open to the University's graduate fellow teams, or to the original students years later._

**Pitch.** A dead hermit leaves his hut "to the first University student who reaches it," and every fellow-team races for it. The will is a test set by a retired archmage, and the hut is far bigger on the inside than out.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know

- Old Brother Corwin lived alone on the Krähenkamm for thirty years, trading honey for salt.
- His will, filed with the Reeve, leaves the hut "to the first student of the University to set foot within, by fair means."
- Three fellow-teams set out the same morning.

### What's Really Happening

- "Corwin" is **Orsolan Vey**, a retired archmage. He is alive, and watches as the hut's grey cat.
- The hut opens onto a **seven-room demiplane**, each room testing a virtue: courage, wisdom, honesty, temperance, mercy, humility, and fellowship.
- The real test is the last: Vey gives the inheritance to whoever helped a rival along the way.

### If Nobody Interferes

1. **Kestra Lom**'s team wins the race through ruthlessness and fails the last room.
2. Vey shuts the hut and walks away to find another heir. The demiplane sits empty and unguarded.
3. A year later, hook 6's **Mother Fennick** moves into the abandoned hut.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)

Three teams race for the hut (Part 1). Inside, room after room tests virtue rather than strength (Part 2). Rivals cheat, help, or fall (Part 3). In the last room, the inheritance goes to whoever helped a rival, and the fellows decide what to do with it (Part 4).

## Facts the Players Must Learn

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|F1|The rooms test virtue, not strength.|Inscriptions over each door (Arcana DC 15).|The cat's behavior: it favors kind acts.|A rival who fails is shown why.|The rakshasa host tells them so.|
|F2|The hermit is alive, and is the cat.|_Detect magic_ or _true seeing_.|The cat avoids every trap.|Vey's handwriting in the will matches the margin notes (Investigation DC 16).|The cat speaks in the last room.|
|F3|Helping a rival is the true test.|The fellowship door's inscription.|A previous heir's journal in Room 4.|Vey's hints at a fire.|The last room's mirror shows only teams that helped.|

## Hooks

1. **The race (default).** The will is read in the Reeve's hall. Whoever arrives first inherits.
2. **Personal.** A wizard PC once corresponded with "Corwin" about bees, and has his letters.
3. **Thread.** If hook 6 or 22 was run, Vey knew both Mother Fennick and Ammon Vell.

- **Decline path:** see "If Nobody Interferes." Mother Fennick takes the hut, and hook 6 returns at a higher tier.

## Scene Plan

|1|The Will Is Read|Meet the rivals and the cat|15 min|
|2|The Race up the Krähenkamm|Sabotage, the shortcut, a rival in trouble|35 min|

|4|Room 4: The Mirror (Humility)|The party's own reflections|35 min|
|5|Climax: The Last Room (Fellowship)|The stone golem door and the inheritance|40 min|
|6|Conclusion|The heir chosen|15 min|

**Short slot (3 hours):** play 3 rooms plus the last.

## Running Notes

- **Race clock:** whoever enters first starts first, but the rooms can be solved in parallel by different teams, and rivals can be met in shared rooms.
- **Reaction tools:**
    - Rivals react to help or hindrance, tracked on a three-step scale: grudge, neutral, debt.
    - Rooms reset if failed, but cost something each time (a spell slot, a hit die, an hour).
- **Rival escalation (Kestra):** compete → sabotage → steal → abandon a teammate.
- **Nudges:**
    1. The cat sits beside a struggling rival.
    2. An inscription reads "No one enters the last room alone."
    3. A rival calls for help.

## Climax

- **Set piece:** a round library with seven doors and a hearth. The **stone golem** door opens only for a team escorting a rival.
- **Choices:**
    - Take the inheritance: the hut's demiplane, and Vey as mentor.
    - Share it with a rival team: a co-held hut, and friends.
    - Refuse it. Vey counts that as passing too, and offers a lesser gift.

## Concluding the Adventure

- **A — Heir by fellowship:** the hut is theirs, a portable demiplane retreat, and Vey's tutelage.
- **B — Shared:** joint stewardship, and the rival team becomes allies.
- **C — Failed the last room:** Vey leaves. A lesser prize (one spellbook) and a lesson.
- **Credits:** 30 for completing the race report. Extra credit: +10 for a demiplane survey for the Department.

# SEED-14 — Grey Wool

**APL 3 (levels 2–4) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> _"The water went sweet, then the wool went grey."_ — a Yati herder, to the Dean's secretary

**Pitch.** Yati sheep are turning grey and listless, and the herders blame the University. The Dean's office sends students to disprove it, and the students find the herders are right.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know

- Flocks watering at the Hollin Springs have grey, brittle fleece. Lambs are born weak.
- The herders say "the University's smoke" did it. The University has no works upstream, so far as anyone admits.
- **Gran Ailidh**, the eldest herder, has sent a formal complaint, and the clans are talking about closing their passes to University field teams.

### What's Really Happening

- In 568 CY, **Dean Haddon** (since retired to the City of Greyhawk) had a failed alchemy program's waste buried in a cave above the springs to avoid an inspection.
- The barrels have rotted. The seep has bred **gray oozes** and an **ochre jelly**, and turned the rats strange.
- **Oswald Pike**, the Dean's secretary, knows. He arranged the original burial as a junior clerk. He wants a report that says "pasture blight."
- The crates in the cave carry a University seal.

### If Nobody Interferes

1. Week 1: more sheep sicken. A herder's child drinks from the spring and falls ill.
2. Week 2: the Yati close the passes to University field teams.
3. Month 2: the oozes spread downstream into the springs themselves, and the pasture is lost for a decade.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)

The students are sent to "disprove the accusation." They meet angry herders and sick flocks and trace the bad water uphill. They find a sealed cave with broken barrels, oozes, and University seals. Pike offers extra credit for a convenient report. They choose between the truth, the bribe, and a quiet fix.

|F1|The water is poisoned from upstream.|Taste-test, slick sheen (Medicine DC 12).|Dead minnows only below a certain rock.|The sick flocks all drink at the same spring.|A dead rat glowing faintly at the spring.|

|F3|The University buried it.|The seal on the crates.|The 568 requisition in the Dean's archive (Investigation DC 13).|Pike's nervous bribe.|A crate stenciled "DEPT. ALCH. — HADDON."|

## Hooks

1. **Job Board (default).** _"The Dean's office requires a field assessment of pasture disease among the Yati flocks."_ 15 credits each.
2. **Personal.** A Yati PC's family flock is among the sick.
3. **Thread.** If SEED-11 has been run, the Lennaghs or Corrishes ask the students by name.

- **Decline path:** another team files "pasture blight." The passes close, and the field campus loses its Yati guides for a year.

## Background

### What the Locals Know

- Folklore students are welcome: the bride's father wants the old ways written down.

### What's Really Happening

- **Lord Thistleby**, a fey noble of the hill, comes once in a century to test whether mortals still keep faith with the old bargain.
- His rules: salt before speech, the first cup to the guest, no thanks given (thanks implies a debt), no names asked, and no iron at table.
- Each rule broken lets him "collect": a voice, a memory, a year, or the couple's luck.
- If enough rules break, his retinue comes down from the fairy ring at midnight to take what is owed.

### If Nobody Interferes

1. **The feast:** a cousin thanks the guest and loses his voice.
2. **The dance:** someone asks his name. The bride's memory of her groom fades.
3. **Midnight:** the retinue arrives, and the Corrish luck is taken for a hundred years.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)

The students arrive as guests and take field notes on the customs (1). The guest arrives, and the rules turn out to be real (2). A drunk cousin breaks one, and the collecting begins (3). ## Background

### What the Locals Know

- Six years after the Old One's return, Iuz's raiders probe the Vesve, and Highfolk's elves fight a quiet war in the trees.

### What's Really Happening
