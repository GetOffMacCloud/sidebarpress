# SEED-09 — The Dead Weather Tower

**APL 3 (levels 2–4) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> *"Wind SW, steady. Cloud low. The birds have taken the roof."* — last entry in the Ridge Station log

**Pitch.** The University's ridge-top weather station has stopped reporting, and its graduate keeper is missing. The students find him alive, trapped by nesting griffons. Then they find out the Bursar has put a price on the eggs.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- Ridge Station is a three-story stone tower with a brass wind-vane, a rain-gauge, and a signal mirror. It has been silent for nine days.
- Its keeper, **Pell Avery**, is a cheerful graduate student who "talks to birds."
- Drovers saw great winged shapes over the ridge.
- A dead mule lies on the switchback path.

### What's Really Happening
- **Griffons** (2) have nested on the tower's flat roof, among the instruments. There are three eggs.
- Avery is barricaded inside, rationing biscuits and writing a thesis on the pair. He doesn't want them harmed.
- The **Bursar** *(GU roster)* has quietly offered 50 credits for the eggs, which sell to Perrender falconers for a fortune.
- **Sava Krell**, an egg-hunter who heard about the bounty, is two hours behind the students with hired muscle.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. Day 10: Avery's food runs out. He tries the stair and is mauled.
2. Day 11: Sava's crew smokes the roof and takes the eggs, killing one griffon.
3. The surviving griffon savages drovers on the ridge path for a season.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The students climb to the silent tower past a dead mule and a **brown bear** feeding on it. They find the tower's door barred and the roof alive with griffons. They reach Avery without provoking the birds and learn about the eggs and the bounty. Sava Krell arrives with smoke-pots and crossbows. The students decide whether the eggs become credits, the nest becomes a fight, or the tower becomes a griffon roost with a weather station on it.

2. **Personal.** A PC's friend or classmate is Avery's lab partner, holding his unfinished thesis.
3. **Thread.** If SEED-21 has been run, Examiner Grenwald wants to see the griffons himself.
- **Decline path:** another team goes. Avery is brought back injured, one griffon is dead, and the drovers' road is closed by attacks.

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** Sava arrives 2 hours after the students reach the tower (or at dusk).
- **Reaction tools:** griffon threat zones: no reaction past 60 feet, a warning dive at 30 feet, attacks on the roof. If one griffon falls, the other fights to the death over the eggs. Sava's crew smokes the roof, then climbs with ropes, then shoots the griffons. If Sava falls, her crew flees.
- **Nudges:** (1) The mirror flashes. (2) Avery's voice. (3) A griffon drops a mule bone at the students' feet.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** a three-story tower. The roof has instruments, the nest, and a 60-foot drop. A smoke-pot fills a floor in 2 rounds.
- **Choices:**
  - Take the eggs: credits, a ruined thesis, an enemy in Avery, and a griffon problem.
  - Drive off Sava and leave the nest: no bounty, and Avery's lasting gratitude.
  - Relocate the nest to the crag with Avery's help (Animal Handling DC 15 for 3 successes). This is the best ending: a griffon-friendly station.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — full success:** Avery rescued, the nest saved or moved, and the station back in service.
- **B — partial:** Avery rescued but the eggs sold or broken. The ridge path stays dangerous.
- **C — retreat or defeat:** the students fall back; Sava takes the eggs or Avery is badly hurt.
- **Credits:** 20 base each. The Bursar's egg bounty pays 50 total if delivered (the vault cap still applies). +5 each for the restored instruments, +5 each for a published share of Avery's thesis.

## Key NPCs
**Pell Avery**, graduate weather-keeper. *Deity:* [Velnius] (a canon weather god; verify at build). *Wants:* the birds safe and his thesis finished. *Knows:* the griffons' routine, the tower's layout. *Reacts:* cheerful → stubborn → will put himself between the eggs and a crossbow.

**Sava Krell**, egg-hunter. *Deity:* [Zilchus]. *Wants:* 50 credits' worth of eggs, plus a Perrender falconer's price. *Knows:* the bounty slip, smoke tactics. *Reacts:* bargain (offers a split) → smoke → shoot → flee at half her crew.

**The Bursar** *(GU roster)*, offstage until the end.
