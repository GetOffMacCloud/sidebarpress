# SP-PBS-04 — The Eclipse Circle

**APL 4 (levels 3–5) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> _"Nine stand watching, one stands blind; shut the eye when the sun goes dim."_ — Yati counting-rhyme, sung over the shearing

**Note on the code:** SP-PBS-04 is provisional. Use the next free code in `10-Adventures`.
## Introduction

The University's astronomers want to watch a total eclipse of the sun from a Flan stone circle in the Clatspur foothills. They hire students to escort the expedition, carry the instruments and record the observations. The circle turns out to be a calendar and a door. For as long as the sun is dark, a stair beneath it leads down to the threshold of the Dawn Hold, a sealed Ur-Flanne hold under the Mounds of Dawn on the far side of Lake Quag. The man who built the expedition's great lens knows this, because he was sent to find it.

**Boxed text:** read it as written or paraphrase it, but keep every clue. **Conventions:** creature counts in parentheses are the scaling dial. Stats are 2014 5e block names, collected in the Bestiary appendix. **ESSENTIAL CLUE** marks facts the players must not miss.

## Background

### What the Locals Know

- The Yati call the ring on Brennock Ridge **the Watchers**: nine standing stones and a tenth, lying flat, called the Threshold.
- One of the nine has a hole bored clean through it at head height. Shepherds call it **the Eye** and won't graze sheep where its shadow falls.
- Yati grandmothers say the stones "keep the count of the sun." Whenever the sun goes dark, the Brannagh clan climbs up and hangs a fresh hide over the Eye. Nobody under fifty remembers why.
- The University's notice board says the eclipse falls on **Goodmonth 14, 576 CY**, a little before noon. Ritterstadt is lending tents and mules.

### What's Really Happening

- The circle is a **Flan eclipse calendar** some 4,000 years old. The notches on the stones' faces count the years between dark suns, and the next notch due is this one.
- It is also a **door**. At totality the sun's corona shines through the Eye and lays a thin bar of light across the Threshold. While that bar lies there, the slab is no longer stone. It is the top step of **the Warden's Stair**, which drops into the Hall of Lintels, the sealed outer gate of **the Dawn Hold**. That hold lies under the Mounds of Dawn in western Perrenland, on the far side of Lake Quag, so the door is a shortcut across the whole country in forty steps.
- **Canon basis:** the Mounds of Dawn have hills that look man-made, haunted Ur-Flanne ruins along the Vestflow and lost tombs near Lake Quag, and the prefect of Exag has a standing reward for investigating them (FtA; LGG pp.85, 146). The name "Dawn Hold" is a house placeholder. Exag itself is a living Perrender city and plays no part here.
- The Flan built the door so the Warden's line could go down and check the seal on whatever lies below. The Wardens died out long ago. Their descendants, the Brannaghs, kept only the habit: cover the Eye, keep the door shut.
- Totality on the ridge lasts **30 rounds (5 minutes)**. When it ends, the stair is stone again, and anyone still on it is shut in until the next dark sun, eleven years away.

### The Villain's Scheme

**Meister Hendrik Saal**, a lens-grinder from Schwartzenbruin, built the expedition's "great glass" on the University's commission. He is a sworn servant of **Iuz**. In the six years since the Old One was freed (570 CY), Iuz's agents have been combing Flan ruins from the Mounds of Dawn to the Clatspurs for the Dawn Hold. Iuz wants a precedent. The Flan remembered the **Horned King**, who conquered the north c. −1200 CY, as ageless. If that record can be made to name an "Old One" who ruled before Iuz, it strengthens his claim to divinity.

If nobody interferes:

1. **Goodmonth 12.** Saal travels with the expedition and pays a little extra to keep his crate off the University's mules. Two hired Perrender swords, **Lutz** and **Marika**, carry it. They don't know whom they serve.
2. **Night of the 12th.** Saal sends Marika to pull up the Brannaghs' hide-pegs and burn the hide they stored in the lee of the Eye.
3. **The 13th.** He sets the tripod up in plain view, rehearses the observation with the astronomer, and quietly sights the true mounting point on the Eye.
4. **The 14th, first contact.** He moves the lens from the tripod to a bracket hammered into the Eye. The bracket is a holed brass disc made to hold the **Night Lens**, a smoked-crystal key he was given in Dorakaa.
5. **Totality.** The door opens. Saal goes down with Lutz and two bound **shadows**.
6. **After totality.** As long as the Night Lens sits in the Eye, the door stays open after the sun returns. Saal has 30 minutes to make a rubbing of the **Tally of the Horned King** in the Hall of Lintels and come back up. Each 10 minutes the door is held open, something comes up the stair (see the Door Clock).
7. Saal walks out, pockets the lens, and heads north toward the Iuz border with the rubbing. The door closes. The Hall's warden is awake now, and the shadows he loosed stay on the ridge.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)

Students escort the University's eclipse expedition to the Watchers (Part 1). On the ridge the Yati keeper of the circle warns them off, and someone sabotages her clan's ritual (Part 2). During the day of waiting, the students can learn that the circle is a calendar and a door, and that the lens-grinder's great glass is a key (Part 3). At totality the door opens. They can stop Saal, follow him down, or close the door on him (Part 4). The ending depends on who comes back up the stair, and what with.

## Hooks

1. **The Job Board (default).** _"Wanted: four to six students of sound body to escort the Department of Natural Philosophy's eclipse expedition to the Brennock Ridge. Duties: portage, camp, guard, record. 40 credits each; extra credit for observations accepted into the Department's report."_ Pays 40 credits each, with up to 10 extra credit per student for a usable record.
2. **Personal: the Yati student.** A PC with Flan or Yati roots receives a letter from a great-aunt: _"If you're going up with the stargazers, take a hide."_ The PC does not need to be a Brannagh.
3. **The Survey thread (if SP hook 1 has been run).** Flan Antiquities asks for rubbings of every carving on the stones. The pulled stakes from "The Dawn Hold Survey" all pointed at this ridge.
4. **The Dwarf's Question (if hook 2 has been run).** The peddler's rune-key has an eye-shaped bow, and Hogar Irongrip, Ritterstadt's master smith and an exile of Clan Irongrip of Bitter Pass, wants to know whether "the Watchers" still stand.

**Decline path.** If the students won't go, the expedition leaves without them. On Goodmonth 15 a Yati boy runs into Ritterstadt: the astronomer is in shock, the porters are dead, and there are shadows on the ridge in daylight. Run Part 4's aftermath as a rescue. Saal escaped with the rubbing (Conclusion E).

## Running the Adventure

### Laws of the Watchers

- **The Eye's shadow.** Animals won't stand in it. Undead won't leave it once totality has begun.
- **Totality.** 30 rounds. Inside the ring it is dark as deep dusk. Stars show, and the corona gives dim light only.
- **The door.** It opens on round 1 of totality and closes at the end of round 30 unless the Night Lens is in the Eye.
- **Shut the Eye.** Covering it with a hide, a cloak or a body closes the door at the end of the current round. Removing the Night Lens during totality does not close the door, since the sun still does that. Removing it afterward closes the door at once.

### The Door Clock (after totality, only while the Night Lens is in the Eye)

- **+10 minutes:** cold air pours up the stair. Shadows already loosed become bold (they act in daylight inside the ring).
- **+20 minutes:** **Warden Brann the Elder** (a **wight**) climbs to the Hall's upper landing, followed by two **Flan dead** (**zombies**) with copper spears.
- **+30 minutes:** the warden reaches the top step and comes out. The Flan dead follow. They attack anyone who isn't carrying a hide or a Yati token.
- **+40 minutes and after:** one **shadow** (1) climbs out every 10 minutes until the lens is pulled.

### Hendrik Saal's camp — alert states

- **Lax:** Saal is working. Lutz and Marika play dice by the crate.
- **Wary** (triggered by questions about the lens or the hide, or someone seen near the crate): the crate is always guarded, Saal stays at the tripod, and Marika shadows the students.
- **Alert** (triggered by the crate being searched, the notebook stolen, or Marika caught): Saal moves early. He mounts the lens at first contact under cover of "a better angle." Lutz guards the Eye's foot with a crossbow, and Marika tries to burn the students' hides.

**Scaled response.** Against a weak party, Saal hides behind the expedition and the astronomer's authority. Against a capable party, he tries to buy them off with a share of the Hall. Against an overwhelming party, he lets his men take the fall and slips down the stair alone at totality.

**Survivors.** If Marika is captured before the 14th, Saal guesses the students know. He shifts to Alert and arranges for the astronomer to send them down the ridge to fetch "forgotten" plates on the morning of the 14th. They can refuse.

### Factions (one line each)

- **The Expedition** (the astronomer, two clerks, four porters): wants a clean observation and a published paper. It doesn't know anything is wrong.
- **The Brannagh clan** (Nessa, Cael, a dozen shepherds below the ridge): wants the Eye covered and the students gone. It distrusts the University, and SP-PBS-02's events may have earned that.
- **Saal's party** (Saal, Lutz, Marika, two bound shadows in the crate's false bottom): wants the door open and the Tally copied. Marika is loyal to the money, Lutz to Marika.

## Part 1 — Ritterstadt and the Road

### Scene 1: The Expedition Musters

_Purpose:_ meet the cast and give everyone a job. Plant F2.

> _Mules stamp in the yard behind the Long Dormitory, loaded with tents, tripods and crates stenciled DEPT. NAT. PHIL. A tall crate with brass corners stands apart from the rest, roped twice and guarded by two Perrender swords who look bored. A thin man in a lens-maker's leather apron is checking the knots himself._

The **astronomer** (cast from the GU faculty roster; see Open Items) briefs the students: duties, pay, and the hour of the eclipse. **Saal** is courteous, precise and slightly too grateful. **Lutz** and **Marika** keep close to the crate.

**Development:** if a student offers to carry the brass-cornered crate, Saal politely refuses: "Glass, you understand." That marks him as worth watching (F2).

### Scene 2: The Foothill Road (compress to one sentence unless the table wants travel)

A long day's climb up the Brennock track. **Cael Brannagh**, 15, joins them at the last farm as an unasked-for guide, sent by his grandmother "to see what the stargazers want." If you need an encounter, use one from the menu below, once only.

**Road menu (once only each):**

- A Yati shepherd driving a flock _away_ from the ridge, two days early.
- Pulled survey stakes in a ditch, all pointing the same way (thread to hook 1).
- A dead sheep, bloodless and unmarked, lying in a perfect line toward the ridge. A shadow tested its bonds the night before.

## Part 2 — The Watchers

### Scene 3: The Keeper of the Count

_Purpose:_ the circle's custodian, the ritual, and the first sabotage. Routes to F1 and F3.

**Nessa Brannagh** meets the expedition at the ring's edge at dusk with a hide over her shoulder and three grandsons behind her. She does not forbid the camp. She lays down terms: no one in the ring on the dark-sun day, and the Eye stays covered.

The astronomer refuses, because the observation needs the ring. Nessa looks to the students. This is their first choice: broker a compromise, side with the University, or side with the Yati.

**Development:**

- **Night of the 12th:** Marika steals into the Eye's lee, pulls the pegs and burns the hide. At dawn there is a black smear, a smell of singed hair, and a single Perrender boot-print in the ash (DC 12 Survival to read it; Cael spots it if no one else does).
- If the students guard the hide, they catch Marika at it. She runs, fights only if cornered, and surrenders at half hit points. She talks for her life and for Lutz's.
- **Nessa's trust** rises if the students guard the hide, return it, side with her, or show a Yati token. At trust she tells F1 and F3 plainly: "The sun goes out and the old stair wakes. My grandmother's grandmother's people went down it. Not one of their line has since."

## Part 3 — The Day of Waiting

### Scene 4: The Watchers — site key

The ring is 60 feet across on a bald ridge-top, with a sheer drop on the north side and a long grass slope down to the camp on the south.

**W1. THE CAMP (south slope)**

> _Tents in two neat rows below the ridge, the University pennant snapping on a spear. A cookfire smokes; the mules are picketed upwind. Above you, nine grey stones stand against the sky like men waiting for a coach._

The expedition's base. The astronomer's tent holds the eclipse tables. The brass-cornered crate is in Saal's tent. **Development:** at Wary, Lutz sleeps across the tent's door.

**W2–W9. THE WATCHERS (eight standing stones)** Each stone is 9–11 feet of lichened granite. Its inner face carries vertical notches in groups, with a sun-sign cut beside every group. Nothing of value. Together they form the calendar (see W10). **Development:** a student who maps the notches against the University's tables (DC 12 Intelligence with the astronomer's help, or DC 15 alone) finds that each group marks one dark sun, and that the last group ends with an empty sun-sign cut in advance: this one. **(Route toward F1.)**

**W11. THE EYE**

> _The tallest stone leans a little east. Through it, at the height of a man's eyes, runs a hole as round as a dinner plate and smooth as glass. Through the hole you can see a slice of the eastern sky and nothing else._

The hole is aligned to the sun's position at this eclipse's totality. Fresh scratches around the hole and a new iron pin hammered into the stone show where Saal's bracket will go (DC 13 Investigation, or automatic after the night of the 13th). **Development:** if the pin is pulled, Saal needs 2 rounds and a hammer to reseat it at first contact. He will try anyway.

**W10. THE THRESHOLD**

> _A tenth stone lies flat in the ring's center, long as a coffin and twice as wide. Its upper face is worn smooth by weather. At its eastern end, a single thin groove runs from edge to edge, straight as a rule._

The groove is where the corona's bar of light will fall. The underside, reachable by digging at the north edge for an hour, carries the **ESSENTIAL CLUE** carving: a stair descending beneath a black sun, nine standing figures around it, and a tenth figure holding a hide over a holed stone while the stair is shown shut (F1, F3). **Development:** digging under the Threshold in sight of the Brannaghs ends Nessa's trust unless she has already shared the secret.

### Scene 5: Saal at Work

_Purpose:_ routes to F2 and F4. Run this as an open scene with time pressure. Every two hours of in-game time the students spend on something else, Saal gets a step further along.

**What can be found:**

- **The crate:** the lens on a velvet bed; under a false bottom, a coffin-sized cavity with chalk wards drawn on it and two cold spots (the bound **shadows**). Also the brass bracket, and the notebook (Handout 1).
- **Saal's coat:** the Iuz token, and Handout 2, his cipher letter (DC 15 Intelligence to break it, or a day with a Department cryptographer afterward).
- **Lutz**, if questioned over drink or dice: F2's Lutz quote. He also says Marika wants out.

**Saal's escalation script:** _deny_ ("Superstition. The Department has my contract.") → _redirect_ (tells the astronomer the students are tampering with University property, and the astronomer threatens to dock their credits) → _infiltrate_ (sends Marika to burn the remaining hides, and to slip the students a sleeping draught in their stew on the 13th, DC 12 Constitution) → _betray_ (at totality, he orders Lutz to shoot whoever is nearest the Eye).

## Part 4 — The Dark Sun (Climax)

### Scene 6: Totality

_Purpose:_ the set piece. Everything learned so far now matters.

**First contact (the 10th hour):** the moon's bite appears. Shadows sharpen. Saal moves the lens to the Eye if he can. Frost begins to form on the Threshold's groove (last resort for F1).

> _The light goes thin and strange, as if the day had been poured through a sieve. Birds stop. The air on the ridge turns cold all at once. Then the last bead of sun winks out, and in the black sky Luna's disc hangs in an aquamarine halo where the sun was. A bar of pale light runs through the Eye and lies across the flat stone — and where it touches, the stone is not there. Stairs go down._

**Play:** in round order, Saal and Lutz act first, then the shadows, then everyone else. The door is open from round 1 to round 30.

**Creatures (APL 4):** **Hendrik Saal** (cult fanatic) (1), at the Eye; **Lutz** (thug) (1), with a heavy crossbow at the Eye's foot; **Marika** (bandit captain, or bandit at APL 3) (1), unless already dealt with; **shadows** (2), released from the crate on round 1. Scaling: APL 3, remove Marika and one shadow. APL 5, add a third shadow and give Saal a scout's longbow-skill hireling. **Tactics:** Saal goes down the stair on round 2 with one shadow and leaves Lutz to hold the Eye. If the students reach the Eye, Lutz tries to keep them off it rather than kill. The shadows go for whoever carries light or a hide. If Saal sees the Eye being covered, he comes back up the stair at once, since the door closing would trap him below. **Development (the three main choices):**

- **Shut the Eye during totality, with Saal below:** the door closes at the end of the round. Saal is trapped in the Hall of Lintels for eleven years, or until something in the Dawn Hold finds him (Conclusion B).
- **Follow him down:** go to Scene 7. The students have until round 30, or longer if the lens stays in the Eye, at the cost of the Door Clock.
- **Pull the lens and let totality run out:** the door closes at round 30. Saal comes up in time only if he has noticed (Wisdom check at round 25, DC 12).

### Scene 7: The Warden's Stair and the Hall of Lintels

Use this only if the students go down. The key is short on purpose: the clock is the encounter.

**H1. THE WARDEN'S STAIR**

> _Steps cut from living rock spiral down around a hollow core. The air smells of cold iron and old smoke. Far below, a light that is not torchlight shows grey._

Forty steps, taking 2 rounds at a hurry. Each step has a sun-notch cut into it, and the steps count backward. Nothing of value.

**H2. THE HALL OF LINTELS**

> _A long hall, its ceiling held up by nine stone lintels, each carved with a face. Niches line the walls; in each, a body in copper torc and cloak sits upright as if waiting to be called. At the far end, a great door of black stone is sealed with three bands of hammered copper. Before it stands a waist-high pillar covered in tiny cut marks._

The **Tally of the Horned King**: the pillar records, in Flan count-marks and pictographs, the coming of the Horned King from the north, the fall of three Flan kingdoms, and the sealing of the Dawn Hold over his tomb and over the thing his line bound (placeholder: "the Bound One"; see SEED-20). The knock behind the black door is the Bound One. **ESSENTIAL CLUE (sequel):** the Horned King was an Ur-Flanne demon-binder, mortal and long dead, and nothing about him connects to Iuz. The precedent Saal was sent for does not exist, so his master will want the Tally destroyed rather than copied. **Creatures:** **Warden Brann the Elder** (wight) (1), seated in the central niche, dormant unless the Door Clock reaches +20 minutes or the Tally is touched by someone without a hide or Yati token; **Flan dead** (zombies) (2–4), in niches, wake with the warden. **Tactics:** the warden speaks Old Flan and will parley with anyone carrying a hide ("Who keeps the count?"). He attacks Saal on sight. **Treasure:** copper torcs (9, 25 gp each to Antiquities, though the Brannaghs consider them grave-goods); the **Sun-Count Disc**, a copper calendar disc on the warden's breast that marks every future dark sun and, on its back, the next door (a plot key; give it a `50-Items` note). Saal's rubbing, if he made one. **Development:** the black door does not open in this adventure. Touching its copper bands brings a slow knock from the other side, three times. Then nothing. Write it down and use it later.

## Concluding the Adventure

**A — The Count Kept (full success).** The Eye was covered or the lens pulled, Saal is captured or dead, and no rubbing leaves the ridge. Nessa gives the students a Brannagh hide-token, which earns them Yati hospitality in later adventures. The Department gets its observation: 40 credits plus up to 10 extra credit each.

**B — Shut In.** Saal was sealed below. The students carry his notebook and letter to the University. The Department quietly asks them to keep it out of the report. The Brannaghs set a watch for the next dark sun, in eleven years.

**C — Down and Back.** The students went down, saw the Tally and came back. Whether they report the Tally's truth (the Horned King was no ageless "Old One," and has nothing to do with Iuz) to the University, Antiquities or nobody is a decision for the sequel.

**D — The Warden Walks (partial).** The Door Clock passed +30 minutes. The warden and the Flan dead are loose on the ridge. If they are defeated or parleyed with (a hide, a Yati PC, Nessa's word), the students get B or C. If not, the Brannaghs lose sheep and men until the University sends someone, possibly the same students.

**E — Saal Escapes (retreat/defeat).** The rubbing goes north. Within a season, Iuz's agents arrive in the Mounds of Dawn with better maps. Loosed shadows haunt the Brennock Ridge. Run "The Old One's Road" (hook 3) as the direct sequel.

**Credits.** Base 40 per student for completing the escort. Extra credit (up to 10 each): a usable record of the eclipse (+5), notch-calendar matched to the tables (+3), and the Tally recorded and returned to the Department (+2). Do not exceed the 600-credit cap.

### Continuing the Adventure

- **The Old One's Road** (hook 3): Iuz's agents follow the buried causeway. If Saal escaped, they know where to dig.
- **The Brass Pilgrim** (hook 20): the automaton arrives in Ritterstadt and recognizes the Sun-Count Disc.
- **Silvervein's Key** (hook 2): the rune-key's eye-shaped bow matches the Eye's bore.
- **The Dawn Hold Survey** (hook 1): someone keeps pulling the survey stakes in the Mounds. Now the students know where the road leads.
- **The knock** behind the black door.

**Recurrence.** Saal, if alive and free, comes back as Iuz's man in Ritterstadt with a new face of respectability. Marika, if spared, can become a reluctant informant.

**Previous play.** If an earlier group already ran this adventure, Saal escaped (E), and the shadows on the ridge are the first rumor the new students hear.

## NPC Dossiers (summary; full notes in `30-NPCs`)

**Hendrik Saal**, Meister Lens-Grinder of Schwartzenbruin

- _Loyalty:_ Iuz, through a handler in Dorakaa. _Deity:_ {Zilchus} in public, [Iuz] in truth.
- _Appearance:_ thin and stooped. His fingertips are stained grey from polishing compound, and he keeps wiping them.
- _Personality:_ courteous and exact. _Quirk:_ he counts under his breath.
- _Wants:_ the rubbing, and promotion out of fieldwork. _Fears:_ coming back to Dorakaa empty-handed.
- _Knows:_ the door, the Night Lens, the Door Clock (roughly), and that the Brannaghs' hide matters.
- _Escalation:_ deny → redirect → infiltrate → betray (Scene 5).
- _Stats:_ cult fanatic; the Night Lens is a wondrous item with no combat use (`50-Items`).

**Nessa Brannagh**, Keeper of the Count

- _Loyalty:_ the Brannagh clan. _Deity:_ [Beory].
- _Appearance:_ small, white-braided, with a tattooed sun-count on her left forearm.
- _Wants:_ the Eye covered and the University gone. _Fears:_ being the Brannagh who failed the count.
- _Knows:_ F1 and F3 in full, the counting-rhyme, and that "the warden waits for the hide."
- _Reaction:_ refuses → bargains (the students guard the hide) → trusts (tells all) → if betrayed, sends the clan to drive the expedition off the ridge.
- _Stats:_ commoner (druid if you want her to fight).

**Cael Brannagh**, her grandson, 15

- _Wants:_ to see a University. _Knows:_ the rhyme, the boot-print, the shepherds' gossip.
