# SEED-24 — The Escaped Specimen

**APL 3 (levels 2–4) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

**Pitch.** An owlbear cub has escaped the University bestiary pens, and Ritterstadt's farmers are taking down their pitchforks. The cub is hiding in a Yati girl's barn, and its mother, sold to the University as "dead," is coming to get it.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- The bestiary keeper bought an owlbear cub from the trapper Kurz last month. The cub got out two nights ago by squeezing through a bent bar.
- Two chickens and a ewe are dead on the Maelduin farm road. Farmer Odo Hasler is raising a posse.
- Kurz swears the cub's mother died in his snare.

### What's Really Happening
- The ewe and chickens were killed by the **mother owlbear**, who is very much alive. She broke out of Kurz's wagon cage on the road from Kottsmort four days ago.
- The cub is in the hayloft of the Maelduin barn. Ina Maelduin, 11, found it, named it "Bramble," and feeds it eggs.
- Kurz sold the University a cub "orphaned" by a dead mother, then tried to sell the live mother to a menagerie in Schwartzenbruin. If either fact comes out, he loses his license and his fee.
- The mother is following the cub's scent toward town and will reach the Maelduin farm around midnight on the second night.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. **Day 1:** the posse searches the woodlots. Ina moves the cub to the hayloft.
2. **Night 1:** the mother tears through the Hasler fence and kills a plough ox.
3. **Day 2:** Kurz quietly tracks the mother, meaning to kill her and hide the evidence.
4. **Night 2:** the mother finds the barn. The posse follows the noise. Someone dies: a farmer, Ina, or the cub.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The students take the bestiary keeper's commission and join a jumpy hunt (1). Tracks and gossip point to the Maelduin farm, and to a second, much larger set of prints (2). Winning Ina's trust shows them the cub and the trapper's lie (3). The mother, the posse, and Kurz converge on the barn at night (4). The students decide who walks away (5).

## Hooks
1. **Job Board (default).** *"REWARD. Juvenile owlbear, escaped. Return ALIVE to the Bestiary. Do not approach with dogs."* 15 credits each.
2. **Personal.** A druid or ranger PC is asked by the bestiary keeper because "it may listen to you."
3. **Thread (hooks 9, 14).** Herders already angry about Grey Wool now blame the University for loose monsters too.
- **Decline path:** the posse handles it. The mother kills two farmers and the cub is clubbed to death. The town demands the bestiary be closed.

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** night 1 is the fence (an ox dies), and night 2 is the barn. If the students move the cub, the mother follows the new scent and arrives 2 hours later.
- **Reaction tools:**
  - **Posse mood track** (0–5; at 5 they shoot first): +1 for each livestock death, −1 for each calm explanation backed with evidence.
  - Kurz escalates: deny → bribe (20 gp) → threaten → try to kill the mother at range.
  - The mother calms if the cub is between her and the students. She attacks anyone who stands between them.
- **Nudges:** (1) Ina's brothers giggling about "the owl in the loft"; (2) the keeper points out that cubs need a mother's warmth; (3) Odo arrives with torches.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** the Maelduin barnyard at midnight, with a burning lantern, a hay-filled loft, a pig wallow, and a split-rail fence. The posse closes in from the lane, Kurz from the treeline, and the mother through the back wall.
- **Choices:**
  - **Reunite** mother and cub and drive them into the hills (Animal Handling DC 13, with Ina's help giving advantage).
  - **Capture both** for the pens. This is hard and dangerous, and the keeper will pay double.
  - **Stand aside** and let the posse settle it.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — reunited and gone:** the farmers are calmed and Kurz is exposed (his license is pulled). 15 credits plus 5 extra credit each, and Ina's lifelong friendship (a Yati ally).
- **B — captured:** 30 credits each. The bestiary gains a breeding pair, and some of the town is uneasy.
- **C — the posse rules:** the cub or the mother dies. 5 credits for "assistance," and Ina never speaks to the University again.

## Key NPCs
**Ina Maelduin**, Yati farm girl, 11. [Beory]. *Wants:* to keep Bramble. *Knows:* the cub eats eggs and hates dogs, and "the big one" was at the fence. *Reacts:* lies, then hides the cub, then begs, then trusts anyone who doesn't lie back.

**Kurz**, trapper. [Obad-Hai, lapsed]. *Wants:* his fee kept and his lie buried. *Knows:* the mother is alive and angry. *Reacts:* deny → bribe → threaten → shoot. *Stats:* scout.

**Odo Hasler**, farmer and posse leader. [Kord]. *Wants:* his stock safe. *Knows:* nothing he's sure of. *Reacts:* the posse mood track. *Stats:* thug.

**The bestiary keeper** *(GU roster)*. *Wants:* the cub alive and the scandal small.
