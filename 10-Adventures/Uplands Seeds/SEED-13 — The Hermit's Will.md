# SEED-13 — The Hermit's Will

**APL 12 (levels 11–13) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

*Tier 3 framing: the race is open to the University's graduate fellow teams, or to the original students years later.*

**Pitch.** A dead hermit leaves his hut "to the first University student who reaches it," and every fellow-team races for it. The will is a test set by a retired archmage, and the hut is far bigger on the inside than out.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- Old Brother Corwin lived alone on the Krähenkamm for thirty years, trading honey for salt.
- His will, filed with the Reeve, leaves the hut "to the first student of the University to set foot within, by fair means."
- Three fellow-teams set out the same morning.

### What's Really Happening
- "Corwin" is **Orsolan Vey**, a retired archmage. He is alive, and watches as the hut's grey cat.
- The hut opens onto a **seven-room demiplane**, each room testing a virtue: courage, wisdom, honesty, temperance, mercy, humility, and fellowship.
- The real test is the last: Vey gives the inheritance to whoever helped a rival along the way.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. **Kestra Lom**'s team wins the race through ruthlessness and fails the last room.
2. Vey shuts the hut and walks away to find another heir. The demiplane sits empty and unguarded.
3. A year later, hook 6's **Mother Fennick** moves into the abandoned hut.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
Three teams race for the hut (Part 1). Inside, room after room tests virtue rather than strength (Part 2). Rivals cheat, help, or fall (Part 3). In the last room, the inheritance goes to whoever helped a rival, and the fellows decide what to do with it (Part 4).

## Hooks
1. **The race (default).** The will is read in the Reeve's hall. Whoever arrives first inherits.
2. **Personal.** A wizard PC once corresponded with "Corwin" about bees, and has his letters.
3. **Thread.** If hook 6 or 22 was run, Vey knew both Mother Fennick and Ammon Vell.
- **Decline path:** see "If Nobody Interferes." Mother Fennick takes the hut, and hook 6 returns at a higher tier.

## Running Notes
- **Race clock:** whoever enters first starts first, but the rooms can be solved in parallel by different teams, and rivals can be met in shared rooms.
- **Reaction tools:**
  - Rivals react to help or hindrance, tracked on a three-step scale: grudge, neutral, debt.
  - Rooms reset if failed, but cost something each time (a spell slot, a hit die, an hour).
- **Rival escalation (Kestra):** compete → sabotage → steal → abandon a teammate.
- **Nudges:**
  1. The cat sits beside a struggling rival.
  2. An inscription reads "No one enters the last room alone."
  3. A rival calls for help.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** a round library with seven doors and a hearth. The **stone golem** door opens only for a team escorting a rival.
- **Choices:**
  - Take the inheritance: the hut's demiplane, and Vey as mentor.
  - Share it with a rival team: a co-held hut, and friends.
  - Refuse it. Vey counts that as passing too, and offers a lesser gift.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — Heir by fellowship:** the hut is theirs, a portable demiplane retreat, and Vey's tutelage.
- **B — Shared:** joint stewardship, and the rival team becomes allies.
- **C — Failed the last room:** Vey leaves. A lesser prize (one spellbook) and a lesson.
- **Credits:** 30 for completing the race report. Extra credit: +10 for a demiplane survey for the Department.
