# SEED-04 — Counting Barrows

**APL 5 (levels 5–6) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

> *"Twelve they laid, and twelve they keep; count them twice and one will sleep."*
> — Yati grave-rhyme

**Pitch.** A history thesis needs an accurate count of the Twelve Howes, a Yati barrow field. Every count comes out different, because one of the mounds is the back of a burrowing beast, and the barrows it breaks open are letting the dead walk.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- The Twelve Howes are Flan barrows on a high meadow. The Yati of the valley leave milk there at Growfest.
- Shepherds say the field "breathes" in hot weather, and that a thirteenth mound comes and goes.
- A University thesis student, Liesl Marr, has counted 11, 12, 13 and 14 howes on four visits. Her tutor says she can't count.

### What's Really Happening
- A **bulette** has made the meadow its hunting ground. Its back, crusted with turf, lies still under the soil like a mound. It surfaces, settles, and moves.
- Its tunnels have breached three howes. Two Flan chieftains rose as **wights** with **zombie** retinues and now walk the field at night.
- The bulette has a breeding hollow under the field, holding one egg.
- The Yati grave-keeper knows the old rites of resealing, but no one has asked her.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. **Night 1:** the wights take a shepherd. He rises as a zombie.
2. **Night 3:** the wights march on the nearest steading.
3. **Week 2:** the egg hatches, and two bulettes hunt the valley.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The students help Liesl recount the Howes, and the numbers won't hold still (1). They find tunnels and a breached howe, and meet the grave-keeper (2). The first night brings the dead (3). Tracking the tunnels leads to the bulette's hollow under the field (4). They must kill the beast or lure it away, and then lay the dead and reseal the barrows (5).

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** each night the wights walk. Night 1 takes the shepherd, night 2 the steading's cattle, night 3 the steading.
- **Reaction tools:**
  - The bulette is drawn to vibration: heavy running or digging summons it in 1d4 rounds.
  - Wights retreat to their howes at dawn and recruit any corpse in reach.
  - A resealed howe ends its wight's walking until disturbed.
- **Tutor escalation:** scoffs → demands the count be 12 → arrives in person → flees on seeing a wight.
- **Nudges:** (1) a "mound" with a heartbeat; (2) the grave-keeper refuses milk to the University; (3) Liesl's count changes while she's watching.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** the breeding hollow under the meadow: loose soil, roots, the egg, and three tunnel mouths. The wights may follow the students down.
- **Choices:**
  - **Kill** the bulette and the egg.
  - **Lure it away** with a tremor trick (rhythmic stamping and a herd driven past) and take the egg to the University.
  - **Bring in the grave-keeper**, who can reseal the barrows during the fight. Each sealed howe drops one wight.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — field made safe:** the bulette is dead or gone, the wights are laid, and the howes are resealed. 30 credits plus 10 extra credit each. Liesl's thesis gets a true count.
- **B — partial:** the bulette is gone but a wight still walks, or the egg is lost. The valley moves its flocks.
- **C — retreat:** the steading burns, and the field is closed by the Reeve.

## Key NPCs
**Liesl Marr**, history student. [Delleb]. *Wants:* her thesis passed. *Knows:* four different counts, and Flan burial lore. *Reacts:* stubborn, then terrified, then brave.

**Old Morna Tíreach**, Yati grave-keeper. [Beory]. *Wants:* the University gone, the dead asleep. *Knows:* the resealing rite. *Reacts:* refuses → tests → teaches if shown respect.

**The tutor** *(GU roster)*. *Wants:* the count to be 12.
