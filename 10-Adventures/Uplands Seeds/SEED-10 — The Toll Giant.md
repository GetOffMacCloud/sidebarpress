# SEED-10 — The Toll Giant

**APL 7 (levels 6–8) · 4–6 characters · one 3–4 hour session**

**Pitch.** A hill giant has built a bridge over the Ridgeford gorge and charges a song per crossing. The merchants want him gone. He is a deserter from Iuz's recruiters, and they are coming to take him back.

---

## Background

### What the Locals Know
- The old ford washed out in spring. Three weeks ago a giant laid two pine trunks across the gorge and sat down beside them.
- He asks every traveler for a song: "a good one, mind." Bad singers are dangled over the edge.
- Kottsmort merchants pay the Reeve to have him "removed," and nobody else wants to try.

### What's Really Happening
- **Grumbold**, a **hill giant**, was pressed into Iuz's service at a hill-giant steading in the north. He deserted on the march south because he didn't like the killing.
- He knows a troop route through the Vesve's northern fringe and the name of the recruiter, a priest of Iuz named Vardaris.
- Vardaris is two days behind with **ogres** (3) and an **orc war chief**, carrying a chain-collar to bring Grumbold back as an example.
- Grumbold built the bridge because he likes company and songs. He has never hurt anyone.

### If Nobody Interferes
1. **Day 1:** Kottsmort hires bounty hunters. They fail and are dangled.
2. **Day 2:** Vardaris arrives at dusk. Grumbold fights and is collared.
3. **Day 3:** the ogres burn the bridge and a passing caravan. Vardaris leaves a skull on the post.

## Adventure Outline (under 100 words)
The students reach the bridge and must sing to cross (1). Grumbold opens up over a shared meal and offers what he knows for a hiding place (2). Signs of pursuit appear in the hills (3). Vardaris's band arrives at the bridge at dusk (4). The students decide Grumbold's fate, and what to do with an Iuz troop route (5).

## Running Notes
- **Clock:** Vardaris arrives at dusk on the second day, or the first if the students dawdle with the merchants.
- **Reaction tools:**
  - Vardaris parleys first and offers coin for Grumbold.
  - The ogres rout if Vardaris falls.
  - The war chief fights to the end.
  - Grumbold's morale: he fights beside friends and flees if betrayed.
- **Nudges:** (1) Grumbold asks for "a song about running away"; (2) smoke on the north ridge; (3) a horn at dusk.

## Climax
- **Set piece:** the pine-trunk bridge over a 60-foot gorge with the river below. The north bank is open ground; the south bank is Grumbold's camp.
- **Choices:**
  - **Defend** Grumbold and hide him in the hills.
  - **Hand him over:** the merchants are happy, and the ending is dark.
  - **Recruit** him to guard the field campus.

## Concluding the Adventure
- **A — defended:** 40 credits each, and Grumbold's map reaches the University and Highfolk (hook 16 gains an ally). 10 extra credit for the map.
- **B — handed over:** 40 credits. The bridge is clear. Grumbold's last song haunts the party (no mechanical cost).
- **C — retreat:** the bridge burns and the map is lost.
