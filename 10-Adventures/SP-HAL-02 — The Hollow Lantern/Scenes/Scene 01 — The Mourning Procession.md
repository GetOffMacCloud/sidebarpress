# Scene 01 — The Mourning Procession

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-02 — The Hollow Lantern/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 00 — Marrowmere at Dusk]] · Next → [[Scene 02 — Crossing the Mere]]

## Purpose
Make it personal. A coffin arrives with a PC's name on it, the guest list is whispered, and Pell breaks. The party should leave knowing the dead are collecting the living, and whose.
**Budget:** 45 minutes. Running long? Don't let the fight start: the specters set the coffin down and go.

## Arrival
> *Fog rolls up the street from the water, thick enough to swallow the candles one by one. Four veiled figures in grey walk out of it, carrying a black coffin on their shoulders. Their feet make no sound on the stones. They come straight up the middle of the street, past the barred doors, and stop in front of you. The brass plate on the lid has a name on it. It's one of yours.*

**To the table:** *"The bearers are waiting for you to move. Do you?"*

## Who Is Here
- **Four specters**, veiled as bridesmaids and pallbearers. *Want:* to deliver the coffin to the named person's doorstep. *React:* silent; hostile only if blocked or the lid is lifted early.
- **[[Gaffer Pell]]**, watching from the inn door. This is where he breaks if he hasn't already.
- The named PC: the ring-bearer by default, or the PC chosen for the *coffin has your name* subplot. If no PC is invited, the plate reads **Mirabel Pell**.

## Beats

### The delivery
The bearers set the coffin on the named person's doorstep (the inn, if the PCs are lodging there) and fade back into the fog. Inside, on a bed of wet lilies: an invitation ([[SP-HAL-02 — Handout — The Invitation]]) and a wedding favor, a sugared almond that tastes of river mud.

Anyone who touches the coffin makes a **Wisdom save DC 12**. On a failure they hear Ysolde's voice whispering the guest list: *"Pell, who carried the cups. Hale, who held my train. Marrow, my own blood. The fiddler. And you."* The four families who "left early," and the PC's name added last.

### Pell breaks
Hearing the list, or seeing the name, Pell comes out. *"I was the page. I carried the cups. I didn't drink because I was working."* Then the confession from Scene 00 if it hasn't come: the sluice wheel, both hands, smiling. He adds the second seed: *"That ring she wore. It was her mother's. She pried it out of the stone hand herself, the week before, and sold it. For the wine."* Standalone: he hands the ring over now.

### If they open it early, or block the street
The specters turn. Run the encounter. They don't pursue past the chapel yard's candle line.

**Terrain:** a fogged street, 10 ft visibility, barred doors on both sides; the chapel yard's candles count as dim light the specters avoid. · **Tactics:** specters pass through walls to flank; they go for whoever touched the coffin. · **DM direction:** this fight is a tax on impatience. Telegraph it: the bearers pause, and the fog goes still, before the lid comes off.

## Clues Available Here
- The guest list → the invitees are the four who fled and their blood
- Pell's confession → Ysolde poisoned the feast; the ring is Rowena's
- The sugared almond → the feast is real enough to taste

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** grey veils, a black lid, lilies gone brown at the edges · **Sound:** no footsteps; one candle guttering · **Smell:** lilies and pond water · **Touch:** the coffin wood sweats cold · **Taste:** sugar, then mud

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party follows the bearers:** they walk down to the water and onto the causeway, which is still dry. A party that follows now crosses early; move to Scene 02 without the tide.
- **If the party hides indoors:** the coffin is on the step at dawn. Mirabel tells them about the whisper instead.
- **If the party skips this scene:** the invitation arrives under a PC's pillow at the inn, and the guest list comes from the wisp in Scene 02.

## Transition
- → [[Scene 02 — Crossing the Mere]]: the church clock strikes ten, and out on the causeway the first stones go under.
