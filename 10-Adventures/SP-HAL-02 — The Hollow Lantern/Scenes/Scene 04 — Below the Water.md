# Scene 04 — Below the Water

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-02 — The Hollow Lantern/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 03 — The Great Hall]] · Next → [[Scene 05 — The Toast]]

## Purpose
The physical proof (the cask) and the monster who'd rather bargain (Edric). Covers H1 (Water Stair), H5 (Kitchens), H6 (Flooded Cellar). Optional, but the party that skips it goes into the toast with less evidence and no ally.
**Budget:** 30 minutes. Running long? Edric talks instead of fighting, and the residue is found in one roll.

## Arrival
> *The servants' stair goes down into water, warm as a bath and black as ink. The kitchens are flooded to the waist. A woman in a cook's apron stands at a wine cask on a trestle, scrubbing its inside with a rag, and the rag comes out clean and goes back in and comes out clean. Past her, a doorway opens on deeper water, and something down there is dragging a chain across stone.*

**To the table:** *"The cook hasn't looked up. The chain has stopped. Which do you go toward?"*

## Who Is Here
- **The cook's ghost** (not a stat block; a haunt). Scrubs the cask forever. Speaks only one line, if asked what she's doing: *"Getting the sweet out."*
- **[[Lord Edric Vane]]**, a **wight**, in the cellar with two **drowned guests** (zombies, swim 20 ft). *Wants:* revenge on his bride; the hall, after. *Reacts:* cold, possessive; opens with a bargain if the party doesn't open with steel.

## Beats

### H5 — The Kitchens
**Investigation DC 13** on the cask: a sweet-smelling residue in the grain of the wood. Anyone who played [[SP-HAL-01-Straw-Men]] knows Sweetsleep on sight; otherwise **Medicine or Nature DC 12** names it a sleeping draught, enough for a hall. That's the physical proof.

### H1 — The Water Stair (if they circle round)
The flooded entry and boathouse. A rotted rowboat tied up, a lady's travel trunk still packed inside, a man's fiddle case beside it. Her escape boat; his fiddle. Nobody came.

### H6 — The Flooded Cellar
Underwater, pitch black; the great sluice wheel still open. Edric rises out of the water in a drowned groom's coat. *"You came for her. Good. So did I."* His terms: help him reach the tower and he'll fight at the party's side; afterwards the hall is his and the living leave. **Insight DC 14:** he means the first part. He'll keep the second if nobody argues.

| If asked… | Edric says |
|---|---|
| What happened | *"She smiled at me over the cup. I should have known then. I woke on the floor with the water at my chin."* |
| About the sluice | *"I got my hand on it. The wheel was already open. I drowned trying to close what she'd opened."* |
| About her Wail | *"She sang once. I was the only one awake to hear it. Don't be near her when she does it twice."* |

Refuse him, or draw, and he fights. His zombies hold the stair; he comes through the water.

Let the players be the ones to decide he's a fight.

## Clues Available Here
- The cask residue → Ysolde poisoned the feast
- The boat and fiddle case → the fiddler was her lover and the boat was his
- Edric's warning → the Wail

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a clean rag, over and over; black water with a grey face under it · **Sound:** scrubbing; a chain · **Smell:** sweetness under rot · **Touch:** water warm as blood · **Taste:** iron

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party allies with Edric:** he goes up with them. At the toast he attacks Ysolde as often as the party, and if she falls he turns to claim the hall. A second problem, deferred.
- **If they kill him:** his cloak is in the silt. The cook's ghost stops scrubbing.
- **If they skip the cellar:** the cask proof is lost; the exposure ending needs the fiddler, Pell, and the diary instead.
