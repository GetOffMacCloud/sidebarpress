# Scene 03 — The Great Hall

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-02 — The Hollow Lantern/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — Crossing the Mere]] · Next → [[Scene 04 — Below the Water]]

## Purpose
A social scene with a cost. The party sits among the dead, learns the bride's secret from the man who ran, and sees Rowena's empty hand for themselves. Covers H2 (Great Hall), H3 (Portrait Gallery), H4 (Chapel).
**Budget:** 40 minutes. Running long? Cut the gallery; keep the fiddler and the chapel.

## Arrival
> *The grand stair climbs out of black water into warm light. Above, glasses clink and a fiddle plays a wedding reel. The great hall is full: dozens of guests in fifty-year-old finery, turning to look at you with wet, friendly faces. A cake slumps on the high table. At the long tables, five chairs stand empty, each with a name card. A footman with no eyes bows and says, "You're late. This way."*

**To the table:** *"He's pulling out a chair for you. Everyone is watching. What do you do with your hands?"*

## Who Is Here
- **The phantom guests**, harmless until provoked; they treat the PCs as late arrivals.
- **[[The Fiddler]]**, in the corner, playing the reel. *Wants:* to be forgiven. *Knows:* he saw her at the wheel and ran. *Reacts:* plays faster when the subject comes near.
- **The eyeless footman** (a specter in livery), seating guests, tallying Scandal.
- Any **sleepwalker** who arrived, seated and humming.

## Beats

### Wedding Etiquette (the Scandal track)
Every breach of manners earns a **Scandal** mark. At three, the music stops, the guests' faces slide off like wet paper, two **specters** attack, and Ysolde knows the party is here.

- [ ] Refusing a seat or a dance when offered
- [ ] Drawing a weapon at the table
- [ ] Speaking of death, drowning, or the year aloud
- [ ] Asking the bride's whereabouts too directly
- [ ] Eating or drinking anything (also **Con save DC 13** or asleep for 1 hour)

Playing along costs nothing but nerve. A bard PC who takes up the reel with the fiddler buys the table one free breach.

### H2 — The Great Hall
Gossip at the tables, if the party asks the right way (Persuasion or Performance DC 12): the bride is "resting"; the groom "went down to see about the cellar"; the four empty chairs "belong to people who were called away early, so rude." Five, if a PC is invited. **The fiddler**, asked gently (**Persuasion DC 12**, automatic for a musician PC who plays the tune back to him): *"I saw her at the wheel. Both hands. I had the boat ready at the water stair and I ran instead. She sang once, the night she died. Don't make her sing again."* That's the Wail, telegraphed.

### H3 — The Portrait Gallery
Warped by damp. Ysolde's portrait is slashed across the eyes. Her brother's is turned to face the wall; turn it round and the jaw is Tobias's (Investigation DC 10 for anyone who met him). **Lady Rowena's** portrait shows her hand on a bible, a pearl ring on it: the ring the party carries, matched at a glance.

### H4 — The Chapel
A hollow bell tower, rope hanging loose, no bell. Rowena's tomb: a stone effigy, one hand open, an empty ring setting carved into the palm, pearl-sized. The ring fits. **If the party sets it there now,** the candles in the chapel light themselves; Rowena will rise at the toast (Scene 05). Nothing else happens yet; let them wonder.

## Encounter (if any)
Only at three Scandal marks.

| APL | Creatures | Difficulty (4 PCs) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 2 specters | 600 adj., hard |
| 4 | 2 specters | 600 adj., medium |
| 5 | 2 specters + 1 ghost (a drowned bridesmaid; she possesses a PC and walks them to the tower) | 1,250 × 2 = 2,500, deadly on paper, but the ghost's goal is delivery, not damage |

**Terrain:** long tables (half cover), a slumping cake, a gallery above. · **Tactics:** the footman goes first; specters come through the tables. · **DM direction:** the fight ends the moment the party leaves the hall. The guests don't pursue; they sit back down and wait for midnight.

## Clues Available Here
- The fiddler's confession → Ysolde poisoned the feast; he was her lover and the boat was his
- The brother's portrait → Tobias is a Marrow
- Rowena's portrait and the empty effigy hand → the ring belongs in the tomb
- *"She sang once"* → the Wail is coming

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** finery gone green at the seams; candle flames that don't move · **Sound:** the reel, glass on glass, no breathing · **Smell:** sugar and mildew · **Touch:** a footman's hand, cold through your sleeve · **Taste:** the air tastes of the cellar

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party takes the Scandal on purpose:** fine. The specters are the price, and the guests go quiet after; the fiddler still talks, from under a table.
- **If they skip the gallery:** Rowena's ring match comes from Pell's line or the effigy itself.
- **If they go straight to the tower:** Ysolde is not there until midnight. The diary is (Scene 05), and the hall is quieter when they come back down.
