# Scene 05 — The Toast

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-02 — The Hollow Lantern/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 04 — Below the Water]]

## Purpose
The climax. The bride comes down the stair, the cups go up, and the party ends it one of three ways with what they've gathered. Covers H7 (Bridal Tower) and the midnight toast in H2.
**Budget:** 40 minutes. **Never cut.** End Scene 04 early instead.

## Arrival
> *Midnight. The fiddle stops in the middle of a bar. Every guest in the hall stands and lifts a cup, and the sound of a hundred chairs scraping back goes on far too long. At the top of the stair a woman in a drowned wedding dress begins to come down, water running from her veil and pooling on each step. She is smiling. She says, "You're all here. At last. Let's have the toast."*

**To the table:** *"She's six steps from the floor. The invitees are reaching for their cups. What do you do, and who does it?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Lady Ysolde Marrow]]**, a **banshee**. *Wants:* the guest list finished; every cup drunk. *Reacts:* treats the PCs as guests until they interfere, then as the people who ruined her wedding.
- **[[Lord Edric Vane]]**, if allied or still around; attacks Ysolde as often as the party.
- **[[The Fiddler]]**, under the table or at her side, depending on whether anyone forgave him.
- **[[Lady Rowena Marrow]]**, a **ghost**, if the ring is in her hand.
- The seated **invitees**: Mirabel, Tobias, Avelin, any invited PC. They drink on their next turn unless stopped (an action and **Charisma DC 13**, or a hand over the cup).

## Beats

### H7 — The Bridal Tower (before midnight)
If the party climbs first: the tower leans over the mere, bedecked with rotting lilies. The **Hollow Lantern** burns in the window. On the dressing table, Ysolde's diary ([[SP-HAL-02 — Handout — Ysolde's Diary]]), last entry: *"Tonight I will be free of all of them."* The dowry chest is here. Ysolde is not; she's on the stair at midnight.

### The toast
Ysolde expects every invitee to drink. Anyone who drinks falls into an endless sleep (no save; the cup is the pact). Stopping an invitee is an action.

### Three endings
**Destroy her.** Full banshee stat block. Her **Wail** is the adventure's deadliest moment: Con save DC 13 or drop to 0 hp. Telegraph it (she draws breath, the candles lean away) and have her save it for below half hp, or for when the party threatens her guests. Edric, if allied, soaks it: he's immune.

**Expose her.** At the toast, the party lays the evidence before the guests: Pell's testimony, the cask residue, the slashed portrait, the fiddler's confession, the diary. **Persuasion DC 15**, advantage for each piece beyond the first (two pieces is enough for advantage; four is enough for the DM to call it). On a success the guests set down their cups and turn to face her. They take her down the stair into the black water. She loses her Wail and half her hp if the party still has to finish her. The fiddler plays her out.

**Return the ring.** If the ring is in Rowena's stone hand before midnight, Rowena's ghost rises at the toast behind her daughter: a stern, kind presence, the only voice Ysolde cannot argue with. *"You sold my ring to drown your guests, child. Come home."* Ysolde's smile goes. Mother and daughter walk down the stair and into the water, and the lights go out one window at a time. The haunting ends forever. No roll.

### Arrived late
If an invitee drinks before the party acts, they sleep and do not wake. Their body goes home; their spirit sits at the table next year. The rest can still be saved; the ending still plays.

**Terrain:** the grand stair (she has the high ground), the long tables, cups within reach of every invitee. · **Tactics:** Corrupting Touch on whoever holds the evidence; Horrifying Visage on the stair; Wail when cornered. · **DM direction:** play her as certain she's the victim. She argues. Let the players argue back; the exposure ending is a conversation with a DC on it.

## Clues Available Here
- The diary → Ysolde poisoned the feast; the fiddler's boat
- The Hollow Lantern → the only light that was ever meant to be seen from the village

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a wet veil; a hundred raised cups; candles leaning away from her · **Sound:** chairs; silence; one breath drawn · **Smell:** lilies, lake, Sweetsleep · **Touch:** water on every step · **Taste:** sugar on the lip of the cup

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party never crossed the water:** Ysolde walks the causeway at midnight with the feast behind her; run this scene on the Reed Landing with the village watching from barred windows. Rowena's ending still works if the ring was set in the hall chapel earlier; otherwise it's gone.
- **If a PC drinks:** they sleep. Rowena's ending wakes them. The exposure ending wakes them at dawn. Destroying Ysolde wakes them cold and alive.
- **If the party flees:** the invitees drink. Morning finds them in their beds, asleep, and next Hallow's Eve the party's names are on the list.
