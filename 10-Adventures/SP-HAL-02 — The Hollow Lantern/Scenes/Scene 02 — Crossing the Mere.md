# Scene 02 — Crossing the Mere

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-02 — The Hollow Lantern/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 01 — The Mourning Procession]] · Next → [[Scene 03 — The Great Hall]]

## Purpose
Get the party onto the water and the sleepwalkers behind them. The wisps give a clue for bread; the tide makes the route a choice.
**Budget:** 45 minutes. Running long? One wisp, one voice, and the sleepwalker chase is a single roll.

## Arrival
> *The tide has come in without a sound. Where the causeway ran out to the hall there is now a line of ripples, and the last dry stone is already wet. Across the mere every window in Marrow Hall is lit, and the fiddle is louder. Out in the reeds, lanterns are bobbing, dozens of them, the way people carry them when they are looking for someone.*

**To the table:** *"Causeway, boat, or marsh. And who's watching the invitees while you're gone?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Gaffer Pell]]**, if he has confessed, with his boat. *Wants:* to row them himself and not go inside.
- **[[Widow Osk]]**, at the reed edge. *Wants:* respect and bread. *Knows:* the safe path, and which wisp will talk.
- **Will-o'-wisps**, the drowned guests, calling PCs by name in voices they know.
- **Sleepwalkers** at 11 PM: [[Mirabel Pell]], [[Tobias Marrow]], [[Sister Avelin]], and any invited PC not protected.

## Beats

### Three ways across
| Route | How | Challenge |
|---|---|---|
| The Causeway | On foot, before 10 PM | Slick stone: **Acrobatics DC 12** per PC or fall in; cold water, 1d4 cold and a soaked pack. After 10 it's waist-deep and the wisps come to meet you. |
| Pell's boat | Pell rows, if he's confessed | Halfway over, something drags at the oars: **Strength DC 14** or the boat drifts into the marsh and the wisps. |
| The Marsh Path | Osk guides, for respect and bread | Safest, but it passes the marsh lights. Osk's bread calms one wisp. |

### Lights on the Marsh
Lanterns bob among the reeds, and voices call the PCs by name: a dead parent, an old friend, a lover. Each light promises a shortcut to the hall. A PC who follows a voice makes a **Wisdom save DC 13**; on a failure they walk into the bog (10 ft of difficult terrain, restrained on a failed **Strength DC 12**, an easy target for Shock).

**Osk's bread.** Set on the water with a word of respect, it calms one wisp, which answers a single question honestly before going out. The right question gets: *"She poured the wine herself."*

### Sleepwalkers (11 PM)
If the invitees aren't under watch, the party passes them wading waist-deep toward the hall, humming the fiddle tune. Waking one takes an action and a **Charisma check DC 13**, or *protection from evil and good*. A sleepwalker who isn't stopped arrives at the feast and takes their seat.

## Clues Available Here

- Sleepwalking Tobias → Tobias is a Marrow
- The hummed tune → the fiddler's reel (musician subplot)

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** lanterns that are never quite where you look · **Sound:** your own name, in a voice you buried · **Smell:** rotting reed, cold iron · **Touch:** mud that holds on · **Taste:** brackish spray

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party crosses before 10:** causeway rolls only, no wisps; the wisp's clue moves to the cook's ghost in Scene 04.
- **If they stay to guard the invitees:** the toast comes to them (see Scene Index). Ysolde walks the causeway at midnight with the feast behind her; run Scene 05 at the Reed Landing.
- **If someone swims:** Con save DC 12 per 100 ft against the cold (1 level of exhaustion on a failure); the wisps love a swimmer.
