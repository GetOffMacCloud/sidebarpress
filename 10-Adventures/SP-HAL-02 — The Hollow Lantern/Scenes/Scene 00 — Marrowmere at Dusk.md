# Scene 00 — Marrowmere at Dusk

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-02 — The Hollow Lantern/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 01 — The Mourning Procession]]

## Purpose
Plant three things before dark: Tobias is hiding something, Pell won't talk sober, and Lady Rowena's effigy has an empty hand. The last one is the seed of the best ending; don't leave the scene without it.
**Budget:** 30 minutes. Running long? Skip the landing; Widow Osk can be met on the marsh in Scene 02.

## Arrival
> *Every grave in the chapel yard wears a candle, hundreds of small flames shivering in the wind off the water. Across the mere, Marrow Hall answers them. One by one its drowned windows fill with gold light, as if someone inside is getting ready for guests. Doors are shutting all along the street. Faint and sweet, a fiddle begins to play.*

**To the table:** *"The inn, the chapel, or the landing. Where do you go before the doors are barred?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Tobias Marrow]]** ("Tobias Fenn"), at the Lantern & Lily. *Wants:* to get through one more Hallow's Eve without saying his name. *Knows:* he's a Marrow; he has an invitation. *Reacts:* polished, careful, keeps glancing at the hall through the window.
- **[[Sister Avelin]]**, at the Chapel of Last Light, lighting every candle herself. *Wants:* the dead laid to rest. *Knows:* the rite of rest; the story of Rowena's hand. *Reacts:* calm, tired, honest.
- **[[Gaffer Pell]]**, at the Reed Landing or the inn's corner. *Wants:* Mirabel kept from the water. *Knows:* he saw Ysolde open the sluice. *Reacts:* gruff, drinks to sleep, won't talk sober.
- **[[Mirabel Pell]]**, with her grandfather. *Wants:* to be treated as capable. *Knows:* she's been humming a tune she's never heard.
- **[[Widow Osk]]**, at the landing putting bread on the water. *Wants:* respect. *Knows:* the marsh path.

## Beats

### The Lantern & Lily
Tobias pours, smiles, and answers every question about the Marrows with a question about the party's journey. **Insight DC 14:** he's hiding something that frightens him. The invitation is tucked in his apron, addressed to *"Master Marrow."* Sleight of Hand DC 13 lifts it; a direct accusation with the ring in hand makes him sit down hard.

| If asked… | Tobias says |
|---|---|
| About the hall | *"It lights up. It has done since before I was born. Nobody goes."* |
| About the Marrows | *"Dead. All of them. There's nobody left to ask."* (Insight DC 14: he flinches on *nobody.*) |
| About the bell | *"Sold, years back. A sexton from somewhere west bought it."* |

### The Chapel of Last Light
Avelin explains the rite without being asked twice: *"A ghost stays for an unfinished thing. Finish it, and it goes."* She doesn't know what Ysolde's unfinished thing is. **If asked about the Marrows**, she gives the seed: *"Old Lady Rowena's tomb is out in the hall chapel. Her effigy has had an empty hand since before I was born. Something was pried out of it. They say Ysolde did it the week before the wedding."* Make sure this lands; repeat it through Pell in Scene 01 if it doesn't.

### The Reed Landing
Pell won't talk sober. Cider and **Persuasion DC 13** get the confession early; otherwise it comes when Mirabel starts sleepwalking (Scene 02) or when the coffin arrives (Scene 01): *"I saw her. In her wedding dress, down at the sluice wheel, turning it with both hands. She was smiling."* Standalone: he gives the party the ring here, "cold in my pocket long enough."

Widow Osk sets bread on the water and says, to no one, *"The water keeps what it's given and gives back what it's owed."* She'll guide the party across later for respect and a loaf.

## Encounter (if any)
None. If the party forces Tobias, he doesn't fight; he weeps.

## Clues Available Here
- Tobias's deflections and the apron invitation → Tobias is a Marrow
- Avelin's empty hand → the ring belongs in Rowena's tomb
- Pell's confession (if unlocked) → Ysolde poisoned the feast; the ring is her mother's

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** hundreds of candles; gold windows on black water · **Sound:** a distant fiddle; shutters closing · **Smell:** tallow, reed smoke, wet rot · **Touch:** cold wind off the mere · **Taste:** small beer, salt bread

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party heads straight for the causeway:** let them; it's dry until 10 PM. The procession passes them on the road instead, and Avelin's seed moves to the portrait of Rowena in Scene 03.
- **If the party skips the chapel:** Pell gives the empty-hand detail with his confession: *"her mother's ring. Pried out of the stone hand herself."*

- → [[Scene 01 — The Mourning Procession]]: at nine, fog comes up the street from the water, and the fiddle stops.
