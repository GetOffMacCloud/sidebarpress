# Handout — Ysolde's Diary

Back to [[SP-HAL-02-The-Hollow-Lantern]] · Found in [[Scene 05 — The Toast]] (H7, the dressing table).

*A small book bound in blue silk, warped and water-stained, open to the last written page. The earlier pages are a girl's diary: horses, a sister's wedding, a fiddler who plays at the harvest fairs. The last entry is in the same hand, pressed hard enough to tear the paper.*

> *The ring is gone and the vial is here.*
>
> *Tonight I will be free of all of them.*
>
> *He has promised the boat at the water stair when the house is quiet. If he is late, I will wait in the tower, where I can see the whole mere.*
>
> *Mother would not understand. Mother is past understanding anything, and her hand did not need the ring. I need the wine more than she needs a stone.*
>
> *When they are all asleep I will open the wheel myself. It only wants two hands.*

**DM note:** counts as evidence for the exposure ending, and confirms the fiddler as the boat's keeper. If a PC is playing the *fiddler's tune* subplot, the harvest-fair fiddler on the earlier pages is their teacher's teacher.
