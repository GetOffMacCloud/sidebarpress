# Handout — The Invitation

Back to [[SP-HAL-02-The-Hollow-Lantern]] · Given in [[Scene 01 — The Mourning Procession]] (or found under a pillow, per the hook).

*A black-edged card, heavy stock, the ink still wet no matter how long it has been held. The hand is elegant and slightly shaking. A ribbon of lake-weed is tied through one corner.*

> *The honour of your presence is requested*
> *at the wedding of*
> **Lady Ysolde Marrow**
> *to Lord Edric Vane*
>
> *Marrow Hall · Midnight · All Hallows' Eve*
>
> *Carriages at eleven. The mere will be lit.*
>
> *— Please, do not be late again.*

**DM note:** the last line appears only on invitations addressed to the four families who fled, and to any PC named on the coffin. Addressed to a PC's own family name if the *blood of the guests* subplot is in play; to *"Master Marrow"* on the one in Tobias's apron.
