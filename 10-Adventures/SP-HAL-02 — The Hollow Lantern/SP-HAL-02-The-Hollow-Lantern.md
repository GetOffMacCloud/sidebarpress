# The Hollow Lantern

**APL 4 (levels 3–5) · 4–5 characters · one 4-hour session**

> *Every Hallow's Eve the drowned manor lights its windows. This year the bride is sending invitations.*
>
> A gothic haunting for **average party level 3–5** (written for APL 4, with scaling for 3 and 5). Fifty years ago, a wedding feast at Marrow Hall ended with every guest asleep at the table and the mere pouring in through the cellar sluice. Every Hallow's Eve since, the half-sunk hall relights its windows and the dead take their seats again. This year, living people in Marrowmere are finding black-edged invitations under their pillows. The bride wants her guest list complete, and at midnight she raises the toast. The party has one night to cross the water, sit at a table of ghosts without giving offense, and work out that the victim of the massacre is the one who caused it.

## Design Frame
- **Thesis:** The dead stay for an unfinished thing. Ysolde's unfinished thing is a lie she tells herself, and the only ways to end the haunting are to finish the lie for her, break it in front of witnesses, or bring her the one voice she can't argue with.
- **Expectation broken:** *The ghost bride is the victim.* She's the poisoner. The guests who "stole her happy ending" are the four who didn't drink.
- **Second break:** the manor's monster-in-the-cellar (Lord Edric) hates the boss more than he hates the party. He'll bargain.
- **What this adventure refuses to do:** It never makes the phantom guests a fight the party must win. The feast is a social scene with a cost, not a room full of hit points. Anyone can walk out of this adventure without killing a thing.
- **Session spine:** the **Midnight Clock** (sleepwalkers at 11, the toast at 12) and the **Scandal track** at the feast (three marks and the guests turn). See [[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-02 — The Hollow Lantern/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]].
- **Tone:** wet lace and candle smoke. A fiddle heard across black water. Every ghost is polite until it isn't.

## What the Locals Know
- The hall lights up every Hallow's Eve, and has for fifty years. Nobody goes out on the mere that night.
- Doors are barred after dark. Candles go on every grave (the *Wake-Lights*) so the dead find their own way home and not yours.
- The reed-cutters put bread on the water. Sister Avelin says that's superstition. The reed-cutters say Sister Avelin's grandmother ran from that wedding, so what does she know.
- Tobias Fenn at the Lantern & Lily came back from the city fifteen years ago and bought the inn. Nobody remembers where his money came from.
- The chapel in the hall has no bell. Old Fenn's grandfather sold it.

## What's Really Happening
Fifty years ago, **Lady Ysolde Marrow** was promised to **Lord Edric Vane**, a cruel, wealthy man, to settle her father's debts. Ysolde traded her mother's wedding ring to a hag for a vial of **Sweetsleep** and poured it into the wine at her own wedding feast. When every guest had slumped asleep at the tables, she went down to the cellar and opened the great sluice. The mere came in. Her plan was to slip away by boat with her lover, the wedding fiddler, and leave Marrow Hall to drown with its secrets.

The water came faster than she expected. The fiddler saw her at the sluice wheel and ran. Ysolde waited in the bridal tower for a boat that never came and drowned there in her veil. She rose as a **banshee**, bound to the house, and she remembers the night differently: *she* was the victim, and the guests who escaped stole her happy ending.

Four people didn't drink: a page boy (now **Gaffer Pell**), a bridesmaid (grandmother of **Sister Avelin**), the fiddler (now a ghost in the great hall), and Ysolde's little brother (grandfather of **Tobias Marrow**, who runs the inn as Tobias Fenn). For fifty years the haunting replayed itself and did nothing more. This year something woke Ysolde fully: her mother's ring came back to Marrowmere (in the chain, the party carries it; standalone, Pell pulled it from an eel trap last spring). Now she sends invitations to the descendants of the four who left early. At midnight the toast is raised, and anyone holding an invitation sleepwalks across the mere to drink it.

**Lord Edric** drowned in the flooded cellar still trying to reach the sluice. He rose as a **wight**, hates his bride, and will help anyone who means to bring her down.

**Lady Rowena Marrow**, Ysolde's mother, lies in the manor chapel. Her effigy's stone hand has an empty ring setting. Return the ring, and Rowena rises as a **ghost** at the toast: the one voice Ysolde cannot argue with.

## Hook
- **The Ring (bundle chain).** The party carries Ysolde's cold, wet ring from Chaff's ledger ([[SP-HAL-01-Straw-Men]]). Since crossing into Marrowmere, the ring-bearer dreams of drowning.
- **The Invitation.** A PC wakes at a roadside inn to find a black-edged card on their pillow, its ink still wet: *"The honour of your presence is requested at the wedding of Lady Ysolde Marrow. Marrow Hall, Midnight, All Hallows' Eve."* See [[SP-HAL-02 — Handout — The Invitation]].
- **The Frightened Grandfather.** [[Gaffer Pell]], an old boatman, hires the party to protect his granddaughter, who received an invitation. He won't say why he's so sure it's real.

*Running standalone:* Pell found the ring in an eel trap last spring and has kept it since, which is why the haunting woke. He gives it to the party when he confesses, saying it has "been cold in my pocket long enough."

## PC Objectives (the plot, as goals rather than events)
- **Find out why the dead are inviting the living.** Routes: Pell's confession (cider + Persuasion DC 13, or watching Mirabel sleepwalk); the coffin's whispered guest list; Widow Osk's wisp, bribed with bread; the diary in the tower.
- **Keep the invitees alive past midnight.** Routes: lock them in with *protection from evil and good* or a *magic circle*; get them out of the valley; cross the mere first and stop the toast; wake a sleepwalker with Charisma DC 13.
- **Reach Marrow Hall after the tide comes in.** Routes: the causeway on foot before 10 PM; Pell's boat; Widow Osk's marsh path; swimming (cold water, Con saves, and the wisps).
- **Sit at the feast without a Scandal.** Routes: play along; bribe the fiddler into covering; sneak the gallery and chapel instead; take the Scandal on purpose and fight.
- **Learn what Ysolde's unfinished thing is.** Routes: the fiddler's confession; the kitchen cask residue; the slashed portrait; the diary; Edric's bargain.
- **End the haunting.** Routes (see Climax at a Glance): destroy her; expose her to the guests; return Rowena's ring.
- *(Optional door)* **Find the missing chapel bell.** Tobias knows where it went. It's the way into [[SP-HAL-03-The-Night-That-Doesnt-End]].

## Subplots (keyed to PCs; assign at the table)
Pick one or two before the session. A subplot that belongs to a PC turns a rescue into a personal stake.
- **Blood of the guests.** A PC is descended from one of the four who fled the feast. Their invitation is addressed in the family name, and the phantom guests treat them as a returning relative. Ysolde addresses them by that name at the toast.
- **The coffin has your name.** By default the Mourning Procession's coffin names the ring-bearer. For a stronger stake, name the PC with the most unfinished business in their own backstory, and let Ysolde speak to that when they meet.
- **The fiddler's tune.** A bard or musician PC knows the wedding reel the hall plays. Their own teacher taught it to them, and their teacher learned it here, from the man who ran.

## Climax at a Glance
Every solution has to be seeded before the party climbs the tower.

| Solution | What it needs | Seeded in | Payoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Destroy her** | Beat a banshee (CR 4) whose Wail can drop a PC to 0 | Scene 03 (her Wail is described by the fiddler: *"she sang once, the night she died"*) | Lights go out, guests fade, invitees wake alive |
| **Expose her** | 2+ pieces of evidence and a Persuasion DC 15 at the toast | Pell's testimony (00), cask residue (04), slashed portrait (03), diary (05), fiddler's confession (03) | Guests drag her into the water; she loses her Wail and half her hit points |
| **Return the ring** | Set the ring in Rowena's stone hand (H4) before midnight | Avelin's remark about the empty hand (00); Pell's confession (00); the gallery portrait of Rowena wearing it (03) | Rowena's ghost rises; mother and daughter walk into the water; the haunting ends forever |
| **Edric's bargain** | Agree terms with the wight in the cellar | Cook's ghost scrubbing the cask (04) | Edric fights beside the party against Ysolde, then wants the hall |

**Adventuring-day check:** APL 4 budget is 6,800 XP for four characters. Worst-case path (procession fight, two wisps, cellar fight, toast) comes to about 4,650 adjusted XP: inside budget, with the social scenes carrying the rest of the night.

## Treasure & Rewards
- **The Hollow Lantern** — a *lantern of revealing* (DMG 179), burning in the tower window for fifty years.
- **Lord Edric's cloak** — a *cloak of the manta ray* (DMG 159), in the cellar.
- **The dowry chest (H7)** — 450 gp in jewelry and silver plate, and 2 *potions of greater healing*.
- **Tobias's gratitude** — if saved, he reclaims the Marrow name and becomes a lifelong patron: lodging, a boat, a little coin.
- **Information** — the name Vesper Hollow, and what the bell did there. Pay story XP for the ending chosen, not just the kills.
- **Advancement** — in the chain, the party reaches 5th level, or 6th if they started at 5th. Award a downtime level before [[SP-HAL-03-The-Night-That-Doesnt-End]].

## Denouement
Grey morning. The Wake-Lights have burned down. Anyone who played the whole night deserves to hear what the survivors say.

### Endings at a Glance
| Ending | How | Marrowmere after |
|---|---|---|
| **Destroyed** | Banshee reduced to 0 hp | The windows go dark for good; the invitees wake cold on the hall floor. Tobias keeps his false name. |
| **Exposed** | Evidence + Persuasion DC 15 at the toast | The guests take her into the water. The hall is quiet, but the fiddler lingers until someone forgives him. |
| **The ring returned** | Ring in Rowena's hand before midnight | Mother and daughter walk into the mere together. The haunting ends forever. Tobias says his real name out loud. |
| **Arrived late** | Toast raised with an invitee seated | That person sleeps and doesn't wake; their spirit sits at the table next year. Partial failure is designed: the rest can still be saved. |

**Hook out — the missing bell.** Tobias stands in the hall chapel staring at the empty tower: *"My grandfather sold that bell the year I was born. A sexton came all the way from Vesper Hollow for it. He wanted a bell that had rung over the drowned, he said. I've written to Vesper Hollow every year since, about buying it back. Every letter comes back with the same reply, in the same handwriting, dated the same day: All Hallows' Eve."* If the fiddler lingers: *"That bell rang the night we drowned. Wherever it hangs, that night hangs with it."*
