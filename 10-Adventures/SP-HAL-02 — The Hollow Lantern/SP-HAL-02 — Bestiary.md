## Variant — Drowned Guest
*Medium undead, neutral evil.* Use the **zombie** (MM 316) with these changes:
- **Speed** 20 ft., swim 20 ft.
- **Waterlogged.** The drowned guest can breathe water, and it has advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks made to grapple a creature that is in water.
- **Drag Under.** A creature grappled by the drowned guest and in water of at least 5 feet is pulled beneath the surface at the end of the guest's turn and begins holding its breath (PHB 183). Challenge stays 1/4 (50 XP); the water does the work.

Two drowned guests hold the cellar stair for Edric. They wear fifty-year-old livery and still carry trays.

## The Cook's Ghost (haunt, no stat block)
Scrubs the cask forever (Scene 04). Can't be fought or dispelled; stops scrubbing if Edric is destroyed or Ysolde is exposed. Says one line: *"Getting the sweet out."*

## Ysolde's tactics
- **Before the toast** she is a hostess. She does not attack a guest who hasn't broken etiquette.
- **Horrifying Visage** on the stair, where the frightened can't close.
- **Corrupting Touch** on whoever holds the evidence or the ring.
- **Wail** (DC 13 Con or 0 hp) is saved for below half hp, or for the moment the party lays hands on an invitee's cup. Telegraph it one round ahead: she draws breath, the candles lean away. Edric is immune and will stand in front of a PC if allied; the exposure ending strips it entirely.
- **Scaling:** APL 3, no Wail. APL 5, two specters on the stair and Edric at her side, attacking her as often as the party.
