# E4 — Thirteen Tolls (Deadly)

*Scene 6 of [[SP-GA05-The-Pilgrims-of-the-Hollow-Bell]]. Patchwall 25, the dark of Luna.*

> [!art]+ Map — The Bell-Tor Crown
> ![[SP-GA05 map-bell-tor.jpg|600]]

## Setup
No Luna, a sliver of Celene, and a hard frost. A torch ring lights the crown and nothing beyond it. Ansgar is casting the [[Hollowing Toll]] on [[The Sun-Bell of Carn Aedh]] with [[The Bone Clapper]]. The barrow field at the Tor's foot browns in a widening circle with every stroke.

**Areas:**
- **T1. The ring.** Tumbled stone wall around the crown; torches every 10 ft. Bright light inside, darkness outside.
- **T2. The bell frame.** Black oak, 10 ft up, reached by a **5-ft. stair** one defender can hold. The bell hangs at the top with its lip at shoulder height from the platform.
- **T3. The oak and the Hanged Stag.** An old oak grows from the ring wall; the white stag hangs from it by its hind legs.
- **T4. The lintel.** A fallen Flan doorway in the north wall, carved with a sun over a bell bound in cloth (F4 Route 1).
- **T5. The south face.** Frozen scree: difficult terrain; moving on it requires a **DC 14 Dexterity save** or slide 20 ft. Ansgar's escape route.
- **T6. The barrow field.** Below the north face. The barrow dead claw out here and climb for 2 rounds.

## The Ritual Clock
- Ansgar (or whoever holds the clapper within 5 ft of the bell) strikes on **initiative count 20**, losing ties, every round.
- **Starting count: 4.** +1 if the wardens stayed at Kalbach; +1 if Ansgar is Alert; +1 if an orc fled uphill to warn him.
- Keep a visible tally on the table (thirteen marks; cross one off per toll). Let Halse count each one aloud.

## Ways to Stop the Bell (F4)
| Method | How | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Muffle it** | Wrap a lambskin, cloak or bedroll over the lip: an action, plus Athletics DC 12 if an acolyte is adjacent. *Silence* also works. | Tolls raise nothing and don't count while it stays on. An acolyte can tear it off with an action. |
| **Take the clapper** | Contested grapple or a disarm (Ansgar has Str 10). | No clapper, no toll. Whoever holds it becomes everyone's target; Cathal hunts its holder. |
| **Consecrate the bell** | A divine caster holds a holy symbol to it for **2 consecutive actions** (1 for a priest of Pelor or another sun god), or two divine casters 1 action each in the same round. | The bell rings once on its own, clear and warm. Every barrow dead on the Tor falls; the bell can never be hollowed. Ending A. |
| **Break concentration** | Damage Ansgar (Con save, DC 10 or half damage). | Tolls stop until he recasts with his last 3rd-level slot. The count is kept. |
| **Kill or rout Ansgar** | Reduce him to half hp (13). | He passes the clapper to Halse and flees down T5. Halse keeps ringing. |
| **Name Him** | Lore Ladder rung 5; an action. | Acolytes within 60 ft: Wis DC 12 or frightened; Ansgar skips one toll to answer. |
| **Cut the Stag** | An action: rope AC 10, 5 hp, or Athletics DC 12 to climb and cut. | Barrow dead's speed is halved for the rest of the fight. |

## Tactics
- **Acolytes** ring the bell stair and cast *sanctuary* on Ansgar first, then *sacred flame* on anyone climbing.
- **Halse** uses *hold person* on whoever reaches the frame, then *spiritual weapon*. She fights to the end. If she gets the clapper, she rings.
- **Ansgar** never stops ringing unless forced. He uses his action for *inflict wounds* on anyone adjacent, or *guiding bolt* on a light-bearer.
- **Barrow dead** go for whoever carries fire or light, then for the clapper-thief.
- **Shared darkvision (a Twilight cleric's *Eyes of Night*) and dark-adapted scouts:** let them flank from outside the torch ring. Reward it.

## Toll 13 — Cathal Red-Torc
> *The thirteenth note doesn't fade. It hangs in the frost. In the ring, the earth humps up and splits, and a tall dead man in a corroded mail shirt stands, a twisted red-gold torc at his throat. He looks at the priest, then at you. He speaks in a language older than the hill.*

Cathal is **not** Ansgar's servant. He wants to know who woke him, and he kills whoever holds the clapper. A divine caster or druid can parley (Religion or Persuasion **DC 15**; advantage with the lambskin, a Yati PC, or a faith that keeps the rest of the dead; anyone who speaks Old Flan or uses *comprehend languages* removes the need to translate). Success: he lays himself down again and leaves the torc (Ending D). Otherwise he fights. See [[Cathal Red-Torc]].

## Development
- **Ansgar flees** at half hp. Pursuit down the frozen scree is a chase, not a cutscene: he has no plot armor. If the wardens catch him, they catch him (Ending A); if he gets away with the clapper, Ending B.
- **Turned orcs** arrive in round 2 from T5 and engage the acolytes.
- **After the fight:** bury the stag with a seed on the Tor (*Beory's tears*). The Yati notice.
