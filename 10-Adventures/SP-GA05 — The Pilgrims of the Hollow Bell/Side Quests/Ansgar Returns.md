# Ansgar Returns

*If Brother-Reaper Ansgar Vell escaped (Ending B or E), he comes back for the bell, or with it.*

- **Opens from:** Ending B (Ansgar fled with the clapper) or Ending E (the bell was hollowed and carried off).
- **Shape:** Pursuit and ambush along the drove roads; a second climax at a churchyard.
- **Band:** APL 4 · pursuit + set piece

## The Seed
- **After B:** Ansgar needs the bell *and* the clapper. On the next new moon he returns to Carn Aedh with new pilgrims, more orcs or hired drovers, and a block and tackle. The Yati send word.
- **After E:** the hollowed bell is travelling south on a handcart. Each churchyard it passes empties. The wardens have until it reaches **Ritterstadt's own churchyard**.
- Ansgar fears dying unremembered. A warden who **Named Him** on the Tor is the one he wants to kill first.
- If Halse survived, she runs the count; if not, a new tallykeeper does, and she is worse.

## Player Lead
Brother-Reaper Ansgar Vell escaped the Bell-Tor with the bone clapper. The bell still hangs, and he needs both.

## Lead Credit
**+10** on top of the session's extra credit. Earned only after Ending B, when the report says Ansgar escaped with the clapper and will come back for the bell at the next new moon. Ending E pays nothing: a hollowed bell is a crisis, not a lead.
