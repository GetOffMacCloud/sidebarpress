# Scene 02 — The Catacombs

[[10-Adventures/SP-OP01 — What the Dead Fear/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 01 — The Grateful Mortician]] · Next → [[Scene 03 — What the Dead Fear]]

## Purpose
Show the party that the dead are being kept and cured, and that something is afraid. They should reach the iron doors suspicious but not yet certain.
**Budget:** 75 minutes. Running long? Cut C1 to one line and go straight to Old Tam.

## Arrival
> *The stair ends in a long gallery of skulls stacked to the vaulted ceiling. Every skull faces the wall. Cold air moves past you, going down.*

**To the table:** *"Who turns one of the skulls around?"*

## Beats
**The ghouls never attack the party.** Only Ossian's mindless and monstrous things do. Play the ghouls like frightened animals, not monsters.

- **C1. The Bone Gallery.** Skulls fill niches up to the vaulted ceiling, and every one has been turned to face the wall. A trail of glistening slime runs up the wall and across the ceiling, heading deeper. Survival DC 13 identifies it as something big that climbs. On a 15 or higher, the trail is twice as wide as anything the party fights in here.
> [!danger] Fight: Bone Gallery (Medium)
> **Ossuary crawlers** ([[Ossuary Crawler]]), drawn by the smell of the larder, cling to the 15-ft. ceiling among the skulls. Ossian tolerates them because they clean up. They drop on the party once the last character is inside. Their feeding tendrils paralyze (DC 13 Con), and a crawler drags a paralyzed victim 10 ft. toward the dark. The party may decide the slime trail is theirs. It isn't.
>
> | Code | PCs | Creatures | Adjusted XP | Difficulty |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | E2a | 4 | 4 ossuary crawlers | 3,600 | Medium |
> | E2b | 5 | 5 ossuary crawlers | 4,500 | Medium |
> | E2c | 6 | 6 ossuary crawlers | 5,400 | Medium |

- **C2. The Embalming Room.** Tables, hooks, and salt barrels. Ghoul limbs hang from the hooks like hams, wrapped in burial linen and dated. A ledger in a neat hand reads: *Brisk, widow. Raised Planting 2nd. Tender by Harvest.* Religion or Arcana DC 15: undead don't rot like this, so someone is curing them. The ledger's first entry is *Ossian, brother. Tough.*
> [!danger] Fight: The Kitchen (Easy)
> A salt pit fills the center of the room, 10 ft. square and 3 ft. deep (difficult terrain). Ossian's failures live in it: **gibbering mouthers** grown from aberrant scraps he couldn't digest. When anyone touches the hooks, the **cured hands** ([[Cured Hand]]) twitch free and scuttle, and the mouthers rise from the salt babbling in the voices of the dead. Arcana DC 13 afterward: mouthers form where aberrant flesh festers, so something in this place isn't human.
>
> | Code | PCs | Creatures | Adjusted XP | Difficulty |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | E3a | 4 | 2 gibbering mouthers + 6 cured hands | 2,400 | Easy |
> | E3b | 5 | 3 gibbering mouthers + 6 cured hands | 3,525 | Easy |
> | E3c | 6 | 3 gibbering mouthers + 10 cured hands | 3,625 | Easy |

- **C3. The Columbarium.** Shelves of urn niches. **Old Tam**, a ghoul who was a cooper in life, is wedged in the mouth of a burial crawl. He doesn't attack. He shrieks "Not him, not him!" and squirms to get away. If the party stops and listens, he gasps, "Bar the door. From the outside. He comes when the doors open," and then he's gone. If they kill him, he dies pleading.
- **C4. The Iron Doors.** A pair of iron doors to the Saint's Ossuary, barred on *this* side with an oak beam. Deep gouges score the doors on the outside, eight feet up. There are none on the inside.

## Wandering Husks
Roll a d6 whenever the party rests, dawdles for 30 minutes, or makes a racket below. On a 1–2, roll again on this table. These fights are attrition, not set pieces (trivial to Easy).

| d6 | Code | Encounter |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | W1 | 6 husks (zombies) shuffling along a corridor, all facing the same way: toward the ossuary |
| 3–4 | W2 | 1 ogre zombie in burial linen and 4 husks, carrying a salt barrel |
| 5 | W3 | 2 ossuary crawlers, feeding on a cured limb that fell off its hook |
| 6 | — | No fight. Ossian's voice speaks in every head at once: *"Hurry, friends. They're getting away."* |

## Clues
| Clue | Points to |
|---|---|
| Slime trail on the ceiling | Something big that climbs |
| Cured, dated limbs; the ledger | Someone is aging the dead to eat |
| *Ossian, brother. Tough.* | The keeper upstairs isn't Ossian |
| Old Tam's terror | The dead are prey, not predators |
| Gouges on the outside, high up | The thing outside is big, and it wants in |

## Alternates
- **The party takes Tam's advice** and leaves the doors barred. Ossian, watching from the chute, sighs and comes for them in C4 instead: run Scene 3's fight in the corridor (no sarcophagi; the bone shelves and the channel still apply), with the larder undead pounding on the inside of the doors.
- **The party burns the ledger or the limbs.** Ossian feels it. He's no longer patient: the trap springs as soon as the doors open.

## Sensory Palette
Salt and clove over rot. Linen. Drips. Somewhere ahead, many quiet voices whispering one word over and over: *shh*.
