## CR check (2014 DMG)
- **Defensive:** 152 hp + three meals (45 hp) = 197 effective hp → CR 9. AC 16 is one over expected; no shift.
- **Offensive:** Multiattack (13 + 13 + 27) + one legendary Claw (13) = 66 damage/round at +8 → CR 10.
- **Average:** CR 9. The round-1 *dominate person* (DC 16) isn't in the math and is why the fight plays Deadly.

## Dressing for stock blocks
- **Sexton Harrow (wight).** His longsword is a shovel (same stats, bludgeoning damage). He won't fight Ossian unless persuaded (Persuasion DC 13). Feeding on him heals Ossian 25.
- **Larder ghouls.** They cower: they take the Dodge action every turn until armed. Their claws can paralyze Ossian (he isn't an elf, and he isn't immune).
- **Pallbearers (ogre zombies).** Wrapped in burial linen, smelling of salt and clove. Undead Fortitude stands. Ossian can't feed on them (they're his butchers, not food).
