# Appendix: NPC Summary

The people of [[Merrow's Rest]], living and otherwise. Inline notation in the scenes follows the house style: **Name** (alignment sex race **statblock**).

## Brother Ossian (OSH-ee-un)
NE drunaghul **[[Brother Ossian|Brother Ossian (Drunaghul)]]**, wearing the face of a LG male human cleric.
The chapel's keeper for forty years, or so everyone thinks. The real Ossian is the first line in the larder ledger. The thing wearing him hired the party to flush its dinner out of hiding.
- **Personality.** Endlessly, wetly grateful. Touches your hand a beat too long. Never blinks.
- **Ideal.** Appetite. The dead are meat that hasn't finished curing.
- **Bond.** Its larder. It has been patient for eight months and won't lose it now.
- **Flaw.** It can't resist gloating once the doors close. It remembers every face that hurt it.

## Sexton Harrow (HAIR-oh)
LN male human **wight**.
The chapel's gravedigger, the first thing Ossian raised, and the only one who came back with his memory. He led the larder into the Saint's Ossuary and barred them in.
- **Personality.** Blunt, tired, courteous. Speaks like a shovel in gravel.
- **Ideal.** Rest. The dead belong in the ground, all of them, himself included.
- **Bond.** The larder dead. He buried every one of them the first time.
- **Flaw.** He couldn't bring himself to use the urn's ash on his own people. He'll ask the party to do it.

## Old Tam
N male human **ghoul**.
A cooper in life, buried in spring. Wedged in a burial crawl in the Columbarium (C3), too frightened to move.
- **Personality.** Shrieking, squirming terror; gasps out a warning if anyone listens.
- **Ideal.** Hiding. *Shh.*
- **Bond.** His widow in the village, who swears he knocked on her door.
- **Flaw.** He runs. Always.

## The Widow Brisk
N female human **ghoul**, wearing a blue burial ribbon.
Pell's mother. Ossian's ledger marks her *Tender by Harvest*. The bonus objective turns on her ([[SP-OP01 — Bonus Objective — Pell's Mother]]).
- **Personality.** Quiet, watchful, still a mother: she puts herself between the twins and the light.
- **Ideal.** Her son mustn't see her like this.
- **Bond.** Pell. She went to the chapel window to look at him once more.
- **Flaw.** Hunger. She knows it's winning.

## The Collier Twins
N male human **ghouls**.
Wil and Ned Collier, brothers and young miners, killed together in the same collapse. They cling to each other behind a sarcophagus lid and whisper *shh* until the fight starts.
- **Personality.** Two voices finishing each other's sentences.
- **Ideal.** Together, whatever comes.
- **Bond.** Each other.
- **Flaw.** If one is taken, the other charges Ossian.

## Pell Brisk
NG male human **commoner**.
A young carter who buried his mother at Merrow's Rest in spring and saw her at the chapel window last month. He waits at the lychgate and won't come in.
- **Personality.** Earnest, grieving, embarrassed by both.
- **Ideal.** Decency. His mother deserves a proper rest.
- **Bond.** His mother's blue burial ribbon; he tied it himself.
- **Flaw.** He follows the party below if nobody stops him (Stream Twist 3).

## Brother Ossian, the Real One
LG male human, deceased.
Forty years the keeper of Merrow's Rest. *Speak with dead* on his remains (none survive; try the ledger's first line, or any old burial who knew him) reports a kind, stubborn man who went down to see why the cellar smelled of lightning.
