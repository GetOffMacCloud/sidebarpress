# Bonus Objective: Pell's Mother

> [!info] Bonus objective at a glance
> **Estimated Duration:** 30 minutes, spread across all three scenes.
> **Location:** the lychgate (Scene 1), the Columbarium, C3 (Scene 2), the Saint's Ossuary, C5 (Scene 3), and back to the lychgate.
> **Prerequisites:** the party has met **Pell Brisk** (NG male human **commoner**), through the *missing mother* hook or at the lychgate in Scene 1.
> **Objectives:** find the Widow Brisk, keep Ossian from feeding on her, give her rest, and return her blue burial ribbon to Pell.

## Setup
Pell waits at the lychgate and won't set foot in the yard. He buried his mother in spring with a blue ribbon he tied himself, and last month he saw her at the chapel window, grey and hungry. He asks the party to look for a woman with a blue ribbon and, if she's what he thinks she is, to let her rest. He offers 50 gp, everything he has.

## Along the Way
- **Scene 1: the register.** The burial register ([[SP-OP01 — Handout — The Burial Register|Handout 2]]) lists *Brisk, widow*, buried in Planting, *taken below for rest*.
- **Scene 2: the ledger.** The larder ledger ([[SP-OP01 — Handout — The Larder Ledger|Handout 3]]) reads *Brisk, widow. Raised Planting 2nd. Tender by Harvest.*
- **Scene 2, C3: the empty niche.** Her urn niche in the Columbarium is labeled BRISK and holds nothing but a few threads of blue silk. Survival DC 12: drag marks lead toward the iron doors. Old Tam, if the party listens, says, "The widow's with the sexton. They're all with the sexton."

## The Widow in the Ossuary
**The Widow Brisk** (N female human **ghoul**) stands behind a sarcophagus lid with the Collier twins. She keeps the blue ribbon wrapped around her wrist.
- *Objectives/Goals:* her son mustn't see her like this; nobody else gets eaten.
- *What does she know?* Ossian feeds on the softest first, and she's next. She'd rather be ash.
- **Developments.**
  - If the party mentions Pell, she gives them the ribbon at once and asks for the ash.
  - If Ossian feeds on her, the ribbon falls into the black water. Perception DC 13 after the fight to find it; the objective can still be finished, but Pell gets the hard version of the news.
  - If the party arms her, she fights beside them (she's a ghoul: claws paralyze), and she asks for the ash the moment the fight ends.

## Completing the Objective
The objective is complete when the Widow Brisk has been laid to rest (ash, *gravecure*, or a clean death that Ossian didn't eat) and the ribbon is back in Pell's hands.

> *Pell turns the ribbon over and over. "She tied it to my wrist when I was small, at fairs, so she wouldn't lose me." He doesn't say anything else for a while.*

## Rewards
- **Pell's purse:** 50 gp (or, in Ritterstadt play, see below).
- **Inspiration:** each character who returned the ribbon gains inspiration.
- **Ritterstadt rules:** +70 extra credit each. See [[SP-OP01 — Ritterstadt Rewards]].

> [!example] Playing the Pillars
> **Combat:** Ossian goes for the Widow first when he's bloodied; a PC who stands over her is a target he has to go through. **Exploration:** the empty niche and the drag marks are the trail. **Social:** Pell at the lychgate, before and after, is the whole strand; give him room.
