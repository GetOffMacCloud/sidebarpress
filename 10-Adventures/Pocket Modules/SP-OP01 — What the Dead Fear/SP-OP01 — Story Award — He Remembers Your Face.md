# Story Award: He Remembers Your Face

**Earned when:** Brother Ossian flees the Saint's Ossuary alive (the *Ossian escapes* ending). Every character who fought him in Scene 3 earns it.

> **He Remembers Your Face.** *You stood in the black water and watched it go up the bone chute. It looked back. Somewhere on the roads, something wears a borrowed face and is grateful to everyone it meets, and it knows yours. Whenever a stranger thanks you a little too much, you'll wonder.*
>
> Note this award on your adventure log. While you have it, the DM may, once per adventure, have one grateful stranger turn out to be Ossian in a new face. You have advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks to see through the disguise of any creature that has eaten a face you knew. This award ends when Brother Ossian is slain.

## For the DM
- **What it opens.** The sequel seed *The Face on the Road* (main note). A table that carries this award into a later Pocket Module or home game has a recurring villain who already knows them.
- **How Ossian uses it.** He doesn't attack. He hires the party again, through a new face, and waits for them to bring him something.
- **Ending it.** If the drunaghul is slain in any later adventure, cross the award off and replace it with nothing; the relief is the reward.
- **Ritterstadt play.** The Registrar notes the award in the student's Conduct Record. See [[SP-OP01 — Ritterstadt Rewards]].
