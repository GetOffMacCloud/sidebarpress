# Handout 4: Ossian's Sketch

*Give to players at: Scene 1, beat 4 (the send-off). Ossian presses it into the hand of whoever carries the keys.*

![[SP-OP01 handout-ossians-sketch.png]]

## What It Shows (and What It Leaves Out)
Ossian drew this himself, in the same neat hand as the larder ledger. It's accurate where he wants the party to go and silent everywhere else.
- **Shown:** the stair, a long hall labeled *old passage (nothing of note)*, the Columbarium as *urn shelves (emptied for safety)*, the long passage south, the iron doors ("lift the beam") and the Saint's Ossuary with **URN HERE** marked on the altar.
- **Left off:** the embalming room and kitchen (C2) and its door; the skulls and the slime trail in the Bone Gallery (C1); the burial crawls; and the bone chute in the ossuary ceiling, which is how he means to arrive.

## Clues for the Table
- **The handwriting matches the larder ledger** (Handout 3). Anyone who has seen both notices on a passive Investigation 12, or automatically if they set the two side by side.
- **The missing door.** In the Bone Gallery, the door to C2 isn't on the map. Ossian, if asked later: "Oh, the old salting room. Nothing there but salt."
- **"Do not linger below."** He means it. He's watching the clock too.
