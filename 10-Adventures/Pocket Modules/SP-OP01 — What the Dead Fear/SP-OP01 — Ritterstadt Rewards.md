# Ritterstadt Rewards: What the Dead Fear

*For tables playing OP1 under the Greyhawk University rules of the Ritterstadt Field Station (RG-08 and [[The Tuition & Leveling Table]], rule set 2026-11-16). Everywhere else, use the treasure in the main note.*

> [!summary] Credits: 700 each
> **Base:** APL 7 × 100 = **700 credits to each student**, whatever their own level. **Ceiling:** no student takes more than **1,400** from this quest, extra credit included.

## The Commission
Ossian's notice reaches the Field Station's Job Board as a sanctioned commission: *clear the catacombs of Merrow's Rest and recover the Urn of Saint Merrow*. He pays the 600 gp fee to the Bursar, half in advance, so the students see none of it. He asked for students by name. He likes them young and far from home.

**The Loadout Package** (Requisition Office, issued to the party; unused items come back):
- 4 *potions of healing* and 1 *potion of greater healing*
- 2 *spell scrolls of lesser restoration* (ghoul, crawler and Ossian paralysis)
- 2 flasks of holy water, 1 antitoxin, a crowbar, 50 ft. of hempen rope
- Lost items are charged to the student who lost them, except under the **Lost in the Field** exception: anything dropped in the drain channel while grappled, or spent while *dominated*, is written off if the session log records it.

## Pay by Outcome
| Outcome | Pay each | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Full success:** Ossian slain | 700 (100%) | The Urn recovered or spent on the dead; the commission is honored even though the client was the monster. |
| **Partial:** Ossian escapes, the larder saved or at rest | 525 (75%) | Story award *He Remembers Your Face*. |
| **Partial:** the dead walk free, or the party retreats with the truth | 350 (50%) | The DM's call within 50–75%. Releasing ghouls draws a Proctor's hearing, not a fine. |
| **Failure or abandonment** | 0 | **Academic Probation**; the quest sits as an **Incomplete** until another party finishes it. |

## Extra Credit
Each line is paid to every student who earned it, on top of base, never past the 1,400 ceiling.

| Achievement | Credits |
|---|---|
| **Field report** on the drunaghul: what it is, how it feeds, what drives it off | +35 to +70 (5–10%) |
| **Pell's Mother** bonus objective completed ([[SP-OP01 — Bonus Objective — Pell's Mother]]) | +70 |
| **Mercy for the larder:** Ossian fed on none of the larder dead, and every one was laid to rest or saved | +70 |
| **Major bonus** (+10%): this quest is designated for **Natural Sciences** (a new aberration) and **Letters & Cultures** (burial rites, the saint's cult). It counts toward Boon 2's field reports. | +70 |
| **Historical Society** (members only): the founding of Merrow's Rest, the saint, and the real Brother Ossian documented from the burial register | +50 |
| **Cartography Boon 2** (Cartography majors with Boon 2 only): a corrected map of the catacombs, crawls included. Ossian's sketch doesn't count; it's his. | +25 per map |

**Lead credits**, each paid when the lead is written up in the field report:
- **The Face on the Road** (only if Ossian escaped): a description of the thing, and the road it took. +25
- **The Walled Crawls**: Harrow's report that "the others are still down there." Filed with the Scouts & Surveyors. +25

*Best case for one student:* 700 base + 70 report + 70 bonus + 70 mercy + 70 major + 50 Society + 25 map + 25 lead = 1,080, under the ceiling.

## Treasure Under University Rules
The University runs on credits, not coin. Everything recovered is logged with the Bursar.
- **Ossian's 300 gp advance** is commission money; it was paid to the Bursar, not the students. In this edition, the grave coins he paid are noted in the field report as evidence (Investigation DC 12 in Scene 1).
- **The grave goods** (900 gp in rings, buckles and eye-coins) belong to the dead. The University returns them to the families on the road. Pocketing one is *selling research property*, a standing offence heard by the Proctor.
- **Buy-Back Clause.** A student who wants to keep an item petitions for Research Rights, pays credits equal to its value, and writes an in-character justification.

## Standing and Records
- **Semester count.** OP1 is a standard sanctioned quest. It counts as one of the **four quests** a student needs between Capstones.
- **Not a Capstone.** It's posted openly and pays credits. To run it as one, rebuild it with `90-Templates/Capstone.md` for 6th-level Candidates at APL 7 (Candidates' level + 1), sealed by a Department Head, with no credit pay.
- **Story award.** *He Remembers Your Face* goes in each student's Conduct Record. The Registrar flags the file; the faculty take it seriously.
- **Pace.** At 700 a quest, a 6th-level student (6,000 cumulative) needs about six full-success quests to reach the 10,000 for 7th, then the Capstone.
