# Scene 01 — The Grateful Mortician

[[10-Adventures/Pocket Modules/SP-OP01 — What the Dead Fear/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 02 — The Catacombs]]

> [!info] Scene at a glance
> **Estimated Duration:** 60 minutes. Running long? Skip the burial register; describe the backward fingers outright.
> **Location:** the chapel and graveyard of [[Merrow's Rest]], at noon.
> **Prerequisites:** none. Any hook brings the party to the lychgate.
> **Objectives:** take the job; notice at least three things wrong with Brother Ossian; survive the Graveyard Rising. *Bonus:* meet Pell Brisk at the lychgate ([[SP-OP01 — Bonus Objective — Pell's Mother|Pell's Mother]]).

## Purpose
Give the party the job, the advance and at least three clues that [[Brother Ossian]] is not what he seems, without proving it.

## Arrival
> *A squat stone chapel. A lychgate hung with funeral bells. Fresh earth on every grave in the yard. Candles burn in the nave although it's noon. A hunched brother in black wool hurries out with his hands spread. "Oh, thank the Saint. Thank you. Thank you for coming."*

**To the table:** *"He's holding your hand a beat too long. Who pulls away first?"*

## Area Information
- **Dimensions & Terrain:** The nave is 40 ft. wide and 60 ft. long, with a 20-ft. vestry off the east side and the apse to the north. The trapdoor and the bone drop are in the apse floor behind the altar. The graveyard is about 80 ft. square inside a waist-high wall, with the lychgate on the south side.
- **Lighting:** Bright daylight in the yard. Candlelit (dim light) in the nave and vestry, even at noon.
- **Sounds & smells:** Funeral bells that stir with no wind; beeswax over something sweeter.

## Creatures/NPCs
**[[Brother Ossian]]** (NE drunaghul **Brother Ossian (Drunaghul)**, wearing a LG male human's face).
- *Objectives/Goals:* get the party below tonight, and get them past the gallery without looking up.
- *What does he know?* Everything. He lies superbly; see Clues.
- *Reacts:* to suspicion with more gratitude; to open accusation with *suggestion*.

**Pell Brisk** (NG male human **commoner**), at the lychgate.
- *Objectives/Goals:* to know his mother is at peace.
- *What does he know?* He saw her at the chapel window last month, grey and hungry. Ossian told him grief plays tricks.

**The chapel cat and the yard dog.** They know.

## Beats
1. **The welcome.** Ossian pours wine in the vestry and tells his story: the dead began to rise in spring, he bars the catacomb door every night, and "something worse" carried the saint's urn below. The urn holds Saint Merrow's ashes and *must* come back up.
2. **The offer.** 600 gp, half now, and a ring of keys. The 300 gp advance is real. Investigation DC 12: some of the coins are grave coins, the kind laid on a dead man's eyes.
3. **The toast.** He raises a cup "to the brave and the quiet dead". This is the moment for the fingers.
4. **The send-off.** He walks them to the trapdoor behind the altar, unbars it, and says he'll pray for them. He presses a neat sketch of the catacombs into the hand of whoever carries the keys ([[SP-OP01 — Handout — Ossian's Sketch|Handout 4]]): "So you don't lose your way." He doesn't follow. (He does: by the bone chute, later.)

## Clues
Ossian lies superbly (Deception +12). Insight reads nothing but grief unless the check reaches 20. His body gives him away. Drop at least three.

| Clue | How the party notices |
|---|---|
| He never blinks. | Anyone who holds his gaze for a minute |
| His skin is too wet in the candlelight, like a peeled egg. | Perception DC 12 |
| He smells of sweet rot and the tang of air after lightning, strongest up close. | Passive Perception 13 |
| When he raises a toast, his fingers fold backward around the cup. | Perception DC 15, or just describe it |
| In the burial register ([[SP-OP01 — Handout — The Burial Register|Handout 2]]), every burial since spring is marked "taken below for rest." Nobody has been interred below since the founding. | Investigation DC 13 or Religion DC 12 |
| The chapel cat won't enter the vestry, and the yard dog won't stop shaking. | Animal Handling DC 10 |
| A round black hole in the apse floor beside the trapdoor. Ossian calls it "the bone drop, for the old remains." | Anyone who looks. Investigation DC 15: the rim is slick with slime, and the shaft below is clean, with no bones in it. |
| Ossian casts *detect thoughts* on a PC. | Wisdom save DC 16. On a success, the PC feels an oily pressure behind the eyes, coming from Ossian's direction. |

## Fight: The Graveyard Rising (Medium)
Ossian has staged a demonstration. As he raises his toast, the lychgate bells ring and the old graves in the yard heave open. Mindless husks (**zombies**) from decades-old burials lurch toward the chapel, with hulking **ogre zombies** in rotted burial linen behind them. Ossian grabs a lantern and "fights" beside the party, with wild swings that never land.

**Clue:** the husks walk past Ossian as if he weren't there (Perception DC 13, or automatic for anyone who watches him for a round). Afterward he's shaken and more grateful than ever: "You see? You *see* what I live with?"

## Developments
- **Unmasked early.** *Detect evil and good* and Divine Sense find an **aberration** where he stands. If the party unmasks him here, he smiles, thanks them for the conversation, and casts *suggestion*: "Go down. Bring me the urn. Then we can talk." If that fails, he fights in the nave without his larder and flees up the bone chute behind the altar once he's bloodied. Go straight to [[Scene 03 — What the Dead Fear]]'s statblock and endings; the dead below can still be saved.
- **Pell Brisk hook.** Pell waits at the lychgate and won't come in. He asks the party to look for a woman with a blue burial ribbon, and offers 50 gp. This starts the bonus objective.
- **The party compares handwriting.** Ossian's sketch and the larder ledger (Scene 2) are in the same hand. Nothing happens now; it pays off below.
- **The party refuses the job.** Ossian thanks them anyway, lets them shelter, and the bells ring at midnight: run the Graveyard Rising, then let him ask again.

## Treasure & Rewards
- **Ossian's advance:** 300 gp in grave coins (Investigation DC 12 to notice what they are).
- **Handouts:** the burial register (Handout 2) and Ossian's sketch (Handout 4).

> [!example] Playing the Pillars
> **Combat:** the Graveyard Rising is a demonstration; let the party feel strong, and let them watch the husks ignore Ossian. **Exploration:** the bone drop, the register and the animals reward anyone who wanders off from the welcome. **Social:** the toast is the scene. Let Ossian talk too long and touch too much.

## Sensory Palette
Beeswax and something sweeter under it. Wet wool. Funeral bells that stir with no wind. A cup that's warm where his fingers were.
