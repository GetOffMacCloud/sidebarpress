# Scene 03 — What the Dead Fear

[[10-Adventures/Pocket Modules/SP-OP01 — What the Dead Fear/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — The Catacombs]]

> [!info] Scene at a glance
> **Estimated Duration:** 60–90 minutes. Running long? Ossian flees at 60 hp instead of 40.
> **Location:** the Saint's Ossuary (C5), beyond the iron doors.
> **Prerequisites:** the party has lifted the beam on the iron doors (or Ossian has come for them in C4; see Scene 2's Developments).
> **Objectives:** hear Harrow's truth; survive the trap; decide what happens to the larder. *Bonus:* lay the Widow Brisk to rest and take her ribbon.

## Purpose
Deliver the truth, spring the trap, and make the party choose what to do with the larder in the middle of the fight.

## Arrival
> *Beyond the doors, a long vault lies under ankle-deep black water. Stone sarcophagi stand in two rows. Every lid has been heaved off and set on end, like a shield wall. Behind them are grey faces. A dozen eyes catch your light, and a voice like a shovel in gravel says, "Close it. Please. Before he hears."*

**To the table:** *"You came down here to kill them. What do you do?"*

## Area Information
- **Dimensions & Terrain:** 40 × 60 ft., 25-ft. ceiling, ankle-deep black water. Full details under *The Arena* below.
- **Lighting:** Darkness, except what the party brings.
- **Sounds & smells:** Black water, bone dust, ghouls whispering *shh, shh* to each other.

## Creatures/NPCs
**[[Sexton Harrow]]** (LN male human **wight**).
- *Objectives/Goals:* rest for his people, by ash rather than teeth.
- *What does he know?* The whole truth: the brother isn't a man, he raised them, he hangs them, he eats them, and he can't fit through the crawls. "He came up the crawls. He said the others are still down there."
- *Reacts:* to kindness with the truth; to drawn steel by raising empty hands.

**The larder:** the Widow Brisk (N female human **ghoul**, blue burial ribbon), the Collier twins (N male human **ghouls**), and Old Tam (N male human **ghoul**) if he made it back. See [[SP-OP01 — Appendix — NPC Summary]].
- *Objectives/Goals:* not to be eaten. Most of them would rather be ash.
- *What do they know?* Ossian feeds on the softest first.

**[[Brother Ossian]]** (NE drunaghul **Brother Ossian (Drunaghul)**), unseen in the bone chute until the trap springs. **Pallbearers** (**ogre zombies**) wait in the chute alcove.

## Beats
1. **Harrow's truth.** If the party asks, Harrow tells it. The brother isn't a man. He raised them, he hangs them, and he eats them, and he can't fit through the crawls. The urn on the altar is all they have: "The saint's ash puts us down clean. Better than his teeth." They've begged each other to use it, and none of them could.
2. **The trap.** It springs when the party has heard enough, after 1 minute of talk, or the moment a PC touches the urn. The iron doors slam and a bar drops across them on the inside. Something unfolds from the bone chute in the ceiling, 25 feet up.

> *Ten feet of glistening worm-flesh, six claw-tipped limbs, and a vertical lamprey mouth ringed with teeth. Brother Ossian's gentle voice comes out of it. "Thank you. They'd hidden so well. And you brought dessert."*

3. **The fight** (below).
4. **The ending** (see [[SP-OP01-What-the-Dead-Fear#Concluding the Adventure]]).

**Pallbearers** are ogre zombies in burial cloth. They wait in the chute alcove and drop down on round 2. Statblocks: [[SP-OP01 — Bestiary]]. Map: [[SP-OP01 — Map Assets]].

### The Arena: the Saint's Ossuary (40 × 60 ft., 25-ft. ceiling)
- **Black water.** Ankle-deep and slick. A creature that takes the Dash or Disengage action, or is shoved, must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity save or fall prone. Ossian ignores this.
- **Sarcophagi (8) and their lids.** Each gives half cover. A Small creature crouched behind a lid has full cover.
- **Bone shelves** line both long walls. As an action, a creature adjacent to a shelf can topple a 10-ft. section of it (Athletics DC 13). Each creature in a 15-ft. line makes a DC 14 Dexterity save, taking 13 (3d10) bludgeoning damage on a failure or half as much on a success. The line becomes difficult terrain, and anything climbing that section of wall falls.
- **Drain channel.** 5 ft. wide and 5 ft. deep along the east wall. Ossian drags grappled prey into it, where they're underwater.
- **Bone chute.** A 5-ft. shaft in the center of the ceiling, leading up to the chapel. It's how he comes and goes.
- **The doors.** Lifting the bar takes an action and an Athletics DC 15 check. Ossian makes it his business to stay near them.
- **The altar and the urn.** The urn holds 1d4 + 2 handfuls of the saint's ash.

### The Larder
Each larder undead is food. As a bonus action, Ossian can **Feed** on one within 5 ft. of him that is grappled, incapacitated, or not hostile to him: it's destroyed, and he regains 15 hit points (25 for Harrow). That gives the party three answers:
- **Protect them.** Fight in front of them. A ghoul can squeeze into a crawl as an action, where Ossian can't reach.
- **Arm them.** With a Persuasion DC 13 check (advantage if the party has been kind to the dead), they fight beside the party. Armed undead are hostile to Ossian, so he can feed on them only once they're grappled or paralyzed. Ghoul claws can paralyze Ossian.
- **Lay them to rest.** As an action, a creature within 5 ft. of a larder undead scatters a handful of ash on it. It crumbles to dust at peace, and Ossian can't feed on it. Harrow will ask for this.

### Tactics
1. **Round 1.** Ossian casts *dominate person* on the hardest hitter ("Protect the feast") and claws the nearest caster.
2. **Grapple and drag.** He hauls a grappled PC behind a sarcophagus, into the channel, or up the wall, anywhere the healer can't reach.
3. **Feed** whenever he's bloodied and a meal is in reach. He scuttles across the ceiling to get to one.
4. **Flee** at 40 hit points or fewer, up the bone chute unless it's blocked. He remembers faces.

**DM direction:** Narrate the dead. Every time Ossian moves toward a ghoul, the ghoul screams a PC's name, if it learned one. Make the larder a person, not a resource.

## Developments
- **The party unmasks the trap early** (searches the ceiling, *detect thoughts*, watches the chute): Ossian drops in immediately and loses his round-1 *dominate person*.
- **The party arms the larder:** the fight gets easier and the ghouls get a say in the ending.
- **The party leaves with the urn and the doors open:** Ossian feeds at leisure. He reaches full hit points, follows them up the chute, and fights them in the nave.
- **Ossian flees:** see *He Remembers Your Face* below.

## Treasure & Rewards
- **On the chute ledge** (Athletics DC 15 to climb, or reached by flight): 900 gp in rings, buckles and eye-coins, a *cloak of protection*, a *periapt of wound closure*, 2 *potions of greater healing*, and a *spell scroll of* [[Gravecure|gravecure]].
- **[[The Urn of Saint Merrow]]** on the altar.
- **Story award:** [[SP-OP01 — Story Award — He Remembers Your Face|He Remembers Your Face]], if Ossian escapes.
- **Bonus objective:** the Widow Brisk's blue ribbon ([[SP-OP01 — Bonus Objective — Pell's Mother]]).
- **Ritterstadt play:** [[SP-OP01 — Ritterstadt Rewards]].

> [!example] Playing the Pillars
> **Combat:** Ossian grapples, drags and feeds; a party that guards the larder and watches the doors beats him. **Exploration:** the bone chute, the drain channel and the toppling shelves are the terrain that wins or loses the fight. **Social:** Harrow's truth, before the trap, is the reveal; let the players ask, and let him answer plainly.

## Sensory Palette
Black water to the ankle. The wet slap of six limbs on stone. Bone dust sifting from the ceiling. Ghouls whispering *shh, shh* to each other.

## Transition
Back up the stair, into a chapel where every candle has gone out. See [[SP-OP01-What-the-Dead-Fear#Concluding the Adventure]].
