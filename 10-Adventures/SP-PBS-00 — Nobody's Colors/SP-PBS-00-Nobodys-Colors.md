# Nobody's Colors

**APL 3 (levels 1–4) · 4–6 characters · one 4-hour session**

> *A family with no clan tries to buy a name with a horse, and hires strangers to carry the lie across a lake.*
>
> A Social / Travel / Heist one-shot for **average party level 1–4** (written for APL 3, with scaling for 1, 2 and 4). Broke would-be students, stranded on Lake Quag's western shore on their way to Greyhawk University, take a job walking a rich boy's "baggage" to the Duffkyyn Derby at Schwartzenbruin. The baggage is a racehorse. The boy forged his father's seal to enter her, the colors are rented from a dead house, and a patient moneylender has paid to see her lose. It is the **prequel** to the Ritterstadt line: whatever the party does, it ends at the northern packet with a patron's letter for the Registrar.

## Design Frame
- **Thesis:** A name can't be bought, but it can be earned in front of witnesses. The Halbritters try to buy a place among the clans with a horse; the only thing that works is a daughter riding in her own name in a dead house's silks.
- **Expectation broken:** *Escort duty is a fight on the road.* The road fight is avoidable and should be. The real danger is paper: a forged seal, a rented color, an unsigned book, and a sponsor who has run.
- **Second break:** the people who *should* make the decisions can't. Corwin won't offer the ride, Adalinde won't ask for it, and Otwin can't be told. The players have to say it.
- **What this adventure refuses to do:** Nobody in it needs killing. The man who wins never touches a weapon. Every hostile is a working person with cousins.
- **Session spine:** the mare's **Condition** (Sound / Sore / Lame), set by Krul's **Sabotage Clock** on the boat and the **Spook die** in the stable rows. See [[SP-PBS-00 — The Mare, the Clock and the Cord]].
- **Tone:** fourteenth-century Alpine town drama. Bells for the hour, clan colors on doorposts, small beer, and a law for everything.

## Perrenland Color
*Canon framing; see Canon Check.*

- **Clan bands.** Every free house paints its clan colors on the doorpost at shoulder height: Meerijder red-over-black, Roodberg blue-over-white, Vuurzward a green so dark it reads as black. A clanless family may paint nothing and wear only undyed cloth, under the *Kleiderordnung* (the dress-law). The Halbritters wear grey. *(The clans are Living Greyhawk tier; the bands and dress-law are homebrew.)*
- **Titles.** *Herr* (a landed man), *Junker* (his son), *Jungfer* (his unmarried daughter), *Ratsherr* (a town councilor), *Schultheiss* (a town's chief officer), *Amtmann* (a steward). Hessel Amtmann's surname is not his job; he is sergeant of the house guard, and Aerden has been asking him for the keys for fifteen years.
- **Names and register.** Perrender speech is mixed in canon (a *Voormann* rules from Schwartzenbruin). The clans and the capital use the German civic forms above. The Kettenhaus and Traft use the Dutch forms of [[The Kettenhaus]] note (*Kettenmeester*, *kontor*, *van Traft*).
- **Feud is lawful.** A clan may declare *Fehde* against a house that wronged it, and the killing that follows is not murder. Killing a clansman without a declared feud is murder, and his clan pursues it for a generation.
- **The Derby peace.** Richfest week on the Flats is under a *Landfrieden*. At the gate, the stewards' men knot a **red cord** through every hilt and scabbard and seal it with wax. Cutting your own cord is a crime; cutting it and drawing is a hanging. Schwartzenbruin's company taverns took the cord from the Derby, which is why [[The Red Cord]] ties blades at its door (see [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]]).
- **Money.** Perrenders count in **marks** (gp), **kleinmarks** (sp) and **pfennigs** (cp). A laborer earns 3 kleinmarks a day; a good horse is 75 marks.
- **Paper.** A debt is a *Schuldbrief*, sealed and witnessed. Forging a seal on one is a hand-cutting offense.
- **The Great Dying.** Thirty years ago a pox took one in three across the lake cantons. Whole houses died, and their colors are still on the Roodberg register because no one has had the heart to strike them. That is why a broker can rent a dead family's silks. *(Homebrew.)*
- **The patrol.** Schwartzenbruin meets every boat at its lake-gate and writes every answer down (see [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]]).

## What the Locals Know
- **In Aerden:** the grey house at the top of the hill breeds honest horses and has no colors. The daughter runs the yard. The son has a new saddle he lets no one see. Sergeant Amtmann has never bought his own ale at the Mountainview Rest in fifteen years. Sivert Vuurzward wants the farm and has said so in the market.
- **On the boat:** Rhennee barges shadow the shore in summer. Nobody sells bloodstock to bargefolk, and the barges never ask why.
- **On the Flats:** a farm mare nobody has heard of is entered under Ostvelt green, a house that died in the Great Dying. She opened at twenty to one. Somebody is laying money against her.

## What's Really Happening
*Homebrew; see Canon Check.*

**The Halbritters.** Three generations ago they came up from Nederboden with money and no clan, bought **Erlenhof**, bred honest horses, and earned a seat on Aerden's council — the most a clanless family can reach. In Schwartzenbruin the clans hold the Derby, the sale ring and the canton remount contracts. No Halbritter horse has ever run at Duffkyyn in Halbritter colors, because there are none.

Two years ago **[[Adalinde Halbritter]]** bred a grey mare out of the old Erlenhof line, the best horse the farm has had in forty years. Stable name **[[Maus]]**. Adalinde broke her, trained her, and is by far the better rider of the two siblings. She has wanted to enter the mare at Duffkyyn since the day she was foaled and has never said so.

**[[Otwin Halbritter]]** has settled the farm's future: a three-year contract for thirty remounts to the canton levy, to be signed before the remount commissioners at the Schwartzenbruin *Rathaus* at noon on Derby Day. It puts Maus in a levy string carrying a crossbowman.

**[[Corwin Halbritter]]** forged his father's seal on [[The Erlen Schuldbrief|a Schuldbrief]] against the Erlen bridge tolls — 400 marks — and paid the entry. Having no colors, he rented a set from a Schwartzenbruin broker, [[Jorick Weisel]]: [[The Ostvelt Silks|Ostvelt green]]. The mare is entered as *Ostvelt Grey*. Corwin means to ride her himself. He isn't good enough, and some part of him knows it.

**Two Derby rules the adventure turns on.**
1. **Colors.** Every entry runs under colors on the Roodberg register. Brokers rent dead houses' colors, and the stewards allow it, but a **living sponsor of record** must sign the book in the stewards' tent before post time.
2. **Rider.** The **sponsor of record names the rider.** Weisel named Corwin. To change the rider, someone must change the nomination, and Weisel means to be nowhere.

**[[Sivert Vuurzward]]** holds the note. He lends money, breeds horses, and wants Erlenhof: the land, the bloodline and the grey mare who comes with the farm if it falls. He knows the seal is forged. He needs only to say in the *Rathaus*, "Herr Halbritter, is this your seal?" Otwin cannot answer no without naming his son a forger before the whole canton. So a 400-mark paper controls a farm worth ten times that. Vuurzward needs the mare to lose, and has arranged it.

**Whose money it is.** Sivert bought the note on Kettenhaus credit, and the House of the Chain holds his paper on it: a Schuldbrief behind a Schuldbrief. This year the Kettenhaus's patron clan, the Hussen, has taken the Voormannship, and the house is calling in paper across the cantons while the window is open. Sivert is the face; the Kettenhaus is the chain. Nothing here needs the party to find out. See [[The Kettenhaus]].

**[[Hessel Amtmann]]** posted the job because the house guard answers to Otwin. He is Corwin's accomplice, and the leak. He married a Vuurzward woman and owes 60 marks on a Schuldbrief her brother holds (Kettenhaus paper, though Hessel doesn't know it). Ten days ago, the day Corwin booked passage, Hessel sold the route, the boat and the date to have it struck out.

**The morning of Day 1.** Old Hartwin the tollkeeper walks up to the house to ask why a stranger in a dark Traft coat was out yesterday dragging a surveyor's chain across the Erlen bridge. (He was a Kettenhaus *Schätzer*, pricing the collateral.) Otwin finds out at dawn. Corwin's wagons left at first light. Otwin, who cannot ride for the gout, sends a boy on a pony to the Meerijder drovers camped at the bridge, with a writ.

## Vuurzward's Scheme
What happens if nobody interferes:
1. **Ten days before:** Hessel sells the route. Vuurzward hires [[Anse Krul]], a farrier, for 40 marks, half paid at Osterschlauss, and opens an account laying money against Ostvelt Grey.
2. **On the lake:** Krul boards the *Kortheim Maid* and pricks the mare's hoof in the small hours of the second night. She walks fine and runs as if on coals.
3. **On the Flats:** if she is still sound, his bravos try again in the stable rows on Derby eve.
4. **At post time:** with no sponsor's signature, the line is struck, or Corwin rides a sore mare to the back of the field.
5. **Day 6:** the note falls due. Erlenhof goes to the sale ring, and the mare with it.

**If the PCs never come:** Corwin gets nine miles before Boedic's drovers stop him at the bridge. Otwin takes the mare home, Maus goes into the levy string, and Vuurzward buys the note's foreclosure the following year.

## Hook
1. **The notice (default).** At vespers in the [[Aerden|Mountainview Rest]], Sergeant Amtmann pins a notice to the post: escort duty, Aerden to Schwartzenbruin, passage paid, thirty marks on arrival. See [[SP-PBS-00 — Handout — The Notice]].
2. **Grete's arrangement.** [[Grete Haslau]] has kept the party alive for three weeks on work-for-board. She tells them the job exists before Amtmann posts it: *"He'll come in tonight and pin something up. Read it before the drovers do."*
3. **Personal stake.** A PC with a Perrender surname is claimed as clan kin by somebody in the first ten minutes, or a PC carries a debt that Vuurzward has quietly bought.
4. **Decline path.** If the party refuses the job, Adalinde finds them at the Rest that night with her own money and a straighter story, and comes with them.

## PC Objectives (the plot, as goals rather than events)
1. **Find out what is in the third wagon.** *(Open it, read Adalinde, hear Boedic say "horse," or ask Tilo about the saddle.)*
2. **Get Corwin and the mare across the Erlen bridge.** *(Clan kinship, the jurisdiction argument, a bribe, a counter-writ, the cattle, night, or a fight.)*
3. **Get a racehorse aboard a passage booked for one man's baggage.** *(Pay Vosk, bribe the clerk, forge the roll, load at the wrong bell.)*
4. **Cross the lake with the saboteur aboard.** *(Stand watch; spot Krul; stop the clock.)*
5. **Get the entry signed.** *(Find Weisel, find a new sponsor, or win Roodberg.)*
6. **Keep the mare sound through Derby eve.** *(The stable rows.)*
7. **Settle who rides.** *(Say it, because nobody else will.)*
8. *(Optional)* **Find out who sold the route.** *(The ten days.)*

## Subplots (keyed to PCs; assign at the table)
- **Perrender surname:** claimed as Meerijder, Roodberg or Vuurzward kin. Boedic's drovers won't raise a hand to kin, and doors open at the Flats. Each use is a favor spent.
- **Knows horses** (animal-handling background or proficiency): the only one besides Adalinde who can tell the mare has been got at, and when.
- **Carries a debt:** Vuurzward has bought it by Scene 5. He mentions it once, kindly, and never again. It is why he makes his offer to *them*.
- **Trade, coin or bookkeeping:** reads Otwin's ledger in a minute (Scene 2) and the odds-book anomaly on sight (Scene 5).
- **Thief, forger or liar:** owns Scene 3.

## Treasure & Rewards
**Getting to Ritterstadt.** Admission costs only a signature on the Scholar's Oath. The northern packet from Schwartzenbruin is one night along the shore and **20 marks**. The Registrar asks a **first-term bond of 80 marks** in coin, or a term of labor on the levy (the Drill Yard, the Archive, the gate rota). **A hundred marks each arrives clear.** *(Recorded in [[RG-08 — Greyhawk University, Ritterstadt Field Station]], both vaults, 2026-10-23.)*

**Fixed.** 30 marks each, 40 if negotiated, paid at the Schwartzenbruin quay. Passage Osterschlauss–Schwartzenbruin (40 marks' worth) already paid. One uncommon item from the Derby prize table, below.

**The Derby prize table.** By Duffkyyn custom, the company that brought a starter to the post draws one prize from the stewards' chest at the sale ring ([[Scene 08 — The Sale Ring]]). It goes to the party, not to each PC. Any branch where the mare reached the Flats under their escort qualifies, run or scratched; the Rhennee sale does not. Roll d6.

**Earned.**
- **Purse share.** Second place pays the owner 500 marks. "One part in five" (Scene 1) is 100 marks to split.
- **Winnings.** 40 marks staked at 4-to-1 to place returns 160 profit plus the stake.
- **Otwin's gratitude.** Where Adalinde rides and the farm stands, Otwin pays each PC's northern packet and half the bond, **60 marks a head**, out of his own strongbox, the first money in fifteen years he hasn't counted twice: *"The Rathaus clerk tells me the northern packet leaves Godsday. You'll be on it. Don't thank me; I'm buying your absence from my daughter's stable."*
- **The Rhennee sale.** 600 marks on the second night. Four hundred clears the note; Corwin gives the party the two hundred over. It costs the Halbritters their name.

**The patron's letter (Extra Credit, outside the 600 limit).** Adalinde brings it on almost every branch: a **letter of field expertise** from a Roodberg breeding patron, addressed to [[Registrar Hedwig Ammerlin]]. It does not open the door; the University takes anyone who signs the Oath. It counts once they are inside. **10 extra credit on its face, plus 10 for each commendation, three at most (40).** See [[SP-PBS-00 — Handout — The Patron's Letter]].

## Denouement
See [[Scene 08 — The Sale Ring]]. Otwin watched the last two furlongs from the public stand and told no one. Krul's "ten days," the odds-book account and Grete's ale line name Hessel Amtmann. Whether to tell Otwin is the last decision, and it is the party's. The northern packet leaves on Godsday.
