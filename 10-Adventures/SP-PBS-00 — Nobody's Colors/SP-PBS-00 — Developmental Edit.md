# SP-PBS-00 — Developmental Edit

Back to [[SP-PBS-00-Nobodys-Colors]] · Board: [[SP-PBS-00 — Board]]

**How to use:** tick the findings you want applied. Claude applies them and marks each "✅ Applied <date>".

## Provenance
SP-PBS-00 was built on 2026-10-23 from the A Tourist In Greyhawk draft `0008 — Nobody's Colours`, after that draft's developmental edit (`0008 - Developmental Edit.md` in the Greyhawk University vault). Twelve findings were applied there (frame, date, Schwartzenbruin, the money, the patron's letter, the Kettenhaus, the Amtmann rename, register, canon tiers, Traft, Krul's three rounds, the mastiff).

### Changed in the move to Sidebar Press
- **Crossing matched to SP-PBS-03:** the northern packet is one night along the shore, 20 marks. To keep the money pressure, the first-term bond is now **80 marks** (a house figure). A hundred marks still arrives clear.
- **The red cord is one custom:** the Derby's Landfrieden cord is the origin of the Red Cord tavern's rule. Cross-linked from [[The Red Cord]] and [[Schwartzenbruin]].
- **Patron's letter rescaled** to SP-PBS-03's reward size: 10 extra credit, plus 10 per commendation, three at most (40). No XP.
- **Canon upgraded** from the SP Lore notes: pfennigs, and longbows and bow-armed cavalry in Clatspurgen and Traft, are LGG canon. Roodberg and Vuurzward are Living Greyhawk tier, not house.
- **Written for APL 3**, with APL 1–4 tables rated for 4 PCs. Bridge numbers kept at your ruling (Deadly on paper, avoidable by design).
- **Pim → [[Tilo]]**, so Aerden's yard boy doesn't share a name with Pim Wobblewright.
- **The Traft road dropped** from the reward branches: with a 20-mark packet everyone can sail. The Quag Road on foot stays as the free option in the prologue.
- **Clue Ledger built** (house requirement): five conclusions, each with three or more routes and a last resort. This also applies the draft's D2 (Hessel's three roads, via Grete's ale and Tilo's word about the sergeant's wife) and D3.
- **Vuurzward can be exposed** (Krul plus the odds-book before Roodberg bars his horses; the note still stands). This replaces "He is not beaten this session" (the draft's D5).
- **Small fixes carried:** "Nobody in nineteen years" is now "Nobody ever thought to ask" (C6); the fourth-place and failed-loading branches are written into Scenes 3, 5 and 7 (C7, C8); Corwin has a block (E3).
