# SP-PBS-00 — Developmental Edit (structure, pacing, railroad check)

Back to [[SP-PBS-00-Nobodys-Colors]] · Board: [[SP-PBS-00 — Board]] · Run sheet: [[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-00 — Nobody's Colors/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]]

> [!info] How to use this note
> Tick the box next to each finding you want applied, then ask Claude to "apply the ticked fixes." Untick, or strike through, anything you reject. The board's *Developmental edit* card stays open until the Major findings are resolved.

## Verdict
The structure holds. It is a situation, not a plot: every decision on the decision map belongs to the party, the climax has six routes seeded in earlier scenes, and there are six lettered endings, including the defeat and sale branches. The 4-hour budget adds up (Act 1 75, Act 2 55, Act 3 90, plus seating and a break: 4:05, with a written cut list). Four things need fixing before release: one geography error, one rules contradiction, one branch that has no play in it, and one clue that doesn't quite follow.

**Classic-module musts: 11 of 11 applicable pass** (keyed-entry order is n/a for a scene-based one-shot).

**Prose checklist (automated pass):** every scene read-aloud is ≤100 words and one paragraph, with no "seems," "appears," or PC feelings. The only flag is "a roll of paper" in Scene 3's box, which is the cargo roll, not a mechanic: no change. The read-aloud-at-the-table item still needs you.

## Findings

### Major
- [x] **1 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-23.* Geography — the Quag Road goes the wrong way.** Scene 0 offers "the Quag Road north on foot, a week" as the free route to Ritterstadt. Per [[Schwartzenbruin]], the Quag Road runs from Highfolk through the city and north along the lake's *western* shore, toward nomad country, not to Ritterstadt across the water. **Fix:** "The free way is the shore road east to Traft and four days north on the caravan track: two weeks on foot, and nobody's idea of safe." That matches the Regional Guide (Traft is four days south of Ritterstadt).
- [x] **2 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-23.* Rules — the Sabotage Clock contradicts itself.** The mechanic note says "No watch set, or the watch failed to notice: one mark already placed," then "No watch at all: three rounds." **Fix:** "**A watch that fails to notice him:** he starts with one mark. **No watch at all:** no roll; he gets his three rounds." Also add the multi-saboteur rule for Scene 6: *one mark per round in which at least one saboteur works at the stall uninterrupted.*
- [x] **3 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-23.* Branch — the Rhennee sale has no Act 3.** Scene 4 says "Scenes 5–7 play as spectators." That is 75 minutes with nothing to decide. It also skips who may sell: Maus is Otwin's horse, and Corwin selling her is a second crime. **Fix:** write the sold branch as a short Act 3:
  - Scene 5: Roodberg strikes the Ostvelt line. Vuurzward learns the mare went to the barges and calls in the note at once; the 400 still clears it.
  - Scene 7 becomes a 10-minute beat: Otwin and Adalinde come off the morning packet to an empty stall. Adalinde's line: *"You sold her to the water."* Then the letter, which she still delivers.
  - Cut Scene 6.
  - Bank the time for the party to spend in the city, and say so in the run sheet.
- [x] **4 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-23.* Clue — Hessel's ale doesn't follow.** Scene 0 makes "he buys his own ale tonight" the tell that Hessel has money he didn't have. But Hessel sold the route to have a debt *struck out*, not for coin. **Fix:** Grete's line becomes *"He's never bought his own ale in this house in fifteen years. Every market day for a year he's paid a Vuurzward man at the well instead. Not this week."* Tilo can confirm it for a pfennig. The clue becomes the debt stopping, which is what actually happened.

### Minor

- [x] **6 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-23.* Krul at the rows.** The Scene 6 table says "+ Krul (if he escaped)", but Krul is also free if Vosk put him ashore or the party let him go. **Fix:** "+ Krul (if he is free)."

### Major (logic and continuity)
- [ ] **CE1 · Otwin can't be in the stewards' tent on Richfest 3.** S3 Development says that if the mare went on the roll, Otwin "knows by Day 3" and is waiting in the stewards' tent in Scene 5 (the morning of Richfest 3). The *Maid* sails Wealsun 28 and lands at dusk on Richfest 2; the next boat, the *Agal Wind*, sails Richfest 1 and lands at dusk on Richfest 3; Otwin can't ride. The roll is also "read aloud every Godsday", which ties his finding out to a weekday rather than to Day 3. **Fix:** Ebbo sends the roll up to Aerden with the evening carter, so Otwin hears of it the night of Day 1, takes the *Agal Wind*, and arrives at dusk on Richfest 3. Move his "sitting in the corner with his ledger" beat from S5 Development to the top of S6 (he is at the stewards' tent when the party walks to the rows). In S7 Alternates, change "since Scene 5" to "since Derby eve".
- [ ] **CE2 · Which way the lake lies.** S3's read-aloud box says the lake is "filling the whole of the north"; S4's Sensory Palette has "grey fog to the north"; S6's Transition has "a packet coming in from the east". But S3 and S4 keep the shore "on the right hand" on the way to Schwartzenbruin, the main note puts Aerden on the western shore in the Yatil foothills, and the barges stand "off the port rail". On those facts the boat sails south with open water to the east, and Aerden's packet comes from the north. **Fix:** S3 "filling the whole of the east"; S4 "grey fog off the port rail"; S6 "a packet coming down from the north". *Overrule this if the region map says otherwise.*
- [ ] **CE3 · The odds move the wrong way.** S5: "opened at twenty to one this morning and has shortened to fourteen", while someone is laying money against her. Money laid against a horse lengthens its price; it doesn't shorten it. **Fix:** "Ostvelt Grey opened at twenty to one this morning and has drifted to twenty-two, which is normal enough for a horse nobody has seen. Place pays a fifth of the odds." This keeps the 4-to-1 place bet the rewards use.
- [ ] **CE4 · Vuurzward "never lies", but he lies once.** The main note's Key NPCs and S5's Who Is Here both say "never threatens, never lies; he leaves things out". S5's table and the Clue Ledger then give him "The only lie he tells" (about Krul). **Fix**, in both places: "Courteous, patient, never threatens. He lies once, about Krul; otherwise he leaves things out."
- [ ] **CE5 · S8's box tells backstory, and gets the order wrong.** "He came for the noon signing and did it… But he watched the last two furlongs from the public stand first, and told nobody, and he is telling you now." The race ran after the noon signing, so "first" is wrong, and the box states things the players can't see. **Fix:** make the box perception only: *"The ring is half empty the morning after. Yearlings go through at a hundred marks a head, a man with a ledger calls prices in a bored singsong, and the whole Flats smells of packing up. Otwin Halbritter is sitting on the rail alone, his stick across his knees."* Move the backstory into the DM text: "He signed the remount contract at noon on Derby Day, then watched the last two furlongs from the public stand and told nobody."
