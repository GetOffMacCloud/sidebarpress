# Handout — The Patron's Letter

Back to [[SP-PBS-00-Nobodys-Colors]] · Given in [[Scene 08 — The Sale Ring]]

## Player Text
*(Heavy paper, folded in three. A blue-over-white seal: the Roodberg band.)*

> To the Registrar of the Greyhawk University Field Station at Ritterstadt, greeting.
>
> The bearers of this letter came to the Duffkyyn this Richfest in the service of Erlenhof, a house with which my family has dealt in horses for three generations. I write what was told me by the Jungfer Adalinde Halbritter, who has never in my hearing said a thing that was not so.
>
> She asks me to set down that they:
>
> - ______________________________________________
> - ______________________________________________
> - ______________________________________________
>
> I commend them to you as persons of field judgment, and I ask that this letter be entered where such letters are entered.
>
> Written in the Roodberg enclosure on the Duffkyyn Flats, Richfest, 576 CY.
>
> *— Margarethe Roodberg, of the Roodberg studs at Clatspurgen*

## DM Notes
Write in up to **three** commendations the party earned, in the patron's words. The letter is worth **10 extra credit on its face, plus 10 per commendation (40 at most)**, entered by [[Registrar Hedwig Ammerlin]] outside the 600-credit limit.

| Commendation | Earned by |
|---|---|
| *crossed the Erlen without spilling a drover's blood* | A bloodless bridge (Scene 2) |
| *stood a watch nobody asked them to stand* | Krul stopped or captured (Scene 4) |
| *brought her to the post sound* | Maus Sound at post time |
| *read a ledger like a clerk and a man like a priest* | The ten-days chain assembled |
| *asked the question nobody in that family would* | The rider question solved, by any route |
| *told her father the truth when it cost them* | Told Otwin about Hessel (Scene 8) |

Margarethe Roodberg never appears in play; she writes the letter offstage. No NPC note, by rule.
