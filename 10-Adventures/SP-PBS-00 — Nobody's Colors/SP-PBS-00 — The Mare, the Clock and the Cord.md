# The Mare, the Clock and the Cord

Back to [[SP-PBS-00-Nobodys-Colors]]

The adventure runs on one law, one track and two clocks. Put them on an index card where the players can see them. **Maus's Condition** is the real stake of Acts 2 and 3. The **Sabotage Clock** is how she loses it. The **Spook die** is what forty frightened horses do to a fight. The **peace-bond** is the law on the Flats.

## Maus's Condition (the track)
[[Maus]] is not a combatant and should never roll initiative.

| State | How | At post time |
|---|---|---|
| **Sound** | The Sabotage Clock never filled | Can place second. Every route in Scene 7 is open. |
| **Sore** | The clock filled once | Animal Handling DC 15 or Medicine DC 13 to detect; automatic for Adalinde and for any PC with a stable, farrier or cavalry background. She can run, but **fourth is her ceiling**, and there is a **1 in 6** she breaks down in the last furlong. |
| **Lame** | The clock filled twice | She cannot run. The entry is scratched, and Vuurzward's offer is the only route left. |

**Give the players the choice, not the roll.** If she is Sore, Adalinde says so plainly: *"She can run. I don't know that she should."* Never roll the breakdown in secret; roll it in the open, before the race, if they choose to run her. A breakdown ends her racing life, not her life: she is still a broodmare, and still worth having.

*Why laming her doesn't spoil Vuurzward's prize:* Krul's trade is a pricked sole, a nail an inch too deep. It heals in a month. Vuurzward wants the mare beaten on Derby Day, not ruined.

## The Sabotage Clock (Scene 4, Scene 6)
The saboteur needs **three uninterrupted rounds** at the mare's stall. Mark them where the players can see: [ ][ ][ ].
- The saboteur spends a whole turn adjacent to the mare to mark one round.
- **Interrupting him at any point resets the count to zero.**
- **Three marks** drop Maus one Condition step, whatever happens to the saboteur afterward.
- **No watch set, or the watch failed to notice:** he starts with one mark already placed. **No watch at all:** no roll; he gets his three rounds.

**Detection (Scene 4):** passive Perception 13 for anyone on watch on deck; Perception DC 15 from below decks (the mare's hooves shifting wrong); a PC who knows horses has advantage and notices one round earlier.

## The Spook Die (Scene 6)
Forty horses in narrow rows. Track a d6 in the open, starting at 0.

| Rises by 1 | Rises by 2 | Falls by 1 |
|---|---|---|
| A shout; a body hitting a stall wall; a lantern dropped or swung; a horse taking damage; a stall door left open | Any fire effect | A PC spends an action calming horses (**Animal Handling DC 12**) |

- **At 3:** the horses in the row scream. Disadvantage on Perception checks in the row, and Maus needs **Animal Handling DC 13** or she is unusable in Scene 7's opening minutes.
- **At 5: stampede.** Every creature in the row makes a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw**, taking 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage on a failure or half as much on a success. The row is difficult terrain for 3 rounds. A creature at 0 hit points takes an automatic 2d6 unless dragged clear, and the bravos will help drag. Nobody wants a dead man on the Flats.

## The Peace-Bond (the law; Scene 5 onward)
During Richfest week the Duffkyyn Flats are under a *Landfrieden* proclaimed in the Voormann's name. At the Flats gate the stewards' men knot a **red cord** through every hilt and scabbard and seal it with wax: *"Landfrieden. Derby peace. Cut it and you hang."*

| Allowed | Breaks the peace |
|---|---|
| Fists, grapples, shoves; improvised weapons (see Scene 6's list) | Cutting your own cord (an action, and a crime) |
| Unarmed strikes, monk features, natural weapons | Drawing a corded weapon in anger (a hanging offense, with forty witnesses) |
| Spells | — |

**If a player cuts and draws anyway, let them.** It works. The stewards' men arrive in 1d4 rounds, and the cost lands in Scene 7: Roodberg strikes the Ostvelt line on the spot unless the party can produce a very good reason.

**One custom, two places.** Schwartzenbruin's company taverns took the red cord from the Derby peace. At [[The Red Cord]], cutting your cord makes every company in the room your enemy; on the Flats, it makes the Voormann's hangman your enemy. See [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]].

## Steward Roodberg's Attitude (Scene 5, Scene 7)
Hostile / **Indifferent** / Friendly. He starts **Indifferent**.
- **One step worse:** blood spilled on the road (a PC recognized by name at the gate), or money offered to him.
- **One step better:** correct papers; a Roodberg or Meerijder name vouching; Krul produced as a witness with the odds-book account.
- **Friendly** halves the DC to bend the sponsor rule in Scene 7.
