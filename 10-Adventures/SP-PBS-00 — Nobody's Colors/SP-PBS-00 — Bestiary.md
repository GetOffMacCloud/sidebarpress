# Bestiary — Nobody's Colors

Back to [[SP-PBS-00-Nobodys-Colors]]

All statblocks use **2014 5e** rules. MM page numbers refer to the 2014 *Monster Manual*. Every creature here is a stock block with a short list of changes, so the session runs from this file alone.

## Standing Rules
- **Nobody here is evil.** Every hostile is a working person doing a paid job in a canton where everyone has cousins. All of them **surrender at or below a quarter of their hit points**, talk when captured, and do not pursue a fleeing party.
- **Killing has a name.** Killing a clansman without a declared *Fehde* is murder, and his clan carries it for a generation. A PC who kills a Meerijder or Vuurzward retainer is recognized at the Flats gate, and Steward Roodberg starts one step worse. Say this aloud **before** initiative.
- **Knocking out is free.** Any melee attack may knock a creature out instead of killing it. Remind the table once, at the bridge.
- **Under the peace-bond** (Scene 6), PCs and bravos alike fight with improvised weapons; see [[Scene 06 — The Stable Rows]] and [[SP-PBS-00 — The Mare, the Clock and the Cord]].

>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 15 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) |
>
> **Saving Throws** Str +4, Dex +5, Wis +2 · **Skills** Athletics +4, Deception +4 · **Senses** passive Perception 10 · **Languages** Common · **Challenge** 2 (450 XP)
>
> **Actions**
> ***Multiattack.*** The captain makes three melee attacks: two with its scimitar and one with its dagger. Or the captain makes two ranged attacks with its daggers.
> ***Scimitar.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.
> ***Dagger.*** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.
>
> **Reactions**
> ***Parry.*** The captain adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the captain must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
>
> **Changes from the bandit captain block:**
> - **Drover's heavy staff** replaces the scimitar: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., 7 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning. On a hit he may forgo 4 damage to force a **DC 13 Strength save** or the target drops one held item.
> - **Multiattack:** two staff attacks, or one staff attack and one shove. The **belt knife** (the dagger) comes out only if a PC has drawn steel first.
> - ***Drover's Footing.*** He ignores difficult terrain created by livestock.
> - ***Call Them Off.*** When a drover within 30 ft. drops to 0 HP, Boedic may end the fight by standing his men down. He takes it **the second time** it is available.
> - **Script:** round 1 he talks, not attacks. His line holds the bridge and does not advance. His crew targets the wagon team, not the party.

> [!example]- Vuurzward Bravo — Thug (CR 1/2, 100 XP), MM p. 350
> *Medium humanoid (any race), neutral* — Scene 6
> Use the **thug** block above, with these **changes**:
> - **Cudgel** replaces the mace (same numbers; wood, so it is legal on the Flats).
> - **Sap:** +4 to hit, 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning, and the target makes a **DC 12 Constitution save** or has disadvantage on its next attack roll. They aim to disable and withdraw, never to kill.

> - ***Drag Him Clear*** (reaction). When a creature within 5 ft., **including a PC**, drops to 0 HP during a stampede, the bravo drags it out of the row.
> - Vuurzward green under plain cloaks.
