# Handout — The Notice

Back to [[SP-PBS-00-Nobodys-Colors]] · Given in [[Scene 00 — Three Weeks in Aerden]]

## Player Text
*(Good paper. A careful hand. A wax seal with no device on it.)*

> **WANTED**
> Four to six persons of good character and steady nerve
> for escort duty on behalf of a gentleman of this town,
> Aerden to Osterschlauss by road, thence by lake passage to **SCHWARTZENBRUIN**,
> to attend the **DUFFKYYN DERBY**.
>
> Departing first bell, the morning after next.
> Passage paid. Board paid.
> **Thirty marks** per person on arrival, bearing arms.
>
> Inquire at **ERLENHOF** — the grey house at the top of the hill — and ask for **Sergeant Amtmann**.

## DM Notes
- The seal has no device because the Halbritters have none. A PC who knows heraldry notices that a gentleman's seal without a device is a clanless gentleman's seal.
- "Bearing arms" is the only lie by omission on the page: the job is escorting a horse, and nobody is meant to draw.
