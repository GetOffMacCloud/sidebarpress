# Scene 00 — Three Weeks in Aerden

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-00 — Nobody's Colors/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 01 — Erlenhof]]

## Purpose
The players leave this scene knowing what [[Aerden]] looks and sounds like, exactly how broke they are, and how they came to share a table — because they chose it. They are not friends. Yet. The notice is the last thing that happens.
**Budget:** 20 minutes. Running long? Skip the market and the butts; keep Grete's board and the notice.

## Why They Are Here
Every one of them is going to Ritterstadt, to enroll at Greyhawk University's field station across Lake Quag. The University takes anyone who signs its Oath. Getting there is the problem. The northern packet out of Schwartzenbruin, ninety miles down the shore, is one night to Ritterstadt's quay and 20 marks a head. At the far end the Registrar asks a first-term bond: 80 marks in coin, or a term of labor on the levy. **A hundred marks each to arrive clear**, and none of them has twenty. The free way is the Quag Road north on foot, a week of it, and nobody's idea of safe.

**Hand this out first.** Each player picks one or writes their own. No two the same.

| How you came to be stuck in Aerden |
|---|
| The coach to the lake left a day early. Nobody told you. |
| You were hired to guard a wool wagon to Osterschlauss. The wool got there. Your pay didn't. |
| You came over the Yatil passes on foot to save the fare. The map said six days. It was fifteen. |
| A letter of credit drawn on a Krestible counting-house. No one in Aerden will honor it. |
| You were sick a week in a farmhouse outside town. The farmer's wife wouldn't take money, so you paid the innkeeper for the farmer's ale instead. That was most of what you had. |
| You won't say. You've been asked twice. |

## Arrival
> *Aerden sits where the last of the Yatil foothills flatten into meadow. Everything is grey slate and cream plaster, and every doorpost in town carries a painted band at shoulder height: red over black, blue over white, a green so dark it looks black in shadow. Two doorposts are bare. One belongs to the inn. The other belongs to a big slate-roofed house at the top of the hill, with paddocks behind it.*

**To the table:** *"You've eaten at Grete's long table for three weeks. Who at it do you still not trust, and why?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Grete Haslau]]**, widow, keeper of the Mountainview Rest. *Wants:* her board squared and her house quiet after vespers. *Knows:* everyone's debts to the pfennig, and that Sergeant Amtmann has never bought his own ale in fifteen years. *Reacts:* she refuses thanks, makes arrangements instead of giving credit, and has already decided the party are decent.
- **[[Tilo]]**, her yard boy, nine. *Wants:* pfennigs. *Knows:* everything. *Reacts:* tells it for a pfennig, in the order he thinks of it.
- **[[Old Hartwin]]**, tollkeeper at the Erlen bridge, at the small table by the door every third night. *Wants:* an audience. *Knows:* every wheel that crosses the bridge by its sound. *Reacts:* talks about the bridge until stopped.
- **The farrier by the well**, a Roodberg cousin who shoes in the open and takes Derby bets between horses.
- **Meerijder drovers**, up from the bridge camp most evenings: red-and-black on their sleeves, loud, honest and good at cards.

## Beats

### The town, as they look
Read the box, then give these one at a time as the players look around, not as a list.
- **The bands are clan colors, and painting them is law.** A house with no band has no clan. The people in the house on the hill wear grey; the dress-law says they may wear nothing else.
- **The green** has arrow-chewed straw targets at the far end. On Godsday mornings the militia shoots there, longbows on foot and short bows from the saddle, and the whole town comes out with small beer to watch.
- **Cheese** is the smell of the place: caves cut into the hill behind the market, milk carts before dawn. Under it, when the wind is west, the burnt-metal smell of the copper smelters at the foot of the pass.
- **Half the men over forty** walk like soldiers and wear a foreign coin on a cord: Furyondy, Veluna, Ket. Perrenland's export is its sons. They notice armed strangers the way a carpenter notices a door hung wrong.
- **The chapel bell** tells the day: first bell at dawn, the noon bell, the third bell when the market closes, vespers at dusk. Nobody owns a clock.
- **Every conversation** gets to the Derby. Duffkyyn is a week off and ninety miles down the shore, and it is the only thing anyone wants to talk about.

### Grete's board
Chalked above the bar where everyone can read it. Fill it in from the players' choices and let them read it.

| | Owes | Worked off |
|---|---|---|
| *(PC)* | 2 marks 6 | 3 days, caves |
| *(PC)* | 1 mark 9 | yard watch, 4 nights |
| *(PC)* | 3 marks — | — |
| *(PC)* | 1 mark 2 | wood, roof, one chimney |

Let one of them notice who has worked off nothing, and let that be a conversation or not.

*Grete, to anyone who tries to thank her:* "Don't. I'll only put it on the board."

### Three weeks at the long table
They know each other's names and what each one eats. Between them they have unloaded milk carts, mucked the Halbritter paddocks for a day-rate (3 kleinmarks, and a look from the woman who runs the yard that said *you've done this before*), stood a night watch on the cheese caves, and each lost four kleinmarks to a drover at cards. The University came up the first week, and since then it is the one thing they don't joke about. If a player wants to say what they mean to study, let them. Don't make them.

### Gossip, if they ask
| Ask | Answer (Tilo, for a pfennig) |
|---|---|
| The grey house? | *"Herr Otwin's got the gout and the Jungfer runs the horses and the Junker's got a new saddle he doesn't let anyone see."* |
| The sergeant? | *"Sergeant Amtmann. His wife was a Vuurzward. She still paints her mother's door green."* |
| The Derby odds? | The farrier by the well: *"Roodberg bay, three to one. You want longer odds, you want a horse nobody's heard of, and there isn't one."* |

### The notice
Dusk, the twentieth day, at vespers. A man in a plain grey house-guard's jerkin, no band on the sleeve, nods to Grete, pins a sheet to the post by the bar, and **buys his own ale**. He drinks half and speaks to the room, not to them:

*"That's an honest job for honest folk. The Junker pays on arrival and I'll vouch for it. Anyone who can walk ten miles and sit a deck for two nights without being sick over the side — Erlenhof, first bell, day after tomorrow. Ask for Amtmann."*

He finishes the ale and leaves. Grete watches him go and says, to nobody: *"Hessel Amtmann. He's never bought his own ale in this house in fifteen years."*

Hand over [[SP-PBS-00 — Handout — The Notice]].

**Then read the table.** Thirty marks is more than any of them owes Grete, and passage is worth another forty. It puts them on the quay the northern packet sails from, with the fare in hand and seventy short of arriving clear. It is also a great deal of money for walking a rich boy to a horse race. Let them talk. Let them not quite trust it. Don't help.

## Clues Available Here
- Amtmann buys his own ale tonight → **he has money he didn't have** (Hessel sold the route)
- Tilo: the sergeant's wife was a Vuurzward → **Hessel and Vuurzward are connected**
- Tilo: the new saddle nobody sees → **the Junker is entering a horse**

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** grey slate, cream plaster, clan bands at shoulder height, Grete's chalk board
- **Sound:** cowbells in the meadows at all hours, the chapel bell, a fiddle played badly at vespers
- **Smell:** cheese, woodsmoke, wet wool, the smelters when the wind is west
- **Touch:** slate underfoot, cold even in summer
- **Taste:** small beer, thin and sour; rye bread hard enough to knock on

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **Don't name the Halbritters before the notice.** "The grey house at the top of the hill" is enough. The name comes with the job.
- **The table refuses the job.** Adalinde finds them at the Rest that night with her own money and a straighter story, and comes with them. Otwin's men are now looking for *her*; see [[Scene 01 — Erlenhof]].
- **Pregens.** The arrival table is the whole session zero. Don't skip it.
