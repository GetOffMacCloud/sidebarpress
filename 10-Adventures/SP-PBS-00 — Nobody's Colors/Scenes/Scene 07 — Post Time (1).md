# Scene 07 — Post Time

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-00 — Nobody's Colors/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 06 — The Stable Rows]] · Next → [[Scene 08 — The Sale Ring]]

## Purpose
The whole adventure. Twenty minutes to the third bell, and three things are true: Corwin can't ride well enough to win and now knows it; Adalinde is standing in the row in riding boots she hasn't explained; the book is unsigned and the nomination is Corwin's. **Neither sibling will raise it. The party has to say it.**
**Budget:** 25 minutes. **Never cut this scene.**

## Arrival
> *Derby Day. The stands are full, the bell for the second race has gone, and the course is a green mile with a crowd four deep along its rail. The morning packet has come in. Otwin Halbritter stands at the end of the row with two house men, looking at the mare, not his son. Adalinde is beside the stall in riding boots. A steward's boy puts his head around the post: "Ostvelt Grey. The book's still not signed. Whoever signs names the rider. Third bell."*

*Now stop talking. Let the table look at it.* If a player asked Adalinde in Scene 1 whether she rides, she looks at that player, not at Corwin, when the steward's boy, [[Benno]], speaks.

## Who Is Here
- **[[Corwin Halbritter]]**. *Wants:* to be taken seriously. *Knows:* he can't win. *Reacts:* won't offer the ride.
- **[[Adalinde Halbritter]]**. *Wants:* to ride. *Knows:* she can place. *Reacts:* won't ask.
- **[[Otwin Halbritter]]**, here for the noon signing and staying for the race. *Wants:* the farm to outlive his children. *Reacts:* says nothing yet.
- **[[Jorick Weisel]]**, five minutes away at [[The Tanner's Rest]], drinking.
- **[[Steward Alric Roodberg]]** and his book.

## Beats

### If they raise the rider question
**Corwin**, after a silence that should be allowed to be uncomfortable: *"She's better than me. She's always been better than me. Everyone here knows it. I knew it in the yard when I was twelve and I've spent seven years — "* He stops. He turns to his sister, and this is the first grown-up sentence of his life: *"Linde. Will you ride her."*

**Adalinde:** *"Yes."* She is already taking the silks out of his hands. *"Give me those. And Corwin, go and stand where Father can see you."*

### If the table sits silent
Do not have Adalinde ask. Prompt with an action: she crouches and starts unbuckling her spurs. She is putting them away, to go and stand in the public stand with her father and watch her brother lose her horse. If the table still doesn't move, they have made a choice. Run it.

### Changing the nomination
Adalinde riding needs the nomination changed. **Every route was seeded earlier:**

| Route | What it takes |
|---|---|
| **Weisel signs** | [[The Tanner's Rest]], five minutes at a run. Find him: Investigation DC 12, or automatic if they asked about him before. Make him sign: Intimidation DC 15, Persuasion DC 17, or 50 marks. Blackmail him with the rented-colors scheme: automatic, and he hates them forever, which costs nothing today. *"I rent cloth. I don't rent promises. If the boy told you I'd stand in a stewards' tent and swear for him, the boy misheard."* |
| **A new sponsor** | A Roodberg or Meerijder breeder willing to sign for a share of the mare's first foal. Automatic with a clan-kin PC who spends the favor; otherwise Persuasion DC 15 in the enclosures. |
| **Roodberg bends the rule** | Persuasion DC 17 (DC 12 with a clan name vouching): the rule serves the race better bent than kept. He does it once, and he writes down why. |
| **Otwin signs** | Only after the party has said Adalinde should ride, never before. Persuasion DC 13 if the ledger argument was won in Scene 2 or he has watched the swap; DC 17 otherwise. He signs for a dead house in his own hand, which is the closest a Halbritter has ever come to colors. |

### The other routes
- **Buy the note down:** stake money at 4-to-1 to place (the purse share, the bet, or both).
- **Scratch her and take the offer:** Corwin says yes if the party tells him to.
- **Run Corwin anyway:** nothing needed.

### Otwin, watching the swap
He doesn't say anything. He looks at his daughter in the silks, and at his son standing in the row with empty hands, and something in his face does arithmetic and comes out different than it went in.

### The race — three sentences, no rolls
**Adalinde rides, Maus Sound:**
> *They go off in a wall of noise and she's nowhere for half a mile: buried, boxed, a grey nothing at the back of a clan field. Then at the turn there's a gap that isn't really a gap, and she takes it. She doesn't win; the Roodberg colt wins by a length and a half. But a grey farm mare in a dead house's colors comes second at Duffkyyn, and up in the public stand an old man with the gout is on his feet without knowing he stood up.*

**Corwin rides:**
> *He rides well. That's the thing nobody expected and nobody will remember: he rides well, for a boy who's never ridden a race, and he finishes sixth in a field of eleven. The mare comes back sound and blowing and pleased with herself. Sixth pays nothing. He knows it before he's out of the saddle, and he doesn't look for his father in the stand.*

**Maus Sore, and they run her** (roll the 1-in-6 in the open, first):
> *No breakdown:* *She runs fourth on three good legs and a lie, and every horseman on that rail knows what they just watched.*
> *Breakdown:* *At the last furlong something goes out of her stride, the whole rail makes one sound, and she pulls up. She'll walk. She won't race. Adalinde is over the rail before anyone can stop her.*

## Development
- **Adalinde rides, Sound** → second; the 500-mark place purse clears the note. Maus is now a broodmare, not a remount, and Otwin strikes her from the levy string in his own hand.
- **Corwin rides** → sixth, sound. The note falls due; Erlenhof goes to the Day 6 ring unless the party has arranged otherwise.
- **Past the third bell** → scratched. Vuurzward's offer stands at Derby-evening prices, which are worse.
- **Sore** → she can run or she can be kept whole, not both. Adalinde says so and lets the party decide: *"She can run. I don't know that she should."* Fourth pays nothing and does not clear the note.
- **Lame** → she cannot run. The entry is scratched, and Vuurzward's offer is the only route left. He knows it.
- **Vuurzward** plays at full intelligence: odds bought down, gone before the last furlong. He has done nothing a court can hang him for. If the party laid Krul and the odds-book before Roodberg (Scene 5), his own horses are barred and he is not here at all.

## Clues Available Here
- The steward's boy → **whoever signs names the rider** (last resort)
- The boots → **Adalinde should ride**
- If someone mentions the purse, Corwin works it out aloud: *"Second's five hundred. The note's four."* → **second place is enough** (last resort)

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** green silks over riding boots, the crowd four deep, a steward's boy at the post
- **Sound:** the bell for the second race, a crowd coming apart at the turn
- **Smell:** crushed turf, horse sweat, a thousand people's beer
- **Touch:** silk still warm from the person who handed it over
- **Taste:** dust off the course

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **Adalinde came instead of Corwin** (they refused the job): the problem at post time is Otwin, who arrives to take the mare home. The same routes work on him; the ledger argument is the strongest.
- **Otwin has been here since Derby eve** (the roll): he stands in the row the whole scene. He speaks only if a PC speaks to him first.
- **The mare was sold to the Rhennee** (Scene 4): a 10-minute beat. Otwin and Adalinde come off the morning packet to an empty stall and a struck line. Adalinde looks at the stall, then at the party: *"You sold her to the water."* She says nothing else until the sale ring, where she still delivers the letter. Otwin says nothing at all; the note is paid, and that is the worst part. Go to [[Scene 08 — The Sale Ring]].
- **Maus failed to settle at Spook 3** (Scene 6): she won't stand for the first ten minutes. Adalinde walks her, so whoever runs for Weisel goes without her to vouch for them.
- **A PC cut a cord in Scene 6:** Roodberg has already struck the line. The only routes left are a very good reason (Persuasion DC 20, with the bravos' cut cords as evidence) or Vuurzward's offer.

## Transition
- → [[Scene 08 — The Sale Ring]]: the morning after, the ring half empty, and an old man sitting on the rail.
