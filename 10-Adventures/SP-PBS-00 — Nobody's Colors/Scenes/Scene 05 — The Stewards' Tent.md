# Scene 05 — The Stewards' Tent

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-00 — Nobody's Colors/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 04 — Two Nights on the Quag]] · Next → [[Scene 06 — The Stable Rows]]

## Purpose
The trap closes, and the antagonist makes a reasonable offer. The party leaves knowing the entry is one complaint from being scratched, having met Vuurzward and heard his offer. **Do not resolve the sponsor problem here**; it is Scene 7's fuel.
**Budget:** 25 minutes. Running long? Vuurzward makes his offer in the stable rows instead.

## Arrival
> *Schwartzenbruin is twenty-six thousand people in slate and timber under a Rathaus tower with a clock on it. Outside the walls the Duffkyyn Flats are a city of their own: striped pavilions in clan colors, cook-fires, a sale ring, and beyond it all the long green curve of the course. At the Flats gate a boy takes your sword, threads a red cord through hilt and scabbard both, knots it, and presses a wax seal onto the knot. "Landfrieden. Derby peace. Cut it and you hang. Enjoy the races."*

**To the table:** *"The Roodberg pavilion is the biggest on the Flats, and the stewards' tent is beside it. Corwin is already walking toward it. Does anyone stop him first?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Steward Alric Roodberg]]**, chief steward of the Duffkyyn. *Wants:* the Derby run properly. *Knows:* the rules by heart, and that half his register is ghosts. *Reacts:* starts **Indifferent**; not bribable with money, very bribable with correct papers. One step worse if the party spilled blood on the road.
- **[[Sivert Vuurzward]]**, hat in hand the entire time. *Wants:* Erlenhof and the mare. *Knows:* the seal is forged; a party member's debt, which he has bought. *Reacts:* never threatens, never lies; he leaves things out. Makes his offer to *them*, not to Corwin.
- **[[Jorick Weisel]]**, the broker, leaving by another flap as they come in.
- **[[Corwin Halbritter]]**, three feet away, waiting to be told.

## Beats

### The dock patrol (2 minutes)
Before the Flats, the *Maid* comes in under the lake-gate, and the patrol meets every boat: a sergeant with a lantern, and a clerk with a ledger. *"Business. Lodging. Company."* Every answer goes down. A horse that came aboard off the roll goes into this ledger instead. It changes nothing today; it is how the city works.

### Steward Roodberg
*"Ostvelt."* He turns the register around and taps a line. *"Ostvelt green. The Ostvelts have been dead thirty years, Junker. I don't mind that. Half this book is ghosts. What I mind is that the man who signed for those ghosts is not standing in front of me."*

| If they… | Roodberg |
|---|---|
| Ask what he needs | *"A living sponsor of record, in this tent, with a hand on this book, before the third bell tomorrow. That's the rule. It's the only thing standing between this race and a field of horses owned by nobody."* And: *"Whoever signs names the rider. That's the other rule."* |
| Try money | He writes in the margin. *"I'll pretend that was for the widows' fund."* Attitude drops one step, and he is now watching them. |
| Ask for a substitute sponsor | Persuasion DC 17, or DC 12 with a Roodberg or Meerijder name vouching. Success here only tells them it *can* be done; nobody signs tonight. |
| Ask to see the odds-book | Give it freely; this is the clue (below). Investigation DC 13 to spot the anomaly, or automatic for a PC with trade or bookkeeping. |
| Produce Krul as a witness | Roodberg listens. With the odds-book account as well, he will bar Vuurzward's own horses from the Derby: a real blow, and it does nothing to the note. |

He closes the book: *"Find him by the third bell, or I strike the line."*

### The odds-book
Ostvelt Grey opened at twenty to one this morning and has shortened to fourteen, which is normal enough for a horse nobody has seen. What isn't normal is the column beside it: somebody has been laying money **against** her, steadily, in small amounts, **from an account opened ten days ago. The account is in the name of Vuurzward.** The purses are posted on the stewards' board: first 1,000 marks, **second 500**, third 200.

### Vuurzward
He comes to them. *"You'll be the ones who came up from Aerden with the boy. Sivert Vuurzward. I lend money, mostly. I breed a little."*

**The offer:** *"I hold a paper with Herr Otwin Halbritter's seal on it. Four hundred marks against the Erlen tolls. The paper says what it says. I would rather not read it aloud in the Rathaus; it would embarrass a family I have no quarrel with, in front of people who'd enjoy it. So. Give me the mare, and the paper goes in the fire, and the farm stands, and everyone goes home."* He puts his hat on. *"Tell the boy that. He'll hear it better from you than from me."*

| If… | Vuurzward |
|---|---|
| Accused of hiring Krul | *"I don't know the name."* The only lie he tells, and he tells it perfectly: **Insight DC 18**. |
| Asked why the mare and not the money | *"Because I've seen her mother run."* The truest thing anyone says in the adventure, and it costs him nothing. |
| Asked straight whether someone in the house sold him the route | *"I don't lie, scholar. Ask me something else."* |
| Threatened | He looks at the red cord on the hilt, then at the man wearing it. *"The Schultheiss of this city is my cousin. I mention it only so nobody wastes an afternoon."* |
| A PC carries a debt | In front of the others: *"Oh, and I bought your paper from a house in Krestible last month. Six marks and a bit. It's nothing. I only mention it so you don't hear it from someone less polite."* He never mentions it again. |

*The players decide:* the offer, and what to tell Corwin.

## Development
- **Offer taken** → Corwin's yes comes if the party tells him to give it. The mare goes to Vuurzward at Derby-evening prices tomorrow; the farm stands. Skip to [[Scene 08 — The Sale Ring]] after a short Scene 7.
- **Krul plus the odds-book before Roodberg** → Vuurzward's own entries are barred. He tips his hat and leaves the Flats. The note still stands.
- **The entry went on the roll in Scene 3** → **Otwin is already here**, sitting in the corner of the tent with his ledger. He says nothing. Corwin sees him.
- **Drovers were killed** → a PC is recognized by name at the gate; Roodberg starts **Hostile**.

## Clues Available Here
- Roodberg's rules → **whoever signs the book names the rider**
- The odds-book account → **Vuurzward paid to stop the mare**; opened **ten days ago** → **the route was sold**
- The posted purses → **second place is enough**
- Vuurzward's non-denial → **someone in the house sold the route**
- Weisel leaving by another flap → **the sponsor has run**

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** striped pavilions, a register of dead houses' colors, a red cord and a wax seal on every hilt
- **Sound:** a sale-ring auctioneer's singsong, horses being walked, a pen scratching in the margin
- **Smell:** trodden grass, cook-fires, horse sweat, ink
- **Touch:** the wax seal, still warm
- **Taste:** Derby beer, stronger than Aerden's

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **The party hunts Weisel tonight** instead of tomorrow. Move the Tanner's Rest beat from Scene 7 here; it becomes a whole scene of leaning on a coward, and Scene 7 opens with the book already signed or Weisel already gone.
- **The party never asks for the odds-book.** Roodberg mentions it himself if a PC says *Vuurzward*: *"Funny. That name's in my other book, too."*
- **Nobody carries a debt.** Vuurzward makes the same offer without the aside.
