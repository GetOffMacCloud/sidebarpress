# Scene 04 — Two Nights on the Quag

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-00 — Nobody's Colors/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 03 — Osterschlauss]] · Next → [[Scene 05 — The Stewards' Tent]]

## Purpose
The adventure's breathing room, its biggest temptation, and its first fight. The party leaves knowing Vuurzward's name and the phrase *ten days*.
**Budget:** 40 minutes (evening one 10, evening two 15, the small hours 15). Running long? Cut the card game, keep the offer, or open on the second night.

## Arrival
> *The shore slides past on the right hand all afternoon and never comes closer or goes further. Every captain on this lake keeps land in sight. Out in the middle there is grey fog that doesn't lift. There are sixteen passengers: a cheese factor and his wife, two Roodberg grooms already drunk, a woman traveling alone who reads, and a thin man near the mast who has said nothing to anyone and was aboard before you were.*

**To the table:** *"Two nights, sixteen strangers, one canvas stall. Who's sleeping next to the horse?"*

*The thin man is [[Anse Krul]]. Do not point at him. Include him every time you list the passengers.*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Anse Krul]]**, farrier. *Wants:* his forty marks. *Knows:* Vuurzward's man paid him, and gave him the boat and the day ten days back; not who told them. *Reacts:* runs before he fights; cornered, names his employer without much pressing.
- **[[Captain Marda Vosk]]**. Knows the price of everything, including the Rhennee offer.
- **The Rhennee**: three long barges, and **[[Zinka of the Kestrel]]** at the rail, who knows the mare's stable name. *Wants:* the mare, for someone she won't name. *Knows:* only that she was told to watch this boat. *Reacts:* makes the 600-mark offer once, reads a fortune for anyone who crosses the plank, and lets the barges drop back if refused.
- **[[Corwin Halbritter]]**, talking too much on the first evening.

## Beats

### Evening one (10 min)
The boat, the passengers, Corwin talking. Seed Krul as a face. A PC who knows horses notices the thin man has farrier's hands (scarred, a burn across one palm) and that he looks at the deck stall the way a buyer looks at a sale ring.

### Evening two — the Rhennee (15 min)
> *Three long barges come out of the dusk without a sound, lantern-light strung along their awnings. They don't hail. They match your speed forty feet off the port rail, as though they had been going that way anyway. Someone over there is playing something with strings. A woman comes to the rail, looks straight at your deck stall, and says in a voice that carries too well across the water: "That's a good little mouse you've got there."*

Nobody on the boat told her that name. She is Zinka, of the barge *Kestrel*. There is a card game on the afterdeck (lose four kleinmarks, win four, it doesn't matter) and a fortune for anyone who crosses the plank.

**The offer**, made to whoever seems to be in charge, which by now isn't Corwin: *"Six hundred marks. Tonight, in coin, and you can count it on your own deck before you hand her over. You look like people who could use six hundred marks."*

| If asked… | She says |
|---|---|
| Why | *"Because she's worth it, and you know she's worth it, or you wouldn't have her in a canvas box in the dark."* |
| Pressed harder | *"I was told to watch this boat. I wasn't told why, and I've stopped asking."* Deliver it and move on. |

**Vosk, if asked:** *"Clan houses don't sell bloodstock to bargefolk. Not* don't like to. *Don't. Do that, and the boy's name is worth less than the wagon it came in. Now. It's not my name, and it's not my six hundred marks."*

*The players decide.* Six hundred marks clears the note with two hundred over, which Corwin splits with the party, and it finishes the Halbritters on the Flats for good. It is a real choice with a real price. **Let them take it.**

### The small hours (15 min)
*Read this to whoever is on watch when Krul is detected.*

> *Something is wrong with the sound of the deck. A horse standing quiet makes a particular noise: a shift, a breath, a hoof set down and settled. What comes from the deck stall now is a hoof being lifted, held, and set down wrong.*

Run the encounter. Krul needs **three uninterrupted rounds** in the stall. Mark them where the players can see.

**Krul, cornered:** *"Easy. Easy. Look at my hands. There's a knife in them and it's four inches long and it's for hooves. A nail an inch too deep, that's all. She walks fine. She runs like she's on coals. Nobody dies. That's the trade."*

**Krul, questioned:** *"Vuurzward's man. Forty marks, half up front, paid me in Osterschlauss."* (The shrug is what makes players want to hit him.) *"He gave me the boat and the day ten days back. Ten days, friend. She wasn't even loaded yet. So don't look at me like that. Somebody in that house talks."*

*The players decide Krul's fate:* hand him to Vosk (she puts him ashore at the next landing, and he walks to Schwartzenbruin for Scene 6); keep him bound aboard (a witness for the stewards' tent and for Roodberg); or let him go for the name.

| 4 | Krul + 2 deckhands, one with a hand crossbow | Easy (800) |

Krul is a **spy**; the bribed deckhands are **thugs** with 20 HP. See [[SP-PBS-00 — Bestiary]].

**Terrain:** a lake cog, 60 feet stem to stern, one mast; the deck stall at the bow against the starboard rail, with the deck's one lantern hung just aft of it. **Darkness:** Krul has shuttered the lantern to a slit (dim light within 10 feet, darkness beyond); his eyes are adjusted, the party's are not. Unshuttering it is an action, or a bonus action for a PC adjacent to it, and lights 20 feet, which ends his advantage in the dark. **A moving deck:** any creature that Dashes, is shoved, or takes a reaction attack makes an **Acrobatics DC 10 check or falls prone**. **The rail:** a shove past it means the lake, **Athletics DC 12** to swim, and the *Maid* takes three rounds to come about. Nobody drowns unless a player insists. **Fire is not an option:** one open flame near the canvas, and Vosk puts the party ashore forty miles from Schwartzenbruin with two days to go. Say so if anyone reaches for a torch.
**Tactics:** round 1 Krul tries to finish the job. From round 2 he runs, over the rail if cornered; he can swim. The deckhands are bribed, not loyal, and surrender the moment Krul goes down or bolts.
**DM direction:** this is a clock, not a slugfest. Interrupting Krul at any point resets the count to zero; three marks drops the mare one Condition step whatever happens to Krul afterward. Detection: **passive Perception 13** if someone is on watch on deck; **Perception DC 15** from below decks; a PC who knows horses has advantage and notices one round earlier. Full rules in [[SP-PBS-00 — The Mare, the Clock and the Cord]].

## Development
- **Krul stopped or captured** → the watch commendation in the patron's letter; Maus stays **Sound**.
- **No one on watch** → no roll. Krul got his three rounds. Maus is sound to look at and **Sore** to run, and Adalinde (or the PC who knows horses) says so plainly at dawn. Act 3 has a clock on it. This is a real branch, not a punishment.
- **The mare sold** → 600 marks on deck. Maus is Otwin's horse, so Corwin selling her is a second crime on top of the seal; the Rhennee don't care. If Adalinde is aboard (the decline path), she refuses flatly, and the sale can only happen over her, which makes it theft. Run the short Act 3 in the Alternates of [[Scene 05 — The Stewards' Tent]] and [[Scene 07 — Post Time]]; cut Scene 6.
- **Krul kept aboard** → he can be produced before Roodberg in Scene 5.

## Clues Available Here
- Krul: *"Vuurzward's man. Forty marks."* → **Vuurzward paid to stop the mare**
- Krul: *"Ten days back."* → **someone in the house sold the route**
- The Rhennee woman knows the stable name → *(dangling: who wanted the mare?)*
- Adalinde, if asked about the race: *"She won't beat the Roodberg colt. She'll beat most of the rest."* → **second place is enough**

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** the shore always on the right hand, grey fog off the port rail, lantern-light along three barge awnings
- **Sound:** strings from the barges, cards slapped on a cask, a hoof lifted and set down wrong
- **Smell:** pitch, wet canvas, horse
- **Touch:** the deck moving under bare feet in the dark
- **Taste:** the Rhennee's cooking, if anyone crosses the plank

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **Nobody talks to the Rhennee.** The woman calls the offer across the water anyway, to Corwin, who looks at the party.
- **The party catches Krul on the first night.** He has a partner among the deckhands; the second night's attempt comes from the bribed deckhand alone (Sabotage Clock as written).
- **Krul goes over the rail and escapes.** He is in the stable rows on Derby eve (APL 3–4 rows).

Lodging. Company."* Every answer goes down; a horse that came aboard off the roll goes into this ledger instead. It changes nothing; it is how the city works (see [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]]). Lodging near the Flats, and a short night.
