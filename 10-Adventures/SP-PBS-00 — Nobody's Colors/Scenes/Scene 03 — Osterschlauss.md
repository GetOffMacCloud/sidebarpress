# Scene 03 — Osterschlauss

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-00 — Nobody's Colors/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — The Erlen Bridge]] · Next → [[Scene 04 — Two Nights on the Quag]]

## Purpose
One clean problem-solving beat; a breather with teeth. Get sixteen hands of grey aboard a passage booked for one man and his baggage. This is the moment Corwin stops being the employer.
**Budget:** 15 minutes. Running long? Narrate the loading and keep only the clerk's one question.

## Arrival
> *The road comes over a rise and the lake is just there, filling the whole of the north: grey-green, flat, with the far shore only a smudge that might be cloud. Osterschlauss is a hundred houses and a stone quay. Tied up against the quay is a fat little cog with one mast and her name painted on the stern, Kortheim Maid. At the head of the gangway stands a clerk with a roll of paper, writing down everything that goes aboard.*

**To the table:** *"Corwin has stopped at the foot of the gangway. He's looking at you. Who goes up first?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Captain Marda Vosk]]**, master of the *Kortheim Maid*. *Wants:* declared cargo and paid fare. *Knows:* what selling a horse to the Rhennee means on the Flats. *Reacts:* asks nothing about weight that's on the roll and everything about weight that isn't; prices everything, including silence.
- **The Hafenmeister's clerk**, with the *Frachtrolle*. The roll is read aloud in Aerden market every Godsday, which is why Corwin couldn't book the horse: booking her would have been telling his father.
- **[[Corwin Halbritter]]**, out of plan.

## Beats

### Corwin runs out of plan
He has been quiet since the bridge. At the foot of the gangway he says, to nobody in particular: *"I had it worked out as far as the boat. I did not — the boat was the plan. What do we do?"*

From here on Corwin asks the party what to do, and means it.

### Vosk
*"Forty marks a head, Osterschlauss to Schwartzenbruin, two nights, and that's what's on the Frachtrolle."* She looks past them at the third wagon for exactly one breath. *"Nine hundredweight of luggage is not on the Frachtrolle. Now. I can be paid to write slowly. I cannot be paid to be surprised. Which is it going to be?"*

- **Honest with her:** she laughs. *"A racehorse. That's the first interesting thing to come down that road in a year. Twenty marks, and she goes in the deck stall, and if the Hafenmeister asks, she's breeding stock going to a Roodberg farm. Which she may as well be."*
- **Hiding it entirely:** *"You understand I have to write something."*

### Getting her aboard — the players decide the method
Take one plan, set one DC, resolve it.

| Approach | Check |
|---|---|
| Bribe the clerk | 10 marks, no roll; he asks why, and remembers |
| Forge a roll entry ("breeding stock, Roodberg") | DC 15 with forgery kit, DC 17 without |
| Load at the far end of the quay at the wrong bell | Stealth DC 12 (group) |
| Pay Vosk to write slowly | Persuasion DC 14, or 20 marks |
| Distract the clerk | Deception or Performance DC 13 |

**Failure is not refusal.** The mare goes aboard either way. Failure means the entry goes on the roll, and the roll is read aloud in Aerden.

## Development
- **The entry went on the roll** → Otwin knows by Day 3 and arrives in Schwartzenbruin a full day early. He is **waiting in the stewards' tent** in Scene 5, and at post time he is already in the row.
- **No trail** → Otwin and Adalinde come in on the morning packet on Derby Day, as planned.
- The deck stall is amidships against the starboard rail, eight feet by ten, canvas-roofed. Note who sleeps nearest it.

## Clues Available Here
- Vosk's price list → **Vosk can be trusted to say what things cost** (she'll price the Rhennee offer honestly in Scene 4)

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** the lake filling the north, a one-masted cog, a clerk's wet pen
- **Sound:** gulls, a gangway creaking under hooves, the clerk reading back an entry
- **Smell:** pitch, fish, lake water
- **Touch:** a rope burn from the halter as the mare balks at the gangway
- **Taste:** spray

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **They missed the *Maid*** (a one-day hold at the bridge): the slower *Agal Wind* sails next morning. Its master takes 50 marks and asks no questions at all. Krul is aboard the *Agal Wind* instead. Arrival is Day 4 at dusk.
- **Adalinde came instead of Corwin** (they refused the job): she has the coin and does the talking; the party handles the clerk.
