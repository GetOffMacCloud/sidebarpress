# Scene 01 — Erlenhof

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-00 — Nobody's Colors/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 00 — Three Weeks in Aerden]] · Next → [[Scene 02 — The Erlen Bridge]]

## Purpose
Hire them, and plant the lie where they can find it. The third wagon must not be handed over; it is there to be found.
**Budget:** 25 minutes. Running long? One question to Adalinde, not three.

## Arrival
> *The grey house sits at the top of the hill with its back to the mountains. The doorpost beside the gate is bare, old wood scrubbed pale by forty years of rain. Behind it the land opens into paddocks full of horses, and a woman in a grey riding coat walks the fence line with a bucket, not hurrying. Three wagons stand in the yard. The third is shuttered and rides low on its springs. A man in a house-guard's jerkin comes out to meet you before you reach the door, and steers you away from it.*

**To the table:** *"The sergeant says the Junker is at the stable. Who looks at the boy, and who looks at the horses?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Hessel Amtmann]]**, sergeant of the house guard. *Wants:* the debt gone and his hands clean. *Knows:* everything; he sold the route ten days ago. *Reacts:* answers a different question than the one asked; says "I couldn't spare a man" three different ways.
- **[[Corwin Halbritter]]**, *Junker*, 19. *Wants:* to be taken seriously once. *Knows:* the horse, the forged seal, the rented colors; not the leak. *Reacts:* talks past the end of his own sentences; goes quiet when someone competent questions the plan.
- **[[Adalinde Halbritter]]**, *Jungfer*, 24. *Wants:* to ride that mare at Duffkyyn, and will not say so. *Knows:* everything about the mare, nothing about the note. *Reacts:* says less than she knows and never less than the truth; watches which of them looks at the horses instead of the boy.
- **[[Maus]]**, in the third wagon, being quiet in the dark.

## Beats

### Amtmann, walking them out
*"You'll find the Junker enthusiastic. That's not a warning. He's nineteen. Answer what he asks and don't volunteer, and you'll get on."*

| If asked… | Amtmann says |
|---|---|
| Why the house guard isn't going | *"Six men. Four are cousins to the tollkeeper, and the tollkeeper is Herr Otwin's man to the bone. You see the difficulty."* He never says what the difficulty is. |
| What's in the wagons | *"The Junker's baggage. He travels heavy. Nobles do."* He looks at the third wagon while he says it. |
| Who else knows about the trip | *"Nobody. That's rather the point."* This is the one lie he tells badly: **Insight DC 12** catches that he is frightened, not why. |

### Corwin, the hiring
He comes out of the stable too fast, wiping his hands, talking before he has crossed the yard: *"You're the ones from the Rest. Good. It's a simple thing. You walk beside the wagon, you look like men who've killed someone, and in Schwartzenbruin I pay you. Ten miles to the water, two nights on a boat, and then it's the Derby. I'll want you sober at the quay. That's the only condition."*

**The players decide the terms.**

| Push | Corwin |
|---|---|
| Once | *"Forty, then. Forty. It's not — fine. Forty."* |
| Twice, or ask for a share | *"A share of — "* He looks at the stable door. *"One part in five of whatever she — of whatever the trip earns. If it earns."* |

*That slip is free:* any player who catches it has **advantage on Insight** for the rest of the scene. Pressing for the share is the first thing that makes Adalinde look at them twice.

### The third wagon
Shuttered, bedded with straw, low on its springs, and it shifts when nothing is touching it. Asked what's in it, Corwin says *"Baggage,"* then *"My baggage,"* then (a third ask, or **Insight DC 13**) *"Why does everyone keep asking me that?"*

**If they open it:** straw, warmth, and a grey mare near enough to white at the flank, who turns her head and looks at them with enormous patience. Corwin folds at once and badly: *"All right. All right. She's a horse. She's* the *horse. If you walk away now I understand. But you'd be walking away from forty marks, and I'd be walking to Schwartzenbruin alone, and I am going to Schwartzenbruin."*

A party that opens the wagon in the first five minutes has not broken anything. Corwin folding early is a better Act 1, and Adalinde sizes them up sooner. **Leave it shut, and it opens itself at the bridge.**

### Adalinde at the fence
She does not come to them; they come to her, or they don't meet her.

*"You took the job, then."* (Beat.) *"He told you it's luggage."*

| If asked… | Adalinde says |
|---|---|
| (They say yes, luggage) | *"It weighs nine hundredweight and it kicked the boards out of that wagon last Godsday. You may draw your own conclusions. I'd draw them before the bridge, myself."* |
| Whose horse it is | *"The farm's."* (A pause just long enough to notice.) *"I bred her. I broke her. Her name is Maus, and if anybody at Duffkyyn calls her that I'll know where they heard it."* |
| Whether the plan will work | *"No."* (If they wait:) *"But he's going anyway, and he's going alone if you don't. So."* She hands the bucket to whoever is nearest, which is the first small test. |
| Whether she rides | *"I ride."* That is the complete answer. **Note who asked**: that player has found the climax three scenes early and should get the payoff in Scene 7. |
| Her father | *"He's signing a remount contract in Schwartzenbruin at noon on Derby Day. Thirty head over three years. It's a good contract."* She says *good contract* the way you'd say *good weather at a funeral*. |

## Development
- The party is employed at 30 or 40 marks, with or without the one-in-five share. Record it; it decides the reward table.
- Hessel watches the party leave from the gate. He does not come.

## Clues Available Here
- The wagon rides low and shifts; Adalinde's nine hundredweight → **there is a racehorse in the third wagon**
- *"I ride."* → **Adalinde should ride**
- Amtmann's bad lie → **Hessel is frightened of someone**

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a bare doorpost, thirty horses in long morning light, a woman in grey with a bucket
- **Sound:** hooves on packed earth, a wagon spring creaking with nobody near it
- **Smell:** straw, horse, neatsfoot oil
- **Touch:** the warm side of the shuttered wagon
- **Taste:** dust from the yard

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **The party refuses the job.** Adalinde finds them at the Rest that night with her own money and a straighter story, and comes along. This removes the Scene 7 rider problem and replaces it: Otwin's men are looking for *her*, and at post time the problem is Otwin, not Corwin.
- **The party takes the job and never looks.** Fine. The bridge reveals it.
- **A PC tries to tell Otwin.** He is in the house with his ledger and the gout. He hears them out, thanks them, and sends the writ an hour sooner. Run Scene 2 with Boedic already standing in the road.
