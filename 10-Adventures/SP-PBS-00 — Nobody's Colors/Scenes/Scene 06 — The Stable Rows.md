# Scene 06 — The Stable Rows

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-00 — Nobody's Colors/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 05 — The Stewards' Tent]] · Next → [[Scene 07 — Post Time]]

## Purpose
The adventure's one real fight, in terrain that makes fighting stupid. Settle the mare's Condition before the scene ends: Sound, Sore or Lame. **Whether the party wins is not the question.**
**Budget:** 30 minutes. Running long? One round and a fled saboteur. If it is 3:40 on the clock and you are still here, end the fight on the next round by any means.

## Arrival
> *Derby eve. The rows run between the pavilions: forty stalls under one long roof, forty horses shifting and blowing in the dark, a lantern every twenty feet turning everything orange. It smells of straw, ammonia, neatsfoot oil and money. Down at the far end, past the Roodberg strings, a lantern that was hanging is now being carried. It has stopped in front of a stall that isn't theirs.*

**To the table:** *"Your blades are corded and sealed. There are pitchforks in the rack, buckets at the post, and a lantern-pole at every third stall. What do you pick up?"*

## Who Is Here
- **Vuurzward bravos** (2–4), Vuurzward green under plain cloaks, cords already cut. *Want:* the mare disabled and themselves gone. *Know:* Herr Sivert said nobody gets hurt. *React:* fight to disable and withdraw; morale breaks at half; they will help drag the fallen clear.
- **[[Anse Krul]]**, at APL 3–4, if he is free (escaped, put ashore by Vosk, or let go).
- **[[Corwin Halbritter]]**, who wants to stand the watch himself.
- **Forty horses**, who will kill whoever is standing when they go.

## Beats

### Corwin and the watch
Before dark, Corwin asks: *"Let me stand it."* If anyone objects: *"It's my horse and my lie. Let me stand one watch like a man instead of a — let me stand one watch."*

He has 11 hit points and will do something brave. **The players decide** whether to let him. If he stands the first watch alone, the bravos come on his watch, and he shouts before he does anything else, which is the bravest thing he could do.

### The peace-bond
Say this at the top of round one, out loud, with the list (it saves six minutes of questions):

| Improvised weapon | Damage | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Pitchfork | 1d6 piercing | Reach |
| Lantern-pole | 1d6 bludgeoning | Fire risk: see the Spook die |
| Water bucket | 1d4 bludgeoning | Shove attempt at advantage |
| Hoof pick | 1d4 piercing | Finesse |
| Saddle girth / lead rope | — | Grapple at advantage; restrains on a success |
| Stall door | — | Half cover, or shove a target prone |
| Feed sack | 1d4 bludgeoning | Heavy; disadvantage on the next attack |

Unarmed strikes, monk features, natural weapons and spells are unaffected, so the characters who don't usually shine in a stand-up fight shine here.

**If a player cuts their cord and draws, let them.** It works. The stewards' men arrive in 1d4 rounds, and the cost lands in Scene 7: Roodberg strikes the Ostvelt line on the spot unless the party can produce a very good reason. A real cost at the worst time is better than a refusal.

### The Spook die
Track it in the open, starting at 0. Full rules in [[SP-PBS-00 — The Mare, the Clock and the Cord]].
- **+1:** a shout, a body hitting a stall wall, a lantern dropped or swung, a horse taking damage, a stall door left open. **+2:** any fire.
- **−1:** a PC spends an action calming horses (**Animal Handling DC 12**).
- **At 3:** the horses scream. Disadvantage on Perception in the row; Maus needs **Animal Handling DC 13** or she is unusable in Scene 7's opening minutes.
- **At 5: stampede.** Everyone in the row makes a **DC 13 Dexterity save**: 7 (2d6) bludgeoning on a failure, half on a success. The row is difficult terrain for 3 rounds. A creature at 0 HP takes an automatic 2d6 unless someone drags it clear, and the bravos will help drag.

A party that grasps the die fights half this encounter with buckets and voices, which is exactly right.

### What the bravos say
*"Nobody's here for you. Walk down the other end and count to five hundred."*
At half strength, seeing the horses start: *"Down! Get him down and get him out, they're going — "*

## Encounter (if any)
| APL | Creatures | Difficulty (4 PCs) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 bravos | Hard (300) |
| 2 | 3 bravos | Hard (600) |
| 3 | 4 bravos + Krul (if he is free) | Hard (1,200) |
| 4 | 4 bravos + Krul (if he is free) | Medium (1,200) |

Bravos are **thugs** with cudgels and saps. Without Krul at APL 3–4, the encounter is Medium (800). See [[SP-PBS-00 — Bestiary]].

**Terrain:** rows a wagon wide, stalls on both sides, a lantern every twenty feet, a lantern-pole at every third stall, a pitchfork rack and a water trough at the near end. Maus is in the fourteenth stall on the left, about two-thirds down, past the Roodberg strings.
**Tactics:** the bravos start at the far end of the row and spend round 1 cutting a stall rope, so a bad initiative can't end the scene on the first turn. Two go for the mare while the rest hold the row. They need **three uninterrupted rounds** at her stall (the Sabotage Clock, as on the boat). They disable and withdraw; nobody wants a dead man on the Flats.
**DM direction:** settle the mare's Condition before the scene ends. Interrupting the clock resets it.

## Development
- **Clock never filled** → Maus keeps her Condition from the boat.
- **Clock filled** → Maus drops one step (Sound → Sore; Sore → Lame).
- **A bravo dragged clear by a PC** → *"Herr Sivert said nobody gets hurt."* The last-resort clue.
- **A cord cut and a blade drawn** → the stewards' men in 1d4 rounds; Roodberg strikes the line in Scene 7 unless the party has a very good reason.
- **Boedic's kin are here** if drovers died at the bridge: two Meerijder drovers in the next row, who watch and do nothing, which is its own message.

## Clues Available Here
- Vuurzward green under the cloaks; cords already cut → **Vuurzward paid to stop the mare**
- *"Herr Sivert said nobody gets hurt"* → last resort for the same

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** orange lantern-light moving where it shouldn't, forty heads turning at once
- **Sound:** horses blowing, a stall rope parting, a scream that isn't human
- **Smell:** straw, ammonia, neatsfoot oil, lamp oil
- **Touch:** a pitchfork shaft worn smooth, the heat of a frightened horse against a stall door
- **Taste:** dust kicked up from the straw

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **The party never sets a watch.** No roll: the bravos get their three rounds and Maus drops one step. Adalinde finds it at dawn and says so.
- **Krul was captured on the boat.** The bravos come alone. If Krul is with the party, he recognizes the bravos and names them.
- **The party moves the mare** to another stall or out of the rows. The bravos search: give the party a free round of surprise when they find the wrong stall.
