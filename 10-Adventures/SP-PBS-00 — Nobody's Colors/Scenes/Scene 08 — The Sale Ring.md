# Scene 08 — The Sale Ring

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-00 — Nobody's Colors/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 07 — Post Time]]

## Purpose
Land it. Hand them the evidence about Hessel, let them decide whether to tell Otwin, and give them the patron's letter on almost every branch.
**Budget:** 10 minutes. Running long? The letter only.

## Arrival
> *The ring is half empty the morning after. Yearlings go through at a hundred marks a head, a man with a ledger calls prices in a bored singsong, and the whole Flats smells of packing up. Otwin Halbritter is sitting on the rail. He came for the noon signing and did it; the contract is signed, thirty head over three years. But he watched the last two furlongs from the public stand first, and told nobody, and he is telling you now.*

**To the table:** *"He doesn't look at you. He says, 'I saw. I saw the whole of it.' Who sits down next to him?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Otwin Halbritter]]**. *Wants:* quiet, to redo the sums. *Knows:* what he saw. *Reacts:* to his daughter, without turning: *"Don't. Don't say anything, girl. I've the ledger in my head and I'm doing the sums again and I'd like quiet for it."*
- **[[Adalinde Halbritter]]**, with a folded paper.
- **[[Corwin Halbritter]]**, wherever his father can see him.

## Beats

### The truth about Hessel
Krul's *ten days*, the odds-book account opened ten days ago, Grete's ale line and Tilo's word about the sergeant's wife: together they name **[[Hessel Amtmann]]**. Leave it as evidence, not a confession. **Whether to tell Otwin is the last decision in the adventure, and it is the party's.**

If they tell him, give them the moment and don't rush it:
> *He listens all the way through without interrupting, which is worse than shouting. "Hessel." He says the name like he's checking a number he already knew was wrong. "Fifteen years. He carried my son on his shoulders at the Godsday fair." He gets down off the rail, slowly, because of the gout. "Right. Thank you. I'd rather have known."*

### The letter
However it ended, even if they took Vuurzward's offer, Adalinde finds them with a folded paper and a face that gives nothing away:

*"The Roodbergs have sold remounts to the University's field station since its charter. The Registrar knows the seal."* She hands it over. *"It's a letter of field expertise. It won't buy your passage, and you don't need it for the door; they take anyone who signs. It's for after. She'll write it in a different column from everyone else's."*

*(If they saved the farm:)* *"Father's paying your passage, and half your bond. He says he's buying your absence from my stable. He isn't. He just can't say the other thing."*

*(And then, because she owes them one true sentence:)* *"Whatever else happened out there, thank you for asking me whether I ride. Nobody ever thought to ask."*

Hand over [[SP-PBS-00 — Handout — The Patron's Letter]] with the commendations they earned written in.

### What happens next
- **The farm stands** (Adalinde placed, or the note bought down): Maus goes home a broodmare. Her first foal is three years out.
- **The note fell due:** Erlenhof goes through this ring this afternoon. Vuurzward bids. The party can stay and watch, or help Adalinde buy the mare back (she has 60 marks; the mare goes for 150).
- **The Rhennee sale:** the farm stands and the name is gone. Nobody in the Roodberg enclosure will look at Corwin.
- **The northern packet** leaves on Godsday: one night along the shore to Ritterstadt's quay.

## Development
- **Told Otwin** → the last commendation in the patron's letter. Hessel is dismissed by the end of Reaping.
- **Didn't tell him** → Hessel keeps his post, and his Kettenhaus paper is struck out. That is all.
- **Left open:** six hundred marks for a farm mare is far too much. Who on the barges wanted her, and how did they know she'd be on that boat? Sivert's 400 and Hessel's 60 both trace to the Kettenhaus of Traft, which holds Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld's Schuldbrief. The party will hear that name again in Ritterstadt.

## Clues Available Here
- Adalinde, last resort: *"Hessel booked the passage. Nobody else knew the day."* → **Hessel sold the route**

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a half-empty ring, a ledger, an old man on a rail
- **Sound:** the auctioneer's singsong, pavilions coming down
- **Smell:** trodden straw, cold cook-fires
- **Touch:** a folded letter with a Roodberg seal
- **Taste:** the last of the Derby beer, flat

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **The party left the Flats after the race.** Adalinde finds them at the lake-gate before the packet sails.
- **Otwin isn't speaking to them** (they took Vuurzward's offer): the Hessel evidence goes to Adalinde instead, and the choice is the same.

## Transition
- → **Ritterstadt.** The northern packet, one night along the shore, and [[The Landing]] at dawn. *(The campaign continues there.)*
