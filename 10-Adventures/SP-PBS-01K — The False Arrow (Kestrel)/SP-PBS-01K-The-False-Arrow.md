# The False Arrow

**APL 3 (level 3) · 4–6 characters · one 3½-hour session**

> *You have been hired by a group of Vallmer priests to travel to Karash, at the base of the Stormwall Mountains, and earn the trust of the Karashi horde. The cold weather and colder welcome you expect to receive make this quite the undertaking indeed.*
>
> A Social / Wilderness one-shot for **4–6 level-3 characters**, Part 1 of *The Plains of Blood and Steel*. A party of Kestrel University freshmen from the Ritterstadt Field Station carries a priest's parley into the winter camp of the Karashi. To be heard, they must pass the horde's three trials while the old Ilkhan's heir calls for war against the Wolf Clans. The war cry was written by someone else: an agent of Malkor has been arming the war faction and staging murders with false Wolf Clan arrows. Everything the party does moves a visible **Favor** track, and the war council at the end plays out on what they earned.

## Date
**Midwinter.** **Travel:** the party leaves Ritterstadt in late autumn. It is three weeks to the hospice, and from there three days down the pass and across the steppe to Karash. [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]] follows later in the year. See [[Timeline]].

## Campaign Frame
The party is made up of **Freshmen (level 3, undeclared)** at Kestrel University's Ritterstadt Field Station, a frontier field campus in the hamlet of Ritterstadt. See **Setting** for what belongs to the Sidebar Press frame.

## Design Frame
- **Thesis:** Trust is earned in front of witnesses, and the party is offered a reason to betray it at every step.
- **Expectation broken:** The "barbarian horde" is the most honest institution in the adventure. The southern merchant is the monster, and the party's own patron has asked them to spy.
- **What this adventure refuses to do:** There is no dungeon and no boss fight. The climax is a council, and the decisive weapon is an arrow laid on the felt.
- **Session spine:** the **Favor** track (0–9), visible all session. See [[SP-PBS-01K — The Favor Tracker]].
- **Tone:** cold, proud, and hospitable. Hunger, grief, and winter are the textures; violence is brief.

## Setting — the Land and the Horde
- The **Karashi** (the Tiger Clans) are horse nomads roaming the steppes north of the Stormwalls, from the Thornwood to the Stormwall foothills and from the Pale Sea to the Vesh river.
- They are ruled by **Ilkhan Sorvash**, "the Old Tiger": aged but undimmed, a capable illusionist, and famous for refusing Malkor's alliance. He spits on the demon-lord.
- Their capital, **Karash**, is a walled hill town at the foot of the Stormwalls (about 3,800 settled), ringed each winter by the horde's tent-city. The horde itself numbers 70,000 or more.
- They are light cavalry with horn bows and lances, riding under tiger banners and tiger-tail standards. The Ilkhan's robe of state is said to be saber-tooth tiger skin.
- They **trade with Amshar and Vallmere and raid both.** Vallmere folk are known as shrewd negotiators with the nomads; more than one raid has ended in horse-for-brandy barter.
- They **war continually with the Wolf Clans (the Ghorak)** to the east, and with the Thornwood tribes.
- **The Weaver**, the Karashi Lady of Threads, is central to their spiritual life. Most Karashi speak no Common.

**Sidebar Press frame:** Kestrel University's Ritterstadt Field Station (Magister Kartner, Professor Federkiel, Professor Vogelsang, the Fiend's Rest and its Job Board), the St. Aldric hospice with Father Brennin and Sister Halya, the Vallmere–Amshar caravan road over the Stormwalls, Khan Temur, Mother Sarnai, Kezrin and his false-flag murders, Yulbar's payments, the three trials, the parley terms, the Favor track, the tiger-claw token, and the Wolf Clan sinew-fletching tell.

## Karashi Color

- **Custom is law.** Guests set down steel, the taster drinks first, letters belong to their bearers, and a shaman's word outranks a khan's in matters of custom.
- **Women ride to war.** Among the Karashi, women are a larger share of the fighters than among other nomads. Batzorig's riders are mixed, and so is Temur's war camp.
- **The art of beguilement.** By the first ilkhans' old decree, the ilkhan studies illusion. Sorvash never uses it on the party, but a PC who knows the tradition (History DC 15) reads his stillness differently.
- **Language.** Most Karashi speak no Common. Sarnai translates and softens; Batzorig and Chuluun speak it badly; Naran knows three words. A PC who attended [[Professor-Ilsabet-Federkiel]]'s Karashi lectures hears what Sarnai leaves out.
- **The Weaver.** The Lady of Threads is the horde's center. Sarnai speaks of threads, cloth, and the loom.
- **The Wolf Clans.** The Ghorak raid the Tiger's herds every winter. They are known for keeping their word, which makes a staged Wolf massacre easier to believe and worse to discover.

## What the Locals Know
- **At Ritterstadt:** the Stormwall pass is closing. The caravans say the Karashi have started "taxing it with arrows," and Father Brennin wants a parley carried by people the nomads won't shoot on sight.
- **On the caravan road:** Tiger raiders are to blame, and the horde is a mob of barbarians.
- **In Karash:** the Wolf Clans murdered Temur's brother. Tsolmon came home with a Wolf Clan arrow in his back, and everyone has heard about it.

## What's Really Happening
A stone hospice of St. Aldric sits in a high Stormwall pass on the Vallmere–Amshar caravan road, kept by [[Father-Brennin|Father Brennin]]. Twice this winter, Karashi raiders have taken its supply trains. Brennin wants a parley: grain, salt, and medicine every spring, if the Tiger's riders leave the road alone.

What Brennin doesn't know:

1. **The horde is splitting.** Ilkhan Sorvash is old. His nephew and heir-presumptive, [[Khan-Temur|Khan Temur]], wants war with the Wolf Clans before the thaw and calls any parley cowardice.
2. **Someone is feeding the fire.** [[Master-Kezrin|Master Kezrin]], an Amshari "horse-trader," is an agent of Malkor. He sells black-steel blades to Temur's riders below cost. Three weeks ago, his men, dressed as Wolf Clan riders, ambushed a five-rider patrol. One rider, **Tsolmon**, made it home with a Wolf Clan arrow in his back and died saying "Wolves." Temur's brother and the other three never came home; Kezrin's men dumped them by a saber-tooth's den. Temur keeps Tsolmon's arrow in his sash. The fletching is wrong. Malkor has also paid the Wolf Clan raider **Yulbar** to keep the Tiger's herds thin, so even the raids Temur rightly resents were bought.
3. **The priests aren't the only ones with an agenda.** [[Sister-Halya|Sister Halya]], Brennin's junior, gives the party a sealed letter to open inside Karash. It asks them to count the horde's spears for Vallmere.

**If the PCs never come:** Temur rides east at the thaw with the horde's young riders and Kezrin's steel. Tiger and Wolf bleed each other white through the spring, and Malkor's agents walk into the gap.

## Hook
The default is the Field Station frame (see [[SP-PBS-01K — Scene 00 — Kestrel University]]). For tables outside it:

1. **The Job Board (default).** Brennin's request is pinned to the Job Board at the Fiend's Rest Public House in Ritterstadt, and [[Magister-Roswitha-Kartner]] reads it aloud: field credit, and Brennin's report on the party comes to her.
2. **The caravan master.** A Vallmere caravan master who lost two wagons in the pass pays the party to carry Brennin's letter.
3. **Personal stake.** A PC owes Brennin a life: the hospice nursed them through a winter fever.

## PC Objectives
Each has more than one route. Objectives 5–7 are optional.

1. **Reach Karash alive** and get an audience. *(Restraint with Batzorig, guest-craft, gifts.)*
2. **Pass the three trials:** the Horse, the Bow, the Fire. *(Ride or gentle the stallion; kill the tiger; answer honestly.)*
3. **Find out who killed the patrol.** *(The ravine evidence, the beater's testimony, Tsolmon's arrow, Kezrin's papers.)*
4. **Win voice at the war council** (Favor 5+) and make the case. *(Persuasion, evidence, or a champion's duel.)*
5. *(Optional)* **Stop Kezrin.** *(Warn Sarnai, spot him, stop him, or chase him.)*
6. *(Optional)* **Decide what to do with Halya's letter.** *(Spy, refuse, or tell Sarnai.)*
7. *(Optional)* **Earn the tiger-claw token** (Favor 7+).

## Subplots (keyed to PCs; assign at the table)
- **Kartner's Wilderness Fieldcraft lectures:** guest-craft locks in the Scene 2 restraint bonus; recognizes Wolf sinew-fletching in the ravine.
- **Federkiel's Karashi lectures:** follows the council without Sarnai's softening; reads Kezrin's ledger.
- **Antiquities or history lectures (Vogelsang):** recognizes the fletching without a roll; knows the beguilement tradition.
- **Scouts & Surveyors member:** carries the terrain log for the Stormwall road (advantage on the Scene 1 journey check).
- **Animal Handling or Survival:** the natural candidate to gentle the black stallion, which earns Sarnai's endorsement.
- **Cleric or paladin:** Brennin's faith is St. Aldric's; Sarnai's is the Weaver. She will ask this PC what their god thinks of fate.
- **Honest face:** Halya chooses this PC for her letter.

## Act Structure
Likely path: Scenes 00 → 07. See [[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-01K — The False Arrow (Kestrel)/Scenes/SP-PBS-01K — Scene Index|Scene Index]] for the run sheet.

### Act 1 — The Road (≈40 min)
- [[SP-PBS-01K — Scene 00 — Kestrel University]] (5 min): the Job Board at the Fiend's Rest; each player names whose lectures they attend.
- [[SP-PBS-01K — Scene 01 — The High Pass]] (15 min): Brennin's letter, Halya's sealed letter, the descent, an earned hazard.
- [[SP-PBS-01K — Scene 02 — The Cold Welcome]] (20 min): Batzorig disarms them; Sorvash's question; the three trials are set.

### Act 2 — The Trials (≈1 hr 50 min with break)
- [[SP-PBS-01K — Scene 03 — Trial of the Horse]] (20 min): ride or gentle the black stallion; weapons return on success.
- [[SP-PBS-01K — Scene 04 — Trial of the Bow]] (35 min): the saber-tooth, and the patrol's bodies.
- [[SP-PBS-01K — Scene 05 — Night in Karash]] (30 min): Kezrin's offer and his men; Halya's letter.
- [[SP-PBS-01K — Scene 06 — Trial of the Fire]] (15 min): three honest answers at Sarnai's fire.

### Act 3 — The Council (≈50 min)
- [[SP-PBS-01K — Scene 07 — The War Council]] (45 min): three rounds, Kezrin's exit, Sorvash's ruling; endings.

## Climax at a Glance
| Round | What it takes | Seeded in |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — The parley** | Persuasion DC 14 (12 with the tiger-claw token) | S1 (Brennin's letter), Favor 7+ |
| **2 — The evidence** | Investigation DC 13, advantage if they spied unseen; **automatic** if they lay Tsolmon's arrow beside the ravine arrows | S2 (Temur's arrow), S4 (ravine), S5 (black steel in the war camp) |
| **3 — The choice** | Persuasion or Insight DC 14 (advantage if Round 2 won), or a champion's duel | S4 (Temur's grief), S6 (honest answers) |
| **Kezrin's exit** | Warned Sarnai (auto-capture), Perception DC 14, a stopping action, or a DC 13 chase | S5 (his visit), S6 (telling Sarnai) |

**Results:** Rounds 2 + 3 won → *The parley holds*. Any two rounds → *A year bought*. Round 2 only → *Truth without trust*. Otherwise → *Heard and refused*. Below Favor 5 → *Soft fail* (if open; see [[SP-PBS-01K — The Favor Tracker]]) or *Cast out*.
