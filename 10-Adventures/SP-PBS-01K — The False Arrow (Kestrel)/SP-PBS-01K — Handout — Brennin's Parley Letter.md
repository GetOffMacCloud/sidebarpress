# Handout 1 — Brennin's Parley Letter

Back to [[SP-PBS-01K-The-False-Arrow]] · Given in [[SP-PBS-01K — Scene 01 — The High Pass]] · Read aloud by Sarnai in [[SP-PBS-01K — Scene 02 — The Cold Welcome]]

*Sealed with the cudgel of St. Aldric, ribboned in hospice grey.*

---

*To Ilkhan Sorvash, the Old Tiger, from Father Brennin, keeper of the hospice of St. Aldric in the high pass — greetings in the name of Aldric, who shelters all who keep the road.*

*Twice this winter your riders have taken our supply trains. I do not write in anger; hunger is not malice, and I know a hungry winter when I see one. I write with an offer, plainly made: each year at first thaw, the hospice will deliver to Karash twenty wagonloads of grain, ten of salt, and such medicines as we can spare — freely given, no price asked — if the Tiger's riders let the caravan road run unharried.*

*This is not tribute from fear. It is bread from neighbors. The road feeds us both, or it feeds neither.*

*The bearers of this letter have my full trust. Hear them, I beg you, as you would hear me.*

*— Brennin*
