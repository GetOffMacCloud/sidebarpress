## Build Notes
- **M1, the Archive:** squat stone, narrow glass windows, slate roof. Six reading desks, map presses on the west wall, the iron grille wall to wall. Shelving aisles 5 ft wide in the stacks. The pike's board is on the far wall, and the coffer's plinth is at the end of the center aisle. The book-chute is on the back wall at shoulder height, opening to the alley. **No light sources anywhere** (the no-flame rule); the Player map should feel dark.
- **M2, the Landing:** timber quay with room for four barges. The *Hecht* is tied at the end. Brewfest trestles with hop-garlands run along the shore; the tally-house and smokehouse stand at the far end. The palisade gate is up the slope.
- **M3, the *Hecht*:** a single-masted lake cog. The open hatch is amidships with a ladder down. The hold holds two hammocks, crates of copper and furs, and the Ashbox on a crate in the middle. The clerk's cabin is aft, with a cot, a writing slope and an empty birdcage.
- **Fog:** export a separate fog overlay for M2 and M3 (lightly obscured beyond 30 ft) for VTT use.
- **DM overlays** mark the A- and H-keys, the guardian's board, the grille's *alarm*, and Snick's usual perch.
