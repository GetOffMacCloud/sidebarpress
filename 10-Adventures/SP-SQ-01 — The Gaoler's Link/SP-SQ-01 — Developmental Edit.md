# SP-SQ-01 — Developmental Edit (structure, pacing, railroad check)

Back to [[SP-SQ-01-The-Gaolers-Link]] · Board: [[SP-SQ-01 — Board]] · Run sheet: [[10-Adventures/SP-SQ-01 — The Gaoler's Link/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]]

> [!info] How to use this note
> Greybeard asked for the full Edit column in one pass, so every finding below that doesn't change the design was **applied directly** (marked ✅). Anything that is a judgment call is under **Needs you**. To reverse a fix, untick it and ask Claude to revert it.

## Verdict
The structure holds. It is a situation, not a plot:
- The party can refuse, report, steal, confess or sting, and each has a written path.
- The climax has five routes, each seeded at least one scene earlier (Climax at a Glance).
- There are five lettered endings, including defeat (E) and discipline (D).

**Pacing.** The 4-hour budget adds up to about 4:05: Act 1 30, Act 2 60, Act 3 50, Act 4 80, plus 15 for seating and 10 for a break. The climax and denouement take about a third of play time, well over the 15% minimum. Nothing in the middle outweighs the finale.

**Logic.** The edit found four real holes and one railroad, all fixed below. The biggest was the handover: as drafted, the "box it first" branch could almost never happen, because the box was in the hold, so the reveal was accidentally guaranteed.

**Classic-module musts: 12 of 12 pass** (re-scored after the fixes).

## Findings

### Major
- [x] **1 · ✅ Branch — the "box it first" branch was nearly impossible.** The Ashbox sat open in the hold and Brandmeier stood at the hatch. To box the Link, the party had to board, which put it within 30 feet of Brandmeier and woke him. One branch of the scene couldn't happen, and the reveal was guaranteed by accident. **Fix:** Fesch now meets the party *alone at the foot of the gangplank*, holding the open Ashbox. Brandmeier is kept aboard at the far rail (about 40 ft) and the bargemen at the top of the gangplank (about 35 ft). Waking now happens only if the party carries the Link aboard, insists on handing it to "the master," or argues long enough (3 rounds) that Brandmeier comes down. *(Scene 06 box, To the table, Who Is Here and Beats; main note, Scheme step 4.)*
- [x] **2 · ✅ Rules — the tethers couldn't last.** As written, a tether had to be planted within **7 days** of its making and held **30 days**. Brandmeier has been tethered since Flocktime (about five months), and Vesharan carries a satchel of live pins a thousand miles from Dorakaa. **Fix:** *Tether* at 7th level or higher now keeps a year unplanted and holds a year once planted. Kermin makes his that way. *(The [[Tether]] spell, At Higher Levels; [[Bone Tether]].)*
- [x] **3 · ✅ Rules — "Snick can't see them" contradicted the Link's law.** The Link blocks *divination*. A quasit's own eyes aren't divination, so Snick should still see the party. **Fix:** new law, **the Ache**. A creature sworn to Iuz feels the unboxed Link within 30 feet as a cold ache behind the eyes, knows it's near, and hangs back. Vesharan still can't see through Snick's eyes near it, because shared senses *are* divination. Also added a definition of **"sworn"** (Iuz's priests, agents, his fiends and his tethered; not a warlock of another patron). *(Laws in [[The Gaoler's Link]] and [[SP-SQ-01 — The Job]]; Snick's Watch; Scene 05 complication 6; Scene 06 Snick line.)*
- [x] **4 · ✅ Motive — why does Vesharan need students at all?** The text implied it but never said it. **Fix:** a "Why students" bullet in What's Really Happening:
  - He can't find the Link by magic.
  - He can't touch it.
  - He can't send his thralls, because a tethered man wakes the moment the coffer opens.
  - Outsiders can't enter the Field Station after dark.
- [x] **5 · ✅ Railroad — the smart choice was punished.** If the party went to Grell or Vogelsang in Scene 02, the text had them shrugged off "until Brewfest 5." That's plot protection for the heist. **Fix:**
  - With no evidence, Grell asks for proof: *"Bring me one fact I can put in a ledger by the dinner."*
  - With **any one of F1–F4**, the Field Station acts. Scene 05 becomes a **sting** (Vogelsang lets the "theft" happen while Hask waits at the Landing), and the party goes to the Confession approach.
  - Also written as a Scene 03 Alternate. *(Scene 02 Development and Alternates; Scene 03 Alternates.)*

### Minor
- [x] **6 · ✅ Continuity — Hogar met a clerk who won't pass the gate.** Hogar's line said he made the box "for that clerk," but Fesch never goes inside the palisade. **Fix:** *"Made a lead box to a Perrender clerk's order. Sent the order by a boy, collected it at the Landing."*
- [x] **7 · ✅ Rules — the guardian's trigger had a gap.** The Archive Pike woke only if the coffer was *moved*, so a rogue lifting the Link out without moving the coffer slipped past it. **Fix:** "opened or moved."
- [x] **8 · ✅ Clarity — half the fee.** "Half now" of 5 marks is now spelled out as 2 marks and 5 kleinmarks.

### From the paper playtest
- [x] **9 · ✅ Dead end — a careless party had no last chance.** A party that boxes the Link on the quay knowing nothing went straight to Conclusion E, with no reveal and no decision. **Fix:** a safety valve. When the Link is boxed, Dolz's scrying clears. She runs to the Landing as the lines come off (*"Whatever was hiding in the Archive just left it. Was it you?"*), which offers one last chance through Hold the boat or Board her. Failure is still possible. *(Scene 06.)*
- [x] **10 · ✅ Route — the Cuthbertine and the seal.** A cleric of St. Cuthbert hears Brandmeier describe a starburst-and-cudgel seal on a family heirloom. **Fix:** DC 10 Religion recognizes it; Brandmeier's answer, *"Grandfather was devout,"* and his glance at Fesch are written in. *(Scene 01.)*
- [x] **11 · ✅ Route — *detect magic* in Scene 01.** It would show Fesch's *disguise self* (illusion) and Brandmeier's collar (enchantment). That's a strong early clue, and the text didn't say so. **Fix:** written into Scene 01 and added to the Clue Ledger under F3. Fesch notices the spell and goes to Wary.

### Not findings (checked and left alone)
- **The climax is reachable even if the party learns nothing in Scene 03.** It is, through the last resorts in Scene 06 and now the Dolz safety valve.
- **Vesharan escaping.** He is *built* to escape, but the scene lists three ways to stop him and doesn't protect him. Not plot armor.
- **Encounter math.** Rechecked at APL 2, 3 and 4 (Scene 05 easy; Scene 06 hard at every APL; Scene 07 easy). The APL 2 Handover fix from the build stands.
- **Scene 04 has no time budget.** It's a site key used by Scenes 03 and 05, as in SP-PBS-03.
