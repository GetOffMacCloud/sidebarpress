# Handout — The Cipher Letter

Back to [[SP-SQ-01-The-Gaolers-Link]] · Found in [[Scene 07 — Turning the Job]] (H3)

## Player Text
![[SP-SQ-01 Handout - The Cipher Letter.png|420]]

*(A single sheet of heavy paper, closed with black wax pressed with three curving horns. In the corner, a small mark in red ink: a skull, grinning.)*

> MY MASTER IN THE HEART OF BONE
>
> ULN VORX UK EVHI FMNS. XZE TSFDH WGS ESLOJ.
> RIVFE FFD PX NAMWTU. RC SHUEU OPF CPT VXHH CHK.
> BUB GMSD ZK BKY MEJFB CK YPC CEYPJTH, CTI AVAZIW.
>
> — D.

## The Key (DM)
- **Cipher:** a simple Bakluni schoolroom shift: each letter moves forward by the position of the word in its line (1st word +1, 2nd +2, and so on). Breaking it takes a **DC 15 Intelligence** check, or a day with Lecturer Dolz or Tock, or *comprehend languages*.
- **The plain text, as players should receive it once broken** (handout image below):

![[SP-SQ-01 Handout - The Cipher Letter (Broken).png|420]]

> *My master in the Heart of Bone —*
> *The Link is found. The hole in the sky is the Archive.*
> *Clean hands will carry it. The merchant holds.*
> *The girl on the levy in the Archive has the gift you described, unschooled.*
> *— D.*

- **DM:** don't make the players solve the shift letter by letter. Give the plain text on a successful check. The cipher block above is set dressing; it doesn't need to decode exactly.

## DM Notes
- **The seal** is the **Horned Society**'s three horns (DC 15 History or Religion). It was made with a [[Counterfeiter's Seal-Ring]], Panshazek's people's tool. **Why it's on a Kermin agent's letter** is the arc's thread [[The Molag Seal]].
- **The grinning skull** is Iuz's (DC 15 Religion). **(F3.)**
- **"The girl on the levy"** is [[Liesl Amsel]]. This is the first written sign that Kermin wants her (thread [[The Girl on the Levy]]).
- **"D."** is Dawud. If the party has Vesharan's name from his own mouth, this confirms it.
