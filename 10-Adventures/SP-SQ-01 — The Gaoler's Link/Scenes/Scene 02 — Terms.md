# Scene 02 — Terms

[[10-Adventures/SP-SQ-01 — The Gaoler's Link/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 01 — Brewfest on the Landing]] · Next → [[Scene 03 — Legwork]]

## Purpose
Strike the deal or refuse it. Hand over Handout 1. Make sure someone at the table remembers the Scholar's Oath.
**Budget:** 10 minutes. Running long? They take the first number.

## Arrival
> *Brandmeier sets a leather purse on the table and doesn't open it. Fesch turns his ledger to a fresh page and uncaps his ink. Across the room, the Job Board's paper rustles every time the door opens.*

**To the table:** *"He's waiting for an answer. Does anyone ask a question first?"*

## Who Is Here
As Scene 01. [[Karet]], the Fiend's Rest's keeper, is wiping the same mug for the fourth time and listening.

## Beats

### The offer
- **Pay:** **5 marks each**, half now. The mark is the gold piece, and a laborer earns 3 kleinmarks a day, so this is more than two weeks' wages for an hour's work: generous enough to tempt, and generous enough that a careful student asks why. **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)** raises it to 8 marks, after a nod from Fesch.
- **The hint:** Brandmeier "knows a Schwartzenbruin house that hires University graduates." (True. It is his own. He doesn't remember that he said it.)
- **The job as he describes it:** the coffer, the sealed stacks behind the grille in the Archive, the handover aboard the *Hecht* before dawn on Brewfest 4.

### Proof
If asked for proof, Brandmeier produces a **deed of pledge** ([[SP-SQ-01 — Handout — The Deed of Pledge]]). It is a good forgery. **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation):** the ink is under a year old, and the University's arms (book, cudgel and quill) are drawn, not pressed.

### The Oath
If no player mentions it, ask a student character what the Scholar's Oath says about this. *"I shall not steal manuscripts…"* Taking University property is a standing offense ("selling research property"). Brandmeier waves it away: *"It isn't the University's. That's the whole point."* Fesch adds, quietly: *"Nothing is being sold. Something is being returned."*

### Refusal
Brandmeier takes it graciously: *"Then drink the ale, and no hard feelings."* Fesch does not, and watches them leave. Go to the decline path in [[SP-SQ-01-The-Gaolers-Link#Hook]]: [[Konstanz Weil]] and [[Rudi Weil]] take the job.

## Development
- **The party takes the money** → half now, and the clock starts. Fesch writes their names in his ledger. They are now in Snick's watch ([[SP-SQ-01 — The Job#Snick's Watch]]).
- **The party takes the money and plans to report it** → that's a fine plan; they still need to know enough to be believed. Vogelsang, told now, laughs: *"A Perrender wants an iron box? Let him want."* Give them Scene 03 to build the case.
- **The party asks to see the clerk's hands** → Fesch keeps his left hand gloved. The burn scar is under it (Scene 06).

## Clues Available Here
- The forged deed (new ink; a drawn seal) → toward **F1**
- *"Nothing is being sold. Something is being returned."* → a line worth remembering at the handover

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a purse that isn't opened, Fesch's neat columns
- **Sound:** the door, the board's papers, a fiddle starting again
- **Smell:** mutton, always mutton
- **Touch:** the deed's paper, too new to be sixty years old

## Alternates

- **A PC goes straight to the Proctor** → Grell takes a statement, says the Field Station will "keep an eye," and does nothing until Brewfest 5. Skip to Scene 03; the party knows they're on their own.
