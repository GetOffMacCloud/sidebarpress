# Scene 04 — The Archive and the Sealed Stacks

[[10-Adventures/SP-SQ-01 — The Gaoler's Link/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 03 — Legwork]] · Next → [[Scene 05 — The Night of the Warden's Dinner]]

## Purpose
The site key for [[The Archive]] (RG-04a §13), used by day in Scene 03 and by night in Scene 05.
**Budget:** reference.

## Laws of the Archive
- **No flame after dark, ever.** A light seen in its windows adds **+2 Heat** at once.
- **Nothing leaves without a countersign.** Lifting a map from the presses is a standing offense: **+1 Heat**.
- **The sealed files stay shut.** They hold the Founders' Compact and other matters the Registrar seals by the Chancellor's standing order (RG-08). Each box opened is **+1 Heat**, and the Registrar will notice and pursue it. *DM:* that is other material; don't open it in this adventure.
- **The Link inside the stacks:** once the coffer (A6) is open, divination within 30 feet fails. See [[SP-SQ-01 — The Job#The Laws of the Link]].

## Recent Events
The Chancellor's crate arrived in 574 under seal. Since summer, Vogelsang has been in the stacks every week with the Registrar's key and no lamp. Verenwyr boarded the book-chute shut years ago and hasn't checked it since.

## Key

**A1. THE DOOR AND PORCH**
> *An iron-studded door under a slate porch. A brass plate reads THE ARCHIVE — DAWN TO DUSK. Beside the boot-scraper stands a rain-butt, and on the door hangs a notice in a fine, angular hand: NO FLAME.*

The only front way in. It's in sight of the palisade path.
**Trap:** none. **Lock:** DC 13 thieves' tools; Verenwyr keeps the key.
**Development:** a party crossing the porch during a gate-rota round (Lax: once an hour) needs a **DC 12 group Dexterity (Stealth)** check or gains +1 Heat.

**A2. THE READING ROOM**
> *Six desks stand under the narrow windows. The Scouts & Surveyors' map presses fill one wall, the Publications shelf holds rows of bound Field Reports, and a cloth covers the Illuminators' copy-table. At the back, an iron grille runs wall to wall.*

The public room. Nothing of value except to a student.
**Creatures:** at Wary, **[[Oswin Kettler]]** (1), checking the presses by feel; he never carries a light here.
**Tactics:** Oswin shouts first (+2 Heat) and asks questions after. A friend of the Scouts can talk him down (DC 14 Persuasion). He doesn't fight.
**Treasure:** none. A map lifted from the presses is +1 Heat and will be missed by Brewfest 5.

**A3. THE GRILLE**
> *Black iron bars run from the flagstones to the rafters. A gate in the middle is fastened with a fist-sized padlock. Beyond it, shelving vanishes into the dark.*

**Lock:** DC 15 thieves' tools, the Registrar's key, or a wax copy (the Registrar Edge).
**Trap:** the padlock carries an *alarm* (audible). The bell rings on the Registrar's desk in [[The Station House]], two doors from the dinner. **Trigger:** opening the lock without the true key or a dispelling. **Effect:** **+3 Heat** at once. **Tell:** DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana), or *detect magic*, shows a faint abjuration on the lock.

**A4. THE SEALED STACKS**
> *Shelves of boxed files stand in rows, each box tied with tape and sealed in the Chancellor's red wax. A crate stenciled CITY — BY ORDER sits on the floor. On the far wall hangs a stuffed giant pike, longer than a man, as if someone gave up deciding where it belonged.*

The stacks. The pike is the guardian (A5); the coffer is on its own plinth at the end (A6).
**Treasure:** the sealed files, which must stay shut (see Laws).

**A5. THE ARCHIVE PIKE**
The pike was mounted on a guardian's frame by a long-dead antiquarian with a sense of humor. It is a **[[Trophy Guardian]]** (1). It animates if the coffer is moved without the watchword, **"by leave of the Registrar."**
**Tactics:** it blocks the grille rather than chasing, bites and drags its victim toward its board, and returns to the wall when it hears the watchword. Each round of combat is **+1 Heat**.
**Development:** the Vogelsang lead gives the watchword automatically; the Registrar lets it slip on a DC 14 Persuasion at the counting-room.

**A6. THE WARD-COFFER**
> *A black iron coffer sits on a stone plinth of its own. The lid is sealed with grey wax pressed with a starburst and a cudgel, and the coffer is colder than the room.*

The Link's coffer.
**Lock and seal:** St. Cuthbert's seal (DC 13 Religion to name it) breaks silently but shows afterward. The lock is DC 17 thieves' tools, or Vogelsang's key from his watch-chain.
**Treasure:** **[[The Gaoler's Link]]** on felt, showing its six sigils. Under the felt lie Vogelsang's provenance slip and study notes ([[SP-SQ-01 — Handout — The Provenance Slip]]) (**F1**, **F4**).
**ESSENTIAL CLUE:** once the coffer is open, any divination the PCs have running shows fog. If Snick is shadowing them, a hiss comes from the grille and the giggling stops. Dolz's taper, if lit outside, gutters out.

**A7. THE BOOK-CHUTE**
Library returns used to come in through a hatch at shoulder height in the stacks' back wall, opening onto the alley behind the Archive. Verenwyr boarded it from inside years ago, and the boards are old.
**Play:** working the boards loose quietly takes a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check or 10 minutes with tools. A Small character can wriggle through. A Medium one needs a DC 15 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check or gets stuck (+1 Heat per round of struggling).
**Development:** this route bypasses A1–A3 entirely, so the grille's *alarm* never comes into it.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** red wax in rows, a glass eye on the wall, black bars against blacker shelving
- **Sound:** nothing at all, which is the point; the mice
- **Smell:** cold stone, paper, old fish-skin from the pike
- **Touch:** an iron coffer colder than the air
- **Taste:** dust
