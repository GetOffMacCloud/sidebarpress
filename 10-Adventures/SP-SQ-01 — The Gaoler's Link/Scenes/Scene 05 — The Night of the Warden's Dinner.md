# Scene 05 — The Night of the Warden's Dinner

[[10-Adventures/SP-SQ-01 — The Gaoler's Link/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 04 — The Archive and the Sealed Stacks]] · Next → [[Scene 06 — The Handover]]

## Purpose
Run the plan. Spend Edges, track Heat, and get the Link out of the stacks. Then let the party discover, before the handover if they're careful, what they're holding.
**Budget:** 50 minutes. Running long? Three checks and an Edge each, and one complication.

## Arrival
> *Brewfest 3, after dark. Laughter and fiddle music spill from the Station House windows, and the whole Field Station smells of malt. Across the yard, the Archive sits squat and black under its slate roof, the one building in Ritterstadt with no light at all. Somewhere a door bangs, and a drunk voice starts a song and gives up.*

**To the table:** *"You have until the midnight bell. Lay out the plan. You have one minute."* Time it. Then play it obstacle by obstacle.

## Who Is Here
- At the dinner in [[The Station House]] hall: **[[Rektor Albrecht Weissmantel]]** (host), **[[Registrar Hedwig Ammerlin]]** (the stacks key on a ribbon), **[[Professor Casimir Vogelsang]]** (the coffer key on his watch-chain; drinking water), **[[Mathias Grell]]**, **[[Garrok Stonebrow]]**, and the rest of the faculty.
- On the palisade path: **[[Jens Ober]]** and another gate-rota Probationer, with a lantern, once an hour.
- Near the Archive at Wary: **[[Oswin Kettler]]**.
- Somewhere in the dark: **[[Konstanz Weil]]** and **[[Rudi Weil]]**, if Fesch made them a backup offer (see complication 3).
- **[[Snick]]**, watching, until the coffer opens.

## Beats

### Playing the plan
When the plan meets an obstacle, call for **one check**, or let a player spend an **Edge**. Track **Heat** in the open ([[SP-SQ-01 — The Job#Heat (0–6)]]). Useful DCs: lock DC 13 (A1) / 15 (A3) / 17 (A6); Stealth past the rota DC 12; the book-chute DC 13 Athletics; Sleight of Hand on a key at the dinner DC 15.

**The inside man.** A PC may attend the dinner as a guest: a senior student, a faculty favorite, or someone's plus-one. They can lift a key (Sleight of Hand DC 15, or DC 12 with the Registrar Edge), watch who leaves, or stall Vogelsang. They miss the job, but they're in the room where it gets noticed.

### Complications (d6; roll once per 30 minutes of job time, or when it's too smooth)
1. Two Freshmen looking for somewhere private try the Archive porch.
2. Vogelsang leaves the dinner early, at the 11th bell. He doesn't drink, and he wants an hour with the Link.
3. **Konstanz and Rudi Weil** are sneaking about too, with a crowbar and a bad plan (Fesch's backup). Rivals, witnesses or recruits, as the party prefers.
4. Jens Ober stops on the palisade path to relieve himself in full view of the porch.
5. Oswin Kettler, worried about the damp, heads for the map presses.
6. Snick spooks a cat, which yowls (+1 Heat). If the party already holds the unboxed Link, Snick keeps its distance (the ache), and this result does nothing.

### Opening the coffer (A6)
The moment the coffer opens, read the ESSENTIAL CLUE from A6: divination goes to fog, and a hiss comes from the grille as Snick loses the party. Then hand over [[SP-SQ-01 — Handout — The Provenance Slip]] if they lift the felt. **The Link is cold.** Anyone holding it feels it.

### Who is shown what
If the party reads the sigils or the slip now, they know **F1** and probably **F4** before the handover. That's the best version of this adventure: they can plan the turn in [[Scene 07 — Turning the Job]] instead of improvising it in Scene 06. If they don't look, Scene 06 will show them.

**Terrain:** narrow aisles between shelving (5 ft), the grille gate (A3) as a choke point, no light.
**Tactics:** the pike blocks the grille gate and bites the bearer of the coffer, dragging them toward its board. It stops at the watchword.
**DM direction:** the fight is a Heat clock, not a killer. Each round is +1 Heat. Remind the table that the watchword exists.

**Being caught.** Nobody at the Field Station fights students to kill. If Grell, Garrok or the gate rota catch the party, they grab, shout and hold. Use **guards** (MM p. 347) fighting nonlethally. A party that draws steel on students has committed a standing offense: note it for Scene 08.

## Development
- **Clean getaway (Heat 0–2)** → nobody knows until Brewfest 5.
- **Seen but not caught (Heat 3–4)** → Grell's inquiry opens at noon on Brewfest 4.
- **Alert (Heat 5–6)** → the inquiry opens before dawn. Scene 06 starts with a proctor looking for the party.
- **Caught** → go to Scene 08 (Conclusion C or D), or let Vogelsang hear them out. A party that tells the truth now plays Scene 07 with the Field Station's help.
- **The Weil cousins caught instead** → they name Fesch within minutes. The party now has a witness, and Fesch has a reason to move the handover up (*Hecht* to Wary).

- The provenance slip and study notes → **F1**, **F4**
- Snick's hiss as the coffer opens → **F4**
