# The Fell Harvest

*A Bestiary of Hunger, Horror & Ruin*

**A setting-agnostic bestiary for 2014 5e: fifty-five creatures and one template (CR 0–15), plus a Rogues' Gallery of twenty-three named villains (CR 4–23)**

> Country horrors and threshold things: bargains that came due, households with standards, bridges that charge for crossing, and bells that ring for the wrong reasons. Every creature has a full statblock, lore, tactics, hooks, and spoils. Most of them can be outwitted as well as outfought.

## Series Code
`SP-BST-NN` is the bestiary line, kept apart from `SP-PBS` (Greyhawk) and `SP-HAL` (Halloween). `01` is this book. Codes are never reused.

## How This Product Is Built
- **Source of truth:** one note per creature in `65-Bestiary/`, made from [[90-Templates/Creature]]. Adventures link to these notes rather than copying statblocks.
- **Manifest and Longform index:** [[65-Bestiary/00 — Bestiary Index]]. The `longform: scenes:` list there must name every shipping note, in order.
- **Board:** [[SP-BST-01 — Board]].
- **Appendices** ([[90 — Creatures by Challenge]], [[91 — Creatures by Environment]]) are static tables generated from frontmatter, so they compile for print. Regenerate them after any CR or environment change.

## Contents
- **Bestiary** (`section: bestiary`): 55 kinds of creature and the Bone Beast Template. There are 12 harvested from our adventures, 2 new, 2 remixes, and 4 folklore creatures; the other 35 were contributed by GrimmGrixis.
- **Rogues' Gallery** (`section: rogues-gallery`, opener [[03 — Rogues' Gallery]]): 23 named villains, all by GrimmGrixis, including six chromatic dragons, the three Echo Sisters, the Marrowmother, and the Gluttonwretch.
- **Minions** (`section: minions`, opener [[04 — Minions]] at 7000, minion templates 7010–7070): seven minion templates applied to any statblock, built to the party's APL (Minion Table APL 1–20), each with a worked FGU-ready example at APL 3. Full-size roles are an optional sidebar in the opener.
- Full lists, with CR, type, origin, and section: [[90 — Creatures by Challenge]] and [[91 — Creatures by Environment]].

## Decisions
- 2026-10-25: **Minions chapter** (Greybeard, revised the same day). Minions are separate, lesser creatures made by **applying a minion template to any statblock**: brute, enforcer, skirmisher, artillery, controller, lurker, or leader. Their numbers are **set by the party's level (APL)**, 4e-style: AC, attack, and DC from the DMG for CR = APL; fixed damage about 40% of that CR's DPR, scaled by type (brute ×1.5, enforcer ×0.75, controller and leader ×0.5); 1 hp; XP is one quarter of a CR = APL creature, and every four count as one creature for the multiplier. The source monster supplies its identity. Full-size role overlays are kept only as a short optional sidebar.
- 2026-10-24: Title is **The Fell Harvest**, subtitle *A Bestiary of Hunger, Horror & Ruin* (Greybeard chose it from a dark-and-grim shortlist).
- 2026-10-24: Batch 2: 57 GrimmGrixis statblocks from `Downloads/Monster Unload` (Haltorix was already in as the Pale Chimera). Greybeard's rulings: credit all to GrimmGrixis; full entries now; **CR policy: keep the stated CR if the math is within 1, re-rate if it's off by 2 or more**, fixing internal arithmetic either way; named individuals go in a **Rogues' Gallery** chapter, and WotC-specific names become generic epithets (Tiamat → the Dragon Queen; Yeenoghu → the Ravening Lord; Ravnica and guild references removed). Every entry carries a changes table and CR working. The writer's brief is [[Bestiary-Entry-Brief]].
- 2026-10-24: The Gluttonwretch moved to the Rogues' Gallery.
- 2026-10-24: Setting-agnostic; 2014 5e; full entries; Obsidian notes plus this product folder. Named villains are entered as a generic type with the named individual as an example (Tithe-Reaper → Josiah Thorne; Threshold Lord → the Gentleman in Grey; Harvest Lord → Thornbury).
- 2026-10-24: Contributed creatures carry `origin: contributed` and a `credit:` field, credited under the title. The Gluttonwretch (GrimmGrixis) was retuned to CR 20 at Greybeard's direction; the working is in its Design Notes.
- 2026-10-24: Every creature carries an FGU Import section (Greybeard's standing rule), generated by `70-Company/Tools/fgu_import.py`. Haunts and templates get none; templates are written into the records of the creatures that use them.
- 2026-10-24: **Art is classic black-and-white pen-and-ink** (Greybeard: home-printed, so color is out of budget). All prompts use the house spines in [[Art-Style-Guide]]: plates are spot illustrations on bare white, and splashes, cover, and openers are heavy-black scenes. Print images are converted to 1-bit at 600 dpi. Tokens come from Greybeard's token maker; see [[SP-BST-01 — Art Brief]].
