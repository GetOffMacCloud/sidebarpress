# Handout — Ashlock's Letter

Back to [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]] · Found in [[Scene 04 — The Cutters' Camp]] (thorough search), or on Ashlock's body.

## Player Text
*(Unsigned draft on cheap paper. Several lines are struck through. The struck lines are shown below as they appear.)*

---

Sister Morrow —

The first of the nine is open. ~~I did not have to dig.~~ A reeve here sold the turf to pay her lord, and her own boy went down into the dark and took the seal from the Mother's breast. He spared me the work. Let the tinker-woman keep the ring on the roads. So long as it rides, the pack is mine.

The crown sits well. The hounds come when I think of them.

I have three for the rite and a fourth marked. When Luna is dark and the small moon goes down, I begin. The man first, then the old woman, then the child. The child will be the best of them.

I will bring you the host by the turning of the year, and the names of the other eight mounds by spring.

The Reaper keeps his own.

— A.

---

## DM Prop Notes
- **Paper:** cheap rag paper, folded in quarters and soft at the folds. Rub one corner with graphite or ash.
- **Hand:** neat, cramped, and clerical. Draw the strike-throughs with a single straight line. He corrects himself like a scribe, not in anger.
- **The sign-off** names his god without naming it. *Religion DC 10:* "the Reaper" is **Nerull**. A cleric PC recognizes it without a roll.
- **When to hand it over:** the moment it is found. Do not paraphrase it. It contains the climax's answers, and the players should have to read it themselves.
- **What it gives them:** the torc is the seal; the crown commands the pack; the rite begins at Celene-set; the order of the captives; Rowan at the mill is "a fourth marked"; and a sequel hook (Sister Morrow and eight more mounds).
