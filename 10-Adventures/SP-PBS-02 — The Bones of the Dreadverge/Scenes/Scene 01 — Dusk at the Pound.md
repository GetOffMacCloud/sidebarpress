# Scene 01 — Dusk at the Pound

[[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-02 — The Bones of the Dreadverge/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 02 — Counting the Missing]]

## Purpose
This scene shows the pack, teaches the party that bludgeoning breaks bone, and plants the climax's key: **the hounds go for the tinker's cart, not the people.** Ashlock enters as a helper.
**Budget:** 30 minutes (5 arrival, 15 fight, 10 aftermath).

## Arrival
> *Waycombe is eleven houses, a mill, and a stone sheep-pound built against the wind. Every shutter on the lane is closed, though the sun has not yet set. Grey ash marks a hand-sized circle on four of the doors. At the pound, a woman stands with a pitchfork, watching the heath to the east, where the heather goes brown and then black.*

Then deliver these one at a time, as their attention finds them:
- Above the heath, the Clatspur ridges stand dark against the last light. Beyond them, everyone in Waycombe knows, lie the Vesve and the Old One's lands.
- A tinker's cart, painted red and yellow, stands with its shafts down beside the pound. Its owner is nailing a board across its back hatch.
- Near the mill, a man in a grey robe sits on a bench beside a woman with a bandaged leg, praying aloud.
- The woman at the pound says one thing without turning around: *"If you're for hire, you're late. If you're passing through, pass quick."*
- The pound holds eleven sheep and room for forty.

*(DM: the man in grey is **Ashlock**, playing the mendicant of Wee Jas. The woman at the pound is **[[Hild-Brennan|Hild Brennan]]**; she gives her name if asked.)*

Let the party talk for two or three exchanges at most. Hild answers in single lines and watches the heath. Then the light goes.

## The Attack
> *The light goes copper, then grey. Something clicks out in the heather, like dice in a cup. Long shapes come over the pound wall with no sound of breath. They are hounds made of bone, strung together with black sinew and old leather. Each wears a ring of green bronze around its neck.*

Statblocks: [[SP-PBS-02 — Bestiary#Bone Hound (CR 1/4, 50 XP)]] · Add one hound for 5 PCs.

**Map (theater of the mind is fine):** The pound is a 40-ft square of waist-high drystone wall (half cover; climbing costs 5 ft of movement). The cart is 10 ft east of the pound gate, the lane runs west, and the mill bench is 60 ft away.

**Tactics (run them strangely; this is the seed):**
- **Round 1:** Half the pack (rounded up) goes **straight for Tamsin's cart** and claws at the boarded hatch, ignoring anyone who is not in the way. The rest go for the sheep.
- Hounds attack PCs who block them or hit them, using Pack Tactics.
- **One hound seizes a ewe** and drags it east at half speed. It does not fight unless it is stopped.
- When half the pack is destroyed, the rest break off east, dragging what they can. **At APL 4, the elk leaves last,** walking, not running.

**DM direction.** Play the cart business without comment. If a player asks why the hounds want the cart, say only *"They're tearing at the back hatch."* Do not explain. This is the moment the climax is built on, and it should feel incidental.

**Bludgeoning.** Describe bludgeoning hits as shattering ("the skull goes like a dropped crock"). Describe piercing hits as slipping between ribs. Tables learn fast.

**Perrender color.** A shutter bangs open and an old man shouts something in Stamtaal, then in Common: *"The Witch's dead! Bar the doors!"* He means Iggwilv. Nobody answers him.

## Aftermath
- **The remains:** loose bone, black sinew that crumbles like old bark, and a **green-bronze collar-ring** with a triple spiral. *History DC 12:* Flan work, very old, and the kind of thing laid in graves. *Religion DC 12:* undead raised as a guardian pack, not by an ordinary necromancer's art. Something is binding them to a place.
- **Ashlock** arrives promptly, kneels by the wounded (Hild's arm, or a PC), and heals.
  - APL 1–2 (Acolyte): he casts *cure wounds* in plain view. *"Wee Jas keeps the gate. She is generous to those who keep her rites."*
  - APL 3–4 (Cult Fanatic): he binds wounds with Medicine. If asked why he does not use magic, he says, *"Wee Jas grants her servants the dead, not the dying."*
- If anyone mentions Iuz, Ashlock nods gravely and lets it stand.
- He asks the party one thing: which of the dead have no bodies to bury. Then he says the deadline line, gently, to Hild:

> **Ashlock:** *"The dark of the moon is a kind night for the dead, sister. Tonight they will be easy."*

- **The cleric subplot.** Before he goes, Ashlock asks a cleric, acolyte, or paladin PC to help him renew the ash circle on the mill door, "where the rain has smeared it," and presses a pinch of ash into their palm. A PC who helps has marked Rowan's door for him (see Scene 2). *Insight DC 13 (advantage for that PC):* his grief is rehearsed.
- He excuses himself "to pray at the wayside shrine" east of town, takes a hooded lantern, and leaves along the Dreadverge track. *(He is going to the Sisters. If the party follows him immediately, see Alternates.)*

## Tamsin's Cart
**[[Tamsin-the-Tinker|Tamsin]]** comes out from under her cart with a hammer. She is grateful in coin-terms: *"That's twice they've come for my wagon. Twice. What's a dog want with pans?"* She does not mention the torc yet. She suspects, but she wants to sell it before anyone tells her it is worth nothing. Scene 2 handles the negotiation.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** copper dusk, black heather, and green bronze bright against grey bone
- **Sound:** a dry clicking, sheep screaming, then the sheep going quiet
- **Smell:** turf-smoke and wet wool. *Absence:* the hounds have no smell at all, though they are close enough to touch.
- **Touch:** cold drystone and sinew that breaks like dry bark
- **Taste:** peat-smoke at the back of the throat

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **The party follows Ashlock at once.** He walks a mile, then puts out his lantern and vanishes into the heather (his Stealth against the followers' passive Perception; in the dark, sight-based Perception has disadvantage). Tracking him (Survival DC 15) leads onto the Dreadverge, so the crossing starts early without the town clues. Hild runs after them with the song and the torc-rumor at the first rest. Start the Rite Clock.
- **The party attacks Ashlock in town.** The villagers defend him: he healed their people. This is a talk-first obstacle. If the PCs shed blood in the lane with no proof, Odila locks her doors to them, and the party loses Scene 2's help. Ashlock surrenders meekly. **If he is held,** at full dark he tries to slip away (his Stealth against the guards' passive Perception). **If he stays held:**
  - The pack runs unsteered that night and hunts by its own law. The hounds still come for the cart.
  - The captives are still bound at the Three Sisters, but no rite can begin. The Rite Clock becomes an **exposure clock**: at 6, Wenna dies of cold, and past 6, Brannoc follows.
  - The Antler Crown is in his pack.

  The party can still return the torc, free the captives, and lay Kethra to rest. Encounter 7 becomes the stag and the hounds guarding the barrow, with no Ashlock.
- **The party never touches the cart.** Hild brings it up in Scene 2: *"Your fighting dogs went for the tinker's cart like it owed them money."*
