# Scene 05 — The Three Sisters

[[00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 03 — Crossing the Dreadverge]] / [[Scene 04 — The Cutters' Camp]]

## Purpose
This is the climax. The outcome is genuinely in doubt, there are four ways to win, and the clock is visible. Then comes a quiet dawn that answers some questions and leaves others open.
**Budget:** 45 minutes for the climax and 15 minutes for the denouement.

## Arrival
Check the **Rite Clock** against the arrival table in [[SP-PBS-02 — The Howl and the Rite]]: early, on time, late, or very late.

> *The mound is a long turf back, twice the height of a man, crowned by three standing stones taller than a horse. A person is lashed upright against each stone. In the middle, a fire burns low and green, and a man in grey stands over it. Branching antlers of dark bone rise from his brow. On the south face of the mound, the turf has been cut away, and a black doorway stands open in the earth.*

Then, one at a time:
- **Captives** (north stone to south-east): **Brannoc**, gagged, struggling; **Wenna**, very still, her lips moving; **Tam**, nine years old, staring at the fire.
- **The pack** circles the foot of the mound, about 60 ft out. Clicking, never stopping.
- **The Barrow Stag** (APL 2+) stands motionless beside the black doorway, white as chalk.
- **Ashlock** speaks without turning, if the party is seen:
> **Ashlock:** *"You are welcome. Every rite wants witnesses."*

If a PC names Iuz or Iggwilv, Ashlock laughs once: *"The Old One has his own dead. These are mine."*

## The Ground (map notes)
- **The mound:** 120 ft long and 60 ft wide, gentle slopes on all sides (normal terrain), with a **10-ft peat-cut face** on the south side. Behind it, the Clatspur ridges block out half the sky.
- **The ring:** a 30-ft triangle of stones, each 15 ft tall. The captives are bound at the stones' bases. The green fire at the center casts dim light out to 20 ft. **Everything else is darkness.**
- **The barrow mouth:** in the south cut face, 30 ft from the ring's center. A 5-ft passage leads 20 ft to a round chamber with a stone bier. **Kethra** lies on it in rotted leather and amber. Her breast is bare where the torc was.
- **The bog path:** north of the mound. Ashlock's escape route, a 5-ft causeway through difficult terrain. The beasts avoid it.
- **Turf stacks** on the south slope give half cover. **The cut face** can be climbed (Athletics DC 10) or descended by jumping (1d6).

## Encounter 7 — The Three Sisters

| 4 | Ashlock (Cult Fanatic variant) + Barrow Stag + 4 bone hounds | Hard (1,700 adj.) |

Add one hound for 5 PCs. Risen captives add a **skeleton** each. Full blocks: [[SP-PBS-02 — Bestiary]].

### Opening position
- The beasts start **60 ft out from the ring**, between the party and the stones. *(This is the round-1 safety rule: nothing reaches a PC or captive on turn one, and no beast will attack a bound captive.)*
- **Early arrival:** the party gets one free round of action before initiative.
- **On time / late:** roll initiative as the party crests the slope.

### The Rite (run every round)
Use the Rite Track from [[SP-PBS-02 — The Howl and the Rite]]:
- Ashlock chants with his **action**. The track ticks at the end of his turn.
- Damage forces a **Con save (DC 10 or half damage)**. On a failure, the tick is lost.
- At 3, the current captive dies and rises. Announce it in one flat sentence.
- The rite breaks if Ashlock drops, is moved 10 ft or more from the center, loses the crown, or the torc goes home.

**Action-economy model (APL 2, 4 PCs, 3 rounds).** The party deals about 18 effective damage per round. Ashlock (13 HP) falls in round 1 or 2 if the party targets him. If they don't, the rite needs 3 uninterrupted rounds to kill Brannoc. The beasts deal about 15 effective damage per round, focused on whoever reaches the ring or carries the torc. A party that ignores the ring loses Brannoc around round 3, and a party that rushes Ashlock takes heavy hits from the stag. Both lines are tense, and neither is a party kill on its own. At APL 3–4, Ashlock's 33 HP means two to three rounds of focused fire, so expect **one captive lost if the party fights only the pack**.

## The Four Solutions
Reward whichever the players find. If they invent a fifth, let them try it, and price it honestly.

### 1. Return the torc (the best ending)
A PC carries the [[Torc-of-the-Hound-Mother|torc]] to the barrow mouth, through the passage (20 ft), and lays it on Kethra's breast (action).
- The bone beasts prefer the torc-bearer as a target. The bearer has disadvantage on Stealth against them. Make the run feel like carrying a lantern through wolves.
- When the torc is laid down, **every bone beast stops, turns, and goes for Ashlock** on its next turn. The crown does nothing. Then, at the end of the next round, the whole pack falls to loose bone.

> *The torc settles into the hollow at the dead woman's throat. Outside, the clicking stops. Then every bone on the mound turns toward the green fire at once, with the same small sound, like a field of dry reeds bending.*

- **DM direction:** let the beasts reach Ashlock before they fall, if they can. Do not narrate his death in detail. Say he went down under them, and that when the bones collapsed, he was under them. If he survives, he is at a quarter of his HP and running for the bog.

### 2. Break the rite
Kill Ashlock, shove him 10 ft from center (Athletics vs. his Athletics or Acrobatics), or keep hitting him to cost him ticks. The rite ends, but **the pack is still awake**. Without a master, it falls back on instinct: it defends the barrow mouth and attacks anyone in the ring except the captives. The fight continues with the rest of the pack. Afterward, the pack wakes again at the next dark of Luna, unless the torc goes home.

### 3. Take the crown
Knock it off (contested Str or Dex check, an action) or loot it from his body. The wearer can steer one beast per round as a bonus action (*Crown-Bearer*). Wearing the crown lets the wearer **hear the pack**, a sound like dry sticks dragged over stone. The wearer can order the pack to stand down (action, Charisma check DC 13; on a success, each beast stops for 1 round). This is a real win, but a temporary one. See the item note: [[Antler-Crown-of-Kethra]].

### 4. Cut them loose
A PC at a stone cuts ropes with an action, or with a bonus action on a **Sleight of Hand DC 10**. A freed captive leaves the rite. Free captives flee downhill toward the party. **Tam does not run. He walks to the barrow mouth and stands looking in** until someone carries him. *(Never explain why.)*

## Ashlock at the End
- **Captured alive:** he does not beg. He tells them the rest of what is in his letter, gladly, and adds:
> **Ashlock:** *"Eight more chiefs sleep under these hills. I was only the first one sent."*
- **Escaped:** he leaves a trail of grey wool into the bog. If he has the crown and the torc went home, the crown is dead. He will find that out later, and he will want to know who did it.
- **Killed:** his sickle, his letter, and the jet scythe-pendant (APL 3–4) on the body.
- **Perrender justice:** a captured Ashlock goes before a cantonal **Rechter** (judge). If the party delivers him with the letter, the court's thanks are worth a favor in Schwartzenbruin.

## Treasure in the Barrow
Kethra's grave goods are amber beads, bronze spear-blades, and a boar-tusk helm, about 150 gp. **Taking any of it sends the Howl to 6 at once.** If the torc has gone home, the pack does not rise, but the PC who took the goods hears clicking at the edge of every campfire until the goods are returned. *(See [[Torc-of-the-Hound-Mother]], Continuity.)* This is the adventure's last test. Do not warn the players. Hild will, if she is present: *"That's hers."*

## Denouement — Dawn at Waycombe
> *By the time the party comes down off the heath, the sky is the color of dishwater. Smoke stands straight up from every chimney in Waycombe. Someone at the pound is counting sheep aloud, and stops, and starts again from one.*

Play these as short, separate beats. Answer questions through people and papers, never by narration.
- **Reunions.** Hild and Brannoc; Wenna and the miller's wife; Odila and Tam. *Do not describe Odila's face.* Describe what she does with her hands (she reaches for the tally-stick and does not cut it).
- **Odila's levy.** She asks the party what to tell the canton's levy-clerk. The party's answer decides her shame. If they tell the truth for her, she pays the cloak-pin as well and never asks them for anything again.
- **Pell.** If he has not confessed to his mother, he asks the party whether he should. Do not resolve this for them. His debt in Schwartzenbruin still stands → [[Pell Marsh's Debt]].
- **The cutters' names.** If the party brought the tally-board, Odila sends the names and the unspent advance to Traft. That is worth a Waycombe debt of honor.
- **The song.** If the torc went home, Mother Gest sings the song that night and stops at the last line. She says the words *came out wrong* and sings it again. Let the players ask what the last line means. *Do not answer.*
- **The open question.** Ashlock's letter and his last words point deeper into the Clatspurs → [[The Nine Chiefs of the Clatspurs]].

## Rewards
- Gold and items per the main note's Treasure section, scaled by APL.
- **Story XP:** one Medium encounter at APL for each of: torc returned · all three captives saved · Ashlock unmasked before the climax · Pell's confession reaching his mother.
- **Debts:** the Hound-Mother's (if the torc went home: once, in a future adventure, a pack of ordinary hounds refuses to attack the party) and Waycombe's (lodging, supplies, and a guide whenever they pass).

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** a green fire, white bone, black earth, three lashed figures against grey stone
- **Sound:** chanting in a language that sounds like breath being let out; ceaseless clicking until it stops
- **Smell:** burning tallow and wet turf. *Absence:* the barrow has no smell of age or rot, only cold stone.
- **Touch:** the torc, which finally warms when it touches Kethra
- **Taste:** ash on the wind from the green fire
