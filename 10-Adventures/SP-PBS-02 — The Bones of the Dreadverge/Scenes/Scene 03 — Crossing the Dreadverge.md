# Scene 03 — Crossing the Dreadverge

[[00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — Counting the Missing]] · Next → [[Scene 04 — The Cutters' Camp]] / [[Scene 05 — The Three Sisters]]

## Purpose
In this night crossing, the two trackers become the game. Every choice spends time (Rite Clock) or draws attention (Howl). It ends with a tactical fight in the cut-trenches, where terrain matters more than numbers.
**Budget:** 60 minutes.

## Setting Out
> *Past the last wall, the track turns to black mud between walls of heather. Luna is dark. Celene hangs in the western sky, a white chip giving no more light than a coin. The wind comes straight off the heath and carries nothing on it at all. Far ahead, on a low swell of ground, three upright shapes stand darker than the sky.*

Explain both trackers plainly and put the card on the table. Then run the crossing as **four legs** on the direct route. Each leg: tick the Rite Clock, tick the Howl (+1, plus any modifiers), make the leg's check, and play the leg's beat.

## The Legs
| Leg | Ground | Check (party leader or guide) | On failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Turf Track.** Mud between heather walls; easy going | none | — |
| 2 | **The Weeping Beck.** A fast brown stream, waist-deep, 30 ft wide | Survival DC 12 to find the **ford-stones** (automatic with Hild) | +1 Rite Clock while searching. Crossing elsewhere: each PC makes Athletics DC 10 or is swept 20 ft and takes 1d6 bludgeoning. |
| 3 | **The Cut-Trenches.** A field of peat-cuttings 10 ft deep | Survival DC 13 (navigation in the dark) | Lost: +1 Rite Clock. **The set-piece fight happens here regardless.** |
| 4 | **The Mound Approach.** Rising heath, with the stones visible | Stealth DC 12 (group) for an unobserved approach | Ashlock is alert on arrival (see Scene 5) |

**Detour:** at the end of Leg 3, drag trails and a guttered fire-glow lead half a mile north to the cutters' camp → [[Scene 04 — The Cutters' Camp]] (+1 Rite Clock).

**The Beck is the party's friend.** Crossing it lowers the Howl by 1, and *the pack will not cross running water except at the ford-stones.* A party fighting with the Beck at its back can only be attacked from the ford, a 5-ft-wide choke point. Tell them if anyone asks Hild. She has seen the hounds pace the far bank.

### Leg beats (one each; read or paraphrase)
- **Leg 1:** A ewe's skull sits upright on a fence-post, facing Waycombe. There are no marks on it. *(The pack drags carcasses home. This one was dropped, and someone placed it here: Ashlock, playing.)*
- **Leg 2:** On the far bank, prints of cloven feet and pads line up at the water's edge and go no farther.
- **Leg 3:** See the set piece below.
- **Leg 4:** From the mound comes one low note on a bone whistle. Every clicking sound on the heath stops at once.

## Howl Encounters (whenever the track hits 3 or 6)
| Howl | APL 1 | APL 2 | APL 3 | APL 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3 — The Pack Tests You** | 2 hounds (Easy) | 3 hounds (Easy) | 4 hounds (Easy) | 5 hounds (Easy) |
| **6 — The Pack Hunts** (reset to 3 after) | 3 hounds (Hard) | 3 hounds + 2 elk (Medium) | 4 hounds + 2 elk (Medium) | Barrow Stag + 6 hounds (Medium) |

- **The Pack Tests You:** The hounds come from downwind in a line, bite once each, and break off when two fall. They are measuring the party.
- **The Pack Hunts:** The hounds circle, and the elk charge through the circle. At APL 4, the **Barrow Stag** appears, white in the dark. If the stag drops to half HP, it walks away. *(It returns at the Sisters with its HP intact; Ashlock's crown mends the pack.)*
- **APL 1 safety valve:** Allow only one "Pack Hunts" per night at APL 1. A second hit at 6 becomes "The Pack Tests You" instead.

## Set Piece — The Cut-Trenches (Leg 3)
> *The heath ends in a field of trenches cut straight into the black earth, each ten feet deep and wide enough for a cart. Stacked turves stand along the lips like rows of loaves. Water lies in the bottoms, catching no light. Halfway across, a turf-spade is driven upright into a stack. Something has gnawed its handle down to the blade.*

### Encounter 5 — The Cut-Trenches
| APL | Creatures | Difficulty (4 PCs) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 bone boars | Easy (150 adj.) |
| 2 | 3 bone boars + 1 bone elk | Medium (400 adj.) |
| 3 | 4 bone boars + 2 bone elk | Medium (600 adj.) |
| 4 | 4 bone boars + 2 bone elk + 2 bone hounds | Medium (1,000 adj.) |

**Terrain (the point of the fight):**
- **Trenches:** 10 ft deep and 15 ft wide, running north–south in parallel, with 10-ft causeways of uncut turf between. A fall deals 1d6 bludgeoning. A creature in a trench is invisible to anything on the causeway that is not at the lip.
- **Trench bottoms:** a foot of black water, which is difficult terrain. *Bog pocket* (the DM marks 2): Athletics DC 11 or be restrained (escape DC 11).
- **Turf stacks:** half cover. A PC can topple a stack into a trench (Athletics DC 12, action) to make a 10-ft ramp, or drop it onto a creature below (Dex DC 12 or 2d6 bludgeoning, which the bone beasts are vulnerable to).
- **Boars charge along the causeways.** Elk leap trenches (a 15-ft jump is within their speed). Hounds run the trench bottoms and come up behind.

**Tactics:** The boars wait in the trenches until the party is on a causeway, then charge from both ends. Relentless keeps one on its feet. The elk come last and target anyone at a lip, knocking them in.

**DM direction:** Describe the space before the first roll. The fight is about falling, ramps, and toppling turf. A party that only swings swords should find this fight harder than its XP says, and a party that topples stacks should find it easy. *Difficulty scales inversely with inventiveness.*

**Found here:** the gnawed spade; drag-marks of a man's heels heading north (to the camp); and a scrap of grey wool caught on a stack (Ashlock's robe; it matches what the party saw in town).

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** black water in straight trenches and Celene's white chip sinking in the west
- **Sound:** the Beck; clicking in the heather that stops when they stop
- **Smell:** cut peat, sour and rich. *Absence:* no night birds and no insects. The heath is silent apart from the party.
- **Touch:** cold water to the knee and turf that gives underfoot
- **Taste:** iron in the Beck water

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **Hild goes home or is hurt.** Without her, the ford needs Survival DC 12. If she is injured, she insists on continuing to Brannoc. If the party sends her back, she leaves her crook (a quarterstaff, and bludgeoning).
- **The party wants a short rest.** It costs 1 Rite segment. If the Howl is 4 or higher, the rest is interrupted by The Pack Tests You.
- **The party splits.** Bone beasts track sound, so the louder group draws the Howl encounter. Cut between groups each leg.
