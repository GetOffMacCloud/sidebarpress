# Bestiary — The Bones of the Dreadverge

Back to [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]]

All statblocks use **2014 5e** rules. MM page numbers refer to the 2014 *Monster Manual*. The custom bone beasts are reskins of MM beasts with a shared **Undead Beast** template, so the night runs from this file alone.

## The Undead Beast Template
Apply this to any MM beast to make one of Kethra's pack:
- Type becomes **undead**, and alignment becomes unaligned.
- **Damage Vulnerabilities** bludgeoning · **Damage Immunities** poison · **Condition Immunities** exhaustion, poisoned
- **Senses** darkvision 60 ft. The beast loses *Keen Smell* (it has no nose) but keeps *Keen Hearing*.
- **Pack-Bound.** The beast obeys the wearer of the [[Antler-Crown-of-Kethra|Antler Crown]]. With no wearer, it hunts the [[Torc-of-the-Hound-Mother|torc]], defends the barrow, and drags carrion home.
- **Hound-Mother's Law.** The beast never attacks a creature bound at the Three Sisters.
- Every pack beast wears a **green-bronze ring** (collar, tusk-band, or antler-band) cut with a triple spiral. *(History DC 12: Flan, very old, grave-work.)*
- CR is unchanged. The vulnerability cancels the immunities for low-level parties armed with maces, staves, and clubs. **Tell the table early that bludgeoning works**: Hild says *"The smith's hammer broke one like a pot."*

If the target is prone, the stag can make one hooves attack against it as a bonus action.
> ***First of the Pack.*** While the stag stands, bone beasts within 30 ft. of it can't be frightened or turned. *(This gives Turn Undead a clear target order. Telegraph it: hounds that flinch from the holy symbol steady when the stag lowers its head.)*

> **CR verification (DMG p. 274):** *Defensive:* 45 HP falls in the CR 1/4 band (36–49) with AC 13 as expected, so defensive CR is 1/4. The poison immunity does not change effective HP meaningfully, and the bludgeoning vulnerability is not adjusted per the DMG. *Offensive:* modelled over 3 rounds (charge round 10 + 7 + 8 = 25, then 10 and 10), DPR is about 15, which is the CR 1 band. The attack bonus is +5, two above the +3 expected, so offensive CR rises to 2. *Average:* (1/4 + 2) ÷ 2 ≈ 1.1, so the stag is **CR 1**. It hits like a CR 2 creature and folds to a warhammer, which is the intent.

## Brother Ashlock — two chassis

[[Brother-Ashlock]] uses an MM statblock by name, chosen by APL. Both versions carry the [[Antler-Crown-of-Kethra|Antler Crown]] and run the rite.

> [!example]- Ashlock, APL 1–2 — Acolyte (CR 1/4, 50 XP), MM p. 342
> Use the Acolyte as written (**AC** 10, **HP** 9, club +2 for 1d4, spell save DC 12, +4 to hit; cantrips *light, sacred flame, thaumaturgy*; 1st level ×3 *bless, cure wounds, sanctuary*), with these changes:
> - **HP 13** (3d8). This gives him one extra hit to survive, so the rite runs for at least a round.
> - ***Crown-Bearer.*** As a bonus action, Ashlock chooses one bone beast within 60 ft. It uses its reaction to move up to half its speed (no attack).
> - ***Rite of the Reaper.*** See the Rite Track in [[SP-PBS-02 — The Howl and the Rite]]. Chanting takes his action.
> - In town (Scene 1), he casts *cure wounds* on a mauled villager in front of the party. His healing is real, and that is his best cover.

> [!example]- Ashlock, APL 3–4 — Cult Fanatic (CR 2, 450 XP), MM p. 345
> Use the Cult Fanatic as written (**AC** 13 leather, **HP** 33, *Dark Devotion*, Multiattack: two dagger attacks +4 for 1d4 + 2; spell save DC 11, +3 to hit; cantrips *light, sacred flame, thaumaturgy*; 1st level ×4 *command, inflict wounds, shield of faith*; 2nd level ×3 *hold person, spiritual weapon*), with these changes:
> - ***Crown-Bearer*** and ***Rite of the Reaper*** as above.
> - His *spiritual weapon* takes the form of a **grey scythe**. Casting it in Scene 5 means he has given up chanting for a round. Let the players notice the trade.
> - Replace the daggers with a **sickle** (same numbers, slashing). The sickle is the tool of Nerull's faithful, and a cleric PC will recognize it (Religion DC 10).
> - In town (Scene 1), he binds wounds with Medicine and prays aloud. He cannot heal by magic, and he gives a soft excuse ("Wee Jas grants her servants the dead, not the dying").

**Tactics (both).** Ashlock wants the rite finished more than he wants the party dead.
1. He chants (action) and steers one beast toward whoever is closest to a captive (bonus action).
2. If a PC reaches a stone, he breaks off to cast *command* ("**Flee!**") or *sacred flame* at that PC, which means the rite does not advance.
3. **Escape route.** At a quarter of his HP or below, or when the torc enters the barrow, he runs north into the bog path (pre-scouted; the beasts give it a wide berth). An escaped Ashlock is a recurring villain, not a failure. If he flees with the crown, it is dead once the pack sleeps, but he does not know that yet.
