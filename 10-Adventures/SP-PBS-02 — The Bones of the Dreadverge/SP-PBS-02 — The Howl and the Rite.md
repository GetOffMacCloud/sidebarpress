# The Howl and the Rite

Back to [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]]

The night runs on two tracks. Put both on the table where players can see them: two rows of six boxes on an index card, with coins or d6s as markers. The **Rite Clock** is time. The **Howl** is attention. Every choice on the Dreadverge spends one or the other.

## The Rite Clock (0–6)

One segment equals about one hour of night. The clock starts when the party leaves Waycombe after Scene 2. When it reaches **6**, Celene sets and Ashlock begins the rite at the Three Sisters, whether or not the party is there.

**Advances by 1 when the party:**
- Travels one leg of the Dreadverge (the direct route is **4 legs**; see [[Scene 03 — Crossing the Dreadverge]])
- Takes a short rest
- Becomes lost (a failed navigation check)
- Detours to the cutters' camp (Scene 4 is 1 extra leg), or searches it thoroughly (another +1)
- Spends a leg circling the Weeping Beck to find the ford-stones (if no one knows the way)

**Does not advance for:** combat, conversation, or a quick search.

**Arrival state at the Three Sisters:**

| Rite Clock on arrival | What the party finds |
|---|---|
| 0–4 | **Early.** Ashlock is preparing. The Rite Track starts at 0, and the party may scout and plan for one full round of unobserved approach. |
| 5 | **On time.** The rite is beginning. The Rite Track starts at 0, but Ashlock is already at the stones and alert. |
| 6 | **Late.** The rite has begun. The Rite Track starts at 1. |
| 7+ (still travelling) | **Very late.** Brannoc has already died and risen as a **skeleton** (MM p. 272), which joins the pack. The Rite Track starts at 1 on Wenna. |

*Designed partial failure: arriving late costs a person, never the whole adventure. Do not add punishment beyond this table.*

## The Howl (0–6)

The pack has no noses. It hunts by sound and by the pull of its stolen torc. The Howl measures how much of the Dreadverge is listening to the party.

**Raises by:**
- **+1** at the start of each leg on the Dreadverge (automatic)
- **+1** for an open flame carried that leg (a lantern with a closed hood does not count)
- **+1** for loud combat (thunder damage, *shatter*, a war-horn, or a fight lasting longer than 3 rounds)
- **+1** for each leg the party carries the **torc** (the pack feels it)
- **+6 (to maximum) at once** if anyone takes grave goods from the barrow

**Lowers by:**
- **−1** for crossing the **Weeping Beck** (the pack will not cross running water except at the ford-stones; see Scene 3)
- **−1** once per leg for a successful **group Stealth check, DC 12** (wrapping buckles, walking in single file, keeping talk to signs)
- **−2** once per night if a PC with the torc wraps it in running-water cloth (a garment soaked in the Beck and kept wet): **Survival or Religion DC 13** to think of it, or Ysolde or Hild suggests it as old practice

**Thresholds (check after each change):**

| Howl | Effect |
|---|---|
| 3 | **The Pack Tests You.** Run *Encounter 3* (see [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge#Encounters]]). The hounds break off after two are destroyed. Howl does not reset. |
| 4–5 | Bone beasts shadow the party at 120 ft, just outside the light. Their clicking stops whenever the party stops. Short rests still work, but a watch-keeper sees eyeless heads at the edge of the heather. |
| 6 | **The Pack Hunts.** Run *Encounter 4*. Afterward, the Howl resets to **3**. |

## The Rite Track (Scene 5 only)

Three captives are bound to three stones, in this order: **Brannoc → Wenna → Tam**.

- At the end of each of Ashlock's turns where he used his **action** to chant, the Rite Track goes up by 1.
- At **3**, the current captive dies, rises as a **skeleton** (MM p. 272) under the crown's command, and the track resets to 0 for the next captive.
- **Interruption.** If Ashlock takes damage during a round, he makes a Constitution save (DC 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher). On a failure, the track does not advance at the end of his next turn.
- **Broken rite.** The rite ends permanently if Ashlock is reduced to 0 HP, the crown leaves his head, he is moved more than 10 ft from the ring's center, or the torc is returned.
- **Freed captives** stop counting. If the current captive is cut loose, the track resets to 0 on the next captive in order.
- **The bone beasts never attack a bound captive.** Offerings at the stones belong to the Hound-Mother. This is the **round-1 safety rule**: no initiative roll can kill a captive before the party acts.

*DM direction: let the players see the track. Announce each tick plainly ("Brannoc stops struggling"). Never describe what Brannoc feels.*

## Returning the Torc

A creature carrying the torc enters the barrow passage (5 ft wide, 20 ft long, under the south peat face) and uses an **action** to lay it on Kethra's breast.

- **Immediately:** every bone beast and every risen skeleton within a mile stops. On its next turn, each one moves its full speed toward **Ashlock** and attacks him if it can, ignoring the crown.
- **At the end of the following round:** every bone beast collapses into loose bone. The crown goes dead in the wearer's hands, and the pack never wakes again. Risen captives also collapse and stay dead; nothing restores them but the means the PHB allows for raising the dead.
- **While the torc is carried in the ring:** bone beasts prefer the torc-bearer as a target, and the bearer has disadvantage on Stealth checks against them. *(Tell the players the hounds keep turning toward that PC. Do not tell them why.)*
