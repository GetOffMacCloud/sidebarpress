# SP-PBS-02 — Developmental Edit (structure, pacing, railroad check)

Back to [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]] · Board: [[SP-PBS-02 — Board]] · Run sheet: [[10-Adventures/SP-PBS-02 — The Bones of the Dreadverge/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]]

> [!info] How to use this note
> Tick the box next to each finding you want applied, then ask Claude to "apply the ticked fixes." Untick, or strike through, anything you reject. The board's *Developmental edit* card stays open until the Major findings are resolved.

## Verdict
The foundation is strong. This is a situation, not a plot. The restitution thesis is real, the climax has four answers the players can work out from earlier scenes, and arriving late is a designed partial failure. Seven things block release:
- The Longform manifest compiles only Act 2.
- There are no defeat or partial endings.
- The Howl track can generate three or four extra fights in Act 2, which together with an over-full Scene 2 pushes the 4-hour slot to about 5 hours.
- There is no running-long guidance.
- Two pieces of plot armor: Ashlock can't be held, and the Barrow Stag resets its HP.
- The Barrow Stag's CR is miscalculated.
- The Clue Ledger cites clues that aren't in the text.

Each has a one-line or one-paragraph fix.

**Classic-module musts: 4 of 12 pass.**
- **Fail:** cover line (1), Locals Know split (2), site field order (3, soft), one box assigning an action (6), ledger routes (8), NPC Knows/escalation (9), branching endings (10), plot protection (12).
- **Pass:** 4, 5, 7, 11.

## Findings

### Major
- [x] **1 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-17.* Build — the Longform list holds only Scenes 03 and 04,** so a compile drops Acts 1 and 3. The Board ticks "Manifest … reviewed" anyway. **Fix:** add Scenes 01, 02 and 05 in order, and untick the Board item until then. *(Verified 2026-10-17.)*
- [x] **2 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-17.* Structure — only the success ending is written.** The denouement opens "The pack sleeps." Nothing covers a retreat, a party wipe, all three captives risen, or a rite broken while the pack is still awake. **Fix:** add a lettered **Endings** block, each with its own reward and Story XP line:
  - **A (full):** the torc goes home.
  - **B (partial):** the rite is broken but the pack is awake, or a captive is lost. The pack returns at the next dark of Luna.
  - **C (defeat or retreat):** use "If the PCs never come." Waycombe empties, and Ashlock leaves with three risen.
- [x] **3 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-17.* Pacing — the Howl track overfires.** Each leg adds +1 automatically, plus +1 for the torc and +1 for an open flame. In the dark of Luna, a party without darkvision carries light, so that is about +3 per leg:
  - Pack Tests on Leg 1.
  - Pack Hunts on Leg 2, and again on Leg 3 or 4.
  - The trench fight nearly always runs past the 3-round limit.
  - "Reset to 3" lands back on the Pack Tests threshold.

  **Fix:**
  - Each Howl encounter happens at most once per night.
  - A reset does not trigger Pack Tests.
  - The torc counts only while carried unwrapped.
  - Redo the main note's adventuring-day line to match.
- [x] **4 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-17.* Pacing — no running-long guidance.** The page budget is 240 minutes; a realistic run is 275–345. **Fix:** add a "Running long" box:
  - At 75 minutes, end S2 with Hild at the door.
  - Skip the Leg 1 beat.
  - Make Pack Hunts the only Howl fight.
  - At APL 1–2, run the trenches with half the creatures.
- [x] **5 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-17.* Railroad — Ashlock can't be held.** S1 alternate: "Ashlock surrenders meekly, then slips away at dusk if he is held." This is a guaranteed escape. **Fix:** "If held, at dusk he tries to slip away (Stealth vs. guards' passive Perception)." Write what happens if he stays held: the pack runs unsteered, the captives still wait at the Stones, and the crown is on him. *(Verified.)*
- [x] **6 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-17.* Railroad — the Barrow Stag resets.** S3: "It returns at the Sisters with its HP intact; Ashlock's crown mends the pack." That makes it an invulnerable spectacle NPC that undoes the party's work. **Fix:** it returns with its current HP. If it was destroyed, use 2 hounds instead in Encounter 7.
- [x] **7 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-17.* Consistency — the Barrow Stag's CR is wrong.** The Bestiary claims "DPR is about 15, which is the CR 1 band." In the 2014 DMG, DPR 15 is the CR 2 band (15–20), and the +5 attack lifts offensive CR to 3. The average is about CR 2 (450 XP), which makes Encounter 7 **Deadly** at APL 2, 3 and 4. **Fix (smallest change):** delete Trampling Charge's bonus-action hooves. DPR drops to about 12, and the existing CR 1 math holds.

The promised "Insight check that unmasks him" doesn't exist. **Fix:** in the S1 aftermath, he asks the cleric PC to mark the mill door with him and hands over the ash. Write the check: "Insight DC 13: his grief is rehearsed."
- [x] **11 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-18.* Consistency — the clock doesn't add up.** "One segment ≈ one hour" from a post-supper departure ends around 2 a.m., but Celene sets "about four hours after midnight." Waiting for dawn means **Very late**, plus Rowan taken.
- [x] **12 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-18.* Structure — the early-arrival state has no in-fiction reason.** "0–4 Early… Rite Track starts at 0", yet Ashlock chants on round 1 before Celene sets. **Fix:** "Seeing witnesses, he begins early." Also list his pre-cast defenses for each arrival state: *sanctuary* at APL 1–2, *shield of faith* at APL 3–4.

The climax (S5 before the denouement) runs about 1,510, roughly 11% of the book against the 15% house rule. **Fix:** cut about 300 words from S3 (its Howl table repeats the mechanic note, so link it instead). Spend them on an S5 Development list: freed captives, the stag's behavior, and what Ashlock does when the torc enters the barrow.
- [x] **14 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-18.* Consistency — where the torc goes.** The S5 box says the torc "settles into the hollow at the dead woman's throat." Everywhere else it goes on her **breast**: S5, the mechanic note, the letter, and the natural-20 lore. **Fix:** change the box to "settles on the dead woman's breast." If Kester's "off her neck" is meant to show he got it wrong, say so in the DM text.
- [x] **15 · ✅ *Applied 2026-10-18.* Consistency — dates.** The Canon Check row says "Iggwilv's conquest 481–491 CY." Per [[Iggwilvs-Conquest-of-Perrenland]], it is conquest 480–481 and rule 481–491. The Perrenland Color line is already correct. The Deviations line says "At 591 CY or later, Perrenland's pact"; the pact is **583**. **Fix:** correct both.

## Guardrails reviewed and kept
These are defensible as written:
- "Then the light goes" (the S1 framing)
- The ghoul paralysis guard
- Hound-Mother's Law and the round-1 safety rule
- Odila locking her doors if the party sheds blood with no proof (a talk-first consequence; the S3/S4 clues cover the loss)
- Tam walking to the barrow mouth
- "Do not narrate his death in detail"
- Kester's confession left to the players

## What works (don't cut)
- **The cart seed:** the hounds claw the cart in S1, and that becomes the return-the-torc solution. This is textbook "seed the key in Episode One."
- **The Rite Clock arrival table:** "Arriving late costs a person, never the whole adventure."
- **Talk-first Tamsin:** she gives up the torc at cost with no roll, which pairs well with Kester's freely chosen confession.
- **Encounter math:** checks out everywhere except the Stag. The trench fight is the best set piece in the book.
- **Folk-memory Iggwilv and the Iuz red herring:** both are labeled as folk belief and closed off plainly at the camp.
