# Scene 03 — Mother Nettle's Cottage

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-01 — Straw Men/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 02 — The Bobbing Game]] · Next → [[Scene 04 — Fields and Stall]]

## Purpose
Give the party the lore that makes the climax winnable, as answers to their questions. Then put the village's only ally in danger from the village's best man, and let words save her.
**Budget:** 35 minutes. Running long? Two questions to Nettle, then the mob arrives.

## Arrival
> *The cottage sits where the fields give up and the wood begins, low and crooked, with bundles of herbs hung so thick in the window that no light gets through. Something has nailed a row of dried crows' feet along the lintel. Before you reach the door it opens a hand's width, and a woman with a face like a walnut looks you over. "If you're here to burn me, you're early. If you're here for help, you're late. Which?"*

**To the table:** *"She's rude, and she's right about something. How do you get past the door?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Mother Nettle]]**, hedge-witch (druid stat block). *Wants:* her neighbors back and to be left alone. *Knows:* hag lore; that the wheel is the key; that Haskell is the stranger nobody suspects. *Reacts:* prickly; respects proof and nerve; says less than she knows and nothing untrue.
- At 10 PM: **[[Father Corwin]]** and a dozen angry villagers (commoners), with torches.

## Beats

### Getting in
Winning her trust takes a **DC 12 Charisma check** (any skill the player can justify), or proof: a straw kernel tooth, the green fingernail, Pip's dolly. A PC who ate the apple gets in free. She looks at them hard:

> *"Your reflection's gone. She'll sell that on. Something that buys reflections will own a piece of you for a long time."*

### Questions, not a speech
Don't deliver the lore as a monologue. Let the players ask.

| If asked about… | Nettle says |
|---|---|
| The scarecrows | *"Hag-work. Straw holds a memory the way a bucket holds water. Badly, and not for long."* |
| The mill | *"A wheel that turns backward unwinds a soul from its body. Stop the wheel and every straw neighbor falls to chaff."* |
| Where the missing are | *"Still breathing, if she means to sell them. A hag doesn't deliver damaged goods."* |
| Widow Haskell | *"Hags love a market. Who's the one stranger in town nobody's suspicious of?"* |
| How to hurt a hag | *"Iron, fire, and a contract read closely. The last one hurts most."* |
| Why she hasn't acted | *"Because I'm one old woman and they'd burn me for trying. Ask the Father."* |

### 10 PM: the mob
Torchlight on the path. Corwin arrives with twelve villagers to drive Nettle out. This is a **talk-first obstacle**: the mob is frightened parents, not cultists, and a party that fights it loses the village.

- Talking them down takes a **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)** check. Advantage if the party produces physical proof (a kernel tooth, chaff from the reel, the fingernail).
- **Unmasking a double in front of Corwin** ends the mob at once. Two of the twelve are doubles; a torch held close makes one flinch, and a **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** picks which. Corwin, shown straw where a parishioner should be, becomes an ally. He rings the chapel bell to keep villagers indoors: **slow the Midnight Clock by one hour.**
- **Intimidation (DC 14)** scatters the mob but not Corwin, and the village remembers.
- **If the party fails or fights,** Nettle flees into the wood. She can be found again with a **DC 15 Wisdom (Survival)** check, costing half an hour on the clock, and she won't join the party at the mill.

### Nettle's gift
If she's protected, Nettle gives the party a sprig of **iron-rue**: when burned, it makes every Straw Double within 30 feet visible as what it is for 1 minute (no save). One use.

## Encounter (if any)
| APL | Creatures | Difficulty (4 PCs) |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Corwin (priest) + 12 commoners | social; never combat as written |

**Terrain:** a narrow path, torches, a cottage with one door. **Tactics:** the mob wants Nettle out, not dead; they'll throw torches at the thatch if cornered. **DM direction:** if the party kills a villager here, that villager's family is in the loft, and the party must face them at dawn.

## Clues Available Here
- "Stop the wheel" → the mill is the source; the intended win
- "One stranger nobody suspects" → Widow Haskell is the hag
- "A contract read closely" → the contract has a loophole
- Two doubles in the mob → the doubles are fake

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** herbs so thick the room is dusk at noon, dried crows' feet, torchlight coming up the path · **Sound:** a kettle, then shouting · **Smell:** rue, woodsmoke, pitch · **Touch:** a bundle of nettles handed over without warning · **Taste:** tea that tastes of pennies

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party arrives after 10 PM:** the cottage is burning and Nettle is gone. Her lore resurfaces in Scene 04 from Pip's mother (who remembers Nettle's warnings) and in the mill's cellar ledger, where a hag's own notes mention the wheel.
- **If the party brings Corwin with them:** he and Nettle argue, and the party moderates. Resolving it (DC 12 Persuasion) gets both allies and no mob.
- **If the party skips Nettle:** the "stop the wheel" clue has to come from the apple vision (the groaning wheel) or Crane's phrases in Scene 04. Make the wheel's importance louder there.
