# Scene 05 — The Mill

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-01 — Straw Men/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 04 — Fields and Stall]] · End

## Purpose
The climax: four jobs at once (the wheel, the sacks, the hag, the contract), a deadline at midnight, and a villain who isn't meant to die. End with the carriage and the ledger.
**Budget:** 70 minutes. Running long? Cut M2 and M5; go from the door straight to the race for the loft.

## Arrival
> *The mill crouches over the millrace, black against the moon. Its great wheel is turning, slow and wet and wrong: backward, against the current, so the river climbs its paddles. Each groan of the axle sounds like a long breath drawn in and never let out. Inside, every surface wears a coat of pale flour that gives off its own faint light, and a line of bare footprints runs through it toward the stairs.*

**To the table:** *"The wheel's outside, the stairs go up, and the clock says eleven. Who's doing what?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Auntie Chaff]]**, green hag, in M3 (invisible if the party confronted her at the stall) or in the loft M4, disguised as a sleeping villager.
- Up to six half-finished **Straw Doubles** on frames in M3, three of which Chaff can wake (bonus action, once per encounter). If a PC ate the apple, their own double is here, smiling.
- Up to twelve sleepers in M4, including [[Pip Tully]], the real [[Hedda Voss]], and [[Aldous Crane]].
- At midnight: [[The Gentleman in Grey]] at the river landing.

## The Mill, Keyed
| Area | Name | What's here |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | The Millrace | The backward wheel, powering every glamour in the village. Jamming it takes a **DC 14 Strength (Athletics)** check, or a sturdy object wedged in the spokes (a longsword snaps; a quarterstaff or a militia pike holds). A creature that falls in takes 1d6 bludgeoning and is swept 20 feet downstream. |
| M2 | The Grinding Floor | The millstone grinds memories, not grain. A PC who touches the glowing flour makes a **DC 12 Wisdom saving throw**. On a failure they relive a stolen memory of a taken villager (a clue, or a gut-punch). On a success they still see where the memory came from: the loft above. |
| M3 | The Workshop | Six half-finished scarecrows on wooden frames, each in a villager's clothes. Chaff wakes up to three as Straw Doubles. If a PC ate the apple, their own double waits here. |
| M4 | The Loft | Sacks hang from the beams, each holding a sleeping villager. A sleeper wakes if its double is destroyed, the wheel stops, or someone casts *dispel magic* (DC 13) on it. |
| M5 | The Grain Chute | A 30-foot drop from loft to cellar. Chaff's escape. |
| M6 | The Cellar | Chaff's lair, river water ankle-deep. The Gentleman's contract (grey wax seal), Chaff's ledger, and her treasure. |

## Beats

### Entering
Crane's bar is on the inside; the door opens to a **DC 12 Strength** check or a window (**DC 10 Dexterity (Acrobatics)** to get in quietly). The footprints in the flour are the doubles' nightly route to the loft.

### The objective
This fight is **not meant to be won by killing Chaff**. Her goal is the sacks; the party's goal is the wheel and the sleepers. The Straw Doubles mostly ignore the party and carry sacks down to the river landing, where the carriage arrives at midnight. A double carrying a sack moves at half speed and needs **2 rounds** to reach the landing from the loft. Every sleeper on the landing at midnight is lost.

Start the climax at **11:50**, ten rounds to midnight. Keep a visible round count.

### Chaff's play
Use her full green hag stat block: Illusory Appearance, Invisible Passage, *minor illusion*, *vicious mockery*. She fights dirty:
- *Minor illusion* of fire in the loft to split the party.
- Lies still among the sacks, disguised as a sleeper, and claws whoever cuts her "sack" open.
- Wakes three doubles the round the party reaches M3.
- **At 41 hit points or fewer,** she uses Invisible Passage and drops down the grain chute. An escaped hag is a recurring enemy, and a better outcome than a dead one.

### Breaking the spell (the intended win)
If the wheel at M1 is stopped, Chaff loses Invisible Passage, every Straw Double collapses into straw at the end of its next turn, and every sleeper wakes, groggy but alive. A party that sends one PC to the wheel while the rest hold the stairs has solved the scene.

### Midnight: the carriage
Whatever happens inside, at the twelfth bell a carriage drawn by four black hares pulls up at the landing. The **Gentleman in Grey** steps out: polite, faceless, and impossible to harm here. He takes whatever sleepers are on the landing as corn dollies and tips his hat. A party holding the contract from M6 can bargain:
- A **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion or Deception)** check, or
- **The loophole.** The contract calls for "twelve folk of Hollow Ford." Chaff has lived in Hollow Ford for three weeks, so she counts. The Gentleman finds that delightful and leaves with a hag-shaped corn dolly (if Chaff is still in the mill; if she fled, he leaves with a promissory note and a look upstream).

He leaves a **grey calling card** good for one small favor. In the chain, it opens an ending in [[SP-HAL-04-The-Tithe-of-Thornbury]].

### The cellar (M6)
After, or during if someone goes looking: 140 gp, 3 *potions of healing*, the *hat of disguise*, the contract, and the ledger. The ledger's two entries that matter are on [[SP-HAL-01 — Handout — The Ledger Page]].

**Terrain:** three floors, one stair, a 30-foot chute, a millrace with a backward wheel. The flour glows: dim light everywhere. **Tactics:** doubles carry, Chaff delays. **DM direction:** the doubles don't count toward the encounter multiplier because they aren't attacking; the real pressure is the round count and the landing. Say "two sacks are on the stairs" out loud every round.

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** glowing flour, sacks turning slowly on their ropes, a carriage with no lamps · **Sound:** the axle's long breath, river water in the cellar, hares' feet on gravel · **Smell:** flour, wet straw, and something sweet from the loft · **Touch:** flour that clings and won't brush off · **Taste:** flour in the teeth, faintly of someone else's childhood

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party stops the wheel before entering:** the mill goes dark and silent. Chaff comes down the stairs as herself, furious and visible, and the fight is a straight one on the grinding floor. The sleepers wake and stumble into the fight; protect them.
- **If the party kills Chaff:** the glamours fail at dawn instead of at once. The carriage arrives, finds no one, and the Gentleman leaves a card anyway, "for the inconvenience."
- **If every sleeper is lost:** twelve corn dollies ride away in the carriage. The party holds the contract and the card. That's a sequel: find the Gentleman's shelves.
- **If the party never finds the contract:** the Gentleman offers to sell it to them. The price is a memory of their choosing.
