# Scene 02 — The Bobbing Game

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-01 — Straw Men/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 01 — The Closing Reel]] · Next → [[Scene 03 — Mother Nettle's Cottage]]

## Purpose
Offer a bargain. One PC can learn where Pip is at the cost of their reflection, and that cost follows them through the bundle. Whether or not anyone bites, leave green scrapings on the barrel.
**Budget:** 20 minutes. Running long? Offer the apple once and don't stall; the scrapings are there regardless.

## Arrival
> *Children crowd a rain barrel in the inn yard, sleeves pushed up, lantern light wobbling on the water. The barrel was filled from the millrace this afternoon. When the first child ducks in, the water goes still and black as a well, and the laughing stops. A hand rises from it, pale, with a green tinge under the nails. It holds out one perfect red apple, and it waits.*

**To the table:** *"The hand is offering the apple to one of you in particular. Who, and what do you do?"*

## Who Is Here
- A dozen children and [[Brann Tully]], who goes white.
- The hand belongs to [[Auntie Chaff]], reaching through the millrace water from the mill. She isn't present and can't be fought here; strike the hand and it withdraws, and the apple floats.

## Beats

### The offer
The hand offers the apple to the PC who seems most curious, most frightened, or most protective of Pip. If refused, it offers it to the next. It never speaks. If a PC asks it a question, it tilts the apple toward them.

### Eating the apple
A PC who eats the apple sees a vision: **Pip Tully asleep in a sack, hanging from a beam in a dusty loft, as a waterwheel groans outside.** They also glimpse six other sacks and a workshop of half-made scarecrows. This is a clean route to the mill and the loft.

**The cost.** The eater's reflection stays in the barrel. From now on, in still water and glass, their reflection is a half-second behind them. Chaff has their likeness: a **Straw Double of that PC** waits in the mill workshop (M3), with the PC's voice and a little of what the PC knows. The ledger in M6 later shows the reflection sold on to [[The Gentleman in Grey]], which pays off in [[SP-HAL-04-The-Tithe-of-Thornbury]]. [[Mother Nettle]] will see it on them at once.

### Refusing
A PC who refuses, or who looks instead of reaching, notices green fingernail scrapings on the barrel's rim, matching Pip's "lady with green fingers." A **DC 12 Intelligence (Nature)** check identifies them as hag-sign; a **DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine)** says the nails were long and the hand was old.

### Brann
When the hand is gone, Brann tips the barrel over. In the mud at the bottom lies a corn dolly, braided with a dark hair. It's Pip's.

## Encounter (if any)
None. The hand has AC 15 and withdraws if it takes any damage; the apple then bobs on the surface, still edible, still a bargain.

## Clues Available Here
- The apple vision → the mill is the source; Pip is alive in the loft
- Green scrapings → Widow Haskell is the hag
- Pip's dolly → the dollies mark targets (feeds Scene 04)

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** black water, a very red apple, a green crescent under each nail · **Sound:** children going quiet, water lapping, nothing else · **Smell:** river mud and, faintly, sweetness · **Touch:** the apple is cold all the way through · **Taste:** the best apple the eater has ever had, then silt

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If a PC grabs the hand:** contest the PC's Strength (Athletics) against +3. On a win, they pull up a wet green arm to the elbow before it tears free and leaves a fingernail in their palm (a clue, and a trophy Nettle respects).
- **If two PCs want the apple:** there is only one. The second gets a vision of their own reflection, alone in a dark glass case.
- **If the party skips the yard:** a child brings the apple to their table at the inn, "from the lady in the water," and the dolly turns up in the mud the next morning.

## Transition
- → [[Scene 03 — Mother Nettle's Cottage]]: Brann, holding Pip's dolly: *"Nettle. She'll know what this is. Corwin will have my head for saying it, but go to Nettle. Go now, before the Father's done preaching."*
