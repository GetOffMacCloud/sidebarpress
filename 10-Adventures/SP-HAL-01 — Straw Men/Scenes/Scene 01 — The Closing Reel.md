# Scene 01 — The Closing Reel

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-01 — Straw Men/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 00 — Arrival at Hollow Ford]] · Next → [[Scene 02 — The Bobbing Game]]

## Purpose
Show the party that something is wrong and that the village can't see it. End with a fight the villagers misread and a trail of wet straw pointing upstream.
**Budget:** 35 minutes. Running long? The scarecrow flees at 15 damage whatever the APL.

## Arrival
> *Lanterns carved from turnips grin from every post on the green. A fiddler saws out a reel while the whole village spins around a bonfire that throws sparks into the cold. Widow Haskell claps time from her stall, and Father Corwin stands at the edge of the light with his hands folded. Past the firelight, the stubble fields run off into the dark, and the scarecrows stand in them like a crowd that came to watch.*

**To the table:** *"Brann pulls you into the line. Who dances, and who watches the edges?"*

## Who Is Here
- Most of Hollow Ford, including every double. The doubles dance on the far side of the circle from the fire.
- **Ada Plum**, a weaver's daughter, 16, who bought a corn dolly this afternoon. She dances near the party.
- Widow Haskell ([[Auntie Chaff]]), clapping, counting.

## Beats

### The reel
Let the dancers roleplay for a minute. Any PC in the line can make a **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check to notice that the dancers on the dark side of the circle never pass close to the bonfire, and that the line keeps bending to keep it that way.

### Eerie encounter: the Harvest Dancers
Halfway through the reel, the dancing lines include one partner too many. A **scarecrow** in a farmer's coat has joined the circle. No villager reacts, even when the party points it out: its glamour makes them see a neighbor. A character who makes a **DC 12 Wisdom saving throw** also sees the truth; anyone the party points it out to gets the save at advantage.

When the music ends, the scarecrow takes Ada Plum's hand and walks her toward the fields.

### The field's edge
- **If the party follows,** the scarecrow lets Ada go at the first stubble row and attacks. To the villagers watching from the green, the party has just drawn steel on a neighbor. Expect shouting, and Voss arriving with militia two rounds after the fight ends.
- **If the party does nothing,** Ada is gone, and her double is back in the dance by 9 PM. Her scarecrow post in the fields is now filled (Scene 04).

### After
The scarecrow leaves a trail of wet straw and river water that runs upstream toward the mill. A **DC 10 Wisdom (Survival)** check follows it in the dark; by daylight it needs no check. Ada, if saved, remembers only "a kind face and cold hands."

**Terrain:** stubble is open ground; the bonfire is 40 feet behind the party and the only bright light. **Tactics:** the scarecrow opens with Terrifying Glare on whoever is closest, then claws the frightened target; it won't approach within 10 feet of a torch. Straw Doubles grapple and drag toward the fields rather than attack. **DM direction:** the fight should look, from the green, like strangers beating a farmer. Narrate the watching faces.

## Clues Available Here
- The dancers avoid the fire → the doubles are fake
- The scarecrow nobody sees → the doubles are fake
- The straw trail upstream → the mill is the source
- Haskell counting the dancers → Widow Haskell is the hag (hindsight)

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** sparks, spinning skirts, one dancer whose face is sacking · **Sound:** the fiddle, boots on packed earth, then a silence when the music stops · **Smell:** smoke, cider, wet hay getting stronger · **Touch:** a hand in the line that is dry as rope · **Taste:** ash on the wind

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party attacks the scarecrow on the green:** the villagers see an assault on a neighbor. Voss arrests whoever struck first; the party spends 20 minutes in the chapel cellar and loses an hour on the clock, unless someone proves the "neighbor" is straw (it burst into chaff: a **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** gets Corwin to look at the pile).
- **If the party destroys the scarecrow outright:** fine. The trail still leads upstream, and the straw it leaves is wet with river water.
- **If the party skips the reel:** Ada's taking happens off stage; her mother comes to the inn at 9 PM asking after her, and the straw trail is on the green for anyone to find.
