# Scene 04 — Fields and Stall

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-01 — Straw Men/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · ← [[Scene 03 — Mother Nettle's Cottage]] · Next → [[Scene 05 — The Mill]]

## Purpose
Three investigation sites in one scene: the fields prove how many are missing, the stall names the hag, and unmasking Voss turns the militia. Any one of them is enough to send the party upstream; all three send them armed.
**Budget:** 35 minutes. Running long? Pick one: the stall or Voss. The fields can be a line of narration.

## Arrival
> *Past the last cottage, the stubble runs silver under a half moon, and the scarecrows stand in it in rows. There are more of them than a field this size needs. Each wears a coat or a shawl or a child's cap, and on every post, tied at the wrist, hangs a thread bracelet, the kind a family ties on a traveler. Back on the green, the tinker's stall is still lit, and the widow is still smiling at nobody.*

**To the table:** *"Three places to look: the fields, the stall, the captain. Who goes where?"*

## Who Is Here
- **The Fields:** seven extra scarecrows, each a Straw Double's resting post. If Ada was taken, an eighth.
- **The Stall:** Widow Haskell ([[Auntie Chaff]]), packing slowly. Four dollies left, one for each remaining target, each braided with a single hair.
- **The Captain:** [[Hedda Voss]] (Straw Double) at the inn, with four real **guards** who trust her.

## Beats

### The fields
There are seven more scarecrows than there were three weeks ago, one for each missing villager. These are the doubles' resting posts: each double stands here, motionless, between its shifts as a villager. Close up, each wears a thread-bracelet tied by its victim's family. A **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check matches bracelets to families; one is Pip's, tied by Brann. Pip's post is still empty (no double was made; Chaff ran short of straw).

A PC who waits by a post for ten minutes sees a double walk out of the village, climb onto it, and go still. **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** to watch unseen. A watching PC gets one tell free.

### The stall
The corn dollies are well made. Each has a single hair braided into its straw, and there is a dolly for every remaining target. A PC who buys one and studies it makes a **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana)** check: on a success, they recognize a sympathetic link, a marker. **Burning a dolly protects its owner**: Chaff can't take a villager whose dolly is ash. Four dollies burned at the stall drops the loft to seven sleepers for the rest of the night.

If confronted with proof, the Widow laughs, packs up her stall in three motions, and heads upstream. She doesn't fight on the green; there are too many eyes, and she'd rather the party follow her into the mill.

| If asked… | Haskell says |
|---|---|
| Where she's from | *"Oh, up the road. Down the road. A tinker's from wherever the cart stops, dear."* |
| About the dollies | *"Little keepsakes. Everyone should have one. Have you got one?"* |
| About Pip | *"The innkeeper's boy? Running wild, I expect. Boys do."* Her nails, if a PC looks, are clean and short. The glamour is good. |
| About the mill | *"Closed for repairs."* The same three words as Crane, in the same cadence. **DC 12 Wisdom (Insight)** catches it. |

### Unmasking Captain Voss
Voss is the most dangerous double because she commands the militia. Exposing her in front of her guards turns them to the party's side. Routes:
- Force her to remove her gauntlets (**DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation)**, or a contested **Strength (Athletics)** against her +1): straw fingers.
- Hold a torch close: she flinches back 10 feet and her sleeve smokes.
- Ask her about last spring's flood, which every real villager remembers: she has nothing.
- Burn Nettle's iron-rue in the common room: every double in sight shows its sacking face.

Once exposed, she fights as a **Straw Double** with 22 hit points and her longsword (+3 to hit, 1d8 + 1 slashing). At 0 hit points she bursts into straw, and the real Hedda wakes in the loft. The four guards follow the party to the mill if asked.

## Clues Available Here
- Seven extra posts with family bracelets → the doubles are fake; seven are missing
- The hair in each dolly → Widow Haskell is the hag; the dollies mark targets
- "Closed for repairs" in Crane's cadence → Widow Haskell is the hag; the mill is the source
- Voss's straw fingers → the doubles are fake

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** silver stubble, rows of posts, a child's cap on sacking, one stall lamp · **Sound:** a bracelet thread snapping in the wind; the stall's shutters going up in three quick motions · **Smell:** hay, river water, and at the stall, lavender over something older · **Touch:** a single hair, coarse, between finger and thumb · **Taste:** dust

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party attacks Haskell on the green:** she drops Illusory Appearance, uses Invisible Passage, and is gone. The villagers saw the party attack a widow. Lose the guards; gain nothing. She's waiting invisible in M3.
- **If the party burns every dolly:** Chaff is furious and comes to them, in the fields, as herself (run Scene 05's climax here if the table prefers, with the wheel still turning a quarter mile away).
- **If the party skips all three:** the straw trail from Scene 01 is still there, and Brann will lead them to the mill himself at 11 PM, carrying a lantern and a cleaver.
