# Scene 00 — Arrival at Hollow Ford

[[10-Adventures/SP-HAL-01 — Straw Men/Scenes/00 — Scene Index]] · Next → [[Scene 01 — The Closing Reel]]

## Purpose
Land the hook, seat the party in the Sheaf & Sickle, and let them notice one thing wrong with one neighbor. Plant the backward wheel where they can see it without knowing it matters.
**Budget:** 25 minutes. Running long? Skip the stalls; keep Brann, the hook, and one tell.

## Arrival
> *The road drops to a slow brown river and a village strung along the crossing. Market stalls are coming down on the green, and the last light catches a quarter mile upstream, where an old mill's wheel is turning. It turns against the current, slow and wet, so the river climbs its paddles. Nobody on the green is looking at it. Turnip lanterns grin from the doorsteps, a fiddler tunes by the bonfire, and the inn's door stands open on warmth and cider.*

**To the table:** *"It's market day in a village you don't know. Who do you talk to first, and what do you want from them?"*

## Who Is Here
- **[[Brann Tully]]**, innkeeper. *Wants:* his son back and the festival to go on. *Knows:* Pip never stays out after dark. *Reacts:* a big laugh that keeps cracking; refills cups nobody asked for.
- **[[Hedda Voss]]**, militia captain (Straw Double). *Wants:* nobody to look too closely at anything. *Knows:* three weeks of memories, nothing older. *Reacts:* too calm; gauntlets on at table; stands far from the fire.
- **[[Father Corwin]]**, priest of the Sower. *Wants:* Mother Nettle gone. *Knows:* something is wrong and has picked the wrong culprit. *Reacts:* stern, frightened, honest.
- **Widow Haskell** ([[Auntie Chaff]]), at the busiest stall on the green. *Wants:* five more dollies sold. *Reacts:* warm, motherly, presses one into any friendly hand for a copper.

## Beats

### The hook lands
Run whichever hook the table chose. Brann hires at 20 gp and all the cider they can drink; if the party came on Nettle's letter, Brann is the first person who admits the village is "off." Either way, Brann says it: *"Hedda says he's hiding. Pip's never hidden from supper in his life."*

| If asked… | Brann says |
|---|---|
| About the mill | *"Closed for repairs. Aldous says. Three weeks now."* He frowns, as if hearing it for the first time. |
| About Voss | *"Steady as a post. Steadier than she used to be, truth told."* |
| About Nettle | *"Father Corwin's got a bee in his bonnet. She's rude. That's not a crime."* |
| About Haskell | *"Lovely woman. Came in with the market. The children adore her."* |

### The first tell
Seat a double near the party: Voss at the next table, or **[[Aldous Crane]]** at the bar repeating that the mill is closed for repairs. A character who studies a suspect for a minute can make a **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) or Intelligence (Investigation)** check. On a success, the DM gives one tell:

- No breath fogs from their mouth in the cold night air
- They won't eat or drink; they push food around their plate
- They flinch from torches and stand far from the bonfire
- A dry corn kernel sits where one tooth should be
- They don't remember things from before three weeks ago
- They smell of damp hay and river water

Give one tell free to the first PC who tests a suspect directly (offers food, holds a candle close, asks about last spring).

### Subplot plant
If using *A face in the loft*, the PC's relative is here, warm and glad to see them, and gets one detail wrong: a dead pet's name, the PC's birthday, which hand the PC favors.

### The wheel
Anyone who looks upstream notices the wheel turns against the current. A **DC 10 Intelligence** check (no proficiency needed) confirms that's impossible without a second source of power. Nobody in the village will discuss it; they change the subject as if they didn't hear.

## Encounter (if any)
None. If the party starts a fight with a suspected double in the inn, Voss's militia (4 **guards**, real) break it up, and the party's standing with the village drops for the night.

## Clues Available Here
- A tell → the doubles are fake
- The backward wheel → the mill is the source
- Brann's "three weeks" and Crane's three phrases → the mill is the source
- Haskell's free dolly → Widow Haskell is the hag (in hindsight)

## Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** turnip lanterns, a gold-and-black river, the wrong-way wheel · **Sound:** fiddle tuning, stall boards clattering down · **Smell:** cider, woodsmoke, and once, close to Voss, wet hay · **Touch:** the cold coming on, a warm roll pressed into a palm · **Taste:** sharp new cider

## Alternates & Re-hooks
- **If the party heads for the mill at once:** Crane meets them on the path with his three phrases and a lantern. The door is barred; the wheel groans. Let them look, then pull them back for the reel with Brann's shout, or let them in early and run Scene 05's M1–M2 as a scouting pass (Chaff is at the stall and the loft is locked).
- **If the party ignores the hook:** the reel comes to them. Scene 01 happens on the green whether or not they care.
- **If the party skips the inn:** the tells resurface on the dance floor in Scene 01, where half the dancers won't come near the fire.

- → [[Scene 01 — The Closing Reel]]: the fiddle finds its tune, and Brann bangs the cider barrel. *"Reel's starting. Everyone dances. Even strangers."*
