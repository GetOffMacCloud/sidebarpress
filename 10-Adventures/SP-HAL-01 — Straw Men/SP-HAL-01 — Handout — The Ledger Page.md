# Handout — The Ledger Page

Back to [[SP-HAL-01-Straw-Men]] · Found in [[Scene 05 — The Mill]], area M6.

> [!note] DM
> A moldy book of bargains going back more than sixty years, in a cramped, hooked hand. Most entries are small: a charm for a copper, a curse for a chicken. Give the players the page below. The ring is wrapped in it, gold set with a pearl, cold and wet to the touch. The last line is in fresh ink and appears only if a PC ate the apple.

---

> *Bargains kept. Bargains owed.*
>
> *Lady Ysolde Marrow, of Marrowmere.*
> *One vial of Sweetsleep, enough for a wedding feast.*
> *Paid in full with her mother's wedding ring.*
>
> *— circled twice in red —*
>
> *The Gentleman in Grey, no fixed house.*
> *Twelve folk of Hollow Ford, delivered at the stroke of midnight, All Hallows' Eve.*
> *Payment: one wish, small, to be named.*
>
> *One reflection, sold to the Gentleman in Grey.*
> *Paid: a second wish, smaller.*

---

**Print notes:** one page, aged paper, a single red circle around the Marrow entry, a water stain across the bottom. Leave the reflection line off a second copy for tables where nobody ate the apple.
