## Straw Double
*Medium construct (glamoured), unaligned*

A scarecrow in a villager's clothes, stuffed with a handful of that villager's memories and glamoured to pass for them. Based on the **commoner** (MM 345).

---
**Armor Class** 10
**Hit Points** 9 (2d8); Captain Voss's double has 22 (4d8 + 4)
**Speed** 30 ft.

---
| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 6 (−2) | 8 (−1) | 10 (+0) |

---
**Damage Vulnerabilities** fire
**Damage Immunities** poison
**Condition Immunities** exhaustion, poisoned
**Senses** passive Perception 9
**Languages** the languages of the villager it copies
**Challenge** 1/8 (25 XP)

---
***Straw Body.*** The double is vulnerable to fire damage and immune to poison damage. It does not need to eat, drink, breathe, or sleep, and it avoids all four.

***False Appearance.*** While the double remains motionless on a scarecrow post, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary scarecrow.

***Glamour.*** The double appears to be the villager it copies. A creature that studies it for 1 minute can make a DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) or Intelligence (Investigation) check; on a success, the creature notices one tell (see [[Scene 00 — Arrival at Hollow Ford]]). A *detect magic* spell shows an aura of illusion. A *dispel magic* spell (DC 13) ends the glamour, revealing a scarecrow. The glamour also ends when the mill's wheel stops turning.

***Shallow Memory.*** The double knows nothing that happened more than three weeks before it was made. It repeats a small set of phrases.

***Unravel.*** When the double drops to 0 hit points, it bursts into a cloud of straw. The sleeping villager it copies wakes in the mill loft.

---
### Actions
***Club.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2 (1d4) bludgeoning damage.

***Grab and Drag.*** The double attempts to grapple one creature within 5 feet (escape DC 10). While it has a creature grappled, it moves at half speed toward the fields or the mill.

### Captain Voss's Double
As above, with 22 hit points, chain mail (AC 16), and a **longsword** (*Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) slashing damage). Challenge 1/4 (50 XP).

### A PC's Double
If a PC ate the apple in [[Scene 02 — The Bobbing Game]], their double in the workshop uses the Straw Double block with the PC's Charisma score, the PC's voice, and one of the PC's cantrips or weapon attacks (DM's choice; +3 to hit, 1d6 damage). It knows the PC's name, their companions' names, and one secret the PC has told at the table.

## The Gentleman in Grey
Not a combatant in this adventure. On the landing he is unharmable: attacks pass through his coat, spells are courteously declined. Treat him as an **archfey patron** for purposes of *detect magic* (overwhelming fey aura) and bargaining. If a later adventure needs numbers, start from a **noble** (MM 348) with the **green hag's** Illusory Appearance and Invisible Passage, legendary resistance 3/day, and immunity to all damage while on a road or a threshold. See [[The Gentleman in Grey]].
