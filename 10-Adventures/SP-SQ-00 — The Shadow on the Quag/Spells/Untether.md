# Untether
*1st-level abjuration*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Bard, Cleric, Paladin, Wizard

You touch a creature and speak its true name, or the name it was born with. The target can immediately repeat a saving throw, with advantage, against each effect currently charming it. If the target is charmed by a [[Bone Tether]], it makes a Wisdom saving throw against the tether's DC with advantage. On a success the charm ends and the tether crumbles to dust.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st. The creatures must be within 30 feet of each other when you target them, and you touch each one as part of the casting.

## Notes
- Worked out at the Field Station during SP-SQ-02 by Doktor Agathe Sauerbrunn and Lecturer Dolz, from the pins the party recovered. It's the University's answer to Kermin's tethers. Any PC who brings Dolz a tether learns it free.
- Kermin's own tethers are DC 18, so *untether* is no sure thing. Pulling the pin is still the best cure.
- *Homebrew.*
