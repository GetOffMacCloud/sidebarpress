# Blackhand
*2nd-level necromancy*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Cleric (Iuz only), Warlock

Your hand turns black to the wrist. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within your reach. On a hit, the target takes 3d8 necrotic damage. Any of the following spells of 2nd level or lower affecting the target also ends: *protection from evil and good*, *sanctuary*, *warding bond*, *shield of faith*, and similar protective wards (DM's choice).

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 2nd, and the spell ends protective wards of a level equal to or lower than the slot you used.

## Notes
- **Conversion** of WGR5's 3rd-level priest spell *blackhand* (pp. 9–13), which Iuz's priests use to negate *protection from evil*. In 5e it becomes a ward-breaking touch. The damage is set to the 2nd-level single-target benchmark.
- **IP:** "blackhand" is a TSR name (WGR5). The Kestrel edition must rename it (suggestion: *ward-rot*).
- Used by the [[Grinning Initiate]] (1/day).
