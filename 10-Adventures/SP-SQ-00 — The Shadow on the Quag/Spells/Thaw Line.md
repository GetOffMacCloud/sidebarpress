# Thaw Line
*1st-level evocation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Self (30-foot line)
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard

A ribbon of heat runs out from your hand in a line 30 feet long and 5 feet wide. Each creature in the line must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 2d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. Along the line:
- ice up to 1 foot thick melts into open water. In winter it refreezes after 1 hour. A creature standing on ice that melts must succeed on the same saving throw or fall into the water.
- fog and mist burn away for 1 minute. A [[Fog-Drowned]] in the line can't use Fog-Fed on its next turn.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 and the melted ice thickness by 1 foot for each slot level above 1st.

## Notes
- An old Ritterstadt ice-fishers' working, written down by Lecturer Hamlin Dreel (Subterranean Studies), whose father fished the Quag. A student can learn it from Dreel before SP-SQ-05 (cost: a term's tuition step, or 20 credits).
- Damage is in line with 1st-level line and cone spells. Its real power is cutting the ice under a pursuer, or cutting a path through fog.
- *Homebrew.*
