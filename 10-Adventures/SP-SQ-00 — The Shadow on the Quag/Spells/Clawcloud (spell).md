# Clawcloud
*2nd-level conjuration*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a raven's feather dipped in blood)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
**Classes:** Cleric (Iuz only), Druid, Warlock

You call down a [[Clawcloud]]: a swarm of ravens that appears in an unoccupied space you can see within range. The swarm is friendly to you and your companions. Roll initiative for it; it has its own turns. It obeys verbal commands you give it (no action required by you). If you give it no commands, it defends itself and otherwise shadows the last creature you pointed out to it, staying out of reach. Its Tail and Tell reports come to you. The swarm disperses when the spell ends or when it drops to 0 hit points.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the spell doesn't require concentration and lasts 8 hours.

## Notes
- **Conversion** of WGR5's 4th-level priest spell *clawcloud* (p. 11): a flock of ravens that can tail a party and peel off to report. In 5e it's mainly a scouting spell. Its swarm is a CR 1/2 combatant, in line with 2nd-level summons.
- A druid can learn it, but the ravens obey grudgingly. They won't attack a creature of the forest.
- **IP:** "clawcloud" is a TSR name (WGR5). The Kestrel edition must rename it (suggestion: *tell-flock*).
