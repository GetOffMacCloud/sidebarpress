# Tether
*5th-level enchantment*

**Casting Time:** 1 hour
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a pin carved from a finger-bone, which the spell turns into a bone tether)
**Duration:** Instantaneous (the tether lasts as described)
**Classes:** Wizard

You whisper into a bone pin for an hour, making it a [[Bone Tether]]. For the next 7 days, any creature can plant the tether on a humanoid that is charmed by the planter, by pushing the pin into the humanoid's clothing or hair. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. If the target succeeds, the pin crumbles to dust.

On a failed save, the target is charmed by the planter for 30 days, as long as the pin remains on its person. While charmed this way:
- the target doesn't know it is charmed, and makes no saving throws to end the effect;
- the target remembers the planter's suggestions as its own ideas (each works like *suggestion*, with no save, and must be given in person or by letter);
- the target refuses a suggestion that would obviously harm it, but finds a reason to do almost anything else;
- as a bonus action, the planter can perceive through the target's senses until the start of its next turn, if the target is within 1 mile.

The effect ends early if the pin is removed, if the target is targeted by *remove curse* or a successful *dispel magic* (DC 15), or if the target is affected by [[Untether]]. It is suppressed while the target is within 30 feet of the unboxed [[The Gaoler's Link|Gaoler's Link]].

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, a tether planted on a target lasts 1 year instead of 30 days.

## Notes
- **Villain magic.** Kermin Mind-Bender makes the tethers, and his tether-mages plant them (WGR5 p. 86: Kermin's mind-control specialty). A PC can learn *tether* only from a Kermin spellbook, and the DM should treat casting it as an evil act.
- **Why so long a casting.** The low-level agent needs only to plant the pin. That is why a CR 2 [[Tether-Mage]] can hold a merchant for months.
- *Homebrew.*
