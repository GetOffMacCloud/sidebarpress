# School of Oblamancy

![[Oblamancy_Sigil.png|300]]

*The second of the **Schools of Blood Magic**. Cruormancy spills blood to kill. Oblamancy offers it up to win, and the blood it offers is rarely the caster's own.*

Oblamancy (from Old Suloise *oblatus*, "offered up") is blood magic that binds: it holds enemies in place, moves allies where they're needed, and ties a company of soldiers into one body. It deals no damage and heals no one. An oblamancer pays for it with **the Tithe**, blood taken from the people standing nearest him, and he doesn't ask first. He knows the power is evil. He has decided that no cost is too high for victory, and that the cost is his to decide.

> [!summary] At a glance
> Wizard-only **battlefield control and coordination** · **No damage, no healing, no temporary hit points** · Every leveled spell has a **Blood (B) cost** of slot level × Intelligence modifier · **The Tithe** takes that blood from anyone nearby who isn't hostile, **without consent** · Spells live only in a **Hemotheurgical Codex** and need a morning bleed · Casting leaves a **Stain**, on the oblamancer alone.

> [!info] Rules base
> **2014 Player's Handbook.** The rules every blood school shares (the Codex, the morning bleed, the lineage, always being found) are in [[The Schools of Blood Magic]]. A player oblamancer requires DM approval **and the table's agreement** (see *DM Notes*).

## Lore — The Battle-Magisters

> [!warning] Homebrew lore
> Everything in this section is Sidebar Press invention, not published Greyhawk canon.

Oblamancy was the other half of the **Suel Imperium's** blood war-art. Cruormancers killed the enemy. The **battle-magisters** stood behind the Imperium's own legions and bled them: a cohort's strength spent to pin a Baklunish charge in place, a rank of spearmen drained so the rank behind could strike twice. The legions feared their magisters more than the enemy, and won anyway.

It is forbidden for the same reasons as [[Wizard — School of Cruormancy|Cruormancy]], and hunted by the same people: the Guild of Wizardry, Greyhawk University, and the clergy of [[Wee-Jas|Wee Jas]]. Soldiers have their own reason. An oblamancer on your side of the field is still an oblamancer.

## Rules of the School

### Wizard Only

Oblamancy spells exist only in an oblamancer's **Hemotheurgical Codex**. No other class, feature, or effect grants them. They can't be copied, scribed onto scrolls, or found as loot.

### Creatures with Blood

Oblamancy affects only **creatures with blood**, whether the spell helps or hinders them. Constructs, elementals, oozes, plants, and most undead can't be targeted, affected, or tithed. Undead that feed on blood (vampires and vampire spawn, for example) do count. When a creature's status is unclear, the DM decides.

### The Blood Component (B)

Every leveled oblamancy spell has the **B (Blood)** component. As part of casting the spell, hit points must be paid equal to **the level of the spell slot × your Intelligence modifier** (minimum 2 per slot level). Cantrips need only a drop of blood and cost no hit points.

| Slot | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | 6th | 7th | 8th | 9th |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Int +3 | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 | 18 | 21 | 24 | 27 |
| Int +4 | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 | 24 | 28 | 32 | 36 |
| Int +5 | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 | 30 | 35 | 40 | 45 |

You can pay the cost yourself or take it with **the Tithe** (below). Either way, the hit point loss can't be reduced or prevented, and it isn't damage, so it doesn't provoke Concentration checks. If the cost can't be paid in full, the spell fails and the slot is wasted.

> [!note] A sharper mind costs more
> Raising your Intelligence raises the cost. That's intended: more power always demands more blood.

### The Stain

Each time you cast an **oblamancy spell of 1st level or higher**, you gain **1 Stain**. You gain **1 more** if the Tithe for that spell drops a creature to 0 hit points. The Stain is yours alone: the people you bleed are never stained by it.

Oblamancy uses the Stain track and the Scouring Stain rules in [[Wizard — School of Cruormancy#The Stain|the Cruormancy note]], with one change: **Blood Debt** requires that you neither cast an oblamancy spell nor use the Tithe during the fight. If [[The Price of Power]] is running, each Stain is 1 Corruption instead.

## School of Oblamancy *(Arcane Tradition)*

> *"I counted them before the battle and I counted them after. The second number was smaller. It was also not zero, and that is the only arithmetic that matters."*

| Wizard Level | Feature |
|---|---|
| 2nd | Hemotheurgical Codex, Sanguine Offering, The Tithe |
| 6th | The Ledger, Pay the Difference |
| 10th | The Greater Good |
| 14th | Whatever It Takes |

### Hemotheurgical Codex
*2nd-level School of Oblamancy feature*

When you choose this school at 2nd level, your master places a **Hemotheurgical Codex** in your hands: a second spellbook, bound to your blood, that holds your oblamancy spells. It contains one **oblamancy cantrip** of your choice (which you know, and which doesn't count against the number of wizard cantrips you know) and both **1st-level oblamancy spells**.

Whenever you gain a wizard level that lets you prepare spells of a new level, **every oblamancy spell of that level appears in your Codex**, written in your blood. These spells are in addition to the spells you add to your ordinary spellbook for gaining a level. Whenever you would learn a new wizard cantrip, you can choose the other oblamancy cantrip.

You prepare oblamancy spells from your Codex as part of your normal list of prepared wizard spells.

### Sanguine Offering
*2nd-level School of Oblamancy feature*

Your Codex is blank until you feed it. Each day, when you prepare your wizard spells, you bleed onto the pages of the oblamancy spells you want to prepare. You **lose hit points equal to the total spell levels** of the oblamancy spells you prepare that day (cantrips cost nothing). This loss can't be reduced or prevented, and it isn't damage. You can recover the hit points normally.

A spell you didn't bleed for stays blank, and you can't prepare it that day. You can't prepare a spell if the loss would reduce you to 0 hit points.

**The Codex accepts only your blood.** The Tithe can't pay this cost. It is the one debt you can't hand to someone else.

### The Tithe
*2nd-level School of Oblamancy feature*

Also at 2nd level, you learn to pay for your magic with other people's blood. When you cast an oblamancy spell, you can take some or all of its Blood cost from **one creature with blood that you can see within 30 feet of you and that isn't hostile to you**. This takes no action. The creature loses that many hit points, feels it happen, and knows you did it.

- **No consent.** A creature that travels or fights at your side gets no saving throw. Any other creature (a bystander, a hireling's mule, a prisoner) makes a **Constitution saving throw** against your spell save DC. On a success, you take nothing from it and must pay that part of the cost yourself.
- **One donor.** You can tithe only one creature for each spell. Any part of the cost you don't take from it, you pay yourself.
- **Only what it has.** You can't take more hit points than the creature currently has. The Tithe ignores temporary hit points. If the creature is shapechanged or transformed, the loss comes from the hit points of its **true form**, not the form it's wearing.
- **Thin blood.** A beast, or any creature with an Intelligence of 3 or lower, pays at **half value**: it loses 2 hit points for every 1 point of cost it covers. A swarm can't be tithed.
- **At 0 hit points.** A creature the Tithe reduces to 0 hit points falls **unconscious and is stable**. A thin-blooded creature dies instead. Features and effects that keep a creature from dropping to 0 (a half-orc's Relentless Endurance, *death ward*) work normally.
- **Not damage.** The loss can't be reduced or prevented, and it doesn't provoke Concentration checks. It doesn't lower the creature's hit point maximum, and the creature can be healed normally.

The Tithe pays only the Blood cost of oblamancy spells.

> [!example] What a spell costs the company
> Verrick (Int +4) casts *blood bond* with a 3rd-level slot: 12 hit points. He takes all 12 from the fighter beside him. An hour later he needs *ligature* and the fighter is down, so he reaches for the pack mule: a 1st-level slot costs 4, the mule's blood is thin, and it loses 8 of its 11 hit points.

### The Ledger
*6th-level School of Oblamancy feature*

Starting at 6th level, you keep the count. You know the current hit points and hit point maximum of every creature with blood within 30 feet of you that you can see and that isn't hostile to you. You also know them for any creature affected by one of your oblamancy spells, for as long as the spell affects it.

In addition, you can **split the Tithe**. When you tithe for a spell, you can divide the cost among any number of eligible creatures, as long as each has an Intelligence of 4 or higher. Thin-blooded creatures still can't be combined: a spell takes from at most one of them.

### Pay the Difference
*6th-level School of Oblamancy feature*

Also at 6th level, when a creature with blood that you can see within 30 feet of you, and that isn't hostile to you, takes damage, you can use your reaction to **move that damage** onto yourself or onto a different non-hostile creature with blood within 30 feet of you. The new creature must have an Intelligence of 4 or higher, and it doesn't need to be willing. It takes the damage in place of the original target, and that damage **can't be reduced or prevented in any way**, including by resistance or immunity.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

### The Greater Good
*10th-level School of Oblamancy feature*

At 10th level, you stop counting the cost at all.

- **Deeper Tithe.** When you cast an oblamancy spell of 1st level or higher that requires a saving throw, you can pay its Blood cost a **second time** to increase the spell's save DC by 1, or a second and **third time** to increase it by 2. You can use the Tithe for each payment, and each payment can come from a different donor.
- **Spent Well.** Once per turn, when a creature hostile to you fails a saving throw against an oblamancy spell of 1st level or higher that you cast, you can choose one creature with blood within 60 feet of you that isn't hostile to you. That creature can immediately use its reaction to move up to half its speed or to make one weapon attack.

### Whatever It Takes
*14th-level School of Oblamancy feature*

Starting at 14th level, there is nothing you won't spend.

**The Tithe can kill.** Your Tithe reaches 60 feet, and it can take a spell's full cost from a creature even if that is more hit points than the creature has. A creature the Tithe reduces to 0 hit points this way is **dying**, not stable. If the cost left over after it reaches 0 equals or exceeds its hit point maximum, it **dies outright**, and nothing that would leave it at 1 hit point saves it. If you tithe a creature that is already at 0 hit points, it dies.

**The Final Count.** As a bonus action, you can give yourself over to the arithmetic. For **1 minute**, you gain the following benefits:

- Hostile creatures that are bloodied (at half their hit points or fewer) have **disadvantage** on saving throws against your oblamancy spells.
- Once per round, you can use Pay the Difference **without using your reaction or expending a use**.

Each time you use the Final Count, you gain **2 Stain**. Once you use it, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

## DM Notes

> [!danger] The character never asks. The players must.
> In the fiction, the oblamancer takes blood without consent. At the table, one player is spending other players' hit points, and from 14th level their characters' lives. **Get every player's agreement before the campaign starts**, and check in again when the oblamancer reaches 14th level. If anyone says no, this school belongs to an NPC.

> [!note] Balance posture
> The oblamancer's spells are strong for their level because he gives up damage entirely and because the cost is heavy: a 5th-level spell at Int +5 takes 25 hit points from someone. The Tithe doesn't lower hit point maximums, so the real price is the party healer's spell slots. Animals are a stopgap for 1st- and 2nd-level spells only: thin blood and the one-donor rule mean nothing short of a person pays for a big spell.

> [!tip] Things to watch
> - **Feats.** *Quicken Pulse* lets an ally make an opportunity attack without using its reaction. With Sentinel or Polearm Master that gets strong. Fine at a no-feats table.
> - **The rogue.** *Heart's Spur* and Spent Well can give a rogue a second Sneak Attack in a round. That's intended, and it's the best reason an ally has to forgive him.
> - **The satchel of rats.** A rat has 1 hit point and pays for nothing. A goat (4 hit points) covers 2 points of cost, a mule (11) covers 5, and a riding horse (13) covers 6. Players will try a wagon of livestock; the one-donor rule is the answer.
> - **Who counts as "at your side."** The no-save rule is for companions. A stranger, an enemy's horse, or a sleeping guard gets the Constitution save, and a hostile creature can't be tithed at all.

> [!question] Open decisions
> - [ ] **Thirsting pace.** Under the shared Stain track, a blood mage at 10 Stain must cast a leveled blood spell every day, which outruns every way of removing Stain. Decide whether that doom clock is intended (it affects Cruormancy and The Price of Power too).
> - [ ] **Move the Stain rules** into [[The Schools of Blood Magic]] so both schools read from one place.
> - [ ] **Sanguine Chains** can chain an ally to an enemy. Ban it or keep it.
