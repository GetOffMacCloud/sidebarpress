# Wee Jas

> Wee Jas, the Witch Goddess, Ruby Sorceress, Stern Lady, and Death's Guardian, is the Suel goddess of magic, death, vanity, and law. She is lawful neutral with lawful-evil tendencies. Her symbol is a red skull before a fireball, sometimes shown as a skull ringed with rubies or flames. She guards the orderly passage of the dead and the lawful use of magic.

## Canon Account
- **Origin:** Suel. She is not commonly worshipped across most of the Flanaess, and her followers are scattered.
- **Depiction:** a beautiful Suel woman in a fine gown, wearing skull ornaments (a ring, bracelet, or necklace). Her colors are ruby-red and black.
- **Portfolio in practice:** death as an ordered transition, the dead (especially the lawful dead), arcane lore, and law. Her clergy include clerics, specialty priests, and wizards.
- **Undead:** her clergy **do not turn undead** (2e). They may be permitted to command undead, most readily lawful or mindless undead, provided it furthers law, magic, or death. Later editions frame this as tolerating undead raised lawfully and not against the dead's will.
- **Relations:** lover of Norebo; on good terms with Phaulkon, Bralm, and most lawful powers. She is honored in Lo Reltarma and among the Scarlet Brotherhood.
- **In Perrenland:** a modest following outside the Old Kerk (see [[Perrenland]]).

## Contradictions
- **Alignment:** lawful neutral in most sources; some depict her drifting toward lawful evil. **Stance:** LN.
- **Undead:** 2e permits commanding some undead; later fan and organized-play material sometimes presents Jasites as hunters of *unlawful* undead. **Stance:** Jasites tolerate lawful, respectful use of the dead and oppose desecration.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- **Why Ashlock's cover works** ([[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]]): a Jasite funeral priest is expected to handle the dead and does not turn undead. Villagers see nothing odd in a Wee Jas mendicant fussing over graves, bones, and the newly dead. That is the right cover for a Nerullite.
- **House detail (not canon):** our "grey-robed funeral mendicant" is a Sidebar Press invention, a humble wandering order that buries the poor. Canon Jasite colors are ruby-red and black. A Religion DC 13 check notices that a Jasite in plain grey with **no ruby or skull ornament** is unusual. This makes a good optional clue against Ashlock.
- A real Jasite NPC would treat Ashlock's rite as **desecration**. Use that as a potential future ally against the Nine Chiefs cell.
