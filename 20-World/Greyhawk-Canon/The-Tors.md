# The Tors

> The Tors are a series of mesa-like hills with flattened crowns, running off the Crystalmist Mountains to form the southernmost region of the Yeomanry League, bordering the Hool Marshes. They are wild, full of bandits, monsters and goblin tribes; packs of wild tyrgs are a particular problem for livestock on their northern edges.

## Canon Account
- **Geography:** hills off the Crystalmists with flattened crowns, much like mesas; the southern edge of the Yeomanry; the Hool Marshes to the south.
- **Dangers:** monsters, bandits and goblins raid into the Yeomanry; **tyrg packs** attack livestock and farms on the northern edges.
- **Melkot:** an independent walled town in the western Tors (see [[Melkot]]).
