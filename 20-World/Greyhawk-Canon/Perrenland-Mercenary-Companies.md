# Perrenland's Mercenary Companies

> In every edition, hired mercenary bands are [[Perrenland]]'s chief export. 1e gives the makeup of a typical band and who hires them. No official TSR or WotC book names an individual company. Named companies, charters and "the swords" as a political voice all come from the fan e-zine *Oerth Journal* #5, which the vault's Region Report 0003 draws on.

## Canon Account (official)
- **1e (576 CY).** The main export is "well-trained merc bands." A typical band:

  | Troop type | Share |
  |---|---|
  | Pikemen | 30% |
  | Pole-armed mountaineers | 20% |
  | Lowland crossbowmen | 30% |
  | Axe and flail foot | 10% |
  | Cavalry (half heavy horse from around Schwartzenbruin, half bow-armed medium horse from the Clatspur region) | 10% |

  High elves from the Quagflow valley add about 10% as scouts and light infantry. Named employers are Veluna, Furyondy, Bissel, and Ket (against Tusmit). The militia can muster within a day.
- **LGG (591).** The bands are still "the nation's chief export," and the elves now come from Highfolk. After the 583 CY pact, mercenaries were offered to Iuz, and "not one man volunteered."

## Fan Material (*Oerth Journal* #5, 1997; not official canon)
- More than 40 mercenary companies call Perrenland home.
- Companies were given **charters** after the borders closed in 490 CY, in thanks for their service during the occupations.
- Perrenland is **neutral ground** for mercenaries of all alignments. Foreign mercenaries get grudging respect if they obey the law.
- **"The swords"** act as an informal advisory voice to the government, alongside the merchants and the scholars.
- **Jürgen of Traft and Jürgen's Ernes** (pp. 22–23):
  - Jürgen is a veteran captain, quietly dignified, with a straight white scar from forehead to chin.
  - The company is the **Ernes**, a troop of medium cavalry whose symbol is a **diving eagle**. Typical work is caravan escort and militia training.
  - They refuse slaving, kidnapping and slaughtering non-combatants.
  - Ernes representatives go along with any band he hires to make sure terms are kept "to the letter." He destroyed at least one band that tried to "re-negotiate."
  - The article is set after the Greyhawk Wars, when Jürgen is in his mid-thirties, so **in 576 CY he is in his mid-twenties**.

## Living Greyhawk Material (campaign-specific; not used)
- The **Pax Mercuri**, a national mercenary organisation with ranks and branches.
- The **Auszug** army units.
- Clan Vuurzward's mercenary tradition.

## Contradictions
- **Elf auxiliaries:** from the Quagflow valley (1e) versus Highfolk (LGG). **Stance:** Quagflow for 576.
- **The "never serve Iuz" rule** exists only in Living Greyhawk. It does not matter in 576, before the pact.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- **Adopted from the fan material:** company charters, neutral ground in winter, "the swords" as one of three unofficial voices, and Jürgen's Ernes. These match Region Report 0003 and underpin [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]]. In 576, Jürgen is a young captain building his name.
- **Homebrew on top:** [[The Hundred]], the red cord, the halved-standard custom, and the charter-roll review by the Council of Hetmen.
