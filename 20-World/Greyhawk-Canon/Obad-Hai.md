# Obad-Hai

> Obad-Hai, the Shalm, is the Flan god of nature, woodlands, hunting and beasts, honored with Beory and Ehlonna by the druids of the Old Faith. Hommlet's folk lean toward this druidical faith.

## Canon Account
- A Flan god of nature and the wild, and the patron of druids.
- **The Old Faith** is the druidical faith of the Flanaess. T1-4 tells the DM that Hommlet's folk "tend towards the Druidical faith (Ehlonna, Obad-hai, and most of all Beory), or follow St. Cuthbert" (PDF p. 121).
- Hommlet's grove is kept by the druid Jaroo Ashstaff. Visitors not of the Old Faith are expected to give a few gold pieces to Jaroo for the needy (T1-4, PDF p. 13).

## Contradictions
- Sources differ on whether the Old Faith is one faith with many gods or Beory's faith with others honored in it. We treat it as the druids' shared faith, with Obad-Hai and Beory foremost.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- **SP-GS01 (homebrew):** the grove's sacred bird, Old Gobbler, eats first of Hommlet's harvest every Brewfest, and the first fruits are the Old Faith's offering. The grove's shrine is carved with the rite.
