# St. Cuthbert

> St. Cuthbert of the Cudgel is a god of common sense, wisdom, zeal and truth, whose church is strong in the central Flanaess. Hommlet's church to him was built after the Temple's fall.

## Canon Account
- A god of common sense, truth and zeal; his holy symbol is a cudgel or a starburst.
- Hommlet has a Church of St. Cuthbert (T1-4, PDF pp. 10–15, area 20), and many of its folk follow him alongside or instead of the Old Faith (T1-4, PDF p. 121).
- Hommlet's canon is Canon Terjon.
