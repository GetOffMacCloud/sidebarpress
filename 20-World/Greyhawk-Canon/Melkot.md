# Melkot

> Melkot is an independent walled town in the western (or southern) Tors, near the Hold of the Sea Princes, supported by mining. An arcane order, the Sons of Marchanter, is based there.

## Canon Account
- Independent city-state; mining economy; a pass through the Tors connects it to the Yeomanry.
- **Sons of Marchanter:** an arcane order based in Melkot (*Living Greyhawk Journal* #4, p. 16).
- Living Greyhawk material has Theodain of Melkot among the first to explore Slerotin's Passage, near the Yeomanry town of Dark Gate, in 576 CY.
