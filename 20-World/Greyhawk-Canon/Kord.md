# Kord

> Kord, the Brawler, is the Suel god of athletics, sports, brawling, strength and courage, and the most popular Suel deity. He is chaotic good in 1e–3e. He is listed in the 1983 boxed set and was fully detailed by Len Lakofka in *Dragon* #86–87.

## Canon Account
- **Title and rank:** the Brawler; intermediate deity (3e).
- **Alignment:** chaotic good (1e–3e).
- **Portfolio:** athletics, sports, brawling, strength, courage. Later sources add storms.
- **Symbol:** an eight-pointed star made of spears and maces.
- **Plane:** Ysgard. The Hall of the Valiant is his followers' afterlife.
- **Family:** son of Phaulkon and Syrul, grandson of Lendor. He has a friendly rivalry with Llerg, sponsored Nazarn's rise to godhood, and is a foe of dragons.
- **Clergy:**
  - Vestments of bright red, white and blue.
  - Favoured weapons: greatsword and bastard sword.
  - Temples are built like arenas.
  - Rites are short and joyous, with rhythmic clapping and chanting.
  - Cowards are excommunicated.
- **Oaths:** "By Kord's sword!" and "By the blue boots of Kord!"
- **Perrenland:**
  - Kord does not appear in the LGG's Perrenland entry.
  - In Living Greyhawk, he is outside the Old Kerk, with "a few followers," and distrusted as a chaotic god.
  - The fan *Oerth Journal* #5 calls him "widely followed by mercenaries" in Perrenland.

## Contradictions
- **Alignment:** chaotic good (1e–3e), unaligned (4e), and probably chaotic neutral in the 2014 PHB (verify). **Stance:** chaotic good.
- **Perrender worship:** official canon is silent, Living Greyhawk says "a few followers," and the fan *Oerth Journal* says "widely followed by mercenaries." **Stance:** the mercenary companies keep Kord loudly. The farmers and townsfolk of the Old Kerk do not. This follows Region Report 0003 and is a fan-sourced extrapolation, so flag it as such.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- A fist-fight "under Kord" (a formal bout between champions, unarmed, to yield or knockout) is how soldiers settle a dispute without bloodshed. *This custom is homebrew,* and it fits the god of brawling and courage. Used in [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]].
- "Wood is Kord's" (the Red Cord's house rule that wooden weapons are fair in a brawl) is also homebrew.
