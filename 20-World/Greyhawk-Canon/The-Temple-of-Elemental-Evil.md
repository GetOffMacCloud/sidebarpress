# The Temple of Elemental Evil

> A fortress-temple near Nulb raised by servants of Zuggtmoy and Iuz, broken at the Battle of Emridy Meadows in 569 CY. In 576 CY it stands ruined and shunned, and its servants are stirring again.

## Canon Account
- Built in the hills near Nulb, out of the way and ideal for "the fell purposes planned for this" (T1-4, PDF p. 6). Its patrons were the demoness Zuggtmoy and Iuz; its forces were part of "the broader tactics of Zuggtmoy, Iuz, and the rest" (T1-4, PDF p. 7).
- It was besieged and destroyed after the great battle, and its demoness sealed within (T1-4, PDF pp. 6, 29–30).
- **576 CY:** "a year ago, the bandits began to ride the roads again." Hommlet sent word to the Viscount, and adventurers began to come back (T1-4, PDF p. 6). Its agents in Hommlet include the traders Rannos Davl and Gremag (T1-4, PDF p. 10).

- *Return to the Temple of Elemental Evil* moves events past 576 CY. Its later history is out of scope for the 576 baseline.

- In 576 CY the Temple itself is quiet and ruined; its surviving servants work through agents and cells.
