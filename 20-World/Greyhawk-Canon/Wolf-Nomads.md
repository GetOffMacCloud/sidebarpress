# Wolf Nomads (Wegwiur)

> The Wegwiur are Baklunish horse-nomads of the Relentless Horde. They range north of Perrenland around Lake Quag, the Howling Hills and the edge of the Burneal Forest. In 576 CY their Tarkhan is **Bargru**, and Iuz is already their enemy to the east.

## Canon Account
- **Origin.** They came east with the Relentless Horde around 320 CY and split from the Tiger Nomads ([[Chakyik]]) after Kha-Khan Ogobanuk died. The Tarkhan holds loose authority over the clan khans and rules by Ogobanuk's Great Yassa.
- **Ruler (576 CY):** "The Fearless Wolf Leader, Tarkhan of all the Wegwiur, Commander of the Relentless Horde," **Bargru** (WoG 1983 p. 42). He still rules in 591 (LGG p. 133).
- **Capital:** Eru-Tovar, which grew from the first tarkhan's camp at the headwaters of the Blackwater.
- **Ungra Balan** is their largest town and their main trading port with Perrenland, where Perrender coin circulates freely. *This detail is 2e/3e (FtA, LGG).* For 576, treat the town as smaller.
- **People.** Baklunish with some Rovers of the Barrens blood. They are light horse and horse-archers who fight with bow and lance and wear little armour. The LGG-era text calls them loyal to their word ("much more so than the Tiger Nomads") and very easily offended. They raid the Tiger Nomads periodically.
- **Customs.** A warrior's rite of manhood is capturing a wild wolf cub. The honoured dead are buried in the Howling Hills.
- **Religion.** Istus and Geshtai, plus Telchur and Kurell taken from the Oerids, plus ancestor worship. See [[Istus]].
- **Economy.** Furs, hides, copper and horses. They mint no coin and barter instead.
- **Iuz.** Hostile by 576 CY, because Iuz claims the Howling Hills burial grounds. In Harvester 578 CY, the Horde crushes Iuz's army at the **Battle of Blackwater Bend** (*Dragon* #56). That battle is still in the future for 576.
- **Heraldry (LGG):** Sable, three wolves courant in pale gules.

## Contradictions
- **Language:** "Ordai" is the 3e name. 1e just says Baklunish. **Stance:** Baklunish, with Ordai as the tribes' own name for it.
- **Population:** about 80,000 in 1983 (fan transcription, verify) versus 120,000 in the LGG. **Stance:** use the 1983 figure.
- **5.5e:** the 2024 DMG names a "Wolf-Mother Bargra Yefkos" for 576. **Rejected.** Bargru rules.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- **Fletching:** canon has no fletching details. The sinew crisscross binding used as the "Wolf arrow" tell in [[SP-PBS-01-The-False-Arrow]] is Sidebar Press invention, and it is documented there.
- Their reputation for keeping their word is what makes a staged Wolf massacre believable. Use it.
- In Ritterstadt continuity, Eru-Tovar is where the Four Founders set out from in 565 (see [[RG-01 — History of Ritterstadt]]).
