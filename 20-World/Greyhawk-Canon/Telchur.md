# Telchur

> Telchur (Icebrother, Father Frost, Master of the North Wind) is the Oeridian lesser god of winter, cold and the north wind, a son of Procan. He is chaotic neutral: bitter and brooding, but not evil. His symbol is a leafless tree in a field of snow.

## Canon Account
- **Portfolio:** winter, cold, the north wind. He teaches that winter lies at the end of every season, stilling life; cold separates the weak from the strong.
- **Family:** one of Procan's children; disliked by his sister Wenta, whose autumn festivities his winter spoils.
- **Worship:** "natural" temples are caves or windbreaks in the snow, which seldom outlast the winter and are reconsecrated each autumn. A major temple, the Iceminster, stands in the north.
- **Needfest:** in some regions (canon names the Bandit Kingdoms and Highfolk) Needfest is called the **Feast of Telchur**, honoring his creation of winter and the start of winter's waning.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- Candlecrown's **Long Night Carol** is a local hymn to Telchur; the Yeomanry calling Needfest "the Feast of Telchur" in places is a house extension of the canon custom (canon names only the Bandit Kingdoms and Highfolk).
- **Mother Sedge's shrine** is a windbreak cave, matching his canon "natural temples," but permanent and kept warm with an ever-burning brazier (house).
- Tyrgs won't enter his shrine (house): they are "Telchur's beasts."
