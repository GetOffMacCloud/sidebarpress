# Iggwilv's Conquest of Perrenland

> The archmage Iggwilv struck from the Lost Caverns of Tsojcanth in the Yatils in 480 CY. She held all of Perrenland as its "Witch Queen" from 481 to 491 CY, when she lost her hold on the demon lord Graz'zt and her rule fell apart. Perrender memory of those ten years still shapes how people react to any organized evil.

## Canon Account
- **c. 460 CY:** Iggwilv first appears in Perrenland's records. She summons and binds the demon prince **Graz'zt**, using knowledge she gained (by some accounts, stole) from Zagig and from the tomb of Tsojcanth.
- **480–481 CY:** From the **Lost Caverns of Tsojcanth** in the Yatils, she attacks Perrenland, and the conquest is complete by 481 CY.
- **481–491 CY:** She rules Perrenland and pushes north toward Lake Quag and the Wolf Nomads. She also looks toward Ket.
- **491 CY:** Graz'zt slips her control. During a ritual involving Tsojcanth, she is weakened, then fights Graz'zt and banishes his earthly form, and her armies collapse into disorder. Perrenland throws off the yoke and destroys the nonhuman legions. Certain valleys in the **southern** cantons keep "an evil tinge" from residents who served her.
- **Aftermath:** Iggwilv is the mother of **Iuz**, sired by Graz'zt. The demigod across the Clatspurs is the Witch's son.

## Contradictions
- **What kind of army?** The LGG-derived account describes **"armies of evil humanoids"** (with demonic backing). Some fan and wiki summaries say she conquered Perrenland **"with an army of undead,"** but none of these cite a primary source we can confirm. **Stance:** canonically her legions were humanoid and fiendish, not undead.
- **Why she fell:** older sources say a ritual mistake freed Graz'zt. *Dungeon* #151 adds the Tsojcanth "living seal" ritual. These are compatible; use the later detail if needed.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- In our adventures, Perrender villagers remember "**the Witch's dead**." Treat this as **folk memory, not canon fact**. Ninety years of retelling has turned humanoid legions and demon-haunted valleys into a march of the dead. That suits [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]], where the villagers' Iggwilv fear is a red herring and Ashlock exploits it. Never state in DM-facing canon text that her army was undead.
- In 576 CY, the oldest Perrenders (born before 491) remember her reign firsthand or through their parents. By 598 CY, it is grandparents' stories.
