# Ekbir

> The Caliphate of Ekbir is a Baklunish theocratic monarchy on the Dramidj Ocean. Al'Akbar is its state faith, and its strengths are the Farises (holy cavalry) and a navy. In 576 CY it is ruled by **Caliph Xargun**.

## Canon Account
- **Ruler:** Xargun, Caliph of Ekbir, in both the 1983 set and the LGG (591, where he is a cleric of Al'Akbar). No "II."
- **Capital:** Ekbir City. Other towns: Fashtri and Zafreh.
- **Borders:** the Dramidj Ocean to the west; the Yatils, the Yecha Hills and most of the Udgru Forest to the north; the Blashikmund River (Tusmit) to the east; the Tuflik River (Zeif) to the south. The Tiger Nomads ([[Chakyik]]) range north of it.
- **Origin:** founded by survivors of the Invoked Devastation as a port, which grew through trade with the northern nomads.
- **Religion:** Al'Akbar first. The state also favours [[Istus]], Mouqol and Geshtai.
- **Language:** Baklunish, with Common not rare. Ancient Baklunish is the scholarly tongue.
- **Military:** light and medium cavalry, the caliph's heavy foot, and the **Farises**, holy mounted warriors founded by Udmey the Seer, who also founded the navy.
- **Economy:** foodstuffs and cloth (1983). The LGG adds fish, wood and shipbuilding.
- **Coinage (2e/3e):** star (pp), cup (gp), charger (sp), galley (ep), sentry (cp). There is no 1e coin name.
- **Relations:** Ket and the Tiger Nomads are "sometimes" enemies. Zeif and Tusmit are allies or enemies "sometimes."

## Contradictions
- **Population:** about 250,000 (1983, verify) versus 1,960,000 (LGG). **Stance:** use the 1983 scale.
- **5.5e:** the 2024 DMG changes the title to "Sultan" and the ruler to "Xargun II." **Rejected.**
- **Ekbir–Zeif relations "near ruin"** is 591 CY material. It does not apply in 576.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- **No canon** supports Ekbir trading with Perrenland over the Yatils, or names an "Ekbiri dialect." Both are Sidebar Press extrapolation in [[SP-PBS-01-The-False-Arrow]] (the Perrenland–Ekbir caravan road; Kezrin's dialect). They are plausible, since Ekbir borders the Yatils and trades with the nomads, but they should be listed as deviations.
- **Kezrin's "Ekbiri silver":** if a coin name is wanted, call it a **charger** (silver; 2e/3e coin name).
