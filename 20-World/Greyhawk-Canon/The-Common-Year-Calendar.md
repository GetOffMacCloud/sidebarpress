# The Common Year Calendar

> The Flanaess reckons dates in Common Years (CY). The year has 364 days: twelve 28-day months plus four 7-day festivals. Oerth has two moons. **Luna**, the great moon, has a 28-day cycle. **Celene**, the small aquamarine "Handmaiden," has a 91-day cycle and is full at the midpoint of each festival.

## Canon Account
**Year order:** Needfest (festival, 7) → Fireseek → Readying → Coldeven → Growfest (festival) → Planting → Flocktime → Wealsun → Richfest (festival) → Reaping → Goodmonth → Harvester → Brewfest (festival) → **Patchwall** (autumn) → Ready'reat → Sunsebb.

**Week:** Starday, Sunday, Moonday, Godsday, Waterday, Earthday, Freeday. The day names come from the Flan calendar. Because 364 divides evenly by 7, each date falls on the same weekday every year, and Godsday is always the 4th, 11th, 18th, and 25th.

**Moons:**
- **Luna:** 28-day cycle, full on Fireseek 11 ("Great Moon's Glory" falls on Readying 11, when Luna is full and Celene new).
- **Celene:** 91-day cycle, full on the 4th day of each festival.
- Both moons are full on Richfest 3–5. Both are new on **Goodmonth 11 ("Dark Night")**.

### Derived phases for late-year months (Sidebar Press calculation)
Counting from the canon anchors (Luna full Fireseek 11 and Richfest 4; Celene full on each festival's 4th day):

| Date | Luna | Celene |
|---|---|---|
| Goodmonth 11 | **New** (Dark Night) | Near new |
| Goodmonth 25 | Full | Waxing |
| Harvester 11 | New | Waxing gibbous |
| Brewfest 4 | Waxing | **Full** |
| **Patchwall 4** | **New (dark of Luna)** | Waning gibbous, 7 days past full |
| Patchwall 18 | Full | Waning |
| Ready'reat 4 | New | Waning crescent |

> The two canon anchors cross-check: this count puts Luna new on Goodmonth 11, which matches the canonical "Dark Night."

## Contradictions
- **Year length:** a few later sources describe a 360-day year with 6-day festivals. **Stance:** 364 days (1983 boxed set).
- **Luna phase tables:** fan-made calendars online give different Luna dates for the late-year months, such as full on the 4th and new on the 18th after Richfest. They conflict with the canon "Dark Night" on Goodmonth 11. **Stance:** use the derived table above.
- **Moonrise and moonset:** canon never gives rise or set times for either moon.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- **Celene is aquamarine.** Use that color in read-alouds and art, not plain white.
- **SP-PBS-02 continuity flag.** The adventure's "dark of Luna in Patchwall" is **Patchwall 4**. On that night Celene is a bright waning gibbous, only 7 days past its Brewfest full. On Earth-like moon geometry it would rise about two hours after sunset and set after dawn, not "four hours after midnight." Two ways to fix it:
  1. **Narrative fix (no rewrite):** canon gives no rise or set times, so declare that Celene runs a fast, low path across the northern sky and sets well before dawn. Change only the read-aloud color from "white chip" to "aquamarine." *Recommended.*
  2. **Calendar fix:** move the adventure to **Goodmonth 11, "Dark Night,"** when both moons are new. That is a perfect night for a Nerullite, but it requires re-keying the deadline away from Celene, which would no longer appear in the sky.
- Nights with notable moon conditions (Dark Night, Great Moon's Glory, the double full moon at Richfest) are strong hooks for rites. Log their use in [[Timeline]].
