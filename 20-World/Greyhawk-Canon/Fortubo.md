# Fortubo

> Fortubo is a Suel god of stone, metals, mountains and guardianship, worshipped by Suel miners and by many dwarves. His symbol is a glowing hammer.

## Canon Account
- **Portfolio:** stone, metals, mountains, guardianship. Lawful.
- **Worship:** Suel miners and smiths; the dwarves of some holds count him among their own.
