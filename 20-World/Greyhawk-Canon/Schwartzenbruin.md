# Schwartzenbruin

> The capital of [[Perrenland]], a fortified lake port on the southwest shore of Lake Quag. In 576 CY canon gives it as the capital, with a population of "25,000+" and its own heavy cavalry, and very little else. Almost all street-level detail comes from 2000s sources.

## Canon Account
- **Location:** the southwest shore of Lake Quag (LGG p. 85). The LGG says it can be reached by water "with some difficulty" up the Velverdyva (LGG p. 153). In 1e, Perrender warships on the Quag escort barges and cogs downriver.
- **Population:** 25,000+ (1e, 576 CY); 27,500 (LGG, 591 CY).
- **Name:** "Black Bear," after an early Ur-Flan tribe that tunnelled beneath the site.
- **History:** Aerdi forces arrived around 97–100 CY. Under the Viceroyalty of Ferrond, the fishing towns of Schwartzenbruin and Traft had to accept Aerdi bailiffs (LGG p. 85).
- **Roads:** the Krestingstrek runs to Krestible and on to Molvar in Ket. The Quag Road runs from Highfolk through the city and north along the lake.
- **Troops:** the canton is known for crossbowmen and heavy cavalry. In 1e, half the cavalry of a typical mercenary band is heavy horse from the Schwartzenbruin area (see [[Perrenland-Mercenary-Companies]]).
- **Government:** the seat of the Voormann and the national council. In 576 CY the Voormann is **Franz** (see [[Perrenland]]).
- **Later detail (Living Greyhawk, post-576; verify each):**
  - Most of the city stands on an island, with a mainland district called the Shore.
  - Old City: Fort Dian and the Girdle of Dian.
  - New City: the Arena, the Longmarket, the Necropolis and Procan's Grotto.
  - The Shore: the Great Barter, the Green Tower lighthouse, Paleen Rock and the Bapthmorda prison.
  - Wards that block teleportation into the city.

## Contradictions
- **Population:** 25,000+ (1e), 26,000 (Oerth Journal #5, fan), 27,500 (LGG). **Stance:** "about 26,000" for 576, as in Region Report 0003. It falls within the 1e "25,000+".
- **Where the Velverdyva leaves the lake:** no source says this in so many words. Canon puts the city on the SW shore and on the river route. **Stance:** Sidebar Press places the city at or near the Velverdyva's outflow, which matches Region Report 0003. This is house extrapolation.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- **Use the 576 layer only:** a walled lake port, the capital, mercenary hiring halls, a closely watched border patrol, and heavy cavalry. Treat the Living Greyhawk districts as optional colour, never as plot keys.
- The Red Cord tavern and Dornhalm's Yard (on the Velverdyva quays) are homebrew locations here. See [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]].
- The Duffkyyn Flats (the Derby ground outside the walls) and the Tanner's Rest are homebrew locations here. See [[SP-PBS-00-Nobodys-Colors]].
