# The Flan

> The Flan are the oldest human people of the Flanaess, which takes its name from them. They are a bronze- or copper-skinned folk with dark hair and eyes. They held the land before the great migrations of the Oeridians and Suel, and they kept the Old Faith of Beory and the druids. In the northwest, the Flan of the Yatil highlands absorbed most later migrants instead of being displaced.

## Canon Account
- **Antiquity:** Before the Migrations, the Flan held the Flanaess, with legendary early realms (the Ur-Flannae, Sulm, and others) and later Flan kingdoms such as Tenh and Geoff.
- **Appearance:** bronze to copper skin; dark, often wavy hair; dark eyes.
- **Faith:** the **Old Faith**, centered on Beory, the Oerth Mother, and kept by druids. Flan greater gods include **Beory, Nerull, Pelor, and Rao**, with Allitur, Berei, Obad-Hai, and others alongside. The Common Year weekday names come from the Flan calendar.
- **Language:** Flan survives as a living tongue near the Yatils. In Perrenland it also survives inside **Stamtaal**, a Common–Flan creole.
- **Northwest:** During the Migrations, the warlike Flan tribes of the Yatil Mountains absorbed most of the Oeridian, Suel, and Baklunish newcomers coming through the Wyrm's Tail pass. Some Flan tribes were still driven from the lowlands by Oeridian clans. Perrenland's population today is mainly of mixed Oeridian and Flan descent.

## Contradictions
- **The Ur-Flannae** are sometimes treated as a separate, older, sinister people, and sometimes as a Flan sorcerer-caste. **Stance:** unresolved; we do not use them yet.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- **Flan chieftain-barrows** (homebrew): our Clatspur barrows, with beast-packs bound by binding objects, are a Sidebar Press invention inspired by Flan antiquity and the Old Faith's tie to the land and its beasts. See [[The-Hound-Mother-of-the-Dreadverge]].
- **Art direction:** Flan figures such as [[Kethra-the-Hound-Mother]] should read bronze-skinned with dark hair, and their grave goods should be green bronze, amber, and boar-tusk. Do not paint them as Celtic-red-haired by default.
- Flan words decay into Perrender place-names through Stamtaal; *Dreadverge* is one example.
