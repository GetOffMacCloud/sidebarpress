# Istus

> Istus, the Lady of Our Fate, is the Baklunish greater goddess of fate, destiny, divination, the future and honesty. She is neutral, aloof even from her own pantheon, and weaves every life on her spindle. She appears as a crone, a haughty noblewoman or a cold young maiden.

## Canon Account
- **Titles:** Lady of Our Fate, Weaver of Our Fate, the Colorless and All-Colored.
- **Alignment and rank:** neutral; greater goddess.
- **Portfolio:** fate, destiny, divination, predestination, the future, honesty.
- **Symbol:** a golden spindle with three strands.
- **The Spindle:** while she holds it she can reach any time or place. If she loses it, she must return home at once.
- **Plane:** the Web of Fate. *On Hallowed Ground* places it in the Outlands. The 1e plane is unconfirmed.
- **Worship:** she is honoured across the Baklunish West (Ekbir, Ket, Tusmit, Zeif, the Paynims) and by both nomad nations, the [[Chakyik]] and the [[Wolf-Nomads|Wegwiur]]. Her eastern shrines are in trade cities: Dyvers, Greyhawk, Rauxes, Rel Mord and Stoink.
- **Clergy:** few, stoic and cynical. They divine for nobles, value honesty, and teach acceptance of one's fate. About 80% are women. They wear grey or black robes, with web patterns for senior ranks, show the holy symbol openly, and keep their hair at least six inches long. Rites use gauze hangings, incense, woodwinds, chanting and meditation. *These are mostly 2e/3e details.*
- **Relations:** aloof from all. Rudd dislikes her because Rudd opposes predestination.

## Contradictions
- **Plane:** the Outlands (2e), a pocket universe, or an Ethereal demiplane, depending on the account. **Stance:** the Web of Fate. Leave its location unstated.
- **Tiger Nomad worship** is explicit only in the LGG (591). Having her honoured by Chakyik shamans in 576 is a reasonable extrapolation, not a 1983 statement.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- Mother Sarnai in [[SP-PBS-01-The-False-Arrow]] is a shaman of Istus. Her thread, cloth and loom imagery fits the spindle canon. Keep "Istus weaves" to the two uses already in the text.
- A follower of Istus values honesty. A lie told to Sarnai is an offence against her goddess, not just against her.
