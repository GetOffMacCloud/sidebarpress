# Chakyik (Tiger Nomads)

> Baklunish horse nomads of the prairie north of the Yatils, between the Dramidj Ocean and the Fler river. Their capital is Yecha. In 576 CY they are ruled by Ilkhan Cligir, "the Unvanquishable Tiger Lord."

## Canon Account
- **Origins:** the Tiger Nomads left the Relentless Horde and formed their own nation after the death of Kha-Khan Ogobanuk in 345 CY.
- **Lands:** the prairie between the Dramidj Ocean and the Fler; the Burneal Forest to the north-west; the Yatils to the south.
- **Capital:** **Yecha**, a hill town in the Yecha Hills behind earth-and-timber walls. Population 3,800 (576 CY), 4,100 (591 CY).
- **Rulers:** **Ilkhan Cligir** (576 CY sources), who rebuffed Iuz's advances. By 591 CY he is dead and **Ilkhan Gajtak**, a fighter/illusionist, rules.
- **The art of beguilement:** by Ogobanuk's decree, both the Tiger ilkhan and the Wolf tarkhan study illusion — a ruler who cannot deceive his enemies is unfit to lead a free people.
- **War:** lightly armored cavalry, unmatched as mounted archers, with horn bows and lances. Women make up a higher share of the fighters than among other nomads.
- **Neighbors:** they trade with and raid Ekbir and Perrenland; the **Wolf Nomads (Wegwiur)** raid them periodically. The Wolves are known for keeping their word more faithfully than the Tigers, and for being easily offended.
- **Faith:** Istus, the Baklunish Lady of Fate, is central.
- **Language:** Baklunish; most do not speak Common.

## Contradictions
- **Who rules?** Cligir in 576 CY; Gajtak by 591 CY. Sidebar Press uses 576 CY, so Cligir.
