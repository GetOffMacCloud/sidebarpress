# The Battle of Emridy Meadows

> In 569 CY the forces of good routed the Horde of Elemental Evil in the field and then besieged and broke the Temple of Elemental Evil. Hommlet's church, inn and seven years of slow recovery all date from it.

## Canon Account
- The Temple's servants made Hommlet "and the lands for leagues around a mockery of freedom and beauty. Commerce ceased, crops withered; pestilence was abroad." Then "a great battle was fought," the evil was routed, and the Temple was besieged and destroyed (T1-4, PDF p. 6). T1-4 does not name the field.
- Later sources name the field **Emridy Meadows**, a series of grassy clearings south of the Velverdyva, named for the Emridy Run, a small stream that joins Imeryds Run from the west.
- The battle is dated **569 CY**. With the 576 CY baseline, the campaign present is seven years later.

## Contradictions
- **Where the field lies.** Sources put it "several leagues" or about 10 miles south of Verbobonc, northwest of Nulb, and near Hommlet. Those can't all be true, because T1-4 puts Hommlet about 90 miles from Verbobonc. See [[Viscounty-of-Verbobonc]].
- **What happened there.** Some fan histories argue the real fighting happened elsewhere along the Velverdyva and the meadows saw only skirmishing. We follow the published accounts.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- **Placement (SP-GS01 ruling, 2026-11-18):** 12 miles north of Hommlet, about 9 miles north-northwest of Nulb, along the Emridy Run. The grave mounds of the dead lie across it, and in SP-GS01 the Gluttonwretch roosts in a dead oak at its center.
- Hommlet's Church of St. Cuthbert and the Inn of the Welcome Wench both date from just after the battle.
