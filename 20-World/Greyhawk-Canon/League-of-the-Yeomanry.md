# League of the Yeomanry

> The Yeomanry League is a free, democratic state in the southwestern Sheldomar Valley, in a basin bounded by the Jotens to the north, the Crystalmists to the west and south, and the Little Hills to the east. Its capital is Loftwick. Its hills, the Little Hills, the Patchwork Hills and the Tors, are mined for silver and gems.

## Canon Account
- **Government:** a league of freeholders who elect spokesmen; capital at Loftwick.
- **Geography:** the Jotens, the Crystalmists, the Little Hills; the valley between the Little Hills and the Tors leads to the Hool Marshes. The Passage of Slerotin opens into the Yeomanry at the foot of the Crystalmists.
- **Peoples:** strongly Suel in descent (the Passage brought Suel refugees here after the Rain of Colorless Fire), with Oeridian and Flan blood; dwarves and halflings in the hills.
