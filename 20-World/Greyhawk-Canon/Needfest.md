# Needfest

> Needfest is the seven-day midwinter festival that begins the Common Year, between Sunsebb and Fireseek. Its fourth day is **Midwinter Night**, the winter solstice, also called the Dim Night, Dark Time and Handmaiden's Glory, when Luna is dark and only Celene shows in the sky.

## Canon Account
- **Needfest 1:** New Year's Day (Starday).
- **Needfest 4:** Midwinter Night (Godsday). Luna is new; only Celene is visible. Holy to druids and nature sects; clerics of Celestian keep all-night moonwatches.
- **Needfest 7:** the Feast of Fools in Greyhawk City (Freeday).
- Called the **Feast of Telchur** in the Bandit Kingdoms and Highfolk (see [[Telchur]]).
- Calendar context: [[The-Common-Year-Calendar]].
