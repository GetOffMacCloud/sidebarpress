# Passage of Slerotin

> The Passage of Slerotin is a vast tunnel beneath the Hellfurnaces, bored by the Suel mage Slerotin, "the last Mage of Power," after the Rain of Colorless Fire (-422 CY). Through it he led the surviving Suel tribes from the ruin of the Suel Imperium into the Sheldomar Valley, emerging in what is now the Yeomanry. He sealed it behind them, trapping one branch, the Lerara, inside.

## Canon Account
- Runs from the Sea of Dust under the Hellfurnaces to the Yeomanry at the foot of the Crystalmists, about 150 miles.
- Twelve (some say eighteen) tribes came through; the Lerara were trapped.
- Living Greyhawk places the Yeomanry town of **Dark Gate** near its mouth, with expeditions exploring it from 576 CY.

## Contradictions
- Living Greyhawk's Dark Gate and the C12 conversion's "Bleak Gate" both name the eastern mouth. **Stance:** Bleak Gate is the mouth (C12); Dark Gate is the nearest Yeomanry town.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- Candlecrown's founders came through the Passage with the apprentice who hid the curse-verse (homebrew).
- Aurel Vasch's master, Ilvan Dross, died at a sealed door in the Passage in Goodmonth 575 CY (homebrew), linking SP-GS02 to C12.
