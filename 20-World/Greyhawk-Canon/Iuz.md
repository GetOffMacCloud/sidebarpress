# Iuz the Old

> Cambion son of Iggwilv and Graz'zt, and a demigod. Zagig and five others imprisoned him beneath Castle Greyhawk from 505 to 570 CY. In 576 CY he has been free for six years, rules from Dorakaa, and is rebuilding: gathering his Boneheart and sending agents across the Flanaess. The Greyhawk Wars (582–584) are still ahead.

## Canon Account
**Before the imprisonment**
- He took control of a few hundred bandits in 479 CY and held the whole Land of Iuz within about a decade. Dorakaa, its Road of Skulls and its watchtowers were already built (WGR5 p. 3).

**Imprisonment (505–570)**
- He was imprisoned in the dungeons of Castle Greyhawk, "generally dated to 505 CY" (WGR5 p. 3). His captors were **Zagyg, St. Cuthbert, Heward, Murlynd, Keoghtom and Kelanen** (WGR5 p. 5).
- How he became a demigod is a secret. Zuggtmoy, Iggwilv, Graz'zt and Lolth are each said to have had a part, and so are the effects of Zagyg's magics in Castle Greyhawk (WGR5 p. 3).

**Release (570)**
- Robilar, Quij and Riggby freed him. Tenser, Bigby and Neb Retnar then fought him, and he fled to the Abyss (WGR5 p. 5; LGG p. 15).
- The backwash "caved in a large part of Castle Greyhawk's deepest dungeon complexes" (WGR5 p. 5).

**After his return**
- He killed those who had changed things in his absence and lengthened the Road of Skulls (LGG p. 62).
- He formed the **Boneheart**: a Greater and a Lesser circle, six spellcasters in each.
- "His agents began to scour the Flanaess, seeking arcane evils and relics" (WGR5 p. 3).
- He keeps a soul gem on a hidden Abyssal plane (WGR5 pp. 5–6).

**Epithets:** "the Old One," "Old Wicked" (WoG 1983).

**The priesthood**
- Rules by fear.
- Old-guard specialty priests gain *change self*, then *fear*, then *enervation* (WGR5 pp. 7, 83).
- Cult spells include *blackhand*, *bonechain* and *clawcloud* (WGR5 pp. 9–13).

**Agents abroad (the Boneshadow)**
- They are "relatively few in number and not overly powerful" (WGR5 p. 6).
- They work through hired hands and charmed servitors who don't know whom they serve (WGR5 pp. 89–90).

**What is *not* true in 576** (all post-War, WGR5 pp. 3–4):
- the Vatun ruse, and the conquests of Tenh and Stonefist;
- the fall of the Horned Society (582) and Iuz's rule of Molag;
- the Pact of Greyhawk (584), the Badlands and the Razing Line;
- post-War Boneheart posts and fiefs.

## Contradictions
- **Iuz free in 576?** The A Tourist In Greyhawk *Player Gazetteer* says he "has not yet returned." **Ruled (Greybeard): free since 570, per canon.**
- **The Wolf Nomads.** WGR5 p. 20 says they are "not at war with Iuz and never have been," and that the two sides trade and spy on each other. The vault's [[Wolf-Nomads]] note says they are hostile by 576 (Iuz claims their Howling Hills burial grounds) and crush Iuz's army at Blackwater Bend in 578 (*Dragon* #56). **Unresolved: Greybeard to rule.** The Shadow on the Quag uses the Wolf Nomad shore as Vesharan's road north either way.
- **Boneheart membership dates.** WGR5 lists the circles as of about 584. Members it says rose after the Wars (Null, Jumper, Xenvelen) are not used before 582. Other members' 576 rank is undated.

## Sidebar Press Stance
Deviations we endorse, with reasoning:

- **The Gaoler's Link (house relic; [[The Gaoler's Link]]).** One link of the fetter that held Iuz from 505 to 570. Its six sigils are the six imprisoners' wards (WGR5 p. 5).
  - **Provenance.** It was dug out of the 570 cave-in (WGR5 p. 5), sold to Greyhawk University in 573, and sent to Ritterstadt in 574. Canon names no such relic; WGR5 leaves the imprisonment's artifacts unspecified.
  - **Blind spot.** Divination fails within 30 feet of it. This mirrors the third ebon skull, which Iuz "cannot detect" (WGR5 p. 75).
  - **The burn.** It burns hands sworn to Iuz. This is house material, from St. Cuthbert's ward.
  - **Why we keep it.** It gives Iuz's relic-hunting (WGR5 p. 3) a target that low-level heroes can steal and protect. It touches no canon outcome: the Wars still happen.
- **Kermin Mind-Bender in the Greater Boneheart by 576** (ruled 2026-11-05). WGR5 p. 86 places him there around 584 and gives no date. He is not among the members it says rose after the Wars.
- **Tether-mages and bone tethers.** House material. They are built on Kermin's mind-control specialty and his recruitment of gifted children (WGR5 p. 86), and on the Boneshadow's use of charmed servitors (pp. 89–90).
- **Rival arms.** Panshazek's priest-spies in the Horned Society (WGR5 p. 88) and Kermin's mages compete for the same prizes. This draws on WGR5's agents hiding missions to keep the credit (pp. 75–76). Master Kezrin (SP-PBS-01) is ruled Panshazek's man.
