# Zuggtmoy

> Zuggtmoy, the Demon Queen of Fungi, is a demon lord of rot, decay and fungal growth. She was the Temple of Elemental Evil's patron alongside Iuz and was sealed beneath it when it fell.

## Canon Account
- A demon lord (demon queen) of fungi, mold and decay, served by fungal creatures and cultists (T1-4).
- Patron of the Temple of Elemental Evil with Iuz; her plans for the Flanaess and her uneasy alliance with Iuz are set out in the module's background (T1-4, PDF pp. 7, 29–30).
- Imprisoned beneath the Temple after its fall; her chambers lie in its deepest levels (T1-4, PDF pp. 76–81).
