# Viscounty of Verbobonc

> A small viscounty on the Velverdyva River between Furyondy and Veluna, ruled from the town of Verbobonc. Hommlet and Nulb lie on its south-eastern fringe, near the ruined Temple of Elemental Evil.

## Canon Account
- **The town of Verbobonc** stands on the Velverdyva; the Viscount rules the surrounding lands down to the Gnarley Forest and the Kron Hills.
- **Hommlet** is "some 30 leagues south-east of the town of Verbobonc, or thereabouts, on the fringe of the territory controlled by the noble Lord the Viscount of Verbobonc," at a crossroads, with the Velverdyva and the Lowroad to the north (T1-4, PDF p. 5).
- **Nulb** lies east of Hommlet on Imeryds Run, which has its only ford there; the Temple of Elemental Evil is in the hills nearby (T1-4, PDF pp. 6, 29).
- After the Temple's fall, Hommlet sent word to the Viscount when bandits rode again (T1-4, PDF p. 6).

## Contradictions
- **Hommlet to Nulb.** T1-4 says Nulb was "but 6 miles distant" (PDF p. 6). The same module's Map 1 and its Interlude make the trip a day and a half on foot, about 30 miles. The vault's T1 conversion follows Map 1: Hommlet–Nulb about 33 miles, Hommlet–Moathouse about 15, Moathouse–Nulb about 24.
- **Emridy Meadows.** Later sources place the battlefield "several leagues" or about 10 miles south of the town of Verbobonc and northwest of Nulb. That can't sit alongside Hommlet being 30 leagues (about 90 miles) from Verbobonc and the meadows lying near Hommlet. See [[The-Battle-of-Emridy-Meadows]].

## Sidebar Press Stance
- **The T1 conversion** uses Map 1's distances (about 33 miles to Nulb).
- **SP-GS01** uses T1-4's own "but 6 miles" (PDF p. 6) and places Emridy Meadows 12 miles north of Hommlet (about 9 miles north-northwest of Nulb), with Verbobonc far to the northwest, as T1-4 says. Greybeard ruling, 2026-11-18. Nobody travels to Nulb in SP-GS01, so the two products only disagree on paper.
