# Zilchus

> Zilchus, the Great Guildmaster, is the Oeridian god of power, prestige, money, business and influence. He is lawful neutral. His clergy are ruthless but scrupulously honest dealmakers, and rulers listen to them.

## Canon Account
- **Titles:** the Great Guildmaster, the Money Counter, Lord of Coin.
- **Alignment:** lawful neutral.
- **Portfolio:** power, prestige, money, business, influence.
- **Symbol:** hands clutching a bag of gold.
- **Plane:** the Outlands, at the Marketplace Eternal (Seat of Luxury).
- **Appearance:** a plain, well-dressed, middle-aged Oeridian man with a gold purse, and a flail "to chastise the dishonest."
- **Family:**
  - Brother of Kurell, from whom he is estranged.
  - Husband of Sotillion.
  - Allies: Rao and Heironeous.
  - Rival: Xerbo.
- **Dogma:** master your own desire for wealth so you can exploit it in others. Politics and war are trade, with words or lives as the currency.
- **Clergy:** they serve guilds, merchants and states. Rites use incense, small offerings of goods, and sermons. Favoured weapon: the dagger.
- **Perrenland:** the LGG lists Zilchus among Perrenland's faiths (p. 85). In Living Greyhawk he is worshipped alongside the Old Kerk but is not part of it. The fan *Oerth Journal* #5 gives him a "large following among merchants."

## Contradictions
- None found.
- Note that his status as **state religion of Ahlissa and Dyvers** comes after the Greyhawk Wars (591 CY). It does not apply in 576.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- Merchants' witnesses and factors in Perrenland keep Zilchus. A Zilchite witness is valued because he records everything exactly and cannot be bought. That makes him dangerous to anyone with a secret debt. Used for Benedikt Roos in [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]].
