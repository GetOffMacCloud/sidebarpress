# Nerull

> Nerull, the Reaper, is the ancient Flan greater god of death, darkness, murder, and the underworld. He is neutral evil. His titles include Foe of All Good, Hater of Life, Bringer of Darkness, King of All Gloom, and Reaper of Flesh. He sees death as slaughter, not release, and every living thing is an affront to him.

## Canon Account
- **Origin:** Flan. He is a greater god of the Flan pantheon alongside Beory, Pelor, and Rao, and is also called **Tharoth** among the Baklunish.
- **Symbol and weapons:** a skull with a scythe or sickle. The favored weapon is the scythe; clerics also use the sickle, dagger, and quarterstaff.
- **Plane:** Carceri (Tarterian Depths).
- **Worship:** Services are held in **full darkness**, with a litany glorifying death and suffering. Sacrifice of the living is common. Temples are hidden even where he is honored, and are often stocked with undead.
- **Clergy:** dress in **rust-red or black**. They commit murder as offerings, and when discovered they **flee and move far away, appearing innocent and killing wayfarers along the road**.
- **Legacy:** Kyuss, the Wormgod and demigod of undead, began as a priest of Nerull in the Flan kingdom of Sulm.

## Contradictions
- **Is Nerull ever appeased?** *Greyhawk Adventures*-era text says even in war and plague, **common folk do not try to appease the Reaper**. Perrenland's entry (see [[Perrenland]]) says the Old Kerk counts Nerull among its gods and that **shrines to appease him are accepted**. **Stance:** both are true. Most of the Flanaess shuns him; Perrenland's Flan-rooted Old Kerk is a regional exception. Even there, *active* worship of the Reaper (murder, sacrifice, raising the dead) is heresy.
- **4e:** the Raven Queen myth is not Greyhawk canon. Ignore it.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- Nerullite clergy in our adventures follow the canon pattern of **wandering murderers who appear innocent**. [[Brother-Ashlock]] in [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]] is a textbook case. He also wears grey (a Wee Jas mendicant's color) instead of Nerull's rust-red or black, as a deliberate disguise.
- The sickle is the giveaway weapon: a cleric PC recognizes it with Religion DC 10.
