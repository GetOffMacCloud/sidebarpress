# Canton of Clatspurgen

> The easternmost canton of [[Perrenland]], covering the Perrender valleys and lower slopes of the [[Clatspur-Range]]. It was the nation's first canton (c. 400 CY) and is known for its highland longbowmen.

## Canon Account
- **Core canon (LGG):** Clatspurgen is one of Perrenland's eight cantons. It and Traft field Perrenland's longbowmen and bow-armed cavalry.
- **Living Greyhawk campaign detail (RPGA, 2000–2008):**
  - First canton, formed in 400 CY at independence.
  - Population about 15,000 urban and 25,000 rural, mostly of the **Roodberg** clan.
  - Represented by the hereditary **Phalzgraf** of the Roodberg.
  - Highland **Vosser** clansmen refuse to recognize the Phalzgraf, which is technically illegal.
  - There is talk of an elvish independence movement near the Kershane Pass.
  - Wealth comes from mining, furs, agriculture, manufacturing, and trade; it is called the richest canton.
  - Places include Clarstadt (fishing village), Tielemannschlauss (temple-town of Ulaa below Mount Sentvoor), and Visthaven (Sub-Canton of Kerhaven).

## Contradictions
None known between editions. The tension is between core canon (a thinly described canton) and Living Greyhawk's detailed regional play.

## Sidebar Press Stance
- Use the LGG core freely. Use Living Greyhawk detail (Roodberg, Vosser, Phalzgraf) as optional color, and cite it as LG when we do.
- **Waycombe** ([[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]]) sits in a high Clatspur valley. That puts it naturally among the Vosser highlanders. A future pass could make Odila's levy dispute part of the Vosser–Phalzgraf quarrel. This is not used yet.
- Our "cantonal council of Clatspurgen" and "levy-office" are generic house terms. If we adopt LG, the levy is owed to the Phalzgraf.
