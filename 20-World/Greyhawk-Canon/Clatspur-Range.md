# Clatspur Range

> The Clatspurs are a short arm of the Yatil Mountains that cuts Perrenland off from the realms to the east. They lie between the Vesve Forest and Perrenland's eastern cantons on Lake Quag. Perrenders live in most of the valleys and on the lower slopes.

## Canon Account
- **Position:** Small arms of the Yatils, between the Vesve Forest (east) and the cantons on Lake Quag (west). A great valley called the **Quagflow** marks the southern end. The **Kershane Pass** and the **Velverdyva River** separate the range from the Yatils to the south.
- **Settlement:** Perrenders occupy most of the valleys and lower slopes, and these communities make up the [[Canton-of-Clatspurgen]]. The peaks hold valuable minerals, but few people mine them.
- **Roads:** The **High Gallery** runs through the Clatspur ridge from Traft until it meets the Quag Road. On the way it crosses the Velverdyva canyon on an old stone bridge called the **Witch's Hinge**.
- **Beyond the ridges:** The Vesve Forest, and past it the lands of **Iuz**.
