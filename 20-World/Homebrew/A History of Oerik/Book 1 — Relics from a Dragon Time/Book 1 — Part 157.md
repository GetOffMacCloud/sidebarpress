## Part 157

### 770 SD

Miska the Wolf Spider was bitterly disappointed. Where were the lizard people? Lolth had returned alone, larger and fatter. Had she eaten them all?

Lolth admitted that she had eaten up a lot of them. They were tasty, and she was always hungry.

Becoming a god had suited Miska. Lolth could see that he had grown larger and fatter too. His wolf head, charcoal grey with burning embers for eyes, had never looked more fierce and noble. The hill people fawned on him. Lolth was jealous of that, and she knew that she was supposed to have the lizard people worshipping her.

She hated the lizard people. They were crude, cold and loathsome. They didn't deserve her. Lolth should have proper followers, not animals.

Lolth decided to live off her brother's success and she titled herself the Mistress of the Sheldar.

Miska determined to send Lolth back into the marshes. Do your job!

Lolth resisted her brother. They could be gods together!

Miska called Lolth a lazy fool, and then the fight was on. Neither of the gargantuan spider demons could seriously hurt each other but they certainly tried. Lolth's webs were ineffective on the Wolf Spider, who brushed them off as if they were wet rags, and Miska's venom-tipped fangs couldn't penetrate Lolth's armored exoskeleton. They rolled about their cave back and forth, screeching and cursing.

When at last they broke away Lolth threatened to summon Mother. She would fix him up! Miska was unimpressed. Do it then! So Lolth did. She hunkered down in the middle of the dank cave and hummed a chant.

An eldritch glow rose from the floor of the cave, coalescing into the slug-shaped, gargantuan Queen of Chaos.
