## Part 149

One cloud giant listened particularly attentively as Gaxx explained the plan.

She was pink-skinned and her white hair was long and brushed her broad shoulders. Her fangs were small, but her tongue was constantly running over them as if she found them annoying.

The Ring of Gaxx had been created using horrid ingenuity and the acquiring of rare magicks.

With the power of the magical Moonstone, gifted by the witless brownie, Princess Gossermay, the Ring would have the power to open a hole between the Fey and Oerth universes.

Now, Gaxx suggested that the question was to decide where to place the hole.

The fire and frost giants began yelling vociferously. Bring the hole over the land of C'mu Ni'er. Bring down the Tuatha de Dannan!

The scarlet, shaggy Gaxx, far smaller than the giants, walked straight up to a fire giant then kicked him hard in the ankle.

"Idiots!" sneered Gaxx, "Oafs! Now listen to me! We all hate the Tuatha de Dannan; we all want to see them burn! But they are strong, and they are much smarter than you lot. Their crafty magic would leave you all dangling on stakes. No! We are going to do something clever."

Gaxx held up his Ring. Its powers were myriad. Gaxx had used it to pinpoint veins of Oerth magic, found deep within that world.

Oerik was a particularly potent continent of Oerth magic. The Good Hills in the Sheldomar Valley were poorly defended and populated by ignorant halflings and foolish gnomes.

"What are halflings?" asked one of the cloud giants, moving forward and pushing the female cloud giant back slightly.

"Runts!" stated Gaxx simply.

He waved the Ring. Once the hills were secured, the Ring of Gaxx would harness the Oerth magic and open up a direct portal to C'mu Ni'er. The giants would then battle the Tuatha de Dannan, but would have a protected base on the other side of the world.

"This will serve two strategies," continued Gaxx. "One will be the destruction of the Tuatha de Dannan and the sacking of C'mu Ni'er, the other..." Gaxx grinned, "a staging post within the central portion of eastern Oerik, giving Gruumsh and his orc armies a strategic foothold. And once he has overrun the Flanaess, each giant general will be given their own vast realm."

The giants cheered.

The cloud giants were chosen to lead the assault. They proudly left the conference to ready their Battle Clouds.

However, one giant chose to teleport directly into the Tuatha de Dannan empire itself.

The giant was spotted straight away by a wood elf, who raised her ram horn to sound an alarm.

"Do not fear me, sister," said the cloud giant, and before the startled elf, the giant shrank and morphed into a golden-haired Tuatha de Dannan.

The wood elf bowed, tears in her eyes, for this lady before her was the great Caowyn.

Caowyn marched through the Black Forest, the home of the Royal House.

The guards bowed reverently as she passed.

Meeting her peers, Caowyn told of the sinister plots of Gaxx. The Tuatha de Dannan would be ready!
