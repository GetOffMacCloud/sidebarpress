## Part 53

### 189 SD

The jann paid a high price for their dalliances with the Suel.

Skin cracked and opened up into ulcerating sores, eyes seeped pus, teeth crumbled and bones dissolved to sand. Who was punishing them so?

It was their own gods, the Elemental Princes, hurling punishments upon their followers in righteous wrath.[^1]

How dare the jann, the Holy Jann, holders of the Material Elements, betray their heritage and inheritance?

Those jann who had not lapsed watched their forsaken brothers and sisters suffer and did nothing. The Suel watched on in terror.

One in twelve jann succumbed in this way. No magic could Cure or Raise them, for the Elemental Princes had no mercy.

Alas, the jann were not free! Their fate during the long Dragon Wars in the Lost Age led them to be bonded with the elements for evermore.

Bound in ritual and worship as part of the Celestial Covenant, their destiny was as unbreakable as their shadestone.

Across the mountains far to the northeast, their kin, the Wind Dukes loved the order and law and grew strong in their righteous Observance.

Oh pity the jann! Their path on Oerth had led them into Questioning their required Place. They were vulnerable to moral decay and physical destruction.

The Suel of Highhold were not passed by without consequence either. A series of earthquakes brought down a mountain, crushing thousands. Great tempests brought floods followed by lightning storms that ignited fires in the villages and farms.

The beautiful bastille of Risewater tumbled down, followed by storms and fires that left death and devastation in long corridors of destruction.

[^1]: Their beliefs, not mine!
