## Part 159

Nerull and Incabulos climbed up the hill towards the cave mouth.

They could feel the crackling magical atmosphere and wanted it for themselves. Imagine the rest of the tribe when they emerged from the cave, endowed with special powers!

But Incabulos was thinking about what might happen when they met the gods who dwelt within. Was it really likely that they would praise and reward the boys? Nerull said that they would be showered with gifts, given secret magic and powerful weapons.

They reached the entrance and Incabulos pulled at Nerull's shoulder. Wait a bit, what if the gods were angry at the intrusion?

Nerull went white, then flattened himself against the hillside.

His bravery was fading.

<center>* * *</center>

Inside the cave the Queen of Chaos could smell the presence of Sheldar humans. They all knew that approaching her cave was forbidden. Who would dare?

The Queen dragged her bulky mass to the cave opening then peered out. She saw nothing.

<center>* * *</center>

The two boys ran as fast as they could back to the lake. Had she seen them? Out of breath, they blundered into the Headman, who grew angry. Silly children! He turned away from them then returned to the doings of the ceremony.

Nerull and Incabulos looked at each other and smiled. They knew that the scolding of the Headman was nothing. They had almost met a god!

Nerull was determined that next time he wouldn't let his friend put him off. He wouldn't falter; he would march confidently up the hill in his warrior headdress and the gods would see that he was a strong, great man, destined to shape the world to his desire.

Incabulos could come with him if he behaved.

<center>* * *</center>

The Queen of Chaos went back into the cave then lumped herself over her puce throne, settling herself down for a slumber.

In the gloomiest recess of the cave something rustled. The Scroll of Vile Darkness felt restless. It had sensed the two boys and they had made the Scroll shiver. It was a confronting feeling: fear.
