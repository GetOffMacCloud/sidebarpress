## Part 118

"I hear you," whispered Moradin.

A brilliant wave of energy flashed in the synapses of Pious Grimm Quartztusk's tormented brain. All the suffering and deprivation that had turned him numb and unaware was blasted away.

He remembered who he was, and where he was. So many years lost!

Quartztusk stood up. His original legs were gone, replaced with artificial clockwork ones. He couldn't recall much about that; he seemed to remember an alchemist and a gnome artificer who owned him for a little while...

There were seven eggs with him in the chamber, moldy yellow and black, and each about four feet in diameter.

The voice inside his head, Moradin, told him that each contained a great evil that would blight Oerth.

Quartztusk touched an egg. Its shell was soft and springy yet tough. He punched it and a dent appeared that quickly returned back into shape.

Quartztusk looked about the chamber for any weapon he could use. He pried out a large loose stone in a wall and tried to pierce the egg. It barely caused a scratch.

Then he remembered his clockwork legs. He turned the key in his hip then leapt up and kicked the egg. The force managed to crack it. Black ooze ran out.

Two more kicks smashed it completely. The ooze steamed sickly, radiating a musty stench.

It clung to his clockwork legs and ran down onto the floor.

Quartztusk repeated the action on the next egg and then the next.

The remaining eggs somehow sensed the danger and each began to wobble and vibrate.

The eggs were hatching!

Quartztusk managed to destroy the fourth egg before a bloated thing inside pushed itself out. But now the fifth, sixth and seventh hatchlings were free! Covered in black, stinking ooze, not yet fully formed, barely blobs with stumpy arms and legs, they let out piercing shrieks.

The shrieks reached the ears of their mother wandering the dungeon level above.

"I hear you," whispered the Queen of Chaos.
