## Part 147

Heward strained to resist stuffing food down the throats of Gumish and Gaxx.

The two scarlet-headed horrids were supposedly here on peaceful business, but how condescending they were! Heward was a Freeberger; he deserved respect. After all, the majority of the people in the district loved him.

"Once again, our apologies, dear little human, for accosting you in such a manner in our chamber," said Gumish.

"Yes," said Gaxx, "after all, you yourself admitted to being a robber."

Princess Gossermay looked askance. She wanted the horrids and Heward to kiss and make up. She could see that Heward was seething.

"Not just a robber, but a Robber King!" Gossermay tried to break the ice. She continued: "Our Heward here is a real rogue; his escapades have even aroused the personal attention of Queen Titania, or so it has been said by her Consort, Oberon!"

Lathia the nymph glanced at Heward with a concerned expression. She knew that Heward had a great sense of humor as long as it wasn't about him. She braced for an outburst.

Heward managed to control himself, although he felt slighted by everyone. Did he deserve such jibes? The feast was a complete waste of time. He didn't trust the horrids, and he no longer trusted the Princess. She was either naive or corrupt. He could not imagine a genuine reconciliation between the two Fey worlds.

The horrids' pale green eyes betrayed their contempt. They glared at everyone at the table, and looked at the sylph, grigg, brownie, pixie and nymph servers as if they were rabble.

The horrids reminded Heward of the goblins in his old home in the Flanaess. Their expressions looked like that too, when they plotted to bring down Good.

Princess Gossermay looked up with excitement. Here came the next course, juniper souffle. A sweet dessert would bring sweetness to the conversation.

Unfortunately, one of the brownies sneezed as she put down Gaxx's souffle. The souffle collapsed with a sigh.

Gaxx did not take this well. He raised his clawed hand to strike the foolish waitress. Gumish grabbed Gaxx's arm and hurriedly brought it down. This was not yet the time for violence.

Heward noticed a flash of light emanating off one of Gaxx's fingers as Gumish grabbed him.

Aha! Gaxx was wearing a ring, beautifully wrought and bejeweled.

Perhaps deep down Heward was a robber after all, for at that moment Heward knew he wanted the ring.
