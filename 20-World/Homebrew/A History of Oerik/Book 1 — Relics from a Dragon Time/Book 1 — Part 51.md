## Part 51

### 178 SD

If you were to walk west along the southern border of the Sulhauts you would, after many hundreds of miles, reach the end of plains and discover a great chain of mountains blocking the western route completely.

Stretching southeast is another mountain range and yet another range extends south. These chains almost completely encircle the basin now known as the Sea of Dust.

178 SD, three large civilizations occupy this basin, two Suel and one elvish. The elven Singing Lands occupy the eastern part nestled right up to the foot of the Hellfurnaces.

Across rivers and a lake to the west lies the Holy Papacy of Risewater consisting of farmlands, temples, villages and a few towns.

Further west extending to the mountain barriers is the Highhold of Emeraldrock, militarily dominating their mines and trading centers. Hungry for wealth, Emeraldrock traded with the Sulhaut dwarves, Risewater (when not at war) and even the Singing Lands.

All the Suloise states shunned magic, so consequently it took much physical exploration to find a viable pass through the western mountain barrier called Darkwall.

A route took Suel explorers to the southwest, where they were stunned to see before them a beautifully laid out city full of domed roofs and spires of unknown stone, shining in the light in a myriad of shades.

The people of that city were tall and were of varying skin types: bronze, pale blue, dusky brown and translucent white. They spoke a language unknown to the east and rode huge black camels.

The inhabitants welcomed the Suel to their home as guests. Trade was set up and within months, bright stone and black camels from the city enriched the lands of Emeraldrock.
