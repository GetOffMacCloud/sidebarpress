## Part 88

Pious Grimm Quartztusk was incredulous. This strange-looking grey dwarf, Turiziz (the Fane of Bore, whatever that meant) was threatening the destruction of his people with just one band of troops.

Did they not understand their dwur kin? The mountain dwarves of the Crystalmists were implacable on their own turf. Pious considered giving the signal to launch his Hammers upon the duergar rabble but... was he underestimating them?

Corrupted they might be, but the duergar were still dwur. They had survived in darkness, bathed in arcane radiation. And this "Fane" looked so calm, his eyes meeting his own with an even steadiness.

Pious composed himself. He was the High Steward because he could make reasoned, considered decisions, unlike his predecessor, Silvervein.

Unfortunately, even considered decisions could be bad ones.

Finally Pious spoke.

"I cannot seriously consider your proposal to submit. Your people have stolen the Fierce Axe, despite us offering you succor. You should return it from the Underdark where you hid it, or... or we will rightly launch bloody vengeance upon thee."

The duergar emissary shrugged, his brilliant cape glowing in the moonlight.

"WAR IT BE," he intoned, then Turiziz turned about and led his kin down the road and away from the city. Pious watched them disappear into the darkness.

<center>* * *</center>

Days went by and nothing more from the duergar was heard. Meantime his hold on the Crystalmists was slipping. The Moradinson cousins, Ironcheek and Copperhelm, had consolidated enough support to occupy their own sections of Grawn.

To remove them now would be bloody, and that was not what Pious wanted. The two cousins were jostling for ascendancy, but neither had turned to violence. Grawn was an angry, divided city, full of robust debate and insult, a volcano gurgling and rumbling, but sleeping still.

Pious Grimm Quartztusk ordered his palace to be further fortified, then worked with advisors on a new plan to retrieve the Fierce Axe.
