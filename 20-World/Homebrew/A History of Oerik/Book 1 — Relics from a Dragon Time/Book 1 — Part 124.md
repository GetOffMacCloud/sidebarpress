## Part 124

### 634–635 SD

The Flan mariners who plied their trade across the Solnor Sea between the eastern coast of Oerik and Mystara and surrounding islands were unhappy with the increasing presence of Thytic galleys from the far west.

The Flan did not sail south to the MaiYuan empire because those people of Hepmonaland were hostile to ships and utilized dimensional magic in their trading with Kara-Tur on the other side of Oerth. The pragmatic Thytics, however, were willing to pay through the teeth if it meant a new trading network. They were willing to spend a lot because they knew ultimately it would lead to great wealth, as they had found from opening up the passage through the dragon lands of the Infinite.

By 634 SD Thytic-controlled harbors dotted the coastlines of Oerik, Mystara, Kodgeland, northern Hepmonaland and countless islands including the large Isle Oeridia located off the coast directly south of the Singing Lands.

In the summer of that year a Flannish warfleet sponsored by several petty kingdoms launched attacks on Thytic ships and their harbors. This was reasonably successful and Thytics were largely erased from the Flanaess and Mystara (though a fair number of Thytics were able to hold out in the Minrothad Islands to the Mystaran south, becoming the origin of the later Thyatis Empire).

As the distance between northwestern Oerik and southeastern Oerik was so great the Thytics were unable to launch any retaliatory strikes. Instead they fortified their western holdings and began building a shipping industry in the newly conquered Oeridia, sending laborers there in numbers amongst the indigenous human Oeridian and kuo-toan fish people tribes.
