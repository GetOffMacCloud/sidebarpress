## Part 30

### −21 SD

Amongst the broad trees of the southern Adri Forest (now part of the Grandwood), a majestic unicorn dug about in the topsoil for delicious dandelion roots. Then, it quickly raised its head, the horn jutting out of its forehead shining a brilliant magenta. Something bad was around, but where...

A great brown, barreling beast, armored and fanged and muscular, burst up from under the unicorn and dragged it down with its shark-like jaws. A bulette had made itself a lair in the forest and had decided that unicorns were most delicious.

Heward, who was checking a rabbit trap, saw the eruptive scene and was incensed. Unicorns were sacred animals and Heward decided that the horrible monster must have ascended from the Underworld. The young Flan warrior went off and found his friends, the brilliant Win-Daled and the silvery elven mage Ye'Cind. Heward told them what he saw and the three friends resolved to hunt down the bulette.

Some nearby pixies had seen the monster emerge from the ground and slake its thirst at a nearby creek. It had only just burrowed down back into the earth. The pixies showed them the spot, then flew up into the trees to watch the coming excitement.

Ye'Cind could see the dirt mound created by the bulette. Heward was all for diving under the ground after it, but Win-Daled thought that a really stupid idea. Instead they tracked around until they found a couple of unicorns nibbling plants on a shady hillock. Ye'Cind wanted to warn the unicorns away, but Win-Daled thought it better to wait. Ye'Cind said that was callous and began neighing in unicorn fashion and stamping the ground.

Heward felt a rumble traveling underneath his feet and drew his sword, but the bulette moved past him and burst up underneath Ye'Cind, throwing her into the air. She tumbled to the ground about twenty yards away and as she spat blood out of her mouth she thought she saw a great flash emanate from Win-Daled that was immediately followed by a loud crack. Bits of bulette rained everywhere.
