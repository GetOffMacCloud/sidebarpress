## Part 131

Once Upon a Time vast glittering dragons ruled the skies and the Oerth and the seas.

Great Dragon Empires vied with each other for supremacy.

There were few beings who could rival the Dragon Lords, but in the deep places, perhaps...

Deep down in the dreamlike depths of the deepest oceans were oceanic trenches.

Great rifts gouged the sea floor, creating networks of underwater caverns and tunnels inhabited by whale-like leviathans, oozing aboleth, fish-like kopru - and the krakens.

The krakens were hyper-intelligent titanic octopi who resented the dragons with their pretensions that they ruled all of Oerth.

Oh ho! exclaimed the krakens. Did the flying lizards really think that they were the Masters? Compared to us, their brains are walnuts!

The genius minds of the krakens, with some assistance by the aboleth, harnessed weird magicks now long forgotten, and begat the illithids.

The illithids, later known to their victims as mind flayers, were octopus-headed, two-legged creatures with vast brains and knowledge.

The illithids rose to the surface, hell-bent on dominating the dragons and heralding a new era of octopoid supremacy.

But what was this white brilliance that bleached the surface in heat and light?

It was the Sun, answered the dragons, Great Sol bringing Life!

The illithids were dismayed. They had formed in the inky depths of the abyss of the Oerth; they could not stand the light!

They skulked at night and hid in caves during the day. They could not overthrow the Sun.

Most illithids left Oerth and conquered dim alien worlds and formed their own empires.

Dragons were still the Masters.

<center>* * *</center>

The illithids had failed.

What was to be done now? The krakens found Religion.

The Elder Gods, those Unknowable Supreme Beings of the Primal times, the times before Oerth was made, they were to be the krakens' salvation.

The Elder Gods were alien and inscrutable, yet the krakens did find themselves endowed with great magic and enormous strength. Would they soon emerge from the waters of the deep and lay waste to the Dragon Empires?

Another attribute was gifted to them, however: Wisdom.

Suddenly the krakens realized that for all their physical power and awesome magical might, they no longer cared for conquest. What were the dragons to them but beautiful scaled animals just trying to get through life like everyone else?

Much better looking inwards, discovering your truth, your world, your Universe.

And then, with a collective inrush of oxygen-rich water, the krakens let go their newly acquired strength and their formidable magicks.

It was time to accept their natural Place.

<center>* * *</center>

All that power was not actually lost to Oerth, for slowly coalescing into existence at the bottom of the world there formed a pearl, a black pearl.

And there it rested for the next eighty thousand years.
