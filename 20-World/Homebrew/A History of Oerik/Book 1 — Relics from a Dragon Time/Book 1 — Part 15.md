## Part 15

> A nutritionally good diet needs to absorb diverse sentient brains. Breeding for different thoughts produces variety which adds to Mindfulness in the literal sense.
>
> For example, I have cultivated a breed of thrall that I call "block-whites." They tend to be largish and the strain has albinism running through it. This albinism adds a delectable tang of apricot to the flavor notes of these creatures.
>
> My good friend Master Thraxlbid has created a breed called "systematic brights" that tend to be of slighter build and brownish in coat. Full of healthy insights, these creatures are delicately flavored and add a smokiness when admixed but are a trifle astringent when devoured singularly (but dietary composition can change the quantity and quality; see Volume 2, page 476).
>
> Devouring a block-white with a systematic bright blends the flavor notes beautifully, and leaves a lingering finish upon the palate.
>
> I have also found my thoughts sharpened and my psionics strengthened after partaking of this particular admixture.
>
> — *Thought Effusions of Grand Experimenter Gybex*, Volume 2, page 3482
