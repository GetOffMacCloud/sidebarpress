## Part 121

### 606 SD

The Thytic mariners who plied their seacraft along the northern coastline of western Oerik were bound by the Hyborian ice to the north, the glaciers to the east and the deadly land of the Infinite to the west.

Making it through the western passage would open up the ability to sail south then east from the bottom of the continent into eastern Oerik, north into the Densac Gulf, east across the Tilva Strait that separates Oerik from Hepmonaland, then either north to the Flannaes or further eastward to Kodgeland and Mystara, even south into the tropical islands of the Pearl Sea.

The Thytics were not a particularly magical people and few had the knowledge to create dimensional gates, so finding a way through the dragon haven of the Infinite was essential if they wished to access the combined riches of multiple continents. Unfortunately the dragon turtles always sank any ships that entered their territory.

Jargon the buccaneer was the first sailor to negotiate a deal[^1] that allowed him and his crew to cross the Infinite waters for the first time in history. They then mapped the western coast, the southern coastline and made contact with the MaiYuan empire of Hepmonaland and the Flan of eastern Oerik.

[^1]: The factual details of the deal are lost to time but dragon turtles of today swear it included a list of items including forty life-sized gold elephants, seven hundred virgins, seventy bejeweled chariots, seven magic wands and the fabled Crown of the Kraken. Unlikely, I know, but who am I to argue with dragon turtles?
