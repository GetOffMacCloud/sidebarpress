## Part 153

### 753–768 SD

The fire and frost giants continued crunching through the Crystalmists.

Without General Kavoss, the giants lost discipline and were soon breaking up into several competing groups. The dwarves of the mountains, having initially struggled against the giants, were able to defend their heartlands with some of the pressure off, but a few clans left to join their brethren in the Sulhauts.

The frost giants merged with ogres and indigenous hill giants and grabbed some of the highest and coldest peaks. The fire giants moved southward into the Hellfurnaces where they warred and raided the Singing Ones to the south, but mostly warred and raided themselves. Three fire giant kingdoms were established by 764 SD.

The cloud giant army that attacked the Tuatha de Dannan and Celenians in the Good Hills was eventually repulsed. The cloud giants returned to their War Clouds and, although some sailed back to C'mu Ne'er, most chose to settle upon the various great mountain ranges across eastern Oerik, including the Griffs, the Lortmils, the Crystalmists, the Yatils and the Corusks.

Once the battles were over and it was clear that the plot to rip a hole between Oerth and the Fey Lands was foiled, Caowyn ordered her sisters to return to C'mu Ne'er. The Tuatha de Dannan left as mysteriously as they had arrived. By 755 SD the gnomes and halflings had reestablished their homes as if nothing had happened, except for a few new stories for the grandchildren.

Heward and Lathia chose to stay in the Flanaess. Heward was glad to be back in the Adri Forest after so many centuries.

Lathia found the Blemu elves agreeable people, though she thought often of Fey. She would stay with Heward for now.

Lathia was pleased that quite a few Fey lived in the Adri, other nymphs, and satyrs, dryads, pixies and sprites. They had their ways into Fey and they would welcome Lathia to holiday with them when she chose.

Heward did keep Lathia in Oerik, but so did the Ring of Gaxx. She had no wish to rip holes between universes, but there were other ways to use its magic. She saw opportunities to further light and laughter.

Gaxx the Horrid, betrayed by Gumish, gained his revenge by impaling Gumish on a wicked spike.

And, across broken worlds, the swords of Gruumsh rang against the power of Corellon Larethian and their forces of Good.
