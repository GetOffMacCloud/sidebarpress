## Part 104

Kavoss the Cyclops was a war hero.

He rippled with muscles and his big round yellow eye burned brightly. He prided himself on both his strength and intelligence. He had survived battles using blade and magic.

Gruumsh was lucky to have the great Kavoss as his foremost general.

And he was about to achieve his greatest victory.

Moradin would die.

Kavoss had stabbed Moradin with a dagger coated with Godrot, and only the quick intervention of the meddling Corellon had prevented Kavoss from administering the death blow.

Kavoss knew that the mortally wounded Moradin was hiding somewhere on the continent of Mystara, and he was determined to find him.

The venom was leaking the godhood out of Moradin. He was trailing godhood as mortals trail blood.

Kavoss twisted the brace around his wrist and uttered a command word. A faint phosphorous glow appeared over the mountains to the east. Moradin was somewhere there!

The dwarven Spears, defenders of Rockhome, looked out from the Citadel and saw a huge number of hobgoblins massing down along the foothills.

Kavoss had called in his troops, and they readied themselves to attack the mountain.

It was an attack doomed to fail, Kavoss knew that. Tens of thousands of hobgoblins would be butchered flinging themselves gloriously into the embedded dwarf fighters, and they probably wouldn't gain an inch.

But conquest of Rockhome was not the objective. Separating Corellon from Moradin was the real hope. Kavoss feared the elf god and knew that Corellon was as clever as he.

Kavoss strode forward on Hucephelus. The giant leucrotta's stag legs tromped the ground, the badger head wheezing sputum as it did so.

Kavoss halted his steed and raised his mighty sword. The first wave advanced...
