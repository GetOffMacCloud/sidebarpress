## Part 1

### −14,000 SD[^1]

Io the Ninefold Dragon soared over Oerth.

They looked down upon it for one last time.

Life had irrevocably changed. What had once been a paradise of banquets, magic and laughter had grown dark.

Avarice had come to Oerth.

And jealousy. And vanity.

The Draconians had broken themselves.

Io sailed across the great continent Oerik,[^2] once a United Utopia, but now a reminder of what had been lost.

Here was the place where Bahamut the Platinum dragon and Tiamat the Chromatic dragon sundered themselves into enmity eternal.

Dragons became fixed, rigid. Trapped in singular form. White remained white. Silver remained silver.

Io had failed to reconnect the rift.

Now they were the Ninefold Dragon when once they were but one.

Nine for nine schisms.

The empire was now empires.

And so much had been lost.

Io hardly saw a single dragon. The land swept out before them in brown, green and blue.

[^1]: Suel date.
[^2]: Most of the names used in this history are those used by the current Common tongue. The continent Oerik was named by the Oeridians who migrated into the Flanaess (Eastern Oerik) some 1,100 years ago. Prior names of Oerik include Sylv, Kazebruk, Jonphur and Xagra.
