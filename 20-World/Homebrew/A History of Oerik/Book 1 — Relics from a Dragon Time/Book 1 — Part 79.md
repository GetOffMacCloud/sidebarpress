## Part 79

Prince Regent Silvervein Moradinson was determined to give a good impression for the duergar delegation.

He assumed they would be a little overawed to see him, a descendant of Moradin himself. How relieved they would be to rejoin their more renowned brethren!

<center>* * *</center>

A table full of fine breads was laid out in front of the duergar delegation. The bread was broken and laid onto golden plates. The duergar ate with obvious enjoyment; surely they were half-starved! The duergar finished their morsels then proceeded to take the valuable plates and store them into their large sacks. Silvervein was a little taken aback. Were his guests stealing right in front of him? The head of the delegation, a little rustic, but noble nevertheless, seemed to realize the problem.

"Thank you so much for the golden trinkets," he said. "You are very kind."

So, the plates were mistaken for gifts? Well, never mind.

Now a gourmet broth was brought forth, with golden spoons. The broth was greedily sucked and slurped, then the spoons were tossed into the duergar's sacks.

"The spoons are beautiful! Shiny!" announced the Rustic. "Thank you very much for these presents." And beamed.

Next came delicately sliced truffles stuffed in eagle eggs. Curiously shaped cutlery accompanied.

"These are forks, your eminences," enthused Silvervein. "An elvish invention, and made of silver, pearls and mithril."

In the sacks they went, with much gratitude.

Silvervein winced when he recalled what was coming next. He would have to explain things carefully to his gently backward guests.

He rose and began to speak, when, too late, the wine was served. The goblets were of crystal and colored a rich red. The duergar had obviously never seen the like.

"Ooohhhh," emoted the head duergar.

"Very special vessels made of ruby glass," stated Silvervein, "an alchemy concocted in an unknown way by our friends, the gnomes."

"Gnomes!" yelped the Rustic. "Gnomes! Those stinking vermin!"

"Ah, but you must understand, my friends, that here on the surface the gnomes and dwur are at peace."

The delegation looked at the Regent with sour expressions.

"The sight of these foul cups is an affront to us," snarled the lead duergar. "If you wish a reconciliation between our peoples, you will give them us, so we can destroy them."

Silvervein was crestfallen.

"Is there nothing I can do to dissuade you?"

The Rustic looked a little less severe.

"We will drink from the accursed cups first, as a token for future understanding."

The wine was drunk, then immediately afterwards the ruby goblets were thrust into sacks.

<center>* * *</center>

After the banquet, the duergar retired to the guest quarters, leaving behind a very deflated Prince Regent.
