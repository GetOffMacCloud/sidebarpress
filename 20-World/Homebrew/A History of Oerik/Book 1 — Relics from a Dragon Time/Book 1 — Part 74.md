## Part 74

### 412 SD

The large rectangular land west of a long gulf running north/south and east of the Sabre mountains in Western Oerik was the home of large clans of Thytic peoples.

Copper-skinned and fair-haired, with brown or green eyes, they were bronze-using horse people. Their numbers had grown into the millions and although many clans were nomadic, the coastline became permanently settled with small towns and farms.

The Thytic culture split between the two main groups and by 412 SD the Urbanites and the Rovers were bitter enemies. Punitive wars were unleashed on all sides. The horse people were terrifying warriors, but the settlers built strong defenses and they had powerful galleys equipped with great catapults able to mobilize and defend the coastline.

Nomads wishing to avoid conflict headed east into the great plains, north of the jann and south of the Wind Dukes. These plains were lightly sprinkled with humanoids and earlier Thytic arrivals armed with stones and bones.

The nomads quickly established themselves and flourished. This caused consternation amongst the Wind Dukes, and wary of conflict, they had their Baklun slaves build stone defenses and trenches to keep the horse people at bay.

Inevitably some Bakluns escaped and joined the Thytics. Amongst them were a few families of Suel who were descended from those captured during the Suel/Wind Duke conflict.
