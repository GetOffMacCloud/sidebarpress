## Part 155

The Hool River spills out of a water-filled caldera that was once the largest volcano in the Hellfurnaces. So huge was its last eruption that the low mountains surrounding the north and south of the volcano were utterly vaporized, the southeastern part reduced to high hills. Consequently the land about the lake was green and rich in minerals and nutrients.

Lolth and Miska settled in these hills and planned their strategy. The Sheldar people who lived hereabouts were neolithic and in deathly fear of the elves from both the Singing Lands behind the Hellfurnaces and Celene far to the east. They were also at war with various humanoid tribes, particularly goblins and xvarts. Miska reasoned that controlling these hillmen would give them the ability to spy on the Singing Lands. Lolth was more interested in going north to the Hool Marshes and recruiting lizard people to use to raid along the river where Flannish and Mai traders were often found collecting water. These sailors were bound to have treasure.

Miska shapechanged into the form of a charismatic Sheldar barbarian and infiltrated the hill clans. Lolth retained her black widow spider form and posed as a goddess in the hope of bringing the lizard people into obeisance.
