## Part 127

### 686–714 SD

When the Mai in Hepmonaland began seafaring it opened up vast new possibilities.

Culturally the Hepmonaland and Kara-Tur provinces had been growing apart for centuries; now there was a rift.

Magic gates that linked the continents, physically on the other side of the world, were for the exclusive use of the powerful, and with the Mai exploring their seas and oceans, the physical distance between Mai and Yuan was no longer tenable.

The Lords and Ladies of Mai received virtually no help from Yuan in their battles against the Flannish and Thytic mariners. They retaliated by sealing the gates.

<center>* * *</center>

The Empress Yehua had her capital situated amongst the gorgeously forested hills of central Kara-Tur. She had grown old and infirm.

Yehua knew that the Mai of Hepmonaland were dissatisfied. She had lost interest in them. Rather than looking inwards, as she did, savoring the beauty of the unseen and the intangible, the Mai arrogantly looked outwards towards conflict and conquest.

Really, she didn't know the Mai anymore. A rude and demanding people!

The Yuan were better off without the moral decay that the Mai brought in. The surrounding nobles wanted her to keep the MaiYuan together, because they coveted the unique food and treasures from across the globe.

Yehua knew that the greatest gifts come from the soul. Look at her, an ancient woman, unable to bear noise or crowds, unable to walk unaided, sometimes forgetful, especially of names, with little tolerance for rich foods, but she was more than content.

She was ecstatic.

<center>* * *</center>

The official dissolution of the MaiYuan empire finally reached an agreement in 714 SD.

The Mai of Hepmonaland and the Yuan of Kara-Tur were separated forevermore.

Powerful wizards, wu-jen, continued to cross between the two new empires, consolidating their knowledge and together dabbling with secret gods.

Perhaps, one day, the wu-jen would overthrow the decrepit remnants of lost glories.

<center>* * *</center>

The Mai Lords and Ladies appointed a new Empress of Hepmonaland. She was Kifabekazi, and she was young, easygoing, insightful and adventurous.
