## Part 76

### 456 SD

There were dwarves, dwur, which had lived in the Underdark for many thousands of years.

They were dwur descended from those who lusted for gold, and had dug down deep under the Oerth in order to get it, and keep it.

In doing so, they had completely cut themselves off from the surface world. The elves never knew of them, the hill and mountain dwarves had forgotten them.

The grey dwur, or duergar as they now called themselves, had survived many dangers in their quest for the treasure of the depths. Fungus people, dragons, aboleths, and slimes, molds and jellies had decimated their population time and time again.

And yet they survived.

Survived and found hidden wonders, great glowing cathedrals to old gods, with vast columns that resonated with the sound of deep bass bells when touched, and massive chalk-white caverns, once the home of Forgotten People, now uninhabited by sentient life. These places began to become familiar, and in time they almost came to like their new dwellings that they now considered to be Home.

Almost, but not quite. The lack of sun and the harsh radiations of the Underdark had driven the joy from their faces. Nothing made them happy anymore. Stoicism and fatalism were their comfort.

Yet they survived.

Survived and made a kingdom in the dark.
