## Part 5

### Oerth

Io had the cosmos, but the Ninefold Dragon felt drawn to Oerth, and Oerik in particular.

Oerik was a large continent divided by the great spine of the Barrier Peaks, the Crystalmists and the Hellfurnaces. Various lesser ranges broke up long forests, grasslands and fertile valleys. This was not particularly different from any of the other continents that dotted Oerth. Perhaps Io saw the loss and scars, for some of the greatest upheavals happened there.

South of Eastern Oerik were two continents, one small and one large, called today Hepmonaland and Melavi. Places of jungle and swamp, many secrets lurked within, shielded from Io's aerial view.

East of Eastern Oerik, within the Solnor Sea, was a large island called Kodge Land, and a little further over, the continent known as Mystara.

Continuing a long way east, past Mystara across the Mystaran Ocean, are two sister continents, one to the north called Kingsland, or C'mu N'eir. The Tuatha de Dannan lived here. South is a larger land known as Adarus.

Io glided southeast. There they were, the Lost Lands, an even bigger emotional wrench than Oerik. Mysterious magic, terrible monsters and strange intelligent beings, further cast-offs beyond Io's understanding, lurked in this terrible land.

Io returned to Oerik, gliding into the western parts. It was definitely drier, and the Olve-relics were scarce. Dracoforms predominated. Did these simpler life-forms still think that the Empires existed?

Io continued west, across the foamy wastes of the enormous Dramidj Ocean, the tiny central islands, and now, with the great peaks of volcanoes rising out of the water like the bones of the Dragon Emperors, great Kara-Tur, a super continent that stretched north into the arctic and south into the Antarctic...

Vast Oerth. Precious Oerth.

The Cast-offs, the Relics lay across the world, chaotic and threatening, for Io couldn't understand them, and to Olve and Dwur, he was only a shadow on the sun.
