## Part 95

Druidism originally began in the benign lands of the Blemu elves, where great forests blanketed the land. Originally it was a cult of human elf worshippers but soon morphed into a nature religion following Beory. As the human population grew in the Flanaess the druids became more distant with the elves, only allowing humans and half-elves into their circle.

In 511 SD the druids debated on the nature of nature itself. Many followed the path of the Circle of Existence that taught of the nature of cycles, life, death, cold, heat, water, land, trees and grass, mountain and hill, pebble and rock. The presence of the Ur-Flan and their knowledge of secret things was a fascination for some. These tempted druids were interested in the workings behind the Circle of Existence and they joined the Ur-Flan in study and experimentation.

Shapeshifting creatures were captured and studied. The natures of gold and silver, iron and copper were questioned. The power of the moon cycles was harnessed. The modern druid's use of moon cycles and shape changing in their magic dates from this time.

Bands of druids roamed the northlands as wolves and crows, bears and boars. Feeling free and as one with all life.
