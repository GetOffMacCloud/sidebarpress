## Part 9

When an occasional elf disappeared from the Vesve Forest, it was sad for the families, but no one was really alerted to what was actually happening. After all, there were baleful things in the woods. So it was that the alien abductions went on wholly unnoticed at first.

Tristram and Isolde, two young wood-elven newlyweds, found themselves all at once in a stony bloodstained cell in the darkness. The cell was cold, hard and smelled. A pool of blood glistened on the floor with parts of a sheep soaking in it. After untold hours, two lurching, tentacled horrors in grey robes came into the cell and grabbed Isolde. Tristram fought back, but a psionic blast smashed him hard into the back wall.

Isolde appeared back in the cell two days later, but Tristram found that her eyes were glazed over, and she did not utter a word or seem to recognize Tristram.

Tristram was next. Faint from a lack of food and water, and nursing a large lump on his head, Tristram was unable to resist when the monstrous beings returned for him. He was placed onto a bloody slab and his arms and legs were bound up with thick lead-lined ropes that smelled of rotten cabbage. There was a brief pause and Tristram was left alone with his fears. But not for too long. At last came the thud, thud of heavy footsteps accompanied by a hissing, gurgling gargle. A huge tentacled pale violet head leered down at him, its tentacle-probes twitching with excitement...
