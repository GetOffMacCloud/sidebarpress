## Part 116

### 579 SD

Festivity was in the air!

A new Goddess had come to the Singing Lands!

The Cantata ended with a six-part fugue. Then the Queen of Chaos leaned forward awkwardly, shifting her bloated body on the bench and clapped the assembled choir.

Everyone cheered!

The Queen was aware that she had become a little corpulent since her marriage, but was it her fault that Zzzlarr had made her so hungry! She had a cane made from one of his mantis forelimbs, and had a cape woven out of the hair from his spider legs.

The Queen had come back to her ancestral lands to lay her eggs.

The Singing Lands was undoubtedly a safer place than the Abyss, and she also desired her magic whip that had been stolen from her. Perhaps in Oerik she would acquire it once more.

The Lowly Ones, now arrogantly calling themselves the Risen Ones, the Suel, had apparently lost it themselves in their war with the Vaati.

The Vaati were a strange, magical people. It would take some work to retrieve it.

The Singing Ones now sang a Song Cycle about the interminable dwarven wars. Why were they boring her with that?

She shifted again upon the bench, a glower growing across her face. If they didn't change the subject soon...

The third song was a jaunty march about dwarves in the Underdark.

Let them all go down there and rot, thought the Queen of Chaos.

Then the song exploded!

A cascade of silvery pyrotechnics burst asunder with blinding radiance.

The Queen couldn't see. Was this supposed to be entertaining?

She blinked several times, then her eyes readjusted.

The stage was empty. The singers were gone!

Then, from above the stage, a deep, ugly voice sang off key in archaic dwarvish:

Huk, okken mutt eingz bedwalin, eingz giblunkzen. Gist za drok!

A chain came dangling down from the rafters and at the end of the chain was a singing dwarf, pitted with scars and with iron legs.

The chain lowered him onto the stage, then, when he was safe on the ground, he unclasped the chain then turned a large key protruding out of his hip. His clockwork legs began to move and he capered about in an amusing way.

The Queen opened her cavernous mouth in shock.

Then she laughed.

Everyone joined in.

The dwarf acknowledged the laughter with the doff of a tatty hat.

Who was this strange disfigured dwarf?

Why, the very one who still prayed nightly to Moradin Soulforger since his capture by the Singing Ones more than one hundred years ago.

He was Pious Grimm Quartztusk.
