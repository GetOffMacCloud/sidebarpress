## Part 106

Moradin was laid out onto an altar, candles lit with ethereal flames at each corner. One by one the designated priests sprinkled holy water over his motionless body. All the while the congregation of clerics intoned the magic verses to prepare the way to the next, more dangerous part of the ritual...

Corellon Larethian looked out atop the battlements of the Rockhome defenses. Down below were multitudes of dead and wounded hobgoblins. Further away in a valley tiny figures had set up rude campsites; the hobgoblins were regrouping. He looked from horizon to horizon for the winged demons sent to attack from above, but saw nothing.

Moradin was now very close to death. His subconscious will was strong and his heart and lungs refused to give up despite the Godrot ravaging him from within. But they would have to give up if he was to ascend into his new state. The verses were repeated six times. As the congregation began the seventh and final repeat the Highest Priest went through the sacred curtain to fetch the sword.

Corellon came down to the barracks. Where were the scouts who had warned the king? The scouts? But none have returned!

The sword was not magical. It was just a sword, but the elderly abbot had blessed it and rubbed it with oil. He looked up at the Highest Priest and smiled faintly. O, yes, he was ready. His feeble body found the sword rather heavy to carry. Would the priest help? Good! The curtain was pulled aside and the Highest Priest with the sword and the hobbling abbot stepped to the altar as the seventh verse was completed. The abbot took a deep breath and took the sword from the priest. He raised it to his lips and kissed it. A little patch of saliva dampened the edge and trickled down the blade. The abbot took another step and raised the sword high above Moradin's throat. He paused for a moment, glanced furtively at the clerics and coughed nervously. Then he plunged the sword down.
