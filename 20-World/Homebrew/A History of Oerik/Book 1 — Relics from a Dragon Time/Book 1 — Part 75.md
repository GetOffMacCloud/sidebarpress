## Part 75

### 448 SD

The gnome population of Eastern Oerik was exploding. The dwarves found this irksome. Gnomes encroached on dwarvish land, expanding out from the Good Hills and into the Sheldomar Valley and up into the Barrier Peaks and the Crystalmists. They set up mines in competition, and dwarves retaliated by imprisoning gnomes. Gnomes retaliated by sealing off mines and destroying roads. There were fights and a few deaths.

Garl Glittergold, the gnome hero and king of the Sheldomar, was saddened by the violence. He persuaded gnomes to abandon the dwarvish places and head into the more inhospitable hills where conflicts with humanoids like kobolds and goblins were far more acceptable.[^1]

But some gnomes remained where they were.

They were ingenious and clever and they exploited hidden fissures and caves that the dwarves could not work. Using magic they dug down deep after minerals never seen by the hill dwarves.

An agreement was reached where gnomes were allowed to work these places for an annual lease paid to the local dwarves.

In 448 SD a gnomish clan called the svirfneblin dug down so deep that they penetrated into the luminous caverns and tunnels of the Underdark.

[^1]: Acceptable to gnomes and dwarves, that is.
