## Part 67

### 209 SD

The migrating jann that fled the Suel conquest were incorporated into the realm of the Wind Dukes. The Vaati placed their "lesser" cousins into a new caste that reflected their position as being above the slaves.

The Wind Dukes were angered by this disruption to the natural order, and the Suel people were designated an Anathema, a Chaotic malady.

What was even worse: their Goddess was formidable and dangerous.

The Wind Dukes, the Vaati, had other difficulties also. Horse tribes in the west were growing and encroaching ever closer. Turning their back upon the beneficent bounties provided by their Masters, some slaves had left their paradise and joined the barbarians!

As for the jann, they grieved.

A great summit was held, after which the third most important Wind Duke of the Houdeam (the first caste) traveled with the most important Wind Duke of the Hakjadeam (the second caste) with bodyguards, to go to and attempt to reason or chastise the reprobate Suel.

The delegation met with two of the most important Suelchiefs, who were accompanied by an annoying dark-haired human called Norebo. Their Goddess had not appeared.

<center>* * *</center>

The Suloise rabble were rude and did not adhere to protocol. Their reception was unadorned by etiquette, the foodstuffs plain and their greetings presumptuous.

The Norebo talked with his mouth full.

The Vaati were reasonable people. Despite their anger with the boorish Suel, they calmly explained their position and politely commanded the humans to set aside the land west of the Darkwall mountains for the jann.

The Suel humans acted incredulous. Were the Wind Dukes joking?

Of course not! The Vaati never joked. To do so would be vacuous. Apparently the Suel did? The brutes must be barely intelligent.

The Suloise then attempted to convince the Vaati that they had no wish to war with them, and in fact were open for trade. But the newly conquered jann lands were now theirs. Grim-faced, the Vaati retired to their camp.

The next morning the Wind Dukes returned, wearing Force Armor and carrying Force Shields. The Houdeam leader held a glowing rod. Talk was done, the Suloise degenerates would now face Justice.
