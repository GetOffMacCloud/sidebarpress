## Part 103

### 526 SD

Moradin the Dwarf God lay dying in the Citadel.

He had been taken to Rockhome, in Mystara, by none other than the Elven God Corellon Larethian. Once enemies, the two gods had joined forces against Gruumsh the Orc Lord and were now staunch companions.

Moradin had been struck by a blade coated with Godrot venom sourced from a rare jellyfish found in the river Styx. Rockhome was chosen as a refuge over the Crystalmists in Oerik, because the Crystalmist dwur were warring and Rockhome was now an independent and united dwarven kingdom.

The battle that had felled Moradin had taken place in the distant world of Toril.

Corellon and Moradin had been magically sealed inside a Labyrinth, cut off from the Material Plane. For years they were there, until Corellon mastered the cryptographic magic and found the only exit. The door had been locked, barred and jammed shut. Moradin smashed it down.

The two gods quickly rejoined the great war against great armies of humanoids bent on total conquest.

The years that had confined Corellon and Moradin had not been kind to the Good folk. Dwarves, elves and humans had found themselves outnumbered again and again. Wargs, orcs, goblins and hobgoblins now swarmed Toril, and magical gates had been placed to invade other worlds.

<center>* * *</center>

The poison burned in Moradin's divine veins.

Godrot blocked all magical healing and shapeshifting. Effectively it turned a god mortal. Now, Moradin was near death. The Dwur clerics held deep discussions. What could they do?

<center>* * *</center>

A few hundred miles to the west, Kavoss the Cyclops, mounted atop his giant bestial steed, Hucephelus, materialized with a company of hobgoblins. Moradin had escaped his clutches but he was here, somewhere, and he would be found!
