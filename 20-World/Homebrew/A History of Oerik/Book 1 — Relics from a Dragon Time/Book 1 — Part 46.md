## Part 46

### 29–55 SD

Win-Daled was reaching the end of her mortal life. She had ruled Risewater, home of the Suloise, for many years and the population had increased, with further settlements to the north and west. But the East was off-limits, too close to the Singing Lands.

Most of her tenure as Suelchief had been relatively easy. Win-Daled was by far the greatest arch-mage in the nation, and she had the mighty elven Ye'Cind and the great Flan wunderkind Heward as friends and advisors.

The Singing One and the Flan roguish warrior were fond of wandering, though, and lately had been frequenting Risewater less and less. Ye'Cind was still a young elf maiden and Heward seemed to have stopped aging at forty.

Win-Daled herself felt old, and she was ready to move on to the next stage of existence. She had hoped Heward would take up the job... oh well... his dalliances with the nymphs, dryads and other feminine creatures of Fey was apparently taking up too much of his time for serious work.

Therefore, Win-Daled chose the son of a gallant former bandit to be her successor. Gryman was intelligent, contemplative and had earned the respect of many of the young Suel in his twenty-three years. He was deeply moved by the honor, and promised to serve the Suel faithfully and fairly. Win-Daled assured him that she knew he would.

At the end of the year, after giving a last speech and wishing everybody well, Win-Daled clutched her heart and fell back on the stage. Her face was serene, and her lips curved with the hint of a smile. She never rose again.

52 SD, Ye'Cind finally joined up with a small grey elf community that had splintered off from Celene and helped to found a new elven nation north of the Vast Swamp.

55 SD, Heward, gallivanting about Fey, crossed paths with a band of jealous satyrs while in the company of a vivacious nymph. They struck him down and placed him in a magical prison. There he remained for a long, long time...
