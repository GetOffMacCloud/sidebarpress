## Part 154

### 769 SD

Lolth and Miska had thrived in the Abyss.

Both children had hunted and haunted Abyssal caves and caverns throughout the 666 Layers. They had enslaved weaker demons and had ambushed and slain stronger ones.

They had suffered small defeats and had come close to the edge of death more than once. They had stolen rare treasures and gained great power.

Magic flowed through their bodies as a part of their very being. Lolth resembled a fat black widow spider with an elven female's head. Miska appeared as a huge bristly wolf spider with a slavering lupine head.

Their mother, the Queen of Chaos, was very fond of them.

The mind of the Queen was full of plots and plans. Sometimes they intersected and fit together beautifully, but they also could be jumbled up and contradictory.

Her ideas could also be overwhelmed by whims and diverse thoughts that would undermine her well-laid webs.

The Queen of Chaos longed to rule the Abyss entire.

She wanted to become Empress of the Singing Lands.

She wanted to confront the Wind Dukes and reclaim her stolen whip.

She wanted her children to thrive.

<center>* * *</center>

Lolth and Miska were filled with excitement but also trepidation, for the Queen of Chaos had decided that the time had come for her beloved children to return to Oerik and make their own way in the world.

Their mother had thought hard upon this step, and she had reached the conclusion that the highlands east of the Singing Lands, where the Hool River began, would be a good place to establish themselves.

Farewell, my Children!

Do me Proud!
