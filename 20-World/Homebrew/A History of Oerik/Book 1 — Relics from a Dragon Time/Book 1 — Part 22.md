## Part 22

9,000 years ago Western Oerik was dominated by great mountain ranges, shallow lakes, semi-arid plains and dry deserts. Rivers trickled through light woodlands, but overall, this chunk of Oerik was harsher with much less rainfall than in Eastern Oerik. Nevertheless, intelligent life did reside here.

Bounded by the Hellfurnaces, the Sulhaut Mountains and the Wubli Range to the south, the Singing Lands with their human "Lowly Ones" flourished in the wettest part of Western Oerik, surrounded by rainforests and beautiful farmland.

The Sulhauts and Crystalmists were home to great dwarven clans led by Moradin the Soul Forger.

North were barbarian hordes, nomadic wanderers who learned to survive in the dry lands. They knew where to find food and sources of water, and they developed complex relationships with each other and flourished. They were divided into many clans and tribes, but were loosely affiliated as the Pambia Nations.

Somewhere in the center of the Northlands around the shores of several lakes were the Vaati (also known as the Wind Dukes). Elemental beings of great power, they built a land famous for its ravishing towers and cultivated gardens.

South of the Wubli Mountains was a large triangle of tropical grasslands and forests inhabited by prehistoric mammals that lumbered happily about.

The western fastness was mostly divided by bands of mountain ranges. Jann and other beings from the Inner Planes dwelled here, as did roving armies of orcs, norkers and ogres, as well as many large animals and monsters. For the most part these lands were the driest in the continent.

On the western side of a long gulf were the Thytan peoples, bronze-age horse nomads that had gated there after deserting from elite fighting units of the illithids. They had coppery skin and dark hair with grey, brown and black eyes, and were disciplined and martial.

The northernmost point of Western Oerik was home to the Infinite, a relic of the dragon empires that disappeared off Oerth some 11,000 years earlier. Bahamut was said to counsel with the Infinite (dragons and dragon-kin) from time to time. It was a mysterious land that didn't tolerate visitors.

To the west were dry lands inhabited by the thri-kreen, mantis warriors, who roamed in huge numbers and fought terrific wars against each other in the rain season, which was part of their mating cycle.

Other exotic monsters could be found right to the edge of the westernmost tip of Oerik: lamias, lammasu, ki-rin, dragonnes, leucrottas and basilisks, cockatrices, sphinxes and purple worms. Past this point lay the vast Celestial Ocean that stretched out all the way to Kara-Tur.
