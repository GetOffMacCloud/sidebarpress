## Part 40

Dweomyne sang in three octaves at once. The sonic blast threw Win-Daled and Ye'Cind to the floor. Dweomyne leapt off her throne and flew into the air, her whip arcing backward ready to lash.

Winded and hands scorching, Ye'Cind unrolled another spell from the smoking Scroll of Vile Darkness and frantically began mouthing the words.

Mocinlyte the succubus, hearing the commotion from the adjacent chamber, burst into the room, determined to help her mistress. She tore at Win-Daled, but the Lowly One coolly raised a hand and turned her to ice. The translucent demoness toppled over and shattered.

The Whip Lady danced forward, still singing, and flicked her whip across Ye'Cind, but Heward was there again and took the brunt, his cheek slicing open. Grimacing with pain, he attempted to sever the whip with his sword, but the magical weapon unnaturally angled away. Quick as a leopard, Dweomyne drew a serrated dagger from her robe, whip curling impatiently in her other hand. With a sonic roar she descended upon Heward and furiously struck at him. Heward struggled with the dagger and the wicked blade slid across the air just missing his throat.

He swung his own weapon and caught Dweomyne in her side as her whip curled around his thigh. The Whip Lady overbalanced and fell, but pulled Heward down with her.

An eldritch flame of violet streaked from Ye'Cind's scroll and streamed into the Whip Lady's mouth, rushing down into her throat. She attempted to blast it away with song, but her esophagus filled with flame.

Now Dweomyne felt the writhing pain she had inflicted upon so many as the unholy fire roasted her from the inside. Even so, she managed to stagger up and flick her whip, tearing the Scroll of Vile Darkness off Ye'Cind and flinging it towards her.

Heward reached out and brushed the scroll with his fingers, but it hissed at him and sparked angrily.

The Whip Lady bent down and took the spitting scroll into her hands. She shuddered and convulsed as the fire rolled out of her throat and back into the scroll. The pain faded and Dweomyne broke into a cruel smile. She would survive yet!

But just as she moved to shape a spell that would turn the trio into dust, another blue light streaked out from Win-Daled and encircled her, holding her off. The Scroll of Vile Darkness, enraged, burst into blinding whiteness that blew out a hole in the wall. With one final spark, the scroll fled the Material Universe, taking Dweomyne with it. As the ruler of the Singing Ones dematerialized, the Whip Lady swung her whip around again, catching Win-Daled around the leg, bringing her down and spoiling the spell.

Yet, as the whip wound around Win-Daled, it left Dweomyne's grip, who was now fully insubstantial. The whip was left on Oerth as the Whip Lady fell through the Astral Plane and plunged into the Abyss.

Ye'Cind, Heward and Win-Daled were too injured and weary to be happy, yet they looked at each other with satisfaction. Dweomyne was gone, and their family was avenged. Win-Daled carefully unwrapped the whip from off her legs, wincing. It thrummed with power as she turned it over in her hands, trying to decipher the magic glyphs etched into the straps.
