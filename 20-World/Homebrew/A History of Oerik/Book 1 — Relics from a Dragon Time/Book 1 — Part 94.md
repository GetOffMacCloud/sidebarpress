## Part 94

### 511 SD

Shapeshifters have always existed on Oerth.

The hengeyokai, kenku and tengu were the dominant peoples of the great temperate lands of Kara-Tur, north of the sprawling jungle empire of MaiYuan.

In Eastern Oerik there were small animals, none larger than a fox, who could assume human form or a hybrid of the two bodies. They tended to live in the remotest regions and kept their abilities hidden. They were few in number too, and their technology and language were rudimentary.

At some point the druids discovered them and questioned their place in the natural world. A circle of druid hierophants, elderly and powerful, became fascinated with their existence. Perhaps the material guise of appearance was not fixed. Perhaps the assumed form of the individual was mutable, and to truly experience reality, one needed to assume new shapes and transcend the supposed limitations of the Physical.

Philosophy is beyond the scope of this history, so we will move on to the Sepia Uplands, where the shadows of these hills meet the vast Vesve Forest.

Here, the Ur-Flan practiced dark magic and demonology. The Ur-Flan were originally a secret cult, but during the late 400s they rejoined the common world and hired themselves out as masters of the arcane: mighty clerics and terrible assassins. Their temples and towers were dotted throughout the human realms and they were tolerated as a small evil in a generally benign society.

Druids of the Vesve regularly met with the hill tribes of the Uplands to trade for mutual benefit. The Ur-Flan had a large presence and were a useful source of information about the hidden world.

When asked about shapeshifters, the darkly dressed Ur-Flan would tell eerie tales:

Foxes disguising themselves as small children, then possessing the human parents,

Badgers that transformed into dwarves and created wars between clans,

Hares that hypnotized all the halflings to open up their larders and give out all their food.

The Ur-Flan were eerie, mysterious and knowledgeable. Some druids became attracted to their ideas and joined their ranks, determined to gain new affinity with the Natural Universe.
