## Part 37

"Rise! Rise! Rise!"

The mandrill-masked acolytes pranced about the dancing girls, chanting the phrase over and over, while the dancers poured snake blood over themselves.

Gibbering toad demons leapt about, snatching random members of the crowd then devouring them messily. The congregation continued to sway and rock with apparent obliviousness. This was no ordinary service, for they were summoning the Most Low, the Berserker Beast, Monstrous Demogorgon himself!

Several high-ranking priests made love to willing succubi, the priests' bodies wrinkled into dry husks that dissolved into demon-larvae.

Grand Priest Noalk'Ota controlled proceedings, frowning slightly as a shadow momentarily passed over the altar. A boa constrictor wrapped around his waist, hissing contentedly. Above him hung a huge kraken wrapped in ropes attached to a wooden scaffold, tentacles wriggling and reaching in every direction. It had only been caught with great difficulty, and scores had been slain by the terrific sea squid. One by one slaves were prodded into the radius of the creature where long fleshy arms plucked them up then deposited them into its beaky maw. The kraken was slowly drying out. When it finally died it would be the best gift of all.

Noalk'Ota felt the thrill welling up from deep inside him. A ritual like this would not happen again in his lifetime, for the cost was enormous and the Great Ones did not like being often disturbed. He involuntarily broke into a little jig when he realized just how soon he was to converse face to face with Demogorgon, who would wisely bestow power upon him equal to the dragon!

O! What a day it was when the Scroll of Vile Darkness came to him! And when he began to decipher its unholy words... this whole procedure was thanks to the artifact now buried deep within the altar before him.

"Rise! Rise! Rise!"

The enormous altar, fashioned into the shape of a horned crimson demon, concealed the Scroll within a cavity within its brain, blasphemous texts wadded behind each jeweled eye.

Noalk'Ota felt the boa tighten its muscles slightly. Good, the dumb beast could feel the awe in the air, the miasma of power that would be his, and his alone! Even the MaiYuan emperor would submit to him.

The kraken was moving less freely now. It was tiring, and in minutes would succumb. He glanced up at the altar demonhead. One glowing eye glinted down at him. One...? And was it his imagination or could he see a small square hole in its forehead?

"Got it!" called the disembodied voice of Heward as his invisible form slipped into the bushes a mile away from the ceremony.

He handed Win-Daled the Scroll of Vile Darkness with relief, for he could feel its throbbing malevolence. Heward held another parchment in his other hand.

"I'll peruse this obscene writing I found in the jeweled eye later myself. Might learn something."

As Ye'Cind and Win-Daled gently chuckled, over at the ceremony the kraken twitched no more. The ground shook and the demon statue burst apart with white fire.

Demogorgon himself, twin baboon heads howling and hooting, tentacled and powerfully muscled, pulled its heavy bulk out from the molten altar and, quickly realizing that the Scroll was not in its position, turned upon the worshippers, the dancers, the acolytes and the High Priest with vengeful fury.

The baboon heads screeched as Demogorgon tore into the congregation, a frenzy of slashing teeth and tentacles. Finally the ceremonial square fell red and silent and Demogorgon returned to the Abyss.

The boa constrictor unwound itself from its dead master and slithered off into the undergrowth.
