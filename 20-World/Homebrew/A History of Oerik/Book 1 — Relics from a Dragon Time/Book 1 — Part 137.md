## Part 137

### 741 SD

Pious Grimm Quartztusk was looking for Nomenwold.

He and his guard of Moradin Hammers traveled through the Sheldomar Valley, marching steadily but without any rush.

Pious was now a very old dwarf and he had retired from active duties in the Crystalmists for quite some time. Despite his age he was still fit, as his clockwork iron legs seemed to imbue him with energy.

Nomenwold was the last known whereabouts of the great gnome alchemist Flinter Glint, who was said to have been employed at various times by the Ur-Flan and the Singing Ones. It was highly likely that Glint was responsible for Pious's clockwork legs and the stalwart dwur thought it was time to seek him out.

The Sheldomar Valley was a hodgepodge of villages, towns, farmland and forests all somehow trying to coexist in the same space.

There were no strong leaders and much of the wealth ended up in the hands of bandit kings or the Celenian elves.

The elves were a constant presence in the valley. They roamed about at will, mainly young, arrogant elves relying on the belief that they held the secret of mighty magic to keep from being challenged.

Occasionally handsome men and women were taken away at night, and children were sometimes taken to Celene to never be heard from again. Half-elves were growing in numbers but they were largely distrusted and shunned by the Sheldar people. This actively turned many of the half-elves into spies and allies of the Celenians.

Pious Grimm and his entourage had diplomatic papers and they arrived at Nomenwold unmolested.

Nomenwold was an innovative place where art, magic and technology were brought together by highly skilled gnomes and halflings to create everything from musical instruments to artificial servants. The Ur-Flan funded much of the research and many of the bandits secretly worked for them.

Pious quickly discovered that Flinter Glint was no longer at Nomenwold. Garl Glittergold had recruited the alchemist to help on the world of Toril against the evil armies of Gruumsh. The Orc still quested to sweep the good peoples from the material universe.

Pious decided to follow after Glint to Toril as well. Perhaps he would meet the great alchemist there, but in reality, his dwur nature stirred within him. Here was a chance to fight side by side with Moradin Soulforger at last.
