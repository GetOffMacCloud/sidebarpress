## Part 148

Heward and Lathia lay together under the flashing stars of a violet night.

Heward had told Lathia about his plan to steal the ring.

"You told me because you want me to counsel you against taking it, don't you?" said Lathia. "But I actually agree with you. I don't exactly know why. Something about the ring makes me uncomfortable. And I was watching the horrids. They are bullies. It would be nice to teach them a lesson."

<center>* * *</center>

Sequestered in their room within Princess Gossermay's manor house, the horrids Gaxx and Gumish were gloating. Gaxx held between two gnarled fingers an oval, pearl-colored stone.

"I told you that the stupid Princess would give this to me."

Gaxx's voice was rough like sandpaper.

Gumish peered at the Moonstone closely. It looked flawless, and within was a faint, pale pink glow.

"You are sure..." he ventured.

"Yes, I have checked. This is the stone gifted to Fey by Bahamut, the Platinum Dragon."

"I still don't quite understand," said Gumish, "why would she give you this if..."

Gaxx lazily kicked over a gaudy chair with his ostrich-like toes.

"Because the ridiculously stupid brownie has forgotten her history. The Seelie world is corrupt with pleasure-seeking and whimsy. The Fey love everything and value nothing!"

<center>* * *</center>

Princess Gossermay held her hand up in triumph. Heward let the brownie into his modest cottage. He led her into the guest room and offered her a drink.

"I told you so," exclaimed Gossermay, sipping her brandy. "For a bag of gems, our district was given a great quantity of mithril ingots; that will increase our influence with the Tuatha de Dannan substantially. And," she took another sip, "our wealth, also."

Yes, thought Heward, but what was the true cost?

<center>* * *</center>

The trading completed, Gaxx and Gumish returned to their home, Cragdelve in UnFey.

There were giants waiting for them.

Fire and frost giants stood together, patiently enduring the squawkbats flying high overhead in a crimson sky. Perpetually at war with each other, Gaxx knew that they had been given great promises by his, and their, master. The giants were willing to work together for a common goal.

There were several cloud giants in attendance too, baring great fangs and wearing light clothing. These giants were not from UnFey but the land of C'mu N'ira on Oerth in the Coarse Plane, where they resented the rule of the powerful Tuatha de Dannan.

Gaxx greeted the giants with a wide leer.

"Comrades," he hissed, "soldiers of Great Gruumsh. I have completed the ring. The conquest can begin!"
