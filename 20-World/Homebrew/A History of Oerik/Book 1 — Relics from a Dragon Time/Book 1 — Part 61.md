## Part 61

Norebo tousled his hair in frustration. The Oathers' plan for total Suloise dominance was being derailed. The War Flind, Molag, had decided to come down from the mountains with large forces of gnolls, flind, bugbears and ogres. With the help of a few rogue Suel and Singing Ones, Norebo watched the Suloise keeps fall to the invaders, one by one.

Norebo observed that the defenders did not fight that hard. Unfortunately, the Oathers' tactics of subtly sapping the will of the populace backfired; the Suloise had not been happy and now their morale was shot.

Norebo urged his Oathers to rally resistance, but this led to little effect. Within a few short weeks, the horrible Molag overwhelmed the lands, with the Holy Papacy of Risewater all that was left in human control.

Under this shadow, Norebo returned to his gambling enterprises in the Flan Eastern Kingdoms, the Oather Headquarters, to process and resolve this unexpected crisis.

<center>* * *</center>

Norebo was in the Shady Satyr (which he owned) when he heard Ye'Cind and her recorder play phenomenal music. Moved as much as anyone, after she finished he offered her a chair at his table.

Ye'Cind knew who Norebo was, but she wasn't intimidated. An elder of Sunndi a little to the southwest, the elf had hoped to meet with this secret head of the mysterious Oathers. Norebo oozed charisma, and his enthusiasm for her musical skill flattered her immensely.

And soon they were deep in conversation. The topic moved into politics and Norebo told of recent Suloise history, and the divide of the Suel peoples into different realms. When he told of how Risewater became a Papacy her ears pricked up.

"I have heard of the Holy Papacy," said Ye'Cind, "but which gods do they worship so fervently? The Suloise when they were called 'Lowly Ones' had the odd god or two, but they were never terribly religious."

"Only one god," answered Norebo, "and She is known only as the Goddess."

"Goddess," mused Ye'Cind, "but which goddess...?"
