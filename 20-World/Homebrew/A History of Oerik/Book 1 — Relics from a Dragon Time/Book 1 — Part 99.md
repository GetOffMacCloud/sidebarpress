## Part 99

Long had she slept.

The duergar found the Undead Dragon slumbering atop a mighty mound of treasure.

When the Grand Drakes entombed her in the Underdark, they appeased her with great gifts. Without a hoard her dragon nature would have become too unstable and nothing magical or otherwise could have held her back for long.

The enormous misshapen skeleton lay over the treasure mound, unaware of her interlopers. Her minds were in the Dream Realms, reminiscing over her lost lives, for she was a hideous reconstruction of several individuals put together by Tiamat.

Once in a while a cloud of ghostly flame would emanate from her half-open mouth and slowly turn to mist.

The duergar entered the cavern and immediately spied the slumbering pile of bones, articulated into the shape of a great dragon. They were torn between terror and Goldust. Once they realized that the puffs of green flame were not at all aimed at them, that the dragon in fact seemed totally oblivious to their presence, they carefully made their way to the foot of the treasure hoard.

Gemstones there were, sapphires and diamonds and rubies and emeralds. There was something odd about their look, their color. Somehow they had been changed by the close proximity to the Undead Dragon.

Each gemstone felt very cold as if they had been buried in ice. And, despite their type, all the gems shone ghostly green in the dark.

The piles of gold and silver appeared dull and bleached, yet the duergar KNEW that they were of great value just the same.

The duergar opened their magically large sacks and bags and filled them with as much treasure as they could carry, being careful not to wake the massive skeletal thing creaking above them.

Too much power lay within the Undead Dragon, and too much time being confined in one place.

Her deathly flame breath had permeated the walls, the ceiling and the floor of the cavern. Over time, like liquid limestone forming stalactites and stalagmites, the ghostly smoke, fire and mist had turned its very surroundings into Undeath.

When the duergar turned and left, shadows silently crept after them...
