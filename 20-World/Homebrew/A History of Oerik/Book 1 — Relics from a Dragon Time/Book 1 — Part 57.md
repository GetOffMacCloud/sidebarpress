## Part 57

### 200 SD

Ye'Cind had been enjoying her life in Sunndi. She had been appointed to the Elder Council despite her youth, as a result of her experience and magical ability.

She was wise and understood the needs of the Flan, elves, dwarves, gnomes and halflings that made Sunndi their home. She had not yet married despite pressure from others to do so. Frankly, she had yet to find somebody interesting enough to live with, so she chose the solitary life.

Ye'Cind frequently traveled abroad. She was a Singing One elf and that land still drew upon her, dark though it was.

The Singing Lands had retreated from demonology, but tens of thousands of Lowly Ones were still enslaved. There was a new cultural interest in the big wide world, with various Singing One traders, adventurers and artists often seen on the road.

Traveling singers would move all around Eastern Oerik, from Celene, to Blemu and the eastern Flannish kingdoms. Ye'Cind liked their singing and who wouldn't? Their voices were supernatural.

Ye'Cind could not sing like her fellows. It felt to her that her complete failure to hold a note was a result of her mind being wired very differently from the other Singing Ones. She thought with a calm but deep intelligence, and unlike her kin, was filled with compassion for others. Naturally she would then be cursed with a voice of wayward pitch.

She was a great mimic though, and could imitate nearly any sound she had ever heard, so long as it was harsh and grating. She was also a natural musician, and could make beautiful sounds from the most unlikely materials.

So, Ye'Cind regularly went to hear Singing One minstrels whenever they came to Sunndi, listening with admiration and not a little jealousy.
