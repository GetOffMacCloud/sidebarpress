## Part 138

### 753 SD

Bice retched in the grass.

The feeling had come over her as soon as she reached the outskirts of the encampment.

To everyone's surprise, a score of tall female elves in glowing white armor had appeared from nowhere and were occupying this portion of the Good Hills of the Sheldomar.

The local gnomes and halflings were dismayed. Were they elite warriors from Celene?

The village elders urged caution but Bice wanted to find out what she could. The young halfling was fast and agile and confident that she could get away fast if she needed to.

She hadn't expected this wave of nausea. The encampment must be enchanted.

<center>* * *</center>

In the days that followed, the elves did not engage with the inhabitants at all, but they moved around the hills as if it was already theirs.

The land taken over was a stretch of highlands that led west into the Jotens, that mountainous spur that stretched out east from the Crystalmists. Some of the land contained important gnomish mines as well as meadows tended by halflings who hunted wild goats.

A delegation was set up to engage with the strange elves, but the magic barrier that made them ill could not be dispelled.

The delegation then set off to the Celene-controlled dominions of the Sheldomar to beg the elves there to pull their kinfolk from the Good Hills.

The Celenian elves knew nothing of these armored elves and dismissed the delegation.

Of course young elves were a little mischievous and arrogant, and liked to goad the locals a bit, but it was all in fun! One thing that they didn't do was don bright armor and play military games. No, these soldiers, if they even existed, were not Celenian.

Nevertheless, the delegation insisted that Celene check the hills for themselves then pull the elves out. The Celenians shook their heads. If the warriors were elves, then they were not from the Empire and were none of their business.

The despondent delegation returned home to find the strange armored elves blasting out the hillsides with fireballs to make steep bluffs and ravines around their camps.

Hailing the elves again met with no response. Perhaps engaging the services of the dangerous Ur-Flan would be necessary...
