## Part 84

The giant form of King Silvervein bore down on Pious Quartztusk and his troops. He had hoped his vast size and possession of the Fierce Axe would prove his legitimacy as the first mortal king of the dwarves. However, this manifestation of might and power simply compounded his blasphemy as Quartztusk saw it. Here was a mere dwarf attempting to emulate the god Moradin.

With a great war cry, the forces of Quartztusk charged at the giant pretend king. The first ranks fell quickly to the dancing and slicing Fierce Axe, but the next ranks pressed forward with spears and attacked the feet and legs. Roaring with pain, Silvervein kicked out at his opponents, then he staggered and tripped over a dwarven fighter. His body slammed onto the road, crushing some of the normal-sized dwarves to pulp.

Pious, wielding his blessed hammer, smote the head of the giant and after several blows managed to cave in the skull. With one last sigh, the spirit of Silvervein Moradinson left the body and, shuddering, the giant reverted back to its normal four-and-a-half-foot size.

Pious Grimm Quartztusk took the Axe of the Dwarven Lords, the Fierce Axe, and held it up for his soldiers to see. Along the road and through the gates they then went, to the Palace of Grawn where the axe could be stored in Moradin's Forge so this ridiculous war could be concluded.
