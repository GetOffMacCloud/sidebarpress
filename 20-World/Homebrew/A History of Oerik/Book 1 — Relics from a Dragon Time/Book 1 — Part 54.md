## Part 54

The apostates of Emeraldrock had unleashed elemental Hell upon the Suel, and they would pay!

The bronze of her armor blazing in the sunlight, Pope Syrul strode out into the town square then called out her Holy subjects. They must take up arms against the Highholders and destroy them.

Syrul gestured towards the sundered bastille, the greatest fortress ever built, laid low as a result of the Highholders' meddling. Was there fear in the eyes of the crowd? Yes, but more for the fear of her than the fear of war. Pope Syrul was known to break the legs of those who misinterpreted her commands.

So it was that the Holy Papacy of Risewater marched yet again on their former comrades. The Highholders had suffered even more than Risewater and had no desire for battle. With the destruction of their mines, the First Families had lost the source of their power. Unable to control their people, lawlessness had broken out, the farmers and miners now brigands and bandits.

The papists fell upon Emeraldrock. Once famed, the Highhold army was in turmoil, the few soldiers left completely outnumbered by the invaders.

Emeraldrock was quickly conquered, but Syrul found there was no subsequent control, for the banditry was endemic.

Risewater bishops were brought in to quell the chaos. Holy Wizards they were, with the teachings of legendary Win-Daled resonating within their heads.

Unfortunately for the Pope, the bishops brought dismay upon Emeraldrock but achieved little. The lands had been burned by the elementals and nothing would grow, and the mines were completely buried by the broken mountain.

Turning away from the destruction, Pope Syrul then ordered her forces to penetrate the Darkwall mountains and deal a lesson to the jann, the abominations who had brought this hardship to the Suloise...
