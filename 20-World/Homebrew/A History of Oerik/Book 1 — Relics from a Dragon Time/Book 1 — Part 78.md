## Part 78

The great spines of the Crystalmists were the center of dwarven kind. Enormous caverns were carved under the mountains, and they glittered with jewels. Above, in the valleys were giant goats and wild cattle providing milk and meat. The dwarves were prosperous and content, numbering upward of three million if the dwarven empire as a whole was counted.

Dwur covered the Jotens, the Sulhaut, the Barrier Peaks and the Crystalmists, and in addition had various holdings throughout the hills and mountains of Eastern Oerik.

Magical gates were used to connect with the continent of Mystara, east of the Solnor Sea, and to the sprawling mountains of Rockhome. The Dwur all proudly served under the benign authority of their High King and god, Moradin Soulforger.

The Prince Regent, Silvervein Moradinson, administered the empire from his home in the Crystalmists. With Moradin elsewhere, working to thwart Gruumsh and his orcish armies, the Prince Regent had big (metaphorically speaking) boots to fill.

The duergar delegation had never seen such wealth, and flaunted so ostentatiously at that. Rows of lutists, harpists and horn players greeted the duergar as they were escorted into the throne room. The grey dwarves scowled.

A feeling came over them, one they hadn't felt before: humiliation. The Overlight dwur, these flabby dwarves with nauseating pink cheeks, who had never faced off against the unspeakable horrors of Oerth as they had, who were they to be so well fed, joyful, and rich?

They would soon learn that the duergar were what remained of the True Dwur, and their grasp was as long as their righteous desperation...
