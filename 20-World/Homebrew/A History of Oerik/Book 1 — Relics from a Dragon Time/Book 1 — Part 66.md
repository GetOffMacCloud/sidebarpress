## Part 66

### 202–208 SD

Syrul the Goddess, wielding the Whip of State, was able to unite most of the Suel peoples in quick order. Norebo, like quicksilver, recognized the power of Syrul and brought his Oathers into line. Other opportunities may come.

Daring raids into the rainforests of the Singing Lands reminded the Singing Ones that the Suel were strong and not to be messed with.

Molag the Flind King was no match for a god, and his subjects were dismayed when Syrul single-handedly obliterated him in plain sight.

Gnolls, flind, bugbears and ogres were dispersed or destroyed. Confronted by the Goddess, brigands not allied with the Oathers gave up their will and loved her.

Syrul had new churches built across the Suel lands and reorganized the religious hierarchy.

There was to be no new pope. A cardinal or bishop was appointed to be the spiritual leader of important settlements.

As for the daily running of the Suloise, that lay in the hands of Suelchiefs all swearing to worship her. She was to be their only god.

In 208 SD, Syrul ordered a Suel army to magically gate across the Darkwall mountains to the west where they surprised the jann enemy.

Syrul slew the great djinn who guarded the pass, then her followers fanatically fell upon the jann, crushing them.

Syrul used her divine power to cut the jann off from Elemental Forces and the jann temples were defiled.

Syrul considered herself a very gracious god, for she allowed many jann families to migrate north after they formally surrendered.

The Suel now had the jann cities and lands. Syrul was proud of her success!
