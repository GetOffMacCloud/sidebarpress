## Part 38

### −17 SD

The Scroll of Vile Darkness was not yet fully grown. Its premature arrival on Oerth meant that it was not quite ready to corrupt all who read its monstrous contents, despite its wants and will.

The Scroll was wholly evil, but a mere magical child, and those curious souls who dared to reach out and unroll the scroll with their greedy minds felt curiously weak, and the tips of their fingers were burned, but the discomfort and injuries were transitory.

The Scroll of Vile Darkness revealed great secrets of summoning and of the Lower Planes, but the elven scholars felt uncomfortable with the scroll and looked for others to probe its depths.

The Scroll was passed through several elven communities until it crossed the border into Celene. The Celenians were less squeamish about morality, keeping human slaves and embracing some of the darker aspects of life. For a price, Celenian wizards eked out its secrets and taught Ye'Cind and Win-Daled many of the spells within.

Win-Daled had never lost the hot anger that seared her heart. Everything she did was to bring her closer to a reckoning with Dweomyne, the Whip Lady, who had taken over the throne of the Singing Lands. Let the evil queen enjoy her final moments, because Win-Daled, Heward and Ye'Cind, with their newly acquired power, would strive to bring her down, bringing solace to the spirits of those Dweomyne had destroyed.
