## Part 135

### 720–729 SD

In just nine years the kuo-toa were all but wiped out of existence.

The coastlines across eastern Oerik, Hepmonaland and Mystara were cleared of the fish men. Raiders invaded islands and coves and destroyed spawning sites. The kuo-toa around Oeridia were utterly eradicated.

Kuo-toa around western Oerik held on for a short time, and perhaps continued to exist on a few undiscovered islands although most were found and put to the sword.

The vengeance of the humans stretched even to Adarus far to the east of Mystara, and the people of the Yuan empire in the continent of Kara-Tur west of Oerik.

The elves in the west were particularly wicked. Uninvolved in human affairs (except for their large human population of slaves) these fell demi-humans teleported from island to island across the oceans, torturing and murdering peaceful kuo-toa for no reason at all but to simply revel in the genocide.

The fish people were all but defenseless. The elves of Ierendi urged restraint. Other aquatic beings including the locathah, dolphins and many sea elves pleaded for the antagonists to show mercy, but humans proved to be the worst monsters of all.

Convinced that the kuo-toa were deliberately disrupting their trade and controlling the fearful krakens, the human ships hunted down kuo-toa without pause.

Strangely, only a minority of captured kuo-toa refused to speak except to exhort their platitudes for their love of Dear Blibdoolpoolp.

Most kuo-toa begged to be unharmed. They had not even heard of Blibdoolpoolp and claimed to be entirely innocent of attacks on the harbors and shipping lanes. The kuo-toa just wanted to live.

They were not believed.

Forevermore the kuo-toa were forced to surrender their place in the warm seas of Oerth. Except for very few remote populations, by the end of 729 SD, the only kuo-toa left alive were those who fled into the subterranean black seas of the Underdark.

Oerth had lost its innocence.
