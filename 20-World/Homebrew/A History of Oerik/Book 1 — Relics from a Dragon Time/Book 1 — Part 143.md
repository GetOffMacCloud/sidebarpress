## Part 143

Hurled rocks smashed into the fortifications of the besieged Tuatha de Dannan.

The great, eerie clouds rolled in, and the giants atop them peered down at the defending demi-humans, grinning ferociously, revealing their long lower canines. From the defenders' angle it was hard to discern the giants' size, but they were easily larger than two tall men.

The giants still had a store of large brown stones, but the Tuatha de Dannan had now unsheathed their glowing swords and thrust them upwards. Long beams of white light arced out from the sword tips and reached into the clouds, lighting them up with pink flame.

Some of the giants found the cloud stuff dissolving underneath them and they fell through the fog then down onto the Thornspikes like heavy sacks of bricks.

The clouds pulled back and began circling around, looking for a new opening.

Then the hippogriff cavalry swooped in, the Celenians slashing out with flashing swords at the giants as their flying mounts scratched with their talons and tore with their beaks.

Giants dropped their rocks and took up huge maces to swipe at the hippogriffs. Many hippogriffs dodged the attacks but those struck fell to the earth lifeless, killing their elven riders as well.

The initiative turned to the giants' advantage and the heavy black clouds erupted with bright lightning accompanied by bass rumbles and crashes.

The aerial cavalry retreated from the assault which allowed the sinister clouds to then swoop back to the hill forts manned by the Tuatha de Dannan.

Thunderbolts struck with radiant power, forcing the Tuatha de Dannan to dive down below the Thornspikes.

Now the giants, at this closer distance revealed to be closer in size to three tall men, leapt off their clouds, then fell...

...no! These giants gently tumbled down from the sky as if they were made of feathers, landing upright upon their stout legs, then immediately marching forward, pulling apart the iron-like Thornspikes as if they were weeds.

<center>* * *</center>

The Battle of the Cloud Giants was fierce, long and bloody.

Every Tuatha de Dannan fought for their rare lives, and they were greatly helped by the Celenians. They fought with swords, arrows and fireballs, and even some of the native gnomes and halflings, realizing the peril of the cloud giants, added their help with short swords, hammers and slings.

<center>* * *</center>

And during the struggle, as the battle reached a frenzy, Kavoss the Cyclops, evil general of Gruumsh the Orc, materialized onto Oerik one hundred miles to the west, at the foot of the lower Crystalmists.

He had brought with him a very special army of Fire and Frost...
