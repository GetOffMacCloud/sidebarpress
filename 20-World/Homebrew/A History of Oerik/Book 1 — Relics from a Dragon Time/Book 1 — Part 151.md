## Part 151

Lathia was very pleased with Heward, for she had discovered that the Ring of Gaxx was very magical indeed.

Heward had lent her the ring, because the nymph was magically gifted. She studied it under the violet, star-filled sky and discovered something curious.

The Ring of Gaxx was not inherently evil despite its creation in UnFey. The item could be used for good.

Lathia was keen to reward her human companion for his cleverness. Many people of Fey didn't rate humans highly, thinking them nothing more than lumbering, coarse oafs.

Lathia knew better. Humans had few obvious gifts, but they knew how to break boundaries and to think outside the box. Something about human creativity made them unique.

Heward would be so happy. The intimate games and pastimes she planned...

<center>* * *</center>

Kavoss broke into Fey riding the ferocious Hucephelus. By his heels snapped yeth hounds, huge, soot-black burly beasts of fury. Several fire giants made up the party.

The yeth hounds bounded ahead, winkling out hidden sprites, brownies and pixies that could raise the alarm and alert Heward.

<center>* * *</center>

Heward was very pleased with Lathia, for she had unearthed some of the best wine that he had ever tasted.

He knew how lucky he was to have found Lathia, a clever, witty, fun-loving nymph that accepted his small faults and his sometimes boorish humanity.

Heward would have moved Lathia closer but she was already as close as two lovers could be.

<center>* * *</center>

Where was Freeberger Heward?

Kavoss terrorized the patrons of the Redcap Inn. One of these sprites, pixies or brownies would know where Heward's house was. One of them would tell!

Hucephelus couldn't contain himself.

"Munchy, munchy! Lunchy, lunchy!" he wheezed in his high-pitched whine.

<center>* * *</center>

Heward couldn't contain himself.

"There once was a maiden, tall slender and fair..."

Heward had a fine singing voice, even if a little weak, but Lathia was enjoying the stillness of the night, so she nudged him with slight annoyance.

Heward was definitely drunk now, and he struggled with his thoughts. Was Lathia enjoying his singing or not?

Then... What was that?

On the wall, his gold-burnished mirror wasn't showing his bleary-eyed reflection.

Looking out of the mirror with a puzzled expression was a middle-aged man with a dark, neat beard and a pointy hat.

A handsome devil, thought Heward, and winked.
