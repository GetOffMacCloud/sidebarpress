## Part 69

### 240 SD

Sephar had locked away the Rod of the Vaati after he had returned to his land. It was placed in a secure chamber within the citadel.

The Suel whip remained wrapped around it, for the Wind Dukes were unable to wrench the rod free from its grip. The magic was highly unstable, causing it to fire out strange effects in unexpected ways, unbearable for the highly lawful Wind Dukes.

<center>* * *</center>

Norebo had returned to the Eastern Kingdoms and the Shady Satyr. It had been a setback that Thurnfedge V had been slain, and now the Whip of State was lost to the Wind Dukes. There would be no more heir to the Suloise throne, but the Oath Society continued on as a thief and spy network linking the Flannish Eastern Kingdoms with Risewater and the other Suel settlements west of the Singing Lands.

The Oathers sucked the money away into their coffers. As a result of their rapacious behavior, the poor in the Kingdoms were increasing and banditry was on the rise.

Some escaped the difficulties by taking to the Solnor Sea, where they worked as sailors and soldiers on sea elven ships. A small few turned on the elves and became pirates.

Flan explorers then discovered lands long known to the elves, including the large island of Kodge Land, and the continent of Mystara.

Flan began to settle these places, displacing or merging with the Mystarans and creating a strong Flannish presence that would culminate far into the future with the creation of the nation called Glantri.
