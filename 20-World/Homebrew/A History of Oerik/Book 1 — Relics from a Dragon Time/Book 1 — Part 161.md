## Part 161

### 774–776 SD

Jento found it amusing that the boy considered himself to be his captive.

Jento was an Ur-Flan, and the necromancy he could unleash could hurl this primitive boy into the Negative Material Plane.

No, Jento chose to go with the boy. He was tired of the pirates anyway, uneducated oafs.

The boy was a savage, dressed in skins and equipped with just a dagger and hand axe, but there was something about him, something that made Jento look at him twice. His eyes. There was a fire in there.

Nerull led the wizard into a glade hidden by two sprawling trees. Once there he stopped then commanded the wizard to reveal his secrets.

Jento nodded, then in a broken Sheldar tongue that he hoped that the boy would understand, he agreed to teach Nerull magic as long as the boy found himself a good place to stay and brought him plenty of food and drink.

<center>* * *</center>

A couple of years passed and Jento the Ur-Flan had made a pretty good living on the outskirts of the lake lands. He had built a small wooden shack and there he was consulted by the hillmen. They came for curses, magical aid, the usual things.

It was awkward sometimes though, because the hillmen were completely in the thrall of the Queen of Chaos and her spider daughter, Lolth. Jento had to make sure that his modest aid didn't bring down their wrath. He used ravens to spy the land and he hid himself away whenever one or both demons walked nearby.

Nerull had become Jento's apprentice. He was a quick learner and growing almost as fast.

Incabulos often joined Nerull in the hut. At first Jento wasn't inclined to teach this boy too. Teaching was taxing and the likelihood that two primitives could pick up the Dark Arts was obviously slim.

Yet, Incabulos picked up on what Nerull was learning almost instinctively. He had an aura about him, an intimidating presence, as if the boy was a stone wall holding back a dark sea.

<center>* * *</center>

Traveling the trail back home with Celene shining blue, Nerull asked Incabulos if he was ready to go back with him to the Cave of Gods.

Yes, said Incabulos.
