## Part 134

The Black Pearl of Power wanted to meet those dear sweet creatures that had descended so far down into the ocean depths showing so much faith and love.

She spun her spherical form around and around, her rotations lifting her off the sea floor.

Up she surged, attracting the attention of a huge shark patrolling in the area.

In the Dragon Times sharks were enormous and cunning, and their leader was the most enormous and cunning of all.

He was Sekolah, he was three thousand years old and he had fought and defeated a leviathan, bringing its python-length tongue back to his clan as proof.

Sekolah followed the black spinning ball as it spiraled up, up into the lighter water where the fish people lived.

The kuo-toa faithful huddled in a cave, willing their zealous comrades to return from their quest to the bottom of the sea. Would that they return soon!

The cave was only partially underwater and a number of galleries were air-filled but the central cavern was filled with seawater. It was from this cavern that a human female with the head and claws of a lobster floated in and swam up to the ledge where the kuo-toa were waiting.

Here was She! Dear Blibdoolpoolp come into the heart and home of the kuo-toa people in their hour of need!

Blessed Dear Blibdoolpoolp! You have heard us! You have come!

The Black Pearl, now transformed through faith into Dear Blibdoolpoolp, used her monstrous azure lobster claws to gain purchase on the ledge and lift herself up to her People.

Blibdoolpoolp rose to her feet, arms outstretched with Acceptance and Love.

Long Bless the kuo-toa! Let them be Bathed in Warm Currents.

It was then that Sekolah struck.
