## Part 58

### 202 SD

Emeraldrock broke into eight states each ruled by a High Chief and each guarded by a tower and keep.

The Holy Papacy of Risewater was as if an island, surrounded by a sea of bandits, humanoids and mercenaries.

The elves of the Singing Ones built and prepared for invasion.

The secret Oath Society was behind it all.

For the High Chiefs were all Oathers. Money flowed in from Oathers and out to Oathers. The population hated the bandits, or the Papacy, or the High Chiefs, or the Singing Ones, but it didn't matter; the Oathers were everywhere.

Mines were reopened, armies were equipped and trained.

In Risewater the Papacy was firmly under the religion of the Goddess as interpreted by Pope Syrul, but what she didn't know was that much of the soldiery she employed were secretly Oathers.

In the Singing Lands, key Lowly Ones were Oathers and spied for Thurnfedge V and his adviser Norebo. Bandits counted Oathers amongst their numbers.

Soon the Suloise nations would implode and the Oathers would openly install Thurnfedge V ruling over His people as was His right.
