## Part 141

It was unusual for independently minded gnomes to summon a Celenian elf for help and Lord Dueillig was intrigued.

He allowed himself to be led into the dungeon where the mysterious female elf was held.

Apparently Phizzat the Ur-Flan had attempted to read the elf's mind and was now more confused than before. What else could be expected from a human magic user? Dueillig did not think that the elven newcomers were a mystery at all. Likely they were Singing Ones seeking to expand beyond the Hellfurnaces, or another nation of elves far to the west.

Lord Dueillig entered the dungeon and saw Phizzat at a desk perusing a scroll. The mage seemed to be so absorbed that he did not look up when the Celenian entered. That was an obvious pretense so Dueillig ignored the human and looked to the cell on his right. Inside the stone cell was the strange woman, still unconscious from the mind probe.

A gnome with a key led Dueillig into the cell. As he entered he noticed Phizzat give him a sideways glance. Oh, so he was interested after all? Humans were so shallow and obvious; a pity there were so many of them now.

Lord Dueillig demanded that Phizzat join him in the cell. The Ur-Flan rose and came forward.

Dueillig pointed to the sleeping elf.

"Wake her," he commanded.

Phizzat made a gesture and within seconds the prisoner opened her eyes. Dueillig peered into her face. Her skin was silver, her hair was silken and her eyes were deep gold. She stared back, wrinkled her nose, then very quietly said:

"Rrrh Ffalbh."

Dueillig turned very pale.

The fools, they hadn't captured an elf at all!

The invaders of the valley weren't elves.

This creature before him was to elf as a Celenian was to a lowly human.

She was a Tuatha de Dannan...
