## Part 150

"So, that's it?" asked Lathia, gazing at the Moonstone ring on Heward's finger.

"Yes, that's it," said Heward with satisfaction, "the very one, with a magic stone gifted by Bahamut."

"Good," said Lathia, "now you won't toss and turn so much in your sleep."

<center>* * *</center>

"Problems?" asked Gumish, raising an eyebrow.

Gaxx was working on his ring, trying to focus its magic. It should be ripping a hole between UnFey and Oerik.

But it wasn't.

Gumish smirked at his fellow horrid. Gaxx was always so full of himself. Always the smart one, always the horrid destined for greatness. Why, Gaxx had even named his ring after himself. Gaxx thought that Gumish was a good and loyal servant. Gumish was only a fair-weather friend; Gaxx should have known that.

<center>* * *</center>

Gaxx had spent the best part of the day on the ring. Gumish had retired, but Gaxx couldn't take that luxury. The cloud giants were about to invade Oerik. Without backup of the ring...

Kavoss burst open the door then stormed up to the hunched horrid, steaming with anger.

"What in Orcus's name is the problem, Gaxx?" boomed the cyclops. "I told the cloud giants that the ring was ready. Have you made me a liar, you red-carpeted beast?"

Kavoss snatched up the ring. It looked perfect, the moonstone cloudy with silvery shapes, yet it wasn't doing anything. Kavoss grabbed Gaxx and dragged the disgraced horrid to the stables.

Inside was Kavoss's steed Hucephelus. The leucrotta was chewing on something gritty and gristly.

Kavoss showed Hucephelus the ring, who wrinkled its snout then winced.

"Is this supposed to be the Ring of Gaxx?" wheezed Hucephelus with his high, weedy voice.

Gaxx tried to regain his dignity, though he had been badly manhandled by Kavoss.

"Of course it is," he snapped. "I made it myself."

The leucrotta let out a laughing squeal. "It has no dragon scent! This ring is fake."

Kavoss sighed slowly. He would discover the truth. He stared at the ring with his great eye unblinking. He glared at the Moonstone for several minutes. Eventually he closed his eye. When he opened it again the Moonstone was gone; instead a dull piece of rock sat glued within a ring of brass.

Kavoss lifted Gaxx off his ostrich feet and readied to hurl him into the maw of Hucephelus. He would pay for tricking the armies of Gruumsh!

"Wait!" cried Gaxx. "It wasn't me. Someone has substituted this for the real one. Let me prove it!"

<center>* * *</center>

Allowed back in his crag, but fiercely watched, Gaxx was able to trace the magic of his real ring. The trail led back to Fey and the District of Princess Gossermay.

Of course! fumed Gaxx, it was stolen by that thief! Heward.

<center>* * *</center>

It was too late to change the plans. The giants would fight in Oerik and they would not have the help of the ring. They would confront dwarves and elves and perhaps the Tuatha de Dannan too.

Kavoss would bring in reinforcements, frost and fire giants. Setbacks were part of war; what was important was the ability to make adjustments.

If the giants could grab a foothold, well, the Ring of Gaxx would soon be reclaimed and the hole between realities would open up just a little later than intended.

All glory to Gruumsh!
