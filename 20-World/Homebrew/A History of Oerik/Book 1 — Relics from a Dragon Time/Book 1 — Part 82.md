## Part 82

### 465 SD

Pious Grimm Quartztusk waved his war hammer.

"To me!" he yelled, and his rebels followed him over the ridge.

The ensuing battle was bloody and distasteful. How had it come to this, dwur vs. dwur? Silvervein Moradinson had proclaimed himself king, a blasphemy, but what was even worse were the dwarves who followed him still.

The rebels outnumbered them, but the Loyalists were well positioned in the mountains.

Pious Grimm had won this day, but many more battles would come. This foolishness was coming with great cost, and not just in precious dwur lives. The devious duergar, along with sizable numbers of goblins, had taken advantage of the war to seize weakly defended mines away from the fighting.

Pious knew that the war was not just in the Crystalmists, but throughout Eastern Oerik.

The Stewards had elected Lord Snuffgut to rule the dwarven lands east of the Jotens and to the edge of the Solnor Sea. He was an important ally for the rebels, and he concentrated his forces upon the duergar, while also taking on the King's Hammers.

Over in Rockhome, the dwarves had seceded from Oerik and appointed Lord Rockfoot as King. This too was a blasphemy, but Rockhome must wait. Silvervein was the main foe for now, and he must be removed from the Crystalmists.

Pious was also conscious that Rockfoot's actions brought Silvervein's forces into Mystara itself; they had forced open the locked magic gate then poured into the western Rockhome foothills vowing to slay the so-called king.

With great battles raging in Rockhome, Pious Grimm Quartztusk hoped that his own dwur would eventually prevail in the Crystalmists.

By the winter of 465 SD, Pious Grimm Quartztusk and his army were less than one hundred miles from the heartland of the Crystalmists, Silvervein the pretend king's capital.
