## Part 8

The illithids gazed upon Oerth. The tentacled, robed, bipedal octopoids were an ancient race, twisted by the Elder gods from corrupt ritual couplings of aboleths and krakens. When dragons ruled the Oerth, the illithids had psionically wandered amongst the stars. It is also thought that these dangerous beings developed the magic used in dimensional gates that allowed interstellar travel across the galaxy.

Over thousands of years, the illithids inhabited planets of lesser creatures, usually non-intelligent, and sucked their meager minds. The banal, rubbery taste failed to sate their hunger: they needed and wanted tastier thoughts. Using dimensional gates, psionics and magic combined, they looked upon the sentient worlds. Dragons were too formidable and numerous on many of these, and so it was necessary to widen the search. Across the vastness of space they traveled, gating into worlds deep within the Underdark, or sojourning upon the surface on moonless nights, for the fiery orbs of space would burn their sensitive tentacle-probes. Eventually they scried the elves of Oerth...
