## Part 65

Thurnfedge V was very angry at the Pope. She had grown very large and flew in the air! This was wrong! Norebo told him to show the Pope he was the boss!

Thurnfedge swung at Pope Syrul with his whip. He missed! Syrul's eyes got very big and colorful. The eyes were hurting him. He cried out...

Ye'Cind's recorder sounded. Syrul hissed and turned upon the Singing One, but before the Goddess could act, she found reality fragmenting around her. Bright silver, blue and golden triangles spun around her and then...

<center>* * *</center>

Syrul sat on a boulder on the bank of a slow-moving creek. There was a darkly wrapped up old woman sitting on a larger stone next to her. The old woman smiled coldly. Syrul did not like that. It was a vaguely contemptuous smile. She would wipe the smile from her face...!

Syrul felt her body spinning. The old woman picked her up in her ogrish hand. She felt small and powerless... And crumpled...

"Little god, you are lost," croaked the old woman.

The woman cackled loudly, shaking the dry leaf in her hand. Then, a young girl all in red appeared from behind the rushes.

"Grandma, let's go back to the hut, I am stewing a wolf."

The old lady dropped the leaf called Syrul and strode after her granddaughter.

Syrul was caught by the wind and sailed across time and space...

... onto a sandy arena. Thousands thronged on its outskirts, eager for blood. A young boy with a cudgel fought against a younger boy armed with a stick.

Syrul the leaf drifted into the path of the two boys. She was crunched into the sand by their feet. Unaware of the Goddess, the combatants feinted around each other with grim determination. The older boy had the height advantage but the boy with the stick was more agile. Cudgel boy finally broke through and whacked stick boy's arm. The look of surprise and hatred in the struck boy was almost demonic...

Syrul felt her reality shatter again, then, a little disoriented, she found herself back in her human form at the ceremony. Enough games! She touched Thurnfedge's arm and her godly wrath blasted him into dust particles.

Syrul bent down and took the whip that had fallen amongst the dust. Now she wished to destroy Ye'Cind, but the elf was gone!
