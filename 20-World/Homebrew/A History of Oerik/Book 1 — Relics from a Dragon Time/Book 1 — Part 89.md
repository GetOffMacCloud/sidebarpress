## Part 89

Three more missions were launched to wrest the Axe of the Dwarven Lords from the Underdark.

All three failed.

Orders were given to seal the cave openings, but the Underdark is vast, and there are always secret ways in and out.

Still, no duergar army appeared on the scene to melee with the surface dwarves. Pious Grimm Quartztusk was most concerned about the duergar living amongst them. They were an obvious danger if they still followed their king. If this was so, then their king would be foolish if he didn't use them.

Pious wanted to banish them, force them into Western Oerik, but many were now integrated in ordinary dwur society. Some had married Crystalmist dwarves.

Pious had reached out to the two Moradinson factions, despite his belief that they were misguided fools. Many dwur followed one or the other; this was a reality. If he could gain agreement with them on at least the duergar issue, that would be one less headache.

His head did ache. More than it had before. Pious fancied that the pounding was Moradin. Moradin was talking to him in a secret language, one that Pious as yet did not understand. Moradin was in trouble somehow.

Somewhere.

After taking another nip of brandy, he focused back upon reality, a reality that was heading towards another bloody war.
