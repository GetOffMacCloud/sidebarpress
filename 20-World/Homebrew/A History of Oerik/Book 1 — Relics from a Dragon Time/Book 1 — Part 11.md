## Part 11

What was Eastern Oerik like 10,200 years ago? No humans, elves predominate, but their slow reproduction rate means there are no more than a million elves on this part of the continent. They are beginning to divide into different groups.

Elves in the dense woodlands who spend much time practicing the Fey mysteries and practice great magicks are becoming the grey elves; those of the forests who prefer creating music and poetry while cavorting with dryads and nymphs and satyrs become the sylvan elves.

Some elves build villages and gardens and trade with the dwarves (who number about 250,000). They tend to be a little more organized than their brethren and have wide interests. They are destined to become the high elves.

Many wild beasts roam the lands. Lamias, griffons and hippogriffs are numerous. Dragons are still sorting out their differences in faraway places, but a handful of bronze, silver and Greyhawk dragons are taking an interest in high elf society and use their influence to order it and help to create permanent structures.

Many deer, boar, horses and aurochs wander at will and they make the lion's share of prey for the monsters, big cats and wolves. The rivers are perilous, filled with enormous fish, and the lakes are mostly unexplored.

The south lands destined to become the Bright Desert are fertile grass and woodlands, the Agrar Hills nestle snugly upon the future Rift Canyon, and the Land of Black Ice is for now just mundane tundra.

The sea is empty of pirates, the land empty of bandits, the Cairn Hills lie undisturbed, keeping their secrets.
