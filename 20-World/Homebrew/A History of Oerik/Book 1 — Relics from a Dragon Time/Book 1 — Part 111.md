## Part 111

### 529 SD

The Table of the Chiefs finally ended the Second Dwarven War.

The war had dragged on and on. There would clearly be no real victors.

The Table accepted that Moradin Soulforger had ascended to the Seven Heavens and was not going to return to his seat in the Crystalmists.

He was to rule forevermore, but only in a spiritual capacity.

It was in Readying of 529 SD that the Lords of the Dwur met atop a great slab of stone overlooking a natural grotto, in elven Blemu.

The elves had not involved themselves with the war, but they were eager for their neighbors to reach a peaceful settlement.

In the end the dwarves agreed to have no more High King.

Moradin would be the King Eternal.

The clans would each be ruled by a Lord, with meetings to be set when issues arose.

The Rockhome dwarves did not accept this and made it clear that their ruler would still be known as king. This caused severe anger at the Table, but Rockhome declared the dwur of Mystara a free people, and decided to split permanently from the Crystalmistian sphere.

Rockhome would carve out their own history and follow their own destiny.

Agreement was reached and the Dwur Empire was ended.

One group of Dwur did not have a voice at the Table.

The duergar that had chosen to live on the surface were given no official status.

It was decreed that they were to choose a Clan to obey, or delve back down to their own king in the skulking Underdark.
