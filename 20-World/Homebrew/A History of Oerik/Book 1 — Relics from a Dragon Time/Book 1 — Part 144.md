## Part 144

Ranks of frost and fire giants marched into the Crystalmists and made war upon the dwarves. The frost giants were pale as ice, full-bearded and bare-chested and wielded huge clubs and battleaxes.

The fire giants had skin of reddish orange and wore thick yellow tunics over ring mail. They grasped tremendously large swords and mauls.

The giants smashed their way through the mountains, slaying dwarf and goblin alike, and no one in their way had time to ponder on who they were and why these two different types of giants were working together.

Kavoss the Cyclops, atop his enormous grey and black striped leucrotta, Hecaphalus, watched his giants crunch through the foothills of the Crystalmists.

He smiled, his one great eye shining with pleasure. His giants were unstoppable. The dwarves and goblins would be expelled or destroyed, then Kavoss would set up fortifications. From there, the giants would move deep into the mountains, recruiting ogres, trolls and hill giants.

With this swollen army, Kavoss would soon conquer this primitive land. His master, Gruumsh, would reward him well.

Phizzat made it back to his chamber then slammed the door.

The Ur-Flan had been lucky to make it back alive! Lord Dueillig and his Celenians were doomed as far as he could tell. Even with the magical Tuatha de Dannan, he couldn't see the cloud giants losing.

He stroked his short black beard. Why was this battle happening anyway? Why would two groups of aliens fight each other in a foreign land?

The Celenians obviously knew something, but they weren't going to share it with him. Perhaps he could practice his scrying technique. He hadn't done it for a while. He was sure he had packed a crystal ball away somewhere...

<center>* * *</center>

Phizzat peered into the mists of the crystal, establishing a picture.

The mists subsided then Phizzat saw an inky black sky filled with stars, underneath which was a handsome man with long brown hair, holding a mug in one hand and a nymph in the other. He was singing loudly.

The man suddenly stopped singing and met Phizzat's eyes through the crystal.

And then the man winked.

"Hi, there, Mr. Spy," the smiling man said, "I'm Heward, and I think I'm hammered!"
