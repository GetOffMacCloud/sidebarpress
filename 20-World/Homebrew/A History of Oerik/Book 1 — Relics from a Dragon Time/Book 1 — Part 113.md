## Part 113

The Scroll of Vile Darkness was restless.

Dweomyne had achieved nothing. The elf had settled for survival over power, and the Scroll was developing true potency within the magical radiation of the Abyss. It could feel new power surging within its vellum.

In a fit of spite, the Scroll sent out magical pulses, a signal in hopes of attracting something more interesting.

One day, after a feast of devil-slugs, Dweomyne's mud hut was visited by a terrible nycadaemon. It scooped up Dweomyne in its huge arms and launched her and the Scroll into the sulfuric atmosphere with a great flap of its bat wings.

Days uncounted passed, with the once haughty Dweomyne reduced to being a plaything. The nycadaemon had taken the Scroll of Vile Darkness for itself and perused its contents each night, for it was ambitious and sought superiority over its kinfolk.

Dweomyne suffered the indignity of her situation as pragmatically as she could. She wandered the iron rooms of the iron castle that was now her home whenever the nycadaemon wasn't watching her, which was quite often. It had grown bored with her and had decided she was of little use.

Dweomyne would have escaped the castle, except it had been manifested as the tip of a mountain peak that was in the shape of a scimitar blade. Entrance and egress were impossible without flight and Dweomyne had long lost access to her spells.

With escape impossible, Dweomyne learned what she could about the nature of the Abyss.

Complicated structures were not built but manifested; the iron castle was created from the will of the most important power in the region, which in this case was the nycadaemon. It had told her when in garrulous moments that Demon Lords had manifested whole cities, and one day it would join their ranks and manifest the biggest metropolis in the Abyss.
