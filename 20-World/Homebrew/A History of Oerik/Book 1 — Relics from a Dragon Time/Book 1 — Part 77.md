## Part 77

### 459 SD

The svirfneblin were surprised to meet dwarves so deep under the Oerth. These dwarves were a dour, grey-skinned people, who spoke a different dwur language and seemed very defensive and paranoid.

The duergar didn't know what to make of the gnomes, for the diminutive beings had not yet appeared on Oerth when the duergar last walked under the sun. They took these demihuman buriers to be a potential existential threat, and this meant that the best course of action would be their utter extermination.

In 459 SD, the duergar began to realize that the svirfneblin were impossible to eradicate, as they could use magic to disappear into the walls and escape. The svirfneblin also attempted parleys, for they were not militaristic people.

Eventually it was decided that a party of duergar would travel to the surface, the Overlight, and learn as much as they could about the new arrivals. What other creatures had followed them to Oerth?

These duergar were the first of their kind to reach and penetrate the Overlight for thousands of years. They expected a barren, slave world ruled by tall elves with dark magicks.

Instead of this dystopia, which in fairness, was close to the historical reality when the grey dwur first entered the Underdark, they found a mixture of lands, some elf-dominated, but also hills and mountains and plains ruled by dwarves, gnomes, halflings and humans, and life seemed good, prosperous and fair.

The king of all dwarves, Moradin Soulforger, did not meet with the duergar, as he surely would have done, because he was currently not on Oerth.

On another world, far away, a mighty orc had raised a mighty army bent upon total war. Gruumsh was his name, and he was clever, strong, cunning and ambitious. He had somehow gained vast power and had survived blows that would have felled any mortal. Moradin had gone to check the orcish gains.

Silvervein Moradinson, one of Moradin's mortal descendants, had been appointed Regent for the length of Moradin's absence. Silvervein wielded a stout, magical weapon called the Fierce Axe, or, as it was also known, the Axe of the Dwarven Lords. Silvervein himself had made this great axe alongside Moradin. The axe conferred authority over all the dwarves of Oerth.

So it was Regent Silvervein Moradinson who excitedly met with the duergar delegation from the mysterious Underdark. He hoped to unite with his lost cousins and forge a bright future together.
