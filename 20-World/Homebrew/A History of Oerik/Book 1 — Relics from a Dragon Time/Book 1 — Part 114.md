## Part 114

Dweomyne had not sung since she had been imprisoned in the iron castle.

She had no wish for the nycadaemon to hear her supernatural voice so she gave nary a hint that she could sing at all. But when the nycadaemon had gathered together a clutch of spiky, gaunt, insectoid mezzodaemons, then left with them to raid another domain of the Abyss, she gave in to the pleasure.

The singing surged through the castle and disturbed the brooding of the Scroll of Vile Darkness.

Why had the nycadaemon not taken it on the expedition? Was the Scroll to be taken for granted? Dweomyne's song reminded the Scroll that the elf still had potential...

Suddenly Dweomyne thought of the Scroll. She stopped singing and tried to remember where it was. Of course, it was up in the nycadaemon's private chamber.

Happiness surged in her chest as she danced up the spiral staircase and entered the room.

It was not even locked. The dretch that cleaned the place were not intelligent enough to not lose a key.

Dweomyne saw the Scroll of Vile Darkness lying atop a small table. She grabbed at it but yelped in pain. A surge of dark magic scorched her arm. Dweomyne watched the Scroll vibrate and smoke, then it settled.

Dweomyne reached for it again, and this time she could take it without injury.

The Scroll was back in her possession.

Ambition whirled within her.

Why had she been so passive? What was survival without power?

Dweomyne laughed, high and strange. Her confidence was back.

"Hello, Abyss," she said, "welcome to your new ruler."
