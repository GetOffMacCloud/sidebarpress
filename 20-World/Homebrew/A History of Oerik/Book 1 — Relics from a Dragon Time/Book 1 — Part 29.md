## Part 29

The serpents had summoned the Whip Lady.

Telepathically linked to the reptiles, Dweomyne had seen the intruders move through the temple. She had been feeling a little lazy lately as the banquet she had attended two evenings ago (for something or other) was still sitting heavily in her stomach. Some action would be good for her metabolism.

Dweomyne arrived too late to save the worshippers, but most of them were unimportant cattle anyway. She was more concerned that some people would have the audacity to blunder in and raid the place. The old ways were dead. The demons were in charge.

She stood on the ceiling, whip curling with anticipation. Gal-Lek and Esmir glanced at each other. They had not expected the Whip Lady. This particular temple was small and located on the outskirts of the main city. What was she doing here? The whip flicked out...

Terrible searing pain shot through their bodies like searing oil. Collapsing onto the marbled, bloodstained floor, Gal-Lek and Esmir spasmed and jerked in agony. The Whip Lady looked down at them with an ugly scowl. She recognized Esmir. He was the weedy husband of Iona, a pathetic merchant who was obviously out for revenge, because she had slain his dear little wife.

The pain would be just the start.

Blissfully unaware of their parents' demise, Win-Daled the Lowly One and Ye'Cind the Singing One diligently continued their training in Blemu. The Adri Forest was awash with golden brown leaves, for it was autumn. They had not been told about the reasons their fathers had returned to the Singing Lands, presumably for business purposes. Adults were strange and inconsistent after all.

When the wind grew chiller, they grew concerned and asked for help from their minders. The Blemu elves used their magic and spies to investigate. To their horror, they found that the Singing Lands had fallen into decadence and evil. Demons wandered the land, the Fey rituals were mocked and cruel rulers governed over all. The Punishers zealously carried out their unjust judgments, and they had executed Esmir and Gal-Lek. Win-Daled had lost her father and young Ye'Cind was now an orphan.

That night, in their room, Win-Daled and Ye'Cind made a pact with each other. They would grow up, here in the Adri, and learn all they could about magic and might. And then... and then they would return to the Singing Lands and kill the Whip Lady.
