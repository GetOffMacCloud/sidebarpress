## Part 158

The Queen of Chaos loomed over Lolth and Miska.

She was a bubbling, festering gelatinous mass of elf, octopus and ochre jelly. Tentacles and mucus were her most obvious traits.

Why had her Children brought her back to Oerik? She attempted to listen as the two demonic siblings hissed and screeched profanities against each other. All irrelevant; how disappointing. She had expected much better. This flyblown bite of land was of no consequence. Didn't she explain this to them?

Conquering the Singing Lands and wresting back her whip from the Wind Dukes were the goals. Why had Miska and Lolth forgotten this? Why did they care about who was a god and who wasn't? What glory was there in ruling over a tribe of savages?

The Queen of Chaos had reached her limit. She gestured violently, which created a dark grey, bubbling porridge, splattering and steaming on the cave floor. Against their protestations, the Queen snatched her wayward children in her slimy tentacles then tossed the two spider demons into the mess. With a gurgle, Lolth and Miska were sucked down out of sight.

Safely imprisoned, now the Queen of Chaos could restore good order!

<center>* * *</center>

The Queen, now proclaimed Mistress of the Martial Arts, took over the administration of the hillmen. With her careful guidance she felt that they may yet be molded into some kind of army, though there were individuals that would be best pickled in jelly. Such a shame she had no humanoids or reptilians in her new army. It had been pathetic that Lolth had eaten instead of recruited. Always thought with her stomach, Lolth did!

<center>* * *</center>

Goods and supplies from the Abyss, fetched by her dretch, had made the cave homely. The Queen of Chaos now sat upon her puce throne and planned her next move.

Outside, near the water-filled caldera, the hill people gathered to give praise to their new goddess that had unexpectedly arrived.

<center>* * *</center>

A boy, maybe eight or nine, pointed to the cave mouth a small distance from the lake. His companion, another boy of about the same age, nodded. As the adults conducted their ceremony the two boys, Nerull and Incabulos, snuck into the cave.

They wanted to find her magic power.
