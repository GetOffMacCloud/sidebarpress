## Part 93

The peoples of northwest Oerik were known as the Pyanor. They sailed the northern sea and set up many trade settlements that grew into large, prosperous cities. The Pyanor were aggressive and their empire swallowed up many of the nomadic tribes that traversed this large land, and these nomads became agricultural farmers and fed the cities.

Other nomads further south and west maintained their lifestyle, and there was some admixture with the new farmers. Bandits were a constant problem.

The Pyanor had local Pashas, knights, who maintained law and order. They met at Council every two years or as required to lodge and revise laws, resolve grievances or make war.

The Pyanor lands stretched out east to the borders of the Vaati, the Wind Dukes. The Vaati had built artificial walls, moats and trenches to keep the Pyanor out and their slaves in, but breaches were made and the Wind Dukes were forced to force out regular incursions.

The Vaati were great magicians and they had thousands of warrior slaves to hand, yet, it was becoming clear that the Pyanor were growing in power and numbers, and eventually would become a serious threat.

Someday, only a great war would determine the fate of the two realms.

And they would be ready.
