## Part 86

### 468 SD

Icy rain fell the night the duergar made their move.

Grey dwur crept into the Grawn prison, snuck past or slew guards they encountered and released many of the prisoners. Ragged dwur ran into the cold air whooping and hollering.

Grawn quickly fell into an uproar, dwarves running everywhere as residents woke up and moved onto the streets trying to figure out what was happening.

Plan A completed, the duergar headed to the sacred heart of Grawn, Moradin's workshop.

With all the excitement, most of the guards had run off to capture the escapees. This meant that the duergar only needed to confront a few old clerics who attended to the sacred place. This done, they cleaned their daggers then entered the workshop.

At the center of the room was Moradin's Forge, which the absent god had used to make wondrous things, or so the story went. Moradin hadn't made anything for the true dwur, the duergar. He had forgotten about them!

Past the Forge, hanging next to the Sacred Apron, was the Fierce Axe, the Axe of the Dwarven Lords. The head duergar took down this symbol of dwarven authority and admired it briefly, turning the blade this way and that, inspecting it.

Yes, this was the Axe.

The duergar covered themselves in shadow and fled, making their way to the secret tunnel that led down into the Underdark.

<center>* * *</center>

Pious Grimm Quartztusk, High Steward of the Crystalmists, was furious.

The Axe was lost! Stolen by the grey dwarves, the duergar, who had also managed to open the jail and free the prisoners, not a few who were political prisoners even now amongst allies plotting revenge.

That damned King Silvervein! It was he who had opened up the Underdark and allowed its corruption to leach into their land. The grey dwarves did not even recognize Moradin as their god!
