## Part 56

### 198 SD

Thurnfedge V absently stroked the whip on his lap, the whip taken by Win-Daled from Dweomyne, and the symbol of his legitimate right to rule.

Norebo looked into the eyes of his king with barely concealed contempt. Thurnfedge V had watery eyes, devoid of color. His teeth were clenched and his nose leaked. It was unfortunate that the noble line had ended up with him. At least he was easily impressed by others, and he fortunately doted upon the sleek Thief Merchant standing before him.

"Then what is to be done?" asked Thurnfedge helplessly.

Thurnfedge never knew what was to be done.

Norebo sighed and pushed a lock of dark hair away from his brow.

"Leave it with me," he said.

In fact, Norebo was not overly concerned with the collapse of the economy in Emeraldrock (which they had been discussing). With Highhold in chaos, and with the Holy Papacy hanging tenuously to its borders, it was going to get pretty rough...

Norebo knew that his prosperity was secure, because he was a member of the Oath, a secret society based in the eastern kingdoms on the coast of the Solnor Sea. The Oath had feelers everywhere. Most of the bandit companies were Oath members, and there were even a handful of Lowly Ones in the Singing Lands who were Brothers.

The major Thief Houses were all Oathers, and lucrative trade passed easily between them. The lands around could be poor, but the Oath Society was only increasing its wealth.

Norebo knew this, because he was the Merchant Prince overseeing it all.

Originally from the east, Norebo had a way of blending in with the Suloise, even with his coppery skin and dark hair. His subtle magic allowed him to be accepted everywhere.

In actuality, Norebo was not concerned about anything much; he found life easy and enjoyable. It was fun to rule behind the scenes watching money flow into Oather coffers. The trick was to stay in the shadows and let the ignorant do the work.
