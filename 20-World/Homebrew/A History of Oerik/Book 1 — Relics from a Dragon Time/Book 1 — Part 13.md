## Part 13

10,200 years ago, Dwurin Sourshadow, the Dwarven Merchant of Fine Wares, was going for a stroll at twilight just outside the high elven town of Fairfeather near the Fals River in what was then lush woodland.[^1] He had had a good run in Fairfeather, and his pouch clinked with coin.

He planned a little dalliance with an elven dancer he had seen performing at a dwarven tavern. She was a free spirit and she seemed quite taken with the ruddy-cheeked, bearded Dwurin (though he thought it more than possible his money had more to do with it than his looks). Being a conscientious fellow, he was quite happy to meet her on her own terms, whatever that may be.

Dwurin spied a low treehouse lit by little magical lights. This was the place. As he approached he felt a strange throbbing in his head that knocked him down to his knees. The lights went out. A heartbeat or two later Dwurin saw a door in the treehouse wrench open which disgorged three tall robed figures that jumped out onto the ground. With them was an unconscious figure that two of them carried, straddled and bound. It was the dancer.

The robed figures moved quickly through the gloom and were seemingly unaware of his presence. In fact, they drew close enough at one stage for Dwurin to make out their faces: octopoid with tentacles writhing. He had heard tales of such creatures. Mind flayers!

Dwurin dutifully reported what he saw to the local elf lord, Gollendi Fairfeather. Gollendi was a little untrusting of the dwarf, but such things as elvish abductions were not unknown, so after condemning the merchant for failing to stop the mind flayers, he and the elvish elders agreed to investigate the matter.

Elven witch knights utilized charmed brain moles to trace the psionic resonances left behind by the illithids and found that they were too late. The mind flayers had succeeded in ferrying the elf maid far and away to a distant world where there was no salvation.

[^1]: Near modern Mitrik in Veluna.
