## Part 31

### −20 SD

Demon worshippers from MaiYuan spread their philosophy to Eastern Oerik which attracted interest amongst diverse peoples. Demonic shrines popped up in quiet, hidden away places where congregants would secretly go to dabble in necromancy, demonology and other evil magicks.

The Celenian elves became very much enamored with the cults of the MaiYuan, the Empire of Merchants across the seas, as did the Singing Ones.

A number of Flan formed small little cults where they plotted small little plans, despite the Blemu anathema to such worship. The Blemu leadership was actually quite worried, because demon worship and the philosophy of rampant hedonism was close to their own beliefs, but the Blemu tempered their Fey hedonism with compassion and empathy.

Win-Daled, Ye'Cind and Heward, the three Heroes, were tasked with winkling out these hidden shrines of evil, but when ideas take hold and when they promote power without consequence, new weeds spring up just as others are cut down. Win-Daled reasoned that unless the evil could be eliminated at the source, the demon cults would continue to grasp and swallow in the good people of Eastern Oerik.
