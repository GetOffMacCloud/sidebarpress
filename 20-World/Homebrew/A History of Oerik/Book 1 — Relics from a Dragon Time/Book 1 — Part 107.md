## Part 107

The sword plunged down then flew out of the abbot's hand.

An arrow vibrated in its hilt. Corellon Larethian leapt to the altar, another arrow already nocked in their bow. The abbot backed away astonished and the Prelate stood there stunned.

Corellon went to the prone body of Moradin and in one move tucked their bow away then drew out a shining sword and menaced the old abbot with it.

"Foul servant of Gruumsh, reveal yourself!"

The dwarf congregation suddenly came to life and full of fury and fear, flew towards the elf god. Corellon Larethian raised their arm, creating a shockwave that knocked most of the dwarves down.

"Hear me out," they announced with an even tone. "The abbot was stabbing Moradin Soulforger."

"Like he was supposed to do," countered the Prelate. "Moradin must be bled by the sword to remove the Godrot that had infected his body."

"Yes," answered Corellon, "but not with a sword in its turn envenomed with Godrot. Behold! The illusion is lifted!"

A blinding flash, then where the feeble abbot had been was the Cyclops Kavoss, in an awkward angle, bent over with his shoulder blades touching the ceiling. The sword that he had held lay in the corner, the arrow stuck in it. It was now clear that a viscous gummy substance oozed over the blade.

Kavoss roared and rushed Corellon with his bulk, but his size made sudden movement in what was for him a confined space rather difficult. Corellon's sword danced then Kavoss was bleeding in three places. Kavoss let out a howling curse then dematerialized, leaving Corellon alone with the dwarves.

<center>* * *</center>

The ritual to save Moradin was restarted and at the important point Corellon themselves performed the bleeding with their own sword. With the gentlest caress, the super-sharp blade nicked Moradin's sallow skin and released the Godrot.
