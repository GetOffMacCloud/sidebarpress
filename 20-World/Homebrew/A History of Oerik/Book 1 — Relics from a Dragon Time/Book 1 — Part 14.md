## Part 14

> *And my mind filled with thoughts not of mineself. Of anger there be none, of frustration there be none, of compassion there be none. Only the pounding pounding of happiness in servitude.*
>
> — *The Book of Trank*, Chapter XII, verse VII

The mind flayer worlds appeared untouchable. Who could resist the illithid psionic blasts? Yet, the anger grew within the elven Eastern Oerik. Were the alien abductions going to go on forever? Could nothing be done?

Three elvish witch knights joined forces with two dwarven warriors to attempt a rescue of the forlorn thralls of the illithids. The witch knights could harness mental powers of their own; for instance, they had mastered telekinesis and telepathy. They also bathed in mystical liquids that endowed them with supernatural abilities.

The three rebel witch knights were condemned by the other members of the community. The mind flayers were an ancient people forged in the Dragon Times. They were not to be trifled with, for their wrath could be catastrophic for Oerth.

Nonetheless, the doughty adventurers planned to enter a mind flayer enclave and rescue as many abducted elves (if still alive) as they could. A brain mole was stolen to help zone in on the correct location. The witch knights shape-changed the party into mind flayers and infiltrated their way in.

Mingling amongst the illithids was not too problematic, but getting in to see the elvish abductees was a problem. Only illithid Selectors were allowed into the slave pens, and they had to study, taste and experiment for years before they were admitted to their illustrious place within mind flayer society. Brain cultivation was a rarefied position that only few could hope to achieve.

Without qualifications, our heroes couldn't hope to legitimately enter and free the slaves. The fateful decision was made to storm the area with their mighty axes and their mighty magicks. Although the Selectors were few, they were among the greatest minds in illithid society. The psionic blasts unleashed upon the interlopers pulled flesh from bone, liquefied their brains and snuffed them all from existence.

The illithids discovered the charmed brain mole and used the poor animal to locate the origin of the intruders.

It was Oerth, and that world would pay...
