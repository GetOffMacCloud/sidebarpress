## Part 28

When Dweomyne was very small, a succubus came into her bedroom every night to hear her sing. Dweomyne adored the succubus, who was tall and lithe, with short crimson horns poking out from long dark tresses. The succubus was apparently very interested in the little girl, demanding that she sing for her.

Dweomyne had a soaring voice that stood out even amongst the Singing Ones, and she could sing softly and delicately, like a cello, or loudly and assertively like a brass orchestra.

Dweomyne was precocious and fickle. Sometimes she wouldn't sing unless the succubus gave her a treat, and sometimes she would sing a happy song when the succubus wanted a tragic one, or she would deliberately change the words and make up nonsense.

The succubus liked the irrationality of the little elf girl. The Singing One was free-spirited, as was she. The songs she responded to the most, though, were angry, vengeful ballads. They made the ichor quiver in her twisted veins, and whenever she was lucky enough to have Dweomyne sing one of those, she would be motivated to stir up delicious trouble when she returned to her home in the Abyss.

The succubus was fickle too. Sometimes, when a song didn't particularly please her, Dweomyne would find sores magically erupt in her skin that would heal very slowly and painfully. Sometimes the succubus would summon big black demon beetles that would scuttle around the room and leave deposits everywhere.

Dweomyne never told on the succubus, for despite the occasional tortures and difficulties, she took the demon to be her special secret friend.

The succubus could be generous too. From time to time the bat-winged demoness would regale Dweomyne with her exploits in the Abyss, her head collection, and useful torture strategies.

One night, after a particularly tormented song involving a slave girl, Dweomyne was gifted with a whip with special powers of pain and death.

<center>* * *</center>

Dweomyne grew up a lover of debauchery, mischief and spite, fed by her relationship with her nightly friend. She led sordid rituals in the once Fey temples now converted to demonology, attended wild parties where she seduced, poisoned and lied her way into the good graces of influential people, and finally gained her place as the head of the Punishers, the Singing Lands' secret police.

Dweomyne became known as the Whip Lady, and it turned out that, like her singing, she was very good at her job...
