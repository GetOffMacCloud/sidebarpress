## Part 20

What of the elves of Western Oerik? Warmer and drier than much of the East, few elves lived this side of the mountains. With magical gates being easily created, the elves of this part of Oerik had actually arrived from the distant continent of Kara-Tur and had little in common with the elves of Eastern Oerik.

The Kara-Turan elves, physically residing on the other side of the globe, lived west of the vast mountain ranges that divide much of the temperate lands of Kara-Tur, running north and south of the continent like a dragon's spine.

To the east, what is now Shou Lung, were the lands of the tengu and kenku, bird people. Their history is complex, and the differences between the lifestyles of the hunter-gatherer tengu and the farming and city-building kenku were mainly a result of differences in religions.

South in the tropical jungles and grassland were numerous human nations that adapted quite quickly after their emancipation from the mind flayers. Their descendants were destined to eventually conquer the bird people and found Shou Lung.

The elves of Kara-Tur were tall with violet, pink, blue, white or brown hair and eyes that were often silvery. A few of these elvish tribes desired freedom from the status quo and sought to form new ways of living far from their brethren. Western Oerik was explored, but few areas were to their liking, Western Oerik being dusty, beige and uninteresting, with stunted, ugly trees and hard, rocky terrain.

One exception was the subtropical basin of rainforest southwest of the Hellfurnaces. It was green and beautiful, with brightly colored birds and beetles as beautiful as gemstones.

The elves were not alone. Humans had already inhabited the rainforest but were not thriving there, being vindictive and cruel, splintered into tiny tribes that warred constantly. The humans were easy prey for the elves, who quickly enslaved them and set up a new elven nation in Oerik.
