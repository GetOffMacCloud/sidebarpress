## Part 63

The ear-piercing squeal echoed through the throne room. Ye'Cind frowned; that noise wasn't supposed to happen. She continued blowing into her recorder and instead of unoerthly music, she produced lowing, squawking and screeching.

"Interesting instrument," said Pope Syrul mildly, eyes glinting, "but I doubt it will catch on."

Syrul turned to Norebo.

"Is this what you brought the elf for, my Lord? Perhaps she will scare the gnolls away?"

The Pope's laughter burned Norebo's ears.

<center>* * *</center>

Ye'Cind in her quarters investigated her wooden recorder. She felt embarrassed for Norebo and embarrassed for herself. She wanted the dark-haired Flan to regain his faith in her. What had gone wrong with her magic flute?

Upon inspection, everything seemed to be in order. The fine dustings of emerald, jasper, jacinth and sapphire were intact. The dark Fey wood was as smooth and polished as ever.

Then she found the dweomer causing the problem: a subtle twisted curse had been placed upon it. Did Pope Syrul place it there? If so, how? Only a powerful being could have secretly cast the spell whilst being fully observed.

Never mind that mystery for now. How to counter the curse? She set to work.

<center>* * *</center>

Thousands of worshippers gathered to mark the Month of the Rising Lammasu, the most holy month of the year. In the moonlight they sang, danced and waved staves of fire.

Pope Syrul and her Cardinals stood on a stone formation and directed the service. The bishops stood below the stone guarding each compass point. There was a light spattering of cool rain that gutted the flames and was not unwelcome on this hot night. Sitting on chairs on a slope behind the ceremony were Thurnfedge V, Norebo and another few VIPs stoically watching the proceedings. Ye'Cind had not been invited.

The ceremony continued, pinkish, luminous clouds appearing above the worshippers and coalescing around the Pope and her Cardinals.

At the sight of this, the worshippers began screaming ecstatically and threw themselves to the ground, thrashing their limbs in every direction.

"All for the Goddess!" cried Pope Syrul.

"All for the Goddess!" echoed the Cardinals and bishops.

"Aaaaaaaaallllllllllooooooorrrroooodddddeeeesssss!!!" cried the crowd.

"All for the Goddess," muttered Ye'Cind, draped in an elven cloak, hiding forty yards away, then she blew on her recorder...
