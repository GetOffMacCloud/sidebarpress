## Part 62

Thurnfedge V was confused. What was a Singing One doing here?

Norebo and Ye'Cind had gated into Risewater and entered the throne room. Norebo explained that the Singing One was called Ye'Cind, and was here as a friend. But how was that possible? The lady was an elf, and elves were enemies.

Norebo asked Ye'Cind to play for the monarch.

Never had Thurnfedge heard such heavenly sounds that emitted from her recorder. Unfortunately, he had no taste for music. Pleasant, he thought, but she's still an elf.

When the piece was over Norebo explained to Thurnfedge that Ye'Cind was here for his benefit, but how could that be?

"Don't you hate me, elf?" he whined.

Ye'Cind glanced with worry at Norebo, who gave her a cunning look and winked.

"Now, why would she hate you, your Greatness? This is the same Ye'Cind that helped Win-Daled liberate our people."

Win-Daled was a woman in the past that Thurnfedge remembered hearing about. She had helped free the Suel with a man from the east and a Singing One? He wasn't sure.

A courtier came into the room and announced the presence of the Pope. Norebo rubbed his hands. Now they were getting somewhere!

Pope Syrul, draped in scarlet, flanked by two Cardinals in red and orange, flounced into the chamber.

"Ah, so these are the visitors," said Pope Syrul addressing Thurnfedge V, then she turned to Norebo.

"Master Norebo, I thought you left these parts. The gnolls outside our gates were bothering you, hmm?"

Norebo smiled.

"I went back for reinforcements. And here she is." He gestured at Ye'Cind.

"An elf? A Singing One? Is she one of our spies?"

"More a secret weapon," said Norebo.

"We want to know about the Goddess," said Ye'Cind bluntly.

The Pope looked at her, eyes narrowing.

"The Goddess? You wouldn't know about her, would you? She is pure Goodness, pure Glory, full of Love and Nourishment! She..."

"Specifically," interrupted Norebo, "who in fact IS the Goddess?"

Syrul paused, her face radiant.

"I am forbidden from pronouncing her once Mortal name," said the Pope, "but She is known to us all. It was She that promoted great Thurnfedge V's ancestor to the throne."

She means Win-Daled, thought Ye'Cind. Was her former companion now a god? Ye'Cind looked intently at Pope Syrul. Was the woman lying? If Win-Daled had ascended to godhood then, as her friend, wouldn't she know?

Ye'Cind pulled out her recorder and placed it to her lips...
