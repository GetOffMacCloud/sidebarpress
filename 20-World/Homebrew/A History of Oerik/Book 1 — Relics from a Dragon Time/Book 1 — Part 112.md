## Part 112

### 579 SD

The return of Dweomyne the Whip Lady to Oerth would seem to be most improbable.

Once the Elven Queen of the Singing Lands, that elven domain southwest of the Hellfurnaces, she had ruled with strength and terror.

Then the Lowly One, Win-Daled, Heward of Eastern Oerik, and Ye'Cind the Singing One traitor had vanquished her, taken her magic whip and plunged her into the Abyss.

Centuries had passed.

Dweomyne lived on spiders, scorpions, slugs, and semi-poisonous berries, and drank oily water with a coppery aftertaste.

The vista was mostly unchanging: marshland with an occasional twisted tree, hillock or low mountain.

The temperature was unchangeably cold, just below comfort. Her skin constantly stung from the tiny insect clouds that infested the air, and the fens around her occasionally erupted with sulfuric belches.

The Scroll of Vile Darkness that she possessed was at first her only guide. The Scroll was slowly lengthening as it matured and new information and spells appeared therein.

Hideous and pathetic dretch, demons with potbellies and spindly arms and legs, wandered the land, and these semi-demons were eventually bullied by Dweomyne into servitude.

The dretch built a mud hut for her, and they scouted and patrolled her territory, warning her of the more dangerous monsters and demons that also inhabited the fens.

Fortunately, encounters with such creatures were rare, as it seemed she resided in an insignificant corner of the Abyss.

Dweomyne lived this way for years and decades and centuries, and, over time, the Abyss changed her. Her silvery skin became blotchy blue and grey, her thick and glossy hair was now thin and brittle, striped black and creamy brown, and weird wriggling worm-like nubs appeared all over her legs. The only pleasure still afforded her was her supernatural singing voice that had not changed...
