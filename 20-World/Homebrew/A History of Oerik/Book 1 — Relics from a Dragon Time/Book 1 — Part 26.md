## Part 26

### −42 SD

The year the demons came to Oerth.

The MaiYuan empire, straddling the tropical regions of Hepmonaland and Kara-Tur, dabbled in demonology, and the power gained from such dark magic encouraged other priests and wizards to turn from their moderate paths. Before long, large sections of the population worshipped the monstrous Demogorgon and other, even weirder things.

The Kara-Turan elves fell into complete debauchery, and divided further into separate entities. The WuzXi were the most extreme, becoming self-worshippers and embracing pure selfishness. The other nations (or schisms) partook of foul and degrading hedonism, corrupting the ancient Fey rituals and embracing cruelty and power. The Singing Lands, in the southeastern corner of Western Oerik, fell further into vice.

Ye'Cind's mother, Iona, was a powerful member of a moderate faction within the Singing Lands, working to resist demon and devil activity. She also advocated for more rights for their slaves, the Lowly Ones. Unfortunately, Singing One society was more enamored with the leadership and values of Dweomyne, the Whip Lady.

Dweomyne was a charismatic Demonancer who openly championed Might versus Right and Vice over Virtue. She carried a magical whip that could inflict severe pain and death with a single lash. Under her stewardship of the Punishment Ministry, she ruled with an iron grip. Dissenters were tortured, slain or banished, and the human slaves, the Lowly Ones, were watched and stalked by monstrous beings of the Lower Planes, who sometimes killed the humans for the sheer pleasure of it.

Iona found her friends and allies melting away, and soon she and her remaining followers were firmly on the outer.

They came, the Whip Lady and her demons, to arrest Iona and execute her. Iona was forewarned by a Lowly One spy, but before she could flee, Dweomyne and her goons cornered her in her chambers.

While her bodyguards fell to the demons, Iona cast a spell to teleport herself away to her family conducting business in Eastern Oerik. She nearly cast it in time, but Dweomyne was too quick with her whip and only Iona's rolling head made it through the magical gate...
