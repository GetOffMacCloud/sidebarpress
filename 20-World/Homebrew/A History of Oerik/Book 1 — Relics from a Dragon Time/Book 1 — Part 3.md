## Part 3

### Olve

Olve inherited the world.

Sprites, pixies, grigs and satyrs frolicked in the meadows, forests and thickets.

Thousands of years pass.

Olve deep in the mysteries of Fey became the grey elves. Olve on the coast became enamored of pearls and other shiny things. They develop sailing and settle on many islands across the ocean. Other elves attempted to take to the mountains and mine for beautiful gems and minerals, but most hated being underground for long and didn't like to work. They returned to the lowlands.

In another world, Olve discovered a different demi-human, the dwarves. Olve brought into Oerik a few clans, magically charmed to be docile and servile. The dwarves seemed to like dirty and ugly things and thrived in dark places, because they were inferior and lowly creations, not like the transcendent Olve lovingly carved by the greatest Dragons.

Many dwarves were sent into the mountains to mine beautiful stones for the elves.

Although there were a few fell monsters, loose relics of the Dragon Age, that roamed the empty places, the beautiful creations, the centaurs, unicorns, and pegasi abounded. It was a good time to be Olve.
