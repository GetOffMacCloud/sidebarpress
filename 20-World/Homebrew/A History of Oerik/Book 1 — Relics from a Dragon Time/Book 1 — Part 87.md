## Part 87

King Druzz of the duergar shifted the haft of his axe. He was quietly confident, but he would be ready if the enemy did make it through.

The axe was the Axe of the Dwarven Lords, and now it belonged in the Underdark.

Druzz was waiting at the end of a long wide tunnel. Marching feet and muffled voices echoed down from a distant branch. The king tensed, only a thin sheen of sweat signified any nervousness.

The sounds grew slightly louder, then louder... the first ranks of Overlight dwur must be reaching the bend!

Then the screaming started.

<center>* * *</center>

It had been five weeks, and the dwarven sortie sent into the Underdark to retrieve the Axe had not returned.

Pious Grimm Quartztusk realized that he had failed. He knew that invading the Underdark would be perilous, but it still stung. More good lives had been lost.

Unfortunately, there were even more pressing problems. Two of the escaped political prisoners, each a Moradinson and cousin to the late Silvervein, had gathered a large number of supporters around them.

The Crystalmist dwur were now divided. Many supported Pious, but with the Axe lost, a growing minority were of the opinion that Silvervein had been a true king after all.

One of the Moradinson cousins (as Silvervein had left no heirs), it was suggested, should be appointed as a new king.

O, Moradin, Return Great King! Return!

Pious Grimm Quartztusk felt his head aching again. He poured himself a mug of fortified wine when a messenger ran into the chamber and urged him to get to the main gate. Pious found a large group of duergar gathering there. They looked officious, wrapped in silver and bronze-colored capes. One carried a flag of truce.

Pious realized that they were waiting for him. He quickly gathered his bodyguard and climbed the stairs to meet them.

As he reached the gates, he saw that the duergar were all heavily armed and armored underneath their capes. Would they attack? Pious paused a moment, but not one duergar attempted to force the gate. Pious moved on.

One duergar, grey-skinned like the others, but with unusually piebald hair, raised a hand in salute.

"Hail, Pious Grimm, leader of the Crystalmist people," he said. "I, Turiziz, Fane of Great Bore, home of the great duergar nation, Command thee to submit to our King, King of All, holder of the Fierce Axe, King Druzz the Terrible! Submit or die!"
