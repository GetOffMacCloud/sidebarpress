## Part 126

### 658–685 SD

Thytic buccaneers returned to the Azure Sea of eastern Oerik in war galleys that eclipsed the Flannish ships. Using hidden coves down the coastline of Hepmonaland and the archipelago stretching across the Pearl Sea, they were well organized with manpower, weapons and supplies.

By 658 SD the Flan naval power was diminished. Flan traders were forced to use Thytic-owned harbors and pay heavy tolls.

The MaiYuan empire took notice at last. The population numbered in the tens of millions, mostly peasants who had no access to the magical gates that led to the continent of Kara-Tur and the other half of the empire. Some of them saw an opportunity for garnering wealth, signing up on Thytic ships. The Thytics exploited them and most MaiYuan ended up as menial laborers and servants, one step up from the slaves taken from Oeridia and elsewhere.

There were exceptions, and a handful of experienced MaiYuan sailors turned pirate. Their pirate ships were sailed vessels modeled after the sea elves, who still plied unmolested around the Solnor Sea, deigning to stay neutral amongst the human squabbling.

The MaiYuan ships were small but fast and very maneuverable. They used fire to bring down the war galleys, then sailed out of bowshot and waited. Merchant ships were attacked at night using bugbear mercenaries. These large, hairy goblinoids could move swiftly and silently, and their ability to see in the dark meant an undetected boarding gave them a huge advantage.

Within decades the MaiYuan empire became more insular. Although the rich and powerful still moved throughout the two continents, the Great Ladies and Lords of Hepmonaland recognized that the Kara-Turan portion of the Empire was geographically very far away, and the Lords and Ladies there had barely taken an interest in Thytic intrusions, whoever they were.

Hepmonaland was essentially on their own.
