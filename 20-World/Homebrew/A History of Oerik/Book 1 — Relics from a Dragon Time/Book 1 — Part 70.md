## Part 70

### 313 SD

After Norebo retired as the head of the Oath Society, several splinter groups formed and vied against each other. The eventual winner was called the Conglomerate, which took control of the Eastern Kingdoms of the Flanaess. The kings became Investors, and the actual head was known as the Magnate.

The Conglomerate sourced and guaranteed funds required for big projects, and these projects were big indeed.

Cities bustled with inns, hotels and gambling houses. Tourism was a big supporter of the economy, with elves, dwarves, halflings and gnomes actively encouraged, as were the Flan from further west, all coming to procure in delight.

Statues of robed merchants greet travelers on wide roads. Markets rich in food and drink are everywhere, for the Conglomerate is generous, as long as you can pay.

The first universities and colleges of Oerik were established here, and beautiful temples to all manner of gods were erected (the priests could worship and promote whomsoever they pleased as long as it didn't interfere with the Conglomerate).

Crime was actually low. Organized crime ironically kept the streets safe for the general public. There was always the odd assassination of course, for there were always rivals and complications to overcome, and unwary or foolish tourists were fair game as long as it did not affect business (nothing too violently gratuitous).

The Kingdoms were now known as the Thel Norusti, and the Thel Norusti stretched its tentacles as far as Kodge Land and western Mystara across the Solnor Sea.
