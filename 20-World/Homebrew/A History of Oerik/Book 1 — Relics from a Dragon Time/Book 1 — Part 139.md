## Part 139

Phizzat the Ur-Flan looked the elf up and down, noticing that she was nearly six feet tall, with blonde hair, golden eyes and silver-hued skin.

His invisible stalker had captured her when she was unprepared, so she wore a simple skirt and shirt made out of rabbit skin.

Phizzat attempted to converse with her but the elf remained silent. A torturer was sent for and was soon put to work. After a few minutes the elf finally spat forth a great many words, all contemptuous of the Ur-Flan and the torturer. Although Phizzat could understand her sentiments somewhat, she did not speak the common elven vernacular but some strange dialect.

Using a Language spell, Phizzat probed further, but gained no useful information, unless being called the bastard whelp of a yeth hound was considered useful, so he tried another spell.

The elf was immediately stricken with a different and far more intense pain than the torture she had just endured; it felt as if her brain was exploding inside her skull and the agony knocked her unconscious almost immediately. With her defenses down the Ur-Flan probed her memories. He would soon make her out.

Phizzat looked inside her mind and found himself staring into the eyes of a giant...
