## Part 25

Win-Daled the Lowly One was amazed to see the people of the Adri and Grandwood, both human and elf, rubbing shoulders and working together. She and her friend Ye'Cind romped about more joyfully than they had ever been able to before.

Ye'Cind's father, Esmir, was rapidly becoming one of the most successful merchants in Oerik. He was dealing with the Blemu leaders and a few human ones too, men and women of the Flan, who had intimate knowledge of trees, flowers and wild animals, and knew how to embrace life. It wasn't long before Win-Daled and her own father, the secretary Gal-Lek, felt free at heart, and no longer the slaves they actually were.

After a blissful three weeks, Gal-Lek made it clear to his master that he did not want to return to the Singing Lands. Win-Daled and Ye'Cind had become something of celebrities in Blemu. The silver-hued Ye'Cind was a master of animal imitations, and Win-Daled, with her bright eyes and white hair, was boisterous and fascinated by everything going. Gal-Lek felt that Blemu would make a good home. Esmir, for all his progressive views, was still the Singing One master, and he continued to need Gal-Lek, for Gal-Lek was an excellent secretary.

In the middle of the argument, a burst of light stunned them both; then, with a heavy thump, a round object rolled into Gal-Lek's legs. In horror, the elf and the Lowly One looked down to see the severed head of Esmir's wife, an expression of terror etched upon her pale face.
