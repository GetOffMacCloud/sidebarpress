## Part 105

Moradin slipped in and out of consciousness.

Dwur clerics in the library pored over their books and sacred scrolls, desperate to find a cure.

Corellon Larethian, dressed in their mail, their sword gleaming, guarded the bedside.

The hobgoblins attacking the Citadel were no random event. Moradin had been struck down by the fiendish Kavoss, and the Cyclops general was bound to try and finish the job.

King Rockfoot was pleased that his Spears were crushing the hobgoblins, but was concerned about new information gleaned from scouts.

A new force was gathering in the west to move in behind the hobgoblins: demonic bat-winged creatures that would take to the skies and pick off the defenders from above.

Rockfoot did not want to see doughty dwarves die even if Rockhome could withstand the attack. He went to Corellon and begged the god to actively take part in the defense.

Corellon was loath to leave Moradin, but was concerned about the new development. As they considered options, the Prelate came in from the library with hopeful news. The old cleric had found a stone tablet written in Draconian that had been deciphered by his scholars. Godrot could be expunged!

The Prelate turned grave. The ritual would be long and difficult. Moradin may never physically rule the dwarves again.
