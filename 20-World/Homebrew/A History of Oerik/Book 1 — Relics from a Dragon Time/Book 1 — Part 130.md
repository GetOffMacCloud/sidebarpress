## Part 130

### 718–720 SD

Unnatural darkness rolled over harbors and ports throughout Oerth.

Oerik, Mystara, Hepmonaland, Adarus, Oeridia, Kara-Tur, it did not matter where, the darkness brought driving rain, hail and typhoons.

Countless ships were ripped off moorings and sunk; many lives were lost. The strange phenomena hit sea trade severely.

Questions were asked: what did the survivors see? There must be evil agents at work, who are they?

Most survivors saw nothing, for the darkness was complete, but a few half-elves thought they saw an enormous blackness rise out of the sea with two great yellow eyes goggling at them just before the rain and wind struck.

Sailors returning from the sea also reported terrifying events. Whales, normally placid, actively hunted and rammed ships, devouring those that fell overboard.

Sea dragons and sea serpents were active in larger numbers, whirlpools and waterspouts appeared from nowhere, seemingly questing for ships to sink.

And, worst of all, giant octopi the size of small islands were wrapping their tentacles around ships and pulling them under. Only a fraction of vessels returned at all.

The destruction to sea trade was so complete as to make the different nations meet together as one. Using magical gates a summit was held on the elvish kingdom of Ierendi off the southwest coast of Mystara. An answer must be found to the disruption!
