## Part 39

Mocinlyte the succubus sat on a cushion and sang for Dweomyne. Her voice had improved, Dweomyne noted. The demon had been practicing.

Good, with some perseverance Dweomyne might be able to get a chorus together one day.

Dweomyne never sang anymore, as she considered it beneath her station. She didn't enjoy her fellow Singing Ones entertaining her either, because the ones with the clearest voices made her a little jealous.

Ruling the Singing Lands was getting tiring. The first glow of happiness in having such power had dimmed. The Lowly Ones were growing bolder and their dirty warrens were dotted throughout the undercity. They had built tunnels under the streets and sneaked and spied.

The Lowly Ones plotted against her, as did many of her fellow Singing Ones. The new head of the secret police was not good enough. Dweomyne had already replaced three of them, and their supporters sought revenge. Her list of enemies was growing.

Mocinlyte finished her song and Dweomyne dismissed the succubus. She was feeling despondent and the music wasn't helping. The leadership wasn't the only problem. It was becoming harder and harder to organize patrols, because too many Singing Ones had put away their swords and gone all in on trading and amassing wealth. Few Singing One elves liked to get their hands dirty these days. Perhaps summoning a pack of demons was the go.

And then a blue light crept into her room and began rising up and circling her as she sat upon her throne. The light scorched her skin when she brushed it and she realized that she was trapped.

A couple of guards were thrown noisily into the room as three figures burst in, eyes ablaze with fury. She did not recognize them, but one was clearly a Lowly One with her white hair, and another looked like a Singing One. The third figure was more striking: a six-foot-tall male human who, although heavily muscled, moved like a cat. The robed Lowly One halted and began mumbling and gesturing. Likely, it was she who had cast the spell on her. Could her concentration be broken...

Dweomyne lashed out with her whip and struck the Lowly One across the face. The blue circle flickered as the woman cowered; the spell was breaking!

Then the Singing One shot out a green light from a black scroll that was smoking in her hands. The ray seared Dweomyne's face, but she clenched her teeth and readied her whip for another lash.

Ye'Cind felt her hand scorch when she launched the attack with the scroll; it was angry again. The Scroll of Vile Darkness was malevolent and vindictive, but she was glad she insisted she bring it. Win-Daled had been confident that her magic would bring down the Whip Lady without aid, but without the scroll they would already have lost.

The Whip Lady lashed out again, but Heward stepped into the way, shielding Ye'Cind but catching the whip on his arm. It curled around his bicep and pulled him to his knees.

"Did you think you could bring me down?" snapped Dweomyne, and then she opened out her larynx and Sang.
