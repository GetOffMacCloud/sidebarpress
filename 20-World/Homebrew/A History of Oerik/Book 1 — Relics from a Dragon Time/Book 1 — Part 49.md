## Part 49

### 155 SD

So many factors created the split between the growing Suloise settlements of southwestern Oerik that it is impossible to convey it all in a short history of this nature. Suel living near mines got rich and nearly everyone else got progressively poorer. Bandits proliferated. Rivalry festered. The Suelchief of Risewater was not recognized as superior to the Suelchief of Emeraldglare (though he considered it obviously so), and the Suelchief of Rockfern decided that she was the superior one (for her wisdom was legendary), and all else must pay a tithe in homage to her.

Yet, a significant number of Suel thought that Thurnfedge III, as great-grandson of Thurnfedge I, appointed by Win-Daled herself, surely had the ascendancy.

Each town, farm and barracks had their own loyalties. Orcs, orogs, gnolls, norkers and ogres took advantage of the divisions and made many successful raids upon the furthest outposts.

Some Suel took the opportunity of becoming mercenaries to anyone who looked strongest on the day, including the humanoids. Half-orcs and half-ogres appeared in Oerik for the first time.

Political differences also had a part in the rupture. Seasonal food shortages meant some wished to trade with their ancient elvish enemies of the East, the Singing Ones. Others wanted more trade with the dwarves and humanoids. Arguments and fights broke out between neighbors. Magic, religions and differing laws all added to the feuding.

Sorcery was banned in Emeraldglare but encouraged in Risewater. Rockfern introduced slavery to punish transgressors, and consequently Risewater Suel razed farms and crops in protest. Inevitably full-blown wars broke out.

Emeraldglare had the best access to copper and tin, and they were sure they would subjugate their enemies.

Rockfern were disciplined and blessed with well-drilled troops, so victory was there for the taking.

Risewater had a lesser army and less metal resources, but they had the best weapon of all, because they had the gods on their side. Let their foes tremble before them!

By the end of 155 SD the Suel, after terrible battles with much loss of life, divided into two major entities: the Holy Papacy of Risewater and the Noble Highhold of Emeraldrock.
