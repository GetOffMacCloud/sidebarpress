## Part 162

### 777 SD

Miska smirked at his sister.

Look what he had done!

The treasure abounded around the cave, and it was all because of him. He had led the Sheldar hillmen on many successful raids into the Singing Lands and now his army was equipped with bronze weapons and armor.

And then there was all of the treasure. What had Lolth achieved? She just sat about eating. Mother should send her back to the ooze!

The Queen of Chaos had her dretch slaves pick up and sort all of the valuables brought in by her son.

Miska was so vain. Most of the stuff was just jewels and gems and shiny metal. Yes, it helped to buy things from the merchants and pirates, but they were heavy, bulky and she had more important things to worry about.

Northwest of the Barrier Peaks were the lands of the Vaati, the Wind Dukes. They had created a puppet nation to their west called Pesh that acted as a buffer for the expanding population of the Pyanor.

The Queen of Chaos decided that Pesh was to be her next goal. Take those lands and the Wind Dukes would be forced to give her back her whip they had stolen from her.

Miska was horrified. They were to leave all the treasure behind?

The Queen tried to mollify him. Who was going to plunder their cave while she and her children plotted in Pesh? The High Priest would take care of it while they were gone.

What, even the dretch weren't going to stay and guard the cave?

The dretch? They were more likely to take the treasure than anyone else. The dretch were coming with them.

Lolth wasn't too excited either. She liked it here. The food was tasty and she doubted that the Wind Dukes would be palatable.

The friction led to a big fight, screeches and screams and roars filling the night.

The next morning the Queen of Chaos slithered out of the cave with her dretch, her two children battered, bruised and defeated.

The Queen was so frazzled that she completely forgot to pick up the Scroll of Vile Darkness.

The cave was empty.

Nerull and Incabulos waited at its mouth, nervous again. They drew deep breaths, then after making sure they hadn't been seen, they crossed the threshold of the Cave of Gods.

The interior was dark but they had cast spells to let them see like cats. Rubbish was strewn all over the place: weapons, cloth, bones, helmets, leather, and there were broken stalactites and stalagmites too, presumably to give the Queen more space to ooze.

Exploring the rear, Incabulos located a partially concealed vault. He cast an opening spell, and the vault door sprang open.

Nerull pushed past Incabulos and went in.

Incabulos tried to come in after his friend, but Nerull blocked him. He was here first!

Nerull was excited by what he saw. So many objects glowed in beautiful colors. They must be very magical.

Yet, one object remained pitch black. It was a scroll.

Nerull knew about scrolls. His master, Jento, had taught him how to read and harness their magic. He reached out to grab it, but as he did so he felt his fingers tingle.

Did Incabulos still want to come in? Good.

Nerull pointed the scroll out to Incabulos. Take it, would you?

Incabulos's eyes were glowing. He recognized the scroll too. How good his friend was, letting him have the best prize.

Incabulos reached out, grabbed it... and he felt searing fire...!
