## Part 156

Miska the Barbarian brought back to the Sheldar the heads of griffons, basilisks, a blue dragon, a peryton and a manticore. He wore the pelt of an androsphinx and wielded a huge broadsword that killed with every strike.

Once he was named the Great Chief, Miska's wolfish nature couldn't be denied. He demanded sacrifices and blood.

Before long, Miska had resumed his natural form and the Sheldar found out that their ruler was a giant, hairy spider.

The tribe was angry at the deception and they ambushed Miska with nets, clubs and spears. However, the demon was too strong and his eight legs struck the tribespeople down like sabers. His fangs were death.

More than a hundred Sheldar lay about the magnificent monster and Miska sat atop the corpses, willing the whole tribe on.

Let me taste your blood!

Finally the Sheldar survivors dropped their weapons and lay themselves prostrate on the ground.

They had found their god.

Lolth wandered the Hool Marshes, checking her thick, sticky, stinking webs.

Here and there were trapped and stuck creatures: giant frogs, lizard people, giant wasps, bone snappers. Lolth wrapped them up then sucked out their juices.

Lolth found the living lizard folk distasteful and much preferred dealing with dead ones.

She had become a stalking demoness, and the intelligent denizens of the marshes had mostly fled downriver where they would rather deal with Flannish pirates.

Fat, sluggish and sated, Lolth the Black Spider went off to find her brother.
