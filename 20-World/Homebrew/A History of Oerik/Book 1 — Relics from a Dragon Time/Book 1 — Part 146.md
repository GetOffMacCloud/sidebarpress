## Part 146

Princess Gossermay wasn't getting anywhere with the kikopath, so she decided to wander down to her cellar and fortify herself with a nip of fortified.

Unsteady and holding the wall as she went down the corridor, the brownie heard scuffling in the chamber she was reaching.

She staggered into the room but was unnoticed by the three individuals flashing their daggers and swords. Princess Gossermay was only two feet high.

But she could bellow.

"What's happening here?" she roared.

Heward, who was now visible, and able to move again, had drawn his sword and was fending off the two strange creatures intent on killing him. He was a little too busy to reply.

The two creatures, however, put away their daggers and retreated.

"That's better," said Gossermay. "If you were fondling each other I wouldn't mind, but fighting? At my Extravaganza?"

Heward put down his sword, then pointed at the two scarlet hairy beings.

"They placed a stun spell upon me; they nearly killed me!"

"You were spying," grated the creature called Gaxx.

Princess Gossermay had had enough. She grabbed Heward's knee, and told him to leave and rejoin the party. She would talk with Gaxx and Gumish alone.

<center>* * *</center>

The last night of the Extravaganza was always the most exciting. Despite the best food and drink long consumed, one hundred round tables were set up for the Intimate Games, where couples, triples and friends would compete in the Fondue Championship.

Food would be flung, cheese would be melted, and clothes were optional.

Heward was still troubled by his encounter in the chamber, so he left Lathia to have fun with her dryad while he went to discover more about the strange beings. Fondues weren't his thing anyway.

The Princess was competing herself, so he couldn't ask her any more questions, and she had not been forthcoming previously. Where were the creatures anyway? Heward had walked around the tables and he had not seen them. Presumably they were still in the manor. Heward took a deep breath, then made up his mind to go in again. This time he would hold his head up high and try to converse with them.

The shaggy scarlet creatures weren't in the chamber, but he met them in the kitchen where they were eating cake and drinking stout.

"Oh, the spy," said Gaxx. "Shall we stun you again?"

Heward held up his hands. He had eavesdropped, he admitted. He had been a thief in a previous life and old habits died hard.

The beings scowled darkly.

Heward then showed the silver medallion around his neck. See the two swanmays intertwined? It meant that he was a Freeberger, an important noble in Fey.

"And why should we care?" asked Gumish.

"You are Fey, aren't you? All the Fey folk respect their Freebergers."

Gaxx snorted. No, they weren't Fey folk. They were UnFey, from the Unseelie world.

Heward hadn't expected that. Why was Princess Gossermay allowing Unseelie here? In his own district?

The UnFey were treacherous, violent, destructive and joyless. It made no sense.

"What do I call you?" he said at last.

They were Gaxx and Gumish, and they were of a species of UnFey called horrids. They had come to this displeasing place on the express invitation of Princess Reverina Gossermay.

Heward didn't believe that. Occasionally, when the magical interdimensions intersected, UnFey creatures would escape into the Fey world and cause havoc, but to actually conjure up a conduit and welcome them?

Times were changing, explained Gaxx. The Unseelie had modernized and now wished to open up to the larger universe for mutual benefit.

One thing the Unseelie had that was elsewhere scarce were large quantities of mithril. That valuable metal so loved by elves and Tuatha de Dannan was common and ordinary. This metal they were willing to share, if provided with the beautiful diamonds and gemstones that were more common in Fey.

The mithril would allow the Princess to become the main supplier of armor and weapons to the Tuatha de Dannan and make her rich.

"Everyone has their price," stated Gaxx.
