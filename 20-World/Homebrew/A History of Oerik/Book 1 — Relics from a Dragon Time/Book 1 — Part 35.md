## Part 35

### −18 SD

The Scroll of Vile Darkness had bathed in the rivers of Hades, had burned in the fires of the Nine Hells and had been rent by the howling fury of Pandemonium. Thrown into the black depths of the Abyss, the scroll was recovered and rebuilt in Tartarus and re-inked in Acheron. It had known loneliness, pain, anger and fear. It had suffered for its art and it was proud and it was hungry, and it was ready to corrupt the living.

The Scroll of Vile Darkness ached to be unfurled by the lost and vengeful. Then, with its secrets read and hidden powers unleashed, let the Multiverse shake in despair!

<center>* * *</center>

"I have a little tickle for you," said the thief guild master to Heward across the table of a dingy room in a warehouse in a neglected part of the capital of the Singing Lands.

"Do tell," said Heward.

"A spy thinks he has discovered how to banish the demons whence they came."

"Yes?"

"Our spy has discovered a magical scroll that contains many secrets of the Lower Planes. If we can find that scroll..."

"I am a little familiar with magic," said Heward, "but no expert. I have a couple of friends in Blemu over in the East."

"I don't really care how you do it," said the guild master as he poured himself a glass of Celenian wine, "but be aware that the scroll is believed to be somewhere in Hepmonaland in the MaiYuan empire."

"May I?" asked Heward, reaching for the bottle.

"No, you may not," snapped the guild master, who was a greedy man and happened to really like Celenian wine.

<center>* * *</center>

The two friends in the West, Win-Daled the Lowly One mage, and Ye'Cind the elf witch warrior, were currently on a mission themselves. They searched for the great immortal Corellon Larethian, a semi-deity and unofficial overseer of the olven races. Over the centuries Corellon had traveled the continents of Oerth, and of other worlds too. They had finally retired to Alfheim in Mystara, the continent directly east of Oerik.

Alfheim was enlightened and free. Like Blemu, the elves of Alfheim were a kind, compassionate people, free of the debauchery of the Singing Lands or Celene. But they were more aloof than Blemu and they distanced themselves from the human tribes around them.

Ye'Cind thought that if she and Win-Daled met with Corellon, they would realize just how fallen the Singing Ones had become, and they would join them in the attempt to stop the murder and slavery of the Lowly Ones.
