## Part 85

### 466 SD

Pretend king Silvervein Moradinson was dead.

Pious Grimm Quartztusk had killed him.

Pious held his head. The headache wasn't going away. Silvervein Moradinson had become a traitor; he had betrayed and usurped Moradin, yet Pious knew that Silvervein had not been a monster.

Though he had died like one.

Pious pushed the dark thoughts away. He had done his duty. He served the mighty Moradin, and all the dwur. Silvervein had made the fatal choice, not Pious.

Pious had taken the Axe of the Dwarven Lords and had locked it away. With that accomplishment, Pious Grimm Quartztusk had managed to stabilize the situation in Grawn. It had been agreed by the dwur nobles that he himself would take on the role of steward until Moradin returned.

Pious hoped that Moradin would return sooner rather than later. He had no ambitions like Silvervein, and he was steward only because he was most fit for the job.

<center>* * *</center>

Despite the end of fighting in the Crystalmists, battling continued in Eastern Oerik.

The dwarves being non-magical people, news had not reached the Loyalist Hammers in the Lortmil Mountains who fought for the (now dead) king.

Reinforcements had been non-existent, so the once chivalrous warriors had turned to ambush tactics against their foes. This reduction of honor didn't sit well with them, and their fall made them more ruthless and merciless, for they had lost much of their self-respect.

The Loyalists believed in their king, and their cause, and that kept them together, for if they persevered, they would eventually prevail, and only Good would come thereafter.

<center>* * *</center>

In Rockhome, the Loyalists were crushed. The remnants surrendered to King Rockfoot. After this, Rockhome made a Declaration of Independence, vowing to be forever free from the rule of the Crystalmist dwur.

Rockhome hired Ur-Flan to seal the magical gates. Again.

<center>* * *</center>

Upon his throne in the Underdark, King Druzz of the duergar dreamed of the future.

His duergar were thriving in the Overlight. The dwur city Grawn counted hundreds of duergar. Duergar had established holdings throughout the Crystalmists, the Barrier Peaks, and further east into the hills of the Suss. Trading networks were pumping bright goods and treasures from the surface into the Underdark.

The duergar had never been so happy.

Some of them were even smiling.

When Druzz thought on this, even he smiled.

Especially when he thought about the plans he had next...
