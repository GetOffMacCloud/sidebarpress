## Part 6

> *Get out of the Mud, for the Sun is sharp.*
>
> — Moradin, from *The Book of the Sombre*, Chapter 13, verse 7

Moradin was born in the Western Crystalmists around 11,500 years ago. He was not affected by the elvish enchantments that enslaved most dwarves. Wise, strong and intelligent, he despaired of the wrongs done to his people. He tried to rouse his fellows to revolution, but they were "affixed to servitude like iron" (Chapter 11, verse 9). Eventually he retreated into the depths of the Oerth, following the gold-lusted dwarves. These dwarves were free but rapacious and cruel. They fought over the control of gold and gem deposits and were willing to stir the dragons from their slumber if it could lead to more wealth.

Moradin had little interest in gold (some interest admittedly, he was a dwarf after all!) and focused on mining useful metals. He created an alloy of iron and mithril that glowed with the natural magic of the Underdark. With this alloy he made a great hammer and forge. Some of the greedy dwarves agreed to be armed by Moradin in order to free their brethren, but when fully equipped, they reneged and made war on the goblinoids who dwelt near great seams of platinum and gold. The goblins, bugbears, ogres and norkers counted amongst them powerful individuals that included barbarians and warlocks. Disrupted from their homeland, and full of fury, they poured out of the tunnels and reached the surface, raiding the elves and faerie peoples for the first time.

Moradin was forced to fight against them as he retreated back to the upper mines. The enchanted dwarves had enough self-awareness to defend themselves from the rampaging norkers and goblins, but their weapons and armor were crude. Moradin was able to use his magical forge to create strong mail. When the charmed dwarves put on the mithril alloy armor, their minds cleared and they were free. Moradin had given them their souls.
