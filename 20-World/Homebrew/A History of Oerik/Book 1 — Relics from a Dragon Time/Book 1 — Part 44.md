## Part 44

### 20s SD

Several nations made up the lands of Eastern Oerik.

The lands surrounding and incorporating the Adri/Grandwood forest was the elven kingdom of Blemu, but with numerous autonomous villages and towns occupied by Flan.

Rangers and druids congregated here and human wizard colleges were established.

West of Blemu were three small states, two Flan and one, set within the Flinty Hills, dwarvish. These states were granted their liberty by the Blemu sovereign.

South of the Thelly River was the home of dwarven clans collectively known as HardThistle. Flan villages also dotted this region, mostly at peace with the dwur.

At the mouth of the Vast Swamp were wild lands dominated by humanoids and monsters.

Much of the west, including what is now Furyondy, the Shield Lands, Nyrond and Celene, and the Celadon, Dim, Suss and Gamboge forests, was the burgeoning Empire of Celene. The Flan were serfs and servants and slaves.

East of Blemu in what is now North Province, were numerous tiny kinglets of Flan war chiefs, effectively a lawless society. They grew wealthy trading with the sea elves who controlled all the major islands of what is now the Aerdi Sea, but spent most of their time using their riches to gain power for themselves over the other chiefs.

West of Celene were Dwur and Sheldar hunter-gatherers. Primarily good, but hard-hearted, they defended the realms against Celenian incursions. They spoke a mixture of different dialects to those east of the Lortmils.

South of Lake Quag and west of the sylvan lands of the Vesve in what is now Perrenland, were a hidden realm of Flan who had contacts with MaiYuan and studied demonology. Calling themselves the Ur-Flan (great Flan), this evil theocracy seeped slowly through Oerik like a dripping tap.

To the north of Oerik were great hosts of humanoids coexisting with large barbarian tribes.

The great ranges of the Barrier Peaks, the Crystalmists and the Sulhauts that bordered Western Oerik were dominated by the mountain dwarves, wisely ruled by the beneficent and steady Moradin, the Soul Forger: the God King for all dwur kind.
