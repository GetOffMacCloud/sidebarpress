## Part 12

Winding around the Material Plane, connecting the Astral Plane with the Ethereal, the Fey with the Shadow and caressing the Inner Planes is the mysterious Plane of the Arcane. There is no known way of entering and exiting the plane, or what would be needed to bring to the Plane in order to survive. Its invisible nature and secret egress mean that very few even know of its existence; possibly there is currently no one extant on Oerth that does, but it plays an important role in limiting magical power.

The Arcane Plane is an infinite single layer, but the layer is broken into huge bands of different colors.\* As the Plane drifts and twirls about in its erratic way, different bands touch different parts of the Prime Material and it is these bands that change the power of magic in the region. In 623 CY, the band around Oerth and its nearby systems allows mages to basically only cast up to 9th-level spells. Back 10,200 years ago a different colored band touched Oerth.

Intelligent, literate beings would often know a spell or two. Elves when attacking would often cast a *magic missile* before reverting to regular arrows. Magic weapons and armor were easier and cheaper to make than now, and those special individuals devoted to studying the magical arts could reach enormous power.

If characters of the Common era were to travel back in time, they would find super-powered elves teleporting from place to place, great magical offensive spells that could obliterate several square miles, and warriors great as demigods. Dwarves did not cast conventional spells but were able to enchant items in mystical ways.
