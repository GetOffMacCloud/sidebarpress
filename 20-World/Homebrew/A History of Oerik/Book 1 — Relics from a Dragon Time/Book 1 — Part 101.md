## Part 101

The duergar raiders babbled excitedly amongst themselves as they left the Drachensgrab hills and headed east towards the decadent empire of the Thel Norusti.

They were going to spend and sell their treasure, and have a good time.

The next night's march saw the duergar walking without talking. Perhaps they were all lost in their own thoughts.

The next night they walked without pause for food or rest. They continued as the moons dropped and Sol rose.

After several days and nights their bodies gave out and they died... but that did not stop them from plodding along the trail.

Behind them came the shadows. They crept silently behind, never overtaking the lost duergar possessed by the cursed treasure they had taken from the Undead Dragon.

Ignoring small settlements, the duergar wights passed by travelers and inhabitants without seeming to notice them at all. The air about them crackled and kept people at bay. Best to keep the strange dwarves to themselves, they thought.

After six weeks the duergar, now incorporeal wraiths, reached the small town of Kenfurr. There was a mill, a tower, a bunch of houses, some inns, a market and a graveyard. It was to the graveyard that they went.
