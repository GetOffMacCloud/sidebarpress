## Part 52

The jann had lived in the west over the Darkwall mountains for thousands of years.

Related to the Wind Dukes far away in the northeast, they worshipped and embraced the Four Great Elements. To this end they built magnificent Temple Cities out of Elemental Shadestone where they held short ceremonies four times a day.

Their land was semi-arid, but they had all they needed, for the beating sun nourished them with Fire, the rocky landscape nourished with Earth, the breeze nourished them with Air and the underground springs nourished with Water.

The trade with the Highhold Suel opened up exciting new paths. Trade with other neighbors had proved to be impossible as the humanoid tribes were aggressive, particularly the mantis thri-kreen.

Trade with the Suloise led to some jann traveling east and exploring the human realms. Taller than most Suel, with piercing eyes, multiple skin colors, billowing violet robes and powerful magic, the jann proved to be an intimidating and threatening presence to many Highholders, creating some friction. Yet, it was the Suel of the Holy Papacy that were least tolerant, the Pope declaring the elementals infidels, and banishing their presence from the Theocracy.

This did not stop a fair few open-minded and adventurous Suel from crossing west and coexisting with the jann in their own beautiful cities.

Despite the ups and downs, trading between the two peoples continued to enrich both and this windfall changed both cultures irrevocably.

Individual jann and Suel grew in power and influence, separating themselves from the traditional classes and castes that anchored the majority. Highholders who had missed out on owning the mines from the lucky First Families now could get very rich very quickly.

There were jann, too, who found themselves attracted to Suel culture, dressing in Suel clothes and utilizing Suel words.

All were watched, and all were judged.
