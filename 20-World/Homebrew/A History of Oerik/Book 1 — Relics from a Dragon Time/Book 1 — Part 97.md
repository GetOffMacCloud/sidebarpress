## Part 97

### 523 SD

Duergar had lived in the hills south of the Suss Forest for nearly sixty years. The ongoing Dwarven Wars meant that reintegration with the surface dwarves was not possible, so they kept to themselves and maintained their old ways.

The duergar spent most of their time underground, the terrain they knew best. They struggled to understand how the Overlight denizens could function in the middle of summer. Down in the dark there were no seasons.

The duergar easily overcame and dominated the local goblins, xvarts, norkers and kobolds. The humanoids were then recruited and used for scouting, exploration, hunting and robbing.

The duergar were satisfied with their cavernous lairs, but were disappointed to find no connection to the honeycomb of tunnels that led into the Underdark and their King.

This fact did not stop a hardy handful from investigating further. They dug new tunnels that bypassed rock falls, dead ends and water-filled chasms.

Eventually, a narrow shaft broke open into a musty, cold network of complex caves. It was a new region of the Underdark, undisturbed for millennia.

There was no fungal luminescence upon the walls, there were no monsters, everything felt untouched.

Food and water would be a problem, so the duergar explorers organized camps to be established at strategic points. Likely, hard work would establish a new link to their homeland, then their empire could expand and seed these dead caves with life.

After a few hours, the duergar struggled to concentrate. A dread feeling touched their bones and made them weigh like lead, but the grey dwur were nothing if not stoic, and they pushed through.

Ghimlit suddenly let out a cry of delight. He had broken through a slab of rock that led him into a series of new caverns. Caverns made of gemstones.

The duergar hurried through the hole and breathed in the beauty.

Unfortunately, they were extremely close to danger.

Not far away lay the hitherto undisturbed resting place of the Undead Dragon...
