## Part 43

### −10 SD to 1 SD

The Singing Ones to the East were attracted to the metals and gems mined by the dwarves of the Crystalmists and the Sulhauts. The dwur were mistrustful of the silver elves, and forbade the Singing Ones from using magical gates in their lands. The Singing Ones thus were forced to travel overland, and this made them prey to Risewater bandits.

Infuriated, the Singing Ones sent soldiers in to patrol the roads, and Risewater countered by recruiting Flan druids, rangers and mages that helped bolster fortifications along the routes.

The roads became dangerous for elves, Lowly Ones and dwarves alike. Trade was seriously disrupted, but Risewater emerged as the victors, for they ended up with large amounts of loot that they spent in Blemu to recruit craftspeople, magicians, clerics and soldiers.

After nearly ten years, Risewater had become a thriving town, but the Singing Ones were preparing for war. The losses they took in mountain trade had encouraged them to find renewed discipline. Young elves trained as fighters and magi. Orcs and a few bands of evil Lowly Ones joined the Singing One forces as mercenaries.

In −1 SD thousands of humanoid and Lowly One troops, led by Singing Ones, rafted en masse across the river and invaded Risewater.

Explosive fireballs sundered the hill the town was on, bringing the larger part of it down with an enormous crash.

Summoned water elementals then washed into Risewater, enveloping all they could find in liquid bubbles of death.

Fires broke out in the higher places still standing above the waterline. Panicked Lowly Ones knew not where to go, and there was complete chaos. The attack continued on after the elementals washed back to their plane, with orcs and Singing One assassins moving in to slay even more.

The attack had been a complete surprise, and well planned. Clearly, the Singing Ones had planted spies, for most of the leaders, including Win-Daled, Heward and Ye'Cind, had actually been dealing with an ogre incursion several miles away.

They heard the distant, dull roar as Risewater and its hill came down. The heroes came upon the attackers in fury. Ye'Cind and Heward's arrows took down waves of orcs, Win-Daled brought lightning splintering down, then Heward drew blades and engaged with the hardier adversaries while Ye'Cind rounded up survivors still able to fight. Soaring above all was the arch-mage Win-Daled, robe fluttering behind her as she sent clouds of death into the Singing Ones.

Amidst all the ruin our heroes were able to destroy the invaders, but this conflict with their neighbors couldn't go on.

One week later, Win-Daled personally secured a peace treaty with the Singing Lands, in return for neutrality and the halting of banditry. This caused consternation amongst some of the Lowly Ones who carried scars from their time in Singing One servitude. Win-Daled was able to win the majority over by emphasizing that the only way forward was to shake off the past and find a new destiny.

Now there was time to grieve the lost, but with security and legitimacy established, Risewater became a haven for more Lowly Ones escaping the Singing Lands.

Risewater was rebuilt and the hill magically restored. The Lowly Ones found a new name for themselves as they began a new calendar commemorating their victory.

From this day forward the Lowly Ones would now be the Risen Ones, in their language, the Suel, the Suloise, and Win-Daled was appointed their first Suelchief.
