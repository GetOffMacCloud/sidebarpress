## Part 128

### 715 SD

The persecution of the kuo-toa continued unabated.

The various seafaring nations traded, colonized and raided each other and established ports and harbors on the islands and coasts. Kuo-toa depended upon the islands and coasts as part of their lifecycle. Unlike other underwater fish people like the locathah, the kuo-toa needed to spend some of their time on land.

The kuo-toa therefore had their landscape trampled, robbed, burned and battled on. Usually the kuo-toa were not the main targets; they were just in the wrong place.

The kuo-toa had no allies either. The intelligent rays were wholly evil and hated everyone else, the locathah saw kuo-toa as "base" and abhorrent, the marine elves treated them as comical lesser beings and other denizens of the deep just liked eating them, so the areas where they could safely dwell were reduced and were reducing severely.

The constant anxiety grew too much. A number of kuo-toa fell into Mass Delusion. They formed a cult worshipping a bizarre being, a human woman with a lobster head and claws. She was said to dwell in the deepest depths of the largest ocean on Oerth and that any creature who encountered her exploded into salt.

The fact that no one before the cult formed had heard of such a thing, and the fact that even the great leviathan of the Solnor had no idea what the kuo-toa were prattling on about, did not seem to matter to the faithful.

Her name was Blibdoolpoolp.
