## Part 122

### 631 SD

The Sheldomar Valley is a large lush land bound by the Hellfurnaces to the southwest, the Crystalmists and Jotens to the west and the Barrier Peaks to the north. The eastern border is flanked by the Lortmils. On the other side of the Lortmils is the powerful elven kingdom of Celene.

In these long lost years before the rise of Tharizdun, Lolth and all the horrors to follow, the Sheldar were the most populous tribe of humankind in the valley. They looked similar to the eastern Flan but dressed rather like the gnomes and halflings who were also abundant here. They spoke their own language, now long forgotten, though traces remain today (623 CY) in the modern languages of the Ull, dialects in Sterich, Keolandish and the blended dialects of the Ulek states.

The Sheldomar Valley had suffered greatly during the Dwur Wars. Much of the crops, fish and livestock were forcibly requisitioned by the dwarven armies, who did pay with gold, but gold does not fill hungry bellies when there is not enough food left to eat.

Despite this lean period, the Sheldar population steadily increased until they numbered in the millions. Some clans had become militant, so mercenaries and soldiers were a constant presence, particularly near the mountains of the west. In the central lands the Sheldar formed petty kingdoms, hostile to strangers, dwarves and elves.

In Celene, where the elves ruled over the humans, there was concern that the stubbornness of the Sheldar, along with the so-called enlightened liberalism of the Flan to the east, would rile up their human serfs. Celene had not gotten along with the elvish kingdom of Blemu to their east (incorporating the modern day Adri and Grandwood forests) for quite some time, as the Blemu had encouraged the human independence that had given rise to the Flan civilization.

The Thytic sea peoples were also diluting the elven influence in the Sheldomar, for their merchant ships sailed into harbors from the Azure Sea in the south. The Thytics brought exotic goods of all kinds from the distant west where they were from, as well as hitherto unknown foods and materials from Melavi and the MaiYuan empire in Hepmonaland.

In 631 SD the Celenian hippogriff aerial cavalry raided several Sheldar and halfling villages nestled within the foothills of the Lortmils. Their mission was to cause fear and awe.

The raids did subdue the Sheldar somewhat, for many Sheldar stories and myths were already about the power, grandeur and terror of elvenkind.
