## Part 123

### 631–633 SD

The Kingdom of Celene took the reluctance of the Sheldar to seriously engage them as an opportunity to annex the lands southwest of the Lortmils between the Lort and Kewl rivers down to and incorporating the Axewood and Silverwood forests (which was one forest at this time).

The sylvan elves of the forest allied with Celene and defended the territory from the southeast. The Sheldar, halflings and gnomes trapped between the elven armies quickly found themselves as subjects of Celene.

The gnomes in particular took umbrage at this and formed guerrilla units. In the winter of 631 SD Celene used goblin and kobold mercenaries to attack the gnomes in their own tunnels.

632 SD saw full-scale war as the gnomes, halflings, with dwarves and Sheldar warriors, counterattacked from the hills near the Kewl, driving out many of the humanoids but falling heavily to elven magic. Garl Glittergold, the gnome king, pragmatic though angry and sad in equal measure, was able to broker peace in 633 SD.

The annexed lands remained part of Celene but the gnomes and halflings could live undisturbed and pay no taxes as long as elves could pass through unmolested. The Sheldar humans who chose to remain in the area were not so lucky, for they were forced into servitude, to be incorporated as serfs for Greater Celene.
