## Part 41

### −14 SD

Chaos reigned in the Singing Lands. With Dweomyne gone, the leadership completely dissolved. Lowly Ones rose up everywhere, slaying or fleeing their Singing Elf masters. The Singing Ones were too apathetic to mount a strong response and the various Houses vied against each other rather than concentrate on the rebellion.

Gangs of Singing One youths banded together and formed into murder squads, slaying Lowly Ones and rival gang members, but they were small in number and many fell to Lowly Ones themselves.

There were Lowly Ones who continued serving their masters, trying to hang on to some sort of normality, but the majority of the young and able Lowly Ones worked with Win-Daled, Ye'Cind and Heward to overthrow the elves entirely.

Major gains were made and several key leaders of the secret police were captured and publicly executed. Overthrow now seemed inevitable.

Then, a secretive group of Singing One mages summoned thousands of orc and ogre shock troops out of magic gates that poured down into the undercity and attacked the Lowly Ones with great fury. Backed up by the Magi, underground hideouts were winkled out and thieves and fighters were slaughtered.

Heward, Win-Daled and Ye'Cind saw that the tide was turning, and concentrated their resources on saving lives. Escape rather than confrontation now seemed to be the best option.

While Ye'Cind and Win-Daled worked on weakening the humanoids, Heward made deals with Lowly One bandits, to help take many families west into the arid plains.

Several thousand Lowly Ones escaped in this way, and once this feat was accomplished, and with the Singing Ones having gained some time using their humanoid allies, and now reorganizing, Win-Daled and Ye'Cind fled into the wilderness, leading a core group of fighters west as well.

The Singing Ones were desperate to regain control, realizing that their lifestyle was under threat, and now new leaders appeared. The priority was not to mount a serious pursuit of the escapees, but to reassert authority on both the Lowly Ones and lawless elves. The remaining Lowly Ones were crushed, with thousands slain and the rest enslaved once more. The Lowly Ones in the west would be dealt with in due course.
