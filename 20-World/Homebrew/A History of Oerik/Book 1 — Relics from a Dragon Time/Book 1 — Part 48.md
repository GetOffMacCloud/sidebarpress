## Part 48

### 151 SD

The influx of gnomes and halflings caused upheaval to parts of Eastern Oerik. Goblins and norkers took exception to halfling settlers in the area now known as Greyhawk. Despite attempts at negotiation, the humanoids viewed the halflings as inherently hostile. Many halflings migrated north and settled around the Whystil Lake.

These halflings mixed in with the Neolithic northern people and became isolated from other demihumans. Within a few generations they would call themselves H'uga, and, ultimately, dominated the Howling Hills and subjugated the goblins there.

Gnomes occasionally came into territorial friction with the dwarves, which the gnomes were keen to resolve, typically by moving further away. Forced therefore into competing directly with goblins and kobolds for resources, the gnomes finally took up arms against the humanoids and usually ended up the victors.

The lawless Flan on the eastern coast morphed into several large competing kingdoms and were constantly at war. The Blemu and Sunndi elven kingdoms found themselves mediating disputes at their borders, and Flannish druids and rangers spent much of their time attempting to reconcile and formalize the Flan nations.
