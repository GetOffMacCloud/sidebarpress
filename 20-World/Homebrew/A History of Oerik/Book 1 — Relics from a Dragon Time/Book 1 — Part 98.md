## Part 98

### −14,000 SD

Tiamat spawned the chromatic dragons in order to gain great power for herself. Weaker than most other Grand Drakes, she hated her brethren and wished to rise above them all.

The blue, black, white, green and red dragons were cowed and subdued by her. Bent to her will they submitted themselves to vile magical rituals that allowed Tiamat to grow an extra head, one for each of the chromatic types.

Tiamat proudly sported her new look, flaunting her Greatness. Bahamut, her brother, would finally recognize her as an equal, and she would no longer be shunted out from the Palace.

But Bahamut, the Platinum dragon, and the greatest Grand Drake outside of Kara-Tur, continued to shun her. In fact, he seemed shocked and appalled.

"Do you not see the new me?" asked Tiamat. "Can you not see my new heads, my gathered power?"

Bahamut turned his face away from his sister.

"I saw it," answered Bahamut, "and I wish I had not. What price did your offspring pay for you to become this abomination?"

Tiamat's eyes flashed, and her various heads glowered.

"My offspring are mine to do with as I please."

"No!" snapped Bahamut. "No! You will never understand our True Way, our True Purpose. Power is not won by exploiting others."

<center>* * *</center>

Desperate to bring down her arrogant brother, Tiamat bred further types. The brown and yellow dragons fled from her rather than submit, so she gained nothing from them.

Enraged, Tiamat flew upon each of the extant metallic dragons, the spawn of Bahamut. One brass, bronze, copper, silver and electrum[^1] dragon were each slain and their bones gathered together. Their glands that allowed their breath weapon attacks were combined, powdered and sprinkled upon the bones. Using necromancy, the bones reanimated into a distorted skeleton of oddly misproportioned bones, each joint connected with ectoplasm.

What was Reborn became the first and most terrible Undead being that ever crawled upon the Oerth.

<center>* * *</center>

Tiamat had shackled the monster with Mighty Magicks. Tiamat would subdue it and take all of its Unnatural power. Then, Bahamut would be thrown down and Tiamat would be Empress!

If she had succeeded in taming the monster, then Tiamat would certainly have taken over the Dragon Empire and the elves would have never inherited Oerth. This did not happen.

The Undead Dragon merely sniffed at her bonds and they shattered. She stared at Tiamat, sizing the multi-headed dragon up. Then, with a ghastly groan, the Thing breathed out a haunted blast of hate that sent Tiamat howling away.

<center>* * *</center>

This Undead Dragon roamed the Oerth for nearly a century before the Celestial Empire, led by Bahamut, managed to trap her underneath the low mountains of the Drachensgrab. She was confined to a small area of the Underdark, sealed with magical traps, and undisturbed and forgotten, until the duergar arrived in 523 SD.

[^1]: Gold dragons are not true metallic dragons but are Kara-Turan drakes who revere Bahamut (there were Occidental gold dragons, but they went their own way and are now presumed extinct).
