## Part 115

Zzzlarr the demon was proud of his appearance.

With a hulking grey, oozing bulk, eight sturdy spider legs, and with long mantis-like arms raised high above his bulbous head, he could usually stare down enemies without resorting to violence.

Zzzlarr was a lover, not a fighter and he had his shiny black eyes upon a new bride.

The Steaming Fens in the 14th Layer of the Abyss had seen a new Mistress in the land.

Apparently once an elf from Oerth with a divine voice, the Abyss had changed her form as she embraced and harnessed the Chaotic Evil atmosphere for her own purposes. Now electric blue and with atrophied legs, she moved about on a mass of violet and pink tentacles.

She was the self-proclaimed Queen of Chaos, and the mummified heads of demons and daemons and demodands hung upon the ramparts of her glorious coral and glass castle that rose up as if growing out from the swamp itself.

Scarlet lily pads a mile wide held aloft villages full of dretch slaves that did whatever the Queen asked of them. Her Songs saturated the air, the beauty of their sounds taming the land itself to her will.

Dainty succubi maidens soared freely above and around the castle, and Zzzlarr heard them chattering excitedly to each other in between tormenting and teasing the pale, ugly dretch.

Zzzlarr slithered off his gargantuan slug mount then slipped up to the gates. The gates swung open and Zzzlarr entered the courtyard. It was full of dainty yellow flowers that stank of rotten cabbage. How romantic!

Dweomyne, the Queen of Chaos, hid behind a pillar. If Zzzlarr wanted her he would have to find her first!

Zzzlarr heard a tinkle of laughter; his new bride was here...
