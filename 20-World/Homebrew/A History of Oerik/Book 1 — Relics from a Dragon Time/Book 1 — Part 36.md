## Part 36

If Corellon Larethian had intervened against the demon-worshipping elves in −18 SD, it might have saved a great deal of woe in the Universe, but there they were, in the rain in a picturesque meadow in Alfheim, picking strawberries, seemingly unmoved by our heroes' entreaties.

Once, they glanced briefly at Win-Daled and Ye'Cind as they told them of the abuse of the slaves in the Singing Lands, but Corellon never stopped slowly picking and placing the strawberries into their basket.

Win-Daled and Ye'Cind started to whine. Didn't the elven god care about the demons? Didn't Corellon strive against evil?

Finally tired of the two, Corellon put the basket down and looked them in the eyes with their steely own. The god's eyes were deep violet, almost black.

"And who are you to tell me what to do?" snapped Corellon Larethian. "Me, with what I have known, what I have seen, and with what I have endured? My Olves are free. There are good elves, there are bad elves. They are all mine to do as they please."

Then they stretched out their arms and it was as if Corellon had become a vengeful wraith, blue and grey flames shooting out from them in all directions, but the fire was cold, and the flames that licked Win-Daled and Ye'Cind made them shiver and sucked away their warmth. Corellon's fury lasted only for an instant... there was a bright flash and the proud elf god was gone...

No, it was not Corellon that had gone, it was Win-Daled and Ye'Cind who had been banished from Alfheim.

Heward, Win-Daled and Ye'Cind pushed on through the Hepmonaland jungle. The trees and shrubs were thick and unyielding. Everything seemed bristly and damp. Weird things moved through the thickets, strange animals and monsters feathered and furred.

The birdsong sounded like rasping saws; there were giant lizards which looked like scaly cats, hidden within the blackberry thorns, darting in and out but having the good sense to keep out of their reach. Heward swore that he saw a man-sized giant bat, wings unfurled, stamping about on stilt-like legs.

Win-Daled stopped them for a moment as she consulted the map Heward had provided them from his Lowly One contact. Somewhere quite near, there should be a city, but where exactly? Ye'Cind hoped that they would get out of the jungle soon. Humidity was very uncomfortable for elves.

Moving on once more, the trees dropped away at the edge of a cliff. Cautiously peering over the edge, the trio heard voices rising up from far below. At the bottom was a bewildering mix of colorful clothes, food and noise. Heward located some stone steps and the three heroes carefully descended. It was soon made clear that they had indeed found the entrance to the city of Ch'ltl of the MaiYuan, and had drawn closer to their goal, to steal the Scroll of Vile Darkness, rumored to be somewhere within.
