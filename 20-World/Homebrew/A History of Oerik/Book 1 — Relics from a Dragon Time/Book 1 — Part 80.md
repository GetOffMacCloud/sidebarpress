## Part 80

### 463 SD

Silvervein Moradinson had forged the Axe of the Dwarven Lords with Moradin, his god ancestor. He was the Prince Regent for millions of Dwur, but he was losing his subjects, for he had shown himself to be a weak leader.

Duergar had begun migrating to the surface and chose the Crystalmists as a new homeland. This upset the gnomes, who warned Silvervein of atrocities the duergar had inflicted on the svirfneblin in the Underdark.

The duergar defended their actions. The svirfneblin gnomes were invaders and must be fought. Some dwarves showed sympathy, others did not.

Silvervein desperately wanted to welcome the lost duergar and assimilate them with his people. Despite their stone-grey complexion, their scowly faces and their gruff manners, they had been dwur once, and they could be dwur again.

Yet, the Underdark had changed them, which was undeniable. The duergar struggled with the concepts of kindness and acceptance. Many were blatantly greedy and brutal. Fights between the duergar and dwarves usually ended with the body of a dwarf found with a dagger in their back the next morning.

A number of dwarves also with the Moradinson name (cousins for the most part), protested loudly. The duergar must be sent back, except for, perhaps, a handful that had adopted civilized ways. Silvervein was at a loss for what to do. Attempts to modify the duergar's behavior were looked upon as hostile. The duergar were the true dwur, and it was the Overlight dwarves that had lost their edge.

Soon the Moradinson clan put their hands up to take the throne by force, if necessary.

The Moradin Hammers, religious bodyguards of the Prince Regent, arrested the most vocal Moradinson elders for sedition. This riled up the general dwur population and soon the bitter sight of Dwur fighting Dwur was seen across the mountains of Eastern Oerik.

In dwarven enclaves further east, the Stewards, mortified at the violence, attempted to contact Great Moradin Soulforger and bring him back to Oerik so that he could sort out the mess.

But Moradin did not answer and he did not return. Locked in an existential war with Gruumsh and his orc armies on another world, all his energies were subsumed in the struggle, and their calls were in vain.

Across the Solnor Sea, in Mystara, the dwarves of Rockhome closed their doors to duergar. They were not true dwur, but twisted and evil phantoms from the depths of Oerth.

This went against the ruling of Silvervein, who was Prince Regent of all the dwur, including Rockhome. The duergar had returned to the surface, and they could go wherever they pleased.

The Hammers were brought into Rockhome to arrest the Rockhome Steward. This was not something that the Rockhome people could tolerate. This was Crystalmistian Overreach!
