## Part 102

The wraiths went into the graveyard and let out a great keening that shattered the silence of the night.

From the graves emerged various undead beings never before seen on Oerth. Most were ghouls, ravenous with hunger who sprung from their graves then went down the road, devouring any stragglers lingering in the early morning darkness.

Others were severely decomposed corpses too far gone to become anything other than zombies or skeletons. They meandered about with no control.

A few of the graves contained the bodies of high-ranked personages from the town's history, and they returned as wights, wraiths and specters.

Once all that could arise had arisen, the fallen duergar crumbled to dust, their negative energy spent.

As for the shadows that had trailed the dead marchers, the shadows created by the necromantic breath of the Undead Dragon, well, they slunk into the cottages and silently murdered everyone in their beds, turning them into shadows as well.
