## Part 17

The wars between the demihumans and the illithids had stretched across more than Oerik and even Oerth. Because gates and portals were easy to conjure up 10,000 years ago, great battles and armies fought on several continents and worlds including Toril.

The outcome after the mind flayers abandoned the field was that a vast number of dazed, confused humans were left behind in many places, on numerous worlds, without any understanding of where they were, or why, as their minds were made blank.

Released from bondage, they were free in theory but alien to the land, with no cultural history, no technology and, frightened, were hostile to each other and practically everything else.

The elves, some of whom had regretted the peace with the dwarves, saw an opportunity to grab new slaves. After all, these savages were creating anarchy in the beautiful forests and glens.

Within one hundred years very few humans would not be in the thrall of their new keepers, the beautiful elves of the Fey.
