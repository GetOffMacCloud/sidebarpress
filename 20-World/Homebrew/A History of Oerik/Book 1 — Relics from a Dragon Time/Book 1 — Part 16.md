## Part 16

The mind flayers gated hordes of their thrall breeds to devastate Oerik. Block-whites and systematic brights, as well as other varieties of human, bred mainly for taste, were also strong and ferocious. Armed with axes and spears, they fought single-mindedly against the dwarves and elves, for they were in the complete thrall of their illithid masters.

The mind flayers were enraged at Oerik for daring to raid their enclave, and in the night the illithids themselves would teleport into the dwellings of powerful leaders and kidnap them. Moradin the Soul Forger himself was ambushed, but he wore a magic helm that thwarted psionic attacks, and he and his bodyguards beat back the mind flayers.

Moradin forged more magic helms and gave them to his elite forces; this blunted many illithid attacks.

Corellon the Elf Lord used different tactics. They summoned invisible stalkers into the mind flayer worlds themselves, where they unleashed their rage upon the visible realm, slaying and rampaging at will. This was an unexpected development that the illithids had not been prepared for. They suffered unacceptable losses before they developed a mental guard that revealed invisibility. Then, in wild fury, the mind flayers released their reserves, the especial blend.

These human breeds were lithe, dexterous, cunning and aggressive. With the added reserves on Oerth, the thralls far outnumbered the demihumans, but their lack of tactics and crude fighting made them largely ineffective after their initial forays.

Eventually, the illithids came to the conclusion that the lesser beings of the Material Plane had reached a new threshold. No longer could the sheer intellectual weight of the mind flayers be enough to assert full control. It was time to withdraw to select worlds of darkness and chilling rain. Let the elves and dwarves and humans destroy themselves.

With their breeding pens stocked, and with their precious tomes and scrolls preserved, the illithids largely retreated from the bright worlds, and those thralls on Oerik and other parts of Oerth and other worlds (which this history covers only lightly) were released from mind control and gained their freedom for better or worse.
