## Part 110

### 528 SD

The Undead Dragon stirred atop her treasure mound.

She had been asleep for many centuries, but something had disturbed her; she wasn't sure what...

A red glow appeared in one eye socket then peered about.

A red glow then manifested in her other eye socket then peered about also.

Then the two glowing eyes synchronized. She was awake!

Something was different.

Dragons know their treasure hoards intimately. They take tremendous care in sorting out the pile to their particular liking and she Knew.

There were possessions Missing.

Yes! Thieves had been here! Thieves had violated her private resting ground!

The dragon skeleton rose to her feet and stretched out her long wing bones, which caused them to creak and whistle.

The bones in her neck rattled when she tossed her head from side to side, hoping to catch a glimpse of the intruders, but they were long gone.

She must leave her place and hunt them down!

With the thrust of her grasping wings she rose into the air and headed towards the exit of her cave.

As she propelled herself forward she felt a sharp vibration surge through her, then heard a crack.

Her tail sheared off and crashed onto her treasure mound, splintering into pieces. In a terrifying chain reaction, her hindquarters broke off next, followed by the wing bones.

Wingless, the rest of the skeleton tore into fragments and fell.

In freefall, she desperately tried to right herself, but she was only the skull. Then, the skull clipped a stalagmite and burst to powder.

The negative forces that had sustained her and given her so much power had bled into the Drachensgrab itself. Her time was done, but, with her bones crumbling into dust, a final burst of negative energy freed itself and fed the cavern so much corruption and hate that the whole cave system totally collapsed.

The lair of the Undead Dragon was destroyed.

After the cataclysmic surge, there was a quiet stillness, almost serenity.

Then everything exploded.

The rocks melted into plasma and magma, reopening the cave.

The treasure was vaporized.

A Ghost Dragon haunted the cave.

Once she was a collection of several beautiful dragons, soaring the lands, reveling in life.

Then she was an assemblage of bones, the creation of vengeful Tiamat.

Now she was formless, powerless, a lost memory.

The Ghost Dragon seeped across the cave, a poisonous mist, then returned to her sleep.

And within the mist a newborn malevolence awoke.
