## Part 145

Pale brown and white centaurs cavorted with each other, completely ignoring Heward and Lathia attempting to push through them across the meadow.

Heward had lived in the Fey lands for about seven hundred years and had grown a small silvery beard to go with his long brown hair. Once magically imprisoned by jealous satyrs, Heward had used his natural charm and wit to finally win them over.

Once free, Heward decided to stay in Fey, for the place was a utopia.

Creatures and beings of myriad hues and shapes mingled freely and there was very little governance.

There were marriages, but also many "free" relationships. No one judged. No one cared.

Crime was rare and usually mischievous rather than malevolent.

The wine was great, and drinking slightly to excess was almost a cultural duty.

And the sky was a brilliant pink during the day and a deep violet at night.

Heward eventually became the Freeberger of this district. Freebergers were ostensibly meant to manage and keep the peace, but in reality were in charge of the parties and banquets.

Lathia was Heward's current companion, and they had trysted for only seventeen years, so the relationship was still in its first bloom.

Lathia was a Folksparker, a friendly, easygoing order of nymphs, dedicated to improving relations between Fey folk.

The centaurs continued their courting and were still blocking the gentle road, which was beginning to irk Heward a bit. He was anxious to taste the best barrels of Ashberry wine, and if he and Lathia were delayed much longer they would have to settle for an inferior batch.

Princess Reverina Gossermay only put on the Convivial Entertainment Extravaganza every six years, and the pixies and brownies who made up the bulk of Heward's district were tremendously excited. The Princess always had quirky games to play along with the entertainments. The Extravaganza was almost a competition for the "most partiest!"

The pale red sun was low in the sky and causing the northern horizon to shimmer with streaks of scarlet and yellow when Heward and Lathia were finally escorted to their reserved seats near Princess Gossermay in the Blue Garden.

The middle-aged, dark-skinned Princess didn't acknowledge them as by now she was deeply sozzled and attempting to flirt with a stag-headed kikopath. Heward did try and make eye contact a couple of times but her eyes, famed for their brightly orange spark, were at this moment glassy and lemon yellow.

Heward nodded sardonically to Lathia; several pixies and brownies were sprawling about the table. Some were even asleep in the blue buttercup beds. Clearly there had been some fairy folk who had made it to the Extravaganza on time!

Lathia found Heward's dry wit rather humorless on this occasion. The human was pouting because the best wine was gone. Heward could be a real child.

Lathia decided that she would enjoy herself at any rate, so she took up a conversation with a fawn-colored dryad.

Sulking and also bored, Heward left the table and went into the manor to see if he could scrounge some forgotten liqueur in the cellars. He strode across a corridor and as he did so he heard deep voices coming from a nearby chamber. Something about the broken dissonance of the voices worried Heward, so he decided to turn himself invisible and eavesdrop.

Heward silently entered the chamber, and there, lounging on a gaudy golden chaise lounge and drinking out of crystal goblets were two Fey beings of a sort that he had never seen before. They looked somewhat like satyrs, but their shaggy lower legs ended with ostrich feet instead of goat hooves. Their skin was dark grey and covered in tattoos of circles and stars. Their shaggy manes and hair were crimson shot with azure blue, and their goat horns were jet black. Their faces were wrinkled and their noses were broad and snouty.

"...firbolgs in agreement as well?" asked one to the other. The voice was cutting and atonal.

"No, you were correct. The cloud giants dealt with them."

The first creature snorted derisively.

"See what comes from sensitivity, Gumish? Problems. Complications. The firbolgs should have been enslaved as soon as..."

Gumish had been wrinkling his nose as the other spoke. Now he raised a spindly hand.

"Gaxx, can you smell something? The air is different."

Heward realized that it was time to go. He turned to the open door but felt his body lift up into the air, then he felt himself hurled across the room.

Groaning in pain, Heward tried to get back to his feet but he couldn't move. What was happening? Could the creatures see him?

Yes, they could. They had drawn emerald-hilted daggers and were bearing down on him...
