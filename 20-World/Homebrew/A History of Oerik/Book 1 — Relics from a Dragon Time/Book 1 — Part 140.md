## Part 140

Phizzat looked into the mind of the captured elf and stared into the face of a giant...

<center>* * *</center>

Caowin glared at the fomorian giant until the lumpy, ugly being finally nodded its head in submission.

At last the war was over; the fomorians had accepted their rule of the realm just as the firbolg giants had a century past.

"Misshapen creature, live now in peace. Your land is our land and our land is your land. Let not a shadow spoil the seed of the flower or the sunlight on the dale."

The malformed giant,[^1] once the Tyrant of Quelm, bowed his oblong head and spoke these words:

"Light of lights, Star of stars, truly your people are giant in spirit as we are in body. You have bested us in battle and in peace. You know the flesh of life and the bones of death. We live in the mountains at your will, and happy we will be in doing so."

Caowin went up to the kneeling giant and kissed its brow. The peace was sealed.

<center>* * *</center>

Phizzat was puzzled. He had learned precious little from his reading of the elf's mind. He had never heard of fomorians or firbolgs, or Quelm. Where in Oerth had these glowing elves come from?

[^1]: Malformed according to Caowyn. Not according to the giants.
