## Part 42

### −12 SD

Risewater was settled by the Lowly Ones two years after fleeing the Singing Lands. It was a fortified settlement built atop a lone hill behind the river Geff in the Western Arids of Oerik.

With the river acting as a natural moat, and nestled within a fertile depression with plenty of large game, nuts and fruits, it was a good place to defend. The wandering Lowly Ones were relieved to establish such a place, for they had struggled to find something permanent. Parched lands and hostile monsters had thinned down their numbers. Hundreds were lost before the greenery around Geff was discovered.

The place was not uninhabited. Tribes of norkers roamed these lands, fighting raiding gnolls from the west and south. The Lowly Ones warred with both, and they searched for and destroyed their hidden lairs.

Heward led many patrols clearing the remaining monsters and humanoids from the vicinity. Farms were established and mines were dug. The Lowly Ones had finally established a new home.

But the Singing Ones had not forgotten the Lowly Ones' treachery. Not being particularly martial, they hired elven mercenaries from Kara-Tur, and what elves! If the Singing Ones were corrupted, what could you call these debauched ghoul-elves that wandered the Unseelie Haunted realms and worshipped the unspeakable unnatural Elder Gods?

Ruthless and implacable, riding massive pitch-black beasts in the shape of capes, armed with throwing stars and battle staves, they poured down onto Risewater and glided their black cape monsters across the river.

The corrupted elves soon overwhelmed the defenders and broke down the gates. Men, women and children were struck down, their souls sucked into the metaphysical maw of the Unseen.

While many Lowly Ones ran about, wailing and shrieking, Win-Daled, Ye'Cind and Heward led a fight back. Win-Daled and Ye'Cind cast translucent missile barrages, but a throwing star sliced through and struck Heward in the thigh.

The ghoul-elves counterattacked with fire bolts and Win-Daled and Ye'Cind were forced to retreat, and things were getting grim, when a sturdy group of young Lowly Ones jumped in, casting devastating ice meteors taught them by the Blemu.

Now the elves fell back, and Heward, despite his injury, rallied hard and brought down several retreating too slowly with a flurry of blows. A couple of riderless black cape monsters took to the air and flew off.

The few ghoul-elves who had not gated back to Kara-Tur turned themselves invisible and spent the next few nights roaming unseen, and killed several leaders including a former thief master, before Heward turned invisible himself, stalked them, then dispatched them.

Following the assault, Risewater was left in peace by the end of −12 SD.
