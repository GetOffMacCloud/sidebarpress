## Part 72

The Gaming Barons of Thel Norusti wanted Norebo dead.

They weren't quite sure how, but somehow Norebo was getting his followers to win every time they played. Now the casinos and gambling houses were all closed. Norebo would pay!

Assassins came to Norebo's house. He wasn't home. A seer was used to trace him and discovered he had fled to Sunndi. Off to Sunndi the posse went.

Sunndi was a paradise. The weather was warm, the plants thrived. Grey elves ruled, but wisely. These were not arrogant elves like Celene, or patronizing, like Blemu. Sunndi was a simple place designed for harmless fun with as little work as possible, free-spirited and sociable. Some Flan lived here amongst the olves, many old retirees from Thel Nostra.

The seer reached the forests of Sunndi, then threw dust into the air. She watched the dust fall back to Oerth and settle. She watched hawks circling in the air, she watched a bear cub chase a badger. She watched a stag move one way, then the other, before disappearing into a thicket.

Then the seer announced that she knew where Norebo was hiding.

The posse marched through the forest until they reached an olven house. They smashed down the door and burst in.

The olven house was the home of Ye'Cind. She was outraged. Who dared to invade her sanctuary? The assassins grabbed at her but only grasped thin air. Ye'Cind had turned into a shade.

Ye'Cind yelled at them, threatening them with retribution, but she seemed unable to hurt them, just as they couldn't harm her.

The assassins eventually ignored her, and began hunting around for the hidden Norebo. Ye'Cind then pulled out her recorder (also made of shade stuff) and blew a dirge.

Three assassins were so overcome by the despairing music that their hearts stopped beating and they fell down like stones. The other assassins, led by the stoic seer, retched but carried on.

One found a locked door. Another blast from the recorder and this time the music was full of merriment. The assassins were dancing with each other, overcome with amorous ideas. The seer threw pepper in their eyes which brought them back to their senses.

The seer then uttered a curse upon Ye'Cind and blasted her with a black ray, but the evil force passed harmlessly through her ghostly form.

While Ye'Cind was distracted, the largest assassin smashed open the door with his axe, then stepped into the light, airy room.

He saw a sleeping figure on a bed. He called out, bringing the assassins rushing in.

They recognized the sleeper. It was Norebo.

And his body lay very, very still in the bed.

Norebo was already dead.

"He died two days ago," murmured Ye'Cind from the doorway, "and it was very peaceful. I played for him as he went."

The assassins took the corpse of Norebo and returned back to Thel Norusti.

The Magnate ordered Norebo to be buried upright and upside down, never to rest in peace!

Afterwards, though, the Magnate and his Investors were unable to stop a great wake held in his memory, both in Thel Nostra and far away in the Suel lands within Western Oerik.
