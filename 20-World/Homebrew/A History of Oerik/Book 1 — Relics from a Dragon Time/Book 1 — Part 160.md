## Part 160

### 774 SD

Her Children had been punished enough.

The two fat spiders crouched down before their Mother, covered in thick, porridgy goo. The Queen of Chaos hoped that this would be a lesson learned.

Lolth and Miska were soon put to work. Miska used scouts to find a winding pass through the Hellfurnaces into the Singing Lands, negotiating safe passage with humanoids and fire giants of the mountains with treasure taken from his personal hoard. He wouldn't argue with his Queen. The Singing Ones, the ultimate targets, were formidable foes so his hillmen forces were used to spy and skirmish with them rather than outright invade.

Lolth was most useful if she were free to terrorize and devour those that fell into disfavor, so the Queen of Chaos made her The Enforcer. The Queen herself now found more time to sleep.

<center>* * *</center>

In Goodmonth 774 SD a pirate barge made it upriver all the way to the lake. The vessel had fled rival brigands and this isolated realm would allow them to heal and recover. They had no idea that the Queen of Chaos ruled here.

They were quickly overcome by her worshippers and robbed of their armor and weapons. The surviving pirates were tortured for days in public by Lolth until she grew bored of them and ate them out in the open.

Incabulos had watched the torture wide-eyed. His mother had forbidden him to watch Lolth's work but he had come regardless. He was in awe of Lolth. How he wished to have power like the gods! Imagine the fear he would create.

Nobody would mess with him anymore, not even Nerull!

But Nerull and he were very good friends; Nerull had always said so. Hopefully the two of them would share Godly Power together.

He hoped so.

The funny thing was that Nerull wasn't there watching the pirates get what they deserved. Incabulos laughed out loud, drawing looks.

Incabulos would need to be careful because he didn't want to give away the fun secret.

Nerull had caught a wizard!
