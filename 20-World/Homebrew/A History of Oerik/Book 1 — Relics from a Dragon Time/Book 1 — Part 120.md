## Part 120

### 579–605 SD

The Singing Lands were not ready for a Demon Queen to rule them.

After Pious Grimm Quartztusk escaped the clutches of the Queen of Chaos, he was quick to tell the elves about the vile happenings in the dungeons below.

A posse of high-powered wizards and fighters went down to investigate, so the grieving Queen dimensionally gated back to the Abyss with her two offspring. She wanted no conflict.

The Queen of Chaos found a quiet, dark place within the Abyss, a small valley hidden by a sulfuric waterfall.

There, safe amongst scarlet toadstools and belching salamanders, the Queen named the two hatchlings.

The male spiderling was called Miska, and the elf-shaped female was called Lolth.

<center>* * *</center>

Pious Grimm Quartztusk made it back to the Crystalmists where he once again took control of the Moradin Hammers.

The Dwur were pleased to see their doughty cleric, for he had been lost in the Singing Lands for a very long time. They were also curious about his clockwork legs, as was Pious himself. Perhaps the gnomes would know more.

<center>* * *</center>

Northwest of the Barrier Peaks, in the land of the Wind Dukes, the tension between the Vaati and their human neighbors was becoming ever more tense. The Pyanor loved to skirmish and bait their neighbors, encroaching closer and closer.

In addition, the human population within the Vaati borders was growing too fast and was becoming unmanageable. The orderly Wind Dukes therefore decided to establish a new borderland between Vaati and the Pyanor.

The new land was called Pesh, and it was stocked with young psionically dominated humans who would be loyal to the Wind Dukes but semi-independent and self-sufficient.

The Vaati hoped that this buffer would keep the Wind Dukes secure for the foreseeable future.
