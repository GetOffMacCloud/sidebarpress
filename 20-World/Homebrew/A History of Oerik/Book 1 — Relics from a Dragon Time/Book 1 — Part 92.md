## Part 92

### 500 SD

Six cities jealously guarded the richest Suel lands; small towns, villages and hamlets filled with struggling inhabitants filled the rest.

Each city had its own ruler chosen from amongst seven families. The Families were competitive with each other, and maneuvered and manipulated their way to success.

The Seven Families and the Twenty-Three Lesser Families were generally well-educated and many of the cleverest individuals had spellcasting ability. The Families lived as luxuriously as possible while the ordinary people eked out a meager existence as laborers, builders and serfs.

Syrul the Goddess nominally ruled over all, and she was devoutly worshipped, but in actuality she had become enamored of the Outer Planes and spent most of her time there.

No central oversight meant that the cities became independent rivals, only united by culture. They warred, allied and reneged continually, each city juggling for ascendancy, but none strong enough to dominate for long.

Syrul looked in on her people occasionally, and she saw that they remained strong. The elves had not reclaimed them, the orcs had not destroyed them. Whether or not the Suel were happy or not, whether they had enough food or wealth, was of little interest to her. Were they still worshipping her? Yes, they were.

Syrul was satisfied.

Roaming between the villages and settlements were the Ranger-Knights. Heroes for the common folk, they rode upon the backs of great prehistoric mammals such as titanotheres and paraceratherium. They fought bandits and humanoids, though some were themselves bandits and outlaws. The cities detested them, but on occasion one city or another used them for their own ends.
