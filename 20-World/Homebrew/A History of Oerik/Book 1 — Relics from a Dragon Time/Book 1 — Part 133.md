## Part 133

For thousands of years the krakens traveled the spirit worlds, their bloated, tentacled bodies slumbering in the dark depths of the oceans while their souls flew through higher dimensions beyond the Known Planes of Existence.

No ego, no passion, no dreams haunted their roaming, and their bodies lay safe and magically protected at the bottom of the world.

Millennia passed and slowly, very slowly, they began to feel a little dissatisfied. Edgy. Bored.

The Wisdom bestowed upon the krakens had turned full circle. Once more they craved to feel, to clutch prey with their tentacles, to squeeze and rend. To seize and devour. To fight. To roar. To dominate.

The krakens were now so enormously wise that they saw the nihilism in perfection, the emptiness of bliss, the loneliness of connection with the raw life force of the cosmos.

They hungered again.

Reawoken in their physical forms, the krakens searched for the Black Pearl, the font of their power.

It was time to overthrow the Dragon Lords and swallow the Oerth.

Millennia passed but the Black Pearl could not be found. The krakens didn't know it but the Pearl had become sentient. She had no love for the krakens and considered them crude, rapacious animals.

She therefore did not reveal herself and stayed lost, invisible as clear water.

The krakens eventually gave up the search and forgot about the Black Pearl, and of the Elder Gods they once worshipped.

The krakens were once more the monsters of the Deep.

The Black Pearl may have stayed lost until the very end of Oerth herself, but one day she heard something intriguing. Some tiny things, quite harmless and insignificant, were wandering down here in the dark, singing with silent voices and radiating pure love. And pure Faith.

She had found her people.
