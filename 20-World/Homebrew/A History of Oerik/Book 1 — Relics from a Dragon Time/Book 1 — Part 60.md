## Part 60

The Shady Satyr in the town of Darvail was well known as the best gambling house in all of the Eastern Kingdoms. The Host Wenches were friendly and efficient, the Card Handlers and Dice Runners scrupulously fair and the food and drink were expensive but excellent. But it was the entertainment that sent its reputation to the skies.

Plays were performed by top actors and magic acts utilized real magic. The singers were exotic elves from the Singing Lands and there were often lutists, trumpeters and drummers from all across the Flanaess. But no performance could reach the heights of Ye'Cind and her recorder.

Ye'Cind would step onto the stage in a traditional elven robe carrying what looked like a wooden flute. She would gaze directly at the audience (who were often still playing cards and dice) then put the recorder to her lips.

Time would stop. No card was turned, no die was cast. The sound that came from Ye'Cind's recorder was of all-encompassing melody. Soft and low then loud and high, then high and low at the same time. It sounded like an orchestra of instruments coming from a single innocuous piece of wood. Nothing of its like had ever been heard on Oerth or any other world.

Who could tell how long she played. But when she finished and put the flute down, the patrons would not clap or yell. Tears in their eyes, even in the coldest assassin, they openly wept. Then ordered more drinks.
