## Part 27

Filled with rage and despair, Esmir decided to return to the Singing Lands with his servant, Gal-Lek. His wife had been murdered by the evil Dweomyne, the so-called Whip Lady. Of this he was sure. A reckoning would be had, and Ye'Cind and the young girl were better off where they were, in Blemu with the elves and Flan.

Esmir and Gal-Lek gated to a quiet temple on the outskirts of the capital. Both recoiled involuntarily; there was the sweet, coppery stench of blood in the air. After each giving a concerned glance, they looked about. The Singing One and the secretary were puzzled: where were the beatific statues of elf and faerie kin, the usual stuff found in the dreamlike temples of the Singing Lands? Instead, ugly twisted serpents coiled around black pillars, staring at the intruders with beady yellow eyes, and pools of blood collected around depressions carved into the floors that were hard not to step into.

Heading down a corridor, Esmir could make out the distant sound of chanting. Slowly drawing closer, the chanting grew louder, harsh, clipped syllables croaked out from the chanters' mouths, so different from the polyphonic harmonies usually emitted by Singing Ones. The snakes hissed and spat as our two heroes drew swords and reached the far doorway from which came the cacophony.

In the main chamber several silvery Singing One priests, in mid chant, were blasted into oblivion by the furious Esmir, bolts of green fire arcing out from Esmir's hand.

Gal-Lek the Lowly One shot past, blade outstretched, and entered into hand-to-hand combat with a couple of priests who were fumbling for their daggers. As they fell, Esmir fired more magical bolts into the room. In quick time a few groggy congregants, brows furrowed in bewilderment, were all that remained.

Esmir wanted answers, but all the disheveled worshippers could manage was a confused stammering.

"The demons are not to be denied," moaned one. "You will submit to her," whispered another.

Esmir grabbed a congregant and waved his blade at their neck.

"Who is she? The Whip Lady?" he spat. "You would have me submit to her, Dweomyne, the Punisher? The one who killed my wife? I will have all the Singing Lands fall to fire and sword before I bend to her! It was the Whip Lady who did the foul murder, for I have seen her mark!"

Crack!!

Gal-Lek felt the stinging edge of the whip caress the tips of his fingers, forcing him to drop his sword.

Crack!!

Esmir fortuitously scrambled backward, the worshipper he had berated taking the blow from the heavy but lithe whip.

For standing on the ceiling, spider fashion, was the sneering Whip Lady, hair tousled about her shoulders, a red and black mottled snake wrapped and spitting around her waist, wielding her fabulous whip.
