## Part 132

Four heroic adventurers entered the Ierendi court and pushed two shackled kuo-toa before the delegation.

The kuo-toa glared defiantly with their bulging fish eyes and burbled incoherently.

The heroes still wore their shell armor and a Conch of Water Breathing hung around three of their necks (the fourth, a sea elf, had a Sponge of Air Breathing around her neck instead). They bowed and told of their underwater expedition. Apparently a large number of kuo-toa had gathered inside a partially submerged cave and had harnessed powerful magic gifted them by their goddess to wreak havoc upon the land folk.

The kuo-toa were interrogated at length but refused to answer any questions put to them except to say that they loved their Dear Blibdoolpoolp over and over.

Who was "Dear Blibdoolpoolp"? Divination indicated she was an ancient Goddess of the Dragon Times, though what exactly her history was could not be determined.

So the kuo-toa and their god were behind the attacks! Dear Blibdoolpoolp must be powerful indeed to summon krakens to her will, for the adventurers had also fought off two enormous krakens during their quest.

The Ierendi Elf Queen was dismayed by these revelations and counseled caution. They needed to know more.

The other delegates, representatives of the Thytic, Mai and Flan Sea Companies, didn't agree.

Disgusting fish men! They would pay for bloodying the nose of the Human Empires...
