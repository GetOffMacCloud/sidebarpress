## Part 91

Pious Grimm Quartztusk nursed his tender head and stared into reality.

Fighting all of the dwur who didn't agree with him was achieving very little. While Moradin remained absent there was no way consensus could be reached for any of the potential rulers, including himself. The best thing he could do therefore was to pull out of the leadership challenge and seek another way.

<center>* * *</center>

Removing the shackles of High Steward from his wretched body left Pious breathing more freely, and his headaches somewhat subsided.

Somewhere, upon the highest peak of the Sulhauts, was said to be a wise spirit from the Seven Heavens.

They were called the shedu, and it was said to be a winged bull with a dwarven head.

Pious determined to travel there and seek their wise counsel, and shortly after, he and his best Hammers left the Crystalmists.

The winding mountain passes were extremely treacherous. Ogres, goblins, kobolds and xvarts swarmed everywhere, and any dwur communities they would come upon would likely be hostile.

After some tough encounters, the decision was made to skirt the mountains altogether and travel west along the outskirts of the elven Singing Lands before heading back north and into the Sulhauts.

Three days after crossing the border their loud clanking weapons and armor were heard by a Singing One patrol.

There were seventeen of them, and they waited until the dwarves had curled up in their blankets before approaching.

Before he knew it, Pious and his men had been tied up, chained and taken away in a wagon, pulled along by the slender but strong elves.

The elves taunted the dwarves as they rolled the wagon along, singing with beautiful voices but using insulting and foul words.

The brightness of their songs contrasted with their pitted words, and Pious's insistence that they were "all on the same side" made them laugh.

"Ugly dwarves!" they chanted. "Ugly monsters! What shall we do with you?"

"Gobble them up!"

"Use their bones for stew!"

"Bury them up to their necks in an ants' nest, with honey!"

The silvery laughter of the silvery elves rang out like crystal bells.

<center>* * *</center>

Pious and his Hammers were taken to the Singing Ones' fair city and locked in the Torture Halls, suffering for the entertainment of jaded elves with sad eyes and a few coins to spare.

They were carefully watched by young and keen guards, so there was never an opportunity to escape.

Every night, in the few hours they were left alone and allowed to somewhat recover, Pious Grimm Quartztusk prayed fervently to Moradin for salvation, but although all of his body hurt, his headaches were gone.
