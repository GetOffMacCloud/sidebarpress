## Part 21

They were known as the Singing Ones.

9,000 years ago elves from Kara-Tur settled in the basin southwest of the Hellfurnaces. They were striking elves, perhaps the most handsome and comely of all the elves of all the world, sporting silvery eyes, skin and hair. They had voices to match.

Almost all of them could sing great long arias or ballads with complex harmony and profound feeling. The Neolithic human tribes of the subtropical forests who heard and saw them working, feasting, gardening and playing were in complete awe.

These tribes were white-skinned and fair-haired despite the clime. They had been especially bred by the illithids for the taste of their brains and the fair complexion was a mere side effect, but that was over a thousand years earlier and quite forgotten.

The humans were no match for the elvish interlopers and were soon thralls like their ancestors (but at least they weren't cultivated and eaten). The Singing Ones thought of the humans as uncouth barbarians but useful workers and had them cultivating, farming and mining, in the latter case, mainly in the mountains to the south.

In just a few generations the humans forgot their tribal names and their hostility towards each other. They were all grouped together with a single elvish word meaning insect, spider or worm, but a more accurate translation would be something like "the Lowly Ones."
