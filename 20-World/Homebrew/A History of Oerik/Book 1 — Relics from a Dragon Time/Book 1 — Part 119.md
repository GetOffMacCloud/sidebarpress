## Part 119

Pious Grimm Quartztusk tried to stamp on the lumpy hatchlings in an attempt to crush them.

They retaliated by spitting acid into his face.

Pious yelped and backed away, clutching his nose.

Angry black eyes popped open in the blobs and they snarled. They shot out more acid at Pious, burning his arm and chest.

Desperately, he grabbed a loose stone and threw it into one of the hatchlings, splattering it into black, sticky goo. The two remaining blobs grew many long black hairy legs and scuttled into different corners of the chamber.

Pious went to take up the stone again, but the goo burned his hand, forcing him to drop it.

Panicked, he backed away, looking for an exit. The trapdoor in the ceiling was still open...

THESE CREATURES ARE A BANE TO OERTH!

The words burned inside Pious's skull almost as much as the acid burned his skin. He must continue to fight!

Pious Grimm Quartztusk raised his hands and roared. A great pillar of blue flame congealed in the air, then struck down and smote the blob to Pious's left.

Another gesture, another violent utterance...

...a giant ghostly blue hammer appeared in his hand. He brought the hammer down on the remaining blob and squashed it...

...but the left blob had regenerated itself. It had grown into a spiderling with a human baby's head. Howling and growling, it advanced on the dwarf.

The second blob regenerated too. It had taken the form of a tiny violet-skinned elvish girl. It advanced as well.

TOO LATE, FLEE FOR YOUR MORTAL LIFE!

The voice of Moradin radiated through Pious's mind again. He turned the key in his hip and leapt up through the hole in the ceiling...

... to find himself face to face with the Queen of Chaos.

"What have you done to my CHILDREN?!?" she cried, then launched herself bodily at him with her gigantic bulk.

Her tentacles quested to grab and rend him, but his clockwork legs sprang him up and out of the horror's grasp. With lightning speed, Pious Grimm burst down a corridor, flew up some stairs, then was up and out of the dungeon and in the open.

"My CHILDREN, my CHILDREN!" shrieked the Queen of Chaos, giving up on Quartztusk and turning to her eggs.

She shambled down into the chamber and saw her eggs smashed into black ooze. She tossed back her head and wailed.

"Never mind, Mamma, the important ones are still alive!"

The softly spoken elvish girl giggled and a drop of venom fell from her mouth and steamed on the floor.

The spiderling with the baby's head barked with approval.
