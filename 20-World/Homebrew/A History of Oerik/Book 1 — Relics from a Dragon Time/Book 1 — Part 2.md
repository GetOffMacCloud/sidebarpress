## Part 2

### −20,000 SD

The Dragon Schism, whereby the dragon families attempted to wrest full control of the Empires, was a major disruption on Oerth. Many worlds were interconnected by portals that allowed empires to rule different planets simultaneously. The effect on Oerth was profound, because the vacuum created by the deaths of countless dragons and dracoforms in the horrific wars allowed new types of intelligent life to emerge out from the shadows.

The first elves, or Olve, as they called themselves, had arrived on Oerth over a thousand years earlier as slave races for the dragons, but during the Schism the Olve liberated themselves: first the Tuatha de Dannan, who discovered and joined with the Fey dimension, then later the lesser Olve, tied closer to Oerth.

At the final end, the various dragon empires had disintegrated, and the dragons themselves had become scarce. Their leaders fled to the Outer Planes and many worlds grew wild and free. The elves proliferated, relics left behind in the apocalypse, and their story is next...
