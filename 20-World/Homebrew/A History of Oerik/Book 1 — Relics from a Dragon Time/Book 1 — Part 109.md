## Part 109

One by one small villages succumbed to the undead.

The uncanny malevolence that had leached out from the Drachensgrab spread out like a noxious mist.

Within months all of the border settlements in the Oerik portion of Thel Norusti were inundated with shadows, ghouls, wights and wraiths. The clerics and sages had no answers. They knew of ghosts, and the Ur-Flan had created skeletons and zombies, but these terrifying apparitions were a mystery.

In the town of Cleghube, a gaggle of young children appeared on the main street playing hopscotch. They were all glassy-eyed and their skin was translucent and clammy.

They had no idea that they were dead.

The children excitedly greeted passersby.

Then they were dead too.

In Gluminspire, a troop of traveling performers set up tents in the square then performed dramas, juggling and music. When crowds of people gathered inside for the show, deep shadows in the corners spread out like spilt ink and engulfed the living.

A squad of rangers from the Adri Forest went into Thel Norusti to quash the undead.

They were quashed instead.

Clerics prayed to the gods for guidance and the Thel Norusti authorities mustered together raw soldiers to combat the undead and hopefully not die.

Throughout the turmoil, the Flan colony run by Thel Norusti west of the Glantri mountains in Mystara found themselves with a serious problem.

Masses of hobgoblins, well-armed and armored and led by a great Cyclops riding a hideous striped beast, had invaded, and now the cities were burning.

The rulers cried for help, but the Oerik Thel Norusti could not come.

They were inundated with undead.

Thus, the young Thel Norusti civilization crumbled into pieces, each piece isolated and forced to deal with their woes independently.

The economy collapsed, and the gaming and mercantile empire run by the Oath Society fell into irrelevance.
