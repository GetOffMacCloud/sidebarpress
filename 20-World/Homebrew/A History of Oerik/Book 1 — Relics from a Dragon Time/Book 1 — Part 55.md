## Part 55

Breaking through the pass of the Darkwall mountains was grueling and costly. Bandits, gnolls and orcs swarmed the open areas and had plenty of hidden holes to retreat and emerge from. Syrul realized that her Papal army was losing numbers too quickly.

The magical bishops desperately punched into the jann enclave eventually, but guarding the city was a gigantic blue-skinned, scimitar-wielding djinn, with elemental servants of all types around her, and the jann as well.

The upshot was that Pope Syrul's army was soundly defeated. The surviving bishops and soldiers were enslaved and taken northeast to the Wind Dukes.

When the news reached the Pope, she wisely withdrew from attacking her foes and concentrated on defense.

Bandits, orcs and Highhold forces raided and tested the papists, so Syrul reached out for Divine power by exhorting all the Risewater civilians to attend the temples and offer gifts and prayers to the Goddess.

Did the Goddess manifest herself and wreak bloody vengeance upon the enemy? No, not exactly, but Pope Syrul and her four Cardinals claimed that the blasts of energy smiting their foes were Divine Wrath.

Over and over, pink and scarlet fire erupted out from the stomachs of the invaders, whether orc, bandit or Highholder. Their comrades writhing and burning into ash, fear overcame the remaining raiders, and they fled.

Syrul breathed a sigh of relief. The Holy Papacy of Risewater was still intact, though barely.
