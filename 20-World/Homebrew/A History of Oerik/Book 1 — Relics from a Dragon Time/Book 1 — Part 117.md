## Part 117

The Queen of Chaos laid her eggs in a secret chamber below a trapdoor. There were seven in all and each egg was large, bulbous, and mottled with sickly yellow with black spots.

The Queen pushed the eggs into a rough pile and partially covered them with dirt.

The Queen had no idea how long it would take before the eggs hatched or even what actually would hatch but she knew the young would be hungry. Very hungry.

Pious Grimm Quartztusk was thrown into the chamber with the eggs. The Queen of Chaos had chosen him to be her spawn's first meal. An absurd little dwarf!

The dwarf with the clockwork legs was no longer sane.

Poor Pious! A hundred years of imprisonment and torture, brainwashing and being dominated had ruined his body and his soul.

Yet, in the back recesses of his mind, Pious clung on to his stubborn hope, the hope that the great god Moradin would finally hear his prayers and save him.

The Queen of Chaos looked fondly upon the eggs for an hour or so, then she slithered out of the secret chamber and levitated up to the higher level of the dungeon.

Her mind was aglow with the myriad possibilities open to her now. Should she return to the Abyss? Rule the Singing Lands? Destroy everything? Would her young inherit her power or be pathetic disappointments?

Now she was hungry again! She wandered off to find someone to eat.

Pious Grimm Quartztusk was left alone with the eggs. Each was almost as big as him. His mind was not aglow: it was blank. He curled up to sleep like a cat.

"I hear you," whispered Moradin.
