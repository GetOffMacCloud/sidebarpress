## Part 81

### 464 SD

Duergar left the Underdark in large numbers, and with the permission of the Prince Regent, they settled throughout all the mountain ranges that divided east from the west. They had lived in the Underdark long enough, and it was time to see the sun and moon again.

Some duergar moved further east and settled in the Lortmil Mountains and the Kron Hills.

These duergar were arrogant and bullying, proclaiming themselves true dwur, and violently attacked the hill dwarves.

The Kron and Lortmil stewards formed a council in order to deal with the problem. It was understood that the duergar were ancestors, and that the Prince Regent in the Crystalmists had allowed them to return, but he couldn't understand the difficulties they were facing while he sat upon his granite stone.

Not wanting to directly go against Regent Silvervein's orders, they attempted to restrict the duergar to settling the emptier area of the hills alongside the Suss Forest.

In Mystara, the Rockhome dwur absolutely forbade the duergar from entering their lands. The Moradin Hammers, religious enforcers of the Prince Regent, demanded that several hundred duergar be allowed to settle there.

Lord Rockfoot of Rockhome, an enormous dwarf nearly five feet tall, ordered the Hammers away, but the Hammers refused and attempted to arrest the lord. Rockfoot slew two Hammers single-handedly before his bodyguards, the Spears, could react. They moved in on the Hammers, and forced them to retire.

In a desperate attempt to legitimize his rule over all dwur kind, Silvervein Moradinson legitimized his rule by having himself crowned King. This was considered a terrible blasphemy by some dwarves, including members of the Hammers who only recognized the absent Moradin Soul Forger as their King. Likewise, the dwarves in Eastern Oerik were outraged, and the Rockhome dwarves closed their magical gates to the Crystalmistians.

By the end of 464 SD, the Moradin Hammers had split into two factions, those who recognized Silvervein as king and those who saw him as a usurper. The Crystalmists erupted into civil war.
