## Part 136

Despite the harpoon bolts stuck in his body and tentacles, Ozmidi was proud.

He had fought well; three warships were downed.

Now it was time to return to the Deep. His wounds would be mended, and he would be revered by his community.

Ozmidi trailed ichor as he descended, attracting a couple of hammerhead sharks who balked then fled when they realized they were dealing with a kraken.

Ozmidi reached the rift in the sea floor that led into the chasm, his home.

He fancied that he heard a burbling hum, and that piqued his curiosity. He pushed off with his tentacles then glided along the rift to where the sounds seemed to be coming from.

Through a tunnel he followed the sounds which led to a great underwater cavern filled with glassy-eyed sea elves chanting in unison.

Circling them was Sekolah the Great White Shark, their god and his Master.

Good! They would greet him with honor, and admire his fresh new scars.

Ozmidi excitedly came down to Sekolah and spun around him like a dog greeting their master. The sight was rather pathetic, because the kraken was four times the size of the shark.

Sekolah, however, fixed his beady black eye at Ozmidi, stark and cold.

"You are bleeding, you stupid creature," snapped the shark, "and you are polluting the waters with its stench!"

Ozmidi was surprised. He was used to praise.

Sekolah turned from Ozmidi and nodded to the sea elves. They continued the chants.

Ozmidi reached out to Sekolah, showing his hurts. Would his Master bless him?

Sekolah ignored the wounded monster. He was looking in dismay at the waters. The ichor was bleeding into the pure waters of the cavern, coloring the sea elves ruddy. This was unfortunate.

But the taste was sultry.

Sekolah gathered his thoughts.

After he had swallowed the Black Pearl of Power he had dreamed of becoming a god and he had managed to create a small sea elf cult.

Blood.

The krakens were very useful. They adored Sekolah, for somehow they sensed a kinship with him, perhaps because of the Black Pearl.

Hungry.

Sekolah was their Patriarch.

Red.

The krakens' immense size and strength allowed Sekolah to plunder the human empires whilst making it seem as if the mad kuo-toa were the ones in control.

Desire.

Now this stupid beast was polluting his great Ascension Ceremony...

Gather your thoughts.

"Do you know what you are doing?" sneered Sekolah. "You are endangering the Ritual. Pull yourself apart!"

Ozmidi's eyes widened in fear.

"Please, your Lord, I have always been your faithful servant, please! Mercy, my..."

Tentacles ripped out of Ozmidi as if a titanic hand was pulling them from his body. The kraken writhed and wailed as fountains of inky ichor turned the cavern water thick and sticky.

The sea elf cultists continued chanting, oblivious to the polluted water covering them all from head to toe.

Sekolah sighed. It was clear to him that the Ritual was compromised but he determined to complete the ceremony regardless. And he was also very hungry. His great jaws opened and he devoured what was left of the kraken.

The Ascension Ceremony was meant to bestow upon each sea elf in the cavern a small piece of power from the Black Pearl, perhaps to allow them to change into dolphin form or imbue them with magical abilities.

As the ceremony proceeded the water in the cavern steamed and boiled. The sea elves were being cooked!

Sekolah cursed. This was not supposed to happen.

Sekolah tried to stop the ceremony but the Ritual was completed; the elves would Ascend.

The boiling steam burned the elves alive. Their bodies drifted down to the cavern floor and lay still.

Sekolah was angry with himself more than anything. He had allowed his emotions to thwart his plans - he was still more shark than god.

But the Ascension was not over. The sea elf bodies cracked open like dried-out husks, and thrusting out from them arose new beings, scaly, intelligent and vicious.

They were the sahuagin, and all those of the sea or near it would soon know them as the Sea Devils...
