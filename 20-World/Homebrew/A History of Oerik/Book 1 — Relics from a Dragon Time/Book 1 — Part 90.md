## Part 90

### 473 SD

War engulfed the dwarf realms.

Most accepted that the Axe was lost, probably forever. There were even rumors that the duergar never stole it at all, that it was actually hidden by one of the other factions in order to deny the true ruler.

The surface duergar themselves split into two opposing groups: those that stayed loyal to the duergar king and plotted for ultimate conquest of the surface dwellers, and those who liked the new lifestyle they had adopted.

Blood flowed freely and few dwarves across Eastern Oerik could escape the turmoil. There were neutrals who migrated to non-dwarven places like Blemu, and south of Celene, and some went to Sunndi where the grey elves were liberal and tolerant of all the good-aligned races, but most dwur picked a side and were caught up in the ferocious battles.

Pious Grimm Quartztusk led his most loyal Hammers against his misguided foes, delivering blow after blow.

He never gave up announcing his considered and correct position. Only Moradin could be king.

Night after night, whether asleep in blankets on the side of a mountain, or tucked within the palace, the pounding of his head continued, just as his Hammers pounded his enemies.

Moradin was calling him, but where was he, and why was he not communicating with his clerics praying continuously in the temples?

Terrible stories had filtered down to him that the humanoids had launched a secret weapon on that distant world of Toril, and that Moradin and the elf god were mortally wounded or imprisoned and their armies crushed.

And throughout the fighting, the duergar king lay tucked away in the Underdark, waiting.
