## Part 108

Moradin recuperated for seven days.

He was up and speaking after two days but there was something more distant about him; he was colder. Corellon had made a joke, and the old dwarf companion didn't even smile.

This was understandable, Corellon reasoned, but they felt some fear just the same.

Moradin had changed.

On the fourth day, Corellon reminded their friend that the wars on Toril were raging, and they must return to it soon and turn the tide.

Moradin looked through Corellon as if they weren't quite there.

The Mountain was calling him, Moradin said.

What mountain?

Moradin's eyes were lost in space.

Mt. Celestia, he said, the highest peak of the Seven Heavens.

On the seventh day, after a feast in his honor prepared by the dwarves, Moradin Soulforger said his farewells then went for a walk in the moonlight.

Corellon followed him from a distance.

Half a mile from the feast, Moradin stopped, sighed, then transformed into a small silver globe. Corellon watched the globe levitate into the air. It shot up into the night sky and disappeared.

"Good luck, Moradin," whispered Corellon. "I hope Heaven is all you hope it to be."

Corellon returned to the feast, a tear in their eye. The Elf god would return to Toril on their own.

Kavoss the Cyclops gathered all the hobgoblins that had survived the battle of Rockhome and led them west towards the cities behind the Glantri mountains.

As he rode Hucephelus, the enormous leucrotta emitting squealing giggles, he pondered his immediate future.

Kavoss had failed to destroy Moradin.

Returning to Toril, and to Gruumsh would be very bad for him. Best to achieve something, even something as mundane as amassing loads of treasure.

Looting the cities near the mountains would be a good idea then, and it would be fun. Kavoss chuckled along with his simpering steed as they trotted on.

Let the mayhem begin.
