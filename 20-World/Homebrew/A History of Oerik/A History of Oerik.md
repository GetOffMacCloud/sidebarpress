# A History of Oerik

*A History of Oerik* is a multi-book history of the continent Oerik by the in-world historian **Damien Mecoles**, compiled by Sidebar Press. Mecoles dates early events in the Suel calendar (SD) and uses current Common-tongue names throughout.

## Dating
- **SD = Suloise Dating**, the calendar of the ancient Suloise Imperium. **1 SD = −5,515 CY**, so CY ≈ SD − 5,515.
- Negative SD years fall before the Imperium's epoch: −14,000 SD ≈ −19,515 CY.
- **Mecoles writes in 623 CY** (≈ 6,138 SD), per Book 1, Part 12. His "years ago" figures are read from 623 CY.

## Lineages (series canon)
- **Suel:** illithid-bred "block-whites" (Book 1, Part 15) → the Lowly Ones, thralls of the Singing Ones in the basin southwest of the Hellfurnaces (Part 21) → the Suel.
- **Baklunish (provisional):** the illithids' "especial blend" reserves (Book 1, Part 16) → the Thytan peoples, coppery-skinned horse nomads who deserted the illithid elite units and gated to the western side of a long gulf in Western Oerik (Part 22) → the Baklunish.
- **Not linked:** the Flan (first named in Book 1, Part 25) and the Fharun (Part 19) are separate peoples; the Flan do not descend from the "systematic brights". The brights' descendants are still unassigned.

## Compiling conventions
- Light copy edit only: Mecoles' voice and line breaks are kept; typos and spacing are fixed. House American spelling applies, except "grey".
- **Corellon Larethian is "they"** throughout the series.
- **No house serial comma:** lists keep Mecoles' own comma usage.
- Class and practice names are lowercase: druidism, druids, rangers.
- Race names are lowercase unless grammar requires a capital (illithids, elves, dwarves).
- Mecoles' own footnotes become Obsidian footnotes in each Part note.
- Every change and open question is logged in each book's Editorial Notes.
