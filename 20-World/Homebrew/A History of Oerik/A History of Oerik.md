# A History of Oerik

*A History of Oerik* is a multi-book history of the continent Oerik by the in-world historian **Damien Mercoles**, compiled by Sidebar Press. Mercoles dates early events in the Suel calendar (SD) and uses current Common-tongue names throughout.

Shareable copy of Book 1 (Claude Docs): https://claude.ai/artifact/8CF2h9u5qvKKVFPPx4PLcw

## Compiling conventions
- Light copy edit only: Mercoles' voice and line breaks are kept; typos and spacing are fixed. House American spelling applies, except "grey".
- Mercoles' own footnotes become Obsidian footnotes in each Part note.
- Every change and open question is logged in each book's Editorial Notes.
