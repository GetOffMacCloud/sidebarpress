# The Archive — Discovered Documents

*Maintained by the Greyhawk University Field Survey Office, Ritterstadt Satellite Station.*

> [!info] What Belongs Here
> This archive collects **primary source material found in the field** — documents, inscriptions, letters, and objects with written content that University parties have recovered, copied, or translated. Each entry records what was found, where, when, and what is known about it. Originals are held in the Academic Registry lockbox. Copies filed here.
>
> Lore earned at the table is the most reliable lore. What appears in this archive has been confirmed by firsthand contact, not rumour.
