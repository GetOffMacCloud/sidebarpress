# Jürgen of Traft

**Perrenish mercenary captain · commander of Jürgen's Ernes (medium cavalry) · fixer.**

A nondescript man of medium build, mid-thirties; tan complexion, jet-black hair, and a straight white scar running forehead to chin (whispered to be from a giant deep in the Yatils). He always wears the **diving-eagle** sigil of his company. A veteran of Perrenland's army and many mercenary campaigns, he carries himself with a quiet dignity that commands obedience from subordinates and respect from peers.

His troop, **Jürgen's Ernes**, saw action in southern Keoland during the Greyhawk Wars. He hires out for tasks from caravan escort to militia training, and is renowned across the southwestern Flanaess for mustering just the right specialists — adventurers, even thieves-guild members — for smaller actions, always with an Ernes representative attached to enforce the contract.

**Refuses:** slaving/kidnapping work, and any job requiring the slaughter of non-combatants (the elderly, infirm, and children). He is known to have destroyed at least one band that tried to "re-negotiate" a contract mid-job.

## Use at the table
Patron / quest-giver, contract enforcer, or honorable rival fixer. His refusals are a dependable moral fixed-point.
