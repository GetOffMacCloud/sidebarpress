# Perrenland — GM Quick Reference

## At a glance
- **Ruler:** Voorman **Karenin** (rotating office). Predecessor **Franz** now runs the spy network.
- **Capital:** Schwartzenbruin (26,000). **Pop.** ~200,000. **Dwarves** 3,000+.
- **Alignments:** LN / LG / N. **Tongue:** Old Oeridian (Flan dialect in highlands).
- **Vibe:** law-abiding, isolationist, nationalistic. Mercenary-friendly; suspicious of foreign mages & merchants.
- **Exports:** foodstuffs, copper, mercenaries.

## Running the people (attitudes)
| Toward… | Reaction |
|---|---|
| Foreign **mercenaries** | Grudging respect if lawful — Perrenland is neutral ground for sell-swords |
| Foreign **mages** | Suspicion → hostility |
| Foreign **merchants** | Suspicion (but native merchants are the Flanaess's best & worldly) |
| **Lawbreakers** | No mercy; ignorance of *local* canton law is never an excuse |
| The **lazy** | Socially exiled to the fringes |

Highlands = matriarchal, Flannish, defer to priestesses of Beory. Lowlands = Oeridian craftsmen.

## Who you'll meet on the road
| Encounter | Composition | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Canton constabulary** | 2–4 light-armored quarterstaff specialists | Watching for suspicious outsiders |
| **Border patrol** | 10–40 mounted medium troops, missile + melee | Usually 1 spellcaster, more often priestly than arcane |
| **Army** (8,000+) | plains heavy/light cavalry · lowland medium infantry/crossbow · mountain pikemen | + dwarven infantry, crossbow, **bear-riders**; 40+ merc companies; militia |
| **Dire-need levy** | gnome companies, Wolf-Nomad horse archers, mages | Emergency only |

## Factions & powers
- **The Confederation** — House of Cantons (legislative) · Council of Hetmen (executive) · Assembly of Judges (judicial). Advisors: merchant men, swords (mercs), scholars (priests/mages). Voorman presides; emergency powers in war/famine.
- **Cantons** — near-absolute local authority; customs vary wildly canton to canton.
- **Druidic circles of [[Beory]]** + **ranger-knights** — guard wild lands & druids; tied to Franz's intelligence web.
- **Yatil dwarves** — old allies; metalwork, pike & crossbow tradition, bear-riders.
- **Sepia gnomes** — buffer vs. hill humanoids; friendly (see [[Sepia Uplands — World State]]).
- **[[Wolf Nomads]]** — north shore of Lake Quag; tenuous alliance courted since the Wars.
- **Ket** — fortified rival to the south (Krestible↔Molvar pass).
- **[[Iuz]]** — refused during the Wars; ongoing threat pressing from the north.

## Named NPC — Jürgen of Traft
Medium build, mid-30s, tan, black hair, forehead-to-chin scar (rumored giant). Wears the **diving-eagle** sigil. Commands **Jürgen's Ernes** (medium cavalry; fought in southern Keoland). Fixer who can assemble specialist bands — always with Ernes attached to enforce the contract. **Refuses:** slaving/kidnapping, killing non-combatants. Destroyed one band that tried to re-negotiate mid-job.
> *Use as:* patron/quest-giver, contract enforcer, or rival fixer. His refusals are a reliable moral fixed-point.

## Deities in play
Beory (Oerth Mother, black she-bear — central) · Atroa (spring) · Velnius (north wind, ernes) · Ulaa (mountains) · Berei (agriculture) · Obad-Hai (freedom) · Zilchus (merchants) · Kord (mercenaries).

## Dangers by region
| Region | Threats |
|---|---|
| **Yatil Mts** (W/S, 10–15k ft) | mountain lions, cave bears, ernes, wyverns; scattered giants/humanoids; blue & red dragons (deep, rare) |
| **Mounds of Dawn** (N foothills) | flightless birds, hydrae, hyaenodons; **phasing time-lost ruins** (Griff-empire citadel legend) |
| **Clatspur Range** (E, ~8k ft) | giant spiders, worgs, giant hornets, wyverns, poison snakes; gnoll/bugbear/kobold tribes |
| **Sepia Uplands** (Clatspur foothills) | writ in name only; hill humanoids held off by gnomes — **campaign home turf** |
| **Lake Quag** (N border) | the **Jaws of Quag** whirlpool mid-lake; legend of the mist-shrouded isle of ancient knowers |

## Travel windows (passes close in winter)
- **Molvar pass:** closed Sunsebb / Fireseek / Readying (snow).
- **Velverdyva canyon trail:** closed Ready'reat–Coldeven (avalanche); river navigable but rapids are deadly.

## Plot levers
- Franz's **intelligence network** (shared with rangers/druids) — an information tap or a leash.
- **Time-lost ruins** in the Mounds of Dawn — instant dungeon/mystery seed.
- **Iggwilv/Graz'zt** residue from the Decade of Tears — buried fiend-lore.
- Border tension with **Ket** and pressure from **[[Iuz]]** — geopolitical timer.
