# Part VII: The Iuz Siphon — What the Enemy Has Noticed

## The Mechanism

Iuz does not know what the Wachturm binding network is. He does not know about the Impression, the Ur-Flan builders, or the six-node apparatus. What he *does* know is that the Sepia Uplands region produces a low, persistent spiritual resonance that his agents — particularly those using Elder Elemental Eye symbolism as a conduit — can tap into passively.

The degrading network leaks spiritual energy the same way a cracked pipe leaks water: slowly, diffusely, under pressure. Iuz's network of **tap nodes** — small stones carved with the Elder Elemental Eye, placed at degraded binding sites — captures this leaked resonance and channels it south through the Elder Elemental Eye's spiritual infrastructure into the Temple of Elemental Evil's operational area around Hommlet, Nulb, and the Temple itself.

He is not powering anything dramatic. He is skimming the overflow from a system he did not build and does not understand, and using it to give his people a minor edge. This is characteristic of Iuz: opportunistic, patient, and difficult to trace to a source.

**Physical evidence the Questers will find:** Elder Elemental Eye glyphs carved into the Flan Waymark's damaged anchor stone (Node 2, E3), into the doorframe of the Ancestor Barrow, and faintly into the exposed stone of the collapsed Node 3 margin site. These are not desecration marks — they are functional conduits, placed quietly by someone who knew exactly where the resonance was leaking.

## The Two Clusters

### Cluster 1 — The Sepia Uplands (Nodes 1 & 2)

Nodes 1 and 2 are both anchored near the Ochre Escarpment (Hex E3). Node 1's missing anchor stone means it leaks freely. Node 2's cracked anchor bleeds steadily. Together they produce a concentrated pool of primordial earth-resonance spillage that the tap nodes collect and forward south.

**Boon — Earthbound Tenacity:** Earth Temple troops and all forces of evil operating in the Hommlet/Nulb/Temple region gain advantage on saving throws against being knocked prone, grappled, or forcibly moved. Temple stone structures are subtly more resilient — secret doors are harder to find (Investigation DC +2), fortifications hold better than their age allows. Players will not consciously notice this until it is removed.

### Cluster 2 — The Ancestor Barrow (~1 mile N of Ritterstadt, D4/D5 border)

See: [[Ancestor Barrow — Hex D4 fringe]]

A Flan burial cairn containing a bound ancestor-spirit resonance, within walking distance of Ritterstadt itself. As a Spirit node rather than elemental, it produces *perceptual resonance* — the Flan ancestor spirits' function of guarding and warning the living, now corrupted and inverted by the tap node placed in the barrow's inner chamber.

**Boon — Veiled from Grace:** Divine detection magic (Detect Evil and Good, Divine Sense, Detect Magic focused on alignment or fiendish/undead auras) operates at reduced clarity across the Hommlet, Nulb, and Temple region. Creatures with the Fiend or Undead type have advantage on saving throws against being revealed by such magic. This has been running for years. It is why Rannos and Gremag have operated in Hommlet undetected. It is why Canon Terjon gets spiritual static every time he looks toward the Trading Post. When Cluster 2 is neutralised, the static clears — and Hommlet becomes abruptly more dangerous for Temple agents.

## The Gloat — How Intelligent Evil Uses This

The more intelligent forces of Elemental Evil experience these advantages as a *quality of the land* — a gift from the Elder Elemental Eye, evidence that the God's favour is with them. They do not fully understand the mechanism. They do not need to.

**Romag** (Earth Temple High Priest), to captives in the Earth Temple dungeon:
> *"The Unquiet Earth feeds us. We stand because the ground remembers who we are. Your footwork is wasted here — nothing falls in this hall that does not choose to."*

He says this as plain fact, the way a man describes the weather. He is not boasting. He is explaining.

**Belsornig** (Water Temple), overheard in conversation with a subordinate — not directed at the party, which makes it land harder:
> *"The veiling holds. Terjon has been sitting in that church for three years and seen nothing. Three years."*

Said with quiet satisfaction. He does not say what the veiling is or where it comes from.

**Lareth the Beautiful**, if he has the party at a disadvantage in T1:
> *"There are old places near your little university. Iuz keeps them occupied. Your church has been trying to see this town clearly for years. Did you never wonder why they couldn't?"*

**Hedrack** (Greater Temple), if the party has already neutralised one cluster and word has reached him:
> *"Something changed, three weeks ago. A pressure I had grown accustomed to was gone. Someone in the north has been busy."*

He is not angry. He is recalibrating. That is worse.

## Consequences of Neutralisation

When the Ritterstadt Questers successfully address a cluster — sealing or destroying the tap node, even if the underlying binding damage remains — the leaked resonance stops flowing south. The boon is removed.

| Cluster Neutralised | Effect in T0-T5 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cluster 1** (Sepia Uplands) | Earthbound Tenacity removed — Earth Temple troops go prone normally; Temple fortifications show their age; secret doors return to normal DC. |
| **Cluster 2** (Ancestor Barrow) | Veiled from Grace removed — divine detection restored; Rannos, Gremag, and all Temple agents in Hommlet become detectable by divine sense. |
| **Both neutralised** | Both boons removed; Hedrack becomes aware something significant has happened north of his operational area. The party may start to feel watched from a direction they hadn't considered. |

## The T5 Thread — Long Game Only

The Impression the Wachturm contains is a breach point where Tharizdun's influence is thinnest. The Temple of Elemental Evil was also built to contain Tharizdun — and the four elemental cults were, unknowingly, feeding him all along. When Iuz siphons the Wachturm network's leaked resonance through the Elder Elemental Eye tap nodes into the Temple's spiritual infrastructure, he is inadvertently feeding the imprisoned God through the same plumbing that was supposed to keep him sealed.

**Iuz does not know this.** He thinks he is skimming useful overflow. He is correct about the skimming and wrong about the overflow being harmless.

The Ritterstadt Questers neutralising the clusters does not just help the T0-T5 party. It starves the Elder Elemental God's slow awakening at the source. This is the T5 connection flagged in the prep notes: *"Determine whether any T0/T1 threads connect to the deeper Temple."* They do. They start in Ritterstadt.

**Do not surface this until T3 at the earliest.** Let it be the moment in T4 or T5 when someone looks at what they have been doing across four modules and realises the scale of it.
