# Iggwilv

**The Witch-Queen · conqueror of [[Perrenland — Concatenated Cantons|Perrenland]] during the Decade of Tears (470s CY).**

In the 470s CY, Iggwilv conquered Perrenland with the aid of Kettite troops and fiends summoned from the netherworld. Her rule — nearly a decade of bloodshed remembered as the **Decade of Tears** — ended when she attempted to force the aid of her one-time consort, the Demon Prince **Graz'zt**, and was **stripped of her powers**. Without her demonic hordes, her Kettite soldiers were quickly driven from the land.

Her conquest is one of the traumas that turned the once-open Perrenlanders insular and led them to close their borders in **CY 490**.

## Hooks
- Buried relics, bound fiends, or hidden research left behind from the occupation — someone may still be collecting them.
- Old sites of her rule in the lowlands as haunted dungeon locales.
