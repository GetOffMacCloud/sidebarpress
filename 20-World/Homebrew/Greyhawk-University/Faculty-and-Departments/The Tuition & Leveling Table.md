Students of the Ritterstadt Field Station enroll at **3rd character level — Freshman Year**. The frontier is not a classroom. Students arrive with their foundational training already in place; the Sepia Uplands determine what they are made of.

To advance, a student must pay their "Tuition" in Credits — the cost of specialised instruction, higher-tier library access, and the University's advanced training facilities.

| **Character Level** | **Academic Year** | **Credit Cost (Cumulative)** | **Narrative Milestone** |
| ------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Level 3 ★** | **1st Year — Freshman** | **0 — Entry Point** | **Enrollment. Undeclared — attend lectures anywhere, shop around. May join one [[Extracurricular Organizations\|student organization]] at any time.** |
| Level 4 | 2nd Year — Sophomore | 1,000 | **Declare your Major** — [[Declared Majors\|Boon 1 (Declaration)]] earned immediately; basic lab access; Requisition Office upgrade tier unlocked. |
| Level 5 | 3rd Year — Junior | 3,000 | **Minor declaration available** (optional, one, from another department); **Boon 2 (Field Certification)** attainable with 3 in-domain field reports; eligible for [[Research Grants\|Standard Grants]]; field team leadership access. |
| Level 6 | 4th Year — Senior | 6,000 | **Boon 3 (Advanced Study)** attainable with a completed Research Grant in your Major's domain; eligible for [[Research Grants\|Major Grants]]; access to the Watchtower Archives. |
| Level 7 | Fellowship | 10,000 | Authorised to lead and commission expeditions; full University faculty recognition. |

> [!important] **Tuition alone does not advance you — every year ends in a Capstone.**
> Reaching the credit threshold makes you *eligible*; ascending requires **passing that tier's Capstone Quest** (assigned under a Department Head's seal, never posted to the Job Board). Before attempting, each student files a **Declaration of Intent** naming their Major; passing converts it to the formal Declaration at the new level. Capstones are pass/fail — no partial credit — and a failed attempt means a minimum one-week wait before retrying. Full rules: [[z_DM Guide - Quest & Credits System]] §*The Capstone Standard.*

> [!TIP] **The Math:** Each academic year costs **Year Number × 1,000 Credits**.
> - Freshman → Sophomore: 1,000 Credits (Year 1 × 1,000)
> - Sophomore → Junior: 2,000 more Credits (Year 2 × 1,000) — 3,000 total
> - Junior → Senior: 3,000 more Credits (Year 3 × 1,000) — 6,000 total
> - Senior → Fellowship: 4,000 more Credits (Year 4 × 1,000) — 10,000 total

## 🎒 The University "Exchange" (Credit Store)

Credits are not only for levelling — they are the currency of the University's logistics.

- **Requisition Upgrades:** Spend **500 Credits** to permanently upgrade the "Standard Issue" kit for your character (e.g., your starting manifest now always includes an extra Antitoxin).

- **The Buy-Back Clause:** Any magic item or significant find recovered during a sanctioned quest belongs to the University. A student wishing to retain an item must petition to purchase the Research Rights by paying **Credits equal to the item's GP value**. A written in-character justification is required. University approval is not guaranteed — items with strategic or research significance will be denied.

- **Tutor Sessions:** Spend **200 Credits** to gain a temporary Instruction Bonus (e.g., +2 to your next relevant skill check after a session with a department specialist).

## ⚖️ The No-Loot Economy

Greyhawk University runs on **Credits**, not gold. This prevents the power-scaling collapse that accumulated wealth causes in standard 5e play.

- **Quests pay Credits, not coin.** No monetary reward for standard field assignments.
- **No XP.** Advancement is purchased at the Tuition table — narrative and mechanical progress are the same thing.
- **No looting.** Magic items, treasure, and recovered materials belong to the University or the quest giver. Return everything with a full accounting.
- **The Loadout Package.** The Requisition Office issues a curated consumable kit before each quest (potions, scrolls, alchemical items). Unused items must be returned. Lost items incur a Credit penalty charged to the responsible student.

> [!note] **The Exception: Capstone Quests**
> Capstone Quests — required for graduation between academic years — are the *only* quests that may include a stipend from an external patron. This is a narrative reward, not a shopping budget — and it scales with standing: **Freshman capstones carry no patron access at all**; supplied gear becomes available at Sophomore tier, cash-equivalent stipends at Junior, and full patron engagement only at Senior and above. See [[z_DM Guide - Quest & Credits System]] for the full stipend table.

## 💡 Design Notes

**Field Report Bonus.** A satisfactory in-character field report submitted after each quest earns a 5–10% Credit bonus on the quest's base value.

**Academic Probation.** A quest whose primary objective fails entirely, or that the party abandons, earns zero Credits and triggers Probation: Capstone ineligibility until cleared by completing one standard quest in good standing. (Abandoning a *Capstone* mid-mission also triggers Probation.)

**Department Specialisation Bonuses.** Students earn a 10% Credit bonus on quests squarely within their declared Major's area of study (from Level 4, once the Major is declared). These bonus-earning quests also count toward Boon 2's three field reports.

**The "Incomplete" Grade.** An abandoned quest sits on the academic record as an Incomplete. No Credits are awarded until the quest is completed — by the original party or another. The original party may petition for partial credit if they contributed meaningfully.

**Gear Requisition Tiers.** Loadout Packages improve as academic standing rises. A Freshman party may requisition basic healing and light tools. A Senior party can access rarer scrolls, higher-tier potions, and specialist field equipment.

**The "Lost in the Field" Exception.** Items genuinely lost to circumstances beyond the party's control may be written off without Credit penalty if the Session Log documents the loss clearly and the DM approves.

**Capstone Quests as Graduation Ceremonies.** Every Capstone completion is a public event within the University — a brief ceremony, a formal notation in the Conduct Record, and a new academic title. NPCs should react differently to a Senior than to a Freshman. The world should feel the difference.
