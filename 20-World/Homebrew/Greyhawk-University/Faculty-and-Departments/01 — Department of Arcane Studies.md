# 🔮 Department of Arcane Studies

*Magic is not a mystery. It is a subject. Study it accordingly.*

The largest and best-funded department at the Field Station, and it lets no one forget it. Arcane Studies treats magic as an observable, classifiable phenomenon — its history, its theory, and its material expressions in the field.

**Department Head:** Magistra Odilia Sternwald — severe, brilliant, allergic to speculation. "Show me the effect, then we may discuss the cause."

---

## Major: Arcane Research

*The scholarly study of magic as a phenomenon — its classification, history, theory, and material expressions.*

- **Boon 1 — Declaration:** Access to the University's restricted magical stacks (Tier 3 library). +10% credits on quests involving magical phenomena, enchanted locations, or arcane artifacts.
- **Boon 2 — Field Certification:** Advantage on Arcana checks to identify magical effects, spell schools, active enchantments, and magic items — regardless of whether you can cast spells yourself.
- **Boon 3 — Advanced Study:** Once per short rest, examine a magic item or observed magical effect for 1 minute: you learn its school, approximate power level (cantrip / low / mid / high), and whether it is ongoing or fading. If examining an item, you may also ask the DM one yes/no question about it ("Is it cursed?" "Does it require attunement?").

---

## Minors Offered

*Open to students whose Major belongs to another department.*

### Thaumaturgic Analysis
Advantage on Arcana checks to identify spells as they are cast, active enchantments, and schools of magic in effect around you.

### Divination & Omenry
Once per long rest, spend 10 minutes reading stars, cards, or entrails, then name a specific course of action. The DM tells you whether it bodes **weal**, **woe**, **both**, or **neither** (as *augury*).

---

## Student Organizations

### The Arcane Fellowship
*Open to all Majors.* A collective of practitioners and researchers who pool spell components, maintain a shared component chest, and hold regular sessions on magical theory.

**Member Benefit:** Access to the shared component chest. Once per expedition, source one material component (non-consumed, value under 10 gp) at no cost. Members are expected to contribute comparable components periodically.

### The Candlewick Society
*Membership by sponsorship.* An after-hours reading salon devoted to "the appreciation of lamplight, beeswax, and quiet study." Meets in a windowless room in the old lecture annex. Very good candles, admittedly.

**Entry Criteria:** A current member must sponsor you, and you must donate one book the Society's shelf does not already hold. The Society is particular about its shelf.

**Member Benefit:** The Society's private reading room never closes. Once per semester, you may consult its odd little collection to gain advantage on one Arcana or History check made during downtime research.

%%
> [!warning] **DM Note — The Candlewick Society's real business**
> The reading salon is a screen for a circle experimenting with **restricted magic** copied — illegally — out of the Tier 3 stacks. They are not evil; they are curious, ambitious, and certain the restrictions are institutional cowardice. That certainty is the hook.
> - **True entry criteria:** the sponsor must vouch that the candidate has *already* broken a library rule and told no one — the donated "book" is a test; the shelf wants transcriptions of restricted texts.
> - New inductees swear the **Wick-Oath** over a lit candle enchanted with a minor abjuration: not binding as a *geas*, but the Society believes it is, which keeps most members honest.
> - One of their transcriptions contains an error that will eventually matter. Sternwald would expel every one of them; two are her best students.
