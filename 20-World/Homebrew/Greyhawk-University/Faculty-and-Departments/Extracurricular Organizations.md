# Student Organizations

Greyhawk University's student organizations are as much a part of an education as any Major. Each is *hosted* by one of the [[00 — Department Index|Eight Departments]] — that's who lends the room and signs for the equipment — but **membership is open to students of every Major**. Clubs are where cross-department friendships (and expedition parties) are made.

---

## Joining an Organization

- **You may join one organization.** Time is finite; the University expects full commitment to the club you choose.
- **Membership is open** to any student in good standing, regardless of Major, unless an entry says otherwise.
- **Most clubs have no application process** — show up, participate, demonstrate commitment. Some have a brief **initiation task** noted in their entry.
- Benefits that refresh "once per semester" reset at semester turnover (see the [[00 — Department Index|academic calendar]]).

> [!warning] **For the DM**
> Organizations should feel alive between sessions: resources occasionally tapped out, collective projects, inter-club rivalries surfacing socially. Standing fixtures — the Fencing Club vs. Scouts & Surveyors feud (annual Field Day), the Illuminators knowing everyone's business, the Mercantile Society's budget votes. Four clubs are not what they appear — the Curio Club (relic ring), the Keys & Tumblers Society (thieves' guild chapter), the Candlewick Society (restricted-magic circle), and the Ramblers' Fellowship (keepers of the Undermap); true natures and inner-ring entry criteria live in the DM blocks of their department notes. Web of tension: Keys & Tumblers despise the Curio Club's sloppiness; the Curio Club covets the Undermap; the Candlewick Society just wants to be left alone, which no one else believes.
