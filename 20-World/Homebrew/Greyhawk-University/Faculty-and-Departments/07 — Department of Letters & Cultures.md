# 📜 Department of Letters & Cultures

*Everything that happened matters. The question is whether we understand it yet.*

Languages, peoples, faiths, and the long memory of the Flanaess. Letters & Cultures is the department that talks to the locals, reads the archive, and reminds the diggers and mappers that every ruin was somebody's home.

**Department Head:** Professor Ilsabet Federkiel — speaks eleven languages, gossips fluently in all of them. Her lectures run long; nobody minds.

---

## Major: Anthropology & Cultural Studies

*The study of peoples — their customs, social structures, histories, and ways of life.*

- **Boon 1 — Declaration:** One regional language. Proficiency with one artisan's tool relevant to a specific culture (chosen with advisor). +10% credits on quests involving communities, cultural sites, or peoples not yet documented by the University.
- **Boon 2 — Field Certification:** Advantage on History and Insight checks related to cultural practices, social customs, community dynamics, and the behavior of specific peoples.
- **Boon 3 — Advanced Study:** After spending 1 hour in a new community (village, faction, social group), ask the DM to identify the community's key power structure, a significant internal tension, and one thing the community is deliberately not discussing with outsiders.

## Minors Offered

*Open to students whose Major belongs to another department.*

### Linguistics & Philology
Learn one additional language of any type. Advantage on checks to decipher unfamiliar scripts when you have written reference material to compare against.

### Theology & Religion
Advantage on Religion checks to identify deities, rites, sacred sites, and holy symbols. Advantage on Persuasion checks when interacting with clergy, monks, or devout NPCs.

---

## Student Organizations

### The Polyglot Circle
*Open to all Majors.* A language exchange — students teach each other their tongues and pool reference materials toward communication across the widest possible range of languages.

**Member Benefit:** The shared reference library grants advantage on checks to understand a language you don't speak fluently when written material is available. Once per session, attempt to convey a simple concept to a speaker of an unknown language by describing your approach to the DM.

### The Historical Society
*Open to all Majors.* Keeps a private archive of pre-University primary sources — documents, artifacts, and records that predate the Field Station's institutional memory.

**Member Benefit:** Archive access grants advantage on History checks involving the periods and regions documented there. When your expedition produces a historically significant discovery documented by the Society, you receive 50 bonus credits beyond the standard quest award.

### The Illuminators' Guild 🔨
*Open to all Majors. Crafters' guild.* Scribes, bookbinders, and mapmakers-of-the-decorative-school. They produce the fair copies that make the University's archive legible — and beautiful.

**Member Benefit:** Once per semester, the Guild fair-copies one of your submissions: that Field Report or Publication earns +10% credits for presentation quality.
