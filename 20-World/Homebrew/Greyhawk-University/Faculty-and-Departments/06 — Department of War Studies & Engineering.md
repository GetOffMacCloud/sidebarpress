# ⚔️ Department of War Studies & Engineering

*Skill without practice is theory. Walls without mathematics are rubble.*

Tactics and trusses under one roof. War Studies & Engineering covers the history and conduct of armed conflict, fortification, siegecraft, logistics, and the structural arts that keep a keep standing — or bring one down.

**Department Head:** Professor Dietrich Mauerbrecher — retired siege engineer. Speaks softly, diagrams loudly. His office door is a salvaged portcullis.

---

## Major: Military Theory

*The academic study of warfare, tactics, fortification, logistics, and the history of armed conflict.*

- **Boon 1 — Declaration:** Proficiency with two martial weapons of your choice. +10% credits on quests involving conflict, defense operations, or military sites.
- **Boon 2 — Field Certification:** Advantage on Initiative rolls in encounters that take place in locations the party has previously scouted or studied.
- **Boon 3 — Advanced Study:** Once per short rest, spend 1 minute analyzing a fortification, battlefield, or tactical position. The DM identifies two or three specific vulnerabilities or tactical advantages of the location.

---

## Minors Offered

*Open to students whose Major belongs to another department.*

### Fortification & Engineering
Advantage on checks to assess structural integrity, identify the construction era and method of a structure, and spot structural weaknesses in fortifications.

### Logistics & Quartermastery
Your expedition requisitions include one additional standard-issue item slot. Advantage on checks to organize, estimate, or stretch supplies in the field.

---

## Student Organizations

### The Fencing Club
*Open to all Majors.* The University's sanctioned combat society — sparring under formal rules, technique refinement, and a resident training master. Successor to the old Duelist's Circle, a rebrand the older members still grumble about.

**Member Benefit:** Access to the training master's sessions. Once per long rest, add your proficiency bonus to one weapon attack roll, declared before you roll.

**Initiation:** Complete one supervised sparring session with a Club member before you are formally recognized.

### The Wrightworks 🔨
*Open to all Majors. Crafters' guild.* Smiths, carpenters, tinkers, and anyone who likes fixing things more than breaking them. Maintains the student workshop behind the drill yard.

**Member Benefit:** Workshop access. Once per expedition, with 10 minutes of work, field-repair a broken piece of mundane equipment or jury-rig / disable a simple mechanism (a jammed winch, a stuck portcullis, a crude trap).
