# ⚗️ Department of Physics & Alchemy

*People keep getting hurt. Someone should know what to do about it.*

Healing, brewing, and the honest study of what kills people — the department the rest of the University pretends not to need until an expedition comes back short a limb. Physic & Alchemy covers medicine, pharmacology, alchemical craft, and (in its quieter east wing) the science of death itself.

**Department Head:** Doktor Agathe Sauerbrunn — unshakably calm, smells of camphor, keeps a packed field bag by her office door at all times.

---

## Major: Medicine & Herbalism

*The healing arts — anatomy, disease, treatment, and the medicinal properties of plants.*

- **Boon 1 — Declaration:** Proficiency with the herbalism kit. Proficiency in Medicine (if not already proficient). +10% credits on quests involving illness, injury, poison, or plague.
- **Boon 2 — Field Certification:** Advantage on Medicine checks to stabilize the dying or diagnose illness. When you use a healer's kit to restore hit points, the target recovers 1 additional hit point.
- **Boon 3 — Advanced Study:** Once per long rest, treat a willing creature for 10 minutes. Remove one of the following conditions if it was caused by disease or poison: Poisoned, Blinded (if toxin-caused), or Incapacitated (if illness-caused).

## Minors Offered

*Open to students whose Major belongs to another department.*

### Practical Alchemy
Proficiency with alchemist's supplies. Once per expedition, with 10 minutes of work and access to your supplies, produce one basic antitoxin, smoke pellet, or acid vial at no cost.

### Necrology
Access to the University's Mortuary Archives. Advantage on checks to identify undead creatures, determine their weaknesses, and identify the method or magic used to create them.

---

## Student Organizations

### The Medics' Circle
*Open to all Majors.* The Field Station's emergency medicine society — practical training in wound management and stabilization under pressure, plus a shared medical supply.

**Member Benefit:** Use a healer's kit from the collective supply once per expedition at no personal cost. Advantage on Medicine checks to stabilize a dying creature or treat injuries between encounters.

### The Alchemist's Bench 🔨
*Open to all Majors. Crafters' guild.* Potion brewing, ingredient sourcing, and the identification of unusual substances, around a shared stock of common reagents.

**Member Benefit:** Once per semester, produce one common alchemical item (antitoxin, basic healing potion, oil flask, acid vial) from shared ingredients at half the normal credit cost. Members contribute ingredients recovered from expeditions.

### The Quiet Vigil
*Open to all Majors.* Students who keep the old custom of sitting overnight watch in the ossuary chapel — part memorial society, part nerve-training, part morbid social club. Other students find them unsettling. The Vigil finds this funny.

**Member Benefit:** Steady nerves. Advantage on saving throws against being frightened by undead.

**Initiation:** Sit one full night's vigil in the ossuary chapel, alone, without a light source. (Nothing happens. Probably.)
