# The Eight Departments

Greyhawk University's Ritterstadt Field Station operates eight academic departments. Each department offers one **Major** (a full course of study with three progressive boons), two **Minors** (narrower fields open to students of *other* departments), and hosts at least one student organization.

## How It Works

- **Freshman (Level 3):** Undeclared. Attend lectures anywhere; shop around.
- **Sophomore (Level 4):** **Declare your Major** — this makes you a member of that department. Boon 1 earned immediately.
- **Junior (Level 5):** Optionally **declare one Minor** — chosen from a department *other than your own*. A Minor grants its single listed boon.
- Full rules, boon progression, and the 10% credit bonus: see [[Declared Majors|Majors & Minors — Rules]].
- Student organizations are hosted by departments but **open to students of every Major**. One club per student. See [[Extracurricular Organizations|Student Organizations — Rules]].

> [!info] **The Academic Calendar**
> Two **semesters** make one academic year. For play purposes, a semester turns over after **four sanctioned quests**. Benefits that refresh "once per semester" reset at turnover.
