# The Curse of Binding

> *Homebrew.* A curse spoken by a mad sorcerer of the Suel Imperium in its last days, binding a host of cold spirits that became the immoths. Its words scattered on the wind; the immoths are driven to gather them back to the Mountain of Ultimate Winter.

## Account
- The sorcerer's apprentice fled with refugees through the Passage of Slerotin and hid one verse in Candlecrown's hymn to Telchur, in old Suloise, to be sung and never written.
- Other refugees hid other verses across the Sheldomar Valley (house: a Keoish lullaby, a Gran March marching song, a Lortmils dwarven work-chant are candidates).
- The apprentice also carved a **counter-verse** that unbinds an immoth from the curse (Candlecrown's mine chapel).
- "What is written, they can take without a voice."

## Sidebar Press Stance
- Source of the open thread **The Hidden Verses** ([[Plot-Threads]]). The immoth itself is the 3e *Monster Manual II* creature, converted; its curse-driven word-gathering follows that book's legend, and the specifics here are ours.
