# The Hundred (Hundertschaft)

> Perrenland's oldest chartered free company, which claims descent from the companies that followed Vartum Perren. For ninety-six years it has carried the shame of a breach it never committed. *(Homebrew.)*

## Canon Account
Homebrew. It builds on canon Perrenland: the mercenary companies as a chief export, neutral ground for rival companies in winter, and the sanctity of contract. See [[Perrenland]].

## Sidebar Press Account
- **Origin:** the company claims its charter descends from the free companies that fought beside Vartum Perren in the Uprising. That claim is company lore, unverified.
- **Device:** a red cord tied in the Hundred's knot.
- **The breach of 480 CY:** contracted to hold the von Eisfeld manor against the Witch's legions, the company marched out on the last night and held the **Kalte Klamm** gorge road while about three hundred people escaped. Ritter Wolfram von Eisfeld had released them in writing and sworn their captain, **Lorenz Kammerer**, to silence. Gerhard von Eisfeld filed a complaint of breach around 505 CY, and it was never answered. The truth is told in [[SP-PBS-03-The-Red-Cord]].
- **Leadership (576 CY):** [[Hauptfrau Ilse Brandauer]]. Its oldest serving man is [[Feldwebel Jost Kammerer]].

## The Halved Standard (custom)
A lord who releases a company from contract before its term **cuts his standard in half** and gives the company one half. The company keeps it as proof of honourable release, to be shown to the Council of Hetmen if the company is ever charged with breach. Two halves that meet along a clean cut are, in Perrender custom, a release that needs no other witness. *(Homebrew; it is the key to SP-PBS-03's climax.)*

## The Red Cord (custom)
In Schwartzenbruin's company taverns, every blade is tied into its sheath at the door. Cutting the cord and drawing steel makes an enemy of every company in the room. *"Wood is Kord's. Steel is murder."* *(Homebrew; it formalises the canon custom of neutral ground for companies in winter.)*
