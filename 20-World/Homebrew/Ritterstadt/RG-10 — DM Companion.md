# RG-10 — DM Companion

## The Secrets, by Layer

Reveal from the top down.

**Layer 1 — The town's business.**
- The Vogt slowed Moot enrollment so a full Moot could not outvote the Ritter on the timber rights.
- Hanz's grain discrepancy is the Larch kin-band trading bow-staves to Hamund Malzer for grain off the book. Jakob Renner, the Reeve, knows.
- Baba Yeska's "Quag salt" is half Traft salt sold back to Traft.
- Three logged Bloom crystals are missing from the Assay House.
- Wilhelm Talg sells surplus lamp-oil to someone who pays in Traft coin.

**Layer 2 — The town's politics.**
- The chest under the Ritter's dais holds letters between Edmund and the Kettenhaus that could weaken Konrad's title. The Vogt knows; Konrad doesn't.
- The Kettenhaus drafted Traft's "dependency form." Foreclosure under canton law is cleaner.
- Ewald in the garrison reports to Iuz's handler at Kottsmort. Stahlwulf feeds him a flawed roster.
- The Pale Ledger's copyists read everything the Illuminators fair-copy.
- Dominie Roth's letters to the Voormann's council are read in the Kettenhaus's post.
- If the Regents withdrew, McCockin money would keep the station open — and both sides know it.

**Layer 3 — The University's bargain.**
- Calloway's charter promised the Regents the Fiend's Embrace "when safely movable." The Regents' Commission holds a second seal and a drafted commission to recover it.
- Chancellor Adalwin Adenauer is waiting on Varek Doss's assessment before giving the word.
- Vogelsang reports to Commissioner Dray and expects the Wardenship.
- The Registrar's sealed files hold AR-001, Hassenrath's second notebook, and the charter correspondence.
- Lecturer Tock has read one line of the clay tablet: *"the one who knew first is not asleep; it is counting."*

**Layer 4 — The ground.**
- **The Silent Years (525–536).** Old Ritter Gerhard von Eisfeld dug beside the Wachturm's foundation for eleven years, believing the Flan songs. In 536 something in the shaft made him stop; the villagers who were left, Hanz among them, backfilled it. It is Hanz's "Kammer." Hassenrath came that winter. Gerhard's journal of the dig is in the locked study; Edmund read it in 565 and said yes to the Founders because of it.
- The trapdoor in the Wachturm's floor, iron-barred by Stahlwulf, sits over the edge of Gerhard's backfill.
- Dagmar of Clan Brock stopped building the fifth bastion on the Wachturm rise in 566 because of what her footings found. Her tools are still on the tower's locked ground floor.
- Node 2 lies under the timber rights. Node 3 lies under Baba Yeska's salt pan. Karet's Hush ledger maps the six stress points.
- The Grey Sickle has a list of the Sepia's barrows copied from the Historical Society.
- Baba Yeska's daughter went north with Iuz's recruiter and writes to her mother. Yeska burns the letters.

## Hooks by Faction (one adventure seed each)

- **Regents' Commission:** Dray's audit year. The party is commissioned under the second seal to "inventory the Founders' Compact items." Do they do the job, or warn the Warden?
- **Pale Ledger:** Yusuf's cloth bolt is stolen on the Traft road by bandits who find a calfskin ledger they can't read.
- **Grey Sickle:** ash circles appear on Peat-cutters' Row the week the Larch barrows thaw.
- **Quag Bargemen:** Quagfludt's harbour-reeve seizes a barge; the party finds the northbound crate aboard.
- **Old Kerk Mission:** Roth wants the foundation stone read; the Wolf Nomad outriders come down off the moor.
- **Iuz:** Ewald asks the party to carry a letter to Kottsmort "for his sister."
- **Kettenhaus:** Traft's dependency form arrives with a clerk and a Needfest deadline.
- **Druid enclave:** Giseth asks for the three missing Bloom crystals, which have started to hum.
- **Wolf Nomads:** Elif Kayim arrives with amber, furs, and the Winter Vanguard rumours.
- **Clan Brock:** the Aerie will acknowledge Dagmar if the town returns her tools.
- **Varek Doss:** arrives early with a Baklunish text and a plan to open the floor.
