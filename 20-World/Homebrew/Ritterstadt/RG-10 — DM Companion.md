# RG-10 — DM Companion

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · ← [[RG-09 — Faculty & Staff Roster]]

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> This chapter is never published. It collects every secret in the book by layer, lists where this book overrode or reconciled campaign canon, and indexes the hooks by building and faction so a DM can pull a session off the shelf.

## The Secrets, by Layer

Reveal from the top down. A party that reaches Layer 4 has become the campaign.

**Layer 1 — The town's business (any session).**
- The Vogt slowed Moot enrollment so a full Moot could not outvote the Ritter on the timber rights.
- Hanz's grain discrepancy is the Larch kin-band trading bow-staves for grain off the book.
- Baba Yeska's "Quag salt" is half Traft salt sold back to Traft.
- The brewer sells the Fiend's Rest the better cask because Karet pays in University chits he can redeem for paper.
- Three logged Bloom crystals are missing from the Assay House.

**Layer 2 — The town's politics (after a few sessions).**
- The chest under the Ritter's dais holds letters that could void Konrad's land-grant. The Vogt knows; Konrad doesn't.
- The Hanselmann Consortium drafted Traft's "dependency form." Foreclosure under canton law is cleaner.
- Ewald in the garrison reports to Iuz's handler at Kottsmort. Stahlwulf has begun feeding him a flawed roster.
- The Pale Ledger's copyists read everything the Illuminators fair-copy, including the Historical Society's archive.
- Dominie Roth's letters to the Voormann's council are read by someone in the Consortium's post.

**Layer 3 — The University's bargain (mid-campaign).**
- Calloway's charter promised the Regents the Fiend's Embrace "when safely movable." The Regents' Commission holds a second seal and a drafted commission to recover it.
- The Chancellor is waiting on Varek Doss's assessment before giving the word.
- Vogelsang reports to Commissioner Dray and expects the Wardenship.
- The Registrar's sealed files hold AR-001, Hassenrath's second notebook, and the charter correspondence.
- Lecturer Tock has read one line of the clay tablet: *"the one who knew first is not asleep; it is counting."*

**Layer 4 — The ground (the campaign's spine; see the campaign vault's Wachturm and Sepia Uplands references).**
- Node 2 lies under the timber rights. Node 3 lies under Baba Yeska's salt pan. The Hush ledger in Karet's apron maps the six stress points.
- Iggwilv's agents opened a shaft beside the Wachturm's foundation during the Silent Years; the villagers backfilled it in 500 CY; it is Hanz Korngut's "Kammer."
- Dagmar of Clan Brock stopped building the fifth bastion on the Wachturm rise in 566 because she found what the tower stands on, and told Edmund von Eisfeld. Her tools are still on the locked ground floor.
- The Grey Sickle has a list of the Sepia's barrows copied from the Historical Society. A degrading seal is a door.
- Baba Yeska's daughter went north with Iuz's recruiter and writes to her mother. Yeska burns the letters.

## Hooks by Faction (one adventure seed each)

- **Regents' Commission:** Dray's audit year. The party is commissioned under the second seal to "inventory the Founders' Compact items" — the first step to moving the Embrace. Do they do the job, or warn the Warden?
- **Pale Ledger:** Yusuf's cloth bolt is stolen on the Traft road by common bandits who find a calfskin ledger they can't read. Three Suel exiles want it back before anyone else reads it.
- **Grey Sickle:** ash circles appear on Peat-cutters' Row the week the Larch barrows thaw. SP-PBS-02's method, a new cell-member, a town that has read the Dreadverge report.
- **Quag Bargemen:** Quagfludt's harbour-reeve seizes a barge; the party is hired to get it back and finds the northbound crate aboard.
- **Old Kerk Mission:** Roth wants the foundation stone read; the Wolf Nomad outriders see the party at it and come down off the moor for the first time in eleven years.
- **Iuz:** Ewald asks the party to carry a letter to Kottsmort "for his sister." It is his handler's report. He is testing whether he can still make himself do it.
- **Hanselmann Consortium:** Traft's dependency form arrives with a clerk and a deadline of Needfest.
- **Druid enclave:** Giseth sends word that the Bloom crystals have started to hum, and asks for the three that are missing.
- **Wolf Nomads:** Elif Kayim arrives with amber, furs, and the Winter Vanguard rumours (campaign canon).
- **Clan Brock:** the Aerie will acknowledge Dagmar if the town returns her tools.
- **Varek Doss:** arrives early, before Graun's report, with a Baklunish text and a plan to open the floor.

## Running Ritterstadt in One Line
**The town counts.** Every session, someone asks what the party brought back, and someone writes it down. Credits, Favor, grain, maps, the Moot Roll, the Hush ledger — the setting's engine is that Ritterstadt keeps score, and the party decides what's worth scoring.
