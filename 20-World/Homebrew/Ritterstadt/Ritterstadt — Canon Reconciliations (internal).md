# Ritterstadt — Canon Reconciliations (internal)

Not part of the book. Material moved out of the gazetteer on 2026-11-13 when the book was reframed for buyers who have no other Sidebar Press or campaign material.

**Vault roles (from 2026-11-13).** The Sidebar Press vault holds the book and builds the modules. *A Tourist In Greyhawk* holds Greybeard's table's campaign notes. The two are no longer kept identical; the Tourist copy of the gazetteer was last synced on 2026-11-13 with its four table-only lines (Bethany McCockin-Simmons ×2, the McCockin Orchard note pointer, "Questers," "Greybeard's ruling").

## From RG-01 DM note

> - **Hassenrath's date.** The campaign vault gives both "approximately 490 CY" and "forty years ago." This book uses **c. 536 CY**, which fits the window, the Silent Years, Hanz's youth, and Sigmund's tenure.

## From RG-01 DM note

> - **Node 4.** Levelled in **571 CY** by a Furyondian road-cutting crew clearing a mound for the Velverdyva trade road. Same loss as the campaign history's Greyhawk Wars account, a decade earlier and possible in 576.

## From RG-10 reconciliations

## Canon Reconciliations

| Campaign canon says | This book reads it as | Why |
|---|---|---|

| Founded 565 by the Four Founders **and** the Ritter's grandfather built the chapel, "the old Ritter held this village," old banners in the Hall | **Erikasrast** from 489; the von Eisfelds from c. 510; **re-founded** in 565 | All facts survive (Greybeard's ruling) |
| Chapel ledgers go back 87 years | Erikasrast's wooden chapel, 489; rebuilt in stone c. 520 by Gerhard | — |
| A gap in the ledgers "about forty years back" **and** "eleven years" | The Silent Years, **525–536** | Fits Hanz's youth and Sigmund's evasion |
| Hassenrath c. 490 **and** "forty years ago" | **c. 536 CY** | Fits the window and the Silent Years |
| Greyhawk Wars destroyed Node 4 (impossible in 576) | A Furyondian road-cutting crew, 571 CY | Same loss, plausible date |
| Lake Quag fish "traded up the road" | Ritterstadt is a **lake port**; the quay lies below the palisade | Sidebar Press ruling |
| Hogar Irongrip is "master smith" **and** Brunhilde runs the Smithy | Hogar runs the **University Forge**; Brunhilde runs the **village Smithy** | Two forges, two trades |

| "Department of Frontier Studies" published the Gazetteer; eight departments listed | Frontier Studies is the station's **imprint** | — |
| The canton of Quagfludt (canon) | **Quagflood** | Sidebar Press ruling (2026-11-13): English form in all Sidebar Press text |
| Voormann rules from "Schwungdorf" | **Schwartzenbruin** | Canon capital |
| Wolf Nomad trader Temür Bayan | **Elif Kayim** | Name clash with Khan Temur (SP-GA01) |
| The Kettenhaus listed among buildings | A Traft lending house; its man in town is Vethkar Graun | — |
