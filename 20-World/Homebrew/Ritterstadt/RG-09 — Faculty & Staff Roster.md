# RG-09 — Faculty & Staff Roster

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · ← [[RG-08 — Greyhawk University, Ritterstadt Field Station]] · Next → [[RG-10 — DM Companion]]

Twenty-two people draw the University's pay at Ritterstadt, plus the Master of the Forge, who lives on Forge Row and is counted there. The eight department heads are campaign canon (`00 — Department Index`), where they are placeholders; this book gives each a line of voice and a second lecturer. Everyone else is new.

**Canon rule:** a head promoted to a full NPC note in the campaign vault overrides anything below.

## Senior Students Who Teach (3)
Counted among the 36 students; they lodge in town.
- **Oswin Kettler** (human, Senior, Cartography) — runs the Scouts & Surveyors' map archive.
- **Rook Ashveil** (half-elf, Senior, Anthropology) — leads Polyglot Circle sessions; the Grugach student the Vesve has not forgotten.
- **Dagmar Brockdottir** (dwarf, Senior, Military Theory) — Garrok's second on the Drill Yard; named for a Founder she has never been told was kin.

## Visiting
- [[Professor Wilhelmina Brandt]] (canon) — Antiquarian Wardcraft, City of Greyhawk. Outranks no one here and finds it intolerable.
- **Commissioner Lucan Dray** — the Regents' envoy, every second Patchwall. See [[RG-06 — Factions]].

## Hooks
- Lecturer Falk knows whose arms are on the Hall's old shield. He is waiting to be asked by the right person.
- Lecturer Tock has translated a line of the clay tablet and told no one, because it frightened him.
- Dagmar Brockdottir's name is on a Founder's tools in the Wachturm's locked floor.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - **Vogelsang** is the Regents' Commission's man on the faculty; he reports to Dray directly and expects the Wardenship when Weissmantel resigns. He is not corrupt. He believes the Embrace should go south and that the station's job is to find things worth sending.
> - **Lecturer Vesk** is a true Grave-cleric and the Grey Sickle's natural enemy, which is why the mendicant of Wee Jas who passed through in Harvester asked the Vater, not the Infirmary, about the ungraved dead.
> - **Lecturer Tock's line** reads, roughly: *"the one who knew first is not asleep; it is counting."*
> - **Ilvadriel** knows what the Larch kin-band fled in the Vesve and has promised not to say. It was not Grugach.
