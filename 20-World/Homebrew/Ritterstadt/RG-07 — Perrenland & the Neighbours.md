# RG-07 — Perrenland & the Neighbours

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · ← [[RG-06 — Factions]] · Next → [[RG-08 — Greyhawk University, Ritterstadt Field Station]]

Ritterstadt is **unincorporated**. It stands in the Sepia Uplands north of the Concatenated Cantons, which govern Perrenland as a loose confederation under an elected **Voormann** at Schwartzenbruin. The Voormann's tax-collectors stop at Traft. The University's charter comes from the City of Greyhawk, which is farther still. In practice, Ritterstadt's foreign policy is conducted by whoever is standing at the Job Board.

## The Concatenated Cantons

Perrenland has eight cantons (*Living Greyhawk Gazetteer*). The four that touch Ritterstadt's world are below; the rest know the station as a line in a Traft merchant's letter.

| Canton | Seat | Stance on Ritterstadt | What they want from the University | What they'd pay for | Standing contact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Traft** | Traft | The gateway: wants the trade, fears the liability. Its 10-man garrison is the nearest Perrender steel. | Maps of the lake road; a physician in Traft one month a year; students who don't die on canton roads | 10 gp a map copy; 50 gp a season for the physician | The Hanselmann Consortium (unofficially); the Traft levy-clerk |
| **Clatspurgen** | Clatspur highlands | Distant cousin. The Dreadverge haunting ([[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]]) made its council aware that old graves wake. | Someone who can tell a barrow from a treasure pit; a copy of the Antiquities department's grave-site survey | A Waycombe debt of honour; cantonal thanks in Schwartzenbruin | Reeve Odila Marsh of Waycombe |
| **Quagfludt** | the Quag shore | Watches the lake. Resents the Rhennee running salt untaxed through "their" water. | A toll agreement for the Landing; charts of the lake's north shore | Recognition of Ritterstadt's fishing rights; a fair on the lake road | A Quagfludt harbour-reeve who visits the Landing twice a year and is not welcome |
| **Yattenheid** | the eastern cantons toward the Vesve | Nervous. Iuz's couriers cross Yattenheid's border on the way to the Sepia. | Warning. Anything the University learns about traffic from the east. | Information in kind; a company of pikes if it ever came to that | The Yatil Mountaineers' free company, which recruits in Ritterstadt |
| **The Voormann's Council** | Schwartzenbruin | Nominal claim; wants the frontier "stable" and will express concern afterward | Nothing it has to pay for | — | Dominie Anselm Roth's letters (see [[RG-06 — Factions]]) |

**The neutrality doctrine.** Perrenland trades with everyone and allies with no one. The University's presence is a private venture: not endorsed, not obstructed. If Ritterstadt falls, the Cantons will mourn it as a tragedy that befell someone else.

**Never ask a Clatspur dwarf about Bitter Pass politics.** The University has a standing rule and a standing fine.

## The Vesve Border and the Larch Kin-Band
The sylvan elves at the Larch camp came down from the Vesve border in 575 after trouble they will not name. Their kin in the forest know they are here. The Vesve's wild elves (Grugach) do not come to Ritterstadt; a Grugach student has left the family, and the family has not forgotten. The Larch kin-band's **Caelith** will carry a letter to the Vesve for a price in grain, and will not promise an answer.

## The Wolf Nomads
The eastern Sepia is their ancestral ground; their routes run south in autumn and north in late spring along the network nodes they no longer have words for. They have never traded at Ritterstadt. **Elif Kayim**'s arrival would be the first time, and the kin-band's rumours would come with the furs. See the campaign Faction Dossiers for how to bring them in — through the standing stones at G5, respectfully, overnight.

## The Chakyik (Tiger Nomads)
Beyond the Wolves, west and north around the Yatils. In the winter of 576 a University field party carried a parley to Ilkhan Cligir at Yecha ([[SP-PBS-01-The-False-Arrow]]). Whatever came of it, the station now has a name among the Chakyik, and the Wolf Nomads know it.

## The City of Greyhawk
Six weeks away by road and barge, and the source of the charter, the grants, the paper, and the Chancellor's expectations. The Regents' Commission's envoy comes every second year. Letters take a month each way. Decisions made in Ritterstadt are therefore made in Ritterstadt, and explained later.

## Hooks
- Traft's levy-clerk arrives with a form that would make Ritterstadt a canton dependency "for its own protection." The Vogt wants it lost in the lake.
- Quagfludt's harbour-reeve seizes a Rhennee barge for unpaid toll. Baba Yeska wants it back tonight.
- Clan Brock sends word that it will acknowledge Dagmar — if the town returns her tools, which nobody has seen since 566.
- Yattenheid's free company offers to garrison the eastern gate for a season. Stahlwulf wants to know who pays them.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - **Traft's dependency form** is the Hanselmann Consortium's idea. Foreclosure under canton law is cleaner than under the Ritter's.
> - **Quagfludt's harbour-reeve** is honest and badly informed. He thinks the Landing's salt is lake salt. If he learns where the real pan is, Node 3 gets a canton survey party.
> - **Dagmar's tools** are in the Wachturm's locked ground floor, where she left them in 566 when she stopped building the fifth bastion and told Edmund von Eisfeld why.
> - **The Yatil Mountaineers'** captain is honest. One of his sergeants is the Grey Sickle's first man in town.
