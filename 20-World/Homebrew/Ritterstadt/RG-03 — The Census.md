# RG-03 — The Census

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · ← [[RG-02 — Government & Law]] · Next → [[RG-04 — Buildings & Services]]

The Vogt's midsummer count of 576 CY: **200 souls** inside the palisade, at the landing, and on the farmsteads within sight of the Wachturm. The Vogt counts hands, not bloodlines; the peoples below are the University's tally, kept for the Regents.

## By People

| People | Souls | Share | Where they live |
|---|---|---|---|
| Humans | 120 | 60% | Everywhere. Oeridian and Flan Perrenders in the town and farmsteads; Rhennee at the landing; Suel "exiles" on the lane behind the Grey Wolf; a Baklunish trading family by the market; most of the University. |
| Dwarves | 30 | 15% | Forge Row under the eastern wall (24): Clan Irongrip and Clan Brock tradesfolk, the Mining Consortium, the Assay House. Six at the University. |
| Elves | 20 | 10% | The **Larch kin-band** (sylvan elves, 14) at the forest edge beyond the east gate; **six high elves** at the University — the Archivist, two lecturers, three students. |
| Gnomes | 20 | 10% | The Burrows under Market Hill (13): prospectors and instrument-makers. Seven at the University, the Bursar among them. |
| Others | 10 | 5% | Four halflings at the landing; two Rhennee-born tieflings nobody discusses; two half-elves (a lecturer and a student), a half-orc student who keeps the kit store, and a goliath drill-sergeant at the University. |

## Hooks
- Hanz's grain log says the town is eating for more than 200. Who is the extra mouth, and where is the grain going?
- The Peat-cutters' Row grandmothers remember songs the Larch elves recognize.
- Yusuf al-Rashad wants a letter carried to Ket and will pay in salt, which the landing would kill for.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - **The two tieflings** are Baba Yeska's grandchildren. The barge-families say their mother was taken by the Mires in 575. She was not; she went north with Iuz's recruiter, and Yeska's price for the landing's loyalty is that nobody asks.
> - **The "retired factor"** behind the Grey Wolf is Hesta Varnholm's handler. See the Pale Ledger in [[RG-06 — Factions]].
> - **The grain discrepancy** (campaign canon, the Mill GM note) resolves to the Larch kin-band: they trade bow-staves to the brewer for grain off the book, because the Moot won't enroll a household that lives outside the wall. The Reeve of Fields knows and lets it ride.
