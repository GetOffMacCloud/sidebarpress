# RG-02 — Government & Law

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · ← [[RG-01 — History of Ritterstadt]] · Next → [[RG-03 — The Census]]

Ritterstadt is **unincorporated frontier**. No canton claims it; the Voormann's tax-collectors stop at Traft. The town governs itself under three instruments, and most trouble in Ritterstadt is a dispute about which of the three applies.

## The Three Instruments

| Instrument | Holder | Governs |
|---|---|---|
| **The Land-Grant** (c. 510, the von Eisfelds) | the Ritter | Land, the palisades, the watch, high justice, the grain store, timber and fishing rights |
| **The Field Station Charter** (568, amended 574) | Greyhawk University, through the Station Warden | Students, faculty, University buildings and land, the Job Board, credits, research property, discipline of the enrolled |
| **The Moot Roll** (574) | the Moot of household heads, through its Speaker | Market days, the common fields and peat-cuttings, the levy of labour for walls and roads, petitions to the Ritter and the Warden |

**The Land-Grant** came north with Ritter Gerhard von Eisfeld around 510 and has passed father to son since: Gerhard, Edmund, Konrad. The 565 re-founding did not change it; Edmund simply let the Founders build inside it.

**The Moot Roll.** The 574 amendment created a Moot of up to **50 seats**, one per enrolled household. Enrolling costs a household a year's labour-levy and a sworn oath on the chapel stone. As of Patchwall 576, **12 households of 50 seats are enrolled** — the number on the Campaign Timepiece. The other households are governed but unrepresented, which is the Moot's standing grievance.

## Law and Justice
- **High justice** (death, maiming, exile) belongs to the Ritter alone, by the Land-Grant. Konrad has passed two sentences of exile and none of death.
- **Low justice** (debt, theft, assault, market fraud) is heard by the Vogt on any day and by the Ritter on court day. Fines are paid in coin, labour, or grain; the Moot Speaker may plead for an enrolled household.
- **University discipline** covers the enrolled. The Proctor of Students hears it; the Warden confirms it. Expulsion returns a student to the Ritter's law as a private person.
- **The watch's writ** runs inside the palisade and along the quay. Beyond the wall, there is no law but what the party brings.
- **Oaths** are sworn on the chapel's foundation stone, witnessed by the Vater.

## The Garrison
- **Strength:** Hauptmann Stahlwulf and seven soldiers (eight bunks; Pieter's things are still on the eighth). Village-hardened men and women who can fight, track, and survive an uplands winter.
- **Watch:** two on duty at all times — one in the Wachturm, one walking the palisade circuit. The roster is nailed to the Guardhouse door, rotated weekly.
- **Escort for hire:** 1 gp a day per soldier, two at most, on the Ritter's approval.
- **The levy:** every enrolled household owes a man or woman to the wall when the Wachturm bell rings twice. The University's students owe nothing by charter and turn out anyway.
- **The breach:** since 18 Planting, the eastern gate is timber-and-rope, and the gap is watched by students on a rota the Proctor posts.
- **Shelter:** when the bell rings, villagers go to the Ritter's palisade or, if they can't reach it, the Big Barn.

## Taxes and Dues
| Due | Who pays | To whom | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hearth-penny | every household | the Vogt | 1 sp per hearth per quarter |
| Mill tithe | anyone grinding | the Ritter | taken at the Mill with the 5 cp grinding fee |
| Fishing tithe | lake boats | the Ritter | one fish in twelve, taken in kind at the quay |
| Peat and timber cuttings | households | the Reeve of Fields | labour-levy, two days a season |
| Market stall | traders | the Moot | 2 cp a day, 1 sp on fair days |
| Quay toll | barges | the Warden of the Landing | 1 sp a hull, 1 gp a cargo |
| University tuition | students | the Registrar | paid in credits earned, never coin |

## Hooks
- The Moot wants its other 38 seats filled before Needfest; the Vogt wants the labour-levy first.
- Court day: a Rhennee bargeman accused of smuggling, a student accused of pocketing Bloom crystals, and a Clan Irongrip emissary who will not say why he has come.
- The Ritter has been locked in the Hall with the Vogt three nights running since a southerner came through.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - **The chest under the dais** holds correspondence between Ritter Edmund and the Kettenhaus that could weaken Konrad's title. Konrad doesn't know. The Vogt does, and has decided the town is better served by a Ritter in debt than by no Ritter at all.
> - **The Moot Roll is the Vogt's instrument.** He had Professor Goldwaage draft the 574 amendment so the labour-levy would rebuild the wall without the Ritter spending coin he didn't have, and he has slowed enrollment on purpose: a full Moot could outvote the Ritter's household on the timber rights.
> - **Ewald** in the garrison reports to Iuz's handler at Kottsmort. Stahlwulf has begun leaving the gate roster where he can read it, with one deliberate error, to see where the error turns up.
> - **The Station Charter's lapsed clause.** The Regents' underwriting ended in 573; the station has run since on McCockin gifts, grants, credits, and Konrad's loan. If the Regents withdraw recognition, the Warden's authority goes with it — but the McCockins would keep the doors open, and Grant McCockin knows it.
