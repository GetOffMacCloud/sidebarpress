# RG-04a — Building Directory, Part 2: The Field Station and the Growing Town

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · ← [[RG-04a — Building Directory, Part 1]] · Next → [[RG-05 — Economy & Trade]]

The buildings the University and the town's newer people added after the re-founding of 565. Each also has its own note in `40-Locations`.

---

## The Field Station

### 12. The Station House *(University administration)*
**Outside.** *A two-storey timber hall beside the Ritter's palisade with the University's arms painted over the door: an open book, a cudgel and a quill crossed beneath it. Ink and sealing-wax on the air.*

**Inside.**
- **The hall:** benches, a notice-rail, the Scholar's Oath in a frame, and the Chancellor's portrait at the far end, facing the door.
- **The Registrar's counting-room:** the credit ledgers; the locked cabinet of sealed files.
- **The Bursar's room:** an iron-bound strong-box with two keys.
- **The Warden's office (upstairs):** the brass commission seal.
- **The yard:** the Quartermaster's kit store.

**Proprietor.** [[Rektor Albrecht Weissmantel]], Station Warden. With him: [[Registrar Hedwig Ammerlin]], Bursar [[Tobrin Fennelwick]], and Quartermaster [[Lecturer Dagny Holmgard]], whose store is kept by the student [[Ulf Hadric]].

**Hours.** Second bell to sunset; the Warden's light burns later.

**What it offers.** Credits paid out; majors declared; grants assessed; expedition kit issued (bog poles, bedrolls, lamp-oil); commissions too sensitive for the Job Board.

**Hooks.** The Bursar is 40 gp short at the quarter. A Probationer swears the portrait moved.

### 13. The Archive *(University library)*
**Outside.** *A squat stone building with narrow windows of good glass and a slate roof — the only slate in town besides the McCockin house.*

**Inside.** The reading room (six desks); the map presses of the Scouts & Surveyors; the Publications shelf (every Field Report since 569); the Illuminators' Guild's copy-table; and at the back, an iron grille padlocked across the **sealed stacks**, key held by the Registrar.

**Proprietor.** [[Saelithan Verenwyr]], Archivist, high elf, 212. *"Paper burns. That is why I have a slate roof and no patience."* Senior student Oswin Kettler keeps the map archive.

**Hours.** Dawn to dusk; no lamps after dark, ever.

**What it offers.** **Research training:** a day's study grants advantage on one History, Arcana, or Religion check about a named subject, if the Archive holds a source. Fair copies (1 gp a page). Map copies (for credits; 10 gp to outsiders). Nothing leaves without the Scouts' countersign.

**Hooks.** A map is missing and its countersign is forged.

### 14. The Long Dormitory *(University lodging)*
**Outside.** *A long, low timber hall with a peat-fire chimney at each end. Boots by the door, and somebody always awake.*

**Inside.** Forty bunks in two bays; maps pinned over half of them; a common hearth in the middle where the student organizations meet; the Proctor's room at the far end.

**Proprietor.** [[Mathias Grell]], Proctor of Students.

**What it offers.** A bunk, for the enrolled. Club meetings in the evening. What students saw in the field.

**Hooks.** The Fencing Club owes the Scouts a wager it can't pay. A bunk has been empty a week; its owner's kit is still in the store.

### 15. The Infirmary *(the north wing of St. Erika's)*
**Outside.** *The chapel's north wing, with a glass-roofed herb garden built against its wall.*

**Inside.** Six cots; a still; drying herbs on one wall and prayer-candles on the other; a door to the chapel that neither the Vater nor the Doktor will close.

**Proprietor.** [[Doktor Agathe Sauerbrunn]], Infirmarian and head of Physic & Alchemy. [[Lecturer Jorund Vesk]] lays out the dead.

**What it offers.** Potions of healing at cost to the enrolled (25 gp), full price to others (50 gp); disease and poison treated; **Medicine & Herbalism** practicals.

**Hooks.** A patient from the last expedition won't stop shivering, and the cot frosts at night.

### 16. The Drill Yard *(University and garrison)*
**Outside.** *A packed-earth square behind the Guardhouse: a pell hacked to half its width, a butts, and a whitewashed shield-wall line. On the fence, carved names: every student who held the line on 18 Planting.*

**Proprietor.** [[Garrok Stonebrow]], Drill-Sergeant (goliath). Senior student Dagmar Brockdottir is his second. The garrison drills here at dawn.

**What it offers.** **Weapon training** for students and, with the Captain's leave, villagers (2 sp a session); shield-wall and formation work for War Studies; the **Scouts' Field Day** each Reaping.

**Hooks.** A Yatil Mountaineer sergeant asks to drill with the students and watches the eastern gate the whole time.

## Forge Row

### 17. The University Forge
**Outside.** *Two hearths and a water-driven trip-hammer under one long roof; the loudest building in town after the Fiend's Rest.*

**Proprietor.** [[Hogar Irongrip]], Master of the Forge, a Clan Irongrip exile.

**What it offers.** Expedition arms and armour; the garrison's spears; repairs at cost for the enrolled; PHB prices otherwise (weapons in 2–4 days). Smithing training for Forge Row only, unless Hogar likes you.

**Hooks.** A Clan Irongrip emissary came to court day and won't say why.

### 18. The Assay House
**Outside.** *A squat stone house on Forge Row with acid-stained steps.*

**Inside.** A long bench of crucibles and brass scales; a locked crystal cabinet; the logbook chained to the wall.

**Proprietor.** [[Brunna Deepdelve]], assayer, Clan Brock.

**What it offers.** Assay 1 gp; appraisal 5% of value; Bloom crystals logged for credits; Appraisal & Provenance lectures.

**Hooks.** Three logged Bloom crystals are missing.

### 19. Forge Row itself
Seven dwarf households, the Mining Consortium's bunkhouse (five journeymen), and the cutting crews under foreman [[Orrim Stoneweir]]. Factor [[Drunik Runevault]] works from the Fiend's Rest back room. See [[Forge Row]].

## Market Hill and the Trades

**Market Hill** ([[Market Hill]]) is the open slope where the market sets up on the first of each month and for the week of the Needfest Fair. Market-mistress: [[Mathilde Tauber]]. Stall fees go to the Moot. The trades below keep shops on or near the hill year-round.

### 20. The Stone Stall — Yusuf al-Rashad, cloth and salt
**Outside.** *The only stone stall on the hill, under an indigo awning that never comes down.*
**Proprietor.** [[Yusuf al-Rashad]], Baklunish merchant up from Ket: round, immaculate, courtly. Tea before prices.
**Sells.** Dyed cloth (2 sp–2 gp a yard), salt (5 cp a pound), Ket spices, needles and thread, letters carried south for a fee.

### 21. Talg's Chandlery — candles, lamp-oil, soap
**Outside.** *A narrow house at the top of the hill smelling of tallow and lye, candles hung in bundles in the window like strings of onions.*
**Proprietor.** **Wilhelm Talg**, human, 50s, a soft-spoken widower who renders every scrap of fat the town sends him and wastes nothing. *"Everything burns eventually. I just decide when."*
**Sells.** Candles ×10 (1 sp), lamp-oil (1 sp a flask), tallow soap (2 cp), torches ×5 (1 sp), sealing wax (5 cp).
**Hook.** He has been buying more lamp-oil than the town burns, and selling it to someone who pays in Traft coin.

### 22. Lederer's Tannery
**Outside.** *Downstream of the Mill, outside the palisade by necessity: vats, stretching-frames, and a smell that keeps it there.*
**Proprietor.** **Grete Lederer**, human, 40s, broad-armed and cheerful about the smell. *"You get used to it. No, you don't. Come in anyway."*
**Sells.** Leather, harness, boots (1 gp), leather armour to order (10 gp, 5 days), waterskins (2 sp), cured hides; buys pelts.
**Hook.** She tanned a hide the Larch elves brought in that won't stop being cold.

### 23. Brotmann's Bakehouse
**Outside.** *A low stone oven-house on the hill's south side, warm on the coldest morning; the queue starts before the second bell.*
**Proprietor.** **Jost Brotmann**, human, 30s, flour to the elbow, gossip to the eyebrows.
**Sells.** Black bread (1 cp), rye loaves (2 cp), honey cakes on fair days (1 cp), travel biscuit, 5 days (5 sp).
**Hook.** Someone has been leaving a loaf at the Wachturm's foot every morning, and it isn't him.

### 24. Webber's Loom-house
**Outside.** *A long room of looms and drying-racks, the clack of shuttles audible from the gate.*
**Proprietor.** **Ingrid Webber**, Flan, 60s, from Peat-cutters' Row; weaves the heavy uplands wool and sings while she does it — the old shrine-tender songs, though she would say they're only work songs.
**Sells.** Wool blankets (5 sp), cloaks (1 gp), heavy uplands weave by the yard.
**Hook.** The Larch elves recognize her songs.

### 25. Malzer's Brewhouse
**Outside.** *A malting-floor and copper on the hill's lower edge; steam and the smell of wet barley.*
**Proprietor.** **Hamund Malzer**, human, 40s, a proud brewer who supplies the Fiend's Rest and resents that Lotte Kesselring brews her own for the Grey Wolf.
**Sells.** Ale by the cask to houses (1 gp); small beer to households.
**Hook.** His barley comes partly from the Larch elves, off the Moot's book — the grain the mill log can't account for.

### 26. Tauber's Cooperage
**Outside.** *Barrel staves stacked like firewood along the Taubers' lane; hoop-iron hanging from the eaves.*
**Proprietor.** [[Reinholt Tauber]], cooper and Moot Speaker. His wife [[Mathilde Tauber]] runs the market.
**Sells.** Barrels and casks (2 gp), buckets (5 cp), tubs; repairs. Cider casks for the McCockins every autumn — his biggest order.
**Hook.** The Moot meets at his workshop when the Ritter's Hall is shut to it.

### 27. Radmacher's Wheelwright
**Outside.** *A yard of spokes, felloes, and half-built wheels next to the cooperage.*
**Proprietor.** **Anno Radmacher**, human, 50s, laconic; builds and mends every cart and sledge in Ritterstadt.
**Sells.** Cart wheels (5 gp), sledge runners (2 gp), cart repair (5 sp–2 gp).
**Hook.** He is rebuilding a pack-merchant's cart whose axle was cut, not broken.

## The Edges of Town

### 28. The Burrows
Gnome-cut rooms under Market Hill. **Proprietors:** [[Nessa Quillfeather]] (the Seam-Finders' chart room) and [[Pim Wobblewright]] (instrument workshop). Compass 25 gp; bog-sounder 15 gp; instrument training. See [[The Burrows]].

### 29. The Larch Camp
Outside the east gate at the tree line. **Proprietor:** [[Caelith of the Larch]], kin-leader; [[Ysmay Larchwind]] teaches archery and woodcraft for grain. Bow-staves 2 gp; never arrows. See [[The Larch Camp]].

### 30. The Landing (the quay)
**Outside.** *Below the palisade, where the town meets Lake Quag: a timber quay with Rhennee barges tied along it in summer and hauled up on rollers for winter, the tally-house with its stove, nets drying on poles, and the smokehouse at the end where the wind takes the smoke out over the water.*
**Inside the tally-house.** A stove, a bench, and no visible ledger.
**Proprietor.** [[Baba Yeska Marovna]], Warden of the Landing; [[Dorrin Hollin]] runs the halfling ferry.
**Sells.** Smoked fish (1 gp a barrel), fresh fish in season (2 cp), "Quag salt" (5 sp a pound), passage to Traft and the lake towns by water. Tolls 1 sp a hull, 1 gp a cargo; the Ritter's fishing tithe is taken here. See [[The Landing]].

*Ritterstadt is a lake port: the quay is the town's front door to the south, as the Sepia road is its back door to the north.*
