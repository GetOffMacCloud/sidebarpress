# RG-05 — Economy & Trade

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · ← [[RG-04a — Building Directory, Part 2]] · Next → [[RG-06 — Factions]]

Ritterstadt lives on four things: **McCockin Cider**, what the lake and the bog give up, what the dwarves and gnomes dig, and what the University pays for. Coin is Perrender — **marks** (gp) and **kleinmarks** (sp) — and scarce; most local trade is in kind, tallied by the Vogt, the Reeve of Fields, and Baba Yeska. As a lake port, Ritterstadt ships south by water from its quay and north and east by the Sepia road.

## Who Pays for the Field Station
- **The McCockins** — the largest private benefactor: endowment gifts, equipment funds, and quiet coverage of more than one student's tuition shortfall.
- **The Regents' Commission** — grants, with a 10% claim on any relic recovered under commission.
- **The Ritter's Kettenhaus loan** — the Long Dormitory and the quay extension.
- **Credits** — the internal currency that keeps students working without coin.

## Imports
Salt (dearer than it should be), cloth and dye (Yusuf al-Rashad), southern steel, wine, paper (the Archive buys every ream that comes north), glass, horses (there are nine in town; the Ritter owns three).

## The Trade Calendar

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Needfest** | The Needfest Fair, one week. The Moot elects its Speaker. Pack-merchants winter over. |
| **Readying–Coldeven** | The lake freezes at the margin; the quay is quiet. Peat burns. The cider-house runs. |
| **Planting** | The Bloom's worst month. Barges run again. The Consortium's cutters go to the bogs. |
| **Flocktime–Wealsun** | Peat cut. First iron. The first semester's quests. |
| **Reaping–Harvester** | Harvest. The midsummer census. The Scouts' Field Day. |
| **Patchwall** | Apples in. The autumn slaughter at the Big Barn. Timber felling begins. The pack-merchants' last run. |
| **First frost to deep winter** | The McCockin press runs; casks go south by barge until the lake closes. |

## Credits and Coin
Students are paid in **credits**, not marks. Credits buy tuition, infirmary care at cost, forge work at cost, and standing; they cannot buy ale. The total credit limit is **600**; extra credit sits outside it. Karet accepts a University chit for a bed and redeems it with the Bursar at the quarter.

## Hooks
- A Traft factor offers Hogar twice the Clatspur price for a season's bog iron, delivered to a barge at night.
- Pim Wobblewright's bog-sounders read wrong near the Hush.
- The Vogt wants to sell the old-growth pines to the north. Nobody can say why anyone would pay that much for them.
