# RG-04 — Buildings & Services

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · ← [[RG-03 — The Census]] · Next → [[RG-04a — Building Directory, Part 1]]

This chapter is the **quick reference**: where things are and what each building offers. Full descriptions, proprietors, price lists, and hooks are in the **Building Directory**: [[RG-04a — Building Directory, Part 1]] (the old village) and [[RG-04a — Building Directory, Part 2]] (the Field Station and the growing town).

## The Lay of the Town

Ritterstadt is a lake port on the north shore of **Lake Quag**, where the Sepia Uplands come down to the water.
- **The quay** lies below the town on the shore: the Rhennee barges, the tally-house, the smokehouse.
- **Dagmar's palisade** (565) rings the town, the chapel, and the shore. The **eastern gate** has been timber-and-rope since 18 Planting.
- **The Ritter's Hall** stands inside its own older palisade (Gerhard's, c. 510), the town's inner wall, with an iron-banded gate onto the **village square**. The **Guardhouse** is built against its eastern side; the **Station House** stands beside it.
- **The Wachturm** rises at the **northern edge** of the village, the oldest thing in Ritterstadt, its bell on the roof.
- **The Farmsteads** — eleven longhouses and the **Big Barn** — lie between the Hall and **the Mill**, which sits at the **southern edge** where a cold stream cuts down a limestone gully.
- **Market Hill** is the slope between the gate and the chapel; **the Burrows** open into its south face.
- **Forge Row** hugs the inside of the eastern wall.
- **The McCockin Orchard** spreads over a south-facing slope at the edge of town, the estate house on its highest ground.
- **The Larch camp** lies outside the east gate at the tree line; **Lederer's Tannery** sits outside the wall downstream of the Mill.

## Services at a Glance
- **Healing:** St. Erika's (free herbal care) · the Infirmary (potions at cost for the enrolled)
- **Rest:** the Fiend's Rest (travellers, Questers, merchants) · the Grey Wolf (locals; pallet 3 cp, rooms 4–7 sp) · the Long Dormitory (enrolled only)
- **Training:** the Drill Yard (weapons) · the Larch Camp (archery, woodcraft) · the Archive (research) · the Assay House (appraisal) · the Infirmary (medicine) · the Burrows (instruments)
- **Information:** the Fiend's Rest for the road, the Grey Wolf for the village, St. Erika's for the past, the quay for the lake, Hanz for the land
- **Arms and repairs:** the Smithy (village work) · the University Forge (expeditions)
- **Shelter in an attack:** the Ritter's palisade; the Big Barn for those who can't reach it

## Hooks
- The Archive's sealed stacks are sealed by the Registrar, not the Archivist, and the Archivist wants to know why.
- The Drill Yard's Field Day ends in a wager the Fencing Club can't pay.
- A barge comes to the quay at night and leaves lighter.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - **The Station House seal** has a twin held by the Regents' Commission. A commission sealed with the second die is an order the Warden cannot refuse.
> - **The sealed stacks** hold AR-001 (Yrith Kael), Hassenrath's second notebook, and Calloway's charter correspondence.
> - **The Assay House** logs Bloom crystals. Three are missing. The Pale Ledger has them.
> - **The quay's night barges** carry Baba Yeska's salt south without toll and, twice a season, a sealed crate north.
