# RG-04 — Buildings & Services

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · ← [[RG-03 — The Census]] · Next → [[RG-05 — Economy & Trade]]

Ritterstadt is laid out on a rise above the north shore of Lake Quag. The **palisade** rings the old hamlet and the University; the **landing** sits below it on the water; **Forge Row** hugs the inside of the eastern wall; the **Larch camp** lies outside the east gate at the tree line; the **farmsteads** spread south along the lake road. The **Wachturm** stands on the highest point inside the wall, on a foundation older than the town.

Buildings marked **(canon)** have full notes in the campaign import (`03 Buildings`). Buildings marked **(new)** are this book's additions.

## The New Buildings

**The Station House.** A two-storey timber hall beside the Ritter's Hall, with the University's arms over the door — an open book, a cudgel and a quill crossed beneath it. The Warden's office, the Registrar's counting-room, the Bursar's strong-box, and the seal that makes a Job Board posting a commission. The Chancellor's portrait hangs in the hall and faces the door.

**The Archive.** Stone-built, the only building in town with a slate roof, because paper burns. The Archivist keeps the field reports, the map archive, the Publications, and the **sealed stacks**: documents the Registry has closed. Students may read; faculty may borrow; nobody takes a map out without the Scouts & Surveyors' countersign.

**The Long Dormitory.** Built with Konrad's loan: forty bunks in two bays, the Proctor's room at the end, and a common hearth where the student organizations hold their meetings. Visiting faculty lodge at the Grey Wolf.

**The Assay House.** On Forge Row. Brunna Deepdelve tests ore, grades bog iron, and — by charter — logs every Bloom crystal and relic that comes out of the ground before the Antiquities department sees it. The Consortium pays her; the University trusts her. Both are usually right.

**The University Forge.** Hogar's. Larger than the village smithy and louder. It makes the watch's spears and the expeditions' steel, and it is the one place in Ritterstadt where a Clan Irongrip dwarf will drink with a Clan Brock dwarf.

**The Drill Yard.** A packed-earth square behind the Guardhouse with a pell, a butts, and a shield-wall line painted in whitewash. War Studies practicals and Stahlwulf's drill share it. The Scouts & Surveyors and the Fencing Club hold Field Day here, legally, mostly.

**The Infirmary.** The chapel's north wing, given to the University in 574. Six cots, a still, and Doktor Sauerbrunn's herb garden under glass. The Vater and the Doktor disagree about whether prayer or tincture does the work and agree that both are charged to the Bursar.

**The Landing.** A timber quay, four barges wintering on the hard, the tally-house, and the smokehouse. The lake is the fastest road south and the Rhennee own it.

**Market Hill.** The open slope between the gate and the chapel. Fair days are the first of each month; the Needfest fair runs a week. Yusuf al-Rashad's is the only stone stall; everyone else sets up boards on trestles.

**The Burrows.** Gnome-cut rooms under Market Hill, dry and warm, where the Seam-Finders keep their charts and Pim Wobblewright keeps his lathe. The University buys every compass he makes.

**The Larch Camp.** Three family-groups of sylvan elves in bark-and-felt shelters at the tree line, with a watch on the Hush. They are not enrolled on the Moot Roll and are not asked to the wall, and turn out when the horn sounds anyway.

**The Hush.** A stretch of the Mire margin east of town where sound dies — no birdsong, no wind; even footsteps land soft. Karet's ledger maps six "stress points" where the quiet runs deepest. The Larch keep a watch on its edge, and the Fiend's Rest owes a free pint to anyone who walks through. Few collect.

## Services at a Glance
- **Healing:** St. Erika's (herbal; advantage on death saves within the hour) · the Infirmary (potions at cost for the enrolled, full price otherwise)
- **Rest:** the Fiend's Rest (5 sp a bed, 1 gp a room) · the Grey Wolf (3 sp a bed for locals, 5 sp for strangers) · the Long Dormitory (enrolled only)
- **Training:** the Drill Yard (weapons) · the Larch Camp (archery, woodcraft) · the Archive (research) · the Assay House (appraisal) · St. Erika's (medicine, apprenticeship)
- **Information:** the Fiend's Rest for the road, the Grey Wolf for the town, the Archive for the past, the Landing for the lake, Hanz for the land
- **Arms and repairs:** the Smithy (village work first) · the University Forge (expeditions and the watch)

## Hooks
- The Archive's sealed stacks are sealed by the Registrar, not the Archivist, and the Archivist wants to know why.
- The Drill Yard's Field Day ends in a wager the Fencing Club can't pay.
- A barge comes to the Landing at night and leaves lighter.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - **The Station House seal** is a brass die the Warden keeps in his desk. The Regents' Commission has a second die. A commission sealed with the second die is a Regents' order, and the Warden cannot refuse it.
> - **The Archive's sealed stacks** hold AR-001 (Yrith Kael), Hassenrath's second notebook (the one Sigmund never saw), and Calloway's charter correspondence with its promise about the Embrace.
> - **The Assay House** logs Bloom crystals. Three logged crystals are missing. Brunna thinks the Curio Club; the Curio Club thinks the Pale Ledger; the Pale Ledger has them.
> - **The Landing's night barges** carry Baba Yeska's salt south without toll and, twice a season, a sealed crate north for a customer she has never met.
