# Faction — The Pale Ledger

> A Scarlet Brotherhood counting-house disguised as three Suel exiles on the lane behind the Grey Wolf. It writes down everything that moves north and sends it south in a calfskin ledger.

Back to [[RG-06 — Factions]] · [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]]

## At a Glance
| | |
|---|---|
| **Parent** | The Scarlet Brotherhood (Kro Terlep) |
| **Cell** | [[Hesta Varnholm]] (the pen); a Suel tailor (the needle); the "retired factor" (the handler) |

## What They Want
- **Said:** to be left alone.
- **Meant:** to know who holds the Sepia Uplands when the Sepia Uplands matter. Counts of iron, students, maps, couriers; the movements of Iuz's agents; samples of Bloom crystals for the Brotherhood's scholars, who want to know whether they can be grown.

## How They Operate
- Hesta copies for the Illuminators' Guild and the townsfolk, and reads everything she copies.
- The ledger goes south twice a year sewn into a bolt of Yusuf al-Rashad's cloth bound for Traft. He does not know.
- The handler meets no one and is seen by everyone.
- They do not kill in Ritterstadt. They leave.

## Relationships
- **Competitors:** Iuz's agents. The Ledger would sell Ewald to Stahlwulf for the right price.
- **Watched by:** Vethkar Graun, who has noticed them. They have noticed him noticing.
- **Unknowing hosts:** Yusuf al-Rashad; the Illuminators' Guild.

## Hooks
- Yusuf's cloth bolt is stolen on the Traft road by common bandits who find a ledger they can't read.
- Hesta offers to fair-copy the party's Field Report for half price.
- Three missing Bloom crystals turn up in a tailor's button-box.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - The Ledger holds the three missing Assay House crystals and a second copy of the Grey Sickle's barrow list.
> - If the ledger is ever read by someone outside the Brotherhood, Hesta leaves within the week, the handler disappears that night, and the tailor stays — because the tailor is the one the Brotherhood can spare.
> - The Brotherhood's interest is long: it expects Iuz and the Bloom to break the north, and means to be the one holding the map when they do.
