## What He Wants
- **Said:** to study the Wachturm.
- **Meant:** to study the Wachturm. That is the problem. Doss wants the sub-floor chamber, the clay tablet, and the node sites, for research. He is not malicious. He has found the most interesting thing in his field in a century and cannot leave it alone.

## How He Operates
- **By proxy.** He commissions appraisers, buys copies, and writes letters. His notes on the Wachturm reached Ritterstadt in Vethkar Graun's luggage.
- **By scholarship.** He has linked the Wachturm to what Baklunish learning calls the **Prior Period** construction network: binding and containment works raised before Baklunish civilization, which Baklunish scholars later catalogued, classified, and officially stopped thinking about.
- **By arriving.** Once the report is in, he will come north in person.

## The Assessment
The Chancellor of Greyhawk University is waiting on Doss to say whether the Fiend's Embrace can be moved south safely (see [[Faction — The Regents' Commission]]). Doss does not yet know that his word is the trigger for the Regents' second seal.

His notes on the Wachturm use the word *catastrophic*, underlined. It is not melodrama. It is a careful man who has done the arithmetic on an unsealed Prior Period site and chosen the most accurate word he has.

## History
- **Before 576 CY.** Years of work on Prior Period sites in the Baklunish west.
- **575 CY.** Doss identifies the Wachturm as part of the network and hires Vethkar Graun, already bound north on Kettenhaus business, to look at it for him.
- **576 CY.** The notes travel north with Graun. Doss waits in Eru Tovar for the report.

## Goals
**Major:**
- Reach the sub-floor chamber, the tablet, and the node sites.
- Learn what the network was built to contain.

**Minor:**
- Give the Chancellor an honest assessment.
- Publish. Eventually. If anyone survives to read it.

## Relationships
- **Useful to:** the Regents' Commission, which waits on his word.
- **Employer of:** Vethkar Graun, on the side.
- **Unaware of:** almost everyone else in this chapter. He thinks he is the only one who has noticed the tower.

## Bringing Him In
Doss becomes an extraordinary ally if someone tells him what the network actually does, and what is at stake, before he starts taking it apart. A scholar who understands that the Wachturm is working machinery, not an excavation, can read the Prior Period records, call on Baklunish institutions, and perhaps work out what kind of repair the original builders designed the network to accept.

He just needs to be frightened in the right direction first.

## Hooks
- Doss arrives early, with a Baklunish text and a plan to open the floor.
- A letter for Vethkar Graun in a Baklunish hand is misdelivered to a student.
- The Chancellor writes to ask the party to escort "a distinguished visiting scholar" from Traft.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - **If the party meets him first** (on the road, in Traft, by letter), they can make him an ally. **If they don't,** he arrives with his own agenda and starts work that speeds up the very collapse he is studying.
> - **What he doesn't know:** what the network contains. He suspects it is large, old, and bad in the way things sealed by a coalition older than civilization tend to be. He is right (see [[RG-10 — DM Companion]] § *The Ground Beneath*).
> - **If he tells the Chancellor "yes,"** the second seal is used within the season, and the reliquary is found empty. Doss is the one person in this chapter who would find that more interesting than the cloak.
