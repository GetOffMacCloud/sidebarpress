## What They Want
- **Said:** an Old Kerk chapel in Ritterstadt, so Perrender souls are not left to a Pholtine frontier priest.
- **Meant:** the cantons won't spend coin on the frontier, but the Kerk will spend a priest. If Ritterstadt is ever brought under a canton, the Kerk means to have been here first. And the elders want to know whether St. Erika's foundation stone is the Flan threshold marker the old songs say it is.

## How They Operate
- Alms to the families the Moot hasn't enrolled.
- Sermons on fair days that never mention the University and are always about it.
- Letters south to the Voormann's council about what the University digs up.

## Origin
The Old Kerk is Perrenland's church, seated at Schwartzenbruin. The cantons will not spend coin on the frontier, but the Kerk will spend a priest: if Ritterstadt is ever brought under a canton, the Kerk means to have been there first — with a congregation, a chapel, and a correspondence. In 575 the elders sent Dominie Anselm Roth north "to serve the Perrender faithful of the frontier."

## History
- **575 CY.** Roth arrives, lodges at the Grey Wolf, and begins preaching on fair days. He is courteous to Vater Sigmund Wolfherz, and patient, and everywhere.
- **575–576 CY.** A congregation gathers: most of Alder Row's Perrender fishers, a few Moot households, the unenrolled the Moot hasn't found work for. Alms do what sermons can't.
- **576 CY.** Roth writes to the Voormann's council that the University is "digging where it should not." He does not know the Hanselmann Consortium reads the Voormann's post.
- **576 CY.** The elders' brief sharpens: determine whether St. Erika's foundation stone is the Flan threshold marker of the old songs. Roth asks, kindly, for help looking at it.

## Goals
**Major:**
- Raise an Old Kerk chapel and a true congregation in Ritterstadt, so Perrender souls are not left to a Pholtine frontier priest.
- Be first in place — congregation, chapel, correspondence — if a canton ever claims the town.
- Answer the elders' question about the foundation stone.
**Minor:**
- Bind the unenrolled households with charity the Moot can't match.
- Keep the letters south flowing to the Voormann's council.
- Stay courteous with Vater Sigmund while gently displacing him.
- Learn what the University is digging up at the Wachturm — and who else wants to know.

## Relationships
- **Allies:** the Moot's Perrender households (cautiously).
- **Rivals:** the Regents' Commission, over the town's future; Vater Sigmund, gently.
- **Enemies:** the Grey Sickle (when they meet); Iuz's agents.
- **Watched by:** the Hanselmann Consortium, which reads the Voormann's post.

## Hooks
- Roth asks the party, kindly, to look at the chapel's foundation stone and tell him what they see.
- The Mission offers to pay for the eastern gate's repair — in exchange for a chapel lot inside the palisade.
- A sermon on Market Hill names the University's dig at the Wachturm in front of the whole town.
