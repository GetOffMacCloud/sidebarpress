# Faction — The Old Kerk Mission

> Perrenland's church, sent north in 575 to serve the Perrender faithful of the frontier — and to be there first if a canton ever claims Ritterstadt.

Back to [[RG-06 — Factions]] · [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]]

## At a Glance
| | |
|---|---|
| **Parent** | The Old Kerk, Schwartzenbruin |
| **Missioner** | [[Dominie Anselm Roth]], lodging at the Grey Wolf |
| **Congregation** | Most of Alder Row's Perrender fishers; a few Moot households |
| **Public face** | Charity, fair-day preaching, and courtesy to Vater Sigmund |
| **Resources** | Legitimacy, the Kerk's purse for alms, and letters to the Voormann's council |
| **Disposition to the party** | Warm, and interested in what they find |

## What They Want
- **Said:** an Old Kerk chapel in Ritterstadt, so Perrender souls are not left to a Pholtine frontier priest.
- **Meant:** the cantons won't spend coin on the frontier, but the Kerk will spend a priest. If Ritterstadt is ever brought under a canton, the Kerk means to have been here first. And the elders want to know whether St. Erika's foundation stone is the Flan threshold marker the old songs say it is.

## How They Operate
- Alms to the families the Moot hasn't enrolled.
- Sermons on fair days that never mention the University and are always about it.
- Letters south to the Voormann's council about what the University digs up.

## Relationships
- **Allies:** the Moot's Perrender households (cautiously).
- **Rivals:** the Regents' Commission, over the town's future; Vater Sigmund, gently.
- **Enemies:** the Grey Sickle (when they meet); Iuz's agents.
- **Watched by:** the Hanselmann Consortium, which reads the Voormann's post.

## Hooks
- Roth asks the party, kindly, to look at the chapel's foundation stone and tell him what they see.
- The Mission offers to pay for the eastern gate's repair — in exchange for a chapel lot inside the palisade.
- A sermon on Market Hill names the University's dig at the Wachturm in front of the whole town.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - Roth has already written that the University is "digging where it should not." He doesn't know the Consortium reads the Voormann's post.
> - If the stone is read aloud in Old Flan, the Wolf Nomad outriders come down off the moor for the first time in eleven years. Roth will be standing next to it.
