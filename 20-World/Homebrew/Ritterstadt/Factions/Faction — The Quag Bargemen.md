# Faction — The Quag Bargemen

> Four Rhennee barge-families under Baba Yeska Marovna. They run the Landing, the ferry, the smokehouse, the salt pans, and the fastest road south — the lake.

Back to [[RG-06 — Factions]] · [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]]

## What They Want
- **Said:** tolls, a dry winter berth, and to be left to the water.
- **Meant:** to hold the Landing as Ritterstadt grows, and with it the road to Traft. Yeska also wants her grandchildren's parentage left unspoken, forever.

## How They Operate
- **Tolls:** 1 sp a hull, 1 gp a cargo, collected by Yeska and spent as she sees fit.
- **Salt:** half Traft salt bought cheap and sold back as "Quag salt"; half from a pan at the Mire margin that should not exist.
- **Night barges:** south without toll; north twice a season with a sealed crate.
- **Loyalty:** to the Landing first, the town second, the Moot when it suits.

## Relationships
- **Allies:** the halfling ferrymen; the Moot (uneasily).
- **Rivals:** the Hanselmann Consortium's road trade; the canton of Quagfludt, whose harbour-reeve wants a toll agreement.
- **Compromised by:** Iuz's recruiter, through Yeska's daughter.

## Hooks
- Quagfludt seizes a barge for unpaid toll. Yeska wants it back tonight.
- Passage to Traft at half toll — and a night's silence about what else is aboard.
- A barge comes to the Landing at night and leaves lighter.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - The salt pan at the Mire margin sits on **Node 3** of the binding network.
> - The northbound crate carries salt, furs, and a letter from Yeska to her daughter, who went north with Iuz's recruiter in 575. The recruiter writes back. Yeska burns the replies. She would set fire to the Landing before letting it be used against the town.
