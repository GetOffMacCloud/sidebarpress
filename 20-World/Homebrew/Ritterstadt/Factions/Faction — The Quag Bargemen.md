# Faction — The Quag Bargemen

> Four Rhennee barge-families under Baba Yeska Marovna. They run the Landing, the ferry, the smokehouse, the salt pans, and the fastest road south — the lake.

## What They Want
- **Said:** tolls, a dry winter berth, and to be left to the water.
- **Meant:** to hold the Landing as Ritterstadt grows, and with it the road to Traft. Yeska also wants her grandchildren's parentage left unspoken, forever.

## How They Operate
- **Tolls:** 1 sp a hull, 1 gp a cargo, collected by Yeska and spent as she sees fit.
- **Salt:** half Traft salt bought cheap and sold back as "Quag salt"; half from a pan at the Mire margin that should not exist.
- **Night barges:** south without toll; north twice a season with a sealed crate.
- **Loyalty:** to the Landing first, the town second, the Moot when it suits.

## Origin
Rhennee barge-families work the waters of the Flanaess and belong to none of them. These four families worked the Lake Quag road for years before Ritterstadt gave them a reason to stop: a town on the north shore, a University paying in coin and credits, and a winter berth. In 575 they wintered at the landing. They stayed.

## History
- **575 CY.** The barge-families winter at the landing and stay. Baba Yeska Marovna becomes Warden of the Landing by acclamation and arithmetic: she holds the toll-book.
- **575 CY.** The Rhennee find a salt pan at the Mire margin where nothing should be dry. Half their "Quag salt" is Traft salt bought cheap and sold back dear; the other half is real — and it sits on Node 3.
- **575 CY.** Yeska's daughter goes north with Iuz's recruiter. The barge-families say the Mires took her. Yeska gets letters. She burns them.
- **18 Planting 576 CY.** When the thing comes out of the margin bog and breaks the eastern gate, the bargemen turn out for the wall without being asked. The town notices.
- **576 CY.** Twice a season, a sealed crate goes north for a customer Yeska has never met.

## Goals
**Major:**
- Hold the Landing as Ritterstadt grows: whoever holds the fastest road to Traft holds the town's future, and Yeska means it to be her.
- Keep the family's secret: the daughter, the grandchildren, the letters.
- Keep the Node 3 salt pan unknown and unvisited.
**Minor:**
- Keep the salt fraud running: Traft salt in, "Quag salt" out, at a premium.
- Fend off Quagfludt's harbour-reeve and his toll agreement.
- Keep the Moot's goodwill without enrolling — the Landing's loyalty is to the Landing first.
- Move the northbound crate without questions, twice a season.

## Relationships
- **Allies:** the halfling ferrymen; the Moot (uneasily).
- **Rivals:** the Hanselmann Consortium's road trade; the canton of Quagfludt, whose harbour-reeve wants a toll agreement.
- **Compromised by:** Iuz's recruiter, through Yeska's daughter.

## Hooks
- Quagfludt seizes a barge for unpaid toll. Yeska wants it back tonight.
- Passage to Traft at half toll — and a night's silence about what else is aboard.
- A barge comes to the Landing at night and leaves lighter.
