# Faction — The Agents of Iuz

> [!art]+ Emblem and face
> ![[Ritterstadt agents-of-iuz.jpg|240]] ![[Ritterstadt ewald-wennerberg.jpg|240]]


> Travellers, traders, a scholar, a soldier: whatever cover the current operative wears. Two separate operations of the Old One work Ritterstadt in 576. Neither knows the other is there.

## What They Want
- **The Fiend's Embrace.** Recovery if possible, destruction if not. It is the cloak flayed from the pit fiend Drokkarn for Iggwilv, Iuz's mother, and its worth to Iuz's cult is theological, not military. They are not after a weapon. They are after a relic.
- **The Wachturm.** Anything the University finds about the tower and what lies beneath it.
- **A weak town.** Ritterstadt destabilized enough that it cannot resist the first two.

## How They Operate
- **The network.** A chain of contacts and dead-drops runs south from Iuz's lands through the Wolf Nomad borderlands into the eastern Sepia. It has been active since the mid-560s, after Iuz's mercenaries lost the race to Cold Stone Keep and word came back that the Embrace had gone to Ritterstadt.
- **The asset in the garrison.** Ewald Wennerberg is a good soldier, and that is the point. He volunteers for the northern watch, asks small questions about large things, and disappears on irregular nights to service a dead-drop about ninety minutes' walk north of the palisade.
- **Slow and careful.** They are not ready to act. They will be when three things are true:
  1. They know exactly where in the town the Embrace is kept.
  2. They have someone inside who can make an opening.
  3. The town is weak: the Ritter's debt called in, the garrison's loyalty in doubt.

## The Other Operation
**Kermin Mind-Bender**, a mage of Iuz's Greater Boneheart, runs his own people in Ritterstadt, after the Chancellor's sealed crate rather than the Embrace. The two operations report up different chains and have never been told about each other. Kermin's people, and the crate, are the GQ adventure series, beginning with *GQ0 The Shadow on the Quag*.

## History
- **565 CY.** Matyara's Fingers of Iuz lose the Embrace to six adventurers at Cold Stone Keep. Iuz's apparatus hears it went south to Ritterstadt.
- **565–575 CY.** The network is built: dead-drops, a handler at Kottsmort, couriers through the borderlands.
- **c. 575 CY.** Ewald Wennerberg joins the Ritterstadt garrison.
- **576 CY.** Kermin Mind-Bender's people reach Ritterstadt by water (the GQ series).

## Goals
**Major:**
- Recover or destroy the Fiend's Embrace.
- Learn what the Wachturm holds.

**Minor:**
- Keep Ewald unidentified.
- Exploit the Ritter's debt, which they did not cause but have noticed.
- (Kermin's operation) See the GQ series.

## Relationships
- **Enemies:** the Regents' Commission, the Old Kerk Mission, the druid enclave.
- **Competitors:** the Pale Ledger, which watches them.
- **Useful:** the Grey Sickle; the Ritter's creditors.
- **Leverage:** the Quag Bargemen, through Baba Yeska's daughter.
- **Fooled by:** the Velvet Shadow, whose rise in the north Iuz's commanders still take for a squabble between chieftains.

## Hooks
- Ewald asks the party to carry a letter to Kottsmort "for his sister."
- A gate roster with one deliberate error turns up in Kottsmort.
- A student is offered a scholarship to "a school in the north" by a pleasant clerk at the Landing.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - **The box is empty.** The Embrace never came to Ritterstadt; Eglantine Price wears it in the Burneal, as the Velvet Shadow. Fifteen years of patient work are aimed at an empty reliquary. The moment Iuz's agents open it, they turn to the people who know where it went: the Founders, and Drunik Runevault first.
> - **The theory.** Iuz's scholar believes the Wachturm's binding apparatus and the Embrace are connected. He is wrong about the Embrace, and right that the tower matters.
> - **Ewald is already known.** [[Vethkar Graun]] identified him as a foreign agent within a fortnight of arriving, and has told no one. Hauptmann Stahlwulf suspects him and has started leaving the gate roster where he can read it, with one deliberate error, to see where it turns up.
> - **How the party stays ahead:** settle the debt, secure the garrison, and keep the truth about the reliquary out of any document that might be copied.
