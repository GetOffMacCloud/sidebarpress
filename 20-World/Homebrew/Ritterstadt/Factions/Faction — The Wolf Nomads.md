# Faction — The Wolf Nomads

> [!art]+ Emblem
> ![[Ritterstadt wolf-nomads.jpg|240]]


> Herders who ride the eastern Sepia on routes older than their own songs. They have watched Ritterstadt for eleven years and never once attacked it. They are waiting to see whether the town knows what it is sitting on.

Back to [[RG-06 — Factions]] · [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]]

## What They Want
- **Said:** nothing. Outriders do not explain themselves to settlers.
- **Meant:** recognition, not land. They want their sacred claim to the moor sites acknowledged, the standing stones left alone, and someone in Ritterstadt who understands what the Wachturm is and takes it seriously.

## How They Operate
- **The routes.** Every band rides the same seasonal paths its grandparents rode. The paths run from one old site to the next. The nomads call them "the ancestor routes" and can no longer say why they matter, only that they must be kept.
- **The prohibitions.** The standing stones at the moor circle (G5) are not to be touched on certain nights of the year. The Wachturm is "the place that must not be opened": exact words, carried in song, with no explanation left attached.
- **Watching.** The outriders saw the chapel's foundation stone the year the Founders came. They know the old Flan carving on it and what it marks. That is why they have never raided Ritterstadt.

## Bringing Them In
Nobody meets a band by riding up to it. The way in is the stone circle at G5:
1. Go to the stones and do not touch them.
2. Spend the night there.
3. Wait.

A party that does this is approached by an outrider within 48 hours. A Flan-descended character, or one who shows real knowledge of the old sites, is approached the first night. A party that pokes at the stones is never approached at all, and the outriders remember its faces.

A band leader who grants an audience can recite the route-songs. Those songs fill gaps in Hassenrath's notes, including the directions to Nodes 5 and 6 (see [[RG-10 — DM Companion]] § *The Ground Beneath*).

## Origin
The Wolf Nomads are the last people in the Sepia who still keep the shrine-tenders' calendar. Their ancestors tended the Flan binding sites before the Migrations. The knowledge was broken and half-lost over the centuries, but the practice survived: the routes, the prohibitions, the songs. They are an institution that has forgotten its own purpose and never stopped doing its job.

## History
- **Before memory.** Flan shrine-tenders keep the sites. Their descendants become herders who keep the routes.
- **565 CY.** The Founders re-found Ritterstadt. Outriders see the chapel stone and decide to watch.
- **566–571 CY.** Something in the far north begins to unite the clans of the Burneal margin. The southern bands hear of it only as rumour (see [[Faction — The Velvet Shadow and the Winter Vanguard]]).
- **576 CY.** The Bloom returns worse than any band remembers. The outriders watch the town more closely than ever. A trader, Elif Kayim, comes south to trade at Ritterstadt for the first time, carrying news from the north.

## Goals
**Major:**
- The moor sites acknowledged as theirs to keep. Not ceded; recognized.
- The Bloom stopped. The Quag Mires are spreading and every Bloom year is worse. This is their most urgent fear, and the reason they are watching Ritterstadt now.

**Minor:**
- Learn whether anyone in Ritterstadt knows anything useful about the Bloom.
- Keep the standing stones undisturbed, by students, surveyors, or loggers.
- Trade. Elif Kayim's reception decides whether others follow him.

## Relationships
- **Watching:** Ritterstadt, the University, and anyone who digs.
- **Indifferent to:** the Regents, the Kettenhaus, the Moot.
- **Enemies without knowing it:** the Grey Sickle, whose list of graves includes the nomads' sites.
- **Kin they have lost touch with:** the northern clans, now riding under an iron order they do not understand.

## Hooks
- Outriders are seen on the ridge above the Larch camp three days running. They do not come closer.
- Dominie Roth asks the party to read the chapel's foundation stone. That same week, an outrider is seen at the edge of the market.
- Elif Kayim reaches the gate with amber, furs, and a cousin who rode north and never came back.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - **What they know without knowing it:** the nights the stones may not be touched are the network's resonance peaks. The routes are a maintenance circuit. The bands are the closest thing the Sepia has to qualified practitioners (see [[RG-10 — DM Companion]] § *The Ground Beneath*).
> - **What they want, if it comes to it:** a working partnership. They have the songs; the party has freedom of movement and University resources. Together they might fix the network.
> - **The northern clans** are no longer free. The Velvet Shadow's patronage has bound many of them, and the iron riders in Elif Kayim's rumours are their own cousins. The southern bands do not know this yet.
