# Faction — The Regents' Commission

> The Board of Regents' standing committee on field stations and antiquities. It funds Ritterstadt, prints its Publications, confirms its Warden — and holds a promise made in 568 that the Fiend's Embrace would come south "when safely movable."

Back to [[RG-06 — Factions]] · [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]]

## What They Want
- **Said:** a productive station, sound ledgers, graduates the Cantons will hire, and the University's name on every map of the north.
- **Meant:** the Regents' tenth of every relic recovered under commission, and the Fiend's Embrace under the Founders' Compact.

## How They Operate
- **Money first.** Grants are written with a clause: 10% of any "object of antiquarian value" recovered under a University commission belongs to the home campus.
- **Paper second.** The second seal makes a Job Board posting a Regents' order the Warden cannot refuse.
- **People third.** Vogelsang reports to Dray directly. The Curio Club is tolerated as a collection arm.

## Ranks and Recognition
Students who submit relics and maps earn **Regents' Commendations** (extra credit, 25 each) and are noticed in the City of Greyhawk. Students who hide finds are noticed too.

## Relationships
- **Allies:** the Chancellor's office; Professor Vogelsang; the Curio Club (unofficially).
- **Rivals:** the druid enclave (over the Uplands' future); the Old Kerk Mission (over who claims the town).
- **Useful:** Varek Doss, whose assessment the Chancellor is waiting for.
- **Dangerous to:** the Ritter's independence and the Warden's conscience.

## Hooks
- Dray's audit year: what goes in his crate, and who decides.
- A commission arrives under the second seal: *inventory the Founders' Compact items.*
- A Regents' Commendation is offered for a relic the party promised to return.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - The drafted commission reads: *Recover and convey the object held at Ritterstadt under the Founders' Compact.* The Chancellor has not given the word; he waits on Doss.
> - If the second seal is used, the Warden resigns, Vogelsang becomes Warden, and the Fiend's Embrace leaves the palisade for the first time since 565. What the binding network does when that happens is the campaign's question, not the Regents'.
