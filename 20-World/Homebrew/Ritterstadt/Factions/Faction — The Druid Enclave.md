# Faction — The Druid Enclave at Ehlonna's Mount

> [!art]+ Emblem and face
> ![[Ritterstadt druid-enclave.jpg|240]] ![[Ritterstadt giseth.jpg|240]]


> A circle of druids that has kept records of the Sepia's seasons for four hundred years. They know the Bloom is getting worse. They suspect why. They have not told Ritterstadt.

## What They Want
- **Said:** to observe, record, and be left to their work.
- **Meant:** the network stabilized, a formal relationship with Ritterstadt that gives them sight of what the expeditions find, the Ur-Flan clay tablet translated, and, at the very least, Ritterstadt not making things worse. They are not confident of the last one.

## How They Operate
- **Records.** Four hundred years of Bloom observations. Each Bloom year is a little worse than the last, and each recovery a little less complete. The enclave knows this is not a natural cycle. Something is winding up.
- **No petitions.** Letters to the enclave go unanswered. Formal requests go unanswered.
- **Demonstrated stewardship.** The enclave answers deeds. Protect a spirit-site, mend a damaged stone, or arrive at the standing stones knowing what you are looking at, and a druid will find you.

## Bringing Them In
Two visits to Ehlonna's Mount in a spirit of respectful inquiry open a real relationship. One visit where someone pockets a Bloom crystal as a specimen closes it for a year.

**Giseth** will say she cannot read the tablet well enough to help. Then she will spend three weeks with it and produce a translation that is nine-tenths right. She charges nothing. She expects something in return, and will not name it until later.

## Origin
The enclave keeps Ehlonna's Mount the way the Flan shrine-tenders once kept the binding sites: faithfully, and without the whole theory. The mount is itself a minor node of the old network. The druids inherited the practice of tending it and lost the reason, as the Wolf Nomads did.

## History
- **Four centuries ago.** The enclave's oldest surviving Bloom record.
- **Every Bloom year since.** A record of a slightly worse Bloom and a slightly weaker recovery.
- **568 CY.** The University chartered at Ritterstadt. The enclave sends no envoy and watches.
- **576 CY.** The Blackfrost Bloom returns. For the first time in four centuries of records, crystals grow on the stones near affected ground. The enclave is alarmed and does not yet know what they mean.

## Goals
**Major:**
- Stabilize the network, because its decay is what drives the Bloom.
- Keep Ritterstadt from making things worse.

**Minor:**
- Translate the clay tablet.
- See the expeditions' findings.
- Learn what the Bloom crystals are, and recover the three that went missing from the Assay House.

## Relationships
- **Rivals:** the Regents' Commission, over the Uplands' future and who may remove what.
- **Wary of:** the Old Kerk Mission.
- **Enemies:** the agents of Iuz.
- **Enemies without knowing it:** the Grey Sickle.
- **Kindred practice:** the Wolf Nomads, though the two have never compared notes.

## Hooks
- Giseth arrives at the gate on foot and asks for the three missing Bloom crystals by catalogue number. They have started to hum.
- A druid is seen at the east forest shrine at dawn, leaving an offering beside the Beckenhaus family's.
- The enclave sends a single question by bird to the Warden: *"Who dug at the Wachturm in 525?"*

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - **The second hypothesis.** The druids do not share it: the Quag Mires behave as if they are *answering* something, not merely spreading. The cold has a direction it is moving toward. They call it **the Cold Behind the Cold**.
> - **Eleven sites.** The enclave has found eleven minor-node sites in the Sepia, each with a genius loci, the east forest shrine near Ritterstadt among them. They have told no one in Ritterstadt.
> - **Giseth's price** is the DM's to set. A suggestion: the party's help at Ehlonna's Mount when the Bloom reaches it, asked at the moment the party next needs her most.
