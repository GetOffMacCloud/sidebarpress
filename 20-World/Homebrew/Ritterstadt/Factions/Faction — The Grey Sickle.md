## What They Want
- **Said:** nothing. They are not here.
- **Meant:** to open the Sepia's old graves on old nodes and raise their guardians as a host for Nerull. The Flan judgment circle at Hex G5 and the barrows under the Larch tree line are on their list. A degrading seal is a door.

## How They Operate
- A mendicant arrives, blesses grieving doors with circles of ash, and asks which of the dead have no graves.
- The marked houses lose someone within a night.
- When the cell has what it needs, it leaves a crown and a pack where a village used to be.

## Origin
A cell of Nerull's faithful gathered in Schwartzenbruin around funerary work — the Reaper's priests are welcome where the dead outnumber the living, and the frontier has always buried more than it baptizes. The cell's doctrine is simple: the old graves of the Flanaess are doors, and a degrading seal is a door ajar.

## History
- **Before 576 CY.** The cell works quietly in the south: tending the unburied, copying barrow-lore, waiting. Its patience is its liturgy.
- **576 CY.** The cell opens the Hound-Mother's barrow at the Dreadverge (see [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]]). Brother Ashlock leads; a Sister of the cell is named in his letter. It works.
- **576 CY.** A Historical Society member copies barrow-lore he doesn't understand; the Illuminators fair-copy it; Hesta Varnholm makes a second. The Sickle has its list: the Flan judgment circle at Hex G5, the barrows under the Larch tree line, and more.
- **Harvester 576 CY.** A funeral mendicant of Wee Jas passes through Ritterstadt, asks the Vater which of the dead have no graves, and moves on. Reconnaissance, in a cassock.
- **576 CY.** A sergeant of the Yatil Mountaineers' free company becomes the cell's first man in town — though the town doesn't know it.

## Goals
**Major:**
- Open the Sepia's old graves on the old nodes and raise their guardians as a host for Nerull.
- Find and exploit every degrading seal in the binding network — a failing seal is an open door.
- (Latent.) If the cell learns of the Fiend's Embrace, claim it: as much as Iuz wants it, for opposite reasons.
**Minor:**
- Map the ungraved dead through mendicant visits: ash circles on doors, questions for the Vater.
- Position the sergeant where the company goes.
- Keep the Historical Society's unwitting source unaware of what he copied.
- Stay beneath the notice of Lecturer Vesk, the one true Grave-cleric in town.

## Relationships
- **Enemies:** the druid enclave and the Wolf Nomads (neither knows yet); Lecturer Jorund Vesk, a true Grave-cleric of Wee Jas.
- **Would use, and be used by:** Iuz's agents.
- **Unwitting source:** the Historical Society's archive, from which their barrow list was copied.

## Hooks
- A second ash circle appears on a door in Peat-cutters' Row the week the Larch barrows thaw.
- A Yatil Mountaineer sergeant asks the party to escort him to "his grandmother's grave" on the high moor.
- A Waycombe letter arrives at the Station House: the Dreadverge report, with a postscript about a Sister in Schwartzenbruin.
