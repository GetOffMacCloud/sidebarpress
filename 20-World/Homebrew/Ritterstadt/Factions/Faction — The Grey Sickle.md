# Faction — The Grey Sickle

> The Nerullite cell of Schwartzenbruin that opened the Hound-Mother's barrow at the Dreadverge. It has a list of the Sepia Uplands' old graves, and Ritterstadt sits among them.

Back to [[RG-06 — Factions]] · [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · First seen in [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]]

## What They Want
- **Said:** nothing. They are not here.
- **Meant:** to open the Sepia's old graves on old nodes and raise their guardians as a host for Nerull. The Flan judgment circle at Hex G5 and the barrows under the Larch tree line are on their list. A degrading seal is a door.

## How They Operate
- A mendicant arrives, blesses grieving doors with circles of ash, and asks which of the dead have no graves.
- The marked houses lose someone within a night.
- When the cell has what it needs, it leaves a crown and a pack where a village used to be.

## Relationships
- **Enemies:** the druid enclave and the Wolf Nomads (neither knows yet); Lecturer Jorund Vesk, a true Grave-cleric of Wee Jas.
- **Would use, and be used by:** Iuz's agents.
- **Unwitting source:** the Historical Society's archive, from which their barrow list was copied.

## Hooks
- A second ash circle appears on a door in Peat-cutters' Row the week the Larch barrows thaw.
- A Yatil Mountaineer sergeant asks the party to escort him to "his grandmother's grave" on the high moor.
- A Waycombe letter arrives at the Station House: the Dreadverge report, with a postscript about a Sister in Schwartzenbruin.
