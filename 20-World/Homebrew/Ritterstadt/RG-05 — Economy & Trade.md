# RG-05 — Economy & Trade

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · ← [[RG-04 — Buildings & Services]] · Next → [[RG-06 — Factions]]

Ritterstadt lives on three things: what the lake and the bog give up, what the dwarves and gnomes dig, and what the University pays for. Coin is Perrender — **marks** (gp) and **kleinmarks** (sp) — and scarce; most of the town's trade is in kind, tallied by the Vogt, the Reeve, and Baba Yeska.

## Imports (what the town cannot make)
Salt (the Rhennee sell it dearer than they should), cloth and dye (Yusuf al-Rashad), southern steel, wine, paper (the Archive buys every ream that comes north), glass, horses (there are nine in town and the Ritter owns three).

## Who Trades With Whom
- **Traft** is the market: everything that leaves Ritterstadt by road goes through it, and the Hanselmann Consortium takes a cut.
- **The lake** is the Rhennee's: cheaper than the road, faster, and untaxed if the Warden of the Landing is paid.
- **Clan Irongrip and Clan Brock** buy bog iron and sell finished steel; they do not explain their prices.
- **Kottsmort** sends furs and amber east-to-west and news west-to-east.
- **The Wolf Nomads** have never traded at Ritterstadt. Elif Kayim's arrival would be the first time.
- **The City of Greyhawk** pays in prestige, paper, and the occasional grant. The Regents' Commission pays in relics-wanted.

## Credits and Coin
Students are paid in **credits**, not marks. Credits buy tuition, infirmary care at cost, forge work at cost, and standing; they cannot buy ale. The total credit limit is **600**; extra credit (a Historical Society discovery, a patron's letter of field expertise) sits outside it. Townsfolk are paid in marks, kind, or labour-levy. The two economies meet at the Fiend's Rest, where Karet accepts a University chit for a bed and redeems it against the Bursar at the quarter.

## Hooks
- The brewer's cask-split: the Fiend's Rest is getting the better ale and the Grey Wolf wants to know why.
- A Traft factor offers Hogar twice the Clatspur price for a season's bog iron, delivered to a barge at night.
- Pim Wobblewright's bog-sounders have started reading wrong near the Hush. He wants a party to walk a sounder along the tree line and bring back the numbers.
