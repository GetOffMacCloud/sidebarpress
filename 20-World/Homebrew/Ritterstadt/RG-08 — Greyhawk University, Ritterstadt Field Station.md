# RG-08 — Greyhawk University, Ritterstadt Field Station

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · ← [[RG-07 — Perrenland & the Neighbours]] · Next → [[RG-09 — Faculty & Staff Roster]]

> *The Field Station exists to map the frontier, and this is the department that holds the pens.* — Department of Exploration & Cartography

Greyhawk University is seated in the **City of Greyhawk**. Its Ritterstadt Field Station is the farthest and smallest of its outposts, chartered in 568 CY to study the Sepia Uplands and to train students by sending them into it. The campaign vault's `02 Greyhawk University` import holds the rules the station runs on: departments, majors and minors, student organizations, research grants, publications, tuition and leveling, and the Academic Registry. This chapter is the institution around those rules.

## The Charter
The **Field Station Charter** (568, amended 574) grants the University:
- the right to **enroll** students and **grant credit** for field work;
- the right to hold **land and buildings** for research: the Station House, the Archive, the Long Dormitory, the Assay House, the Forge, the Drill Yard, the Infirmary wing;
- the **Job Board**: commissions posted under the Warden's seal are University business and carry University protection;
- **research property**: Bloom crystals, relics recovered under commission, maps, and field reports belong to the University until released;
- **discipline** over the enrolled, short of high justice, which stays with the Ritter.

In return the University owes the town a physician, a forge, a share of its maps, and students on the wall when the horn sounds twice.

## Students

**Enrollment.** Anyone the Registrar admits. The station's test is survival, not pedigree; the Chancellor's office prefers it did not say so in print. Admission costs a signature on the Scholar's Oath and a first-term bond paid in labour-levy if coin is short.

**Progression.** Students advance by **level**, and the Registrar's **Tuition & Leveling Table** (campaign canon) prices each step in credits.
- **Probationer** — levels 1–2. Admitted, not yet enrolled on the Board. Works the Drill Yard, the Archive, and the gate rota. Quests only in company with an enrolled party.
- **Freshman** — level 3. Undeclared. Attends lectures anywhere.
- **Sophomore** — level 4. Declares a **Major**; first boon.
- **Junior** — level 5. May declare one **Minor** from another department.
- **Senior and beyond** — the station has had nine. Three teach here now.

**Credits.** Students are paid in credits, never coin. Commissions award credits by mission class; maps earn 25 per submission; majors add 10% on related quests. The **credit limit is 600**. **Extra credit** — a Historical Society discovery (50), a patron's letter of field expertise, a Publication the home campus prints — sits outside the limit. Credits buy tuition, infirmary care at cost, forge work at cost, and standing. They do not buy ale. The Bursar redeems Karet's chits at the quarter.

**The Scholar's Oath.** *I shall not steal manuscripts, duel on the quad, set fire to the boathouse (again), or bring livestock into the Archive. I shall attend my lectures, honor my masters, and remember that tuition is non-refundable.* It hangs in the Station House. Nobody reads it before signing. The boathouse clause is the Landing's fault.

## The Academic Calendar
Two **semesters** make a year. For play, a semester turns over after **four sanctioned quests**; benefits that refresh "once per semester" reset at turnover. The year's fixed points:
- **Needfest** — the Needfest Fair; the Warden's address; majors declared.
- **Planting** — first commissions of the year; the Bloom's worst month.
- **Reaping–Harvester** — the Scouts' Field Day; the midsummer census.
- **Patchwall** — the Regents' audit (every second year); Publications submitted.
- **Sunsebb** — the pass closes; the station winters on what it has.

## Discipline
Heard by the Proctor, confirmed by the Warden. Fines in credits; suspension from the Board; expulsion, which returns a student to the Ritter's law as a private person. Standing offences: pocketing Bloom crystals unlogged; taking a map from the Archive without the Scouts' countersign; selling research property; drawing steel inside the palisade; asking a Clatspur dwarf about Bitter Pass politics.

## Student Life
- **Organizations** (campaign canon): the Scouts & Surveyors, the Ramblers' Fellowship, the Polyglot Circle, the Historical Society, the Illuminators' Guild, the Fencing Club, the Curio Club, and the rest listed in `Extracurricular Organizations`. One club per student.
- **Lodging:** the Long Dormitory, forty bunks. Married students and the senior three lodge in town.
- **Mess:** the Fiend's Rest after dark; mutton, always mutton.
- **Rivalries:** the Scouts and the Fencing Club over which discipline keeps parties alive; Antiquities and Letters & Cultures over the Historical Society's archive; Arcane Studies and everyone over lab space.
- **Traditions:** the Hush ale (a free pint for anyone who walked through the Hush — rarely redeemed); the Needfest bonfire on Market Hill; the first-expedition toast at the Fiend's Rest for every party that comes home.

He has not given Dray the word because he is waiting for Varek Doss to tell him whether moving it is survivable. If Doss says yes, the second seal is used within the season.
> - **The Warden** knows about the second seal and has drafted his resignation twice.
> - **The Registrar's sealed files** are sealed on the Chancellor's standing instruction: anything that touches the Founders' Compact, the Embrace, or the tower's sub-floor.
> - **Starting level.** The campaign's house rules say students begin at 1st level while the department index calls Freshmen 3rd level. This book reconciles them with the **Probationer** rank at levels 1–2.
