# RG-01 — History of Ritterstadt

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · Next → [[RG-02 — Government & Law]]

Ritterstadt has two birthdays, and the town celebrates only one of them. The founding legend says the Four Founders built it in 565 CY. The chapel's ledgers say someone has been keeping records on this shore for 87 years. Both are true.

## Before the Founders (player-safe)

**489 CY — Erikasrast.** Perrender fisher-families out of Traft raise a Pholtine waystation on the north shore of Lake Quag where the Sepia tracks meet the water: a chapel to the martyr Erika, a drying-shed, a peat-cutters' row. They call it *Erikasrast* — Erika's Rest. The chapel ledgers begin.

**489–500 CY — The Silent Years.** The ledgers stop. Iggwilv's reign over Perrenland (481–491) reaches even this shore; her garrisons take what they want from the lake villages and the folk scatter into the Uplands or south. When the Witch's power collapses in 491, the shore stays empty for most of a decade. Old folk in Ritterstadt still call the north wind off the Mires *the Witch's breath*.

**500–565 CY — The Thin Hamlet.** Twenty or thirty souls at most: fishers, peat-cutters, a Pholtine chaplain, and a smith when they could keep one. The old tower on the rise above the shore — the Wachturm — is left alone by the frontier rule that says what has stood this long should be let stand. Around **536 CY** a scholar from the City of Greyhawk, **Hassenrath**, winters here, pays for the chapel's stained-glass window, asks too many questions about the tower, and walks north. He does not come back. His notes stay with the chaplain.

## The Founding (player-safe)

**565 CY — The Trek.** Four people leave Eru Tovar and strike north through the Quag Mire margin to the ruins of Cold Stone Keep. They come back to the thin hamlet after their failed attempt at recovering the **Fiend's Embrace**, an ancient demonic artifact, and building the town as a station for the Knights of the Hart against the dangers of the Uplands and the forces of Iuz while keeping watch on the distant Burneal Forest where their former companion, Eglantine.

The Four Founders:
- **Ritter Edmund von Eisfeld** — a Perrender knight-errant with a cantonal land-grant he had never used. He gives the town his name and his line.
- **Magistra Wren Calloway** — a scholar of the City of Greyhawk, Hassenrath's former student. She sees what a field station here could learn.
- **Dagmar of Clan Brock** — a mountain dwarf engineer who plans the palisade and the forge.
- **Tam Hollin** — a halfling guide of the valley floors who knew the Mire margin paths.

**565–566.** The palisade goes up around Erikasrast's chapel and sheds. The Ritter's Hall is raised. The hamlet is renamed **Ritterstadt**.

**568 — The Charter.** Calloway returns to the City of Greyhawk and comes back with a **Field Station Charter** from the University's Board of Regents: the station may enroll students, grant credit, and hold land for research, and the Chancellor underwrites its first five years. The imprint *Department of Frontier Studies* dates from this charter.

**569 — The First Cohort.** Twelve students. Tam Hollin leads the first expedition into the Mires and is lost there; the party returns with two of his maps and none of him.

**571.** Calloway goes south for good to sit on the Regents' Commission. **Rektor Albrecht Weissmantel** arrives as Station Warden. The Fiend's Rest opens its door.

**572.** Ritter Edmund dies in a winter crossing. His son **Konrad** inherits the land-grant, the hall, and a duty his father never fully explained. **Ruprecht Ackermann** is appointed Vogt.

**573 — The Loan.** To build the Long Dormitory and the lake landing, Konrad borrows 800 gp from the Hanselmann Consortium of Traft against the town's timber rights.

**574 — The Moot and the Departments.** The Station Charter is amended: a **Moot** of household heads gets a voice in town matters, and the University formalizes its **eight departments**. **Hogar Irongrip**, an exile of Clan Irongrip, takes the forge.

**575.** Rhennee barge-families winter at the landing and stay. A kin-band of sylvan elves comes down from the Vesve border after trouble there and takes the forest edge. The population passes 150.

**576 — The Bloom Year.** The Blackfrost Bloom returns. Black ice in Planting, undead closer to the wall than anyone remembers. On the night of **18 Planting** something comes out of the margin bog and breaks the eastern gate; two of the watch die holding it. The gate and forty feet of palisade are still under repair in Patchwall. Vethkar Graun of the Hanselmann Consortium arrives to assess the debt. The census, taken at midsummer, counts **200 souls**.

## Hooks
- The Silent Years' missing ledger pages: a Pholtine scribe in Traft claims to hold a copy.
- Tam Hollin's third map, never recovered, marked a dry path to Cold Stone Keep.
- Dagmar of Clan Brock's palisade plans show a fifth bastion that was never built — on the rise where the Wachturm stands.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - **Why the Silent Years are silent.** Iggwilv's garrison did not merely loot Erikasrast. Her agents took an interest in the tower and opened a shaft beside its foundation before withdrawing. The shaft was backfilled by the returning villagers in 500 CY and is the "second entrance" Hanz Korngut has never gone back to. See [[RG-10 — DM Companion]].
> - **Hassenrath's date.** The campaign vault gives both "approximately 490 CY" and "forty years ago" for his visit. This book uses **c. 536 CY**, which fits the window, Vater Sigmund's age, and the surviving ledgers. The 490 figure is treated as a scribal error in the Sepia Uplands history.
> - **Node 4.** The campaign history says the Greyhawk Wars destroyed Node 4 in the Kron Hills. In 576 CY the Wars have not happened. This book's reading: Node 4 was levelled in **571 CY** by a Furyondian road-cutting crew clearing a mound for the Velverdyva trade road — the same effect, a decade earlier, and no one responsible knew what they destroyed.
> - **The Founders' bargain.** Calloway's charter was granted because she promised the Regents the Fiend's Embrace would come to the City of Greyhawk "when safely movable." It never has. The Regents' Commission has not forgotten. See [[RG-06 — Factions]].
