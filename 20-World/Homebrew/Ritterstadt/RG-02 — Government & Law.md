# RG-02 — Government & Law

Back to [[Ritterstadt — Gazetteer (Book)]] · ← [[RG-01 — History of Ritterstadt]] · Next → [[RG-03 — The Census]]

Ritterstadt is **unincorporated frontier**. No canton claims it; the Voormann's tax-collectors stop at Traft. The town governs itself under three instruments, and most trouble in Ritterstadt is a dispute about which of the three applies.

## The Three Instruments

| Instrument | Holder | Governs |
|---|---|---|
| **The Land-Grant** (565) | the Ritter | Land, the palisade, the watch, high justice, the grain store, timber and fishing rights |
| **The Field Station Charter** (568, amended 574) | Greyhawk University, through the Station Warden | Students, faculty, University buildings and land, the Job Board, credits, research property, discipline of the enrolled |
| **The Moot Roll** (574) | the Moot of household heads, through its Speaker | Market days, the common fields and peat-cuttings, the levy of labour for walls and roads, petitions to the Ritter and the Warden |

**The Moot Roll.** The 574 amendment created a Moot of up to **50 seats**, one per enrolled household. Enrolling costs a household a year's labour-levy and a sworn oath on the chapel stone. As of Patchwall 576, **12 households of 50 seats are enrolled** — the number on the Campaign Timepiece. The other households are governed but unrepresented, which is the Moot's standing grievance.

## Law and Justice
- **High justice** (death, maiming, exile) belongs to the Ritter alone, by the Land-Grant. He has passed two sentences of exile and none of death.
- **Low justice** (debt, theft, assault, market fraud) is heard by the Vogt on any day and by the Ritter on court day. Fines are paid in coin, labour, or grain; the Moot Speaker may plead for an enrolled household.
- **University discipline** covers the enrolled. The Proctor of Students hears it; the Warden confirms it. Expulsion returns a student to the Ritter's law as a private person — which is the threat behind every warning.
- **The watch's writ** runs inside the palisade and to the landing. Beyond the wall, there is no law but what the party brings.
- **Oaths** are sworn on the chapel's foundation stone, witnessed by the Vater. Perrenders treat an oath on the stone as binding past death. The Flan among them treat it as binding past the stone.

## The Watch
- **Strength:** Hauptmann Stahlwulf, seven soldiers (one bunk empty since a winter wolf took its holder in the northern pass), two wolfhounds, and the Ritter's armoury: 8 spears, 6 short swords, 4 crossbows, 3 suits of chain (one in need of repair).
- **Rotation:** two on, four off; night watch in two-hour stands. The eastern gate is thin between the second and fourth hour past midnight. Stahlwulf knows.
- **The levy:** every enrolled household owes a man or woman to the wall when the horn sounds twice. The University's students owe nothing by charter and turn out anyway.
- **The breach:** since 18 Planting, the eastern gate is timber-and-rope, and the gap is watched by students on a rota the Warden posts.

## Taxes and Dues
| Due | Who pays | To whom | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hearth-penny | every household | the Vogt | 1 sp per hearth per quarter |
| Fishing tithe | lake boats | the Ritter | one fish in twelve, taken in kind at the landing |
| Peat and timber cuttings | households | the Reeve of Fields | labour-levy, two days a season |
| Market stall | traders | the Moot | 2 cp a day, 1 sp on fair days |
| Landing toll | barges | the Warden of the Landing | 1 sp a hull, 1 gp a cargo |
| University tuition | students | the Registrar | paid in credits earned, never coin (see [[RG-08 — Greyhawk University, Ritterstadt Field Station]]) |

## Hooks
- The Moot wants its other 38 seats filled before Needfest; the Vogt wants the labour-levy first. Someone will hire the party to persuade households one way or the other.
- Court day: a Rhennee bargeman accused of smuggling, a student accused of pocketing Bloom crystals, and a Clan Irongrip emissary who will not say why he has come.
- The empty eighth bunk. Stahlwulf is selective. She might take a PC.

> [!danger] DM Eyes Only
> - **The chest under the dais** holds correspondence between Ritter Edmund and the Hanselmann Consortium that could invalidate Konrad's land-grant. Konrad doesn't know. The Vogt does, and has decided the town is better served by a Ritter in debt than by no Ritter at all. If the Consortium learns of the letters, foreclosure becomes annexation.
> - **The Moot Roll is the Vogt's instrument.** He drafted the 574 amendment so that the labour-levy would rebuild the wall without the Ritter spending coin he didn't have. He has slowed enrollment on purpose: a full Moot could outvote the Ritter's household on the timber rights.
> - **Ewald** in the garrison reports to Iuz's handler at Kottsmort. Stahlwulf checked his story and found nothing. She has started leaving the eastern-gate roster where he can read it, with one deliberate error, to see where the error turns up.
> - **The Station Charter's lapsed clause.** The Regents' five-year underwriting ended in 573. The station has run on credits, grants, and Konrad's loan since. If the Regents' Commission withdraws recognition, the Warden's authority evaporates and the Job Board becomes a tavern corkboard.
