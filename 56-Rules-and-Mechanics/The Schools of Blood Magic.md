# The Schools of Blood Magic

*Every blood mage carries two books. One holds his spells. The other holds his life.*

Blood magic is taught in three schools, each a wizard arcane tradition. They share one tradition of teaching, one kind of book, and one set of costs. This note holds the rules they share. Each school's own note holds its features and spells.

| School | Practitioner | What it does | Whose blood | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cruormancy** | cruormancer | Kills. Purely offensive. | His own | [[Wizard — School of Cruormancy]] |

## The Hemotheurgical Codex

A blood mage carries **two books**: an ordinary spellbook for his ordinary wizard spells, and a **Hemotheurgical Codex** for his blood magic. His master gives him the Codex when he takes up the art.

- **Spells appear; they aren't learned.** Blood spells appear in the Codex, written in its owner's blood, as he gains levels (each school's 2nd-level feature says when). There is no other way to get them. They can't be copied from another book, scribed onto scrolls, taught by anyone but the Codex, or found as loot.
- **One book, ever.** The Codex can't be copied, not even by its owner. A wizard's usual right to copy his own spellbook doesn't apply.
- **It accepts only its owner's blood.** Each morning the blood mage bleeds onto the pages of the spells he wants to prepare (see *The Morning Bleed*). Nobody else's blood feeds it. An oblamancer may spend other people's blood on his spells, but his Codex drinks only his own.
- **Anyone holding its owner's blood can wake its pages.** A vial of it, a bloody bandage, or the mage himself in chains is enough. That is why a lost Codex is a catastrophe.
- **Rituals.** A blood ritual spell, if one is ever written, can be cast from the Codex without the morning bleed.

## The Morning Bleed

Each day, when a blood mage prepares his wizard spells, he **loses hit points equal to the total spell levels of the blood spells he prepares** that day. Cantrips cost nothing.

- The loss can't be reduced or prevented, and it isn't damage. He recovers it normally, through rest, potions, or magical healing.
- A blood spell he didn't bleed for stays blank, and he can't prepare it that day.
- He can't prepare a blood spell if the bleed would reduce him to 0 hit points.

In practice the whole party pays for it: someone spends a healing spell every morning, or the blood mage starts the day hurt. Preparing fewer blood spells is the only way to bleed less.

## Losing the Codex

A Codex is worth more than its owner's life, and the tradition treats it that way.

1. **Recover it.** The blood mage must get the Codex back and **kill everyone who held it or might have read it.** Any of them might have kept a drop of his blood.
2. **Or confess.** If he fails, he must confess to his master. His master uses the blood mage's own blood to destroy the Codex, wherever it is, and everyone near it. **The blood mage doesn't survive.**

There is no third option. A blood mage who runs from his confession is found (see below).

## Always Found

A blood mage **can always be found** by those above him in the tradition. No spell or item that blocks detection or scrying protects him: *nondetection*, *mind blank*, an amulet of proof against detection and location, and every similar effect simply fail.

Only *wish* can hide a blood mage. When it does, his Codex goes blank forever, and he can never cast blood magic again. Masters don't explain why.

## Master and Apprentice

- **One apprentice at a time.** A master trains one apprentice. If the apprentice dies, the master looks for another. If the master dies, the apprentice becomes the master.
- **No orders.** Masters don't command their apprentices. Once an apprentice begins adventuring, the master relies on the quality and breadth of the training he gave. An apprentice owes his master nothing but the secrecy of the art.
- **Someone above.** Masters answer to someone. Apprentices aren't told who, or where.

> [!tip] A player blood mage
> A PC who takes a blood school at 2nd level is an **apprentice**, which means an NPC master found him and gave him his Codex. If that master dies, the PC becomes a master, and learns what masters know.
