# The Price of Power

*Magic remembers everything you take from it, and it always gets paid.*

The Price of Power is a corruption subsystem for **forbidden arcane magic**. A character who touches forbidden power gains **Corruption**. Once the door is open, the arcane caster can strike **bargains**: pulling more out of a spell than it should give, in exchange for more Corruption. As Corruption rises, it leaves **Marks** on the body and the mind, and at 20 the Price takes the character entirely.

> [!summary] At a glance
> **Arcane only** · Corruption comes from forbidden spells, dark tomes, cursed arcane items, and bargains · Any arcane spellcaster with **1 or more Corruption** can spend more of it on a menu of **bargains** · Each bargain after the first in a day costs more · Thresholds at **5 / 10 / 15 / 20** bring Marks and banes · **20 is Claimed** · Once you reach 5, you never drop below it.

> [!info] Rules base
> **2014 Player's Handbook.** Built to sit under any forbidden arcane tradition. It replaces the Stain track of the [[Wizard — School of Cruormancy|School of Cruormancy]] one for one (see *Cruormancy*, below).

## Gaining Corruption

Only **arcane** sources give Corruption. Divine curses, fiendish pacts, and lycanthropy are other stories.

| Source | Corruption |
|---|---|
| Casting a **forbidden spell** of 1st level or higher (a cruormancy spell, or any spell the DM rules forbidden) | 1 per casting |
| Reading a **forbidden tome** for the first time (a cruormantic grimoire, a lich's journal, a book of the Elder Elemental Eye) | 1d4, once per tome |
| Attuning to a **cursed arcane item** | 2 on attunement, then 1 each dawn while still attuned |
| Striking a **bargain** (below) | The bargain's cost |
| A feature that says it adds Stain or Corruption | As stated |

Corruption doesn't fade with rest. Track it on the character sheet where the player can see it. It's meant to be watched.

## Bargains

> *The first one is always cheap.*

Any **arcane spellcaster** (a wizard, sorcerer, warlock, or bard, or an eldritch knight or arcane trickster) who has **at least 1 Corruption** can strike a bargain. A character with 0 Corruption hasn't opened the door yet and can't bargain.

- A bargain costs **no action**, and you take it at the moment its trigger describes.
- You can strike only **one bargain per turn**.
- **The debt compounds.** Each bargain after your first since your last long rest costs **+1 Corruption for every bargain you've already struck** since that rest. The second costs +1, the third +2, and so on.

| Bargain | Base cost | Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Twist the Weave** | 1 | A creature is about to make a saving throw against a spell you cast | It makes the save with disadvantage. |
| **Unbroken Focus** | 1 | You make a Constitution saving throw to maintain concentration | You succeed automatically. |
| **Unbound Casting** | 2 | You cast a spell | The spell can't be countered. |
| **Overreach** | 2 | You cast a spell using a spell slot | The spell is cast as if you'd used a slot one level higher, up to 9th. |
| **Forbidden Recall** | Spell level (min. 1) | You take the action to cast a spell | You cast a spell you don't have prepared, using a slot as normal. A wizard picks from their spellbook. Anyone else picks from their class's spell list, and the spell must be of a level they can cast. |
| **Full Measure** | 3 | You roll damage for a spell | Every damage die for one target counts as rolling its maximum. |
| **Wellspring** | Slot level | Bonus action, once per long rest | You regain one expended spell slot of 5th level or lower. |
| **Refuse the Grave** | 3 | You drop to 0 hit points but aren't killed outright | You drop to 1 hit point instead. |

> [!example] The debt compounds
> Mirela has 6 Corruption. In the first fight of the day she uses **Twist the Weave** (1) and goes to 7. In the second fight, **Overreach** costs 2 + 1 = 3, so she's at 10. Desperate in the third fight, she takes **Refuse the Grave** for 3 + 2 = 5 and goes to 15. That's three bargains in one day, and she went from Marked to Hollowed.

## The Track

| Corruption | Stage | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | **Touched** | No outward sign. You can bargain. *Detect evil and good* registers you faintly, as a desecrated object would. |
| 5 | **Marked** | Roll your first **Mark** (table below). You have disadvantage on Deception checks to hide your practice. Your Corruption can never again drop below 5. |
| 10 | **Hungering** | If you finish a long rest without having struck a bargain or cast a forbidden spell since your previous long rest, you gain 1 level of exhaustion, which can't be removed until you do one. Magical healing from good-aligned divine casters restores only half as many hit points to you. Roll a second Mark. |
| 15 | **Hollowed** | Your hit point maximum drops by 1 for each Corruption above 10. Undead, fiends, and things that shouldn't be awake recognize you on sight. Roll a third Mark. |
| 20 | **Claimed** | The character is lost. Either they become an NPC in service to whatever collected the debt, or they die and rise within three nights as something that hungers. |

### Marks

Roll a d8 at each of Marked, Hungering, and Hollowed. Reroll a duplicate.

| d8 | Mark |
|---|---|
| 1 | Your shadow lags half a heartbeat behind you. |
| 2 | Your eyes lose their color and catch the light like a cat's. |
| 3 | Ink, wine, and blood darken when you touch them. |
| 4 | Candle flames lean away from you. |
| 5 | Animals won't stay in a room with you unless they're bound or trained. |
| 6 | Your voice carries a faint second voice, a word behind your own. |
| 7 | Your skin is cold to the touch, always. |
| 8 | Sigils you never learned surface on your skin when you cast, then fade. |

A *disguise self* or similar illusion hides a Mark, but *true seeing* and *detect evil and good* see through it.

## Paying It Down

Nothing pays the debt cheaply, and **once you reach 5 Corruption, it can never drop below 5**. Above that floor, these are the only ways back:

- ***Greater restoration.*** The spell removes **1d4 Corruption**. A creature can benefit from this only once per tenday.
- **Renunciation.** You destroy a forbidden source you own (burn the grimoire, break the cursed item, unmake the focus) and you mean it. You lose **1d4 Corruption**. Each source counts only once, and a source you could copy again or retrieve doesn't count.
- **Penitence.** At a temple of a deity who opposes the forbidden art (Wee Jas, Pelor, St. Cuthbert, Rao, or Boccob for arcane abuse), you spend **5 days of downtime** under vigil and pay **250 gp** in tithes and reagents. At the end, make a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw**. On a success, you lose **1 Corruption**. On a failure, the gold is spent and you gain **1 level of exhaustion**. You can attempt this only once per tenday, and only at a temple willing to take you.
- ***Atonement.*** An *atonement* granted by a deity who opposes the art, with a quest attached, removes **all Corruption above 5**.

## Cruormancy

When The Price of Power is running, a [[Wizard — School of Cruormancy|cruormancer]] uses this track **instead of the Stain track**:

- Every point of **Stain** the school would give you is **1 Corruption** instead. That includes 1 per leveled cruormancy spell and 2 for each use of *Red Apotheosis*.
- The Price of Power's stages, Marks, and Paying It Down replace the Stain stages and Scouring Stain. **Blood Debt** still works, and it removes 1d4 Corruption.
- A cruormancer opens the door with their first leveled cruormancy spell, so most will be bargaining by their second session.
- Everything else in the school is unchanged: the Blood (B) component, creatures with blood, the Codex and its morning bleed, and the features.

## DM Notes

> [!note] Design intent
> Stain is a tax: you pay it and get nothing back. The Price is a temptation: every point buys something, right when the player wants it most. The escalation is there so the player can feel the slope. One bargain a day is a slow slide. Three bargains in a bad day is a cliff.

> [!tip] Dials
> - **Pace.** For a longer campaign, double the thresholds (10 / 20 / 30 / 40) and the Paying It Down amounts.
> - **Collection.** From Hollowed onward, once per long rest the DM may offer a bargain the character didn't ask for, at no cost. Refusing it costs 1 Corruption. Use it rarely, and only when the stakes are high.
> - **Who collects.** By default the Price is magic's own hunger and has no face. If your campaign wants one, it can be a voice from the Elder Elemental Eye, a lich who wrote the tome, or Iuz's court quietly keeping the ledger.

> [!warning] Table consent
> This is a player-facing temptation system. Explain the track, the floor at 5, and Claimed at 20 before anyone opens the door. The point is for players to choose the slide, not to be ambushed by it.
