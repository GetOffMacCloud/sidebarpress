Back to [[SP-PBS-02-The-Bones-of-the-Dreadverge]]

These are Simple Layout battlemaps: one per tactical encounter, with **1 square = 5 ft**. They are placeholders for playtest and VTT use until finished Inkarnate maps replace them.

## What's on the DM copies only
- **Cut-Trenches:** creature starts (B boar, E elk, H hound; use the APL table in the scene note), both bog pockets, the party entry from the west, and a terrain legend.
- **Three Sisters:** the captives' rite order (1 Brannoc, 2 Wenna, 3 Tam), Ashlock (A), the Barrow Stag (S), hound starts on the 60-ft pack ring, the 20-ft dim-light radius, the bog escape path, and a barrow-chamber inset (20-ft passage to the bier).

## VTT exports
- PNG exports at **100 px/square** (Foundry default) are kept outside the vault. A 30 × 30 map is 3000 × 3000 px; a 36 × 30 map is 3600 × 3000 px. In Fantasy Grounds, set the grid to 100 px.
- For Roll20, scale to 70 px/sq (2100 × 2100 and 2520 × 2100). For print at 1-inch squares, re-export the SVG at 300 px/sq.

## To replace with Inkarnate
- Use one Battlemap-style map per encounter, at the same grid sizes. Paint the trenches and the barrow cut with the Mask Tool ("wall = absence of floor").
- Put trap, bog, and start markers on a **DM Only** object layer, and export both a Player and a DM copy as PNG.
