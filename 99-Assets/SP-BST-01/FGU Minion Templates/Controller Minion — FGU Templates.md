# Controller Minion — FGU Templates (APL 1–20)

Generic **controller minions** for Fantasy Grounds Unity, one for each party level. Rules and signature options: [[Controller Minion]] · Table: [[04 — Minions]] · All types: [[FGU Minion Templates — Index]].

**Paste:** **NPCs → Import Text**, mode **2022 – Monsters of the Multiverse**. Paste one block, then **rename and reskin** it in FGU: name, type, token, and **damage type** (set to bludgeoning by default). It uses the default signature; swap in another from [[Controller Minion]] if you prefer.

```text
Controller Minion (APL 1)
Medium humanoid, unaligned
Armor Class 13
Hit Points 1 (minion)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
10 (+0) 12 (+1) 12 (+1) 12 (+1) 14 (+2) 12 (+1)
Senses passive Perception 12
Languages any one language (usually Common)
Challenge 1 (50 XP) Proficiency Bonus +2
Minion. If the minion takes any damage, it drops to 0 hit points. If it succeeds on a saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, it takes no damage instead.
Actions
Strike. Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 3 bludgeoning damage.
Hamper (Controller). In place of its Strike, the minion targets one creature it can see within 60 feet of it. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or its speed becomes 0 until the end of its next turn.
```
