# SP-PBS-01 — Map Assets

Back to [[SP-PBS-01-The-False-Arrow]]

These are Simple Layout battlemaps: one per tactical encounter, with **1 square = 5 ft**. They are placeholders for playtest and VTT use until finished Inkarnate maps replace them. The Duel Ring has no Player copy; the DM copy carries nothing the players shouldn't see.

## What's on the DM copies only
- **Ravine:** party entry (P), the tiger's den (T) and the mate's start (T2, for 5–6 PCs), the patrol's bodies (B), the three beaters on the rim (S, **scouts**, won't descend), both snow drifts (difficult terrain), the frozen stream (Acrobatics DC 10 or prone), and the 20-ft shale walls (Athletics DC 15).
- **Night Camp:** the guest tent with the party asleep (P), three thug starts (A; the third only at 5–6 PCs), the scout lookout (L), Naran's arrival point with two riders and the bucket line (N; round 3, or round 1 with Sarnai's endorsement), the 5-ft ring of fire once the tent burns (1d6 fire on entering), and the torchlit war camp edge.
- **Duel Ring:** Cligir's throne, Sarnai (S), the party's champion (C), Temur's champion (B, **berserker**), the brazier, and the 30-ft ring. The crowd is a wall: there is no exit until Sarnai raises her hand.

## VTT exports
- PNG exports at **100 px/square** (Foundry default) are kept outside the vault: Ravine 3600 × 1600 px, Night Camp 2400 × 2400 px, Duel Ring 2000 × 2000 px. In Fantasy Grounds, set the grid to 100 px.
- For Roll20, scale to 70 px/sq (2520 × 1120, 1680 × 1680, 1400 × 1400). For print at 1-inch squares, re-export the SVG at 300 px/sq.

## To replace with Inkarnate
- Use one Battlemap-style map per encounter at the same grid sizes. The ravine is a winter cleft: paint the shale walls with the Mask Tool and keep the frozen stream as a distinct texture so the Acrobatics hazard reads at a glance.
- Night Camp wants a felt-tent cluster with a single large guest tent; leave the fire ring as a DM-layer overlay so the Player copy shows the camp intact.
- Put start markers, hazards, and the beaters on a **DM Only** object layer, and export both a Player and a DM copy as PNG. The Duel Ring needs only one export.
